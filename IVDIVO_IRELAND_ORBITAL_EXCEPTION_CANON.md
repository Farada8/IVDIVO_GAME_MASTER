# 1. PRIMARY CANON LAW

Orbital cities and habitats do **not** transfer their closed orbital technology stack to planetary states, governments or ordinary planetary societies as a matter of principle.

This restriction applies across Earth.

The United States, Canada, the wider Eurasian commonwealth/council, China and other planetary powers may become highly advanced through their own development, but they **do not possess the closed orbital technological layer**.

**Ireland is the sole planetary exception.**

For a reason that is currently **UNKNOWN** and must not be invented casually, the orbital cities grant Ireland deep / near-complete access to closed orbital technologies that are unavailable everywhere else on the planet.

This canon concerns orbital-city technology sharing. It does not automatically supersede separate Galactic Confederation Contact law.

---

# 2. IRELAND INSIDE THE WIDER EURASIAN SYSTEM

Ireland remains part of the wider Eurasian political/commonwealth architecture.

However, membership in that larger system does **not** grant other members access to the Irish/orbital technology stack.

The Irish exception is non-transferable unless the Founder later changes canon.

Therefore the wider Eurasian system can be highly developed, successful and technologically sophisticated while Ireland still exists on a qualitatively different technological level.

---

# 3. CIVILIZATIONAL GAP

Ireland is not merely richer, better administered or twenty years ahead of the rest of Earth.

The difference is **qualitative rather than incremental**.

Ireland lives partly according to an orbital-derived technological and civilizational layer that no other planetary society possesses.

Do not reduce this future to improved versions of 2020s infrastructure, transport, housing, bureaucracy, insurance, ordinary roads, ordinary airports or other present-day systems when orbital-derived technology should have transformed the underlying problem.

---

# 4. MULTIVECTOR IRELAND — EXPANSION OF HABITABLE SPACE

Ireland faces enormous demand for residence and limited historical land area.

It does not solve this only by restricting population or by conventional suburban expansion.

It creates new habitable volume in multiple directions.

## CANON habitat types

- supertall vertical cities and megastructures;
- flying / atmospheric cities;
- large subterranean cities designed as normal long-term living environments rather than bunkers;
- submerged and underwater cities with complete artificial atmospheres and full civic life;
- floating Atlantic cities;
- artificial oceanic islands;
- mobile oceanic settlements;
- integrated surface-ocean, underwater, underground, vertical and atmospheric habitats.

These settlements are not isolated showcase projects. They form one connected Irish civilizational network.

Future Ireland remains historically rooted in the island, but is no longer spatially an island civilization. Its effective living territory extends downward into the crust, outward across and beneath the Atlantic, upward into the atmosphere and onward into orbital space.

---

# 5. IRELAND AS PLANETARY SPACEPORT CIVILIZATION

Ireland is a planetary spaceport civilization rather than merely a country that possesses a spaceport.

The transport, logistics, habitation and civic systems linking ground, underground, ocean, atmosphere and orbit are integrated into ordinary life.

A person can move through a continuous Irish civilizational stack:

`SUBTERRANEAN IRELAND -> HISTORIC / SURFACE IRELAND -> VERTICAL CITY LAYERS -> ATLANTIC FLOATING / UNDERWATER CITIES -> ATMOSPHERIC CITIES -> ORBITAL IRISH NODES -> LUNAR / EXTRAPLANETARY IRISH NODES`

Exact travel times and mechanisms remain TO DESIGN and must obey later technical passports. The key canon point is that these layers function as one connected civilization rather than as isolated futuristic attractions.

---

# 6. DUBLIN — NOT A PRESERVED 21ST-CENTURY CITY

Future Dublin must not be written by default as rain, traffic, two-storey streets and a few futuristic gadgets added on top.

Historic Dublin survives as a protected and lived-in cultural layer, but it exists inside a much larger three-dimensional urban system.

Dublin may include:

- preserved historic districts and old civic fabric;
- immense vertical urban structures rising above them;
- deep subterranean residential, civic and transport layers;
- orbital-transfer infrastructure integrated into the metropolitan system;
- atmospheric access nodes and high-altitude settlements;
- direct links to Atlantic floating and underwater cities;
- visible or culturally present orbital-city infrastructure overhead.

The visual and social contrast between the old city and the orbital-derived city is part of the setting. Old stone, Georgian streets, churches, pubs and historic institutions can remain real without defining the technological level of the whole metropolis.

---

# 7. SMITH ARRIVING IN DUBLIN

When Smith travels to Dublin in the future saga period, he already knows that he is entering the most technologically advanced planetary society on Earth.

He is **not** surprised that Ireland is futuristic. He is familiar with its reputation, infrastructure and strategic status.

The dramatic value of his arrival comes from scale, lived detail, jurisdiction, cultural contrast and what Ireland has become — not from a naive 'man from the past sees technology' reaction.

Smith may pass through a city in which the historic Dublin layer remains physically present while, above and below it, the Irish system continues vertically toward atmospheric and orbital settlements.

His Dublin line with Enia therefore takes place inside a civilization that visibly connects old Earth history with the most advanced planetary future.

---

# 8. ORBITAL AND LUNAR CONTINUITY

Above the planetary layers exist orbital cities and habitats with their own jurisdictions and identities.

Ireland has privileged technological and infrastructural connection to this orbital layer, but this does **not** mean that all orbital cities are politically Irish.

Beyond orbital space, human settlements may also exist on or around the Moon and later other bodies. Some Irish-linked nodes, communities, educational facilities, research installations or settlements may exist there.

Exact ownership, sovereignty and chronology remain TO DESIGN. Do not silently turn every orbital or lunar city into Irish territory.

---

# 9. POST-MONEY SOCIAL DIRECTION

Ireland becomes the first major planetary society to move beyond money as the primary mechanism of everyday access and distribution.

The exact allocation, scarcity, contribution, reputation, rights and governance mechanisms remain TO DESIGN.

Do not reduce this to a simplistic single social-credit score.

---

# 10. GLOBAL EFFECT

Because no other planetary society combines access to the closed orbital technology stack with Ireland's new social order and multidirectional habitat system, Ireland becomes the strongest migration, cultural and civilizational magnet on Earth.

People seek to move there from the United States, Canada, Australia, Eurasia and other advanced societies not because those societies are necessarily poor or failed, but because Ireland represents a genuinely different level of life.

Tourism, study, temporary residence and permanent participation must be distinguished. Demand to live inside the Irish system greatly exceeds the capacity to absorb everyone immediately, even while Ireland continually creates new living volume through vertical, atmospheric, subterranean, floating and underwater cities.

---

# 11. WORLD-MAP PRINCIPLE

The future Earth must not be mapped only as modern nation-states with upgraded technology.

The saga world map has at least three simultaneous dimensions:

1. **PLANETARY POLITICAL MAP** — old and transformed states, including the United States, Canada, a larger Eurasian commonwealth/council, Ukraine and other planetary powers.
2. **VERTICAL / HABITAT MAP** — underground, surface, oceanic, atmospheric and orbital human settlement layers.
3. **TECHNOLOGY-ACCESS MAP** — ordinary planetary technological development versus closed orbital technology, with Ireland as the sole planetary exception currently established in canon.

Ireland is therefore disproportionately important on the world map despite its small historical land area.

---

# 12. UNKNOWN / DO NOT PREMATURELY SOLVE

Why the orbital cities made Ireland the sole planetary exception remains a major world mystery / future story line.

Do not explain it by default as Irish moral superiority, simple political favoritism or a convenient treaty.

The reason must be story-earned and Founder-approved.

---

# 13. CONTINUITY LOCKS

1. Orbital cities do not generally give their closed technology stack to planetary societies.
2. Ireland is the sole planetary exception currently established in canon.
3. No other Earth state or bloc may silently receive equivalent orbital technology without a new Founder decision.
4. Ireland remains part of the wider Eurasian political/commonwealth architecture while retaining non-transferable privileged orbital access.
5. Ireland expands through vertical, atmospheric, subterranean, floating Atlantic and underwater urban environments.
6. Future Dublin is a multi-layer planetary-spaceport metropolis; the historic city survives but does not define the whole technological level.
7. Smith already knows Ireland's extraordinary status when he travels to Dublin; he should not react as though discovering an unknown futuristic country for the first time.
8. Irish technological privilege does not automatically make every orbital or lunar settlement politically Irish.
9. Do not describe future Ireland primarily through extrapolations of 2020s transport, housing, bureaucracy or infrastructure when orbital-derived systems should have transformed the problem.
10. Exact reason for the Irish exception remains UNKNOWN until Founder-approved story development resolves it.
