This proves bounded transferability of the sensor logic to a second book fixture. It does NOT prove literary superiority, reader response, market response, or universal correctness.

## DEDUPE AGAINST CURRENT MECHANISMS
1. Existing audio `SECOND-PROJECT REPLICATION PROTOCOL` already establishes the general evidence law: independent second project, positive regression, unauthorized-change/false-positive control, human evidence before domain promotion. It is audio-specific and should NOT be reused as book implementation.
2. Existing audio cross-project promotion schema likewise proves the global policy that duplicate/synthetic/machine-only evidence is insufficient for promotion. Do not copy its audio fields into book state.
3. Current Self-Improvement v2 already owns candidate lifecycle, evidence separation, promotion/readback and WIP governance.
4. Therefore MF-C03 should enter as a **book-domain sensor module/contract under the existing system**, not as a parallel meta-engine.

## ALLOWED APPLICATION
MF-C03 may be used immediately as bounded QA on WORKING book material when:
- exact book/source revision is known;
- the test cannot silently modify story/canon;
- positive findings still require Story/Character/Human Scene interpretation before prose repair;
- a healthy no-change control is present where practical;
- deterministic signal is never called Human Signal.

## PROMOTION GATE
Before any claim that MF-C03 is a universal book-domain authority, require at minimum:
1. one additional independent book/genre replication beyond LESSON ZERO + BLOODBOUND;
2. known-positive and healthy-negative controls;
3. measured false-positive rate and manual override count;
4. at least one human/editor adjudication showing that flagged state/causality failure corresponds to a real reader-visible/story defect;
5. no-change control proving accepted prose is not needlessly rewritten;
6. fresh-main regression/readback and explicit integration decision.
