## RECONCILED STORY CORE

**Hero:** Ollie.

**Want:** become genuinely useful by helping commission a real orbital home people will actually live in.

**Private want:** become the person people call when something real breaks; stop feeling like his competence lives in side tasks while Ethan gets visible responsibility.

**Why now:** first Youth Cohort orbital residency coincides with a real housing-capacity deadline and commissioning of a new residential ring.

**Opposition:** coupled mixed-occupancy environmental failure; relocation deadline; certification rules; resident conflict; local distrust; evidence hierarchy; Ollie’s need to prove himself.

**Wrong strategy:** produce the cleanest model and treat resident/body reports as secondary to certified instrumentation.

**Price:** credibility, placement status, friendship time, clean launch and public recognition.

**Midpoint:** Ollie’s analysis materially supports limited opening; real mixed occupancy produces an unmodelled transition and the ring fails.

**Low point:** ring closed, Ollie’s interpretation implicated, residents distrust outsiders, relocation still required.

**Climax:** with narrow `SAFE-STATE SEAL` authority, Ollie physically isolates a local segment before fixed sensors cross automatic threshold because resident embodied warnings match the corrected lag model.

**Immediate cost:** residents turned back; local migration throughput collapses; one family loses the expected room; Ollie appears to have overreacted; public commentary blames a youth trainee before confirmation.

**Payoff:** delayed fixed sensors confirm the trend; isolation prevented propagation.

**Final moral action:** Ollie refuses language claiming full-ring commissioning succeeded.

**Resolution:** safe segments open and become homes; unsafe sections stay closed with redesign path; relocation succeeds imperfectly; Ollie becomes trusted for acting responsibly inside uncertainty.
