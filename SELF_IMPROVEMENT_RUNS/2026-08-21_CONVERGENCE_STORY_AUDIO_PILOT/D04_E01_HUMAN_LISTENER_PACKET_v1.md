# D04 SEVEN NIGHTS — E01 HUMAN LISTENER PACKET v1

Status: READY_FOR_FUTURE_HUMAN_EVIDENCE / NOT EXECUTED.

## First-listen protocol
- Use the current assembled E01 audio only after clean-dialogue + sound/mix gates pass.
- One uninterrupted listen.
- No synopsis, glossary, character list, clue explanation or coaching before first response.
- Collect answers before replay.
- Record listener device: headphones / phone speaker / other.
- Human responses are evidence; model predictions are not substitutes.

## Questions — first response
1. Who do you think the main character is? Describe her job/function in one sentence.
2. What is the central problem you think she is trying to understand or stop?
3. What did you understand about the repeating-cycle / sequence rule? State it in your own words.
4. What, if anything, made you suspicious of Rowan at this point? Separate what the story proved from what you merely suspect.
5. What did you understand from the technical/medical dialogue without replay?
6. Did NORMAL TRANSFER and SECOND TRANSFER sound meaningfully different? If yes, what extra change did you hear?
7. Which two principal voices were easiest to distinguish? Which were hardest?
8. Did any character sound villainous before the evidence justified that impression? Who and why?
9. Did Lena sound clinical/professional, mystical/prophetic, panicked, or something else?
10. Did Rowan sound like a professional with status, an alpha-romance caricature, suspicious, neutral, or something else?
11. Did the relationship material feel chosen and bounded, or did performance imply intimacy/authority not earned by the text?
12. At what exact moment were you most confused? Describe what you thought was happening.
13. At what moment did you most want to keep listening?
14. What fact/clue do you expect to matter later?
15. What question are you emotionally waiting to have answered next?

## Confidence
For Q1–Q6, listener marks confidence 1–5 after answering. Low-confidence correct guesses are not equivalent to clear comprehension.

## Pass/fail use
This packet does not define one global score. Diagnose by failure surface:
- character identity failure -> casting/performance/edit first;
- rule/clue comprehension failure -> clean dialogue/edit/performance before music;
- transfer-state failure -> engineering prototype/sound design;
- premature villain coding -> casting/direction;
- romance/power distortion -> pair performance/direction, and source text only if repeated after performance repair;
- widespread confusion at same story fact across clean-dialogue and mixed versions -> story/source review may be reopened only after localization.

## Protected rule
Do not coach the listener into the intended answer and then count the corrected answer as first-pass evidence.
