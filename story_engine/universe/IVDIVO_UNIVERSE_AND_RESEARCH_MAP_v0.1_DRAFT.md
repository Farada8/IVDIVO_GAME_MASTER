## 1. Why this map exists

The story should not teach IVDIVO as a lecture. It should open a question the listener wants to investigate.

Core cycle:

STORY -> ANOMALY -> HUMAN COST -> PARTIAL EXPLANATION -> BIGGER MYSTERY -> RESEARCH DOOR.

The story answers only what the hero needs now. The research layer shows where ideas come from, which source systems exist, where they overlap, where they differ, and what remains open.

## 2. Approved spatial architecture

[APPROVED / WORLD-002]
The universe uses two different coordinates.

### A. Vertical ascent
1. Physical World.
2. Thin World.
3. Metagalactic World / Metagalactic Spirit-World in DEC-016 wording.
4. Fiery World.
5. Synthesis World.

### B. Orbital parallel worlds
Confirmed names in WORLD-002: Hell, Paradise, Aidia, Demonic World, Omar World. Exact mappings, motion parameters, and complete composition remain TO-VERIFY.

A vertical world is not merely a stronger version of the previous one. It changes perception, action, responsibility, and consequence. An orbital world is not a next ascent stage; it is an independent parallel reality.

## 3. Separate axes that are not worlds

[APPROVED]
Human Parts, Departments, IVOAvatars, Statuses, Initiations, authorities, Instruments, and Forms are not worlds. They interact with worlds but form separate coordinates.

For narrative design this means a hero can simultaneously:
- be located in one world;
- have one Status;
- have a different practical competence;
- have or lack an Initiation;
- hold a service function;
- possess limited authority;
- use a specific Instrument or Form.

These must not collapse into one generic "hero level".

## 4. Five worlds as five types of discovery

### 4.1 Physical World

[APPROVED]
Bodies, territories, materials, resources, objects, buildings, visible manifestations of threats, and concrete costs of decisions.

Narrative function: the audience enters IVDIVO through a familiar world. The listener should first care about a person, then encounter the impossible.

Research questions for youth:
1. What do different philosophical and religious systems call a human being: body, consciousness, soul, spirit, layered being?
2. Why do many traditions distinguish visible form from invisible cause?
3. Can a physical event be interpreted as a manifestation of another level in those systems?
4. What does incarnation mean across different traditions?
5. Where does a scientific question end and a philosophical model begin?

Source families to compare: IVDIVO; Blavatsky; Mahatma Letters; philosophical/religious models of the human; modern consciousness research only as a separately labelled scientific layer.

### 4.2 Thin World

[APPROVED]
Hidden states, images, links, Echoes, Distortions, true and false information, invisible causes of events. WORLD-002 working verb: distinguish and reveal.

Narrative function: first major rupture of the ordinary worldview. The hero detects what others do not, but cannot yet know whether it is truth, distortion, external influence, or misperception.

Do not reduce the Thin World to decorative mist. It must create its own tasks: discrimination, verification, causal tracing, and risk of false interpretation.

Research questions:
1. What do different sources mean by subtle, astral, psychic, or invisible levels?
2. Do those terms actually describe the same model?
3. How do traditions discuss dream, image, thought, telepathy, symbol, and post-mortem states?
4. How can a symbolic experience be distinguished from a claim about external reality?
5. Why is discernment so important in many source traditions?
6. What bodies or modes of perception are attributed to a human beyond the physical level?

Sources: Blavatsky; Alice Bailey; Agni Yoga; Daniil Andreev; IVDIVO internal corpus. Keep systems separate.

### 4.3 Metagalactic Spirit-World

[APPROVED / WORLD-002 + DEC-016]
Systems, large-scale relations, Departments, flows, agreements, authorities, coordination of territories and worlds. DEC-016 adds that the Thin World leads vertically only into the Metagalactic Spirit-World; no direct Thin -> Fiery or Thin -> Synthesis transition exists.

Narrative function: a personal mystery is reclassified as part of a larger system. The hero discovers that events involve not only beings but Departments, services, agreements, powers, territories, hierarchical relationships, and conflicts of responsibility.

This is the natural world for introducing Hierarchy through action: who may act, why, within which domain, and to whom they answer.

Research questions:
1. What is Hierarchy in different traditions, and why is it not necessarily a simple power ladder?
2. How does spiritual hierarchy differ from administrative office?
3. What does initiation mean in source systems: knowledge, access, state change, duty, responsibility?
4. How do different traditions describe Teachers, Brotherhood, Mahatmas, Hierarchs, and service?
5. Can a person know a law without having authority to represent Hierarchy?
6. Why should higher access increase responsibility rather than merely power?
7. What source systems describe planetary, solar, and larger scales of organization?

Sources: Mahatma Letters; Alice Bailey; Agni Yoga; Blavatsky; IVDIVO internal corpus. Similar terminology must not be treated as proof of identical systems.

### 4.4 Fiery World

[APPROVED]
Fire, purification, remelting, destruction of obsolete Form, irreversible transformation, and responsibility for change.

Narrative function: knowledge is no longer sufficient. The hero must endure an irreversible change. The transition has a personal cost: loss of old Form, old mode of action, old safety, or old identity.

Research questions:
1. Why is fire associated with both destruction and transformation across many traditions?
2. What does Agni Yoga call the Fiery World, and what roles do Heart and Hierarchy play there?
3. What roles does fire play in theosophical and Bailey models?
4. How does symbolic fire differ from a claim about an independent world?
5. Why is transformation often linked to readiness and responsibility?

Sources: Agni Yoga; Alice Bailey; IVDIVO; comparative Blavatsky material, with source terms separated from project adaptation.

### 4.5 Synthesis World

[APPROVED]
The highest world of connection and creation: common Forms and laws, combination of worlds, Parts, Instruments and authorities, creation of a new configuration of reality.

Narrative function: not a final "power level" but a transition from investigating existing worlds to responsibility for creating and coordinating new configurations.

Research questions:
1. What does IVDIVO mean by Synthesis and how is it different from merely combining elements?
2. How do philosophical traditions describe unity and plurality?
3. What does it mean to move from knowing a world to participating in its transformation?
4. What limits must creation have so it does not become arbitrary?
5. How do Parts, Instruments, Forms, Fires, authority, and responsibility relate?

Primary source basis: IVDIVO internal corpus. Other systems are comparative, not automatic canon.

## 5. Orbitals as expansion, not ladder

[APPROVED]
Orbitals are independent parallel worlds. They may have their own inhabitants, factions, economies, threats, alliances, temptations, and alternate routes. They are not automatically lower or higher.

For narrative expansion this creates four movement types:
1. horizontal movement through a world territory;
2. vertical movement along the five-world axis;
3. orbital movement into a parallel reality;
4. campaign movement through consequences that change routes and inter-world relations.

Research rule: do not invent a detailed orbital before source verification. Each requires a passport: vertical-world linkage; principle of existence; inhabitants; resources; threats; entry/exit; physical-world influence; source basis; IVDIVO canon vs comparative source vs project synthesis.

## 6. Initiation as a primary research axis

[CANON BOUNDARY]
WORLD-002 explicitly says Initiation is not a world. Exact formal definitions and levels must be extracted from the IVDIVO corpus and related sources rather than freely invented.

For youth-facing stories, Initiation should be revealed through questions:
- What changed after Initiation?
- What can the person now perceive?
- What can they withstand?
- What action became available?
- What responsibility was acquired?
- Can capability exist without right of use?
- Can access or rights be lost while some competence remains?
- Who confirms Initiation and on what basis?
- Can an old classification system misread a new type of human?

These are research questions, not final canon answers.

## 7. Hierarchy as something the audience wants to investigate

Wrong approach: a ten-minute explanation of how Hierarchy is structured.

Working approach: the hero faces a case where two people hold different kinds of right, status, knowledge, or responsibility. Understanding the actual structure becomes necessary to act correctly.

Research door after the story:
- What do different sources call Hierarchy?
- Do they distinguish office, attainment, initiation, and authority?
- Why is service linked to responsibility?
- How is the Teacher-student relation described?
- What counts as abuse of right?
- Are loss and restoration of access described?
- What happens to hierarchy when the human being itself changes?

## 8. Youth-facing delivery formula

Each major narrative block has three layers.

LAYER A — STORY
A living hero, problem, relationships, risk, action, adventure, discovery.

LAYER B — UNIVERSE REVEAL
One or at most a few IVDIVO laws that directly change the plot.

LAYER C — RESEARCH DOOR
After the episode/chapter: a short research path for listeners who want to go deeper.

Example end card:
"Research 01: What do different traditions mean by the Thin World?"
- 5-minute audio introduction;
- concept map;
- 3–5 primary/research sources;
- comparison questions;
- block: "IVDIVO internal model";
- block: "what remains disputed or unknown".

## 10. Expansion order for the narrative franchise

[OUR SYNTHESIS — not canon]
Preferred reveal order for a cold audience:

1. Physical anomaly — the impossible appears before the cosmology.
2. Thin cause — first access to an invisible level; discernment matters more than power.
3. Initiation and the price of access — seeing does not automatically mean having a right to act.
4. Hierarchy and Metagalactic Spirit-World — the local mystery expands into Departments, authority, worlds and responsibility.
5. Orbital expeditions — parallel realities cannot be arranged on a simple good/bad or higher/lower ladder.
6. Fiery transformation — decisions become irreversible and Form itself changes.
7. Synthesis — characters move from discovering worlds to responsibility for joining, restoring, or creating configurations.

This is a franchise-scale reveal map, not a requirement that every book traverse every stage.

## 11. Canon collision to resolve

WORLD-002 defines the vertical:
Physical -> Thin -> Metagalactic -> Fiery -> Synthesis.

DEC-017 says that in the Metagalactic Spirit-World a character attains Absolute Fire and, after confirmation, may transition to the Synthesis World.

The current documents do not explicitly say whether this is:
- a special route bypassing the Fiery World;
- a later decision partially modifying WORLD-002;
- a game mechanic that does not change the cosmological vertical;
- or wording requiring clarification.

Do not silently choose an interpretation. Mark OPEN / TO-RESOLVE until a founder decision.

## 13. Success criterion

After the first story, a young listener does not need to know the whole IVDIVO system. They should:
1. remember one strong human story;
2. understand one working law of the world;
3. sense a much larger architecture behind it;
4. want to ask a question of their own;
5. have a simple route to sources and independent research.

If the story explains everything, the mystery is dead. If it explains nothing, there is nothing to investigate. The target is controlled knowledge deficit: enough to understand the event, not enough to close the universe.
