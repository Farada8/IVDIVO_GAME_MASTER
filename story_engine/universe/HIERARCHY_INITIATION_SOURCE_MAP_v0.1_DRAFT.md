## 1. Initiation — first source-grounded definition

[IVDIVO SOURCE]
In `01ФЧС-2013-09-21-22-Харьков-Сердюк-В.-Книга`, Initiation is explicitly explained as the capacity to contain/assimilate a certain Light after sufficient maturation. The source further says the task of the Initiations is to carry Light associated with presences and, by containing that Light, to be able to act in attunement with those expressions. It also calls Initiations "one of the stages of growth" and a method within Hierarchy.

Therefore the minimum source-grounded model is:

INITIATION = a growth/access/containment coordinate tied to the capacity to contain a definite Light and act in correspondence with it.

This is stronger than the earlier vague project wording "access level" but must not yet be expanded into a complete formal definition without more source passages.

## 2. Initiation is not the whole person and not the same as Parts

[IVDIVO SOURCE]
The 2018 practical corpus explicitly distinguishes acting by Parts from acting with Initiations/Statuses: a person can work with Parts while "not engaging either Initiations or Statuses." This supports separate coordinates rather than one general level.

The Atlas/Paradigm tables also place Parts, Initiations, Statuses, Creating Syntheses, Synthesisness, Powers/Authorities of Perfections, Hierarchizations, and Ivdivost as distinct named positions in a larger sequence.

[PROJECT CANON]
WORLD-002 independently confirms that Parts, Statuses, Initiations, authorities, Instruments and Forms are not worlds and form separate axes.

## 3. Competence

[IVDIVO SOURCE]
`03СиИВО-2025-03-01-02-Зеленогорск-Шайдулина-И.-Практики` gives an unusually useful direct statement: competence includes "our Initiations, Statuses, rights, beginnings, abilities, skills of Synthesis."

This means competence in the source is not merely job skill. It is a composite field that can include multiple inner and operative coordinates.

[IVDIVO SOURCE]
`1-Генезис...2024` further distinguishes "human life", "competent life", "authoritative/full-authority life" and an "eternal" life with the Father. Preparation by Parts and competences enters the authoritative life, but authoritative life itself is identified with authorities/powers — an expression into which the Father has introduced the person.

[OUR SYNTHESIS]
For narrative design, this supports a useful non-equivalence:

PARTS ≠ INITIATION ≠ STATUS ≠ COMPETENCE AS A COMPOSITE ≠ AUTHORITY/POWERS ≠ SERVICE ROLE.

But the exact algebra of these relations is not yet canonically formalized.

## 4. Status

[IVDIVO SOURCE]
The corpus repeatedly places Status separately from Initiation and separately from later coordinates such as Creating Syntheses, Synthesisness, Powers of Perfections and Hierarchizations. Practical materials also speak of concrete statuses alongside Initiation and competence.

[OPEN]
A clean primary-source sentence of the form "Status is ..." has not yet been found in the reviewed material. Therefore do NOT define Status merely as social rank, title, office, or access level.

Working research question:
What does Status designate in IVDIVO that Initiation does not?

## 5. Hierarchy

[IVDIVO SOURCE]
The 2013 corpus describes a historical model in which Hierarchy gave planetary and solar Initiations and explicitly links Initiation with Hierarchy. It also presents higher Initiation as relevant to hierarchical relation and describes Teachers and Hierarchs as positions within that structure.

[IVDIVO SOURCE]
The 2024 Genesis material treats "human life in Hierarchy" as a distinct mode of realization and repeatedly requires a concrete "дело" — something the person actually does in Hierarchy. It links this to "иерархизация бытия" and later to determination of one's hierarchical realization.

This suggests Hierarchy is not merely a chart of superior beings; in the corpus it is also a domain of lived realization, relation, work, and responsibility.

[OPEN]
Formal source definition still needed:
- what constitutes Hierarchy ontologically;
- what Hierarchization adds beyond Initiation and Status;
- relation between Hierarchy, office/department, avataric function, and authority;
- whether multiple simultaneous hierarchical relations/jurisdictions are source-grounded or later project synthesis.

## 6. Hierarchization

[IVDIVO SOURCE]
The Atlas/Paradigm sequence gives `Иерархизации` as a separate position after Powers/Authorities of Perfections and before Ivdivost. The 2024 Genesis corpus uses "Иерархизация бытия" in the context of human life in Hierarchy.

[OPEN]
Do not reduce Hierarchization to promotion in an organizational hierarchy. Its formal operation must be extracted from primary IVDIVO sources.

## 8. First robust distinctions for story/research use

The following are now supported enough to use as QUESTIONS/CONFLICTS, but not all as final definitions:

1. Parts are not Initiations or Statuses.
2. Initiation is a distinct growth/containment coordinate and is tied in an early source to containing Light.
3. Status is separate from Initiation, but its exact formal definition remains open.
4. Competence can include Initiations, Statuses, rights, beginnings, abilities and Synthesis skills; it is broader than ordinary skill.
5. Authorities/powers are treated separately from competence and are tied to an authoritative mode of life/action.
6. Hierarchy is connected to Initiation but also to lived realization and concrete work.
7. Hierarchization is a separate coordinate and must not be equated with office promotion.

## 9. What this changes for youth-facing IVDIVO

[OUR SYNTHESIS]
The first public educational hook should NOT begin with a hierarchy chart. It should begin with a contradiction that forces the distinction:

A person can perceive/contain something by Initiation, have a certain Status, possess competence, and still face a separate question of authority and hierarchical realization.

The research path then asks:
- What did the source actually call Initiation?
- Why are Status and Initiation listed separately?
- What is included in competence?
- What changes when a person enters a mode of authority?
- What does it mean to live/act in Hierarchy rather than merely know about it?

This gives young audiences an investigation rather than a catechism.
