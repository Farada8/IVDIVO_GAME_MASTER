## 1. Exact eight-depth source chain found in Paradigma T3

Paradigma T3 explicitly gives eight levels of human preparation/development:

1. Human — Parts
2. Initiate — Initiations
3. Servant — Statuses
4. Hypostasis — Creating Syntheses
5. Teacher — Synthesisnesses
6. Lord — Powers of Perfections
7. Avatar — Hierarchizations
8. Father — Ivdivosts

The text states that Initiation/Status are not abstract titles: they express depth of mutual organization with kinds and levels of matter and concrete abilities/preparations.

This is a SOURCE MODEL and must not be converted automatically into project WORLD-002 or an RPG ladder.

## 2. Initiation

2013 source: Initiation is described through containing a definite Light after maturation sufficient to assimilate it; the purpose is to carry that Light and act in attunement with it.

Paradigma T1 additionally describes Initiation as forming qualitative energy/light/spirit/fire capacity of the human.

## 3. Status

Paradigma T1 links the Servant evolution to the Status of assimilation of Beginnings of Creation. Status therefore is not safely reducible to social prestige or office title.

## 4. Creating Synthesis

Paradigma T1: the Hypostasis evolution is tied to Creating Synthesis; systems of Beginnings of Creation contained, accumulated and applied by a human are synthesized into a whole Creating Synthesis with external and internal creative effect.

## 5. Synthesisness

Paradigma T1 ties Teacher to Synthesisness and describes it as coordination/realization of metagalactic processes and mutual coordination of human and Metagalaxy. Another T1 passage links synthesisness of reproduced properties in the human with Love.

## 6. Powers of Perfections

Paradigma T1: Lord evolution develops Powers of Perfections; result is mastery of metagalactic matter and management of it.

Paradigma T2: when a person develops/perfects specific capabilities, corresponding powers/authorities are fixed on the basis of those perfections.

This supports keeping capability/perfection and authority/power related but not identical.

## 7. Hierarchization

Paradigma T1 describes Hierarchization as a constant application of direct Will of IVO with corresponding duties by the human as Avatar.

Paradigma T3 also uses hierarchization more broadly for organization of systems/complexes of fire-images and for evolutionary organization.

Therefore personal Avataric Hierarchization and general structural hierarchization must be distinguished by context.

## 8. Ivdivost

Paradigma T3 contains a direct contextual definition: Ivdivost is a set of multiple conditions synthesized by corresponding fire-image phenomena within a defined cloud environment.

The same volume places Father at the eighth level, Ivdivost, and describes Father as free creation of IVDIVO conditions rather than merely existence under those conditions.

This is currently the strongest source-grounded clue for the function of Ivdivost.

## 9. IVDIVO versus Hierarchy — 2024 update

A 2024 Genesis source explicitly separates operational domains:

- in IVDIVO: official/job authorities and action with the Father;
- in Hierarchy: interaction/work with citizens and public human realization of the Avatar;
- service is in IVDIVO, while people know the person through Hierarchy.

This is a major contemporary distinction. It means `office authority`, `hierarchical realization`, and `public human work` cannot be collapsed into one coordinate.
