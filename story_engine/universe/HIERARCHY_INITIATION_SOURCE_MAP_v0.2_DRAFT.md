## 3. Status — major v0.2 update

[IVDIVO SOURCE]
The 2023 Second Synthesis practice explicitly calls four named Statuses the four standards/competences of that Synthesis:
- First Metaplanetary Status;
- First IVDIVO Metaplanetary Status;
- First Metagalactic Status;
- First IVDIVO Metagalactic Status.

The practice then places separate packages of Beginnings of Syntheses into each separate Status and later refers to transformation of the four new Statuses.

[SOURCE-SUPPORTED INFERENCE]
Status therefore cannot safely be reduced to ordinary social rank, job title, prestige or a generic access level. In this source it behaves as a structured, scale/context-specific competence/standard-bearing coordinate that can receive internal content and be transformed.

[OPEN]
A clean abstract primary-source formula `Status = ...` is still missing. We still need to determine:
- what exactly a Status fixes or expresses;
- why Status is structurally after Initiation;
- whether Status can exist without corresponding Initiation;
- whether Status can be lost, transformed, inherited or restored;
- relation between personal Status and office/service role.

## 6. Powers / Authorities — major v0.2 update

[IVDIVO SOURCE]
The 2025 Third Synthesis practice separately styažayet/acquires:
- 16384 Competences of the Initiate;
- 16384 Vitalities of human realization;
- 16384 Powers/Authorities `in the full-authority realization`;
- 16384 synthesis-cosmic realization.

This directly separates competence, vitality/realization, authority and synthesis-cosmic realization.

[IVDIVO SOURCE]
The 2024 Genesis school gives an especially important case: Avatars of the Council are said to possess all Powers/Authorities for work with Synthesis Cores even if those Synthesis Cores are absent in them. The work is harder without the cores, but the Powers remain.

Robust distinction:
AUTHORITY/POWER ≠ CURRENT OPERATIONAL CAPACITY OR SUBSTRATE.

A person may possess a legitimate Power/Authority for an action while lacking an inner resource that would make the action easy or fully operational.

This source distinction is extremely important for IVDIVO stories because it prevents `power`, `ability`, `competence` and `right` from collapsing into one stat.

[OPEN]
Need exact definition of `Powers/Authorities of Perfections` specifically, not just general Powers, and relation to right, permission, office and Hierarchy.

## 7. Hierarchization — major v0.2 update

[IVDIVO SOURCE]
The 2024 Higher School of Genesis explicitly titles one axis:
`Human Life in Hierarchy with 512 Avatars IVO — hierarchization of being`.

The text describes simple human interaction `with` Avatars, entering into the essence of Hierarchy, reciprocal manifestation, and concludes this is Hierarchy / Metagalactic Human Life. The same corpus later requires self-determination in Hierarchy and determination of one's hierarchical realization.

[SOURCE-SUPPORTED INFERENCE]
Minimum current reading:
HIERARCHIZATION is not safely reducible to office promotion or subordination. It is connected with lived integration/realization of human being in Hierarchy through relationship, self-determination, expression and concrete `дело`/activity.

[OPEN]
Need primary formulas defining Hierarchization directly and clarifying:
- what is being hierarchized;
- whether Hierarchization can change without Status;
- relation to Authorities of Perfections;
- relation between personal Hierarchization and administrative/service hierarchy.

## 8. Ivdivost

[IVDIVO SOURCE]
The Atlas places Ivdivost separately after Hierarchization. The 2020 Second Synthesis practice separately acquires `Hierarchyzation`, `IVDIVOSTI`, and Conditions in the same practice, supporting their non-equivalence.

The Atlas also contains numerous functional `-ivdivost` expressions tied to movement, sensation, feeling, thought, meaning, essence, idea, rights and other functions.

[SOURCE-SUPPORTED INFERENCE]
Ivdivost appears to be a broad integrative IVDIVO-specific coordinate/expression rather than a synonym for Hierarchization. This is not yet enough for a formal definition.

[OPEN]
Search exact formulas for:
- `Ивдивость — это`;
- `Ивдивость каждого`;
- relation Hierarchization → Ivdivost;
- relation to IVDIVO as House/environment/organization;
- whether Ivdivost is quality, state, realization, organization, or several of these depending on context.

## 9. Strongest non-equivalences now supported

The corpus currently supports keeping the following coordinates separate:

PARTS ≠ INITIATION ≠ STATUS ≠ CREATING SYNTHESIS ≠ SYNTHESISNESS ≠ POWERS/AUTHORITIES ≠ HIERARCHIZATION ≠ IVDIVOST.

In addition:
- Competence can be composite and in some source formulations includes Initiations, Statuses, rights, beginnings, abilities and Synthesis skills.
- Powers/Authorities remain distinguishable from competence and from current operational capacity.
- Office/service position must not be assumed identical to personal hierarchical realization.

Do not convert this sequence into a conventional RPG level ladder until transition relations are source-extracted.

## 10. Why this matters for youth research

[OUR SYNTHESIS]
The strong educational mystery is no longer `who is higher?` but `what exactly has changed in the person?`

A youth-facing story can eventually make the listener distinguish:
- what the hero can perceive or contain;
- what Status is active;
- what the hero is competent to perform;
- what legitimate Powers/Authorities the hero has;
- what is actually realized;
- how the hero is related to Hierarchy;
- what IVDIVO-specific integrative quality is present.

The story should reveal one contradiction at a time. The research module then sends the listener to the source map rather than presenting a single simplified ladder.
