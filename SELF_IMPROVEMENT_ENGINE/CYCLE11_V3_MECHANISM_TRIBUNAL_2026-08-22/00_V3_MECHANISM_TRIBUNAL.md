## C42 — true-gap selection

Result: **NO NEW UNIVERSAL MODULE SELECTED.**

The strongest apparent gap, `POLICY_RESISTANCE_GATE`, fails the novelty test at universal scope. Its useful question is:

Disposition: `MERGE/NO_OP` at universal-engine level; retain the wording as a Red-Team diagnostic pattern.
