# WHAT WORKS

1. **Maya is not Ethan/Aoife/Ollie.** Her POV is status-sensitive, competitive, impatient with supervision and highly alert to social humiliation.
2. **World arrives through resistance.** Spin transition, route timing, adaptation hold and host rules all obstruct something Maya currently wants.
3. **The orbit is inhabited.** Commuters, children, sports bags, dinner, transit complaints and host routines prevent “space museum” effect.
4. **Adults are not stupid or oppressive.** Nara grants Maya more freedom than expected and defines only the information needed for shared risk.
5. **Book-One consequence advances rather than repeats.** Maya receives real autonomy; future failure can now come from how she uses it.
6. **Chapter closes with changed behavior.** Maya watches locals before choosing her lane. She has not become obedient; she has become more observant.

# MAJOR 1 — TOO MANY CHARACTERS SHARE THE SAME QUICK-WIT REGISTER

Maya, Nara and Kellan all answer quickly, invert phrases and land clean jokes.

Risk:
- ensemble voices flatten into “IVDIVO witty dialogue”;
- Nara can sound like an older Maya;
- Kellan can sound like Ethan with fewer schedules.

## PATCH

### Maya
Keep sharpness, challenge, refusal to concede embarrassment.

### Nara
Make her drier and less eager to win exchanges. She should often answer literally, then return to practical action.

### Kellan
Give him fewer perfect replies. Make his humor more local / observational / lazy; sometimes he lets a joke die rather than finish the exchange.

Target: remove or flatten roughly 20–25% of joke-response chains.

---

# MAJOR 2 — HOST-RULE CONVERSATION RISKS BECOMING POLICY EXPOSITION

The privacy discussion is important, but the middle currently states the model too completely.

Risk:
reader hears author explain “orbital privacy philosophy.”

## PATCH

Keep only what Maya actively negotiates now:
- late return;
- where she sleeps;
- what happens if transit closes;
- no continuous tracking.

Move controlled-work-zone / medical / legal edge cases to later scenes if they become necessary.

Prefer Nara pointing to a concrete recent station closure / household routine rather than enumerating categories.

---

# MAJOR 3 — TRANSFER THREE SEQUENCE IS SLIGHTLY OVERLONG

The failure needs to be felt, but the chapter spends many beats proving the route is slower.

## PATCH

Compress one layer of:
- cabin physical explanation;
- adaptation-system enumeration;
- countdown reaction.

Protect:
- Maya chooses “not recommended” because it is not forbidden;
- she loses more time than recommended route;
- local girl’s `Geometrically` response;
- Maya is more bothered by being unmemorable than by being wrong.

That is the scene’s real payload.

---

# MAJOR 4 — DINNER COMEDY ALMOST BECOMES A SITCOM BUTTON

The misunderstanding `You weren’t the girl` is funny and reveals Maya’s attention to the local girl, but the sequence extends several exchanges beyond the reveal.

## PATCH

Keep reveal and one beat of embarrassment.
Cut the longer interrogation about whether the girl rescued her / whether Maya fell.

Do not over-signal a romance/attraction line before the local girl exists as a character.
