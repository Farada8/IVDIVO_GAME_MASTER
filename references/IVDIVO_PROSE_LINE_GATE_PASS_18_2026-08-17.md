## USE
Run after story, scene and dialogue causality are already viable. This gate is a diagnostic/repair pass, not a prose style template.

## A. POV / VOICE
1. Could this observation only plausibly come from the current POV?
2. What does this POV notice first, value, misread, ignore, fear, optimize, mock or desire?
3. Does narration contain information this limited POV cannot know?
4. Could a paragraph be moved unchanged into another protagonist’s POV? If yes, test for generic narrator voice.
5. Are syntax, vocabulary, metaphor density and attention pattern character-specific rather than globally standardized?

## B. OBJECT -> ACTION -> IMPLICATION
1. Is the event shown before it is interpreted?
2. Does a second sentence merely explain what the first action/image already proved?
3. Is moral/theme commentary arriving after the dramatic evidence has already landed?
4. Can abstract realization be replaced by object/condition + action + changed state?

## C. CROWD / LEAP
Crowd exact detail when it changes:
- causality;
- spatial orientation;
- clue interpretation;
- emotional/social pressure;
- world use;
- character perception.

Leap when it is only:
- routine transit;
- repeated handling;
- connector procedure;
- blow-by-blow movement with no new state;
- explanation already inferable.

Question: `What is lost if this connective detail disappears?`
If answer = nothing, cut/compress.

## D. RHYTHM
1. Are sentence lengths and constructions varied?
2. Does rhythm follow pressure/thought rather than a default generated cadence?
3. Are short fragments being used selectively rather than as the default signal for importance?
4. Are long sentences controlled and legible rather than banned automatically?
5. Do paragraph breaks reflect scene/attention shifts rather than artificial emphasis every sentence?

## E. CAUSAL CLARITY / AGENCY
When plot comprehension depends on agency:
- WHO/WHAT acted?
- WHAT did they do?
- WHAT changed?

Flag abstract noun/passive chains that hide causal responsibility unless the concealment is deliberate story action.

## F. STRESS / LINE ENDING
1. Does the sentence/paragraph end on the actual weight-bearing word/image/change?
2. Is a strong image followed by a weaker explanatory tail?
3. Is every paragraph ending like an aphorism/punchline? If yes, de-pattern.

## G. REDUNDANCY / CLUTTER
Flag:
- repeated meaning;
- already implied information;
- redundant interpretation around dialogue;
- generic filter phrases when direct experience is stronger;
- modifier piles compensating for weak noun/verb choices;
- accidental cliche unless character-specific usage is intentional;
- repeated gestures and stock beats.

Concision law: `ENOUGH WORDS`, not `FEWEST WORDS`.
Do not cut lived texture, relationship behavior, world-use detail or rhythm merely to shorten.

## H. READ-ALOUD / SOUND
Check aloud or by inner-ear for:
- repeated cadence;
- same sentence openings;
- breathless clause chains;
- accidental rhyme/alliteration;
- hard-to-say dialogue;
- tonal jumps;
- repeated filler beats;
- monotone short-sentence hammering.

## I. IVDIVO GENERATED-TEXT WATCH
Existing watch:
- excessive tiny paragraphs;
- repeated syntax;
- `There it was`;
- `That mattered`;
- `That was worse`;
- repeated `X looked at Y`;
- moral explanation after action;
- symmetrical group dialogue;
- perfect thematic formulations.

Pass 18 adds:
- repeated `Not X. Not Y. Z.`;
- repeated diagnostic one-line fragments;
- serial aphoristic paragraph endings;
- every POV using the same metaphor density;
- automatic short-sentence compression that destroys thought complexity;
- narration of uneventful transitions.

## J. SEVERITY
### FATAL / MAJOR
Only when systemic line problems damage story function, for example:
- POV knowledge breach changes plot logic;
- action geography/agency becomes incomprehensible;
- multiple protagonists effectively share one narrative consciousness;
- explanation systematically smothers implication and destroys scene propulsion.

### MEDIUM
- repeated generated cadence across scene/chapter;
- generic POV description;
- excessive transition logistics;
- noncausal blow-by-blow action;
- systematic explanatory tails;
- important action repeatedly buried in abstractions.

### POLISH
- local repetition;
- one weak line ending;
- accidental sound clash;
- a few filter words;
- local cliche;
- isolated cadence duplication.

## K. REPAIR LAW
1. Do not reopen GREEN architecture for line polish.
2. Repair only the smallest unit that fixes the diagnosed problem.
3. Preserve character irregularity and manuscript language.
4. A beautiful sentence that stops the story is suspect.
5. A plain sentence that carries action perfectly does not need decoration.
6. Re-run scene turn and continuity after any line repair that changes information or action.

## FINAL GATE QUESTION
After the line pass, is the prose:
- clearer without being flatter;
- more character-specific without being mannered;
- faster where nothing matters and denser where something does;
- rhythmically varied without calling attention to technique;
- still serving the story rather than demonstrating craft?
