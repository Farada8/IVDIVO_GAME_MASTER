# IVDIVO REFERENCE MECHANISM CARDS — PASS 08

Date: 2026-08-16
Status: REFERENCE ONLY / WORKING TOOL. Not canon. Not plot prescriptions.
Method: REFERENCE → ABSTRACT MECHANISM → TRANSFORM THROUGH IVDIVO → ORIGINAL STORY.

Purpose: convert the strongest newly studied sources into reusable story-engine cards. These cards are not summaries. Each is a causal machine that can be tested against STORY GATE.

---

## CARD S01 — THE TRUTH THAT DAMAGES THE SETTLEMENT
SOURCE MECHANISM: Agatha Christie, *Ordeal by Innocence*.
SOURCE FACT: a case believed closed is reopened by exculpatory evidence. The correction does not merely solve an intellectual error; it destroys the family’s adaptation to the official solution.

ABSTRACT MACHINE:
CLOSED CASE → STABLE SOCIAL STORY → NEW EVIDENCE → OFFICIAL STORY COLLAPSES → EVERY RELATIONSHIP IS REINTERPRETED → SOMEONE NEEDS THE OLD STORY TO SURVIVE.

IVDIVO TRANSFORM — SMITH:
An Old Earth Security incident was officially neutralized decades ago. Smith receives evidence proving the blamed entity/person was not responsible. The old verdict is tied to survivor compensation, secrecy agreements, careers, jurisdictional legitimacy and a later treaty. Correcting the record makes the present more dangerous than leaving the lie intact.

STORY REQUIREMENT:
The new evidence must force action now. It cannot exist only to create lore.

CLIMAX REQUIREMENT:
Smith must choose between preserving a useful false settlement and exposing the truth despite a concrete present cost. The current threat/case still closes.

FAILURE MODE:
A twist that changes only reader knowledge but changes no relationship, institution or action.

## CARD S02 — THE INSTITUTION THAT TRAINS PEOPLE TO LIE TO IT
SOURCE MECHANISM: John le Carré, *The Spy Who Came in from the Cold*.
SOURCE FACT: compartmentalization is operationally necessary, but once agents learn omission, cover and manipulation, the institution itself loses full visibility into its own network.

ABSTRACT MACHINE:
SECRECY NEEDED FOR SURVIVAL → PEOPLE LEARN TO WITHHOLD → WITHHOLDING BECOMES CULTURE → COMMAND HAS INCOMPLETE MODEL → OPERATION USES PEOPLE WHO DO NOT KNOW ITS TRUE PURPOSE → MORAL COST EXCEEDS ORIGINAL MISSION FRAME.

IVDIVO TRANSFORM — OLD EARTH SECURITY ↔ CONFEDERATION:
Old Earth Security withholds historical agreements and nonhuman contacts because disclosure once caused deaths. Confederation security withholds technical/ontological data because premature exposure could alter planetary conditions. Cooperation begins while both sides are operating with intentionally incomplete maps.

STORY REQUIREMENT:
Neither side can simply be "the liar". Each concealment must have a defensible operational reason and a real victim.

CLIMAX REQUIREMENT:
The protagonists must act before full truth is available, then pay for the layer that was withheld.

FAILURE MODE:
Endless secret files with no causal effect on a decision.

## CARD S03 — THE DETECTIVE METHOD AS OCCUPATIONAL DAMAGE
SOURCE MECHANISM: Thomas Harris, *Red Dragon*.
SOURCE FACT: the investigator’s unusual usefulness lies in reconstructing the offender’s thinking; this same process is psychologically contaminating and threatens the ordinary life he rebuilt.

ABSTRACT MACHINE:
SPECIAL METHOD → BETTER PREDICTION → DEEPER IDENTIFICATION WITH THREAT → DAMAGE TO SELF/RELATIONSHIP → METHOD BECOMES BOTH TOOL AND HAZARD.

IVDIVO TRANSFORM — SMITH:
Smith’s advantage is not a free paranormal power. His old-system method requires entering old agreements, symbolic logics, alien legal categories or archived mental models deeply enough to predict an entity’s next act. Doing that makes ordinary human assumptions temporarily unreliable for him.

PRICE OPTIONS:
- he begins interpreting loved ones through case logic;
- he risks accepting the entity’s definitions of consent/personhood;
- he becomes less legible to Confederation partners;
- the method reactivates an old failure or oath.

STORY REQUIREMENT:
Show the ordinary life he can actually lose before the method damages it.

FAILURE MODE:
"He understands monsters" as an effortless superpower.

## CARD S04 — THE WRONG ONTOLOGY, NOT THE EVIL VILLAIN
SOURCE MECHANISM: Peter Watts, *The Things*.
SOURCE FACT: a being can sincerely interpret assimilation as connection/improvement while humans interpret the same act as annihilation.

ABSTRACT MACHINE:
SIDE A: "I AM HELPING / CONNECTING" ↔ SIDE B: "YOU ARE DESTROYING ME".
Conflict comes from incompatible definitions of self, body, continuity, consent and survival.

IVDIVO TRANSFORM — SMITH / CONFEDERATION:
A nonhuman entity may believe memory-sharing, body-merging, replacement, duplication or consciousness transfer preserves a person. Humans may experience the same procedure as death. Confederation science may partly validate the entity’s continuity model while Old Earth treaties classify it as predation.

STORY REQUIREMENT:
The entity’s belief must alter tactics. It cannot simply be a philosophical speech before attacking.

CLIMAX REQUIREMENT:
A boundary must be enforced or negotiated in action. The immediate danger closes even if the metaphysical question remains open.

FAILURE MODE:
"Both sides are right" used to avoid choosing whom to protect in the current scene.

## CARD S05 — DETECTIVE STORY AS HYPOTHESIS ENGINE
SOURCE MECHANISM: Umberto Eco, *The Name of the Rose* + Postscript.
SOURCE FACT: detective pleasure comes from conjecture—forming an explanatory model from signs and then discovering where the model succeeds or fails.

ABSTRACT MACHINE:
OBSERVABLE PHENOMENA → MODEL A → ACTION BASED ON A → CONTRADICTION → MODEL B → BETTER ACTION → RESOLUTION WITH RESIDUAL UNKNOWN.

IVDIVO TRANSFORM — SMITH:
Every major case should contain at least two plausible ontological models, not just a hidden answer. Example classes: biological entity / juridical person / machine process / ritual consequence / interdimensional ecology / deliberate deception.

STORY REQUIREMENT:
Wrong hypotheses must cost time, trust, access or lives. Otherwise deduction has no dramatic weight.

STORY GATE QUESTION:
What evidence would force Smith to abandon the model he emotionally prefers?

FAILURE MODE:
Terminology escalates but the protagonist never changes behavior.

## CARD S06 — BELIEF CAN BE OPERATIONALLY REAL WITHOUT BEING ONTOLOGICALLY TRUE
SOURCE MECHANISM: Umberto Eco, legendary lands / cultural-history material.
SOURCE FACT: imagined or disputed places can produce real expeditions, frauds, institutions, borders and political action.

ABSTRACT MACHINE:
BELIEF → INVESTMENT → INSTITUTION → RULES/VIOLENCE/ECONOMY → REAL CONSEQUENCES, regardless of original truth-value.

IVDIVO TRANSFORM — OLD EARTH ARCHIVES:
An archive category may have begun as folklore, mistaken cosmology or deliberate cover, yet centuries of treaties and containment practice built around it now have real force.

STORY REQUIREMENT:
Start with the observable consequence: checkpoint, payment, forbidden route, missing people, ritualized bureaucracy. Ontology later.

FAILURE MODE:
Lore dump about ancient beliefs with no present actor who wants something.

## CARD O01 — YOUTH LIFE IS THE CLUE DELIVERY SYSTEM
SOURCE MECHANISM: *Harry Potter and the Prisoner of Azkaban* + Ray Bradbury, *Something Wicked This Way Comes*.
SOURCE FACT: apparently ordinary youth material—pets, homework, birthdays, jealousy, library visits, family tensions, seasonal rituals—does not interrupt the mystery; it carries causal information and emotional stakes.

ABSTRACT MACHINE:
ORDINARY PROBLEM → SOCIAL FRICTION → SMALL OBJECT/ROUTINE → LATER REINTERPRETATION → MYSTERY PAYOFF.

IVDIVO TRANSFORM — ORBITAL YOUTH:
Use transit passes, maintenance shifts, sports fixtures, habitat pets, dorm permissions, work rosters, food credits, mixed-species etiquette, AI tutors and family calls as clue carriers.

STORY REQUIREMENT:
At least one mundane element introduced for a character reason must later become causally necessary.

FAILURE MODE:
"Youth life" scenes that can be removed without changing investigation, relationships or climax.

## CARD O02 — TEMPTATION MUST MATCH THE PRIVATE DESIRE
SOURCE MECHANISM: Bradbury, *Something Wicked This Way Comes*.
SOURCE FACT: supernatural danger works because it offers forms of fulfillment tailored to age, fear, regret and longing.

ABSTRACT MACHINE:
PRIVATE LACK → OFFER THAT APPEARS TO SOLVE IT → ACCEPTANCE COST → FRIEND/RELATIONSHIP PRESSURE → CHOICE BETWEEN DESIRE AND SELF.

IVDIVO TRANSFORM — ORBITAL YOUTH:
A dangerous technology/anomaly should offer what a specific young person wants: adult legal status, a better body, instant qualification, social visibility, romantic desirability, restored parent, freedom from gravity/illness, unrestricted travel, elite AI access.

STORY REQUIREMENT:
The offer must be genuinely attractive. Refusal cannot be obvious from the start.

FAILURE MODE:
Generic evil object that merely threatens everyone equally.

## CARD O03 — ABILITY AFTER WOUND, STATUS AND ECONOMICS
SOURCE MECHANISM: Marie Lu, *The Young Elites*.
SOURCE FACT: ability is embedded in stigma, family economics, body shame, abuse, attraction, belonging and public fear before it becomes spectacle.

ABSTRACT MACHINE:
SOCIAL WOUND → PRIVATE DESIRE → ABILITY APPEARS AS APPARENT ANSWER → GROUP OFFERS BELONGING → POWER AMPLIFIES CONTRADICTION → PRICE.

IVDIVO TRANSFORM — ORBITAL YOUTH:
Synthetic embodiment, interface privilege, unusual cognition or nonhuman affinity must alter already-existing problems: rent, apprenticeship, family expectations, body-image, dating, species prejudice, school/work status.

STORY REQUIREMENT:
We should understand what the character wanted one week before the ability mattered.

FAILURE MODE:
Character exists primarily to demonstrate a cool capability.

## CARD O04 — BOOK TWO MAY OPEN THROUGH A NEW COMPLETE HERO
SOURCE MECHANISM: Philip Pullman, *The Subtle Knife*.
SOURCE FACT: Book Two introduces Will through an independent urgent story—care for his mother, secrecy, missing father, intrusion, accidental killing—before connecting him to the larger fantasy line.

ABSTRACT MACHINE:
NEW HERO → OWN ORDINARY WORLD → OWN WHY-NOW CRISIS → IRREVERSIBLE ACTION → CROSSING POINT → MERGE WITH EXISTING SAGA.

IVDIVO TRANSFORM:
A later Orbital Youth / Smith / Confederation book may begin with a new hero living a different layer of civilization. The saga is encountered as consequence, not as exposition.

STORY REQUIREMENT:
Before the crossover, new hero must already have HERO / WANT / WHY NOW / PRICE.

FAILURE MODE:
New character introduced only because the old cast needs a specialist.

## CARD O05 — EXISTING CIVILIZATION BEFORE CATASTROPHE
SOURCE MECHANISM: Neal Stephenson, *Seveneves*.
SOURCE FACT: orbital infrastructure has budgets, staffing shortages, robotics, mining, family histories, PR, technical culture and status before the survival emergency transforms it.

ABSTRACT MACHINE:
NORMAL SYSTEM WITH FRICTIONS → EXTERNAL SHOCK → EXISTING JOBS/RELATIONSHIPS BECOME SURVIVAL FUNCTIONS → OLD INEFFICIENCIES BECOME DEADLY → NEW POLITICS EMERGES FROM OLD STRUCTURE.

IVDIVO TRANSFORM — ORBITAL YOUTH:
Emergency episodes should not create a generic station. A school timetable, internship dispute, housing shortage, maintenance backlog, transport rule or sports event should already exist and then become part of the emergency chain.

STORY REQUIREMENT:
The emergency must permanently change at least one ordinary institution after resolution.

FAILURE MODE:
Characters become interchangeable technicians once the alarm sounds.

## CARD C01 — CURIOSITY SCALES INTO FIRST CONTACT
SOURCE MECHANISM: Carl Sagan, *Contact*.
SOURCE FACT: the protagonist’s childhood habit of opening devices, testing claims and asking how things work is the same underlying drive later operating at civilizational scale.

ABSTRACT MACHINE:
CHILDHOOD/ORDINARY CURIOSITY → VOCATION → ANOMALOUS SIGNAL → VERIFICATION → POLITICAL/RELIGIOUS/SCIENTIFIC CONFLICT → PERSONAL COST.

IVDIVO TRANSFORM — CONFEDERATION:
A first-contact or forbidden-knowledge protagonist should not suddenly become "the curious scientist" because plot needs one. The trait must exist in ordinary life and relationships first.

STORY REQUIREMENT:
The same character strength that reaches the truth must create a relationship/institutional cost.

FAILURE MODE:
First contact handled only at council-room level.

---

## CARD C02 — CONTACT CHANGES INSTITUTIONS BEFORE BODIES MEET
SOURCE MECHANISM: Carl Sagan, *Contact*.
SOURCE FACT: the message changes verification protocols, international cooperation, security behavior, religion, public discourse and jurisdiction before any physical encounter.

ABSTRACT MACHINE:
SIGNAL/EVIDENCE → WHO OWNS IT? → WHO CAN VERIFY IT? → WHO MAY ACT? → WHO FEARS THE CONSEQUENCES? → INSTITUTIONAL REALIGNMENT → ONLY THEN PHYSICAL/ONTOLOGICAL CONTACT.

IVDIVO TRANSFORM — CONFEDERATION / EARTH:
Because formal Confederation intervention is restricted, information passing through cadets/remote channels can itself trigger Earth political, scientific and orbital conflicts without a Confederation ship entering the system.

STORY REQUIREMENT:
At least three institutions must want different things from the same evidence.

FAILURE MODE:
"The world learns aliens exist" montage with no specific actor, jurisdiction or price.

# COMBINATION ENGINES

## SMITH COMBO A — THE MERCY LIE
Combine S01 + S02 + S05.
An old case was intentionally closed on a false culprit because the false story prevented a larger conflict. New evidence proves innocence. Smith must reopen it, but both Old Earth Security and Confederation have different reasons to preserve parts of the lie. Investigation proceeds through competing models, not a simple conspiracy reveal.

Minimum complete story:
HERO: Smith.
WANT: establish what is happening now and stop recurrence.
WHY NOW: new evidence coincides with a fresh event.
OPPOSITION: present threat + institutional dependence on old settlement.
WRONG STRATEGY: assume truth automatically improves safety.
PRICE: loses protection/trust/access by reopening case.
MIDPOINT: official culprit genuinely innocent, but old containment worked for a reason.
CLIMAX CHOICE: expose/alter the settlement while still preventing present harm.
RESOLUTION: current recurrence stopped; record/treaty permanently changed.
SERIES HOOK: deeper origin remains open only after current case closes.

Status: OPTION, not canon.

## SMITH COMBO B — THE BENEVOLENT INVASION
Combine S03 + S04 + S05.
A nonhuman process acts as though it is rescuing people. Smith can predict it only by temporarily accepting its definition of personhood. Confederation science finds that parts of its continuity model are not nonsense. Smith must nevertheless defend human consent while recognizing that "kill the monster" may be ontologically equivalent to killing the people it believes it has preserved.

Status: OPTION, not canon.

## ORBITAL YOUTH COMBO A — THE ORDINARY THING THAT SHOULD NOT KNOW
Combine O01 + O02 + O03.
A mundane object/service in youth life—AI tutor, transit token, repair drone, dorm access system, sports implant—starts responding to a private desire it should not know. The young character first uses the advantage socially, then discovers that the service is learning/connecting through a forbidden channel.

Status: OPTION, not canon.

## ORBITAL YOUTH COMBO B — BOOK-TWO CROSSING
Combine O04 + O05 + C01.
Open with a new young hero in another habitat/job/family system. Their local crisis is complete enough to stand alone before an irreversible action brings them across the existing saga line. The crossover happens because of what they chose, not because established heroes recruit them as a plot device.

Status: OPTION, not canon.

# STORY GATE — REFERENCE MECHANISM CHECK
For any story using these cards:
1. Who wants what before the anomaly appears?
2. Why must action happen now?
3. What ordinary relationship or livelihood can be lost?
4. What model of reality does the protagonist initially believe?
5. What evidence forces behavior to change?
6. Who benefits from preserving the wrong model?
7. What does the opposition believe it is doing?
8. What mundane object/job/routine becomes causal?
9. What does the protagonist choose at climax that cannot be undone?
10. What price remains after success?
11. Is the current main conflict actually closed?
12. Is any series hook opened only after that closure?
