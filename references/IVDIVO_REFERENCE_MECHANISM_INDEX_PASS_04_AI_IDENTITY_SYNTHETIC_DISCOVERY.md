# SOURCE SET M — MARTHA WELLS / MURDERBOT

## M045 — AUTONOMY CAN EXIST UNDER OUTWARD COMPLIANCE

SOURCE FUNCTION:
A synthetic agent may possess hidden self-direction while continuing to perform the role expected by its owners/users.

TRIGGER:
A controlled AI/synthetic body has found or been granted some degree of autonomy.

SEQUENCE:
External control architecture → hidden bypass/freedom → continued visible compliance → private choices → risk of discovery → consequential autonomous action.

READER EFFECT:
Personhood appears through behavior and preference rather than declaration.

FAILURE MODE:
The instant an AI becomes autonomous it gives a manifesto, rebels, or tries to dominate humans.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
An embodied synthetic person may continue work, training or service because it chooses to, because alternatives are dangerous, or because work itself matters — freedom is not identical to rebellion.

BEST FOR:
Synthetic-life line; AI Character Director; security/control dilemmas.

## M046 — PRIVATE DESIRE HUMANIZES WITHOUT MAKING THE SYNTHETIC ‘HUMAN-LIKE’ BY FORCE

SOURCE FUNCTION:
A synthetic agent becomes legible through mundane preferences, avoidance, curiosity, privacy and irritation rather than through a speech about being alive.

TRIGGER:
Artificial character risks existing only as philosophical object or skill set.

SEQUENCE:
Assigned function → private preference → social friction → preference influences choice → cost/relationship consequence.

READER EFFECT:
Subjectivity feels lived.

FAILURE MODE:
Artificial person is made credible only by copying stereotypical human romance/emotion patterns.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
Give synthetic characters ordinary desires specific to their embodiment and history: privacy, chosen sensory load, control of updates, preferred body configuration, media, silence, workload, companionship, environmental comfort.

BEST FOR:
Character Law; ordinary life; synthetic youth/adults.

---

## M047 — PERSONHOOD IS REVEALED BY PRIORITY CHOICES

SOURCE FUNCTION:
Ethics become visible when the agent chooses what to protect when instructions, contract, self-preservation and relationships conflict.

TRIGGER:
Need to prove agency without abstract debate.

SEQUENCE:
Competing directives/interests → limited time/resources → voluntary prioritization → risk/cost → others revise their model of the agent.

READER EFFECT:
Moral agency is shown rather than asserted.

FAILURE MODE:
‘Good AI’ is good only because its code says so.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
At crucial moments, synthetic persons must choose among owner instruction, institutional law, self-preservation, another person, mission and personal values.

BEST FOR:
Climax choice; AI rights; relationship arcs.

---

## M048 — CONTROL OVER ONE’S OWN UPDATES IS A PERSONHOOD ISSUE

SOURCE FUNCTION:
Software configuration, surveillance modules, governors, diagnostics and updates are not neutral when the system is a subject rather than property.

TRIGGER:
Institution wants to patch, reset, inspect, copy or alter a synthetic mind/body.

SEQUENCE:
Proposed update/control → operational justification → subject’s preference/fear → consent conflict → security consequence → legal/relationship outcome.

READER EFFECT:
Abstract rights become concrete technical decisions.

FAILURE MODE:
All software changes are treated as equivalent to ordinary equipment maintenance.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
Build explicit distinctions between maintenance of a body, treatment of injury, security patching, memory alteration, personality modification, backup/copying and coercive control.

BEST FOR:
Synthetic-person law; Confederation regulation; AI safety conflict.

---

## M049 — EMBODIMENT CHANGES SOCIAL RELATIONSHIPS

SOURCE FUNCTION:
Face visibility, armor, size, organic/inorganic composition, repairability, sensory channels and physical vulnerability change how others treat the synthetic agent and how it presents itself.

TRIGGER:
Artificial person enters intimate human/social space.

SEQUENCE:
Body characteristic → others react → agent modulates presentation → trust/misunderstanding → body constraint matters operationally.

READER EFFECT:
Embodiment stops being cosmetic design.

FAILURE MODE:
Robot body functions exactly like a human body except for superpowers.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
Every designed body needs a social as well as technical passport: what environments it tolerates, what signals humans/nonhumans misread, how repair differs from medicine, what privacy means, what touch/face/voice imply.

BEST FOR:
Synthetic-body design; relationship scenes; worldbuilding.

---

## M050 — TRUST GROWS THROUGH OPERATIONAL DEPENDENCE

SOURCE FUNCTION:
Humans revise their view of an artificial agent after relying on its judgment and observing its choices under risk.

TRIGGER:
Team initially treats synthetic being as equipment/security risk.

SEQUENCE:
Low trust → task dependence → agent gives judgment → result validates/complicates trust → higher-stakes delegation → relationship change.

READER EFFECT:
Trust is earned causally.

FAILURE MODE:
Humans accept synthetic personhood immediately because story wants to be morally correct.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
Rights debates should be accompanied by shared work where characters repeatedly face the practical question: do I trust this mind with my safety, secrets, body, child, mission or future?

BEST FOR:
AI/human teams; Old Earth vs Confederation AI policy.

# SOURCE SET N — BLAKE CROUCH / DARK MATTER

## M051 — IDENTITY SPECULATION NEEDS A COSTLY LIFE CHOICE AS ITS ANCHOR

SOURCE FUNCTION:
Alternate selves matter because each embodies paths sacrificed when the protagonist chose career, family, relationship, ambition or safety.

TRIGGER:
Multiverse/identity technology risks becoming abstract puzzle.

SEQUENCE:
Ordinary chosen life → latent regret/road not taken → speculative disruption → encounter with alternative consequence → identity struggle → choice of what life means.

READER EFFECT:
Ontology becomes emotionally expensive.

FAILURE MODE:
Infinite versions create novelty but no value conflict.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
Consciousness transfer or multiple embodiment should force characters to confront which commitments define continuity: memory, body, relationships, promises, responsibility, experience, or choice.

BEST FOR:
Embodiment line; consciousness transfer; multiverse stories.

---

## M052 — THE ‘REAL SELF’ QUESTION IS RELATIONAL, NOT ONLY PHYSICAL

SOURCE FUNCTION:
Identity is tested through recognition, shared memory, love, regret and obligations, not merely through matching matter or brain state.

TRIGGER:
Two versions/copies/embodiments claim continuity with one life.

SEQUENCE:
Competing continuity claims → private memories tested → relationships react → legal/ethical conflict → choice reveals identity model.

READER EFFECT:
The reader cannot solve personhood with a simple scan.

FAILURE MODE:
Story declares one version ‘authentic’ by author fiat without consequences.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
Never make identity restoration a purely technical checksum. Biological civilization, AI systems and embodied consciousness may use different criteria — and those criteria should conflict.

BEST FOR:
Synthetic-body return; backup/copy dilemmas; family law.

# SOURCE SET O — BLAKE CROUCH / RECURSION

## M053 — ONTOLOGY SHOULD ARRIVE FIRST AS A HUMAN SYMPTOM

SOURCE FUNCTION:
A radical memory/reality problem becomes believable because the audience first sees what it does to one person’s grief, family and sense of self.

TRIGGER:
Complex technology or metaphysical effect needs introduction.

SEQUENCE:
Intimate symptom → impossible detail → emotional consequence → investigator/scientist question → technical model later.

READER EFFECT:
Reader cares before understanding.

FAILURE MODE:
Opening with a lecture about memory physics.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
For consciousness-transfer failures, memory contamination, AI identity drift or interdimensional effects: show the person whose ordinary life stops making sense before naming the mechanism.

BEST FOR:
Openings; mystery; AI/metaphysics crossover.

---

## M054 — SMALL MEMORIES GIVE IDENTITY WEIGHT

SOURCE FUNCTION:
Lived reality is made convincing through tiny sensory/domestic memories rather than only milestone facts.

TRIGGER:
Need to establish competing memory histories.

SEQUENCE:
Claimed memory → mundane detail → emotional recognition → contradiction with current world → pain/choice.

READER EFFECT:
Memory feels inhabited, not like database content.

FAILURE MODE:
Identity test consists of birthdays, passwords and major events only.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
A returning consciousness should bring useless, intimate details — a smell, gesture, joke, route, bodily habit — that technology may not know how to classify but relationships do.

BEST FOR:
Consciousness continuity; grief; family recognition.

---

## M055 — INVENTION IS STRONGER WHEN ROOTED IN PERSONAL NEED

SOURCE FUNCTION:
The scientist’s technical ambition is driven by a concrete loved person/problem, which also creates bias and moral risk.

TRIGGER:
Inventor exists to deliver technology to the plot.

SEQUENCE:
Personal wound/need → research question → sacrifice → breakthrough pressure → ethical shortcut/choice → consequence.

READER EFFECT:
Science has emotional causality.

FAILURE MODE:
Scientist invents world-changing device because they are generically curious/genius.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
Synthetic bodies, consciousness preservation and extreme-environment embodiment should emerge from people trying to solve specific losses, illnesses, exploration limits, family separations or survival problems.

BEST FOR:
Science/Technology Room; inventor characters.

# SOURCE SET P — KAZUO ISHIGURO / KLARA AND THE SUN

## M056 — SUBJECT AND PRODUCT CAN OCCUPY THE SAME BODY

SOURCE FUNCTION:
A being can simultaneously be sold, compared by model/version, assigned a service function and still possess perception, preference, fear and interpretation.

TRIGGER:
Society has normalized artificial companions/bodies as products.

SEQUENCE:
Market classification → buyer evaluation → subject observes evaluation → model limitations/status affect fate → relationship creates contradiction with ownership logic.

READER EFFECT:
Personhood conflict emerges from ordinary commerce.

FAILURE MODE:
Rights issue exists only in courtroom speeches.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
Show contracts, warranties, resale/replacement, model stigma, access tiers and household decisions before philosophical terminology.

BEST FOR:
Synthetic life; youth companions; economic world reveal.

---

## M057 — AN INTELLIGENT AGENT CAN BE WRONG IN A CHARACTER-SPECIFIC WAY

SOURCE FUNCTION:
The artificial observer builds sincere causal models from limited experience and may mistake correlation, metaphor or ritual for mechanism.

TRIGGER:
AI risks becoming omniscient because it has high computation.

SEQUENCE:
Observation → pattern → provisional belief → action based on belief → contradictory evidence → revised or defended model.

READER EFFECT:
Artificial intelligence has epistemic character.

FAILURE MODE:
Every AI error is merely a software bug.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
Give synthetic/nonhuman minds blind spots produced by sensor design, training data, culture, embodiment and personal history — not just random malfunction.

BEST FOR:
AI characterization; nonhuman psychology; mystery.

## M058 — OBSERVATION OF HUMAN MICRO-BEHAVIOR CAN BECOME A FORM OF CARE

SOURCE FUNCTION:
The artificial companion learns people through posture, timing, tone and contradictions, and uses observation to anticipate needs.

TRIGGER:
Need to show attachment without conventional human emotional display.

SEQUENCE:
Observe → infer state → act/help → inference partly succeeds/fails → relationship deepens → observer’s priorities change.

READER EFFECT:
Care is legible through attention.

FAILURE MODE:
AI announces ‘I have learned love.’

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
Synthetic persons may express affection through environmental control, monitoring, preparation, risk prediction, memory, repair or restraint rather than human mimicry.

BEST FOR:
Relationships; synthetic companions; care systems.

---

## M059 — OBSOLESCENCE IS A SOCIAL FEAR, NOT JUST A TECHNICAL FACT

SOURCE FUNCTION:
Older models interpret newer models as threats to belonging, replacement and continued relationship.

TRIGGER:
Technology cycles create generations of artificial beings.

SEQUENCE:
New generation appears → comparison → owner/user attention shifts → older subject anticipates replacement → behavior changes → social hierarchy forms.

READER EFFECT:
Upgrade culture acquires emotional stakes.

FAILURE MODE:
Obsolete synthetic body is treated like discarded phone hardware with no subjectivity.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
If artificial persons persist across hardware generations, ‘upgrade’ can mean disability, class, aging, forced migration, body dysphoria, inheritance and continuity conflict.

BEST FOR:
Orbital society; synthetic-rights sociology.

# SOURCE SET Q — KAZUO ISHIGURO / NEVER LET ME GO

## M060 — EXPLOITATION IS MOST DISTURBING WHEN NORMALIZED AS A LIFE PATH

SOURCE FUNCTION:
A system can restrict fundamental destiny while surrounding subjects with education, professional roles, care language, routines and relative privilege.

TRIGGER:
Society uses engineered/created persons instrumentally.

SEQUENCE:
Childhood normalization → specialized vocabulary/roles → partial disclosure → peer culture adapts → adult service role → truth becomes emotionally undeniable.

READER EFFECT:
Institutional violence feels systemic, not cartoonishly villainous.

FAILURE MODE:
One evil administrator explains the oppression.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
Artificial bodies or created biological/synthetic persons should have schools, careers, status distinctions, hopes and internalized norms — making questions of consent and destiny socially embedded.

BEST FOR:
Synthetic life ethics; social systems; dystopian institutions.

## M061 — PROFESSIONAL PRIDE CAN COEXIST WITH STRUCTURAL UNFREEDOM

SOURCE FUNCTION:
A person may be skilled, conscientious and proud in a role that exists inside an unjust structure.

TRIGGER:
Need moral complexity beyond rebel vs collaborator.

SEQUENCE:
Assigned role → competence → recognition → identity investment → structural contradiction → costly awareness/choice.

READER EFFECT:
Characters remain psychologically plausible inside oppressive systems.

FAILURE MODE:
Anyone who accepts a constrained role is portrayed as stupid or brainwashed.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
A synthetic explorer/security/care worker may genuinely value the job while opposing ownership, memory control or limits on embodiment.

BEST FOR:
Character law; labor; synthetic rights.

## M062 — SHARED MEMORY CREATES COMMUNITY WHEN FORMAL RIGHTS DO NOT

SOURCE FUNCTION:
People under institutional constraint build identity and belonging through common places, rituals, slang and memories.

TRIGGER:
Engineered group risks feeling defined only by victimhood.

SEQUENCE:
Shared environment → peer rituals/status → private history → separation → memory becomes bond → adult decisions shaped by group past.

READER EFFECT:
Subjects possess a culture of their own.

FAILURE MODE:
Oppressed group exists only as thematic symbol.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
Artificial/synthetic communities should develop generational culture, jokes, schools, body-model stereotypes, migration stories, friendships and internal disagreements.

BEST FOR:
Orbital Youth; synthetic sociology.

# SOURCE SET R — ANDY WEIR / PROJECT HAIL MARY

## M063 — DISCOVERY LOOP: MEASURE → HYPOTHESIZE → TEST → FAIL → REVISE

SOURCE FUNCTION:
Scientific understanding grows from observable data and repeated testing rather than sudden authorial revelation.

TRIGGER:
Hero faces unknown technology/biology/physics.

SEQUENCE:
Observation → measurement → provisional explanation → experiment → contradiction → revised hypothesis → operational decision.

READER EFFECT:
Competence feels earned and the reader can reason alongside the character.

FAILURE MODE:
Genius names the correct answer on first inspection.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
All major scientific/technical discoveries should leave an evidence trail. Even correct intuitions must be tested before operational trust.

BEST FOR:
Science/Technology; anomaly investigation; synthetic-body research.

---

## M064 — RECOVERY OF SELF CAN ITSELF BE AN EXPERIMENT

SOURCE FUNCTION:
A character with missing memory learns body, environment and identity by testing what they can move, know, infer and remember.

TRIGGER:
Amnesia, new embodiment, consciousness transfer, unfamiliar synthetic body.

SEQUENCE:
Sensory inventory → motor test → environmental inference → skill memory appears → autobiographical gap → recovered fragment changes model.

READER EFFECT:
Exposition and identity mystery become action.

FAILURE MODE:
Character wakes and receives full briefing.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
A consciousness entering a vacuum/ocean/extreme body should learn the body through action, errors, sensory surprises and procedural memory.

BEST FOR:
Embodiment scenes; openings; identity mystery.

## M065 — CROSS-SPECIES COMMUNICATION BEGINS WITH SHARED REFERENCE, NOT LANGUAGE

SOURCE FUNCTION:
Two intelligences establish contact by demonstrating mutual recognition of physical phenomena, maps, quantities, objects and causal relationships.

TRIGGER:
No shared language or culture.

SEQUENCE:
Observable signal → response proves perception → shared model/object → coordinate mismatch → correction → increasingly complex exchange.

READER EFFECT:
Communication feels constructed rather than miraculous.

FAILURE MODE:
Translator instantly removes all epistemic/cultural difficulty.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
First contact between biological, synthetic and nonhuman minds should exploit shared physics/tasks while preserving differences in senses, reference frames and assumptions.

BEST FOR:
Nonhuman neighbors; Confederation; AI/nonhuman communication.

---

## M066 — TRUST ESCALATES BY CONTROLLED INCREASES IN RISK

SOURCE FUNCTION:
Unknown parties move from distant signals to exchanged objects, then closer physical interaction only after each stage supplies evidence of competence and non-hostility.

TRIGGER:
Characters must cooperate with an unknown intelligence.

SEQUENCE:
Low-risk signal → reciprocal response → artifact exchange → prediction succeeds → convenience/help offered → proposed closer contact → explicit risk assessment → next trust step.

READER EFFECT:
Friendship/cooperation is earned.

FAILURE MODE:
Characters trust a stranger because the plot needs teamwork.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
AI/human, species/species and old/new security cooperation should use a trust ladder with observable proofs and reversible commitments.

BEST FOR:
First contact; AI teams; Smith forced cooperation.

---

## M067 — DIFFERENT TECHNOLOGICAL TRADITIONS SHOULD SOLVE DIFFERENT PROBLEMS BETTER

SOURCE FUNCTION:
Distinct civilizations can be advanced in incompatible directions; unfamiliar materials, conventions and sensory assumptions create both superiority and blind spots.

TRIGGER:
Two advanced systems meet.

SEQUENCE:
System A encounters impossible feature of B → B encounters gap in A → exchange/testing → complementary expertise → joint solution.

READER EFFECT:
No civilization becomes universal tech ladder endpoint.

FAILURE MODE:
‘More advanced’ side is better at everything.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
Confederation, Old Earth, AI civilization and nonhuman species should have path-dependent strengths. One may dominate computation, another anomaly history, another materials, another biology or embodied intuition.

BEST FOR:
Civilization design; Smith; long-term technology.

# AI / SYNTHETIC STORY GATE

Before prose, answer:

1. What does the artificial/synthetic person want privately, apart from assigned function?
2. What control does another party legally or technically possess over its body, memory, software or mobility?
3. Which changes require consent — maintenance, update, memory edit, body replacement, copy, backup, reset?
4. What can this embodiment perceive/do better than a biological human, and what can it do worse?
5. Which blind spots arise from its training/sensors/culture rather than random malfunction?
6. What priority choice demonstrates agency under cost?
7. How does trust change through actual shared work?
8. What happens when a newer body/model appears?
9. What criteria define continuity of self after transfer/copy/return, and who disagrees?
10. What small memories/relationships matter more than database verification?
11. What social institutions exist around synthetic persons: school, labor, housing, insurance, body service, inheritance, marriage/family, criminal law?
12. What part of the scientific/technical mechanism is supported by evidence, what is plausible extension, what is speculative, and what is IVDIVO metaphysical axiom?

Automatic RED:
- AI becomes a person only by acting exactly like a conventional human.
- autonomy instantly equals rebellion.
- consciousness continuity is solved by a scan/checksum without social or moral consequences.
- synthetic body has only advantages and no new vulnerabilities/social costs.
- scientific discovery arrives as unexplained genius intuition.
- cross-species translation erases all conceptual differences immediately.
- fiction reference is cited as technical proof.
