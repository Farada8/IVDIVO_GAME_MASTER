### Useful mechanism
Romance has its own causal arc: attraction does not become a story until it collides with beliefs/fears that make intimacy difficult. The useful abstraction is not the named beat sheet but the repeated approach/retreat pattern under changing stakes.

Observable progression:
- normal life already contains a wound/belief/defense;
- encounter/attraction destabilizes that strategy;
- characters experience moments of increased intimacy/vulnerability;
- closeness activates fear/defense rather than simply producing steady progress;
- withdrawal or rupture tests whether the old self-protective strategy is still acceptable;
- later choice requires a changed behavior, not merely a declaration of feeling.

### IVDIVO caution
Do not import a romance-novel beat sheet mechanically into Orbital Youth.
Romance may be subplot, co-plot or main plot depending on the book. The IVDIVO main story still requires its own causal closure.

### Useful observable mechanisms
- people can want connection while pulling away because they expect rejection;
- acceptance may be maintained by hiding parts of the self, creating a relational paradox;

- a basically loving relationship can lose energy/engagement without either person becoming a villain;
- reconnection requires changed behavior and renewed contact, not only correct explanation.

# PASS 15 — EXECUTABLE RELATIONSHIP ENGINE

## CORE LOOP

PRIVATE NEED
-> ATTRACTION / AFFILIATION / DEPENDENCE
-> SMALL RISK OF VULNERABILITY
-> REWARD / CONNECTION
-> NEW COST OR FEAR
-> DEFENSIVE STRATEGY
-> MISREAD / WITHHOLD / JEALOUSY / STATUS PRESSURE
-> RUPTURE OR DISTANCE
-> CHOICE BETWEEN OLD PROTECTION AND NEW RELATIONSHIP BEHAVIOR
-> PRICE
-> CHANGED RELATIONSHIP STATE

The relationship must change behavior outside the relationship itself.

## CARD 1 — RELATIONSHIP HAS TWO WANTS, NOT ONE
For every important relationship scene:
A wants something from B.
B wants something from A or from the situation.
These wants need not be symmetrical.

Do not write `the romance wants them together` as a substitute for character wants.

## CARD 2 — CLOSENESS CREATES NEW RISK
Closeness should expose something:
- status vulnerability;
- family secret;
- money problem;
- job conflict;
- fear of dependence;
- belonging conflict;
- ideological difference;
- bodily/synthetic identity issue;
- future-location choice.

If closeness only makes scenes softer, it is dramatically weak.

## CARD 3 — DEFENSE MUST HAVE A HISTORY
A character's withdrawal/jealousy/avoidance should be an understandable strategy that once protected something.

Do not diagnose characters in prose.
Show the strategy:
- joke first;
- disappear after intimacy;
- demand certainty;
- test loyalty;
- keep options open;
- overwork;
- hide need;
- flirt publicly to repair status;
- refuse help.

## CARD 4 — CONNECTION / DISCONNECTION RHYTHM
Strong relationships do not need constant harmony.

Use:
CONNECT -> MISREAD -> DISTANCE -> ATTEMPTED REPAIR -> PARTIAL RECONNECTION.

But each cycle must escalate or reveal something new. Repeating the same misunderstanding is not development.

## CARD 5 — JEALOUSY NEEDS A THREAT MODEL
Jealousy should answer:
`What does the character believe they are about to lose?`

Possible losses:
- exclusivity;
- status;
- belonging;
- access;
- future plan;
- proof of being chosen;
- social face.

Generic possessiveness without a specific perceived loss is thin.

## CARD 6 — ROMANCE CHANGES LOGISTICS
A real relationship changes:
- time;
- routes/transport;
- housing decisions;
- spending;
- work availability;
- secrecy;
- peer-group membership;
- family contact;
- risk-taking;
- future plans.

If none change, the relationship may be decorative.

## CARD 7 — REPAIR IS AN ACTION
`I'm sorry` is not automatically repair.

Repair may require:
- returning access;
- telling a costly truth;
- showing up when inconvenient;
- changing a work/family decision;
- accepting a boundary;
- taking social blame;
- giving up leverage;
- allowing independence.

## CARD 8 — BREAKUP / DISTANCE MUST CHANGE THE MAIN STORY
If a major relationship ruptures, at least one should change:
- decision quality;
- ally network;
- housing/work;
- information flow;
- risk tolerance;
- status;
- main-conflict strategy.

Otherwise the breakup is detachable melodrama.

## CARD 9 — YOUTH RELATIONSHIP = SOCIAL ECOSYSTEM
For teenagers/young adults, include the surrounding network:
FRIENDS / FAMILY / WORK / SCHOOL-TRAINING / STATUS / MONEY / PARTY-SPORT-HOBBY / DIGITAL OR AI-MEDIATED COMMUNICATION.

Romance changes the network and the network changes the romance.

## CARD 10 — DIFFERENT FUTURES CREATE PRESSURE
A powerful young-adult conflict is not `do they love each other?` but:
`Can they choose compatible futures without one person disappearing into the other's life?`

Orbital examples:
- different habitats;
- expedition contract;
- Earth return;
- synthetic-body training;
- career licensing;
- family obligation;
- housing assignment.

# IVDIVO RELATIONSHIP GATE v0.1

Before a major relationship arc is GREEN:

1. What does A want from B now?
2. What does B want from A now?
3. What private need makes this relationship unusually important?
4. What existing life does each person already have outside it?
5. What vulnerability does closeness expose?
6. What defensive strategy appears under pressure?
7. What does each misread about the other?
8. What concrete external choice is changed by the relationship?
9. What does the peer/family/work network think or do?
10. What material/logistical consequence follows from greater closeness?
11. What causes the first serious rupture or withdrawal?
12. What repair action costs something?
13. What old behavior must change for the relationship to survive/evolve?
14. What happens if they do NOT end together?
15. Does the relationship still function as character/story pressure if all romantic dialogue is removed? If not, it may be too dependent on declarations.

# SMITH / ADULT RELATIONSHIP APPLICATION

Smith relationships should create operational tradeoffs:
- who gets told classified information;
- who can ask him to stop;
- what domestic life a case damages;
- what Enia/colleague/family member sees in his method that he cannot;
- what personal loyalty conflicts with jurisdiction or containment duty.

Do not use adult relationships only to humanize him between cases.

---

# RED TEAM ADDITIONS

MAJOR:
- relationship exists only in dialogue, never logistics/choices;
- jealousy has no specific perceived loss;
- breakup repeats the same argument from earlier chapters;
- one partner has no independent life or future;
- apology instantly repairs structural harm;
- attraction appears before ordinary personhood is established;
- romantic conflict could be removed without changing main-story action.

MEDIUM:
- every vulnerable moment is verbal confession;
- friends/family never react to relationship change;
- connection/disconnection cycles repeat without escalation;
- relationship conflict is generated only by avoidable failure to communicate.

# RESULT

IVDIVO relationship engine:

INDEPENDENT LIVES -> ATTRACTION/AFFILIATION -> VULNERABILITY -> CONNECTION -> EXTERNAL COST -> DEFENSE -> MISREAD/STATUS PRESSURE -> RUPTURE -> COSTLY REPAIR OR SEPARATION -> CHANGED FUTURE.

Relationship is story when it changes what characters choose, risk, hide, lose and become.
