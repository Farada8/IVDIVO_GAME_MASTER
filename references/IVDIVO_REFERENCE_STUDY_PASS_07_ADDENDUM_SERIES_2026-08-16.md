# IVDIVO REFERENCE STUDY — PASS 07 ADDENDUM: SERIES PROGRESSION

Date: 2026-08-16
Status: REFERENCE ONLY.

## Philip Pullman — The Subtle Knife
Drive ID: 1IPByF9BFrmi9oOuMkAlhf2-OLpUkU8Ln
Status: PRIMARY YOUTH/FANTASY REFERENCE.

### Mechanism
Book Two expands the saga by introducing a new protagonist with a complete pre-existing personal crisis rather than merely resuming Book One's protagonist. Will is twelve, already carrying adult responsibility for a mentally unstable mother, hiding family problems from institutions, searching for information about his missing father, and becomes implicated in a death before the larger fantasy line absorbs him.

### IVDIVO transformation
A sequel may widen the saga through a new HERO / WANT / WHY NOW / PRICE engine that can stand on its own before intersecting the existing cast. The new hero must arrive with ordinary life, family, fear, guilt and an immediate problem—not as a franchise delivery device.

### Anti-copy
Do not copy portals, knife, missing-father structure, Lyra/Will relationship or plot sequence.

## Marie Lu — The Rose Society (Young Elites 2)
Drive ID: 1P61XgKzs7fsvMEPR_4k3P3u012fPE36I
Status: PRIMARY SERIES/YOUTH REFERENCE.

### Mechanism
Consequences of Book One are inherited rather than reset. Rejection, guilt and betrayal convert Adelina's original longing for belonging into a drive to assemble her own group and control the conditions of belonging. Her powers and internal voices grow with her damage; the sequel changes her strategy and moral trajectory rather than repeating the initiation arc.

### IVDIVO transformation
Book N must begin from the permanent emotional/social consequences of Book N-1. A changed protagonist needs a changed WANT and WRONG STRATEGY. Power growth should amplify character contradiction and cost, not simply increase spectacle.

### Anti-copy
Do not copy societies, powers, plague, named relationships or political map.

## Marie Lu — The Midnight Star (Young Elites 3)
Drive ID: 1vPbK3KApACa1bht-j688fk80vWbFSN_b
Status: PRIMARY SERIES/YOUTH REFERENCE.

### Mechanism
The former persecuted outsider is now a ruler and conqueror. The dramatic question therefore changes from survival/belonging to governance, legitimacy and what the character does with acquired power. Personal trauma persists inside public policy: fear, punishment, rebels, territorial control, economic decisions and intimate relationships now test the protagonist at a different scale.

### IVDIVO transformation
A later saga book should not simply give the same hero a larger enemy. Change the social role and therefore the kind of choices the hero must make. If a victim becomes authority, test them through responsibility, administration, opposition and the temptation to reproduce the system that harmed them.

### Anti-copy
Do not copy conquest arc, characters, magic system or specific political conflicts.

## SERIES LAW reinforced by this mini-corpus
1. A sequel must inherit consequences, not restore starting positions.
2. A sequel can introduce a new protagonist, but that protagonist needs a complete story engine before crossover.
3. Power/status escalation must create new moral and practical responsibilities, not just stronger action scenes.
4. Relationships must change function across books; the same friendship/romance/conflict cannot simply replay.
5. A Book Two/Three hook is not enough: each volume still needs its own main conflict, climax choice and completed resolution.
