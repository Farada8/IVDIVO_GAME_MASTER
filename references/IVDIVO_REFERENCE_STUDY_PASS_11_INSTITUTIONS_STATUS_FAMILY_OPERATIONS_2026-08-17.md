## 1. WILLIAM RITTER — BLOODY FOOL FOR LOVE

Metadata verified from EPUB:
- Title: Bloody Fool for Love
- Author: William Ritter
- Publisher: Disney Book Group / Hyperion
- First edition: 2022
- Licensed Buffy prequel; audience 14–18
- Source completeness: LIKELY_FULL
- Source role: DERIVATIVE / LICENSED FRANCHISE REFERENCE

### Observed story engine
Spike returns to London with a newly inflated reputation after killing a Slayer, while his relationship with Drusilla is destabilized by Angelus's departure. He wants to prove that he is enough for her and adopts the wrong strategy: acquire an extraordinary relic that he believes can make her stop dwelling on the past and focus on their future. That private insecurity generates an external heist/conflict with Gunnar's organized supernatural underworld.

The important craft feature is not vampires. It is that the nonhuman world behaves like a society:
- poor housing;
- money shortages;
- shops and goods;
- reputation;
- formal status declaration;
- guild appointments;
- criminal hierarchy;
- territorial expectations;
- public-relations cleanup after occult violence;
- infrastructure damage that continues after the fight.

The climax destroys Spike's plan but reveals that the relic was never the actual relationship solution. Drusilla's relational position is not what Spike imagined. His wrong strategy came from insecurity, not lack of power.

### Abstract mechanisms
**NONHUMAN CIVIL SOCIETY**
A nonhuman population becomes dramatically convincing when it has mundane coordination problems in addition to danger: property, hierarchy, services, reputation, obligations, money, cover stories and cleanup.

**PRIVATE INSECURITY -> EXTERNAL OPERATION**
A protagonist may launch a dangerous mission because they are trying to solve an emotional problem with an operational/tool-based solution.

**STATUS IS A PRACTICAL ASSET**
Reputation changes access, expectations, enemies and institutional obligations.

**POST-CLIMAX SYSTEM CONSEQUENCES**
Destroying the threat can create structural, legal and public-cover problems that other institutions must absorb.

### IVDIVO use
Smith / Old Earth Security:
- A nonhuman case should not default to creature taxonomy.
- A hidden nonhuman community may have internal law, service networks, businesses, reputation systems, property and agreements with old Earth institutions.
- Smith may know which practical institution to call before Confederation science even understands that the institution exists.

Orbital Youth:
- A mixed-species/nonhuman peer group should have social rules and ordinary material life, not exist as exotic decoration.

### Anti-copy
Do not copy vampires, demon guilds, Buffy relationships, relics, named institutions, London supernatural geography or plot sequence.

### Recommended permanent shelf
PRIMARY: `27_SCREEN_TV_MEDIA_REFERENCE / tie-in novelizations`
Secondary conceptual index: nonhuman institutions / youth franchise mechanisms.

## 2. HILARY A. HALLETT — INVENTING THE IT GIRL

Metadata verified from EPUB:
- Title: Inventing the It Girl
- Author: Hilary A. Hallett
- Publisher: Liveright / W. W. Norton
- Subject: Elinor Glyn, modern romance, celebrity authorship, early Hollywood
- Source completeness: LIKELY_FULL
- Source role: NONFICTION / CULTURAL BIOGRAPHY / STATUS-MEDIA REFERENCE

### High-value observation
The useful material is not a plot template. It is a model of how status is produced by institutions.

The book traces how literary scandal, sexual discourse, celebrity authorship, the Hollywood star system, publicity, audience segmentation and economic structures turned individuals and labels into mass-cultural status objects.

Important systemic observations:
- the star system can convert previously unknown workers into highly valuable public personalities;
- industries actively decide what forms of glamour and respectability are marketable;
- audience composition changes production strategy;
- stigma such as 'trash' can coexist with enormous commercial reach and cultural influence;
- a simple cultural label such as 'It Girl' can make a complex social quality legible, repeatable and marketable;
- public reputation is produced by writers, studios, publishers, photographers, press, social circles and audiences together.

### Abstract mechanism
**STATUS IS PRODUCED, NOT JUST POSSESSED**
Popularity is not only a personality trait. It can be an output of institutions, media systems, employers, platforms, sponsors and classification labels.

### IVDIVO use
Orbital Youth sociology:
- distinguish peer popularity from institutionally manufactured status;
- ask who makes a young pilot, athlete, expedition member, synthetic-body performer, habitat creator or student into a public figure;
- show what public status changes in work access, housing, romance, friendships, safety and family expectations;
- allow local orbital media ecosystems to create different status categories from Earth.

Producer / market-worldbuilding:
- entertainment systems should not be generic future social media; they need incentives, intermediaries and audience economics.

### Anti-overreach
Historical Hollywood is not a direct forecast of orbital media. Extract only the mechanism of institutional status production.

### Recommended permanent shelf
PRIMARY: `28_MEMOIR_BIOGRAPHY_LIVED_EXPERIENCE`
Cross-index: sociology/status/media institutions.

## 3. E. A. ALLEN — PERCY ST. JOHN AND THE CHRONICLE OF SECRETS

Metadata verified from EPUB:
- Title: Percy St. John and the Chronicle of Secrets
- Author: E. A. Allen
- Histria Kids
- Copyright 2021 / ebook issue 2022
- Setting includes St. Ambrose monastery, Switzerland, 1911
- Source completeness: LIKELY_FULL
- Source role: YOUTH HISTORICAL MYSTERY / ADVENTURE REFERENCE

### Observed engine
Percy is a young orphan whose father's murder and later survival as a thief shaped a socially dubious but unusually useful skill set. Inside a monastery, ordinary institutional routines become the detection grid: prayers, attendance, rooms, pilgrims, hierarchy, rules, forbidden spaces, relics, records and permissions.

A recurring investigative strength is noticing deviations from routine. One simple example is not merely asking who was present at prayers but who was absent.

Percy's thief skills — locks, deception, unauthorized access, practical trickery — make him effective while remaining morally and institutionally uncomfortable. He is not simply rewarded for being talented.

The mystery mixes competing explanations: crime, religious belief, folklore/supernatural interpretation and intelligence activity. The local case materially resolves: the missing sacred object/book is recovered, the cipher is solved and the immediate criminal problem closes. Only after that resolution does the larger series direction open: French intelligence sees a use for Percy's unusual abilities and he leaves the monastery with Gabriel.

### Abstract mechanisms
**ROUTINE AS CLUE GRID**
To detect an anomaly, establish how the institution normally works first. A missing person, wrong access, delayed maintenance action or absent worker only means something against a known baseline.

**DISREPUTABLE SKILL -> INSTITUTIONAL ASSET**
A skill developed in a morally ambiguous context can become valuable to a legitimate institution without becoming morally clean.

**FRIEND / NARRATOR AS ETHICAL COUNTERWEIGHT**
A close companion can reveal cost, affection, doubt and moral friction without forcing the hyper-competent protagonist to explain themselves constantly.

**LOCAL RESOLUTION -> NEW ROLE HOOK**
The current mystery closes before the next professional life opens.

### IVDIVO use
Smith:
- old security expertise may include methods considered improper or obsolete by Confederation procedure;
- competence should create jurisdiction and ethical problems, not automatically confer authority.

Orbital Youth:
- habitat routines — work rosters, transport windows, school/training attendance, maintenance intervals, access permissions — can function as natural clue architecture.
- a young character can notice a clue precisely because they know what is usually boring and normal.

### Anti-copy
Do not copy monastery mystery, thief-recruitment sequence, relic/cipher, named intelligence services, characters or supernatural ambiguity.

### Recommended permanent shelf
PRIMARY: `09_ADVENTURE_HISTORICAL_FICTION / general historical fiction`
Secondary: youth mystery mechanisms.
Physical move pending Drive ID resolution.

## 4. BERNADETTE JIWA — THE MAKING OF HER

Metadata verified from EPUB:
- Title: The Making of Her
- Author: Bernadette Jiwa
- Publisher: Penguin / Dutton
- Dublin 1966 and 1996
- Source completeness: LIKELY_FULL
- Source role: CONTEMPORARY / FAMILY LITERARY FICTION REFERENCE

### Observed engine
A long-buried family secret does not return merely because somebody decides 'the truth must come out.' It is activated by a present practical emergency.

Joan and Martin gave up a daughter for adoption decades earlier. In 1996 that daughter, Emma, contacts them because her young son has leukemia and needs a compatible bone-marrow donor. The old moral/family secret is therefore forced into the present by a concrete biological need.

The revelation does not remain information. It alters:
- Joan's marriage;
- Carmel's understanding of her parents;
- Emma's relationship to the biological family;
- Joan/Carmel closeness;
- family business dynamics;
- Carmel's career decision;
- housing and domestic separation;
- Joan's willingness to remain in the life structure that previously contained the secret.

The ending produces permanent change rather than a confession-reset: Joan leaves the old marital arrangement, Carmel separates from her father's business trajectory and pursues her own development plans, and mother/daughter relations change.

### Abstract mechanisms
**DORMANT SECRET ACTIVATED BY PRESENT NEED**
Past information becomes story only when a current need makes inaction impossible.

**REVELATION MUST REDISTRIBUTE RELATIONSHIPS**
A secret reveal is not a twist unless it changes trust, leverage, obligations, identity and choices.

**FAMILY BUSINESS = RELATIONSHIP INFRASTRUCTURE**
Work and money can encode family hierarchy. Leaving a job can therefore also be leaving a relational role.

**WORLD THROUGH ORDINARY SYSTEMS**
Dublin emerges through jobs, builders' merchants, housing, church/social norms, adoption context, family expectations, money and changing economic possibilities rather than historical lecture.

### IVDIVO use
Orbital Youth / family architecture:
- a parent's past secret should affect present housing, work, permission, money, identity or bodily need;
- family secrets should be activated by causality, not by author convenience;
- a young person's career choice can be a relationship decision at the same time.

Smith:
- old agreements/secrets become strongest when a present person or institution suddenly needs something only the old record can provide.

### Recommended permanent shelf
PRIMARY: `10_CONTEMPORARY_LITERARY_FICTION`
Secondary: family / relationship architecture.

## 5. THEODORE TAYLOR — TO KILL THE LEOPARD

Metadata verified from EPUB:
- Title: To Kill the Leopard
- Author: Theodore Taylor
- historical wartime fiction
- U-boat / Allied shipping / intelligence / deception system
- Source completeness: LIKELY_FULL
- Source role: HISTORICAL OPERATIONS / ACTION / SECURITY REFERENCE

### Observed engine
The novel gives both sides operational competence. German U-boat activity, Allied merchant shipping, radio direction finding, tracking stations, convoy systems, technical work and eventually a disguised Q-ship/counter-ambush all operate through procedures rather than generic battle spectacle.

A key mechanism is distributed information: one station intercepting a signal is not enough. Bearings, message timing, traffic plots and multiple observers become a usable operational picture when combined.

Another mechanism is adversarial deception. The apparent merchant ship must behave convincingly as an ordinary vulnerable ship long enough for the enemy to commit. The climax then pays off earlier operational knowledge: ship handling, weapon limits, submarine movement, decoy structures and prior observation.

A personal relationship also becomes an information-security surface. Kammerer's involvement with Hélène intersects with espionage suspicion and official-record risk. Private life and operational security are not separate universes.

### Abstract mechanisms
**DISTRIBUTED FRAGMENTS -> OPERATIONAL CERTAINTY**
No witness or sensor needs the whole answer. Reliability emerges from intersection.

**DECEPTION MUST SURVIVE ROUTINE BEHAVIOR**
A cover works only if ordinary procedures, appearance and timing support it under pressure.

**RELATIONSHIP AS INFOSEC SURFACE**
Affection, attraction, guilt and trust can create leaks or procedural distortion without anyone being a cartoon traitor.

**CLIMAX PAYS OFF OPERATING KNOWLEDGE**
The decisive action should use constraints and competencies taught earlier, not a surprise capability introduced at the end.

**COMPETENCE != MORAL EQUIVALENCE**
Opposing sides can both be technically capable without the story claiming that their causes or actions are ethically equivalent.

### IVDIVO use
Smith / Old Earth Security:
- combine sensor/anomaly fragments from incompatible systems before declaring ontology;
- Old Earth procedure and Confederation science can each hold one part of the picture;
- a containment or negotiation operation may depend on behaving normally long enough for the other side to reveal intent;
- relationships, family or informal contacts should be treated as operational variables where relevant;
- climax should pay off known limits, jurisdictions, tools and procedures.

### Recommended permanent shelf
PRIMARY: `09_ADVENTURE_HISTORICAL_FICTION / 01_WARTIME_20C_HISTORICAL_FICTION`
Secondary: action / operations mechanism reference.

# PASS 11 — INTEGRATED MECHANISM CARDS

## CARD 1 — NONHUMAN CIVIL SOCIETY
REFERENCE BASIS: Ritter + IVDIVO existing Smith law.

Threat/neighbor population should be tested for:
PROPERTY -> SERVICES -> STATUS -> INTERNAL LAW -> MONEY/EXCHANGE -> TERRITORY -> FAMILY/KIN -> REPUTATION -> DISPUTE RESOLUTION -> CLEANUP.

A nonhuman society with none of these may be a monster catalogue rather than a civilization.

## CARD 2 — STATUS IS PRODUCED
REFERENCE BASIS: Hallett + Storr Pass 09 + Steinberg Pass 09.

For important youth status ask:
WHO OBSERVES -> WHO LABELS -> WHO AMPLIFIES -> WHO PROFITS -> WHAT ACCESS CHANGES -> WHO RESENTS IT -> WHAT HAPPENS WHEN STATUS FALLS.

## CARD 3 — ROUTINE AS CLUE GRID
REFERENCE BASIS: Allen + Veronica Mars Pass 10 + Seveneves Pass 07/08.

NORMAL ROUTINE -> SMALL DEVIATION -> ONLY INSIDER NOTICES -> WRONG EXPLANATION -> SECOND DEVIATION -> PATTERN -> ACTION.

Worldbuilding therefore becomes mystery infrastructure instead of exposition.

## CARD 4 — COMPROMISED SKILL AS ASSET
REFERENCE BASIS: Allen + Smith production law.

PAST SURVIVAL/ILLEGAL/OLD-SYSTEM SKILL -> CURRENT USEFULNESS -> INSTITUTIONAL DISTRUST -> NECESSITY -> PRICE/OVERSIGHT -> CHANGED ROLE.

Do not transform controversial competence into uncomplicated superpower.

## CARD 5 — SECRET ACTIVATED BY NEED
REFERENCE BASIS: Jiwa + Christie Pass 07.

OLD SECRET -> PRESENT PRACTICAL NEED -> CONTACT/NEW EVIDENCE -> RELATIONSHIP REDISTRIBUTION -> ACTION -> PERMANENT CHANGE.

The present need is what makes the reveal a story rather than lore.

## CARD 6 — RELATIONSHIP AS OPERATIONAL SURFACE
REFERENCE BASIS: Taylor + Jiwa + Pass 10 youth relationship mechanisms.

PRIVATE RELATIONSHIP -> WITHHELD INFO / TRUST / ACCESS / DISTRACTION / LEAK / PROTECTION -> PROCEDURAL CONSEQUENCE.

Do not add romance/family as a parallel track if it changes nothing operationally.

## CARD 7 — CLIMAX AS PROCEDURAL PAYOFF
REFERENCE BASIS: Taylor + McKee/Truby Pass 09.

EARLY LIMIT/RULE -> REPEATED USE -> FAILURE/LEARNING -> ADVERSARY ADAPTS -> HERO CHOOSES -> SAME KNOWN SYSTEM USED DIFFERENTLY -> PRICE -> RESULT.

No endgame gadget/ability that bypasses the story's established constraints.

# NEW STORY GATE QUESTIONS FROM PASS 11

Before GREEN, ask where relevant:

1. What normal routine establishes the baseline from which the hero can detect a deviation?
2. If status matters, who actually produces/amplifies that status and what material access does it change?
3. If a secret exists, what present need makes it impossible to leave buried now?
4. Does the protagonist have a useful skill that an institution distrusts, restricts or morally questions?
5. What private relationship can alter access, information, judgment or procedure?
6. Which operating fact/rule introduced before midpoint materially enables the climax?
7. If nonhuman beings form a recurring population, what ordinary institutions keep their life functioning?
8. After destructive action, who handles legal, physical, social and infrastructural consequences?
9. Does the current case/book resolve before a new employer, jurisdiction or series direction appears?

# RED TEAM ADDITIONS

MAJOR if:
- a nonhuman society has powers but no ordinary coordination system;
- youth status exists without an amplifier, audience or consequence;
- a secret is revealed because the author needs a twist rather than because the present creates pressure;
- the protagonist's unique skill is useful with no ethical/institutional cost;
- romance/family never changes information, access, judgment or action;
- climax depends on a new capability rather than accumulated operational knowledge.

MEDIUM if:
- world routine is shown repeatedly but never becomes causal;
- status is described but never changes behavior;
- post-climax institutional consequences vanish immediately.
