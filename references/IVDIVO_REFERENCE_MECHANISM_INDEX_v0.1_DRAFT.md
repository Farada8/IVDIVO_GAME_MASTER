# SOURCE SET A — JOHN TRUBY / THE ANATOMY OF STORY

## M001 — DESIRE → ACTION → LEARNING → DECISION → NEW ACTION

SOURCE FUNCTION:
Story movement grows from a character wanting something, acting to obtain it, discovering new information, then changing course.

TRIGGER:
Hero has a concrete desire and opposition makes the first method insufficient.

SEQUENCE:
Want → attempt → resistance → discovery → decision → revised attempt → increased price.

READER EFFECT:
Causality feels organic because the next event is caused by what the protagonist did and learned.

FAILURE MODE:
A plot where events arrive from outside while the hero merely receives information.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
For every major chapter, require the protagonist to leave with a different operational model than they entered with. New information must alter behavior, not merely expand lore.

DO NOT COPY:
No Truby examples, terminology webs, example plots, or scene sequences are imported.

---

## M002 — STORY AS AN ORGANISM, NOT AN EXTERNAL TEMPLATE

SOURCE FUNCTION:
Character, plot, revelations, story world, moral conflict, scenes and dialogue work as interdependent systems.

TRIGGER:
A book outline begins to feel like disconnected beats or mandatory act markers.

SEQUENCE:
Core problem → character need/desire → opposition → story world pressure → revelations → choices → climax.

READER EFFECT:
The novel feels like one developing thing rather than a sequence of assigned events.

FAILURE MODE:
Three-act or beat-sheet compliance without internal causality; episodic fragments that can be rearranged without consequence.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
Before prose, test whether removing one major subsystem changes the others. If not, the architecture is probably decorative rather than causal.

---

## M003 — AUDIENCE PARTICIPATION THROUGH CONTROLLED WITHHOLDING

SOURCE FUNCTION:
A story gives enough information for the reader to form models while withholding enough to force active interpretation.

TRIGGER:
Mystery, identity, institutional secrecy, interpersonal misreading, unknown technology.

SEQUENCE:
Observable fact → partial explanation → contradiction → revised model → consequential reveal.

READER EFFECT:
The audience is simultaneously feeling the story and solving it.

FAILURE MODE:
Arbitrary withholding that characters would naturally disclose; mystery based on author cheating.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
Use especially in SMITH, Old Earth Security and AI/identity lines. Every withheld fact must have an in-world reason and later alter action.

---

## M004 — CHANGE IS DRIVEN BY DESIRE UNDER RESISTANCE

SOURCE FUNCTION:
Opposition forces a character to struggle; struggle creates the conditions for change or exposes refusal to change.

TRIGGER:
Hero’s current strategy collides with a person/system/world that will not yield.

SEQUENCE:
Desire → resistance → escalation → self-exposure → choice → changed or confirmed self.

READER EFFECT:
Character arc is produced by story rather than attached as commentary.

FAILURE MODE:
Hero ‘learns a lesson’ in dialogue after the plot is over.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
Every major character arc must be traceable to specific failed actions and costly choices.

---

## M005 — TELEVISION AS A MINI-SOCIETY OF SIMULTANEOUS CHANGE

SOURCE FUNCTION:
Multiple characters pursue different desires inside one social system; their changes interact.

TRIGGER:
Ensemble serial setting: orbital city, school/work habitat, security institution, mixed-species neighborhood.

SEQUENCE:
Shared institution → divergent wants → collisions → cascading consequences → altered relationships/status.

READER EFFECT:
World feels inhabited because no one exists only for the protagonist.

FAILURE MODE:
Equal-airtime committee scenes or parallel subplots that never causally touch.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
ORBITAL YOUTH should operate as a living social ecology, not a hero plus supporting exposition machines.

# SOURCE SET B — JAMES SCOTT BELL / CONFLICT & SUSPENSE

## M006 — TROUBLE IS THE ENTRY PRICE OF A SCENE

SOURCE FUNCTION:
A scene needs meaningful trouble: an obstacle, danger, social cost, inner clash, relationship pressure, or incompatible agenda.

TRIGGER:
A scene exists mainly to deliver information, atmosphere or worldbuilding.

SEQUENCE:
Want → friction → tactical response → counterpressure → changed state.

READER EFFECT:
Even quiet scenes possess forward tension.

FAILURE MODE:
Confusing ‘conflict’ with shouting, violence or constant melodrama.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
A meal, commute, lesson, repair shift or date can carry story if someone wants something the situation resists.

---

## M007 — CONFLICT AS COMPETING WILL

SOURCE FUNCTION:
Drama intensifies when at least one conscious actor pushes toward a goal against resistance; strongest interpersonal conflict uses incompatible agendas.

TRIGGER:
Two people/institutions need outcomes that cannot both occur unchanged.

SEQUENCE:
Agenda A → Agenda B → tactic → countertactic → leverage shift → choice/cost.

READER EFFECT:
Opposition feels intelligent instead of mechanical.

FAILURE MODE:
Obstacle exists only because the plot needs delay.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
Core for OLD EARTH SECURITY ↔ NEW CONFEDERATION SECURITY: each side must possess legitimate aims, resources, blind spots and non-negotiable interests.

---

## M008 — SUSPENSE AS A LOCAL QUESTION INSIDE A LARGER CONFLICT

SOURCE FUNCTION:
The central conflict supplies the long arc; individual moments tighten around immediate uncertainty.

TRIGGER:
Reader knows the broad problem but needs a reason to continue this page/chapter.

SEQUENCE:
Large unresolved conflict → local danger/question → narrowed options → delayed answer → consequence.

READER EFFECT:
Page-turning propulsion without requiring a giant cliffhanger every chapter.

FAILURE MODE:
Fake danger repeatedly reset with no cost.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
Vary suspense questions: Will they be exposed? believed? paid? selected? forgiven? trapped? forced to reveal ability? late for work? lose jurisdiction? survive?

---

## M009 — TENSION NEEDS BUILDUP AND RELEASE

SOURCE FUNCTION:
Sustained engagement comes from modulation, not permanent maximum intensity.

TRIGGER:
A draft is either flat or exhausting.

SEQUENCE:
Anticipation → pressure → partial release → new complication → stronger anticipation → payoff/aftershock.

READER EFFECT:
Emotional rhythm and recoverable attention.

FAILURE MODE:
Every paragraph screams urgency; reader becomes numb.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
Alternate crisis with ordinary life that carries consequences: food, transport, family, training, money, repairs, intimacy, embarrassment.

# SOURCE SET C — JESSICA BRODY / SAVE THE CAT! WRITES A YOUNG ADULT NOVEL

## M010 — YA IS AN AGE LENS, NOT A SINGLE GENRE

SOURCE FUNCTION:
The same age category can host SF, fantasy, mystery, horror, romance, thriller and social drama.

TRIGGER:
ORBITAL YOUTH risks becoming ‘school story in space’ or being trapped in one tonal lane.

SEQUENCE:
Young protagonist’s developmental pressure + chosen genre engine + concrete social world.

READER EFFECT:
Youth life remains central while plots can vary radically.

FAILURE MODE:
Adult genre story with teenage ages pasted onto the cast.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
Each Orbital Youth book/episode should ask what this conflict means specifically for independence, status, first work, family, friendship, attraction, future and identity.

---

## M011 — STORY-WORTHY HERO BEFORE WORLD COMPLEXITY

SOURCE FUNCTION:
Reader commitment begins with a protagonist whose situation, contradictions and stakes create investment.

TRIGGER:
Worldbuilding or franchise lore begins before the reader cares about a person.

SEQUENCE:
Specific ordinary life → desire/frustration → humanizing contradiction → disturbance → active choice.

READER EFFECT:
The world becomes relevant because it affects someone we follow.

FAILURE MODE:
Mechanical ‘likability trick’ or saintly hero designed only to win sympathy.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
CHARACTER BEFORE ABILITY. Introduce capability after ordinary pressures and personal wants are legible.

---

## M012 — DEVELOPMENTAL ENGINE + GENRE ENGINE

SOURCE FUNCTION:
YA stories often work best when the external plot and a developmental problem pressure each other.

TRIGGER:
A spectacular plot could happen identically to a 40-year-old protagonist.

SEQUENCE:
External problem → peer/family/status consequence → wrong youthful strategy → escalating cost → mature/changed choice.

READER EFFECT:
Genre action and coming-of-age become one story.

FAILURE MODE:
Separate ‘teen drama subplot’ pasted between action scenes.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
In ORBITAL YOUTH, orbital work, housing, transport, AI, mixed-species culture and emergency systems must materially change adolescence and independence.

# SOURCE SET D — FRIDAY NIGHT LIGHTS / “IT’S DIFFERENT FOR GIRLS”

## M013 — SOCIAL ECOLOGY THROUGH ONE SHARED DAY

SOURCE FUNCTION:
A single institutional day can carry multiple intersecting pressures: sport, reputation, gender, family, romance, disability, ambition and peer hierarchy.

TRIGGER:
Need to reveal society without exposition.

SEQUENCE:
Shared place/event → different character stakes → cross-cut pressures → public consequences → private fallout.

READER EFFECT:
The setting feels like a society rather than a backdrop.

FAILURE MODE:
Five parallel vignettes that never affect each other.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
Use one orbital event — docking day, qualification exam, habitat match, maintenance shutdown, game, festival, job allocation — to expose several characters’ incompatible stakes.

---

## M014 — PUBLIC STATUS / PRIVATE PAIN ASYMMETRY

SOURCE FUNCTION:
Characters perform one identity publicly while carrying a contradictory private reality.

TRIGGER:
High-status youth, team captain, celebrated cadet, influencer, gifted operator, rich child, nonhuman exchange student.

SEQUENCE:
Public role → social expectation → private wound/secret → public test → loss of control → status change.

READER EFFECT:
Immediate depth without biography dump.

FAILURE MODE:
Secret exists only for melodramatic reveal and does not change behavior.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
Orbital reputation systems, work ratings, school cohorts and public feeds can make ordinary embarrassment as consequential as physical danger.

---

## M015 — WORLD REVEAL THROUGH JOBS, MONEY, FAMILY AND BODY

SOURCE FUNCTION:
Social reality emerges from what people do, earn, owe, fear, perform and physically manage.

TRIGGER:
Need to explain an institution or culture.

SEQUENCE:
Concrete task/body constraint → relationship reaction → institutional expectation → social meaning.

READER EFFECT:
Context is learned almost invisibly.

FAILURE MODE:
Characters pause to explain facts they all already know.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
Reveal orbital civilization through rent, oxygen/energy budgets, transit, repairs, school-work pathways, medical access, synthetic-body access, family schedules and status rules.

# SOURCE SET E — CONSTANTINE TV / PILOT + “A FEAST OF FRIENDS”

## M016 — OBSERVABLE ANOMALY BEFORE ONTOLOGY

SOURCE FUNCTION:
The audience sees an impossible physical pattern before receiving the supernatural explanation.

TRIGGER:
Introduction of anomaly/nonhuman/metaphysical phenomenon.

SEQUENCE:
Small wrongness → repeated pattern → impossible escalation → expert recognition → provisional explanation.

READER EFFECT:
Curiosity and dread precede terminology.

FAILURE MODE:
Opening with a lecture about invisible systems.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
Core rule for SMITH and metaphysics: phenomenon first; taxonomy later.

---

## M017 — RELUCTANT EXPERT RE-ENTERS BECAUSE THE CASE HITS A PERSONAL FAULT LINE

SOURCE FUNCTION:
The protagonist’s return to action is caused by a threat that pierces the strategy of avoidance.

TRIGGER:
Experienced operative has withdrawn, denied responsibility or tried to live normally.

SEQUENCE:
Avoidance strategy → anomaly → personal signal/victim → refusal → escalation → voluntary re-entry.

READER EFFECT:
‘Why now?’ is personal, not merely procedural.

FAILURE MODE:
Hero joins because headquarters assigns a mission.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
SMITH cases should activate something Old Earth Security knows, owes, covered up, promised or failed — never generic monster-hunting assignment logic.

---

## M018 — COMPETENCE SHOWN AS ACTION UNDER IMPERFECT CONTROL

SOURCE FUNCTION:
Expertise is demonstrated through recognition, tools, improvisation, humor and partial failure instead of a résumé speech.

TRIGGER:
Introducing a specialist, psychic, synthetic operator or old-system veteran.

SEQUENCE:
Signal noticed → rapid hypothesis → intervention → complication → adaptation → outcome/cost.

READER EFFECT:
The character feels skilled but not omnipotent.

FAILURE MODE:
Power solves the scene immediately, or side characters narrate how brilliant the hero is.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
Every unusual ability needs operational limits, false positives, countermeasures and consequences.

---

## M019 — CASE-OF-THE-WEEK CAN REOPEN CHARACTER DEBT

SOURCE FUNCTION:
A present anomaly is linked to an old relationship, failure or moral debt, making the case emotionally non-interchangeable.

TRIGGER:
Serial procedural risks repetition.

SEQUENCE:
New case → old person/history surfaces → professional objective conflicts with personal debt → harder choice → permanent relationship/character consequence.

READER EFFECT:
Episode closes its case while advancing character history.

FAILURE MODE:
Mythology hook replaces resolution of the current case.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
Each SMITH book/episode resolves the immediate security problem first, then leaves only consequences or a new hook.

---

## M020 — TRAINING IS DRAMA WHEN CONTROL ITSELF IS THE PROBLEM

SOURCE FUNCTION:
Ability training becomes a scene of vulnerability, trust, fear and risk rather than tutorial exposition.

TRIGGER:
Character learning psychic/AI/synthetic/metaphysical capability.

SEQUENCE:
Need for control → mentor asks for risky surrender/experiment → resistance → partial success → unexpected signal → relationship shift.

READER EFFECT:
World rule, character fear and relationship develop in one scene.

FAILURE MODE:
Training montage that only lists powers.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
Use for youth interfaces, synthetic-body embodiment, AI partnership, perception abilities and nonhuman communication.

# FIRST CROSS-SOURCE SYNTHESIS

## S01 — IVDIVO CAUSAL STORY LOOP

Combine M001 + M006 + M008:

`WANT → ACTION → TROUBLE → LOCAL SUSPENSE QUESTION → NEW INFORMATION → DECISION → CHANGED ACTION → COST`

Use as the minimum causal engine for chapters and major scenes.

A chapter that contains information but no changed action fails this loop.

---

## S02 — ORBITAL YOUTH SOCIAL STORY LOOP

Combine M010 + M012 + M013 + M014 + M015:

`ORDINARY ORBITAL LIFE → YOUTH WANT/STATUS NEED → SHARED INSTITUTIONAL PRESSURE → PUBLIC/PRIVATE CONTRADICTION → BAD OR IMMATURE STRATEGY → SOCIAL/RELATIONSHIP COST → EXTERNAL PLOT ESCALATION → CHOICE THAT CHANGES INDEPENDENCE`

This prevents ‘ordinary Earth school transplanted to space.’

---

## S03 — SMITH / OLD EARTH SECURITY CASE LOOP

Combine M003 + M007 + M016 + M017 + M018 + M019:

`OBSERVABLE ANOMALY → OLD SYSTEM RECOGNIZES SOMETHING → NEW SYSTEM OFFERS A DIFFERENT MODEL → JURISDICTION/INTEREST CONFLICT → ACTION TESTS BOTH MODELS → OLD DEBT/ERROR SURFACES → FORCED COOPERATION OR BREAK → IMMEDIATE CASE RESOLVED → PERMANENT CONSEQUENCE`

Neither old nor Confederation security is automatically correct.
