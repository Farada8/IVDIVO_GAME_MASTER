# SOURCE SET J — JOHN LE CARRÉ / TINKER TAILOR SOLDIER SPY

## M035 — THE MOST DANGEROUS THREAT MAY ALREADY POSSESS TRUST

SOURCE FUNCTION:
Institutional danger is strongest when the suspected adversary has legitimate credentials, history, relationships and access inside the system.

TRIGGER:
Security organization assumes threat comes from outside.

SEQUENCE:
Trusted system → anomalous failure → competing explanations → suspicion becomes politically dangerous → evidence must be gathered without alerting the institution → trust network fractures.

READER EFFECT:
Paranoia has structural cause rather than atmosphere alone.

FAILURE MODE:
Obvious traitor telegraphed by villain behavior.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
Old Earth Security and Confederation Security should both face actors whose formal status is valid. ‘Authorized’ must not mean ‘safe’; ‘secretive’ must not mean ‘guilty.’

BEST FOR:
SMITH; Continuity; Mystery Architect.

---

## M036 — BIOGRAPHY IS OPERATIONAL EVIDENCE

SOURCE FUNCTION:
Old postings, injuries, absences, friendships, institutional gaps and career anomalies become clues to present security truth.

TRIGGER:
A case involves a veteran organization with decades of hidden history.

SEQUENCE:
Present anomaly → personnel/history question → incomplete record → human witness/memory → contradiction → revised institutional model.

READER EFFECT:
History feels causally alive.

FAILURE MODE:
Archive lore exists only to explain worldbuilding.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
SMITH’s Old Earth archive should contain successes, crimes, missing periods, informal agreements, personnel debts and deliberately incomplete records that matter to current action.

BEST FOR:
Old Earth Security cases; Historical Depth Engine.

---

## M037 — INFORMATION ACCESS IS A FORM OF POWER

SOURCE FUNCTION:
Who may ask, know, copy, compare or revisit information determines what can be discovered.

TRIGGER:
Mystery inside a classified institution.

SEQUENCE:
Need information → access barrier → indirect route / old contact → partial record → detection risk → leverage shift.

READER EFFECT:
Investigation gains stakes before physical danger arrives.

FAILURE MODE:
Characters simply open the database containing the answer.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
Confederation may possess superior analytics but lack access to old terrestrial compartments; Old Earth may possess historical keys but lack the science to interpret new evidence.

BEST FOR:
SMITH; institutional mystery; AI-security interface.

---

## M038 — INSTITUTIONAL LOYALTY IS NOT THE SAME AS MORAL LOYALTY

SOURCE FUNCTION:
Characters can protect an institution, a country, a colleague, a doctrine or the truth — and those loyalties can conflict.

TRIGGER:
Evidence threatens the legitimacy of one’s own side.

SEQUENCE:
Discovery → loyalty conflict → conceal/reveal dilemma → interpersonal pressure → choice → institutional consequence.

READER EFFECT:
Moral ambiguity emerges from real obligations.

FAILURE MODE:
One side is ‘corrupt bureaucracy’ while the hero alone represents truth.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
Neither Old Earth nor Confederation Security is automatically right. A good officer may conceal something for defensible reasons; a reformer may expose something and cause real harm.

BEST FOR:
SMITH Director; antagonist design; climax choice.

# SOURCE SET K — JAMES ROLLINS / SIGMA FORCE / SANDSTORM

## M039 — ONE ANOMALY, MULTIPLE PROFESSIONAL MODELS

SOURCE FUNCTION:
A strange event is read differently by security, military, science, curatorial/historical and investigative actors.

TRIGGER:
Evidence is genuinely ambiguous across disciplines.

SEQUENCE:
Observable event → each institution proposes a model → each model suggests a different action → models compete → new evidence invalidates or partially confirms several → forced synthesis.

READER EFFECT:
Exposition becomes conflict and inquiry.

FAILURE MODE:
The ‘smart scientist’ instantly explains everything while everyone else is stupid.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
Old Earth recognizes historical pattern; Confederation understands physics/biology better; local civilian experts know context; none has the whole answer.

BEST FOR:
SMITH; Science/Technology; Mystery Architect.

---

## M040 — JURISDICTION EMERGES FROM RISK CLASSIFICATION

SOURCE FUNCTION:
The same event may be treated as crime, terrorism, accident, scientific anomaly, heritage issue or national-security incident; classification decides who takes control.

TRIGGER:
A crisis sits at the boundary between agencies.

SEQUENCE:
Incident → provisional classification → authority claim → expert contradiction → evidence shifts category → authority struggle → operational compromise.

READER EFFECT:
Bureaucracy becomes story because classification changes what characters can do.

FAILURE MODE:
Jurisdiction argument has no effect on action.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
An unknown craft, synthetic person, nonhuman organism or dimensional anomaly should trigger different legal/security regimes depending on interpretation.

BEST FOR:
Old Earth ↔ Confederation conflict; world law.

---

## M041 — EXPERTISE CREATES SUSPICION AS WELL AS VALUE

SOURCE FUNCTION:
The person uniquely able to interpret evidence can also become a suspect because of proximity, history or privileged access.

TRIGGER:
Only one specialist understands the object/event.

SEQUENCE:
Expert summoned → expert reveals rare knowledge → institution notices connection → assistance and suspicion rise together → expert must act under scrutiny.

READER EFFECT:
Competence carries social cost.

FAILURE MODE:
Expert is treated as infallible exposition device.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
Smith or an Old Earth specialist may know too much because their system encountered the phenomenon before — which raises the question of what they failed to disclose.

BEST FOR:
SMITH; character pressure; interrogation scenes.

---

## M042 — OPERATIONAL STORY CAN BEGIN WITH A CIVILIAN SYSTEM FAILURE

SOURCE FUNCTION:
High-concept threat enters through ordinary security work, maintenance, transport, museum procedure, customs or another mundane institution.

TRIGGER:
Need to reveal extraordinary threat without opening with a battle.

SEQUENCE:
Routine task → small irregularity → procedure invoked → procedure proves inadequate → anomaly escalates → specialist/security system enters.

READER EFFECT:
Threat feels embedded in a real world.

FAILURE MODE:
Anonymous disposable victim exists only to be killed in teaser.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
Use orbital customs, repair crews, hospital intake, cargo inspection, housing maintenance, traffic control, school lab, body-service clinic or registry office as anomaly entry points.

BEST FOR:
SMITH openings; Orbital Youth crossover.

# SOURCE SET L — CONSTANTINE TV / CROSS-CHECK FOR OLD SPECIALIST LOGIC

## M043 — OLD KNOWLEDGE CAN BE CORRECT ABOUT PATTERN AND WRONG ABOUT SOLUTION

SOURCE FUNCTION:
Experienced occult/specialist knowledge identifies danger quickly, but personal history, incomplete doctrine or limited tools can still distort response.

TRIGGER:
Veteran operative encounters a familiar class of threat.

SEQUENCE:
Recognition → confident intervention → complication → missing variable exposed → adaptation / outside expertise → cost.

READER EFFECT:
Legacy expertise remains valuable without becoming magical omniscience.

FAILURE MODE:
Old expert is always right because the story romanticizes tradition.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
Old Earth Security should sometimes recognize what Confederation does not, but Confederation science should sometimes reveal why Old Earth’s established remedy was incomplete, harmful or based on a false ontology.

BEST FOR:
Core SMITH engine.

---

## M044 — PERSONAL DEBT DISTORTS PROFESSIONAL JUDGMENT

SOURCE FUNCTION:
A past failure or relationship changes what an operative is willing to risk now.

TRIGGER:
Present case echoes old unresolved harm.

SEQUENCE:
Old contact/pattern returns → guilt/loyalty activated → professional recommendation becomes biased → team resistance → costly choice → permanent consequence.

READER EFFECT:
Case becomes character-specific.

FAILURE MODE:
Trauma is backstory decoration that never affects tactics.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
Smith’s historical cases must create current blind spots, favors, promises and lines he will or will not cross.

BEST FOR:
Character Director; SMITH; climax design.

Combine M035 + M037 + M039 + M040 + M043:

`ANOMALY → OLD EARTH CLASSIFIES BY HISTORY → CONFEDERATION CLASSIFIES BY CURRENT SCIENCE/LAW → DIFFERENT AUTHORITY CLAIMS → EACH SIDE POSSESSES INFORMATION THE OTHER LACKS → ACTION TESTS BOTH MODELS → EACH MODEL PROVES PARTLY RIGHT/PARTLY WRONG → FORCED SYNTHESIS OR COSTLY SPLIT`

Use when the antagonist is not simply an external entity but a failure of trust, treaty, archive, mandate or old agreement.

Combine M041 + M043 + M044:

`SMITH RECOGNIZES PATTERN → HIS KNOWLEDGE MAKES HIM NECESSARY → SAME KNOWLEDGE MAKES HIM SUSPECT → PERSONAL HISTORY BIASES HIS SOLUTION → NEW SCIENCE/ALLY CHALLENGES HIM → HE MUST CHOOSE BETWEEN OLD DOCTRINE AND NEW EVIDENCE → PRICE`

This prevents Smith from becoming a generic infallible monster hunter.

# SMITH STORY GATE — NEW QUESTIONS

Before prose, answer:

1. What does Old Earth Security know that Confederation genuinely does not?
2. What does Confederation understand better than Old Earth?
3. Which risk classification gives each side jurisdiction?
4. What historical success makes the old method credible?
5. What historical crime/error makes it dangerous?
6. What access/record/person does one side need from the other?
7. Why can neither side solve the threat alone?
8. What personal debt or institutional loyalty biases Smith?
9. What evidence changes the classification of the case?
10. What is permanently different after the immediate case is resolved?

Automatic RED:
- Confederation officials are stupid only to make Smith look competent.
- Old Earth lore contains the complete answer from the beginning.
- Jurisdiction conflict does not change action/resources/access.
- Main case remains unresolved to advertise the next episode/book.
