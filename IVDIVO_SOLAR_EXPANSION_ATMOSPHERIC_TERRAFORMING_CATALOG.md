# 1. CORE CIVILIZATIONAL CAUSALITY

## CANON

Solar-System settlement develops causally from the technologies required to build orbital and lunar cities.

Terraforming is not invented first as a separate “planet-changing machine.” Humanity first learns to create, maintain and recycle complete artificial atmospheres inside orbital habitats and lunar cities. Those systems scale from room -> building -> district -> city -> enclosed landscape -> regional environment -> planetary engineering.

Core progression:

`ORBITAL CLOSED ECOLOGIES -> LUNAR CITIES -> INDUSTRIAL ATMOSPHERE PRODUCTION -> ENCLOSED LUNAR LANDSCAPES -> PARATERRAFORMING -> PLANETARY TERRAFORMING -> SOLAR-SYSTEM SETTLEMENT`

The story principle is: **first humanity builds a home with its own atmosphere; then a city; then a landscape; eventually it realizes it is learning to build worlds.**

---

# 2. OXYGEN PRODUCTION — LUNAR INDUSTRIAL ORIGIN

## CANON PRINCIPLE

The Moon becomes the first large-scale laboratory for industrial atmosphere production.

Lunar regolith contains oxygen chemically bound inside mineral oxides. Lunar construction therefore couples materials production with oxygen extraction: processing regolith for metals, glass, ceramics and structural materials also releases oxygen for life-support and atmosphere systems.

This is not “free oxygen”; it requires energy, processing plants, thermal/electrochemical separation and recycling infrastructure.

A key setting principle is that **the growing lunar city partly produces its own atmosphere from the same material stream used to build itself.**

## WORKING NAME

`ATMOSPHERIC FOUNDRY / АТМОСФЕРНАЯ КУЗНИЦА`

This name is WORKING, not locked terminology.

---

# 3. ATMOSPHERE IS MORE THAN OXYGEN

## CANON

Human habitat atmospheres require a complete controlled gas mixture and ecological cycle, not pure oxygen.

Strategic inputs include:

- oxygen;
- nitrogen / buffer gases;
- water;
- carbon;
- hydrogen;
- trace gases;
- biological nutrients such as phosphorus;
- stable microbiological and ecological cycles.

The scarcity of volatile elements on the Moon drives expansion beyond the Moon.

This gives the asteroid belt, carbonaceous bodies and icy objects direct economic and civilizational importance.

# 4. CAUSAL EXPANSION OF THE SOLAR SYSTEM

## CANON DIRECTION

Human expansion should not be written as random flag-planting. Each settlement wave creates material and strategic demand for the next.

Working causal chain:

`EARTH ORBIT -> MOON -> NEAR-EARTH / VOLATILE-RICH ASTEROIDS -> MARS -> CERES / MAIN BELT -> JOVIAN SYSTEM -> SATURNIAN SYSTEM -> FARTHER SOLAR-SYSTEM NODES`

Exact chronology remains TO DESIGN.

The important production rule is: **every new world should answer a concrete need created by the previous stage.**

# 5. LUNAR PARATERRAFORMING

## CANON DIRECTION

The Moon is the first place where humans scale life-support from sealed buildings to landscape-sized environments.

Progression may include:

- sealed districts;
- linked pressure corridors;
- covered or enclosed craters;
- large artificial atmospheres;
- water cycles;
- soil formation;
- vegetation;
- clouds and local weather inside controlled regions.

The Moon therefore becomes the conceptual bridge between habitat engineering and terraforming.

## KEY WORLD QUESTION

When a controlled environment becomes tens or hundreds of kilometres across, the distinction between “city” and “terraforming” begins to break down.

---

# 6. PARATERRAFORMING BEFORE FULL TERRAFORMING

## CANON DIRECTION

Early planetary settlement does not require instantly transforming an entire planet into Earth.

Humans first create regional habitable environments: enclosed valleys, crater systems, subsurface basins, atmospheric membranes, pressure-controlled territories and artificial hydrological cycles.

Mars can therefore contain large habitable regions with breathable atmospheres while vast areas outside remain recognizably Martian.

This preserves planetary identity and gives the saga visually and socially distinct settlement zones.

# 7. ATMOSPHERIC SEED

## WORKING TECHNOLOGY CONCEPT

`ATMOSPHERIC SEED / АТМОСФЕРНОЕ СЕМЯ` is a working concept for a self-expanding industrial-ecological complex that uses local materials and imported volatiles to create habitable environments.

Possible staged process:

1. **Mineral Phase** — extraction, processing, construction materials, water and elemental separation.
2. **Pressure Phase** — accumulation and management of atmospheric mass.
3. **Thermal Phase** — heat balance and regional climate control.
4. **Hydrological Phase** — stable water circulation, precipitation, lakes/ice and drainage.
5. **Biological Phase** — microbial ecology, soil formation and early photosynthetic systems.
6. **Ecological Phase** — robust multi-species food webs and resilient biospheres.
7. **Open Habitat Phase** — large territories usable without ordinary pressure suits.

Exact engineering, speeds, energy requirements and limits are TO DESIGN and require technical passports before plot-critical use.

# 8. MARS

## CANON DIRECTION

Mars becomes the first major planetary-scale test of the technologies developed through orbital and lunar habitat engineering.

Oxygen can be produced from water and from carbon-dioxide processing, but oxygen alone is not the decisive challenge. Long-term habitability also requires pressure, thermal control, water circulation, buffer gases, nutrients and ecological stability.

Mars therefore creates demand for interplanetary movement of volatile-rich material and accelerates settlement of asteroids and Ceres.

---

# 9. TERRAFORM WORLD OR ADAPT HUMAN?

## CANON LONG-TERM CIVILIZATIONAL QUESTION

IVDIVO should not assume every environment must be converted into Earth.

Two parallel strategies emerge:

1. **CHANGE THE WORLD FOR THE HUMAN** — terraforming / paraterraforming.
2. **CHANGE THE HUMAN CARRIER FOR THE WORLD** — complete artificial/synthetic bodies designed for vacuum, oceans, radiation, extreme atmospheres, temperature regimes and other environments.

This links Solar-System colonization directly to the established long-term IVDIVO line of consciousness/spirit embodiment into specialized bodies.

Some worlds may be partially terraformed; others may remain physically alien and be inhabited through specialized bodies.

---

# 10. IRELAND AS EARTHSIDE PLANETARY-ENGINEERING TESTBED

## CANON CONNECTION

Because Ireland is the sole planetary society with deep access to closed orbital technology, its terrestrial habitat systems become important precursors and testbeds for off-world engineering.

Direct technology families may connect:

- Irish underwater cities -> settlements beneath alien oceans or ice;
- Irish subterranean cities -> lunar and Martian underground habitats;
- Irish atmospheric/flying cities -> high-atmosphere settlement technologies;
- Irish floating Atlantic cities -> modular mobile habitat engineering;
- Irish artificial-atmosphere systems -> lunar, Martian and later planetary environment engineering.

Ireland is therefore a place where orbital technology returns to Earth, is naturalized into planetary civic life, and later feeds back outward into Solar-System settlement.

# 11. CONTINUITY LOCKS

1. Terraforming grows from orbital/lunar habitat engineering rather than appearing as an unrelated invention.
2. Lunar construction and oxygen production are industrially coupled through regolith processing.
3. Atmosphere design includes nitrogen/buffer gases, water, carbon and ecological cycles; oxygen alone is insufficient.
4. Demand for volatiles helps drive asteroid and outer-system expansion.
5. The Moon is an early large-scale paraterraforming laboratory.
6. Mars is settled progressively; regional paraterraforming can precede global terraforming.
7. Exact Atmospheric Foundry and Atmospheric Seed implementations remain TO DESIGN until technical passports are written.
8. Specialized synthetic bodies remain a parallel alternative to transforming every world into Earth.
9. Ireland is an Earthside testbed linking orbital habitat technology to later planetary-engineering systems.
