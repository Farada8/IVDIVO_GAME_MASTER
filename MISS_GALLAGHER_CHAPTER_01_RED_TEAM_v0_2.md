# VERDICT

**PASS WITH REWRITE**

The v0.2 draft solves the largest structural failure of the earlier opening: ontology is now mostly encountered through Smith’s work rather than delivered as a universal lecture. Smith has a clearer immediate clock, diagnostics have causal progression, and Enya has at least a provisional independent objective.

However, the draft introduces a new mistake: it weakens the strongest uncanny image from the original material by moving Enya’s recognition of Smith from the **shared wall with no direct line of sight** to a corridor where she can physically look at him.

That should be corrected.

---

# RT-01 — STRONGEST ORIGINAL IMAGE WEAKENED

**Severity:** 9/10  
**Symptom:** v0.2 ends the counter-observation at the conference corridor. Enya looks directly across people at Smith.  
**Why it hurts:** the original shared-wall smile proves something impossible without explaining it. Direct sight introduces ordinary explanations and reduces dread.  
**Repair:** restore the wall event as the Chapter 1 climax. Let Enya stop on the other side, turn toward Smith’s exact position and smile through the wall. Move the actual closed-conference access operation to Chapter 2.

---

# RT-02 — TWO CHAPTER CLIMAXES COMPETE

**Severity:** 7/10  
**Symptom:** room investigation already produces a major anomaly, then the draft continues through conference logistics to a second recognition beat.  
**Why it hurts:** the chapter spends its strongest reveal and then asks the reader to keep processing procedure before giving a weaker version of the same reveal.  
**Repair:** Chapter 1 ends at recognition + department pressure. Chapter 2 begins with pursuit into the conference.

---

# RT-03 — INSTITUTIONAL EXPLANATION STILL SOMETIMES PRE-EMPTS EVIDENCE

**Severity:** 6/10  
**Symptom:** paragraphs explaining why departments remove failed hands partially explain a system before present-day procedure demonstrates it.  
**Repair:** keep only what Smith personally knows is necessary for the clock. Let later messages, review protocol, missing colleagues or actual containment procedures prove the rest.

---

# RT-04 — PAST DARK-MAGICIAN MEMORY IS FUNCTIONAL BUT TOO LONG FOR THIS POSITION

**Severity:** 5/10  
**Function worth preserving:** proves Smith has met powerful resistance and remained unbribable/cold, so Enya’s silence is genuinely new.  
**Problem:** full anecdote temporarily releases present pressure.  
**Repair:** compress to 1–2 paragraphs now; expand later only if the old case becomes causally relevant.

---

# RT-05 — ENYA’S AGENCY IS PRESENT BUT STILL MOSTLY SEEN AS A CLUE

**Severity:** 7/10  
**Symptom:** her delivery objective mainly gives Smith a route.  
**Repair:** in Chapter 2, allow her objective to force a decision that conflicts with what Smith wants. She must be able to sacrifice/lose something that matters to her, not merely carry a plot document.

---

# RT-06 — SMITH’S FIRST DEVIATION MAY BE SLIGHTLY TOO EXPLICIT

**Severity:** 4/10  
**Symptom:** `SUBJECT LOCATED. CLASSIFICATION PENDING.` is a visible professional deviation very early.  
**Assessment:** potentially good, because it is framed as accuracy rather than rebellion.  
**Repair:** retain if the department response creates consequence. Do not interpret it in prose as “his first act of freedom.” Let the reader infer the shift much later.

---

# RT-07 — BODY/CARRIER GEOMETRY NEEDS ONE CLEAN RULE

**Severity:** 6/10  
**Symptom:** reader can ask where Smith’s own body is when he enters a carrier and how physical distance works.  
**Repair:** do not explain cosmology, but establish one observable rule when story requires it: remote entry, body left somewhere, concurrent awareness, or another canon mechanism. Until canon is extracted, avoid prose that accidentally commits to incompatible mechanics.

**Status:** CANON CHECK REQUIRED.

# RT-08 — SOME SENTENCES EXPLAIN THE EFFECT AFTER ALREADY SHOWING IT

**Severity:** 4/10  
Examples of failure type, not line-edit prescription:

**Repair:** trust selected concrete beats. Remove second explanations during prose edit.
