## 0065 Борислава — синтезфизическое тело / Движение
Question: how do perception, intention, posture, load and feedback become one physical action?
Scene: industrial rehabilitation test hall. Camera at floor level behind a moving weighted sled. Борислава, 54, compact and powerfully built, is caught mid-step on an uneven pressure track while carrying an asymmetrical load with a second worker. One sensor strip shows that her apparently stronger first strategy twists the load. She stops, changes foot angle and breathing, and both move the same weight with less force and no torsion. The result is a real transported object, not a glowing anatomy diagram.
Cultural construction [Р]: Bosnian/Balkan workwear derived from layered wool vest, wrap-belt and gusseted trousers; deep red/charcoal/ivory. Conductive embroidery follows actual load seams and lights only when forces become coherent.
Instrument: pressure floor, inertial load frame, mechanical joint-angle flags, weighted sled. Sacred signature: one clean moving axis appears through dust, fabric tension and floor markers.

## 0066 Аглая — эфирное тело / Ощущение
Question: how does a weak external change become a reliable bodily signal without overload?
Scene: coastal sensory-thermal laboratory in Senegal. Аглая, 31, very tall and thin, sits sideways inside a wind-and-humidity tunnel with one sleeve removed. A technician changes only one variable at a time: air speed, moisture, temperature. She places no hand on a hologram; instead she slides physical skin-contact membranes along her forearm and identifies the threshold where sensation becomes stable enough to guide action. Another participant independently repeats the calibration.
Cultural construction [Р]: Senegalese wrap and boubou geometry compressed into a short ventilated laboratory layer over technical trousers; woven edge becomes a real humidity sensor and glows only where condensation closes the circuit.
Instrument: capillary skin membranes, airflow vanes, thermal tiles, pulse bell. Sacred signature: a barely visible pressure wave revealed by fine suspended fibres.

## 0067 Соня — астральное тело / Чувство
Question: how does relationship alter behaviour and atmosphere without being reduced to an aura?
Scene: Philippine disaster-recovery dormitory mock-up used for social-environment testing. Wide view through two adjacent rooms. Соня, 63, short and heavy-set, is not central; she rearranges one movable wall panel while two families negotiate distance, privacy and shared care. Breathing, voice level, movement paths and fabric tension visibly change. The test succeeds when people voluntarily remain in the shared space without crowding or withdrawal.
Cultural construction [Р]: Filipino baro/tapis structural logic translated into breathable modular care clothing; piña-like translucent reinforcement. Embroidery responds to actual acoustic vibration along the collar and remains dark elsewhere.
Instrument: movable acoustic wall, tension curtains, floor path markers, resonant voice strips. Sacred signature: the room’s hanging membranes settle into a stable shared rhythm; no floating emotional colours.

## 0068 Гелена — ментальное тело / Мысль
Question: how does thought become a manipulable model that can be tested and corrected?
Scene: Korean underground transit design testbed. Top-down camera over a room-size physical network made from rails, blocks and timed gates. Гелена, 39, narrow athletic body, lies prone on a raised service bridge to reach one hidden junction. Her first model routes people efficiently but traps maintenance access. A technician below physically removes one block; Гелена rebuilds the route around the objection and then sends real test carts through it.
Cultural construction [Р]: hanbok-derived volume is reduced into a sharply articulated engineering jacket and wide work trousers; goreum logic becomes functional fastening. Fine geometric embroidery lights only along the route currently being physically tested.
Instrument: physical modular transit model, timed gates, weighted carts, removable constraints. Sacred signature: the model’s correct path becomes readable through moving objects, not formulas in air.

## 0069 Полли — причинное тело / Смысл
Question: can a locally successful action remain meaningful after its long consequences are included?
Scene: Scottish island coastal engineering test site. Полли, 47, broad-shouldered and freckled, stands knee-deep in a wave basin, not posing, pulling one removable model sea-wall segment out of a long physical coastline. The segment protected houses in the first test but destroyed the fishing inlet in the delayed second wave cycle. Residents and engineers watch the basin; she replaces it with a porous staggered structure and runs the full cycle again.
Cultural construction [Р]: Hebridean wool, fisher smock and tartan weaving logic translated into waterproof technical layers; checked conductive yarn lights only on the sections carrying the verified long-cycle load.
Instrument: large wave basin, sediment bed, delayed-flow dye, removable coast modules. Sacred signature: a long return-wave line that finally reaches shore without destructive concentration.

## 0070 Зои — буддическое тело / Суть
Question: what quality remains essential when decoration, status and accidental features are removed?
Scene: Greek marble-and-ceramic conservation laboratory, but no classical goddess staging. Зои, 76, small, stooped, strong hands, sits at a low rotating work table. Five repaired structural tiles look different. She sands away one beautiful cosmetic layer from the most admired sample, revealing that only three repairs preserve the same load-bearing joint principle. Younger restorers then reproduce the joint in different materials without copying appearance.
Cultural construction [Р]: island Greek work dress and apron logic in matte linen/wool; blue-black woven key patterns exist only at structural seams and glow faintly when pressure transfers correctly.
Instrument: rotating compression table, sectioned repair samples, mineral abrasives, mechanical load needle. Sacred signature: three different objects cast the same structural shadow under one narrow light.

## 0071 Миранда — атмическое тело / Идея
Question: how does an idea become a sustained direction rather than a decorative concept?
Scene: Peruvian high-altitude prosthetics and mobility field station. Миранда, 28, 153 cm, powerful lower body, is walking uphill beside a user testing a new leg system. The first elegant design fails on loose scree. She does not redesign at a desk; crouched on the slope, she changes the attachment geometry, then steps back while the user chooses a different route and completes it independently.
Cultural construction [Р]: Andean layered wrap, lliclla/load-carrying logic and dense woven bands translated into compact mountain technical clothing. The woven direction band lights only while the user’s chosen line remains mechanically viable.
Instrument: adjustable prosthetic joint fixture, slope gauge, interchangeable sole plates, physical route stakes. Sacred signature: a single ascending line remains after many possible tracks disappear in dust.

## 0072 Констанца — аматическое тело / Право
Question: where is the legitimate boundary of action, access and intervention?
Scene: Argentine tissue-engineering and rehabilitation clinic. Констанца, 58, tall, soft-bodied, grey curls, sits between two work zones rather than at their head. A regenerative scaffold can technically be extended into healthy tissue, but the patient has authorised only a narrower intervention. Констанца physically clips a bright section off the surgical template before activation. The team adapts the procedure inside that boundary, and the patient later tests movement with the preserved unaffected region intact.
Cultural construction [Р]: Patagonian/Argentine riding and wrap logic translated into clinical workwear with broad belt, short coat and articulated trousers. Silver-blue woven border marks authorised limits and lights only along the consented edge.
Instrument: physical surgical template, boundary clips, tension scaffold and mechanical range-of-motion frame. Sacred signature: an open boundary line around the work area; power stops exactly where right ends.
