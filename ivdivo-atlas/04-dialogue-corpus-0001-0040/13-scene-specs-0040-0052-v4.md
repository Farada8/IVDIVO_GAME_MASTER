## 0040 Любляна — совершенство любви
Source anchors: Гражданская Конфедерация; ИВО-реализация; Стандарт. Preserve the developed distinction: love is dignity + care + freedom + boundary + responsibility, not sentimentality.

**New scene:** five flood-displaced communities have built five visibly different temporary dwellings around a shared alpine weaving hall. Любляна sits on the floor among equals, side profile, not on a throne. She cuts one connecting warp rather than tying everything together: a family has refused one form of assistance and the shared system must preserve that refusal without withdrawing other rights. Other people continue to weave separate coloured bands into one load-bearing emergency canopy.

**Artistic identity:** Slovenian/Alpine-Balkan construction; 29–31, 180 cm, dense strong build, short dark hair, olive skin. Short fitted wool-linen upper layer, broad working skirt/trouser hybrid, apron-like load panel, mountain footwear. The woven border glows only along the band whose consent has just been verified.

**Instrument:** a full-size tension loom of mutual recognition; each band can carry load independently. No heart symbol, orb or raised-hand blessing. Sacred signature: five non-concentric luminous tension arcs that remain separate while supporting one roof.

Crews at different levels respond independently; hierarchy is shown as ordered responsibility, not rank or worship.

**Artistic identity:** Ethiopian highland woman, 61–65, 174 cm, very lean, long limbs, close silver curls, dark skin. Layered white cotton construction derived from shamma with narrow woven tibeb bands integrated into a climbing/maintenance harness. Only the load-bearing bands illuminate as the pressure equalises.

**Instrument:** a weighted vertical balance-tree with mechanical shutters and visible water columns. Sacred signature: nested load paths rising behind her like an open architectural halo, never a crown.

**Instrument:** paired lacquered resonance bridges, bone-conduction handle and paper-smoke trace strip. Sacred signature: echo fronts reflected in dust and wood fibres, not a literal eye or x-ray hologram.

## 0043 Павла — провидение
Source anchors: Воспитание; высшая космическая реализация; Константа.

**New scene:** an Armenian mountain orchard-school during a climate shift. Павла is seen from high above as a small moving figure crossing terraces while children and elders plant different tree generations. She is not predicting images of the future. She removes one mature but water-hungry tree from the teaching sequence and reallocates its irrigation to a mixed orchard whose benefits will arrive after the present generation. Older villagers challenge the choice; the children recalculate seasonal water stones and continue without her.

**Artistic identity:** Armenian woman, 72, 163 cm, broad-backed, strong forearms, silver hair in a tight low knot, deep-lined face. Wool coat construction derived from highland chokha/arkhalig logic without military styling, layered apron, leather mountain boots; narrow woven pomegranate-vine geometry glows only on the sleeve linked to the opened water gate.

**Instrument:** a physical generational water-calendar: concentric stone channels, removable seed weights and copper seasonal gates. Sacred signature: repeated seasonal arcs projected by real sunlight through the irrigation device.

## 0044 Млада — прозрение
Source anchors: Искусство; высшая иерархическая реализация; Красота.

**New scene:** inside an Adriatic stone theatre under restoration, Млада works high on scaffolding, viewed from below through hanging canvas. A century-old decorative repaint is visually attractive but destroys the load-bearing rhythm of the original mural architecture. Mid-action she stops, turns the grazing-light comb sideways and removes one narrow strip. The hidden proportional line suddenly links three distant fragments; other restorers change their entire repair plan.

**Artistic identity:** Croatian/Dalmatian woman, 33–35, 176 cm, long narrow torso, muscular shoulders, cropped chestnut curls, freckled skin. Split work tunic and wrap skirt/trousers based on coastal linen construction, red-black woven edges used as tool loops; only one embroidery line flashes where it coincides with the rediscovered proportional axis.

**Instrument:** flexible grazing-light comb + micro-scraper with interchangeable mineral teeth. Sacred signature: the mural itself becomes her halo when the original spatial rhythm reconnects; no floating mandala.

## 0045 Олёна — око

**Artistic identity:** Mongolian woman, 44, 168 cm, powerful hips and thighs from riding, broad cheekbones, dark sun-browned skin, heavy black braid. **Instrument:** three-axis foldable bronze-and-horn sighting frame mounted to the saddle, with no lenses and no giant eye symbolism. Sacred signature: converging real sightlines across landscape dust.

## 0046 Кирилла — истина
Source anchors: Мировоззрение; высшая синтезная реализация; Истинность.

**New scene:** open-sided coastal evidence workshop in Ghana after a collapsed sea wall. Кирилла sits low at a long bench among fishers, masons and officials. Two explanations are politically attractive; neither survives the material samples. She does not announce truth. She places three salt-soaked stone cores into a hand press; one cherished explanation fails when the fracture pattern repeats. A local mason, not Кирилла, points to the real construction defect and the group redraws responsibility.

**Artistic identity:** Akan/Ghanaian woman, 56–60, 160 cm, heavy-set, strong neck and hands, very dark skin, shaved head with a thin silver line. Garment built from narrow woven-strip logic: wrapped lower body, short structured upper work jacket, wide load belt, flat sandals/boots. Gold-coloured woven lines are not decorative light: only the strip touching the verified sample becomes luminous.

**Instrument:** manual split-witness press, saline bath, fracture plates and weighted recording paper. Sacred signature: a single clean fracture line repeated across stone, paper and garment seam.

## 0049 Мирулина — сверхмировое тело
Source anchors: Мир; совершенная человеческая реализация; Могущество.

**New scene:** Icelandic geothermal world-complex containing several independent climate habitats. Мирулина is filmed in a narrow maintenance tunnel, close side profile, braced with both feet while turning a huge basalt-and-steel manifold. One habitat is overheating while another is freezing. She cannot simply increase total power; she must preserve each world’s boundary and redistribute heat without making them one environment. Through inspection windows, distant ecosystems remain visibly different.

**Artistic identity:** Icelandic woman, 59–62, 188 cm, broad and heavy, pale weather-reddened skin, cropped white hair, large hands. Dense wool/fishskin-inspired weather construction, layered short coat over insulated trousers, broad shoulder cape used as real heat shield. Reflective woven borders brighten from thermal radiation only on the active side.

**Instrument:** geothermal manifold with stone valve drums, steam sight-glasses and pressure counterweights. Sacred signature: aurora-like emission appears only in condensing steam where two balanced flows meet.

## 0050 Огни — поядающий огонь
Source anchors: План Синтеза; совершенная ядерная реализация; Практика.

**New scene:** Moroccan high-temperature recycling atelier built into an old clay industrial quarter. Огни leans deeply over a refractory trench, face protected, using both hands on long tongs to lower a contaminated composite artwork into a selective furnace. The fire is not destruction for spectacle: resin, poison and failed adhesive are consumed while recoverable glass, copper and ceramic remain. Apprentices sort the surviving materials for new work.

**Artistic identity:** Moroccan woman, 68–72, 166 cm, thin sinewy body, sun-darkened skin, narrow face, cropped grey curls. Djellaba/burnous construction translated into layered heat-resistant hood, split side panels, leather apron and wrapped forearms; geometric embroidery becomes a temperature-responsive ceramic thread and glows only near the furnace-facing edge.

**Instrument:** porous refractory crucible, long counterweighted tongs, ash separator. Sacred signature: a dark-edged white flame boundary that stops exactly at the recoverable material.

**Artistic identity:** Swiss woman, 42–45, 173 cm, square athletic build, ash-brown pixie cut, pale skin, grey eyes. Alpine work-jacket construction with short tailored wool layers, suspenders, reinforced knees and climbing shoes; narrow regional woven geometry becomes conductive seam markers that light only on the subsystem currently carrying responsibility.

**Instrument:** modular mechanical interlock board made of real levers, brake keys and cable-tension samples mounted inside the tower—not a holographic command table. Sacred signature: three independent lines remain separate until the rescue sequence mechanically permits their intersection.
