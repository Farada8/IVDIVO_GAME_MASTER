## Operating rule

The 100-person layer does **not** mean all departments run equally on every task. The Showrunner selects only the specialists required by the current production stage. Internal disagreement is resolved in this priority order: story causality → character truth → emotional impact → reader engagement → continuity → world logic → genre → market → franchise. FATAL Red Team findings stop the pipeline; MAJOR findings must be repaired before prose.
