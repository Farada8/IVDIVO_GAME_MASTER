# 1. REPAIRED STORY CORE

> On the first orbital residency of the Experimental Human Youth Cohort, Ollie finally gets the real maintenance work he has wanted and a chance to prove he is useful outside workshops and youth exercises; but his own timing analysis helps remove a safety objection to opening a new residential ring before a mass relocation deadline. When the partial opening produces a coupled environmental failure, Ollie must rebuild the model from the lived behavior of residents, bodies and infrastructure he previously treated as secondary data, then choose under migration pressure to physically isolate a section before standard sensors demand it — sacrificing the clean launch and his own recognition in order to open only what is actually safe.

This is the current A-story.

# 2. HERO — OLLIE v0.2

## External want
Earn a real orbital maintenance / habitat-systems placement and help commission a place people will live in.

## Private want
Become the person people call when something real breaks.

He is tired of being the useful one whose competence remains in workshops, models and side tasks while Ethan naturally occupies the visible leadership position.

He dislikes this envy and would never state it cleanly.

## Why now
The first Youth Cohort orbital residency places him inside a real commissioning team during a housing-capacity deadline.

## Fear
That adults like his work only when consequences are simulated.

## Wrong strategy
Make himself indispensable by producing the cleanest model in the room.

## Midpoint responsibility
Two live commissioning models remain plausible:
- conservative model: unexplained settling delays may become dangerous at realistic mixed occupancy;
- permissive model: delays are transient interface/calibration effects inside safe margins.

Ollie’s timing analysis appears to falsify the conservative trigger sequence.

Adults do not open the ring “because a kid said so.”
But his result materially removes one reason to delay.

After the failure he must own:

> I did not cause the defect, but my interpretation changed what other people reasonably believed they could do.

## Need
A working habitat includes people not as loads or requirements but as causal participants whose behavior and embodied feedback can change the system.

## Climax action
After retraining / review, Ollie receives a narrow local emergency permission:
`SAFE-STATE SEAL` — isolate one residential segment and stop inbound transit to it when defined local conditions are met.

During migration, residents/Iri report the early embodied signature while fixed sensors still predict recovery.

Ollie triggers SAFE-STATE SEAL **before** standard threshold breach.

Immediate cost:
- residents already moving into that segment are routed back out;
- migration backs up;
- one family loses the room it thought it had that night;
- Ollie appears to have overreacted for several minutes.

The later data show the isolation prevented propagation of the coupled oscillation.

## Final choice
He refuses any report or publicity language implying full-ring commissioning succeeded.

## Arc
`invisible maker -> hungry apprentice -> useful analyst -> overconfident causal participant -> discredited -> listener/rebuilder -> trusted under uncertainty`.

# 3. WORKING ORBITAL CITY IDENTITY

Everything in this section is `WORKING / OPTION`, not political canon.

## Working city name
`VELA`.

Do not lock until naming pass.

## Founding function
Vela began as a logistics / construction / transfer complex and accreted residential rings over generations of orbital expansion.

Its culture therefore respects maintenance and operational competence more than polished prestige.

## Architectural signature
A non-rotating central transit/service spine connects multiple rotating residential/work rings with different gravity and environmental profiles.

The city feels braided rather than vertically stacked.

## Local youth joke about planet-born visitors
Planet-born teenagers are expected to:
- stand in the wrong place during spin transition;
- assume every route is shortest in ordinary Euclidean “map” terms;
- schedule social life without accounting for transfer queues.

Do not turn this into one repeated slur/catchphrase.

## Local social complaint
Even in an extraordinary civilization, everyone complains about transfer queues, maintenance closures, bad host assignments and the fact that the best communal view spaces are always booked.

## Political tension
Growth / housing capacity vs maintaining orbital autonomy and safety standards under increasing Earth demand.

No universal Orbital Government implied.

# 5. MINIMUM ECONOMIC LOOP — CITY-SPECIFIC WORKING MODEL

Do not design all orbital economics.

For Book Two only:

## Baseline access
Residency guarantees basic life-support-compatible housing, essential food range, medical safety and ordinary intra-city transit.

## Scarcity still exists
Scarce goods include:
- compatible housing in specific rings;
- private space;
- high-demand travel windows;
- workshop time;
- entertainment/sport bookings;
- premium imported planetary goods;
- discretionary fabrication resources.

## Youth work
Ollie’s apprenticeship provides a modest discretionary civic/work allowance plus workshop access privileges.

This matters because he can choose between:
- buying/importing something for himself;
- using scarce fabrication time;
- contributing resources to a maker project;
- social spending with friends.

Exact currency/credit terminology remains TO DESIGN.

## Housing allocation
Housing cannot simply be bought by the richest applicant because compatibility and life-support capacity matter.

Working factors:
- household need;
- environmental/body compatibility;
- wait time;
- work/school location;
- accessibility;
- available module type.

No single social-credit score.

# 8. AOIFE ERROR v0.2

Aoife does **not** simply violate a clear promise.

Sequence:
1. she records Iri / local peer in an agreed context;
2. source approves the excerpt;
3. Aoife removes obvious identifying/private material;
4. she misses an inference channel: background service sound, maintenance marker, body-interface detail, route timing or environmental signature;
5. specialist viewers infer body/status information;
6. Earth media clip the excerpt and attach a political narrative;
7. source feels exposed even though literal approval was respected.

Aoife’s responsibility:
She understands media/inference better than her source and should have anticipated recontextualization.

Her lesson:
`permission to publish content is not automatic permission for every identity inference that content enables`.

Repair action:
She voluntarily withdraws/withholds additional high-value material and gives source meaningful veto over the final identity-sensitive section.

Cost:
Her documentary becomes less marketable / less exclusive.

# 12. MIDPOINT CAUSAL PATCH

Before partial opening:
- Mara/Safety team still disagree over whether observed settling delays scale dangerously.
- Ollie’s model predicts the trigger sequence requires a partition transition not present in the planned opening profile.
- Samir is uncertain; resident reports remain non-certified.
- Ollie states his conclusion with appropriate caveat but clearly enough that it matters.
- Commissioning board allows the partial opening under restrictions.

During failure:
- real resident movement creates an unmodelled transition through ordinary behavior;
- oscillation begins;
- resident/body signal rises first;
- fixed sensors follow late.

Ollie’s mistake is causal and defensible.

---

# 13. CLIMAX PATCH

During migration:
- the corrected model predicts oscillation can propagate if one segment remains fed by incoming residents for several more minutes;
- fixed sensors have not yet crossed automatic isolation threshold;
- Iri/resident embodied warnings match the early signature;
- Mara is managing wider emergency and cannot micromanage every local boundary;
- Ollie has SAFE-STATE SEAL authority for this exact local condition after retraining.

He triggers it.

Immediate consequences:
- transit gate closes;
- people already promised rooms are turned back;
- one waiting household blames him personally;
- throughput target fails;
- social media reports “Youth trainee shuts housing segment” before technical review.

Then the delayed fixed sensors confirm the trend that would have triggered later.

The action works because the discovered mechanism was real, not because Ollie “believed in himself.”
