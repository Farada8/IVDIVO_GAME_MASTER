# CHAPTER 29 — SEGMENTS

## SCENE 89 — WHAT CAN OPEN
**POV:** Ollie
**Want:** turn “segments, not people” into an actual plan.
**Obstacle:** each segment has different route geometry, density, environmental profile and resident demand.
**Action:** engineers/safety/resident reps identify zones that can be certified under specific occupancy patterns.
**Ollie role:** narrow model support, not board authority.
**World reveal:** certification changes how people can actually live—delivery paths, school routes, guests, shift traffic.
**Turn:** only part of Ring Six can open before relocation.
**Price:** the third option saves some capacity by making incompleteness visible.
**Exit:** resident list must be rebuilt around real segment compatibility.

## SCENE 90 — NOT FIRST COME, NOT BEST PERSON
**POV:** Ollie
**Want:** believe allocation can now be “fair.”
**Obstacle:** compatible space is still scarce.
**Action:** housing staff balance household need, environmental fit, work/school location, wait time and accessibility.
**Conflict:** residents dispute priorities; no single formula ends argument.
**Ollie pressure:** tempted to optimize a score.
**Turn:** he does not propose one.
**Character change:** accepts that transparent criteria can still produce painful choices.
**Exit:** Lea’s household is split by the segment plan.

## SCENE 91 — LEA DOESN'T WIN
**POV:** Ollie
**Want:** see at least one familiar waiting family benefit.
**Obstacle:** Lea’s household cannot all move in the first safe phase.
**Lea:** furious at the outcome yet still supports partial opening because others can move.
**Turn:** Ollie cannot reward the resident who helped humanize the problem.
**Price:** the solution refuses protagonist-centered justice.
**Exit:** Aoife decides to spend a day with waiting residents without recording.

# CHAPTER 35 — SAFE-STATE

## SCENE 107 — SEAL
**POV:** Ollie
**Immediate want:** protect the segment without destroying migration flow.
**Choice:** triggers SAFE-STATE before fixed automatic threshold.
**Physical action:** inbound transit gate closes; local systems isolate; flow reroutes.
**Immediate cost:** families already moving are turned back; one household loses the room expected that night; queue grows; service schedules break; public feeds report “Youth trainee shuts housing segment.”
**Important:** adults do not praise him in the moment.
**Turn:** for several minutes, fixed sensors still look recoverable.
**Emotional pressure:** Ollie has to live inside the possibility that he overreacted.
**Exit:** delayed readings begin to move.

## SCENE 108 — LATE CONFIRMATION
**POV:** Ollie
**Immediate want:** know whether the seal was justified.
**Event:** fixed sensors cross the trend the corrected model predicted.
**Result:** isolated segment shows contained oscillation tendency; neighboring segments remain stable.
**Payoff:** early seal prevented propagation.
**Mara:** acknowledges operational effect, then immediately asks for full local log/reconstruction.
**No victory speech.**
**Turn:** Ollie’s action is validated technically but remains costly socially.
**Exit:** the family turned back wants to know who closed their route.

## SCENE 109 — YOUR ROOM IS STILL THERE
**POV:** Ollie
**Immediate want:** explain the decision to affected residents without hiding behind technical language.
**Obstacle:** family does not care that his model was right; they care they have nowhere stable tonight.
**Action:** explains only what he knows, what he chose, and what remains uncertain.
**Resistance:** resident is angry anyway.
**Turn:** Ollie does not demand understanding.
**Character payoff:** accepts that a correct safety decision can still injure people socially/materially.
**Exit:** formal commissioning review begins.

## SCENE 110 — NOT A SUCCESSFUL OPENING
**POV:** Ollie
**Immediate want:** finish review without losing the legitimate good that did happen.
**Obstacle:** institution can truthfully say migration broadly succeeded and wants clean language.
**Action:** refuses wording that implies **full-ring commissioning succeeded**.
**He supports:** safe occupied segments + continued closure/redesign of unsafe sections.
**Price:** no clean launch metric, no simple young-engineer headline, no “saved the station” identity.
**Turn:** Mara accepts factual distinction because it is correct, not because Ollie earned moral approval.
**Climax completion:** physical choice + truthful naming of incomplete success.
**Exit:** life begins inside parts that are actually open.
