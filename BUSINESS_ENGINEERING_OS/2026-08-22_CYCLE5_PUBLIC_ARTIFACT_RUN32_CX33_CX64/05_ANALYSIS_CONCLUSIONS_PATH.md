# CYCLE5 ANALYSIS / CONCLUSIONS / PATH TO GOAL

## 1. PRIMARY mutation: discovery -> qualification/evidence readiness
Public-artifact testing shows that tender discovery/alerts are already handled by official eTenders and multiple Irish procurement tools. The differentiated hypothesis is narrower: **Tender Qualification & Evidence Readiness**.

Output candidate: live tender + supplier capability/evidence passport -> qualification unknowns, mandatory evidence checklist, missing/expired evidence, MEAT/criteria map, deadlines/clarifications, bid/no-bid rationale and next safe action. It may automate extraction but may not invent supplier capability or predict awards.

## 2. Retrofit survives as orchestration
SEAI schemes show real workflow/funding activity but also first-class dependencies on registered companies/installers, chartered engineering, site facts and declarations. AI scope: intake, programme routing, evidence completeness, partner orchestration, checklist/status tracking. Human scope: design, engineering, site, installer, safety/regulatory judgments.

**PILOT:** Retrofit Orchestration & Evidence Administration.

## 3. Generic AI diagnostic is bundled
LEO Digital for Business already provides free digital-system analysis, optimization and implementation guidance; Grow Digital follows completed Digital for Business work. Generic paid AI workflow audit is therefore weak.

**PILOT mutation:** Post-Digital-for-Business AI Implementation + Measurement + Governance Evidence. Begin after diagnosis: implement one bounded workflow, instrument baseline/after measures, document human controls and governance evidence. ROI remains null until measured.
