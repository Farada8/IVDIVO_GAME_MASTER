# ENGINEERING CONTRACTS + PROOFS v1

## EC-01 Evidence firewall
No market claim may move from E2 public proof to E3 without direct buyer/supplier evidence.
TEST: ledger row has evidence_source + date + proof_level.

## EC-02 Reconstruction truth
Every historical element = FACT / ANALOGY / ARTISTIC INTERPRETATION.
TEST: concept board has legend before external issue.

## EC-03 Structural handoff
Concept art cannot be issued as construction design.
TEST: any buildable object above agreed low-risk threshold has named structural engineer gate.

## EC-04 Kinetic safety
No exposed hazardous drive elements; overspeed/fail-safe/lockout/service access mandatory.
TEST: hazard review before prototype.

## EC-05 Electrical modularity
Solar/charging is an isolated replaceable module, not embedded irreversibly into artwork.
TEST: disconnect + service replacement path documented.

## EC-06 Material weatherability
Outdoor ceramic/glass/mosaic/metal systems require supplier datasheet or project-specific technical acceptance.
TEST: no “outdoor suitable” label based only on appearance.

## EC-07 Maintainability
Every non-passive component has owner, access path, replaceable part and inspection interval.
TEST: maintenance card exists before tender/fabrication.

## EC-08 Procurement-fit boundary
Funding eligibility != buyer demand != award probability.
TEST: three fields tracked separately.

## EC-09 Quote integrity
Public web price != fabrication quote.
TEST: cost model only promotes line item after supplier quote or documented catalogue price with scope parity.

## EC-10 Anti-middleman value
Studio must own at least two high-value functions: source/story synthesis, artistic concept, object design, material/scenographic language, specialist fabrication coordination, QA/art direction.
TEST: proposal states owned deliverables.
