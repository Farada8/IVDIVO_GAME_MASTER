## Strategic conclusion
The proven spending category is not "tourist signs". It is a wider market: visitor experience, interpretation, wayfinding, public art, signature landmarks, exhibition/heritage fit-out and specialist fabrication. The studio should enter first as a specialist artistic concept + object-design partner, not as a full prime contractor for €100k-€300k visitor projects.
