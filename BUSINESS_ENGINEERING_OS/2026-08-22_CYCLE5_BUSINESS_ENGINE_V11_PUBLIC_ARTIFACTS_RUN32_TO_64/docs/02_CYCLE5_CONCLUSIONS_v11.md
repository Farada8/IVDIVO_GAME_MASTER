# BUSINESS ENGINE v1.1 — CYCLE5 CONCLUSIONS

## Proven this cycle
- Source lineage is a first-class business object; a reissued tender cannot silently inherit stale volatile fields.
- Public artifacts can be generated while preserving E2+ evidence ceiling.
- Hard exclusions and explicit unknowns are safer than aggregate opportunity scores.
- Business artifacts now participate in selective invalidation and lock-aware recomputation.
- Public support/grant pathways are separated from buyer demand/payment evidence.
- Progress is measured by decision delta or unique information, not document count.
- Self-Improvement now has a pruning path: duplicate mechanisms merge; high false-positive mechanisms narrow; unused mechanisms wait for telemetry.

## Not yet proven
- No paired manual-vs-engine timing has been observed.
- No observed error-reduction benchmark has been run on a human baseline.
- No E3 buyer interaction or E4 payment exists under NO_OUTREACH.
- No actual contribution margin / CAC / conversion / sales-cycle proof exists.
- Full tender-document ingestion for Skerries/Ballybunion remains a next step.

## Development direction
The next bottleneck is no longer opportunity discovery. It is `SOURCE DOCUMENTS -> ARTIFACT -> MEASURED OPERATOR EFFORT -> DECISION DELTA -> REAL BUYER/PAID EVIDENCE when authorized`.
