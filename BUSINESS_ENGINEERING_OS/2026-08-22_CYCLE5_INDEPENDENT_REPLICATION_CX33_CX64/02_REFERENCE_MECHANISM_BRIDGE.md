# REFERENCE MECHANISM BRIDGE — CYCLE5 INDEPENDENT REPLICATION

**Authority:** REFERENCE ONLY. These source mechanisms shape experiment design; they do not prove current demand, buyer willingness-to-pay, or business superiority.

## Testing Business Ideas
Applied mechanism: break candidate into explicit desirability/feasibility/viability hypotheses; choose an experiment that generates evidence and can change the decision. Cycle5 translation: each public-artifact test requires a falsifiable claim, evidence target, sample/stop rule and KEEP/MUTATE/KILL/HOLD disposition.

## The Lean Startup
Applied mechanism: validated learning / experiment / measure / pivot-or-persevere. Cycle5 translation: generic tender discovery and generic AI diagnostic were not defended because they already existed in the plan; public substitute evidence caused a MUTATE decision.

## Getting to Plan B
Applied mechanism: analogs, antilogs and leaps of faith plus a dashboard; business-model dimensions must cohere. Cycle5 translation: every active candidate keeps its fatal unknowns explicit and economics null rather than inventing revenue/gross-margin/cash assumptions.

## The Invincible Company
Applied mechanism: explore/exploit portfolio discipline and uncertainty reduction. Cycle5 translation: the Business OS keeps one PRIMARY + at most two PILOTs; artifact/test evidence changes WIP rather than merely adding more opportunity ideas.
