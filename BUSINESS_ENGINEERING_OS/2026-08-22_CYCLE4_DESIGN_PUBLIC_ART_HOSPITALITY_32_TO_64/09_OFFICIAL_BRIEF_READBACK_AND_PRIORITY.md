## Opportunity A — Clúain na Coillte, Monksland, Co. Roscommon
**Authority:** Roscommon County Council Arts Office official public-art page + 2026 Artist Brief PDF.  
**Process:** one-stage open themed.  
**Budget:** €112,500 including VAT.  
**Deadline:** 4pm Monday 7 September 2026.  
**Context:** new 51-home social housing development; expected completion April 2027.  
**Intent:** permanent public artwork or suite; establish place/identity, welcome/belonging, shared focal point; community integration is a core priority.

### Site state
Final artwork location is not locked in the brief; candidate zones include a large central green, secondary green, and walls at entry/access points. Final location is to be discussed with the successful artist, Council, design team and contractor. Therefore the engine must not invent a fixed wall/site geometry at submission stage.

### Required proposal evidence
- current CV, max 2 pages;
- 3 relevant previous works with context, descriptions, timeframe, overall budget, photos/links;
- artistic design/concept proposal with creative direction;
- preferred location(s), proposed scale and materials;
- preliminary images/visualization;
- indicative budget and delivery timeline.

### Assessment logic
- quality of concept: artistic merit, relevance to location/context, material approach;
- visuals, scaled if possible;
- experience in original/community-engaged public art and project management, including maintenance requirements;
- feasibility through costs and timeline, inclusive of research, design, consultation, collaboration, fabrication, installation and handover.

### Delivery/insurance signals
Artist budget responsibility is all-inclusive: fees, travel, design, research, materials, fabrication, site preparation, installation, lighting/site works, insurance, documentation and maintenance file. If successful, Public Liability cover stated in the brief is €6.5m; Employers Liability €13m where relevant; tax clearance is required.

### Engine fit
Strong candidate for `BDES24 CommissionRouteCompiler`, `BDES25 StakeholderAndConsentMap`, `BDES28 ProposalProofPackCompiler`, `BDES23 CostBandEstimator`, `BDES21 MaterialDurabilityLifecycleEngine`, `BDES30 DesignRedTeam`.

## Opportunity B — Shannon Valley, Ballaghaderreen, Co. Roscommon
**Authority:** same official Roscommon Artist Brief.  
**Process:** one-stage open themed.  
**Budget:** €40,500 including VAT.  
**Deadline:** 4pm Monday 7 September 2026.  
**Budget topology:** €15,000 allocated to Artist in Residence; remaining €25,500 for development/design/fabrication/installation of permanent artwork.  
**Context:** residential regeneration; socially engaged process is central, not ancillary.

### Critical distinction
This is not simply a smaller version of the Monksland commission. The process requires direct work with residents; participatory methodology and lived-experience sensitivity are central. A fully resolved final artwork is not expected at submission, but the artistic approach, indicative concept, engagement methodology and delivery logic must be strong. The brief notes benefit from partnering with a local artist experienced in socially engaged practice; there is no studio space in the residency and it is to be completed on site.

### Engine consequence
Do not route a purely visual mural/monument proposal here without a credible engagement/residency operating model. `BDES17 HospitalityFlowEngine` is not the relevant flow module, but the general Programme/UserJourney/Stakeholder/Offer/RedTeam stack applies. A future `CommunityEngagementOperatingModel` adapter may be warranted only after pilot evidence, not as another top-level meta-engine now.

**Process:** one-stage open competition.  
**Budget:** €21,500 including VAT/taxes/charges.  
**Deadline:** 17 September 2026.  
**Target completion:** January 2027, subject to chosen proposal/timeline.  
**Site:** Rectory Garden, with two indicated potential locations.

### Required submission evidence

- no more than 10 previous-work images in one PDF;

### Engine fit
