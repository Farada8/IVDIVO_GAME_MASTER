**Prompt:** Is a book title enough to route design knowledge?  
**Execution:** No. Compile `physical_file -> canonical_work -> source_passport -> mechanism -> stage jurisdiction -> fixture`. A source must state applicability and limits. **KEEP**.

## P05 — Define the minimum project object
**Prompt:** What information must exist before a concept is treated as an engineered project rather than an image idea?  
**Execution:** Site, users, programme, journey, design intent, composition/spatial system, materials, technical unknowns, maintenance, commission routes and K/D/T/E grades are required. **KEEP**.

## P06 — Resolve style-first versus site-first design
**Prompt:** Should a preferred style drive the initial project?  
**Execution:** Site/context and project purpose govern; style is compiled later as a mechanism vector. `SITE_BEFORE_STYLE` becomes hard contract. **KEEP**.

## P07 — Resolve decoration versus programme
**Prompt:** Can hospitality design begin with murals, furniture and mood boards?  
**Execution:** No. Programme, capacity, adjacencies, FOH/BOH and user journeys precede art integration. Art may become spatial architecture after programme is stable. **KEEP**.

## P08 — Compile Design Brief authority
**Prompt:** What should freeze scope before visualization?  
**Execution:** DesignBriefContract includes goals/non-goals, users, site evidence, functions, constraints, story thesis, success criteria, evidence grades, unknowns and decision owners. **KEEP**.

## P09 — Replace “beautiful composition” with an executable composition model
**Prompt:** What must a mural/monument composition engine explicitly control?  
**Execution:** Primary/secondary/tertiary masses, focal path, figure groups, axes, negative space, light hierarchy, viewpoint and detail budget. **KEEP**.

## P10 — Make viewing distance a first-class variable
**Prompt:** How do we prevent a large mural from becoming a collage of tiny scenes?  
**Execution:** Require 50m/20m/5m or project-specific distance tiers with silhouette, mass, narrative and detail tests. B90 DistanceReadabilityGate compiled. **KEEP**.

## P11 — Define Style Genome rather than artist-name imitation
**Prompt:** How should “Metaphysical Renaissance” or historical influences be represented?  
**Execution:** As mechanisms: space logic, edge treatment, colour architecture, figure grouping, light, drapery, symbolic density, temporal transitions, material language. Artist names are references, not a sufficient prompt. **KEEP**.

## P12 — Test a monolithic image prompt architecture
**Prompt:** Should one giant prompt contain story, composition, figures, materials, light, perspective, camera and exclusions?  
**Execution:** This creates instruction competition and weak diagnosis. Replace with staged compilation plus stable prompt layers. **MUTATE** -> B92 GenerativePromptCompiler.

## P13 — Compile image-failure diagnosis
**Prompt:** How should the engine respond when a generated mural/interior is wrong?  
**Execution:** Classify composition/anatomy/perspective/style/architecture/material/light/continuity failures and repair the smallest upstream object. Random re-rolls are disallowed as the default. **KEEP**.

## P14 — Define mural-specific technical gate
**Prompt:** What is missing if a mural concept has only dimensions and a render?  
**Execution:** Substrate, moisture, cracks, preparation, coating system, drainage, UV, access equipment, edge details, weather and repaint/maintenance cycle. **KEEP**.

## P15 — Define sculpture/monument technical gate
**Prompt:** Which issues must be exposed before calling a sculpture “buildable”?  
**Execution:** Anchoring dependency, centre-of-gravity assumptions, climbability, sharp edges, corrosion, drainage, fabrication, lifting, installation, inspection and specialist structural responsibility. **KEEP**.

## P16 — Compile small-architecture kit-of-parts logic
**Prompt:** How can gates, benches, pavilions, markers, shelters and lookouts become a coherent national/route family?  
**Execution:** Use repeatable component system with site-specific modules, common identity, replaceable parts and maintenance logic. B96 compiled. **KEEP**.

## P17 — Compile hospitality flow model
**Prompt:** Is a guest circulation diagram enough for a bar/restaurant/hotel?  
**Execution:** No. Guest, staff, food, beverage, waste, housekeeping, delivery and emergency/service paths must be separate objects before conflict resolution. **KEEP**.

## P18 — Compile FOH/BOH conflict gate
**Prompt:** What operational failures should the design engine detect early?  
**Execution:** Cross-traffic, long service path, delivery conflicts, dirty/clean intersections, visible service clutter, queue blockage and insufficient staging are explicit conflict types. **KEEP**.

## P19 — Decide how art enters an interior
**Prompt:** Should mural/mosaic/sculpture be added after interior planning?  
**Execution:** Not by default. B99 determines whether art changes orientation, sequence, light, acoustics or identity. If yes, it enters the architectural brief; otherwise it is labelled decorative layer. **KEEP**.

## P20 — Couple lighting and acoustics with experience
**Prompt:** Can lighting and acoustics remain late engineering notes?  
**Execution:** No. Day/night, task/accent/ambient, face/food/art lighting, glare, reverberation, privacy and soundscape affect the primary experience and must be designed before final visualization. **KEEP**.

## P21 — Compile materials for Irish exterior conditions
**Prompt:** What prevents an outdoor concept from using materials as pure aesthetic choices?  
**Execution:** Exposure context is mandatory: moisture, salt/coastal air, wind, UV, impact, freeze/thaw where relevant, cleaning, repairability, patina and replacement cycle. **KEEP**.

## P22 — Define buildability and maintenance maturity
**Prompt:** When does a concept become commission-proposal ready?  
**Execution:** Fabrication route, access, sequencing, specialist dependencies, cleaning, inspection, replacement and maintenance ownership must be visible; unresolved FATAL technical risks block readiness. **KEEP**.

## P23 — Test aesthetic-only cost estimation
**Prompt:** Can the engine estimate project cost mainly from appearance and size?  
**Execution:** No. This produces false precision. Replace with assumption-banded estimator tied to design maturity, quantity confidence, access/fabrication/installation and explicit unknowns. **MUTATE** -> B103.

## P24 — Compile commission-route architecture
**Prompt:** How does an art/design idea become a business opportunity without inventing a buyer?  
**Execution:** Map route classes—public commission, developer, hospitality operator, tourism/visitor attraction, private owner, grant-supported culture, competition, partnership, prototype—then use public evidence only to E2/E2+. **KEEP**.

## P25 — Compile stakeholder/consent map
**Prompt:** Who must be represented besides the paying client?  
**Execution:** Owner, operator, authority, heritage stakeholders, neighbours/community, fabricator, design team, maintenance owner and end users. Conflicts stay explicit. **KEEP**.

## P26 — Compile tourism-experience value model
**Prompt:** What makes an Irish art intervention a visitor experience rather than a static object?  
**Execution:** Visit trigger, arrival, orientation, story, interaction, dwell, photo moment, night mode, accessibility, repeatability and local spillover hypotheses are modelled. **KEEP**.

## P27 — Compile commercial offer packaging
**Prompt:** Should every prospect receive a full multidisciplinary design service?  
**Execution:** No. Package capability into bounded offers with different proof/cost requirements: concept audit, mural concept, site-art feasibility, hospitality art integration, visitor-route concept, concept+visualization, coordination. **KEEP**.

## P28 — Compile proposal proof pack
**Prompt:** What must a commission-facing package contain beyond renders?  
**Execution:** Site evidence, brief, rationale, before/after, programme, visitor journey, materials, technical risks, cost-band assumptions, maintenance, phases, proof grades and exclusions. **KEEP**.

## P29 — Test one-score project ranking
**Prompt:** Should all projects receive one weighted “best opportunity” score?  
**Execution:** No. A single score hides fatal conditions and incomparable dimensions. Use separate gates/axes for artistic fit, site evidence, D-grade, T-grade, commission evidence, cash/time and reversibility; fatal gates override averages. **MUTATE**.

## P30 — Compile Design Red Team
**Prompt:** Which independent review axes are mandatory?  
**Execution:** Narrative/history, site, composition, operations, technical, material/lifecycle, accessibility, safety, heritage, cost, maintenance and commissioning. Findings are FATAL/MAJOR/MINOR with fix and retest. **KEEP**.
