## Portfolio conclusion
The strongest recurring pattern is not asset ownership first. It is intelligence/coordination/implementation around externally funded spending streams, followed by customer-funded or supplier-funded delivery only after market proof.

Primary business archetypes emerging:
1. Procurement / supplier intelligence for construction, water, grid, offshore wind and solar.
2. AI implementation/compliance for Irish SMEs, especially construction/tourism.
3. Retrofit lead qualification and workflow coordination.
4. Grant/funding architecture where implementation work exists beyond free guidance.

## Anti-hype conclusion
Public budgets and policy signals prove forced activity and market motion, not willingness-to-pay for our proposed service. Cycle5 should select 1 PRIMARY + up to 2 bounded pilots from the shortlist and seek the smallest market evidence permitted by the current outreach policy.
