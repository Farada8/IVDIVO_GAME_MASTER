# CYCLE5 — ANALYSIS / CONCLUSIONS / PATH TO GOAL

## 1. The bottleneck changed
Cycle4 proved that many public signals and opportunity hypotheses can be found. Cycle5 shows that finding more signals is no longer the main constraint. The constraint is **proof that a compact deliverable changes a real buyer's decision enough to justify money**.

## 2. Three top WIP artifacts now exist
- OP01 can compress five live procurement notices into a smaller review/partner/no-bid set for a synthetic SME archetype.
- OP03 can compile current SEAI rules into deterministic grant-readiness branches.
- OP19 can turn a construction admin workflow into a bounded human-controlled AI/digital pilot specification.

That demonstrates production capability, not market demand.

## 3. The largest cross-cutting assumption is portfolio-wide
Across the 32 Cycle4 Opportunity Objects, the dominant repeated assumption is: **publicly observable opportunities can be converted into materially better decisions than existing free search**. Testing this once with strong before/after/comparator evidence is more valuable than creating 32 more business ideas.

## 4. OP19 has the strongest substitution warning
Ireland already offers Digital for Business, Grow Digital/Grow Digital Voucher, EDIHs and an explicit “start small and scale” AI-adoption path. Therefore a generic “AI diagnostic” is weakly differentiated. OP19 must either narrow to a construction-specific execution job (e.g. quote/job-pack traceability) or be demoted.

## 5. OP03 has rule-engine potential but recurring-value uncertainty
The public rules are structured enough to compile a useful qualification engine. However, the current artifact does not show how much contractor/OSS time it saves, whether the job repeats often enough, or whether another existing CRM/OSS workflow already solves it.

## 6. OP01 remains the best next E3 candidate
OP01 matches the current zero-cash constraint and has frequent, expiring public inputs. The sample demonstrates triage compression without software. Its next decisive proof is not another tender scan: it is a real SME decision/comparator observation under voluntary E3 conditions.

## 7. Cash truth is now a first-class business gate
Cycle5 makes five distinctions non-negotiable:
- grant reimbursement ≠ upfront cash;
- programme budget ≠ our revenue;
- price hypothesis ≠ paid price;
- accounting profit ≠ cash survival;
- machine production time ≠ founder delivery time.

## 8. Owned IP and knowledge evidence are now separated
D01 `THE WIFE AT HIS WEDDING` is company product/IP and can later enter product/channel/economics experiments. It is not a business-theory source and therefore does not increase the 78-file knowledge evidence count.

## Path to goal
`PUBLIC SIGNAL -> CANONICAL STATUS -> ARTIFACT -> BEFORE/AFTER COMPARATOR -> E3 BUYER BEHAVIOUR -> E4 PAID COMMITMENT -> OBSERVED DELIVERY TIME/COST -> CONTRIBUTION/CASH MODEL -> REPEAT BUYER -> POWER/SCALING`.

The next business-engine improvement should optimise this conversion chain, not expand idea inventory.
