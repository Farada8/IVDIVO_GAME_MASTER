# P87 — MOM TEST / ANTI-FLUFF BUYER INTERVIEW FILTER

**Status:** PREPARED / NO OUTREACH EXECUTED.

Allowed question shapes:
- “Tell me about the last time you had to decide whether to bid a tender.”
- “Show me how you currently find, save and compare relevant notices.”
- “What happened the last time a relevant deadline or qualification detail was missed?”
- “Who is involved in bid/no-bid and what information do they need?”
- “What do you currently pay for or spend time on to solve this?”
- “What happened the last time a retrofit lead turned out not to be grant-ready?”
- “Walk me through the last quote/job pack that required rework because information was missing.”

Reject/rewrite:
- “Would you use this?”
- “Would you pay €X?”
- “Do you think this is a good idea?”
- any product pitch before current behaviour/workaround/consequence is understood.

No messages/calls are sent by this protocol. It is a future E3 instrument only.

# P88 — E3 VOLUNTARY BUYER CONVERSATION EVIDENCE PROTOCOL

A conversation may promote one claim to E3 only when it records:
1. participant's business role/decision context;
2. a recent concrete instance of the target job/problem;
3. current workaround/process;
4. consequence/cost/delay/risk in the participant's own account;
5. captured date and voluntary context/consent;
6. which exact hypothesis the evidence updates.

Keep raw notes separate from interpretation. One conversation does not prove market size, repeatability, price or payment. Compliments, future intent and “sounds useful” remain below E3 for payment/value validation.

# P89 — E4 PAYMENT PROOF CONTRACT

Valid instruments: `DEPOSIT`, `PAID_PILOT`, `PURCHASE_ORDER`, `SIGNED_PAID_ORDER`.

Minimum binding fields:
- payer identity / organisation;
- scope/deliverable;
- amount and currency;
- instrument/receipt/PO reference;
- date;
- stop/refund/cancellation boundary where applicable.

Not E4: verbal interest, “send me a proposal”, hypothetical price answer, free pilot, unsigned quote, grant eligibility, programme budget, investor interest.

E4 proves a bounded paid commitment. It does not by itself prove repeatability, unit economics or scalable demand.

# P90 — NULL-SAFE PRICING EXPERIMENT PROTOCOL

Initial state: `price = null`.

Allowed progression:
`NULL -> EXTERNAL_REFERENCE_HYPOTHESIS -> BUYER_REACTION -> PAID_COMMITMENT -> REPEATABILITY_TEST`.

A competitor price, procurement budget or grant amount may inform a range hypothesis but cannot validate our price. No “€99/month” or other figure is promoted merely because it looks commercially plausible.
