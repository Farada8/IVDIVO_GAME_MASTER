# BUSINESS ENGINEERING OS — CYCLE5 CONTRACTS C153–C184

C153 `SIGNAL_AGE_CLOCK` — every time-sensitive signal carries `published_at`, `observed_at`, age and SLA state; unknown dates cannot be silently treated as fresh.  
C154 `OFFICIAL_URL_CANONICAL_IDENTITY` — tracking parameters/fragments do not create independent sources; canonical URL and resource/version identity are retained.  
C155 `SYNDICATION_NOT_INDEPENDENT_EVIDENCE` — repeated press/search copies are one evidence family unless independent underlying evidence is demonstrated.  
C156 `PROCUREMENT_STATUS_CANONICAL_NOTICE` — open/closed/award/PIN decisions require canonical notice state and deadline; conflicting indexed snapshots force revalidation.  
C157 `SOURCE_SUPERSESSION_GRAPH` — newer policy/programme/notice versions supersede, not merely coexist with, prior decision authority.  
C158 `PROGRAMME_BUDGET_NOT_BUYER_PROOF` — programme/contract budget never proves a buyer person, willingness-to-pay for our service or obtainable share.  
C159 `OFFICIAL_ACCESS_PATH_ONLY` — public buyer/access-path proof must resolve through official registers/directories/portals; guessed personal emails are forbidden.  
C160 `DISRUPTION_CLASSIFICATION_EVIDENCE` — nonconsumption/overshot/undershot labels remain hypotheses until evidence supports the causal conditions.  
C161 `MOTIVATION_ABILITY_SEPARATION` — regulation/funding can change motivation while tools/training/access change ability; do not collapse them.  
C162 `INCUMBENT_MOTIVATION_ASYMMETRY` — incumbent disadvantage requires a demonstrated motivation/process mismatch, not startup preference.  
C163 `WHY_NOW_FALSIFIER_REQUIRED` — every active opportunity states public evidence that would kill/reshape its WhyNow thesis.  
C164 `SECTOR_HALF_LIFE` — tender/grant/policy/law signals use distinct revalidation horizons and may be superseded earlier.  
C165 `CROSS_PORTFOLIO_FATAL_ASSUMPTION` — assumptions recurring across opportunities are tested once at the shared causal layer before 32 separate tests.  
C166 `SHARED_ASSUMPTION_GRAPH` — experiments link to all opportunity objects they can update; evidence cannot be counted multiple times.  
C167 `DECISION_QUALITY_EXPERIMENT` — no-outreach tests must record decision state before/after and resolved uncertainty, not just artifact production.  
C168 `PUBLIC_ARTIFACT_NOT_MARKET_PROOF` — sample deliverables can reach E2+ only; they cannot create E3 buyer or E4 payment evidence.  
C169 `RETROFIT_QUALIFICATION_RULE_TRACEABILITY` — every qualification branch cites current official rule authority and exposes missing BER/HLI/contractor/approval facts.  
C170 `AI_DIAGNOSTIC_START_SMALL` — AI workflow pilots begin with bounded assistive tasks and human-controlled authoritative fields; AI is not assumed necessary.  
C171 `HUMAN_DELIVERY_TIME_MUST_BE_OBSERVED` — machine generation time cannot substitute for founder/manual service delivery telemetry.  
C172 `TENDER_BRIEF_CANONICAL_STATUS` — tender shortlist artifacts show canonical notice ID/status/deadline and explicit unknown qualification barriers.  
C173 `SEAI_RULES_CURRENT_PRIMARY` — grant readiness uses current SEAI primary pages; cached summaries are discovery only after rule change.  
C174 `AI_WORKFLOW_DIAGNOSTIC_BOUNDARY` — workflow advice distinguishes automation, AI assist, human approval and legal/regulatory handoff.  
C175 `MOM_TEST_ANTI_FLUFF` — future interviews ask about recent behaviour, current workaround and consequence; hypothetical praise/payment questions are rejected.  
C176 `E3_CONVERSATION_EVIDENCE` — E3 requires an identifiable buyer-role context, recent concrete example, existing workaround and consequence; interest alone is insufficient.  
C177 `E4_PAYMENT_PROOF` — only deposit/paid pilot/PO/signed paid order bound to payer+scope can establish E4.  
C178 `PRICE_NULL_BEFORE_SIGNAL` — price stays null until external evidence creates a hypothesis; public competitor pricing is not our validated price.  
C179 `FOUNDER_CASH_TIMELINE` — deposits, supplier payments, payroll/tax, receivables and reimbursements are timed events; aggregate profit cannot hide a cash trough.  
C180 `REIMBURSEMENT_NOT_UPFRONT_CASH` — grants paid after expenditure create bridge requirements unless another real funding source covers the gap.  
C181 `FUNDING_TOPOLOGY_SOURCE` — customer-, supplier-, grant-, debt- and investor-funded routes remain separate and require route-specific evidence.  
C182 `WORKING_CAPITAL_STRESS` — material-heavy services remain HOLD when material/labour/deposit/receivable/supplier timing inputs are null.  
C183 `CONTRIBUTION_MARGIN_FULL_COST` — contribution margin includes direct cost, founder delivery time and pickup/rework; unknown price keeps result null.  
C184 `WIP_QUEUE_DELAY` — service capacity and WIP are bounded; high utilisation is treated as delay/reliability risk rather than evidence of healthy scale.
