1. `SI-C5-A SOURCE_CLASS_SEPARATION` — knowledge source, public market signal and owned product asset must be different object classes.
2. `SI-C5-B CANONICAL_STATUS_BEATS_SEARCH_SNAPSHOT` — conflicting indexed procurement deadlines/statuses must fail closed to canonical notice revalidation.
3. `SI-C5-C ARTIFACT_PROOF_CEILING` — self-produced sample deliverables cannot auto-promote market evidence beyond E2+.
4. `SI-C5-D HUMAN_TIME_TELEMETRY_HONESTY` — machine generation duration cannot be substituted for founder/manual delivery time.
5. `SI-C5-E CROSS_PORTFOLIO_ASSUMPTION_TESTING` — repeated causal assumptions should be tested once and propagated through a provenance graph.

Promotion requirements: multi-cycle regression, at least one real E3/E4 evidence transition without leakage, no degradation of current Cycle4 authority, Red Team review, and durable GitHub+Drive readback.
