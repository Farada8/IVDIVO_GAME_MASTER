# CYCLE5 — SELF-IMPROVEMENT + RED TEAM

## Self-Improvement Business Bridge V3 — CANDIDATE

Cycle5 does **not** allocate new universal authority simply because new code exists. The learning bridge emits candidates only.

Required learning record:
`DEFECT → ROOT_CAUSE → REPAIR → RETEST → RESULT → EVIDENCE_HASHES → REUSE_CONDITIONS`

Promotion requires repeated independent evidence plus explicit authority review.

## Earliest-cause taxonomy

1. `SOURCE` — stale/wrong/duplicate/correlated public source.
2. `IDENTITY` — File Library / Drive / byte-hash / edition/work identity error.
3. `OPPORTUNITY` — wrong buyer/workload/why-now hypothesis.
4. `ARTIFACT` — sample fails to reduce decision workload.
5. `TARGETING` — wrong organisation/role.
6. `ACCESS` — channel or willingness to review fails.
7. `VALUE` — real buyer sees no material usefulness.
8. `MONEY` — value exists but no positive commercial commitment.
9. `DELIVERY` — paid work cannot be delivered reliably/efficiently.
10. `ECONOMICS` — measured margin/cash timing is unattractive.
11. `POWER` — repeatable value lacks durable benefit/barrier.
12. `PORTFOLIO` — opportunity consumes WIP but contributes little information/value.

Repair the earliest proven layer and invalidate only dependent descendants.

## Red Team severity

### FATAL if allowed
- public or synthetic evidence promoting E3/E4;
- raw copyrighted library copied to public GitHub;
- stale tender presented as currently open;
- buyer/money receipt not bound to exact artifact lineage;
- WIP expansion beyond three without explicit portfolio decision.

### MAJOR
- target directory treated as buyer fit;
- price range treated as WTP evidence;
- free competitor/internal process omitted from alternative map;
- pilot duplicates primary information rather than adding independent learning;
- unit economics estimated as known before paid delivery.

### MEDIUM
- source observed date missing;
- duplicate public announcements overweighted;
- role hypothesis presented as named contact;
- excessive artifact polish before buyer evidence.

## Current learning ceiling

Engineering evidence: code/contracts/tests/artifact samples can be proven internally.
Market evidence: remains max E2+ until a real buyer interacts with a hash-bound sample.
Commercial evidence: remains below E4 until real money/deposit/PO is observed.

No outreach, buyer review, payment, repeat buyer, unit economics or market winner is claimed by Cycle5 engineering work.
