# CYCLE 4 SYNTHESIS + PATH TO IMPROVEMENT

## What changed
1. Library completeness is separated from canonical-work completeness.
2. Broken files use explicit remediation/quarantine rather than contaminating evidence.
3. K5 is mechanism-scoped and executable.
4. Frameworks are conditionally routed instead of stacked into an additive score.
5. Contradictions survive as first-class objects until evidence resolves them.
6. Uncertainty can remain binary/interval/scenario/null without fake precision.
7. Measurement is governed by value of information.
8. WIP is bounded to one primary + two pilots.
9. Material economics/legal/provider gaps produce explicit STOP states.
10. Creative grants/residencies/commissions now have a domain adapter: chat/aggregator discovery is not application authority.
