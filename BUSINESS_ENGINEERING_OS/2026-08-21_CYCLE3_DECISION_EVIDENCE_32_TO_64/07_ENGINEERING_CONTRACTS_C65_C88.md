# BUSINESS ENGINEERING OS — CYCLE 3 ENGINEERING CONTRACTS C65–C88

C65 `RAW_BOOKS_DRIVE_ONLY` — raw copyrighted book binaries remain private Drive; GitHub contains derived metadata only.
C66 `BYTE_HASH_NOT_WORK_IDENTITY` — exact bytes may establish physical identity; different bytes do not establish different works.
C67 `DUPLICATE_NO_EVIDENCE_WEIGHT` — exact duplicates cannot increase confidence.
C68 `K_E_NON_SUBSTITUTABLE` — knowledge proof K* cannot become market proof E*.
C69 `DECISION_RELEVANCE` — every measurement/experiment names the decision it can change.
C70 `UNCERTAINTY_EXPLICIT` — important unknowns stay null/ranged, never filled with plausible fiction.
C71 `VALUE_OF_INFORMATION` — evidence acquisition is prioritized by expected decision value, cost, latency and downstream unlock.
C72 `COST_OF_DELAY` — urgency is expressed as economic/strategic delay consequence, not emotional pressure.
C73 `META_WIP_LIMIT` — backlog size is unlimited; active experimental WIP is bounded.
C74 `SMALL_BATCH_CANARY` — prefer the smallest reversible experiment that can discriminate between meaningful alternatives.
C75 `MOM_TEST_EVIDENCE` — compliments, hypotheticals and generic enthusiasm cannot count as demand proof.
C76 `COMMITMENT_ADVANCEMENT` — commitment/advancement must be captured separately from positive sentiment.
C77 `POLICY_RESISTANCE` — material interventions state intended effect, guardrails, delays and compensating responses.
C78 `DOUBLE_LOOP_ESCALATION` — repeated defect after local repair triggers model/boundary/objective review.
C79 `CONTRADICTION_PRESERVATION` — dissenting mechanisms are routed by context; never averaged into mush.
C80 `NEGATIVE_EVIDENCE_FIRST` — fatal deal-breakers can kill an opportunity before positive scoring.
C81 `VANITY_METRIC_FIREWALL` — activity, traffic, document count, prompt count and compliments are not outcome metrics.
C82 `HUMAN_HANDOFF_COMPLETENESS` — unresolved legal, market, practitioner or owner judgment remains an explicit handoff.
C83 `ROUTE_VECTOR_NOT_MAGIC_SCORE` — CREATE/BROKER/ACQUIRE uses vector comparison, not one opaque total.
C84 `MODEL_BOUNDARY_REVIEW` — causal models state what is excluded and why.
C85 `TIME_HORIZON` — a decision states short/medium/long consequences and material delays.
C86 `HYPE_FIREWALL` — internal AI agreement and repeated documents do not become independent evidence.
C87 `ROLLBACK_BEFORE_CANARY` — reversible experiments define rollback trigger and target before execution.
C88 `SELF_IMPROVEMENT_SELF_APPLIES` — business-engine upgrades enter candidate→pilot→evidence→promotion; no automatic v3 promotion.
