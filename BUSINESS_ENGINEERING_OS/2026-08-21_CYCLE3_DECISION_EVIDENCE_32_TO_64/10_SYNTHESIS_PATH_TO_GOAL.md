## Core finding

The current bottleneck is no longer “more business books.” The library is already broad enough to construct mechanisms. The next bottleneck is **live, fresh, external signal architecture** followed by bounded opportunity evidence.

Books can prove that a framework exists. They cannot prove:
- that a buyer currently has the problem;
- that budget exists;
- that willingness-to-pay exists;
- that the economics work;
- that financing is available;
- that a specific opportunity is legal/operationally viable.

Therefore Cycle4 must not become another literature sprint.
