# RUN32 EXECUTION LEDGER

All 32 prompts executed as bounded analysis. Outputs are decision aids, not market proof unless marked confirmed.

## P01 — Evidence boundary
**Prompt:** Separate confirmed Irish market evidence from creative hypotheses.

**Result:** Confirmed: public-art commissions, heritage/visitor interpretation procurement, trail/signage procurement and public/community funding mechanisms exist. Hypothesis: councils will buy our specific sculptural 'Memory Fragments' without a commissioned design process. Keep separate.

## P02 — Buyer map
**Prompt:** List who can actually pay.

**Result:** Primary buyers: County/City Councils, OPW/state bodies, tourism/visitor attractions, community/heritage organisations with funding, architects/exhibition/interpretation contractors as specialist subcontract clients. Private hospitality/estate buyers are secondary and need separate proof.

## P03 — Fastest money
**Prompt:** Rank routes to first paid work.

**Result:** 1) artist/public-art applications where artist is directly procured; 2) paid concept/visual package to community/council/architect partner; 3) specialist illustration/reconstruction subcontract; 4) larger trail/visitor packages after references. Large build contracts are later.

## P04 — Anti-middleman
**Prompt:** Define indispensable function.

**Result:** Own research synthesis, artistic direction, site-specific concept, visual reconstruction, material language, proposal pack, supplier coordination and case-study IP. Fabrication may be outsourced initially.

## P05 — Product taxonomy
**Prompt:** Reduce ideas to sellable packages.

**Result:** Four entry offers: Place Study; Visual Reconstruction; Public Art Concept; Heritage Stop Concept. Advanced products: Memory Fragment, Kinetic Story Machine, Smart Heritage Node, Trail System.

## P06 — Pilot county
**Prompt:** Choose pilot geography logic.

**Result:** Start concentrated, not all-Ireland: one county/region gives density, coherent routes and repeat buyers. Wicklow is suitable as a design pilot because existing concept work is already substantial; selection is operational, not proof of superior market.

## P07 — Pilot set
**Prompt:** Choose five pilot project archetypes.

**Result:** Castle/ruin fragment; monastic forest stop; lost railway stop; harbour/pier scene; craft/forge scene. Together demonstrate material, narrative and visitor-use range.

## P08 — Website role
**Prompt:** Define website function.

**Result:** Database + portfolio + buyer funnel, not ad blog. Every place page can grow from indexed source -> story -> reconstruction -> object proposal -> paid case study.

## P09 — Website data model
**Prompt:** Define records.

**Result:** Place, Story, Source, Person, Reconstruction, Object, Route, Buyer, Opportunity, FundingRoute, Asset, CaseStudy, Partner, EvidenceSignal.

## P10 — Content velocity
**Prompt:** How to scale without trash quality.

**Result:** Two-tier production: many source-grounded indexed place records; few deep authored flagship cases. Do not mass-generate invented history.

## P11 — Historical truth
**Prompt:** Define reconstruction labels.

**Result:** DOCUMENTED / INFERRED FROM ANALOGUES / ARTISTIC HYPOTHESIS. Every visual reconstruction carries source notes.

## P12 — Material system
**Prompt:** Create palette logic.

**Result:** Use 1 dominant mass + 1 secondary craft material + 1 accent. Example stone/concrete 70%, ceramic/mosaic 20%, bronze/glass 10%. Avoid decorative overload.

## P13 — Ceramic/mosaic viability
**Prompt:** Define technical gates.

**Result:** High-fired/frost-resistant ceramic; exterior-rated adhesive/bedding; drainage and differential movement; impact/vandal resistance; replacement module strategy.

## P14 — Stained glass outdoors
**Prompt:** Define safe form.

**Result:** Use protected laminated artistic glass, thick cast glass or recessed protected assemblies; conventional exposed leaded glazing is a maintenance/safety risk outdoors.

## P15 — Kinetic wind theatre
**Prompt:** Define engineering contract.

**Result:** Wind input -> gearbox/cam/links -> slow bounded motion. Must have overspeed protection, manual lockout, safe pinch clearances, corrosion design and service access. Mechanism must fail safe.

## P16 — Solar node
**Prompt:** Define energy use priority.

**Result:** Priority loads: lighting + device charging; optional low-power sensors. Mechanical theatre should remain operable or safely idle without electricity where possible.

## P17 — Charging promise
**Prompt:** Define realistic visitor utility.

**Result:** USB-C/wireless charging is feasible as a design feature but requires electrical/battery/weatherproofing and maintenance proof; connectivity/phone calls still depend on network availability unless separate communications infrastructure is provided.

## P18 — Water magic
**Prompt:** Assess pouring-jug concept.

**Result:** Creative mechanism viable as a concept; outdoor implementation needs closed-loop water, drainage, slip control, water quality/maintenance and winter mode. Treat as premium, not first pilot.

## P19 — Scenographic imitation
**Prompt:** Define authenticity rule.

**Result:** New theatrical fragments may evoke lost architecture but may not masquerade as original archaeological fabric. Date/interpretation markings and physical separation from authentic remains are required.

## P20 — Viking ship fragment
**Prompt:** Convert wow idea into product.

**Result:** A half-buried ship-form can be landscape sculpture + shelter + story object. Historically exact reproduction is optional; label as artistic interpretation unless evidence supports a reconstruction.

## P21 — Lost house cutaway
**Prompt:** Convert idea into visitor value.

**Result:** Exterior/interior split makes daily life legible; strong for estates, famine/local-history, vanished villages. Use durable replicas rather than vulnerable museum props.

## P22 — Railway ghost stop
**Prompt:** Convert idea into trail use.

**Result:** Strong on greenways/former rail corridors: shelter + bench + historic map/timetable + architectural memory. Buyer proof must come from route operators/councils, not nostalgia alone.

## P23 — Monastic room
**Prompt:** Convert sacred idea carefully.

**Result:** Use contemplative reconstruction, reading/quiet function and artistic glass; avoid invented devotional claims. Heritage/ecclesiastical consultation where relevant.

## P24 — Procurement entry
**Prompt:** How to enter larger projects.

**Result:** Do not prime-contract museum-scale work initially. Target specialist role: artwork, reconstruction, storytelling, material/art concept, or public-art component under established architect/interpretation contractor.

## P25 — Partner map
**Prompt:** Define initial subcontractors.

**Result:** Structural engineer, fabricator/metalworker, stone mason, ceramic/mosaic specialist, glass specialist, electrician/solar integrator, groundworks installer, historian/archaeologist as needed, Irish-language editor where applicable.

## P26 — Cost engine
**Prompt:** Define first usable costing method.

**Result:** Use supplier quotes and labour day rates by work package. Three-point range only where quote absent. Flag confidence; no invented margin.

## P27 — Funding engine
**Prompt:** How to use grants without dependence.

**Result:** Map project objectives to funding programme and eligible applicant. Build concept first; never assume artist/company is eligible applicant. Often council/community group applies and studio is paid supplier.

## P28 — Sales pack
**Prompt:** What does a buyer receive before contract.

**Result:** One-page place opportunity, 2–3 visuals, visitor problem, concept, dimensions/materials, indicative delivery route, approvals risks, cost-confidence statement, funding-fit notes, next paid step.

## P29 — Paid concept offer
**Prompt:** Define lowest-risk first invoice.

**Result:** Sell a bounded concept package: source review + site/story synthesis + 2 concept directions + one developed visual + preliminary materials/risks + delivery roadmap. Price must be set from actual effort/market testing, not invented here.

## P30 — Success metrics
**Prompt:** Measure real traction.

**Result:** Qualified buyer replies, meetings, paid concept conversions, repeat invitations, tender shortlists, subcontract requests, actual fabrication/install case studies. Website traffic alone is secondary.

## P31 — Kill criteria
**Prompt:** When to stop an idea.

**Result:** No identifiable buyer; repeated buyer rejection; approvals make siting unrealistic; maintenance burden unacceptable; fabrication cost exceeds buyer value; no credible differentiation from commodity signage.

## P32 — Cycle synthesis
**Prompt:** What should be built next.

**Result:** Build 5 flagship case pages + buyer/outreach list + evidence ledger + paid concept template. Continue 64 prompts only against bottlenecks; do not ritualistically execute all if evidence gate is missing.
