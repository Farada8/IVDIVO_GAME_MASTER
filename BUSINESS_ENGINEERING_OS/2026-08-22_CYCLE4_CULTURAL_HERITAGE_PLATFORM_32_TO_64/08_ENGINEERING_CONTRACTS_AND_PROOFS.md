# ENGINEERING CONTRACTS & PROOFS — CULTURAL HERITAGE PLATFORM

These contracts are fail-closed. A concept may remain artistically attractive while failing business or engineering acceptance.

## C-HER-01 — Evidence Contract
**Input:** claim about demand, buyer, funding, cost, regulation, fabrication or performance.
**Required proof:** source identity + date/freshness + what exactly it proves + evidence state.
**PASS:** claim is bounded to what evidence supports.
**FAIL:** inference is presented as market fact.

## C-HER-02 — Buyer Proof Contract
**Input:** proposed product/service.
**Required proof:** named buyer class + observed comparable spend/request/procurement + purchase route.
**PASS:** real spend path exists.
**FAIL:** only user admiration, website traffic, grants or competitor existence.

## C-HER-03 — Historical Authenticity Contract
**Input:** reconstruction/scenographic object.
**Required proof:** documented elements separated from analogue-based inference and artistic hypothesis.
**PASS:** visitor cannot reasonably mistake new artistic work for original archaeology.
**FAIL:** fabricated history or unlabelled imitation presented as authentic fabric.

## C-HER-04 — Material Durability Contract
**Input:** exterior material system.
**Required proof:** supplier/engineer suitability for exposure, moisture/freeze, corrosion, UV, impact, movement, cleaning and repair.
**PASS:** specified installation and replacement/maintenance path.
**FAIL:** indoor art technique assumed suitable outdoors without evidence.

## C-HER-05 — Siting/Structural Contract
**Input:** freestanding wall, shelter, pavilion, sculpture or heavy object.
**Required proof:** landowner authority, planning/heritage path, structural design, foundation concept, wind/action loads, public-access risk.
**PASS:** engineer/authority route identified before fabrication commitment.
**FAIL:** visual concept treated as construction-ready.

## C-HER-06 — Kinetic Safety Contract
**Input:** wind/water/solar mechanical theatre.
**Required proof:** bounded speed/force, guarding and pinch clearances, overspeed protection, manual isolation, fail-safe state, service access, corrosion strategy.
**PASS:** loss of wind/power/control leaves safe object.
**FAIL:** exposed mechanism can accelerate, trap, strike or require unsafe servicing.

## C-HER-07 — Solar/Electrical Contract
**Input:** PV, battery, lighting, charging, sensors/comms.
**Required proof:** load budget, storage sizing, protection, IP/weather rating, battery safety, vandal resistance, electrical compliance, maintenance and winter mode.
**PASS:** essential function remains safe through low-energy state.
**FAIL:** decorative concept promises power/communications without engineered supply.

## C-HER-08 — Water Contract
**Input:** fountain, pouring jug, recirculating feature.
**Required proof:** closed-loop or supply route, drainage, water hygiene, slip control, freeze/winter mode, pump/filter service.
**PASS:** safe maintainable hydraulic cycle.
**FAIL:** standing/escaping water creates health or slip hazard.

## C-HER-09 — Funding Compatibility Contract
**Input:** grant/funding scheme.
**Required proof:** current scheme rules, eligible applicant, eligible cost type, timing and procurement obligations.
**PASS:** funding route is possible for the actual applicant/project.
**FAIL:** funding existence is described as customer demand or guaranteed award.

## C-HER-10 — Cost & Quote Contract
**Input:** concept or tender price.
**Required proof:** quote/work-package basis for research, design, engineering, fabrication, logistics, groundworks, installation, insurance, contingency, maintenance and taxes.
**PASS:** confidence shown per line and margin calculated from supported inputs.
**FAIL:** total contract value is treated as artist income/profit.

## C-HER-11 — Installation & QA Contract
**Input:** fabricated object.
**Required proof:** approved drawings/spec, incoming QC, foundation/site checks, installation method, snag list, public-safety inspection, as-built record.
**PASS:** handover has evidence and ownership/maintenance instructions.
**FAIL:** installed visual appearance alone is accepted as completion.

## C-HER-12 — Maintenance Contract
**Input:** public outdoor object.
**Required proof:** inspection frequency, cleaning/finish plan, replaceable parts, mechanism/electrical service, expected wear and responsible party.
**PASS:** lifecycle burden known to buyer.
**FAIL:** maintenance is silently externalized.

## C-HER-13 — Commercial Pilot Contract
**Input:** new cultural product line.
**Required proof:** paid concept or written buyer request + real quote stack + site/approval route.
**PASS:** move toward fabricated pilot.
**FAIL:** produce expensive prototype solely from enthusiasm.

## C-HER-14 — Learning Promotion Contract
**Input:** proposed engine improvement.
**Required proof:** provenance, defect/opportunity, bounded pilot, observed evidence, regression check and application path.
**PASS:** reusable mechanism promoted through existing IVDIVO Self-Improvement lifecycle.
**FAIL:** chat idea becomes global rule without test.

## Proof hierarchy used by this project
E0 — idea only.
E1 — sourced public signal / comparable project.
E2 — multiple corroborating public signals / explicit procurement category.
E3 — direct buyer/partner evidence, quote or written request.
E4 — paid pilot/contract and delivery evidence.
E5 — repeated paid delivery / retention / recurring procurement.

Public research can usually reach E1–E2. It may not be relabelled E3+ without direct external evidence.
