# IVDIVO CULTURAL HERITAGE PLATFORM — BUSINESS ENGINEERING CYCLE 4
Date: 2026-08-22
Status: WORKING INTEGRATION CANDIDATE / NOT AUTOMATIC BUSINESS TRUTH

## Founder objective
Build a real Irish cultural-project platform that can originate and sell place-based cultural projects:
- visual history and reconstruction;
- public-art and mural concepts;
- interpretive/visitor information;
- small architectural forms and sculptural landscape objects;
- theatrical/kinetic/solar-powered heritage installations;
- routes, books, exhibitions and digital layers.

The immediate economic objective is not “get a grant”. It is:
1. build demonstrable work;
2. identify real buyers and real funding channels;
3. win paid commissions/subcontracts;
4. use grants/funding as accelerators when structurally appropriate.

## Non-negotiable evidence law
Every business claim is labelled:
- CONFIRMED — supported by a real buyer, procurement, published funding scheme, contract, supplier quote, or direct market evidence;
- HYPOTHESIS — plausible but not yet commercially verified;
- TEST_REQUIRED — next evidence action is defined;
- REJECTED — evidence contradicts or economics fail.

No invented margins, invented demand, invented conversion rates or invented grant eligibility.

## Product stack
P1 PLACE STORY — researched place page / historical narrative / source map.
P2 VISUAL RECONSTRUCTION — evidence-based artistic reconstruction.
P3 PUBLIC ART CONCEPT — mural, sculpture, mosaic, stained-glass or mixed-media site proposal.
P4 HERITAGE STOP — shelter/bench/info-panel integrated architectural stop.
P5 MEMORY FRAGMENT — sculptural architectural fragment: wall, arch, room, harbour, railway, forge, monastic cell.
P6 KINETIC STORY MACHINE — wind/solar/water-driven mechanical micro-theatre.
P7 SMART HERITAGE NODE — solar generation, lighting, charging, optional comms/weather/digital layer.
P8 TRAIL SYSTEM — 3–20 linked stops with common design language.
P9 VISITOR EXPERIENCE PACKAGE — story, route, visuals, objects, interpretation and delivery specification.
P10 PUBLICATION/EXHIBITION — book, exhibition, prints and education outputs as secondary monetisation.

## Current strongest creative language
Not plastic signage. Durable, place-specific, materially rich cultural objects:
stone, architectural concrete/GFRC, oak, bronze, brass, corten/stainless where appropriate,
high-fired ceramics, engobes/glazes, smalti/stone/Roman-style mosaic, protected stained-glass or laminated artistic glass,
lime finishes, relief, carved ornament, patina, controlled weathering.

## Main conclusion from Cycle 4
The strongest business is not “selling signs”.
It is a cultural project studio/platform that creates evidence-based place concepts and becomes a specialist designer/artist/producer or subcontractor inside funded public-art, heritage, tourism and route projects.
The immediate proof path is 3–5 exceptional pilot concepts for real places + direct outreach + paid small package before any large fabrication investment.
