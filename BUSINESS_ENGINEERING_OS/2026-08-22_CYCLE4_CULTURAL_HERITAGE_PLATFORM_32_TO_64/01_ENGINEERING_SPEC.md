## Fail-closed rules
- No buyer evidence => do not call it a market.
- No site authority/landowner route => do not call installation feasible.
- Protected monument proximity => archaeology/heritage/planning review before build promise.
- Outdoor glass/ceramic/mosaic => weather, vandalism, impact and maintenance proof required.
- Moving mechanisms => pinch-point, wind-load, maintenance, lockout and fail-safe analysis required.
- Solar/charging => electrical design, battery safety, weatherproofing, vandal resistance and maintenance required.
- Water feature => hygiene, freeze, slip, drainage and recirculation gate.
- “Historical reconstruction” must distinguish documented fact from artistic hypothesis.

## Business proof contract
A product line may move from HYPOTHESIS to CONFIRMED only when at least one of:
A. real buyer requests/purchases comparable scope;
B. tender/procurement explicitly buys comparable scope;
C. paid pilot;
D. trade partner confirms recurring demand plus price mechanism;
E. multiple independent current market signals with buyer identity and spend path.

## Quote contract
Every quote separates:
research | concept | artist/design fee | engineering | fabrication | site works | installation | insurance | contingency | maintenance | VAT/tax assumptions.
Never infer profit from contract value.

## Product records
Each concept has:
ID, place, county, buyer candidates, historical source set, object class, dimensions,
material palette, interaction, utilities, planning/heritage risk, maintenance class,
fabrication route, cost confidence, funding routes, evidence status, next gate.
