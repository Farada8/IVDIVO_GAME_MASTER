# BUSINESS EVIDENCE & GATES

## Confirmed categories (market existence)
- Public art commissions exist and directly procure artists.
- Heritage/visitor interpretation projects exist and procure design/content/fabrication teams.
- Trail/wayfinding/interpretive infrastructure is procured by public bodies.
- Irish rural/town/heritage/outdoor funding programmes can finance relevant projects when the eligible applicant and scope match.

## Not yet confirmed
- Recurring Irish buyer demand specifically for our “Memory Fragment” product.
- A repeatable price/margin for sculptural heritage stops.
- Demand for wind-driven historical micro-theatre as a standalone procurement category.
- Buyer willingness to pay for solar charging as a differentiator.
- Book/print/card sales sufficient to fund living costs.

## First commercial gate
Before large fabrication:
1. 20 buyer/procurer evidence records.
2. 10 direct buyer/partner conversations.
3. 3 real fabrication quote stacks.
4. 1 paid concept commission OR written request to price/develop a comparable concept.
5. one approved pilot path with site/landowner/funding/permission route.

## Revenue priority hypothesis
H1: direct public-art commission — fastest high-value artist route, irregular.
H2: paid heritage concept/reconstruction package — best controllable entry product if buyer interviews validate.
H3: specialist subcontract to established interpretation/architecture contractor — likely lower sales friction after portfolio proof.
H4: fabricated proprietary heritage objects — later, only after real orders.
H5: books/prints/merchandise — secondary until sales evidence exists.
