# CYCLE7 SYNTHESIS — PATH TO GOAL

## What changed
Cycle6 identified the missing authority. Cycle7 converts that missing authority into explicit acquisition, provenance, join, review and proof-transition machinery.

The central business-engineering problem is now not `find more opportunities`; it is:

`CAN WE TURN AN AUTHORITATIVE TENDER PACK + VERIFIED SUPPLIER PROFILE INTO A MATERIAL BETTER DECISION THAN FREE eTENDERS DISCOVERY, AND WILL A REAL TARGET USER VALUE THAT DELTA?`

## Strongest conclusion
The current procurement product hypothesis survives only as a **residual decision-intelligence job**, not as a tender-alert service.

Free/native layer already covers discovery. The candidate paid layer is:
- complete pack/revision monitoring;
- exact requirement extraction;
- source-located evidence ledger;
- supplier capability/provenance join;
- fatal/curable gap routing;
- clarification/deadline/change control;
- independent decision audit;
- persistent rationale/history.

## Shortest route to useful proof
1. Obtain complete current official pack through authenticated/exportable route or user-provided authoritative files.
2. Obtain one real supplier capability packet with field-level provenance and appropriate privacy minimisation.
3. Run requirement join and produce BID/HOLD/NO-BID candidate rationale.
4. Run blind same-packet PA4 review.
5. Put the exact artifact in front of one real target supplier/bid manager and record before/after decision + time/error delta.
6. Only then test external commitment/WTP and a bounded paid pilot.
7. Only after observed price + time + variable cost calculate contribution margin/capacity.

## Stop conditions
Kill or reshape OP01 if any of the following repeats across real cases:
- complete substitute coverage leaves no material residual job;
- artifact fails to change decisions or materially reduce burden/errors;
- pack acquisition is operationally inaccessible at acceptable cost/latency;
- target supplier data cannot be maintained with acceptable privacy/compliance burden;
- real external users consistently reject commitment/payment at any viable delivery cost.

The next cycle is therefore evidence acquisition and real decision utility, not another broad market scan.
