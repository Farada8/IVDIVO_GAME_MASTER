# OP03 SAMPLE — RETROFIT LEAD QUALIFICATION / GRANT-READINESS PACK
**Status:** PUBLIC-RULE ARTIFACT / NOT AN SEAI ELIGIBILITY DETERMINATION
**As-of:** 2026-08-22
**Target:** retrofit contractor / One Stop Shop / lead qualification team.

## Qualification questions
1. Which route applies: homeowner/landlord/AHB/company/non-domestic?
2. For relevant home routes, was the dwelling built and occupied before 2011?
3. Have works already started?
4. Is SEAI grant approval in place before starting relevant works?
5. Is the installer/contractor SEAI-registered for the measure?
6. Single measure or whole-house One Stop Shop?
7. If OSS, can the scheme’s current post-works energy requirement be met, subject to professional assessment?
8. Has the same measure already received grant/EEOS support?
9. Missing evidence: MPRN, build/occupancy date, ownership, BER, technical assessment, quote, registered-provider route, tax clearance where relevant?
10. Traditional/pre-1940 building? If so route to Traditional Building Professional/appropriate OSS.
11. Non-domestic solar/business upgrade? Use separate non-domestic rules and registered-provider requirements.
12. Cash timing: grant deducted upfront through OSS, or paid/reimbursed after works/forms under the applicable route?

## Current official-source facts used
- SEAI individual home-grant routes require approval before starting relevant works and use registered contractors for applicable measures.
- A registered One Stop Shop can manage the complete upgrade; grants are deducted from the cost upfront under that route.
- Current One Stop Shop eligibility includes pre-2011 property criteria plus scheme-specific post-works energy requirements.
- Traditional/pre-1940 homes have a dedicated route requiring appropriate professional oversight.
- Non-domestic Solar PV/business supports are a separate route and use registered companies where required.

## Boundary
This routes evidence; it does not make an SEAI/legal eligibility guarantee, contractor price, or buyer-demand claim. All time-sensitive rules must pass freshness/supersession checks before reuse.
