# OP19 SAMPLE — AI WORKFLOW DIAGNOSTIC FOR A CONSTRUCTION SME
**Status:** PUBLIC-ARTIFACT CANARY / NO BUYER-DEMAND CLAIM
**As-of:** 2026-08-22
**Workflow:** public tender → screen → qualification evidence → bid calendar → document pack → owner decision.

|Stage|Manual burden|Failure mode|Candidate intervention|Human control|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|Alert intake|portal/email scanning|noise/missed deadlines|dedupe + status/deadline extraction|owner selects markets|
|First screen|reading summaries|attractive but ineligible bids|typed bid/no-bid checklist|bid manager approves|
|Qualification|hunting certificates/insurance/projects|late blocker discovery|evidence registry + expiry alerts|human validates docs|
|RFT review|repeated reading|lost requirements|anchored requirement extraction|no unsourced inference|
|Calendar|manual dates|clarification/submission miss|deadline scheduler|human confirms portal|
|Pack assembly|copy/paste|stale CV/cert/version|controlled manifest|human signs/submits|
|Decision|intuition|sunk-cost bidding|fit/capacity evidence + fatal overrides|owner decides|

## 30-day low-risk route
1. Instrument one live tender before buying/building a platform.
2. Measure alert→decision time and missing-document surprises.
3. Automate extraction, dedupe, deadlines and evidence expiry first.
4. Keep submission/commercial claims human-controlled.
5. Re-run on 3–5 tenders.
6. Decide only then whether a repeatable service/software product exists.

## Irish support context
Local Enterprise Office `Digital for Business` supports analysis of existing digital systems and implementation guidance. `Grow Digital` can support eligible new off-the-shelf software plus configuration/training for qualifying firms. Current official eligibility material includes analytics/AI systems among eligible software examples, while bespoke/custom software and regulatory-compliance systems are not assumed eligible.
