# Self-Improvement Findings from Real Public-Art Briefs

1. `OfficialBriefPassport` must precede concept generation: public-art commissions encode different engagement, insurance, site and scoring contracts.
2. Final-site uncertainty is not a defect. Clúain explicitly postpones final siting until after selection; engine must support `PARTIAL_SITE_AUTHORITY`.
3. Eligibility is applicant-specific evidence. A real open call does not prove a particular applicant is eligible/competitive.
4. Budget must compile commission-specific rules. Clúain is all-inclusive; Inis also imposes minimum 25% artist-fee allocation.
5. Submission completeness is machine-checkable because both briefs specify finite evidence packets.
6. Public opportunity evidence remains E2+/PA4-style public validation; it cannot become buyer interaction, award or willingness-to-pay.
7. Inis selection criteria can compile into a proposal-proof matrix; Clúain assessment headings compile into fatal-gated evidence checks rather than one magic score.
8. Site/design/technical proof remain independent: official location language is not structural/buildability proof.
9. Highest-value next data are applicant portfolio fit + site geometry/visit evidence + concept options + technical/cost evidence, not another broad opportunity scan.
10. Repeated NO-GO results are useful: the engine exposed missing deliverables instead of manufacturing a polished unsupported application.

**Self-Improvement disposition:** CAPTURE / CANDIDATE EVIDENCE ONLY. Two official briefs are not enough for global promotion.
