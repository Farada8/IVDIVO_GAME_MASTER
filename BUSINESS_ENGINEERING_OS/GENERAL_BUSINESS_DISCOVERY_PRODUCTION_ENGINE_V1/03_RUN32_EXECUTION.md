No market claim is created by this Run32. It validates the architecture and identifies where older heuristic behavior must be replaced with evidence-gated behavior.

## Main conclusions
1. The general engine must optimize **uncertainty reduction per euro/hour**, not prompt throughput.  
2. The engine needs one shared state machine, while vertical modules remain specialized.  
3. The strongest global invariant is `SIGNAL -> FATAL ASSUMPTION -> DECISIVE TEST -> OFFER -> EXTERNAL EVIDENCE -> TRANSACTION -> DELIVERY -> REPEATABILITY`.  
4. Unit economics, WTP, market proof and scalability must remain null/unknown until observed or bounded by explicit assumptions.  
5. A good business engine must be able to say **HOLD/KILL/DO NOT BUILD** as readily as GO.  
6. Self-improvement should reduce false positives, duplicated work and expensive experiments before attempting autonomous complexity.
