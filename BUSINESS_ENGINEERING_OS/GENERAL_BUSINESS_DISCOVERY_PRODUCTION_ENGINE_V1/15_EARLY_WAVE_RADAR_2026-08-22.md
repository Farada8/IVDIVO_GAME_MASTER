## Non-negotiable evidence boundary

This radar does **not** identify a guaranteed “next Bitcoin”. It identifies asymmetries where:
1. several independent structural signals are already observable;
2. entry can be tested cheaply and reversibly;
3. the founder can participate mostly remotely;
4. learning/assets can compound if the wave expands;
5. failure does not require material capital loss.

`EARLY_SIGNAL != GUARANTEED_WINNER`  
`HYPE != MARKET PROOF`  
`LARGE TAM CLAIM != ACCESSIBLE REVENUE`  
`PUBLIC REGULATION != OUR WTP`  
`PROTOCOL ADOPTION != OUR CUSTOMER`  
`CHEAP LEARNING OPTION > LARGE IRREVERSIBLE BET`

## Fresh signal set used in this pass

### S1 — AI Act transparency rules are live now
European Commission guidance published 20 July 2026; Article 50 transparency obligations apply from **2 August 2026**. Providers/deployers in scope may need user disclosures, labels and machine-readable marking for certain AI-generated/manipulated content. The Commission states enforcement began 2 August 2026.

Signal families: `REGULATORY_FORCING_FUNCTION + FRESH_GUIDANCE + LIVE_ENFORCEMENT`.

### S2 — Cyber Resilience Act reporting gate is imminent
European Commission CRA implementation material states reporting obligations begin **11 September 2026**. Manufacturers of products with digital elements must report actively exploited vulnerabilities and severe incidents through the Single Reporting Platform; Commission guidance and first standardisation work are arriving in 2026, while full CRA application is later.

Signal families: `REGULATORY_FORCING_FUNCTION + NEW_REPORTING_INFRASTRUCTURE + STANDARDS_FORMATION`.

### S3 — EU Digital Product Passport moved from concept to infrastructure
The European Commission launched the **Digital Product Passport Registry and testing environment in July 2026**. The ESPR 2025–2030 working plan prioritises textiles/apparel, furniture, tyres, mattresses, iron/steel and aluminium, plus horizontal repairability/recycled-content work.

Signal families: `REGULATORY_FORCING_FUNCTION + NEW_DIGITAL_INFRASTRUCTURE + SUPPLY_CHAIN_DATA_BOTTLENECK`.

### S4 — EU Digital Identity Wallet rollout is approaching
The EUDI framework requires Member States to provide wallets by the end of 2026. Commission material says more than 550 companies/public bodies have participated in pilots. Ireland is one of the seven frontrunner countries in the EU age-verification rollout. The Commission also states private companies may provide compatible age-verification apps.

Signal families: `PLATFORM_RAIL + STANDARDS_FORMATION + IMMINENT_ROLLOUT + LOCAL_IRELAND_SIGNAL`.

### S5 — European Business Wallet is still pre-wave
The Commission proposed European Business Wallets in November 2025; 2026 technical work is defining secure communications, identity/access, discovery and interoperability. Commission roadmap targets political agreement by end-2026. EU Inc. proposals explicitly contemplate Business Wallet use for digital company operations.

Signal families: `POLICY_PRECURSOR + TECHNICAL_STANDARD_FORMATION + FUTURE_ADMINISTRATION_RAIL`.

### S6 — Agentic commerce is becoming real infrastructure, not one-vendor hype
Independent infrastructure moves now converge:
- Google AP2 is an open agent-payment protocol; in April 2026 Google donated AP2 to FIDO and released v0.2 with autonomous “Human Not Present” support.
- Visa announced a strategic OpenAI collaboration in June 2026 for secure agentic commerce.
- Mastercard launched Agent Pay for Machines in June 2026 with more than 30 initial ecosystem supporters.
- A2A reported 150+ supporting organisations and production use in April 2026.
- MCP was donated to the Linux Foundation’s Agentic AI Foundation after broad cross-platform adoption; OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Microsoft, AWS and others support the foundation.
- Linux Foundation launched DNS-AID in May 2026 for decentralised discovery/verification of agents and MCP servers.

Signal families: `PLATFORM_RAIL + OPEN_STANDARD + PAYMENT_RAIL + ENTERPRISE_DEPLOYMENT + DISCOVERY_BOTTLENECK + MULTI_VENDOR_CONVERGENCE`.

## CF-01 — AI Act Article 50 Transparency Implementation Pack
**Wave stage:** `EARLY_COMMERCIAL / LIVE FORCING FUNCTION`  
**Founder cash:** EUR0–300 planning range before any external sales spend.  
**Remote:** FULL.  
**Founder physical load:** NONE.  
**Why now:** obligations started 2 August 2026 and detailed Commission guidance is fresh.  
**Offer shape (non-legal):** AI-system/content inventory; disclosure/label placement map; machine-readable-marking workflow inventory; chatbot disclosure UX; provenance/evidence folder; implementation checklist; regression test after changes.

## CF-02 — CRA Incident-Reporting Readiness Operations
**Wave stage:** `EARLY_COMMERCIAL / DEADLINE-DRIVEN`  
**Founder cash:** EUR0–300 before specialist review.  
**Remote:** FULL.  
**Why now:** CRA reporting obligations begin 11 September 2026; the Single Reporting Platform is being operationalised now.  
**Offer shape (bounded):** incident evidence capture workflow; 24h/72h/final-report routing clock; role matrix; source-of-truth log; notification packet assembler; dry-run exercise; supplier/dependency contact inventory.  
**Boundary:** security assessment, vulnerability validation, conformity and legal interpretation require competent specialists. This is operational/documentation tooling, not cybersecurity certification.  
**Compounding asset:** reusable incident/evidence orchestration engine applicable to other regulated reporting.  
**Fatal unknown:** credibility barrier and whether target manufacturers already have MSSP/legal coverage.  
**Cheapest test:** create a synthetic incident dry run and have an independent cybersecurity practitioner red-team the operational packet before any market use.

# OPTIONALITY NEXT — EARLY WAVE RADAR

## OW-01 — Agentic Commerce Merchant Readiness Layer — **PRIMARY EARLY-WAVE CANDIDATE**
**Wave stage:** `EARLY_COMMERCIAL`  
**Remote:** FULL.  
**Founder cash at risk:** EUR0–500 for learning/prototype; no paid acquisition required for first proof.  
**Founder physical load:** NONE.  
**Independent signal count:** HIGH (OpenAI/Visa, Google/FIDO/AP2, Mastercard, A2A, MCP/AAIF, DNS-AID).  
**Why now:** commerce is shifting from “human browses website and clicks buy” toward software agents discovering, comparing, authorising and purchasing. Multiple competing protocols are appearing before a single dominant implementation pattern has solidified.  
**New bottleneck:** merchants can have products and checkout systems that work for humans but are poorly structured, discoverable, permissioned or testable for agents.

### Possible low-capital business wedge
A vendor-neutral `AGENTIC COMMERCE READINESS` service/tool for SMEs and ecommerce operators:
1. merchant/catalog machine-readability audit;
2. agent discovery/readiness audit;
3. product/availability/returns/fulfilment data quality;
4. checkout/authentication boundary map;
5. ACP/AP2/UCP-style compatibility inventory where relevant;
6. agent-safe policies and deterministic transaction constraints;
7. synthetic-agent test harness and failure report;
8. later: connectors/adapters rather than replacing payment processors.

### What compounds if early
- compatibility test corpus;
- merchant integration templates;
- protocol adapters;
- vertical-specific schemas;
- benchmark dataset of agent failure modes;
- reputation/SEO-like positioning around “agent discoverability/readiness” before terminology stabilises.

### Why this has better asymmetry than generic dropshipping
The bet is not on owning inventory. It is on a **new distribution rail**. If agentic shopping becomes a material channel, every merchant may need some version of readiness/discovery/transaction compatibility. If it fails, the prototype effort still transfers to structured commerce data, APIs and automation.

### Fatal thesis killers
- dominant platforms make merchant integration completely automatic/free;
- protocols converge in a way that removes external implementation work;
- consumers/enterprises do not adopt agent-led purchasing at meaningful rates;
- existing ecommerce platforms solve the readiness layer before independents can build distribution.

### Cheapest reversible test
Build a public synthetic merchant fixture and a protocol-neutral `Agent Commerce Readiness Scanner v0` that reports structured data, agent discoverability, transaction boundary and policy gaps. Do not sell or spend yet. Measure whether it finds non-trivial failures across 10 public merchant fixtures.
