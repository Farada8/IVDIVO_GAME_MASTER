## P41 — Route comparison

Route labels are hypotheses, not observed acquisition channels.

### OW-01 — Agentic Commerce corrected diagnostic

- **Direct:** TEST CANDIDATE. Best-fit buyer role is a technical owner of a custom/headless or non-managed commerce stack with protocol/version compatibility risk. Advantage: problem and artifact can be discussed technically. Risk: founder must not drift back into generic “AI-ready ecommerce” consulting.
- **Referral:** TEST CANDIDATE. Referral source hypothesis: commerce engineering consultants or implementation specialists who encounter protocol drift but do not maintain independent conformance tooling.
- **Partner:** TEST CANDIDATE. Partner hypothesis: headless-commerce/system-integration teams needing an independent regression/conformance layer. Must not become white-label generic setup work.
- **Tender:** HOLD for first test. Slow cycle and procurement overhead are disproportionate before WTP proof.
- **Marketplace:** HOLD/KILL for first test. Managed-platform marketplaces bias toward commoditized setup services already weakened by platform-native UCP/default capabilities.
- **Inbound:** HOLD. Useful only after evidence exists; content traffic is not substitute for buyer evidence.

### CF-01 — Article 50 technical transparency diagnostic

- **Direct:** TEST CANDIDATE. Buyer-role hypothesis: technical/compliance owner at an AI provider or deployer with Article 50 implementation responsibility.
- **Referral:** TEST CANDIDATE. Referral hypothesis: legal/privacy/AI-governance advisers who need technical evidence objects but should retain legal interpretation.
- **Partner:** PRIMARY HYPOTHESIS. A technical evidence pack is complementary to legal/governance work and should remain explicitly non-legal/non-certifying.
- **Tender:** HOLD. Do not infer public-sector budget from regulatory applicability.
- **Marketplace:** HOLD. Generic compliance marketplaces risk collapsing a bounded technical diagnostic into unsupported compliance claims.
- **Inbound:** SECONDARY/HOLD until one real buyer problem is observed.

### CF-03 — DPP supplier-data / Registry readiness diagnostic

## P47 — Sample and stopping rule

If later authorized, experiments run sequentially in the current General Business order: `OW-01 -> CF-01 -> CF-03`.

Per lane maximum sample: **12 qualified targets**, staged `4 + 4 + 4`.

After each stage of four:

- remove invalid/wrong-role targets from the denominator and replace only within the 12-target cap;
- record actual delivered messages separately from prepared targets;
- do not change CTA mid-stage;
- do not reinterpret silence as rejection or demand.

Stopping rules:

- **SAFETY STOP:** complaint, legal/compliance uncertainty about outreach method, privacy issue, misrepresentation risk, or authority drift -> stop immediately.
- **WRONG-MARKET STOP:** more than half of attempted targets fail the predeclared qualification spec -> stop and repair targeting before further contact.
- **LOW-SIGNAL HOLD:** after 8 valid delivered messages, zero qualified replies -> hold; do not scale volume automatically. This is low signal, not proof of no market.
- **CONTINUE TO 12:** at least one qualified reply or materially useful negative/substitution evidence appears by 8, and no safety stop exists.
- **COMMERCIAL ADVANCE:** only an E7+ event allows a pricing/commercial follow-up experiment; only E9 is transaction evidence.

No statistical market-size claim is permitted from this sample.
