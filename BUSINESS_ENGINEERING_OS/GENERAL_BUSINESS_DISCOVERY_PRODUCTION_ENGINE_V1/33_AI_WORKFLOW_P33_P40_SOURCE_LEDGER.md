# P33–P40 TRADE PREQUAL WORKFLOW — SOURCE LEDGER

Observed: **2026-08-22**

## Free / DIY substitute
Google Forms Help: `https://support.google.com/docs/answer/6281888?hl=en`
- Forms can collect structured responses;
- responses can be stored in Google Sheets;
- forms can be shared/published for respondents.

Google Forms product: `https://workspace.google.com/intl/en_ie/products/forms/`
- user-friendly forms, cross-device responses, response analysis and export to Sheets.

## SaaS-native substitute
Tradify Ireland: `https://www.tradifyhq.com/ie`
Tradify job tracking / enquiries: `https://www.tradifyhq.com/ie/features/job-tracking-software`
Tradify estimating: `https://www.tradifyhq.com/ie/features/estimating-software`
Tradify price lists: `https://www.tradifyhq.com/ie/features/price-list-management-software`

Observed capabilities include enquiries, customer records, quoting/estimating, price lists/templates, jobs, invoices and follow-up. Therefore a generic CRM/quote application is not a credible differentiated wedge for this run.

## Publicly funded substitute / complement
LEO Digital for Business: `https://www.localenterprise.ie/portal/digital/the-digital-for-business-support.html`

Observed scope includes:
- analysis of existing digital systems;
- identification of gaps;
- optimisation/integration of solutions;
- guidance on new/enhanced digital implementation;
- paperwork reduction, CRM integration and process streamlining.

This makes generic “digital transformation advice” a weak paid proposition unless the implementation layer creates a measurable incremental result.

## Evidence boundary
Vendor claims about time saving or ratings are not used as proof of our workflow economics.
`COMPETITOR_FEATURE != BUYER_WTP_FOR_OUR_OFFER`.
`PUBLIC_SUPPORT_AVAILABILITY != NO_COMMERCIAL_WEDGE`, but it requires a free/public-alternative substitution check.
`SYNTHETIC_BASELINE != OBSERVED_OPERATOR_TIME`.
