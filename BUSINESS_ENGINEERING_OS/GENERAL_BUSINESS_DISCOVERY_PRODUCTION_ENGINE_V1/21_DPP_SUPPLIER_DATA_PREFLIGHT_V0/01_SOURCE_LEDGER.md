# P-EW04 DPP SOURCE LEDGER

**Date:** 2026-08-22  
**Authority preference:** European Commission / EUR-Lex primary sources.

## SRC-DPP-01 — European Commission: DPP Registry live
URL: https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/news/digital-product-passport-registry-now-live-2026-07-20_en

Used for:
- Registry + testing environment launched 20 July 2026;
- DPP detailed product information is decentralised;
- economic operators register DPP unique identifiers and associated metadata;
- six DPP standards were already published at launch;
- first implementation deadline referenced by Commission: 18 February 2027 for certain large batteries.

## SRC-DPP-02 — European Commission: DPP Registry
URL: https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/single-market/digital-product-passport/dpp-registry_en

Used for:
- Registry acts as indexing service;
- stores unique identifiers, registration data and high-level metadata, not the full detailed product information;
- exact additional Registry information may depend on applicable delegated acts / Union legislation;
- DPP detailed data remains under responsibility of economic operators / DPP service providers;
- test environment and economic-operator user guide are available.

## SRC-DPP-03 — EUR-Lex: Regulation (EU) 2024/1781 (ESPR), Annex III
URL: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?uri=OJ:L_202401781

Used for:
- candidate categories of DPP data that product-specific delegated acts may specify;
- examples include unique product identifier, GTIN/equivalent, commodity codes, compliance documentation, manufacturer/economic-operator identifiers, facility identifiers, importer/responsible economic operator information and DPP service-provider reference;
- generic Annex III categories are not treated as a universal mandatory field list for every product.

## SRC-DPP-04 — European Commission: Digital Product Passport overview/timeline
URL: https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/single-market/digital-product-passport_en

Used for:
- phased product-group implementation;
- furniture is a priority group under ESPR planning but that priority is not treated as proof of a current mandatory DPP for this synthetic furniture fixture;
- registry operational milestone 20 July 2026;
- product-specific requirements continue to arrive by delegated acts / sector legislation.

## Evidence boundary
This ledger supports an engineering data-readiness sample only. It does not establish legal applicability for a real customer/product, registry enrolment success, conformity, certification, buyer demand, WTP or profitability.
