## Evidence-safe interpretation of P13

A public company, directory entry or reachable website is a **SCREENING CANDIDATE**, not a verified buyer. Therefore P13 is implemented as a 10-slot reachable screening queue per lane with a public access path and qualification gate. A slot becomes `VERIFIED_BUYER_ROLE` only after first-party organizational/role evidence; it becomes demand evidence only after behavior.

`PUBLIC_SCREENING_CANDIDATE != BUYER != BUDGET_OWNER != DEMAND`.

## P09 — user / buyer / budget owner separation

### OPP-33 — SME Business Retrofit Grant-Ready Delivery / Partner Network
- **User:** person doing the project work inside the SME — owner-manager, facilities/operations lead, property manager or administrator.
- **Buyer:** person authorized to engage a coordinator/contractor — often owner/MD/GM in small firms, but must be verified per case.
- **Budget owner:** person controlling capital/maintenance spend — owner, finance lead/CFO, board or landlord/tenant depending on premises arrangement.
- **Influencers/gates:** SEAI-registered company/installer, energy auditor/engineer where relevant, landlord, accountant, lender, insurer.
- **Critical rule:** occupant != building owner != buyer. Tenure and authority must be explicit before a retrofit recommendation is commercialized.

### OPP-36 — AI Transparency Implementation Pack
- **User:** staff operating the AI-enabled workflow: customer service, marketing, sales, operations, product or IT.
- **Buyer:** MD/CEO/COO/Head of Operations/Head of Digital who can commission implementation support.
- **Budget owner:** owner/finance/department head controlling digital/compliance/consulting spend.
- **Reviewer/gate:** legal/DPO/compliance specialist where interpretation is required. Reviewer is not automatically the buyer.
- **Critical rule:** vendor user != deployer legal role; role classification requires evidence and cannot be inferred from product marketing alone.

### OPP-37 — Tourism AI Discoverability & Bookability
- **User:** owner-manager, reservations/front desk, marketing/e-commerce staff, or external web/booking vendor who acts on findings.
- **Buyer:** owner/GM/commercial manager responsible for direct demand and booking performance.
- **Budget owner:** owner/GM/finance or marketing budget holder.
- **Influencers/gates:** web agency, booking-engine provider, OTA/channel manager, destination body/Fáilte supports.
- **Critical rule:** website visitor != operator user; OTA customer != operator buyer.

## P14 — non-leading interview / observation protocol

### Core rules
- Ask about the last real event, not hypothetical willingness.
- Prefer artifacts/screens/data over opinions.
- Never begin with the proposed solution or price.
- Ask what they did, who approved it, what it cost in time/process, and what happened next.
- “Would you buy/pay?” is not accepted as primary evidence.

### Shared core questions
1. “Tell me about the last time this problem actually occurred.”
2. “What triggered you to act, if anything?”
3. “What did you do first, second and third?”
4. “Who else had to approve or supply information?”
5. “What documents/tools/providers did you use?”
6. “Where did the process stop or get repeated?”
7. “What did you do instead when it did not move?”
8. “What evidence would you need before taking the next step?”
9. “Can you show the artifact/data/process you used?”
10. “What happened after the last attempt?”

Lane-specific observation:
- OPP-33: map last building/energy project, tenure, quotes, grant step, installer/engineer dependencies.
- OPP-36: inventory actual AI systems, customer touchpoints, disclosures, owners, vendor controls and unresolved actions; do not ask for legal conclusions.
- OPP-37: observe real discovery-to-booking path, analytics if voluntarily supplied, website/booking/OTA control boundaries.

## P15 — behavioral evidence stronger than verbal interest

Evidence ladder is ordinal, not a magic score:

`E0_SCREENING -> E1_STATED_PROBLEM -> E2_ARTIFACT_OR_DATA_RELEASE -> E3_RESOURCE_COMMITMENT -> E4_COMMERCIAL_COMMITMENT -> E5_TRANSACTION -> E6_REPEAT`.

Examples:
- **E0:** public fit signal only. Not demand.
- **E1:** describes a real recent problem/process. Still not behavior sufficient for fatal-test PASS.
- **E2:** supplies building facts, current quote/report, AI-system inventory, analytics, access screenshot or process artifact.
- **E3:** schedules site/review session, assigns internal owner, approves a change, requests partner introduction/implementation, gives bounded system/access cooperation.
- **E4:** requests quote/proposal, agrees defined paid pilot/deposit terms, signs non-binding/qualified intent where appropriate. Intent != cash.
- **E5:** deposit/payment/binding order with provenance.
- **E6:** repeat/renewal/referral or comparable second transaction.

Fatal tests 33/36/37 require at least predeclared E2/E3-style behavior; positive words alone cannot pass them.

### Contradiction rules
1. Newer first-party observed behavior outranks older stated intent for the same claim.
2. Transaction provenance outranks meeting/reply sentiment; it does not prove repeatability.
3. A contradiction never disappears through averaging; it creates `CONTRADICTED` until resolved.
4. Public screening evidence can establish candidate existence/access path only, not buyer role or demand.
5. Unverified role => budget authority UNKNOWN.
6. Stale time-sensitive source cannot be silently reused.
7. Missing evidence stays NULL/UNKNOWN; do not impute.
8. One organization cannot be counted twice through multiple contacts for the same behavior event.
9. Vendor marketing claims cannot prove end-customer outcomes.
10. Interview summary must link to raw notes/artifact provenance before promotion above E1.
