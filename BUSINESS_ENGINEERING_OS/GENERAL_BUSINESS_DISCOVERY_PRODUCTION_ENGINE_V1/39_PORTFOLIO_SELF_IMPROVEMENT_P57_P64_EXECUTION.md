## P62 — Systematic error detection

Detected recurring error classes:

1. **Artifact optimism** — existence of an asset/tool is mistaken for deployable/retail/paid readiness.
2. **Public-reference substitution** — public price/rate is mistaken for actual transaction cost.
3. **Engineering-to-market promotion** — a passing internal mechanism is mistaken for WTP/repeatability.
4. **Platform/default blindness** — native capability is checked too late, after a generic wedge is already imagined.
5. **Unknown compression** — missing evidence is pressured into PASS/FAIL to complete a workflow.
6. **Authority staleness** — parallel `main` work can invalidate a branch assumption before merge.
7. **Milestone CI freezing** — old workflows encode an exact global progress count and then break legitimate future work.
8. **Scope creep by adjacency** — technical evidence packs drift toward legal/compliance/certification claims unless explicitly prohibited.
