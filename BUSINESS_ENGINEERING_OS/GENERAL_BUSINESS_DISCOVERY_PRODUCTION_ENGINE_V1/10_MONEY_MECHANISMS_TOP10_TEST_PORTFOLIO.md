## Explicitly demoted

### Generic non-EU dropshipping
`M11 = MUTATE`, not a default first test.
Reason: from 1 July 2026 Ireland applies €3 customs duty per item line on low-value non-EU e-commerce packages; VAT/IOSS, delivery, returns and commodity competition add further friction. It can survive only with sufficient margin/differentiation, but EU-stock fulfilment is the cleaner initial experiment.

### Private label
`M15 = HOLD_SEQUENCE` until a product shows repeat sales under dropship/small-batch fulfilment.

### Micro-SaaS
`M23 = TEST_AFTER_PROBLEM`; concierge/manual proof before code.

### Equipment purchase / asset-as-service
`M29/M30 = TEST_BEFORE_ASSET`; utilisation evidence before capex.

### Small-business acquisition
`M31 = WATCH_CAPITAL`; potentially attractive, but not the fastest €0–€5k experiment.
