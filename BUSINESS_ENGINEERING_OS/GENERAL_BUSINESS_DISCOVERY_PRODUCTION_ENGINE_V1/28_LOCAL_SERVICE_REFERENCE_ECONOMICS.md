## Labour sensitivity
Two-person crew, public references only:
- €350/day crew × 3–5 days = €1,050–€1,750;
- €500/day crew × 3–5 days = €1,500–€2,500.

If instead the public individual-painter range €200–€350/day is applied to two people:
- 2 × €200 × 3–5 days = €1,200–€2,000;
- 2 × €350 × 3–5 days = €2,100–€3,500.

The spread is too wide to infer subcontract cost. This is precisely why a scope-equivalent quote is still required.

## Business consequence
Low-price subcontract arbitrage is fragile. A route becomes more plausible only if one or more are true:
1. verified subcontract cost is materially below retail reference while quality/insurance/defect responsibility are acceptable;
2. the offer earns a premium through fixed scope, preparation quality, scheduling, warranty/closeout and buyer convenience;
3. self-delivery has better contribution than subcontracting;
4. acquisition can be monetized as referral/lead-only when delivery margin is insufficient.
