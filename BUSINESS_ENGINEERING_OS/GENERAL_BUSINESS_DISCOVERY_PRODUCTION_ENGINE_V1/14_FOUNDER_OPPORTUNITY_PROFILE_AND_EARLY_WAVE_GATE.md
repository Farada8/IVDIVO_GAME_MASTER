# FOUNDER OPPORTUNITY PROFILE + EARLY WAVE / ASYMMETRIC UPSIDE GATE

**Date:** 2026-08-22  
**Status:** FOUNDER-DIRECTED CORRECTION / MUST PRECEDE PORTFOLIO DEEP-DIVE  
**Scope:** General Business Discovery & Production Engine

## Why this gate exists
Recent Business Engineering work over-weighted opportunities that were easy to verify publicly, especially procurement, while under-weighting the Founder’s actual operating constraints and strategic objective.

The corrected order is:

`FOUNDER FIT -> EARLY-WAVE FIT -> CHEAP FALSIFICATION -> PORTFOLIO SELECTION -> ONLY THEN DEEP DIVE`

A detailed, official or large opportunity is not automatically a good opportunity.

## Founder operating profile
### Capital
- Preferred initial founder cash: **EUR 0–500**.
- Hard default ceiling for a new experiment/opportunity: **EUR 3,000**.
- Anything above EUR 3,000 is `OUT_OF_PROFILE` unless the founder’s own irreversible exposure remains <= EUR 3,000 because the activity is customer-funded, partner-funded, pre-sold, commission-based, brokered, subcontracted, financed without personal exposure, or otherwise structurally de-risked.
- Contract face value does not matter by itself; what matters is founder cash at risk, working-capital exposure and delivery obligation.

### Delivery model
- **REMOTE-FIRST**.
- Preferred founder physical participation: **zero or near-zero**.
- Prefer work deliverable from laptop/home/online.
- Physical fulfilment is acceptable only when it is optional, occasional, or executed by a partner/subcontractor while the company provides design, research, coordination, sourcing, brokerage, sales, documentation, automation, management or IP.
- Avoid business models that require the Founder to personally work on-site as the core production unit.

### Infrastructure burden
Prefer opportunities that do not require, before proof:
- premises/warehouse;
- vehicles;
- expensive equipment;
- inventory ownership;
- payroll-heavy team;
- large insurance/credential stack;
- long receivables financed from founder cash;
- regulated specialist liability that the company cannot currently carry.

## Strategic objective: find early waves
The engine must not only ask “what can make money today?” It must continuously monitor **newly forming markets, technologies, regulations, distribution channels, cost collapses and behavioural shifts** where acting early can create disproportionate optionality later.

The target pattern is analogous to the economic logic of buying/participating very early in a later-large wave, not a claim that any current asset or technology is “the next Bitcoin”.

Core law:

`EARLY_SIGNAL != GUARANTEED_WINNER`

`HYPE != ADMISSIBLE EARLY-WAVE EVIDENCE`

`ASYMMETRIC_UPSIDE_REQUIRES_CAPPED_DOWNSIDE`

## Wave-stage classification
Every discovery candidate must be tagged:
- `PRE_WAVE`: technical/regulatory/social precursor exists but commercial adoption is not yet demonstrated.
- `EMERGING`: multiple independent signals show formation; low competition/low awareness remains plausible.
- `EARLY_COMMERCIAL`: first real buyers/revenues/deployments exist, market structure still forming.
- `EXPANDING`: adoption is visibly growing and competitors are entering.
- `CROWDED`: common knowledge, high competition, acquisition costs/credential burden rising.
- `LATE_COMMODITIZED`: price competition and low differentiation dominate.

Default preference:
`EMERGING > EARLY_COMMERCIAL > PRE_WAVE > EXPANDING > CROWDED > LATE_COMMODITIZED`

`PRE_WAVE` can outrank later stages only when the test is extremely cheap, learning is transferable and downside is tightly capped.

## Early-wave signal families
The monitoring engine should look for intersections of at least two independent signal families:
1. **Capability threshold crossing** — a technology suddenly becomes good/cheap/fast enough for practical use.
2. **Cost collapse** — inference, compute, sensors, production, logistics, software or content-generation costs fall by an order large enough to unlock a new business model.
3. **New platform/distribution rail** — APIs, marketplaces, app ecosystems, payment rails, identity rails, agent protocols, new ad/discovery channels.
4. **Regulatory forcing function** — a new obligation creates mandatory work, documentation, data, verification, adaptation or tooling demand.
5. **Subsidy/budget creation** — new public/private budget appears before supplier capacity matures.
6. **Standards/infrastructure formation** — interoperability or standards reduce adoption friction.
7. **Developer/open-source acceleration** — rapid tool/library/repository growth around a new capability.
8. **Enterprise experimentation becoming deployment** — pilots convert into recurring operational budgets.
9. **Behavioural migration** — users start changing how they search, buy, create, communicate, work or learn.
10. **New bottleneck** — one new technology removes an old bottleneck and creates a different scarce service around integration, verification, orchestration, data, trust, rights, support or distribution.
11. **Supply scarcity** — demand is visible but competent suppliers are still few.
12. **Cross-domain spillover** — a capability proven in one industry starts entering another before established vendors adapt.

One article, one influencer claim, one price spike or one vendor launch is insufficient by itself.

## Asymmetric-upside vector
Do not collapse to one magic score. Track:
- `FOUNDER_CASH_AT_RISK_EUR`
- `REMOTE_DELIVERABILITY`: FULL / MOSTLY / PARTIAL / LOW
- `FOUNDER_PHYSICAL_LOAD`: NONE / LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH
- `TIME_TO_FIRST_TEST`
- `TIME_TO_OPTIONALITY`
- `WAVE_STAGE`
- `INDEPENDENT_SIGNAL_COUNT`
- `COMPETITION_DENSITY`: LOW / FORMING / MEDIUM / HIGH / UNKNOWN
- `MARKET_EXPANSION_MECHANISM`
- `MARGINAL_COST_LEVERAGE`
- `AI_AUTOMATION_LEVERAGE`
- `IP_OR_DATA_ACCUMULATION`
- `RECURRING_REVENUE_POTENTIAL`
- `GLOBAL_REACH_POTENTIAL`
- `TRANSFERABLE_LEARNING_VALUE`
- `DOWNSIDE_CAP`
- `UPSIDE_OPTIONALITY`: LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH / UNKNOWN
- `NEXT_CHEAPEST_DECISIVE_TEST`

Unknowns remain UNKNOWN; the engine must not manufacture upside numbers.

## Mandatory pre-deep-dive gates
Before a tender, product, service, acquisition, investment-like opportunity or project receives a deep analysis, answer:

1. `REMOTE_FIRST_PASS?`
2. `FOUNDER_CASH_AT_RISK <= EUR 3,000?`
3. `FOUNDER_PHYSICAL_LOAD <= LOW?` (preference; MEDIUM requires unusually strong reason, HIGH is normally reject)
4. `CAN_TEST_BEFORE_BUILD/SPEND?`
5. `WAVE_STAGE?`
6. `WHY_NOW?` — what changed recently that makes this possible or urgent?
7. `WHAT_COMPOUNDS_IF_EARLY?` — data, audience, brand, relationships, IP, distribution, rank, learning, scarce capability, installed base, optionality?
8. `WHAT_IS_THE_CHEAPEST_WAY_TO_BE_WRONG?`
9. `WHAT_SIGNAL_WOULD KILL THE THESIS?`
10. `IS_THIS ALREADY CROWDED/COMMODITIZED?`

If these questions cannot be answered, deep-dive is blocked.

## Routing dispositions
- `EARLY_WAVE_PRIORITY`: strong founder fit + EMERGING/EARLY_COMMERCIAL + capped downside + real compounding mechanism.
- `CHEAP_OPTIONALITY_WATCH`: PRE_WAVE with very cheap reversible probe.
- `NORMAL_CASHFLOW_CANDIDATE`: fits founder constraints but has no early-wave asymmetry; may still be useful for cash generation.
- `PARTNER_OR_BROKER_ONLY`: attractive market but physical/capital delivery should be externalized.
- `OUT_OF_PROFILE_CAPITAL`: founder exposure > EUR 3,000 without structural de-risking.
- `OUT_OF_PROFILE_PHYSICAL`: founder physical labour is core delivery burden.
- `TOO_LATE_OR_CROWDED`: weak differentiation and no special access/edge.
- `HYPE_ONLY`: insufficient independent evidence.

## Existing WIP correction
Existing General Business WIP is **not grandfathered**.

- Large construction procurement such as Ballybunion is not a PRIMARY business opportunity under this profile; it remains a procurement engineering/reference fixture unless a bounded remote/broker/subcontracting opportunity exists inside it.
- Direct physical painting/EWI/roofing is not a preferred strategic lane because founder physical participation is high; it may remain ordinary cashflow/reference activity but should not dominate discovery.
- EU dropshipping, POD/digital commerce, AI workflow services, paid intelligence, brokerage, sourcing, lead generation and other remote mechanisms must be re-screened for wave stage, crowding, capital-at-risk and compounding advantage before being called PRIMARY.

## Continuous radar law
The Business Engine should maintain a separate **EARLY WAVE RADAR** from ordinary near-term cashflow discovery.

Two outputs are required:
1. `CASHFLOW NOW` — realistic low-capital remote opportunities that can be tested quickly.
2. `OPTIONALITY NEXT` — emerging opportunities where a small action now may create a valuable position later.

Do not force the same candidate to satisfy both roles.

## Evidence boundary
This gate changes discovery/routing logic only. It does not prove demand, WTP, revenue, profitability, investment return, legal eligibility or that any monitored wave will succeed.

No autonomous spend, asset purchase, speculation, outreach, listing, contract acceptance or investment action is authorized by this artifact.
