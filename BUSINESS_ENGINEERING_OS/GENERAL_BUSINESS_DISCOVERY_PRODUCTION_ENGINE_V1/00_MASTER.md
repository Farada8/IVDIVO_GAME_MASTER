# IVDIVO GENERAL BUSINESS DISCOVERY & PRODUCTION ENGINE v1

**Date:** 2026-08-22
**Scope:** top-level Business Engineering orchestrator across procurement, construction/retrofit, AI implementation, creative/design, hospitality, digital products, services, acquisitions and other opportunity lanes.
**Status:** ENGINEERING CANDIDATE / NO NEW MARKET PROOF / NO AUTONOMOUS OUTREACH

## Purpose
Turn a raw business idea or current market signal into a controlled path toward a real offer, buyer interaction, transaction evidence, repeatability and portfolio learning.

This engine does not replace existing verticals. It routes them.

Upstream authority already available:
- Cycle4 Live Opportunities B113–B144: signal taxonomy, buyer workload, budget owner, fatal assumptions, VOI experiments, cash topology, CREATE/BROKER/ACQUIRE, portfolio/red-team.
- Current Business evidence laws: K != S != E; public signal != buyer proof; economics and WTP remain null until observed.
- Existing procurement vertical remains one channel only.

## Canonical state machine
`RESTORE -> DISCOVER -> QUALIFY -> PROBLEM -> VALUE -> OFFER -> ECONOMICS -> EXPERIMENT -> SALES_GATE -> TRANSACTION -> DELIVERY -> RETENTION -> SCALE -> LEARN`

Every transition is evidence-gated. Prompt count never substitutes for evidence.

## Evidence boundary
- K: knowledge/source quality.
- S: current public/market signal.
- E: external market evidence.
- PA: artifact maturity where relevant.

`K != S != E != CASH`.

A source can improve reasoning; it cannot prove demand.
A public signal can justify investigation; it cannot prove willingness to pay.
A polished offer can improve conversion; it cannot prove a transaction.

## Portfolio rule
Maximum active WIP = 3:
- 1 PRIMARY lane;
- up to 2 PILOTS.

All other opportunities remain WATCH / HOLD / KILL / PARK.

## Current production objective
Move from opportunity intelligence to a general business production loop:
`OpportunityObject -> BuyerProblem -> FatalAssumption -> CheapestDecisiveTest -> OfferPrototype -> EconomicsModel -> SalesExperiment -> EvidenceLedger -> Scale/Hold/Kill`.

## External-action boundary
The engine may prepare research, artifacts, offers, scripts, pricing hypotheses, experiment plans and decision packets.
It may not autonomously contact buyers, submit tenders, accept contracts, incur spend or represent legal/tax/professional compliance without explicit authorization.
