### European Commission — Digital Product Passport Registry
Source: https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/news/digital-product-passport-registry-now-live-2026-07-20_en

Use boundary: confirms operational infrastructure and timing only. It does not prove that a specific company needs or will pay for a readiness diagnostic.

### European Commission — Digital Product Passport for Batteries
Source: https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/single-market/digital-product-passport/batteries_en

Use boundary: informs target-role/specification logic. Sector membership alone is not enough to infer applicability.
