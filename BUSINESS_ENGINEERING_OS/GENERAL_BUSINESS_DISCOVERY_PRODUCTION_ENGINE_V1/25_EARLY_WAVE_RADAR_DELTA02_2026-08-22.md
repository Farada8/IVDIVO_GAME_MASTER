**Stage:** `EMERGING_TO_EARLY_COMMERCIAL_INFRASTRUCTURE`  
**Route:** `OPTIONALITY_NEXT_PRIORITY`

### Fresh signal stack
1. Linux Foundation launched DNS-AID on 27 May 2026 for decentralized publication, discovery and verification of AI agents and MCP servers through DNS.
2. Linux Foundation announced Agent Name Service (ANS) on 23 June 2026 for portable trusted identity, verification and discovery of agents.
3. A2A passed 150 supporting organizations and reported production use by April 2026, with registry/testing/discovery work on its roadmap.
4. Agentic AI Foundation reached 247 organizations on 13 August 2026 after adding 57 members in three months, including finance and enterprise participants.

First-party sources:
- https://www.linuxfoundation.org/press/linux-foundation-announces-dns-aid-project-to-advance-decentralized-ai-agent-discovery
- https://www.linuxfoundation.org/press/linux-foundation-announces-intent-to-launch-agent-name-service-to-establish-trusted-identity-infrastructure-for-ai-agents
- https://www.linuxfoundation.org/press/a2a-protocol-surpasses-150-organizations-lands-in-major-cloud-platforms-and-sees-enterprise-production-use-in-first-year
- https://www.linuxfoundation.org/press/agentic-ai-foundation-welcomes-57-new-members-gaining-major-financial-services-players-and-apac-leaders

### Testable low-capital wedges
- agent/DNS discovery publication validator;
- ANS/DNS-AID metadata readiness checks;
- A2A/MCP discovery + identity compatibility matrix;
- trust/discovery drift monitoring across changing standards;
- agent identity manifest/test harness for software teams.

### Why it may compound
A reusable validation corpus, protocol adapters, compatibility fixtures and migration history can become an asset even if individual protocols change.

### Kill / mutation conditions
- major DNS/cloud platforms make all relevant publication/verification conformance automatic with no cross-vendor gap;
- standards fragment without real production convergence;
- discovery/identity moves behind proprietary registries with no economically useful open interoperability layer;
- buyer pain remains invisible after bounded evidence testing.

## EW-D02-02 — AI Tokenomics / Cost-to-Value Instrumentation — 87/100
**Stage:** `EMERGING_EARLY_COMMERCIAL`  
**Route:** `CASHFLOW_BRIDGE_PRIORITY + OPTIONALITY_NEXT`  
**WIP:** `RADAR_ONLY`

### Fresh signal stack
Linux Foundation launched the Tokenomics Foundation on 4 August 2026 with 30 initial industry members. Its stated roadmap includes:
- vendor-neutral AI cost definitions;
- full cost of AI, not tokens alone;
- cost-to-serve per call/work unit;
- AI value/ROI measurement;
- token cost telemetry in the FOCUS specification;
- practitioner education/certification.

First-party sources:
- https://www.linuxfoundation.org/press/linux-foundation-launches-the-tokenomics-foundation-to-define-the-economics-and-roi-of-ai-value
- https://www.linuxfoundation.org/press/linux-foundation-announces-tokenomicon-a-new-conference-for-the-economics-of-ai

### Testable low-capital wedges
- vendor-neutral AI cost ledger;
- cost-per-task / cost-per-resolved-work normalization;
- model/provider comparison with cache/reasoning/tool-call cost separation;
- AI spend-to-business-outcome evidence pack;
- FOCUS-aligned export/preflight when the specification stabilizes.

### Why it may compound
Usage/cost normalization schemas, provider adapters, benchmark histories and outcome mappings can become reusable across many AI systems.

### Kill / mutation conditions
- incumbent FinOps products absorb the standard before a small independent wedge exists;
- enterprise-only integrations make low-capital delivery unrealistic;
- customers care about invoice reduction but not normalized outcome/value measurement;
- standards remain descriptive without operational adoption.

`WTP = UNKNOWN`
