## Self-improvement
The candidate `EVIDENCE_FIRST_PORTFOLIO_GATE` is compared against an old weighted-score control on adversarial fixtures. Local reduction in false accepts may produce `LOCAL_KEEP` only. It cannot globally promote the Self-Improvement engine.
