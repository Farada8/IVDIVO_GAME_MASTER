# CYCLE 1 — SYNTHESIS + PATH TO GOAL

## What changed
The system is no longer an idea generator. It is now a typed decision architecture:
`LIBRARY -> SIGNAL -> OPPORTUNITY -> ROAD TEST -> ASSUMPTION -> TEST -> CASH -> CONSTRAINT -> BUILD/BROKER/ACQUIRE -> PROOF -> PORTFOLIO -> LEARNING`.

## Strongest imported mechanisms
1. **Mullins** — Seven Domains; micro-market can be a deal-breaker; reshape before business plan.
2. **Christensen** — nonconsumption/undershot/overshot signals and motivation/skill asymmetries.
3. **JTBD / demand-side** — buyer progress and switching forces, not product labels.
4. **Getting to Plan B** — analogs/antilogs, leap-of-faith assumptions, linked revenue/margin/operating/working-capital/investment models.
5. **Testing Business Ideas / Lean / Right It** — assumption-first, behavior over opinion, strongest feasible evidence, learning before build.
6. **Customer-Funded Business** — customer cash as financing architecture.
7. **Goldratt** — current constraint and throughput before local optimization; highlight human decisions when material data are unavailable.
8. **Meadows** — feedback loops, stocks/flows and delays.
9. **Ruback/Yudkoff + Deibel** — acquisition entrepreneurship as peer path to startup creation.
10. **Osterwalder** — EXPLORE/EXPLOIT portfolio and business-model pattern mutation.

## New strategic law
Every meaningful opportunity must compare at least three entry architectures:
- `CREATE`
- `BROKER / ORCHESTRATE`
- `ACQUIRE`

For a zero-founder-cash objective, the winner is not the highest theoretical margin. It is the path with the strongest evidence and smallest founder cash gap that can later compound into cashflow, IP, customer relationships or physical assets.

## Current constraint
Not shortage of books and not shortage of frameworks. The bottleneck is:
`69-book Drive library -> canonical Source Passports -> mechanism coverage -> live-signal opportunity objects`.

## Path to goal
### Stage 1 — Knowledge compiler
Canonicalize library, extract mechanisms, bind provenance, remove duplicates, encode contradictions.

### Stage 2 — Live signal graph
Feed current legislation, budgets, procurement, funding, hiring, patents, research, infrastructure, prices and company changes into typed signal objects.

### Stage 3 — Opportunity compiler
Map signals to affected actors, jobs/pains, WhyNow, asymmetries, Seven-Domain gates and fatal assumptions.

### Stage 4 — Zero-capital architecture
Compare service-first, broker/orchestration, customer-funded delivery and acquisition structures by founder cash gap and evidence.

### Stage 5 — Proof transition
While `NO_OUTREACH=true`, stop at E2+. When founder later authorizes external proof, E3/E4 require actual buyer interaction/payment, never model simulation.

### Stage 6 — Capitalization
Only after proof: debt/grants/investors/equipment/property/acquisition become accelerators rather than substitutes for demand.

## Stop rule
Do not create another top-level business engine unless a specific observed defect cannot be solved by these modules. The next engineering work must reduce the current constraint.
