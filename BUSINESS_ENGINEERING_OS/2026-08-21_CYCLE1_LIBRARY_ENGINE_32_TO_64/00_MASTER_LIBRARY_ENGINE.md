## Source Passport contract
Every source promoted into the Mechanism Bank must record: canonical title/author/edition, Drive ID/URL, observed file IDs/formats, integrity, duplicate relation, knowledge scope, excluded professional/jurisdiction scope, mechanisms, conditions, failure modes, engine targets, source anchors and contradictions.

A Source Passport proves **provenance/scope** only. It never proves buyer demand, ROI, legal compliance or willingness to pay.
