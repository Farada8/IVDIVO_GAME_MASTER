## Primary concept
### THE LAKE HOLDS THE LIGHT
A freestanding contemporary sculptural threshold: two slightly offset mineral/stone planes form a circular/elliptical aperture. The protected inner reveal carries blue-green smalt / exterior ceramic mosaic and restrained 316 stainless inlay. Incised ordered marks transition into water-ripple geometry.

The concept translates island/enclosure, water/passage and memory/learning without replicating a round tower, high cross, church ruin or manuscript.

- preferred candidate: Site 2, conditional on verified circulation, services, planting, access, ground and sightlines;
- Site 1 remains fallback;
- indicative envelope: approx. 2.1m H x 1.6m W x 0.35m D;
- final dimensions, foundation, alignment and material specification require site survey/engineering.
