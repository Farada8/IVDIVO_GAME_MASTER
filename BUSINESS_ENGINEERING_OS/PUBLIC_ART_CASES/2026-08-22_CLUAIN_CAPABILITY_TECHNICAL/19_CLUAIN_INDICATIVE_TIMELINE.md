# PAC8-19 — CLÚAIN INDICATIVE DELIVERY TIMELINE

**Status:** application-stage indicative programme only. Weeks are counted from formal appointment / confirmed project start. The housing development is expected to complete in April 2027; exact artist appointment, access dates and construction interfaces are not yet known.

## Delivery programme

### Phase 0 — Appointment + mobilisation — Week 0–1
Outputs: contract mobilisation, document review, stakeholder map, H&S/insurance/tax actions required after selection.
Gate: formal appointment; Council requirements.

### Phase 1 — Site confirmation + survey coordination — Weeks 1–3
Outputs: agreed candidate site, dimensional/site survey inputs, access constraints, utilities/foundations/substrate questions, photographic record.
Gate: Council + Sweeney Architects + Coolsivna coordination.

### Phase 2 — Community-engagement research — Weeks 2–6
Outputs: consultation plan, neighbouring-community engagement, resident/future-user input where feasible, evidence log.
Gate: participant access; safeguarding/data-minimisation rules.

### Phase 3 — Developed design — Weeks 4–8
Outputs: refined concept, final siting proposal, scale/material family, consultation integration, maintenance strategy.
Gate: site evidence + consultation findings.

### Phase 4 — Technical design + approvals — Weeks 7–11
Outputs: fixing/foundation/substrate strategy, fabrication drawings/specification, risk review, maintenance/handover plan, bound cost updates.
Gate: engineering/design-team review; exact site facts.

### Phase 5 — Procurement + fabrication — Weeks 10–18
Outputs: material procurement, fabrication/production, workshop QA, mock-ups/samples where useful.
Gate: approved technical package; supplier lead times.

### Phase 6 — Site preparation + installation — Weeks 17–21
Outputs: access setup, substrate/foundation preparation, installation, finishing, protection.
Gate: construction programme; site possession; weather/access.

### Phase 7 — Snagging + handover — Weeks 21–22
Outputs: snag closeout, cleaning, maintenance information, photographic record, handover.
Gate: installation complete.

### Phase 8 — Contingency / construction-interface reserve — up to 2–3 weeks
Purpose: absorb site-access, weather, construction-interface or approval movement without inventing guaranteed dates.

## Overall indicative envelope
Nominal programme: approximately 22 weeks from appointment, with a bounded 2–3 week contingency reserve where needed. The planning objective is to coordinate installation/handover with the housing-development programme and, where appointment timing permits, complete the public-art work before or around the expected April 2027 development completion. This is not a promise of an exact installation date before appointment, survey and contractor coordination.
