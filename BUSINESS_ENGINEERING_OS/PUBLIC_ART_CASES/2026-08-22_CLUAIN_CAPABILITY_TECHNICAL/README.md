## Concept direction
Primary working direction: **THE LIVING THRESHOLD / THE MEADOW OPENS**.

A contemporary site-responsive public artwork treating Clúain na Coillte as a threshold between remembered meadow/woodland landscape and a new shared civic home. For the entrance feature-wall class, the wall appears to open into woodland/hedgerow/meadow through controlled architectural depth; paths and human-scale motifs of arrival, play, meeting and continuity connect landscape memory to the new community.

This is **not** a fake historical architecture proposal and not a literal reuse of Shillelagh imagery. Trompe-l’oeil perspective remains conditional on actual geometry/viewpoint.

Fallback/site-flexible mechanism: **ROOTS / ROUTES / HOME** as relief/mosaic/sculptural/civic-marker language if the final commissioned site is a green rather than a wall.
