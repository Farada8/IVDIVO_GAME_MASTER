**Status:** application-stage indicative programme only.  
**Anchor:** weeks counted from formal appointment / confirmed project start.  
**External context:** the housing development is expected to complete in April 2027. Exact artist appointment, access dates and construction interfaces are not yet known.

## Overall indicative envelope
Nominal programme: **approximately 22 weeks from appointment**, with a bounded **2–3 week contingency reserve** where needed.

The intended planning objective is to coordinate installation/handover with the housing-development programme and, where appointment timing permits, complete the public-art work before or around the expected **April 2027** development completion. This is not a promise of an exact installation date before appointment, survey and contractor coordination.
