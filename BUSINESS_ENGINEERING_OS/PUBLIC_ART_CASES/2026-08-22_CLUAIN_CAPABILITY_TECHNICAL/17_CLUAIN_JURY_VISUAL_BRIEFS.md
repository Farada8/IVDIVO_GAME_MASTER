# PAC8-17 — CLÚAIN JURY VISUAL BRIEFS

**All visuals are indicative concept studies only. Final siting, dimensions, perspective, substrate, fixing, access and material system remain subject to post-selection survey and design-team coordination.**

## VISUAL A — THE LIVING THRESHOLD / THE MEADOW OPENS
**Preferred site class:** proposed entrance feature wall / entrance wall.

Create a premium public-art competition visual for Clúain na Coillte, Monksland, County Roscommon. The artwork should interpret the name “Meadow of the Woodland” and the transition from remembered farmland/hedgerow/woodland edge into a new 51-home community. Use a controlled architectural-illusion system: the wall appears to open, not violently break, into a deep Irish meadow and woodland-edge landscape. Subtle displaced stone/render planes, shadowed reveals and false architectural depth can evoke Giulio Romano/Pozzo intelligence, but the result must remain contemporary civic art rather than imitation Renaissance architecture.

Foreground human-scale motifs: path, gate/threshold, child-scale play traces, neighbours meeting, pollinator plants, hedgerow species and a restrained suggestion of routes toward Athlone/Monksland. No invented local historical figures. No fake medieval ruins. No generic Celtic clichés. Figures should be few, contemporary and secondary to the place-making image.

Visual hierarchy: from 50–80 m the wall reads as one luminous opening into meadow/woodland; from 15–30 m the path, hedgerow, community silhouettes and material transitions appear; close up, botanical and surface details reward viewing. Palette: limestone/render neutrals, meadow green, hedgerow dark green, soft sky blue, warm grasses, limited copper/gold accents. Irish daylight, realistic weather, high-end architectural visualisation. Include a small note: “INDICATIVE CONCEPT — FINAL GEOMETRY SUBJECT TO SITE SURVEY.”

## VISUAL B — ROOTS / ROUTES / HOME
**Preferred site class:** central green / smaller green; non-wall fallback.

Create a site-flexible permanent public-art concept for Clúain na Coillte that can exist as a low sculptural/relief/mosaic civic marker rather than a mural. Three linked forms represent ROOTS, ROUTES and HOME. Roots are abstracted from hedgerow and woodland root systems; routes use flowing lines recalling paths, local movement and connections to Greater Athlone; home is expressed through a sheltered shared form rather than a literal house icon.

Material language: durable stone/concrete base, coloured ceramic or smalt mosaic, weathering/corten-like metal only where appropriate, subtle relief, tactile surfaces at human height, integrated biodiversity motifs. Avoid sharp projections, climbable hazards and fragile detailing. Show the work in a green-space setting with paths and future playground/community use nearby, but do not pretend this is the exact approved site.

The object should feel contemporary, robust, calm and civic, with strong silhouette and readable identity without text. Include plan/side/front mini-views and note: “SITE-FLEXIBLE CONCEPT — FINAL SCALE, FOUNDATION AND MATERIALS SUBJECT TO SURVEY/ENGINEERING.”

## VISUAL C — THE COMMON MEADOW / 51 SIGNS OF BELONGING
**Preferred site class:** wall, path edge, green-space hybrid.

Create a contemporary community-engaged public-art concept for a 51-home development. Do not make 51 portraits or 51 equal icons. Instead, show a coherent field of 51 small resident-derived marks grouped into larger visual constellations: routes, plants, everyday objects, words/shapes, play, work, weather, memory and aspirations. These micro-signs are subordinated to one strong overall meadow/woodland composition so the artwork remains visually coherent.

The concept should demonstrate how future consultation can genuinely alter content without handing over overall artistic authorship. Use a contemporary archive/collage language: engraved or relief marks, ceramic inserts, painted or mosaic fragments, a restrained map/path structure, occasional figurative silhouettes. Show one hero view plus a close-up showing how individual marks become visible at short distance.

No claim that actual residents have already supplied these symbols. Label all sample symbols as “PLACEHOLDER EXAMPLES FOR FUTURE CONSULTATION.”

## Jury presentation rule
For the application, present A as the strongest authorial proposition, B as evidence of site flexibility and technical realism, and C as evidence of a credible community-engagement method. They are a coherent family, not three unrelated designs.
