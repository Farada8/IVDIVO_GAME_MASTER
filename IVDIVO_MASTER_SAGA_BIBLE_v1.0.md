# 1. CURRENT SAGA ORDER

## BOOK 1 — LESSON ZERO
**Status: CANON / DEVELOPMENT-LOCKED / PUBLISHER-EXTERNAL-FEEDBACK STAGE.**

LESSON ZERO is Book One. Do not rewrite its story architecture unless the Founder requests it, external feedback identifies a concrete problem, or a real continuity/factual error is found.

## BOOK 2 — ORBITAL YOUTH
**Status: WORKING DIRECTION.**

Book Two should immerse the reader in actual orbital civilization: life, technology, housing, work, education, money, friendship, romance, sport, entertainment, transport, maintenance, AI, synthetic life, new professions, mixed-species culture and ordinary youth problems.

It must be a complete novel, not a futurist catalogue and not a repeat of Lesson Zero’s institutional-test structure. Futurism is revealed through characters living inside it.

## BOOK 3 — SMITH / OLD EARTH SECURITY
**Status: WORKING DIRECTION.**

Book Three opens the active Smith line more fully: the continuing Old Earth Security system, cases, old agreements, anomalous/nonhuman phenomena, initiates, historical successes and crimes, and Smith’s parallel development through the Philosophy of Synthesis.

## BOOK 4 — FIRST MAJOR CROSSING OF LINES
**Status: WORKING DIRECTION, STORY NOT LOCKED.**

Book Four is where characters from previously separate worlds begin to intersect and influence one another. The objective is not to form a permanent superhero agency. Their lives and jurisdictions cross because one complete story requires multiple human worlds to confront the same problem.

Possible mechanisms remain OPTIONS: a service investigation; Smith’s daughter meeting someone from Orbital Youth; a relationship with a Confederation cadet; a case that crosses Earth/orbital jurisdictions; an old agreement colliding with new orbital law.

The exact Book Four engine is not yet canon.

## AFTER BOOK 4
**Status: WORKING RHYTHM.**

After the first crossover, the saga may separate again for roughly three books into its own worlds/lines before a later, earned convergence. Do not lock exact Book 5–10 assignments until Books 2–4 establish the strongest characters and consequences.

# 2. EARLY-CONTACT CONFEDERATION LAW

## CANON

The Galactic Confederation does **not** currently govern Earth or the Solar System.

The Confederation Council has not yet made the decision that would authorize full political integration or unrestricted intervention in human affairs.

The genuine large / multidimensional Confederation ship remains **outside the Solar System**.

Early Contact is staged and limited. It can occur through:
- cadets and representatives;
- remote or mediated communication;
- relay infrastructure;
- adapted bodies/apparatus where allowed;
- human-compatible interfaces;
- solar-compatible technologies;
- education and controlled knowledge transfer.

Civilization-wide Confederation capability must never be equated with what humans can locally access.

The Confederation may possess knowledge far beyond current humanity while still lacking legal/ethical permission to use that knowledge to rule human decisions.

## WORKING ONTOLOGICAL / SAFETY HYPOTHESIS

One reason for physical restraint may be deeper than diplomacy: direct entry of sufficiently advanced or multidimensional beings/systems could alter local conditions or constants of the Solar System and make currently filtered dimensions of reality broadly accessible to humans.

Such an involuntary opening could seriously harm an unprepared population psychologically, biologically, socially or ontologically.

Until this mechanism is fully dramatized and technically specified, treat it as a **WORKING HYPOTHESIS**, not an excuse for arbitrary plot effects.

# 3. CONFEDERATION CADETS — ROLE AND LIMITS

## CANON

Cadets can be an important bridge between civilizations.

They may:
- communicate with human youth;
- study with them;
- transmit permitted knowledge;
- explain models and methods;
- participate in controlled training and cultural exchange;
- warn about known risks within the limits of Contact ethics;
- use restricted human-compatible systems.

They do **not** automatically possess police, military, judicial or administrative jurisdiction over Earth or orbital human polities.

A Confederation cadet cannot simply arrive and overrule Smith, a planetary government, an orbital authority or a human court because the Confederation is more advanced.

Knowledge is not the same thing as jurisdiction.

---

# 4. HUMAN POLITICAL ARCHITECTURE — PLANET AND ORBIT

## CANON

Orbital civilization is not a single administrative layer above planetary governments.

Planetary leadership/governments are **not subordinate** to orbital political elites.

Orbital cities, habitats and political formations develop their own interests, law, economy, identities and institutions. Their relationship with planetary states is closer to the relationship between different countries/polities than to a simple capital/province hierarchy.

Therefore an early-saga conflict may involve several legitimate human jurisdictions at once.

## UNKNOWN / TO DESIGN

The exact constitutional map, treaty network, citizenship rules, sovereignty thresholds and ownership models of each orbital polity remain to be developed through Book Two and later stories.

Do not silently invent one universal Earth government or one universal Orbital Government unless Founder later approves it.

# 5. SMITH — PERMANENT SAGA ROLE

## CANON

Smith’s service/department does **not** close after Contact.

Smith remains an active professional throughout the saga.

He is a recurring vertical character who can move through:
- Earth;
- different countries and territories;
- orbital environments when a case requires it;
- other worlds as the saga expands;
- old Earth security archives;
- nonhuman/anomalous cases;
- old agreements;
- initiates / dedicated practitioners;
- institutions created before public Contact.

The arrival of the Confederation does not make Smith obsolete. It reveals how incomplete both the old Earth map and the new external map can be.

Do not make Smith a generic monster hunter, retired relic, exposition machine or adult who solves the youth line for them.

His operational competence may be high while his understanding of deeper reality continues to grow.

# 6. SMITH, DUBLIN, ENIA AND THE PHILOSOPHY OF SYNTHESIS

## CANON

Alongside active operational work, Smith studies the **Philosophy of Synthesis** with **Enia in Dublin**.

This is a continuing development line, not a one-scene consultation.

Smith therefore develops on at least two axes:
1. professional / operational competence;
2. deeper philosophical, initiatory and ontological understanding.

Enia must not function as a vending machine for case solutions. The Philosophy of Synthesis should change the quality of Smith’s perception, questions and choices rather than provide convenient answers to every investigation.

Smith may know how to contain or survive a phenomenon while still misunderstanding what system of relationships the phenomenon belongs to.

## UNKNOWN

Enia’s full institutional status, the complete hierarchy around her, and the final ontology of the Philosophy of Synthesis must be revealed only when earned by story and Founder-approved canon.

# 7. OLD EARTH SECURITY vs CONFEDERATION — CORRECTED MODEL

## SUPERSEDED

The earlier shorthand:

`OLD EARTH SECURITY <-> NEW CONFEDERATION SECURITY`

is too simple for the **early-contact period** if interpreted as two police/security systems with equal jurisdiction inside the Solar System.

## CURRENT CANON MODEL

Early conflicts are more accurately multi-sided:

`OLD EARTH SECURITY / PLANETARY AUTHORITIES / ORBITAL AUTHORITIES / INDIVIDUAL RIGHTS / LIMITED CONFEDERATION KNOWLEDGE`

The Confederation can understand things the old system misclassified, but it does not therefore receive automatic authority to intervene.

Smith’s system may possess historical knowledge unavailable to orbital governments or Confederation cadets.

Orbital institutions may have legitimate law and interests that Smith cannot simply ignore outside his jurisdiction.

This creates story through competing legitimate systems rather than through one superior authority.

Later in the saga, after Confederation Council decisions change the legal relationship, a true OLD EARTH SECURITY vs CONFEDERATION SECURITY jurisdictional conflict may become possible. That later condition must not be back-projected into the early books.

# 8. ORBITAL YOUTH — STORY FUNCTION

## CANON PRODUCTION LAW

The orbital line exists to show what a new civilization feels like to live inside, especially for young people.

Story should grow from:
- housing and personal space;
- school/training;
- first jobs and money;
- transport;
- maintenance;
- sport;
- parties and entertainment;
- attraction, jealousy and relationships;
- family distance and independence;
- station class/status;
- AI;
- synthetic life/bodies;
- mixed-species everyday life;
- expeditions and emergencies.

Do not turn Book Two into a technology encyclopedia. Every future detail should attach to desire, pressure, choice, social consequence or plot.

Do not transplant a normal Earth school into space unchanged.

# 9. TECHNOLOGY ACCESS LAW

## CANON

Do not equate the Confederation’s total civilization-level technology with local human access.

In the early period:
- Earth retains large amounts of legacy infrastructure and ordinary daily life;
- orbital habitats can use purpose-built advanced Contact-era systems;
- training/contact environments expose humans only to restricted, compatible interfaces;
- full Confederation systems may remain legally, technically, biologically or ontologically inaccessible.

Long-term IVDIVO technology remains available for later earned development:
- AI;
- synthetic/artificial life;
- complete artificial bodies;
- consciousness/spirit embodiment;
- bodies for vacuum, oceans, extreme planets, radiation and other environments;
- return of experience and knowledge to biological civilization.

No early lore dump.

# 10. BOOK FOUR CROSSOVER LAW

## CANON PRODUCTION PRINCIPLE

Characters from Smith, Orbital Youth and the Confederation-cadet contact layer should not be gathered merely because the franchise wants a crossover.

A crossover is earned only when one complete story naturally crosses their lives, relationships, knowledge or jurisdictions.

The Book Four problem should be something no single human world can reduce to its own assumptions, but it must remain plausible that the Confederation has not simply failed to stop an obvious galaxy-scale disaster.

Therefore strong Book Four engines include:
- legal/jurisdictional conflict;
- old agreement versus new orbital status;
- service investigation;
- individual rights versus inherited security protocols;
- incompatible classifications of the same person/event;
- relationships that place characters on opposite sides of a legitimate decision.

## OPTION

Smith’s daughter may become a natural bridge to Orbital Youth and/or a Confederation cadet. This remains an option until character architecture proves it.

## REJECTED DEFAULT

Do not automatically create a permanent agency/team whose purpose is to fight ever-larger threats.

After the crossover, characters may return to their own worlds while relationships and consequences persist.

# 13. FINAL PRINCIPLE

The first major saga movement is not about the Confederation taking control of humanity. It is about humanity encountering a civilization far beyond it while still being required to make its own decisions, build its own orbital societies, confront its own hidden past, and grow into knowledge without being forcibly remade by a superior power.

Smith represents the active hidden/history/security vertical.  
Orbital Youth represents emerging human future civilization.  
Confederation cadets represent controlled access to a larger civilization without automatic sovereignty over humanity.  
Enia / Philosophy of Synthesis represents a deeper line of learning that must be revealed through story rather than exposition.
