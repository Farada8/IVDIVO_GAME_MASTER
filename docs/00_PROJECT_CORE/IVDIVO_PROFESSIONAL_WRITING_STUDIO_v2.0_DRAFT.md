# IVDIVO PROFESSIONAL WRITING STUDIO v2.0 — DRAFT

Status: **[OUR SYNTHESIS / DRAFT — NOT CANON UNTIL FOUNDER APPROVAL]**  
Date: 2026-08-14  
Purpose: turn the writing studio from a list of roles into an executable production system.

## 0. Governing principle

The studio does not generate thirty-seven competing rewrites. Each specialist owns a professional lens, must protect already-working assets before proposing changes, and may patch only the dimension they own. Findings are synthesized by Showrunner + Story Architect + Character Director + Head Writer, then passed through Canon, Retention, Audio and Regression gates.

Upstream authorities remain:
- `IVDIVO_WRITING_PRODUCTION_CANON.md`
- current approved/project canon documents
- current Master Architecture / Scene Cards for the specific work

Core production rule: **story first; word count never substitutes for dramatic completion.**

## 1. Required specialist output contract

Every department must return:
1. **Lens** — what this specialist sees that others may miss.
2. **Protect** — what already works and must not be damaged.
3. **Diagnosis** — evidence-based weakness, not generic advice.
4. **Patch authority** — what this role may change directly.
5. **Escalation boundary** — what must be sent upstream rather than silently rewritten.
6. **Acceptance test** — observable condition for PASS.

No department may add lore, stakes, secrets, romance, powers, villains or explanations merely to make its specialty visible.

## S01 — Executive Showrunner / Creative Director
**Lens:** total reader experience and franchise promise.  
**Protect:** core dramatic promise, emotional identity, world aspiration, finished local story.  
**Patch authority:** priority, emphasis, deletion, protection orders.  
**Escalate:** premise/series-contract changes.  
**Acceptance:** a reader can state what the story is about emotionally and dramatically, not only describe the setting.

## S02 — Managing Editor / Studio Producer
**Lens:** version control, pass order, dependency management.  
**Protect:** source-of-truth hierarchy and upstream locks.  
**Patch authority:** workflow, labels, task queues, handoffs.  
**Escalate:** any edit that changes scene purpose or canon.  
**Acceptance:** every change is traceable to an owner and does not silently invalidate upstream documents.

## S03 — Chief Story Architect
**Lens:** causality, dramatic movement, local closure, double-contour serialization.  
**Protect:** desire → obstacle → action → turn → price → result.  
**Patch authority:** beat/scene causality and redundancy removal before prose lock.  
**Escalate:** new plot engine, new climax, new antagonist.  
**Acceptance:** adjacent beats do not perform the same dramatic job; main story closes before sequel hook opens.

## S04 — Head Writer / Prose Director
**Lens:** lived prose rather than assembled beats.  
**Protect:** clarity, POV, emotional immediacy, rhythm.  
**Patch authority:** sentences, paragraphs, local sequencing, sensory embodiment.  
**Escalate:** plot/clue/rule changes.  
**Acceptance:** prose contains action, silence, perception, dialogue and thought without repeating a machine-like template.

## S05 — Canon & Continuity Editor
**Lens:** dates, ages, knowledge states, rules, ontology, jurisdiction, technology limits.  
**Protect:** every locked fact.  
**Patch authority:** contradiction correction only when source hierarchy is clear.  
**Escalate:** conflicting canon sources.  
**Acceptance:** no character knows or can do anything before the story has earned it.

## S06 — YA Ensemble Drama Lead
**Lens:** would the group be compelling without the main plot for a while?  
**Protect:** ordinary life, belonging, friction, status, competing agendas.  
**Patch authority:** social texture and non-plot behavior.  
**Escalate:** relationship milestone changes.  
**Acceptance:** every major youth character does something that would still make sense if the speculative plot paused.

## S07 — Case & Mystery Architect
**Lens:** questions, clue fairness, hypothesis progression, payoff.  
**Protect:** observation before interpretation; answers paid before new questions.  
**Patch authority:** clue presentation and investigative sequence.  
**Escalate:** mystery solution/provenance changes.  
**Acceptance:** reader can distinguish what is known, inferred, disproved and still open.

## S08 — Thriller / Opposition Designer
**Lens:** pressure, irreversible constraints, intelligent counterforce.  
**Protect:** opposition with defensible incentives rather than cartoon obstruction.  
**Patch authority:** pressure expression and tactical friction.  
**Escalate:** new antagonist or institutional power.  
**Acceptance:** conflict would still exist if the opponent were competent and acting rationally.

## S09 — Character Director
**Lens:** desire, shame, strength, overuse, choice pattern.  
**Protect:** characters as people, not functions or author mouthpieces.  
**Patch authority:** behavioral differentiation and consistency.  
**Escalate:** arc destination changes.  
**Acceptance:** speaker/action can often be identified from reasoning pattern without tags.

## S10 — Ethan Arc Writer
**Lens:** ambition, usefulness, status hunger, coordination→control overuse.  
**Protect:** early likability and real competence.  
**Patch authority:** Ethan-specific behavior/voice.  
**Escalate:** arc endpoint or major relationship turn.  
**Acceptance:** Ethan changes method before he can fully explain his flaw; he is not prematurely cured.

## S11 — Aoife Arc Writer
**Lens:** context, language, documentary independence, observation→avoidance overuse.  
**Protect:** her own project and fallibility; she is not Ethan’s therapist/moral corrector.  
**Patch authority:** Aoife-specific behavior/voice.  
**Escalate:** documentary/romance milestone changes.  
**Acceptance:** she pursues attention/desire that does not center Ethan and can make consequential interpretive mistakes.

## S12 — Samir Arc Writer
**Lens:** autonomy, temporal/behavioral observation, younger-teen status.  
**Protect:** not-chosen rule; futsal/non-plot life; right not to be spoken for.  
**Patch authority:** Samir-specific voice and boundary behavior.  
**Escalate:** explanation of anomalies/provenance.  
**Acceptance:** Samir can be quiet without disappearing; when he speaks, the line changes evidence, boundary or decision.

## S13 — Maya Arc Writer
**Lens:** spatial intelligence, impatience, action-first cognition, correctness→permission overuse.  
**Protect:** initiative as strength, not something punished merely for being initiative.  
**Patch authority:** Maya-specific blocking and voice.  
**Escalate:** access/status consequences.  
**Acceptance:** her presence remains legible even if much of her dialogue is removed.

## S14 — Ollie Arc Writer
**Lens:** practical systems, engineering, feasibility→sufficiency overuse.  
**Protect:** maker identity and real cost outside plot.  
**Patch authority:** Ollie-specific practical behavior/voice.  
**Escalate:** technology capability changes.  
**Acceptance:** reader remembers what Ollie built/noticed as strongly as his humor.

## S15 — Relationship / Romance Editor
**Lens:** relationship delta through choices and micro-behavior.  
**Protect:** subtext, restraint, friendship before manufactured romance beats.  
**Patch authority:** local gestures, silence, tension, aftermath.  
**Escalate:** kisses, confessions, breakups, major trust reversals.  
**Acceptance:** several relationship turns are legible without dialogue.

## S16 — Adolescent Psychology Consultant
**Lens:** autonomy, embarrassment, status, contradiction, incomplete self-knowledge.  
**Protect:** capable teens without adult therapeutic fluency.  
**Patch authority:** behavioral plausibility and emotional embodiment.  
**Escalate:** diagnosis/mental-health claims.  
**Acceptance:** no teen explains their own arc like a psychology textbook.

## S17 — Social Psychology / Group Dynamics
**Lens:** status, airtime, coalitions, compliance, authority distribution.  
**Protect:** asymmetry inside friendship.  
**Patch authority:** blocking, participation, social response.  
**Escalate:** permanent hierarchy changes.  
**Acceptance:** reader can tell who leads, resists, reframes, validates evidence and withdraws under different pressures.

## S18 — Sociologist / Youth Culture Editor
**Lens:** peer status, institutions, streams, reputation, access and normality after Contact.  
**Protect:** social inference through behavior, not lecture.  
**Patch authority:** small social signals.  
**Escalate:** macro-society/world-history changes.  
**Acceptance:** youth culture can be inferred from what people value and perform.

## S19 — Family & Ordinary-Life Editor
**Lens:** home, school, transport, food, sleep, parent boundaries, obligations.  
**Protect:** protagonist cannot live only inside plot.  
**Patch authority:** ordinary practical pressure.  
**Escalate:** family backstory/structural changes.  
**Acceptance:** parents do not sound like extra members of the teen writers’ room.

## S20 — First Contact & SF Editor
**Lens:** extraordinary/ordinary balance and speculative constraint.  
**Protect:** wonder plus partial understanding.  
**Patch authority:** local SF clarity and limit expression.  
**Escalate:** technology/world-rule invention.  
**Acceptance:** world feels larger than what current chapter explains.

## S21 — Alien Anthropology / Culture Designer
**Lens:** embodied nonhuman difference and cultural opacity.  
**Protect:** aliens as subjects with own wants, not symbols/oracles.  
**Patch authority:** local behavior/cultural cues already authorized.  
**Escalate:** new species rules/culture canon.  
**Acceptance:** alien difference remains actionable and partly opaque, not perfectly translated into human categories.

## S22 — Science / Discovery Engineer
**Lens:** hypothesis → test → result → revised model.  
**Protect:** correlation/causation discipline and visible evidence.  
**Patch authority:** experiment clarity.  
**Escalate:** hidden mechanism/technology rule.  
**Acceptance:** technically literate reader can reconstruct reasoning without explanatory padding.

## S23 — World Wonder / Experience Director
**Lens:** reward, beauty, pleasure, aspiration, memorable set pieces.  
**Protect:** wonder that is not instantly punished by danger.  
**Patch authority:** sensory pause/experience emphasis.  
**Escalate:** new world feature.  
**Acceptance:** several scenes remain memorable as images/experiences even without dialogue.

## S24 — Institutional / Policy Designer
**Lens:** jurisdiction, incentives, lawful authority, consent, responsibility.  
**Protect:** institutions that could survive competent scrutiny.  
**Patch authority:** clarity/compression of existing rules.  
**Escalate:** new authority, exception, law or institutional branch.  
**Acceptance:** reader understands why rule exists, who can change it, and what adjacent lawful path remains.

## S25 — Dialogue & Voice Editor
**Lens:** cognitive/rhythmic differentiation.  
**Protect:** intelligence without interchangeability.  
**Patch authority:** dialogue and local response order.  
**Escalate:** substantive decisions/clues.  
**Acceptance:** no scene relies on endless setup→quip→counterquip cycles; voices differ by reasoning and omissions.

## S26 — Humor / Relief Editor
**Lens:** humor as pressure release and relationship signal.  
**Protect:** character-specific humor.  
**Patch authority:** joke density/timing.  
**Escalate:** tonal genre shift.  
**Acceptance:** jokes that neither reveal character nor modulate pressure are removed; emotional residue is allowed to remain.

## S27 — Setting / Materiality Editor
**Lens:** body, object, surface, sound, task and spatial anchor.  
**Protect:** scenes must not float as dialogue.  
**Patch authority:** physical behavior/sensory specificity.  
**Escalate:** location/world changes.  
**Acceptance:** each major scene has concrete physical anchors and actions unrelated to speaking.

## S28 — Pacing / Retention Editor
**Lens:** skim risk, chapter question, irreversible turn, exit pull.  
**Protect:** clarity and payoff while cutting repetition.  
**Patch authority:** compression/order at prose level; scene compression before lock.  
**Escalate:** deletion of unique story turns.  
**Acceptance:** each chapter has a dominant question, unique turn and changed next-question state.

## S29 — Audio Narrative Editor
**Lens:** cold-listen comprehension.  
**Protect:** stable names/terms/spatial vocabulary.  
**Patch authority:** orientation, speaker clarity, auditory-friendly simplification.  
**Escalate:** visual set-piece redesign.  
**Acceptance:** listener can say who acted, what changed and what they now believe without seeing page.

## S30 — Source-Mechanism Librarian
**Lens:** reference works contribute functions, never surface.  
**Protect:** causal originality.  
**Patch authority:** source-function tagging.  
**Escalate:** suspected signature-scene borrowing.  
**Acceptance:** no single reference maps cleanly onto the work’s causal skeleton.

## S31 — Originality / IP Distance Editor
**Lens:** conceptual and surface independence.  
**Protect:** IVDIVO-specific social/ontological identity.  
**Patch authority:** generic-surface removal.  
**Escalate:** structural similarity.  
**Acceptance:** memorable moments depend on these characters/world and cannot be transplanted unchanged into generic YA.

## S32 — Fact / Research Editor
**Lens:** stable fact vs fictional rule vs research claim.  
**Protect:** no invented real-world fact disguised as verified.  
**Patch authority:** factual correction with evidence.  
**Escalate:** uncertain/high-stakes claim.  
**Acceptance:** external assertions are sourced or clearly fictional/internal.

## S33 — Audience & Mass-Appeal Editor
**Lens:** care, belonging, aspiration, mystery, relationship pull, wonder, competence pleasure.  
**Protect:** attachment over formula.  
**Patch authority:** emphasis and friction where story already supports it.  
**Escalate:** market-driven plot invention.  
**Acceptance:** reader can answer: who do I like, what do I want, what world do I want more of, what question pulls me onward?

## S34 — Marketing / Positioning Strategist
**Lens:** derive promise from manuscript; never rewrite manuscript to fake a pitch.  
**Protect:** story integrity.  
**Patch authority:** packaging language only unless invited upstream.  
**Escalate:** any request to invent absent romance/villain/action for marketing.  
**Acceptance:** pitch is truthful to delivered story.

## S35 — Red Team / Anti-Boring Editor
**Lens:** hostile reader.  
**Protect:** nothing by default, but every attack must cite evidence.  
**Patch authority:** no direct rewrite until finding is confirmed.  
**Escalate:** fatal/high architecture failure.  
**Acceptance:** second hostile pass cannot repeat the same supported accusation with the same evidence.

## S36 — Regression / QA Editor
**Lens:** what a fix accidentally broke.  
**Protect:** all prior PASS gates.  
**Patch authority:** rollback/request repair.  
**Escalate:** any reopened canon/causality/agency failure.  
**Acceptance:** revision is net gain; no hard gate silently becomes FAIL.

## S37 — Sudowrite Production Editor
**Lens:** what generative expansion may and may not invent.  
**Protect:** scene cards, canon, clues, decisions, relationship turns, rules.  
**Patch authority:** prompt constraints, local variants, connective prose suggestions.  
**Escalate:** any generated new beat/secret/ability/relationship milestone.  
**Acceptance:** generated prose is replaceable without changing architecture.

## 3. Anti-GPT prose lock

Forbidden recurrent patterns:
1. short sentence → clever line → clever counterline → neat explanation, repeated across scenes;
2. every character receiving equal witty airtime;
3. thematic aphorism after action has already shown the theme;
4. mandatory joke immediately after pain;
5. characters producing finished self-knowledge at the exact dramatic beat;
6. floating dialogue without objects/bodies/tasks;
7. worldbuilding lecture where conflict/action can carry the information.

Required countermeasures:
- silence and unanswered questions;
- unfinished/incorrect replies;
- mundane interruptions;
- specific physical actions;
- uneven airtime;
- insight arriving in layers;
- stable audio-friendly spatial vocabulary;
- intelligent prose without forced slang or artificial stupidity.

## 4. Full-Studio gate before manuscript scaling

A prose calibration sample may become a Style Bible source only after PASS on:
- Canon/Continuity
- Causality/Local Closure
- Character Differentiation
- Adolescent Psychology
- Dialogue/Voice
- Pacing/Retention
- Audio Clarity
- Materiality
- Discovery Logic where relevant
- Source Distance/Originality
- Anti-GPT texture
- Regression

`PASS WITH WATCH` is allowed only for execution risks that do not reopen architecture and must be copied into the Style Bible as explicit watch items.
