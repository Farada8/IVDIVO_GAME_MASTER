## Primary Purpose

Make the climax legally and operationally credible.

The hero must have enough real responsibility to cause harm and later perform the decisive action, but must not possess implausible city-scale command authority. Smith must possess historical competence without becoming sovereign. The Confederation cadet must possess superior knowledge without jurisdiction. The orbital security director must possess present lawful authority without becoming infallible.

Exact agency names, titles, age requirements, statutory language, technical numbers and constitutional forms remain **UNKNOWN** until later continuity/world lock.

## 1. Hero Authority Passport

### WORKING ROLE LABEL
`Operations Routing Trainee / Junior Dispatch Operator`.
Exact in-world title UNKNOWN.

### Career Origin
The hero comes from a local maintenance/apprentice track and has already demonstrated:
- physical familiarity with transfer-spine partitions, access points and service routes;
- experience reading local equipment state rather than only central dashboards;
- emergency-drill training;
- manual life-safety routing practice;
- knowledge of how residents/work crews actually move through the habitat.

This prior maintenance history is why the hero has useful local control knowledge. It is not a hidden special ability.

### 1.1 Normal-Shift Authority — WORKING
The hero may, within assigned sectors and approved procedures:
- monitor route occupancy and environmental/partition state;
- execute pre-authorized transit closures and reopenings;
- sequence local doors/partitions/locks inside certified operating envelopes;
- reroute routine passenger/service traffic;
- issue local movement holds and route advisories;
- acknowledge alarms and escalate them to senior control;
- run approved diagnostic cycles;
- coordinate with maintenance crews and emergency services;
- enter factual operator notes into permanent log;
- use local life-safety panels during drills or when central automation fails, within bounded procedures.

The hero does **not** normally decide why a city-scale security quarantine exists. They implement route consequences of decisions made at a higher authority level.

### 1.2 Initial-Incident Authority
When the legacy-class signature appears, the orbital director orders a hard seal. The hero is legitimately assigned to execute the local transfer-spine portion of that seal.

**THE HERO CAUSES THE FULL STATIC BOUNDARY STATE THROUGH A LAWFUL ACTION THEY ARE ACTUALLY QUALIFIED TO PERFORM.**

Therefore protagonist-caused escalation is credible and does not require insubordination or accidental button-pushing.

### 1.3 Actions the Hero May Not Normally Take
Without higher authorization, the trainee/junior operator may not:
- cancel a city-security classification;
- reopen a quarantined connection into the inhabited city;
- override the director’s city-scale containment order;
- command security teams;
- authorize sector detachment;
- accept jurisdiction on behalf of the orbital polity;
- grant Smith/OES operational control;
- treat Confederation advice as binding human authority;
- waive liability for civilian exposure;
- conceal or delete incident logs;
- improvise unrestricted access through multiple security zones.

If the climax required any of these powers, the hero role would be implausible.

### 1.4 Local Manual Life-Safety Control — Why It Exists
**WORKING WORLD MECHANISM:** critical habitat infrastructure must retain bounded local control when central automation, network synchronization or command routing becomes unreliable.

Therefore transfer-spine segments have local/manual life-safety panels capable of:
- closing the immediately adjacent segment;
- opening one local passage within certified pressure/environment limits;
- transferring local control from failed central automation to a physically present qualified operator;
- executing a predefined emergency passage sequence;
- writing an immutable local audit record even if central systems disagree.

These controls cannot open the whole city or erase quarantine status. Their design purpose is to prevent central failure from trapping people behind an automation fault.

### 1.5 Why Central Command Cannot Simply Disable Every Local Panel
**WORKING SAFETY PRINCIPLE:** a habitat whose central system can remotely remove every local life-safety exit during a synchronization failure contains a catastrophic single point of failure.

Central command may:
- revoke normal network credentials;
- prevent ordinary remote routing;
- lock non-life-safety functions;
- flag local manual operation as unauthorized/high-risk;

but it cannot make every physically local certified emergency passage impossible without defeating safety architecture meant for fire, decompression, control loss or network partition.

This must be established before climax, not invented in the final scene.

### 1.6 The Last Override — What Changes Legally
The Last Override does **not** transfer command to the hero.

It creates a disputed narrow liability/permission exception for **ONE** life-safety passage action when all activation conditions are claimed to be met.

It changes the hero’s act from:
`obviously unauthorized opening of a quarantine`

into:
`a locally executable emergency action for which the operator can assert a surviving legacy life-safety defense, while accepting immediate review and possible suspension.`

The orbital director may still dispute whether conditions are satisfied. That dispute is dramatic pressure.

### 1.7 Hero Liability
Every decisive local action generates:
- operator identity;
- time/state snapshot;
- sensor inputs available at the moment;
- commands issued;
- local manual acknowledgements;
- any cited emergency basis;
- post-action results.

The hero cannot be anonymous. They know before acting that the decision will be auditable.

If the corridor spreads the unknown condition, the hero may face severe professional/legal consequences in addition to mass-casualty guilt. If the corridor works, success does not automatically erase the procedural violation or disputed authority question.

### 1.8 Automatic Post-Incident Consequence — WORKING
After an operator deliberately invokes a disputed emergency exception against a standing security order:
- operational access is suspended pending review;
- incident logs are preserved;
- operator is removed from active duty;
- certification progression pauses;
- an independent review determines whether emergency conditions were reasonably met.

This gives the story a natural personal price without requiring a vindictive director.

## 2. Orbital Security Director Authority Passport

**WORKING ROLE FUNCTION:** senior present-day orbital security/emergency authority responsible for protecting the inhabited habitat/system from unknown propagation risk. Exact title/institution UNKNOWN.

### 2.1 What the Director Can Lawfully Do
Within current orbital emergency framework, director may:
- classify incident as security/life-safety quarantine;
- close transit sectors;
- freeze docking/movement;
- order shelter-in-place;
- allocate security/emergency/transport resources;
- restrict operator credentials;
- authorize controlled diagnostic tests;
- authorize exterior extraction;
- prioritize populations under rescue constraints;
- prepare emergency sector isolation/detachment;
- order detachment if relevant threshold is reached;
- accept/reject operational proposals under current law;
- initiate post-incident investigation and suspension.

This authority is real. Smith does not simply outrank it.

### 2.2 What the Director Cannot Do
Director may not:
- make Old Earth historical evidence cease to exist because inconvenient;
- falsify sensor data;
- order the Confederation cadet as a subordinate absent separate agreement;
- transform Confederation knowledge into human jurisdiction;
- retroactively erase an applicable inherited life-safety clause merely by declaring it obsolete;
- ignore current orbital due-process/individual-rights requirements where operationally possible;
- knowingly continue a tactic after evidence crosses a threshold that current doctrine itself requires to be reconsidered.

The director remains constrained by evidence and their own law.

### 2.3 Director’s Core Liability
Director is responsible for **WHOLE-HABITAT RISK**, not merely trapped sector.

**FALSE OPENING RISK:** open corridor → unknown effect may reach inhabited city → catastrophic population-scale consequence.

**FALSE CONTAINMENT RISK:** maintain isolation → isolated sector may become unrecoverable → smaller but immediate casualty set.

This asymmetry is why director remains rational even after cadet midpoint test suggests isolation is active harm.

### 2.4 Director Decision Thresholds — Functional, Numbers UNKNOWN
- **D0 — CONTAINMENT THRESHOLD:** unknown signature + independent control disagreement justify initial seal.
- **D1 — ALTERNATIVE EXTRACTION THRESHOLD:** ordinary shelter cannot remain safe through investigation window → authorize exterior/partial extraction.
- **D2 — DETACHMENT PREPARATION THRESHOLD:** projected control coherence approaches latest safe separation point → begin detachment preparation.
- **D3 — DETACHMENT COMMIT THRESHOLD:** sector will reach life-safety/control loss before a validated evacuation path can clear enough occupants → commit to protecting larger habitat.
- **D4 — TACTIC-REVISION THRESHOLD:** independently measured boundary-state test produces predicted reduction AND receiving-sector sensors show no corresponding propagation → current risk model must be revised.

Director changes tactics at D4. They do not keep resisting to preserve antagonist status.

### 2.5 Why Director Cannot Simply Accept Smith’s Annex Early
At first contact, director does not know:
- whether document is authentic;
- whether present signature truly matches;
- whether later law superseded it;
- whether historical success had hidden costs;
- whether old operators misunderstood causality;
- whether present habitat architecture changes risk;
- whether Smith’s institution has current operational standing in this jurisdiction.

Requiring verification is competent behavior.

### 2.6 Why Director Cannot Simply Accept Cadet Model at Midpoint
Cadet can demonstrate isolation affects phenomenon. That does **not** yet prove a corridor into populated system is safe.

Director distinguishes:
`our containment tactic is harmful`
from
`opening toward the city is safe.`

That distinction keeps opposition alive without stupidity.

## 3. Smith / Old Earth Security Authority Passport

### 3.1 Smith Has
- OES identity/standing appropriate to later continuity;
- access to/authentication capacity for relevant historical archive;
- expertise in old hazard classification and prior operation;
- ability to state what Annex required historically;
- ability to authenticate whether archived activation criteria are genuinely present in old record;
- professional standing to warn current authorities and provide evidence.

### 3.2 Smith Does Not Have by Default
- command of orbital security;
- unilateral right to reopen orbital infrastructure;
- police/judicial authority over orbital citizens;
- authority to order trainee;
- power to invoke Confederation intervention;
- certainty about present phenomenon;
- complete explanation of why old rule worked.

### 3.3 Smith’s Crucial Climax Function
Smith authenticates empirical continuity: present pattern is sufficiently close to historical hazard class for Annex condition to be credibly invoked.

He does not perform routing action.

### 3.4 Required Historical Imperfection
Source case behind Annex must contain a morally/institutionally compromised element. Candidate classes, exact choice later:
- people isolated too long because old system prioritized secrecy;
- population moved without adequate consent;
- nonhuman/anomalous subject misclassified as hostile contamination;
- evidence suppressed to preserve agreement/system;
- successful physical containment paired with unjust human cost.

Useful emergency rule survived despite old system’s failure, not because system deserves retrospective moral victory.

A. Annex cannot physically open anything.  
B. Smith cannot command hero with it.  
C. Hero cannot invoke it before specific evidence thresholds exist.  
D. Clause covers only narrow life-safety passage action, not general incident command.

## 7. Hero Authority Through the Acts

Hero has bounded legitimate routing responsibility and wants permanent certification. Hero obeys and executes hard seal. This is the causal mistake.

### EARLY ACT II
Hero remains inside chain of command, logs discrepancies, supports current containment and discounts Annex until verified.

### MID ACT II

### LATE ACT II

### CRISIS

Hero accepts named responsibility under disputed Annex conditions and performs **ONE** local segmented passage action. This act creates evidence that changes director’s decision model.

### AFTER CLIMAX
Director legitimately takes over city-scale rescue support. Hero does not become commander.

### RESOLUTION
Hero is suspended/reviewed. Emergency is closed. Meaning of professionalism has changed permanently.

## 8. Liability Drama — Why This Is Story, Not Lore

Legal architecture matters only where it changes action:
- trainee status lets hero perform initial seal;
- sponsor/director controls career hero wants;
- current command can revoke central credentials;
- local safety design leaves one bounded manual action possible;
- Annex offers disputed personal liability basis, not supremacy;
- hero decides whether evidence is strong enough to accept responsibility;
- success changes rescue tactics but does not erase price.

Any legal exposition not serving one of these action functions should be cut.
