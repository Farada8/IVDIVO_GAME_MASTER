## 1. Dialogue / Voice Matrix

### HERO — Iri-function, identity conditional
Inherited basis: withholds, redirects, answers selectively. Local-worker precision comes through what the hero notices/refuses to dignify, not speeches. Operations training adds procedural vocabulary, but under pressure the hero shortens rather than becomes eloquent.

Default moves: correct practical assumption; ask which state/before-or-after; answer only supported part; turn abstraction back into route/action; go quiet when status threatened.

Forbidden: polished constitutional arguments; thematic monologues about authority; youth policy-committee voice; omniscient anomaly terminology.

Voice change: early hero hides uncertainty behind clean institutional phrasing; late hero names uncertainty and attaches personal responsibility to bounded action.

### DIRECTOR
Core: scope / duty / exactness / thresholds. Asks what is known, inferred, and what happens if wrong. Never needs to shout. Under pressure gets shorter. No villain bureaucracy. Change is threshold/action, not ideology.

### SMITH
Core: historical competence / provenance / professional restraint. Separates what archive says from what OES claimed it meant. Admits incomplete chain. Gains force when disclosing evidence damaging to OES. No mysterious-old-man aphorisms, species identification or authority superiority.

### CONFEDERATION CADET
Core: confidence bounds / physical relation / near-peer asymmetry. States falsifiers; distinguishes unknown from unsupported inference; refuses human legal questions outside competence. No miracle tech, command language, complete ontology or philosophy speeches.

### R1-FRIEND
Core: material life / shared history / refusal to let career consume relationship. Talks money, route time, meals, shifts, housing. Not motivational partner or theme delivery system. May remain angry about material cost after rescue.

### CIVILIANS
- CIV-A: pay / supervisor / proof-of-absence language.
- B guardian + young person: logistics/control vs local-social knowledge/embarrassment; interrupt each other.
