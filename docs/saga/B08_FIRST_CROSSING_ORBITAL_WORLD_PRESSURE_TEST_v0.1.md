# IVDIVO B08 — FIRST CROSSING — Orbital World Pressure Test v0.1

**Status:** WORKING / NOT CANON / NOT FROZEN  
**Parent engine:** THE LAST OVERRIDE / THE SEALED TRANSFER SPINE  
**Date:** 2026-08-17

## Test
B08 passes only if the protagonist has a real orbital life before the emergency and if replacing orbit with an ordinary terrestrial building materially breaks the plot.

## Work / first job
Before crisis establish trainee/junior operations status, bounded authority, sponsor/director evaluation pressure, one boring task done well, maintenance knowledge and a small non-catastrophic social/professional mistake.

Career stakes: permanent certification, shift eligibility, placement stability, consequential work and sponsor loss.

## Money
Show one housing cost/commute tradeoff, a trainee-pay or shift-premium consequence, and visible missed-work cost for at least one civilian. Suspension changes income trajectory/housing timing but does not melodramatically make the hero destitute.

## Housing
R1 shared-housing plan is the emotional price. Before crisis two young adults compare location, cost and route time around the hero's expected placement. After rescue the original move cannot simply continue; application/deposit/timing/location must change materially.

## Transport
Transfer Spine must appear as normal civic infrastructure before becoming crisis site. Show at least three ordinary uses: shift commute, exterior service/docking transfer, youth/social/education movement, cargo/service, housing-to-work route. Closure causes missed shifts, alternate-route overload, delayed services and family/social friction.

## Ordinary relationship
R1 shared-housing close friend is structurally green but exact identity remains continuity-conditional. Book 2 gives Iri an earned peer/rival/trust relationship with Ollie, so Ollie is a legitimate future candidate only if Books 3–7 preserve closeness, co-location and plausible shared-housing planning. Do not force Ollie and do not invent a romance only to raise stakes.

Required before incident: housing/route conversation, conflict over certification tunnel vision, ordinary humour/food/errand unrelated to anomaly. Friend is not hostage and may initially oppose risking the hero's career.

## Social network / youth status
Add one family, former maintenance-peer, neighbourhood or training-cohort anchor. Include embarrassment/status beats: public correction, teasing about ambition, frustration at trainee label, loss of console access in front of known peers.

## Food / fatigue / body
Use bad shift food, missed meals, sleep debt, queues, medication/environment needs. Do not use body diversity as exposition and do not lock synthetic ontology unless prior continuity earns it.

## Automation / AI
Automation is lived infrastructure: route sequencing, occupancy, pressure/fire partitions, docking, logs, prioritization support. B08 is not automatically an AI-personhood story and not an evil-AI story. Local manual controls exist because orbital civilization expects bounded automation failure.

## Law / bureaucracy
Show law only where it changes action: who can close route, open one cell, sign log, accept liability, detach sector, suspend operator; why Smith can authenticate history but not command; why cadet can provide evidence but not jurisdiction.

## OES
Reveal through archive authentication, provenance conflict, suppressed after-action record and Smith's disclosure of institutional wrongdoing. OES preserved one useful empirical rule while hiding that its original response worsened casualties.

## Confederation contact
Cadet: one conceptual correction + one falsifiable test + uncertainty. No command, miracle tech or ship-scale intervention. Social asymmetry: cadet can be right and later leave; hero must live with the human career/legal aftermath.

## Civilian faces — mandatory
Use 4–6 recurring civilian functions rather than a faceless trapped population. Recommended functions:
- outer-node shift worker with wage/schedule pressure;
- parent/guardian + young person returning from ordinary activity;
- older/mobility-limited technical worker with local knowledge;
- two apprentices/young workers whose status rivalry changes under pressure;
- service/cargo worker whose time-sensitive delivery tests cargo assumptions;
- local emergency/maintenance worker inside affected zone.

At least three need wants unrelated to survival.

## Public information / rumour
One early reassurance becomes outdated; civilians compare displays with local observation; rumour emerges because systems genuinely disagree. Do not make Aoife mandatory merely because media exists.

## Orbital geography
Show through action that physical nearness can still require several operational boundaries; alternative routes have different transit/environment costs; housing desirability depends on route reliability; maintenance staff know service topology passengers do not; one connector can disrupt daily life without destroying the city.

## Wonder / normality — mandatory repair
Before crisis include one lived wonder beat showing why orbital life is worth protecting, plus one ordinary humour beat tied to housing/route/food/work. No lore paragraph.

## Reader Advocate

- Failure cost: trapped people, connector, moral/professional identity.
- Success cost: clean career/housing future.
