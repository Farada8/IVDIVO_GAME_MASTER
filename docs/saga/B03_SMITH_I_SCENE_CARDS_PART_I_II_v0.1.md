# PART I — THE CALL BEFORE THE FALL

## CH1 — WRONG ADDRESS

### S001 — NORMAL EMERGENCY TRAFFIC
**POV:** Nika / tightly local opening.  
**Place:** Koren regional emergency centre.  
**Want:** clear the current queue accurately before worsening weather creates backlog.  
**Why now:** rain has raised ordinary call volume; no anomaly is expected.  
**Obstacle:** incomplete callers, route confusion, simultaneous routine incidents.  
**Action:** Nika performs normal 112-style questioning and dispatch discipline.  
**Turn:** an incoming caller gives an exact Upper Gorge location, injury description and request for help — but line metadata is impossible/incomplete.  
**Change:** anomaly enters through normal work, not spectacle.  
**Setup:** calls are recorded; location, caller wording and background sound are preserved.  
**Forbidden:** spooky voice effects, prophecy terminology.

### S002 — EMPTY SITE
**POV:** Jana.  
**Want:** verify whether the call represents an actual person in danger without wasting scarce teams.  
**Why now:** caller sounded credible; mountain weather makes delay costly.  
**Obstacle:** responders reach the marked location and find no crash, casualty or missing person.  
**Tactic:** expand search radius / cross-check road cameras, vehicle reports and local patrol knowledge.  
**Turn:** nothing supports the reported event.  
**Price:** resources/time diverted from real weather preparation.  
**Exit:** Jana orders the event logged as unexplained false emergency, not “supernatural.”

### S003 — THE LATER ECHO
**POV:** Jana/Nika via incident flow.  
**Want:** handle a later ordinary accident nearby.  
**Obstacle:** details begin matching the earlier call: injury type, sound environment, partial location geometry.  
**Turn:** the earlier recording contains a sound/event detail that could not reasonably have been known.  
**Change:** impossible call becomes evidence rather than nuisance.  
**Exit pull:** if the call came first, what exactly was it a recording of?

## CH2 — THE CALL THAT WORKS

### S004 — SECOND WARNING
**POV:** Nika.  
**Want:** decide whether to escalate an impossible second call or treat it as contamination/hoax.  
**Resistance:** first call wasted resources; credibility risk.  
**Tactic:** applies stricter verification questions and checks independent feeds.  
**Turn:** caller gives a detail about a vehicle/route not yet publicly known but operationally plausible.  
**Change:** Nika escalates to Jana rather than dismissing.

### S005 — BOUNDED RESPONSE
**POV:** Jana.  
**Want:** use the warning without moving the whole rescue system around it.  
**Why now:** if true, minutes matter.  
**Obstacle:** warning could again be empty; sending a full team has opportunity cost.  
**Choice:** dispatch one bounded unit and alert nearby resources without mass mobilisation.  
**Turn:** unit encounters an emerging incident before casualties worsen.  
**Result:** people are genuinely saved.  
**Relationship delta:** Nika’s trust in warning information rises; Jana’s skepticism becomes conditional rather than dismissive.

### S006 — MORAL VALUE
**POV:** Jana.  
**Want:** preserve professional discipline after an impossible success.  
**Obstacle:** responders now ask why the service should ever ignore such calls.  
**Action:** Jana creates a temporary enhanced-verification rule, not blind obedience.  
**Turn:** warning information now has institutional moral weight.  
**Exit:** the next warning will be harder to ignore.
