## 1. Current Story Engine

FIRST CROSSING is generated by one complete emergency in which previously separate competencies and jurisdictions become indispensable to the same immediate human problem.

Core pressure field:

`HUMAN PROCEDURE / ORBITAL AUTHORITY / LEGACY AUTHORITY / CONFEDERATION KNOWLEDGE / YOUTH CONSEQUENCE`

This is **not** a council-room crossover, a protected-person plot, a kidnapping/protection plot, or formation of a permanent franchise team.

### Operational premise — WORKING
A young orbital dispatcher/trainee must execute an evacuation through a closed sector. Smith holds or receives access to an old **EMERGENCY ANNEX** that appears to preserve a narrow pre-Confederation override for exactly this class of emergency. Orbital security regards that authority as obsolete, non-binding or dangerously under-specified. A Confederation cadet contributes one tightly bounded technical/epistemic correction showing that isolation — the apparently safest response — is technically wrong. The active institutional opponent is a lawful orbital security director whose duty is to prevent an unknown factor from entering an inhabited system.

The crossing matters only if no line can solve the emergency alone.

## 2. Story Core — Restored, Not Yet Complete

### HERO — WORKING / EXACT IDENTITY UNKNOWN
A young orbital dispatcher/trainee with a real operational role. Their usefulness comes from actual work, local routes, timing, access rules, people they know and decisions they are authorized — or almost authorized — to make. They are not a chosen one, protected subject or policy prodigy.

### WANT — UNKNOWN / CHARACTER PASS REQUIRED
Do not confuse the operational task with the private dramatic WANT.

Operational objective: execute the evacuation through the closed sector and get specific people to safety.

Private WANT must come from the hero’s ordinary life, status, work, family, friendship or future and must exist before the emergency.

### WHY NOW — WORKING EVENT / EXACT TRIGGER UNKNOWN
An emergency requires movement through a sector that orbital procedure has closed. At the same time Smith’s legacy Emergency Annex becomes potentially actionable. The exact physical incident that creates this need remains UNKNOWN and must be designed causally rather than chosen for spectacle.

### OPPOSITION — WORKING
A lawful orbital security director plus incompatible procedures/authority.

The director’s duty is rational: prevent an unknown factor from entering or propagating through an inhabited orbital system. They must have evidence, responsibility, adaptive tactics and something real to lose if they are wrong.

### WRONG STRATEGY — UNKNOWN
Must be the hero’s own active strategy, not merely institutional disagreement around them.

### PRICE — UNKNOWN
Must be personal, irreversible enough to matter, and tied to the hero’s ordinary life rather than abstract civilization stakes.

### MIDPOINT — WORKING STRUCTURAL CANDIDATE
The Confederation cadet provides one constrained technical/epistemic correction showing that continued isolation is not neutral safety: under the actual conditions, isolation itself worsens or guarantees the dangerous outcome. The cadet supplies knowledge, not command authority and not a turnkey technological solution.

### CLIMAX CHOICE — UNKNOWN
Must be a concrete action the HERO performs or authorizes under pressure. It cannot be officials reaching agreement. Smith, the orbital director and the cadet can change the option set, but none may take the protagonist’s defining choice away.

### RESOLUTION — REQUIRED
The B08 emergency must be fully closed inside this novel. Legal/institutional consequences may persist, but the main story cannot stop at a new committee, treaty negotiation or sequel hook.

### SERIES HOOK — BLANK BY DESIGN
Open only after the B08 main conflict resolves.

## 4. Smith / Old Earth Security Function

Smith is not automatically the protagonist.

He contributes:
- knowledge of the Annex and the system that created it;
- provenance and limits of the old authority;
- understanding of what historical emergency it was designed for;
- awareness of past success, compromise or wrongdoing attached to that authority;
- willingness to act professionally under uncertainty.

Key dramatic question:

**WHY, and under what narrow conditions, did this pre-Confederation emergency authority survive into the orbital era?**

Required constraint:
The Annex cannot mean “Smith can overrule everyone.” If it does, the story collapses into a magic credential. Its value must come from one narrow circumstance that current law/procedure failed to replace cleanly.

## 5. Orbital Authority / Opposition

### WORKING OPPONENT FUNCTION
A lawful orbital security director, not a villain caricature.

They require:
- a concrete duty;
- evidence for closure/isolation;
- authority that is real in the present system;
- a defensible fear of contamination/propagation/unknown entry;
- adaptive tactics as new evidence appears;
- personal/institutional price for allowing an exception;
- a line they will not cross;
- at least one point where they are more correct than Smith or the hero.

The opposition becomes weak if the director simply ignores obvious evidence so the heroes can win an argument.

## 6. Limited Confederation Cadet Function

The cadet may:
- recognize one physical/system relation humans have modeled incorrectly;
- translate that finding into human-usable evidence;
- explain a limit or consequence of isolation;
- make the choice space harder and more accurate.

The cadet may **not**:
- command human orbital security;
- arrest, overrule or govern human institutions;
- deploy unrestricted Confederation technology to end the crisis;
- become the smartest person who solves every layer;
- replace the hero’s climax action.

**Knowledge is not jurisdiction.**

## 7. Youth / Orbital-Life Function

The young protagonist must live inside orbital civilization before the emergency arrives.

Required story pressure where relevant:
- shift/work status;
- money or career consequence;
- housing/commute/access;
- friend or romantic pressure;
- family expectation/distance;
- embarrassment/status;
- boredom/routine before crisis;
- a mistake with social as well as operational consequence.

The hero must cause the plot, not merely carry messages between Smith, orbital security and the cadet.

Teen/young-adult dialogue must not become an adult policy committee.

### Candidate A — IRI / LOCAL WORKER → OPERATIONS TRAINEE — WORKING LEAD

**Relationship pressure:** the lawful orbital security director is not a stranger villain but the senior professional who sponsored or approved Iri’s operations track. Iri genuinely wants this person’s trust.

**Why this is strong:** Smith supplies legacy authority; cadet supplies one epistemic correction; orbital director supplies real present authority/opposition; Iri supplies the indispensable local action and pays the personal price.

## 13. Exact Emergency Incident Competition v0.1

### Option E1 — THE SEALED TRANSFER SPINE — WORKING LEAD

**Observable event:** an outer orbital transfer/service sector registers a legacy-class anomalous signature during routine movement. Orbital security seals the sector before the unknown factor reaches the inhabited city.

**Immediate consequence:** after isolation, systems inside the sealed volume begin developing repeatable timing/control distortions. People can still live there briefly, but safe occupancy time is shrinking. The closure that protects the city is also making the isolated sector progressively less stable.

**Evacuation problem:** the only route capable of moving the trapped population at required throughput crosses the closed transfer spine. Exterior removal is unavailable or too slow under the current geometry/traffic/environment constraints.

**Old Earth contribution:** Smith identifies the signature class or operational pattern in an Emergency Annex written after an older event. The Annex does NOT explain the ontology. It records a narrow empirical rule: under specified conditions, hard isolation must not be maintained; a controlled transit corridor must remain dynamically open.

**Confederation contribution:** the cadet recognizes why the old empirical rule worked. The unknown effect is not behaving like ordinary contamination carried by people or cargo. The sealed boundary creates or intensifies the dangerous coupling. This is one correction, not a full solution.

**Orbital director’s rational position:** opening the sector may admit an unknown factor into a populated system. The old Annex is incomplete, historically secret and legally disputed. The cadet has knowledge but no human jurisdiction. The director therefore orders continued isolation while preparing to sacrifice/detach the affected sector if thresholds worsen.

**Hero necessity:** only the local operations/dispatch system can physically sequence the route, partitions and convoy. Smith cannot do it. The cadet cannot command it. The director will not authorize it. The hero knows the lived/local routing constraints and must execute.

**Climax action:** activate the narrow LAST OVERRIDE: not a magic code, but a disputed legacy emergency clause protecting one local operator decision when present closure doctrine and the Annex conflict under defined casualty thresholds. The operator still must perform the route correctly and becomes personally auditable/accountable.

**Resolution requirement:** the controlled evacuation proves the interaction model, collapses the dangerous sealed-state condition, and closes the physical emergency. The legal legitimacy of the Annex can remain disputed only after nobody is still trapped and the main danger is over.

**Red Team:**
- Avoid abstract “field topology” lectures. Show sensor/time/control symptoms first.
- Do not make the anomaly a monster.
- Do not make Smith’s archive perfectly explanatory.
- Do not let the cadet predict everything.
- Define why exterior evacuation is unavailable before the crisis, not as a late convenience.
- Define hard thresholds for when sacrifice/detachment becomes rational.

**Score:** 47/50 provisional.

**Strengths:** clean clock; understandable security logic; strong visual hook.

**Weaknesses:** shifts too much story onto the returning vessel and Smith’s prior operation; youth dispatcher risks becoming a remote traffic controller. It also drifts toward a protected-vehicle/protected-people structure.

**Score:** 40/50.

**Strengths:** very legible physical action.

**Weaknesses:** relies on coincidence between a current debris emergency and an unrelated legacy mystery. The crossing feels assembled rather than inevitable.

**Score:** 33/50.
