**Status:** WORKING / NOT CANON / NOT FROZEN  
**Parent engine:** THE LAST OVERRIDE / LEGACY EMERGENCY ANNEX  
**Emergency:** THE SEALED TRANSFER SPINE

## Map law
Every chapter must answer WHO WANTS WHAT / WHY NOW / WHAT STOPS THEM / WHAT CHANGES.

Main dramatic chain:
`WANT CERTIFICATION + STABLE ADULT LIFE → TRUST CURRENT PROCEDURE → EXECUTE HARD SEAL → SEAL WORSENS CRISIS → DEFEND PROCEDURE LONGER THAN EVIDENCE JUSTIFIES → DISCOVER OES HISTORY + CADET CORRECTION → FACE RATIONAL DETACHMENT CHOICE → ACCEPT PERSONAL LIABILITY → EXECUTE MOVING SEGMENTED CORRIDOR → RESCUE SUCCEEDS → LOSE CLEAN CAREER/HOUSING FUTURE → GAIN HARDER PROFESSIONAL AGENCY.`

# PART I — THE LIFE THE HERO WANTS

## Ch1 — THE GOOD ROUTE
Hero wants R1 friend to agree that a housing option near the operations route is worth the cost. Permanent-certification review approaches. Friend sees hero building the whole future around one sponsor/placement. They keep the option open but do not treat certification as guaranteed. Housing, money, commute and humour exist before anomaly.

## Ch2 — BORING WORK
Hero wants a flawless transfer-spine shift under Director observation. A minor dashboard/physical-state mismatch delays routine traffic. Maintenance history lets hero fix the mundane issue and earns trust. This establishes local panels/topology before climax and provides a lived orbital wonder beat.

## Ch3 — PEOPLE WHO LIVE HERE
Peak movement period introduces recurring civilians through ordinary wants: shift work, family timing, cargo deadline, apprentices, social plans. Hero makes a small status mistake by dismissing a complaint as transition noise and gets corrected/teased.

## Ch4 — REVIEW WINDOW
Director tests whether hero can manage uncertainty. Hero gives the clean procedural answer instead of admitting a nagging discrepancy. Director rewards them with more consequential responsibility. False belief is rewarded.

## Ch5 — FIRST DISAGREEMENT
Independent systems disagree about command order during live transfer. Hero escalates correctly but classifies the mismatch as non-blocking pending review. Director orders heightened monitoring.

## Ch6 — HARD SEAL
The anomaly crosses a legitimate security threshold. Director wants to protect the inhabited habitat; hero wants to execute perfectly. Hero lawfully creates the full static isolation state. City-side risk appears contained; hero receives professional approval.

# PART II — THE CORRECT DECISION GOES WRONG

## Ch7 — FALSE SUCCESS
Hero stabilizes the trapped population while review proceeds. Inside the sealed assembly, timestamps/door states begin disagreeing faster. Hero notices worsening after seal but lacks proof of causation.

## Ch8 — THE OUTSIDE WAY
Director chooses conservative rescue via exterior craft. It saves real people but throughput is too slow. Civilian priority conflicts expose ordinary orbital life rather than generic disaster extras.

## Ch9 — THE OLD WARNING
Smith introduces a narrow Emergency Annex whose hazard signature resembles the present event. Director admits it as evidence subject to authentication, not current authority. Hero sees it as a challenge to local institutional legitimacy.

## Ch10 — CLEAN CHAIN
A worker inside reports that a brief manual cycle reduced mismatch. Hero logs it but treats it as anecdotal because certification pressure makes clean present procedure emotionally meaningful. Wrong strategy deepens without stupidity.

## Ch11 — AUTHENTIC, NOT BINDING
Annex provenance is authenticated while the historical summary still calls the old case successful containment. E1 begins. Present applicability remains unproven; hero feels vindicated in waiting.

## Ch12 — THE PRICE OF WAITING
Official messages become outdated as indicators and physical states disagree. Missed shifts, medication, childcare, food and wage consequences appear. Hero sees that “holding pattern” itself is costly.

# PART III — THE MODEL BREAKS

## Ch13 — E2
Independent safety systems issue incompatible next-step sequences. Inner-spine occupants are increasingly unreachable by exterior rescue. Detachment becomes a real future contingency.

## Ch14 — THE CONTRADICTION IN THE FILE
Smith investigates why the Annex demands dynamic passage while the old summary celebrates static containment. He discovers restricted/compartmentalized evidence implying OES’s clean narrative is incomplete.

## Ch15 — THE CADET’S QUESTION
Cadet proposes one falsifiable relation: if static synchronized isolation is part of the hazard, temporarily changing one bounded cell-pair state should reduce an independent mismatch measure. Hero resents that cadet can be right and later leave the human consequences.

## Ch16 — MIDPOINT TEST
Director authorizes the smallest safe diagnostic cycle. Hero/local operators execute it. The predicted mismatch drops and then returns when the static state resumes. Static isolation is no longer neutral safety. MIDPOINT.

## Ch17 — THE FIRST STATIC SEAL
Smith discloses the hidden OES after-action record: OES’s old seal worsened the event, local evidence was discounted, secrecy delayed rescue, and a moving segmented passage eventually saved people. OES loses moral leverage but gains epistemic honesty.

## Ch18 — STILL NOT SAFE
Hero wants a corridor now. Director correctly distinguishes “closure harms them” from “opening toward the city is safe.” Full corridor is refused; exterior extraction increases; detachment preparation begins. Opposition strengthens after truth.

# PART IV — RESPONSIBILITY HAS A COST

## Ch19 — LOCAL LOGS
Hero investigates local logs/manual-cycle records using legitimate maintenance knowledge. Role conflict narrows access. Evidence supports boundary-state interaction but cannot prove zero propagation risk.

## Ch20 — THE FUTURE THEY PLANNED
R1 friend wants hero not to destroy years of work on uncertain evidence. Hero wants understanding. Argument reveals certification is emotionally being used as proof of adulthood/trust. No clean reconciliation.

## Ch21 — WHO GETS OUT FIRST
Exterior evacuation reaches its practical ceiling while priority disputes sharpen. A majority will still remain at detachment decision time. Director’s conservative approach is useful but insufficient.

## Ch22 — THE CORRIDOR PROPOSAL
Hero proposes a segmented moving corridor with independent receiving-side monitoring. Director refuses because one wrong packet could expose the city. Hero is removed from contested routing functions.

## Ch23 — KNOWLEDGE IS NOT PERMISSION
Hero wants Smith or Cadet to tell them the corridor is justified. Both refuse: Smith authenticates history but does not command; cadet states physics/uncertainty but not legality. Hero loses the option of transferring responsibility upward.

## Ch24 — PREPARING TO LOSE THE SECTOR
Director prepares controlled detachment: redundant pressure barriers, utility isolation, independent outer-node supply, structural couplers, station-keeping craft. The threat becomes physical and partly irreversible.

## Ch25 — THE CLEAN EXIT
Director offers hero a career-saving stand-down while senior command owns detachment. Standing down preserves the desired future by transferring responsibility after hero helped create the harmful static state. Hero cannot accept that psychologically.

## Ch26 — WHAT THE JOB ACTUALLY MEANS
Hero reconstructs the narrow defensible action space: local life-safety control + disputed Annex exception + current evidence + immutable audit. Hero chooses named local responsibility, not broader anti-authority rebellion.

# PART V — THE LAST OVERRIDE

## Ch27 — LOCAL CONTROL
Normal network credentials are withdrawn. Hero reaches/activates a physically local life-safety panel, cites the disputed Annex basis, transfers one cell to local control and creates an immutable operator record. Career point of no return.

## Ch28 — FIRST PACKET
Hero moves 8–16 people through the smallest segmented corridor capable of producing decisive evidence: enter cell, close/verify behind, open next only after verification. No full path to the city exists at once.

## Ch29 — FALSE ALARM
Receiving-side monitoring flags a possible propagation event. If real, corridor must stop. Independent verification shows whether the alarm is genuine or an unrelated transient without changing the anomaly rules. Route continues only on evidence.

## Ch30 — D4
First-packet data shows both required conditions: isolated-side mismatch falls and no corresponding receiving-side propagation appears across independent sensors. Director’s own tactic-revision threshold is crossed. They change tactics immediately.

## Ch31 — RESCUE AUTHORITY
Director now uses legitimate authority to allocate crews, triage, receiving capacity and traffic around the validated local sequence. Opposition becomes cooperation without institutional merger.

## Ch32 — THE STUCK CELL
A transfer cell develops a rule-consistent local actuator/verification failure. Hero’s maintenance knowledge from Ch2 plus an inside maintenance worker restore or route around the local control problem. Old maintenance identity becomes asset rather than something to outgrow.

## Ch33 — LAST PEOPLE THROUGH
Final trapped group cannot simply move because of a concrete ordinary obligation/relationship, not generic panic. Local knowledge resolves the conflict. Population clears before E4.

## Ch34 — END THE STATIC STATE
Hero + Director terminate the harmful fixed isolation state in safe sequence. The mismatch collapses below emergency threshold; detachment is no longer needed as human-sacrifice measure. MAIN PHYSICAL CONFLICT CLOSED.

# PART VI — THE COST IS REAL

## Ch35 — SUSPENDED
Hero is relieved, operational access suspended and certification progression paused. Director’s factual report records both the disputed/unauthorized action and evidence that it prevented the worse outcome. No revenge, no instant promotion.

## Ch36 — AFTER THE RESCUE
Smith preserves/discloses the compromised OES history rather than restoring a clean myth. Cadet leaves without jurisdictional reward. R1 friend and hero lose/delay the planned housing future and design a smaller/less convenient next plan. Director sponsorship ends or hardens into factual respect. Hero accepts review and an uncertain career. People are safe; main emergency is complete. Series hook remains blank until later saga architecture earns it.
