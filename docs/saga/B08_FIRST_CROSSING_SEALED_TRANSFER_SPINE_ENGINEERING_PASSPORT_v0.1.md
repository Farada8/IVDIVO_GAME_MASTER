## Functional geometry

`MAIN HABITAT / RECEIVING CONCOURSE ↔ CITY-SIDE STRUCTURAL NECK ↔ SIX-CELL TRANSFER SPINE ↔ OUTER TRANSFER NODE ↔ EXTERIOR RESCUE`

The spine is a peripheral mobility/logistics connector, not a residential district. Required functions survive any later geometry change:
- several independently sealable pressure/fire cells;
- no need to open the whole route at once;
- adjacent maintenance/service path with local panels;
- city-side and outer-side isolation points;
- controlled structural separation from the inhabited habitat.

## Local manual life-safety control
A physically present qualified operator can control one adjacent transfer cell if central automation/network synchronization becomes unreliable. Local panels can close/open one certified passage, transfer one cell to local life-safety control, execute a bounded emergency sequence, and write immutable audit logs. They cannot cancel quarantine, open the whole city, erase logs, command security, or authorize detachment.

This is the protagonist's climax instrument, not a secret superpower.

## The Last Override — segmented moving corridor
The route is not one open tube into the city. A packet moves cell by cell:
1. group enters a cell;
2. previous boundary closes and is locally verified;
3. only then does the next cell open;
4. at least one verified barrier remains between the moving group and the receiving system;
5. receiving-side independent sensors watch for propagation;
6. the corridor can halt at any cell.

The moving boundary simultaneously tests the Annex's empirical rule, the cadet's model and the hero's local competence.

## Detachment
The spine/outer node is connected through a deliberately separable structural neck for catastrophic emergencies. Preparation includes redundant pressure isolation, utility decoupling, independent emergency supply, structural coupler release and controlled station-keeping separation.

Detachment is controlled emergency decoupling, not a Hollywood explosion.

It protects the larger habitat but may condemn remaining occupants because the outer assembly has bounded independent endurance while the control disagreement continues to worsen.
