## 1. Story Core

### HERO — WORKING CONDITIONAL LEAD
Former local maintenance-apprentice function, currently Iri in working Book-2 material, later training in orbital operations/dispatch. Usefulness comes from legitimate local work: service topology, emergency routing, manual life-safety controls, people, schedules and operating constraints.

### PRIVATE WANT
Earn permanent operations certification and a stable adult place in the habitat on their own professional record.

### WHY NOW
During routine movement through an outer transfer/service spine, independent systems begin reporting sequence/timing disagreement. Orbital security reasonably seals the sector. The hero executes the seal cleanly. The fully static isolation state then strengthens the dangerous control mismatch instead of containing it.

### OPPOSITION
Primary active opponent: a lawful orbital security director who sponsored or approved the hero’s move into operations. Their goal is rational: prevent an unknown factor from propagating into the inhabited city while minimizing losses in the isolated sector.

The deeper opposition is asymmetric risk: opening the corridor may expose the city; keeping it closed increasingly makes the isolated sector uninhabitable.

### WRONG STRATEGY
Because the hero wants professional legitimacy, they over-trust the current chain of authority. They classify early local timing discrepancies as non-blocking transition noise, prioritize current procedure over Smith’s legally awkward legacy warning, and complete the full hard seal before the evidence is strong enough to justify defiance.

### CAUSAL ERROR
The hero materially worsens the crisis through competent action: the static synchronized isolation boundary is the condition amplifying sealed-state discoherence.

### PRICE
To correct the mistake, the hero must knowingly destroy the clean career path they were trying to secure. Executing the Last Override means suspension/review, loss of the director’s sponsorship and probable loss of the preferred placement attached to certification. The profession may remain open, but the future they planned is gone.

### MIDPOINT
A limited Confederation cadet identifies a physical relation humans have modeled incorrectly and makes one falsifiable prediction: briefly changing the boundary state should reduce one measurable component of the dangerous condition rather than spread it. A controlled test supports the prediction.

This changes knowledge, not jurisdiction. The cadet cannot command the habitat and cannot provide a turnkey technological rescue.

### POST-MIDPOINT REVERSAL
The orbital director accepts that the cadet may be technically correct but still refuses a full corridor because the downside is asymmetric: one wrong opening could endanger the inhabited city, whereas continued closure sacrifices a smaller sector. Opposition therefore strengthens after the reveal instead of collapsing.

### CRISIS
The director begins preparing detachment of the affected transfer sector before control coherence falls below safe-separation limits. This may save the city but would strand or condemn remaining occupants and permanently sacrifice infrastructure.

The hero is offered a career-saving exit: stand down, preserve certification, let senior command own the detachment.

### CLIMAX CHOICE
The hero refuses the clean exit. Smith has established the narrow provenance and empirical limits of the Emergency Annex; the cadet has established the physical correction; neither can execute the local action.

The hero accepts named operator responsibility and uses established manual/local life-safety routing knowledge to initiate the first narrow segment of the Last Override: reopen a controlled evacuation corridor through the sealed transfer spine against the standing isolation order.

### CLIMAX MECHANISM
The first corridor segment is a measurable operational test under rescue conditions. Live data must show both:
1. the dangerous isolated-state condition is reducing as predicted;
2. no propagation appears in the receiving system.

When those thresholds change, the orbital director changes tactics immediately and uses legitimate present authority to expand/support the evacuation.

### RESOLUTION
The physical emergency is fully closed inside B08. Remaining occupants are evacuated; the dangerous state is reduced/terminated by ending the harmful static-boundary condition according to established rules; the larger city is not contaminated/propagated into.

The hero is relieved and formally suspended/reviewed after the rescue. No instant promotion and no heroic exemption. The director files both the violation and the evidence that the action prevented the worse outcome. Sponsorship ends or changes fundamentally. Smith and the hero end with harder professional respect, not recruitment. The cadet leaves without acquiring human jurisdiction.

### CHANGE
The hero’s new operating belief is not “break rules when you feel right.” It is:

`SOURCE OF KNOWLEDGE ≠ SOURCE OF AUTHORITY.`

A responsible operator must distinguish evidence from institutional comfort, record uncertainty, act only within a defensible local responsibility when procedures conflict, and accept the consequences afterward.

BLANK BY DESIGN. Open only after the B08 main conflict is closed and only from earned consequences.
