# B07 — THE UNLISTED CITY

## Batch 87 — Parallel Strands / Convergence / Nonlinear Evidence Gate

Status: WORKING APPLICATION
Decision: KEEP STORY ENGINE / DO NOT REBUILD FOR COMPLEXITY

## Core principle

B07 should not become more complex merely because IVDIVO's universe is large.

`ONE MAIN STORY ≠ ONE POV`, but every substantial strand must change the same dominant causal engine.

## Proposed strand audit

### Kaira / procedural responsibility

Owns:
- ambition / placement pressure;
- procedural model;
- bounded authority;
- transition from procedural perfection to evidence-based responsibility and bounded discretion.

Must causally affect:
- treatment of the unlisted/distributed life;
- institutional response;
- final exception/decision architecture.

### Evidence / anomaly strand

Owns:
- traces;
- source status;
- competing interpretations;
- controlled tests;
- proof ceiling.

Must not become:
- ontology lecture;
- archive dump;
- convenient confirmation of Kaira's preferred theory.

### Nonhuman / distributed conscious life

If given direct or indirect strand ownership, it must have:
- its own preference/want;
- consent/refusal capacity;
- actions that humans cannot substitute;
- relationships or commitments beyond being rescued;
- at least one indispensable decision in the climax.

It must not exist as an exotic POV camera.

### Institutional / cadet / adult countermodel

Must retain:
- actual competence;
- evidence;
- real public/safety benefit;
- understandable constraints;
- a blind spot or category limit rather than cartoon villainy.

## Convergence Gate

Do not count a briefing or shared room as convergence.

Real convergence requires:

`STRAND A ACTION/EVIDENCE → CHANGES STRAND B OPTIONS → FORCES NEW DECISION → SHARED ENGINE MOVES`.

Examples of acceptable convergence:
- a Kaira procedural choice changes what evidence can legally/ethically be gathered;
- the nonhuman entity refuses a proposed continuity solution, invalidating the human plan;
- institutional evidence proves that a seemingly compassionate exception would create a different real risk;
- a cadet's technical action creates a trace that changes classification and therefore authority.

## Nonlinear evidence option

B07 may use archive fragments, incident records, prior classifications, or nonchronological evidence only if the reader can eventually reconstruct:

`WHAT HAPPENED → WHO RECORDED IT → WHEN → WHAT THEY THOUGHT IT MEANT → WHAT LATER EVIDENCE CHANGES THAT MODEL`.

Do not use shuffled evidence as artificial opacity.

## POV allocation

Do not add POV merely to equalize Kaira, cadets, adults, robots, and the unlisted life.

A new POV is justified only if it gives unique causal work that cannot be achieved honestly through the current perspective.

## Existing B07 protections retained

- Ch6 retains ownership of the true inciting disaster; do not move catastrophe to Ch1 merely for hook intensity.
- middle pressure must rotate function; avoid `anomaly → briefing → anomaly → briefing`.
- climax should favor bounded, documented discretion over louder spectacle.
- the nonhuman life must exercise agency in the decisive sequence.
- local book problem must close before saga hook.
- proof ceiling remains below universal claims about souls, dimensional worlds, or transferable consciousness unless independently earned.

## Canon firewall

Imported Bell examples involving rent, business, or money are structural examples only.

B07 must not reintroduce salary/rent/credits into ordinary orbital life. Use genuine nonfungible stakes: placement, responsibility, trust, time, access to a unique mission, family independence, consent, reputation, relationships, evidence integrity, safety.

No operational human consciousness-transfer/body-swap technology is authorized by this craft source.

## Acceptance test

Before any new B07 strand or POV is added, answer:

1. Who owns it?
2. What does that person/entity want locally?
3. What unique knowledge/action/relationship does it contribute?
4. What changes because of it?
5. Which other strand's options does it alter?
6. Where does it converge causally?
7. Could it be removed with no change to the climax?

If answer 7 is yes, compress, merge, or bank it for another book.
