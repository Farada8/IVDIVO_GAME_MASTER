# B07 — THE UNLISTED CITY
## Batch 92 Troubleshooting · Flashback · Tool-Routing Application Gate

Version: v0.1 DRAFT
Date: 2026-08-18
Decision: KEEP STORY ENGINE / REPAIR CANON REGRESSIONS / DO NOT REBUILD

## Protected engine
Kaira's strongest developmental line remains:

`PROCEDURAL PRECISION -> EVIDENCE EXCEEDS CATEGORY -> BOUNDED DISCRETION -> DOCUMENTED ACCOUNTABILITY`.

The distributed / unlisted nonhuman person is not cargo or a revelation-object. Personhood requires independent preference, evidence/model, consent/refusal and at least one indispensable decision in the climax.

## 1. Diagnose before adding plot
When B07 feels slow or underpowered, first test:
- duplicated anomaly/briefing function;
- repeated procedure;
- weak consequence;
- missing relationship pressure;
- evidence-status not changing;
- scene that should be summary;
- writer fatigue.

Do NOT treat fatigue with a new villain, death, betrayal, institution, secret technology or second mystery.

## 2. Flashback gate for Kaira
Kaira's procedural history, family expectations and prior training may appear only at the narrative resolution required by the present.

Preferred order:
`present trigger -> short relevant memory/summary -> current interpretation/choice changes`.

A full flashback must materially change current evidence, relationship, responsibility or decision. Do not create a museum of formative incidents merely to explain why Kaira values procedure.

## 3. No Past-Explains-Person
Kaira is not reducible to one parental conflict, one humiliating mistake or one academy event.

Her character should emerge from repeated present behavior:
- how she classifies uncertainty;
- what she does when procedure works;
- what she does when it fails;
- what she does when two legitimate obligations conflict;
- whether she documents her own exception honestly.

## 4. Tangent / new-technology gate
Any newly discovered subplot, device, academy practice or institution must pass:

`CANON -> CHARACTER TRUTH -> DOMINANT B07 ENGINE -> DOWNSTREAM CONSEQUENCE -> PERSONHOOD -> KEEP/BANK/DISCARD`.

Interesting technology that does not change Kaira's choices, the evidence chain, the nonhuman person's agency or the endgame is an idea-bank asset, not B07 canon.

## 5. Post-money firewall
No salary, rent, debt, credits or disguised purchasing-power hierarchy.

Use genuine nonfungible pressures:
- Human Contact placement;
- qualification / training window;
- trusted responsibility;
- particular living arrangement or family independence;
- specific mission/team position;
- time;
- consent;
- reputation;
- relationship;
- safety;
- evidence integrity.

## 6. Consciousness-transfer firewall
No routine human transfer into service bodies or continuity grids unless separately Founder-approved by later canon.

If a scene needs remote or hazardous embodiment, prefer canon-compatible alternatives such as telepresence, nonsentient external frames, protective systems, or independent conscious robots acting by their own consent.

## 7. Consciousness-school epistemic firewall
If specialist practitioners appear:

`OBSERVATION -> REPRODUCIBLE PRACTICE EFFECT -> SCHOOL INTERPRETATION -> COMPETING INTERPRETATION -> POSSIBLE ONTOLOGICAL CLAIM`.

The school's terminology cannot become proof of dimensional worlds or soul mechanics merely because the culture is technologically advanced.

## 8. Scene routing
For each candidate scene:

`PRIOR CONSEQUENCE -> OWNER -> PRESENT WANT -> PRESSURE -> CHANGED ACTION/KNOWLEDGE/RELATION -> RESULT -> NEXT LINK`.

If exact dialogue/action does not matter, compress procedure, transit, repeated training and briefing into summary/indirect mode.

## 9. Anti-fragment protection
Do not turn surgical trimming into one-line paragraph confetti.

Use:
`SCENE NECESSITY -> SHOW/TELL -> POV -> DIALOGUE MODE -> PARAGRAPH INTENT -> READER-STATE CONTINUITY -> SENTENCE RHYTHM`.

Preserve orientation and complete thought-units. Short lines remain only when they earn contrast, revelation, pause, timing or end-weight.

## 10. Endgame personhood
The final solution must not be `Kaira correctly classifies the nonhuman person and saves it`.

Stronger:
`Kaira creates a bounded accountable option space -> nonhuman person supplies/refuses/chooses an indispensable condition -> human system responds to that agency -> consequence/aftermath`.

This preserves Kaira's competence without making another consciousness an object of her moral growth.

## Final verdict
No structural rebuild is authorized by Batch 92.

Use the new gates to prevent:
- flashback over-explanation;
- tangent canon drift;
- writer-fatigue escalation;
- plot-puppet behavior;
- post-money regression;
- consciousness-transfer regression;
- personhood collapse.

B07 remains a viable engine pending these repairs and the existing canon corrections.
