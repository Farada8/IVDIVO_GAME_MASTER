# ORBITAL YOUTH — BATCH 58 CAMBRIDGE APPLICATION GATE
## Post-Scarcity · Robot Personhood · SF Mode · Politics · Epistemology

Status: WORKING / ADDITIVE — DO NOT REBUILD LOCKED MATERIAL WITHOUT A CONCRETE DEFECT
Date: 2026-08-17

## VERDICT
Use Cambridge as a **genre-sanity and ideology firewall**, not as a new plot template.

The active Orbital Youth rule remains:
`ONE MAIN STORY + SPECIFIC HUMAN/NONHUMAN PRESSURE + ORBITAL DIFFERENCE THAT CHANGES CAUSALITY`.

## 1. POST-MONEY LIFE TEST
Before prose, for each major youth stake ask:
- Is this actually scarce/non-fungible?
- Can robotized production simply manufacture more of it?
- If yes, why is the character treating it like money-era scarcity?

Preferred stakes:
- one specific person's trust;
- time/attention;
- membership or responsibility in a concrete team;
- unique mission/experience;
- consent/relationship;
- reputation after a public choice;
- access that is limited for safety/competence, not artificial purchasing power;
- physically unique compatible location/time window.

Forbidden shortcut: rename money as `credits`, `points`, `allowance` or status tokens and reconstruct consumer scarcity.

## 2. FUTURE-NORMALITY TEST
The world should reveal itself because a teenager:
- assumes something;
- makes a local mistake;
- jokes about a normal institution;
- gets blocked by a real compatibility/safety rule;
- disagrees with a conscious robot;
- chooses between two non-fungible commitments.

Do not pause to explain why post-money society is post-money.

## 3. CONSCIOUS ROBOT TEST
For every named conscious robot:
- What does it want that is not “help the humans”?
- Whom does it care about or disagree with outside the youth cohort?
- What can it refuse?
- What cost can it impose or accept?
- Does its speech/tactic change by relationship?

If the scene survives replacement with a smart terminal, rewrite the robot as a person or remove the pseudo-personality.

## 4. HUMAN/ROBOT RELATIONSHIP TEST
Do not use robots as an exotic B-story designed to teach a human a moral lesson. Require mutual pressure:
`TWO COMPLETE AGENTS → DIFFERENT INTERESTS/ASSUMPTIONS → CHOICE → COST → NEW TERMS OF RELATIONSHIP`.

## 5. ORBITAL WORLD CAUSALITY
For every major habitat/transport/gravity/environment rule:
`RULE → OPTION/CONSTRAINT → YOUTH CHOICE → CONSEQUENCE`.

If it produces only visual wonder, compress it.

## 6. POLITICAL/INSTITUTIONAL LEGITIMACY
Advanced administration does not eliminate disagreement. Where a collective choice affects persons, identify:
- decision authority;
- evidence access;
- appeal/review route;
- affected-person standing;
- robot-civilization standing where applicable;
- ability to refuse or negotiate.

“Optimization says so” cannot close a moral/political conflict.

## 7. COMPETENCE ≠ MORAL AUTHORITY
A technically brilliant cadet, engineer, adult or robot can be correct about mechanics and wrong about consent/distribution/legitimacy. Preserve competent countermodels.

## 8. CONSCIOUSNESS-SCHOOL EPISTEMIC GATE
Current orbital society has high external technology but limited development of consciousness/dimensional disciplines because long-term trained practitioners are few.

Every claim must be tagged:
`OBSERVED / REPRODUCED IN PRACTICE / SCHOOL INTERPRETATION / CONTESTED / UNKNOWN / WORLD FACT`.

No school lecture may silently become civilization-wide truth.

## 9. CONSCIOUSNESS-TRANSFER FIREWALL
Do not use:
- routine human consciousness uploads;
- body hopping;
- soul transfer as engineering;
- licensed teenage transfer into service/vacuum bodies.

Historical SF examples remain reference mechanisms only.

## 10. ONE MAIN STORY CHECK
Before accepting a book engine write one sentence in this form:
`[YOUNG PERSON/TEAM] must [CONCRETE LOCAL OUTCOME] because [SPECULATIVE/ORBITAL CONDITION] has changed [RELATIONSHIP/RESPONSIBILITY/ACCESS/SAFETY], and failure costs [NON-FUNGIBLE STAKE].`

If the sentence reduces to “they explore advanced orbital life,” reject the engine.

## 11. READER-EFFECT CHECK
For any intended critique ask what the story actually rewards. If the “bad” institution gets all competence, glamour and victories while the critique exists only in dialogue, the reader-effect structure contradicts the theme.

## 12. LESSON ZERO / LOCKED MATERIAL
This source does not by itself justify reopening development-locked Book One. Apply only when a concrete current-text defect is demonstrated.
