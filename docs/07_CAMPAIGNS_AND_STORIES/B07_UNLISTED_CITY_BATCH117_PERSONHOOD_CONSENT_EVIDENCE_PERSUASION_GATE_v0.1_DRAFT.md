# B07 — THE UNLISTED CITY
## BATCH 117 — PERSONHOOD · CONSENT · EVIDENCE · PERSUASION GATE

Date: 2026-08-19
Status: ADDITIVE PRODUCTION CONTROL / DO NOT REBUILD ENGINE

## Verdict

**KEEP STORY ENGINE. REPAIR / STRENGTHEN PERSONHOOD AND EVIDENCE CAUSALITY. DO NOT REBUILD FOR MORE SPECTACLE.**

The Wells *System Collapse* mechanism lab strengthens the existing B07 direction but does not authorize importing Murderbot technology, contract economy, alien infection, governor modules or cadence.

## 1. Personhood threshold before moral certainty

The distributed/unlisted life must not become a person merely because Kyra feels it is one.

Required progression:

`ANOMALOUS PATTERN → MULTIPLE TRACES → ALTERNATIVE NON-PERSON MODELS → REPEATED CHOICE-LIKE BEHAVIOR → RELATION-SPECIFIC / CONTEXT-SENSITIVE RESPONSE → COUNTEREVIDENCE → PERSONHOOD THRESHOLD`.

Before threshold:
- preserve uncertainty;
- avoid motive-language presented as fact;
- protect evidence from destruction;
- keep multiple interpretations alive.

After threshold:
- object-treatment becomes an ethical and causal problem;
- decision ownership must move toward the entity.

## 2. The entity must own an outcome-critical choice

Human characters may:
- protect continuity;
- open alternatives;
- prevent immediate destruction;
- translate evidence;
- explain risks;
- negotiate institutional space.

They may not legitimately determine the entity's future merely because their option is technically safest.

Required climax chain:

`HUMAN EVIDENCE → PERSONHOOD THRESHOLD → ENTITY'S OWN MODEL / PREFERENCE → CONSENT / REFUSAL → HUMAN PLAN MUST CHANGE → BOUNDED ACCOUNTABLE DECISION → AFTERMATH`.

Acceptance test:
If the entity's preference can be deleted and the human climax still works unchanged, personhood has not been causally written.

## 3. Capability to preserve is not ownership

Do not let Kyra's competence create paternalism by accident.

`I CAN SAVE / MOVE / PRESERVE YOU ≠ I MAY CHOOSE YOUR FUTURE`.

A technically excellent rescue can still be illegitimate if it removes the affected person's decision.

## 4. Control provenance before blame

If any nonhuman behavior is mediated by:
- damaged infrastructure;
- inherited protocols;
- coercive control;
- environmental coupling;
- another intelligence;
- human intervention;

then use:

`ACTION → CONTROL PROVENANCE → AVAILABLE CHOICE → MOTIVE HYPOTHESES → RESPONSIBILITY STATUS`.

Do not call an entity hostile, deceptive or unstable until available agency is distinguished from controlled output.

## 5. Evidence architecture

Every major personhood / continuity claim should carry:

- trace;
- source;
- source condition;
- observed vs reported status;
- interpretation;
- alternative interpretation;
- confidence;
- discriminating test;
- downstream action.

Operational ledger:

`WORLD FACT → TRACE → OBSERVER / SENSOR → INTERPRETATION → COMPETING MODEL → TEST → STATUS UPDATE → ACTION`.

## 6. Dangerous effect is not hostile intent

A distributed system can create risk without wanting harm.

Mandatory separation:

`EFFECT ≠ INTENT`.

This prevents a cheap shift from first-contact mystery into monster plot.

## 7. Institutional factions

No institution in B07 should behave as a single mind.

For every major institution, track:
- official rule;
- decision authority;
- operational staff;
- internal faction;
- private incentive;
- information asymmetry;
- who can refuse;
- who bears cost;
- appeal/review path.

A competent institution may be wrong about personhood while being right about physical risk. A dissenter may be right for self-serving reasons. Preserve mixed causality.

## 8. Persuasion is not proof

If Kyra or the team must convince an audience that the unlisted life is a person, they may need a comprehensible narrative rather than raw technical data.

Use:

`SOURCE MATERIAL → SELECTED EXPLANATION → HUMAN-LEGIBLE STORY / DEMONSTRATION → SOURCE PACKAGE → AUDIENCE UPDATE`.

But:

`EMOTIONAL LEGIBILITY ≠ EVIDENCE UPGRADE`.

A compelling presentation may cause investigation or policy change. It does not make an uncorroborated hypothesis true.

## 9. Real audience gate

For hearings / broadcasts / negotiations:

`WHO IS SPEAKING? → WHO IS NAMED? → WHO IS ACTUALLY BEING PERSUADED? → WHAT DO THEY ALREADY BELIEVE? → WHAT EVIDENCE / STORY CAN ALTER THAT MODEL?`

Do not write institutional dialogue as five smart people explaining the theme to each other.

## 10. Kyra's arc

Protected arc:

`PROCEDURAL PRECISION → EVIDENCE EXCEEDS CATEGORY → BOUNDED DISCRETION → DOCUMENTED ACCOUNTABILITY`.

System Collapse strengthens this because the mature move is not “rules are bad.” It is:
- distinguish rule function from category failure;
- preserve evidence;
- create safe option space;
- act within bounded authority;
- document why an exception was necessary;
- return decision ownership to the affected person wherever possible.

## 11. Success is allowed

Do not make Kyra repeatedly fail merely to prove growth.

Protected engine:

`COMPETENT ACTION → REAL SUCCESS → MORE TRUST / ACCESS / RESPONSIBILITY → MORE COMPLEX PERSONHOOD / CONSENT DECISION`.

The first technically correct intervention should be allowed to work if that success creates the next ethical/causal burden.

## 12. Nonhuman voice and embodiment

Do not write the distributed entity with a generic poetic-alien voice or generic machine voice.

Its communication should arise from:
- embodiment/topology;
- information bandwidth;
- available channels;
- relation history;
- current goal;
- uncertainty;
- consent/refusal strategy.

`EMBODIMENT → AFFORDANCE / LIMIT → TACTIC`, not `humanlike voice → personhood`.

## 13. Memory / continuity evidence

If continuity or identity depends on records, distributed state or recovered history:

`SUBJECTIVE / SYSTEM MEMORY → INDEPENDENT TRACE → COMPARISON → CONFIDENCE STATUS`.

No single log, restored record or remembered state should be infallible by default.

## 14. Ending contract

Preferred ending form:

`LOCAL OPERATIONAL PROBLEM RESOLVED → PERSONHOOD / CONSENT DECISION MADE BY RIGHT OWNER → COST → INSTITUTIONAL AFTERMATH → CHANGED KYRA → GLOBAL ONTOLOGY MAY REMAIN OPEN`.

Do not solve the origin of consciousness or the complete metaphysics of the entity in the finale unless the evidence architecture truly supports it.

## 15. Canon firewalls

Do NOT import from Murderbot:
- corporate debt/contract economy into post-money orbital normality;
- governor-module technology;
- routine human consciousness transfer;
- alien infection ontology;
- sarcastic fragment cadence as IVDIVO house style.

Consciousness-practice rule remains:
`OBSERVATION → REPRODUCIBLE EFFECT → SCHOOL INTERPRETATION → COMPETING INTERPRETATION → ONTOLOGY CEILING`.

## 16. Scene acceptance test

A B07 scene survives if it changes at least one of:
- personhood confidence;
- evidence status;
- consent / decision ownership;
- relationship trust;
- institutional authority;
- available option space;
- Kyra's strategy;
- entity's strategy;
- physical continuity risk.

If it only repeats that the phenomenon is strange or morally important, compress / merge / delete.

## Final production instruction

B07 should become stronger not by making the entity more spectacular, but by making the **evidence threshold, agency transfer and decision ownership causally unavoidable**.
