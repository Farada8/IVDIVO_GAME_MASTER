# 2. PROPOSED CH7 — FAMILIAR SHAPE

## Existing function to preserve

Samir detects a transition pattern resembling the old relational-state family; resemblance is explicitly not lineage; Ethan does not outrun Samir's evidence.

## Proposed embodiment

Do **not** start on a diagnostics screen.

Start with an ordinary Rivergate/public-use consequence during a youth observation period: the five are moving through Rivergate as participants/observers rather than sitting in the audit suite.

A small Lattice behavior repeats in the real space:

`person A adjusts first → system opens next option differently than when person B adjusts first`.

The behavior is not dangerous and may even be useful.

Samir notices the *order* before he sees the diagnostic trace.

Only then do they request the permitted audit view to verify whether the visible outcome actually corresponds to an order-sensitive transition.

### Causal chain

`LIVED EVENT`
→ `SAMIR NOTICES ASYMMETRY`
→ `ALTERNATIVE EXPLANATION: geometry/load/randomness`
→ `REQUEST AUDIT TRACE`
→ `ORDER-SENSITIVE TRANSITION CONFIRMED`
→ `SAMIR: resemblance, not match`
→ `ETHAN WAITS FOR HIS FULL SENTENCE`
→ `NEW QUESTION: what family of methods produces this operator?`

### Why stronger

- clue starts in life, not a screen;
- reader can observe before experts interpret;
- Samir's skill becomes attention, not exposition;
- fair-play improves because reader sees the anomaly;
- Ethan's growth is behavioral: he does not seize the theory.

# 3. PROPOSED CH8 — NOT YOUR DATA

## Existing function to preserve

Voss requests deeper comparison; Samir refuses raw data; derived comparison remains possible; Calder protects refusal despite public-safety value.

## Dramatic engine

Make this a **negotiation scene**, not a policy seminar.

Each party must want something:

- Voss wants enough comparable structure to test whether the resemblance matters.
- Samir wants to preserve control over personal/historical records.
- Ethan wants the answer but cannot ethically make Samir the cost of getting it.
- Calder wants a process that preserves rights and still allows legitimate safety review.
- Aoife watches how language changes when `useful` threatens to become `required`.

### Negotiation progression

`REQUEST RAW ACCESS`
→ `REFUSAL`
→ `PUBLIC-SAFETY ARGUMENT`
→ `BOUNDARY HOLDS`
→ `ALTERNATIVE: DERIVED FEATURE SET`
→ `QUESTION OF WHO CONTROLS DERIVATION`
→ `CALDER DEFINES REVIEWABLE PROCESS`
→ `PARTIAL DEAL`

### Focal point

Not `Samir says no` alone.

The turn is:

`NO` changes the design of the investigation itself.

The group must solve the mystery under rights constraints.

# 4. PROPOSED CH9 — THE SIMPLE MACHINE

## Existing function to preserve

Ollie's maker team demonstrates breadcrumb rovers; Rivergate calls them useful but crude; `crude ≠ wrong`.

## Expand its causal role

This chapter should be the Movement II **ordinary-life anchor**.

The maker team has its own deadline/reputation/problem independent of Lattice. Rivergate's interest interrupts Ollie's normal world rather than replacing it.

### Required dual objective

Ollie wants:
1. his team demonstration/project to succeed on its own terms;
2. Rivergate engineers to evaluate the rover without annexing the maker team into somebody else's infrastructure program.

### Plot function

The rover must reveal a design principle relevant to the later crisis:

`local state + visible marker + no prediction required`.

Do not make it magically superior to Lattice. It should be:

- cheaper;
- slower;
- less integrated;
- robust under central failure;
- incapable of some Lattice benefits.

That preserves the later moral tradeoff.

# 5. PROPOSED CH10 — SIGN HERE

## Existing function to preserve

Ethan co-signs limited expansion because current evidence supports it.

## Required evidence bundle before signature

The chapter must explicitly use evidence earned in Ch6–9:

**FOR expansion**
- Lattice prevented a real incident;
- predictive coordination improves accessibility/safety;
- no established hold-threshold violation yet;
- current provenance resemblance is not proof of system defect.

**AGAINST complacency**
- inferred relations need stronger review;
- order-sensitive logic merits provenance inquiry;
- raw personal data is unavailable by right;
- fallback quality is materially lower/different.

### Ethan's decision

He signs **with conditions**, not because the story needs the system live for later chapters.

His reasoning must be reproducible from evidence available to reader.

### Focal point

The signature is not a reward.

It is the first time Ethan knowingly authorizes a system that he believes is useful **and** incompletely understood.

This advances the Book Two question:

`What uncertainty am I responsible for carrying?`
