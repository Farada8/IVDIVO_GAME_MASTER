**Version:** v0.1 DRAFT  
**Status:** production control / not canon until founder approval  
**Derived from:** Batch 23 Zunshine + prior fair-play, Herman and Oatley passes

## Purpose

Prevent the Book Two mystery from silently converting:

`observation → interpretation → hypothesis → origin claim`

without preserving source, confidence and alternatives.

For each major clue, maintain:

`ID | CONTENT | SOURCE | EPISTEMIC CLASS | CONFIDENCE | ALTERNATIVE | WHO KNOWS | WHAT ACTION IT CAUSES | WHAT MAY NOT YET BE CLAIMED`.

# SOURCE TYPES — BOOK TWO STANDARD

Every clue should use one of these tags:

- `DIRECT OBSERVATION`
- `SYSTEM LOG`
- `SYSTEM INFERENCE`
- `DECLARED PARTICIPANT DATA`
- `SELF-REPORT`
- `THIRD-PARTY REPORT`
- `MEMORY`
- `EDITED MEDIA`
- `RAW MEDIA`
- `INSTITUTIONAL SUMMARY`
- `TECHNICAL DOCUMENTATION`
- `ANALYTIC COMPARISON`
- `STATISTICAL PATTERN`
- `HYPOTHESIS`

No `HYPOTHESIS` may silently become `DIRECT OBSERVATION` in later dialogue.

---

# AOIFE FUNCTION LOCK

Aoife's causal specialty in Book Two:

`RECORD → SOURCE → SEQUENCE → CONTEXT → PUBLIC INTERPRETATION → CONSEQUENCE`.

She should own at least one major turn where the **same factual material** changes meaning when its source/context chain is restored.

This prevents her from becoming merely the character who carries a recorder.

---

# VOSS RELIABILITY LOCK

Default production assumption:

**Voss is substantially good-faith and technically competent.**

She may:
- have incomplete provenance;
- inherit assumptions;
- defend system tradeoffs;
- be wrong about consequences;
- underestimate social meaning.

She should not become a hidden liar without new founder-level story justification. Mystery is stronger if competent people can be sincerely embedded in incomplete systems.

---

# ETHAN DECISION LOCK

The climax should not require Ethan to know the ultimate historical truth.

He must decide from bounded evidence about the **current authorized system**:

`CURRENT HARM / RISK`
+ `CURRENT BENEFIT`
+ `UNCERTAINTY`
+ `AVAILABLE FALLBACK`
+ `AUTHORITY SCOPE`
→ `HOLD / NO HOLD / BOUNDED ACTION`.

Series-level origin mystery remains partially unresolved after the local decision.
