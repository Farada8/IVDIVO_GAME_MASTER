## VERDICT
Use Iglesias as a **reader-effect and scene/dialogue diagnostic**, not as a screenplay-format retrofit.

## LESSON ZERO
Book One remains development-locked. Batch 66 does not itself justify reopening it.

If a current-version reader problem is found, repair in this order:
1. ONE MAIN STORY / causal engine;
2. scene necessity and changed state;
3. scene vs summary / direct vs indirect dialogue;
4. POV access and distance;
5. paragraph intent;
6. sentence rhythm;
7. wording.

Do not respond to `flat` by automatically adding conflict, a cliffhanger, trauma, a ticking clock or shorter sentences.

## FUTURE IVDIVO BOOKS
### Scene acceptance
A scene may survive without overt conflict if it causes a meaningful action, knowledge, relationship, trust, responsibility, access, emotional or reader-model change.

### Dialogue
Every major conversation should be checked for:
`PERSON-SPECIFIC WANT → TACTIC → RESPONSE → TACTIC CHANGE → CHANGED STATE`.

If exact wording does not matter, compress.

### Subtext
Use indirectness when directness has a believable cost. Allow direct speech where trust, safety, earned climax or simplicity makes it truthful.

### Exposition
Do not disguise worldbuilding by manufacturing arguments. Require an in-world reason why information is needed now and a consequence after it is learned.

## ORBITAL YOUTH FIREWALL
Emotional familiarity comes from:
- trust;
- belonging;
- autonomy;
- consent;
- love/intimacy;
- responsibility;
- reputation;
- mastery;
- time;
- unique experience/opportunity.

It does **not** justify wages, rent, credits, consumer hierarchy or servant-AI defaults.

## CONSCIOUS ROBOTS
No universal robot voice. Named conscious robots require independent agenda, relationships, refusal rights, preferences and relation-specific speech/tactics. Petlike helplessness is not a substitute for personhood.

## RARE CONSCIOUSNESS SCHOOLS
Keep dialogue and exposition epistemically typed:
`OBSERVATION → REPRODUCIBLE PRACTICE EFFECT → SCHOOL INTERPRETATION → COMPETING INTERPRETATION → POSSIBLE ONTOLOGY`.
Characters may speak confidently; narration must not silently upgrade confidence into universal fact.

## ANTI-CHOP GATE
Do not optimize short-sentence percentage.

If prose feels chopped:
`SCENE NECESSITY → SHOW/TELL → POV → DIALOGUE MODE → PARAGRAPH INTENT → RHYTHM → WORDING`.

A short line remains protected when it has a real contrast/accent function. A long paragraph remains protected when it creates controlled time, cognition, intimacy or suspense.

## READER-EFFECT CHECK
After structural passes, sample major scenes and ask:
- What does the reader know now that they did not know before?
- What future state are they modeling?
- What do they hope/fear/wonder about?
- What changed emotionally because of context, not merely because the prose named an emotion?
- Is the intended effect earned by causality/personhood?

## FINAL RULE
`READER EFFECT VALIDATES STORY DELIVERY; IT DOES NOT OVERRIDE CAUSAL TRUTH, CANON, PERSONHOOD OR MEDIUM-APPROPRIATE PROSE.`
