# 0. BOOK-TWO PROOF CEILING

By the end of Book Two the evidence may support only:

- modern Lattice uses relational-state methods with a documented human development chain;
- at least one formal feature resembles the older R-14 / RL-0 family;
- one older Confederation archive family contains an analogous rare order-sensitive operator;
- the specificity is sufficient to keep a provenance problem open;
- a second nonhuman pre-membership archive contains an equivalent root-class / relational-matrix notation;
- Earth is therefore not the only unexplained occurrence;
- direction of transmission and ultimate source remain unknown.

The book may **not** establish:

- Matrix as a confirmed organization;
- Confederation origin;
- human origin;
- hidden common-source civilization;
- deliberate pre-Contact seeding of Earth;
- chosen-five design;
- Samir as selected/prophetic subject;
- covert Confederation management of Earth.

Any prose crossing this ceiling is a BLOCKER unless the entire evidence architecture is deliberately rebuilt.

# 2. CLUE RECORDS

## B2-C01 — DIVERGENT COMPANION ROUTES

**First appearance:** Ch1 — THE BADGE  
**Type:** observational clue  
**Raw observation:** Taven and Mel receive different route instructions while the public system still reports companion continuity as maintained.  
**Reader knows:** yes.  
**Characters know:** Ethan, Samir, Aoife, Maya, Taven, Mel, nearby staff.  
**First interpretation:** routing layer may preserve a relationship by predicting that one companion will compensate for divergent instructions.  
**Alternative:** UI lag / state-label lag / ordinary route optimization artifact.  
**Counterevidence:** after the course stop, the public status changes to explicitly coupled routing, suggesting state-label/model behavior really did change.  
**Action caused:** establishes reason for Rivergate youth audit.  
**Payoff:** later supports inquiry into inferred relations and accommodation burden.  
**Allowed claim:** system models relation behavior.  
**Forbidden claim:** this is already proof of R-14 lineage.

# 5. RED-HERRING / ALTERNATIVE-MODEL LEDGER

The story should preserve viable alternatives rather than invent disposable suspects.

## H1 — ordinary modern optimization convergence
Explains: inferred accommodation, burden concentration.  
Fails to explain alone: rare cross-archive operator specificity if independent convergence becomes statistically/structurally implausible.

## H2 — human legacy transmission into Lattice
Explains: Lattice/RL-0 family resemblance.  
Fails to explain alone: older Confederation and second nonhuman occurrence unless there is outbound human transmission impossible by chronology.

## H3 — Confederation-to-Earth transfer
Explains: Earth/Confederation resemblance.  
Weakness: missing transfer chain and second civilization pre-membership chronology.

## H4 — independent convergence
Explains: similar problem spaces can produce similar formal tools.  
Must remain alive even after rare operator match, though confidence may decline.

## H5 — unknown older shared source/tradition
Explains: multiple occurrences without directional transfer.  
Status: hypothesis only; no source identity allowed in Book Two.

These are not “twist suspects.” They are causal models competing against the same evidence.
