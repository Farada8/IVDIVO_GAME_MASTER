# IVDIVO — BATCH 132 APPLICATION GATE
## Agency · Causality · POV · Nonhuman Personhood

Status: DRAFT / additive production control. Does not supersede higher canon authority.

## A. Character agency gate
For every major character scene:

PRESENT WANT / RESPONSIBILITY → OPTIONS → CHOICE / REFUSAL / DELAY / COOPERATION → CONSEQUENCE → CHANGED OPTION SPACE.

Do not equate agency with aggression, speed or rebellion.

## B. Main-story gate
A vivid character line is accepted only if it changes the dominant causal engine, a key relationship, evidence status, authority/access, or the final option set. Otherwise compress, bank or move it to another book.

## C. Coincidence gate
Coincidence may create a problem or opportunity. It may not cheaply solve the decisive problem. Convert convenient solutions into prepared causality, earned discovery or explicit uncertainty.

## D. Reader-causality gate
If a reaction feels arbitrary:
- identify the accessible cause;
- verify when the reader receives it;
- check the character's model and current options;
- if surprise is intended, plant retrospective support.

## E. POV gate
Keep distinct:
WHO SPEAKS / WHO PERCEIVES / WHO KNOWS / WHO INTERPRETS / WHO DECIDES.

A close POV inference is not a world fact. No telepathic access to another person, robot, institution or consciousness school without an established information path.

## F. Conscious robot gate
OWN WANT → OWN MODEL/EVIDENCE → OWN RELATIONSHIPS → CONSENT/REFUSAL → CHOICE → CONSEQUENCE.

Compliance, silence, delay or refusal must not be automatically read as lack of personhood.

## G. Orbital Youth gate
Do not restore salary/rent/credits as easy stakes. Prefer trust, consent, specific responsibility, qualification, unique access, belonging, privacy, reputation, mastery, relationships, time and unique opportunity.

## H. Consciousness-school gate
Narrative causal clarity cannot upgrade a school's explanation into ontology.

OBSERVATION → REPRODUCIBLE EFFECT → SCHOOL INTERPRETATION → COMPETING INTERPRETATION → INDEPENDENT TEST → ONTOLOGY CEILING.

## I. Anti-choppiness gate
Do not render every stimulus/reaction as a separate short sentence or paragraph. First decide scene weight and paragraph intent. Preserve short lines only when they earn contrast, revelation, timing, decision or genuine perceptual break.

## J. Manuscript lock
LESSON ZERO Book One remains development-locked unless a concrete current defect, repeated reader evidence, acquisition issue, continuity/copy defect or audio-discovered problem justifies reopening.
