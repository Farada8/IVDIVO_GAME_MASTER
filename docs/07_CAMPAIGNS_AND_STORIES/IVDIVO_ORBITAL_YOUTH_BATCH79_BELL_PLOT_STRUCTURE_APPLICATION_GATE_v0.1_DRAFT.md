## 1. ONE MAIN STORY

Do not translate LOCK into one omnipotent protagonist.

For Orbital Youth use:

**ONE DOMINANT CAUSAL ENGINE + DISTRIBUTED PERSONAL OBJECTIVES + CAUSAL RECONVERGENCE.**

Every substantial personal line must change the same book-level engine through action, information, relationship, consent, responsibility, strategy, cost or final choice.

If a line only demonstrates another technology, another district or another youth issue, it is a candidate for compression/merge/deletion.

## 2. POST-MONEY OBJECTIVE TEST

Strong trackable objectives include:

- earn/retain a real role in a team or mission;
- obtain another person's voluntary trust/consent;
- complete a difficult qualification because a specific responsibility depends on it;
- preserve a relationship while taking a necessary public action;
- determine whether evidence is sufficient for a bounded decision;
- repair a system without externalizing the cost onto the same people;
- protect a conscious nonhuman agent without appropriating its choices;
- secure time, access or a unique opportunity that is genuinely nonfungible.

Reject:
- salary;
- rent;
- credits/allowance as disguised money;
- consumer status;
- artificial material scarcity for ordinary manufacturable goods.

## 3. RESISTANCE WITHOUT VILLAIN FLATTENING

Best opposition for IVDIVO often has:

**real benefit + evidence + legitimate constituency + leverage + limitation/blind spot.**

Adults, institutions and conscious robots may oppose a youth proposal while remaining competent and partly right.

This produces a stronger final choice than discovering that the opponent was simply corrupt/evil.

## 4. SUCCESS MUST REMAIN POSSIBLE

Do not turn Bell's confrontation into setback monoculture.

Protect scenes in which:
- a repair genuinely works;
- an adult keeps a promise;
- a robot cooperates because it chooses to;
- a relationship becomes safer;
- a young character makes a correct judgment;
- an institution proves useful.

A success earns plot value when it increases responsibility, dependence, trust, later cost or the difficulty of the next decision.

## 5. ORBITAL SETTING MUST BE CAUSAL

Before giving page-space to a technology/ring/gravity zone/transport rule:

**WORLD RULE → HUMAN/ROBOT CONSEQUENCE → AVAILABLE CHOICE → ACTION → RESULT.**

If the detail can be replaced by any other futuristic spectacle without changing the scene, it is decorative world tourism.

## 6. CONSCIOUS ROBOT LEAD TEST

Bell's 'Lead' is not restricted to a biological human.

For any named conscious robot:
- independent present want;
- relation-specific tactics;
- consent/refusal rights;
- obligations not created solely by the youth protagonists;
- life/relationships outside the focal scene;
- consequence if its own choice succeeds or fails.

If removal/replacement with a smart terminal changes nothing, rewrite personhood before adding more lore.

## 7. QUIET YOUTH-LIFE GATE

Ordinary orbital life is not filler merely because no accident occurs.

Keep it when it:
- establishes a post-money default through behavior;
- changes friendship/romance/family terms;
- shows who voluntarily contributes and why;
- changes future willingness to cooperate;
- creates/repairs consent;
- earns reader attachment/reward;
- changes what a later high-pressure choice costs.

Otherwise compress.
