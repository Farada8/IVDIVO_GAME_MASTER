# IVDIVO B07 — THE UNLISTED CITY
## Batch 83 Opening Contract & Canon Gate

Status: WORKING / ADDITIVE AUDIT
Decision: **KEEP ENGINE / DO NOT REBUILD FOR BELL.**

## WHY THE CURRENT OPENING MOVEMENT IS STRUCTURALLY PROMISING

The current dossier states that before the true evacuation disaster in Chapter 6:

- Kaira's want and wrong strategy are already legible;
- Kaira/Sari competition carries attraction and ideology;
- Tem's humour/boundaries operate through work;
- CAM-4 enters as an ordinary maintenance correlation;
- the anomaly escalates from routine correlation to coordinated outcome;
- Chapter 6 then delivers the true disaster and bounded cadet authority.

This is a valid opening architecture because **the book does not need to spend its disaster in Chapter 1**.

Preferred progression:

`CADET NORMAL / WANT → SMALL SPECIFIC PRESSURE → RELATIONSHIP / COMPETENCE SIGNAL → ORDINARY ANOMALY → MODEL TENSION → TRUE EVACUATION EVENT`.

## KEEP

1. Kaira's ambition/procedural competence before disaster exposition.
2. Sari as a person/countermodel, not merely romantic obstacle.
3. Tem's boundaries expressed through action/work.
4. CAM-4 first appearing through ordinary maintenance evidence rather than a supernatural-style reveal.
5. Chapter 6 retaining ownership of the true evacuation-scale inciting event if Chapters 1–5 maintain sufficient forward pressure.

## REPAIR / CANON FIREWALL

The dossier also contains older language about:
- expiring housing;
- atmospheric cost;
- material career stakes.

These phrases require canon review. They must **not** silently reintroduce ordinary wages/rent/credits/purchasing hierarchy.

Replace the underlying pressure, where needed, with genuinely nonfungible constraints such as:
- a specific habitat placement or family arrangement;
- timing/compatibility constraints;
- Human Contact placement;
- certification window;
- responsibility already accepted;
- training slot;
- access to a specific mentor/team/mission;
- relationship/family consequence;
- reputation/trust.

Do not merely rename money as `allocation points` or `credits`.

## OPENING CONTRACT FOR B07

Reader should learn early that this book promises:

**a cadet emergency / evidence / personhood problem inside Confederation life, carried by Kaira's procedural intelligence, relationships and changing responsibility.**

Therefore reject an opening retrofit that promises:
- generic military action;
- hidden-villain thriller;
- consciousness-transfer adventure;
- universal metaphysical revelation;
- romance-first story that sidelines evacuation/CAM-4 causal engine.

## CH1–6 ACCEPTANCE TEST

For each opening chapter ask:

`WHAT DOES KAIRA WANT NOW?`
`WHAT NORMAL RULE OF TESSERA/CADET LIFE IS MADE VISIBLE?`
`WHAT SPECIFIC THING DEVIATES?`
`WHAT DOES KAIRA DO THAT SARI/TEM/ANOTHER CADET WOULD NOT DO?`
`WHAT CHANGES BY THE END?`
`WHAT PROMISE TO THE READER BECOMES STRONGER?`

If a chapter only teaches world lore, compress/rebuild it.

## SYMPATHY FIREWALL

Do not injure, humiliate or victimize Kaira early merely to make her lovable. Bond should principally come from:

`SPECIFIC WANT + COMPETENCE + PROCEDURAL BLIND SPOT + RELATIONSHIP PRESSURE + CONSEQUENCES`.

## CAM-4 PERSONHOOD / MYSTERY FIREWALL

Before personhood is established, keep evidence status explicit:

`TRACE / CORRELATION → INTERPRETATION → ALTERNATIVE MODEL → NEW EVIDENCE → BOUNDED ACTION`.

Do not create a false opening hook by making CAM-4 instantly speak like a person or by revealing ontology before the book earns it.

## PROSE/RHYTHM NOTE

Bell's `grab the reader` must not be translated into short-sentence saturation.

B07 opening rhythm should follow:

`READER EFFECT → POV → DURATION → PARAGRAPH INTENT → SENTENCE RHYTHM`.

Quiet competence, rivalry, ordinary city life and a slowly sharpening anomaly can carry the opening if their causal state changes are clear.
