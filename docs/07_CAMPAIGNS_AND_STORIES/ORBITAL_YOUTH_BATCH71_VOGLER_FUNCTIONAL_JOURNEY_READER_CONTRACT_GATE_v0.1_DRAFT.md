# ORBITAL YOUTH — BATCH 71 VOGLER APPLICATION GATE
## Functional Journey · Reader Contract · Post-Money / Robot-Personhood Firewalls

Status: WORKING / ADDITIVE. DO NOT RETROFIT accepted architecture into a twelve-stage template.
Date: 2026-08-17

## VERDICT
Use Vogler only to test transitions, commitment, preparation, decisive pressure, aftermath and return. Do not rebuild Orbital Youth around Hero's Journey labels.

## 1. POST-MONEY THRESHOLDS
A youth threshold is strong when the young person gains/loses a real role, responsibility, relationship, consent boundary, qualification, trusted access, unique experience or time with a specific person.

Rejected substitutes: wages, rent, consumer credits, artificial status currency.

Test:
`What can the protagonist do / no longer do after this threshold that was not true before?`
If the answer is only “they are in a cooler orbital location,” the threshold is decorative.

## 2. COMPETENT GUARDIANS
Instructor, administrator, parent, conscious robot or procedure may restrict access for legitimate safety/personhood reasons. The youth may need to demonstrate readiness, negotiate terms, accept limits or find a different path. The story does not require humiliating or defeating the guardian.

## 3. ENSEMBLE FUNCTION MIGRATION
Do not freeze Ethan/Maya/Ollie/Aoife/Samir/new youth/robots into permanent Hero/Mentor/Ally roles. Evidence, warning, comic release, testing, leadership and insight may migrate by scene. Maintain independent life and motive for every named major person.

## 4. CONSCIOUS ROBOTS
Archetypal convenience never overrides personhood. A robot can refuse a request, disagree with a human interpretation, protect another robot's interests, choose not to disclose information or negotiate its own terms.

Replacement test: if the robot can become a smart terminal with no change to consent, relationship or causal outcome, rewrite the personhood layer.

## 5. CONSCIOUSNESS SCHOOLS
Do not use “mentor,” “initiation,” “third eye,” “vibration,” synchronicity or telepathy vocabulary as factual proof.

Required chain:
`practitioner observation → reproducible practice effect → school interpretation → competing interpretation → explicit knowledge limit → possible ontological conclusion`.

The bottleneck remains trained practitioners, not computational resources alone.

## 6. ORDEAL / CLIMAX
No death/rebirth requirement. Prefer a bounded decision where several defensible options retain costs. A quiet choice about trust, permission, responsibility, publication, coordination or refusal can carry climax function.

## 7. REWARD / RETURN
After a major result, protect enough aftermath to register what changed. Strong post-money “elixirs” include:
- delegated responsibility;
- earned trust;
- revised relationship terms;
- qualified access;
- a learned limit;
- responsibility to another person/community;
- a durable new collaborative protocol.

Do not translate these into points/credits.

## 8. POLARITY
Useful Orbital Youth polarities are competing models, not Good vs Evil:
- autonomy ↔ coordinated responsibility;
- prediction ↔ consent;
- efficiency ↔ person-level fairness;
- specialist interpretation ↔ empirical caution;
- local optimization ↔ system-level consequences.

Allowed outcomes include synthesis, coexistence, bounded authority, appeal, reform and separation.

## 9. READER CONTRACT
Before prose, state the book's promised experience in one sentence. Do not promise an orbital coming-of-age / mystery / relationship story and then spend the middle as a technology encyclopedia.

Every major world-reveal should pay into character choice, relationship, mystery evidence or causal opportunity.

## 10. RHYTHM / “ОБРУБКИ”
Vogler's catharsis/body-response material supports modulation, not short-sentence quotas. After strong pressure, a longer reflective paragraph or quiet scene may be the correct structural move.

Repair order remains:
`ONE MAIN STORY → scene necessity → show/tell → POV → dialogue mode → paragraph intent → sentence rhythm → wording`.

## 11. LOCK POLICY
LESSON ZERO Book One remains development-locked. Vogler supplies no independent defect strong enough to reopen it. Future books may use these gates prospectively; accepted architecture is changed only for a concrete causal/personhood/canon failure.
