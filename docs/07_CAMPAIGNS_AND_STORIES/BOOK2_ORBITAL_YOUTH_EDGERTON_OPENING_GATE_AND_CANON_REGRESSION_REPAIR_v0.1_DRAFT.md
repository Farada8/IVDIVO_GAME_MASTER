# 3. REMOVE / REBUILD BEFORE PROSE

## 3.1 MONEY / ALLOWANCE REGRESSION

Current v0.2 packet includes a youth `discretionary civic/work allowance`, buying/importing goods, social spending and currency/credit terminology to design.

**REJECT.**

Founder Correction says mature orbital city life no longer uses money as the ordinary organizing mechanism and explicitly forbids credits as disguised money.

Replace Ollie's motivation with:
- trust;
- real responsibility;
- qualification;
- workshop/project access based on safety/competence;
- reputation among practitioners;
- belonging to the commissioning team;
- mastery;
- authorship/contribution;
- unique experience;
- eligibility for future missions/projects.

These are not points and not hidden currency.

## 3.2 SCARCITY LANGUAGE

Ordinary fabricated goods must not become valuable merely because they are expensive.

Allowed scarcity must have a physical/social reason such as:
- one compatible location at one time;
- limited safe occupancy while a ring is being commissioned;
- unique view/event/time slot;
- bounded expert attention;
- unique historical object;
- limited mission seat;
- safety-certified access;
- privacy;
- environmental compatibility;
- transport geometry during maintenance;
- one person's consent/trust;
- irreplaceable time.

A fabrication queue can exist only if there is a real bounded physical/safety/coordination reason, not because society recreated money by another name.

## 3.3 `WORK PLACEMENT` LANGUAGE

Human work/contribution remains valid. Survival-income logic does not.

Tomas / Ollie / apprentices may care intensely about a role because it carries:
- identity;
- responsibility;
- team membership;
- mission continuity;
- caregiver obligations;
- time commitments;
- mastery;
- reputation;
- access to a unique project.

Do not frame losing a placement as losing livelihood unless Founder later creates a specific exception.

## 3.4 BODY / SYNTHETIC EMBODIMENT REGRESSION RISK

The v0.2 packet wisely says Iri ontology is not locked, but `non-human-default body/environment interface` can drift into a forbidden assumption.

Current orbital civilization does **not** possess ordinary human consciousness-transfer / soul-transfer technology.

Before prose, Iri must receive one explicit ontology passport:
- ordinary biological nonhuman species;
- conscious robot person;
- other already-canon body category that does not require human mind transfer;
- or remain undescribed beyond directly observable requirements until canon is decided.

Never imply `human mind uploaded into synthetic body` as normal orbital technology.

## 3.5 DIMENSIONAL KNOWLEDGE FIREWALL

Do not use high orbital technology as evidence that engineers/cadets understand dimensional worlds, soul transfer or subtle bodies.

If a specialist school enters Book Two:
- identify practitioner lineage;
- years of training;
- reproducibility limits;
- disagreement among schools;
- what mainstream science accepts versus disputes.

The bottleneck is trained practitioners, not fabrication capacity.

# 4. EDGERTON OPENING APPLICATION

## Opening job
The opening must show **the moment Ollie's desire for real usefulness becomes causally entangled with real responsibility**.

Do not begin with:
- orbital history lecture;
- tour of Vela;
- explanation of post-money economics;
- list of advanced technologies;
- broad statement that Ring Six is important;
- arbitrary disaster unrelated to Ollie's later mistake.

## Working opening architecture — NOT CANON PROSE

### Beat 1 — Ordinary competent work
Ollie is assigned a low-status but real commissioning verification task.

He wants something very specific: not prestige in the abstract, but evidence that the senior team trusts him with consequences.

### Beat 2 — Small irregularity
A timing/settling discrepancy appears during an otherwise successful test.

It is not yet an emergency.

### Beat 3 — Character-specific action
Ollie notices a pattern others reasonably treat as low priority and tests it instead of merely reporting anxiety.

His competence matters.

### Beat 4 — Result
His analysis correctly resolves or narrows the immediate issue.

The result earns him deeper access / responsibility in the commissioning analysis.

### Beat 5 — Hidden future irony
The same evidence culture that rewards clean, reproducible technical analysis will later help him undervalue lived resident/body signals that are harder to formalize.

### Changed state
He ends the opening **closer to what he wants and therefore more exposed to the book's real problem**.

This is stronger than forcing an opening failure.

# 5. SURFACE / DEEP COUPLING

## Surface goal
Help commission a specific safe residential environment / solve the current commissioning evidence problem.

This is observable.

## Book-level human conflict
Ollie equates usefulness with being the person who can produce the cleanest technically legible answer.

The book pressures him toward a harder definition:

`REAL RESPONSIBILITY = COMPETENCE + UNCERTAINTY + OTHER PEOPLE'S EVIDENCE + CONSEQUENCE OWNERSHIP`.

Do not state this as a lesson in narration.

Let it emerge from choices.

# 7. WORLD REVEAL GATE

The orbital city should be learned through what Ollie must do.

Good reveal:
- route geometry changes where he can physically reach a system;
- mixed body/environment requirements alter the commissioning model;
- conscious robot colleague refuses or challenges a human assumption;
- post-money contribution changes why someone volunteers for difficult work;
- housing compatibility makes relocation emotionally real without rent;
- a maintenance closure changes a date, friendship or family arrangement.

Weak reveal:
- paragraphs explaining how advanced the city is;
- characters telling each other facts they all know;
- expensive-object stakes;
- Earth capitalism with renamed credits.

# 8. POSITIVE ORBITAL LIFE PROTECTION

The ring/city must be desirable before and after the failure.

Do not make every:
- festival;
- sport;
- friendship;
- transport experience;
- conscious-robot encounter;
- new technology;
- independence milestone

turn immediately into danger.

Reader desire to live/visit there is an asset, not dead time.

Positive scenes remain only if they also alter relationship, status, knowledge, future choice or A-story behavior.
