## VERDICT

Apply Bell Chapter 7 only as a scene-level diagnostic layer.

## B07 SCENE ROUTER

For each scene:

`PRIOR CONSEQUENCE → OWNER → PRESENT WANT → MODE → ATTENTION/PRESSURE ENGINE → CHANGE → OUTCOME → NEXT CAUSAL LINK`.

Allowed modes: ACTION / REACTION / SETUP / DEEPENING / SUMMARY-INDIRECT / HYBRID.

## KAYRA

Do not force Kayra to fail in every scene.

Preferred escalation:

`procedural competence works → trust/responsibility increases → evidence no longer fits existing category → old tactic reaches its limit → bounded accountable discretion becomes necessary`.

This preserves her character arc without making competence a fake setup for repeated humiliation.

## MIDDLE-SCENE ROTATION

Avoid:
`anomaly → briefing → explanation → anomaly → briefing → explanation`.

Rotate pressure across:
- operational coordination;
- evidence/provenance;
- relationship trust;
- consent/personhood;
- institutional authority;
- technical constraints;
- public/social interpretation;
- personal responsibility.

If a briefing mainly communicates procedure already implied by prior scenes, summarize it and dramatize the decision/relationship/evidence turn instead.

## QUIET SCENES

Reaction/intimacy/ordinary-life scenes are protected when they alter later behavior, trust, commitment, evidence interpretation or willingness to take responsibility.

Do not insert generic anxiety merely to raise Bell-style intensity.

## NONHUMAN PERSONHOOD

Any conscious nonhuman person involved in the central continuity/personhood problem must retain:
- an independent objective;
- evidence or interpretation of its own where plausible;
- consent/refusal;
- relationships outside Kayra's utility;
- at least one consequential decision that the human team cannot substitute for.

It is not a `deepening` device or complication for Kayra.

## SHOW/TELL ROUTING

Dramatize in real time when exact action/wording changes:
- consent;
- evidence status;
- authority;
- relationship terms;
- technical outcome;
- personhood recognition;
- final bounded choice.

Compress:
- repeated travel;
- routine checks;
- repeated meeting process;
- procedural recap;
- low-value explanatory dialogue;
- logistics that do not constrain action.

## INTENSITY FIREWALL

Do not equate intensity with:
- more casualties;
- shorter sentences;
- louder arguments;
- constant cliffhangers;
- generic fear.

Intensity may come from a narrowing choice, evidence reclassification, loss of procedural certainty, consent conflict or a quiet refusal.

## CANON FIREWALLS

- No salary/rent/credits as default orbital stakes.
- No operational human consciousness-transfer/body-swapping.
- Conscious robots/nonhuman persons are not tools.
- Specialist consciousness-school interpretation does not become ontology without evidence.

## PROSE CHECK

Before line editing a choppy passage:

`Does this need a scene? → should part be summary? → is POV stable? → does direct dialogue matter? → should adjacent microparagraphs merge? → only then revise sentence rhythm.`
