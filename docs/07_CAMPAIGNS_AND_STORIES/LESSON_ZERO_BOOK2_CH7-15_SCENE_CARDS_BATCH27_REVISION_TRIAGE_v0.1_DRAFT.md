Every chapter must advance the live Book Two question:

`SCENE FORM`

Adjacent chapters may not share both:
1. the same dominant dramatic form; and
2. the same outcome family.

## E. Rights protection

Samir's refusal of raw personal/historical data is legitimate. The plot must adapt to rights, not punish rights as obstruction.

# 3. CHAPTER 8 — NOT YOUR DATA

Samir, with Calder as institutional counterweight.

### SCENE FORM
Negotiation / boundary-setting.

### HUMAN ARENA
A real institutional negotiation with status asymmetry; not a lecture on privacy principles.

### READER QUESTION
`Can they get enough evidence without turning a person into infrastructure?`

### INFORMATION DELTA
Before: more data appears desirable.  
After: the evidence ceiling itself becomes part of the case.

### EMOTIONAL / RELATIONSHIP DELTA

# 4. CHAPTER 9 — THE SIMPLE MACHINE

## Locked architectural outcome

Ollie's breadcrumb rovers demonstrate an independent, low-tech fallback. Rivergate sees them as useful but crude. `Crude ≠ wrong`.

### OWNER
Ollie.

### PRIOR CONSEQUENCE
Ch8 establishes that a robust audit cannot depend on unrestricted information. The story now needs a second kind of independence: physical/system fallback.

### IMMEDIATE OBJECTIVE
Demonstrate a simple mechanism on its own engineering terms and determine what it can preserve when predictive integration is absent.

### PRESSURE / OBSTACLE / SUPPORT
- crude system lacks Lattice's efficiency;
- physical markers/local state create maintenance burden;
- maker-team identity should not be swallowed by Rivergate;
- adult engineers can legitimately criticize capacity/scale;
- Ollie must distinguish `works` from `is sufficient`.

### BULL'S-EYE
The fallback succeeds at a narrower task that Lattice performs more elegantly, proving independence is materially possible but costly.

### OUTCOME
**Practical success with limitation.** The system earns strategic relevance without becoming a magical replacement.

### NEXT INTENTION
Quantify what service/safety level survives if Lattice is narrowed or held.

### A-STORY VARIABLE
Fallback feasibility / cost of intervention.

### FUTURE PAYOFF
Makes the later Hold actionable and costly rather than symbolic.

### SCENE FORM
Physical demonstration / maker-work / engineering stress test.

### HUMAN ARENA
Ollie's ordinary maker-team world and reputation should remain visible; Rivergate is the visitor/consumer of this work, not its owner.

### READER QUESTION
`What can still work when the clever system is unavailable?`

### INFORMATION DELTA
Before: fallback is an idea.  
After: fallback has measurable capability and measurable inferiority.

### EMOTIONAL / RELATIONSHIP DELTA
Ollie's “less elegant” solution gains authority because it survives contact with reality.

# 6. CHAPTER 11 — WHO PAYS

## Locked architectural direction

Repeated small delays/burdens begin clustering on the same kinds of users. Maya asks for burden analysis by person rather than only aggregate efficiency.

### OWNER
Maya.

### PRIOR CONSEQUENCE
Ch10 expansion gives Lattice more real use and therefore enough lived distribution to reveal who absorbs marginal inconvenience.

### IMMEDIATE OBJECTIVE
Show that an apparently minor routing inconvenience is not just a number when repeatedly assigned to actual people, then force the audit to measure burden distribution.

### PRESSURE / OBSTACLE / SUPPORT
- each individual delay is defensible/minor;
- aggregate system may still improve;
- repeated affected users may themselves be cooperative and not complain loudly;
- staff can offer plausible geometric/load explanations;
- sample-size and comparison-class issues matter.

### BULL'S-EYE
Maya reframes the metric from `Did the system optimize?` to `Who had to adapt so the optimization worked?`

### OUTCOME
**Discovery success creates ethical cost.** A pattern becomes testable, not yet fully proved.

### NEXT INTENTION
Normalize comparable cases and test whether accommodation burden follows a behavioral prediction rather than ordinary route/load factors.

### A-STORY VARIABLE
Fairness / hidden externalization.

### FUTURE PAYOFF
Sets up Ch14 `PROBABLE YES` and the climax threshold.

### SCENE FORM
Lived field consequence → audit request, not screen-first diagnosis.

### HUMAN ARENA
Queue, route, access, service or movement involving identifiable people. The exact event must be frozen before prose; do not invent it ad hoc mid-draft.

### READER QUESTION
`If everyone gains a little but the same helpful people keep paying, is the system fair?`

### INFORMATION DELTA
Before: aggregate success.  
After: distribution becomes a separate variable.

### EMOTIONAL / RELATIONSHIP DELTA
Maya's physical/social attention corrects the team's tendency to treat model success as abstract.

# 9. CHAPTER 14 — PROBABLE YES

## Locked architectural direction

Samir identifies a variable/logic corresponding to predicted accommodation/willingness strongly enough to support a bounded local claim.

### OWNER
Samir, with Ollie/Voss as falsification pressure.

### PRIOR CONSEQUENCE
Ch11 shows distributional suspicion; Ch13 shows declared action can conflict with inferred compliance/preference.

### IMMEDIATE OBJECTIVE
Test whether the burden pattern is actually associated with predicted accommodation, rather than a correlated innocent variable.

### PRESSURE / OBSTACLE / SUPPORT
Must rule out plausible alternatives:
- route geometry;
- accessibility needs;
- staffing/load;
- sample imbalance;
- safety rules;
- another correlated behavior variable.

### BULL'S-EYE
A falsification attempt removes the strongest benign alternative and leaves the accommodation hypothesis standing under defined conditions.

### OUTCOME
**Bounded proof, not universal condemnation.** `PROBABLE YES` means the local claim crosses a support threshold; it does not prove malicious intent or universal behavior.

### NEXT INTENTION
Determine what operational/rights threshold this supported pattern triggers and how it should be communicated publicly before expansion/opening pressure increases.

### A-STORY VARIABLE
Evidence threshold / inferred willingness.

### FUTURE PAYOFF
Provides a legitimate evidentiary component for later Hold while leaving system benefit intact.

### SCENE FORM
Falsification/test scene with a real alternative hypothesis, not exposition reveal.

### HUMAN ARENA
Keep at least one visible affected case in memory/foreground so formal proof remains attached to human consequence.

### READER QUESTION
`Did they prove the thing they feared—or merely find a correlation they like?`

### INFORMATION DELTA
Before: pattern suspicion.  
After: bounded supported mechanism under specified conditions.

### EMOTIONAL / RELATIONSHIP DELTA
Samir's caution becomes productive authority: his refusal to overclaim is what makes the eventual claim credible.
