## 1. PURPOSE
Apply strict-read mechanisms from Alice LaPlante Chapters 5–10 without retrofitting manuscripts to a new formula.

## 2. LESSON ZERO
Book One remains development-locked. A craft source alone is not evidence for reopening it.

For any future regression pass, diagnose in this order:
`CAUSAL DEFECT? → POV ACCESS/DISTANCE? → SHOW/TELL ROUTING? → DIALOGUE MODE? → PARAGRAPH? → SENTENCE?`

Do not perform line-level smoothing when the actual defect is scene proximity or information access.

## 3. ORBITAL YOUTH

### 3.1 Bounded knowledge
A close-POV teenager may know:
- local routes and institutions;
- peer norms;
- personal/family experience;
- training rules actually taught;
- public facts and personally obtained evidence.

They may not automatically know:
- total civilization-level economics/governance;
- hidden robot-civilization decisions;
- specialist-school ontologies;
- historical provenance unavailable to them;
- author-only future facts.

### 3.2 World reveal
Reveal post-money society through lived particulars and choices, not thesis paragraphs.

### 3.3 Character differentiation
For each primary character specify at least three relationship contexts and how tactic/register changes while identity remains continuous.

## 4. CONSCIOUS ROBOTS
A named conscious robot fails personhood QA if it can be replaced with a smart terminal without changing consent, relationships, refusal, conflict or consequences.

No universal robot dialect. Dialogue differences must arise from history, goals, embodiment, culture, relationship and leverage.

## 5. SPECIALIST CONSCIOUSNESS / YOGA SCHOOLS
Epistemic firewall:
`OBSERVATION → REPRODUCIBLE PRACTICE RESULT → SCHOOL INTERPRETATION → COMPETING INTERPRETATION → ONTOLOGICAL CLAIM`.

Narration may not silently upgrade an earlier stage into the last one.

The small number of long-term practitioners remains a real developmental bottleneck; advanced external technology does not solve this automatically.

## 6. MYSTERY / FAIR PLAY
Maintain source status for every important proposition:
- observed trace;
- POV inference;
- witness/source claim;
- institutional interpretation;
- independent confirmation;
- established world fact.

If the current POV is unreliable or limited, the reader still needs an evidence channel sufficient to recalibrate the model.

## 7. DIALOGUE
Use direct dialogue when exact wording changes:
- decision;
- consent;
- evidence status;
- trust;
- power;
- relationship;
- emotion with downstream consequence.

Use narration/indirect dialogue for routine, repeated process and facts whose wording is not itself consequential.

Important dialogue scenes must remain embodied: silence, gesture, object use and environment may be part of the tactic.

## 8. PROSE / CHOPPINESS
Before editing short paragraphs, ask:
1. Should this material be a scene at all?
2. Should the conversation be direct?
3. Is POV distance oscillating?
4. Is one thought unnecessarily split into micro-actions/reactions?
5. Only then: does sentence rhythm need repair?

Short sentences remain valid as contrast, impact or voice. They are not a target metric.

## 9. ONE MAIN STORY
Soft causality is allowed only when downstream state changes.
A quiet scene belongs if it changes emotion, relationship, status, knowledge/model or readiness to choose in a way that affects the common causal engine.

## 10. CURRENT VERDICT
No rebuild authorized for LESSON ZERO or Orbital Youth solely because of this research pass. Apply these gates prospectively and surgically when a concrete defect appears.
