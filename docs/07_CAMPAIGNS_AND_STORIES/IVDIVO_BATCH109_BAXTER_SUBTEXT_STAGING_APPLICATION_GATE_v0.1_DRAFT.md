## GLOBAL RULE

Subtext is an **additive surface-pressure layer**. It cannot repair broken causality, canon, evidence provenance, personhood or scene necessity.

Before applying Baxter:
`ONE MAIN STORY → CAUSALITY → CHARACTER/PERSONHOOD → SCENE FUNCTION → POV/EVIDENCE → THEN SUBTEXT/STAGING`.

## ROOM 917 — MYSTERY-PRIMARY ROMANCE

### Protect
- Elena and Julian remain independent agents.
- Relationship-state changes may alter disclosure, access, willingness to cooperate, risk tolerance and next investigative tactic.
- Mystery evidence remains separately sourced and corroborated.

### Stage rather than explain
- distance from the sealed wall / receiver / switchboard;
- who holds or relinquishes the recorder/receiver;
- who blocks or leaves an exit open;
- who watches Elena versus the evidence;
- Julian's choice to answer directly, partially, or not at all;
- Margot's control of seating, access, documents and who is made to wait.

### Hard firewall
`ATTRACTION / TRUST / KISS / CONFESSION / SILENCE ≠ CLUE CORROBORATION`.

A relational beat may change what Elena does with evidence. It may not change what the evidence proves.

### Anti-overcoding
Not every Elena–Julian exchange should be cryptic. After sufficient earned pressure, a plain direct sentence can carry more weight than another evasive line.

## ORBITAL YOUTH

### Youth voice
Do not import contemporary Earth slang as the primary youth marker. Build voice partly from:
- what the teenager notices;
- what is normal enough to ignore;
- what produces embarrassment or pride;
- how attention shifts under a mentor, peer, parent, crush or conscious robot;
- how quickly the character answers or redirects.

### Post-money staging
Show post-money normality through **absence of attention** to ordinary fungible goods. Hyperattention should cluster around nonfungible stakes: another person's time, consent, a mission slot, trust, privacy, unique access, reputation, mastery, belonging or relationship terms.

### Conscious robots
No universal `robot voice` or human-face default.
For each named conscious robot define:
`EMBODIMENT-SPECIFIC SIGNALS → RELATION-SPECIFIC ATTENTION → OWN WANT → CONSENT/REFUSAL → TACTIC → CONSEQUENCE`.

Human interpretation of a robot cue is only an interpretation unless independently corroborated.

## B07 — THE UNLISTED CITY

### Kyra
Use staging to show procedural precision before explaining it:
- boundaries she notices;
- exact placement of tools/records;
- access zones;
- who has standing to speak;
- what she checks before acting;
- where she physically positions herself relative to rule, evidence and affected person.

Keep accepted arc:
`PROCEDURAL PRECISION → EVIDENCE EXCEEDS CATEGORY → BOUNDED DISCRETION → DOCUMENTED ACCOUNTABILITY`.

### Distributed nonhuman person
Do not encode personhood through petlike or human facial cues. Build a signal surface native to its embodiment. Human observers may misread it.
The nonhuman person must retain:
`OWN PREFERENCE → OWN SIGNAL / EVIDENCE → CONSENT OR REFUSAL → INDISPENSABLE CHOICE → CONSEQUENCE`.

## CONSCIOUSNESS / PRACTICE SCHOOLS

Subtext and bodily response are valid characterization, not ontology.

Hard evidence ladder:
`PRACTITIONER OBSERVATION → REPRODUCIBLE EFFECT → SCHOOL INTERPRETATION → COMPETING INTERPRETATION → POSSIBLE ONTOLOGICAL FACT`.

A practitioner avoiding a question, showing fear, or responding bodily may reveal their relationship to a belief. It does not prove that belief true.

## DIALOGUE / SCENE QA

For every dialogue-heavy scene ask:
1. What does each agent want right now?
2. Who actually listens to which line?
3. Who is attending to another problem?
4. What is conveyed by space/object control rather than speech?
5. Does silence alter state or merely decorate?
6. Are gestures character-specific or filler?
7. Does any bodily cue get incorrectly promoted to motive certainty?
8. Is a direct statement stronger than another layer of evasive subtext?
9. Does the scene change action, knowledge/evidence, relationship, authority, responsibility, strategy or future options?
10. If not, should it be summary/indirect/merge/delete?

## ANTI-FRAGMENT ROUTER

Do not create Baxter-like surface texture by inserting more one-line gestures or micro-reactions.

Use the established order:
`SCENE NECESSITY → ORDER/DURATION/FREQUENCY → POV/EVIDENCE → SHOW/TELL → DIALOGUE/SUBTEXT → PARAGRAPH INTENT → READER-STATE CONTINUITY → SENTENCE RHYTHM → WORDING`.

If five micro-paragraphs preserve one actor/time/place/focus and do not contain real turns, merge or compress before polishing.

## LESSON ZERO BOOK ONE

**NO DEVELOPMENT-LOCK REOPEN.**
Baxter is available for targeted dialogue/staging QA only if an actual current-text defect is identified or reader evidence requires it.

## ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

Baxter application passes only if:
- causality remains intact;
- exact evidence status remains intact;
- personhood is not reduced to cues;
- consent remains explicit where required;
- subtext does not become universal evasiveness;
- staging changes or reveals a meaningful relational/social condition;
- prose rhythm is not made choppier by added micro-actions.

Final rule:
**USE SUBTEXT TO LET THE READER INFER PRESSURE; NEVER USE IT TO HIDE FACTS THE STORY OWES OR TO REPLACE CAUSAL EVIDENCE.**
