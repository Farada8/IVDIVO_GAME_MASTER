# 2. CHAPTER TWO — YOUR CALL

## PATCH 2A — hold-scope list compression

### Current issue
The legal precision is necessary, but the prose repeats the Book One staccato template:

`Not the whole building. Not emergency translation. Not alarms...`

### Replace that exclusion ladder with

The hold was not symbolic, but it was narrow. Ethan could pause **predictive relational routing** in approved youth and mixed-family pilot zones; he could not touch emergency translation, alarms, manually declared accessibility routes or adult-only operations. Even inside his own scope, the hold required a defined trigger and a review state.

### Preserve immediately after

The actual trigger list:
- unreviewed participant-safety risk;
- scope violation;
- material mismatch between declared and inferred preference;
- loss of required participant override;
- restricted action outside approved youth condition.

### Why
Legal precision remains, but the prose stops treating every limitation as a separate dramatic punch.

## PATCH 2B — consequence responsibility

**PROTECT VOSS'S DISTINCTION.**

The exchange separating responsibility for a justified decision from total causal ownership of every downstream event is essential Book Two material.

However, the following explanatory sequence should be tightened on line edit:

`The difference between Book One and this room was suddenly very small and very large... Here, somebody had drawn the line in advance. Then given him a key.`

At WCF, Ethan had crossed a line because uncertainty became intolerable. Here the line had been drawn in advance, documented, and attached to his name.

The key was real.

### Why
Keeps one earned short emphasis sentence instead of stacking several abstractions.
