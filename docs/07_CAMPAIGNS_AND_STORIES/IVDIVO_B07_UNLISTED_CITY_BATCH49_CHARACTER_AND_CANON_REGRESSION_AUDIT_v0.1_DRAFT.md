# IVDIVO B07 — THE UNLISTED CITY
## Batch 49 Character & Canon Regression Audit v0.1

Date: 2026-08-17
Status: ADDITIVE WORKING AUDIT — DOES NOT APPROVE B07 CANON
Reference craft source: McKee *Character* strict lifecycle v1.0

## Executive verdict
**KEEP THE STORY ENGINE / REPAIR SPECIFIC CANON AND CHARACTER RISKS.**

B07 already has a strong one-book causal engine: Kyra wants the single Human Contact placement; an evacuation exposes a distributed city ecology excluded from the operational list; saving its continuity requires evidence, consent, engineering, institutional negotiation and a real personal price. McKee does not justify rebuilding that spine.

The current dossier does, however, contain two clear regressions against the latest orbital boundaries plus one ontology ambiguity that must be resolved before prose.

## A. Canon regressions — STOP before prose

### A1 — Money / credit / rent language has returned
Current dossier includes family beats built around `жильё, деньги, назначения`, a call beginning with `жилья, кредита и окончания аренды`, and a stated economic frame inherited from contemporary Earth.

**Required repair:** remove ordinary money/credit/rent as daily organizing stakes in mature orbital/confederative city life. Preserve the emotional function through nonfungible stakes:
- location / preferred habitat;
- independence from family;
- assignment or practice slot;
- time with a specific person;
- unique civic responsibility;
- access to a particular program/place;
- family expectation / prestige / continuity of a civil-service tradition.

Do not rename money `credits`.

### A2 — Routine licensed service-body transition conflicts with current technology boundary
Current chapter plan treats cadets as routinely `переходят в лицензированные сервисные тела`, uses `решётки непрерывности`, an overlap failure, and later makes those grids the key technology for transferring CAM-4 continuity.

As currently worded, this reads as ordinary operational transfer/continuity of human consciousness between bodies. That is incompatible with the current orbital directive that operational human consciousness/soul transfer is **not** available in this civilization.

**Required repair:** before prose choose a canon-safe alternative. Examples of function-preserving replacements:
- remotely operated non-conscious service frames;
- telepresence rigs with continuous biological POV retained in the original body;
- external protective morphologies/exosystems that do not relocate subjectivity;
- environmental capsules/drones;
- nonhuman robotic persons doing hazardous-zone work by their own consent, if their civilization and agency are properly written.

The climax technology must then be rebuilt so CAM-4 continuity is not casually derived from a human consciousness-transfer technology that canon says does not exist.

### A3 — Tem ontology is ambiguous
Tem is treated relationally as a friend/person who can refuse diagnostic access, but the climax also calls for `синтетический диагностический контур Тем` as if Tem can be reduced to hardware.

Before prose define one of these:
1. **conscious robotic/synthetic person** — independent interests, relationships, consent, right to refuse, no ownership/tool language; or
2. **non-conscious device/interface** — then do not stage personhood/consent beats as though it were a person.

A conscious person may voluntarily provide a subsystem or capability, but the text must preserve the distinction between **person** and **equipment**.

## B. Character architecture — what already works

### B1 — Kyra has a clear want + MO
External want: Human Contact Residency.
Current MO: procedural perfection, correct categorization, defensible compliance, career cleanliness.

This is stronger than a generic `ambitious cadet` trait because it predicts action. Early scenes repeatedly test the same method under different conditions.

### B2 — The book attacks the method, not merely the goal
Kyra does not need to learn `career is bad`. The stronger arc is:

`PROCEDURAL PERFECTION → EVIDENCE-BASED RESPONSIBILITY → BOUNDED DISCRETION WITH ACCOUNTABILITY`.

She can still want Contact at the end. What changes is what she believes competent service requires.

### B3 — Sari is a useful countermodel
Sari's model privileges explainable improvisation, reserve for unlisted dependencies and transparency. He should not become `the correct boy who teaches Kyra the theme`.

Keep both sides costly:
- Kyra's procedure protects people from arbitrary heroics;
- Sari's flexibility catches realities outside the list;
- transparency can become coercive exposure;
- secrecy can become irresponsible unilateralism.

### B4 — Daal is strongest as competent opposition
Daal's arithmetic and authority are legitimate. Her refusal should continue to protect actual registered residents, not hide corruption. This supports IVDIVO's competent-countermodel rule.

### B5 — Iven works best as relationship pressure, not plot oracle
His fear comes from a prior rescue failure and gives the anti-Kyra case emotional legitimacy. Remove the money/rent frame; keep the deeper relationship conflict:

`I know what unverified rescue promises can cost → I don't want my daughter building identity from a beautiful uncertainty.`

## C. McKee-derived character gates for B07

### C1 — Kyra relationship-register matrix
Before prose, specify how Kyra's behavior differs with:
- Sari — peer/rival/intimate possibility;
- Tem — friend + nonhuman/synthetic person or defined alternative;
- Iven — parent/family legacy;
- Daal — authority;
- CAM-4 — unknown distributed agency whose status is unproven;
- ordinary residents — people whose safety makes her abstractions costly.

If Kyra uses the same clipped competence register and the same tactic with all six, voice/character work is not finished.

### C2 — Sari independent-life test
Sari needs at least one want and one relationship that are not functions of Kyra. His Human Contact candidacy is a start. Add a non-Kyra cost/commitment that affects his decisions.

### C3 — Tem independent-life test
If Tem is a conscious person, the dossier must answer:
- What does Tem want when Kyra is absent?
- Who besides Kyra matters to Tem?
- Why does Tem participate in repair work?
- What risk is unacceptable to Tem?
- What would Tem do if Kyra left the book?

### C4 — CAM-4 must not be a moral vending machine
CAM-4 should not exist solely to teach Kyra `ask what the subject wants`.
It already has stronger material: long-term preferences, costly care patterns, refusal, mistakes, local attachments, acceptable losses. Preserve behavior that is not optimized for Kyra's arc.

### C5 — Character evidence remains provisional
Do not let one crisis prove `who Kyra really is`. Use repeated evidence:

`early procedural success → safe refusal → unauthorized test → relational mistake with Tem → revised request → crisis choice → aftermath behavior`.

This produces earned inference without essentialist `true self` language.

## D. Arc and scene causality

### Kyra's provisional MO ladder
1. **Opening:** answers rules perfectly.
2. **Early field work:** treats anomalies as categorization errors.
3. **First adaptation:** accepts a local safety delay when category is recognized.
4. **Blind spot:** assumes Tem diagnostic access because the need seems obvious.
5. **Model change:** accepts that moral claim without operational plan is insufficient.
6. **New tactic:** asks for bounded tests, consent, risk, exit conditions.
7. **Crisis:** uses narrow authority to create time for verification, knowing it costs placement/certification.
8. **Aftermath:** does not convert sacrifice into entitlement to praise or to CAM-4's future choices.

This is a coherent character arc because tactics change while the underlying values — competence, usefulness, responsibility — remain recognizable.

## E. Specific dossier repairs

These chapters currently depend on human service-body transfer and continuity grids. They must be re-architected together, not line-edited separately.

**Do structural repair first:** choose a canon-safe technical mechanism, then rebuild all setup/payoff beats around it.

### Chapter 4 / family line
Replace credit/rent with a post-money family independence pressure. Do not merely delete the nouns; preserve the causal job of the scene: Iven offers a safe future path that threatens Kyra's sense of self-authorship.

### Chapter 17 / Tem refusal
Keep the refusal beat if Tem is conscious. It is one of the best relationship-specific tests in the dossier because Kyra repeats, on a friend, the same category error she is fighting institutionally.

### Chapter 28 / Kyra–Sari repair
Keep `repair by behavior`, not total romantic fusion. Sari does not surrender his career to prove love; Kyra does not need instant forgiveness. This is stronger than a completion fantasy.

### Chapter 35–36
Keep consequences: certification delay, lost CAM-4 memory, wounded residents, Sari's placement, local provisional status. Do not award Kyra a substitute medal that cancels the price.

`WANT (Contact place) → MO (procedural perfection) → REAL EMERGENCY → CATEGORY FAILURE → EVIDENCE → RELATIONSHIP FAILURE → OPERATIONAL COUNTERMODEL → CONSENT → COSTLY BOUNDED CHOICE → LOCAL RESULT → AFTERMATH`.

The story engine is strong enough to survive the canon repairs. Fix technology/economy first; then preserve the character causality already doing useful work.
