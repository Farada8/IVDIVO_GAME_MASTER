# 1. ONE MAIN STORY — CONTROL STATEMENT

**Concrete task:** audit Rivergate's predictive relational-routing system before public opening and determine when it may operate, when participant choice must override inference, and whether fallback is usable enough for a legitimate hold.

**Hero:** Ethan.

**Hero's specific burden:** he has a real Youth-Scope Hold and must eventually decide whether to use it while both action and inaction have visible costs.

**Opposition:** not an evil engineer and not a useless system. Opposition is the collision among:
- Lattice's real safety benefit;
- inferred relations / inferred accommodation;
- burden concentration;
- incomplete provenance;
- limited audit time;
- imperfect fallback;
- different legitimate human priorities.

**Climax owed:** live opening-weekend hold decision.

**Local ending owed:** Rivergate/Lattice gets a concrete operating settlement. Provenance may remain open only after the public-system story closes.

# 3. OPENING MOVEMENT CAUSAL CHAIN

The first six chapters must read as one advancing story, not six thematic demonstrations:

`CH1 — ETHAN USES SMALL AUTHORITY CORRECTLY`
→ anomaly proves relational inference can alter real routes
→ Rivergate recruits group

`CH2 — AUTHORITY IS FORMALLY GIVEN`
→ Ethan learns both hold and non-hold create accountable consequences
→ access to actual system opens

`CH3 — SYSTEM'S LOGIC IS DEMONSTRATED`
→ declared vs inferred relation becomes audit problem
→ fallback/provenance questions become actionable
→ opening clock + audit deliverable become explicit

`CH4 — FIRST THRESHOLD TEST`
→ evidence is uncomfortable but insufficient
→ Ethan chooses not to hold
→ establishes threshold discipline

`CH5 — RESPONSIBILITY DISTRIBUTED ACROSS GROUP`
→ Maya/Nico, Samir/data, Aoife/recording roles acquire independent decision rights
→ prevents Ethan-only story machine

`CH6 — LATTICE GENUINELY SAVES`
→ shutdown now has moral cost
→ reader enters Movement II with a real conflict rather than an obvious answer.

# 4. READER-RETENTION QUESTIONS OWNED BY OPENING

These are not all mysteries. They are distinct forward-pressure channels.

### Main suspense
Will Ethan eventually have to stop a system that is genuinely making Rivergate safer—and what will stopping it cost?

### Ethical/technical curiosity
When does prediction of likely cooperation become exploitation of willingness?

### Provenance mystery
Why does Lattice share a rare relational shape with older human / nonhuman structures?

### Relationship pressure
Can the five carry different responsibilities without Ethan turning their roles into his project?

### Character suspense
Can Ethan tolerate a justified decision whose outcome still looks bad afterward?

### Ordinary-life promise
Will Ollie's maker work, Maya's responsibility for Nico, Aoife's media role and Samir's data boundaries remain real lives rather than support functions?
