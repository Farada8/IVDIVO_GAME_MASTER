# IVDIVO — BATCH 189 APPLICATION GATE
## Goal Legitimacy · Relationship Closure · Durable Change · Nonhuman Agency

**Source scope:** Christopher Booker, *The Seven Basic Plots*, strict read through Part Three Ch25.  
**Authority:** diagnostic only. Does not override Founder/direct canon, active reconciler, locked manuscript or evidence ledger.

## Standing law
`NO NEW EVIDENCE → NO NEW GLOBAL REWRITE`.

This batch is applied only when a current scene/book shows a concrete defect.

## LESSON ZERO Book One
### Decision
**KEEP DEVELOPMENT LOCK.**

Booker does not establish a new current defect. No retrofit to archetypal marriage, anima/animus, quest/rebirth structure or mandatory transformation is permitted.

### Local repair only if evidence appears
Open a patch only for a concrete repeated defect such as:
- formal success presented as complete resolution while the book's governing responsibility/relationship problem remains unchanged;
- relationship label presented as consent/trust without behavioral evidence;
- coincidence stacking that forces an outcome;
- discourse alignment hiding the cost of a protagonist's action;
- surface reset falsely presented as character development.

## Orbital Youth
### Gate OY-B189-01 — Qualification is not belonging
`CREDENTIAL / ROLE / ACCESS → ACTUAL SOCIAL / RELATIONAL / RESPONSIBILITY STATE`.

A cadet passing an exam or earning access may still lack belonging, trust or decision legitimacy.

### Gate OY-B189-02 — Success may increase responsibility
Protect:
`COMPETENT ACTION → REAL SUCCESS → MORE TRUST / ACCESS / RESPONSIBILITY → HARDER NEXT CHOICE`.

Do not force a setback merely to satisfy a dark/tragic pattern.

### Gate OY-B189-03 — Goal legitimacy
For any institutional or mission objective:
`WHO SET IT? → WHO BENEFITS? → WHO BEARS COST? → WHO MAY REFUSE? → WHAT DOES SUCCESS CHANGE?`

Post-money firewall remains active. Stakes are trust, consent, privacy, responsibility, time, unique opportunity, role, mastery, belonging and relationships — not salary/rent/credits by another name.

### Gate OY-B189-04 — New-world payment
A new orbital district, training system or technology receives full scene weight only when it changes affordances/constraints and therefore a character's action or relationship.

`NOVELTY ≠ STORY FUNCTION`.

## ROOM 917
### Gate R917-B189-01 — Recognition is not proof by feeling
`OLD INVESTIGATIVE MODEL → DISCRIMINATING TRACE / TEST → MODEL UPDATE → NEXT TACTIC`.

Recognition must be caused by evidence, not by romance or thematic intuition.

### Gate R917-B189-02 — Relationship closure is separate
`KISS / CONFESSION / REUNION / FORMAL COUPLE STATUS ≠ TRUST / DISCLOSURE / BOUNDARIES / CONSENT`.

And absolute standing firewall:
`TRUST / ATTRACTION / KISS / CONFESSION / SILENCE / INFLECTION ≠ CORROBORATION`.

Relationship change may alter source access or willingness to disclose. It does not change what a trace proves.

### Gate R917-B189-03 — Narrative glamour audit
If Elena or Julian receives sympathetic interiority/charisma while causing harm or hiding evidence, verify that consequences remain visible and that POV alignment does not convert their frame into world fact.

## B07 — THE UNLISTED CITY
### Gate B07-B189-01 — Rescue is not ownership
`DISCOVERED / CREATED / RESCUED ≠ OWNED`.

Human success at preserving a distributed life form does not grant humans the right to choose its desired continuity.

After independent personhood threshold:
`ENTITY MODEL / PREFERENCE → CONSENT / REFUSAL → ENTITY CHOICE → HUMAN OPTION SPACE CHANGES`.

### Gate B07-B189-02 — Goal legitimacy
Even a technically excellent preservation plan must answer:
- who defined preservation as the objective;
- what forms of continuity the entity itself recognizes;
- what costs are imposed;
- who can refuse;
- whether humans are preserving a person or a human category of that person.

### Gate B07-B189-03 — Nonhuman embodiment firewall
No Booker-style symbolic mapping of nonhuman/strange embodiment to monstrosity, shadow, incompleteness or moral darkness.

## General endings gate
For every major book ending, record separately:
1. objective outcome;
2. evidence/knowledge outcome;
3. relationship/consent outcome;
4. authority/responsibility outcome;
5. personhood/decision-ownership outcome;
6. cost;
7. future option space;
8. optional saga vector.

`LOCAL RESOLUTION → COST / AFTERMATH → CHANGED STATE → OPTIONAL SAGA HOOK`.

A sequel hook cannot substitute for the local answer the book promised.
