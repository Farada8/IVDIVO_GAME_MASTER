# IVDIVO — BATCH 100 APPLICATION GATE
## Swain Reader Effect · Resolution · Philosophy Firewall

**Status:** additive production control; not a canon rewrite.

## 1. ONE MAIN STORY
A book may use many reader effects, but they must support one dominant causal engine. Do not create a second catastrophe/mystery/romance merely because the current emotional mode has run too long.

## 2. Reader-effect routing
Before intensifying a sequence, identify what the reader is supposed to feel or anticipate:

`curiosity / hope / worry / intimacy / wonder / competence / humor / discovery / relational pressure / ethical uncertainty / earned relief`.

If the only answer is `more worry`, test for tension monoculture.

## 3. YA / Orbital Youth
Do not buy attachment with trauma. Strong youth-reader bond may come from:
- first real responsibility;
- competence under bounded authority;
- an intelligent mistake;
- awkward belonging;
- friendship/romance under consent constraints;
- humor and status friction;
- a unique opportunity that cannot simply be manufactured;
- consequences of being trusted.

Post-money stakes remain trust, time, consent, responsibility, relationships, reputation, mastery, specific role and unique experience. No salary/rent/credits substitutes.

## 4. Conscious robots
Do not use helplessness, petlike behavior, 'secret humanity' or sacrificial utility as automatic empathy shortcuts.

A named conscious robot needs:
`OWN WANT → OWN MODEL/EVIDENCE → OWN RELATIONSHIPS → CONSENT/REFUSAL → CHOICE → CONSEQUENCE`.

Human emotional response to the robot is not evidence of the robot's motive or ontological status.

## 5. Consciousness-practice schools
Never use reader reassurance, awe, mystical beauty or emotional catharsis as proof of metaphysical claims.

Maintain:
`practice observation → reproducible effect → school interpretation → competing interpretation → possible ontological conclusion`.

## 6. Endings
The local book ending is not required to enact poetic justice.

Use:
`BOOK PROMISE/QUESTION → DECISIVE CHOICE/ACTION → CONSEQUENCE → COST → AFTERMATH → CHANGED STATE → OPTIONAL SAGA VECTOR`.

Mystery proof ceiling remains active: local operational truth may be resolved while global ontology remains unknown.

## 7. LESSON ZERO
No development-lock reopening solely because Swain offers a different reader-satisfaction theory. Reopen only for a demonstrated current-manuscript defect or strong external reader evidence.

## 8. Revision order
Reader-effect weakness is diagnosed after structural validity:

`CAUSAL ENGINE → SCENE NECESSITY → POV/EVIDENCE → RELATIONSHIP/CHARACTER → DIALOGUE MODE → PARAGRAPH/RHYTHM → WORDING → READER-EFFECT TEST`.

Do not manufacture danger to repair prose rhythm; do not line-edit a structurally unnecessary scene.
