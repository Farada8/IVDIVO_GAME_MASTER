# IVDIVO — BATCH 134 APPLICATION GATE
## Bickham: Exposition · Dialogue · Voice · Reader Orientation

Status: additive production-control layer. Does not override canon or reopen locked manuscripts by itself.

## 1. Exposition gate

Before any explanatory material enters a scene, answer:

1. Who needs, notices, recalls, asks for, disputes or acts on this information **now**?
2. Why is it salient now rather than earlier/later?
3. What is the source and evidence-status?
4. Is the information already inferable from action/world cues?
5. What changes after the information is received?

Preferred chain:

`CURRENT QUESTION / NEED → MINIMUM NECESSARY INFORMATION → SOURCE / ACCESS → ACTION / TEST / DECISION → CHANGED STATE`

Reject:

`AUTHOR WANTS READER TO KNOW → paragraph / speech`.

## 2. Theme gate

Delete or mute the explicit thematic speech in a diagnostic copy. If the thematic question disappears, fix actions and consequences.

`THEMATIC QUESTION → COMPETING MODELS → CHOICES → CONSEQUENCES → READER INFERENCE`

Do not turn competent adults, institutions, schools or conscious robots into strawmen in order to prove the preferred theme.

## 3. Dialogue gate

For meaningful exchanges:

`WANT → VERBAL / NONVERBAL TACTIC → LISTENER ATTENTION / RESPONSE → TACTIC CHANGE → CHANGED STATE`

Exact words deserve real-time scene when they change trust, consent, evidence, authority, relationship terms, decision, emotion or future options.

If the exchange only carries routine process or already-known information: use indirect dialogue / summary / compression.

## 4. Ensemble gate

Do not simplify every group scene into one-on-one dialogue merely because it is easier to write.

Track:
- who is actually listening;
- who already knows the fact;
- who misunderstands it;
- who interrupts;
- who has standing to answer;
- who stays silent strategically;
- who controls evidence/access;
- whose goal changes because of another person's line.

A group scene is justified when these asymmetries matter to ONE MAIN STORY.

## 5. Reader orientation / anti-choppy gate

Minimum reader model during dialogue:

`WHERE / BODY + PRESENT WANT + RELATIONSHIP / STATUS + CRITICAL PHYSICAL CHANGE + POV INTERPRETATION WHEN NEEDED`

Do not create:

`line → glance → hand movement → sensory fragment → thought → line`.

If several microbeats form one perceptual or decision unit, combine them into a coherent paragraph before line polishing.

## 6. Attribution gate

Use the least conspicuous form that preserves clarity:

`NO TAG / SAID-STYLE TRANSPARENT TAG / MEANINGFUL ACTION BEAT / INDIRECT REPORT`.

No numeric quota for `said`. No thesaurus-tagging. Do not add meaningless action merely to avoid a dialogue tag.

## 7. Voice gate

Character voice comes from:

`WORLDVIEW + RELATIONSHIP + STATUS + PRESENT GOAL + ATTITUDE → WORD CHOICE + SYNTAX + RHYTHM + REGISTER + OMISSION`.

### Orbital Youth
No generic contemporary-Earth teen slang layer. Future youth voice must reflect what this society treats as normal, embarrassing, prestigious, intimate or unfair.

### Conscious robots
No universal robot register. Require own goal, relationship-specific speech tactics, knowledge limits and the right not to answer.

## 8. Mystery / evidence gate

Never allow exposition to collapse:

`SOURCE / TRACE → OBSERVED / REPORTED → INTERPRETATION → HYPOTHESIS → CORROBORATION → WORLD FACT`.

A confident explanation remains a sourced claim.

## 9. Consciousness-practice gate

`PRACTITIONER OBSERVATION → REPRODUCIBLE EFFECT → SCHOOL INTERPRETATION → COMPETING INTERPRETATION → INDEPENDENT TEST → ONTOLOGY CEILING`.

Intensity, eloquence or prestige do not upgrade ontology.

## 10. Post-money worldbuilding gate

Do not explain the economy as a lecture. Show ordinary causal normality and reserve attention for truly non-fungible stakes: time, trust, consent, relationships, unique opportunities, specific responsibility, reputation, mastery, privacy and belonging.

## 11. Repair order

`BOOK ENGINE → CHAPTER FUNCTION → SCENE NECESSITY → SCENE WEIGHT / MODE → POV / EVIDENCE → EXPOSITION OWNER → DIALOGUE TACTICS → PARAGRAPH INTENT / READER ORIENTATION → SENTENCE RHYTHM → WORDING`.

A lecture that should be cut is not a line-edit problem. A routine conversation that should be summarized is not improved by extra gestures.

## Locked manuscript policy

LESSON ZERO Book One remains development-locked unless concrete current reader evidence, continuity evidence, acquisition evidence, audio evidence or a real text defect establishes a problem. Batch 134 alone is not grounds for reopening it.
