# IVDIVO Batch 150 — Emotional Dimensionality Application Gate

Status: DRAFT. Additive diagnostic gate; does not authorize global manuscript rebuilds.

## Purpose

Prevent flat emotional labeling, arbitrary contradiction, therapeutic-retrofit YA arcs, microgesture clutter, and evidence errors while preserving character-specific emotional complexity.

## Gate A — Character emotion must have a current causal root

Before intensifying an emotion, answer:

`WHAT VALUE / CONCERN IS ACTIVE? → WITH WHOM? → UNDER WHAT CIRCUMSTANCE? → AT WHAT COST? → WHAT TACTIC FOLLOWS?`

A label such as angry, scared, in love, grieving, loyal, brave, or ashamed is not sufficient.

## Gate B — Same value may produce different surfaces

Accept an apparently contradictory behavior only when the bridge is legible through changed circumstance, relationship, knowledge, embodiment, or competing priority.

Do not add an opposite trait just to manufacture complexity.

## Gate C — Growth requires seeds, not compulsory transformation

For a major later behavioral shift, locate earlier capacity or precedent. If none exists, either plant a truthful precursor or reduce the magnitude of the shift.

Allowed end states include:
- partial change;
- tactical change;
- relational change;
- stable values under stronger test;
- negative change;
- deferred series change.

## Gate D — Emotion and evidence remain separate

For mystery / investigation:

`EMOTIONAL DISPLAY → POV INTERPRETATION → ALTERNATIVE MODEL → CORROBORATION`.

Never promote fear, confidence, tears, anger, attraction, silence, hesitation, or apparent evasiveness directly to motive/guilt/world fact.

For ROOM 917:
`ELENA↔JULIAN TRUST / ATTRACTION / DIRECTNESS ≠ CLUE CORROBORATION`.

## Gate E — Youth differentiation

For Ethan, Maya, Ollie, Aoife, Samir and future youth casts, distinguish emotional response through:
- what each person protects;
- what each person notices;
- evidence threshold;
- relationship-specific risk;
- first tactic;
- tactic change after resistance;
- cost they will accept/refuse.

Do not distinguish them primarily through current Earth slang, quirks, or identical witty banter.

## Gate F — Conscious robots / nonhuman persons

Do not require human facial/physiological cues.

Use:
`EMBODIMENT + OWN PRIORITIES + OWN MODEL/EVIDENCE + RELATIONSHIP + CONSENT/REFUSAL → TACTIC / CHOICE → CONSEQUENCE`.

Apparent emotional contradiction may arise from competing priorities or constraints and does not imply humanization.

## Gate G — Anti-choppy prose

Forbidden default:
`line → glance → hand → breath → one-line thought → line → expression`.

Repair order:
`SCENE NECESSITY → POV/EVIDENCE → EMOTIONAL TURN → PARAGRAPH INTENT → READER-STATE CONTINUITY → SENTENCE RHYTHM → WORDING`.

Several microbeats belonging to one perceptual/decision unit should normally remain in one coherent paragraph.

## Gate H — LESSON ZERO lock

Batch 150 does not reopen Book One.

Standing rule:
`NO NEW EVIDENCE → NO NEW GLOBAL REWRITE`.

Only a concrete current defect — repeated reader confusion, audio evidence, continuity/canon error, character behavior that lacks causal support, or demonstrable microgesture/choppy-prose defect — authorizes a targeted patch.
