## A. Choppiness diagnostic

When a passage feels fragmentary, do **not** begin by increasing sentence length.

Run in order:

1. Is the scene itself necessary?
2. Is real-time scene the correct narrative mode, or should this be summary/indirect/compressed?
3. Is POV/evidence access stable?
4. What is the paragraph's single function?
5. What is the topic of each sentence?
6. Does the next sentence begin from information the reader currently holds?
7. Is the major new information receiving deliberate stress?
8. Only then adjust sentence rhythm and wording.

### Merge trigger

If several short units preserve the same actor, time, place, causal question and perceptual/decision unit, test one coherent paragraph or sentence before editing them independently.

## B. Institutional / post-money language

For words such as `allocation`, `priority`, `qualification`, `access`, `contribution`, `assignment`, `authorization`, `service` and `scheduling`, ask:

`WHO DECIDES? → BY WHAT RULE? → WHO MAY REFUSE/APPEAL? → WHO BEARS THE COST? → WHAT OPTION SPACE CHANGES?`

If the noun merely hides salary/rent/credits or obscures power, repair the world/causal model rather than polishing the wording.

## C. Mystery / evidence clarity

Clarity must not collapse epistemic levels.

Keep explicit distinctions among:

`OBSERVED / REPORTED / INFERRED / HYPOTHESIS / CORROBORATED / CONTESTED / UNKNOWN`.

A syntactically elegant sentence may not smuggle an inference into the grammatical position of fact.

ROOM 917 standing firewall:

`TRUST / ATTRACTION / KISS / CONFESSION / SILENCE / INFLECTION ≠ CORROBORATION`.

## D. Conscious robots / nonhuman persons

Grammar must not erase personhood or consent.

`was assigned / was authorized / was redirected / was allocated` requires a decision-owner audit whenever the affected agent is conscious.

More sensors do not imply omniscience. Preserve knowledge provenance and the entity's own refusal/choice.

`PRACTITIONER OBSERVATION → REPRODUCIBLE EFFECT → SCHOOL INTERPRETATION → COMPETING INTERPRETATION → INDEPENDENT TEST → ONTOLOGY CEILING`.
