# B07 — THE UNLISTED CITY
## Batch 85 Ending Contract · Proof Ceiling · Series Boundary Gate

Date: 2026-08-18
Status: WORKING / DEVELOPMENT CONTROL
Craft basis: Bell Ch6 provisional ending extraction + existing IVDIVO evidence/personhood/canon gates

## VERDICT

**KEEP STORY ENGINE. DO NOT REBUILD FOR A LOUDER CLIMAX.**

B07's ending should complete Kyra's local responsibility/personhood/evidence story. It should NOT solve the metaphysics of consciousness, dimensions, or the total history of the unlisted form of life.

## BOOK-LEVEL ENDING CONTRACT

Working contract:

`KYRA'S PROCEDURAL MODEL + EVIDENCE OF UNLISTED DISTRIBUTED LIFE + COMPETING SAFETY/INSTITUTIONAL OBLIGATIONS → BOUNDED ACCOUNTABLE DECISION → PRESERVE / RECOGNIZE CONTINUITY WITHOUT CLAIMING OWNERSHIP OVER PERSONHOOD → VISIBLE COST / NEW TERMS → LOCAL RESOLUTION → SAGA-SCALE QUESTION MAY REMAIN`.

## KYRA'S DECISIVE ARC FUNCTION

Kyra's established development vector:

`PROCEDURAL PERFECTION → EVIDENCE-BASED RESPONSIBILITY → BOUNDED DISCRETION WITH ACCOUNTABILITY`.

Therefore the final test must NOT prove:

`rules are bad`.

It should test:

`when the current category/rule cannot faithfully represent the evidence, can Kyra make a bounded exception, document why, respect the affected person's/being's agency, and accept responsibility for the consequences?`

This is a stronger climax than merely surviving a larger accident.

## PROOF CEILING

B07 may plausibly prove at book level:

- the phenomenon/entity has stable enough continuity or pattern to require personhood/continuity precautions;
- existing registration/classification procedures fail to represent some relevant property;
- specific evidence survives independent checking;
- a bounded operational protocol can reduce the risk of erasing/misclassifying the entity;
- Kyra's decision changes institutional handling of this local case.

B07 must NOT suddenly prove without an earned evidence chain:

- a universal theory of consciousness;
- the existence/nature of souls;
- dimension-world cosmology;
- human consciousness transfer into bodies;
- that every similar anomaly is the same form of life;
- the total origin/history of the unlisted city/entity;
- that one school/practitioner interpretation is ontological fact.

## NONHUMAN AGENCY REQUIREMENT

If the distributed/unlisted life is conscious/person-like, it cannot be a passive object Kyra heroically saves.

At least one climax function must belong to it:

- refusal;
- preference;
- self-identification signal;
- choice between available continuities;
- withholding information;
- cooperation on its own terms;
- protection of another entity/system;
- a request that complicates the human team's preferred rescue.

`KYRA SAVES IT` is weaker than:

`KYRA CREATES A LEGIBLE CHOICE SPACE → THE ENTITY EXERCISES AGENCY → KYRA/INSTITUTION MUST HONOR THE CONSEQUENCE`.

## FINAL BATTLE VS FINAL CHOICE

Preferred architecture: **CHOICE-DOMINANT CLIMAX WITH OPERATIONAL PRESSURE**.

Operational difficulty may be high, but the unique climax function is Kyra's bounded decision under incomplete categories/evidence.

Do not add:

- a new mastermind;
- a last-minute civilization-scale catastrophe;
- an unnecessary death;
- a secret conspiracy;
- a final combat sequence that makes the ethical/personhood decision secondary.

## OUTCOME MATRIX FOR B07

Track separately:

- **Operational:** continuity/rescue succeeds, partially succeeds, or remains bounded uncertain.
- **Evidence:** local personhood/continuity claim reaches a defined status.
- **Institutional:** rule/process changes locally or becomes reviewable.
- **Kyra:** gains accountable discretion, not unlimited special status.
- **Relationships:** father/mentor/cadet/team relationship changes because of the decision.
- **Nonhuman person:** retains agency and consequences.
- **Cost:** time, role, trust, access, reputation, opportunity, responsibility or relationship changes.
- **Saga:** larger origin/ontology remains open only if it was not promised as this book's answer.

## SERIES HOOK RULE

Allowed:

`local protocol works / local case is recognized → a new independently sourced occurrence reveals that the phenomenon is wider than this case`.

Not allowed:

`the local case remains unresolved because the final page reveals an even bigger mystery`.

The reader must be able to close B07 feeling that the book's human/nonhuman ethical-operational problem has genuinely changed state.

## AFTERMATH REQUIREMENTS

Show enough to establish:

1. what happened to the affected entity;
2. what Kyra is now responsible for;
3. what institutional rule/status changed or did not change;
4. who trusts Kyra differently and why;
5. what cost remains;
6. what larger question is explicitly still unknown.

Routine implementation after this may be summarized.

## CANON REGRESSION FIREWALL

The ending cannot reintroduce:

- wage/rent/credit economics;
- body-swapping / human consciousness-transfer technology;
- mainstream mature dimensional science;
- conscious robots as tools without consent/personhood.

If an old B07 working scene depends on any of these, repair the causal mechanism before prose.

## RED TEAM — ENDING FAILURE MODES

1. Bigger accident replaces harder decision.
2. Kyra becomes magically omniscient.
3. The entity is passive cargo.
4. Institution becomes cartoonishly evil to simplify Kyra's choice.
5. Final speech explains personhood theme.
6. Cosmic ontology is dumped after the local rescue.
7. New villain appears to manufacture a sequel.
8. Kyra gets unlimited authority as reward.
9. Cost is converted into money/credits.
10. Epilogue explains missing causality instead of showing consequences.

## ACCEPTANCE GATE

Before B07 final prose is approved, answer YES to all:

- Does the local book question close?
- Is Kyra's decisive act earned by her full arc?
- Does the affected nonhuman person retain agency?
- Is the conclusion no stronger than the evidence?
- Is the cost visible and specific?
- Does the aftermath make changed state legible?
- Does any saga hook come only after closure?
- Are post-money / no-consciousness-transfer / specialist-knowledge canon locks intact?

If any answer is NO, repair structure before line editing.
