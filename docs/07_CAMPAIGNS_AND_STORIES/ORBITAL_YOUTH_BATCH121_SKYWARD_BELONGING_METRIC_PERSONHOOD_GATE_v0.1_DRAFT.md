## VERDICT

Use Skyward as a mechanism laboratory for youth competence, belonging after qualification, metric gaming, peer/institution culture gaps, distributed team competence and nonhuman personhood.

Do **not** import its military scarcity economy, merit chits, food/resource deprivation, militarised adolescence, cosmology or action-prose cadence.

## ORBITAL YOUTH PRODUCTION GATES

### 1. Qualification is not belonging
After any cadet passes an exam, certification or selection, separately audit:
- physical/logistical access;
- social inclusion;
- mentor attention;
- meaningful work;
- right to speak/appeal;
- peer recognition;
- privacy/autonomy;
- actual relationship changes.

`QUALIFIED ≠ INCLUDED`.

### 2. Post-money stakes remain nonfungible
Translate source-world scarcity/status only into real IVDIVO constraints:
`TIME / TRUST / CONSENT / ROLE / QUALIFICATION / UNIQUE MISSION SLOT / PRIVACY / REPUTATION / RESPONSIBILITY / BELONGING / RELATIONSHIP`.

No salary/rent/credits/merit-chit reskin.

### 3. Role-specific competence matrix
For every major youth character define:
- one domain of real competence;
- one embodied/knowledge/relationship limitation;
- one situation where that pairing changes team options;
- one situation where another character is better.

Fail if the cast can be sorted on one universal competence ladder.

### 4. Metric gaming audit
For every formal score, ranking, qualification metric or algorithm:
`WHAT IS MEASURED? → WHAT IS THE REAL TASK? → CAN A RATIONAL AGENT WIN THE METRIC WHILE HARMING THE TASK?`

If yes, the story should expose revision, appeal, workaround, governance cost or changed trust.

### 5. Policy / culture divergence
Separately record:
- official rule;
- peer status signal;
- instructor reward signal;
- public narrative;
- actual behavior under pressure.

Do not equate a sensible written policy with a healthy lived institution.

### 6. Success → responsibility before setback
Before inventing a failure after competence, test:
`REAL SUCCESS → MORE TRUST / ACCESS / RESPONSIBILITY → HARDER NEXT CHOICE`.

This is the preferred youth escalation when causally available.

### 7. Dream ownership
A prestigious path is not automatically every prepared character's authentic goal.

`SHARED TRAINING / FAMILY HISTORY / EXPECTATION ≠ SHARED LIFE WANT`.

Leaving a path may be a mature agency choice rather than failure.

### 8. Stigma/public narrative firewall
`OLD PUBLIC STORY → AUDIENCE MODEL → ACCESS / REPUTATION EFFECT` is legitimate causality.

But:
`PUBLIC STORY ≠ CURRENT PERSON MOTIVE / CHARACTER TRUTH`.

### 9. Public disclosure does not upgrade evidence
A disclosed secret may change belonging, relationships and opportunity space.

`MORE PEOPLE KNOW X ≠ X IS BETTER PROVEN`.

### 10. Distributed climax
Before a major ensemble climax assign functions:
- evidence holder;
- interpreter;
- embodied/technical specialist;
- countermodel holder;
- consent/relationship owner;
- bounded decision owner.

Fail if one protagonist owns all functions without a necessary causal reason.

### 11. Conscious robot / nonhuman personhood
For every named conscious robot/nonhuman:
`OWN WANT → OWN MODEL/EVIDENCE → OWN RELATIONSHIPS → CONSENT/REFUSAL → OWN CHOICE → CONSEQUENCE`.

Assigned role, creator order, architecture or useful capability does not define identity.

### 12. Control provenance before responsibility
If a synthetic person acts under inherited commands, governor layers, coercive protocols or locked constraints:
`ACTION → CONTROL PROVENANCE → AVAILABLE CHOICE → OWN PREFERENCE → RESPONSIBILITY STATUS`.

Never infer motive directly from controlled action.

### 13. Nonhuman development is not humanization
Do not measure a robot's growth by becoming more human in voice, body, sexuality, family form or emotional display.

Growth can be increased self-authorship, revised commitments, new relationships, boundary negotiation, refusal or changed responsibility.

### 14. Local answer / global ontology ceiling
A book may establish a useful operational fact while leaving deeper origin/ontology unknown.

For consciousness-practice material preserve:
`OBSERVATION → REPRODUCIBLE EFFECT → SCHOOL INTERPRETATION → COMPETING INTERPRETATION → ONTOLOGY CEILING`.

### 15. Predictable policy attack surface
If another intelligent agent can model institutional response:
`VISIBLE OBJECTIVE → PREDICTABLE POLICY → ADVERSARY / COUNTERPART MODELS POLICY → NEW OPTION / DECOY / PRESSURE`.

Useful without requiring a villain.

### 16. Youth voice / ensemble differentiation
Differentiate by:
- what each notices;
- what each considers normal/embarrassing/desirable;
- humor mechanism;
- relationship-specific tactics;
- risk response;
- competence fingerprint.

Do not use one universal witty voice or imported 2020s Earth slang.

### 17. Prose cadence firewall
`SUSPENSE / ACTION ≠ FRAGMENT DENSITY`.

Use the IVDIVO router:
`BOOK ENGINE → CHAPTER/SCENE FUNCTION → ORDER/DURATION/FREQUENCY → POV/EVIDENCE → SHOW/TELL → DIALOGUE/SUBTEXT → PARAGRAPH/READER-STATE → SENTENCE RHYTHM → WORDING`.

### 18. LESSON ZERO Book One lock
Skyward creates no automatic permission to reopen the development-locked manuscript. Reopen only on a concrete current-version defect or external reader evidence.

## RED TEAM QUESTIONS BEFORE PROSE

1. What qualification/title does the youth have, and what belonging still remains unearned?
2. What does the institution officially measure, and what does the mission actually require?
3. Which informal behavior is rewarded despite official policy?
4. Which success creates a more difficult responsibility instead of a punishment?
5. Which team member supplies a capability nobody else supplies?
6. Who has the right to refuse in the key decision?
7. Which public belief is merely a social model rather than evidence?
8. Which local mystery question can be answered without claiming global ontology?
9. Does a conscious robot/nonhuman make an independent choice that changes human options?
10. Have Earth money/status mechanics re-entered under renamed terms?
