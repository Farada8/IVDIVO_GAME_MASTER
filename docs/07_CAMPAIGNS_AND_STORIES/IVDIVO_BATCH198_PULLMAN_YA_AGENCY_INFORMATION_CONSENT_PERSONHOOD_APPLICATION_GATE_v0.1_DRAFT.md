# IVDIVO — BATCH 198 APPLICATION GATE
## Pullman: YA Agency / Information / Consent / Personhood

**Date:** 2026-08-22  
**Status:** PRODUCTION GATE / DRAFT

## 1. Youth agency
A young character may act decisively from incomplete knowledge without being written as foolish.

Required chain:
`WHAT THEY KNOW → WHAT THEY INFER → WHAT THEY WANT → WHAT THEY DO → CONSEQUENCE → WHAT THEY LEARN`.

## 2. Adult / child knowledge split
For every youth-institution scene, map:
- child knowledge;
- adult knowledge;
- information intentionally withheld;
- information legitimately inaccessible;
- what each side thinks the other side knows.

Do not let narrator knowledge leak into child POV.

## 3. Care / coercion audit
For every mentor, parent, academy, clinic, program, lab, or administration:
`REAL BENEFIT → INFORMATION ASYMMETRY → SURVEILLANCE / CONTROL → RIGHT TO REFUSE → COST → APPEAL → LEGITIMACY`.

Real care does not erase coercion; coercion does not prove that every benefit is fake.

## 4. Euphemism gate
For terms such as `allocation`, `intervention`, `qualification`, `service`, `adjustment`, `stabilization`, or `treatment`, ask:
`WHAT PHYSICALLY / SOCIALLY HAPPENS TO WHOM?`

If the clean noun hides decision ownership or cost, surface the actor and consequence.

## 5. Consent
`FOLLOWING / COMPLIANCE / SILENCE / NONRESISTANCE ≠ INFORMED CONSENT`.

Especially for children, robots, dependent persons, trainees, and captive/controlled subjects.

## 6. Orbital Youth
High-value application:
- adults and teens should notice different things in the same institution;
- youth errors should be intelligible from their evidence;
- institutional benefits may be genuine but bounded;
- peer coordination should matter;
- post-money stakes remain time, trust, privacy, role, access, responsibility, mastery, belonging, consent, relationship, unique opportunity, safety;
- do not rebuild economic coercion through points/ranks/credentials without explicit world justification.

## 7. B07 — THE UNLISTED CITY
Do not use appearance, human emotional response, institutional label, or one classifier to decide personhood.

`TRACE → MULTIPLE MODELS → INDEPENDENT TEST → PERSONHOOD STATUS`.

After threshold:
`ENTITY MODEL/PREFERENCE → CONSENT/REFUSAL → ENTITY CHOICE → HUMAN OPTION SPACE CHANGES`.

`CREATED / DISCOVERED / RESCUED ≠ OWNED`.

## 8. ROOM 917
For each major clue:
`TRUE TRACE → SOURCE / LINEAGE → ELENA ACCESS → JULIAN ACCESS → READER ACCESS → MODEL A/B/C → DISCRIMINATING TEST → STATUS UPDATE`.

Standing firewall:
`TRUST / ATTRACTION / KISS / CONFESSION / SILENCE / INFLECTION ≠ CORROBORATION`.

## 9. Mission source check
Before a protagonist spends many chapters pursuing an objective:
- who actually stated the objective?
- is it documented or inferred?
- what evidence supports it?
- what would prove the objective is wrong?

This is mandatory for mystery, rescue, audit, research, and technical mission stories.

## 10. Good local action / bad downstream coupling
After a successful rescue, fix, disclosure, qualification, or policy:
`WHAT SYSTEM NOW RECEIVES THE CHARACTER / RESULT? → WHAT NEW RISK OR RESPONSIBILITY EXISTS?`

Success is allowed. Its consequences must be modeled.

## 11. Counter-authority firewall
If a corrupt/abusive authority claims X, the story may legitimately doubt X. It may not conclude `not-X` solely from the authority's corruption.

`DISREDITED SOURCE → ALTERNATIVE HYPOTHESIS → NEW TEST`.

## 12. LESSON ZERO Book One
No global rewrite authorized.

Open a local patch only if current manuscript evidence shows:
- child/adult POV knowledge leak;
- care/consent contradiction;
- institutional euphemism hiding a required material consequence;
- mission based on an unverified assumption;
- distributed evidence collapsed into protagonist monopoly;
- current reader/audio confusion tied to information order;
- choppy prose caused by unnecessary micro-dramatization.

Standing law:
`NO NEW EVIDENCE → NO NEW GLOBAL REWRITE`.

## 13. Prose / paragraph firewall
Pullman's English prose alternates long descriptive/reflexive passages with action. Do not imitate local cadence mechanically.

`YA / FANTASY / SUSPENSE ≠ SHORT-SENTENCE DENSITY`.
`BEAT / REVEAL / PROPHECY / TURN ≠ SENTENCE BREAK`.

Repair order remains:
`BOOK ENGINE → CHAPTER FUNCTION → SCENE ADMISSION → ORDER/DURATION/FREQUENCY → SCENE WEIGHT/MODE → POV/EVIDENCE → PARAGRAPH INTENT / READER-STATE → SENTENCE RHYTHM / CONTRAST → WORDING`.
