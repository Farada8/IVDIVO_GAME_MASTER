## Conscious robots
- no universal "robot irony";
- more sensors do not equal omniscience;
- sarcastic self-report is sourced testimony, not motive fact;
- silence/compliance is not consent.

## Consciousness schools
Narrative paradox, mystical irony or philosophical elegance may generate hypotheses. They do not raise school interpretation to world fact.

## Revision / reader test
When a beta reader misses an irony:
1. Was the literal surface coherent enough to be recognized?
2. Were the necessary contradictory/contextual cues present?
3. Was the required shared ground supplied fairly?
4. Did multiple appropriately positioned readers miss it?
5. Would clarification preserve the intended reader effect?
6. Is the passage actually meant to be unstable?

Do not "fix" ambiguity merely because one reader chose a different plausible model.
