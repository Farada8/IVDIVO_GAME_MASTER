# ORBITAL YOUTH — BATCH 122 NEVERMOOR APPLICATION GATE

Version: v0.1 DRAFT
Date: 2026-08-19
Authority: additive mechanism gate only; Founder/canon/current-book causality outrank source fiction.

## USE CASE

Use *Nevermoor* when an Orbital Youth story involves:
- stigma/public category;
- a desired school, guild, training center or cohort;
- qualification/trials;
- found family and prior family tension;
- mentor protection / withheld information;
- high-impact talent or nonhuman category;
- belonging that must be lived before it is formally named.

Do not import magic, patronage economics, consciousness travel or Wundersmith ontology.

## GATES

### 1. Stigma causal audit
For every public label:
`ORIGIN → EVIDENCE? → WHO REPEATS IT → WHAT IT CAUSES SOCIALLY → HOW THOSE CONSEQUENCES LOOK LIKE CONFIRMATION`.

Fail if repetition is treated as proof.

### 2. Surface want / real relational want
A youth may say they want admission, rank or prestige. Test whether the actual want is:
- belonging;
- autonomy;
- trust;
- chosen family;
- recognition;
- relationship;
- unique responsibility;
- mastery.

Do not write the deeper want as a therapist's diagnosis; prove it through choices.

### 3. Desired institution must contain real good
The institution should offer something the reader understands wanting.

Avoid `prestigious academy → secretly evil` as default YA machinery.

Preferred model:
`REAL GOOD + REAL COMMUNITY + IMPERFECT PROCEDURE + COMPETING INTERNAL VIEWS + APPEAL / REFORM / EXIT`.

### 4. Trial / metric audit
For each test:
`OFFICIAL TRAIT → ACTUAL TASK → INFORMAL ADVANTAGE → WHAT BEHAVIOR WINS → DOES THAT BEHAVIOR PREDICT REAL ROLE COMPETENCE?`

### 5. No hidden money through social scoring
Friendship, support and belonging are not currencies.

`RECIPROCITY ≠ DEBT LEDGER`.

Post-money society must not become a universal status-points economy.

### 6. Lived belonging before title
Before induction/badge/qualification resolves a belonging arc, show concrete ordinary-life evidence:
- someone waits for them;
- they know where things are;
- they have a role in shared routines;
- disagreement does not threaten expulsion;
- another person trusts them with something specific;
- they can refuse without losing personhood.

### 7. Person before category
Before revealing/using a high-impact label (rare talent, synthetic architecture, lineage, school status, nonhuman class):
`BEHAVIOR + RELATIONSHIPS + CHOICES → PERSON MODEL → CATEGORY`.

Never reverse this into category determinism.

### 8. Mentor protective-withholding audit
If an adult hides material information:
`WHAT IS HIDDEN? → WHY? → WHAT DECISION THE YOUTH CANNOT MAKE → WHO BENEFITS? → WHEN DISCLOSED? → WHAT TRUST COST?`

Good intent does not erase consent cost.

### 9. Decision-owner legitimacy
If adults design a safeguard around a youth or conscious nonhuman:
`WHO IS AFFECTED? → WHAT DO THEY KNOW? → CAN THEY REFUSE? → CAN TERMS BE REVISED? → WHO OWNS FINAL DECISION?`

### 10. Mixed-family rule
Prior family is not automatically wholly bad; found family is not automatically wholly safe.

Require relation-specific evidence rather than family-category moral sorting.

### 11. Source provenance in mystery
A pleasant guide, mentor, archive, official or school doctrine is not self-authenticating.

`SOURCE IDENTITY → ACCESS → INCENTIVE → CORROBORATION → EVIDENCE STATUS`.

### 12. Conscious robot/nonhuman worker
If a conscious robot performs institutional/service work:
- independent want;
- independent relationships;
- right to refuse;
- embodiment-specific capability/limits;
- life outside usefulness to protagonist.

Fail if replacement by a smart appliance leaves the story unchanged.

### 13. Ordinary life makes the extraordinary home
Orbital worldbuilding should accumulate through routines, spatial habits, jokes, routes, favors, disagreements, meals/meetings and privacy—not only spectacular technology reveals.

### 14. Ontology ceiling
Nevermoor's Gossamer/Wunder is fiction-only. For IVDIVO consciousness practice retain:
`OBSERVATION → REPRODUCIBLE EFFECT → SCHOOL INTERPRETATION → COMPETING INTERPRETATION → ONTOLOGY CEILING`.

### 15. Youth voice
Build youth distinction from attitude, attention, social inference, humor mechanism and relationship tactics. Do not import 2020s Earth slang as proof of youth.

### 16. Prose rhythm
`YA / WONDER / SUSPENSE ≠ SHORT-SENTENCE DENSITY`.

Use the IVDIVO prose router before sentence-level cadence repair.

## RED TEAM BEFORE STORY LOCK

1. Is the public stigma actually evidenced or merely repeated?
2. What does the protagonist want beneath the formal badge/rank?
3. Is the institution genuinely worth wanting?
4. Does the selection process reward the value it claims to reward?
5. Are friendships becoming hidden transactions?
6. Has belonging been lived before it is declared?
7. Is a category overwriting the person's behavioral evidence?
8. Is a mentor deciding 'for their own good' without informed participation?
9. Does a conscious nonhuman have an independent refusal path?
10. Has magical/speculative source ontology leaked into IVDIVO canon?
