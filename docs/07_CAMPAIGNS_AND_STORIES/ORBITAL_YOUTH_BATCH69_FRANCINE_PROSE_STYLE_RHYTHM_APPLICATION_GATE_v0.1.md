# ORBITAL YOUTH — BATCH 69 FRANCINE PROSE APPLICATION GATE

Date: 2026-08-17
Status: ADDITIVE STYLE / CHARACTER / READER-EFFECT CONTROL

# 1. PURPOSE
Use Francine Prose's close-reading discipline after causal architecture is stable. Do not retrofit Book Two to a literary-style template and do not reopen Book One without evidence.

# 2. PROSE-PASS ORDER
`ONE MAIN STORY → scene purpose → show/tell → POV/distance → dialogue mode → paragraph intent → sentence rhythm → exact word`.

A sentence/paragraph metric is diagnostic only.

# 3. CHARACTER VOICE TEST
For Ollie, Ethan, Aoife, Samir and Maya compare a full page, not isolated catchphrases:
- what each notices;
- what each omits;
- preferred sentence structures;
- certainty/uncertainty language;
- relation-specific changes;
- what physical/social details enter POV;
- how direct speech changes under pressure.

If names can be swapped without the page changing, voice differentiation is insufficient.

# 4. ORBITAL DETAIL TEST
A lived detail is preferable to explanatory worldbuilding. Examples of functions:
- make post-money normality visible;
- reveal a body's/environment's practical condition;
- alter route/access/timing;
- expose relationship/status;
- create memory or emotional resonance.

But do NOT force every detail into future payoff. Some details legitimately establish ordinary reality.

# 5. DIALOGUE + LISTENING
Design listeners, not just speakers. In a five-person group scene, not everyone should process the same sentence equally. Track who is distracted, defensive, interested, already informed, avoiding the topic or listening for a different problem.

This can replace artificial quip distribution and repetitive `everyone reacts once` ensemble prose.

# 6. GESTURE
Do not insert cups, shrugs, glances and transit-window looks merely to break dialogue. Gesture must be character-specific or causally/socially meaningful.

For conscious robots, embodiment-specific actions can function as gesture, but must not become a generic mechanical tic.

# 7. POST-MONEY / FUTURE VOICE
Do not signal futurity by stiff vocabulary or exposition. The stronger signal is what characters treat as ordinary. They may casually assume material abundance while arguing intensely over trust, mission access, time, privacy, consent, responsibility or relationships.

# 8. CONSCIOUSNESS-SCHOOL EPISTEMICS
A practitioner may sound certain and still be wrong. Preserve distinction among personal experience, reproducible effect, lineage interpretation, disputed theory and established world fact.

# 9. ENDING / READER CONTRACT
A Book-Two ending need not solve the entire civilization or future lives of the ensemble. It must resolve the local causal problem and leave a changed relational/social state. Continuing lives are not a failure of closure.

# 10. BOOK ONE
No automatic rewrite. If a passage is challenged for 'choppiness', diagnose its function locally. Preserve deliberately isolated lines when their contrast/emphasis earns them.
