## Rewrite Diagnosis · Process Routing · Research · Structural Transitions

Status: **ACTIVE PRODUCTION GATE / source synthesis provisional**

## 1. Rewrite permission

A new craft source does not authorize a rewrite.

Before touching a locked or accepted manuscript, record:

`CURRENT DEFECT → EVIDENCE → DEFECT LEVEL → DEPENDENCIES → PROPOSED MINIMAL FIX → RETEST`.

If no concrete defect exists:

**NO NEW EVIDENCE → NO NEW GLOBAL REWRITE.**

### LESSON ZERO Book One

Development lock remains intact. Seger strengthens rather than weakens the lock because her rewrite model begins with diagnosis and whole-script dependency awareness.

## 2. Planning-document authority

Treatments, beat sheets, scene cards, generated alternatives and Writers’ Room recommendations are not canon merely because they are polished or newer.

`WORKING PLAN → CANON CHECK → CHARACTER/PERSONHOOD → CAUSALITY → DRAFT EVIDENCE → ACCEPT / REPLAN / BANK / DISCARD`.

## 3. One Main Story test

Use treatment-level compression to test causal relation:

`EVENT A → what changes? → why does B become possible/necessary? → EVENT B`.

If an attractive event can be removed without altering later action, evidence, relationships, authority or decisions, test it for merge/bank/delete rather than line-polishing it.

## 4. Research ownership

Every important technical/world fact entering prose should answer:

- who needs or notices it now?
- how do they know it?
- is it observation, report, interpretation or corroborated fact?
- what action/relationship/constraint changes because of it?

In mystery and consciousness-school material:

`EXPERT / SCHOOL CLAIM ≠ VERIFIED ONTOLOGY`.

## 5. Medium firewall

Seger’s screenplay mechanics do not automatically transfer into novel prose or audio drama.

Reject automatic import of:

- page-per-minute assumptions
- fixed act percentages
- screen-only visual economy as prose syntax policy
- “image/action first” as a ban on interiority

Retain functional questions:

- is the audience oriented?
- is the central causal problem active?
- does a transition change available options/direction?
- does the ending close the local promise?

## 6. Structural transition test

A real transition changes one or more:

`GOAL | OPTIONS | RELATIONSHIP | EVIDENCE | AUTHORITY/ACCESS | RESPONSIBILITY | STRATEGY/MODEL | FUTURE OPTION SPACE`.

A new location, technology, episode number or arbitrary page marker is not a transition by itself.

## 7. Writers’ Room routing

Do not ask every specialist to optimize every draft sentence.

`GENERATE LIVE MATERIAL → OBSERVE DEFECT → ROUTE TO RELEVANT DESKS → REPAIR → RETEST`.

This protects voice, discovery and natural paragraph/sentence rhythm.

## 8. Anti-choppy prose gate

When prose feels fragmented, diagnose upward before changing sentence length:

`SCENE NECESSITY → NARRATIVE MODE → POV/EVIDENCE → EXPOSITION/DIALOGUE → PARAGRAPH INTENT → READER-STATE CONTINUITY → SENTENCE RHYTHM → WORDING`.

Screenplay concision is not a novel KPI.
