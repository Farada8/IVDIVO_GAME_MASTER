# IVDIVO — BATCH 54 LAPLANTE APPLICATION GATE
## Revision · Meaning · Epistemic Discipline

Date: 2026-08-17
Status: WORKING / ADDITIVE PRODUCTION CONTROL

This document applies the strict LaPlante synthesis to active and future IVDIVO manuscripts. It does not replace current canon, accepted story architecture or development locks.

## 1. NON-NEGOTIABLE ROUTING ORDER
Before line editing:

`ONE MAIN STORY / CAUSAL ENGINE → SCENE NECESSITY → SHOW/TELL MODE → POV ACCESS/DISTANCE → DIALOGUE MODE → PARAGRAPH INTENT → SENTENCE RHYTHM → WORDING`.

If a defect exists higher in the stack, do not repair it cosmetically lower in the stack.

## 2. LESSON ZERO BOOK ONE
Current rule: **DO NOT REOPEN DEVELOPMENT LOCK merely because LaPlante has now been fully synthesized.**

A new rewrite is permitted only if current-version evidence demonstrates a concrete defect such as:
- focal character knows information without a legal path;
- a scene is routine/logistics that should be compressed;
- dialogue repeats what narration/action already established;
- the scene demonstrates a change and the narrator then explains the same change;
- line rhythm is genuinely harming reader effect after structural integrity is confirmed;
- beta/reader evidence repeatedly identifies the same problem.

New theory alone is not evidence.

## 3. FUTURE ORBITAL YOUTH
### World reveal
Do not explain the post-money civilization in thesis paragraphs.

Prefer:
`ordinary material abundance → specific nonfungible want → resistance → choice → consequence`.

Examples of legitimate nonfungible stakes:
- a particular person's trust or time;
- membership in a specific team;
- a unique mission/role;
- reputation after a public decision;
- consent or intimacy;
- mastery requiring lived practice;
- an irreplaceable relationship;
- a specific place/time/environment that cannot be duplicated merely by manufacturing another object.

Do not rebuild money as credits, allowance points or purchasing prestige.

### Conscious robots
A conscious robot is not a worldbuilding device or helper archetype. Test:
- independent want;
- relationship network beyond protagonist;
- right to refuse;
- own epistemic limits;
- relationship-specific speech/tactics;
- consequence if replaced by a nonsentient terminal.

If replacement changes nothing, personhood is not yet written.

## 4. SPECIALIST CONSCIOUSNESS / DIMENSIONAL SCHOOLS
Mandatory evidence stack:

`OBSERVATION → REPRODUCIBLE PRACTICE EFFECT → SCHOOL INTERPRETATION → COMPETING INTERPRETATION → ONTOLOGICAL FACT`.

Do not promote a practitioner statement to civilization-wide science.

High external technology does not remove the bottleneck created by a very small population of practitioners willing and able to perform years or decades of yoga/meditation/inner training. Robots and analytics can measure correlates; they cannot manufacture the missing history of lived practice.

## 5. MYSTERY / EVIDENCE
Use explicit source-monitoring:

`WHAT WAS OBSERVED? → WHO OBSERVED IT? → DIRECT TRACE OR INTERPRETATION? → ALTERNATIVE MODEL? → WHAT WOULD DISCRIMINATE?`

Forbidden transitions:
- `Samir thinks X → X becomes narration fact`;
- `school tradition says Y → civilization knows Y`;
- `system predicts behavior → system knows motive`;
- `a real anomaly exists → preferred ontology is therefore proven`.

## 6. MEANING WITHOUT EXPLANATION
For repeated image/object/event:

`FIRST APPEARANCE → LATER EXPERIENCE CHANGES ASSOCIATION → RETURN UNDER NEW PRESSURE → MEANING SHIFTS`.

Do not append an authorial sentence explaining the symbol after the reader has already experienced it.

## 7. REVISION STAGE GATE
### Generative draft
Protect discovery. Do not run the full QA stack after every sentence.

### Deep development
Repair engine, POV, causal sequence, character function, world assumptions.

### Constructive revision
Repair scene function, information timing, repetitions, continuity, transitions, relationship progression.

### Line/copy stage
Repair paragraphing, cadence, diction, grammar and local continuity.

**Prohibition:** do not use line polish to conceal a development defect; do not use new development theory to destabilize a locked book without evidence.

## 8. “CHOPPY PROSE” TEST
Before merging short lines, ask:
1. Is the scene itself necessary?
2. Could the process be summary instead of dramatized beat-by-beat?
3. Are we jumping POV distance?
4. Does this exact dialogue need to be direct?
5. Does each paragraph have a distinct intent?
6. Is the short sentence contrastive, or merely habitual?

Preserve a short line when it carries impact, contrast, revelation, focus shift or deliberate breath. Remove accidental fragmentation.

## 9. FEEDBACK / RED TEAM
Red Team output must separate:
- **observed reader effect / defect**;
- **diagnosis hypothesis**;
- **repair option**.

A repair option is not canon merely because an agent or source proposed it.

For early material, record the strongest **hot spots** before listing weaknesses so revision does not destroy the very mechanism making the material alive.

## 10. NO HEALING RETROFIT
Especially in YA:
- no mandatory epiphany;
- no forced “lesson learned” speech;
- no trauma inserted to justify depth;
- no neat healing solely to satisfy an arc model.

Growth may be partial, behavioral, relational or methodological. Refusal to change is allowed if its cost is causal and visible.

## 11. APPLICATION VERDICT
LaPlante adds a **revision and epistemic control layer**, not a new plot template.

Use her to protect living material from formula, to route scene/narration correctly, to distinguish POV belief from world fact, and to prevent line editing from becoming a substitute for structural repair.
