# IVDIVO — BATCH 170 APPLICATION GATE
## Chatman: Story / Discourse / POV / Evidence

**Status:** DRAFT / PRODUCTION CONTROL  
**Source status:** Chatman strict-complete  
**Purpose:** prevent structural, evidence and prose defects from being repaired at the wrong layer.

## A. Universal defect router

Before rewriting any prose, answer in order:

1. **STORY:** Is the underlying event/state actually wrong or unnecessary?
2. **CAUSALITY:** Does it change downstream options, evidence, relationships, responsibility or decision?
3. **DISCOURSE ORDER:** Is the material revealed at the correct point?
4. **DURATION:** Should it be full scene, summary, ellipsis, stretch or pause?
5. **FREQUENCY:** Is repetition deliberate and meaning-changing, or redundant?
6. **POV/ACCESS:** Who may perceive/know/infer this?
7. **VOICE:** Who is speaking/narrating, and in whose vocabulary/attitude?
8. **EVIDENCE STATUS:** Fact, source report, inference, hypothesis, contested, unknown?
9. **PARAGRAPH:** Is the reader-state continuous?
10. **SENTENCE/WORD:** Only now perform line edit.

## B. ONE MAIN STORY gate

Multiple POVs and strands are allowed.

Each recurring strand must satisfy:
`LOCAL EVENT → UNIQUE STATE CHANGE → EFFECT ON DOMINANT ENGINE / MAJOR LATER CHOICE → PAYOFF`.

A shared briefing, recap or co-presence does not equal convergence.

## C. LESSON ZERO Book One

**Standing result: KEEP DEVELOPMENT LOCK.**

Chatman does not create a new reason to rebuild the book. Reopen only if current text shows a specific:
- story/discourse mismatch;
- reveal-order defect;
- duration/mode defect;
- POV knowledge leak;
- narrator/evidence presupposition error;
- repeated reader confusion traceable to one of these layers.

`NO NEW EVIDENCE → NO NEW GLOBAL REWRITE`.

## D. Orbital Youth

### D1. World reveal
`WORLD RULE → POV-SPECIFIC ATTENTION → AFFORDANCE / FRICTION → CHOICE → CONSEQUENCE`.

Do not explain the whole post-money civilization. Let normality appear through what characters barely notice and what genuinely remains scarce: time, trust, consent, privacy, responsibility, role, relationship, unique opportunity.

### D2. Multiple POV
Track:
`PERCEIVER | KNOWLEDGE ACCESS | INTEREST OWNER | VOICE | EXIT STATE`.

Saga-level ensemble equality does not imply equal POV time inside one book.

### D3. Conscious robots
`MORE SENSORS ≠ OMNISCIENCE`.
Their perceived space must be filtered by embodiment, priorities, task, relationship and knowledge limits. Human labels such as “cold”, “obedient”, “erratic”, “helpful” remain interpretations unless independently supported.

## E. ROOM 917

Maintain a five-layer clue model:

`WORLD FACT → PHYSICAL/AUDIO TRACE + SOURCE → ELENA/JULIAN ACCESS → INTERPRETATION → DISCOURSE PLACEMENT → READER MODEL`.

Hard firewalls:
- `RELATIONSHIP STATE ≠ EVIDENCE STATUS`.
- `TRUST / ATTRACTION / KISS / CONFESSION / SILENCE ≠ CORROBORATION`.
- Presupposition in dialogue/narration may not silently upgrade a clue hypothesis.

A clue can be delayed for suspense, but its objective provenance may not be mutated by presentation.

## F. B07 — THE UNLISTED CITY

Personhood sequence:

`PATTERN → INDEPENDENT PERSONHOOD EVIDENCE → HUMAN CATEGORY FAILS → ENTITY'S OWN MODEL/PREFERENCE BECOMES DECISION-RELEVANT → CONSENT/REFUSAL → ENTITY CHOICE CHANGES HUMAN OPTION SPACE`.

Do not let human narration or institutional terminology classify away agency.

## G. Consciousness-practice schools

Keep these layers separate:

`PRACTITIONER EXPERIENCE → REPRODUCIBLE EFFECT → SCHOOL INTERPRETATION → COMPETING INTERPRETATION → INDEPENDENT TEST → ONTOLOGY CEILING`.

A narrator's generalization, vivid imagery, inherited doctrine or intense subjective certainty does not raise ontology status.

## H. Anti-choppiness / prose gate

Do not repair suspense, interiority or action by increasing fragment density.

Use:

`CHAPTER FUNCTION → SCENE ADMISSION → ORDER/DURATION/FREQUENCY → MODE → POV/EVIDENCE → PARAGRAPH INTENT → READER-STATE CONTINUITY → SENTENCE CONTRAST → WORDING`.

Routine recoverable transitions are prime candidates for ellipsis/summary. A sequence such as `door → look → microthought → reply → gesture → walk` should only be dramatized beat-by-beat if those exact beats change story state.

## I. Regression gate

After any accepted repair, recheck:
- canon consistency;
- clue provenance;
- POV access;
- downstream scene dependencies;
- relationship continuity;
- chapter function;
- protected payoff;
- paragraph/rhythm regression.

No framework is allowed to create a new defect merely to satisfy its own taxonomy.
