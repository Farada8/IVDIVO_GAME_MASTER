# B07 — THE UNLISTED CITY
## Batch 84 — Middle Pressure · Adhesive · Escalation Audit

Status: ADDITIVE / WORKING / DO NOT REBUILD

## VERDICT

**KEEP STORY ENGINE. REPAIR MIDDLE PRESSURE GEOMETRY; DO NOT RETROFIT BELL'S DEATH-STAKES FORMULA.**

The existing engine remains strong: Kaira's procedural competence / Human Contact ambition intersects an evacuation and discovery of an unlisted distributed lifeform whose continuity must be protected without assuming the right to define or own it.

Bell Chapter 5 is useful here primarily for middle engagement, adhesive, action/reaction and diagnosis of sluggish or overweight middles.

## B07 MAIN ADHESIVE

Kaira should remain in the engine because of an earned combination of:

- accepted cadet responsibility;
- direct involvement in the evidence chain;
- operational consequences of actions she participated in;
- relationship obligations to named people/agents;
- bounded institutional authority/standing;
- unresolved question of preserving another being's continuity without appropriating consent.

Do NOT make her stay because of invented poverty, expiring rent, salary dependence, debt, arbitrary imprisonment, secret blackmail or a new family hostage plot.

## MIDDLE PRESSURE ROTATION

Avoid repeating:

`new anomaly → briefing → explanation → procedural disagreement → another anomaly`.

Preferred rotation:

1. **Operational pressure** — immediate evacuation/containment decision with incomplete model.
2. **Evidence pressure** — trace supports more than one causal interpretation.
3. **Relationship pressure** — Kaira's procedural method conflicts with someone she respects, not a straw opponent.
4. **Consent/personhood pressure** — what counts as preservation if the distributed lifeform cannot be treated as property or passive specimen?
5. **Institutional pressure** — competent officials need auditable reasons; caution has real benefits.
6. **Technical pressure** — a locally successful solution changes another variable.
7. **Public/social pressure** — interpretation spreads faster than proof, changing what actions are politically/socially possible.
8. **Responsibility pressure** — Kaira gets more standing precisely because an earlier decision worked.

The sequence need not follow this exact order; the key is qualitative variation.

## STAKES LADDER — POST-MONEY SAFE

Do not use Bell's `professional death` literally as job/income collapse.

Legitimate B07 stakes:

`Human Contact placement / trust / qualification / access to a real mission / evidence integrity / relationship / consent / distributed-life continuity / public legitimacy / safety / time window / responsibility for a decision`.

Escalation is strongest when a new stake changes Kaira's available strategy rather than merely making the disaster larger.

## SUCCESS IS ALLOWED

Protect at least one middle beat where Kaira's procedural competence **works**.

Desired causal pattern:

`correct procedure → real success → increased trust/authority → more complex responsibility → old procedure alone becomes insufficient`.

This is stronger than `Kaira is wrong repeatedly until she learns the theme`.

## COUNTERMODEL REQUIREMENT

The most credible institutional/technical opponent should be correct about something important:

- false positives can cause harm;
- undocumented claims cannot justify irreversible action;
- emergency systems need repeatable thresholds;
- preserving continuity without knowing ontology can create new risk.

Kaira's development comes from learning bounded discretion with accountability, not from discovering that procedure is stupid.

## ROBOT / NONHUMAN PERSONHOOD

Any conscious robot participating in the middle needs:

`OWN WANT → OWN EVIDENCE → OWN RELATIONSHIPS → CONSENT / REFUSAL → CONSEQUENCE`.

Do not use a conscious robot merely as tool, translator, ethical lecturer or Bell-style added complication.

For the unlisted lifeform, avoid premature anthropomorphic certainty. Preserve:

`OBSERVED PATTERN → EVIDENCE OF CONTINUITY/RESPONSE → COMPETING MODELS → OPERATIONAL PRESERVATION CHOICE → ONTOLOGY STILL BOUNDED`.

## OVERWEIGHT-MIDDLE TRIAGE

Before adding new material:

- merge officials who perform identical procedural opposition;
- compress repeated briefings;
- combine repeated anomaly-analysis scenes if evidence status does not change;
- preserve ordinary-life/relationship scenes only when they alter a later choice, standing, trust or interpretation;
- bank attractive technology/worldbuilding that does not affect this book's causal engine.

## FAILURE MODES TO RED-TEAM

- `meeting → model → meeting` repetition;
- every anomaly being larger than the last;
- new character introduced solely to create resistance;
- family/romance subplot disconnected from the lifeform/evidence engine;
- death/injury added because the middle feels quiet;
- monetary stakes returning under `cost/credit/allocation` vocabulary;
- conscious robot used as equipment;
- Kaira becoming the only person who sees, understands and decides everything;
- school's/metaphysical interpretation being used to explain the lifeform without proof.

## SCENE ACCEPTANCE QUESTION

For each middle scene:

`WHAT CHANGED THAT MAKES THE NEXT DECISION DIFFERENT?`

Valid answers can be action, evidence, relationship, consent, authority, risk model, responsibility or available option. If the answer is only `we learned more lore`, compress or rebuild.

## DECISION

Bell does **not** justify rebuilding B07 around life/death escalation. Use Chapter 5 to make the middle more causally varied, more person-specific and less meeting-heavy while preserving the accepted engine.
