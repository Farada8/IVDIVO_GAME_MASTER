# ORBITAL YOUTH — BATCH 81 BELL IDEA TOURNAMENT APPLICATION GATE

Date: 2026-08-18
Status: WORKING / ADDITIVE — DOES NOT REBUILD ACCEPTED ARCHITECTURE WITHOUT A CONCRETE DEFECT

## Purpose

Apply the strictly read opening of Bell Chapter 3 to future Orbital Youth / IVDIVO ideation without turning brainstorming into canon or fracturing ONE MAIN STORY.

## Gate 1 — Generate beyond the first plausible answer

For any unresolved story problem, produce materially different variants before locking.

Examples of dimensions that must vary:
- who decides;
- what they want;
- what evidence they have;
- which relationship is exposed;
- which physical/social rule of the orbital city matters;
- who can refuse;
- what successful action costs later;
- what state changes afterward.

Do not count `same event in another ring` or `same dilemma with a different gadget` as a new engine.

## Gate 2 — Post-money idea pressure

When seeking youth stakes, start with genuinely scarce/non-fungible conditions:

- trust of a specific person;
- consent;
- time;
- membership/role with real responsibility;
- unique mission or training window;
- mastery;
- reputation after an accountable choice;
- privacy;
- friendship/love;
- family expectation;
- opportunity that cannot simply be manufactured by robots.

Do not generate salary/rent/credits as shorthand for stakes.

## Gate 3 — Technology must alter causality

A new technology survives premise selection only if it changes:

**AVAILABLE ACTIONS / KNOWLEDGE / RELATIONSHIP / RESPONSIBILITY / COST / RISK / ACCESS.**

If it is merely impressive, bank it as world material rather than forcing it into the novel.

## Gate 4 — Conscious robot independence

For every idea containing a conscious robot, answer before selection:

1. What does the robot want here?
2. What can it refuse?
3. What relationship exists outside the human protagonist?
4. What does the robot risk or protect?
5. Would the scene materially change if replaced by an advanced terminal?

If answer 5 is `no`, personhood is not yet written.

## Gate 5 — Consciousness-school epistemics

A compelling premise may involve a practitioner or school, but preserve:

**OBSERVATION → REPRODUCIBLE EFFECT → SCHOOL INTERPRETATION → COMPETING INTERPRETATION → POSSIBLE ONTOLOGICAL CLAIM.**

No generated twist is allowed to jump directly from `a practitioner reports X` to `the universe is proven to work as X`.

## Gate 6 — Author passion without cast homogenization

Founder/project interests may generate story energy. They do not dictate every character's conclusion.

For each major ensemble member, require:

**SAME PRESSURE → DIFFERENT PERSONAL HISTORY / VALUE PRIORITY → DIFFERENT FIRST TACTIC → DIFFERENT SECOND TACTIC → DIFFERENT COST.**

## Gate 7 — ONE MAIN STORY selection

After generation, ask of every attractive secondary idea:

`Does this change, test, complicate, expose, repair or resolve the dominant causal engine?`

If not, bank it for another book.

## Gate 8 — Keep success as an option

Do not bias idea generation toward failure-only variants.

A strong branch can be:

**competent action → real success → increased trust/authority/dependence → harder responsibility later.**

This is often stronger for Orbital Youth than `mistake → disaster → larger disaster`.

## Gate 9 — Ordinary-life generator

Before adding catastrophe, generate at least several variants from ordinary orbital life:

- travel/coordination under different environmental rules;
- friendship under asymmetric schedules or embodiments;
- privacy and public recording;
- family expectations;
- qualification and responsibility;
- conflict between personal autonomy and coordinated infrastructure;
- relations with conscious robots;
- limited specialist-practice communities.

Ordinary does not mean filler if the state of a relationship, obligation, knowledge or future choice changes.

## Gate 10 — Selection output

The winning variant must be expressible as:

**CURRENT STATE → PRESSURE → CHARACTER-SPECIFIC CHOICE → WORLD/OTHER-PERSON RESPONSE → CHANGED STATE → NEXT NECESSITY.**

If it cannot, it is still a seed, not a scene/plot solution.
