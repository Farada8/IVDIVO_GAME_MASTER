## Orbital Youth
### Threshold audit
A cadet crosses a real threshold only when at least one changes:
- access;
- responsibility;
- rules;
- evidence/knowledge;
- relationship/consent;
- future option space;
- real cost of exit.

A new district, vehicle, training room, technology, or uniform is not a threshold by itself.

### Initiation ethics
No rite/trial gets moral legitimacy from being traditional, difficult, prestigious, or archetypal.
Check:
`WHAT DO THEY KNOW? → CAN THEY REFUSE? → CAN THEY APPEAL? → WHO MONITORS WELFARE? → WHO OWNS THE DECISION? → WHO BEARS THE COST?`

### Success remains legal
Do not force every trial into failure.
`COMPETENT ACTION → REAL SUCCESS → MORE TRUST / ACCESS / RESPONSIBILITY → HARDER NEXT CHOICE` remains protected.
