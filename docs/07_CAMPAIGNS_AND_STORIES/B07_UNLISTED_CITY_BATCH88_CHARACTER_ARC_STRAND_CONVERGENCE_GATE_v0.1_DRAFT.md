# B07 — THE UNLISTED CITY
## Batch 88 Character Arc + Strand Convergence Gate

Status: WORKING / DRAFT
Date: 2026-08-18
Verdict: **KEEP STORY ENGINE / REPAIR, DO NOT REBUILD**

## 1. Kyra arc
Do not force a generic YA transformation or 'rules are bad' lesson.

Preferred trajectory:
`PROCEDURAL PRECISION → SUCCESS UNDER KNOWN CATEGORIES → GREATER TRUST/RESPONSIBILITY → EVIDENCE EXCEEDS CATEGORY → BOUNDED DISCRETION → DOCUMENTED ACCOUNTABILITY → CHANGED RELATION TO PROCEDURE`.

The endpoint is not rebellion. It is more mature competence: knowing when a category is inadequate, what evidence justifies an exception, who bears the cost, and how the exception remains reviewable.

### Verification requirement
The arc is not complete when Kyra says she has changed. It is complete only when she later:
- handles evidence differently;
- asks a different question;
- grants or withholds authority differently;
- protects another person's consent differently;
- accepts a specific cost/responsibility she previously would have displaced into procedure.

## 2. Distributed/unlisted personhood strand
This strand may not exist merely as Kyra's moral lesson or rescue-object.

Required independent fields:
`OWN WANT → OWN MODEL/EVIDENCE → OWN CONSTRAINT → CONSENT/REFUSAL → RELATIONSHIPS → IRREPLACEABLE ACTION`.

If removing Kyra would cause the nonhuman person to have no understandable life, goal, relation, or choice, the strand has failed personhood.

## 3. Strand convergence
Do not count a shared briefing as convergence.

For each major strand, record:
`LOCAL ACTION → WHAT IT CHANGES IN ANOTHER STRAND → NEW OPTION/CONSTRAINT → LATER PAYOFF`.

Preferred convergence types:
- Kyra's procedural decision changes what evidence can legally/operationally be collected;
- nonhuman refusal changes the technical team's feasible rescue plan;
- technical finding changes institutional classification;
- institutional response changes relationship/consent conditions;
- evidence from one strand removes Kyra's easiest procedural option.

## 4. POV gate
A new POV is permitted only when it buys unique evidence, lived embodiment, countermodel, relationship pressure, or decision ownership.

Do not add a nonhuman POV merely to provide exotic sensory prose. If used, it must preserve epistemic limits and independent agency.

## 5. Nonlinear evidence gate
If logs, recordings, archived fragments, or delayed testimony are reordered:
`WORLD FACT → SOURCE → TIME CUE → POV/INTERPRETATION → READER MODEL → LATER EVIDENCE → MODEL REVISION`.

Do not use chronology confusion as a substitute for mystery.

## 6. Canon firewalls
- no salary/rent/credits disguised as orbital stakes;
- no operational human consciousness transfer/body swapping;
- no automatic elevation of specialist-school interpretation into metaphysical fact;
- conscious robots/nonhumans retain personhood and consent;
- local evidence cannot exceed the book's proof ceiling.

## 7. Scene-level acceptance
A scene involving a secondary strand must change at least one of:
- action state;
- evidence/knowledge state;
- relationship state;
- authority/access state;
- consent state;
- future option space.

Otherwise compress, merge, or remove before line editing.

## 8. Ending implication
Kyra's final change should be demonstrated by bounded action under uncertainty, not by an explanatory speech. The nonhuman person must retain at least one decision that Kyra cannot make for them.
