## 1. Dominant story factor decision

Card's MICE diagnostic is used here only to prevent accidental genre drift.

### REJECTED DEFAULT
`MILIEU PRIMARY:` teenagers tour increasingly spectacular orbital technologies until the reader has seen enough future civilization.

### REJECTED DEFAULT
`IDEA PRIMARY:` teenagers discuss how post-money economics, robotic consciousness or dimensional schools work until the thesis is explained.

### PREFERRED ORBITAL YOUTH BOOK SHAPE
`EVENT PRIMARY + CHARACTER CO-PRIMARY + MILIEU STRONG SECONDARY + IDEA SUBORDINATE.`

Meaning:
1. one concrete external problem begins;
2. one protagonist (or tightly controlled co-lead) wants a specific outcome;
3. orbital civilization makes the problem possible and changes available actions;
4. relationships/identity/autonomy change because of the attempt;
5. the local problem is actually resolved;
6. only then may the saga leave a larger hook.

### Story Gate OY-C35-01 — ENDING CONDITION
Complete before outline approval:
`This novel is over when __________________.`

PASS examples:
- the gravity league safely completes/rebuilds its season after the protagonists expose and solve the real body-classification problem;
- the missing cadet is found and the specific social/route conflict that caused the disappearance is resolved;
- a dangerous qualification/expedition selection is completed under changed rules after the hero's choice exposes a flaw.

FAIL examples:
- the reader now understands orbital society;
- the teenagers have seen enough technologies;
- we have introduced the robot civilization;
- we have explained the schools of consciousness.

## 2. Character hierarchy gate

The recurring youth ensemble must not be treated as five equal protagonists by default.

For each Book Two candidate classify every recurring person:
- `PRIMARY MAJOR` — owns the book's central desire/decision;
- `SECONDARY MAJOR` — has independent desire that causally collides with A-story;
- `SUPPORTING MAJOR` — recurrent and consequential but does not require complete independent novel arc;
- `MINOR` — one/few strong functions, can be memorable;
- `WALK-ON / MILIEU PERSON` — social reality only.

### Story Gate OY-C35-02 — NO ENSEMBLE FOG
If Ethan, Maya, Ollie, Aoife, Samir, new cadets, instructors and robotic persons all receive:
- equal introduction length;
- equal jokes;
- equal private wounds;
- equal POV;
- equal mystery function;
- equal climax contribution;
then the book has not chosen its hierarchy.

A later book may rotate protagonist ownership. Equality across the saga does not require equality inside every installment.

## 3. Post-money motive test

Removing money does **not** remove motive. It raises the standard of motive design.

Candidate continuing motives, derived from established Book-One behavior and therefore to be revalidated rather than blindly copied:

### ETHAN
Possible burn: trusted responsibility / legitimacy / proving competence can coexist with respecting limits.
Risk: repeats Book One if every story is again `Ethan wants authority`.
Book-Two use only if the new activity changes the desire (e.g. responsibility for a team, expedition, sport, research or relationship rather than institutional access itself).

### MAYA
Possible burn: autonomy, bodily competence, sport/exploration, resistance to paternalistic control.
Risk: becoming only the anti-authority quip machine.
Needs a positive desire that she chooses, not merely a rule she rejects.

### OLLIE
Possible burn: mastery, reliability, building something that works under real conditions, pride in craft.
Risk: gadget function without social consequence.
Need: a machine/system choice that costs trust, status, team membership or safety—not money.

### AOIFE
Possible burn: authorship, context, truth, being trusted to record/publish without reducing people to content.
Risk: recorder = personality.
Need: competing loyalties around what to show, withhold, contextualize or credit.

### SAMIR
Possible burn: epistemic autonomy, privacy, understanding patterns without becoming other people's instrument.
Risk: walking mystery detector.
Need: a desire unrelated to merely identifying anomalies; curiosity must collide with consent, belonging or self-definition.

### Story Gate OY-C35-03 — MOTIVE WITHOUT DISGUISED CURRENCY
Reject any replacement such as:
- points;
- credits;
- prestige score;
- access tokens treated exactly like money;
unless the story proves the system's distinct social logic and the Founder approves it.

Use real scarcity where fabrication cannot solve the problem:
- one seat on an expedition;
- one role on a team;
- a finite training window;
- trust of a specific person;
- unique live event;
- reputation after a public choice;
- consent;
- time;
- responsibility;
- relationship.

## 4. Conscious robot character gate

A conscious robot is not automatically `MILIEU` just because robotic production is infrastructure.

If a robotic person appears as a named recurring actor:
1. assign hierarchy level;
2. define own motive;
3. define what they want from humans/other robots independent of serving the hero;
4. define relationship-specific voice/register;
5. define what they can refuse;
6. define what the human protagonist misunderstands about them.

### Story Gate OY-C35-04 — PERSON OR TOOL
If replacing the named robot with a smart appliance leaves the scene unchanged, either:
- it is not actually a robotic person in that scene; or
- the personhood has not been dramatized.

## 5. Consciousness-school gate

The rare schools/practitioners dealing with yoga, meditation, subtle perception or dimensional questions must not become lore vending machines.

Their strongest Card-compatible use is **character + motive**, not world exposition.

For any practitioner character define:
- why they spent years training when most people did not;
- what ordinary social life they sacrificed or chose;
- what school/teacher shaped their language;
- what they can actually reproduce;
- what they only believe/interpret;
- what competing school would disagree;
- what they want in the present scene.

### Story Gate OY-C35-05 — PRACTITIONER BASE REALITY
A high-tech city cannot solve a thin practitioner base by simply fabricating equipment. If a consciousness claim matters to the plot, identify the trained human evidence chain and its limitations.

## 6. POV / voice application to Orbital Youth

### Relationship-register matrix required for any PRIMARY MAJOR
Write diagnostic samples (not publication prose) for the same character speaking to:
- best friend;
- parent;
- instructor;
- romantic interest;
- robot person;
- younger cadet;
- formal panel/review.

If only slang changes, FAIL.
Look for changes in:
- sentence length;
- directness;
- jokes;
- what is omitted;
- technical vocabulary;
- willingness to admit uncertainty;
- politeness;
- challenge/deferral behavior.

### POV temperature
Do not use constant deep POV solely because YA is supposed to feel intimate.
Use deeper penetration for:
- shame;
- attraction;
- identity threat;
- bodily risk;
- irreversible choice.
Allow cooler distance for:
- spatially complex action;
- group choreography;
- letting behavior reveal a relationship without commentary.

## 7. Card test against the current compatible candidates

### GRAVITY LEAGUE
Card-dominance fit: **STRONG**.
- EVENT: injury / unsafe competitive format / season risk.
- CHARACTER: identity, recognition, body, team belonging.
- MILIEU: variable gravity and mixed-body sport are causal.
- IDEA: fairness/safety classification emerges through action.

Risk: sports mechanism becomes detached from the larger orbital social world.
Repair: make team selection, romance/friendship, robotic/nonhuman competitors and bodily difference matter to the same conflict.

### SPIN CHANGE
Card-dominance fit: **STRONG**.
- EVENT: missing youth + closing/reconfiguring route window.
- CHARACTER: control, trust, peer loyalty, privacy.
- MILIEU: rotating/reconfiguring city physically causes the deadline.

Risk: generic missing-person thriller with orbital wallpaper.
Repair: disappearance must arise from a specifically orbital social condition and the final solution must require legitimate local knowledge/relationships, not only geometry.

### PURE TECHNOLOGY-TRAINING TOUR
Card-dominance fit: **WEAK / REJECT** unless rebuilt around one complete contest, failure, mission, relationship or investigation.
