## 0. CORE CORRECTION

The major youth lines do **not** occur in different centuries.

Working synchronized epoch: **2035**.

Three youth environments exist at the same time:

1. **EARTH YOUTH** — ordinary terrestrial life: school, work, gym, streets, public transport, family, sport, first relationships, old infrastructure, uneven access to post-Contact technology.
2. **ORBITAL YOUTH** — first rapidly expanding orbital cities/habitats, built largely from scratch after Contact and therefore able to deploy the newest permitted technologies much faster.
3. **CONFEDERATION HUMAN TRAINING YOUTH** — a human-compatible educational and professional interface to Confederation knowledge and technology; advanced, but not the full technological/ontological reality of the Confederation.

The ancient Smith line is historical depth / archive / flashback / separate historical-book material. Smith also exists in the 2035 present through his current carrier/body and his daughter.

The three contemporary youth groups can meet, become friends, fall in love, compete, resent one another, visit one another, and gradually connect the separate worlds of the saga.

---

# 1. WHY THE TECHNOLOGY LEVELS CAN DIFFER IN THE SAME YEAR

The key law is:

> **Available technology is not the same thing as the technological level of the entire civilization.**

After Contact, technology diffuses unevenly because capital, industrial capacity, infrastructure, legal permissions, talent, security priorities and strategic interests migrate unevenly.

Therefore 2035 can contain simultaneously:

- legacy Earth infrastructure from the 20th–21st centuries;
- new orbital infrastructure designed entirely after Contact;
- controlled Confederation training environments using devices/processes that humans cannot yet independently manufacture or deploy broadly.

This is not a timeline contradiction. It is a technology-distribution and infrastructure asymmetry.

---

# 2. CAPITAL MIGRATION TO ORBIT

Founder direction: a major share of wealthy/private capital moves into Orbit because the most profitable new opportunities are there.

Reasons inside the story world can include:

- Contact-enabled construction;
- new materials and energy systems;
- new financial jurisdictions;
- AI and synthetic-person industries;
- biotechnology / neural-tech / embodiment R&D;
- space manufacturing;
- strategic infrastructure;
- security from Earth-based psychic/mental influence risks;
- prestige, scarcity and first-mover advantage;
- tax / legal / regulatory competition;
- ownership of new habitats, logistics and services.

The decisive social consequence:

> **the future is built first where new capital is concentrated.**

Orbit therefore receives disproportionate investment in new schools, clinics, housing, transport, AI infrastructure, labs, synthetic-person systems, advanced security and youth institutions.

---

# 3. EARTH DOES NOT STOP DEVELOPING — IT DEVELOPS UNEVENLY

Earth in 2035 is not technologically frozen.

New systems appear, but:

- slowly;
- in premium districts first;
- in private clinics before public systems;
- in elite schools before ordinary schools;
- in corporate campuses before old neighborhoods;
- in military/security/Contact institutions before consumer life;
- through expensive licensed imports before local production;
- with large geographic and class differences.

Therefore a teenager can pass an old bus stop on the way to a school with a new AI tutor, while a private clinic across town already uses Contact-derived diagnostics.

This coexistence is desirable, not an error.

---

# 4. DELIBERATE EARTH-ORBIT ASYMMETRY

Founder direction: a significant part of the orbital elite is not interested in Earth catching up too quickly.

Important nuance: do **not** reduce this to "all rich people are villains." The system should emerge from aligned interests of multiple actors.

Possible mechanisms:

- restrictive licensing of Contact-derived technology;
- patents / black-box service contracts;
- orbital-only infrastructure dependencies;
- export controls justified by safety;
- insurance restrictions;
- requirements Earth municipalities cannot afford;
- talent recruitment from Earth into Orbit;
- strategic control of key manufacturing;
- lobbying against rapid open transfer;
- standards written around orbital infrastructure;
- intentional delay of terrestrial deployment when Earth modernization would reduce orbital rents or political leverage.

Some actors sincerely believe Earth must be slowed for safety. Others benefit economically from the gap. Others want political separation. Others simply follow incentives.

This produces a structural conflict rather than a cartoon conspiracy.

---

# 5. WHY SOME ORBITAL ELITES MAY WANT EARTH TO LAG

Once major fortunes are tied to Orbit, Earth may cease to be the primary place where those elites expect to create future wealth.

Earth can become, from their perspective:

- an old market;
- a labor source;
- a consumer base;
- a cultural/historical homeland;
- a political risk zone;
- a resource/logistics node;
- a place whose slower modernization preserves price differences and dependency.

A more advanced Earth could threaten:

- orbital monopoly rents;
- migration premiums;
- exclusive schools/clinics;
- security-service markets;
- high-value technology licensing;
- legal/jurisdictional advantages;
- social prestige of orbital residency.

Therefore some powerful actors may actively resist rapid terrestrial diffusion while publicly using language of safety, compatibility, regulation, transition or infrastructure readiness.

Again: this is an in-world political-economic conflict, not a claim about real-world elites.

---

# 6. YOUTH EXPERIENCE OF THE ASYMMETRY

The macroeconomics must be visible through ordinary adolescent life.

### Earth teen

- works after school because family income is tight;
- sees orbital influencers using technology unavailable locally;
- applies for an orbital scholarship;
- cannot access a therapy/treatment available to wealthy families;
- hears that a parent’s workplace is moving R&D off-Earth;
- is told the new neural safety system is "not yet certified for Earth public schools";
- resents friends who are leaving.

### Orbit teen

- lives inside new infrastructure;
- assumes some technology is normal until an Earth friend visits;
- feels guilty about privilege;
- may still lack freedom because habitat security is intense;
- knows family wealth depends on keeping a certain technology exclusive;
- discovers parents privately lobby against Earth deployment.

### Confederation-training teen

- uses equipment neither Earth nor Orbit fully controls;
- may be prohibited from sharing technical details;
- is accused by friends of "having everything" while being under strict access and surveillance;
- learns that the human training interface is itself simplified;
- sees that both Earth and Orbit misunderstand the real scale of Confederation capability.

---

# 7. CENTRAL SOCIAL QUESTION

This asymmetry gives the 2035 saga a recurring question:

> **Who gets the future first, and who decides when everyone else is allowed to have it?**

This should generate many independent YA plots:

- scholarship competition;
- parents choosing migration;
- class resentment;
- friendship broken by unequal access;
- a life-saving technology withheld by licensing;
- a teen leaking a restricted design;
- orbital students visiting Earth and seeing infrastructure decay;
- Earth students discovering that "safety delay" partly protects profit;
- a parent losing a job because the company moved upward;
- a family becoming wealthy because of orbital contracts while old friends remain Earthbound;
- a romance split by residency status;
- a teen refusing to move because their sibling cannot qualify.

The technology must intensify familiar youth pain, not replace it.

# 8. THREE-ZONE TECHNOLOGY LADDER — 2035

## EARTH

Broadly available:
- advanced personal AI;
- improved mixed-reality interfaces;
- selective neural/cognitive tools;
- early Contact-derived medical/material applications;
- physical AI/robots in some sectors;
- conventional transport and infrastructure still dominant.

Uneven / premium / institutional:
- high-end neural security;
- advanced regenerative medicine;
- new synthetic-body interfaces;
- rare Contact materials;
- advanced autonomous labs;
- some post-Contact energy systems.

## ORBIT

Common in new habitats:
- newer autonomous infrastructure;
- high-density AI coordination;
- programmable/adaptive materials;
- advanced robotics;
- integrated cognitive-security architecture;
- highly automated transport/logistics;
- advanced medicine;
- synthetic-person integration;
- more aggressive experimental education/work systems.

Still restricted:
- dangerous embodiment classes;
- strategic Confederation propulsion/navigation;
- deep subject-transfer technologies;
- multidimensional systems incompatible with Solar conditions.

## CONFEDERATION HUMAN TRAINING CENTER

Accessible under supervision:
- higher-order simulation/world-model systems;
- controlled embodiment prototypes;
- alien-compatible interfaces;
- restricted matter/field manipulation;
- diagnostic systems beyond human industry;
- training tools that depend on Confederation infrastructure.

Not accessible as ordinary human property:
- full Confederation interstellar ship architecture;
- unrestricted strategic systems;
- technologies humans cannot manufacture or safely operate;
- the full multidimensional civilizational layer.

# 9. STORY RULE — SAME AGE, DIFFERENT FUTURES

The emotional strength comes from contemporaries living at different apparent technological ages.

A 17-year-old on Earth can wait in the rain for a bus.

A 17-year-old in Orbit can cross a habitat in an autonomous transit field.

A 17-year-old in the Training Center can spend the day learning on a device Earth will not reproduce for decades.

At night all three can be in the same call arguing about:

- who likes whom;
- who lied;
- who is leaving;
- who got chosen;
- whose parents are impossible;
- who has money;
- who feels left behind.

This is a core IVDIVO youth-series aesthetic:

> **The scenery changes. Adolescence remains recognizable.**

---

# 10. LONG-RUN CONSEQUENCE

The 2035 asymmetry is not static.

Technology gradually flows:

`CONFEDERATION TRAINING / CONTACT INTERFACE → ORBITAL DEPLOYMENT → PREMIUM EARTH → BROADER EARTH`.

But the flow is contested by:

- safety;
- law;
- manufacturing capability;
- monopoly;
- politics;
- class power;
- sabotage;
- genuine incompatibility;
- youth activism and leaks;
- public-sector attempts to catch up.

Later books can show technology becoming ordinary that earlier books treated as elite or impossible.

This provides visible civilizational history without requiring major time jumps.

---

# 11. PRODUCTION LOCK FOR BOOK 2 AND THE MULTILINE SAGA

When plotting any 2035 youth book, the studio must record:

1. Which zone is the primary arena: Earth / Orbit / Confederation Human Training.
2. Which technology is normal there.
3. Whether the same technology exists in the other two zones.
4. If not, why not: cost / infrastructure / law / capability / strategic restriction / deliberate market delay.
5. Which ordinary youth problem the asymmetry creates.
6. Who benefits from the gap.
7. Who pays for the gap.
8. Whether the book changes the diffusion pathway.

No technology may appear everywhere merely because it exists somewhere.

No zone may be treated as a different historical century.

They are contemporaneous parts of one civilization in rapid, unequal transition.
