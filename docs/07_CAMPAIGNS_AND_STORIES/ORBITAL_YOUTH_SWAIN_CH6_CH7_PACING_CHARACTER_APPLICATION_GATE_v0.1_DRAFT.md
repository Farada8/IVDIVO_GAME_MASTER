## PURPOSE
Apply the strict-read mechanisms from Swain Chapters 6–7 without importing his 1965 moral, gender, economic or psychological assumptions.

## A. MIDDLE RHYTHM
Do not build Orbital Youth as a straight sequence of louder technical failures.

Preferred pressure rotation:
`operational → relationship → evidence → ordinary-life valley → authority/responsibility → consent/personhood → technical → public/social → intimate/personal → decisive local problem`.

A valley is not filler if it changes trust, model, commitment, reader bond, relationship, evidence interpretation or next strategy.

## B. ORDINARY POST-MONEY LIFE
Use lower-pressure scenes to prove the future through lived behavior rather than exposition.

Allowed ordinary material includes meals, transit, training, recreation, home arrangements, maker work, friendships, human-robot everyday interaction and specialist practice — but only when it pays character/world/relationship/contrast or causal work.

Do not smuggle back:
- salary;
- rent;
- credits as disguised money;
- consumer-status hierarchy;
- AI/robot servant default.

Scarcity may legitimately exist in non-fungible things: time, trust, consent, a particular person, unique mission slots, reputation, mastery, compatible space, singular experiences and responsibility.

## C. SUCCESS PERMISSION
Competent young characters may succeed.

Strong escalation pattern:
`competent action → real success → more trust/access/responsibility → harder next choice`.

Do not force a setback after every success merely to keep tension high.

## D. DELAY VS COMPLICATION
Before dramatizing a transport delay, bureaucratic pause, repair wait or scheduling issue:
- if it only consumes time, compress unless lived delay itself matters;
- if it changes options, evidence, relationships, authority, safety or future cost, it may be a true complication.

## E. POV SWITCHING
No POV switch for ensemble fairness.

A switch must buy unique:
- evidence;
- inaccessible action;
- lived consequence;
- relationship pressure;
- countermodel;
- decision;
- interpretation.

## F. CHARACTER HIERARCHY
Saga equality does not mean equal book-level attention.

Assign per-book hierarchy:
PRIMARY MAJOR / SECONDARY MAJOR / SUPPORTING MAJOR / MINOR / WALK-ON.

This controls POV count, backstory, secrets, humor, climax ownership and page time.

## G. RELATION-SPECIFIC CHARACTERIZATION
For every major youth character, verify different tactics with:
- close friend;
- family;
- instructor;
- rival;
- intimate/romantic interest;
- public audience;
- conscious robot;
- institution.

If all contexts produce the same smart/sarcastic voice, revise character strategy rather than slang.

## H. CONSCIOUS ROBOTS
No named conscious robot exists merely as mentor, assistant, victim, exposition source or moral lesson.

Required minimum:
`OWN WANT + OWN RELATIONSHIPS + OWN MODEL/EVIDENCE + CONSENT/REFUSAL + OWN CHOICE + CONSEQUENCE`.

The same robot may communicate differently with different persons without becoming less coherent.

## I. CONSCIOUSNESS SCHOOLS
Keep the epistemic ladder:
`practice observation → reproducible effect → school interpretation → competing interpretation → possible ontology`.

Do not let emotional certainty, institutional prestige or specialized terminology jump directly to world fact.

## J. ENDING CONTRACT
A book ending must first resolve its local promise:
`book question → bounded decisive action → consequence → cost → aftermath → changed state`.

Only after that may a saga vector remain open.

Do not use final ontology dump to answer questions the evidence architecture did not prove.

## K. ANTI-FRAGMENTATION / PROSE
For technical or high-pressure scenes:
`INTENSITY != SHORT-SENTENCE DENSITY`.

Repair order:
`BOOK ENGINE → CHAPTER FUNCTION → SCENE NECESSITY → ORDER/DURATION/FREQUENCY → SHOW/TELL → POV/EVIDENCE → DIALOGUE MODE → PARAGRAPH INTENT → READER-STATE CONTINUITY → SENTENCE RHYTHM → WORDING`.

Repeated logistical micro-actions default to compression, not sentence-by-sentence polishing.

## VERDICT
Use Swain for causal involvement, pressure/valley rhythm, state change and behavior-under-pressure. Reject formulaic disasters, moral reward machines, dated gender psychology and function-over-personhood.
