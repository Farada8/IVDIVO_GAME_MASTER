## Purpose

Apply le Carré's evidence-provenance and distributed-investigation mechanisms without importing Cold-War bureaucracy, secrecy culture or translation cadence into IVDIVO.

## ROOM 917

### Required evidence ledger
For every major clue store:

`CLUE / CLAIM | ORIGINAL TRACE | CURRENT REPORTER | ACCESS BASIS | SOURCE LINEAGE | INDEPENDENT? | ALTERNATIVE EXPLANATION | CURRENT STATUS | NEXT TEST`.

### Elena ↔ Julian firewall
Relationship changes may alter:
- what Julian shares or withholds;
- what Elena is willing to test;
- access to rooms, records or people;
- emotional risk and next investigative tactic.

Relationship changes may **not** independently raise the proof status of a clue.

`ATTRACTION / TRUST / KISS / CONFESSION / SILENCE ≠ CORROBORATION`.

### Source-laundering audit
If hotel management, newspaper archives, old staff testimony and Julian all repeat the same historical claim, determine whether they actually derive from one original document/person. Four repetitions may still be one source.

### Proof ceiling
The season may prove the operational/historical chain the clue architecture actually supports. It must not use the finale to convert relationship intuition into evidence or local hotel evidence into unsupported global paranormal ontology.

## B07 — THE UNLISTED CITY

### Classification firewall
`UNLISTED / ANOMALY / ORGANISM / SERVICE PATTERN` are operational labels, not proof of moral status or absence of personhood.

### Personhood threshold
Require multiple partially independent traces, for example:
- persistence of preference across contexts;
- self-model or stable distinction between self/other;
- relationship-specific responses;
- consent/refusal;
- action that changes human option space;
- independent observations not all derived from one sensor/model.

Once the threshold is crossed, the human team no longer owns the final preservation choice.

`HUMAN EVIDENCE → PERSONHOOD THRESHOLD → ENTITY'S OWN MODEL/PREFERENCE → CONSENT/REFUSAL → HUMAN OPTIONS CHANGE → BOUNDED ACCOUNTABLE DECISION`.

### Source lineage
If all apparent evidence of personhood is produced by one classifier or one shared sensor model, this is **not** independent corroboration.

## ORBITAL YOUTH / GOVERNANCE

### Institutional competence without infallibility
A policy can be useful, competent and evidence-based while becoming self-reinforcing through selective access.

Audit:

`WHO CONTROLS INPUTS? → WHO DEFINES VALID EVIDENCE? → WHO CAN APPEAL? → WHO HAS INDEPENDENT AUDIT ACCESS? → WHAT DATA WOULD FORCE MODEL REVISION?`

### Predictable-policy risk
A stable safety or allocation rule can become an exploit if other agents can accurately model institutional response. Test policies against strategic adaptation, not only normal compliance.

### Post-money firewall
Do not translate le Carré's status/resource networks into salary/rent/credits. Use trust, bounded authority, access, responsibility, reputation, consent, time and unique opportunity only where genuinely non-fungible.

## CONSCIOUS ROBOTS / NONHUMAN AGENTS

Human observers' repeated description of a robot as `helpful`, `unsafe`, `cold`, `obedient` or `erratic` does not independently prove motive.

Use:

`BEHAVIOUR → CONTROL PROVENANCE → ROBOT'S OWN MODEL/EVIDENCE → CONSENT/REFUSAL → RELATIONSHIP-SPECIFIC CHOICE → CONSEQUENCE`.

## CONSCIOUSNESS-PRACTICE SCHOOLS

### Lineage independence
Three schools are not three independent confirmations if all derive their ontology from one ancestral doctrine.

Store:

`OBSERVATION → REPRODUCIBLE EFFECT → SCHOOL INTERPRETATION → LINEAGE → COMPETING INTERPRETATION → INDEPENDENT TEST → ONTOLOGY CEILING`.

High technological sophistication does not increase the evidentiary status of a traditional interpretation by prestige alone.
