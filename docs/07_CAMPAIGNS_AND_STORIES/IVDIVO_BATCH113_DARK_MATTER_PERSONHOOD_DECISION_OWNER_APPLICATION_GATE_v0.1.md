## 3. CONSCIOUS ROBOTS
Never prove personhood with one humanlike behavior.

Minimum personhood evidence architecture:
`OWN WANT → PERSISTENT RELATIONSHIPS → OWN MODEL/EVIDENCE → PREFERENCE/REFUSAL → CONSENT → CHOICE THAT CHANGES OTHER AGENTS' OPTIONS → CONSEQUENCE`.

Human interpretation of a robot's cue remains interpretation.

## 4. B07 — THE UNLISTED CITY
If the distributed unlisted life is sufficiently evidenced as a conscious person, then the climax must not be:
`Kyra correctly decides how to preserve it`.

Required structure:
`HUMANS ESTABLISH OPTIONS/CONSTRAINTS → ENTITY EXPRESSES/ENACTS PREFERENCE → ENTITY CAN REFUSE → ENTITY'S CHOICE CHANGES THE CONTINUITY PLAN → KYRA ACCEPTS DOCUMENTED RESPONSIBILITY FOR THE PROCEDURE/EXCEPTION`.

Kyra's arc remains:
`PROCEDURAL PRECISION → EVIDENCE EXCEEDS CATEGORY → BOUNDED DISCRETION → DOCUMENTED ACCOUNTABILITY`.

Personhood does not turn the entity into cargo for that arc.

## 5. ROOM 917
Elena may trust Julian more or less because of relational evidence. That can change:
- what she asks;
- whether she discloses a clue;
- whether they separate or cooperate;
- what risk she accepts.

It cannot change:
- what the old plan proves;
- what the switchboard trace proves;
- whether a recording is authentic;
- the provenance/status of a clue.

`RELATIONSHIP STATE ≠ EVIDENCE STATUS`.

## 7. GOVERNANCE / PROCEDURE
Before any vote/allocation/lottery/protocol:
1. Who is affected?
2. Who is claiming a right?
3. Who owns the decision?
4. What consent exists now?
5. Can the affected agent refuse?
6. What appeal/alternative exists?
7. Does the procedure optimize among claimants while excluding the subject?

If yes, the procedure is structurally illegitimate even if mathematically fair.

## 8. PROSE / RHYTHM
Do not import Crouch's clipped thriller style wholesale.

Use:
`EFFECT → DURATION → PARAGRAPH INTENT → SENTENCE RHYTHM`.

Fragments are retained only when they earn contrast, shock, hesitation, end-weight, perceptual fragmentation or acceleration.

## 9. FINAL PRODUCTION TEST
For identity/personhood stories ask:

`WHAT DOES THE PROTAGONIST THINK THE PROBLEM IS?`
`WHO IS ACTUALLY AFFECTED?`
`WHAT EVIDENCE ESTABLISHES PERSONHOOD/IDENTITY?`
`WHO HAS BEEN LEFT OUT OF THE DECISION?`
`WHAT CHOICE CAN ONLY THAT AGENT MAKE?`

If the final answer still belongs entirely to the protagonist, the personhood architecture is incomplete.
