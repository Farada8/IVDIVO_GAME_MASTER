## ORBITAL YOUTH
For any powerful system, training program, infrastructure repair or institutional change:
`DIRECT BENEFIT → SECOND-ORDER EFFECT → WHO GAINS ACCESS → WHO BEARS NEW RESPONSIBILITY → WHAT KNOWLEDGE SPREADS → WHAT NEW OPTION/DEPENDENCY APPEARS`.

Do not manufacture monetary scarcity. Use time, trust, role, safety, consent, unique opportunity, privacy, responsibility and relationships.

## B07 — THE UNLISTED CITY
If humans alter conditions to preserve/move/disclose the distributed life:
1. identify direct survival/continuity effect;
2. model downstream effects on its relationships, habitat and autonomy;
3. track who learns enough to reproduce/interfere with the method;
4. preserve the entity's current consent/refusal;
5. do not assume deleting one archive/device deletes the knowledge.

## CONSCIOUS ROBOTS
Memory continuity can be identity evidence, but:
`RESTORED MEMORY ≠ PRESENT CONSENT`.

If old memories/relationship records are restored, the current robot/person retains the right to reinterpret, refuse or renegotiate the relationship.

## CONSCIOUSNESS-PRACTICE SCHOOLS
Vivid memory, shared altered-state memory and internally consistent recollection do not prove ontology.

Preserve:
`OBSERVATION → REPRODUCIBLE EFFECT → SCHOOL INTERPRETATION → COMPETING INTERPRETATION → ONTOLOGY CEILING`.

A small number of long-trained practitioners remains a real knowledge bottleneck even in a highly technological civilization.

## GOVERNANCE / HIGH-IMPACT TECH
Before deployment:
- Who controls the capability?
- Can subjects refuse?
- Is consent informed and revocable?
- What happens when know-how proliferates?
- Does first-mover advantage pressure others into premature adoption?
- Is destruction of hardware actually containment?
- What independent rediscovery paths exist?

## ROOM 917 / MYSTERY
Recovered memory/testimony remains source material with provenance. Multiple aligned memories increase evidential interest but do not automatically establish supernatural causality.

## PROSE / STRUCTURE

## MASTER TEST
For every "successful" intervention:
`WHAT ELSE DID SUCCESS CHANGE?`

For every containment plan:
`WHAT SURVIVES THE DESTRUCTION OF THE TOOL?`
