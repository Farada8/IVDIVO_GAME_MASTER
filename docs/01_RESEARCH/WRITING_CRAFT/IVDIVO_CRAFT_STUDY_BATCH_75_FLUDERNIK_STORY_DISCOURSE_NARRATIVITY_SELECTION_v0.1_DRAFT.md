# 1. CENTRAL PRODUCTION VALUE
Fludernik gives IVDIVO a clean diagnostic separation:

`WHAT HAPPENED / EXISTS IN STORYWORLD` ≠ `HOW THE BOOK PRESENTS IT` ≠ `WHAT THE READER CURRENTLY INFERS`.

This is especially useful for mystery, POV, flashbacks, ordinary-life pacing and adaptation.

# 2. PROVISIONAL GATES
## B75-G01 — STORY / DISCOURSE DIAGNOSIS
Before changing canon facts because a chapter feels weak:
`STORY PROBLEM? → DISCOURSE ORDER/DURATION/POV PROBLEM? → READER-INFERENCE PROBLEM?`.
Repair the lowest correct layer.

## B75-G02 — CANON FACT VS PRESENTATION
Reordering revelation, changing focalizer or compressing time may change reader experience without changing world fact. Track both explicitly.

## B75-G03 — SELECTION IS PERSPECTIVE
Every omitted/included world detail creates a viewpoint on the world. Absence from prose does not mean absence from canon.

## B75-G04 — SOURCE-CONSTRAINED INFERENCE
For archives/history/mystery:
`SOURCE → FIXED POINTS → GAPS → ALLOWED HYPOTHESES → CONTRADICTING EVIDENCE`.
Do not build an exciting theory first and force evidence to fit it.

## B75-G05 — MEDIA TRANSFORMATION
For prose/audio/video adaptation:
`STORY FUNCTION → MEDIUM-SPECIFIC DISCOURSE TOOL`.
Do not preserve a visual or screenplay device merely because it existed in the source version.

## B75-G06 — QUIET NARRATIVITY
A scene does not need physical conflict to qualify as narrative progress if lived experience changes:
`RELATION / MODEL / COMMITMENT / IDENTITY / EVIDENCE / FUTURE CHOICE`.

## B75-G07 — QUIET-NARRATIVE ABUSE FIREWALL
If deleting the quiet scene changes none of the above, it is still filler.

## B75-G08 — TIME/SPACE ANCHOR
At every POV transition:
`WHO → WHERE → WHEN → WHAT THEY KNOW`.
Flashback/analepsis/summary must preserve reader orientation unless disorientation is deliberate and paid off.

## B75-G09 — THEORY LABEL IS NOT REPAIR
`TERM → OBSERVED EFFECT → WHY IT FAILS/WORKS → REPAIR`.
Do not mistake narratological vocabulary for editorial evidence.

## B75-G10 — PERSONHOOD FIREWALL
Fludernik's human/anthropomorphic framing is a narrative-theory model, not IVDIVO ontology. Conscious robots have subjecthood without needing human imitation.

# 3. CROSS-SOURCE POSITION — EARLY
- **With Chatman/Ribó:** reinforces story/discourse separation and duration/order/frequency analysis.
- **With Zunshine/Herman:** adds a discourse layer between evidence and reader inference.
- **With Wolf:** selection in prose does not limit world-database existence.
- **With Truby/McKee:** provides a counterweight to event-chain absolutism; causality can be relational/experiential, but filler remains rejected.
- **With Rosenfeld/LaPlante:** supports summary/scene routing and non-formulaic narrative movement.

# 4. IVDIVO APPLICATION
## Mystery
Track five different layers:
`WORLD FACT → TRACE/SOURCE → POV INTERPRETATION → DISCOURSE PLACEMENT → READER MODEL`.
A reveal can alter the last two without altering the first.

## Orbital Youth
Ordinary-life chapters may carry real progression through first responsibility, belonging, intimacy, embarrassment, trust, competence and social orientation rather than fabricated danger.

## Robots
Do not code robot experience as narratively meaningful only when “human-like”. Personhood is canon, not analogy.

## Audio adaptation
Change discourse tools to suit sound: narrator access, dialogue, silence, temporal signals and sonic transitions can realize the same causal/story function differently from prose.
