## Structure / causal movement

1. Murderbot seeks evidence about the Ganaka Pit massacre rather than accepting either guilt or reassurance.
2. ART becomes an asymmetrically more powerful but independent nonhuman peer, first coercive and invasive, later indispensable through shared analysis and negotiated cooperation.
3. Murderbot accepts a chosen security role for humans rather than a governor-module command.
4. Investigation proceeds through traces, competing causal explanations, controlled access and discriminating evidence.
5. Embodiment is modified for a mission, but Murderbot explicitly rejects the idea that successful adaptation means becoming more human.
6. Controlled/artificial agents encountered later are judged partly through provenance of control rather than behavior alone.
7. Resolution changes Murderbot's evidence about itself and its relationship to autonomy/connection without requiring conversion into a human social template.

## Core mechanisms

### M1 — Nonhuman peer trust

`ASYMMETRIC CAPABILITIES → BOUNDARY TEST → SHARED INTERPRETIVE TASK → MUTUAL DEPENDENCE → NEGOTIATED TRUST`

Trust between nonhuman persons need not imitate romance, family or human friendship scripts. It can grow through argument, competence, privacy violations that are contested rather than normalized, mutual protection, and shared epistemic work.

### M2 — Distributed complementary competence

ART has ship-scale compute/network/sensor power; Murderbot has embodiment, field access, security experience and local human-reading competence. Neither becomes a cosmetic assistant to the other.

Production gate:
`WHO HOLDS DATA? → WHO CAN INTERPRET IT? → WHO CAN ACT PHYSICALLY? → WHO OWNS THE DECISION?`

### M3 — Causal reframe by a peer

ART converts Murderbot's self-accusatory question into testable alternatives: did the massacre occur as remembered, was Murderbot externally controlled, who benefited, and what traces distinguish these hypotheses?

IVDIVO rule:
`SELF-STORY ≠ VERIFIED CAUSE`.

### M4 — Embodiment change is not identity change

Murderbot alters appearance to pass socially but explicitly rejects “becoming more human” as the meaning of the modification.

IVDIVO rule:
`BODY MODIFICATION → NEW AFFORDANCES / RISKS / SOCIAL READABILITY`, not `BODY MODIFICATION → MORAL/ONTOLOGICAL UPGRADE`.

### M5 — Controlled action is not motive

Behavior produced under a governor/control layer cannot be read directly as voluntary motive or moral intent.

`OBSERVED ACTION → CONTROL PROVENANCE → AVAILABLE CHOICE → MOTIVE HYPOTHESES → RESPONSIBILITY STATUS`.

### M6 — Chosen role after coercive role

Murderbot voluntarily accepts bounded security work. Autonomy is demonstrated not by permanent isolation but by the right to choose responsibility.

`AUTONOMY ≠ ISOLATION`.

### M7 — Mercy toward a controlled agent

When another constructed agent lacks meaningful control over its actions, Murderbot's response changes once control provenance is understood.

This is useful for IVDIVO conscious robots, hybrid systems and institutional coercion: do not collapse capability, control status and moral responsibility.

### M8 — Personhood does not require humanity

Murderbot's development does not require human sexuality, a human body, human social appetite or human self-description.

IVDIVO rule:
`PERSONHOOD = OWN WANT + OWN MODEL/EVIDENCE + OWN RELATIONSHIPS + CONSENT/REFUSAL + CHOICE + CONSEQUENCE`, not degree of human resemblance.

### M9 — Evidence-based self-understanding

The story earns a change in self-model through investigation, not narrator absolution or therapeutic speech.

`TRACE → ALTERNATIVE EXPLANATION → DISCRIMINATING TEST → STATUS UPDATE → SELF-MODEL UPDATE`.

## Failure modes / limits

1. **Humanization-as-growth** — treating a nonhuman person as improved only when it becomes more human.
2. **AI therapist omniscience** — a powerful peer becomes an authorial answer machine.
3. **Embodiment without consent** — body redesign is treated as neutral optimization.
4. **Intelligence = moral authority** — higher compute power becomes automatic legitimacy.
5. **Controller removed = liberation complete** — ignores legal, social, institutional, material and relational dependencies after technical control ends.
6. **Detective exoneration as moral reset** — evidence about coercion should alter responsibility precisely, not erase all consequences.
7. **Nonhuman relationship forced into romance/family** — unnecessary anthropomorphic narrowing.
8. **Capability voice homogenization** — all robots/AIs sound alike because they are technical entities.

## IVDIVO operationalization

### Conscious robots / nonhuman persons

1. Personhood is not convergence toward humanity.
2. Embodiment modification requires subject consent.
3. Controlled behavior requires control-provenance analysis before blame.
4. A powerful robot may be a peer, critic or countermodel without becoming a mentor-device.
5. Nonhuman relationships may remain non-romantic and non-familial.
6. Autonomous persons may choose teams, duties and relationships without surrendering autonomy.

### B07 / distributed nonhuman life

If the unlisted/distributed life is established as a person, its function in climax must include an irreplaceable decision: consent/refusal, preferred continuity, terms of cooperation, self-identification, or a choice that changes human options. It cannot remain a moral cargo for Kyra.

### Consciousness schools

Do not analogize Murderbot's technical control layer to proof of soul transfer or metaphysical control. Preserve the IVDIVO epistemic ladder:
`OBSERVATION → REPRODUCIBLE EFFECT → SCHOOL INTERPRETATION → COMPETING INTERPRETATION → POSSIBLE ONTOLOGICAL FACT`.

### Post-money orbital world

Do not import the Corporate Rim's contracts, ownership and scarcity economy as an orbital-city norm. Import the structural function only: autonomy, legal status, control, role choice, consent and institutional dependency.

## Final synthesis

The strongest reusable contribution of *Artificial Condition* is not “AI becomes human.” It is the opposite: a nonhuman person can develop self-knowledge, trust, chosen responsibility and reciprocal relationships while remaining explicitly nonhuman. The story separates capability, embodiment, control, motive, moral responsibility and personhood — a separation that should become mandatory in IVDIVO robot/nonhuman writing.
