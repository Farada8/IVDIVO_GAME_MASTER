# IVDIVO CRAFT STUDY — BATCH 88
## Bell: Complex Plots · Character Arc · Verification · Convergence

Status: DRAFT / OPERATIONAL
Date: 2026-08-18
Primary source: James Scott Bell, *Plot & Structure*, Chapters 8–9

## Production gates

### B88-G01 — Strand Causal Contribution
Every active strand must change the dominant book engine or earn a necessary payoff. Mere thematic echo is insufficient for a large recurring strand.

### B88-G02 — Real Convergence
Two strands converge only when one changes the other's available action, knowledge, authority, relationship, risk, cost, or decision.

### B88-G03 — POV Switching Cost
A POV switch must purchase unique narrative value unavailable more cleanly from the current focalizer.

### B88-G04 — Nonlinear Recoverability
Nonlinear ordering may delay causality but must preserve enough temporal/source cues for retrospective reconstruction.

### B88-G05 — World Fact / Presentation Separation
Reordering fragments changes reader knowledge, not the ontology of what happened.

### B88-G06 — Arc Behavioral Verification
No important character change is accepted until later behavior demonstrates it.

### B88-G07 — No Mandatory Transformation
A protagonist may remain stable, change partially, revise strategy rather than values, or reach a wrong conclusion with consequences.

### B88-G08 — Pressure Specificity
Pressure must target a concrete value, model, relation, commitment, tactic, or responsibility. Generic suffering is not character development.

### B88-G09 — Developing Return
When an earlier phrase/object/rule/action recurs, its meaning or consequence should change.

### B88-G10 — Epiphany Optional
The story may let changed behavior precede or replace explicit self-explanation.

### B88-G11 — Series No-Reset
Later books inherit changed relationships, knowledge, responsibilities, reputations, and boundaries even when the protagonist's core value remains stable.

### B88-G12 — Robot Arc Personhood
A conscious robot's arc cannot be defined as becoming more human. Change must arise from that person's own embodiment, relations, commitments, consent, models, or strategy.

### B88-G13 — Post-Money Youth Arc
Do not use earnings, rent, credits, or consumer independence as default maturation. Prefer autonomy, trust, mastery, consent, responsibility, belonging, reputation, privacy, relationship terms, time, or unique opportunity.

### B88-G14 — Theme Through Consequence
A thematic claim must survive removal of explicit thematic dialogue because choices and outcomes already carry it.

## Failure-mode screen
ENSEMBLE EQUALITY RETROFIT; BRIEFING CONVERGENCE; POV NOVELTY; NONLINEAR FOG; MANDATORY TRANSFORMATION; ARC DECLARED NOT VERIFIED; EPIPHANY DOWNLOAD; SUFFERING INFLATION; SERIES RESET; ROBOT-BECOMES-HUMAN; THEME SPEECH; COSMETIC MOTIF.

## Writer's Room use
Apply this batch at architecture/revision stage, not as a sentence-generation checklist. First protect the living scene; then test causal contribution, convergence, behavioral verification, and accumulated consequence before line editing.
