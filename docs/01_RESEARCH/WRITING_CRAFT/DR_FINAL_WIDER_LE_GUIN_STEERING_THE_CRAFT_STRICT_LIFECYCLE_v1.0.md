## 2. Structure map
### Introduction
Craft as learnable skill; exercises as awareness/training rather than final products; narrative as movement/change; writer ultimately responsible for judgment of the work.

### 1 — The Sound of Your Writing
Prose has an aural dimension. Rhythm, sound, pacing and sentence-to-sentence movement are structural, not ornamental.

### 2 — Punctuation and Grammar
Punctuation controls how prose is heard and parsed. Grammar is a toolset, not a moral purity system. Intentional deviation differs from uncontrolled error.

### 3 — Sentence Length and Complex Syntax
Sentence length is functional and relational. Short and long forms gain power through contrast. Paragraphing is part of architecture and long rhythm.

### 4 — Repetition
Repetition can generate rhythm, motif, structure, emphasis and recurrence of meaning; it is not automatically redundancy.

### 5 — Adjectives and Adverbs
Modifiers are legitimate tools. The defect is lazy, redundant, vague or habitual qualification, not membership in a banned word class.

### 6 — Verbs: Person and Tense
Person and tense create different narrative fields and freedoms. Changes require control and reader orientation. Passive constructions are legitimate when functional.

### 7 — Point of View and Voice
First person, limited third, involved author, detached author and observer modes create different reader relationships and knowledge architectures. POV is not merely pronoun choice.

### 8 — Changing Point of View
POV switching can be powerful when controlled and functional. Momentary, unmotivated head-hopping is costly.

### 9 — Indirect Narration, or What Tells
Telling, summary, indirect narration and exposition are legitimate narrative modes. SF/fantasy exposition should be integrated into the story rather than delivered as an expository lump. Polyphony matters.

### 10 — Crowding and Leaping
Drafts may crowd material; revision selects what is load-bearing. Narrative leaps omit vastly more than they show. Focus and trajectory matter more than exhaustive dramatization.

### Peer Group Workshop
Critique the work rather than the author; reader reaction is evidence about effect; repeated reactions matter; proposed fixes remain proposals. Final artistic authority remains with the writer.

## 3. Core claims retained after Red Team
1. **Narrative rhythm is a system-level property.** Sound, punctuation, syntax, sentence length, paragraphing and repetition interact.
2. **There is no universal optimum sentence length.** Sentence length is chosen by function and contrast with neighboring sentences.
3. **Short syntax is not inherently faster or better.** Sustained short/simple syntax can become monotonous and choppy.
4. **Paragraph breaks are architectural decisions.** They signal connection/separation and participate in long-form rhythm.
5. **Punctuation is semantic and rhythmic control.** It changes how relations among clauses are understood and heard.
6. **Grammar is a repertoire of tools, not a moral code.** Active/passive, existential constructions and deliberate deviations are functional choices.
7. **Repetition is not automatically bad.** Repeated language, images, actions or structures can accumulate meaning.
8. **POV is knowledge + relationship + voice, not only grammatical person.**
9. **POV changes must earn their switching cost.**
10. **Telling is not inferior to showing.** Summary and indirect narration are necessary for pace, selection, worldbuilding and transitions.
11. **Exposition must become part of the story's causal/relational fabric.**
12. **Plot/conflict are powerful but not universal definitions of story.** Thought, relation, discovery, parting, change and quiet action can move narrative.
13. **Omission is an active craft decision.** Leaping is as important as crowding.
14. **Exercises train perception/control; exercise output is not automatically publishable or canonical.**
15. **Reader response is evidence about effect; reader repair instructions are hypotheses.**

## 4. Mechanism bank
### LG-M01 — Mind's Ear
`TEXT → READ/HEAR → RHYTHM / BREAK / EMPHASIS DIAGNOSIS → REVISION`

Use aloud/audio passes to detect jerky, droning, overloaded or falsely emphatic prose.

### LG-M02 — Sentence Contrast
`READER EFFECT → NEEDED DURATION / CONTINUITY → SENTENCE SHAPE → CONTRAST WITH NEIGHBORS`

Do not optimize average length or short-sentence percentage.

### LG-M03 — Paragraph Architecture
`CURRENT FOCUS / STATE → CONNECTION OR SEPARATION? → PARAGRAPH BOUNDARY → NEXT READER STATE`

A paragraph break requires a real structural/rhythmic reason, not a visual-density quota.

### LG-M04 — Functional Repetition
`ELEMENT RETURNS → CONTEXT / PRESSURE / RELATION / MEANING CHANGES → ACCUMULATED VALUE`

If nothing changes, repetition is probably duplication.

### LG-M05 — Tense / Person Field
`TIME / ACCESS / VOICE NEED → PERSON + TENSE → READER FIELD → CONSISTENCY / SIGNALLED SHIFT`

Treat Le Guin's distinctions as craft heuristics, not hard linguistic laws.

### LG-M06 — POV Access Ledger
`WHO PERCEIVES → WHAT THEY KNOW → WHAT THEY INFER → WHAT THEY CANNOT KNOW → WHAT NARRATION MAY STATE`

### LG-M07 — POV Switching Cost
`NEW POV → UNIQUE PERCEPTION / EVIDENCE / RELATION / DECISION VALUE? → SWITCH OR STAY`

### LG-M08 — Expository Lump Router
`INFO X → WHO NEEDS/NOTICES IT NOW → WHY NOW → HOW IT CHANGES ACTION/RELATION/MODEL → SCENE / SUMMARY / INDIRECT / DOCUMENT`

### LG-M09 — Polyphony
`CHARACTER WORLDVIEW + RELATIONSHIP + WANT + STATUS → LANGUAGE / RHYTHM / OMISSION`

A character is not an authorial mouthpiece.

### LG-M10 — Crowding / Leaping
`DRAFT MATERIAL → LOAD-BEARING? → KEEP / COMPRESS / OMIT → TRAJECTORY RETEST`

### LG-M11 — Focus / Trajectory
`CENTRAL FOCUS → EACH PASSAGE CONTRIBUTES / COMPLICATES / REFRAMES → TRAJECTORY`

A seemingly digressive passage can belong if it contributes to the central trajectory.

### LG-M12 — Feedback Contract
`READER REPORT → OBSERVED EFFECT → REPEATABILITY → DEFECT LEVEL → REPAIR HYPOTHESES → AUTHOR DECISION`

## 5. Failure modes
- **SHORT-SENTENCE MONOCULTURE** — choppy/drumming prose mistaken for speed.
- **LONG-SENTENCE SLUDGE** — complexity without clear syntactic joints.
- **PARAGRAPH CONFETTI** — one-line breaks without genuine focus/state shift.
- **VISUAL-DENSITY KPI** — paragraph length controlled by arbitrary numeric rule.
- **GRAMMAR-CHECKER FLATTENING** — automation normalizes intentional prose rhythm.
- **ACTIVE-VOICE DOGMA** — passive/existential constructions banned regardless of function.
- **ADJECTIVE/ADVERB PURGE** — tool class confused with misuse.
- **TENSE-FASHION RULE** — present/past chosen because it is allegedly more immediate/literary.
- **PRONOUN-SWAP POV REPAIR** — changing I/he/she without repairing access/voice/reader relation.
- **MOMENTARY HEAD-HOP** — short unearned excursions into another mind.
- **POV TELEPATHY** — narration states knowledge unavailable to the focal consciousness.
- **EXPOSITORY LUMP** — world information delivered because the author wants the reader to know it.
- **POLYPHONY COLLAPSE** — ensemble characters share one clever authorial language.
- **SHOWING MONOCULTURE** — routine process dramatized beat by beat.
- **ACTION MONOCULTURE** — movement/conflict mistaken for narrative change.
- **DETAIL CROWDING WITHOUT FOCUS** — specificity that does not contribute to trajectory.
- **REPETITION WITHOUT DEVELOPMENT** — recurrence without changed meaning.
- **EXERCISE-TO-CANON LEAK** — training output becomes story fact without validation.
- **READER-FIX AUTHORITY** — reader's proposed solution treated as proof of the correct repair.

## 6. Cross-source synthesis
### Le Guin × Francine Prose × Hodgins × Zinsser × Williams/Bizup
Strong convergence: prose flow is not reducible to short sentences. Reader-state continuity, syntax, paragraph architecture and contrast control rhythm.

### Le Guin × Clark
Short forms can stop as well as accelerate. Long forms can flow rapidly when syntactically clear. Numeric brevity rules are weak proxies.

### Le Guin × LaPlante × Rosenfeld × Scofield × Bickham
Scene/summary/telling/showing are modes to route by function. Routine process and transition often belong in compression rather than full scene.

### Le Guin × Genette × Chatman
Discourse selection and omission matter as much as story facts. What is excluded, summarized or delayed changes reader experience.

### Le Guin × Card/Kress/Fludernik
POV requires controlled knowledge access; person/tense labels alone do not define narrative intimacy or authority.

### Le Guin × Truby/Bell/Bickham — conflict firewall
Le Guin is a strong corrective to conflict absolutism. IVDIVO retains meaningful resistance where needed but does not require overt conflict/disaster in every scene.

### Le Guin × mystery corpus
A narrator's or POV character's interpretation remains sourced interpretation. Honest limited POV can be wrong without becoming a cheating narrator.

## 7. IVDIVO production gates
### Gate LG-G01 — Prose Rhythm
`READER EFFECT → PARAGRAPH FLOW → SENTENCE CONTRAST → PUNCTUATION → WORDING`.

### Gate LG-G02 — Anti-Choppiness
If several short lines share actor/time/place/focus and form one perceptual/decision unit, test merge/reorder before polishing each line.

### Gate LG-G03 — Paragraph Boundary
Every paragraph break should correspond to an actual change in focus, relation, beat-group, speaker, time, evidence state, tactic, emphasis or deliberate breath.

### Gate LG-G04 — Tense / Person
Choose for narrative field and function; preserve clarity across shifts.

### Gate LG-G05 — Passive Agency
Passive voice is allowed. If an institutional sentence hides power, ask:
`WHO DECIDED? → WHO ACTED? → WHO COULD REFUSE? → WHO BEARS COST?`

### Gate LG-G06 — POV Fairness
`OBSERVED / REMEMBERED / REPORTED / INFERRED / HYPOTHESIS / UNKNOWN` must not silently collapse into world fact.

### Gate LG-G07 — POV Shift Payment
A new consciousness must buy unique perception, evidence, relationship pressure or decision value.

### Gate LG-G08 — Exposition Ownership
No explanation enters because only the author wants it there.

### Gate LG-G09 — Character-Owned Voice
Shared theme does not justify shared wording, rhythm, metaphor or wit.

### Gate LG-G10 — Crowding / Leaping
Revision distinguishes load-bearing material from padding; omitted transitions do not need replacement if reader inference is sufficient.

### Gate LG-G11 — Conflict Firewall
No scene is rejected solely because it lacks overt conflict. It must, however, change a downstream-relevant state or contribute load-bearing focus/trajectory.

### Gate LG-G12 — Developing Repetition
Recurring image/line/action must return with changed context, pressure or meaning.

### Gate LG-G13 — Exercise Firewall
`EXERCISE OUTPUT ≠ CANON`.

### Gate LG-G14 — Feedback Evidence
Repeated reader/audio effect can reopen a layer; a repair suggestion does not automatically determine the repair.

- post-money norms should emerge through what characters notice and do not notice;
- several POVs are allowed when each pays a unique causal/epistemic cost;
- young voices arise from worldview, relation and normality, not a universal Earth-2020s slang layer;
- quiet relationship/discovery/decision scenes can move story without obligatory confrontation.

### ROOM 917
Use:
`WORLD FACT → TRACE → POV ACCESS → INTERPRETATION → ALTERNATIVE MODEL → CORROBORATION STATUS`.

Audio performance, silence and subtext may alter inference but not evidence:
`TRUST / ATTRACTION / KISS / CONFESSION / SILENCE / INFLECTION ≠ CORROBORATION`.

Technical/acoustic exposition should enter because the investigation needs it now and change the next action/model.

### B07 / nonhuman personhood
Human labels and POV interpretations do not define personhood. After independent threshold:
`ENTITY MODEL/PREFERENCE → CONSENT/REFUSAL → ENTITY CHOICE → HUMAN OPTION SPACE CHANGES`.

### Consciousness schools
Vivid subjective narration remains experience, not ontology:
`PRACTITIONER OBSERVATION → REPRODUCIBLE EFFECT → SCHOOL INTERPRETATION → SOURCE LINEAGE → COMPETING INTERPRETATION → INDEPENDENT TEST → ONTOLOGY CEILING`.

## 9. Science / market / authority firewall
Do not promote Le Guin's practitioner judgments about psychology, education, publishing or linguistic usage into scientific or current-market facts without independent verification.

Her strongest contribution here is craft-level: control of prose sound/rhythm, syntax, paragraphs, POV, narration, exposition, selection and omission.

## 10. Final synthesis
The most important IVDIVO integration is not a new formula. It is a hierarchy:

`STORY / SCENE FUNCTION → NARRATIVE MODE → POV / KNOWLEDGE → PARAGRAPH ARCHITECTURE → READER-STATE CONTINUITY → SENTENCE CONTRAST / RHYTHM → PUNCTUATION / WORDING`.

Therefore:

`ACTION / SUSPENSE / MYSTERY / EMOTION ≠ SHORT-SENTENCE DENSITY`  
`BEAT ≠ SENTENCE BREAK`  
`PARAGRAPH BREAK ≠ VISUAL-DENSITY KPI`  
`COMPRESSION ≠ CHOPPING`  
`TELLING ≠ FAILURE`  
`CONFLICT ≠ THE ONLY FORM OF MOVEMENT`  
`POV INTERPRETATION ≠ WORLD FACT`.
