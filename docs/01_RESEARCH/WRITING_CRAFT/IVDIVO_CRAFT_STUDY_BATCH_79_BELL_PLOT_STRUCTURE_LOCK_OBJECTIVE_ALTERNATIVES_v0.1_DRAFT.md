## 1. LOCK AS DIAGNOSTIC COMPRESSION

Bell's Lead / Objective / Confrontation / Knockout is useful as a compact diagnostic, not a mandatory architecture.

IVDIVO translation:

- **FOCAL AGENT** — whose access/choice currently organizes reader attention?
- **TRACKABLE OBJECTIVE** — what concrete state is this agent trying to create, prevent, discover, preserve or renegotiate?
- **MEANINGFUL RESISTANCE** — what person, rule, uncertainty, relationship, physical condition, evidence gap or competing good prevents a frictionless answer?
- **LOCAL CLOSURE / RESONANCE** — what question/state does the book actually settle, at what cost, leaving what changed state?

### Failure mode: LOCK literalism

Symptoms:
- ensemble collapsed into one 'real' hero;
- every opposing force becomes a villain;
- every scene must block the objective;
- finale forced into victory/defeat spectacle.

Repair:
Use ONE MAIN STORY as a causal engine, not protagonist monopoly.

## 2. ONE MAIN STORY — BELL CORRECTION

Preferred IVDIVO form:

**ONE DOMINANT CAUSAL ENGINE → DISTRIBUTED CHARACTER OBJECTIVES → INTERACTION → CONSEQUENCES → CAUSAL RECONVERGENCE.**

A subplot/objective belongs if it changes at least one of:
- action options;
- evidence/model;
- relationship/consent;
- responsibility/authority;
- cost/risk;
- strategy;
- final bounded choice.

If it only repeats theme or showcases worldbuilding, compress/merge/remove.

## 3. OBJECTIVE QUALITY GATE

A usable objective is reader-trackable. It can be:
- obtain/earn a concrete role or mission place;
- protect a person/system without violating consent;
- determine whether an evidence model is reliable enough for action;
- repair a defined failure;
- preserve/renegotiate a relationship;
- stop a specific harmful process;
- reach a bounded operational answer.

Weak substitutes:
- 'grow up';
- 'understand life';
- 'learn about orbital society';
- 'become better';
- 'explore the mystery' without a decision/action threshold.

Developmental need may accompany the objective but does not replace it.

## 4. MEANINGFUL RESISTANCE ≠ MISERY

Bell's confrontation principle is useful only after correction.

Allowed resistance:
- competent countermodel with real evidence;
- limits of authority/data/access;
- incompatible but legitimate needs;
- consent boundary;
- physical environment;
- uncertainty;
- prior successful strategy becoming insufficient;
- relationship history;
- time or truly nonfungible opportunity.

Not required:
- villain;
- death stakes;
- constant setbacks;
- humiliating protagonist;
- artificial deadline;
- hidden money scarcity.

A scene may succeed. Success becomes story when it changes responsibility, trust, dependence, next objective or later cost.

## 5. KNOCKOUT → LOCAL CLOSURE

Replace the rhetorical idea of a knockout with:

**DECISIVE LOCAL CHOICE → RESULT → COST/AFTERMATH → CHANGED STATE.**

A series novel must close its local main problem before opening the next saga question.

No requirement for:
- triumph;
- death;
- total explanation;
- metaphysical answer;
- final speech stating the theme.

## 6. ALTERNATIVE GENERATION GATE

Before locking a major turn, scene solution, reveal or ending:

1. generate several materially different alternatives;
2. test character truth;
3. test world/canon truth;
4. test causal force;
5. test freshness against reference corpus;
6. test downstream consequences/payoff;
7. select only then.

This converges with Bernays/Painter Five-Fork, Bell *Art of War* premise tournament and Truby alternative design.

### Failure mode: first-adequate-answer protection

A workable first answer is not evidence that it is strongest.

## 7. DIALOGUE-AS-ACTION, CORRECTED

Bell's 'dialogue as a weapon' is one useful subset.

IVDIVO router:

**WANT → VERBAL/NONVERBAL TACTIC → REACTION → TACTIC/MODEL CHANGE → CHANGED STATE.**

Tactics include:
- confront;
- persuade;
- negotiate;
- reassure;
- test;
- invite;
- refuse;
- consent;
- withhold;
- misdirect;
- repair;
- bond;
- cooperate;
- remain silent.

### Failure mode: dialogue combat monoculture

If every conversation is status combat, relationships and voices become mechanically alike.

## 8. QUIET MATERIAL GATE

Bell's compression of dull process is useful, but ordinary life is not automatically dull.

Keep a quiet/ordinary scene if deletion would change:
- relationship;
- commitment;
- evidence/model;
- consent;
- future choice;
- reader bond;
- post-money normality necessary to prevent Earth-default assumptions;
- earned reward/relief that makes later pressure matter.

Otherwise use summary/transition/merge.

## 9. WORLD/SETTING CORRECTION

Bell sometimes treats setting as a source of freshness. IVDIVO requires a stronger causal test:

**WORLD RULE → AVAILABLE/UNAVAILABLE ACTION → CHARACTER CHOICE → CONSEQUENCE.**

Orbital technology is not scenery inventory. A rule earns page-space when it changes action, relationship, risk, timing, consent or inference.

## 10. CANON FIREWALLS

### Post-money
Do not import Bell's money/food/status examples into orbital society as rent, salary, credits or disguised purchasing power.

Relevant nonfungible stakes:
trust, consent, role, mission place, time, relationship, mastery, reputation, responsibility, unique experience, access that has a real operational basis.

### Conscious robots
A conscious robot can be a focal agent or lead. It requires independent objective, relationships, consent/refusal and consequences. Never reduce it to plot assistance.

### Publishing advice
Bell's 2004 agent/query/market/process claims are historical context and require current verification before business use.
