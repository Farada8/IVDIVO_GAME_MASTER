## CENTRAL CLAIMS — TRANSFORMED FOR IVDIVO
1. A romance has an observable relationship arc; chemistry alone is not progression.
2. External plot and relationship plot work best when they causally alter each other.
3. Both partners require meaningful agency and should remain independently intelligible persons.
4. Attraction, trust, vulnerability, boundaries and commitment can be tracked as changing relationship state.
5. Repeated contact needs a credible causal basis, but causal contact does not justify coercion.
6. A rupture is strongest when it grows from established values, fears, evidence, boundaries and choices rather than an arbitrary misunderstanding.
7. A late relational proof is strongest when it costs the acting character something specific and leaves the other person free to accept or refuse.
8. Resolution should change the terms of the relationship without erasing personality.

## PRIMARY IVDIVO MECHANISM
`TWO COMPLETE AGENTS → SUSTAINED CAUSAL CONTACT → RELATIONSHIP STATE CHANGE → EXTERNAL CHOICE CHANGE → COST → NEW TERMS OF RELATIONSHIP`

Expanded scene braid:
`INDEPENDENT WANT / BOUNDARY → INTERACTION → NEW INFORMATION / RESPECT / ATTRACTION / TRUST → CHANGED BOUNDARY → PRESSURE → CHOICE → COST → NEW TERMS`

External-story braid:
`EXTERNAL EVENT / CHOICE → TRUST / DESIRE / BOUNDARY CHANGE → RELATIONSHIP STATE CHANGE → THAT CHANGE ALTERS NEXT EXTERNAL CHOICE`

## RELATIONSHIP STATE LEDGER
For a major relationship scene, track only the axes that actually change:
- Agent A present want / boundary
- Agent B present want / boundary
- trust
- attraction / desire
- respect
- privacy / disclosure
- consent / permission
- dependency / voluntary reliance
- evidence known or believed
- future expectation
- next joint or conflicting decision
- price paid

A scene containing chemistry but no changed state may still be atmospheric or pleasurable, but it is not automatically relationship progression.

## FAILURE MODES
1. **Beat-Sheet Retrofit** — forcing a live book to imitate all Hayes beats after the fact.
2. **Mandatory Wound** — inventing trauma because a romance framework expects emotional damage.
3. **Partner-as-Cure** — treating another person as the missing piece required to make a protagonist whole.
4. **Forced-Proximity Consent Erosion** — using causal contact as permission to override boundaries.
5. **Breakup Mandate** — manufacturing separation because the beat sheet expects one.
6. **Grand-Gesture Inflation** — substituting spectacle/publicity for credible character-specific cost.
7. **Romance Replaces Evidence** — trust, love or intuition upgrades a mystery hypothesis into proof.
8. **HEA Leak into Mystery-Primary** — romance genre closure displaces the promised case answer.
9. **Symmetric Growth Requirement** — forcing both agents to transform in equal ways.
10. **Chemistry Without State Change** — repeated looks/touches/banter that never alter trust, boundaries or choices.
11. **Detached Romance Lane** — relationship scenes that do not affect the dominant causal engine.
12. **Romance Beat as Plot Exemption** — suspending character truth, world rules or evidence standards to reach a romantic milestone.

## CROSS-SOURCE COMPARISON
### Hayes + Rosenfeld / Swain / Bell
Relationship change can be a genuine scene outcome. A scene need not end in external defeat; altered trust, boundary, obligation or future strategy can be causal movement.

### Hayes + Card / Corbett / McKee Character
Two partners remain independent persons. Relationship-specific behavior matters more than generic romance-role labels.

### Hayes + Zunshine / Herman
`RELATIONAL BELIEF ≠ VERIFIED EVIDENCE`. A character may trust someone and still be wrong about the world. Emotional confidence never silently changes evidence status.

### Hayes + Iglesias / Oatley
`CHARACTER FEELS X ≠ READER FEELS X`. Attraction written on the page does not guarantee reader investment; context, expectation, specificity and consequence must earn it.

### Hayes + Brody / Vogler
Beat systems are maps/repertoires, not mandatory formulas. Product promise and causal function outrank template compliance.

## IVDIVO OPERATIONALIZATION
### Romance Weight Dial
- **MYSTERY-PRIMARY**: external case/evidence remains the dominant engine; use Hayes selectively for trust, desire, boundaries and relational causality.
- **BALANCED**: relationship and external engine should repeatedly alter one another.
- **ROMANCE-PRIMARY**: Hayes may be used at higher density, but still as a diagnostic architecture rather than a compulsory 20-beat template.

### Consent firewall
Sustained causal contact may come from shared work, investigation, school, family/social network, mission, evidence chain or voluntarily repeated interaction. It never grants entitlement to intimacy.

### Post-money romance stakes
Use nonfungible stakes: trust, privacy, time, consent, future coordination, belonging, reputation, unique opportunity, concrete responsibility, relationship terms. Do not reintroduce salary/rent/credits as a shortcut.

### Conscious robots / nonhuman persons
A romantic or intimate nonhuman agent must have:
`OWN WANT → OWN RELATIONSHIPS → OWN MODEL/EVIDENCE → CONSENT/REFUSAL → CHOICE → CONSEQUENCE`.
No agent exists as a service, cure, missing half or empathy device for a human protagonist.

## MANUSCRIPT / PROJECT VALIDATION
### ROOM 917
Declared routing: **MYSTERY-PRIMARY**.
Elena↔Julian relationship beats may alter:
- who shares evidence;
- who receives access;
- privacy boundaries;
- willingness to cooperate;
- interpretation of risk;
- the next investigative action.

They may **not** alter what raw evidence objectively proves. A kiss, confession, attraction or decision to trust cannot close the Room 917 mystery.

### ORBITAL YOUTH / future IVDIVO books
A relationship scene is accepted when it changes the ONE MAIN STORY, an earned relationship state, or a future character choice. Ordinary-life intimacy is valid, but repeated chemistry without changed terms is not automatic progression.

## FINAL SYNTHESIS
Hayes is retained as a **relationship-causality specialist**, not as universal plot authority.

IVDIVO law derived from the useful core:

**RELATIONSHIP PROGRESSION IS A CHANGE IN TERMS BETWEEN INDEPENDENT AGENTS THAT ALTERS WHAT THEY CAN OR WILL DO NEXT.**

Romance may change trust, risk, access, disclosure and choice. It does not replace evidence, consent, causal logic or personhood.
