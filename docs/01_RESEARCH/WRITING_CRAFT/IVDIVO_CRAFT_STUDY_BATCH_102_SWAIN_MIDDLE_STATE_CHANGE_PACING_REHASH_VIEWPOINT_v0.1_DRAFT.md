## CORE SYNTHESIS
The strongest transferable idea from Swain's middle design is not `more trouble` but **continued consequential change**.

IVDIVO middle engine:

`CHANGED STATE -> CHARACTER / TEAM REASSESSMENT -> OPTION SET CHANGES -> STRATEGY OR COMMITMENT CHANGES -> ACTION -> CONSEQUENCE -> NEXT CHANGED STATE`

Track state across at least eight axes:
1. physical / operational situation;
2. knowledge / evidence status;
3. relationship / trust / intimacy;
4. authority / access / role;
5. responsibility / obligation;
6. strategy / causal model;
7. emotion / commitment;
8. future option space.

A quiet scene is causal when one or more of these axes changes and the change constrains later action.

## MIDDLE STATE-CHANGE AUDIT
For every middle chapter/scene ask:
- What was materially different at entry?
- What does the focal owner want now?
- What pressure, support, information, or relationship changes the available options?
- Which state axis changes by the end?
- What previous strategy is weakened, strengthened, or invalidated?
- What new action becomes possible or necessary?
- Which later scene depends on this change?

If none of these can be answered, the unit is a candidate for MERGE / SUMMARY / INDIRECT / DELETE.

## SUCCESS-BASED PROGRESSION
Do not preserve motion by sabotaging competent characters.

Preferred pattern when appropriate:

`COMPETENT ACTION -> REAL SUCCESS -> MORE TRUST / ACCESS / RESPONSIBILITY -> HARDER NEXT CHOICE`

This is especially useful in YA, training, engineering, institutional and rescue stories.

## REHASH FIREWALL
Repeated information is not progression merely because a new speaker says it.

A repeated fact earns space only if at least one of the following changes:
- source reliability;
- evidence status;
- interpretation;
- relationship consequence;
- authority consequence;
- tactical implication;
- emotional meaning;
- future option set.

Otherwise compress.

## VIEWPOINT AS CAUSAL ACCESS
A POV switch is justified only when the new mind supplies something unavailable or materially different:
- unique evidence;
- unique lived consequence;
- different relation to the same fact;
- different authority / risk;
- different causal model;
- an indispensable decision.

`NEW POV != NEW PLOT`.

## PACING
Pacing is not short-sentence density.

Track separately:
- event density;
- information density;
- decision density;
- emotional processing time;
- scene duration;
- paragraph rhythm;
- sentence rhythm.

A high-pressure moment may require deliberate slowing to preserve uncertainty, sensory orientation, consent, or evidence evaluation.

## FAILURE MODES
- STATE-NEUTRAL MIDDLE
- SETBACK MONOCULTURE
- SUCCESS ERASED TO PRESERVE TENSION
- INFORMATION REHASH
- BRIEFING LOOP
- NEW-POV COSMETICISM

- COMMITMENT WITHOUT NEW OBLIGATIONS
- NEW TECHNOLOGY WITHOUT OPTION-SPACE CHANGE
- QUIET SCENE WITHOUT DOWNSTREAM CONSEQUENCE

## CROSS-SOURCE COMPARISON
### Rosenfeld
Supports prior consequence -> scene intention -> result -> next intention. Less mechanical than Swain's pulp-era escalation.

### Chatman / Ribó / Fludernik
Support non-physical change: knowledge, discourse placement, relation and interpretation can carry causality.

### Herman / Zunshine
Require evidence provenance and explicit confidence status. New information is not automatically new truth.

### LaPlante / Prose / Lodge
Support quiet, observational and reflective material when it changes meaning, choice, or relationship.

### Bell / McKee
Useful on trackable objective and changing tactics, but IVDIVO rejects compulsory failure, death stakes and short-syntax action formulas.

## PRODUCTION GATES B102
### B102-G01 — STATE CHANGE
Every substantive middle unit must change at least one relevant story state with downstream effect.

### B102-G02 — REHASH STATUS
Repeated information must change source/evidence/interpretation/consequence or be compressed.

### B102-G03 — SUCCESS PERMISSION
Competent success is allowed and should often increase responsibility rather than be neutralized.

### B102-G04 — POV COST
A POV switch must buy unique causal, evidentiary, relational or decision value.

### B102-G05 — PROCESS COMPRESSION
Routine logistics, repeated testing, commuting, setup and procedural talk default to summary/indirect unless exact action or wording changes the story.

### B102-G06 — PACING LAYER SEPARATION
Do not diagnose pace solely from sentence length.

### B102-G07 — ONE MAIN STORY PAY-IN
Every strand must change the dominant causal engine, not merely coexist beside it.

### B102-G08 — PERSONHOOD FIREWALL
A conscious robot/nonhuman cannot be treated as a mere obstacle, clue source or complication. It retains independent want, evidence/model, consent/refusal, relationships, choice and consequence.

### B102-G09 — CONSCIOUSNESS-SCHOOL EPISTEMICS
Practice experience may change a character's model or decision without upgrading school interpretation into world fact.

### B102-G10 — POST-MONEY STAKES
Escalation in orbital settings uses trust, time, role, consent, access, responsibility, reputation, relationship, mastery, unique opportunity or safety—not disguised salary/rent/credits.
