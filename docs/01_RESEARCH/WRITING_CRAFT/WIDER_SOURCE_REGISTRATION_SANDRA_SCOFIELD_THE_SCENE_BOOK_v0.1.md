## Early mechanisms — provisional
### 1. SCENE IS A HIGH-IMMEDIACY MODE, NOT THE WHOLE OF NARRATIVE
Scofield explicitly rejects the idea that every story must be a rosary of fully developed scenes.

Working router:
`FULL SCENE / EMBEDDED SCENE / SCENE FRAGMENT / SUMMARY / COMMENTARY`.

This strongly converges with LaPlante, Rosenfeld, Bell and Swain.

### 2. ACTION + INTERIORITY CAN SHARE THE SAME PROSE SPACE
Prose can combine external event with memory, thought and subjective response rather than choosing film-like action or static introspection.

### 3. SCENE WEIGHT MUST VARY
Not every scene should receive equal development or reader time.

### 4. PRACTICE MATERIAL MAY BE DISCARDED
Writing a prior scene or exploratory scene can clarify story history even when the scene never enters the final manuscript.

### 5. CREATION AND ANALYTIC REVISION ARE DISTINCT
Exercises and craft models are best used to coolly inspect/restructure material after or alongside discovery, not to erase intuitive generation.
