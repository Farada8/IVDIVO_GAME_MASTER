## Brandilyn Collins — Inner Rhythm · Character-Specific Expression · Prose Restraint

Status: DRAFT / source FULL READ still in progress

Source: Brandilyn Collins, *Getting Into Character*, Edition 2 (2015)

## Scope

This batch closes strict reading of Secret #5 `Inner Rhythm` and extracts mechanisms that are safe to operationalize before the book's overall lifecycle is complete.

## Core finding

The useful craft distinction is not `fast scene / slow scene`, but the relationship among:

`INNER PROCESS -> OUTER BEHAVIOUR -> CHARACTER-SPECIFIC FILTER -> READER EFFECT`.

Inner and outer rhythms may align or contradict each other. The contradiction can itself generate subtext and character specificity.

## Production mechanisms

### B152-G01 — INNER / OUTER RHYTHM SEPARATION

Track separately:

- speed / pressure of thought and emotion;
- speed / density of visible action;
- communication tempo;
- scene/environment tempo.

Do not infer one mechanically from another.

### B152-G02 — EXPRESSION OPTION SET

Before selecting a gesture or verbal tell, generate more than one plausible expression of the same internal state.

`INNER STATE -> OPTION SET -> CHARACTER / RELATIONSHIP / OBJECTIVE FILTER -> SELECTED EXPRESSION`.

### B152-G03 — NO EMOTION-TO-GESTURE LOOKUP

Ban generic correspondences such as:

- fear -> trembling;
- guilt -> looking down;
- anger -> clenched fists;
- attraction -> breath catching;

unless the specific character, embodiment and situation actually earn that expression.

### B152-G04 — PRIVATE EXERCISE / PUBLIC PROSE FIREWALL

Physical rhythm drills, character interviews and embodied improvisations may generate material privately. The final prose must select only the expressions that are load-bearing.

### B152-G05 — STILLNESS CAN CARRY HIGH PRESSURE

A scene with little visible motion can remain active if knowledge, relationship, evidence, commitment or option space is changing under pressure.

### B152-G06 — RHYTHM IS NOT TYPOGRAPHY

Do not encode every inner beat as a fragment, sentence break or one-line paragraph.

`BEAT / INNER TURN != SENTENCE BREAK`.

### B152-G07 — PARAGRAPH BEFORE FRAGMENT

When several micro-actions belong to one perceptual / decision unit, test a coherent paragraph before preserving isolated lines.

### B152-G08 — BODY CUE EVIDENCE STATUS

A body cue is observable evidence about behaviour. It is not automatically evidence of motive, truthfulness, guilt or consent.

### B152-G09 — RELATION-SPECIFIC RHYTHM

The same character may have different inner/outer relations with friend, parent, instructor, rival, intimate partner, public audience or conscious robot.

### B152-G10 — NONHUMAN RHYTHM WITHOUT HUMAN MIMICRY

For a conscious robot or other nonhuman person, express timing through its actual embodiment, attention, communication constraints, priorities and choice architecture. Human-style facial leakage is optional, not a personhood requirement.

## Failure modes

- **FRAGMENT FEEDBACK LOOP** — emotional pressure causes fragments; fragments are then mistaken for proof of emotional pressure.
- **MICROGESTURE CONFETTI** — every line receives a look, hand movement, breath or posture change.
- **AUTHOR-BODY CLONING** — every character receives the writer's own stress responses.
- **EMOTIONAL TELEMETRY** — narrator reports every internal fluctuation rather than selecting consequential changes.
- **OUTER-MOTION FETISH** — stillness mistaken for lack of scene activity.
- **ROBOT HUMANIZATION BY TELL** — artificial personhood signalled through stock human nervous gestures.
- **RHYTHM KPI** — prose judged by short-sentence percentage rather than reader effect.

## Cross-source comparison

### Collins × Stanislavski

The strongest convergence is that inner and outer tempo can diverge. Physical rhythm exercises are aids to discovery, not final form.

### Collins × Le Guin / Prose / Lodge / Zinsser

All converge against sentence-length dogma. Short prose gains force from function and contrast; long prose can sustain pressure, continuity and accumulation.

### Collins × Swain / Bickham

Swain/Bickham clarify causal sequence. Collins clarifies why the same causal stimulus can produce very different behaviour in different people.

### Collins × Card / Corbett / McKee Character

Character specificity emerges from priorities, relationships and tactics under pressure rather than from isolated quirks.

## IVDIVO operational synthesis

Use the following order when emotional prose feels generic or choppy:

`SCENE FUNCTION -> POV KNOWLEDGE/EVIDENCE -> INNER PRESSURE -> OUTER CONSTRAINT -> CHARACTER OBJECTIVE -> RELATIONSHIP -> EXPRESSION OPTION SET -> PARAGRAPH INTENT -> SENTENCE RHYTHM`.

Do not begin by adding gestures or shortening lines.
