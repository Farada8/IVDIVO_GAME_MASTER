# 5. Main mechanisms gained

1. **Active commitment without percentage doctrine**  
   Disturbance should generate processing and a meaningful choice/accepted responsibility, then a trackable objective.

2. **Trackable middle**  
   Brody's strongest beat-sheet value is protection against a premise middle that becomes disconnected episodes.

3. **Premise payoff**  
   Distinctive YA/SF experience must actually occur through causal scenes.

4. **Midpoint as operational state change**  
   Replace false-victory/false-defeat requirement with material change in model, goal, method, relationship, evidence or constraints.

5. **Aftermath causality**  
   Reflection earns space when it changes next action/relationship/interpretation; mandatory Dark Night rejected.

6. **Relationship personhood**  
   B-story/theme-teacher model rejected as production law. Supporting people require independent want, cost, life and refusal rights.

7. **Multiple POV connection**  
   Single/multi/blended track vocabulary is useful, but saga equality is not equal screen time and each POV must connect causally to ONE MAIN STORY.

8. **Rites-of-Passage without wrong-way monoculture**  
   Youth developmental pressure is useful; mandatory avoidance → hard-truth acceptance is not.

9. **Buddy Love without completion myth**  
   Relationship can transform both agents without framing either as an incomplete half.

10. **Institutionalized without institutional binary**  
    Group/belonging/choice is a strong YA engine, but IVDIVO expands beyond join/escape/destroy to reform, negotiated membership, bounded refusal, plural institutions and institutional learning.
