## 3. MAJOR MECHANISMS EXTRACTED THIS PASS
### A. Reader-state continuity
A sentence is not judged only locally. The next sentence must connect to what remains active in the reader’s mind: subject, actor, time, location, image, knowledge, tone and emphasis.

Operational form:
`STATE LEFT BY A → WHAT B ASSUMES → MISMATCH? → SIGNAL / REORDER / REWRITE`.

### B. Clutter above the word level
Clutter can be a repeated sentence or paragraph, not only an unnecessary adjective/adverb. This directly supports IVDIVO anti-fragmentation and anti-restatement passes.

### C. Simplicity ≠ shortness
No short-sentence KPI. A long sentence may be transparent; a short sentence may be vague, repetitive or theatrically fragmented.

### D. Bounded selection
Large subject/world → select one causal/intellectual corner. Reinforces:
`SAGA SCALE ≠ BOOK SCALE`
and
`WORLD DATABASE ≠ BOOK NARRATIVE`.

### E. Lead contract
Opening function:
`ATTENTION + ORIENTATION + PROMISE`.
No mandatory accident/action/mystery/gimmick.

### F. Stop-when-done ending
Do not repeat the already-proved lesson in a school-essay summary. End on earned consequence, image, fact, echo, quotation or precise cutoff.

### G. Trust the material / trust the reader
Specific fact, behavior or detail should often produce its own emotional/intellectual effect. Avoid labeling the material as surprising, important or symbolic before the reader encounters it.

### H. Rewriting as sequencing
Revision includes moving information earlier, signalling shifts, repairing transitions and changing sentence order—not only cutting words.

## 4. NEW PRODUCTION ROUTER
For prose/“обрубки”:
`ONE MAIN STORY / CAUSALITY → SCENE NECESSITY → SHOW/TELL → POV → DIALOGUE MODE → PARAGRAPH INTENT → READER-STATE CONTINUITY → SENTENCE RHYTHM → WORDING`.

New anti-fragmentation diagnostic:
- same actor/time/place/focus across several short units with no genuine beat → test recombination;
- protect short standalone lines when they perform real contrast, revelation, silence, breath, comic timing, decision or end-weight;
- read aloud after recombination to restore variation.
