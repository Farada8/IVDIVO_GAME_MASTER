# IVDIVO CRAFT STUDY — BATCH 51
## McKee Action: Design · Pace · Subgenre · Form

Date: 2026-08-17
Status: SYNTHESIS DERIVED FROM STRICT-COMPLETE SOURCE
Source: Robert McKee with Bassim El-Wakil — *Action*

## PURPOSE
Extract the second-half mechanisms that remain useful after removing narrow genre dogma.

## 1. INCITING INCIDENT — FUNCTIONAL VERSION
McKee's useful distinction is not the 25% placement claim. It is:

`BALANCE DISTURBED → CHARACTER IDENTIFIES A SPECIFIC OBJECT/OUTCOME → ACTION BECOMES NECESSARY`.

IVDIVO gate: delay an inciting disturbance only when prerequisite context genuinely changes reader interpretation. Do not front-load lore to “prepare” it.

## 2. OBJECT OF DESIRE ≠ MOTIVATION
Two separate questions:
- What observable result does the character pursue?
- Why does this character care about that result?

Failure mode: abstract goal such as “become independent” used as plot objective. Independence is developmental meaning; the A-story still needs a trackable result.

## 3. PROGRESSION WITHOUT SCALE INFLATION
McKee describes action progression as points of no return and rising danger. IVDIVO generalizes:

`OLD TACTIC FAILS / BECOMES INSUFFICIENT → NEW INFORMATION OR LIMIT → DIFFERENT TACTIC → NEW COST/RELATIONSHIP/ACCESS STATE`.

A later scene is stronger when it forces different thinking, not merely greater danger.

## 4. CAPABILITY LIMITS
The strongest transferable chapter is Tactics.

Use:
- specific tactic immunity;
- loss of habitual tool;
- meaningful capability ceiling;
- no obvious off-switch;
- no unlimited technology.

For advanced IVDIVO technology, every major capability requires explicit limits that arise from physics, rights, consent, coordination, time, environment, compatibility, governance or information—not arbitrary author prohibition.

## 5. SET PIECES AS CAUSAL UNITS
A set piece is not “big action.” It is a prolonged pressure movement with:
- an immediate objective;
- changing tactics;
- meaningful constraints;
- sensory/physical reality;
- altered operational state;
- altered information/relationship state.

For novels, do not imitate screenplay choreography density. Preserve only consequential moments.

## 6. PRAISE / MOCKERY AS POWER MANAGEMENT
Useful abstraction:
- how characters describe another actor changes perceived leverage;
- mockery can accidentally destroy threat credibility;
- praise without counter-evidence can accidentally make a protagonist overpowered.

IVDIVO gate: reputation dialogue must be paid off by observed behavior. Words cannot substitute for deeds.

## 7. CRISIS / CLIMAX — BOUNDED CHOICE
Rejected: mandatory self-sacrifice and mandatory Life/Death question.

Retained:
`EASY SOLUTIONS REMOVED → LAST MEANINGFUL ALTERNATIVES → CHOICE → COST → LOCAL RESULT`.

The climax should solve the book's local problem through established agency. It need not be the physically largest event.

## 8. RESOLUTION
A resolution scene exists to show relevant ripple effects.

Gate:
`LOCAL RESULT → WHO/WHAT CHANGED → WHAT COST REMAINS → STOP`.

Do not append a false ending or new villain merely to generate a franchise hook.

## 9. RHYTHM / TEMPO / INTENSITY
McKee separates three variables:
- rhythm = scene length and spacing;
- tempo = energy/activity within a scene;
- intensity = proximity/severity of danger.

IVDIVO prose consequence:
**sentence length is not a proxy for all three.**

A long sentence can intensify slowed perception. A short sentence can create impact. Neither is inherently “action style.”

## 10. DIMINISHING RETURNS
Track repetition across:
- emotion;
- movement geometry;
- obstacle type;
- tactic;
- reveal type;
- relationship beat;
- paragraph/sentence cadence.

Repeated form is allowed when meaning changes. Repetition without changed meaning becomes reader adaptation.

## 11. SUBGENRE AS SOURCE-OF-CONFLICT MAP
McKee's four families can be abstracted beyond action:
- environment/nature;
- institution/social hierarchy;
- individual strategic opposition;
- time/irreversibility.

Do not personify all four as villain. Use them to identify where resistance actually comes from.

## 12. LABYRINTH AS WORLD-ACTION MECHANISM
Especially useful for orbital cities, rescue geography and mixed-environment spaces:

`SETTING RULE → ROUTE/ACCESS LIMIT → TACTICAL CHOICE → CONSEQUENCE`.

The environment becomes story when its geometry changes available actions.

## 13. HIGH ADVENTURE — USE WITH CAUTION
Useful: action may carry larger social/philosophical meaning.
Rejected: archetypal claims as authority.

IVDIVO principle:
**meaning emerges from specific consequences, not from declaring the plot mythic.**

## 14. FORM VERSUS FORMULA
Strong convergence with IVDIVO:
- know recurrent functions;
- reject page-count formulae;
- reject compulsory Hero's Journey retrofit;
- reinvent execution for this world and these people.

## NEW PRODUCTION GATES

**B51-G01 Object/Motive Separation** — identify both independently.

**B51-G02 Inciting Sufficiency** — what minimum context must precede disturbance for full effect?

**B51-G03 Qualitative Progression** — does the next pressure demand a different strategy/model?

**B51-G04 Capability Ceiling** — what real limit prevents obvious superior solution?

**B51-G05 Set-Piece Dual State Change** — both action state and information/relationship state change.

**B51-G06 Reputation Paid by Action** — praise/mockery has observed evidence.

**B51-G07 Bounded Crisis Choice** — real alternatives, not fake binary.

**B51-G08 No False-Ending Reflex** — local closure first.

**B51-G09 Rhythm/Tempo/Intensity Separation** — diagnose independently.

**B51-G10 Repetition Meaning Test** — repeat only with changed meaning/function.

**B51-G11 Source-of-Conflict Map** — environment / institution / person / time identified explicitly.

**B51-G12 Labyrinth Causality** — setting geometry changes action, not just description.

**B51-G13 No Mythic Retrofit** — thematic scale must be earned by events.

**B51-G14 Form Not Formula** — no mandatory beat/page template.

## FINAL BATCH PRINCIPLE

`ACTION QUALITY = HUMAN STAKE × SPECIFIC TACTICS × REAL LIMITS × QUALITATIVE PROGRESSION × MODULATION × EARNED CHOICE`.
