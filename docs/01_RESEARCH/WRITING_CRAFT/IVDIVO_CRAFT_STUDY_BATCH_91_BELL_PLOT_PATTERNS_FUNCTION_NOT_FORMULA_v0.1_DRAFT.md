# IVDIVO CRAFT STUDY — BATCH 91
## Bell: Plot Patterns · Function, Combination, and Anti-Formula Firewall

Status: DRAFT / OPERATIONAL
Date: 2026-08-18
Primary source: James Scott Bell, *Plot & Structure*, Chapter 12

## Core synthesis
Plot patterns are a repertoire for generation and diagnosis, not templates to retrofit onto a living manuscript.

Bell surveys Quest, Revenge, Love, Change, Adventure, Chase, One Against, One Apart, Power, Death Overhanging, Allegory, and combinations. IVDIVO retains the functional questions while rejecting mandatory act geometry, death-stakes doctrine, and compulsory transformation.

## Pattern router
Before using a pattern ask:
`WHAT HUMAN/PERSONHOOD QUESTION DOES THIS PATTERN ORGANIZE? → WHAT ACTIONS DOES IT GENERATE? → WHAT RELATIONSHIPS DOES IT PRESSURE? → WHAT COST/PAYOFF DOES IT REQUIRE? → DOES IT STRENGTHEN ONE MAIN STORY?`

## IVDIVO adaptations
- **Quest:** pursuit of a specific outcome/answer/role/relationship; not necessarily a missing-object hunt or self-completion arc.
- **Revenge:** may become accountability/redress/restoration; do not manufacture murder for visceral motivation.
- **Love:** two complete agents under mutual pressure; no 'missing half' psychology required.
- **Change:** optional; stable, partial, negative, tactical, relational, or deferred arcs remain valid.
- **Adventure:** sequence needs cumulative consequence; new places alone do not create progression.
- **Chase:** pursuit/avoidance can be legal, epistemic, social, technical, relational, or physical.
- **One Against:** institution/countermodel must retain competent defenders and real benefits; no straw dystopia.
- **One Apart:** isolation must arise from a character-specific strategy/relationship history, not generic antihero styling.
- **Power:** authority/access/control must have explicit limits, consent rules, accountability, and costs.
- **Death Overhanging:** rejected as universal. Substitute meaningful loss/cost appropriate to the book.
- **Allegory:** idea function may organize a story, but persons must remain persons rather than thesis puppets.

## Pattern combination gate
Combining two patterns is allowed only when both pressure the same dominant causal engine. Two patterns that merely coexist create dual-engine bloat.

## Post-money Orbital Youth firewall
Do not translate classic stakes into salary, rent, credits, consumer independence, or hidden economic points. Functional stakes include trust, consent, responsibility, role, qualification, time, privacy, belonging, reputation, mastery, relationship terms, safety, and unique opportunity.

## Conscious personhood firewall
A conscious robot/nonhuman may occupy any plot function but is never reduced to an archetypal service role. Preserve independent want, consent/refusal, relationships, evidence/model, and consequence ownership.

## Failure modes
PATTERN RETROFIT; THREE-ACT NUMEROLOGY; DEATH-STAKES INFLATION; QUEST-AS-TOURISM; REVENGE-MURDER GENERATOR; LOVE-AS-INCOMPLETENESS; CHANGE-AS-MANDATORY LESSON; INSTITUTION-AS-STRAW TYRANNY; ROBOT-AS-PLOT FUNCTION; ALLEGORY-PUPPET; DUAL-PATTERN DUAL ENGINE.

## Writers' Room use
Use pattern cards during idea generation/red-team comparison. Never use them as a compliance checklist during sentence drafting.
