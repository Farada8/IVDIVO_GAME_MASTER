# DR FINAL — MICHAEL BREAULT — NARRATIVE DESIGN: THE CRAFT OF WRITING FOR GAMES

Status: `STRICT_LIFECYCLE_SYNTHESIZED_v1.0`

## Source passport
Michael Breault's *Narrative Design: The Craft of Writing for Games* is a practitioner/teaching text centered on narrative design as an embedded production discipline. Its strongest evidence comes from long professional experience in digital and analog games and teaching assignments derived from production tasks. Its authority is strongest for collaborative narrative production, iteration, player-facing information, asset/pipeline constraints and game-specific writing practice; it is weaker as a source of universal literary theory, psychology, market prediction or prose style.

## High-value claims retained

### 1. Narrative must be embedded upstream
A narrative specialist added only at the end becomes a script doctor repairing damage created by earlier design decisions. Strong narrative integration starts while goals, mechanics, world, character, assets and pipelines are still changeable.

IVDIVO translation:
`NARRATIVE / STORY AUTHORITY MUST ENTER BEFORE DOWNSTREAM ARTIFACTS HARDEN`.

### 2. Plan is a starting model, not the implemented truth
The GDD is explicitly a starting point that changes during implementation. Discipline-specific documents and actual implementation diverge from early plans.

IVDIVO translation:
`PLAN ≠ ACTUAL TEXT ≠ CANON ≠ EVIDENCE STATUS`.

### 3. Every element must justify its user-facing function
Breault repeatedly asks why a mechanic, line, feature or story element exists and what experience it produces.

IVDIVO translation:
`ELEMENT → INTENDED READER/PLAYER EFFECT → ACTUAL EFFECT → KEEP / REVISE / REMOVE`.

### 4. Environmental storytelling can replace explanation
World damage, rebuilt materials, props, notes, layout and sound cues can let the audience infer history rather than receive a lore speech.

IVDIVO translation:
`WORLD FACT → LOAD-BEARING TRACE → POV NOTICE → READER INFERENCE`.

### 5. Iteration is more valuable than worship of the initial idea
The source repeatedly privileges implementation, playtesting, feedback and revision over the presumed brilliance of the original concept.

IVDIVO translation:
`SEED → IMPLEMENT → TEST → FEEDBACK → REVISE → RETEST`.

### 6. Dialogue has real production and attention cost
In games every voiced line carries production cost; unvoiced dialogue still costs player attention and interaction time.

Transfer to IVDIVO audio:
`EXACT LINE → STORY / RELATION / EVIDENCE / PERFORMANCE FUNCTION → PRODUCTION COST → KEEP / COMPRESS / INDIRECT / CUT`.

This does **not** imply that novel dialogue must imitate game text-box brevity.

### 7. Feedback should be integrated early and repeatedly
QA/playtesting ignored until release produces preventable failures. But feedback is evidence of effect, not automatic authority over the fix.

IVDIVO translation:
`READER/QA REPORT → OBSERVED EFFECT → DEFECT LEVEL → REPAIR HYPOTHESES → RETEST`.

### 8. Feature freeze protects quality
Once a system reaches a late stage, adding new features can damage polish and stability.

IVDIVO translation:
`DEVELOPMENT LOCK / FEATURE FREEZE → FIX PROVEN DEFECTS; DO NOT ADD NEW STORY FEATURES WITHOUT NEW EVIDENCE`.

### 9. Collaboration requires explicit handoffs and interdependencies
Game production is a dependency network, not a queue of isolated specialists.

IVDIVO translation:
`ARTIFACT A CHANGE → EXPLICIT DEPENDENCY GRAPH → SELECTIVE INVALIDATION → ONLY AFFECTED DOWNSTREAM RECHECK`.

### 10. The audience is a target model, not an abstraction
The intended audience affects expectations, difficulty, information burden and interaction design.

IVDIVO translation:
`AUDIENCE MODEL → PROMISE / COGNITIVE LOAD / GENRE EXPECTATION`, not `AUDIENCE MODEL → FLATTEN CHARACTER OR CANON`.

## Claims rejected or bounded
- `Story should serve gameplay` is valid for many games, not novels. In prose fiction the story is the primary product.
- Flow challenge/skill theory is useful for game difficulty, not a direct law of narrative tension.
- Bartle/Quantic player taxonomies are game-audience tools, not personality science.
- “Every game has a story” is a broad design metaphor, not a useful universal narratological fact.
- “Dialogue should be crisp and concise” is strongly mediated by game UI/voice production cost; it does not establish a short-sentence style law for novels.
- Hero's Journey assignments and Hero/Shadow framing in syllabi are teaching tools, not mandatory IVDIVO architecture.
- Career/market/platform observations are historical 2020-era industry material and require current verification before operational use.
- Expensive assets making story cheaper than mechanics is a game-production fact, not a prose-writing law.

## Cross-source synthesis

### Breault × Mayer × Seger
All converge on `PLAN = HYPOTHESIS`. Actual implementation/text must drive revision of planning artifacts.

### Breault × Lamott × LaPlante × Swain
Creation and iteration should precede heavy criticism. Tools are strongest after live material exists.

### Breault × Zinsser × Blundell
Audience time is a finite resource. Compression is about function and information cost, not short-sentence density.

### Breault × Chatman × Genette
Environmental or interactive disclosure changes discourse order/access without changing underlying world fact.

### Breault × Ephron × le Carré
A prop, sound or environment cue is a trace. Its interpretation remains provisional until provenance/corroboration supports it.

### Breault × current IVDIVO Book Engine
The new engine's authority registry, dependency graph, selective invalidation, explicit reference classes and release gates are structurally aligned with the real production problem Breault describes: dozens of disciplines change upstream assumptions while narrative must remain coherent.

## Failure modes
1. SCRIPT-DOCTOR NARRATIVE — narrative concerns arrive only after architecture hardens.
2. GDD FOSSIL — old plan treated as truth despite changed implementation.
3. DOCUMENT DRIFT — actual work changes but reference documents remain stale.
4. FEATURE-CREEP AFTER LOCK — new “good ideas” destabilize a release candidate.
5. LORE-DUMP FRICTION — background delivered in speech/text instead of traces and consequences.
6. DIALOGUE-COST BLINDNESS — lines retained without story/relationship/evidence value.
7. FEEDBACK THEATER — collecting feedback without changing the artifact or testing alternatives.
8. COMMITTEE STORY LOSS — collaboration becomes lowest-common-denominator writing or unclear decision ownership.
9. PLAYER/READER EXPERIENCE ABSOLUTISM — audience preference used to erase story truth or originality.
10. GAME-TO-NOVEL LEAKAGE — interactive constraints misapplied as prose laws.
11. GENRE-EXPECTATION RIGIDITY — convention mistaken for mandatory architecture.
12. ASSET-ECONOMICS LEAKAGE — production-cost logic mistaken for narrative-value logic.
13. LATE QA SURPRISE — defect first seen near release despite earlier detectable signals.
14. TEAM-HANDOFF AMNESIA — changed upstream artifact fails to invalidate dependent narrative work.

## IVDIVO standing gates added
- `EMBEDDED NARRATIVE GATE`: story authority participates before downstream structure locks.
- `ACTUAL STATE OVER PLAN GATE`: current text/state wins over obsolete planning assumptions, subject to canon authority.
- `TRACE BEFORE LECTURE GATE`: prefer lived/environmental evidence when it can carry the information honestly.
- `DIALOGUE PRODUCTION VALUE GATE`: exact spoken lines must justify their reader/listener and production cost.
- `FEATURE FREEZE GATE`: after development lock, only evidence-backed repair may reopen affected layers.
- `DEPENDENCY HANDOFF GATE`: every upstream change identifies explicit downstream consumers.
- `AUDIENCE EXPERIENCE GATE`: intended audience shapes promise/load, not canon truth.
- `ITERATION EVIDENCE GATE`: feedback changes a hypothesis only through tested revision, not prestige.

## Manuscript / project validation

### LESSON ZERO Book One
No new defect strong enough to reopen development lock. Breault instead strengthens the existing rule:
`NO NEW EVIDENCE → NO NEW GLOBAL REWRITE`.

### ROOM 917 / audio
High relevance. Spoken dialogue has literal production cost and downstream voice/mix dependencies. Lines should be isolated when acoustic/performance function differs, but compression must not destroy clue provenance, consent or relationship state.

### Orbital Youth
Use environmental/social traces to show future normality rather than exposition. Do not restore present-day money/status defaults merely to give characters familiar stakes.

### B07 / nonhuman personhood
Environmental/system traces may trigger a personhood hypothesis; classification is not proof. After independent personhood threshold, the entity must own consent/refusal and a consequential choice.

### IVDIVO Book Engine
Strong validation of:
- explicit artifact authority;
- dependency graph;
- selective invalidation;
- versioned planning vs actual-state distinction;
- staged feature freeze / release gates;
- feedback evidence with regression testing.

## Final synthesis
Breault is most valuable to IVDIVO as a production-systems source. Its deepest transfer is not a game-writing recipe but a governance principle: narrative quality is downstream of collaboration, state accuracy, dependency management, audience-time discipline and iterative validation. Story problems become expensive when discovered after the rest of the system has already committed to them.
