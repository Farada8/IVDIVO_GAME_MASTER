## 1. Production synthesis
Oatley's highest-value contribution is the idea that fiction works by letting the reader construct a selective working model of people, goals, causes and possible worlds. IVDIVO uses that idea as a craft diagnostic, not as a literal theory of brain function.

### Gate B185-G01 — Simulation Model
`WORLD DATABASE → SELECTED STORY MODEL → POV ACCESS → READER INFERENCE`.

A book is not required to display the entire world database. It must provide the subset required for the reader to model the current causal problem.

### Gate B185-G02 — Goal Adoption Before Sympathy Manipulation
Reader attachment may arise because a goal/plan/concern is legible and worth simulating.

Do not automatically add:
- trauma;
- illness;
- humiliation;
- victimhood;
- a child/animal rescue;
- sentimental suffering
merely to force sympathy.

### Gate B185-G03 — Concern/Appraisal/Emotion Causality
`CONCERN → EVENT → APPRAISAL → EMOTION → TACTIC / CHOICE`.

If an emotion is named but does not alter attention, relationship, strategy, decision or later behavior, test whether it is decorative.

### Gate B185-G04 — Character Feeling ≠ Reader Feeling
`CHARACTER FEELS X ≠ READER FEELS X`.

Reader effect depends on context, prior attachment, uncertainty, goal adoption, contrast, discourse timing and individual reader differences. Validate by beta/audio evidence where important.

### Gate B185-G05 — Behavior–Motive Separation
`OBSERVED BEHAVIOR → MOTIVE MODEL A / B / C → DISCRIMINATING EVIDENCE → UPDATE`.

Never silently rewrite `he did X` into `therefore he wanted Y`.

### Gate B185-G06 — Model Update
A load-bearing new action should normally do at least one:
- reinforce a reader model;
- complicate it;
- expose a contradiction;
- revise it;
- force a new question.

Repeated behavior that adds no new inference risks becoming character wallpaper.

### Gate B185-G07 — Prediction / Reason / Consent Firewall
`PREDICTED BEHAVIOR ≠ REASON ≠ CONSENT`.

A system may correctly predict that a person or robot will yield, help, wait or comply while remaining wrong about why and having no right to treat that predicted behavior as available resource.

### Gate B185-G08 — Interpretation Plurality / Fact Stability
Multiple reader interpretations may legitimately coexist around motive, theme or moral meaning.

But load-bearing facts remain stable:
- who acted;
- what trace exists;
- source lineage;
- timestamp/order;
- measurement;
- consent status;
- evidence status.

### Gate B185-G09 — Creative Variant Is Not Canon
`EXERCISE / WHAT-IF / PLAY VARIANT → CHARACTER/WORLD/CAUSAL TEST → KEEP / BANK / DISCARD`.

Creative exploration is laboratory work. It does not become canon merely because it produced a vivid scene.

### Gate B185-G10 — Identification Without Humanization
A conscious robot/nonhuman person can be compelling because the reader can model its goal, plan, constraints and choices.

Do not make personhood depend on:
- human face/body;
- human slang;
- human trauma;
- romance;
- anthropomorphic emotional display.

### Gate B185-G11 — Feedback as Reader-Model Data
`READER REPORT → WHAT MODEL/EFFECT FORMED? → REPEATABILITY → DEFECT LEVEL → REPAIR HYPOTHESES → TEST`.

A beta reader is authoritative about their own experience, not automatically about the correct repair or world canon.

### Gate B185-G12 — Science Firewall
`CRAFT MODEL ≠ CURRENT EMPIRICAL CONSENSUS ≠ IVDIVO SCIENCE / ONTOLOGY`.

Oatley's 2011 neuroscience, mirror-neuron, evolutionary and fiction-effects claims require updated scientific verification before factual use.

## 2. Cross-source synthesis
### Oatley × Herman
Oatley explains reader simulation of goals/emotions; Herman strengthens person-level reasons, ecological context and distributed cognition.

### Oatley × Zunshine
Oatley: readers infer minds.  
Zunshine: every inference must retain source/metarepresentational status.

Result:
`BEHAVIOR → READER MIND MODEL`, never `BEHAVIOR → MIND FACT`.

### Oatley × Storr
Both use prediction/model-building language. Retain prediction as diagnostic. Reject Storr-style universal sacred-flaw/control/status requirements and any science overclaim.

### Oatley × Card / Corbett / McKee Character
Actions under pressure reveal useful evidence, but relationship-specific self and competing values prevent one-action essentialism.

### Oatley × Genette / Chatman
Reader-model formation is downstream of discourse architecture. Order, duration, frequency, focalization and voice must be diagnosed before assuming the story fact itself is wrong.

### Oatley × Ephron / le Carré / Chudnoff
In mystery, mental simulation is never proof. Source lineage, alternative explanations and discriminating evidence remain authoritative.

## 4. Science status — 2026
Modern meta-analytic/review literature supports small and heterogeneous relations/effects between fiction reading and outcomes such as empathy/mentalizing; effect sizes are not large and mechanisms/generalization remain contested.

Production consequence: **do not promise that reading or writing fiction automatically improves empathy, moral character or social cognition.**

## 5. Anti-choppiness consequence
Reader simulation requires a coherent evolving model. Repeated fragment breaks can reset actor/focus/relation without buying an effect.

Standing routing:
`SCENE/MODE → POV/KNOWLEDGE → MODEL CONTINUITY → PARAGRAPH JOB/TOPIC CHAIN → READER-STATE → SENTENCE RHYTHM/CONTRAST → WORDING`.

`IDENTIFICATION / EMOTION / SUSPENSE ≠ SHORT-SENTENCE DENSITY`.
