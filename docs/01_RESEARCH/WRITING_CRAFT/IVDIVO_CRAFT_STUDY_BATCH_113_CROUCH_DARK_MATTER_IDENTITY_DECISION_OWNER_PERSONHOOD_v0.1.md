Status: ACTIVE SYNTHESIS

## WHY THIS BATCH EXISTS
This batch extracts mechanisms from the completed strict read of *Dark Matter* without importing its speculative physics or thriller cadence into IVDIVO canon.

## PRIMARY MECHANISMS

### 1. Identity Evidence Web
`IDENTITY CLAIM → MULTIPLE INDEPENDENT TRACES → RELATION-SPECIFIC BEHAVIOR → CONTRADICTIONS → CONFIDENCE UPDATE`

A person is not proven by one memory, one body, one mannerism or one private phrase.

### 2. Competing-Model Suspense
`OBSERVATION → MODEL A/B/C → DISCRIMINATING EVIDENCE → MODEL UPDATE → ACTION`

Suspense can come from uncertainty about which causal model explains the facts, not only from physical threat.

### 3. Decision-Owner Legitimacy
`FAIRNESS BETWEEN CLAIMANTS ≠ LEGITIMACY WHEN THE AFFECTED PERSON IS EXCLUDED`

Before evaluating procedural fairness, identify who legitimately owns the decision.

### 4. Love Is Not Ownership
`LOVE / HISTORY / SACRIFICE ≠ CONSENT / OWNERSHIP / PROOF`

Relationship intensity can change decisions but cannot replace present agency.

### 5. Path-Dependent Asymmetry
`SIMILAR AGENTS → UNIQUE RECENT EXPERIENCE → PRIVATE INFORMATION → NEW UNPREDICTABLE OPTION`

Useful when highly competent agents can otherwise predict each other.

### 6. Counterfactual Desire
The road not taken becomes dramatically useful only when it changes present behavior, temptation, comparison, resentment, values or choice.

### 7. Affected-Agent Reframing
`PROTAGONIST PLAN → AFFECTED AGENT REFRAMES THE PROBLEM → THEIR CHOICE CHANGES END STATE`

A strong climax can transfer final agency away from the protagonist without weakening the protagonist's arc.

## NEW PRODUCTION GATES

### B113-G01 — Identity Multi-Evidence Gate
No synthetic/nonhuman identity conclusion from a single tell.

### B113-G02 — Behavior/Motive Separation
Observed behavior updates a motive model; it does not prove motive.

### B113-G03 — Decision Owner First
Before designing a vote, lottery, allocation, rescue or continuity protocol, name the affected person's decision rights.

### B113-G04 — Consent Cannot Be Inherited
Past consent, shared history or prior relationship status does not automatically authorize a present choice.

### B113-G05 — Claimant Symmetry Is Secondary
Equal claims among outsiders do not outrank the subject's agency.

### B113-G06 — Path-Dependent Strategy
If equally smart actors are stuck in mutual prediction, seek legitimate asymmetry from recent experience/evidence rather than making one actor stupid.

### B113-G07 — Counterfactual Must Pay
An alternate path/world only earns space if it changes action, model, relationship, evidence, consent or future options.

### B113-G08 — Relationship Is Evidence Context, Not Proof
Trust/intimacy can change disclosure and action; it cannot upgrade physical/archive evidence.

### B113-G09 — Local Closure / Open Ontology
Resolve the book's operational/personhood problem without forcing a final metaphysical answer.

### B113-G10 — Fragment Style Firewall
Do not import thriller fragments as a default suspense rhythm.

## FAILURE MODES
- identity-by-single-tell
- behavior-to-motive leap
- procedural fairness among wrong decision owners
- relationship-as-prize
- competence-as-moral-legitimacy
- alternate-world tourism
- ontology leak from fictional quantum premise
- claimant symmetry replacing consent
- thriller fragment monoculture
- counterfactual theme repetition without present consequence

## IVDIVO MASTER RULE
When a story involves contested identity, synthetic persons, distributed life, copies, branches or continuity claims:

`WHO IS THIS? → WHAT EVIDENCE SUPPORTS IT? → WHO IS INTERPRETING THAT EVIDENCE? → WHAT ALTERNATIVES REMAIN? → WHO OWNS THE AFFECTED DECISION? → WHAT CAN THEY REFUSE? → WHAT CHOICE CHANGES THE WORLD?`
