## 5. Main mechanisms extracted provisionally

1. **Setting as possibility system**
   `WORLD RULE → OPTIONS / LIMITS → CHOICE → CONSEQUENCE`.

2. **Knowable novel world**
   Vast saga scale does not require encyclopedic novel scope.

3. **Research must pay into design**
   Research counts as story development when it changes event/character/world choices.

4. **Alternative generation before commitment**
   Major beat choices benefit from multiple plausible alternatives before selection.

5. **Genre as evolving expectation field**
   Conventions must adapt when society changes; contemporary wage/status assumptions cannot be back-projected into post-money orbital YA.

6. **Character through consequential choice**
   Useful, but corrected against mandatory catastrophe, hidden opposite self and universal deep arc.

7. **Structure ↔ character co-design**
   A changed operative character model must alter event logic, not only biography/dialogue.

8. **Theme by consequence / Counter-Idea strength**
   Ethical meaning must survive removal of explanatory speeches; opposition needs real evidence and benefit.

9. **Expectation/result gap**
   `EXPECTATION → ACTION → ACTUAL RESPONSE → MODEL UPDATE → STRATEGY CHANGE`.

10. **No gap monoculture**
    Expected success/competence/cooperation may still change state and must not be mechanically sabotaged.

11. **Reaction specificity**
    Familiar events can remain fresh through person-specific interpretation and choice.

12. **Inciting incident by function, not spectacle**
    Central imbalance/problem can be quiet; fixed screenwriting percentages are rejected as novel law.
