## Why this batch exists

This batch records only the net-new synthesis and production consequences; it does not repeat previous notes.

## New synthesis 1 — CHARACTER IS A CAUSAL NETWORK, NOT A PROFILE
A useful character model is not:
`traits + age + appearance + wound + catchphrase`.

Use:
`ACTION UNDER CONDITIONS + MOTIVE + PAST EFFECT + RELATIONSHIP NETWORK + HABITS + CAPABILITIES + PREFERENCES + BODY CONSEQUENCES`.

The strongest parts are the ones that change current choice or reader expectation.

### Production test
For a major character, remove each profile fact mentally. If its removal changes no scene, relationship, expectation or decision, it is background inventory, not active characterization.

## New synthesis 2 — RELATIONSHIP-SPECIFIC SELF
A stable person can behave differently without inconsistency because different relationships activate different histories, risks and roles.

For major IVDIVO characters, maintain a compact matrix:
- with closest friend;
- with family;
- with authority/mentor;
- with rival;
- with intimate/romantic interest;
- with stranger/public;
- with conscious robotic person;
- when alone.

Do not solve voice differentiation with slang alone. Change what the person wants from the interaction, what they permit themselves to say, syntax/formality, humor, avoidance, status behavior and disclosure.

## New synthesis 3 — READER CONTRACT AS THREE LIVE QUESTIONS
Every substantial opening and every major scene transition should survive:
1. **CARE:** why does this matter to the reader now?
2. **BELIEVE:** what causal evidence makes the behavior/world event credible?
3. **UNDERSTAND:** is the intended uncertainty clear even when the answer is withheld?

This is especially useful for IVDIVO because dense future-world material often fails not from complexity itself but from unclear uncertainty: the reader cannot tell what is unknown to characters versus merely unexplained by the author.

## New synthesis 4 — CLARITY OF MYSTERY ≠ ANSWER
For suspense/mystery:
`KNOWN FACT → CLEAR QUESTION → CONTROLLED UNKNOWN → EVIDENCE CHANGE → REVISED QUESTION`.

Bad ambiguity:
- unclear speaker;
- unclear source of claim;
- unclear whether a term is common knowledge;
- unclear whether the hero actually knows something the narration is hiding.

Good ambiguity:
- reader knows exactly what fact is missing and why characters cannot yet know it.

## New synthesis 5 — CHARACTER HIERARCHY IS PROMISE MANAGEMENT
A memorable minor character can accidentally look important enough to promise an arc. Therefore page-time, POV, secrets, backstory, wounds, humor and unique competence must be allocated intentionally.

### Ensemble rule
`SAGA EQUALITY ≠ BOOK-LEVEL EQUALITY`.
One book may center Ethan; another Maya; another a new cadet or robot. The other established characters remain real but do not each require equal POV or climax functions.

## New synthesis 6 — CHOSEN COST OUTPERFORMS PAIN INFLATION
When the narrative wants to reveal values, a chosen cost is usually stronger than simply increasing injury.

Use:
`TWO GOODS / VALUES → NO COST-FREE OPTION → VOLUNTARY SACRIFICE → CONSEQUENCE`.

Do not default to death, torture or catastrophe to make a scene important.

## New synthesis 7 — BODY DETAIL IS ONLY ACTIVE WHEN CAUSAL
Especially important for future-world writing:
`BODY FEATURE / CONDITION → SENSATION OR CAPABILITY → ACTION OPTION → SOCIAL/RISK CONSEQUENCE → DECISION`.

This is compatible with current orbital canon firewall: ordinary orbital civilization does not possess routine human consciousness/soul transfer. Future embodiment lines remain later/specialized and cannot be smuggled into Book Two as normal infrastructure.

## New synthesis 8 — POV DEPTH SHOULD BREATHE
Use depth as a scene control:
- cinematic: action geography, public behavior, ambiguity, recovery;
- light interior: efficient interpretation and attitude;
- deep interior: high-stakes subjective pressure, shame, fear, desire, altered perception.

Failure mode: every paragraph equally deep creates interpretive over-explanation; every paragraph cinematic wastes prose interiority.

## IVDIVO gates activated by this batch

### B37-G01 — CHARACTER NETWORK
No primary/secondary-major character without at least two meaningful relationships outside the immediate A-story function.

### B37-G02 — RELATIONSHIP REGISTER
Voice sheet must describe how register changes by relationship, not only vocabulary.

### B37-G03 — READER CONTRACT
Opening and major transitions answer CARE / BELIEVE / UNDERSTAND.

### B37-G04 — CLEAR UNKNOWN
Mystery may hide answer, not the existence/ownership of the question.

### B37-G05 — ATTENTION BUDGET
Named character tier declared before POV/backstory expansion.

### B37-G06 — MEMORABLE MINOR FIREWALL
Eccentricity does not automatically earn subplot or POV.

### B37-G07 — CHOSEN COST BEFORE PAIN
Before increasing physical harm, test whether a value-conflict cost creates stronger character evidence.

### B37-G08 — PLAUSIBILITY PREP
Important extraordinary choice receives preparation proportional to improbability.

### B37-G09 — NO REAL-LIFE DEFENSE
`It happened in reality` never replaces causal setup in fiction.

### B37-G10 — FAIR POV
No hiding decisive known information solely to preserve twist.

### B37-G11 — POV DEPTH FUNCTION
Depth change must serve scene function, not stylistic randomness.

### B37-G12 — BODY CAUSALITY
Physical description either changes action/perception/social meaning or is compressed.

### B37-G13 — NO IDEA SERVANT
Technical/philosophical character has independent wants, attachments and consequences.

### B37-G14 — CURRENT EVIDENCE OVERRIDE
Dated craft generalization loses to current canon, current psychology and actual beta-reader evidence.
