## 1. Why this source matters
Herman is most useful to IVDIVO as an epistemic correction to strong causal craft schools. Truby/McKee help make narrative causality legible; Herman helps prevent characters, investigators and authors from mistaking a coherent causal story for verified truth.

Central distinction:

`WORLDING THE STORY = reader constructs a world from cues`

`STORYING THE WORLD = characters/institutions use stories to interpret experience and act`

Both are causal, but neither automatically establishes ontology.

## 2. Master gate

`OBSERVED ACTION / TRACE → POSSIBLE REASONS → SOURCE + PERSPECTIVE → CONTEXT/HISTORY → PROVISIONAL MODEL → ALTERNATIVE → NEW EVIDENCE / TEST → STATUS UPDATE → ACTION`.

Do not skip from observed behavior to motive.
Do not skip from prediction to consent.
Do not skip from coherent explanation to world fact.

## 3. New production gates

### B64-G01 — BEHAVIOR ≠ MOTIVE
Observed action is evidence about possible reasons, not direct access to reason.

### B64-G02 — PREDICTION / CONSENT FIREWALL
`PREDICTED ACTION ≠ WANTED ACTION ≠ CONSENTED ACTION ≠ SYSTEM RIGHT TO EXPLOIT ACTION`.

### B64-G03 — MULTICAUSAL PERSON
For major behavior, test relationship, environment, history, project, belief, immediate desire and social position before accepting one neat cause.

### B64-G04 — WORLDING / STORYING SEPARATION
Store separately:
- reader-supported fact;
- source claim;
- character/institution interpretation;
- causal story derived from that interpretation.

### B64-G05 — CAUSALITY FALSIFIABILITY
For important inferred causal link ask: `what evidence could show this explanation is wrong?` If none, the theory is overgenerated.

### B64-G06 — BOUNDED EXPLANATORY SUFFICIENCY
Do not require total ontology to permit local action. Specify the minimum evidence needed for the next bounded decision.

### B64-G07 — LOCAL / GLOBAL CAUSALITY SPLIT
A book may prove a local mechanism while preserving global origin as unknown.

### B64-G08 — CATEGORY REVISION
When a person/nonhuman violates a category, test whether the category is wrong before declaring the person defective.

### B64-G09 — PERSPECTIVE MATRIX
`possible viewpoints → chosen viewpoint → omitted viewpoint → information effect → later repair cost`.

### B64-G10 — FAIR OMISSION
Never hide information known by the actual focal consciousness solely for surprise.

### B64-G11 — DISTRIBUTED INTELLIGENCE
Allocate evidence, interpretation, tools, lived experience, counterargument and final authority to different agents when appropriate.

### B64-G12 — POSITIONING IN DIALOGUE
Track the social role each utterance assigns: expert, child, outsider, partner, threat, operator, witness, dependent, authority.

### B64-G13 — MULTIMODAL TALK
Dialogue revision includes silence, gesture, gaze, body, space, artifacts and status—not just words.

### B64-G14 — EMOTION STATUS
Separate felt state / display / cultural term / another person's inference / narrator label.

### B64-G15 — RETELLING PROVENANCE
`event → teller → version/purpose → omission/emphasis → recipient → changed model → action`.

### B64-G16 — NARRATIVE CHUNK SCOPE
A book/case may close a bounded problem while the saga remains open. Do not confuse local closure with cosmological explanation.

### B64-G17 — TEMPORAL ACTION MODEL
Use duration/order/frequency to make reasons and consequences legible, not to demonstrate technique.

### B64-G18 — RETROSPECTIVE COHERENCE FIREWALL
A late theory that makes every detail fit requires independent support before being promoted to fact.

### B64-G19 — TRANSFORMATION ARCHITECTURE
For any body/state transformation test what changes/persists, sensorium, affordances, social/risk conditions and reversibility. Historical transformation tropes do not authorize consciousness transfer in IVDIVO.

### B64-G20 — APPRENTICESHIP BOTTLENECK
Knowledge requiring years of embodied practice cannot be mass-produced merely because infrastructure/AI is abundant.

### B64-G21 — PLACE AS COGNITIVE SCAFFOLD
Use maps/interfaces/signage/architecture/embodiment/local expertise to distribute reasoning into the environment.

### B64-G22 — STORY-AS-CAUSE
A false story can be causally important if people act from it. Show consequence without upgrading its truth status.

## 4. Failure modes to scan
- behavior-as-motive;
- algorithmic prediction laundering into consent;
- one-cause psychology;
- category-as-person;
- transparent archive;
- omniscient protagonist;
- fake POV withholding;
- dialogue-as-transcript;
- emotion-word literalism;
- causal `after therefore because` leap;
- theory that explains all possible outcomes;
- facts too disconnected to support action;
- transformation trope laundering into ontology;
- embodied change without changed affordances;
- lore insert without present effect;
- school teaching treated as instant database transfer;
- historical cognitive theory treated as current settled science.

## 5. Cross-school synthesis

### With Truby / McKee
Keep causal spine, scene consequence and choice. Add `evidence status` for causal claims made by characters/institutions.

### With Zunshine
Combine source-monitoring with reason-modeling. The resulting mystery ledger is stronger than clue lists alone.

### With Kress / Card / Corbett
Wants, tactics and contradiction remain useful, but they are operational models—not total explanations of a person.

### With Wolf
Wolf governs project-level canon provenance; Herman governs in-world/reader model provenance.

### With LaPlante / Ribó
Organic form and soft causality remain legitimate, but a quiet scene must still alter emotional, relational, epistemic or action readiness.

## 6. IVDIVO applications

### Conscious robots
A robot may be highly predictable and still have reasons unknown to humans. Do not equate technical model accuracy with right to decide for the robot. Give robots independent projects, relationships, embodiment and refusal.

### Specialist consciousness schools
Use lineage, practice, cases, reproducible effects and competing interpretations. Keep school narrative separate from objective ontology.

### Mystery / metaphysics
Use a proof ceiling. Resolve local operational question before global metaphysics. A strange temporal/psychic correlation does not prove a dimensional mechanism.

### Orbital Youth
Let tools, environments, other people and robots carry parts of the solution. No teenager needs to become an implausible polymath just to preserve protagonist centrality.

### Post-money society
The reader's default Earth causal model can reintroduce money if ordinary coordination is not shown. Build the alternate norm through causal routine and decisions, not exposition.

## 7. Style / reader effect
Herman reinforces a useful paradox: narrative coherence aids understanding but can also create false confidence. Therefore prose should be causally clear while epistemic claims remain properly labeled.

Production maxim:

**MAKE THE STORY LEGIBLE; KEEP THE MODEL REVERSIBLE UNTIL THE EVIDENCE EARNS CERTAINTY.**
