## CONTINUOUS FULL READ LOG
### Foreword — COMPLETE
Swain frames the book explicitly as a toolbox for fiction creation rather than literary-quality law. He emphasizes devices that have proven useful in commercial storytelling and says no writer uses all techniques.

### Chapter 1 — FICTION AND YOU — STRICT COMPLETE
Core architecture introduced:
1. group words into motivation-reaction units;
2. group M/R units into scenes and sequels;
3. group scenes/sequels into story pattern;
4. create characters that give story life.

Eight traps:
1. unrealistic view;
2. hunt for magic secrets;
3. try to learn the hard way;
4. refuse to follow feeling;
5. attempt to write by rules;
6. do not want to be wrong;
7. bow down to the objective;
8. fail to master technique.

Most valuable craft distinction: Swain separates creation from criticism. Feeling/excitement drives the generative state; principles and rules-of-thumb are used later in reappraisal. He explicitly describes rules as checklist rather than blueprint.

Chapter 1 closes by arguing that skill is learned through writing live stories, accepting error, examining process mistakes, and gradually building a personal tool kit. Direct transition to Ch2 verified.

### Chapter 2 — THE WORDS YOU WRITE — STARTED
Strict read completed through opening framework:
- star writers often work largely by ear;
- clarity, accuracy and specificity matter more than grammatical prestige;
- words can help, hinder or clutter;
- three immediate goals: choose right words, make copy vivid, keep meaning clear;
- three working jobs introduced: Selection / Arrangement / Description.

## PROVISIONAL MECHANISMS

### S09-M01 — CREATION / CRITIQUE SEPARATION
`GENERATIVE FEELING / DISCOVERY -> LIVE MATERIAL -> REAPPRAISAL -> PROCESS DIAGNOSIS -> TOOL -> REVISION`.
Do not run all IVDIVO production gates simultaneously while a scene is being discovered.

### S09-M02 — RULES AS CHECKLIST, NOT BLUEPRINT
Craft principles diagnose and expand options. They do not outrank current canon, character truth, causality, personhood or reader evidence.

### S09-M03 — FEELING -> TECHNIQUE
Feeling identifies what the writer is trying to transmit; technique shapes the transmission.
IVDIVO firewall: subjective conviction is not evidence about world ontology, science, motive or another person's consent.

### S09-M04 — ERROR-BASED LEARNING LOOP
`WRITE -> OBSERVE FAILURE / READER EFFECT -> LOCATE PROCESS GAP -> APPLY TOOL -> RETEST`.
This converges with current Writers' Room diagnostic routing.

### S09-M05 — PROCESS, NOT MAGIC SECRET
Swain's value is decomposition of complex writing effects into learnable operations. Reject any later conversion of those operations into deterministic formulas.

## CROSS-SOURCE NOTES
- **Lamott / LaPlante:** strong convergence on separating generation from criticism/revision.
- **Bell:** Bell inherits Swain's practical-tool orientation; Swain himself is less formula-absolutist in Ch1 than some later Bell systems.
- **Bernays/Painter / LaPlante:** correct Swain's sweeping dismissal of exercises. Targeted exercises can work as controlled diagnostic/generative probes.
- **Zinsser / Francine Prose:** likely convergence with Ch2 on functional wording, clarity, specificity and reader experience; full comparison waits for Ch2 completion.
- **Herman / Zunshine:** provide necessary epistemic correction: feeling and plausible causal story are not verified cause, motive, consent or ontology.
