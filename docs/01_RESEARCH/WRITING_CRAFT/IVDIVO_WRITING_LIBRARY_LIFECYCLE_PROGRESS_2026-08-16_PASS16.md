## 3. Mechanisms extracted this pass

- motivation must be legible in the culture of the storyworld;
- unusual motivation requires proportionally more causal/backstory support;
- conflicting good values produce stronger internal conflict than arbitrary contradiction;
- frustration is most useful when it reveals character-specific response and changes strategy;
- breaking points require accumulated pressure + prior restraint + consequential action;
- emotion must change behavior / relationship / interpretation, not merely decorate prose;
- body cues should be character/environment specific;
- POV distance is a continuum and uncontrolled close↔distant oscillation creates reader vertigo;
- filter words are distance controls, not universal errors;
- sequel design must distinguish stable core traits from earned changes and avoid series reset;
- critic/revision cognition should not dominate generative drafting too early.
