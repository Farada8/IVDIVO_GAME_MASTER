## 2. CORE MECHANISM — STRUCTURAL TRANSITION

Seger’s useful kernel is not page arithmetic. It is state transition.

IVDIVO form:

`PRIOR STATE → EVENT / REVELATION / DECISION → GOAL / OPTION / RELATION / EVIDENCE / AUTHORITY / RESPONSIBILITY CHANGE → NEW GOVERNING PROBLEM OR STRATEGY`

A structural turn is valid when the story after it cannot honestly proceed under exactly the same operating assumptions as before it.

### TURNING POINT VALIDITY TEST

A candidate turn must change at least one load-bearing dimension:
- objective;
- available options;
- evidence status;
- relationship terms;
- authority/access;
- responsibility/obligation;
- strategy/model;
- future cost or option space.

A louder event that changes none of these is spectacle, not a structural turn.

## 3. MIDPOINT — FUNCTION, NOT MANDATORY TWIST

Seger’s SHANE example is useful because its midpoint raises the danger and focus without necessarily changing the whole direction of Act Two.

IVDIVO synthesis:

`MIDPOINT = MATERIAL RE-WEIGHTING OF THE STORY MODEL`

Possible midpoint functions:
- evidence confidence changes;
- a useful system reveals a hidden cost;
- responsibility expands;
- relationship terms change;
- a countermodel becomes credible;
- an option disappears or becomes available;
- a successful test makes the next decision harder;
- the same objective now has a different meaning or price.

Rejected universalizations:
- mandatory false victory / false defeat;
- mandatory twist;
- mandatory catastrophe;
- mandatory midpoint percentage.

## 4. SECOND TRANSITION / ENDGAME PRESSURE

Seger emphasizes urgency entering the final movement and sometimes uses ticking clocks.

IVDIVO keeps the function and rejects the mandatory device.

`NEW EVIDENCE / SUCCESS / FAILURE / AUTHORIZATION / RELATION CHANGE → OLD STRATEGY EXPIRES → FINAL PROBLEM BECOMES BOUNDED → DECISIVE ACTION BECOMES POSSIBLE`

Urgency may come from:
- evidence becoming actionable;
- consent being granted or withdrawn;
- a decision window becoming ethically or technically bounded;
- new authority/responsibility;
- a relationship becoming irreversible;
- a physical constraint;
- a known opportunity closing.

It does not require a bomb, deadline, death threat, chase, or artificial catastrophe.

## 5. TWO-PART TRANSITION

Useful abstract form:

`OLD MODEL / STRATEGY FAILS OR EXPIRES → SHORT PROCESSING GAP → NEW STIMULUS / EVIDENCE / MODEL → NEW DIRECTION`

Seger’s film-specific proximity advice is treated as medium-dependent, not a novel law. In prose the relevant test is reader-state continuity: the reader must not lose the governing question between the two halves.

## 6. MOMENTUM — IMPORTANT CORRECTION

A recurring failure in story development is equating momentum with physical activity.

IVDIVO definition:

`MOMENTUM = CAUSAL STATE CHANGE + PRESSURE ON THE NEXT OPTION`

Therefore:
- action density is not momentum;
- short-sentence density is not momentum;
- a quiet evidence turn may generate more momentum than a chase;
- a real success may generate momentum by increasing trust, access, responsibility, or cost.

Strong YA progression:

`COMPETENT ACTION → REAL SUCCESS → MORE TRUST / ACCESS / RESPONSIBILITY → HARDER NEXT CHOICE`
