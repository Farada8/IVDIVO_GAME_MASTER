## Ch4 — Focal point

Operational extraction:

`BEFORE STATE → FOCAL POINT X → AFTER STATE / MOMENTUM`

The focal point is the scene's converging/turning moment. It is not required to be a climax, twist, epiphany, disaster, or loud line. In IVDIVO it may be an evidence-status change, consent change, relationship boundary, authority/access change, model revision, or decision.

## Ch5 — Pulse

Operational extraction:

`BOOK PULSE → SCENE-SPECIFIC PULSE → EVENT SUFFICIENT TO EXPRESS IT → CHANGED STATE`

Pulse is persistent underlying pressure: need, desire, question, fear, obligation, unresolved relationship, or model. It is not synonymous with overt conflict. Procedural or quiet scenes may have strong pulse if a live question or responsibility remains active.

## Ch6 — Tension

Operational extraction:

`LIVE QUESTION / PULSE → TENSION SOURCE → MAINTENANCE / ESCALATION → RELEASE MODE → CHANGED STATE → DOWNSTREAM CONSEQUENCE`

Important limits:
- tension is not equivalent to violence or confrontation;
- tension can be entirely anticipatory/internal;
- tension can remain unresolved into the next scene;
- release may be reconciliation, deferral, suppression, insight, confrontation, or a beneficial surprise;
- suspense does not imply short-sentence density.

## Ch7 — Negotiation

Operational extraction:

`AGENT A WANT / BOUNDARY ↔ AGENT B WANT / BOUNDARY → TACTICS → REFRAME / CONCESSION / REFUSAL → NEW TERMS OR COLLAPSE → CONSEQUENCE`

Negotiation is a more flexible frame than conflict. It allows asymmetric power, subtext, refusal, changed terms, and agency without requiring a fight. A weaker agent may seize decision ownership by reframing what is actually being decided.

## Cross-source synthesis

Scofield strengthens and corrects several stricter schools:

- Bell/Swain/Bickham scene pressure remains useful, but `disaster` is not mandatory.
- Rosenfeld/LaPlante support quieter causal modes and changed-state logic.
- Baxter supports subtext/non-listening; Scofield adds negotiation and pulse as structural engines.
- Zunshine/Herman provenance rules remain above relational interpretation: trust or emotion does not upgrade evidence.
- Le Guin/Prose/Lodge/Zinsser remain controlling for prose rhythm: analytical beats are not sentence or paragraph boundaries.

## IVDIVO implications

### ONE MAIN STORY
Different scenes may use action, tension, negotiation, reflection, summary, or embedded-scene modes while still serving one causal engine. Mode variety is not subplot sprawl.

### Mystery / evidence
`RELATIONSHIP STATE ≠ EVIDENCE STATUS`. Trust, attraction, authority, fear, or confidence may change investigative action but do not themselves corroborate a claim.

### Conscious nonhuman personhood
After personhood threshold, humans may not negotiate solely among themselves about the entity. The entity must retain its own want/model, consent/refusal, and decision that changes human option space.

### YA / institutions
Power asymmetry is not the same as lack of agency. A young character can negotiate by reframing terms, documenting evidence, refusing, delaying, appealing, or accepting bounded responsibility.

### Prose / choppiness
Beats, tactics, and action-response units are analytical units, not typographic units. Do not render them mechanically as one-line paragraphs. Preserve paragraph-scale perceptual/decision units.
