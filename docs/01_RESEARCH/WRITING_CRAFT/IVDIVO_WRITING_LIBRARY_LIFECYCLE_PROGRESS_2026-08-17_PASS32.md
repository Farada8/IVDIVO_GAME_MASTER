## 3. MAIN MECHANISMS ADDED

1. **Human Value Before Spectacle** — physical action must change a human/relational/civic state.
2. **Character-Specific Reaction** — identical pressure should produce different attention, tactics and costs across major characters.
3. **Bounded-Choice Convergence** — remove easy tools/access/certainty, then force an earned decision rather than merely increasing danger.
4. **No Obvious Off-Switch** — avoid early Achilles-heel telemetry.
5. **Resolution Ownership** — coincidence may start trouble but cannot perform the decisive solution.
6. **Excitement Modulation** — constant maximum intensity produces adaptation.
7. **Convention Function Test** — preserve function, reinvent execution.
8. **Causal Dimensionality** — contradictory traits count only when they recur coherently and change action/relationship.
9. **Competent Countermodel** — courage, dignity, skill, patience, loyalty and real evidence can strengthen opposition without villain caricature.
10. **Asymmetric Leverage** — status, information, legitimacy, access, institutional authority, social trust, technical capacity and time can create pressure without mortality inflation.
11. **Specific Vulnerability** — ask what exact opposing tactic bypasses the protagonist's normal competence rather than assigning a generic flaw.
12. **Power Limits Generate Strategy** — unrestricted capability creates “why not just…” holes; real limits generate ingenuity.
13. **Subtext Leverage** — calm speech can carry more pressure than shouting when power and consequence are already legible.
