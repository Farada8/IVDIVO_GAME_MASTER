# IVDIVO CRAFT STUDY — BATCH 209
## K.M. Weiland — Character Arcs: Model, Want, Pressure, Behaviour, Proof

**Status:** DRAFT / SOURCE FULLY READ  
**Date:** 2026-08-22  
**Source:** *Creating Character Arcs Workbook* (2017)

## Why this source matters
Weiland provides a highly explicit character-arc workbook with five trajectory families: Positive Change, Flat, Disillusionment, Fall and Corruption. Its strength is forcing a writer to connect internal model, external goal, pressure, tactics and observable behavioural proof. Its weakness is over-promoting planning vocabulary (`Lie`, `Truth`, `Ghost`, fixed percentages) into universal psychology and story law.

## B209 production laws
### B209-G01 — Character Model, Not Moral Label
`CURRENT MODEL / VALUE → PRESENT WANT → TACTIC → CONSEQUENCE → UPDATE OR TESTED STABILITY`.

Use `Lie/Truth` only when the story genuinely contains a false proposition and a better-supported model. Otherwise use neutral production language.

### B209-G02 — Belief Status Firewall
`CHARACTER BELIEF ≠ SELF-REPORT ≠ POV INTERPRETATION ≠ WORLD FACT`.

Any factual realization still needs source/provenance/corroboration.

### B209-G03 — Want / Need Diagnostic
Separate:
- what the character presently tries to obtain/do;
- what capability, knowledge, relationship condition or strategy would let the character act better.

Do not assume Want is always external or Need is always moral/spiritual.

### B209-G04 — Ghost Optionality
`PAST CAUSE MAY HELP EXPLAIN CURRENT MODEL; TRAUMA IS NOT REQUIRED`.

A belief may emerge from repeated social reinforcement, incentives, culture, institution, relationship history or ordinary experience.

### B209-G05 — Behavioural Arc Proof
`EARLY ANALOGOUS PRESSURE → RESPONSE A` versus `LATER ANALOGOUS PRESSURE → RESPONSE B`.

Narrator claims of change are insufficient if tactics, boundaries, evidence thresholds and choices remain the same.

### B209-G06 — Tested Stability Is a Real Arc
A protagonist may keep a core value while changing tactics, knowledge, relationships and responsibility.

`STABILITY ≠ FLAT CHARACTER`.

### B209-G07 — Negative Arc Separation
Do not merge epistemic disillusionment with ethical corruption.

- Disillusionment: model becomes darker/more constrained.
- Fall: worsening choices create worsening model/option space.
- Corruption: better model/value is available but repeatedly rejected for competing benefit.

### B209-G08 — Midpoint Re-weighting
`NEW EVIDENCE / EXPERIENCE → MODEL RE-WEIGHTING → STRATEGY CHANGE`.

No mandatory 50%, false victory/defeat or moral epiphany.

### B209-G09 — Outcome Can Be Success
A structural turn need not be defeat.

`COMPETENT ACTION → REAL SUCCESS → MORE TRUST / ACCESS / RESPONSIBILITY → HARDER NEXT CHOICE`.

### B209-G10 — Arc Pays Into ONE MAIN STORY
`CHARACTER CHANGE / STABILITY → MAJOR CHOICE / RELATIONSHIP CHANGE → MAIN ENGINE CONSEQUENCE / PAYOFF`.

If no downstream payment exists, compress or bank the arc.

### B209-G11 — Theme Through Consequence
`THEMATIC QUESTION → COMPETING MODELS → CHOICES → CONSEQUENCES → READER INFERENCE`.

No authorial moral answer key.

### B209-G12 — Supporting Character Personhood
Supporting agents do not exist to prove the protagonist’s Truth.

`BOOK FUNCTION ≠ STORYWORLD PERSONHOOD`.

### B209-G13 — Nonhuman Arc Firewall
A conscious robot/nonhuman does not require human trauma, a Ghost, human emotional display or “becoming more human.”

`OWN PRIORITY → OWN MODEL/EVIDENCE → OWN RELATIONSHIPS → CONSENT/REFUSAL → CHOICE → CONSEQUENCE`.

### B209-G14 — YA Trauma Firewall
Adolescent depth is not `WOUND → LIE → HEALING` by default.

Use autonomy, belonging, privacy, consent, mastery, first responsibility, reputation, relationships and competing good values.

### B209-G15 — Resolution as Proof, Not Moral Speech
`LOCAL OUTCOME → COST / AFTERMATH → CHANGED BEHAVIOUR / RELATIONSHIP / MODEL → FUTURE OPTION SPACE`.

### B209-G16 — Framework Percentage Firewall
25/50/75/88/98 percentages are optional diagnostic landmarks only.

### B209-G17 — No Mandatory Death Cloud
Third-act pressure does not require literal/symbolic death.

### B209-G18 — No Protagonist Knowledge Monopoly
The climax may use distributed evidence and competence; the protagonist can own a bounded decision without owning every discovery.

### B209-G19 — Exercise Output Is Discovery Only
Workbook answer, character interview or alternate beat:
`DISCOVERY_ONLY → CANON / CHARACTER / CAUSALITY / EVIDENCE / PERSONHOOD TEST → PROMOTE OR BANK`.

### B209-G20 — Arc Beat Is Not Typography
`ARC BEAT ≠ SENTENCE BREAK ≠ PARAGRAPH BREAK`.

No staccato retrofit around “Moment of Truth,” reversal, climax or recognition.

## Failure modes
- **LIE/TRUTH ONTOLOGY** — craft label treated as objective reality.
- **GHOST MANDATE** — trauma inserted because an arc worksheet expects it.
- **GHOST-SIZE ARITHMETIC** — bigger change assumed to require bigger trauma.
- **THERAPY ARC RETROFIT** — every YA protagonist forced into healing.
- **MIDPOINT EPIPHANY FACTORY** — mandatory 50% moral revelation.
- **DEATH-CLOUD THIRD POINT** — symbolic/literal death inserted mechanically.
- **PROTAGONIST SAVIOR MONOPOLY** — all evidence, competence and decision ownership collapsed into one hero.
- **SUPPORTING-PERSON FUNCTION COLLAPSE** — allies exist only to validate the hero.
- **OBJECTIVELY HAPPY/UNHAPPY ARC LABEL** — ending ethics/readership flattened into workbook category.
- **THEME ANSWER KEY** — complex question converted into slogan.
- **MORAL SELFLESSNESS KPI** — selflessness assumed universally positive regardless consent/context.
- **ARC-PERCENTAGE RETROFIT** — live manuscript forced to 25/50/75/88/98 positions.
- **ARC-BEAT CHOPPINESS** — analytical turns rendered as isolated one-line paragraphs.

## Cross-source synthesis
- **Card / Corbett / McKee Character / Swain:** choice and tactic under pressure outperform trait labels.
- **Herman / Zunshine / Oatley:** reasons/motives remain provisional models; belief is not fact.
- **Truby / Brody / Vogler / Bell:** beat frameworks are diagnostic lenses, not simultaneous obligations.
- **Kress / Collins:** frustration response and contextual emotional expression differentiate characters.
- **Le Guin / Prose / Hodgins / Clark:** beat structure does not dictate sentence/paragraph length.
- **Hayes:** relationship change occurs between complete agents; partner-as-cure is rejected.

## IVDIVO arc card
For each major character record:
`CURRENT MODEL/VALUE | PRESENT WANT/RESPONSIBILITY | RELATIONSHIP-SPECIFIC RISK | PRESSURE/EVIDENCE | TACTIC | OUTCOME | WHAT CHANGED? | LATER PROOF CASE`.

Possible changed-state axes:
- knowledge/evidence;
- strategy/model;
- trust/relationship;
- consent/boundary;
- authority/access;
- responsibility;
- emotional commitment;
- future option space.

## Manuscript-facing conclusion
For `LESSON ZERO` Book One: no new defect evidence; do not retrofit Weiland beats.

For future IVDIVO books: use Weiland after the causal engine is known, primarily to test whether character trajectories are visible in choices and downstream consequences.
