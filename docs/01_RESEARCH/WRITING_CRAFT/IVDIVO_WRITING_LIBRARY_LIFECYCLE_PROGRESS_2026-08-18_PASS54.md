## 4. MAJOR NEW MECHANISMS
### A. Place-as-action
`PLACE RULE / CULTURAL PRACTICE → PERSON/ROBOT ACTIVITY → FRICTION/CHOICE → CONSEQUENCE`.
Detail accumulation without action is tourism prose.

### B. Source distillation with provenance
`RAW SOURCE → CONTEXT → ESSENTIAL CLAIM → COMPRESSED FORM → MEANING-PRESERVATION TEST → ATTRIBUTION/STATUS`.
Useful for archives, testimony, logs, specialist schools and mystery evidence.

### C. Technical explanation ladder
`CURRENT QUESTION → MINIMUM NEEDED FACT → ACTION/TEST → RESULT → NEXT QUESTION`.
This replaces encyclopedia exposition.

### D. Missing-actor / governance test
For bureaucratic language:
`WHO CHOOSES? WHO ACTS? WHO CAN REFUSE? WHO BEARS COST? WHO CAN APPEAL?`

### E. Critique from evidence
Writers' Room notes should follow:
`CONCRETE PASSAGE/BEAT → READER EFFECT → DIAGNOSIS → COMPARISON → REPAIR OPTIONS`.

### F. Humor truth-first
`REAL PATTERN → CHARACTER-SPECIFIC ANGLE/EXAGGERATION → COMIC EFFECT`.
No canon distortion merely to earn a joke.

### G. Editorial diagnosis vs wording
A reviewer can correctly identify a problem and still propose the wrong wording. Preserve author/character intent while repairing reader failure.
