## 1. Structure before cosmetic craft

McKee's strongest early production point is upstream diagnosis. False motivation, redundant characters, empty subtext and causal holes are story defects; vivid description and sharp dialogue cannot compensate for them.

IVDIVO order:
`BOOK ENGINE → CAUSALITY → CHARACTER MOTIVE → SCENE FUNCTION → POV / INFORMATION → DIALOGUE / PROSE / RHYTHM`.

This reinforces the rule against cosmetic repair when the actual defect is structural.

## 2. Ordinary life vs portraiture failure

McKee's critique of understructured `personal story` is useful only after correction. Ordinary life is not the problem. The problem is **event accumulation without changed state**.

Keep a quiet/ordinary scene when it changes:
- relationship;
- decision;
- self-model;
- evidence/knowledge;
- emotional readiness;
- future obligation;
- social status/belonging;
- strategy.

Compress/delete when it merely proves that life contains realistic detail.

## 3. Spectacle vs story failure

McKee's opposite failure mode is advanced action/effects treated as entertainment by themselves.

Orbital Youth translation:
`TECH / SPECTACLE → PERSON-SPECIFIC OPTION CHANGE → ACTION / RELATIONSHIP / RISK CHANGE → CONSEQUENCE`.

A spectacular ring, robot capability, transport system or environmental feature does not earn a scene because it is technologically impressive.

## 4. Fact is not narrative truth

`IT HAPPENED` is not a structural justification.

A factual or researched detail earns page space by being selected and interpreted inside a causal/POV design. This is relevant both to realism research and to importing technical reference material into IVDIVO.

## 5. Story triangle as variables, not hierarchy

McKee's Archplot / Miniplot / Antiplot framework is most useful when decomposed into variables:
- causal continuity;
- closure/openness;
- external vs internal conflict;
- active/single vs multiple/passive protagonists;
- linear/nonlinear time;
- consistent/inconsistent reality.

Do not treat one corner as inherently higher quality.

## 6. Change scale

Meaningful change may be obvious or nearly invisible. IVDIVO's existing `changed state` model is therefore stronger than `big event` thinking.

Possible changed states:
`physical | relational | emotional | epistemic | reputational | ethical | access/status | strategy`.

## 7. Form must serve the story, not identity politics

McKee's critique of choosing minimal/anticlassical form merely to reject Hollywood transfers well:

**Do not choose form because it signals sophistication.**

The same applies in AI-assisted writing:
- nonlinear chronology is not automatically deep;
- multiple POV is not automatically sophisticated;
- long lyrical paragraphs are not automatically literary;
- clipped syntax is not automatically tense;
- open ending is not automatically mature.

Choose the form that produces the intended reader effect and causal experience.

## 8. Historical-market firewall

McKee's audience/budget arguments describe an older film-industry environment. They must not become current IVDIVO market law and must never re-import monetary logic into post-money orbital society.

Use only the durable principle:
**more formally demanding designs may require stronger reader orientation and clearer promises.**

## 9. New provisional gates

- **MCK-G01 STRUCTURE BEFORE POLISH** — no dialogue/line fix for a causal defect.
- **MCK-G02 ORDINARY LIFE ACCUMULATES** — quiet scenes need changed state.
- **MCK-G03 SPECTACLE CHANGES OPTIONS** — tech/action must alter consequence.
- **MCK-G04 FACT IS NOT FUNCTION** — researched truth needs story function.
- **MCK-G05 FORM VARIABLE TEST** — diagnose causality/closure/POV/time/reality separately.
- **MCK-G06 CHANGE SCALE** — subtle internal/relational change counts if consequential.
- **MCK-G07 NO PRESTIGE FORM** — form cannot be chosen mainly to signal sophistication.
- **MCK-G08 HISTORICAL MARKET FIREWALL** — old Hollywood economics are context, not canon.

## 10. Cross-source synthesis

**McKee + Truby:** converge strongly on story architecture before surface polish.

**McKee + Rosenfeld:** Rosenfeld prevents the changed-state principle becoming constant external confrontation.

**McKee + Card:** Card corrects any tendency to reduce ensemble design to protagonist geometry.

**McKee + Le Guin/Clark/Prose:** structural priority does not license syntactic monotony or thin prose.

## 11. Orbital Youth implication

The strongest immediate application is a double filter for every future-facing scene:

`IS THIS ONLY A REALISTIC DAILY DETAIL?`  
If yes, what changes because of it?

`IS THIS ONLY A COOL TECHNOLOGY?`  
If yes, what human/robot option or consequence changes because of it?

If neither answer produces a changed state, compress or remove before line editing.
