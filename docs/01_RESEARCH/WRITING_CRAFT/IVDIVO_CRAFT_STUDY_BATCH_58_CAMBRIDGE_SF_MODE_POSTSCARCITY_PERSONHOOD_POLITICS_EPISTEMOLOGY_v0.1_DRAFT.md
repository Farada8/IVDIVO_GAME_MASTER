# IVDIVO CRAFT STUDY — BATCH 58
## Cambridge SF: Mode · Post-Scarcity · Personhood · Politics · Epistemology

Status: WORKING / OPERATIONALIZED
Date: 2026-08-17
Source basis: strict-complete Core #39 — *The Cambridge Companion to Science Fiction*.

## PURPOSE
Convert the completed historical/critical SF read into callable production gates without turning genre history into current science or IVDIVO canon.

## PRIMARY SYNTHESIS
`SF MODE IS NOT THE STORY.`

Production chain:
`CURRENT CANON / SOCIETY → CHARACTER NORMALITY → SPECIFIC DIFFERENCE → OPTION/CONSTRAINT → CHOICE → CONSEQUENCE → READER MODEL CHANGE`.

## PRODUCTION GATES

### B58-G01 — LOCAL ENGINE BEFORE SF MODE
Name the book's causal engine separately from its speculative condition.
PASS examples: rescue + habitat constraint; mystery + unknown signal; romance + human/robot social boundary.
FAIL: “the plot is life in an orbital city.”

### B58-G02 — SPECULATIVE DIFFERENCE MUST ALTER CAUSALITY
A technology/social rule earns major space only if it changes what a character can do, know, risk, refuse, owe or become responsible for.

### B58-G03 — FUTURE NORMALITY
Do not make competent inhabitants explain their ordinary world to one another. Reveal normality through action, assumptions, jokes, mistakes, routes, permissions and consequences.

### B58-G04 — AUTHOR INTENT / READER EFFECT SPLIT
Do not defend a scene with “it is supposed to criticize X.” Audit what the story rewards: who wins, who looks competent, who pays, what gets romanticized.

### B58-G05 — POST-SCARCITY STAKES
In post-money orbital life, route stakes toward genuinely non-fungible pressures: time, trust, role, consent, belonging, responsibility, attention, reputation, relationship, unique opportunity, compatible place or lived experience.
Do not convert these into hidden credits.

### B58-G06 — TECHNICAL / NORMATIVE FIREWALL
Ask separately:
1. Does the system work technically?
2. Is its use legitimate, consensual and acceptably distributed?
Technical competence never settles the second question.

### B58-G07 — POLITICAL FORUM
Where collective interests conflict, show a legitimate route for disagreement, appeal, negotiation or revision. “The administration optimizes everything” is not political legitimacy.

### B58-G08 — CONSCIOUS ROBOT PERSONHOOD
A conscious robot requires independent want, relationships, obligations, right of refusal, characteristic tactics and consequences.
REPLACE-WITH-TERMINAL TEST: if replacing the robot with software changes nothing important, personhood is not written.

### B58-G09 — REPRESENTATION ≠ AGENCY
Presence/diversity does not equal agency. Test who initiates, refuses, changes the causal state, owns evidence and pays costs.

### B58-G10 — LEGACY IDEOLOGY FIREWALL
Before importing a familiar SF icon, test its inherited assumptions: frontier, colonization, servant robot, rational engineer as moral authority, one culture/planet, one religion/species, military hierarchy.

### B58-G11 — COGNITION EFFECT ≠ SCIENTIFIC PROOF
A technically persuasive passage can create a feeling of rationality while being scientifically false. Current science must be verified separately.

### B58-G12 — HARD-SF HARDWARE FETISH CHECK
Hardware detail, militarized language and emotional coldness are not evidence of scientific seriousness. Detail must alter model, choice or consequence.

### B58-G13 — SPECTACLE MUST PAY
A large image/set piece may be a reader reward, but at book scale it must change action state, information state, relationship state or responsibility.

### B58-G14 — CULTURAL SPECIFICITY
Future culture is written through history, ordinary practice, language/categories, relationships and institutional effects—not declarations that “society is diverse.”

### B58-G15 — BELIEF / PRACTICE / ONTOLOGY SPLIT
For consciousness or dimensional schools keep separate:
`OBSERVATION → REPRODUCIBLE PRACTICE EFFECT → SCHOOL INTERPRETATION → COMPETING INTERPRETATION → ONTOLOGICAL CLAIM`.

### B58-G16 — BODY-TRANSFER / TRANSCENDENCE CANON FIREWALL
Historical SF use of uploading, body swapping, reincarnation or transcendence is genre genealogy only. It does not authorize operational human consciousness-transfer technology in current IVDIVO orbital civilization.

### B58-G17 — EDITORIAL MONOCULTURE TEST
Every Writers’ Room rule must answer: does this diagnose a failure while leaving multiple valid forms, or does it force every book toward one template?

### B58-G18 — SCALE / INTIMACY BRAID
Large-system change should periodically narrow into one specific relationship/decision; the intimate line should in turn change the systemic problem.

## FAILURE MODES TO RED-TEAM
- future tutorial dialogue;
- wonder without consequence;
- bigger-machine escalation;
- technical competence treated as moral authority;
- post-scarcity replaced by points/credits;
- robot-as-servant default;
- alien/nonhuman used only as metaphor for human growth;
- static utopia lecture;
- “it works” institutional legitimacy;
- politics without forums;
- current identity categories pasted unchanged into future society;
- spirituality flattened into superstition or omniscient truth;
- historical trope leaking into technical canon;
- SF spectacle replacing ONE MAIN STORY.

## CROSS-SOURCE ROUTING
- Causality: Truby/McKee first; Cambridge checks whether SF difference actually feeds it.
- POV/mode: Lodge/LaPlante first; Cambridge checks future-normality and genre shorthand.
- Mystery: Zunshine + Truby; Cambridge adds conceptual-breakthrough function.
- YA: Brody + youth labs; Cambridge removes contemporary-material assumptions that no longer fit future society.
- Synthetic people: Card/McKee Character + Murderbot/Ishiguro labs; Cambridge supplies historical stereotype firewall.

## STOP RULE
When a live problem has a causal solution supported by current canon plus 2–4 relevant sources, stop expanding theory and write/test the story.
