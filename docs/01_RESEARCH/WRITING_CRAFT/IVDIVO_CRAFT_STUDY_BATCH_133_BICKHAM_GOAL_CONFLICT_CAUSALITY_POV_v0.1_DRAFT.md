## Extracted mechanisms

### 1. Goal → prospective reader question
Bickham's useful core is that a character's selected goal lets the reader project forward. IVDIVO adaptation:

`PRESENT WANT / RESPONSIBILITY → COMMITMENT → TRACKABLE QUESTION → ACTION → CONSEQUENCE`

The goal does not need to be literally "essential to happiness." It needs to matter enough to alter behavior and future options.

### 2. Agency is not aggression
Bickham overidentifies strong character with persistent outward struggle. IVDIVO gate:

`AGENCY = MEANINGFUL OPTION SET + CHOICE / REFUSAL / DELAY / COOPERATION + CONSEQUENCE`

A character who deliberately does not act because an evidence threshold is not met can be highly agentic.

### 3. Resistance is broader than person-vs-person conflict
Bickham defines conflict narrowly as two motivated people with opposing goals. Useful for direct confrontation, but too restrictive for IVDIVO.

Production form:

`PRESENT WANT → MEANINGFUL RESISTANCE / SUPPORT → TACTIC → CHANGED STATE`

Resistance can come from another person, an institution, a world rule, incomplete evidence, incompatible values, embodiment, time, consent, or a genuine tradeoff.

### 4. Causal credibility has layers
Bickham separates background, recent motivation, and immediate stimulus. IVDIVO adaptation:

`BACKGROUND CAPACITY → CURRENT MOTIVE → IMMEDIATE CONTEXT / NEW INFORMATION → PROCESSING → ACTION → CONSEQUENCE`

But we reject total motivational overdetermination. The reader needs sufficient causal legibility, not a complete psychological explanation.

### 5. Coincidence audit
Coincidence is most damaging when it repeatedly solves a character's problem. Prefer:

`NEED → SEARCH / CHOICE → EARNED ENCOUNTER / INFORMATION → CONSEQUENCE`

Coincidence may introduce a problem or opportunity; it should not repeatedly provide the solution.

### 6. Stimulus/response is a continuity diagnostic, not a prose metronome
Bickham's strongest contribution is reader-legible ordering. His absolute rule that stimulus must be immediate, external and physical is rejected.

IVDIVO form:

`READER-PERCEIVABLE CAUSE / NEW INFORMATION → PROCESSING → RESPONSE / CHOICE → NEXT STATE`

Memory, delayed processing, accumulated evidence and prior commitments can also be legitimate causes if the bridge is clear.

### 7. Story-level focal ownership ≠ scene-level POV ownership
Bickham demands one dominant viewpoint and treats neutral/observer narration as invalid. This is too absolute and conflicts with Le Guin, Kress, Provost, Fludernik and modern multiple-POV practice.

IVDIVO distinction:

`BOOK CAUSAL / FOCAL ENGINE ≠ SCENE POV ACCESS OWNER`

A book may have multiple POV characters while preserving ONE MAIN STORY.

### 8. POV access and world fact are different
Useful Bickham core:

`WHAT THIS POV CAN PERCEIVE → WHAT THEY KNOW → WHAT THEY INFER → WHAT THEY CANNOT KNOW`

IVDIVO extension:

`POV INTERPRETATION ≠ WORLD FACT`

Critical for mystery, conscious robots, institutions and consciousness-practice schools.

### 9. Environment → interpretation → behavior
Bickham's room/speech example contains a strong mechanism beneath its dated filter-word prescription:

`ENVIRONMENTAL DETAIL → POV-SPECIFIC EVALUATION → INTERNAL / RELATIONAL CHANGE → ACTION`

We do not require repeated "he saw / she felt / he knew" filter constructions; those can increase distance in close POV.

## Cross-source comparison

- **Swain** supports causal continuity but is less absolute than later summaries make him: scene/sequel and M/R are diagnostic tools, not a requirement to fragment prose into microbeats.
- **Card / Kress / Provost / Le Guin** support multiple and/or more flexible POV architectures. Bickham's single-dominant-POV rule is therefore a useful beginner simplification, not an IVDIVO law.
- **LaPlante / Rosenfeld / Chatman** show that causal movement may be emotional, relational or epistemic rather than only person-vs-person conflict.
- **Zunshine / le Carré / Fludernik** require a stronger evidence firewall than Bickham: perception and interpretation must not silently become fact.

## New production gates

**B133-G01 — Trackable Want:** a major line needs a current want/responsibility that changes behavior.

**B133-G02 — Agency Without Aggression:** refusal, delay, cooperation and bounded non-action count when chosen and consequential.

**B133-G03 — Resistance Classification:** do not manufacture an antagonist when a world rule, evidence problem, relationship or value tradeoff already supplies pressure.

**B133-G04 — Causal Layering:** separate background capacity, current motive and immediate trigger/context.

**B133-G05 — Coincidence Solution Firewall:** coincidence may complicate; repeated coincidence may not solve.

**B133-G06 — Reader-Legible Cause:** if a response feels uncaused, repair the causal bridge before polishing the sentence.

**B133-G07 — No S/R Fragmentation:** do not turn cause/response auditing into choppy `stimulus. reaction. thought. gesture.` prose.

**B133-G08 — POV Knowledge Boundary:** every close-POV claim must be classifiable as observed, known, inferred, reported or unknown.

**B133-G09 — POV Switch Payoff:** a new POV must buy unique action, evidence, relationship pressure, lived consequence or decision.

**B133-G10 — Focal Engine / POV Separation:** ONE MAIN STORY does not require one POV.

**B133-G11 — Filter-Word Function:** use "saw/felt/knew" only when the mediation itself matters; do not mechanically insert them to prove POV.

**B133-G12 — Quiet Agency:** contemplative or low-action scenes survive if they change model, trust, consent, commitment or future choice.

## Failure modes

- WIMP LABEL FALLACY — mistaking quietness, fear or sensitivity for absence of agency.
- CONFLICT MONOCULTURE — forcing every pressure into a person-vs-person fight.
- MOTIVE OVERDETERMINATION — explaining every behavior with a complete psychological cause.
- COINCIDENCE RESCUE — lucky events repeatedly provide solutions.
- STIMULUS/RESPONSE METRONOME — fragmenting prose into mechanical micro-units.
- SINGLE-POV DOGMA — distorting an ensemble book to satisfy a beginner rule.
- POV TELEPATHY — close POV knows what it has no route to know.
- FILTER-WORD OVERMARKING — repeatedly saying saw/felt/knew instead of rendering perception naturally.
- INTERPRETATION-TO-FACT LEAK — POV belief silently becomes canon.

## IVDIVO implications

For Orbital Youth, competence can create stronger story movement through success: `competent action → real success → increased trust/access/responsibility → harder next choice`.

For conscious robots: compliance, pause or refusal cannot be interpreted as absence of agency. Preserve `OWN WANT → OWN MODEL/EVIDENCE → OWN RELATIONSHIPS → CONSENT/REFUSAL → CHOICE → CONSEQUENCE`.

For consciousness-practice schools: POV conviction is not ontological proof. Preserve `observation → reproducible effect → school interpretation → competing interpretation → independent test → ontology ceiling`.
