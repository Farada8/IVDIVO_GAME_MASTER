## 1. Real-life source material is not finished character design
Bickham's useful mechanism is not literal exaggeration for its own sake. The stronger operational form is:

REAL OBSERVATION → FICTIONAL TRANSFORMATION → CHARACTER-SPECIFIC WANT / HISTORY / RELATIONSHIPS / TACTICS → PRESSURED CHOICE → READER MODEL.

A copied acquaintance is not a finished fictional person. Observed material can seed voice, behavior, contradiction or history, but character construction must supply causal intelligibility and story-specific agency.

### Firewall
Reject the strong Bickham claim that fictional people must be simpler or more internally consistent than real people. Card, Corbett, McKee Character, Herman and Zunshine collectively support a more nuanced model: characters can remain contradictory and partially opaque if the text gives enough evidence for the reader to build and revise a model.

## 2. Agency is not aggression
Bickham attacks passive protagonists by insisting on story goals and struggle. IVDIVO adapts this to:

PRESENT WANT / RESPONSIBILITY → AVAILABLE OPTIONS → CHOICE / REFUSAL / DELAY / COOPERATION → CONSEQUENCE → CHANGED OPTION SPACE.

Agency includes refusing, waiting for more evidence, negotiating, delegating, asking for consent, or choosing not to act when the evidence threshold is not met.

### Failure mode: ACTIONISM
Mistaking visible aggression or constant initiative for agency.

### Gate
If a character's non-action is intentional, evidence-based and costly, it can be highly agentic.

## 3. Coincidence repair
Bickham's Section 10 usefully distinguishes motivation/background from coincidence. IVDIVO repair sequence:

CONVENIENT EVENT → identify what the character could have done to seek / enable / expose it → plant causal preparation → preserve uncertainty → payoff.

Coincidence may introduce trouble or opportunity; it should not cheaply solve the decisive problem.

## 4. Stimulus/response as reader-continuity diagnostic, not prose metronome
Bickham's Section 11 argues for immediate external stimulus and external response. The useful kernel is:

READER-PERCEIVABLE CAUSE / NEW INFORMATION → CHARACTER PROCESSING → RESPONSE / CHOICE → NEXT STATE.

But IVDIVO rejects the absolute rule that every meaningful response requires an immediately visible external stimulus. Internal memory, delayed processing, stable commitments, bodily states and prior evidence can legitimately drive action when the causal bridge is clear.

This aligns with Swain's own later anti-mechanical warnings.

### Repair gate
When a reaction feels arbitrary, ask:
1. What changed in the character's accessible information / body / relationship / memory / obligation?
2. Is that cause available to the reader at the right time?
3. Does the response fit the character's current model and options?
4. If surprise is intended, is retrospective support present?

## 5. POV ownership
Section 12 begins the viewpoint block. Provisional IVDIVO distinction:

WHO SPEAKS ≠ WHO PERCEIVES ≠ WHO KNOWS ≠ WHO EVALUATES ≠ WHO OWNS THE DECISION.

Do not let close POV become telepathy. Do not let a narrator's confident language silently upgrade a character inference into world fact.

## 6. ONE MAIN STORY application
Character agency and causal clarity must serve one dominant engine:

LOCAL WANT → ACTION / REFUSAL → CHANGED STATE → EFFECT ON MAIN ENGINE.

A character can be vivid and active yet still belong in another book. If their line does not alter the dominant causal engine, bank/compress it.

## 7. Orbital Youth / post-money application
Replace easy Earth stakes (salary, rent, purchasing status) with actual non-fungible stakes:
- trust;
- consent;
- specific responsibility;
- qualification or mission role;
- access to a unique person/place/window;
- reputation;
- belonging;
- privacy;
- relationships;
- mastery;
- time.

## 8. Conscious robot personhood
Do not interpret machine compliance as passivity or lack of agency.

OWN WANT → OWN MODEL/EVIDENCE → OWN RELATIONSHIPS → CONSENT/REFUSAL → CHOICE → CONSEQUENCE.

A robot's refusal, delay, request for clarification, or boundary-setting can be more agentic than an action-heavy service response.

## 9. Consciousness-school epistemic firewall
Bickham's demand for apparent causal clarity cannot be used to force premature metaphysical explanation.

OBSERVATION → REPRODUCIBLE EFFECT → SCHOOL INTERPRETATION → COMPETING INTERPRETATION → INDEPENDENT TEST → ONTOLOGY CEILING.

Narrative causality is not verified ontology.

## 10. Anti-choppiness implication
Stimulus/response does NOT justify:

stimulus sentence → reaction sentence → thought sentence → gesture sentence → new one-line paragraph.

Use reader-state continuity and paragraph intent. If several beats are one coherent perceptual/decision unit, combine them syntactically and paragraphically.
