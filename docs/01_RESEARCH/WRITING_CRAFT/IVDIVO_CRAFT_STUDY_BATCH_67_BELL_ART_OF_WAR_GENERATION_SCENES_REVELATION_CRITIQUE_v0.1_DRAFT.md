# IVDIVO CRAFT STUDY — BATCH 67
## Bell — Generation / Scene Bull's-Eye / Progressive Revelation / Critique Triage

Date: 2026-08-17
Source: James Scott Bell, *The Art of War for Writers*
Status: APPLIED SYNTHESIS

# 1. WHY THIS SOURCE MATTERS
Bell is most useful when treated as a compact toolbox, not as a unified theory. The strongest transferable mechanisms concern production discipline, generation/revision separation, premise comparison, scene focus, progressive revelation, dialogue-as-action, RUE and critique triage.

# 2. CORE SYNTHESIS
## 2.1 Hot generation / cool revision
`GENERATE → COMPLETE LIVE MATERIAL → DIAGNOSE → STRUCTURAL REPAIR → PROSE/LANGUAGE REPAIR`.

Do not make a first-draft sentence satisfy every studio gate simultaneously.

## 2.2 Premise tournament
Before development lock, compare multiple real engines rather than defend the first one because time was invested.

`HUMAN WANT → WORLD-SPECIFIC PRESSURE → COUNTERMODEL → END CONDITION → FRESHNESS → READER PROMISE`.

## 2.3 Scene bull's-eye
A dramatized scene earns its space when it contains an irreplaceable change or experience:
- decision;
- permission/refusal;
- evidence-status change;
- relation change;
- cost;
- discovery;
- earned emotional aftershock.

Bull's-eye does NOT mean mandatory twist, argument or danger.

## 2.4 Outer / inner dual ledger
Track:
- action / material / social state;
- model / relationship / commitment state.

A scene can move one strongly and the other lightly.

## 2.5 Progressive revelation
`OBSERVABLE FACT → PROVISIONAL MODEL → CONTRADICTION → REVISED MODEL → CONTROLLED REVEAL → ACTION`.

IVDIVO fairness override: do not conceal from the reader facts the active POV already knows without a legitimate in-world reason.

## 2.6 Dialogue as tactic
`WANT → TACTIC → REACTION → NEW TACTIC → CHANGED STATE`.

Agreement, silence and directness remain valid if they genuinely change trust, consent, evidence, commitment or intimacy.

## 2.7 RUE as anti-restatement
The highest-value use of `Resist the Urge to Explain` is deleting sentences that merely restate meaning already produced by action/dialogue/image.

# 3. MAJOR FAILURE MODES
- mandatory physical/professional/psychological `death` stakes;
- mandatory heroic lead;
- mandatory wound, brokenness or redemption;
- exposition disguised as artificial argument;
- `action first` treated as universal opening law;
- dialogue required immediately in every opening;
- every chapter forced into a cliffhanger;
- fast drafting treated as moral/professional superiority;
- progressive revelation used to cheat POV knowledge;
- historical agent/query/platform advice treated as current fact;
- Bell's business/economic assumptions imported into post-money orbital worldbuilding.

# 4. CROSS-SOURCE RESOLUTION
Bell + LaPlante: generate freely, revise diagnostically.
Bell + Rosenfeld: scenes need intention/change, but conflict is not mandatory.
Bell + McKee Dialogue: speech is action/tactic; indirect dialogue and silence remain legitimate.
Bell + Zunshine/Herman: reveal architecture must preserve source/provenance/evidence status.
Bell + Card/Corbett/Kress: depth does not require wound/redemption; use desire, relation and specific response.
Bell + Iglesias: reader effect must be tracked independently from the character's named emotion.

# 5. B67 PRODUCTION GATES
- **B67-G01 Stage Before Tool:** identify writing stage and actual defect before selecting craft tactic.
- **B67-G02 Generation Protection:** no full studio audit while generating every sentence.
- **B67-G03 Premise Tournament:** compare ≥3 viable engines before lock when lock is not Founder-imposed.
- **B67-G04 Scene Bull's-Eye:** every full scene must have an irreplaceable change/experience.
- **B67-G05 Outer/Inner Dual Ledger:** track action-state and model/relation-state independently.
- **B67-G06 Backstory Present Payoff:** past information must change present understanding/tactic/relation or be compressed.
- **B67-G07 Fair Revelation:** delay interpretation, not naturally available POV fact.
- **B67-G08 Dialogue Tactic Change:** repeated same-tactic lines are trim candidates.
- **B67-G09 No Explanatory Echo:** do not explain an effect the reader has already received.
- **B67-G10 Earned Recall:** setup/payoff emotional recall must be planted and must trigger action.
- **B67-G11 Effect/Repair Separation:** reader criticism identifies possible effect; writer independently diagnoses repair.
- **B67-G12 No Formula Import:** no compulsory death, wound, redemption, battle, cliffhanger or heroic lead.
- **B67-G13 Market-Date Firewall:** historical publishing tactics require current verification before business use.

# 6. ANTI-CHOP INTEGRATION
Bell's RUE and scene-bull's-eye mechanisms strengthen the current repair order:

`ONE MAIN STORY / CAUSALITY → SCENE NECESSITY → SHOW/TELL → POV → DIALOGUE MODE → PARAGRAPH INTENT → SENTENCE RHYTHM → WORDING`.

A sequence of short lines is not repaired by merely joining sentences if the real defect is repeated exposition, unnecessary micro-scene staging, same-tactic dialogue or an explanatory echo after an already clear beat.
