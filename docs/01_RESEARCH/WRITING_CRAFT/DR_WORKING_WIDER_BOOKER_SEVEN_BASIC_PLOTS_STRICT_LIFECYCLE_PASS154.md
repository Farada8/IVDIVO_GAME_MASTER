## Retained mechanisms after Red Team

### 1. External success ≠ resolved story state
Booker repeatedly contrasts outward achievement with what he calls inner/archetypal completion. The metaphysics are rejected, but the structural distinction is useful:

`EXTERNAL OBJECTIVE RESULT → RELATION / MODEL / RESPONSIBILITY / AGENCY STATE → ARE THEY ACTUALLY DIFFERENT?`

A credential, prize, marriage, rescue, promotion, public acclaim or successful technical fix does not automatically resolve the book's governing human problem.

### 2. Formal relationship status ≠ relationship resolution
Marriage, pairing, declaration, rescue or reunion can occur while trust, consent, dependency, boundaries and future terms remain unresolved.

`FORMAL STATUS CHANGE ≠ RELATIONAL STATE CHANGE`.

Use:
`RELATIONAL CLAIM → CHARACTER-TRUE ACTION → COST → OTHER AGENT REMAINS FREE → NEW TERMS`.

### 3. Goal legitimacy audit
A Quest-like structure can produce strong momentum while the pursued goal itself is ethically, institutionally or evidentially defective.

`TRACKABLE GOAL → WHO DEFINED IT? → WHO BENEFITS? → WHO BEARS COST? → WHO MAY REFUSE? → WHAT HAPPENS IF IT SUCCEEDS?`

`GOAL ACHIEVEMENT ≠ GOAL LEGITIMACY`.

### 4. Voyage/novum must change the model
A character's entry into an unfamiliar world, technology or institution earns story space only if it changes available actions, relationships, evidence, responsibility or model.

`NEW SYSTEM → AFFORDANCE / CONSTRAINT → MODEL UPDATE → CHANGED ACTION / RELATIONSHIP → CONSEQUENCE`.

Otherwise it is world tourism.

### 5. Recognition = model revision, not mystical completion
Booker's Comedy language is metaphysical, but the usable mechanism is:

`MIS-MODELLED STATE → DISCRIMINATING EVIDENCE / EXPERIENCE → RECOGNITION → RELATION / ACTION TERMS CHANGE`.

Recognition does not require marriage, heterosexual union or metaphysical wholeness.

### 6. Narrative alignment / glamour can distort accountability
A charismatic or focalized central figure can receive reader sympathy even while causing harm.

Audit:
`WHO RECEIVES INTERIORITY / CHARISMA / HUMOUR / BEAUTY / SCREEN-TIME? → WHO BEARS CONSEQUENCES? → DOES DISCOURSE HIDE THE COST?`

This is a reader-effect / discourse audit, not a moral prohibition on difficult protagonists.

### 7. Surface reset ≠ durable character change
Amnesia, disguise, relocation, new body, title, relationship label or public status can create the appearance of rebirth without changing strategy, responsibility or relation model.

`SURFACE RESET → SAME MODEL / TACTIC? → NOT A CHARACTER CHANGE`.

Durable change requires evidence in later choices:
`MODEL / VALUE / RELATION → PRESSURE → NEW TACTIC / DECISION → CONSEQUENCE`.

### 8. Corpus-pattern audit ≠ author diagnosis
Ch25's Hardy discussion invites a useful longitudinal manuscript audit but makes speculative biographical/psychoanalytic leaps.

Safe use:
`REPEATED STORY PATTERN ACROSS WORKS → TEXTUAL OBSERVATION → POSSIBLE CRAFT TENDENCY → TEST IN CURRENT MANUSCRIPT`.

Forbidden leap:
`RECURRING FICTION PATTERN → AUTHOR PSYCHOLOGICAL DIAGNOSIS`.

### 9. Market-imposed ending / retrofit audit
Booker's Hardy examples include endings altered under publication pressure. Useful production question:

`ENDING STATE → FOLLOWS CAUSALLY FROM BOOK? → OR PATCHED TO SATISFY FORMAT / MARKET / TEMPLATE?`

Modern market requirements must be verified separately; historical anecdotes do not create 2026 policy.

### 10. Coincidence-stack audit
When repeated coincidences systematically force the protagonist toward a predetermined outcome, the reader may feel authorial manipulation.

`COINCIDENCE MAY COMPLICATE; IT MUST NOT REPEATEDLY SOLVE OR FORCE THE REQUIRED END STATE`.
