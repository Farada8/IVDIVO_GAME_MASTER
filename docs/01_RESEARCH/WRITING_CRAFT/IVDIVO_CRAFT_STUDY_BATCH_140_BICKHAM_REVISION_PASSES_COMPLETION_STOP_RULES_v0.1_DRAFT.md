## 1. What §35 adds that is genuinely durable

Bickham's twelve-step revision checklist is dated in some particulars but structurally useful because it **separates diagnostic passes** instead of trying to repair everything at once.

Recovered sequence:
1. take a brief break;
2. check general project/requirement fit;
3. read straight through as a reader, not an editor;
4. repair problems found in that read-through;
5. reexamine the opening;
6. audit viewpoint;
7. audit chronology/time scheme;
8. audit motivation and why the character remains causally involved;
9. audit helpful coincidence;
10. inspect chapter/section endings;
11. inspect total story logic;
12. inspect whether the ending pays the question/promise created by the beginning.

The production value is not the exact order of Bickham's 1992 checklist. The durable mechanism is:

`COOL → READER READ → DEFECT MAP → ISOLATED PASSES → RETEST → STOP`.

### IVDIVO correction
Do **not** inherit these Bickham assumptions as laws:
- the opening must always use threat;
- one POV must dominate;
- every chapter ending requires twist/disaster;
- the ending must close every ambiguity;
- plan/guideline fit is more important than live story truth.

Use functional replacements:
- opening: `ATTENTION + ORIENTATION + PROMISE + FORWARD PRESSURE`;
- POV: `ACCESS VALUE > ROTATION/FREQUENCY RULE`;
- chapter ending: `CHANGED STATE + READ-ON VALUE`;
- ending: `LOCAL BOOK PROMISE CLOSED + GLOBAL UNKNOWN MAY REMAIN EXPLICIT`;
- plan: `HYPOTHESIS, NOT CANON`.

## 2. Isolated-pass architecture for the IVDIVO Writers' Room

### Pass A — book engine / canon
Question: **Is this still one book?**
- one dominant causal engine;
- canon hierarchy respected;
- no old salary/rent/credits or consciousness-transfer regression;
- local book proof ceiling defined.

### Pass B — causality / state change
Question: **What changes, and what does that change cause?**
- chapter start/end states;
- unearned coincidence;
- alligator complications;
- state-neutral repetition;
- consequence harvesting.

### Pass C — character / personhood / relationships
Question: **Would these choices still belong to these people if plot labels were removed?**
- present wants;
- relation-specific tactics;
- consent/refusal;
- independent nonhuman agency;
- no supporting-person function collapse.

### Pass D — POV / evidence / epistemics
Question: **Who can know what, from which source, with what confidence?**
- observed vs reported vs inferred;
- source lineage;
- POV access limits;
- ontology ceiling;
- romance/trust does not become corroboration.

### Pass E — scene / mode / duration
Question: **Does this material deserve this amount of reader time?**
- full scene / embedded scene / fragment / summary / commentary;
- logistics compression;
- processing placement;
- scene weight variation.

### Pass F — dialogue / exposition / subtext
Question: **Why do these exact words need to exist?**
- dialogue tactics;
- actual listener attention;
- exposition owner;
- silence and action beats only when functional;
- no writers-room turn-taking symmetry.

### Pass G — paragraph / reader-state continuity
Question: **Does each paragraph own one coherent movement of attention/thought/action?**
- merge paragraph confetti;
- protect intentional isolation;
- preserve transition and reference continuity.

### Pass H — sentence rhythm / wording / copy
Question: **Does syntax create the intended reader effect without flattening range?**
- no short-sentence KPI;
- no purple→choppy overcorrection;
- remove clutter only after higher layers are stable.

### Pass I — reader/audio retest
Question: **Did the repair actually improve the experience?**
- beta-reader repeated effects;
- audio stumble / ambiguity;
- continuity/QC evidence;
- reopen earlier pass only when new evidence requires it.

## 3. Revision stop rule

Bickham correctly warns both against stopping too early and revising forever. IVDIVO therefore requires an explicit stop condition.

### STOP when
- dominant causal engine is stable;
- FATAL/MAJOR defects are closed or explicitly accepted;
- evidence/POV chain is coherent;
- no repeated state-neutral scenes remain;
- prose has syntactic range and paragraph coherence;
- retest does not show a repeating material defect;
- next proposed change is taste churn rather than evidence-based improvement.

### REOPEN when
- independent readers converge on the same failure;
- audio exposes repeated cadence/orientation problems;
- continuity/canon contradiction is found;
- evidence provenance is wrong;
- a chapter/scene does not cause the promised downstream state;
- acquisition/editorial feedback identifies a concrete market-facing defect that can be repaired without corrupting story truth.

**Gate:** `NO NEW EVIDENCE → NO NEW GLOBAL REWRITE`.

## 4. Historical-market firewall — §§36–38

Bickham's manuscript-format, postal-submission, response-time and rejection-count advice is historically situated. It is useful as publishing history, not current operational law.

### Reject as current requirements
- specific paper weights/margins;
- disk backup instructions as state-of-the-art practice;
- SASE/postal conventions;
- fixed response-time expectations;
- fixed number of rejections before a manuscript has been “tried”;
- any 1992 assumption about agent/editor access.

### Retain only stable functions
- follow the current recipient's actual submission requirements;
- remove avoidable presentation friction;
- preserve redundant backups/version history;
- distinguish rejection/market fit from craft diagnosis;
- keep creating new work instead of letting one submission freeze the whole practice.

**Important:** any current publishing-market decision must be checked against current primary/industry sources at the time of submission. This craft book does not supply 2026 market truth.

## 5. Cross-school comparison

### Bickham + Bell
Strong convergence: cool the draft, read it as a reader, then move from macro diagnosis toward polish. Bell supplies the stronger correction that outline/planning is provisional.

### Bickham + LaPlante
LaPlante protects discovery and organic form from over-systematization. Use Bickham's checklist **after** a live draft exists, not as a preflight cage around every sentence.

### Bickham + Zinsser / Prose / Le Guin
Bickham's forward-motion rhetoric must not become short-sentence density. Later style sources show that clarity, rhythm and reader-state continuity can require long syntax and full paragraphs.

### Bickham + Zunshine / le Carré
Bickham's “story logic” becomes more rigorous when upgraded to source provenance and evidence status. Characters can act rationally on a false model; the narrative must preserve how that model was formed.

### Bickham + Card / Herman
Cast economy is subordinate to personhood. A minor functional role can be telescoped; an autonomous agent with independent wants, relationships, consent or source provenance cannot be erased merely for efficiency.

## 6. New production gates

- **B140-G01 READER-FIRST REVISION:** first post-cooling read is diagnostic, not line-editing.
- **B140-G02 ONE-PASS-ONE-QUESTION:** each revision pass has one dominant defect layer.
- **B140-G03 MACRO-BEFORE-MICRO:** wording pass forbidden while causal/scene/POV defects remain open.
- **B140-G04 OPENING FUNCTION NOT THREAT:** opening must generate forward pressure, not mandatory danger.
- **B140-G05 POV ACCESS VALUE:** POV distribution follows unique narrative value, not quota.
- **B140-G06 TIME-SCHEME AUDIT:** chronology, travel, duration and sequence must remain physically/socially coherent.
- **B140-G07 CAUSAL INVOLVEMENT:** protagonist/agent remains because responsibility, relation, evidence or world conditions justify continued involvement.
- **B140-G08 COINCIDENCE SOLUTION BAN:** coincidence may complicate but does not repeatedly solve.
- **B140-G09 CHAPTER END CHANGED STATE:** read-on value does not require disaster/cliffhanger.
- **B140-G10 LOCAL CLOSURE / GLOBAL OPENNESS:** book question closes while saga ontology may remain bounded unknown.
- **B140-G11 REVISION STOP RULE:** no endless revision without new evidence.
- **B140-G12 HISTORICAL MARKET FIREWALL:** old submission specifics never become current policy.
- **B140-G13 BACKUP/PROVENANCE:** current files require versioning/backups/source-of-record discipline.
- **B140-G14 CONTINUE CREATING:** publication/submission waiting must not halt generation of future work.

## 7. Failure modes added

- REVISION OMNIBUS — every desk edits at once.
- LINE-EDIT PREMATURITY — beautiful sentences inside structurally wrong scenes.
- THREAT RETROFIT — moving disaster earlier solely to satisfy opening dogma.
- CLIFFHANGER HOMOGENIZATION — every chapter ends with the same artificial beat.
- ENDING ANSWER-KEY — local ending explains global saga metaphysics.
- PLAN COMPLIANCE OVER STORY TRUTH.
- INFINITE REVISION WITHOUT READER EVIDENCE.
- SUBMISSION-ERA LEAK — historical market mechanics treated as current rules.
- REJECTION-AS-CRAFT-PROOF — market outcome treated as direct evidence of manuscript quality.

## 8. LESSON ZERO / future-book application

### LESSON ZERO Book One
Remain development-locked. Bickham's completed book supplies **diagnostic gates**, not a reason to rewrite.

If reopened for a concrete defect, use the isolated-pass architecture and touch only the proven layer.

### Orbital Youth / future IVDIVO
Protect competent success. Do not insert threat/disaster merely because Bickham prefers it. A better chain remains:
`COMPETENT ACTION → REAL SUCCESS → MORE TRUST/ACCESS/RESPONSIBILITY → HARDER NEXT CHOICE`.

### Mystery / consciousness schools
Ending may establish a bounded operational fact while leaving ontology unknown. Do not convert a desire for an unequivocal ending into metaphysical overclaim.

### Conscious nonhuman persons
Story logic applies to the nonhuman agent's own reasons as well as the human protagonist's. Human need does not substitute for robot/nonhuman motive or consent.

## Final synthesis
Bickham's greatest durable value is **troubleshooting discipline**: isolate the problem, restore causal and reader clarity, then stop. His weakest material is where 1992 commercial conventions are presented as universal craft or current market law.

The IVDIVO implementation is therefore diagnostic, layered, evidence-driven and explicitly anti-formula.
