# 1. PROVISIONAL MECHANISMS

## B78-M01 — SIMPLE ≠ SHORT

Clark's simplicity principle is best operationalized as:

`UNDERSTAND THE THING → NAME IT WITH THE CLEAREST ADEQUATE LANGUAGE → RETAIN NECESSARY COMPLEXITY → REMOVE PRESTIGE FOG`

A long sentence may be simple in logic; a short sentence may be opaque. The production target is intelligibility, not low sentence length.

### IVDIVO use

Technical orbital vocabulary is acceptable when it names a real mechanism. Terms such as `allocation`, `priority`, `qualification`, `access`, `contribution`, `assignment`, and `service` must not become euphemisms that conceal who decides, who acts, who may refuse, or what is actually scarce.

### Failure mode

**TECHNICAL PRESTIGE FOG** — terminology creates an impression of advanced civilization while hiding causality or smuggling obsolete economic assumptions back into the world.

---

## B78-M02 — DISTINCTIVE WORD ECHO CONTROL

Purposeful repetition can bind a passage or book. Accidental repetition of a distinctive word can make prose feel mechanically generated.

Operational test:

`REPEATED WORD / IMAGE → deliberate motif? → meaning changed or intensified? → YES keep / NO vary or cut`

### Failure mode

**DISTINCTIVE ECHO WITHOUT DEVELOPMENT** — a noticeable word returns because the drafting model is stuck, not because the story is creating resonance.

---

## B78-M03 — SPECIFICITY CREATES REALITY

Concrete names, sensory particulars, local nouns and precise actions can carry world information more efficiently than generalized explanation.

IVDIVO transformation:

`ABSTRACT WORLD CLAIM → 2–4 causal ordinary-life particulars → character response → reader inference`

Example class, not prose prescription: post-money civilization should be legible through what is trivial to obtain and what remains nonfungible — time, trust, consent, a specific role, a unique opportunity, reputation, lived skill.

### Failure mode

**WORLD THESIS BEFORE LIFE** — the narration announces how civilization works before the reader has encountered people living inside those rules.

---

## B78-M04 — ASSOCIATIVE RIFF AS GENERATION, NOT CANON

Free association can generate unexpected images, mechanisms or scene directions. It is a discovery method, not evidence that every generated connection belongs in the final story.

`RIFF → OPTIONS → CHARACTER/WORLD/CAUSALITY TEST → SELECT → CANON ONLY AFTER VALIDATION`

### Failure mode

**ASSOCIATION = CANON** — a clever thematic link is mistaken for a world fact or plot necessity.

---

## B78-M05 — SENTENCE LENGTH CONTROLS READER MOTION

The useful finding is functional variation:

- longer syntax can accumulate, continue, delay, observe, reason or sustain pressure;
- shorter syntax can stop, pivot, strike, isolate or release.

Therefore:

**`ACTION / SUSPENSE ≠ SHORT-SENTENCE DENSITY`.**

### Anti-fragment rule

Do not optimize a manuscript toward a target percentage of short sentences. Diagnose reader effect first.

`READER EFFECT → PACE → PARAGRAPH INTENT → SENTENCE RHYTHM`

### Cross-source convergence

Converges with Le Guin, Francine Prose, Lodge, Zinsser and McKee: contrast and function matter more than a numerical sentence-length norm.

---

## B78-M06 — PARAGRAPH = UNIT OF THOUGHT / ATTENTION

Paragraph size is variable. A one-line paragraph can be powerful when it performs a real break in attention, revelation, decision, timing or contrast. Repeated one-line paragraphs can destroy cohesion when they merely fragment one continuous thought.

Operational test:

`SAME ACTOR + SAME TIME + SAME PLACE + SAME THOUGHT/TACTIC/EVIDENCE STATUS?`

If yes across several short paragraphs, test merge before editing individual sentences.

### Failure mode

**PARAGRAPH CONFETTI** — line breaks create artificial urgency or pseudo-literary emphasis without a real shift in reader attention.

---

## B78-M07 — RESTRAINT UNDER SERIOUS MATERIAL

High-emotion material often becomes stronger when the prose does not demand a predetermined emotion.

`SERIOUS EVENT → SPECIFIC CONSEQUENCE / BEHAVIOR / DETAIL → READER INFERENCE`

rather than

`SERIOUS EVENT → AUTHORIAL EMOTIONAL LABEL → INTENSIFIER → SECOND EXPLANATION`.

### Cross-source convergence

Clark's restraint aligns with Iglesias's reader-effect distinction and LaPlante's warning about sentimentality. Tragedy, sacrifice, disability, rejection or injury do not automatically create depth.

### Failure mode

**TRAGEDY AS EMOTIONAL SHORTCUT**.

---

## B78-M08 — LADDER OF ABSTRACTION

Strong prose can move deliberately between concrete instance and larger idea.

IVDIVO router:

`CONCRETE OBSERVATION → PATTERN → LIMITED INTERPRETATION → RETURN TO CONCRETE TEST`

This is especially useful for institutional and consciousness-school material.

### Consciousness-school epistemic firewall

`PRACTITIONER OBSERVATION → REPRODUCIBLE EFFECT → SCHOOL INTERPRETATION → COMPETING INTERPRETATION → POSSIBLE ONTOLOGICAL CLAIM`

Do not jump from middle-level abstract terminology to world ontology.

### Failure mode

**ABSTRACTION FLOAT** — concepts accumulate without a testable referent, actor or consequence.

---

## B78-M09 — PROCESS AS SELECTION

The provisional Clark process reinforces an existing IVDIVO principle:

`EXPLORE / COLLECT → FOCUS → SELECT → ORDER → DRAFT → REVISE`

World database, research archive and reference library should be larger than the book. Selection is a craft operation, not loss of worldbuilding.

### Failure mode

**DATABASE DUMP AS PROOF OF WORLD DEPTH**.

---

## B78-M10 — EMPHASIS CREATES A PAYOFF CONTRACT

When prose gives unusual emphasis to an object, fact or setup, readers infer future relevance.

Operational distinction:

- **emphasized/promised detail** → requires payoff, reinterpretation or deliberate non-payoff with meaning;
- **lived-reality detail** → may simply make the world/person concrete.

This preserves the Ribó/Francine Prose correction: not every detail must become a Chekhov gun.

### Failure mode

**FALSE OBJECT PROMISE**.

---

## B78-M11 — INTERNAL CLIFFHANGER IS OPTIONAL

A chapter can end with an unresolved question, delayed answer or transferred pressure, but chapter-end hooks are not mandatory.

Allowed ending functions include:

`DECISION / CONSEQUENCE / REVELATION / RELATIONSHIP CHANGE / EMOTIONAL AFTERSHOCK / QUESTION / TEMPORARY RELEASE`.

### Cross-source comparison

Converges with Rosenfeld: cliffhanger monoculture flattens rhythm and makes every chapter feel like promotion for the next one.

---

## B78-M12 — KEY QUESTION AS ENGINE, NOT SLOGAN

A durable central question can help organize reader attention, but it must be tied to a concrete causal problem.

IVDIVO transformation:

`BOOK-LEVEL QUESTION → TRACKABLE PROBLEM → CHANGING ACTIONS / EVIDENCE / RELATIONSHIPS → LOCAL ANSWER`

Do not substitute a philosophical question for ONE MAIN STORY.

---

## B78-M13 — READER REWARDS / GOLD COINS

The middle of a book should not consist solely of withholding, setback and worsening pressure. Earned rewards may include:

- competence actually working;
- genuine intimacy or friendship;
- humor;
- discovery;
- beauty or wonder with consequence;
- a successful repair;
- a partial answer;
- an ordinary-life scene whose meaning has changed;
- real help from a person or conscious robot.

### Failure mode

**MISERY-AS-MOMENTUM** — every midpoint beat removes something, making the reader emotionally numb and making later stakes easier rather than harder to believe.

---

## B78-M14 — DEVELOPING REPETITION

Repeated image/action/phrase can unify a long narrative if its meaning changes.

`RETURN → CONTEXT CHANGE → MEANING CHANGE → READER RECOGNITION`

If nothing changes, cut or vary it.

---

## B78-M15 — VARY ANGLE / SCALE / MODE

A single causal story can legitimately vary observational distance and rhetorical mode.

Novel adaptation:

`READER EFFECT → INFORMATION NEED → FOCALIZATION/DISTANCE → DURATION → SYNTAX`

Do not import literal camera grammar into prose.

# 2. NEW / REINFORCED PRODUCTION GATES

### B78-G01 — SIMPLE/TECHNICAL DISTINCTION
Technical language must name a real distinction or mechanism.

### B78-G02 — HIDDEN ACTOR CHECK
For institutional abstractions ask: who decides, acts, may refuse, bears consequence, and can appeal?

### B78-G03 — DISTINCTIVE ECHO
A conspicuous repeated word must develop meaning or be revised.

### B78-G04 — DETAIL SPECIFICITY
Prefer character/world-specific particulars over generalized civilization claims.

### B78-G05 — SENTENCE FUNCTION
Sentence length follows reader effect, never an action/suspense quota.

### B78-G06 — PARAGRAPH BREAK INTENT
A paragraph break requires an actual attention/state shift.

### B78-G07 — RESTRAINT UNDER TRAUMA
Do not make the prose tell the reader how devastated to feel after the event already demonstrates its cost.

### B78-G08 — ABSTRACTION GROUNDING
Every important abstract term must resolve to observable rule, evidence, actor or consequence.

### B78-G09 — EMPHASIS/PAYOFF CONTRACT
Emphasized details create stronger payoff obligations than ordinary lived-reality details.

### B78-G10 — NO CLIFFHANGER QUOTA
Chapter endings rotate function.

### B78-G11 — MIDDLE REWARD
A long stretch of pressure needs earned positive/meaningful reader payoff, not necessarily plot victory.

### B78-G12 — DEVELOPING MOTIF
Repeat only with changed context or meaning.

### B78-G13 — PROSE NOT CAMERA
Use focalization/distance/duration rather than screenplay camera imitation.

### B78-G14 — POST-MONEY CONCRETE SIGNAL
Show post-money life through causal ordinary behavior, not economic exposition or disguised credits.

### B78-G15 — ROBOT DETAIL PERSONHOOD
A detail attached to a conscious robot should reveal independent preference, relationship, embodiment, decision or consent when relevant; avoid generic AI mannerisms.

# 3. IVDIVO APPLICATION — ANTI-“ОБРУБКИ” ROUTER

Current consolidated order:

`ONE MAIN STORY / CAUSALITY`
→ `SCENE NECESSITY`
→ `ORDER / DURATION / FREQUENCY`
→ `SHOW / TELL`
→ `POV / FOCALIZATION`
→ `DIALOGUE MODE`
→ `PARAGRAPH INTENT`
→ `READER-STATE CONTINUITY`
→ `SENTENCE RHYTHM`
→ `WORDING`.

Clark's provisional Tools 18–19 reinforce the lower half of this pipeline but do **not** supersede structural diagnosis.

A short sentence may remain if it performs a true strike, pivot, contrast, timing beat, revelation or stop. Several short paragraphs carrying the same continuous thought should first be tested as one coherent paragraph.
