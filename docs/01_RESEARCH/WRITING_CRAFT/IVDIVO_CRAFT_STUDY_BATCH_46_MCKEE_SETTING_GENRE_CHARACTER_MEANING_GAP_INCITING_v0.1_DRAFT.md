## 1. High-value mechanisms retained

### 1.1 Setting is a possibility system
Useful formulation:
`WORLD RULE → POSSIBLE ACTIONS → IMPOSSIBLE/UNLIKELY ACTIONS → CHARACTER CHOICE → CONSEQUENCE`.

Setting is not a catalog of attractive facts. Its function is to make certain actions, mistakes, relationships and consequences possible while ruling others out.

### 1.2 Small enough to know deeply
McKee's strongest setting advice is not literal smallness but **knowability**. A saga may imply an immense civilization, while one novel needs a deeply understood dramatic subset.

IVDIVO transformation:
`VAST CANON WORLD ≠ VAST NOVEL FOCUS`.

### 1.3 Research must feed invention
Three useful research channels:
- memory;
- imagination;
- factual/source research.

But the production test is:
`WHAT STORY CHOICE CHANGED BECAUSE OF THIS RESEARCH?`

If nothing changed, the research may still be useful reference work, but it has not yet become story development.

### 1.4 Generate alternatives before committing
For a major scene/turn:
`FIRST PLAUSIBLE IDEA → 5+ ALTERNATIVES → CHARACTER/WORLD TEST → FRESHNESS TEST → CAUSAL TEST → CHOOSE`.

This converges with Bernays/Painter Five-Fork and current IVDIVO Red Team practice.

### 1.5 Genre = promise + constraint + evolving convention
Useful core:
- readers bring expectations;
- conventions can help orient them;
- freshness comes from execution and changed social reality;
- conventions evolve as society evolves.

Therefore a future post-money YA/SF novel cannot inherit current-market assumptions unchanged.

### 1.6 Character is expressed in consequential choice
McKee's strong version:
`PRESSURE → ALTERNATIVES → CHOICE → ACTION → CONSEQUENCE → READER MODEL OF PERSON`.

IVDIVO correction: pressure need not mean catastrophe. A bounded choice involving trust, consent, loyalty, responsibility, privacy, autonomy or a unique opportunity can reveal character strongly.

### 1.7 Structure and character are co-designed
Changing a character's operative decision logic without changing the event sequence is usually cosmetic. If the person would now choose differently, the story architecture must absorb that change.

### 1.8 Meaning should be carried by consequences
Retained principle:
`VALUE CONFLICT → COMPETING ACTIONS → CONSEQUENCES → READER INFERENCE`.

Do not make ethical/philosophical dialogue perform work that the events failed to perform.

### 1.9 Counter-Idea must be strong
A story becomes didactic when one side is allowed intelligence/evidence and the other is made stupid.

IVDIVO form:
`PREFERRED MODEL ↔ COMPETENT COUNTERMODEL`.

Both need real benefits, evidence and costs.

### 1.10 The expectation/result gap
Useful causal mechanism:
`DESIRE → SUBJECTIVELY REASONABLE ACTION → EXPECTED RESPONSE → ACTUAL RESPONSE → GAP → MODEL UPDATE → NEW ACTION`.

This is strong when the gap reveals something true about person/world/system.

### 1.11 Reaction is often more original than event
A familiar event can remain fresh when different people interpret and answer it differently.

This is especially valuable for ensemble YA:
`ONE EVENT → FIVE PERSON-SPECIFIC REACTIONS → DIFFERENT CHOICES / RELATIONSHIP EFFECTS`.

### 1.12 Inciting incident defined by function, not volume
Retained function:
- changes the relevant equilibrium;
- creates a durable problem/desire;
- launches the central causal engine;
- changes what the protagonist/team must now do.

A quiet social or procedural event may be stronger than an explosion.

### 1.13 Start when the moment is ripe
Useful rule:
`MINIMUM CONTEXT REQUIRED FOR FULL READER RESPONSE → CENTRAL DISTURBANCE`.

Reject fixed percentages as novel law.

## 2. Important corrections / rejected universals

### Gaps are not mandatory every beat
McKee's formulation easily becomes complication addiction.

Reject:
`ACTION → ALWAYS WORSE/DIFFERENT RESULT`.

Allow:
- earned competence;
- correct prediction;
- cooperation;
- intimacy;
- recovery;
- successful training;
- a system genuinely working.

These remain dramatic when they alter trust, responsibility, dependency, evidence or future stakes.

### Maximum risk is not the measure of all value
For post-money Orbital Youth, meaningful price may be:
- a relationship;
- trust;
- privacy;
- consent;
- responsibility;
- reputation;
- a unique mission or experience;
- access to one specific person/place/time window;
- having to admit error publicly;
- losing autonomy.

Do not escalate automatically to death/physical catastrophe.

### Hidden opposite self is not mandatory
A major character does not need a theatrical contradiction between surface and `true character` merely because McKee prefers this form.

Complexity may arise from:
- competing good values;
- different selves in different relationships;
- incomplete self-knowledge;
- changing strategy;
- accumulated consequences.

### Deep arc is not mandatory for every major person
Some characters reveal themselves, deepen, or change relationships without undergoing total inner transformation.

### Genre taxonomy is historical, not ontological
McKee's 1990s categories are useful lenses. They are not a final map of contemporary fiction, YA, audio, serial, hybrid or future-civilization storytelling.

### `Obligatory Scene` is a promise test, not one standardized showdown
Keep:
`WHAT CONFRONTATION / DECISION / PAYOFF HAS THE STORY TAUGHT THE READER TO EXPECT?`

Reject mandatory physical face-off.

### Inciting incident at 25% is not a production KPI
The right location is the earliest point where the reader can understand why the disruption matters.

## 3. Cross-source synthesis

### McKee × Truby
Shared:
- causal structure over cosmetic polish;
- world constraints;
- theme demonstrated by action.

Added by McKee:
- expectation/result gap;
- audience promise through genre;
- setting as probability contract.

Added by Truby:
- clearer reveal/action information-state logic;
- more explicit scene weave and action-learning progression.

### McKee × Rosenfeld
Rosenfeld is the strongest correction against gap/complication monoculture:
- scenes may succeed;
- support can be dramatic;
- contemplation may cause next action;
- transition economy matters.

### McKee × Card
McKee: choice under pressure.
Card: independent personhood, relationship-specific self, reader contract.

IVDIVO synthesis:
`CHOICE REVEALS PERSON, BUT PERSON IS MORE THAN A PLOT FUNCTION`.

### McKee × Herman/Oatley
McKee's subjective expectation model converges with goals/plans/world response.
Herman/Oatley add a crucial distinction:
`BEHAVIOR ≠ REASON ≠ CONSENT`.

### McKee × Le Guin / Prose / Clark
Structural repair first; prose still matters afterward. Do not translate pressure into permanent short-sentence cadence.

## 4. Production gates B46

**B46-G01 WORLD POSSIBILITY** — every emphasized world rule changes credible action/choice/consequence.

**B46-G02 KNOWABLE NOVEL WORLD** — vast saga context is allowed; dramatized subset must be deeply knowable.

**B46-G03 RESEARCH PAYOFF** — identify the design choice changed by research.

**B46-G04 ALTERNATIVE GENERATION** — major turns require multiple plausible solutions before commitment.

**B46-G05 GENRE PROMISE** — know what expectation is being created.

**B46-G06 FUTURE-CONVENTION UPDATE** — do not import obsolete money/work/status/romance assumptions into orbital society.

**B46-G07 CHOICE REVEALS PERSON** — important choices must expose a value/model/relationship, not only move logistics.

**B46-G08 STRUCTURE-CHARACTER COHERENCE** — changed character logic requires changed event logic.

**B46-G09 COUNTERMODEL STRENGTH** — opposition gets real intelligence/evidence/benefit.

**B46-G10 THEME BY CONSEQUENCE** — ethical idea survives removal of explanatory speeches.

**B46-G11 GAP CAUSES UPDATE** — surprise changes model or strategy.

**B46-G12 NO GAP MONOCULTURE** — expected success is permitted when it changes state.

**B46-G13 REACTION DIFFERENTIATION** — ensemble members do not all interpret the same event identically.

**B46-G14 INCITING FUNCTION** — central disturbance creates durable causal pursuit/problem.

**B46-G15 MOMENT RIPE** — no unnecessary setup before central engine; no premature disturbance before meaning is legible.

**B46-G16 QUIET EVENT ALLOWED** — function outranks spectacle.

**B46-G17 POST-MONEY RISK** — meaningful value does not require price/wages/death.

**B46-G18 ROBOT FREE WILL** — a conscious robot can be protagonist/opponent only as a desiring agent with choice and consequence.

**B46-G19 AUTHENTIC DETAIL** — selective causal detail beats encyclopedic SF explanation.

**B46-G20 NO AUTOMATIC CLIMAX MAXIMALISM** — climax must be decisive, not necessarily the biggest physical event.
