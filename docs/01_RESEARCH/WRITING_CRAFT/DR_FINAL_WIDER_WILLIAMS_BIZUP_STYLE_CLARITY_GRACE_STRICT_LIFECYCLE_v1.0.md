## High-value claims after strict read

### 1. Revision is the natural home of most style principles

Conscious monitoring of every stylistic principle while discovering a draft can paralyze production. Use the tools mainly after viable material exists.

**IVDIVO synthesis:**

`LIVE DRAFT → OBSERVED DEFECT → RELEVANT TOOL → REPAIR → RETEST`.

### 2. Clarity is strongly driven by agency and action structure

Readers process many sentences more easily when grammatical subjects make important actors visible and verbs carry important actions. This is a diagnostic tendency, not a ban on passives or nominalizations.

**Gate:**

`WHO ACTS? → WHO DECIDES? → WHO MAY REFUSE? → WHO BEARS COST? → DOES THE GRAMMAR HIDE THEM?`

### 3. Choppiness is often a topic-chain problem, not a sentence-length problem

A paragraph can contain individually grammatical, concise sentences and still feel jerky because each sentence begins from a new subject/topic rather than carrying forward the reader's active state.

**Reader-state model:**

`STATE LEFT BY SENTENCE A → WHAT SENTENCE B ASSUMES → MATCH / MISMATCH → REORDER / SIGNAL / MERGE / REWRITE`.

### 4. Old-to-new movement supports cohesion

Sentence beginnings often benefit from familiar/active material while new or important information often receives later stress. This is a flexible reader-processing pattern, not a mandatory syntactic template.

### 5. Paragraph coherence depends on controlled topic chains

A coherent paragraph usually has a relatively small set of related sentence topics serving one paragraph-level purpose. Topic-hopping creates the sensation of montage even when every sentence is clear alone.

### 6. Stress position is a real design tool

Important new information often gains force near the end of a sentence or clause. End-weight should be varied and functional; constant use becomes mannerism.

### 7. Concision is removal of low-value meaning, not sentence chopping

Useful compression removes repeated claims, empty framing, needless abstraction and bureaucratic padding. It may produce a longer single sentence if that sentence replaces five artificial fragments.

**Law:** `COMPRESSION ≠ CHOPPING`.

### 8. Sentence shape and length are functional

Long sentences can clarify complex logical relationships when well shaped; short sentences can emphasize, contrast or stop the reader. Neither is intrinsically superior.

**Law:** `ACTION / SUSPENSE / INTERIORITY ≠ SHORT-SENTENCE DENSITY`.

### 9. Technical noun stacks can conceal relations

Dense strings of abstract nouns force readers to reverse-engineer who did what to whom. Specialist terms are justified only when the audience can resolve them or when the term is itself needed.

### 10. Ethical clarity matters where prose carries authority

Obscurity can hide responsibility, overstate certainty or make contestable claims appear inevitable. Clear prose should reveal enough agency and logical relation for readers to evaluate a claim.

### 11. Source representation is part of style ethics

Quotation, paraphrase and summary must preserve source meaning and provenance. Compression must not manufacture a stronger claim than the source supports.

## IVDIVO operationalization

### Anti-choppiness router vNext

`BOOK ENGINE → CHAPTER FUNCTION → SCENE ADMISSION → ORDER/DURATION/FREQUENCY → SCENE WEIGHT/MODE → POV/EVIDENCE → EXPOSITION/DIALOGUE MODE → PARAGRAPH TOPIC CHAIN → OLD→NEW / READER-STATE CONTINUITY → STRESS POSITION → SENTENCE RHYTHM/CONTRAST → WORDING`.

If four fragments preserve the same actor, time, place and decision unit, test merge/reorder before editing the fragments independently.

### Institutional / post-money language

Terms such as `allocation`, `priority`, `qualification`, `access`, `contribution`, `assignment` and `scheduling` must resolve to actual agents, rules and consequences. They may not become euphemisms that silently recreate money or erase decision ownership.

### Mystery / evidence

`CLEAR SYNTAX ≠ HIGHER EVIDENCE STATUS`.

A clean sentence must still distinguish observed trace, reported claim, inference, hypothesis and corroborated fact.

### Conscious robots

Passive institutional grammar must not erase the difference between assignment, coercion and consent. More sensors do not create omniscience; linguistic clarity must preserve knowledge limits.

### Consciousness schools

Prestigious technical language does not upgrade epistemic status. Preserve:

`PRACTITIONER OBSERVATION → REPRODUCIBLE EFFECT → SCHOOL INTERPRETATION → COMPETING INTERPRETATION → INDEPENDENT TEST → ONTOLOGY CEILING`.

## Final synthesis

Williams/Bizup adds a missing micro-discourse layer to the IVDIVO engine. The main contribution is not a new preference for short or simple prose. It is the ability to distinguish:

`SENTENCE CORRECTNESS` from `PARAGRAPH COHERENCE`,

and to diagnose choppiness as a failure of **topic continuity, old→new information movement and stress placement** before treating it as a sentence-length defect.
