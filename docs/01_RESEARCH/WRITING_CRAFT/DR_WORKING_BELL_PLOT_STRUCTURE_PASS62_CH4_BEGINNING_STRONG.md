## PRIMARY MECHANISMS EXTRACTED

### 1. Opening Contract — IVDIVO Adaptation

Bell's commercial-language `grab the reader` is retained as a reader contract, not an action mandate:

`ATTENTION → ORIENTATION → PERSON/SUBJECT BOND → STORY PROMISE → FORWARD QUESTION / PRESSURE`

The opening may achieve this through action, voice/attitude, a frame, a teaser, a strange but causal ordinary-life situation, relationship pressure, evidence, or a meaningful disturbance.

### 2. Hook ≠ Catastrophe

A disaster, murder, chase, explosion, secret villain, ticking clock or immediate life-and-death threat is not required.

For IVDIVO, especially YA/orbital books, a hook may be:
- first real responsibility;
- a relationship imbalance;
- a socially meaningful mistake;
- a small anomaly with a credible cost;
- a competence test;
- a consent problem;
- a strange rule of ordinary life;
- a specific future opportunity that may be lost;
- a voice/attitude strong enough to make the reader inhabit the person.

### 3. Opening Mode Router

Before adding spectacle, test which opening mode best serves the book:

`ACTION / VOICE-ATTITUDE / RELATIONSHIP / MYSTERY-EVIDENCE / FRAME / PROLOGUE / TEASER / ORDINARY-LIFE DISTURBANCE`

These are alternatives, not a checklist.

### 4. Prologue Debt Gate

Bell treats prologue as functional rather than merely labeled. IVDIVO adds a stricter debt test:

`PROLOGUE INFORMATION/EFFECT → WHY CAN'T MAIN NARRATIVE DELIVER IT BETTER? → WHAT PROMISE DOES IT CREATE? → WHERE IS THAT PROMISE PAID?`

A prologue is rejected if it exists only to add generic danger, lore, ancient history or artificial mystery.

### 5. Teaser Debt Gate

A teaser can generate curiosity by borrowing a later charged moment, but it creates a debt.

`TEASER → SPECIFIC QUESTION → MAIN STORY CAUSAL CONNECTION → LATER RECONTEXTUALIZATION/PAYOFF`

Do not use a teaser merely because the true opening is quiet. First test whether the quiet opening already has sufficient voice, relationship, responsibility or anomaly pressure.

### 6. Bond Router — corrected beyond Bell

Bell emphasizes identification, sympathy, likability and inner conflict. IVDIVO keeps these as options, not requirements.

Reader bond can arise through:

`EMPATHY / FASCINATION / COMPETENCE / RELATIONSHIP / MORAL UNCERTAINTY / HUMOUR / SPECIFICITY / MYSTERY / VULNERABILITY / VALUES-IN-ACTION`

No mandatory trauma, jeopardy, victimization, pet-saving, wound or manufactured likability.

### 7. Sympathy Manipulation Firewall

Bell's jeopardy/hardship/underdog/vulnerability devices are useful generators, but can become emotional manipulation.

Before using them, ask:

`WOULD THIS EVENT STILL BELONG IN THE STORY IF IT DID NOT MAKE THE READER FEEL SORRY FOR THE LEAD?`

If no, redesign.

### 8. Ordinary-World Disturbance

A beginning gains movement when the current pattern is disturbed.

IVDIVO version:

`ESTABLISHED NORMAL → SPECIFIC DEVIATION → PERSON-SPECIFIC RESPONSE → NEW QUESTION / RESPONSIBILITY`

The deviation may be small. What matters is that it changes the reader's model or the character's next action.

### 9. World Introduction Through Consequence

Opening worldbuilding should be causal:

`WORLD RULE / CUSTOM / TECHNOLOGY → WHAT THE POV PERSON DOES DIFFERENTLY → CONSEQUENCE / RELATIONSHIP / QUESTION`

Avoid glossary-first orbit-city openings.

### 10. Opposition Personhood From Page One

Bell's end exercise asks the writer to justify the opposition from its own point of view. IVDIVO extends this:

`OWN WANT → OWN EVIDENCE/MODEL → OWN RELATIONSHIPS → REAL BENEFIT / LOGIC → LIMIT / BLIND SPOT → RIGHT TO REFUSE`

This is mandatory for conscious robots and highly desirable for competent institutions/adults.

## CROSS-SOURCE SYNTHESIS

- **Edgerton / Bell:** early disturbance and forward pressure are useful, but not synonymous with explosion/action.
- **Lodge / Francine Prose:** multiple opening forms can work; voice, character, place, mystery or conceptual pressure can carry attention.
- **Zinsser:** best general synthesis remains `ATTENTION + ORIENTATION + PROMISE`.
- **Rosenfeld:** first-scene intention and consequence matter more than mandatory prologue/action.
- **Iglesias/Oatley:** reader feeling comes from context and goal/plan simulation, not from merely naming a character's emotion.
- **Corbett/Card/McKee Character:** personhood and character-specific behaviour are stronger than sympathy hacks.

## FAILURE MODES ADDED

1. **Action-Opening Monoculture** — every book starts with danger because danger is mistaken for hook.
2. **Teaser Borrowing Without Payoff** — a future scene is borrowed but adds no durable interpretive contract.
3. **Prologue Lore Dump** — prologue exists to unload history/world facts rather than create necessary narrative work.
4. **Sympathy Purchase** — injury, victimization, rejection or vulnerability inserted primarily to buy reader affection.
5. **Cardboard Opposition at Opening** — antagonist/institution exists only to make Lead look good.
6. **World-Tour Opening** — strange setting is displayed but changes no immediate action.
7. **Hook Without Book Promise** — opening is exciting but trains reader to expect a different novel.
8. **Lead-Likability Compliance** — protagonist is softened until their actual character engine disappears.
