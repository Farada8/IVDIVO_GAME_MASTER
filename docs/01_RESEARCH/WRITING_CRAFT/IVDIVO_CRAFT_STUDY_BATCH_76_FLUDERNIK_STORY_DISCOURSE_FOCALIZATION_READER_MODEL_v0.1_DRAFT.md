## Fludernik — Story / Discourse / Focalization / Reader Model

# PURPOSE
Turn Fludernik's descriptive narratology into compact IVDIVO production gates without turning terminology into a writing formula.

# PRIMARY SYNTHESIS
`WORLD FACT → SOURCE/TRACE → DISCOURSE SELECTION & ORDER → NARRATOR/VOICE → FOCALIZER/ACCESS → READER MODEL → REFRAME/PAYOFF`.

The most important production distinction is:
**a story problem, a discourse problem, a POV problem and a reader-model problem are not the same defect.**

# B76 PRODUCTION GATES
## B76-G01 — Story / Discourse Separation
Before rewriting canon, ask whether the event is wrong or merely presented in the wrong order/duration/POV.

## B76-G02 — Paratext Contract
Title/blurb/genre/cover establish expectations before page one. Opening prose must honor or intentionally revise that promise.

## B76-G03 — Primacy Reframe Threshold
An early wrong reader model acquires inertia. Later correction needs real evidence/contradiction, not one explanatory sentence.

## B76-G04 — Who Speaks / Perceives / Evaluates
Track separately. Do not infer POV depth from grammatical person alone.

## B76-G05 — Fallible POV ≠ Unreliable Narrator
A sincere character can be wrong. Do not convert limited knowledge into authorial cheating or continuity error.

## B76-G06 — Narrative-Level Provenance
Character/archive/institution/school assertions keep their source label until independently upgraded.

## B76-G07 — Causal Strand Convergence
Parallel strands converge only when their interaction changes action, evidence, relationship or decision. A recap meeting does not count.

## B76-G08 — Character Possible Worlds
Track conflicts among:
- what the character wants;
- what they believe is obligatory;
- what they think they know;
- what the world actually permits.
Useful for post-money stakes without fake material scarcity.

## B76-G09 — Order / Duration / Frequency Router
Before line editing slow prose, choose whether material belongs as scene, summary, slowdown, pause, iterative statement or omission.

## B76-G10 — Scene/Summary Equality
No automatic `show > tell` hierarchy.

## B76-G11 — Etic Opening Budget
Future-world characters need not explain their normality, but opening reader orientation has a finite budget of unexplained axes.

## B76-G12 — Reality-Effect Detail
Not every detail needs plot payoff. Some details legitimately create lived reality, POV, social texture or memory.

## B76-G13 — Constructed Discourse ≠ Falsehood
Selection/editing does not automatically invalidate a source; evidence status must be evaluated separately.

## B76-G14 — Speech Representation Continuum
Route dialogue through `reported → indirect → free-indirect → direct` according to reader effect and story function.

## B76-G15 — Figurative Source Ownership
Metaphor/image should have a discoverable narrator/POV source rather than one universal authorial lyric voice.

## B76-G16 — Nonverbal Mind Gate
Do not force every feeling/motive into explicit inner wording. Psycho-narration can represent nonverbal mental states.

## B76-G17 — POV Experience ≠ Ontology
A genuine experience does not prove the character's metaphysical explanation.

## B76-G18 — Typology Minimalism
Use only terminology that changes diagnosis or repair.

## B76-G19 — Medium Affordance Router
Preserve story function; redesign presentation for prose/audio/film/game.

## B76-G20 — Provisional Early POV Diagnosis
Do not classify an entire book's focalization from its first paragraph when later evidence may change the model.

## B76-G21 — Term → Observable Effect
Every narratological label used in production must map to a visible text effect and a potential repair.

## B76-G22 — Description Can Move Story
Description is functional if it changes subjective experience, orientation, attention, action options or symbolic/relational meaning.

## B76-G23 — Formal Novelty Decay
Present tense/unusual pronouns/other marked devices normalize over reading time; novelty cannot carry the whole effect.

## B76-G24 — Chapter Break ≠ Beat
Do not retrofit chapter segmentation into beat-sheet mathematics.

## B76-G25 — Reader Evidence Gate
Repeated actual-reader confusion is evidence. Reader repair suggestions remain hypotheses.

# CROSS-SOURCE RESOLUTION
Fludernik does not replace Truby/McKee/Brody/Rosenfeld. It routes defects before those tools are applied:
1. Is the problem in story/cause?
2. in discourse order/duration/frequency?
3. in narrator/focalizer/access?
4. in reader-model framing?
5. only then choose scene/plot/line-edit tool.

# IVDIVO HIGH-LEVEL ROUTER
`CANON FACT → CAUSAL FUNCTION → DISCOURSE PLACEMENT → KNOWLEDGE ACCESS → READER MODEL → SCENE MODE → PARAGRAPH/RHYTHM/WORD`.

Do not perform cosmetic prose repair before this routing.
