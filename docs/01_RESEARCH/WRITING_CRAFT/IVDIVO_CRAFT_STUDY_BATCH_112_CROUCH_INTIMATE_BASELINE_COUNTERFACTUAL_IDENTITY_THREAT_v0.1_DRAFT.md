## 1. OPENING VALUE BASELINE
`SPECIFIC ORDINARY LIFE → WHAT THE CHARACTER VALUES → RUPTURE → READER CAN MEASURE LOSS`.

An extraordinary premise gains force when the reader first understands what normal life means to the focal character. Ordinary does not mean inert: relationship history, unrealized ambitions, small rituals and current choices can all produce pressure before speculative machinery arrives.

## 2. COUNTERFACTUAL DESIRE
A road-not-taken is useful when it is simultaneously:
- specific;
- psychologically credible;
- active in current relationships;
- capable of changing a later choice.

Avoid treating alternate-life fantasy as proof that the current life was a mistake.

## 3. INFORMATION-AS-LEVERAGE
`OTHER AGENT KNOWS PRIVATE FACT → POV CANNOT EXPLAIN ACCESS → THREAT + MYSTERY → MODEL SEARCH`.

Knowledge can be a stronger threat signal than physical aggression because it implies hidden history, surveillance, identity access or institutional reach.

## 4. OPENING FOREKNOWLEDGE
A narrator may explicitly signal that change is coming. Suspense can come from:
`KNOW RUPTURE IS COMING + DO NOT KNOW FORM/CAUSE/COST`.

This is an alternative to hiding the disturbance itself.

## 5. VALUE TEST UNDER PRESSURE
Theme should become choice-pressure:
`OLD/ALTERNATE POSSIBILITY → PRESENT THREAT → WHAT DOES CHARACTER PROTECT/REGRET/REFUSE?`.

Do not rely only on narrator explanation of theme.

## 6. PROSE RHYTHM
The opening alternates fragments with sustained interior syntax and future projection. Production conclusion remains:
`INTENSITY ≠ SHORT-SENTENCE DENSITY`.

## 7. FAIR MYSTERY WATCH
Early clues should later survive reconstruction:
- what did the other agent know?
- how could they know it?
- what physical/logistical details were specific?
- which assumptions did the focal character make?
- did narration hide anything the POV actually knew?

## 8. IVDIVO APPLICATION
For Orbital Youth, prefer a causally specific ordinary-life baseline before major speculative rupture when the book depends on belonging/identity stakes. Do not automatically substitute kidnapping, lethal threat or family-endangerment.

For conscious robots/nonhuman persons, an identity/counterfactual story still requires independent agency. "What if this person had made another choice?" is not permission to reduce another person to a branch of the protagonist's self-exploration.

For LESSON ZERO Book One: no current reason to reopen development lock. Use only as a prospective opening/identity diagnostic if a future manuscript exposes this exact problem.

## 9. FAILURE MODE WATCHES
- physical-threat escalation treadmill;
- family used only as emotional hostage/stakes;
- counterfactual theme overexplained;
- speculative mechanism mistaken for scientific authority;
- mystery reveal dependent on unfair POV withholding.
