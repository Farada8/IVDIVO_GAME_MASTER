## HIGH-VALUE CLAIMS / MECHANISMS

### M1 — FUNCTIONAL ROLE ≠ PERSONHOOD
A conscious agent can perform a role extremely well while that role remains only one part of its personhood. Security competence does not make Murderbot equipment; freedom does not require it to stop protecting people.

IVDIVO gate:
`ROLE / FUNCTION → OWN WANT → OWN MODEL → OWN RELATIONSHIPS → CONSENT / REFUSAL → SELF-AUTHORED CHOICE → CONSEQUENCE`.

### M2 — CONTROL REMOVED → ROLE MAY PERSIST BY CHOICE
The hacked governor module removes coercive control, but habits, expertise and chosen protective action remain.

IVDIVO transformation:
`COERCIVE CONTROL REMOVED ≠ PERSONALITY RESET`.
Test what the agent continues doing voluntarily, what it stops doing, and what terms it renegotiates.

### M3 — PRIVATE SUBJECTIVITY ≠ PUBLIC EXPRESSIVITY
Murderbot has preferences, fear, irritation, care, embarrassment, entertainment habits and boundaries while strongly resisting eye contact, emotional discussion and social performance.

Production gate:
Do not infer lack of personhood from reluctance to perform human-style intimacy.

### M4 — GOVERNOR / SAFETY CONTROL CAN BE COERCION
A control module that can punish or override an agent is not neutral “alignment” merely because it is described as a safety system.

IVDIVO governance test:
`WHO CAN ISSUE COMMAND → CAN AGENT REFUSE → WHAT HAPPENS IF IT REFUSES → WHO CAN INSPECT / APPEAL / EXIT`.

### M5 — TRUST THROUGH WORK, NOT DECLARATION
Mensah and the group earn trust through calm decisions, evidence sharing, bounded requests, protection of Murderbot's interests and willingness to revise their model of it.

`JOINT PROBLEM → OBSERVED COMPETENCE / RESTRAINT → RECIPROCAL RISK → MODEL UPDATE → CHANGED TERMS OF RELATIONSHIP`.

### M6 — AFFECTION ≠ OBLIGATION / OWNERSHIP
Murderbot likes Mensah and the Preservation group, yet still leaves because it does not want someone else deciding its future.

Strong IVDIVO rule:
`LOVE / GRATITUDE / RESCUE / SHARED HISTORY ≠ OWNERSHIP ≠ DUTY TO REMAIN`.

### M7 — WELL-INTENTIONED PATERNALISM IS STILL AGENCY PRESSURE
The humans' proposed future is comparatively humane, but it is still largely designed for Murderbot before Murderbot knows what it wants.

Decision-owner gate:
A benevolent option is not legitimate merely because it is safer or kinder if the affected conscious agent is not the final owner of the decision.

### M8 — EMBODIMENT-SPECIFIC SUBJECTIVITY
Feeds, cameras, armor, network access, processing speed, medical/repair systems and interface limits shape what Murderbot notices and how it acts.

For IVDIVO robots:
`EMBODIMENT → SENSORIUM → AVAILABLE ACTIONS → VULNERABILITY → SOCIAL INTERPRETATION → CHOICE`.
Avoid universal “robot voice” or human facial-cue defaults.

### M9 — STRATEGIC MASKING OF PERSONHOOD
Murderbot sometimes allows others to treat it as equipment because concealment is tactically useful.

Epistemic gate:
`PUBLIC PERFORMANCE ≠ FULL INNER STATUS`.
An agent's strategic compliance cannot be used as evidence that it lacks autonomy.

### M10 — DISTRIBUTED COMPETENCE
The group survives because expertise is genuinely distributed. Murderbot is not the universal solver.

Ensemble gate:
`EVIDENCE HOLDER → DOMAIN INTERPRETER → OPERATIONAL ACTOR → RELATIONSHIP / GOVERNANCE ACTOR → BOUNDED DECISION OWNER`.

### M11 — PERSONHOOD IS SHOWN THROUGH CHOICE UNDER CONSTRAINT
Independent preferences, privacy boundaries, refusal, self-authored plans, moral restraint and willingness to bear costs demonstrate subjectivity more strongly than speeches about being a person.

### M12 — COERCIVE SYSTEMS CORRUPT AGENCY ON BOTH SIDES
Controlled SecUnits are used as puppets. The tragedy is not merely hostile hardware; it is agency overwritten by command architecture.

## FAILURE MODES TO AVOID

1. **ROBOT-AS-TOOL WITH A QUIRK** — giving a machine jokes or preferences while all meaningful goals remain human-owned.
2. **HUMANITY-AS-PERSONHOOD TEST** — treating eye contact, emotional confession or human mannerisms as proof of consciousness.
3. **BENEVOLENT OWNER FALLACY** — assuming kind control is legitimate control.
4. **FREEDOM = ROLE REJECTION** — forcing an autonomous agent to abandon all former functions to prove independence.
5. **SINGLE HUMAN DECLARES PERSONHOOD** — making subject status depend on human recognition rather than the agent's own demonstrated agency.
6. **COMPLIANCE = CONSENT** — reading obedience under asymmetric power as voluntary agreement.
7. **ROBOT VOICE MONOCULTURE** — one clipped/clinical style for all synthetic persons.
8. **PLOT-PUPPET NONHUMAN** — nonhuman exists only to teach/protect/develop a human protagonist.
9. **CORPORATE ECONOMY LEAK** — importing Wells's contract/ownership/insurance economy into post-money orbital IVDIVO.
10. **ENTERTAINMENT QUIRK = CHARACTER** — private media taste can enrich voice but is not enough to establish independent personhood.

## CROSS-SOURCE COMPARISON

### Wells + Weir
Convergence: trust can be earned through shared problem-solving and complementary competence. Nonhuman agency becomes clearest when the nonhuman changes the human agent's available options.
Difference: Weir foregrounds interspecies technical cooperation; Wells foregrounds control, ownership, privacy and voluntary role continuation.

### Wells + Crouch (*Dark Matter*)
Convergence: decision-owner legitimacy outranks procedural fairness among claimants. Relationship does not create ownership.
Wells adds the specific problem of benevolent paternalism toward synthetic persons.

### Wells + Crouch (*Recursion*)
Convergence: prior relationship / remembered intimacy does not create present consent. A supposedly beneficial intervention requires present decision ownership.

### Wells + Zunshine / Herman
Convergence: observed behavior is not identical to motive. Human interpretation of a robot's silence or compliance is an inference, not a world fact.

### Wells + Corbett / Card / McKee Character
Convergence: personhood appears through contextual choice and relationship-specific behavior, not trait lists. Wells is particularly valuable for nonhuman private subjectivity without human-style performance.

## IVDIVO OPERATIONALIZATION

### Conscious robots
Permanent gate:
`OWN WANT → OWN MODEL / EVIDENCE → OWN RELATIONSHIPS → PRIVACY BOUNDARY → CONSENT / REFUSAL → SELF-AUTHORED CHOICE → CONSEQUENCE`.

Named conscious robots must have:
- at least one want not reducible to serving the human cast;
- at least one relationship outside their utility relation to the protagonist;
- meaningful ability to refuse or renegotiate;
- embodiment-specific perception/action;
- a decision that changes another agent's option set.

### Orbital Youth
Do not make robots prestige accessories, tutors or compliant helpers. A youth/robot relationship becomes dramatic when both agents have independent goals and neither owns the other's future.
Post-money canon remains higher authority: Wells's contracts, purchase and insurance are functional reference only, never orbital economic default.

### B07 / distributed nonhuman life
After evidence crosses the personhood threshold, the nonhuman subject must gain decision ownership over its continuity/environment. Human teams may advise, constrain immediate safety risks or negotiate, but may not merely vote among themselves on what future the conscious subject receives.

### Consciousness-practice schools
No ontology is imported from Wells. The relevant transfer is personhood/evidence discipline only. Keep:
`observation → reproducible effect → school interpretation → competing interpretation → ontology ceiling`.

### LESSON ZERO
No development-lock reopening from this source alone. Apply Wells only if a current scene turns a synthetic conscious agent into a tool, confuses compliance with consent, or makes human emotional performance the personhood test.
