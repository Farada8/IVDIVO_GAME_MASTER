## 6. Material production consequences

### A. Relationship-specific character model
Major characters must exist across multiple relationships, not only as members of one protagonist-facing team.

### B. Reader contract
Major scenes and transitions should preserve:
- reason to care;
- reason to believe;
- clarity about what is known vs intentionally unknown.

### C. Character hierarchy
Do not give every established hero/cadet/robot equal POV, biography, jokes, secrets and climax functions in every book.

### D. Post-money Orbital Youth
Character desire must be rebuilt from values that cannot simply be fabricated: trust, responsibility, mastery, belonging, consent, intimacy, reputation, unique time/mission positions and lived experience. Do not turn these into hidden currency.

### E. Conscious robots
A named robot must have relationships, wants, refusals and obligations independent of being useful to the human cast.

### F. Specialist consciousness schools
Reveal them through practitioners, lineage, long training, reproducibility and disagreement—not generalized civilizational omniscience.

### G. Book One lock
No global rewrite of `LESSON ZERO` Book One was authorized. Card supplies no specific current defect strong enough to override the existing development lock.
