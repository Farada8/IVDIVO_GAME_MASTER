## 1. WHY THIS SOURCE MATTERS
Lodge is an anti-formula counterweight. Instead of giving one ideal architecture, he studies multiple working techniques in context. This is useful after Truby/McKee/Bell/Brody because it prevents production gates from collapsing into one aesthetic.

## 2. OPENING
A beginning is a threshold into an unfamiliar rhetorical system. The reader must learn voice, names, relationships, world facts and narrative rules while deciding whether the effort is worth continuing.

Production rule:
`OPENING EFFECT = ATTENTION + ORIENTATION + PROMISE`, not necessarily immediate action.

Valid dominant opening functions include:
- character contradiction;
- voice;
- mystery/confession;
- story question;
- place;
- dialogue;
- jeopardy;
- frame;
- metafiction.

Failure mode: asking the first two pages to perform every function at full volume.

## 3. AUTHORIAL VOICE
Narrator commentary is not automatically “telling error.” It may supply:
- irony;
- thematic scale;
- social/historical perspective;
- encyclopedic information;
- comic self-awareness.

Gate: authorial commentary must add a layer unavailable from simply paraphrasing the POV character.

## 4. SUSPENSE
Core:
`QUESTION → DELAY → ATTENTION → ANSWER/UPDATE`.

But delay need not mean acceleration. Detailed thought, perception and temporal expansion can intensify suspense when the unresolved question remains live.

IVDIVO rule:
`SUSPENSE PRESSURE ≠ SHORT-SENTENCE PERCENTAGE`.

## 5. YOUTH SKAZ
A convincing young voice is an artistic illusion built from:
`ATTITUDE + RECURRENT WORD CHOICES + SYNTAX HABITS + HYPERBOLE/UNDERSTATEMENT + SOCIAL JUDGMENT + IMPLICATION + RHYTHM`.

Literal transcription of speech is not the target. Topical slang is fragile and insufficient.

For Orbital Youth, voice should emerge from what the youth take for granted:
- post-money material abundance;
- robots as social persons;
- orbital movement/infrastructure;
- different adult institutions;
- unusual privacy/identity assumptions.

They should not sound like 2020s Earth teenagers with space nouns pasted in.

## 6. ADDRESSEE AS VOICE ENGINE
Letters/messages/testimony differ from diary because the writer anticipates a particular recipient.

Use:
`RECIPIENT → DESIRED EFFECT → EXPECTED OBJECTION → OMISSION/EMPHASIS → SELF-REVELATION`.

Strong for:
- Aoife recordings/messages;
- OES reports;
- school disciplinary statements;
- robot-human formal communications;
- family messages.

## 7. POV
POV is not simply pronoun choice. It determines:
- perceptual access;
- information access;
- emotional alignment;
- moral weighting;
- which character becomes a subject rather than an object.

Lodge also demonstrates that close/limited POV does not require all narrative language to be identical to the character’s spoken vocabulary.

Gate:
`PERCEIVER → KNOWLEDGE LIMIT → LANGUAGE DISTANCE → READER ACCESS → FUNCTION`.

## 8. MYSTERY CONTRACT
Before designing clues, define what kind of answer the book owes:

A. **SOLVABLE PUZZLE** — reader expects causal answer.
B. **BOUNDED OPERATIONAL MYSTERY** — enough must be known to act/resolve case; ultimate ontology may remain open.
C. **IRRECOVERABLE HUMAN MYSTERY** — uncertainty is itself part of tragic/ethical meaning.
D. **RETROSPECTIVE REINTERPRETATION** — answer mainly changes how previous evidence is understood.

B03 currently belongs primarily to B, not A.

## 9. INTERIORITY MODES
Do not use one fixed “deep POV” texture.

Available continuum:
`EXTERNAL NARRATION ↔ FREE INDIRECT STYLE ↔ DIRECT INTERIOR THOUGHT`.

Each changes pace, authority and intimacy. Direct interior monologue can become slow and trivial if overused; modulation preserves texture.
