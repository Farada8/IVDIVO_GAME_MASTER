# IVDIVO CRAFT STUDY — BATCH 211
## Lisa Cron — Reader Models · Causal Meaning · Fair Reveal · Setup/Payoff · Revision

**Source:** Lisa Cron, *Wired for Story* (2012)  
**Lifecycle status:** `STRICT_LIFECYCLE_SYNTHESIZED_v1.0`  
**Class:** WIDER  
**Date:** 2026-08-22

## Why this source matters
Cron is most useful not as “brain science,” but as a bridge between:
- character appraisal,
- reader prediction,
- scene causality,
- setup/payoff,
- knowledge-state tracking,
- and revision.

The source is strongest where it asks what a fact **means to this person now** and how that meaning changes the next choice.

## Production mechanisms

### B211-G01 — Event Significance
`EVENT / TRACE → WHO CARES? → BY WHAT GOAL / VALUE / RELATION? → APPRAISAL → ACTION`.

Large objective event without person-specific significance can remain dramatically weak.

### B211-G02 — World / Character / Reader Separation
Track separately:
`WORLD FACT | POV ACCESS | POV BELIEF | READER BELIEF`.

Never let a character’s confident interpretation silently become canon.

### B211-G03 — Fair Reveal
A reveal is earned when:
`TRUE EARLY TRACE → PLAUSIBLE INITIAL MODEL → ADDITIONAL TRUE TRACES → DISCRIMINATING REVEAL → RETROSPECTIVE SUPPORT`.

No subplot invented on the last page.

### B211-G04 — Known-Information Fairness
If a close POV already knows a load-bearing fact, narration may not erase it solely to manufacture surprise.

### B211-G05 — Setup Debt
Any salient capability, warning, object, anomaly, relationship promise, or institutional rule creates expectation debt.

Resolve, transform, explicitly disconfirm, or remove it.

### B211-G06 — Causal Payment
`SCENE OUTCOME → LATER DEPENDENCY`.

If later chapters do not depend on what changed, test compression/merge/delete.

### B211-G07 — Genuine Success
Do not force `solution → worse problem` every time.

Prefer when appropriate:
`COMPETENT ACTION → REAL SUCCESS → MORE RESPONSIBILITY → HARDER NEXT CHOICE`.

### B211-G08 — Productive Uncertainty
Good mystery:
`CLEAR QUESTION + PARTIAL SPECIFICS + MULTIPLE PLAUSIBLE MODELS`.

Bad vagueness:
`SECRET EXISTS + NO USABLE PARTICULARS`.

### B211-G09 — Specificity Filter
`POV SALIENCE + STORY FUNCTION + LIVED REALITY → DETAIL`.

Reject sensory checklists and detail-density KPIs.

### B211-G10 — Motive Inference
`BODY LANGUAGE / PAUSE / SELF-REPORT / UTTERANCE → MOTIVE HYPOTHESIS`.

Require corroboration for motive, guilt, consent or personhood claims.

### B211-G11 — Backstory Present Payment
`PAST → PRESENT MODEL / RELATION / TACTIC / EVIDENCE CHANGE`.

No museum flashback.

### B211-G12 — Subplot Main-Engine Payment
`SUBPLOT CHANGE → MAIN ENGINE OR MAJOR LATER CHOICE`.

Breathing room alone is not sufficient reason for a large subplot.

### B211-G13 — Processing Variation
Reaction may be immediate, delayed, embedded, summarized or implied through later changed behavior.

Do not mechanically insert a microreaction after every beat.

### B211-G14 — Reader-State Feedback
Ask beta/audio listeners:
- what do you think is happening?
- what does the POV know?
- what do you expect next?
- what felt inconsistent?
- where did you lose causal orientation?

Their answers reveal model mismatches.

### B211-G15 — Science Firewall
Cron’s neuroscience claims are explanatory rhetoric unless independently verified.

Craft law must survive removal of the neuroscience explanation.

## Cross-source verdict
Cron’s strongest mechanisms are retained where they converge with:
- Zunshine/Herman/Oatley on mental-model updating;
- Ephron/le Carré on fair evidence and inference;
- Bickham/Swain/Scofield/Bell on changed-state causality;
- Genette/Chatman/Fludernik on knowledge/discourse separation.

Cron is corrected by:
- Le Guin/LaPlante/Hodgins/Scofield on quiet movement and show/tell;
- Card/Corbett/McKee Character on non-wound character depth;
- Genette/Kress on POV architecture;
- current evidence discipline on popular neuroscience.

## IVDIVO standing synthesis
`WORLD FACT → TRACE → ACCESS → APPRAISAL → MODEL → CHOICE → CONSEQUENCE → READER MODEL UPDATE`.

This is a production lens, not a mandatory plot template.
