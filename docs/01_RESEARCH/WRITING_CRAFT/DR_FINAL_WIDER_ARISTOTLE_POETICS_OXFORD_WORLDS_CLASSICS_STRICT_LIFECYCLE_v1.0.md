# DR FINAL — ARISTOTLE — POETICS — OXFORD WORLD'S CLASSICS

Status: `STRICT_LIFECYCLE_SYNTHESIZED_v1.0`

## Source passport
Aristotle's *Poetics*, in Sir Anthony Kenny's 2013 Oxford World's Classics translation/edition, is a foundational source for plot unity, causality, scale, discovery, probability/plausibility, representation and stylistic clarity. The volume is unusually useful because Kenny's Introduction situates Aristotle against Plato and later criticism, while the appended Sidney, Shelley and Dorothy L. Sayers texts expose later reinterpretations and limits.

Authority is high for the history of literary theory and Aristotle's own conceptual distinctions. It is **not** modern psychology, sociology, gender theory, science or a universal recipe for novels. Aristotle's prescriptions for Attic tragedy must be separated from transferable mechanisms.

## High-value mechanisms retained

### 1. `AFTER ≠ BECAUSE`
A sequence is not causal merely because B follows A.

IVDIVO gate:
`A → B` is load-bearing only when B follows from A through established probability, necessity, world constraints, choice, relationship state or evidence logic.

For scene/chapter audit:
`IF A WERE REMOVED OR ALTERED, WOULD B'S OPTION SPACE / EVIDENCE / RELATIONSHIP / DECISION MATERIALLY CHANGE?`

### 2. ONE MAIN STORY = unified action, not one person
Aristotle explicitly rejects the assumption that everything happening to one hero forms one story.

IVDIVO translation:
`ONE MAIN STORY ≠ ONE POV ≠ ONE CHARACTER ≠ ONE LOCATION`.

`STRAND A / B / C → EACH CHANGES THE SAME DOMINANT CAUSAL ENGINE → CONVERGENCE / PAYOFF`.

Chronological coexistence is insufficient.

### 3. Scale must be cognitively graspable
A work can be too small to register or too sprawling to retain as a whole.

IVDIVO translation:
`SAGA SCALE ≠ BOOK SCALE`.

A novel selects a bounded causal problem that can be held as a coherent reader model even when the world is vast.

### 4. Episode payment
If moving/removing an episode makes no discernible difference to the whole, it is not structurally load-bearing in Aristotle's strong sense.

IVDIVO uses a less absolute version:
`SCENE / CHAPTER → ACTION / EVIDENCE / RELATIONSHIP / AUTHORITY / RESPONSIBILITY / MODEL / FUTURE OPTION CHANGE`.

Satellites may still earn reader-time through character, atmosphere, humor, lived reality or preparation, but their function must be explicit.

### 5. Causal surprise
Aristotle's strongest surprise is both unexpected and consequentially produced by prior events.

IVDIVO translation:
`SETUP / TRUE TRACE → READER MODEL → EVENT / REVEAL → MODEL REVISION → RETROSPECTIVE CAUSAL SUPPORT`.

This is stronger than arbitrary twist insertion.

### 6. Discovery should arise from the course of events
Recognition by external token or authorial declaration is weaker than recognition emerging from prior causal action/information.

IVDIVO reveal ladder:
`TRACE → SOURCE → INTERPRETATION → ALTERNATIVE MODEL → DISCRIMINATING EVIDENCE → DISCOVERY → ACTION`.

### 7. Resolution must issue from the story
The internal problem should not be solved by a late external mechanism that has not been earned.

IVDIVO gate:
`NO DEUS-EX-MACHINA SOLUTION`.

New technology, authority, archive, hidden person, coincidence or metaphysical rule may not enter at the end solely to solve the book's central problem.

### 8. Core before episodes
Aristotle advises laying out the basic story first, then expanding episodes.

IVDIVO translation:
`CAUSAL CORE → EPISODE / SCENE EXPANSION → ACTUAL-TEXT VALIDATION`.

This remains a planning aid, not authority over a stronger live draft.

### 9. Plausibility is world- and model-relative
His `probable impossibility > implausible possibility` is useful when bounded.

IVDIVO translation:
A speculative fact may be impossible in present reality yet credible inside a clearly established fictional world. But internal world rules and evidence cannot be changed merely to rescue a scene.

`SF POSSIBILITY ≠ EVIDENCE LICENSE`.

### 10. Character choice and contextual consistency
Aristotle links character to meaningful choice and allows `consistently inconsistent` characters.

IVDIVO translation:
`VALUE A + VALUE B + RELATIONSHIP + KNOWLEDGE + CURRENT COST → PRIORITY NOW → TACTIC / CHOICE`.

Contradiction can deepen character when context and competing values explain it.

### 11. Staging/spectacle cannot substitute for story
Performance can intensify an effect, but the causal story should work without relying on spectacle alone.

IVDIVO translation:
`VISUAL / AUDIO / ACTION AMPLIFIER ≠ CAUSAL ENGINE`.

Especially relevant to audio/video production: great sound, music or imagery cannot repair a broken clue chain or decision.

### 12. Style = clarity plus controlled deviation
Aristotle rejects both flat all-common diction and writing composed entirely of exotic/metaphorical language.

IVDIVO translation:
`CLEAR BASE → SELECTIVE DEVIATION → CONTRAST / EMPHASIS`.

No ornament-density KPI. Metaphor is strongest when it detects a meaningful relation, but:
`METAPHOR ≠ EVIDENCE`.

### 13. Simultaneous strands are legitimate when germane
Epic can represent simultaneous incidents; variety is useful when the incidents remain germane to the issue.

IVDIVO ensemble gate:
`PARALLEL STRAND → UNIQUE LOCAL FUNCTION → EFFECT ON DOMINANT ENGINE → CONVERGENCE / PAYOFF`.

### 14. Contextual criticism before declaring contradiction
Aristotle asks whether a supposed inconsistency concerns the same thing, in the same sense, and under the same circumstances.

IVDIVO gate:
Before marking continuity error:
`SAME ENTITY? SAME TIME? SAME KNOWLEDGE STATE? SAME RELATIONSHIP? SAME SENSE? SAME SOURCE AUTHORITY?`

### 15. Detective-fiction paralogism — retained only as fair reader inference
Sayers highlights Aristotle's false inference pattern: a true consequent can lead the audience to infer a false antecedent.

IVDIVO fair-play version:
`TRUE TRACE B → PLAUSIBLE EXPLANATION A → READER MAY INFER A → LATER EVIDENCE SUPPORTS DIFFERENT CAUSE C`.

The trace remains true. The author does not lie about the evidence.

### 16. Mystery character reveal must remain retrospectively credible
Sayers' strongest useful point: the culprit/reveal may surprise, but the reader should later see how earlier behavior could support it.

IVDIVO:
`SURPRISE → RETROSPECTIVE SUPPORT`, not `SURPRISE → CHARACTER RETCON`.

## Claims rejected or bounded
- Plot primacy over character is retained as a diagnostic for causal structure, not a universal hierarchy for every novel.
- Aristotle's ideal tragic protagonist, mandatory fall, unhappy ending, pity/fear target and limited family-story prescriptions are specific to his preferred tragedy model.
- Statements about women, slaves, social rank and moral inferiority are historical biases, rejected as IVDIVO production norms.
- `Good character` is not a requirement that every important character be morally admirable.
- Tragedy > epic is a genre preference/argument, not a universal quality hierarchy.
- Aristotle's psychology of soul/emotion and catharsis is ancient theory, not contemporary clinical evidence.
- `Poetry more philosophical than history` is retained as a distinction between fictional modeling and historical particularity, not permission to alter facts/evidence.
- Sidney's moral/didactic supremacy claims and Shelley’s claims linking drama quality to societal virtue are philosophical rhetoric, not empirical laws.
- Sayers' formulation that detective fiction should close every loose end is too strong for series mystery and bounded ontology; the **book promise** must close, not every saga question.
- Sayers' `characters second` formulation is not universal; character, evidence and causal action interact.
- The source's ancient grammar/metre prescriptions are not modern prose rules.

## Cross-source synthesis

### Aristotle × Truby × Bell × Swain
Strong convergence on causal sequence and consequences. IVDIVO rejects their more rigid formulae while retaining state-changing action.

### Aristotle × Chatman × Genette
Chronology is not unity. Story facts and discourse presentation are separate. One action can be distributed across POVs/times while remaining causally unified.

### Aristotle × Ephron × le Carré × Zunshine
Mystery works through true traces, source lineage, interpretation and model revision. Aristotle/Sayers add the `paralogism` mechanism; modern IVDIVO requires fair evidence provenance.

### Aristotle × Card × Corbett × Herman
Aristotle's action emphasis is balanced by modern person-model work. Character is neither a dossier nor a mere plot puppet; choices emerge from values, relationships, knowledge and constraints.

### Aristotle × Le Guin × Prose × Williams/Bizup × Hodgins
Style should use functional contrast. Clarity is not equivalent to sentence shortness, and sustained short syntax becomes choppy.

### Aristotle × Breault × IVDIVO Book Engine
`resolution from the story itself` aligns with upstream authority, explicit dependency and no late unregistered story mechanism. Core-before-episodes maps naturally to versioned story core → causal outline → scenes, while actual text can still force a justified replan.

## Failure modes added
1. AFTER-BECAUSE FALLACY — chronology mistaken for causality.
2. HERO-UNITY FALLACY — everything involving one hero treated as one story.
3. EPIC-SCALE LEAK — saga/world scope overwhelms book causal scope.
4. EPISODIC SUCCESSION — episodes follow without probable/necessary state dependency.
5. TOKEN-REVEAL — recognition relies on arbitrary sign without causal preparation.
6. AUTHOR-DECLARES-DISCOVERY — reveal is asserted rather than earned by events/evidence.
7. DEUS-EX-SOLUTION — late mechanism solves the book from outside its established causal system.
8. SPECTACLE-SUBSTITUTION — visual/audio/action intensity stands in for story causality.
9. PROBABILITY-RETCON — world rules are bent after the fact to rescue an outcome.
10. METAPHOR-AS-EVIDENCE — figurative relation treated as factual corroboration.
11. RETCON-AS-REINTERPRETATION — early trace changes instead of later meaning changing.
12. PARALOGISM-CHEATING — author supplies a false trace rather than allowing a false inference from a true trace.
13. CHARACTER-RETCON REVEAL — twist requires previously impossible character behavior.
14. TRAGEDY-TEMPLATE OVERFIT — Aristotle's preferred tragedy treated as universal novel architecture.
15. ANCIENT-BIAS IMPORT — historical gender/status/moral assumptions silently become IVDIVO norms.
16. ORNAMENT MONOCULTURE — every line made metaphorical/unusual, destroying clarity and contrast.

## Anti-choppiness synthesis
Aristotle provides no support for fragment-density as narrative energy. His style argument is about clarity, appropriateness, mixture and controlled deviation.

Standing law remains:
`ACTION / SUSPENSE / EMOTION / DISCOVERY ≠ SHORT-SENTENCE DENSITY`.

Repair order:
`BOOK ENGINE → UNIFIED ACTION → CHAPTER FUNCTION → SCENE ADMISSION → ORDER/DURATION/FREQUENCY → POV/EVIDENCE → PARAGRAPH/READER-STATE → SENTENCE RHYTHM/CONTRAST → WORDING`.

## Final synthesis
Aristotle is most valuable to IVDIVO not as a 2,300-year-old formula to obey, but as an early causal theorist. The enduring mechanisms are unity of action, consequential sequence, earned recognition, bounded scale, internal resolution and contextual plausibility. The historical prescriptions around tragedy, morality, social rank and audience response are retained as intellectual history and explicitly prevented from becoming automatic production law.
