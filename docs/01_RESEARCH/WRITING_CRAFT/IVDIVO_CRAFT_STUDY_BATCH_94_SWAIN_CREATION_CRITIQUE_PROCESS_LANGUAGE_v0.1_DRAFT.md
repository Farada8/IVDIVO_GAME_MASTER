## HIGH-VALUE MECHANISMS

### B94-G01 — GENERATION AND CRITIQUE ARE DIFFERENT MODES
`CREATE / DISCOVER -> PAUSE -> REAPPRAISE -> DIAGNOSE -> REVISE`.
Do not demand simultaneous compliance with every production gate while generating live prose.

### B94-G02 — RULES AS CHECKLIST, NOT BLUEPRINT
Swain's own Ch1 explicitly resists writing by rules and recommends principles as after-the-fact diagnostic support.
Production implication: no craft source outranks current story evidence.

### B94-G03 — FEELING IS A GENERATOR, NOT EPISTEMIC AUTHORITY
A writer's excitement can identify fertile material, tone and reader effect.
It cannot establish:
- world fact;
- another person's motive;
- scientific truth;
- metaphysical ontology;
- consent.

### B94-G04 — PROCESS-ERROR DIAGNOSIS
When copy fails, search for the missed process link before cosmetically polishing the line.
`OBSERVED FAILURE -> LEVEL -> MISSING/WRONG PROCESS -> TOOL -> RETEST`.

### B94-G05 — MISTAKE PERMISSION
Discovery requires permission to be wrong. First-draft uncertainty is not a signal to add more rules.

### B94-G06 — TECHNIQUE LIBERATES ONLY AFTER INTERNALIZATION
The goal of the tool is facility: reduced cognitive load during later work, not visible obedience to a formula.

### B94-G07 — LANGUAGE ENTRY ROUTER (PROVISIONAL)
Swain Ch2 begins with three tasks:
`SELECTION -> ARRANGEMENT -> DESCRIPTION`.
Evaluate them through clarity, accuracy and specificity rather than grammar/status display.

### B94-G08 — WRITERS' ROOM STAGING
Recommended IVDIVO pass order:
`DISCOVERY -> CANON/ONE MAIN STORY -> CAUSALITY -> CHARACTER/PERSONHOOD -> SCENE -> POV/EVIDENCE -> DIALOGUE/SUBTEXT -> PARAGRAPH/RHYTHM -> WORDING -> READER TEST`.
No 100-agent simultaneous veto wall during discovery.

## FAILURE MODES
1. **RULE-AS-BLUEPRINT** — story exists to satisfy the system.
2. **ANALYST-KILLS-DISCOVERY** — critique interrupts every generative sentence.
3. **FEELING-AS-FACT** — subjective conviction is promoted to world truth.
4. **TALENT-DETERMINISM** — historical claims about innate top-tier talent treated as useful production science.
5. **1965-MARKET AS CURRENT** — magazines, agents, gender assumptions or commercial norms imported into 2026 production.
6. **EXERCISE-DISDAIN AS LAW** — Swain's personal dislike of exercises suppresses useful controlled practice.
7. **READER-BUTTON DETERMINISM** — narrative effect treated as mechanically guaranteed.
8. **MICRO-PROCESS OVERFIT** — later M/R machinery applied to every sentence without regard to summary, contemplation, narration or distance.

## CROSS-SOURCE COMPARISON

### Swain × Lamott
Convergence: loose creation must be protected from premature editorial control.
Difference: Swain is more technique/process oriented; Lamott more discovery/hot-spot oriented.
Synthesis: generate freely, then diagnose at the correct level.

### Swain × LaPlante
Strong convergence: creation and constructive revision are different cognitive jobs.
LaPlante supplies stronger stage-specific revision routing and protects organic form from formula.

### Swain × Bernays/Painter
Conflict: Swain dismisses exercises broadly; Bernays/Painter show controlled exercises can isolate components and generate alternate routes.
IVDIVO: exercises are optional labs, never substitutes for the live manuscript.

### Swain × Bell / Bickham-descended schools
Swain is a foundational ancestor of M/R and scene/sequel systems. Later schools often harden his heuristics into formula. Preserve function, reject monoculture.

### Swain × Herman / Zunshine
Needed epistemic correction: emotional plausibility and coherent causal narration do not prove motive, consent or ontology.

## IVDIVO APPLICATION

### LESSON ZERO
No global rewrite. Use Swain only when a live prose passage has a continuity/process defect.

### ORBITAL YOUTH
The generation pass should permit ordinary-life material, competent success and relationship scenes before structural gates select the strongest causal version.

### CONSCIOUS ROBOTS
Never use reaction mechanics to imply that a robot's outward response transparently reveals motive. Behavior, motive and consent remain separate.

### CONSCIOUSNESS SCHOOLS
A writer's or practitioner's felt certainty remains evidence about experience, not proof of metaphysical explanation.
