## 1. Structure map

### Introduction — `WHY THIS BOOK MAY NOT BE FOR YOU`
Frey distinguishes durable craft principles from pseudo-rules, while clearly limiting his own framework to dramatic fiction. This built-in scope warning is one of the book's strongest safeguards.

### Ch1 — Fictive Dream
Reader involvement through specificity, sensory experience, sympathy/identification/empathy, goal attachment and participation in character decisions.

### Ch2 — Suspense
Story questions, apprehension, menace, countdown/lit-fuse devices, fair hooks and payoff.

### Ch3 — Memorable Characters
Agency, goals, competence, distinctive detail, ruling passions, situational motives, contrasts and exaggerated/wacky factors.

### Ch4–5 — Premise
Premise as causal shorthand; causal chains, opposing forces, situational/multi-character designs, testing irrelevant complications, changing premise to explore alternate story directions, multipremise/subplot structures.

### Ch6 — Voice
Narrator vs author, tone, specific detail, narrative attitude, first/third-person myths, authorial presence, voice imitation as exercise.

### Ch7 — Author/Reader Contract
Formal/genre/POV expectations, fair play, unreliable narration, coincidence, verisimilitude, early promises and later payoff.

### Ch8 — Seven Deadly Mistakes
Timidity, prestige imitation, ego-writing, weak revision, persistence/lifestyle advice, drafting vs editing.

### Ch9 — Writing with Passion
Author concern, values, vision, commitment to material; mixed with strongly historical commercial/publishing claims and hyperbole.

## 3. High-value claims and mechanisms

### F01 — Pseudo-rule test
A rule that sounds literary or traditional may not survive comparison with successful texts.

IVDIVO translation:
`RULE → FUNCTION CLAIM → COUNTEREXAMPLES → KEEP AS TOOL / NARROW / DISCARD`.

### F02 — Reader-bond multiplex
Frey distinguishes sympathy, identification and empathy. Useful salvage: reader bond has multiple routes.

IVDIVO:
`GOAL / CONCERN + SPECIFIC CONTEXT + RELATIONSHIP + COMPETENCE / VULNERABILITY / FASCINATION + CHOICE UNDER PRESSURE → READER BOND`.

No trauma is required.

### F03 — Story-question legitimacy
A question introduced for suspense incurs a payoff debt.

`QUESTION / FUTURE POSSIBILITY → READER MODELS OUTCOMES → CURRENT PRESSURE → PAYOFF / MODEL UPDATE`.

### F04 — Nonphysical menace
Meaningful threat can be social, relational, professional, reputational or opportunity-based, not only physical.

IVDIVO post-money translation:
`trust / consent / privacy / role / belonging / unique opportunity / responsibility / relationship / safety`.

### F05 — Premise as causal hypothesis
Premise can reveal what events belong together, but it must not become a dictatorship over live material.

`PREMISE / THEMATIC CAUSAL HYPOTHESIS → DRAFT EVIDENCE → CHARACTER / CANON / CAUSALITY TEST → KEEP / REPLAN`.

### F06 — Reorderability test
If major scenes can swap positions without altering downstream states, the story may be episodic.

`SCENE A EXIT STATE → SCENE B DEPENDENCY`.

If dependency is absent, test merge/rebuild/reorder.

### F07 — Situational ensemble
Multiple people can respond differently to the same condition.

Useful only when:
`LOCAL RESPONSE → changes another strand / main engine → convergence / payoff`.

Shared vessel or shared setting alone is not ONE MAIN STORY.

### F08 — Subplot payment
A subplot earns space when its local changes affect the main story or a major later choice.

`LOCAL WANT → LOCAL CHANGE → A-STORY EFFECT → PAYOFF`.

### F09 — Voice as attitude + specificity
`VOICE = ATTITUDE + SALIENCE + SPECIFICITY + WORD CHOICE + SYNTAX/RHYTHM + RELATIONSHIP/REGISTER`.

Grammatical person is not narrative distance.

### F10 — Reader contract
Opening/form/genre/POV establish rules of attention and information.

`OPENING PROMISE + INFORMATION RULES + MODE EXPECTATIONS → FAIR PAYOFF / LEGIBLE RE-CONTRACT`.

### F11 — Unreliable narration fairness
`UNRELIABLE POV ≠ AUTHORIAL CHEATING`.

The reader must have enough evidence to recognize that the narrator's model may be wrong.

### F12 — Re-dream the scene
A scene with a structural defect may need to be freshly imagined from a different entry/objective/staging, not paraphrased line by line.

`STRUCTURAL DEFECT → RE-IMAGINE SCENE`.
`LINE DEFECT → LINE EDIT`.

### F13 — Draft/edit separation
`GENERATE / DRAFT → DISTANCE → STRUCTURAL DIAGNOSIS → REVISION → POLISH`.

This strongly converges with Lamott, LaPlante, Swain, Bell, Seger and Mayer.

### F14 — Passion as selection pressure
Author concern can guide which questions deserve sustained exploration.

`AUTHOR CONCERN → THEMATIC QUESTION → COMPETING MODELS → CHARACTER-TRUE CHOICES → CONSEQUENCES → READER INFERENCE`.

`AUTHOR BELIEF ≠ WORLD FACT`.

## 4. Failure modes / rejected universals

1. **PSEUDO-RULE REBOUND** — replacing one rigid rule with Frey's preferred rigid rule.
2. **SYMPATHY-BY-SUFFERING** — buying reader attachment with injury/victimhood instead of character causality.
3. **INNER-CONFLICT MONOCULTURE** — assuming every major scene requires binary inner conflict.
4. **MENACE MONOCULTURE** — treating suspense as continuous threat.
5. **COUNTDOWN RETROFIT** — inserting a ticking clock because suspense feels weak.
6. **PREMISE DICTATORSHIP** — forcing live material to prove a prewritten premise.
7. **REORDERABLE EPISODICITY** — scenes do not alter each other's option space.
8. **WACKY-QUIRK DEPTH** — exaggeration replaces independent motivation/relationships.
9. **DUAL-CHARACTER POP-PSYCH** — treating transactional-analysis style categories as scientific personality truth.
10. **FIRST-PERSON EPISTEMIC LAUNDERING** — narrator imagines offstage minds and text presents the reconstruction as fact.
11. **UNRELIABLE-NARRATOR CHEATING** — withholding known decisive information only to manufacture twist.
12. **MAXIMUM-CAPACITY HERO** — characters always act at theoretical optimum; intelligent mistakes become impossible.
13. **HARM-AS-COURAGE** — brutality/trauma mistaken for artistic courage.
14. **CRITIQUE HAZING** — useful reader effect reports confused with total authority over repair.
15. **OBJECTIVE-CORRELATIVE OVERREACH** — every emotion forced into a dramatized symbolic scene.
16. **RE-DREAM = RESTART EVERYTHING** — global rewrite when only one scene is defective.
17. **WRITER-BLOCK MORALIZATION** — fatigue, uncertainty or health constraints mislabeled cowardice.
18. **LIFESTYLE EXTREMISM** — sacrifice relationships/work/life as proof of seriousness.
19. **MARKET-1994 LEAKAGE** — historical commercial advice treated as current policy.
20. **PASSION-AS-SERMON** — author values delivered as speeches rather than consequences.
21. **100%-SUCCESS MYTH** — motivational hyperbole treated as forecasting.
22. **GENRE-HIERARCHY LEAKAGE** — commercial/literary categories treated as quality hierarchy.
23. **FORM-SWITCH WITHOUT CONTRACT** — formal novelty without orientation or reader payoff.
24. **COINCIDENCE SOLVES CORE PROBLEM** — chance repeatedly delivers answer/resources.
25. **INTENSITY-AS-FRAGMENTATION** — suspense translated into short-sentence density.

## 5. Cross-source comparison

### With Lamott / LaPlante / Swain / Bell / Seger / Mayer
Strong convergence: generation and evaluation are different cognitive tasks; macro diagnosis precedes prose polish.

**IVDIVO law:**
`LIVE MATERIAL → OBSERVED DEFECT → DEFECT LEVEL → RELEVANT TOOL → REPAIR → RETEST`.

### With Truby / Brody / Bell
Frey's premise can help expose causal structure, but is less reliable as a blueprint. Truby/Brody can similarly become formulaic.

**IVDIVO law:**
`FRAMEWORK = DIAGNOSTIC LENS, NOT STORY AUTHORITY`.

### With Zunshine / Herman / le Carré / Fludernik
Frey's discussion of unreliable narration and first-person access needs stronger provenance discipline.

**IVDIVO law:**
`WORLD FACT → SOURCE/TRACE → POV ACCESS → INTERPRETATION → READER MODEL`.

### With Card / Corbett / McKee Character / Collins
Frey's ruling passions are useful but insufficient as total personality causes.

**IVDIVO law:**
`BASE PRIORITIES + SITUATIONAL PRIORITY + RELATIONSHIP + CURRENT COST → TACTIC → CHOICE → CONSEQUENCE`.

### With Le Guin / Francine Prose / Lodge / Clark / Zinsser
Frey offers no justification for treating suspense as a short-syntax KPI.

**IVDIVO law:**
`ACTION / SUSPENSE ≠ SHORT-SENTENCE DENSITY`.

## 6. IVDIVO operationalization

### ONE MAIN STORY
Use Frey's causal-premise and reorderability tests, but never force every thread to restate the premise.

`SCENE EXIT STATE → DOWNSTREAM DEPENDENCY` must be legible.

### YA
Reader bond may come from competence, belonging, humor, responsibility, curiosity, autonomy, relationship or moral uncertainty — not mandatory suffering.

Strong escalation remains:
`COMPETENT ACTION → REAL SUCCESS → MORE TRUST / ACCESS / RESPONSIBILITY → HARDER NEXT CHOICE`.

### Post-money civilization
Translate old commercial stakes into truly scarce/nonfungible stakes:
`time / trust / consent / privacy / role / unique opportunity / reputation / mastery / belonging / relationship / safety`.

Do not reintroduce salary/rent/credits.

### Conscious robots / nonhuman persons
No human emotional or narrative template is required for personhood.

`OWN PRIORITY → OWN MODEL/EVIDENCE → OWN RELATIONSHIPS → CONSENT/REFUSAL → CHOICE → CONSEQUENCE`.

### Mystery
`QUESTION → TRACE/SOURCE → INTERPRETATION → ALTERNATIVE MODEL → CORROBORATION → STATUS UPDATE → ACTION`.

Relationship state never substitutes for evidence state.

### Consciousness schools
`PRACTITIONER OBSERVATION → REPRODUCIBLE EFFECT → SCHOOL INTERPRETATION → COMPETING INTERPRETATION → INDEPENDENT TEST → ONTOLOGY CEILING`.

Frey's authorial passion or fictive-dream rhetoric cannot upgrade metaphysical claims.

### Style / choppy prose
Repair order remains:
`BOOK ENGINE → CHAPTER FUNCTION → SCENE ADMISSION → SCENE WEIGHT/MODE → POV/EVIDENCE → PARAGRAPH INTENT → READER-STATE CONTINUITY → SENTENCE RHYTHM → WORDING`.

A beat is not a sentence break.

## 8. Final synthesis

Frey is most useful when read against his own opening warning: principles should help generate and diagnose dramatic fiction, not become holy rules. His strongest contribution to IVDIVO is a cluster of **reader-contract, causal-premise, suspense-question, voice and structural-reimagination mechanisms**. His weakest material is where those mechanisms are universalized into psychology, morality, lifestyle or 1994 publishing doctrine.

**Retained IVDIVO synthesis:**
`CLEAR STORY PROMISE + CAUSAL DEPENDENCY + CHARACTER-SPECIFIC AGENCY + FAIR INFORMATION + READER EFFECT`, while preserving framework pluralism, evidence provenance, personhood, consent and live-draft discovery.
