## 2. Strong mechanisms retained

### A. Relationship state progression
Romance progression is not chemistry alone. Each major relational scene should change at least one of:
- trust;
- desire;
- boundary;
- knowledge of the other;
- vulnerability/intimacy;
- expectation of future contact;
- consent/permission;
- willingness to depend;
- relationship definition;
- cost of separation.

Working engine:
`INDEPENDENT WANT/BOUNDARY → INTERACTION → NEW INFORMATION/RESPECT/ATTRACTION/TRUST → CHANGED BOUNDARY → PRESSURE → CHOICE → COST → NEW TERMS OF RELATIONSHIP`.

### B. External plot / relationship braid
Useful core from Hayes: the relationship arc and external story must affect each other causally.

IVDIVO version:
`EXTERNAL EVENT / CHOICE → TRUST / DESIRE / BOUNDARY CHANGE → RELATIONSHIP STATE CHANGE → THAT CHANGE ALTERS NEXT EXTERNAL CHOICE`.

If the mystery/rescue/institutional plot can be removed without changing the relationship, or romance can be removed without changing decisions in the external story, the braid is weak.

### C. Two complete agents
Hayes's "hole-hearted → whole-hearted" metaphor is not adopted as universal psychology.

IVDIVO standard:
`TWO COMPLETE AGENTS → UNIQUE MUTUAL PRESSURE → RELATIONAL CHOICE → PRICE → NEW TERMS OF RELATIONSHIP`.

No mandatory wound, trauma, fear of love, brokenness, or personality repair. Healthy people may still face timing, conflicting values, autonomy, consent, incompatible responsibilities, different future plans, privacy, trust calibration, or uncertainty.

### D. Adhesion becomes sustained causal contact
Hayes's forced-together beat is useful functionally but must not become coercion by default.

IVDIVO version:
`SUSTAINED CAUSAL CONTACT` may arise from shared work, overlapping responsibility, evidence chain, school/team, family/social network, mission, place, promise, reciprocal interest, or willingly repeated interaction.

A forced-proximity trope never overrides consent/personhood.

### E. Break-up / black-moment causality
Strong retained mechanism: a relationship rupture should emerge from established models, choices, values, boundaries, or prior omissions — not merely from a random explosion.

But literal break-up is optional. Alternatives:
- trust downgrade;
- temporary separation;
- refusal;
- renegotiation of boundaries;
- incompatible commitments;
- consent conflict;
- revelation that changes what relationship is currently possible.

### F. Grand Gesture becomes diagnostic costly action
Do not require public spectacle, airport race, humiliation, money, sacrifice, or life/death stakes.

IVDIVO version:
`RELATIONAL CLAIM → COSTLY / SPECIFIC CREDIBLE ACTION → OTHER AGENT REMAINS FREE TO ACCEPT / REFUSE`.

"Grand" means diagnostic: the action proves a changed priority or boundary at a real cost to this person.

### G. Ending = changed relational terms, not personality transplant
Hayes's warning against "too perfect" endings is retained.

IVDIVO:
`LOCAL RELATIONAL RESOLUTION → PERSONALITY CONTINUITY → CHANGED TERMS → OPTIONAL FUTURE VECTOR`.

HEA/HFN is mandatory only when the work is sold as romance-primary genre. It is not automatically required for a mystery-primary book with romantic elements.

## 3. Failure modes

1. BEAT CHECKLIST ROMANCE — every named beat appears but relationship state barely changes.
2. CHEMISTRY WITHOUT CAUSALITY — attraction/banter does not affect decisions.
3. WOUND MANDATE — inventing trauma because a beat sheet expects it.
4. TWO BROKEN HALVES — partner is treated as a cure for an incomplete person.
5. COERCIVE ADHESION — "can't walk away" erases meaningful consent.
6. MISUNDERSTANDING MONOCULTURE — repeated avoidable misunderstandings substitute for real value conflict.
7. BLACK-MOMENT ACCIDENT — random external danger produces the rupture without character causality.
8. GRAND-GESTURE SPECTACLE — size replaces specificity and cost.
9. LOVE-SOLVES-MYSTERY — romantic recognition substitutes for evidence.
10. EXTERNAL-PLOT DECORATION — external story never changes relational choices.
11. ROMANCE DECORATION — romance can be deleted without changing external decisions.
12. PERSONALITY TRANSPLANT HEA — characters lose their defining traits once paired.
13. CONSENT-AS-OBSTACLE — refusal is framed merely as something persistence must overcome.
14. ROMANCE WEIGHT OVERRIDE — full romance architecture is forced onto mystery-primary material.

## 4. Production gates

### B107-G01 Relationship State Change
Every protected romance scene records BEFORE state → interaction/evidence → AFTER state.

### B107-G02 External Braid
At least every major relationship turn must change a later external choice, and major external turns should affect relationship state when romance is structurally important.

### B107-G03 Two Complete Agents
Neither partner may exist as the other's missing piece or therapeutic device.

### B107-G04 Consent Firewall
Pursuit, adhesion, protection, secrecy, sacrifice, intimacy, and grand gesture never override the other agent's right to refuse.

### B107-G05 No Mandatory Wound
A wound may exist only if independently true to the character and causal.

### B107-G06 Sustained Contact Cause
Repeated proximity must have a believable cause after the initial encounter.

### B107-G07 Rupture Ownership
If a rupture occurs, identify which prior choices/models/values causally produced it.

### B107-G08 Relational Evidence
Do not call the couple "closer" without observable evidence: disclosure, changed behavior, increased trust, accepted dependence, defended boundary, negotiated consent, or future planning.

### B107-G09 Costly Credible Repair
Repair action must cost this specific character something meaningful and leave acceptance free.

### B107-G10 Romance Weight Dial
Apply density according to MYSTERY-PRIMARY / BALANCED / ROMANCE-PRIMARY. Hayes never overrides the project dial.

### B107-G11 Mystery Evidence Firewall
A kiss, confession, trust, intuition, or love cannot prove a mystery claim.

### B107-G12 Character Continuity at Resolution
HEA/HFN or relational resolution cannot erase voice, ambitions, friendships, work, disagreements, or independent goals.

## 5. Cross-source synthesis

Hayes + Rosenfeld: scene intention/outcome should produce relationship state change, not just a named beat.

Hayes + Corbett/Card: partners remain independent people with relation-specific selves and tactics.

Hayes + Zunshine/Herman: belief about the other is a model; behavior/evidence may update it but does not reveal motive with certainty.

Hayes + Brody: multi-track/relationship tracks may converge, but convergence requires causal interaction, not co-presence.

Hayes + McKee/Swain/Bell: structural commitment, reaction, cost, and consequence are useful; mandatory defeat/death/dark-night formulas are rejected.

Hayes + IVDIVO Romance Weight Dial: relationship architecture is scaled to the genre promise rather than applied uniformly.
