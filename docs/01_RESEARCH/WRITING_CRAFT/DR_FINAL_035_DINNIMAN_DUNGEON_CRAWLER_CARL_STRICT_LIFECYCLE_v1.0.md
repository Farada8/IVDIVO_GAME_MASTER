## CORE CLAIMS / MECHANISMS

### M1 — Spectacle Incentive Distortion
`MEASURED/BROADCAST BEHAVIOR → ATTENTION METRIC → RESOURCE/STATUS CONSEQUENCE → SELF-PRESENTATION CHANGE → REAL TASK DISTORTION`.

Views, followers, favorites and patrons are not cosmetic. They influence survival resources. Once actors know they are watched, performance becomes a second objective that can compete with the real task.

IVDIVO use: any rating, reputation, recommendation, qualification or visibility system must be checked for Goodhart-style drift. What does the metric reward that the actual mission does not?

### M2 — Official Legality ≠ Legitimacy / Consent
The extraction/game system presents catastrophic appropriation and lethal rules as legitimate procedure.

Gate: `PROCEDURE IS VALID → WHO AUTHORIZED IT? → WHO CAN REFUSE? → WHO BEARS COST? → WHO CAN APPEAL?`.

Institutional correctness is not moral legitimacy.

### M3 — Classification ≠ Personhood / Moral Status
Boss, mob, NPC, pet, crawler and sponsor are system categories. Intelligent targets repeatedly show fear, history, social organization, preference and suffering.

Gate: `SYSTEM LABEL → OBSERVED CAPACITIES → OWN WANT/MODEL/RELATIONSHIPS → CONSENT/REFUSAL → PERSONHOOD STATUS`.

A label can govern tactical rules without proving ontological or moral status.

### M4 — Designed Atrocity + Personalized Blame
The system constructs situations that incentivize killing, embeds vulnerable beings inside combat logic, rewards participation and then taunts participants for what they did.

Gate: `CHOICE ARCHITECTURE → AVAILABLE OPTIONS → INCENTIVES → ACTION → WHO DESIGNED THE OPTION SET → HOW RESPONSIBILITY IS DISTRIBUTED`.

Do not assign all guilt to the visible actor when the institution manufactured the choice set.

### M5 — Authority Narrative ≠ Evidence
Frank's plausible law-enforcement/moral story initially competes with Carl's suspicion. Later traces and broadcast reconstruction show that the account was fabricated.

Gate: `CLAIM → SOURCE → INDEPENDENT TRACE → INCONSISTENCY → ALTERNATIVE MODEL → STATUS UPDATE`.

Official-sounding biographies are not evidence.

### M6 — Edited Story ≠ Event Record
Broadcasts reorder, omit and frame events for entertainment. A montage can reveal true material while still manipulating causal interpretation.

Gate: `WORLD EVENT → RAW TRACE → EDIT/SELECTION/ORDER → PUBLIC STORY → AUDIENCE MODEL`.

A true clip can create a false causal impression.

### M7 — Consent Under Influence
Charisma, magical influence, platform incentives and social pressure can change behavior.

Gate: `BEHAVIORAL COMPLIANCE ≠ CONSENT`.

For IVDIVO, any powerful persuasion technology or high-status agent must separate influence effectiveness from legitimate agreement.

### M8 — Personhood Without Humanization
Donut gains language, intelligence, skills and new social agency while remaining recognizably Donut and explicitly choosing to remain a cat. Growth does not require becoming human.

For conscious robots/nonhuman persons: `DEVELOPMENT ≠ HUMAN CONVERGENCE`.

### M9 — Capability–Affordance Fit
The same item/skill has different value depending on body, stats, knowledge, environment and team composition.

Gate: `TOOL CAPABILITY → USER CAPABILITY → ENVIRONMENTAL CONSTRAINT → ACTUAL AFFORDANCE`.

Do not treat technology as a universal solution independent of operator/context.

### M10 — Accepted Responsibility as Story Adhesive
Carl repeatedly remains involved because he chooses responsibility for other people, not because of salary, debt or artificial imprisonment.

Useful post-money engine: `CHOICE TO HELP → REAL OBLIGATION → COST → NEXT CAUSAL INVOLVEMENT`.

### M11 — Duty Can Survive Institutional Collapse
Caregivers continue caring after the old institution/world has effectively ended. Role identity and relationship obligations persist even when formal structures disappear.

Useful for Orbital Youth: belonging and responsibility can be stronger stakes than material scarcity.

### M12 — High Technology ≠ Total Restoration
System healing does not erase every age/body/history limitation. Capability enhancement and embodiment remain distinct.

Gate: `TECHNICAL REPAIR → WHAT IS RESTORED? → WHAT REMAINS? → WHAT NEW DEPENDENCY/COST APPEARS?`.

### M13 — Rules Are Endogenous
Administrators monitor behavior, patch exploits, alter countdowns and react to participant strategies.

Gate: `AGENT DISCOVERS EXPLOIT → SYSTEM OBSERVES → RULE/POLICY UPDATE → STRATEGY SPACE CHANGES`.

In adaptive institutions, rules are not static scenery.

### M14 — Public Attention Is Governance Power
Shows, patrons and host institutions can reward, punish, expose, ridicule and reframe participants. Audience attention becomes an allocation mechanism and a political weapon.

Use with caution in IVDIVO: public reputation should not become hidden currency unless canon explicitly supports that mechanism.

### M15 — Moral Choice Under Incomplete Causality
Carl learns that his booby trap contributed to a child's injury and later death, but the larger causal chain includes parental choices, system incentives, misinformation and combat constraints.

Gate: `MY ACTION → DIRECT EFFECT → DOWNSTREAM CHOICES BY OTHERS → SYSTEM CONDITIONS → RESPONSIBILITY BOUNDARY`.

Avoid both total self-exoneration and total personalized blame.

## CROSS-SOURCE COMPARISON

With Zunshine/Herman: Dinniman strongly confirms provenance and interpretation separation. Public narratives, system labels and behavioral traces are distinct from motive/world fact.

With Weir: both reward evidence-driven competence and tool/context reasoning, but Dinniman adds adversarial incentive architecture and spectacle governance.

With Nevermoor/Skyward: all three show that institutional classification and prestige can reshape belonging; Dinniman is the strongest warning about metrics becoming direct survival incentives.

With Wells/Murderbot: Dinniman reinforces nonhuman agency, but Donut's arc warns against assuming increased capability requires human embodiment or human social convergence.

With McKee/Bell/Swain: DCC has extreme retention pressure, but its achievements, countdowns and boss loops are product-mode devices, not universal scene laws. IVDIVO retains state change, causal pressure and earned payoff without importing the entire gamified cadence.

## IVDIVO OPERATIONALIZATION

### Orbital Youth
- Qualification/rating systems require a metric-vs-mission audit.
- Post-money society must not convert reputation into hidden purchasing power unless canon explicitly defines it.
- A teenager can remain causally engaged through accepted responsibility rather than financial need.
- Formal membership, rating or administrator praise never proves legitimacy/competence/personhood.
- Public recordings must distinguish raw event, edit, interpretation and public reaction.

### B07 — THE UNLISTED CITY
- Once distributed nonhuman life crosses a personhood threshold, human classification cannot remain decisive.
- Human authorities must not decide its continuity solely as an asset-management problem.
- Required climax gate: `EVIDENCE → PERSONHOOD THRESHOLD → ENTITY'S OWN MODEL/PREFERENCE → CONSENT/REFUSAL → HUMAN OPTION SPACE CHANGES → BOUNDED ACCOUNTABLE DECISION`.
- If a system incentivized the harmful interaction, responsibility must include the choice architecture, not only the operator.

### Mystery / evidence stories
- Public footage is never automatically raw evidence.
- A persuasive authority account remains a claim until corroborated.
- Multiple traces should discriminate between motive/identity models.

### Conscious robots
- System category, owner label, task assignment or behavioral compliance does not establish motive or consent.
- Development must preserve nonhuman specificity where appropriate.

### Consciousness schools
- DCC contributes no scientific/metaphysical evidence.
- Continue strict ladder: `observation → reproducible effect → school interpretation → competing interpretation → ontology ceiling`.
