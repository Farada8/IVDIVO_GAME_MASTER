## Structure map

The novel's investigation is assembled through dispersed recollection, witness testimony, institutional records, access history, social networks and operational reconstruction. Smiley does not solve the case through a single privileged clue or unexplained intuition. The reader's model changes as apparently independent facts are shown to share provenance, access channels and institutional context.

## Core claims and mechanisms

### 1. Evidence provenance before belief

`TRACE / REPORT → SOURCE → ACCESS POSITION → BIAS / BLIND SPOT → CLAIM → CORROBORATION / CONTRADICTION → STATUS UPDATE`.

A statement becomes useful only after the story establishes who could know it, how they obtained it and what could distort it.

### 2. Source independence

`MULTIPLE REPORTS ≠ MULTIPLE INDEPENDENT SOURCES`.

The same originating source can circulate through different people and appear to become corroboration. A mystery ledger therefore needs both a **claim graph** and a **source-lineage graph**.

### 3. Official version is not world fact

An institution can sincerely stabilize around a wrong model. Repetition, prestige and internal documentation raise social authority but not evidentiary independence.

### 4. Institutional capture can be self-reinforcing

`ACCESS CONTROL → EVIDENCE FILTER → OFFICIAL MODEL → RESOURCE / STATUS REWARD → STRONGER ACCESS CONTROL`.

The institution need not be populated by fools or villains. Competent people can be trapped inside a distorted information architecture.

### 5. Compartmentalized competence

Different honest actors can each possess a correct fragment while remaining globally wrong. Missing channels, not low intelligence, can explain persistent institutional error.

### 6. Negative evidence requires an expectation model

Silence, absence, missing paperwork or non-response is meaningful only when the story has independently established what should normally exist or happen.

### 7. Relationship truth and evidence truth are separate

`INTIMACY / LOYALTY / BETRAYAL → ACCESS + PRESSURE + INTERPRETATION`, but not proof.

A relationship may explain how a secret was possible without proving who committed the act.

### 8. Memory is sourced evidence

A recollection has time, viewpoint, emotional context and possible distortion. `PRIVATE MEMORY ≠ WORLD FACT`.

### 9. Distributed investigation

Smiley's competence is powerful partly because other people own indispensable pieces of the solution. Evidence collection, witness access, technical reconstruction, memory and operational execution remain distributed.

### 10. Quiet suspense

Suspense can be produced through withheld provenance, unstable interpretation, conversational asymmetry and documentary reconstruction. `SUSPENSE ≠ SHORT-SENTENCE DENSITY`.

### 11. Information-state scenes

An interview or meeting deserves full scenic treatment when exact wording or behaviour changes evidence status, trust, relationship, interpretation or next tactic. Otherwise compress.

### 12. Fair retrospective support

A final reveal should permit backward reconstruction. Earlier details gain changed status after the model changes; they were not arbitrary decorations.

### 13. Proof ceiling

The investigation can establish identity, access and operational mechanism while leaving inner motive, emotional meaning or ultimate self-interpretation partly uncertain.

## Failure modes

1. **Source Laundering** — one source reappears through several mouths and is miscounted as corroboration.
2. **Claim Count = Independence Fallacy** — many assertions are treated as many evidence streams.
3. **Official Model Lock-In** — institutional prestige replaces evidence testing.
4. **Compartmentalization Blindness** — competent characters are written as irrational when the real issue is missing access.
5. **Relationship-as-Proof** — love, betrayal, jealousy or familiarity substitutes for corroboration.
6. **Memory-as-Fact** — recollection is given omniscient authority.
7. **Absence-as-Proof** — silence is treated as decisive without an expectation baseline.
8. **Investigator Omniscience** — protagonist independently notices, understands and solves every layer.
9. **Villainization of Institution** — a complex feedback failure is reduced to cartoon evil.
10. **Cold-War Template Leakage** — secrecy/class/bureaucracy from the source is imported into unrelated IVDIVO governance.
11. **Translation-Cadence Leakage** — Russian translation rhythm is treated as le Carré's English style.
12. **Last-Minute Mole Reveal** — solution depends on information unavailable earlier.
13. **Meeting-as-Exposition** — conversations merely repeat known facts.
14. **Suspicion-by-Weirdness** — an odd mannerism is treated as evidence of guilt.

## IVDIVO operationalization

### Mystery evidence ledger

For every major claim store:

`CLAIM | WORLD-FACT STATUS | ORIGINAL SOURCE | CURRENT REPORTER | ACCESS BASIS | INDEPENDENT? | BIAS / BLIND SPOT | CORROBORATION | CONTRADICTION | NEXT TEST | DOWNSTREAM ACTION`.

### Source lineage

If Source B learned X from Source A, and Source C learned X from B, B and C do not independently corroborate A.

### Institutional model audit

When an institution repeatedly produces the same conclusion, ask whether the same access architecture produced all inputs.

### Personhood evidence

For nonhuman/distributed entities, do not let one anomalous behaviour prove personhood. Use independent traces, persistent preferences, self-model, relationship-specific behaviour, consent/refusal and choices that alter others' option space.

### Consciousness-practice schools

Multiple schools are not independent corroboration if they derive the same ontology from a shared lineage. Keep:

`OBSERVATION → REPRODUCIBLE EFFECT → SCHOOL INTERPRETATION → COMPETING INTERPRETATION → ONTOLOGY CEILING`.

## Final synthesis

The book's highest-value contribution to IVDIVO is an **evidence-provenance architecture for distributed investigation inside imperfect institutions**.
