## Mechanisms added this pass

### 1. Objective is function, not compulsory physical activity

`SCENE ENTRY → PRESENT WANT / ACTIONABLE QUESTION → TACTIC → RESPONSE / RESISTANCE / SUPPORT → TACTIC OR MODEL UPDATE → OUTCOME → CHANGED STATE`.

A legitimate objective may be to decide, verify, refuse, negotiate, understand, protect a boundary, obtain consent, cooperate, or wait for an evidence threshold.

### 2. Objective ≠ tactic

A scene-level want may remain stable while tactics change. Repeating the same tactic under new wording is not progression.

### 3. Subtext condition

`SURFACE UTTERANCE → UNSTATED OBJECTIVE / RELATIONAL PRESSURE → LISTENER MODEL → RESPONSE / TACTIC CHANGE → CHANGED STATE`.

Subtext is justified when the speaker does not want to state something directly or does not need to because shared context carries it.

### 4. Direct-honesty payoff

Subtext is not a universal quality metric. If characters have spent many scenes avoiding a truth, earned direct speech may be the actual turning point.

### 5. Dialogue-exposition firewall

`AUTHOR WANTS READER TO KNOW X` is not a character objective.

Before a line carries information:
`WHO NEEDS / WANTS THIS INFORMATION NOW? → WHY THIS INTERLOCUTOR? → WHAT DOES SAYING / WITHHOLDING IT CHANGE?`

If there is no causal answer, use narration/summary or restructure the scene.

### 6. TIME channels are options, not a checklist

Thought / Inflection / Movement / Expression can clarify subtext, but adding all four after every line creates microgesture confetti and choppy prose.

### 7. Evidence firewall

`SUBTEXT INFERENCE ≠ MOTIVE FACT ≠ CORROBORATED WORLD FACT`.

A look, pause, tone, omission, attraction, distrust, or apparent evasiveness can change the POV's model; it does not by itself prove guilt, intention, personhood, or ontology.

## IVDIVO application

### ONE MAIN STORY
A dialogue scene pays into the main engine when its exact wording changes evidence, trust, consent, authority/access, commitment, relationship terms, strategy/model, or future options. Otherwise test summary/indirect/compression.

### Youth
Do not simulate youth by Earth-current slang. Subtext arises from relationship, status, shared history, embarrassment, autonomy, privacy, consent, belonging, competence, and what the future culture considers obvious or unsayable.

### Conscious robots / nonhuman persons
Do not require human facial/mannerism cues. Use embodiment-specific signals, independent objectives, knowledge limits, relationships, consent/refusal, and choices. Compliance or silence is not automatically consent or motive evidence.

### Mystery
`SURFACE LINE / GESTURE → POV INTERPRETATION → ALTERNATIVE MODEL → CORROBORATION TEST`.
Relationship trust, attraction, confession, silence, or inflection may change investigative behavior; they do not change the evidence itself.

### Choppy prose
Do not turn TIME into `line → gesture → thought → line → expression`. First decide scene necessity, dialogue mode, paragraph intent, and reader-state continuity. Several beats that form one perceptual/decision unit should normally remain in one coherent paragraph.
