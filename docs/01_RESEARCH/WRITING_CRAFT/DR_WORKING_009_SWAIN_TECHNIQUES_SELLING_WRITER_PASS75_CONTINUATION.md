## CHAPTER 4 — FINAL SYNTHESIS

### 1. Scene and sequel are functions, not a mandatory ping-pong template
Swain's durable distinction is between:

`EXTERNAL ATTEMPT / PRESSURE -> CHANGED STATE`

and

`PROCESSING / REAPPRAISAL -> NEW GOAL / STRATEGY`.

IVDIVO therefore does not require literal alternation of full scenes and full sequels.

### 2. Time-unity vs topic-unity
Swain's scene is normally unified by continuous time. His sequel can compress time, place and incidental activity because one current problem/topic supplies unity.

IVDIVO routing:
`POST-EVENT CONSEQUENCE -> CURRENT PROCESSING QUESTION -> SELECT RELEVANT MATERIAL -> COMPRESS INCIDENTAL TIME/SPACE -> NEW COMMITMENT -> NEXT ACTION`.

This is a major anti-chop mechanism. Travel, waiting, routine, repeated procedure and low-value microreaction do not need beat-by-beat dramatization merely to preserve continuity.

### 3. Compression / transition / credibility
Swain explicitly identifies these as the three main problems of sequel-writing. The useful production gate is:

`HOW MUCH PROCESSING DOES THE READER NEED TO BELIEVE THE NEXT DECISION?`

Too little produces an unearned strategy jump. Too much produces rumination drag.

### 4. Incident and happening are legitimate
Swain admits lower-conflict incidents/happenings can add realism even though they cannot sustain attention indefinitely.

IVDIVO correction:
`NO OVERT CONFLICT != NO STORY FUNCTION`.

A quiet unit may earn full scene-space if it changes relationship, evidence-status, identity, obligation, trust, consent, model or future choice. Otherwise compress it.

### 5. No mandatory disaster
Swain's commercial template prefers `GOAL -> CONFLICT -> DISASTER`, but his deeper mechanism is change of situation.

IVDIVO:
`PRESENT WANT -> MEANINGFUL RESISTANCE / SUPPORT -> TACTIC -> OUTCOME -> CHANGED STATE`.

Outcome may be failure, partial success, costly success, correct restraint, ordinary success that creates more responsibility, or a new information state.

### 6. Processing can be delayed, embedded or implied
Swain explicitly allows a time-break after a high-impact outcome before processing. IVDIVO generalizes:

`CHANGED STATE -> IMMEDIATE PROCESSING / DELAYED PROCESSING / EMBEDDED PROCESSING / IMPLIED PROCESSING`,

selected by reader effect, character truth and pacing need.

### 7. Character-specific processing
Different people need different amounts and modes of processing. This applies to youth ensemble design and to conscious robots.

For a conscious robot, do not force a stock human `emotion -> thought -> decision` display. Preserve personhood through its own:
`evaluation -> competing obligations -> consent/refusal -> strategy -> consequence`.

### 8. Show/tell firewall
Swain's claim that summary cannot truly come alive is treated as a historically situated preference, not an IVDIVO law. LaPlante, Rosenfeld, Lodge, Prose and later Bell demonstrate that scene, summary and indirect presentation are complementary tools.

IVDIVO:
`SCENE WHEN EXACT WORDS/ACTIONS DESERVE READER TIME; SUMMARY/INDIRECT WHEN PROCESS OR ELAPSED MATERIAL DOES NOT`.

### 9. Anti-mechanical scene/sequel gate
Do not draft every passage as:
`goal -> conflict -> disaster -> reaction -> dilemma -> decision`.

Instead:
`LIVE DRAFT -> SCENE/PROCESSING FUNCTION AUDIT -> CAUSAL GAP CHECK -> PACE CHECK -> REPAIR ONLY WHERE NEEDED`.

### 10. ONE MAIN STORY link
A sequel/processing unit earns its place when the new decision changes the dominant causal engine. If it merely restates the previous event, it is a compression/merge candidate.
