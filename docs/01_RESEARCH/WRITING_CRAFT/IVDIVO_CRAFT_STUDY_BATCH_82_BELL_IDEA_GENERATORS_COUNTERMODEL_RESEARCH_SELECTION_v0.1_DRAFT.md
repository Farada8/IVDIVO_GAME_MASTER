## PURPOSE

Translate Bell's Chapter 3 from a list of idea tricks into a controlled IVDIVO production layer that maximizes discovery without allowing generated material to bypass canon, personhood or ONE MAIN STORY.

## MASTER ROUTER

`SEED → DIVERGENT GENERATION → MATERIAL VARIANTS → TEMPORARY NO-CENSOR ZONE → EVALUATION → KEEP / BANK / DISCARD`

Evaluation order:

1. Canon compatibility
2. Independent personhood
3. Character truth
4. Causal viability
5. ONE MAIN STORY contribution
6. Reader promise / effect
7. Originality / reference transformation
8. Payoff capacity
9. Proof ceiling / evidence integrity
10. Prose only after structural acceptance

## GENERATOR FAMILIES

Bell's methods can be clustered into functions instead of treated as twenty commandments.

### A. QUESTION GENERATORS
- What-if
- issue / controversy
- trend extrapolation
- news event
- research discovery
- freewrite: `what I really want to write about is...`

### B. CHARACTER GENERATORS
- character first
- obsession

- worst credible pressure for this particular person

### C. FORM / EXPECTATION GENERATORS
- title
- opening line

### D. SENSORIAL / ASSOCIATIVE GENERATORS
- mental imagery

### E. END-STATE GENERATORS
- ending image / decisive choice first

None of these generators has canonical authority.

## PRODUCTION GATES

### B82-G01 — DIVERGE BEFORE CONVERGE
Do not let canon/audit desks kill every imperfect seed at birth. Generate multiple materially different candidates first.

### B82-G02 — MATERIAL VARIANT
A variant counts as new only if it changes at least one of:
- decision owner;
- want;
- evidence;
- relationship under pressure;
- world rule;
- tactic;
- cost bearer;
- post-scene state.

Changing only planet, visual design, profession label or technology name is cosmetic variation.

### B82-G03 — BILATERAL ISSUE TEST
For contested questions:
`STRONG CASE A + STRONG CASE B + INDEPENDENT PEOPLE + CONSEQUENCES`.
If one side exists only to be defeated, rebuild the countermodel.

### B82-G04 — RESEARCH MUST BECOME ACTION
`FACT → WHO CARES → WHAT OPTION CHANGES → ACTION/TEST → CONSEQUENCE`.
Otherwise store it in the world database.

### B82-G05 — PATTERN, NOT COPY
References may yield abstract mechanism or movement, never distinctive protected execution.

### B82-G06 — ENDING CANDIDATE IS REVOCABLE
Ending-first generation is allowed. Ending preservation is not mandatory.

### B82-G07 — PROLOGUE IS OPTIONAL
No mandatory shock, murder, chase or mystery prologue.

### B82-G08 — OBSESSION IS NOT MANDATORY PATHOLOGY
Use directional energy and repeated choice. Do not manufacture trauma to justify intensity.

### B82-G09 — NO DEATH-INFLATION
Idea size is measured by meaningful non-fungible cost and irreversible/important state change, not body count.

### B82-G10 — ROLE ≠ MONEY
In post-money settings, role generation means responsibility, training, practice, social function, access, relationships and time — not salary/rent/credits.

### B82-G11 — PASSION / POTENTIAL / PRECISION
- Passion = sustained creative/character energy.
- Potential = strength of reader promise and causal runway.
- Precision = remove/book-bank elements that do not serve this book.

### B82-G12 — IDEA BANK PROTECTION
A strong idea that weakens ONE MAIN STORY is not discarded; it is banked for another book/project.

### B82-G13 — ROBOT PERSONHOOD AT IDEATION
A named conscious robot requires its own want, relations, right of refusal and causal stake before it can be accepted into an engine.

### B82-G14 — SPECIALIST KNOWLEDGE PROVENANCE
A practice school, expert or archive may generate a hypothesis; it cannot generate ontological truth by authorial fiat.

## FAILURE MODES

1. **Early-Censor Freeze** — every seed audited before it can develop.
2. **First-Idea Lock** — first plausible engine becomes emotionally protected.
3. **Cosmetic Variantism** — many variants that only repaint the same causal system.
4. **Author-Proxy Collapse** — all protagonists inherit the founder's own reasoning style.
5. **Trend Colonization** — current topic overrides character/world truth.
6. **Research Tourism** — fascinating facts with no choice or consequence.
7. **Reference Imitation** — mechanism study slides into copied execution.
8. **Death Inflation** — body count used to prove importance.
9. **Prologue Tax** — forced shock opening that the actual book cannot pay off.
10. **Idea Hoarding** — multiple strong engines crammed into one novel.
11. **Robot-as-Argument** — conscious robot exists only to embody one side of a human debate.
12. **School-as-Truth-Machine** — specialist interpretation becomes canon without evidence chain.

## CROSS-SOURCE POSITION

Bell Ch3 is a **divergent-generation layer**.

Use after/with:
- Lamott / LaPlante discovery protection;

Then converge with:
- Truby/McKee/Brody structural testing;
- Card/Corbett personhood and motivation;
- Zunshine/Herman evidence/provenance;
- Wolf/Cambridge world/canon constraints;
- Rosenfeld scene architecture;
- Zinsser/Prose/Le Guin-style line/rhythm work only later.

## ORBITAL YOUTH IDEA TOURNAMENT TEMPLATE

For each seed, generate at least five materially different engines using different owners/pressures.

Candidate non-money stakes:
- one real mission place;
- consent from a specific person/robot;
- trust of a mentor/team;
- reputation after a public decision;
- privacy;
- responsibility for another person's safety;
- mastery / qualification;
- belonging without surrendering autonomy;
- unique time window;
- scarce lived specialist experience.

For every top candidate, write:

`WHO WANTS WHAT?`
`WHO/WHAT CAN LEGITIMATELY RESIST?`
`WHY IS THE COUNTERMODEL INTELLIGENT?`
`WHAT WORLD RULE CHANGES OPTIONS?`
`WHAT IS THE NON-FUNGIBLE COST?`
`WHAT DOES SUCCESS CHANGE?`
`WHAT DOES FAILURE CHANGE?`
`WHAT IS THE LOCAL END CONDITION?`

Only then select the Story Core.
