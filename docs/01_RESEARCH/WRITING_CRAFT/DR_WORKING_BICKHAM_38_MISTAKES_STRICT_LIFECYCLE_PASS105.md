## Key mechanisms from §§27–34
1. Separate generation from critique.
2. Avoid premature audience/family self-censorship during discovery, then run ethics/canon/personhood validation later.
3. Emotional access is useful material; emotional certainty is not evidence.
4. Reader feedback must be decomposed into observed effect and proposed repair.
5. Professional advice deserves domain-sensitive weight, not automatic obedience.
6. Market awareness may inform positioning but does not become story authority.
7. Style posture can be ornamental or performatively tough/cynical.
8. Removing purple prose must not create choppy faux-tough prose.
9. Harvest downstream consequences of an existing event before inventing another event.
10. Reuse/combine minor functional roles when that improves causal density, but do not erase independent personhood merely for plot economy.
