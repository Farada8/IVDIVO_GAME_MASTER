## HIGH-VALUE MECHANISMS RETAINED
### Delay vs complication
`DELAY = time/logistics cost`
`COMPLICATION = changed option space / evidence / relationship / authority / responsibility / future consequence`

### Peaks and valleys
Maximum tension cannot be the only rhythm. Lower-pressure material can legitimately carry ordinary life, reflection, relationship change, evidence processing, humor, setting and decision-making.

### Pacing / syntax
`INTENSITY != SHORT-SENTENCE DENSITY`.
Longer or more flowing syntax can deliberately alter subjective time; short and long forms need functional contrast.

### POV switching cost
A switch requires reader reorientation and must buy unique action, evidence, lived consequence, relationship pressure, countermodel or decision value.

### Character function vs personhood
`BOOK FUNCTION != STORYWORLD PERSONHOOD`.
Cast economy is useful, but named conscious persons — human or robot — retain independent wants, relationships, consent/refusal and consequences.

### Behavior before explanation
Prefer observing recurring behavior across contexts before assigning a single explanatory motive. Do not reduce a person to one diagnostic label or backstory cause.

### Relation-specific characterization
A major character should not present identically with friend, parent, instructor, rival, intimate partner, public audience, conscious robot, or institution. Different relationship pressure should produce different tactics while preserving identity.
