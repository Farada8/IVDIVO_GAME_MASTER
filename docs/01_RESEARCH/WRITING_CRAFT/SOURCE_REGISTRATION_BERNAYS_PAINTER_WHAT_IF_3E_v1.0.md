## Source-use boundary
This source is used as a **controlled-exercise laboratory**, not as a universal theory of fiction.

Primary IVDIVO value:
- isolate one craft variable at a time;
- generate multiple variants before selection;
- test POV/distance/voice/scene-summary/dialogue choices;
- discover character behavior outside current plot;
- reveal process assumptions through constraints;
- support targeted revision after a real defect is identified.

### Critical law
`EXERCISE OUTPUT ≠ CANON`.

No exercise result enters Saga Bible, manuscript authority or world ontology until it separately passes canon, causality, personhood, evidence, ONE MAIN STORY and reader-effect gates.
