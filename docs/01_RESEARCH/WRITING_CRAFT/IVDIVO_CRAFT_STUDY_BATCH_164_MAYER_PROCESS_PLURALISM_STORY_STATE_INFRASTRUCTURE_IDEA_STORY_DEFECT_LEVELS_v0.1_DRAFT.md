# IVDIVO CRAFT STUDY — BATCH 164
## Bob Mayer — Process Pluralism · Story-State Infrastructure · Idea/Story Defect Levels

**Source status:** WIDER / FULL READ IN PROGRESS through Tool 5.  
**Do not treat this batch as final-source synthesis.**

## 1. Central synthesis
Mayer is most useful when stripped of his dated publishing advice and some rigid commercial-fiction prescriptions.

The durable system is:

`DISCOVERY / IDEA → EXTERNALIZED PLAN → LIVE DRAFT → OBSERVED STORY STATE → PLAN-vs-ACTUAL COMPARISON → CORRECT-LEVEL REVISION`.

This is highly compatible with IVDIVO's current Narrative OS and runtime tooling.

---

## 2. Mechanism M164-01 — Process pluralism / functional equivalence
Different writers can distribute the same thinking differently:
- extensive outline before prose;
- discovery draft followed by heavy rewriting;
- hybrids.

Production gate:
`DOES THE PROCESS PRODUCE CAUSAL CLARITY + LIVE CHARACTER BEHAVIOR + A USABLE MANUSCRIPT?`

Do not judge the writer by allegiance to planner/pantser identity.

### Failure modes
- METHOD IDENTITY LOCK
- PROCESS MONOCULTURE
- TOOL AS WRITING AVOIDANCE

---

## 3. Mechanism M164-02 — Story Grid ≠ Outline
Mayer's strongest operational distinction:
- OUTLINE = intended future state;
- STORY GRID / continuity record = what the manuscript actually contains.

IVDIVO law:
`PLAN = HYPOTHESIS; LOCKED TEXT / OBSERVED STATE = EVIDENCE`.

When they diverge:
1. detect divergence;
2. determine whether live material is stronger or defective;
3. check canon / character / causality;
4. update plan or repair manuscript;
5. never silently rewrite history so the outline appears correct.

### Failure modes
- OUTLINE/ACTUAL CONFLATION
- PLAN BECOMES CANON BY FORMATTING
- RETROACTIVE PLAN LAUNDERING

---

## 4. Mechanism M164-03 — Externalized story-state infrastructure
Useful author-side artifacts:
- character registry;
- fact/continuity ledger;
- spatial maps;
- timeline/time-zone map;
- story grid;
- research summaries with source/page provenance;
- chapter start/end state records.

Purpose: remove memory burden and expose contradictions.

These are not worldbuilding trophies. If maintaining the tool costs more cognitive energy than it saves, simplify it.

### IVDIVO extension
For major projects maintain distinct ledgers:
`PLAN / ACTUAL TEXT / CANON / EVIDENCE STATUS`.

Do not merge them.

---

## 5. Mechanism M164-04 — Spatial/temporal rules are causal
Maps and diagrams matter because:
- travel consumes time;
- walls/doors/access determine possibility;
- technology has range and failure conditions;
- people cannot be in two places without a mechanism;
- reader orientation is part of causal comprehension.

Gate:
`WORLD/SPATIAL RULE → AVAILABLE/UNAVAILABLE ACTION → CHOICE → CONSEQUENCE`.

---

## 6. Mechanism M164-05 — Source-preserving research compression
Research notes should preserve provenance sufficiently to recover the source.

`QUESTION → SOURCE/PAGE → OBSERVATION/CLAIM → INTERPRETATION → STORY USE`.

This anticipates later Zunshine/le Carré evidence discipline.

### Failure modes
- RESEARCH WITHOUT PROVENANCE
- EXPERT FLUENCY → FACT
- LORE DUMP
- RESEARCH PRIDE

---

## 7. Mechanism M164-06 — Idea ≠ Story
An idea is seed/premise/question/image/character possibility.
A story is the causal embodiment:

`WHO → WANTS WHAT → UNDER WHAT CONDITIONS → DOES WHAT → WORLD RESPONDS → STATE CHANGES → NEXT CHOICE`.

A high-concept sentence can be clear while the story remains nonexistent.

### Gate
Before scene architecture, answer:
`WHAT DOMINANT CAUSAL ENGINE TURNS THIS IDEA INTO A BOOK?`

---

## 8. Mechanism M164-07 — Defect level before polish
Mayer repeatedly distinguishes failures of idea, character, story construction, POV, pacing and execution.

Production routing:
`OBSERVED DEFECT → DEFECT LEVEL → DEPENDENCIES → MINIMAL CORRECT FIX → REGRESSION TEST`.

No sentence polish may be presented as repair for a broken book engine.

### Failure modes
- LINE POLISH OVER ENGINE DEFECT
- SUBPLOT POLISH OVER PREMISE DEFECT
- NEW EVENT ADDICTION

---

## 9. Mechanism M164-08 — Writer emotion ≠ reader effect
Personal passion is useful fuel but not validation.

`WRITER FEELS X ≠ CHARACTER FEELS X ≠ READER FEELS X`.

Reader effect must be earned through context, attachment, expectation, uncertainty, choice, contrast and consequence.

---

## 10. Mechanism M164-09 — Originating promise, not immutable premise
Mayer treats the original idea as unusually fixed. That is too rigid.

Cross-source correction from Bell, Seger, Lamott, LaPlante and Swain:

`ORIGINATING PROMISE / HYPOTHESIS → DRAFT EVIDENCE → CHARACTER/CANON/CAUSALITY TEST → KEEP / REPLAN / BANK / DISCARD`.

Protect the mechanism that made the idea alive; do not protect its first wording at the expense of the actual story.

### Failure modes
- PREMISE ATTACHMENT
- PREMISE IMMUTABILITY
- FIRST-IDEA LOCK

---

## 11. Mechanism M164-10 — Reader-time communicates story weight
Mayer's chapter-level pacing observation generalizes well:

`TEXTUAL DURATION / SPACE → IMPLIED IMPORTANCE`.

If routine logistics receive as much real-time space as an irreversible decision, reader weighting becomes distorted.

Cross-source integration with Scofield:
`SCENE WEIGHT / MODE MUST MATCH CONSEQUENCE`.

---

## 12. Mechanism M164-11 — Backstory ownership and timing
Useful core:
- reader needs only the past required to interpret the present;
- memory is person-sourced and may be distorted;
- flashback must earn reader time through present consequence.

IVDIVO:
`PAST MATERIAL → WHY NOW? → SOURCE/POV → PRESENT MODEL CHANGE → PRESENT ACTION`.

---

## 13. Rejected / downgraded claims
Do NOT operationalize as universal laws:
- fiction is mainly escape/entertainment;
- novice should avoid complexity as a general rule;
- original idea must never change;
- suspense is integral to practically every story;
- crisis must be darkest moment;
- protagonist and antagonist must confront in climax;
- all loose ends must be resolved;
- action should always be shown;
- fixed word-count / market / submission / web-marketing guidance from 2003;
- commercial genre formulas as quality standards.

---

## 14. IVDIVO cross-source position
### Aligns strongly with
- Lamott / LaPlante: protect discovery, critique later;
- Bell / Seger / Swain: plan is tool, revision by level;
- Scofield: reader-time and scene weight;
- Wolf: world database ≠ book narrative;
- Zunshine / le Carré: preserve source/provenance;
- Fludernik: story fact ≠ discourse presentation;
- Zinsser: cut structural clutter before word clutter.

### Corrected by
- Lodge / Prose / LaPlante on show/tell flexibility;
- Truby / Brody / Vogler / Bell on avoiding formula stacking;
- contemporary IVDIVO evidence/personhood rules.
