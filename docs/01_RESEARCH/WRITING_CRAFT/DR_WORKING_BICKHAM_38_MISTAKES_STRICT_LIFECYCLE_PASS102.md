## Main mechanisms extracted this pass

1. **Research provenance before confidence**
   - A plausible analogy is not a verified fact.
   - Technical/historical claims need an identifiable source appropriate to the domain.
   - Research may generate story possibilities, but generated possibilities remain hypotheses until canon/causality validation.

2. **Observation vs inference split**
   - Writers should sharpen concrete observation.
   - Immediate conclusions about a person are interpretations, not equivalent to observed traces.

3. **Causal Complication / Alligator Test**
   - A complication earns its place when it changes the current causal problem, current objective, evidence state, relationship, authority, or option space.
   - Unrelated spectacle is not escalation.

4. **Scene question as diagnostic, not mandatory binary template**
   - Useful question: what exact state is this scene trying to change?
   - IVDIVO does not require every scene to have an explicit yes/no question, personal opponent, or negative disaster.

5. **Processing has causal value**
   - Quiet processing is legitimate when it changes model, relationship, evidence-status, commitment, strategy, or next objective.
   - Processing may be compressed, delayed, embedded, or implied by changed later behavior.

6. **Summary remains legitimate**
   - Bickham's `no summary in scene` is retained only as a warning against summarizing the precise action/wording whose real-time experience is the point.
   - Cross-source synthesis with Swain, LaPlante, Rosenfeld, Scofield, Lodge, and Bell preserves summary/indirect mode for process, repetition, logistics, and intermediate time.

## Cross-source corrections

- **Against mandatory disaster:** Bell/Rosenfeld/Swain support outcomes including success, costly success, restraint, partial result, and failure. What matters is changed state and downstream causality.
- **Against mandatory person-opponent:** resistance may be another agent, institution, physical constraint, missing evidence, consent, time, embodiment, or competing reasonable values.
- **Against summary prohibition:** scene weight and mode are functional choices, not morality.
- **Against anti-discovery absolutism:** Bickham's hostility to characters/ideas departing from plan is too rigid. LaPlante/Lamott/Bell support discovery material followed by structural/canon validation.

## IVDIVO production implications

### Research / science / mystery
`CLAIM → SOURCE → DOMAIN AUTHORITY → OBSERVATION/MEASUREMENT → INTERPRETATION → ALTERNATIVE MODEL → CONFIDENCE → ACTION`

### Scene complication
`CURRENT OBJECTIVE / QUESTION → COMPLICATION → DOES IT CHANGE THE SAME CAUSAL ENGINE? → YES: KEEP / NO: BANK, MERGE, OR DELETE`

### Processing
`OUTCOME → WHAT CHANGED? → WHAT MUST THE POV REASSESS? → OPTION SET → NEW STRATEGY / COMMITMENT → NEXT ACTION`

### Anti-choppiness
Do not render processing as a mechanical chain of one-line microbeats. If emotion, inference, bodily perception, and decision form one continuous mental unit, they may belong in one coherent paragraph.
