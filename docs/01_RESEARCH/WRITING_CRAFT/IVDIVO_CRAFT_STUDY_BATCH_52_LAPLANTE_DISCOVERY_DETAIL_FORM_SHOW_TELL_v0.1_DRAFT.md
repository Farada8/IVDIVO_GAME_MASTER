## 1. CENTRAL SYNTHESIS

The strongest transferable LaPlante principle so far is not a beat model. It is a **division of labor between discovery and diagnosis**:

`GENERATE / NOTICE / DISCOVER → FIND THE LIVE MATERIAL → DIAGNOSE FORM → REVISE → LINE EDIT`.

A story can be harmed both by no craft and by craft applied too early. Production gates are therefore tools for revision/validation, not a demand that a first generative pass consciously satisfy every rule simultaneously.

## 2. DISCOVERY WITHOUT CANON DRIFT

LaPlante's `not knowing` is valuable when transformed for a continuity-heavy saga:

`HARD CANON / KNOWN FACT → WHAT REMAINS UNKNOWN INSIDE IT? → SPECIFIC OBSERVATION → COMPETING INTERPRETATIONS → TEST / CHOICE`.

This lets the Writers' Room discover story without using discovery as permission to overwrite Founder canon.

### Gate B52-01 — DISCOVERY FIREWALL
Exploratory prose can discover motives, relationships, scene solutions and interpretations. It cannot silently change hard technology/economy/ontology boundaries.

## 3. PARTICULAR BEFORE THESIS

A world claim such as `post-money`, `advanced`, `mixed human/robot`, `specialist consciousness schools` is not a scene.

Better order:
`particular person → particular environment/rule → behavior → friction/choice → consequence → reader infers social pattern`.

### Gate B52-02 — WORLD THESIS REJECTION
If the paragraph exists mainly to prove a worldbuilding proposition, rebuild around a lived particular.

## 4. DETAIL HIERARCHY

Specificity is necessary but not automatically valuable.

A detail earns space when it changes at least one of:
- what the POV character notices;
- what the reader understands about a relationship/person;
- spatial/technical possibility;
- causal interpretation;
- tension/question;
- rhythm/emotional texture.

Prefer details that perform more than one job.

### Gate B52-03 — DETAIL DOUBLE-PAY
Important unusual detail should pay at least twice unless its single function is exceptionally strong.

### Gate B52-04 — CATALOGUE TEST
If five details could be reordered/deleted without changing perception, action or implication, the passage is probably cataloguing rather than rendering.

## 5. METAPHOR AND SYMBOL

Do not ask first `what is this like?`; establish `what is it?`.

Metaphor is useful when a comparison creates a fast, precise cognitive/emotional re-seeing. It fails when it is ornamental, strained, clichéd or requires explanation.

Symbol should accumulate through recurrence/context. It must not be installed as a prosthetic for weak story architecture.

### Gate B52-05 — METAPHOR DOMAIN OWNERSHIP
Comparisons should emerge from the POV character's knowledge, body, culture and attention rather than one universal author-metaphor voice.

### Gate B52-06 — NO SYMBOL RETROFIT
Do not add symbolic objects to manufacture depth after causal/character work has failed.

## 6. FORM AS DIAGNOSIS, NOT COMMAND

LaPlante's Ch4 is a strong cross-check against Truby/McKee/Brody overreach.

Useful abstractions:
- conventional causal arc;
- modular/mosaic design;
- tension may rise/fall rather than climb monotonically;
- epiphany may occur or not;
- character may change, fail to change, partially change, or misunderstand.

The production question is not `does this obey the model?` but:

`WHAT FORM IS THIS MATERIAL ALREADY TRYING TO BECOME? → DOES THAT FORM CREATE A COMPLETE READER EXPERIENCE?`

### Gate B52-07 — NO EPIPHANY FACTORY
Do not add a realization paragraph because the story shape appears to require one.

### Gate B52-08 — NO CHANGE MANDATE
A character may refuse an earned opportunity. The refusal must still create a consequential state.

### Gate B52-09 — MODULAR CAUSALITY TEST
A modular sequence is allowed, but modules must accumulate pattern/meaning/relationship. `Can reorder freely with no changed effect` is a warning.

## 7. SHOW / TELL ROUTING

LaPlante directly rejects `show, don't tell` as a universal rule.

For IVDIVO:

**DIRECT SCENE** when exact wording/action/gesture is itself consequential.

**NARRATION / SUMMARY / INDIRECT** when the reader benefits more from compression, thought, history, interpretation, time transition or voice.

### Gate B52-10 — PROXIMITY BY FUNCTION
Choose narrative proximity from the reader effect required at that moment.

### Gate B52-11 — NO FRAME-BY-FRAME ROUTINE
Do not dramatize routine transport, setup, repair, training or repeated procedure unless the exact process changes state.

### Gate B52-12 — TELLING QUALITY, NOT TELLING BAN
Weak narration should first be diagnosed for vagueness/generalization. Do not automatically convert it into a scene.

## 8. SENTIMENTALITY / MELODRAMA

Sentimentality is an unearned conventional emotion. It can be sweet, tragic, heroic, cynical or grim.

For IVDIVO, high-risk buttons include:
- injured child;
- death as automatic seriousness;
- chosen-one language;
- robot innocence/obsolescence;
- parental rejection;
- romantic confession;
- sacrifice;
- institutional cruelty.

### Gate B52-13 — EARN THE RESPONSE
Before asking for a large emotion, provide the character-specific causal particulars that make this instance different from the stock category.

### Gate B52-14 — GRIMNESS IS NOT DEPTH
A darker outcome does not receive automatic artistic credit.

## 9. VOICE THROUGH ATTENTION

Voice is strongly affected by what the perceiving intelligence selects.

Orbital test: show the same transport hall to Ethan/Maya/Ollie/Aoife/Samir or to a conscious robot. If each notices the same three things in the same order for the same reason, differentiation is not yet deep enough.

### Gate B52-15 — ATTENTION FINGERPRINT
`WHAT THIS PERSON NOTICES → WHY THIS PERSON NOTICES IT → HOW IT AFFECTS THEIR NEXT ACTION`.

## 10. PROSE / RHYTHM APPLICATION

A large source of choppy prose can exist above sentence level. If narration has been replaced by unnecessary micro-scenes, line joining alone will not solve it.

Repair order:
`SCENE NECESSITY → SHOW/TELL ROUTING → PARAGRAPH INTENT → SENTENCE RHYTHM → WORD CHOICE`.

Short lines remain legitimate for contrast, impact, realization, perceptual shock or deliberate breath.

### Gate B52-16 — CHOPPINESS ROOT-CAUSE
Before merging short paragraphs, test whether the material should be compressed into narration or removed entirely.

## 11. CURRENT IVDIVO APPLICATION

### LESSON ZERO
No lock reopening. This source so far strengthens the existing `new theory ≠ defect evidence` policy.

### ORBITAL YOUTH
The post-money civilization should be inferred through ordinary lived particulars. Example classes of stakes: time with a particular person, unique mission slot, trust, consent, role, autonomy, reputation, relationship, responsibility, specialist training and irreplaceable experience. Do not summarize the civilization as a thesis before the reader has lived it.

### CONSCIOUS ROBOTS
Avoid sentimental `innocent machine` shorthand. Render personhood through specific wants, refusals, relationships, habits of attention and independent consequences.

### SPECIALIST CONSCIOUSNESS SCHOOLS
Use LaPlante's unknown-within-known distinction to preserve epistemic humility:
`practice observation ≠ interpretation ≠ ontology ≠ civilization-wide fact`.
