# IVDIVO CRAFT STUDY — BATCH 176
## Hallie Ephron — Fair-Play Mystery · Clue Ledger · Misdirection · POV Fairness · Revision

**Status:** DRAFT / OPERATIONALIZED FROM STRICT FULL READ  
**Source:** Hallie Ephron, *Writing and Selling Your Mystery Novel* (2005)

## Executive synthesis

Ephron's strongest contribution is a practical mystery-production layer, but IVDIVO upgrades it with source lineage, evidence-status and personhood firewalls.

### Core engine
`TRUE TRACE → SOURCE / LINEAGE → ACCESS → INTERPRETATION → ALTERNATIVE MODEL → DISCRIMINATING TEST → STATUS UPDATE → ACTION`.

### Mystery fairness
- The reader may misunderstand a true clue.
- The author may control salience and context.
- The author may not retroactively change the trace.
- Close POV may not hide already-active knowledge only to manufacture a surprise.
- Behavior/body language creates hypotheses, not motive facts.
- Intuition may generate a test; it cannot substitute for evidence.
- Multiple confirming outputs from one source lineage remain one source lineage.

### Scene routing
`ROUTINE INVESTIGATION → SUMMARY / INDIRECT`
`EVIDENCE / CONSENT / RELATIONSHIP / AUTHORITY TURN → REAL TIME`

### Suspense
Suspense is anticipation and may deliberately expand subjective time.
`SUSPENSE ≠ SHORT-SENTENCE DENSITY`.

### Revision
`MACRO MYSTERY ENGINE → CLUE / SOURCE LOGIC → CHARACTER / POV → SCENE FUNCTION → DIALOGUE → PARAGRAPH / RHYTHM → WORDING`.

## New production gates

**E176-G01 — TRACE TRUTH**  
A red herring may be misinterpreted; the observed trace itself must remain true.

**E176-G02 — SOURCE LINEAGE**  
Count independent origins, not number of documents/people repeating one origin.

**E176-G03 — KNOWN-BUT-HIDDEN POV**  
Do not conceal fact X from the reader when the established POV character already actively knows X solely to create surprise.

**E176-G04 — OBSERVATION / MOTIVE FIREWALL**  
Gesture, pause, clothing, silence or evasiveness → motive hypothesis, never guilt/motive fact.

**E176-G05 — PRODUCTIVE UNCERTAINTY**  
Reader knows the question and viable models; the answer may remain unknown.

**E176-G06 — MISDIRECTION / DISTRACTION**  
Misdirection participates in the causal question; distraction is unrelated noise and must be cut unless it pays elsewhere.

**E176-G07 — CLUE SALIENCE**  
A fair clue must be available but need not be underlined.

**E176-G08 — FORESHADOWING RETROSPECTIVE AHA**  
Early detail remains literally true and gains meaning later.

**E176-G09 — CULPRIT SUPPORT**  
Culprit requires early capability/access/motive-model/textual support; no late personality transplant.

**E176-G10 — INVESTIGATION MODE**  
Compress repetitive process; dramatize discriminating tests and changed states.

**E176-G11 — Q&A TACTICS**  
Question scenes require wants, resistance/evasion and a changed evidence/relationship state.

**E176-G12 — INTUITION STATUS**  
Intuition = hypothesis generator; proof requires independent evidence.

**E176-G13 — SUSPENSE DURATION**  
Allow slowed subjective time and connected syntax; no fragment-density target.

**E176-G14 — MYSTERY CONTRACT**  
State whether the book promises a solvable puzzle, bounded operational answer, reinterpretation, or irrecoverable human mystery.

**E176-G15 — PROOF CEILING**  
Final answer cannot exceed what the evidence ladder actually supports.

**E176-G16 — BACKSTORY NOW**  
Past material earns space only by changing the current model/relationship/decision.

**E176-G17 — FEEDBACK EFFECT FIRST**  
Reader effect is evidence; proposed repair is a hypothesis.

**E176-G18 — MARKET FIREWALL**  
2005 query/agent/small-press advice is historical, not current policy.
