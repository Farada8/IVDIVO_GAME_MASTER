## High-value mechanisms extracted so far
1. `WORLDING THE STORY` vs `STORYING THE WORLD`: separate what the reader can infer from evidence from the story characters/institutions tell themselves about that evidence.
2. `OBSERVED ACTION → MULTIPLE REASONS → CONTEXT/HISTORY → PROVISIONAL MODEL → NEW EVIDENCE → MODEL UPDATE`.
3. `PREDICTED BEHAVIOR ≠ REASON ≠ CONSENT ≠ RIGHT TO ALLOCATE BURDEN`.
4. Person-level integrity: character is not reducible to category, trait vector, diagnosis or model output.
5. Retellings are versioned social artifacts: selection/omission/context/medium can change alignment and downstream action.
6. Perspective is a distributed sequence, not just a POV label. Omitted viewpoints can shape later cognitive repair if omission is fair to the chosen focal architecture.
7. Distributed intelligence can arise from people + records/tools + institutions + environment; no protagonist knowledge monopoly required.
8. Dialogue positions self/other in roles inside larger relationship narratives; words, silence, gesture, gaze, body, space and artifacts all participate.
9. Emotion labels are culturally mediated and must be separated from felt state, display and interpretation.
