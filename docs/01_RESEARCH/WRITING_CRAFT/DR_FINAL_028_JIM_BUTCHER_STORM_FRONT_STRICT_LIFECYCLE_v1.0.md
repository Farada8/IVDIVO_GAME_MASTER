## STRUCTURE MAP

1. Two apparently separate jobs enter Harry's life: Monica Sells's missing-husband request and Murphy's impossible double murder.
2. Hidden-world expertise lets Harry convert magical traces into procedural hypotheses while ordinary witnesses, phone records, crime scenes and social networks continue to matter.
3. The two cases repeatedly cross: Victor's lake house, Linda, ThreeEye, Marcone, Gimpy and Monica progressively collapse the apparent separation.
4. Harry's secrecy toward Murphy creates a second causal engine inside the same book: protective withholding damages shared situational awareness and trust.
5. Multiple authorities create asymmetric pressure: police, White Council, Marcone's organization and supernatural actors each control different information, sanctions and resources.
6. Evidence eventually identifies Victor Sells and explains the murders/ThreeEye operation. The local mystery becomes bounded enough for decisive action.
7. The climax combines magical rules, mundane tactics, character choice and institutional observation. Harry refuses the direct dark-magic solution, while Morgan's witnessed evidence changes the Council's model of Harry.
8. Local case closes: Victor is stopped, ThreeEye operation exposed, the Doom is lifted, Murphy survives but the relationship remains damaged. The wider supernatural world and Harry's ongoing temptations remain open for the series.

## HIGH-VALUE MECHANISMS

### M1 — CASE BRAID
Two case lines become ONE MAIN STORY only when evidence from one line changes the model or available actions in the other.

`CASE A TRACE → REFRAMES CASE B → CASE B PRESSURE / EVIDENCE → REFRAMES CASE A → CAUSAL CONVERGENCE → SINGLE ENDGAME`.

Gate:
Parallel investigations that merely coexist are not braided.

### M2 — HIDDEN-WORLD PROCEDURAL
Worldbuilding enters through professional action:
`RULE / LIMIT → OBSERVATION → TRACE → HYPOTHESIS → TEST / INTERVIEW → ACTION → CONSEQUENCE`.

Use for IVDIVO mystery/SF: reveal unusual systems because the protagonist must act through them, not because the reader needs a lecture.

### M3 — EVIDENCE STATUS MUST OUTRANK PROTAGONIST CONFIDENCE
Harry frequently feels certain before evidence warrants certainty. The novel sometimes later corroborates him, but this is not permission to equate intuition with proof.

Permanent IVDIVO gate:
`OBSERVED TRACE ≠ POV INTERPRETATION ≠ MOTIVE ≠ CORROBORATED FACT`.

### M4 — MULTI-AUTHORITY PRESSURE
Different institutions control different consequences:
- police: legal procedure / arrest / crime evidence;
- White Council: magical law / execution threat;
- Marcone: criminal information / violence / street network;
- supernatural actors: hidden-world capabilities.

Use:
`AUTHORITY A KNOWS X / CAN DO Y → AUTHORITY B KNOWS Z / CAN DO W → PROTAGONIST MUST NAVIGATE INCOMPATIBLE RULES`.

### M5 — PROTECTIVE WITHHOLDING CAN BE AN OPERATIONAL FAILURE
Harry withholds information from Murphy to protect her and himself. Result: she loses the shared model, distrust grows, and her independent action puts her at greater risk.

Gate:
`I KNOW MORE → I WITHHOLD "FOR YOUR GOOD" → OTHER AGENT ACTS WITH DEGRADED MODEL → TRUST / SAFETY COST`.

This is directly relevant to romance, YA mentorship, governance and conscious-robot relationships.

### M6 — COMPETENCE + BLIND SPOT
Harry is technically competent but socially and epistemically imperfect. Competence does not confer moral or interpretive infallibility.

`DOMAIN COMPETENCE → STRONG ACTION → OUT-OF-DOMAIN ASSUMPTION → RELATIONSHIP / EVIDENCE COST`.

### M7 — SOURCE-OF-SOURCE PROVENANCE
Small witnesses and intermediaries can form a clue chain: witness → another source → object/location → new witness → converging evidence.

Use explicit provenance:
`WHO SAW / HEARD → UNDER WHAT CONDITIONS → WHO PASSED IT ON → WHAT IT ACTUALLY SUPPORTS`.

### M8 — RULED MAGIC / TECHNOLOGY CREATES TACTICS
Magic works best as story machinery when it has costs, interfaces, traces, limitations and countermeasures. Harry repeatedly solves problems by changing venue/tactic rather than by simply increasing power.

IVDIVO transformation:
`CAPABILITY → LIMIT → WORKAROUND → NEW VULNERABILITY → TACTIC`.

### M9 — MUNDANE ACTION CAN OUTPERFORM THE SPECIAL SYSTEM
A physical object can break a magical circle; conventional weapons sometimes matter; institutional observation can alter legal status.

Gate:
Do not let advanced technology/magic monopolize causality. Keep ordinary physics, social rules, records and human choices alive.

### M10 — INSTITUTIONAL COUNTERMODEL CAN UPDATE
Morgan begins with a strongly biased model of Harry. Once he personally witnesses evidence inconsistent with that model, duty forces him to update his testimony even though personal dislike remains.

`PRIOR MODEL → DISCONFIRMING OBSERVATION → DUTY / EVIDENCE STANDARD → STATUS UPDATE WITHOUT PERSONAL CONVERSION`.

This is stronger than antagonist-to-friend conversion.

### M11 — LOCAL CLOSURE + SERIES OPENNESS
The murderer and immediate operational case are resolved; major relationships and world systems remain imperfect.

`LOCAL CASE QUESTION → PROOF → DECISIVE ACTION → CONSEQUENCE → RELATIONSHIP / INSTITUTIONAL AFTERMATH → SERIES VECTOR`.

Do not withhold the local promised answer merely to create a sequel hook.

### M12 — AFTERMATH MAY PRESERVE DAMAGE
Murphy survives and resumes professional contact, but trust is not magically reset. Local success does not erase relational cost.

Gate:
`CASE RESOLVED ≠ RELATIONSHIP RESET`.

## FAILURE MODES / NONTRANSFERABLE ELEMENTS

1. **POV CERTAINTY AS EVIDENCE** — intuition described with confidence must not silently become world fact.
2. **GENDER-ESSENTIALIST INFERENCE** — gender stereotypes used as motive analysis are not valid evidence.
3. **MALE-GAZE PERSON REDUCTION** — sexualized description cannot replace personhood/characterization.
4. **SEXUALITY-AS-CHARACTER SHORTCUT** — sexual behavior does not establish morality, motive or intelligence.
5. **SOULGAZE / THIRD-SIGHT = TRUE SELF** — source-world supernatural mechanism only; not IVDIVO psychology or ontology.
6. **LOVE-POTION CONSENT COMEDY** — chemically/magically induced desire is impaired consent; do not borrow as harmless romantic device.
7. **PATERNALISTIC SECRET-KEEPING** — "I protected you by withholding" must carry evidence/relationship consequences.
8. **CASE-LOOP REPETITION** — clue→interview→attack→clue can become mechanical unless each cycle changes the model, relationship, authority or option set.
9. **HERO KNOWLEDGE MONOPOLY** — procedural mystery weakens if only the protagonist can ever interpret anything.
10. **MONETARY STAKES LEAK** — rent/cab fees/consulting payments are source-world mechanics, not post-money orbital defaults.
11. **SPECIAL POWER = MORAL AUTHORITY** — competence/power never establishes legitimacy.
12. **INSTITUTION = CARDBOARD OPPRESSOR** — Morgan works better when duty can force truthful revision.
13. **THRILLER DANGER INFLATION** — repeated attacks are not a universal requirement for suspense.
14. **ONTOLOGY BY NARRATOR ASSERTION** — supernatural explanations remain source-world fiction, not evidence for IVDIVO consciousness/metaphysics.

## CROSS-SOURCE COMPARISON

### Butcher + Zunshine
Strong convergence on provenance/source monitoring. Butcher supplies vivid case execution; Zunshine provides the stricter epistemic firewall needed to prevent protagonist interpretation from becoming fact.

### Butcher + Weir
Convergence: observation → hypothesis → test → model update. Weir is cleaner and more scientific about uncertainty; Butcher adds witness chains, institutions and hidden-world rules.

### Butcher + Crouch
Convergence: competing models can create suspense. Crouch is stronger on identity/decision-owner questions; Butcher is stronger as an urban case-engine laboratory.

### Butcher + Wells
Convergence: competence does not confer ownership/legitimacy; withholding/control can create agency failures. Wells is the stronger consent/personhood authority.

### Butcher + Bell / Rosenfeld / Swain
The case loops demonstrate scene/chapter changed-state progression. Use craft sources to prevent repeated clue/attack scenes from becoming functionally identical.

### Butcher + le Carré / Veronica Mars
Shared value: institutions possess partial knowledge and conflicting incentives. The protagonist's information network is itself a narrative system.

## IVDIVO OPERATIONALIZATION

### Mystery / ROOM 917
Use a clue ledger with explicit status:
`TRACE → SOURCE → OBSERVATION → INTERPRETATION → ALTERNATIVE → CORROBORATION → ACTION`.

Braid mystery and relationship causally:
`CLUE → TRUST / DISCLOSURE CHANGE → NEXT INVESTIGATIVE ACTION CHANGES`.
But never:
`TRUST / ATTRACTION → CLUE BECOMES TRUE`.

### SMITH / institutional cases
Design at least two competent authorities with different powers, evidence and incentives. A useful institutional opponent may update under verified evidence without becoming friendly.

### Orbital Youth
Do not import Harry's rent/job/cash engine. Translate function into nonfungible stakes: trust, qualification, responsibility, access, mission role, privacy, reputation, consent, relationships, time.

Youth investigation gate:
A teenager may be competent while still having domain-specific blind spots. Do not manufacture stupidity; make mistakes arise from a reasonable but incomplete model.

### Conscious robots
Human interpretation of a robot's behavior remains an inference. Preserve:
`OBSERVED BEHAVIOR → POSSIBLE MOTIVES → ROBOT'S OWN MODEL / CHOICE → CONFIDENCE UPDATE`.

### Consciousness-practice schools
Butcher's Sight, soul gaze, demons and magical laws are fiction mechanisms only. Keep IVDIVO epistemic ladder:
`practitioner observation → reproducible effect → school interpretation → competing interpretation → ontology ceiling`.
