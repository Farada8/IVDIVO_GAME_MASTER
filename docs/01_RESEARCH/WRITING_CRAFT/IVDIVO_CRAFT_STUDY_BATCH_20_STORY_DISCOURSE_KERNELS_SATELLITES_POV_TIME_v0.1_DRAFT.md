# 1. SOURCE PASSPORT

Chatman separates narrative into two analytic levels:

- **STORY** — events + existents (characters/settings): what happens and what exists in the storyworld.
- **DISCOURSE** — the way that story is selected, ordered, paced, focalized and transmitted to the reader.

This is high-value for IVDIVO because many apparent prose defects are actually **discourse defects**, while many apparent pacing defects are actually **story-selection defects**.

Operational consequence:

`BAD READING EXPERIENCE ≠ AUTOMATICALLY BAD STORY EVENT`

A valid event can be badly placed, over-expanded, repeated, disclosed through the wrong POV, or given the wrong amount of textual time.

---

# 2. KERNELS AND SATELLITES

Chatman's strongest directly transferable mechanism is the distinction between major events (**kernels**) and minor/elaborative events (**satellites**).

A kernel is a story event that opens/closes alternatives and materially changes the causal possibility tree. A satellite elaborates, completes, delays, anticipates, characterizes or enriches the consequences of kernels. Removing a satellite may impoverish texture, rhythm, characterization or meaning, but need not break the basic causal skeleton.

## IVDIVO translation

### KERNEL TEST
A beat/scene is a likely kernel if removing it breaks at least one of:

- later decision logic;
- available alternatives;
- relationship state;
- authority/access status;
- clue state;
- irreversible cost;
- climax possibility;
- causal route into the next movement.

### SATELLITE TEST
A beat/scene is likely satellite if removal leaves the causal skeleton intact but damages one or more of:

- characterization;
- emotional preparation;
- setting reality;
- humor;
- thematic resonance;
- suspense/foreshadowing;
- social texture;
- reader attachment;
- rhythm.

**Important:** SATELLITE ≠ FILLER. A satellite can be aesthetically or emotionally indispensable even when structurally deletable in the narrow logical sense.

### Failure mode

Using kernel/satellite as a brutal cutting rule would flatten YA fiction into plot summary. The correct use is to understand **function and expansion budget**, not to delete all satellites.

# 3. RETROSPECTIVE KERNEL IDENTIFICATION

Chatman notes an important limitation: kernels are often identifiable only retrospectively, because an early action acquires its structural function from what later happens.

## IVDIVO gate

Do not label an early beat “minor” only because its local action is small.

Ask:

`WHAT LATER EVENT MAKES THIS EARLY DETAIL NECESSARY?`

This is especially relevant to:

- casual consent language that later becomes binding;
- ordinary maker-tech that later becomes fallback infrastructure;
- a joke/object that later becomes a motif/payoff;
- a tiny act of restraint that later proves character change.

# 4. STORY VS DISCOURSE DIAGNOSTIC

For every suspected slow/repetitive section, diagnose two separate questions:

## Story question
`SHOULD THIS EVENT EXIST?`

## Discourse question
`IS THIS THE RIGHT WAY / PLACE / DURATION / POV TO PRESENT IT?`

A section can fail one and pass the other.

Examples:

- right event, wrong duration → compress;
- right event, wrong order → move;
- right revelation, wrong chapter → restore reveal ownership;
- right information, wrong POV → refocalize;
- wrong event entirely → cut/replace;
- right satellite but overexpanded → summarize.

This prevents line editing from hiding a selection problem.

# 5. ORDER · DURATION · FREQUENCY

Chatman, following Genette, distinguishes three temporal dimensions that are especially useful for manuscript diagnostics.

## ORDER
Story chronology and discourse presentation order may differ.

IVDIVO use:
- flashback only when delayed disclosure produces a reader effect unavailable in chronology;
- do not move evidence earlier merely because exposition becomes easier;
- protect reveal ownership.

## DURATION
The amount of discourse time devoted to an event need not match story duration.

IVDIVO use:
- 30 seconds of crisis may deserve 8 pages;
- three routine weeks may deserve one paragraph;
- prose rhythm is partly a duration-allocation problem, not sentence-length alone.

## FREQUENCY
A repeated event can be narrated repeatedly, once as a pattern, or revisited with changed meaning.

IVDIVO use:
- recurring thematic conflicts should often be compressed as pattern until a recurrence produces a **new decision / cost / interpretation**;
- avoid full-scene repetition of the same developmental lesson.

# 6. SUSPENSE AND SURPRISE

Chatman treats suspense and surprise as complementary rather than mutually exclusive.

Operational distinction:

- **SUSPENSE**: reader has enough information to anticipate a meaningful future possibility.
- **SURPRISE**: reader's current model is abruptly violated/revised.

A sequence can move:

`SURPRISE → SUSPENSE PATTERN → EXPECTED OUTCOME PRESSURE → TWIST / SECOND SURPRISE`

## IVDIVO consequence

Do not hide all danger/information in order to create mystery. Giving the reader facts can create stronger suspense than withholding them.

For Book Two this favors:

`WE KNOW LATTICE HAS REAL BENEFITS + WE KNOW IT CONCENTRATES BURDEN + WE KNOW ETHAN HAS A HOLD`

rather than hiding those facts until the climax.

The question becomes:

**what will Ethan do, and what cost will become real?**

# 7. POV IS NOT THE SAME AS NARRATIVE VOICE

Chatman separates perspective from expression. A character can supply perception/conception/interest while a narrator supplies the actual wording.

IVDIVO application:

For limited third person, audit separately:

`WHO PERCEIVES?`
`WHO UNDERSTANDS / MISUNDERSTANDS?`
`WHOSE INTERESTS ORGANIZE ATTENTION?`
`WHOSE WORDING / NARRATIVE VOICE IS THIS?`

This matters for Anti-GPT prose: a paragraph can technically stay in Ethan POV while sounding like a generic essayistic narrator.

### POV contamination test

Flag sentences that:
- interpret another character beyond Ethan's evidence;
- use vocabulary Ethan would never notice or conceptually privilege without narrative justification;
- explain the theme from outside his current understanding;
- state future significance the focal character cannot know.

---

# 8. CHARACTER AS TRAIT PARADIGM — USE WITH LIMITS

Chatman resists reducing characters to pure plot functions and allows a character to be modeled as an open set/paradigm of traits inferred by readers over time.

Useful IVDIVO translation:

`TRAIT CLAIM` must be supported by repeated but non-identical behavioral evidence.

Example:
Ethan is not “controlling” because the bible says so. The reader infers the pattern through how he handles time, access, uncertainty, consent, authority, procedures and other people’s decisions.

### Failure mode

Do not freeze a living character into a trait checklist. Traits should predict tendencies, not eliminate contradiction, context or development.

- satellite hierarchy;
- POV specificity;
- suspense through known tradeoffs;
- protection of reveal order;
- payoff discipline.

# 11. NEW PRODUCTION GATES

### Gate HX — Story/Discourse Split
Before revising prose, identify whether the defect is in event selection or presentation.

### Gate HY — Kernel Necessity
Every major scene must identify which causal/choice alternative it changes.

### Gate HZ — Satellite Function
A satellite must carry at least one concrete aesthetic/emotional/foreshadowing/social function; repeated satellites with identical function become compression candidates.

### Gate IA — Retrospective Kernel Check
Do not cut an early small beat before checking later payoffs that depend on it.

### Gate IB — Duration Allocation
Narrative space must follow dramatic importance, not clock duration.

### Gate IC — Frequency Compression
Repeated pattern should be summarized unless the recurrence changes meaning, decision, relationship or cost.

### Gate ID — Suspense Information Sufficiency
Do not withhold facts that would create stronger anticipation if known.

### Gate IE — Surprise Model Revision
A surprise must revise the reader’s causal/character model, not merely insert random novelty.

### Gate IF — POV / Voice Separation
Check perception, interpretation, interest and wording as separate layers.

### Gate IG — Trait Evidence
Character traits must emerge from repeated contextualized behavior rather than declarative labeling.

### Gate IH — Satellite Expansion Budget
Atmosphere, humor, ordinary life and relationship texture are protected only at the amount of discourse time their current function earns.
