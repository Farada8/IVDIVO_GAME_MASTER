# IVDIVO CRAFT STUDY — BATCH 72
## Anne Lamott: Discovery Drafting · Character Discovery · Plot Treatment · Feedback · Voice

Status: RESEARCH SYNTHESIS / PRODUCTION GATES
Date: 2026-08-18
Primary source: Anne Lamott, *Bird by Bird*
Strict source status: W010 STRICT LIFECYCLE SYNTHESIZED v1.0

# PURPOSE
Convert Lamott’s strongest transferable mechanisms into IVDIVO production controls while rejecting autobiographical, spiritual, market and formula claims that do not belong in canon.

# SYNTHESIS
Lamott is strongest as an antidote to premature control. Her process assumes that useful character, image, relation and meaning may emerge only after imperfect exploratory prose exists. IVDIVO therefore separates discovery from validation rather than asking the complete Writers’ Room to police every sentence at birth.

# PRODUCTION GATES
## B72-G01 — GENERATION / EVALUATION SEPARATION
During exploratory drafting, do not require simultaneous compliance with every scene, style, market and line-edit gate. Keep only hard canon/continuity boundaries active.

## B72-G02 — HOT-SPOT PRESERVATION
Before cutting a messy draft, identify what is alive: voice, image, relationship, specific conflict, unexpected behavior, rhythm or question. Preserve the mechanism even if the surrounding prose is discarded.

## B72-G03 — SHORT ASSIGNMENT ≠ SHORT PROSE
Task chunking is a workflow device. It must never be translated into a target for sentence, paragraph, scene or chapter length.

## B72-G04 — CHARACTER MODEL UPDATE
Treat early character notes as hypotheses. A pressured scene may reveal unexpected but credible behavior; update the model instead of forcing the behavior back into the outline.

## B72-G05 — PLOT PUPPET REJECTION
If a character performs an action only because the outline requires the next beat, stop. Either supply a character/world cause or rebuild the beat.

## B72-G06 — CAUSAL PLOT TREATMENT
For structurally unstable books, write a chapter-level causal treatment:
`START STATE → OWNER/WANT → CHANGE → END STATE → WHY END STATE CREATES NEXT CHAPTER`.
Do this before line polishing.

## B72-G07 — AUTHOR-EXPLANATION TEST
If the author must verbally explain relationships, motives or missing links that are not visible in the manuscript, the manuscript is structurally incomplete.

## B72-G08 — RESEARCH SURPRISE
Research calls/observations should answer the target knowledge gap but may also reveal adjacent usable details. Do not import everything learned.

## B72-G09 — DIRECT-DIALOGUE NECESSITY
Use exact dialogue only when exact wording changes something meaningful. Routine conversation may be compressed.

## B72-G10 — VOICE DE-BORROWING
Imitation is allowed as a learning scaffold. Before final prose, identify what function was borrowed and rebuild it through IVDIVO-specific character/world attention.

## B72-G11 — FEEDBACK EFFECT ≠ FEEDBACK REPAIR
Reader says “slow,” “confusing,” “cold,” “too much,” etc. = evidence of effect. Reader’s proposed fix = hypothesis requiring diagnosis and testing.

## B72-G12 — NO LIKABILITY REQUIREMENT
Reader attachment may arise through fascination, competence, vulnerability, mystery, clarity, humor, relation or moral/tactical uncertainty. Do not manufacture niceness.

## B72-G13 — NO SUFFERING INFLATION
Lamott’s repeated illness/death material is autobiographical context, not a craft requirement. Pressure/cost must serve story causality.

## B72-G14 — NO MANDATORY CHANGE/HEALING
A character may change, partially change, refuse change or confirm an existing stance. The choice must still alter consequences.

## B72-G15 — INTUITION GENERATES; EVIDENCE VALIDATES
Intuition may suggest a scene, character turn or world hypothesis. It does not override canon, continuity or evidence-status.

## B72-G16 — POST-MONEY EXPERIENCE GATE
Ordinary orbital life should reveal post-money society through concrete behavior. Never replace money with “credits,” “points” or another hidden currency unless the world explicitly establishes a narrow non-money accounting mechanism.

## B72-G17 — ROBOT PERSONHOOD DISCOVERY
If drafting reveals that a conscious robot wants something outside its assigned plot function, test and preserve it when canon-compatible. Do not reduce it to Ally/Mentor/tool for convenience.

## B72-G18 — PROSE FRAGMENTATION ROUTER
Before repairing choppy prose:
`SCENE NECESSITY → SHOW/TELL → POV DISTANCE → DIALOGUE MODE → PARAGRAPH INTENT → SENTENCE RHYTHM → WORD`.
Do not solve a scene-level defect by merely lengthening sentences.

# FAILURE MODES TO RED-TEAM
- beautiful vignette accumulation without causal book spine;
- “characters told me” used to excuse implausible actions;
- theme chosen first, confirming details selected afterward;
- first-draft freedom carried unchanged into final draft;
- borrowed contemporary YA voice pasted onto orbital teenagers;
- all robots given one artificial register;
- spiritual intuition promoted to world ontology;
- workshop majority vote treated as authorial authority;
- line editing performed while chapter order/motivation remains unstable.

# CROSS-SOURCE POSITION
Lamott + LaPlante: strongest combined source pair for `DISCOVERY → REVISION STAGE ROUTING`.
Lamott + Bell Art of War: `GENERATE HOT → REVISE COOL`.
Lamott + Prose/Pushman: discovery first, close-reading precision afterward.
Lamott vs Truby/McKee/Brody/Vogler: formula systems remain diagnostic maps after material exists, not birth certificates every scene must reproduce.

# IVDIVO MASTER ROUTER
`DISCOVERY DRAFT → HOT-SPOT → CANON CHECK → ONE MAIN STORY → CAUSAL TREATMENT → SCENE ACCEPTANCE → POV/KNOWLEDGE → DIALOGUE/SUBTEXT → PARAGRAPH/RHYTHM → LINE EDIT → READER TEST`.

This router supersedes any workflow that asks all specialist agents to rewrite simultaneously.
