## 3. FINAL CLAIM EXTRACTION
1. Character becomes legible through **interaction, choice and behavior under circumstance**, not trait inventory.
2. Character design and discovery form a loop: initial hypothesis must remain revisable after pressured scenes reveal unexpected but plausible behavior.
3. Story function is not personhood. A mentor, opponent, witness, love interest, cadet or conscious robot must possess wants and relationships beyond the protagonist-facing role.
4. A concrete desire gives character movement; deeper meaning may remain layered, but the scene/book still needs a trackable external objective where appropriate.
5. Response to frustration is often more diagnostic than the obstacle itself; different people should change tactic differently.
6. Vulnerability is situational and relational, not synonymous with likability, illness or trauma.
7. Secrets are one possible information-pressure mechanism, not mandatory proof of depth.
8. Contradiction works when different contexts, loyalties or relationships make apparently inconsistent behavior causally coherent.
9. Over-explaining motive flattens character; behavior may have multiple simultaneous causes and remain partly uncertain.
10. Useful biography is selective and behavioral: past material earns space when it alters present expectation, tactic, relationship or perception.
11. Physicality matters when it changes perception, affordance, risk, social response or action; body inventory alone is inert.
12. Social environment—family, peer group, vocation, education, place, institutions, affiliations—changes what a person believes possible, costly or normal.
13. Political/institutional belief should arise from lived stakes and competing goods, not authorial sermon.
14. Quirks are fingerprints, not substitutes for agency or character architecture.
15. Protagonist choice follows disturbance, stakes and story question; ONE MAIN STORY does not require one person to perform every causal function.
16. Character change is not compulsory. Steadfastness, partial change, strategy change, relationship change or refusal may all be dramatically valid if consequences follow.
17. Mystery/problem stories need both an overarching question and a trackable outer objective or they risk episodicity.
18. A strong opponent embodies a legitimate countermodel with leverage, evidence and cost/blind spot; context does not equal moral exoneration.
19. Secondary characters may have limited book function while retaining independent storyworld life.
20. Scenes externalize inner conflict by forcing options, tactics, choice and responsibility.
21. Scene reflection/sequel is useful after pressure when it processes meaning and changes the next decision; explanatory duplication is not.
22. POV combines **who perceives, what can be known, and narrative distance**. Stable ground earns reader trust; changes require purpose.
23. Unreliable narration is usually partial/local, not an all-purpose license for continuity errors.
24. Voice combines style, worldview and attitude; character voices differ through what they notice/want and how they approach people, not slang alone.
25. Dialogue is action: characters use words, silence, gesture and evasion to do something to one another. Literary dialogue is compressed speech, not transcription.
26. The book closes by rejecting universal one-path craft claims: tools should help writers investigate living material, not force every story into one mandatory architecture.

## 4. IVDIVO MECHANISM EXTRACTION
### M15-01 — DESIGN ↔ DISCOVERY LOOP
`DESIGN HYPOTHESIS → PRESSURED SCENE → UNEXPECTED-BUT-PLAUSIBLE BEHAVIOR → UPDATE CHARACTER MODEL → TEST AGAIN`.

### M15-02 — CHARACTER INDEPENDENCE TEST
`What does this person want when the protagonist is absent? Who else matters to them? What would they refuse? What continues off-axis?`

### M15-03 — NONFUNGIBLE WANT ENGINE
For post-money Orbital Youth:
`SPECIFIC ROLE / TRUST / RELATIONSHIP / CONSENT / MASTERY / TIME / UNIQUE EXPERIENCE / RESPONSIBILITY → ACTION → RESISTANCE → COST`.
Do not recreate money as points, credits or abstract status currency.

### M15-04 — FRUSTRATION FINGERPRINT
`WANT → RESISTANCE → CHARACTER-SPECIFIC TACTIC → RESULT → STRATEGY CHANGE`.
If the ensemble responds with the same wit, defiance or competence move, voices are only cosmetic.

### M15-05 — CONTEXTUAL CONTRADICTION
`RELATIONSHIP / POWER / HISTORY / RISK changes → different but causally coherent behavior`.
Prefer this to arbitrary trait opposites.

### M15-06 — MULTI-CAUSE MOTIVE MODEL
`OBSERVED ACTION ≠ SINGLE MOTIVE`.
Hold several plausible motive models until behavior/context/evidence makes one more likely. Strong alignment with Zunshine evidence provenance.

### M15-07 — SCENE-BASED BIOGRAPHY
Select past moments that changed expectation, attachment, coping strategy or social position; do not build encyclopedic backstory unless it pays into present causality.

### M15-08 — EMBODIMENT CAUSALITY
`BODY / SENSORIUM / CAPABILITY → ACTION OPTIONS → SOCIAL RESPONSE → RISK → CHOICE`.
For conscious robots/nonhuman persons, embodiment is a present affordance system, not evidence for human consciousness-transfer technology.

### M15-09 — SOCIAL ECOLOGY CHARACTER GATE
`FAMILY / PEERS / INSTITUTION / HABITAT / VOCATION / LINEAGE → NORMALITY + LEVERAGE + OBLIGATION → SCENE BEHAVIOR`.
Worldbuilding becomes character pressure rather than exposition.

### M15-10 — COUNTERMODEL OPPONENT
`REAL BENEFIT + OWN DESIRE + LEVERAGE + EVIDENCE + BLIND SPOT / COST`.
A competent adult, institution or conscious robot can oppose the protagonist while being right about important things.

### M15-11 — BOOK FUNCTION ≠ STORYWORLD PERSONHOOD
A supporting role may be small in this book. It still needs enough independent desire/relations/choice to exist as a person in the world.

### M15-12 — SCENE CHARACTER ENGINE
`SETUP / PRESENT STATE → COMPETING WANTS → TACTICS → TURN / DECISION / REVELATION → CHANGED RELATION OR STATE → NEXT INTENTION`.
Reflection is retained when it processes pressure into a new decision; duplicate explanation is cut.

### M15-13 — POV GROUND
`POV OWNER → OBSERVABLE → KNOWABLE → INFERRED → DISTANCE`.
No telepathic access to institutional, robot, cultural or metaphysical facts without a path of knowledge.

### M15-14 — VOICE = STYLE + WORLDVIEW + ATTITUDE
For each viewpoint/major speaker define not just vocabulary but habitual attention, assumptions, rhythm, social strategy and relation-specific register.

### M15-15 — DIALOGUE AS TACTICAL ACTION
`WANT → VERBAL/NONVERBAL TACTIC → REACTION → TACTIC CHANGE → CHANGED STATE`.
Direct dialogue earns space when exact wording changes trust, consent, evidence, relationship, power, emotion or decision; otherwise summary/indirect mode may be stronger.

## 5. FAILURE MODES
1. **Plot Puppet** — character exists only to serve plot/protagonist.
2. **Trait Collage** — interesting adjectives without causal life.
3. **Initial-Impression Prison** — first archetype/image never evolves.
4. **Biography Without Behavior** — history has no present tactical effect.
5. **Personalization Overreach** — writer's analogy erases material/cultural difference.
6. **Complete-Knowledge Fantasy** — character treated as psychologically solved.
7. **Mandatory Trauma Retrofit** — wound added to manufacture depth.
8. **Mandatory Secret Retrofit** — shame/secret added because theory expects it.
9. **Contradiction Checklist** — random opposites mistaken for complexity.
10. **Psychoanalysis as Canon** — historical craft psychology imported as science.
11. **Motive Monoculture** — one clean cause explains every behavior.
12. **Vulnerability Inflation** — escalating damage/illness to force empathy.
13. **Body Inventory** — appearance without affordance/social consequence.
14. **Earth-2020s Sociology Import** — wages/rent/class assumptions imported into post-money orbitals.
15. **Author Mouthpiece Politics** — institutions/characters exist to deliver thesis.
16. **Quirk as Character** — tic/slang substitutes for desire/tactics.
17. **Mandatory Transformation** — every protagonist forced through therapeutic lesson.
18. **Multi-Protagonist Fog** — saga equality confused with equal book-level attention.
19. **Moral Caricature Opponent** — countermodel denied real benefits/evidence.
20. **Supporting-Character Servitude** — ally/robot/love interest lacks off-axis life.
21. **Scene Conflict Monoculture** — every scene forced into overt confrontation.
22. **Reflection Duplication** — sequel restates what action already proved.
23. **POV Telepathy** — focal character knows what they cannot know.
24. **Unreliable-Narrator Excuse** — continuity error defended as subjectivity.
25. **Universal Voice Costume** — everyone uses same authorial wit with different slang.
26. **Dialogue Transcription** — realistic filler reproduced instead of dramatic speech.
27. **Subtext Compulsion** — every line forced to conceal trauma/secret.

## 6. CROSS-SOURCE COMPARISON
- **Corbett + McKee Character:** McKee is stronger on modus operandi/cast architecture; Corbett is stronger on discovery, sociological life and preventing role-derived puppets. Use together.
- **Corbett + Card:** Card provides reader contract, character hierarchy and POV-depth discipline; Corbett provides source/discovery and social/relational texture. `SAGA EQUALITY ≠ BOOK-LEVEL EQUAL POV` remains.
- **Corbett + LaPlante:** both resist rigid formula and support live generation before over-control. LaPlante is stronger on revision stage/show-tell epistemics; Corbett on character discovery and social embodiment.
- **Corbett + Truby:** Truby's character web supplies structural opposition; Corbett prevents nodes in that web from becoming mere functions.
- **Corbett + Zunshine:** Corbett warns against single motive certainty; Zunshine supplies formal source/evidence-status discipline. Together: behavior supports a model of mind but does not prove it.
- **Corbett + Rosenfeld:** Rosenfeld is stronger on scene intention/endings; Corbett strengthens why each participant wants something different and how social history changes tactics.
- **Corbett + Lodge:** Lodge protects plural prose modes; Corbett's voice work should not become slang/template enforcement.
- **Corbett + Wolf:** Wolf governs world/canon provenance; Corbett ensures the people living inside that world are not delivery devices for world rules.

## 7. IVDIVO OPERATIONALIZATION
### ONE MAIN STORY
One causal engine may contain several independent people. Character independence does not justify unrelated subplots; each major line must change the shared problem, relationship network, evidence state or final choice.

### Orbital Youth / post-money civilisation
Money/rent/consumer status are not default motivation. Build concrete nonfungible wants and social ecology: responsibility, mastery, trust, belonging, mission place, attention/time, intimacy, consent, reputation, vocation, relationship and unique experience. These are stakes, not a replacement currency system.

### Conscious robots
Mandatory gates:
- independent desire,
- relationships not mediated by the protagonist,
- right to refuse,
- embodiment-specific affordances,
- own social/cultural normality,
- relation-specific voice/tactics.
If removal/replacement by a smart terminal leaves consent, relationship and conflict unchanged, personhood has not been written.

### Consciousness schools / dimensional knowledge
Character belief and school doctrine stay separate from ontology:
`PRACTITIONER OBSERVATION → REPRODUCIBLE PRACTICE EFFECT → SCHOOL INTERPRETATION → COMPETING INTERPRETATION → POSSIBLE WORLD FACT`.
Corbett's spirituality/psychology passages cannot establish metaphysical canon.

### YA ensemble
For each youth define:
`PRIVATE WANT → FRUSTRATION FINGERPRINT → KEY RELATIONSHIP ASYMMETRIES → VULNERABILITY WITHOUT DAMAGE-INFLATION → ROLE IN ONE MAIN STORY → OFF-AXIS LIFE`.
Five distinct biographies are insufficient if all five solve pressure with identical sarcasm/competence/defiance.

### Prose / line editing
Repair order remains:
`CAUSAL ENGINE → SCENE NECESSITY → CHARACTER WANT/TACTIC → POV → DIALOGUE MODE → PARAGRAPH INTENT → RHYTHM → WORDING`.
Do not cure structural character flatness with verbal polish.

## 10. SYNTHESIS
Corbett's durable contribution to IVDIVO is not a five-item personality checklist. It is the insistence that character emerges through **desire, social context, interaction, contradiction, behavior and scene**, while remaining partly discoverable rather than exhaustively solved.

Final IVDIVO formula:
`INDEPENDENT PERSON → SPECIFIC WANT → SOCIAL/RELATIONAL CONTEXT → CHARACTER-SPECIFIC TACTIC → PRESSURE → CHOICE → CONSEQUENCE → UPDATED CHARACTER MODEL`.

The writer designs enough to create causal pressure, then allows scenes to reveal a person more complex than the original role. Theory remains a diagnostic tool, not the character's prison.
