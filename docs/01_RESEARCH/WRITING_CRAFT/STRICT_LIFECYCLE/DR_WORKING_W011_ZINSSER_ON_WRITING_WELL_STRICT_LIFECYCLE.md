# 2. CLAIMS / MECHANISMS EXTRACTED TO DATE
## W011-C01 — No single drafting ritual is mandatory
Contradictory personal processes can both work. Judge process by whether it produces strong finished prose, not conformity to ritual.

## W011-C02 — Clear writing is downstream of clear thinking
Confusion often enters before syntax. A sentence can be grammatical and still fail because the writer has not decided exactly what relation, actor, time shift or claim is being expressed.

## W011-C03 — Rewriting is a primary craft operation
`GENERATE WITHOUT PARALYSIS → REWRITE WITHOUT SENTIMENTAL ATTACHMENT`.

## W011-C04 — Clutter includes duplicated thought, not merely excess words
A whole sentence or paragraph may be clutter if it repeats an already understood beat or tells readers what they can infer.

## W011-C05 — Simplicity is functional clarity, not formal shortness
Long sentences can be clear; short sentences can be muddy, repetitive or theatrically fragmented.

## W011-C06 — Voice survives pruning when pruning targets nonfunction
Zinsser's strongest version of simplicity removes prestige language, duplicated meaning and obscured agency while preserving human presence, enthusiasm, rhythm and point of view.

## W011-C07 — Reader craft and writer attitude are different systems
Craft owes readers clarity and continuity. The writer does not owe every reader agreement, likability or generic mass-audience conformity.

## W011-C08 — Unity is controlled selection, not total coverage
Useful variables: principal POV/pronoun, tense, mood/attitude, scale of coverage and the main point. The writer may discover a better mode while drafting, but the final text must be reconciled into a coherent reading contract.

## W011-C09 — Reduction is an architectural decision
A huge subject becomes writable through a bounded corner, concrete people and selective time/place. `SAGA SCALE ≠ BOOK SCALE` is reinforced.

## W011-C10 — Leads establish an attention contract; endings should stop after the work is done
The opening need not use one formula. Specific fact, narrative, voice, curiosity, humor or a person can all open well. Endings fail when they summarize what readers already understood.

## W011-C11 — Surplus research improves selection only until it delays writing
Collect more material than will be used, then choose the most informative and vivid details. This supports `WORLD DATABASE ≠ BOOK NARRATIVE`.

## W011-C12 — Facts can carry color without decorative delay
Do not separate “information” and “color” by writing a vague theatrical lead before the real material. Specific material often supplies its own surprise, humor and atmosphere.

## W011-C13 — Trust the reader with inference
Do not pre-label a fact as surprising, important or symbolic if the fact can produce that effect directly. Give the reader room to perform part of the meaning-making.

## W011-C14 — Sentence continuity is a reader-state problem
At each sentence boundary ask what noun, image, time, location, subject, tone and inference are still active in the reader’s mind. A locally correct sentence can be globally wrong if it attaches to the wrong lingering state.

## W011-C15 — Rewriting is sequencing as well as cutting
Repair includes moving information earlier, signaling shifts, strengthening transitions, changing sentence length, replacing vague words and making the last word/phrase carry intended weight.

## W011-M01 — FUNCTIONAL WORD TEST
For each word/phrase/sentence ask what new reader-facing function it performs. Delete or rebuild components that merely duplicate meaning, inflate status or obscure agency.

## W011-M02 — CLARITY BEFORE PRESTIGE
When technical/institutional language sounds more important than it is precise, rewrite until actor/action/relationship is legible.

## W011-M03 — BRACKET PASS
Temporarily bracket suspected clutter rather than immediately deleting it. Re-read without it and test whether meaning, voice, causal relation or rhythm is actually lost.

## W011-M04 — READER-STATE CONTINUITY PASS
For every adjacent sentence pair:
`STATE LEFT BY A → WHAT B ASSUMES → MISMATCH? → SIGNAL / REORDER / REWRITE`.
Track subject, actor, time, place, knowledge, tone and emphasis.

## W011-M05 — ONE-POINT / BOUNDED-CORNER TEST
Before drafting a large subject, define the bounded narrative/intellectual corner and what single durable change in reader understanding the piece/book is designed to produce.

## W011-M06 — LEAD CONTRACT
`ATTENTION → ORIENTATION → PROMISE` without mandatory action, mystery or gimmick.

## W011-M07 — STOP-WHEN-DONE ENDING TEST
Once the local work is complete, do not add a school-essay summary. Prefer an earned final image, fact, consequence, echo, quotation or precise cutoff.

## W011-M08 — MATERIAL SURPLUS / NARRATIVE SELECTION
`COLLECT > NEEDED → IDENTIFY HIGH-UTILITY DETAILS → SELECT → DISCARD SURPLUS → WRITE`.

## W011-M09 — READ-ALOUD RHYTHM TEST
Read at paragraph/chapter scale. Diagnose same-length sentence monotony, accidental choppiness, false emphasis and transition failures by ear.

# 3. FAILURE MODES / FIREWALLS
1. **Simplicity = Shortness Confusion** — rejected.
2. **50% Cut as KPI** — rejected. Zinsser's example is a diagnostic provocation, not a universal target.
3. **Delete Every Adverb / Passive Absolutism** — functional diagnostics, not bans.
4. **Nonfiction Rule Imported Unchanged into Fiction** — medium differences matter.
5. **Plain Language = Flat Voice** — clarity must preserve personhood, attitude and rhythm.
6. **First Person = Natural Voice Law** — rejected for fiction; voice and focalization are separate variables.
7. **One Point = One-Dimensional Novel** — rejected. ONE MAIN STORY may contain multiple themes and relationship pressures; the gate concerns dominant causal/reader contract, not intellectual poverty.
8. **Surprise Ending Mandatory** — rejected. Fitness/closure outrank surprise.
9. **Historical Usage Advice as Current Authority** — Zinsser's language/market examples are historically situated; current prescriptive claims require fresh verification if operationalized.
10. **Institutional Jargon Used as Worldbuilding** — jargon must denote real mechanisms rather than prestige fog.
11. **Over-Explanation / Significance Nudging** — avoid telling readers a fact is amazing, tragic or symbolic when staging already establishes the effect.
12. **Feature-Lead Theater** — fragments, fake suspense and withheld subject matter are not automatically engaging.

# 4. CROSS-SOURCE POSITION — CURRENT
## Zinsser + Francine Prose
Prose asks writers to attend to every word and cadence; Zinsser adds a ruthless function/clarity filter. Combined result: precision without flattening rhythm.

## Zinsser + Lamott
Lamott protects discovery; Zinsser protects the reader from discovery-draft excess:
`DISCOVERY DRAFT → STRUCTURAL REVISION → CLARITY/CLUTTER PASS → RHYTHM/VOICE PASS`.

## Zinsser + Pushman
Agreement that paragraph and sentence form serve intention; no formal length target.

## Zinsser + McKee/Truby/Rosenfeld
Zinsser operates primarily below story architecture. Therefore his pruning pass must come after ONE MAIN STORY / causal / scene acceptance. Clear sentences cannot rescue a structurally unnecessary scene.

## Zinsser + Wolf/Ribó
Unity and bounded selection reinforce `WORLD DATABASE ≠ BOOK NARRATIVE` and `SAGA SCALE ≠ BOOK SCALE`.

# 5. IVDIVO OPERATIONALIZATION — CURRENT
## A. Prose / “обрубки”
Do not translate simplicity into short sentences. Repair order remains:
`ONE MAIN STORY / CAUSALITY → SCENE NECESSITY → SHOW/TELL → POV → DIALOGUE MODE → PARAGRAPH INTENT → READER-STATE CONTINUITY → SENTENCE RHYTHM → WORDING`.

New Zinsser-specific regression test:
- if several short sentences preserve the same actor, time, focus and thought without meaningful shift, test recombination;
- if a short sentence creates a real contrast, revelation, beat, breath or end-weight, protect it;
- if a long sentence makes actor/action/relation hard to recover, rebuild its logic rather than merely shorten it.

## B. ONE MAIN STORY
Use Zinsser's reduction principle at book scale: the saga can be vast, but one book selects a bounded causal corner. Do not prove the entire civilization in every installment.

## C. Worldbuilding / post-money civilization
Prefer concrete mechanisms over abstractions. Terms such as `allocation`, `access`, `qualification`, `contribution`, `scheduling`, `priority` and `assignment` must reveal actual constraints and decisions. They must not become euphemisms for purchasing power, salary, rent or hidden credits.

## D. Conscious robots
Clarity must not homogenize robot voices. The function pass removes fog, not personhood. Named robots retain relation-specific diction, independent goals, consent and the right to refuse.

## E. Mystery / evidence
Avoid significance labels before the evidence. Present trace/source/context cleanly enough for the reader to form a model, then let later evidence revise it.

## F. Specialist consciousness schools
Use plain epistemic status language inside production records:
`OBSERVED → REPRODUCIBLE EFFECT → SCHOOL INTERPRETATION → COMPETING INTERPRETATION → POSSIBLE ONTOLOGICAL CLAIM`.
Do not let prestige vocabulary promote interpretation into fact.
