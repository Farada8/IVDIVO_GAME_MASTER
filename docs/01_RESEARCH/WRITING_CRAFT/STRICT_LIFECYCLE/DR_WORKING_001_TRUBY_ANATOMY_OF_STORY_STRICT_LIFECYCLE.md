# 4. PROVISIONAL CLAIMS / MECHANISMS FROM CONTIGUOUSLY READ MATERIAL

These are working notes only. Full-source synthesis is forbidden until FULL READ closes.

### T1 — ORGANIC CAUSALITY OVER EXTERNAL ACT MARKERS
Truby's strongest opening claim is that story parts should grow from internal causal relations rather than mechanical act breaks.

IVDIVO working translation:
`CAUSE → CHARACTER INTERPRETATION → ACTION → CONSEQUENCE → LEARNING → REVISED ACTION`.

### T2 — STORY = ACTION + LEARNING
Desire initiates action; new information changes decisions/course. This is directly compatible with IVDIVO's existing chapter causality requirement.

### T3 — STORY SUBSYSTEMS MUST INTERACT
Character, plot, revelations, world, moral pressure, symbols, scenes and dialogue should not behave as independent layers pasted together.

Working gate:
> If removing a subsystem leaves every other major subsystem unchanged, it may be decorative rather than causal.

### T4 — AUDIENCE THINKING + FEELING
Truby correctly treats audience participation as both experiential and inferential. Information control should let the reader build/revise models while emotionally living the action.

Correction already required from later IVDIVO source-monitoring work:
- withholding must be fair;
- do not hide facts the POV naturally knows merely to manufacture suspense.

### T5 — MULTIPLE STORY MOVEMENT PATTERNS
Linear single-hero movement is not the only viable form. Branching/comparative structures can emphasize society and connections among people.

IVDIVO relevance:
- Orbital Youth can use branching ensemble movement without becoming five unrelated mini-books;
- ONE MAIN STORY still requires causal convergence.

### T6 — PREMISE AS MACRO DIAGNOSTIC
A one-line premise can expose whether the story has a coherent character/action trajectory.

Correction:
- premise is not absolute destiny;
- Truby's early claim that premise failure cannot be saved is directionally useful but too categorical;
- premise refinement remains iterative under current IVDIVO change-control rules.

### T7 — DO NOT RUN FROM HOT PREMISE DIRECTLY INTO SCENES
Strong convergence with current IVDIVO production pipeline: structural exploration should precede polished prose when the story engine is not stable.

# 5. PROVISIONAL FAILURE MODES

- **ANTI-TEMPLATE BECOMES TEMPLATE** — Truby criticizes three-act mechanics but can itself become a rigid organic-story prescription if used mechanically.
- **UNIVERSAL GROWTH CLAIM** — not every strong story requires positive maturation; refusal/failure/static integrity may be valid.
- **WITHHOLDING OVERREACH** — information hiding cannot override POV fairness.
- **SUBSYSTEM TOTALIZATION** — not every short/simple story needs maximal development of every web.
- **PREMISE FATALISM** — weak first premise line may be diagnostically useful but stories can discover their actual premise through development/revision.
- **FORM HIERARCHY** — branching/multiperspective does not automatically mean more advanced/better fiction.
