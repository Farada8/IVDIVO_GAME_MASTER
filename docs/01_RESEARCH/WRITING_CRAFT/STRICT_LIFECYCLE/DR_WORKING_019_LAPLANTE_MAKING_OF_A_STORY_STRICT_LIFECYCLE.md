## 3. PROVISIONAL CLAIM EXTRACTION — READ MATERIAL ONLY

### L19-C01 — Generative mode and evaluative mode are different cognitive jobs
Early generation benefits from suspension of premature censorship; craft/revision becomes more useful once material exists.

### L19-C02 — Strong creative writing combines surprise with conviction
Freshness without plausibility becomes arbitrary; plausibility without surprise becomes received language or cliché.

### L19-C03 — Strong prose resists paraphrase
If a summary preserves nearly all of a passage's effect, the writing may be under-rendered. Details, voice, rhythm, perspective and implication should add irreducible value.

### L19-C04 — Sentimentality is an unearned stock response, not merely sweetness
Cynicism, grimness, uplift, tragedy, heroism and victimhood can all become sentimental when the response is prepackaged rather than earned by specific context.

### L19-C05 — Attention is a source of voice
What a narrator/POV character notices, ignores, compares and remembers differentiates voice more reliably than ornamental diction or slang.

### L19-C06 — Write into what remains unknown inside familiar material
A known topic can remain artistically alive if the writer locates what they genuinely do not understand about it rather than transmitting a settled lesson.

### L19-C07 — Particular before generalization
Starting from a thesis and choosing details to prove it creates propaganda/travelogue effects. Starting from specific particulars allows a more complex general pattern to emerge.

### L19-C08 — Detail needs hierarchy
Concrete specificity is not a license for catalogs. Select details that render world, POV, relation or implication; excess specificity can crowd the reader out.

### L19-C09 — Metaphor must earn its cognitive cost
First establish what the thing is. Comparison is valuable when it clarifies/complicates perception; strained cleverness and metaphor pile-up slow the prose and advertise the writer.

### L19-C10 — Symbols are strongest when accumulated, not pasted on
A recurring image/object may gather symbolic force through story context. Conscious symbolic insertion cannot substitute for character, causality and scene work.

### L19-C11 — Form is discovered and diagnosed, not imposed automatically
Conflict-crisis-resolution, epiphany, change and other conventional models can diagnose some stories after generation; they are not universal creation laws.

### L19-C12 — Character change is optional; consequential opportunity is not
A character may change, fail to change, misunderstand or decline an opportunity. What matters is that a significant state/opportunity has passed and the story earns its closure.

### L19-C13 — Show and tell are complementary tools
Scene gives eyewitness immediacy; narration gives compression, thought, history, interpretation, time control and voice. `Show, don't tell` is not a universal production rule.

### L19-C14 — The right unit is functional proximity, not maximum dramatization
Important exact words/actions may deserve scene. Routine/process/background can be summarized. Sometimes narration is more elegant and powerful than dramatization.

## 4. PROVISIONAL MECHANISMS

### M-L19-01 — GENERATE / DIAGNOSE SPLIT
`GENERATE WITHOUT PREMATURE POLICING → IDENTIFY LIVE MATERIAL → DIAGNOSE STRUCTURE → REVISE → LINE EDIT`.

### M-L19-02 — SURPRISING-YET-CONVINCING TEST
For a major choice/detail/reveal:
`FRESH? → CHARACTER/WORLD-TRUE? → CAUSALLY PREPARED? → READER CAN RECONSTRUCT WHY?`

### M-L19-03 — UNKNOWN-INSIDE-KNOWN
`KNOWN MATERIAL → WHAT IS STILL GENUINELY UNCERTAIN? → OBSERVABLE PARTICULARS → ALTERNATIVE MODELS → TEST/CHOICE`.

### M-L19-04 — PARTICULAR-TO-GENERAL
`SPECIFIC PERSON / OBJECT / ACTION / PLACE → PATTERN → POSSIBLE MEANING` rather than `THEME → ILLUSTRATIVE DETAIL`.

### M-L19-05 — DETAIL DOUBLE-PAY
A selected detail should ideally do at least two jobs among world, POV, character, relation, tension, causality, foreshadowing or rhythm.

### M-L19-06 — SHOW/TELL ROUTER
Use DIRECT SCENE when exact action/wording changes state. Use NARRATION/SUMMARY when compression, thought, context or time movement is the stronger reader experience.

## 5. PROVISIONAL FAILURE MODES
1. Premature Editor — revision intelligence kills generative discovery before material exists.
2. Writerly Voice Costume — ornate diction substitutes for perception.
3. Sentiment Button — stock emotional event is expected to generate feeling automatically.
4. Grim Sentimentality — darkness/cynicism treated as inherently profound.
5. Thesis-First Detail Selection — world evidence is cherry-picked to prove a predetermined message.
6. Sensory Checklist — one detail for every sense without hierarchy.
7. Detail Crowding — specificity excludes reader participation and forward motion.
8. Metaphor Pile-Up — comparison replaces clear rendering.
9. Symbol Retrofit — author pastes symbolic meaning onto structurally weak material.
10. Epiphany Factory — every character receives a packaged realization.
11. Mandatory Change Arc — refusal/failure to change is treated as invalid fiction.
12. Theory-before-Material — beat/arc formulas determine what must happen before the story earns it.
13. Show-Don't-Tell Dogma — narration is deleted regardless of function.
14. Scene Inflation — routine/logistics are dramatized because scene is assumed superior.

## 6. PROVISIONAL CROSS-SOURCE COMPARISON

### With Truby / McKee / Brody
LaPlante provides a necessary anti-formula counterweight. Truby/McKee can sharpen causal diagnosis; Brody can identify YA developmental pressure. LaPlante correctly warns against turning any of them into an externally imposed creation template.

### With Rosenfeld
Strong convergence on scene/narration routing and revision-stage discipline. Rosenfeld is stronger on scene engineering; LaPlante is stronger on discovery, ambiguity and resisting false closure.

### With Card / McKee Character
Convergence: character is specific behavior/perception, not label. LaPlante adds that selecting what the character notices is itself character/voice construction.

### With McKee Dialogue
Convergence on dramatization when exact words/actions matter. LaPlante more strongly protects narration as voice and interpretive compression.

## 7. PROVISIONAL IVDIVO PRODUCTION GATES

**L19-G01 Generate Before Policing** — do not run every production gate while generating a first living version of a scene.

**L19-G02 No Sentiment Buttons** — death, injury, child danger, robot innocence, parental rejection, first love and sacrifice must be made specific and causal before emotional payoff is expected.

**L19-G03 Grimness Is Not Depth** — dystopian or tragic tone requires the same evidentiary specificity as optimism.

**L19-G04 Character Attention Fingerprint** — each major POV must notice a different subset of the same environment for character-specific reasons.

**L19-G05 Unknown-Inside-Known** — mystery/metaphysical material must retain genuine questions and testable distinctions rather than author-certified answers.

**L19-G06 Particular Before World Thesis** — orbital society is revealed through specific lived choices, not paragraphs chosen to prove that the society is post-money, advanced or ethical.

**L19-G07 Detail Function** — unusual technology/object receives textual attention in proportion to its story/POV function.

**L19-G08 No Metaphor Repair** — do not decorate an unclear action or weak image with figurative language; clarify the perception first.

**L19-G09 No Symbol Repair** — symbolism cannot rescue weak causal architecture.

**L19-G10 Change Is Not Mandatory** — arc can be change, refusal, partial change, mislearning or confirmation, provided consequences are real.

**L19-G11 Theory Is Diagnostic** — craft school maps the existing problem; the story does not exist to validate the school.

**L19-G12 Show/Tell by Reader Effect** — choose scene/narration based on what the reader must experience, not a blanket hierarchy.

**L19-G13 No Logistics Scene by Default** — transport, setup, repair process and repeated procedure are compressed unless exact action changes state.

**L19-G14 Discovery Does Not Override Canon** — exploratory drafting may reveal story possibilities; Founder directives and approved canon remain higher authority.
