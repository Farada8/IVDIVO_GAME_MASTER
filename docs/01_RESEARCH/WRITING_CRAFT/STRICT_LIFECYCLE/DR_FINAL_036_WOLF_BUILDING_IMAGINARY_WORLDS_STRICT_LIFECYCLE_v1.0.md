## SYNTHESIZED CLAIMS

### W36-C01 — WORLD AND STORY ARE DISTINCT SYSTEMS
An imaginary world can contain far more places, histories, institutions, languages, objects and relationships than any single narrative needs. World depth is therefore compatible with aggressive narrative selection.

### W36-C02 — COMPLETENESS IS INFERENTIAL, NOT EXHAUSTIVE
A fictional world can never be literally complete. Perceived completeness comes from enough interconnected rules, landmarks and consequences for the audience to infer what has not been stated.

### W36-C03 — CONSISTENCY IS GENERATIVE
Once world rules exist, they constrain future additions and also generate story possibilities. Consistency should create options and consequences, not protect obsolete drafts from current canon.

### W36-C04 — AUDIENCES FILL GAPS WITH DEFAULTS
Unspecified material is reconstructed from Primary-World assumptions plus already-established world rules. In future/post-money fiction this is dangerous: if a normality is not naturally overridden, readers may import money, wage work, AI-servant assumptions or contemporary family/institutional defaults.

### W36-C05 — WORLD STRUCTURES ARE RELATIONAL
Maps, timelines, genealogies, nature, culture, language, mythology and philosophy become convincing when they constrain and explain one another rather than existing as independent lore categories.

### W36-C06 — INCOMPLETENESS CAN BE PRODUCTIVE
Bounded unknowns, partially understood history and unresolved structures can make a world feel larger, provided the gap is not merely an authorial contradiction or missing design work.

### W36-C07 — WORLDS OUTLIVE INDIVIDUAL NARRATIVES
Series, sequels, prequels, interquels, ancillary works and transmedia can continue one world without making every individual story an incomplete fragment.

### W36-C08 — EXPANSION CREATES CANON PRESSURE
Long-lived worlds accumulate additions, reinterpretations and contradictions. Retcon is therefore a governance problem: new material needs provenance, authority and explicit supersession rules.

### W36-C09 — TRANSMEDIA ADAPTATION PRESERVES SALIENT INVARIANTS
A world cannot be represented identically across prose, audio, film, game or reference documents. Strong adaptation preserves salient world relationships and causal rules while changing medium-specific presentation.

### W36-C10 — CANONICITY AND TRUTH ARE DIFFERENT
A document, doctrine, map or testimony can canonically exist inside the storyworld while being incomplete, biased or factually wrong inside that world. `OFFICIAL IN-WORLD SOURCE ≠ WORLD FACT`.

### W36-C11 — AUTHORSHIP EXISTS IN CIRCLES
Original creator, collaborators/licensed contributors and audience/fan contributions have different authority. Social influence does not automatically equal canon authority.

## IVDIVO MECHANISMS

### M36-01 — WORLD DATABASE / BOOK NARRATIVE SPLIT
`WORLD DATABASE` may hold far more material than appears in prose. `BOOK NARRATIVE` admits only material that changes action, choice, relationship, reader inference, necessary orientation or causal possibility.

### M36-02 — DEFAULT PROPAGATION
`CHANGED DEFAULT → INFRASTRUCTURE CONSEQUENCE → SOCIAL NORMALITY → CHARACTER ASSUMPTION → CHOICE / FRICTION`.
A changed noun is not worldbuilding unless its consequences propagate.

### M36-03 — DEFAULT-OVERRIDE TEST
For every unstated everyday rule ask: `What Earth/default assumption will the reader insert?` If that default conflicts with current canon, correct it through ordinary causal life rather than lecture.

### M36-04 — WORLD-INFERENCE GRID
For a major invented system check only relevant links among:
`ENVIRONMENT → MATERIALS → TECHNOLOGY → INSTITUTIONS → LANGUAGE → CULTURE → HISTORY → PERSONHOOD → DAILY LIFE`.
Do not fill every cell for display; use the grid to catch impossible downstream effects.

### M36-05 — CANON PROVENANCE
For lore claims store:
`CLAIM → SOURCE → AUTHORITY LEVEL → STATUS → SUPERSEDES → REASON → DOWNSTREAM CONSEQUENCES`.

### M36-06 — IVDIVO AUTHORITY ORDER
`Founder/direct user canon > approved Saga Bible/Narrative OS > active production reconciler > book-specific locked manuscript > research/craft application > working draft/old concept > reference fiction`.
AI-generated working documents never self-promote to canon merely because they are newer or more detailed.

### M36-07 — CANON STATUS TAGS
Every consequential lore item should be classifiable as:
`CURRENT CANON / SUPERSEDED / WORKING / VARIANT-WHAT-IF / REFERENCE-ONLY / IN-WORLD CLAIM`.

### M36-08 — RETCON LEDGER
Never silently overwrite a contradiction. Record what changed, which authority changed it and which downstream books/files require repair.

### M36-09 — SALIENT-INVARIANT TRANSMEDIA GATE
Before adaptation identify what cannot change without creating a different world: ontology limits, personhood, causal technology limits, social normalities, relationship history and book-ending consequences. Presentation may change by medium.

### M36-10 — STRUCTURED UNKNOWN
Unknown ontology can remain unknown. Store `KNOWN / REPRODUCIBLE / SCHOOL INTERPRETATION / CONTESTED / UNKNOWN` instead of saturating ambiguity into false canon.

### M36-11 — FRANCHISE EXPANSION / BOOK CLOSURE
`LOCAL STORY PROBLEM RESOLVED → COST/AFTERMATH → CHANGED WORLD STATE → OPTIONAL SAGA EXPANSION`.
A larger world is not permission for an unfinished novel.

## FAILURE MODES
1. **World Encyclopedia in Disguise** — prose exists to prove database depth.
2. **Primary-World Default Leak** — money, wage labour, AI-servant logic or modern consumer status silently repopulate a future world.
3. **Consistency Fetish** — obsolete working lore outranks current Founder canon or stronger story causality.
4. **Infinite Completeness Trap** — production waits for impossible total specification.
5. **World Logic Without Character Consequence** — elegant systems never change anyone’s behavior.
6. **Mystery as Missing Author Work** — contradiction is praised as ambiguity although no coherent bounded model exists.
7. **Ancillary-Material Dump** — maps, timelines and histories are copied into dialogue/exposition.
8. **Novel Serves Wiki** — scenes are chosen primarily to tour another world feature.
9. **One Changed Noun, Unchanged Society** — futuristic terminology with contemporary social defaults.
10. **Silent Retcon** — later working file changes canon without provenance.
11. **Newest-File Authority Fallacy** — recency is mistaken for authority.
12. **Official-Is-True Fallacy** — in-world institutional statement becomes world fact without evidence.
13. **Transmedia Literalism** — prose/audio/game are forced to reveal the same information in the same way.
14. **Fan/Reference Canon Leak** — useful mechanisms from external fiction become IVDIVO facts.
15. **Ontology Saturation** — intentionally partial consciousness-school knowledge becomes mainstream scientific certainty.

## CROSS-SOURCE SYNTHESIS
- **Wolf + Truby/McKee:** world may exceed story; each book still needs one causal spine and closure.
- **Wolf + Ribó/Lodge:** `WORLD DATABASE → NARRATIVE SELECTION → DISCOURSE PRESENTATION` separates lore existence from prose delivery.
- **Wolf + Cambridge SF:** historical genre conventions are possibilities, not canon defaults; world consequences must transform inherited tropes.
- **Wolf + Zunshine:** external canon provenance and internal evidence provenance should use parallel status discipline.
- **Wolf + LaPlante:** incomplete knowledge can remain productive only when authorial uncertainty is distinguished from character/school uncertainty.

## IVDIVO OPERATIONALIZATION
### Post-money orbital civilisation
Do not explain post-money society as a manifesto. Plant ordinary causal signals that prevent readers from restoring Earth-2020s defaults. Scarcity may remain for genuinely nonfungible things—another person’s time, unique location, singular mission slot, consent, trust, reputation, relationship, lived expertise—not for generic manufactured objects under disguised credits.

### Conscious robot civilisation
Robot personhood is a salient invariant. A named conscious robot needs independent wants, relationships, refusals, obligations and social existence. If replacing the robot with a smart terminal leaves consent/conflict/relationship unchanged, personhood has not been written.

### Consciousness schools / dimensional knowledge
Preserve the hierarchy:
`PRACTITIONER OBSERVATION → REPRODUCIBLE PRACTICE EFFECT → SCHOOL INTERPRETATION → COMPETING INTERPRETATION → ONTOLOGICAL CLAIM`.
Small practitioner populations remain a real knowledge bottleneck. High engineering capability does not automatically solve this domain.

### LESSON ZERO / Book One
No new defect from Wolf justifies reopening development lock. World database depth should remain outside the manuscript unless reader evidence exposes a concrete orientation or continuity failure.

### Future books
Before introducing a new city, technology, school or robot institution ask:
1. Which current canon source authorizes it?
2. Which Primary-World default must be overridden?
3. What does it change for a character now?
4. What world systems must it causally affect?
5. What can remain unknown/off-page?
6. Does the local book still close without explaining the whole world?
