## 3. CORE CLAIMS
### Z38-C01 — Behavior supports mind-models but does not prove motive
Readers routinely infer beliefs, intentions and feelings from action, language and gesture. The inference is useful and fallible.

### Z38-C02 — Fiction can calibrate mental-state explicitness
A writer can state a mind-state, imply it through behavior, route it through another character, or hold it uncertain. Reader participation is a design variable.

### Z38-C03 — Source is part of information
A representation is not only `X`; it may be `A says X`, `B remembers X`, `system inferred X`, `narrator now believes X`, etc. Source tags affect how freely the information should be allowed to reshape the reader’s model.

### Z38-C04 — Source-monitoring is fallible
Characters and readers can confuse who said, thought or inferred something. That fallibility can be narratively productive but cannot be used to excuse authorial continuity errors.

### Z38-C05 — Information can remain under advisement
A claim may be retained without being promoted to world-fact. Later evidence can raise, lower or redirect its truth-status.

### Z38-C06 — Unreliability can be local and changing
A narrator/POV can be accurate about event sequence and wrong about motive, accurate now and self-exonerating in remembered material, or reliable in one domain and unreliable in another.

### Z38-C07 — Retrospective reweighting is a central reader pleasure
Later evidence can make earlier scenes mean something different without invalidating the earlier observations themselves.

### Z38-C08 — A lie is structurally expensive
One lie can force re-evaluation of many other characters’ beliefs and subsequent actions. Many independent liars create combinatorial cognitive load.

### Z38-C09 — Detective stories require focused uncertainty
“Everyone may be lying” is manageable only if the story narrows the relevant question-space and keeps the information architecture legible.

### Z38-C10 — Material clues matter because they imply agents and actions
A trace does not solve a mystery by itself. It matters through reconstruction: who produced it, under what conditions, for what possible reason, and which alternate model also fits.

### Z38-C11 — Genre hybrids compete for reader attention
Mystery, romance, institutional conflict and other engines may each demand intensive tracking of minds. Combining them at equal maximum intensity can overload the book. Strong hybrids often share evidence, characters or causal events rather than running separate engines in parallel.

### Z38-C12 — Cognitive possibility does not determine literary history
Genre conventions arise through culture, technology, markets, history and author/reader contingencies. Cognitive accounts must not erase historical/cultural explanation.

### Z38-C13 — Reader effect is emergent
Mind-tracking, emotion, personal memory, ethics, style and cultural knowledge interact. No single cognitive mechanism fully explains why a novel moves a particular reader.

## 4. IVDIVO MECHANISMS
### M-Z38-01 — SOURCE-TAGGED EVIDENCE
`CONTENT → SOURCE → MODE → CONFIDENCE → ALTERNATIVE MODEL → UPDATE CONDITION`.

Modes:
- direct observation
- direct interior access
- witness report
- document/log
- system inference
- remembered report
- school/ideological interpretation
- narrator interpretation
- independent confirmation

### M-Z38-02 — BELIEF / FACT FIREWALL
`CHARACTER BELIEVES X ≠ CHARACTER OBSERVED X ≠ SYSTEM INFERRED X ≠ WORLD FACT X`.

No later chapter may silently promote one category into another.

### M-Z38-03 — EVIDENCE STATUS LADDER
`TRACE → PROVISIONAL INTERPRETATION → CORROBORATION / CONTRADICTION → MODEL REVISION → ACTION`.

Every important reveal must change either behavior, evidence status, relationship, or the next test.

### M-Z38-04 — MULTIPLE MOTIVE MODELS
`ACTION → MOTIVE A / B / C → NEW EVIDENCE → REWEIGHT`.

Especially useful for YA relationships, institutional conflict and conscious robots.

### M-Z38-05 — LOCAL UNRELIABILITY
For a narrator/POV ask separately:
- reliable about what happened?
- reliable about sequence?
- reliable about another person’s motive?
- reliable about own motive?
- reliable about remembered interpretation?

### M-Z38-06 — LIE SPHERE OF INFLUENCE
For every deliberate lie:
`LIAR → WHO RECEIVES IT → WHAT THEY DO BECAUSE OF IT → WHO ELSE IS AFFECTED → WHAT MUST BE REVISED WHEN LIE BREAKS`.

### M-Z38-07 — MATERIAL TRACE → MIND / ACTION RECONSTRUCTION
`TRACE → PRODUCER → CONDITIONS → IMPLIED ACTION → POSSIBLE PURPOSE → ALTERNATIVE PRODUCER/PURPOSE → TEST`.

### M-Z38-08 — DOMINANT INFERENCE ENGINE
When mixing mystery/romance/institutional/YA engines, decide which inference problem dominates this stretch of the book. Secondary engines should preferably share the same event/evidence rather than requiring separate high-load tracking systems.

### M-Z38-09 — RETROSPECTIVE FAIRNESS
A reveal is stronger if the reader can return to earlier scenes and see:
- observations were available;
- the prior interpretation was reasonable;
- the new interpretation is better supported;
- the author did not hide a fact the POV naturally knew.

## 5. FAILURE MODES
1. **Source Loss** — `Samir thinks X` becomes `X is true` two chapters later.
2. **System-Mind Telepathy** — prediction of behavior is treated as knowledge of motive.
3. **Evidence/Interpretation Collapse** — raw log and analyst conclusion are presented as one fact.
4. **Unreliable-Narrator Alibi** — continuity error is excused after the fact as POV unreliability.
5. **Liar Inflation** — many characters lie only to make mystery “harder.”
6. **Cognitive Fog** — reader cannot tell what is known, suspected, contradicted or merely remembered.
7. **Material-Clue Fetish** — technical trace exists without an agent/action reconstruction.
8. **Mystery/Romance Double Saturation** — two independent inference-heavy engines compete at full intensity without causal overlap.
9. **Ad-Hominem Proof** — unreliable source is taken to make every statement false; evidence must still be checked.
10. **Authorial Answer Key** — final explanation simply declares source truth-status instead of paying off prior tests.
11. **Historical-Cognitive Determinism** — genre or reader behavior is explained as if cognition alone caused it.
12. **Scientific Overreach** — 2006 cognitive/clinical claims become IVDIVO technical canon.
13. **Mind Explanation Saturation** — narration translates every gesture into motive and removes reader inference.
14. **Inference Starvation** — ambiguity is preserved without enough evidence for any usable reader model.

## 6. CROSS-SOURCE SYNTHESIS
### LaPlante
Strong convergence on `observation ≠ interpretation` and POV fallibility. LaPlante is stronger on practical POV/revision routing; Zunshine is stronger on tracking the provenance and truth-status of representations.

### Card + McKee Character
They emphasize choice/action as evidence of character. Zunshine adds the warning that evidence supports a model; it does not certify a motive.

### McKee Dialogue
Dialogue is verbal action. Zunshine adds a second layer: a line is also evidence about what the speaker wants the listener to believe. Indirect speech can preserve source while compressing wording.

### Truby / mystery architecture
Truby’s revelation chain becomes more rigorous when each revelation has a source and confidence state. `REVELATION → MODEL CHANGE → ACTION` remains, but Zunshine prevents premature promotion of hypotheses.

### Brody / YA
YA developmental pressure benefits from social inference: belonging, attraction, authority and identity often turn on what young people think others think of them. Zunshine prevents this from becoming explanatory teen-psychology narration by letting behavior remain interpretable.

### Le Carré / mystery-fiction labs
Information asymmetry and compromised trust become tractable through source-tagged evidence rather than generalized suspicion.

## 7. IVDIVO PRODUCTION GATES
**Z38-G01 Source Tag Required** — every major non-observed claim has a source.

**Z38-G02 Evidence Status Required** — OBSERVED / REPORTED / INFERRED / HYPOTHESIS / CORROBORATED / DISPROVEN / UNKNOWN.

**Z38-G03 No Silent Promotion** — hypothesis cannot become world-fact between scenes without new evidence.

**Z38-G04 Behavior Is Not Motive** — render choice first; preserve at least one plausible alternate motive until evidence closes it.

**Z38-G05 No System-Mind Telepathy** — AI/robot/system prediction of action is not motive knowledge.

**Z38-G06 Conscious Robot Opacity** — a conscious robot has an interiority other agents cannot automatically read; it may choose what to reveal.

**Z38-G07 Lie Cost Ledger** — every deliberate lie must have a bounded sphere of influence and downstream consequences.

**Z38-G08 No Liar Inflation** — add another liar only if the causal story requires it.

**Z38-G09 Material Trace Must Reconstruct Agency** — clue must imply action/conditions, not exist as decorative forensic texture.

**Z38-G10 Retrospective Fairness** — major reveal must make earlier evidence more intelligible without author cheating.

**Z38-G11 Mystery Load Budget** — define the dominant inference engine per sequence; secondary engines share evidence where possible.

**Z38-G12 Unreliability Domain Test** — specify exactly what a POV is unreliable about.

**Z38-G13 Specialist-School Epistemic Ladder** — `practitioner observation → reproducible practice effect → school interpretation → rival interpretation → ontology`, never collapse levels.

**Z38-G14 Prediction/Consent Firewall** — predicted cooperation does not equal willingness, consent, duty or permission to allocate burden.

**Z38-G15 Science Firewall** — narrative/cognitive mechanisms may be used; scientific claims require contemporary primary science.

## 8. MANUSCRIPT / PROJECT VALIDATION
### LESSON ZERO / Orbital Youth
The source-monitoring gate protects the existing `behavior vs motive`, `prediction vs consent` and `system inference vs person` distinctions. It argues against explanatory prose that certifies adolescent motives too early and against systems that “know” why a person will cooperate merely because they predict that cooperation.

### B03 — THE EMPTY RESCUE
The current engine is an excellent fit for Zunshine because the central hazard is not merely future information but **how professionals weight its source and truth-status**.

Required evidence states:
- precursor transmission = OBSERVED SIGNAL
- identity of apparent caller = UNKNOWN / reconstructed state possible
- predicted incident = HYPOTHESIS until independent hazard evidence exists
- responder-created detail in earlier transmission = CORROBORATED TRACE of causal entanglement
- historical OES doctrine = INSTITUTIONAL REPORT / incomplete because concealment altered provenance
- Smith’s model = PROFESSIONAL INTERPRETATION, not cosmological truth

This strengthens ONE MAIN STORY: each call should change both `action state` and `evidence status`. The book must not resolve by proving the ontology of the precursor phenomenon. It resolves when Smith/Jana build a response architecture that works under bounded uncertainty.

Specific B03 gate:
`CALL → SOURCE STATUS → INDEPENDENT EVIDENCE → RESPONSE → RESPONSE-GENERATED TRACE → MODEL UPDATE`.

The historical concealment is also source-monitoring drama: OES inherited a doctrine after its causal/moral provenance was sanitized. The danger is not “old institution bad”; it is **a correct rule detached from the evidence and cost that defined its valid scope**.

## 9. FINAL SYNTHESIS
Zunshine’s strongest transferable contribution is not a universal explanation of why humans read fiction. It is a rigorous reminder that narrative information has **provenance, confidence, interpretation and downstream cognitive cost**.

For IVDIVO, especially mystery, AI, institutional and consciousness-school lines:

`WHAT HAPPENED?` is insufficient.

Also track:
`WHO SAYS/INFERS IT? → WHAT EXACTLY WAS OBSERVED? → WHAT MODEL WAS BUILT? → WHAT ALTERNATIVE STILL FITS? → WHAT WOULD CHANGE THE STATUS? → WHAT ACTION IS JUSTIFIED NOW?`

That structure creates fair mystery, prevents lore leakage, protects conscious personhood and allows characters to act decisively without pretending to possess cosmological certainty.
