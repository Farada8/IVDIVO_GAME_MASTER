## 4. PROVISIONAL MECHANISMS

### DIA-S01 — DIALOGUE AS ACTION
`NEED/DESIRE → VERBAL INTENTION → WORDING/TACTIC → REACTION → NEXT TACTIC`.
A line is not finished merely because it sounds natural.

### DIA-S02 — THREE SPEECH TRACKS
Useful distinction:
- to others;
- to self;
- to reader/audience.
For prose, internal and narratized character speech are part of voice/action design, not automatically exposition defects.

### DIA-S03 — DIRECT / INDIRECT DIALOGUE ROUTING
Use exact quoted speech when wording itself changes power, consent, evidence, relationship, decision or emotion.
Use indirect/summarized dialogue when exact wording is less important than interpretation, repetition, process or compression.

### DIA-S04 — EXPOSITION NEED-TO-KNOW TIMING
`FACT → when reader/character needs it → who can reveal it naturally → what it changes`.
Reject mechanical withholding: "not before needed" cannot become false mystery.

### DIA-S05 — EXPOSITION AS AMMUNITION
Facts are strongest when someone uses them tactically in a live conflict rather than reciting them neutrally.

### DIA-S06 — SPEECH CHARACTERIZATION
Vocabulary, locution, syntax, rhythm and register can reveal social/relational identity, but must not become caricature/slang substitution.

### DIA-S07 — SAID / UNSAID / UNSAYABLE
Track three layers without assuming every character has a deep subconscious secret. `Unsayable` is a possible layer, not mandatory psychoanalytic machinery.

### DIA-S08 — ACTIVITY / ACTION
Surface talk/activity earns a scene when an underlying action changes state. "Talking" is not itself a dramatic action.

### DIA-S09 — TEXT / SUBTEXT
Text is what is said; subtext is what the character is doing/feeling/wanting beneath it. Subtext should often be inferable from context, behavior and reaction rather than author explanation.

## 5. PROVISIONAL FIREWALLS
- **ALL TALK HAS ONE CLEAN PURPOSE** — real/fictional speech can be mixed, hesitant, affiliative and exploratory; the useful gate is whether the scene changes, not whether each sentence has one labeled tactic.
- **SUBCONSCIOUS UNIVERSALISM** — do not invent an "unsayable" trauma/desire simply because McKee's model expects depth.
- **SHOW-DON'T-TELL DOGMA** — prose narration and summary remain legitimate.
- **MINIMAL EXPOSITION = FALSE MYSTERY** — do not hide what POV/contract says reader should know.
- **VOICE = DIALECT/SLANG** — character specificity must include values, relation, attention and strategy, not just vocabulary markers.
- **SCREEN MEDIUM CONTAMINATION** — later screen-specific brevity rules cannot automatically govern novels/audio.

## 6. EARLY IVDIVO GATES
**DIA-G01 EXACT-WORDS TEST** — quote dialogue when the wording itself matters.

**DIA-G02 VERBAL ACTION** — identify what the speaker is trying to do to/with the listener.

**DIA-G03 REACTION REQUIRED** — important dialogue must alter response/tactic/state; no five-person position recital.

**DIA-G04 EXPOSITION ROUTING** — fact appears because someone needs/uses/discovers it now.

**DIA-G05 INDIRECT COMPRESSION** — routine/repeated talk may be summarized.

**DIA-G06 SUBTEXT INFERENCE** — do not explain after a line what competent staging/context already makes inferable.

**DIA-G07 POST-MONEY REGISTER** — future youth dialogue must not casually smuggle wages/rent/credits back into orbital canon.

**DIA-G08 ROBOT SPEECH PERSONHOOD** — conscious robot speech expresses its own goals/relations/culture, not merely human-assistance functionality.
