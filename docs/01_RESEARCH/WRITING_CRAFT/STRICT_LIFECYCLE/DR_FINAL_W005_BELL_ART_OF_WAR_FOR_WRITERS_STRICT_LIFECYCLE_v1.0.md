Practitioner craft manual / career-strategy collection.

## Strengths
- concise, operational exercises;
- strong separation of drafting heat from revision control;
- useful scene, dialogue, backstory and reader-bond heuristics;
- repeated emphasis on deliberate practice and finishing work;

# 4. CLAIM EXTRACTION
## W005-C01 — Diagnose before choosing tactics
Craft tools work best after identifying stage, actual defect and desired reader effect.

## W005-C02 — Deliberate practice beats status anxiety
Improvement should be directed at observable weaknesses through examples, exercises and feedback, not abstract ambition.

## W005-C03 — Generation and revision are different cognitive jobs
Bell's recurring `write hot / revise cool` distinction supports free generation followed by controlled diagnosis.

## W005-C04 — Finishing full works creates learning unavailable from perpetual polishing
Completion gives evidence about whole-book causality, pacing, endings and endurance.

## W005-C05 — Premise selection deserves comparison before commitment
Generating multiple candidate premises and testing them is more robust than defending the first idea because work has already been invested.

## W005-C06 — Outer and inner story are separate but interacting tracks
External action and internal interpretation/relationship change should be tracked independently rather than conflated.

## W005-C07 — Reader bonding is behaviorally earned
Identification can come through recognizable values, competence, hardship, vulnerability, moral choice and specificity; sympathy gimmicks are not required.

## W005-C08 — Scene strength requires a clear reason for dramatization
Bell's `bull's-eye` is usefully translated as the scene's irreplaceable state-changing moment, not necessarily a twist or confrontation.

## W005-C09 — Exposition should be routed by necessity, not ideology
Delay/cut nonessential explanation; use dialogue, thought, narration, behavior or objects according to function. Bell favors active embedding, but IVDIVO does not require conflict as camouflage.

## W005-C10 — Progressive revelation creates durable reader modeling
Withhold conclusions while preserving fair access to observable evidence and reasons for nondisclosure.

## W005-C11 — Dialogue is a tactic, not transcript
Every meaningful line should arise from a want/relationship/constraint and change tactic, information, power, trust, consent, emotion or decision.

## W005-C12 — RUE is strongest as anti-restatement
Do not explain what the reader has already understood from action, dialogue or image.

## W005-C13 — Voice requires character-specific perception and rhythm
Voice journals can generate material, but discovered facts are hypotheses until they fit canon and causal needs.

## W005-C14 — POV intimacy is a controlled variable
Third person can be warmed through character-filtered perception; first person gains intimacy but also creates knowledge constraints.

## W005-C15 — Criticism should be converted into a specific diagnostic problem
A repeated reader reaction is evidence of an effect; the reader's proposed repair is not automatically correct.

# 5. MECHANISM EXTRACTION
## W005-M01 — STAGE-FIRST ROUTER
`CURRENT STAGE → OBSERVED FAILURE → READER EFFECT → FITTING TOOL → PATCH → RETEST`.

## W005-M02 — HOT / COOL PRODUCTION SPLIT
`GENERATE WITHOUT FULL GATE LOAD → COMPLETE LIVE MATERIAL → COOL DIAGNOSIS → STRUCTURAL REVISION → LINE EDIT`.

IVDIVO consequence: do not ask a draft paragraph to satisfy the whole 100-profession system while it is being born.

## W005-M03 — PREMISE TOURNAMENT
`3+ PREMISES → HUMAN WANT → OPPOSITION/COUNTERMODEL → WORLD-SPECIFIC PRESSURE → FRESHNESS → END CONDITION → READER PROMISE → SELECT`.

This aligns with the existing Studio + Red Team engine-comparison rule.

## W005-M04 — OUTER / INNER DUAL LEDGER
For every major movement track separately:
`WHAT HAPPENED?` and `WHAT CHANGED IN MODEL / RELATIONSHIP / COMMITMENT?`

Neither ledger must move at identical speed.

## W005-M05 — SCENE BULL'S-EYE
`WHY DRAMATIZE THIS? → WHAT IRREPLACEABLE CHANGE OCCURS? → WHAT EARLIER MATERIAL PREPARES IT? → WHAT LATER ACTION DEPENDS ON IT?`

A quiet choice, permission, refusal, evidence reclassification or relational shift qualifies.

## W005-M06 — GENERATIVE FLIP, NOT CLICHE REVERSAL LAW
Generate the obvious version, then 3–5 alternatives that change role, setting, social expectation, tactic or relation. Select the version that is causally and character-specifically strongest; do not keep a reversal merely because it is surprising.

## W005-M07 — ACTIVE BACKSTORY ROUTER
`PRESENT NEED → MINIMUM PAST FACT → CURRENT EMOTION/TACTIC → CONSEQUENCE`.

Backstory can also be narrated directly when compression is more honest; `action first` is a default, not a law.

## W005-M08 — PROGRESSIVE REVELATION WITH FAIRNESS
`OBSERVABLE FACT → PROVISIONAL MODEL → CONTRADICTION → REVISED MODEL → CONTROLLED REVEAL → ACTION`.

Fairness firewall: never hide from the reader information the active POV naturally knows merely to manufacture suspense.

## W005-M09 — DIALOGUE AS RELATIONAL ACTION
`WANT → VERBAL/NONVERBAL TACTIC → REACTION → TACTIC CHANGE → CHANGED STATE`.

Pure agreement can still work when it changes trust, intimacy, consent, knowledge or commitment; conflict is not mandatory.

## W005-M10 — RUE / NO RESTATEMENT
If behavior or dialogue already delivered the meaning, delete the explanatory sentence unless it adds a genuinely new layer.

## W005-M11 — Q-FACTOR AS EARNED RECALL
`EARLY EMOTIONAL ANCHOR → LIGHT RECALL → LATER PRESSURE → ANCHOR RETURNS → NEW ACTION`.

Use as setup/payoff, not as motivational speech injection.

## W005-M12 — CRITIQUE TRIAGE
`REACTION → COOLING PERIOD → REPEATED EFFECT? → NAME DEFECT → TEST IN TEXT → DESIGN TARGETED PASS`.

Do not defend the draft reflexively and do not obey every suggested fix.

# 6. FAILURE MODES
## W005-F01 — Mandatory Death Overhang
Bell repeatedly frames stakes as physical/professional/psychological death. IVDIVO rejects this as universal. Trust, relationship, autonomy, consent, responsibility, unique opportunity, role or evidence can carry sufficient cost.

## W005-F02 — Mandatory Heroic Lead
A protagonist need not have conventional heroic qualities to earn reader investment.

## W005-F03 — Mandatory Wound / Brokenness
Past trauma and inner struggle are options, not required depth generators.

## W005-F04 — Redemption Monoculture
Redemption can be powerful but is not the compulsory moral architecture of every novel.

## W005-F05 — Conflict Camouflage for Exposition
Putting facts into an argument does not automatically make exposition dramatic. If the argument exists only to hide information delivery, compress or reroute it.

## W005-F06 — Action-First Dogma
Some openings legitimately begin with voice, relationship, place, observation, mystery, contemplation or ordinary life. Functional test: attention + orientation + promise.

## W005-F07 — Dialogue-on-Page-One Dogma
Useful for query samples, not a universal narrative law.

## W005-F08 — Cliffhanger Chapter Monoculture
Every chapter need not force continuation through danger. Decision, resonance, reveal, changed relationship, new question, satisfaction or emotional aftershock can carry exit energy.

## W005-F09 — Progressive-Revelation Cheating
Delaying a reveal becomes unfair if POV already knows it and has no in-world reason not to surface it.

## W005-F10 — Fast-Draft Moralization
Writing quickly may help generation but is not proof of professionalism or quality. Slow writers can be equally rigorous.

## W005-F11 — Historical Publishing Advice Treated as Current
Agent/query/platform/promotion advice reflects its market period. It must not be treated as current 2026 guidance without fresh verification.

## W005-F12 — Career Economics Imported into Storyworld
Bell's money/job/publishing language must never leak into post-money orbital worldbuilding.

# 7. CROSS-SOURCE COMPARISON
## With LaPlante
Strong convergence on separating generation from revision and protecting live material from premature control. LaPlante is the stronger corrective against mandatory conflict, healing and formula.

## With Rosenfeld
Convergence on scene intention, consequence and meaningful endings. Rosenfeld is more nuanced on contemplation and non-conflict scenes; Bell's bull's-eye is retained as `irreplaceable change`, not mandatory confrontation.

## With McKee — Dialogue
Convergence on dialogue as action and tactics. IVDIVO keeps McKee/Rosenfeld/LaPlante's broader allowance for silence, indirect dialogue, agreement and relational change.

## With Zunshine / Herman
Bell's progressive revelation is useful only under provenance/evidence discipline: character interpretation must not become world fact, and fair withholding must preserve source status.

## With Card / Corbett / Kress
Bell's heroic/wound/redemption framing is narrowed. Character depth is built from specific desire, relations, response under frustration and choice, not mandatory trauma.

## With Iglesias
Premise and scene craft gain an explicit reader-effect dimension: `CHARACTER FEELS X ≠ READER FEELS X`; reader promise must be validated rather than assumed.

# 8. IVDIVO OPERATIONALIZATION — PRODUCTION GATES
## BELL-W005-G01 — GENERATE BEFORE FULL AUDIT
Do not load the entire studio rubric into first-draft sentence generation. Generate a coherent scene/section, then audit.

## BELL-W005-G02 — PREMISE TOURNAMENT BEFORE DEVELOPMENT LOCK
Compare at least three viable engines/premises before major production commitment unless Founder has locked one directly.

## BELL-W005-G03 — SCENE BULL'S-EYE
Every fully dramatized scene must have a specific irreplaceable change or earned experience. Otherwise compress, merge or cut.

## BELL-W005-G04 — OUTER / INNER LEDGER
Track action-state and interpretation/relationship-state separately.

## BELL-W005-G05 — NO EXPLANATORY ECHO
After behavior/reveal, remove sentences that only tell the reader what was already shown.

## BELL-W005-G06 — BACKSTORY MUST PAY PRESENT-TENSE RENT
Past information enters because it changes current understanding, tactic, relationship or consequence.

## BELL-W005-G07 — FAIR PROGRESSIVE REVELATION
Delay interpretation, not naturally available POV knowledge. Every withheld fact needs an in-world reason.

## BELL-W005-G08 — DIALOGUE TACTIC CHANGE
Repeated lines pursuing the same tactic without altered pressure are trim candidates.

## BELL-W005-G09 — Q-FACTOR MUST BE EARNED
A late inspirational recall requires an earlier emotionally meaningful anchor and must cause action rather than explain theme.

## BELL-W005-G10 — NO FORMULA IMPORT
No mandatory death, wound, redemption, cliffhanger, dialogue-on-page-one, battle or heroic lead.

## BELL-W005-G11 — CRITIQUE EFFECT / REPAIR SEPARATION
Treat repeated reader reaction as evidence; test the diagnosis; choose repair independently.

## BELL-W005-G12 — MARKET-DATE FIREWALL
Any agent/query/promotion claim from this book is historical until separately current-verified.

# 9. MANUSCRIPT VALIDATION
## LESSON ZERO BOOK ONE
No new Bell finding justifies reopening the development lock. Existing protections already implement the strongest mechanisms: causal scene chain, character-specific action, progressive mystery, anti-restatement and stage-specific revision. Apply W005 only if a concrete current defect appears.

## ORBITAL YOUTH / FUTURE IVDIVO
High-value applications:
- use premise tournaments without importing money/rent/credits;
- treat post-money stakes as trust, responsibility, belonging, consent, relationship, mastery, unique mission/role/time/opportunity;
- use progressive revelation for rare consciousness schools while preserving `observation → reproducible effect → school interpretation → competing interpretation → possible ontology`;
- give conscious robots independent desire/relationships/refusal rights; do not make them reader-bond props or Q-factor devices for humans;
- use outer/inner ledgers so technological success can coexist with new relational or ethical cost;
- use scene bull's-eye to prevent orbital tourism and microdramatized logistics.

## AUDIO / DRAMA PROJECTS
Bell's dialogue/action and progressive-revelation mechanisms transfer well, but `dialogue must conflict` does not. In audio, exposition should first be routed through need, sonic evidence, relation, decision and consequence; argument is only one carrier.

# 10. SYNTHESIS
The durable value of *The Art of War for Writers* is not its military metaphor or period publishing strategy. Its strongest contribution to IVDIVO is operational discipline:

`GENERATE LIVE MATERIAL → DIAGNOSE STAGE → TEST PREMISE / SCENE FUNCTION → TRACK OUTER + INNER CHANGE → REVEAL FAIRLY → LET DIALOGUE ACT → REMOVE EXPLANATORY ECHO → REVISE COOLLY → RETEST`.

The book is therefore integrated as a **tool-selection / production-discipline / scene-action / revision source**, with explicit firewalls against formula, historical market advice, mandatory death/redemption/wounds and career economics leaking into orbital canon.
