# 3. SOURCE PASSPORT
## Primary domain
Nonfiction prose craft, editing, clarity, selection, voice, research and professional writer attitude.

## Transferable strength for IVDIVO
- post-architecture clarity;
- paragraph/sentence continuity;
- worldbuilding selection;
- technical exposition;
- institutional-language diagnostics;
- dialogue/source compression;
- place through human activity;
- voice and rhythm;
- editorial workflow;
- evidence/provenance discipline.

## Limits
- nonfiction assumptions do not transfer unchanged to fiction;
- several usage prescriptions and market/editorial examples are historically situated;
- Zinsser often prefers active verbs/shorter forms, but these are heuristics, not fiction-wide formal laws;
- his casual use of “robotic” as a synonym for impersonal prose is incompatible with IVDIVO's conscious-robot personhood and is not imported;
- memoir truth claims do not override fictional canon/continuity discipline.

# 4. CLAIM EXTRACTION
## W011-C01 — No single drafting ritual is mandatory
Judge process by whether it produces strong finished prose.

## W011-C02 — Clear prose begins with clear relations in the writer's model
A grammatical sentence can remain conceptually unclear if actor, action, relation, time or claim is undecided.

## W011-C03 — Rewriting is not cosmetic cleanup
It includes deletion, resequencing, transition repair, focus change, logic repair and tonal reconciliation.

## W011-C04 — Clutter may be a whole thought, sentence or paragraph
Duplicated meaning is clutter even when every individual word is grammatical.

## W011-C05 — Simplicity is functional clarity, not shortness
Long syntax can be clear and short syntax can be muddy or theatrically fragmented.

## W011-C06 — Voice survives useful pruning
Cut prestige fog and redundancy, not personality, attitude, cadence or character-specific perception.

## W011-C07 — Unity is controlled selection
A large subject becomes readable by bounding viewpoint, time, scale, tone and governing purpose.

## W011-C08 — Openings establish an attention contract
`ATTENTION + ORIENTATION + PROMISE`; no single compulsory opening form.

## W011-C09 — Endings should stop when the local work is complete
Do not append a school-essay restatement of meaning already earned.

## W011-C10 — Research/world database should exceed prose
Surplus material improves selection only until it begins delaying or bloating the actual work.

## W011-C11 — Reader-state continuity is a sequencing problem
Each sentence inherits active entities, images, time, place, knowledge and tone from the previous one.

## W011-C12 — Specific facts can carry atmosphere and emotion without decorative preamble
Color can be information.

## W011-C13 — Place becomes meaningful through selected human activity
Detail accumulation alone is not worldbuilding.

## W011-C14 — Interview/source material requires ethical compression
Raw speech is not sacred transcription, but shaping must preserve intended meaning and provenance.

## W011-C15 — Technical explanation succeeds through sequence
A reader can follow sophisticated material when each unit answers the question generated by the previous unit.

## W011-C16 — Institutional abstraction tends to erase actors
If prose names `allocation`, `process`, `implementation`, `service`, or `priority` without showing who decides/acts and under what constraint, clarity and accountability fall.

## W011-C17 — Specialized activity is most vivid when embodied and socially situated
Sport/science/profession becomes story through difficulty, body, culture, history and stakes rather than scoreboards or jargon.

## W011-C18 — Criticism should move from concrete evidence to diagnosis
Verdict adjectives without examples are weak; evidence, comparison and governing idea are stronger.

## W011-C19 — Humor works best when truth precedes joke machinery
Heighten a real pattern; do not invent a convenient “truth” merely to justify a gag.

## W011-C20 — Casual voice is constructed
Apparent ease usually rests on revision, cadence and exact word choice; fake breeziness/slang/chumminess is not voice.

## W011-C21 — Curiosity is a research engine
`What else?` and `why?` often matter more than performing expertise.

## W011-C22 — Fixation on final product can corrupt early decisions
Premature optimization for publication/franchise/output encourages structural compromise.

## W011-C23 — Writing is a chain of decisions at multiple scales
Macro-selection, section order, transition, word choice and final emphasis interact.

## W011-C24 — Memory is selective and perspectival
Competing memories can be sincere without being identical; this does not erase the need for provenance.

## W011-C25 — Editorial collaboration should preserve both clarity and authorial identity
A good editor repairs what readers cannot follow without replacing the writer's voice or beliefs.

# 5. MECHANISM EXTRACTION
## W011-M01 — FUNCTIONAL COMPONENT TEST
For word, sentence, paragraph, scene-adjacent exposition:
`WHAT NEW FUNCTION? → FACT / RELATION / IMAGE / ATTITUDE / CAUSAL LINK / EMPHASIS / RHYTHM / ORIENTATION`.
No new function = cut/merge/rebuild candidate.

## W011-M02 — CLARITY BEFORE PRESTIGE
`TERM → ACTOR → ACTION → CONSTRAINT → CONSEQUENCE`.
If a technical term cannot be unpacked into a mechanism, it may be prestige fog.

## W011-M03 — BRACKET PASS
Bracket suspected clutter; read with and without; restore only what carries meaning, relation, voice or rhythm.

## W011-M04 — READER-STATE CONTINUITY
`STATE LEFT BY A → ASSUMPTION OF B → MATCH/MISMATCH → SIGNAL / REORDER / REWRITE`.
Track active subject, time, location, evidence status, image, tone and emphasis.

## W011-M05 — BOUNDED-CORNER TEST
`VAST WORLD/SUBJECT → WHICH CAUSAL CORNER DOES THIS BOOK OWN?`
Supports `SAGA SCALE ≠ BOOK SCALE`.

## W011-M06 — LEAD CONTRACT
`ATTENTION → ORIENTATION → PROMISE`.

## W011-M07 — STOP-WHEN-DONE ENDING
`LOCAL WORK COMPLETE? → PAYOFF / AFTERMATH / ECHO / CLEAN CUT`.
Do not append thematic summary by reflex.

## W011-M08 — MATERIAL SURPLUS / NARRATIVE SELECTION
`COLLECT > NEEDED → TAG FUNCTION → SELECT HIGH-UTILITY MATERIAL → DISCARD SURPLUS`.

## W011-M09 — PLACE-AS-ACTION
`PLACE RULE / CULTURAL PRACTICE → PERSON/ROBOT ACTIVITY → FRICTION/CHOICE → CONSEQUENCE`.
Reject tourism catalogue.

## W011-M10 — SOURCE DISTILLATION / PROVENANCE
`SOURCE → RAW CLAIM → CONTEXT → CLEANED/COMPRESSED VERSION → DOES MEANING SURVIVE? → ATTRIBUTION/STATUS`.
Useful for interviews, archives, testimony, AI/robot logs, specialist schools and mystery evidence.

## W011-M11 — TECHNICAL EXPLANATION LADDER
`CURRENT QUESTION → MINIMUM NEEDED FACT → NEXT QUESTION → NEXT FACT → OPERATIONAL CONSEQUENCE`.
Avoid encyclopedic dump.

## W011-M12 — MISSING-ACTOR TEST
When prose becomes bureaucratic, ask:
`WHO CHOOSES? WHO CAN REFUSE? WHO BEARS COST? WHO CAN APPEAL?`
This is both clarity and governance worldbuilding.

## W011-M13 — CRITICISM / RED-TEAM ROUTER
`CONCRETE PASSAGE/BEAT → READER EFFECT → DIAGNOSIS → CROSS-SOURCE COMPARISON → REPAIR`.
No taste-label-only notes.

## W011-M14 — HUMOR TRUTH-FIRST
`OBSERVED HUMAN/SYSTEM PATTERN → CHARACTER-SPECIFIC EXAGGERATION/ANGLE → COMIC EFFECT`.
No universal wit.

## W011-M15 — EDITORIAL NEGOTIATION GATE
`EDITOR FLAGS EFFECT → AUTHOR IDENTIFIES INTENT → JOINTLY TEST REPAIR → PRESERVE INTENT + RESTORE CLARITY`.
Reviewer diagnosis may be right even if proposed wording is wrong.

# 6. FAILURE MODES / FIREWALLS
1. **Simplicity = Shortness KPI** — rejected.
2. **50% Cut Rule as metric** — rejected.
3. **Delete Every Adverb / Passive** — rejected as absolute law.
4. **Nonfiction Prescription Imported Unchanged into Fiction** — rejected.
5. **Plain Language = Flat Voice** — rejected.
6. **First Person = Natural/Authentic Voice by default** — rejected for fiction.
7. **One Point = One-Dimensional Novel** — rejected.
8. **Surprise Ending Mandatory** — rejected.
9. **Historical Publishing/Usage Advice as Current Authority** — requires current verification before real-world operational use.
10. **Institutional Jargon as Worldbuilding** — rejected if no mechanism beneath it.
11. **Significance Nudging** — telling reader that a detail is important/tragic/amazing when staging already does it.
12. **Feature-Lead Theater** — fragments/fake suspense in place of subject.
13. **Travelogue Catalogue** — place described without activity/choice.
14. **Culture-from-One-Anecdote** — central idea must not become stereotype.
15. **Interview Quote Theater** — quote used because colorful, not because evidential/character/causal.
16. **Transcript Worship** — raw speech retained even when unreadable or nonfunctional.
17. **Expertise Dump** — technical truth shown without a reader/character question sequence.
18. **Bureaucratic Dehumanization Imported as Robot Ontology** — conscious robots are persons; `robotic = inhuman` is explicitly rejected.
19. **Sports/Training Scoreboard** — metrics replace embodied/social experience.
20. **Critique-by-Adjective** — `flat`, `weak`, `beautiful` without evidence.
21. **Humor-first Truth-second** — forcing world/character facts to support a joke.
22. **Fake Youth Breeziness** — slang and chumminess substitute for worldview.
23. **Final-Product Tyranny** — optimizing early drafts for franchise/publishability before causal core.
24. **Memory = Fact** — sincere recollection does not equal verified event.
25. **Editor Voice Takeover** — clarity repair makes every character sound like the editor.

# 7. CROSS-SOURCE COMPARISON
## Zinsser + Lamott
Lamott protects discovery; Zinsser disciplines the resulting excess:
`DISCOVERY → HOT-SPOT → STRUCTURAL SELECTION → CLARITY/CONTINUITY → RHYTHM/VOICE`.

## Zinsser + Francine Prose
Prose protects cadence, specificity and close reading; Zinsser adds function, deletion and clarity. Combined: precision without mechanical minimalism.

## Zinsser + Pushman
Paragraph and formal breaks need real intention. Both reject fragment density as a quality metric.

## Zinsser + McKee / Truby / Rosenfeld
Zinsser runs later. A clarified unnecessary scene remains unnecessary:
`STORY → SCENE → POV/MODE → PROSE`.

## Zinsser + Wolf / Ribó
Bounded selection reinforces:
`WORLD DATABASE ≠ BOOK NARRATIVE`
and
`SAGA SCALE ≠ BOOK SCALE`.

## Zinsser + Zunshine / Herman
Zinsser's source/technical clarity complements evidence-status discipline: clear writing must distinguish observation, report, interpretation and verified model.

## Zinsser + Chiarella / McKee Dialogue
Direct speech is not sacred. Compress when exact wording does not alter state; preserve exact wording when it changes relationship, consent, evidence, power or decision.

# 8. IVDIVO OPERATIONALIZATION
## A. Anti-`обрубки` master route
`ONE MAIN STORY / CAUSALITY → SCENE NECESSITY → SHOW/TELL → POV → DIALOGUE MODE → PARAGRAPH INTENT → READER-STATE CONTINUITY → SENTENCE RHYTHM → WORDING`.

Short line policy:
- protect genuine contrast, revelation, silence, breath, comic timing, danger recognition, decision or end-weight;
- recombine when adjacent short units retain same actor/time/place/focus/thought;
- never optimize short-sentence percentage.

## B. Post-money Orbital Youth
World language must not resurrect money invisibly.
For `allocation / contribution / qualification / access / priority / assignment / scheduling / service`, ask:
`WHAT IS ACTUALLY SCARCE? → WHO DECIDES? → CAN IT BE FABRICATED? → WHAT HUMAN/ROBOT TIME, CONSENT, SAFETY, SPACE, EXPERTISE OR UNIQUE OPPORTUNITY IS CONSTRAINED?`
If answer reduces to purchasing power, repair canon logic before prose.

## C. Conscious robot civilization
Named robots:
- independent want;
- relation-specific register;
- other relationships outside protagonist;
- right to refuse/withhold;
- embodiment-specific action where relevant.
Never use `robotic` as shorthand for emotionally empty/inhuman.

## D. Specialist consciousness schools
Production notes preserve:
`OBSERVED → REPRODUCIBLE EFFECT → SCHOOL INTERPRETATION → COMPETING INTERPRETATION → POSSIBLE ONTOLOGICAL CLAIM`.
Clear language must reduce prestige fog, not erase genuine uncertainty.

## E. Technical/learning scenes
Route:
`CHARACTER QUESTION → MINIMUM TECHNICAL ANSWER → ACTION/TEST → RESULT → NEXT QUESTION`.
Expertise does not require making experts stupid or novices passive.

## F. Place / habitat
`PLACE RULE → HUMAN/ROBOT PRACTICE → CHARACTER RESPONSE → CHOICE → CONSEQUENCE`.
No orbital sightseeing unless the sightseeing itself changes story state.

## G. Writers' Room feedback
Use:
`EVIDENCE → READER EFFECT → DIAGNOSIS → REPAIR OPTIONS`.
Do not let a reviewer replace character voice merely because the reviewer correctly detected confusion.
