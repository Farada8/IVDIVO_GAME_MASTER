## 3. CLAIM EXTRACTION

### W001-C01 — Craft rules are contextual tools, not absolutes
The textbook repeatedly treats craft as informed choice rather than obedience to universal rules. IVDIVO keeps standards and gates, but each gate must identify reader effect and story function rather than enforce ritual compliance.

### W001-C02 — Episodic accumulation is not progression
Adding more scenes that demonstrate the same state does not create a story. Progress requires a change in action, relationship, knowledge, commitment, status or meaning.

### W001-C03 — Ordinary life becomes narrative through meaningful deviation
Routine can establish normality economically; a deviation matters when it changes something specific for this character.

### W001-C04 — Plot shapes are a repertoire
Problem stories, visit/collision forms, quests, slice-of-life pressure, epiphany structures and relationship counterpoint can all work. Form follows engine.

### W001-C05 — Intelligent mistakes are superior to idiot plots
A competent person may make a wrong choice when their model is limited but reasonable. The reader should be able to reconstruct why the mistake was made.

### W001-C06 — Relationship counterpoint can drive causality without zero-sum victory
Two complete agents can change each other's understanding, tactics or relationship without one defeating the other.

### W001-C07 — Show and tell are modes, not moral categories
Scene, summary, half-scene, indirect dialogue and exposition should be selected by what information, time-scale and reader experience are needed.

### W001-C08 — Character is revealed by tactical response
What a person wants matters, but the more discriminating signal is how their tactic changes when the first move fails or when the other person responds unexpectedly.

### W001-C09 — Backstory is often most powerful as pressure on present behavior
A writer may know more history than the reader receives. Backstory earns page-space when it changes present interpretation, subtext, choice or relationship.

### W001-C10 — Dialogue is action and can be routed by importance
Direct dialogue, indirect dialogue and summary are all legitimate. Exact wording deserves dramatization when wording changes the state of the scene.

### W001-C11 — Trigger words and ricochet targets create dialogue continuity
Lines feel causally connected when speakers respond to specific words, implications or third-party targets rather than taking turns delivering prepared positions.

### W001-C12 — Voice is attitude + attention + rhythm
Voice is not slang. It grows from what a consciousness notices, how it values what it notices and the recurring syntactic/rhythmic choices used to express that stance.

### W001-C13 — Setting is an active relationship, not decor
Place can shape perception, memory, decision, access, mood and social behavior. Description without relation to an observer or action risks tourism.

### W001-C14 — Stillness can be narratively active
A scene can be quiet or physically static while changing emotion, interpretation, relationship or reader understanding.

### W001-C15 — POV is knowledge + distance + attitude, not pronouns
First/third person does not by itself determine intimacy. The key questions are what the focal consciousness can know, how close narration sits to it and how its attitudes filter the world.

### W001-C16 — Revision has different scales
A draft may need polish, restructuring or a near-reboot. The repair scale must match the defect scale.

### W001-C17 — Reader feedback is evidence of effect, not authority over solution
Repeated reader friction is meaningful evidence. The writer still owns the repair and may choose a third solution not suggested by any critic.

### W001-C18 — Rhythm comes from pattern plus variation
Repetition, sound, syntax, stanza/paragraph shape and pause create expectation. Effective rhythm often depends on breaking or modulating the established pattern.

### W001-C19 — Short syntax is not inherently fast or strong
Monosyllables, fragments and short units can slow, weight or ritualize a passage. Pace must be diagnosed by reader effect, not length statistics alone.

### W001-C20 — Titles, openings and endings participate in the information contract
Titles/epigraphs can carry orientation or expectation. Endings often turn or recontextualize meaning; they do not need a cliffhanger or lesson.

### W001-C21 — Figurative language should increase precision of experience
Metaphor/simile/symbol are useful when they reveal a relation or perception more precisely, not when they conceal unclear action behind decoration.

### W001-C22 — Images should do work
Imagery can represent the world, reveal observer consciousness, establish tone, advance causality or carry thematic/symbolic weight. Decorative accumulation is not enough.

### W001-C23 — Sensory filters are optional emphasis, not automatic errors
"I saw / I heard / I felt / I thought" can create unnecessary distance, but are correct when the act of seeing/hearing/feeling/thinking is itself important.

### W001-C24 — Syntax is a meaning system
Hypotactic/periodic syntax can delay and layer thought; paratactic/running syntax can accumulate, accelerate or create abundance. Word order changes emphasis, agency and reader processing.

### W001-C25 — Dramatic, narrative and lyrical modes can be blended
Dramatic mode approximates real-time action; narrative mode compresses and orients; lyrical mode heightens sound/attention and can slow or suspend time. Strong prose switches as function changes.

### W001-C26 — Form can generate content, but experiment must alter meaning
Lists, braids, hermit-crab structures, visual page experiments and constraints are useful when the form creates new relations or reader effects. Otherwise they become gimmicks.

### W001-C27 — Memory and evidence require status labels
Memory is fallible; speculation can be artistically useful when it is marked as speculation. Admitting uncertainty can increase trust.

### W001-C28 — Research should be allowed to revise the writer's model
Research is not merely evidence collection for a predetermined thesis. Unexpected information can redirect the question, form or meaning.

### W001-C29 — Place should be researched and experienced as a system
Direct observation, history, social context and embodied experience can combine. A place is strongest when it shapes consciousness/action rather than serving as a scenic backdrop.

### W001-C30 — Body and interiority are inseparable in prose
Embodied detail can reveal memory, social pressure, vulnerability, identity and thought. Body writing should avoid objectification and gratuitous exposure.

## 4. MECHANISM EXTRACTION / PRODUCTION GATES

### W001-M01 — EPISODICITY TEST
`SCENE N → STATE CHANGE → WHY SCENE N+1 EXISTS BECAUSE OF IT`
If the answer is only "another example of the same problem," merge/compress/cut or introduce a real counter-movement.

### W001-M02 — ROUTINE → DEVIATION → RESPONSE
Use for ordinary-life openings, especially post-money Orbital Youth. Establish the normal pattern economically; break it around a nonfungible stake.

### W001-M03 — PLOT-SHAPE ROUTER
Identify the dominant local engine before selecting structure:
`problem / investigation / relationship counterpoint / visit-collision / quest / slice-of-life pressure / revelation`.

### W001-M04 — INTELLIGENT MISTAKE
`LIMITED MODEL + REAL EVIDENCE → PLAUSIBLE CHOICE → ERROR/COST → MODEL REVISION`.

### W001-M05 — COUNTERPOINT RELATIONSHIP ENGINE
`FRAME A ↔ FRAME B → INTERACTION → MUTUAL PRESSURE → TACTIC/UNDERSTANDING SHIFT → NEW TERMS`.

### W001-M06 — SHOW/TELL ROUTER
Use **direct scene** when exact behavior/words/staging change consent, trust, evidence, power, relationship or emotional state.
Use **summary / narration / indirect dialogue** for routine, repeated process, long spans, logistics, backstory or lower-value wording.

### W001-M07 — HALF-SCENE COMPRESSION
When a full scene is too slow but a summary is too abstract, preserve one or two concrete actions/lines inside compressed narration.

### W001-M08 — TACTIC-SHIFT CHARACTER TEST
`WANT → TACTIC 1 → RESPONSE → TACTIC 2`.
If five major characters respond to frustration with the same tactic, differentiation is cosmetic.

### W001-M09 — DIALOGUE MODE ROUTER
`SUMMARY → INDIRECT → DIRECT`
Increase dramatization only as exact wording becomes more consequential.

### W001-M10 — TRIGGER-WORD CHAIN
Every line in a tense exchange should react to a word, implication, omission, status move or physical cue from the previous beat.

### W001-M11 — RICOCHET TARGET
A line spoken to A may actually target B or the room. Track who is formally addressed versus who is meant to feel the consequence.

### W001-M12 — VOICE MATRIX
`ATTITUDE + ATTENTION + RELATIONSHIP + RHYTHM + DICTION RANGE`.
Do not use slang as the primary differentiator.

### W001-M13 — SETTING AS ACTION
`PLACE RULE/FEATURE → WHAT IT MAKES EASIER/HARDER → CHARACTER CHOICE → CONSEQUENCE`.

### W001-M14 — STILLNESS STATE CHANGE
A quiet scene passes if removing it would change later interpretation, decision, relationship, emotional readiness or knowledge.

### W001-M15 — POV ACCESS MATRIX
`OBSERVABLE / KNOWN / INFERRED / UNKNOWN / IMPOSSIBLE-TO-KNOW`.
Prevent POV telepathy and accidental world-bible narration.

### W001-M16 — REVISION SCALE ROUTER
`LOCAL WORDING DEFECT → line edit`
`SCENE PURPOSE/MODE DEFECT → scene restructure`
`CAUSAL SEQUENCE DEFECT → sequence rebuild`
`ENGINE DEFECT → development rewrite`.

### W001-M17 — RHYTHM EXPECTATION/BREAK
Establish a pattern through syntax/repetition/paragraph length, then vary it when meaning or pressure changes. Do not optimize a percentage.

### W001-M18 — READ-ALOUD SOUND PASS
Check stress, repeated consonants/vowels, accidental rhyme, monotony, breath and sentence landing after structural revision.

### W001-M19 — PARAGRAPH INTENT
Every paragraph break should have a function:
`focus shift / speaker change / temporal-spatial shift / revelation / contrast / deliberate breath / emphasis`.
Random one-sentence fragmentation fails.

### W001-M20 — ENDING OPERATOR
Choose after the story state is clear:
`decision / consequence / image resonance / callback / true insight / false insight / changed relation / bounded unresolved question`.
Do not append a lesson because the genre expects one.

### W001-M21 — METAPHOR PRECISION
First establish what is literally happening. Then ask whether comparison sharpens perception for this POV. Do not use metaphor to hide unclear action.

### W001-M22 — DETAIL DOUBLE-PAY
Prefer details that do at least two jobs:
`world + character / tone + causality / clue + setting / relationship + culture / rhythm + image`.

### W001-M23 — SENSORY FILTER FUNCTION
Delete "saw/heard/felt/thought" when it merely inserts distance. Keep it when perception or cognition itself is the beat.

### W001-M24 — SYNTAX AGENCY PASS
Test who/what is grammatical subject, what information arrives late, and where the sentence lands. Syntax can shift perceived agency without changing facts.

### W001-M25 — MODE MIX
Route a passage among:
`DRAMATIC = action/real-time`
`NARRATIVE = compression/orientation`
`LYRICAL = heightened sound/attention/time dilation`.
Do not keep a chapter in one mode by habit.

### W001-M26 — TITLE/HEADING CONTRACT
Treat title, epigraph and section headings as early information. If they reveal, misdirect or frame, that effect must be intentional.

### W001-M27 — SPECULATION LABEL
`FACT / MEMORY / REPORT / INFERENCE / SPECULATION`.
Never upgrade one category silently into another.

### W001-M28 — RESEARCH MODEL-REVISION LOOP
`QUESTION → RESEARCH → SURPRISE/CONTRADICTION → REVISED QUESTION/MODEL → STORY CONSEQUENCE`.

### W001-M29 — FORM-FUNCTION TEST
For any conspicuous form/constraint, ask: "What meaning or reader operation becomes possible only because of this form?" If nothing, simplify.

### W001-M30 — FEEDBACK THIRD-WAY
`READER EFFECT EVIDENCE → DIAGNOSE ROOT → GENERATE MULTIPLE FIXES → PICK OWN SOLUTION → RETEST`.

## 6. CROSS-SOURCE COMPARISON

### With LaPlante
Strong convergence on anti-formula craft, show/tell flexibility, revision as re-seeing, writer ownership and separating effect diagnosis from prescribed repair. W001 adds stronger cross-genre sound/syntax/form drills.

### With Lodge
Converges on plurality of narrative devices, youth voice as constructed attitude rather than slang, and reader-effect-based choice. W001 is more exercise-oriented and less novel-specific.

### With Truby / McKee / Bell
W001 preserves causality and purposeful change but is a useful counterweight to overt-conflict, climax and escalation monocultures. It legitimizes quiet/counterpointed/lyrical modes without legitimizing filler.

### With Rosenfeld
Strong convergence on scene/summary routing, intention and revision. W001 adds explicit indirect/half-scene routing and cross-genre rhythm/form tools.

### With Corbett / Card / McKee Character
Converges on character-specific tactics and relation-dependent behavior. W001's acting/method exercises are best treated as diagnostic generation tools, not psychological laws.

### With Zunshine
Memory/speculation/research material strengthens provenance discipline: belief, recollection, inference and fact need separate statuses.

### With Wolf / Cambridge SF
Converges on world detail as functional rather than encyclopedic. W001 adds a practical test: setting and image must be filtered through lived relation and action.

### With P53 / IVDIVO production gates
W001 should not become another layer of mandatory simultaneous checks during generation. It is best used as a **routing and revision library** after a living scene exists.

## 7. IVDIVO OPERATIONALIZATION

### ONE MAIN STORY
Use W001-M01 and M03 before adding material. A scene may be quiet, relational or lyrical, but it still needs a downstream state change. Counterpoint is allowed; five parallel mini-novels are not.

### Post-money Orbital Youth
Best opening pattern is often:
`NORMAL MATERIAL ABUNDANCE → NONFUNGIBLE PRESSURE → CHARACTER-SPECIFIC RESPONSE`.
Stakes: trust, consent, role, time, reputation, belonging, responsibility, unique access/experience — not renamed money/credits.

### Conscious robots
Use `BOOK FUNCTION ≠ PERSONHOOD`.
A conscious robot needs independent wants, refusal rights, relationship-specific tactics and a voice matrix. "AI voice" is not a valid default.

### Specialist consciousness schools
Apply the nonfiction provenance gate:
`OBSERVATION → REPRODUCIBLE PRACTICE EFFECT → SCHOOL INTERPRETATION → COMPETING INTERPRETATION → POSSIBLE ONTOLOGICAL FACT`.
Speculation must remain labelled. High technology does not collapse epistemic uncertainty.

### Mystery / suspense
Use Intelligent Mistake + Speculation Label + Research Model Revision. A wrong theory should be supported by real partial evidence. Revelation must alter the model or action; not every unknown needs metaphysical resolution.

### Dialogue / subtext
Use direct dialogue only when exact wording matters. Otherwise compress. This reduces screenplay-like micro-beats and protects novelistic cadence.

### Prose / "обрубки"
Repair order:
`ONE MAIN STORY / CAUSALITY → SCENE NECESSITY → MODE ROUTING → POV → DIALOGUE MODE → PARAGRAPH INTENT → RHYTHM → WORDING`.
Do not treat short-sentence percentage as the target variable.

### Reader effect
Use rhythm as expectation + variation. Read aloud after structural work. A suspense passage may slow down; a short sentence may create weight rather than speed.

## 9. SYNTHESIS

W001's central IVDIVO contribution is a **cross-genre routing principle**:

`MATERIAL / READER EFFECT → CHOOSE STORY FORM → CHOOSE SCENE MODE → CHOOSE POV / DIALOGUE / RHYTHM → REVISE AT THE CORRECT SCALE`.

The source is most valuable not because it supplies another universal theory, but because its fiction, poetry and nonfiction modules repeatedly demonstrate that different forms solve different artistic problems. That makes it a useful counterweight to formula-heavy schools while preserving discipline through state change, tactical specificity, evidence status and revision.
