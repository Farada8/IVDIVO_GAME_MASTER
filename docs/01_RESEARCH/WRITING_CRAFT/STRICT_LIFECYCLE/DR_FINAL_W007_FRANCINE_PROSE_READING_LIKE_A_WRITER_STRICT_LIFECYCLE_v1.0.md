# 2. SOURCE PASSPORT
## Strongest contribution
Micro-to-macro prose apprenticeship through exact reading: word choice, syntax, paragraph emphasis, narrative distance, character creation, dialogue, detail, gesture, and resistance to simplistic craft rules.

## Limitation
Not a comprehensive causal-architecture system. Examples are predominantly literary and historical; the book demonstrates possibilities rather than supplying a universal production template.

## IVDIVO authority
CRAFT / RESEARCH SOURCE. Useful as a precision/style counterweight to structural schools. Never overrides Founder correction, current canon, locked continuity, or Narrative OS.

# 3. CLAIM EXTRACTION
## W007-C01 — Close reading is technical apprenticeship
Read admired prose word by word and sentence by sentence; ask what each choice contributes rather than imitating surface style.

## W007-C02 — Rules are subordinate to successful effect
Great works repeatedly violate workshop maxims. `Show, don't tell`, mandatory likability, explicit motivation, fixed character change, tidy endings and sustained description are not universal laws.

## W007-C03 — Word choice is causal compression
A single word can establish social relation, attitude, class, uncertainty, character or subtext more efficiently than explanatory paragraphs.

## W007-C04 — Sentence form carries meaning beyond information
Length, order, cadence, repetition, punctuation and sound shape reader experience. Short is not inherently stronger; long is not inherently literary.

## W007-C05 — Paragraphs are units of emphasis and perception
Opening/closing positions matter. Paragraph shifts control focus, knowledge, tempo and perspective. One-sentence paragraphs can be powerful when function earns them.

## W007-C06 — Narration is not reducible to first vs third person
Narrators possess attitude, knowledge limits, rhythm and personality. Omniscient does not mean objective; close third may approach first-person intimacy.

## W007-C07 — Character can be made through radically different methods
Physical description is optional. Action, thought, dialogue, social relation, narrated judgment, detail, contradiction and response under pressure can all create character.

## W007-C08 — Dialogue should accomplish multiple organic effects, not authorial tasks
Strong dialogue can simultaneously reveal character, relation, subtext, status and plot movement because those effects arise from the speakers' situation—not because the writer stuffed required information into quotation marks.

## W007-C09 — Inattention and asymmetry are realistic conversational forces
Fiction often falsely assumes everyone listens perfectly. Partial attention, parallel concerns, evasion and unequal investment can produce powerful dialogue.

## W007-C10 — Distinctive dialogue is not necessarily literal realism
Speech can be highly stylized and still feel true when language is consistent with character and world. Historical/future dialogue should not default to stiff pseudo-period speech.

## W007-C11 — Specific detail creates credibility and memory
Well-chosen particulars can establish world, character, history and emotional truth; details need not be symbolic or extreme.

## W007-C12 — Gesture can bypass explanation
A precise physical action can reveal status, desire, shame, habit, relationship or unconscious conflict more efficiently than explicit interior labeling.

## W007-C13 — Stock gesture is weak gesture
Cigarettes, drinks, nods and shrugs are not useful merely as spacers. A gesture earns space when it is specific and illuminating.

## W007-C14 — Motivation can remain partial, contradictory or opaque
People sometimes act without one clean explanatory cause. Fiction need not reduce every action to childhood trauma, diagnosis or explicit motive.

## W007-C15 — Character change is not mandatory
A story may depict stasis, failure to change, contradiction or unresolved life. What matters is truthful consequence and reader understanding, not mandatory arc compliance.

## W007-C16 — Authorial judgment is a choice, not an obligation
Chekhov demonstrates one powerful mode: state the problem accurately and let the reader participate in judgment. Other traditions judge more openly. Choose consciously.

## W007-C17 — Likability is not the reader contract
Interest, complexity, curiosity, fascination, fear, recognition and concern can sustain reader investment in difficult or unpleasant characters.

## W007-C18 — Endings need not solve all life
Local artistic closure may coexist with continuing trouble. A satisfying ending is not synonymous with happy ending or complete future accounting.

## W007-C19 — Revision must not kill living material
Precision/cutting are valuable, but line-level optimization can destroy energy. Preserve what makes the story alive while improving clarity and economy.

# 4. MECHANISM EXTRACTION
## W007-M01 — CLOSE-READING DIAGNOSTIC
`READER EFFECT → exact word/sentence/paragraph causing effect → technical function → transferable principle → IVDIVO-specific transformation`.
Do not copy the source's surface language.

## W007-M02 — WORD-PRESSURE TEST
For an important word ask:
`Why this word? → what social/POV/emotional inference does it create? → what weaker generic word would erase?`

## W007-M03 — SENTENCE-RHYTHM ROUTER
`desired reader effect → duration/pressure/breath → sentence architecture → aloud test`.
No target short-sentence percentage.

## W007-M04 — PARAGRAPH INTENT
Each break should have a reason: focus change, temporal movement, speaker/action transition, revelation, contrast, breath, emphasis or controlled isolation.

## W007-M05 — CHARACTER METHOD MIX
Choose among:
`action / thought / dialogue / narrated assessment / relation / detail / gesture / omission`.
Do not require all channels for every character.

## W007-M06 — DIALOGUE INATTENTION MATRIX
Track not just `what speaker wants`, but `what listener is actually attending to`. Misaligned attention can generate subtext and relationship truth.

## W007-M07 — DETAIL DOUBLE-PAY
Prefer details that do at least two jobs among credibility, POV, character, setting, causality, memory, tension or thematic resonance. But do not force every detail into foreshadowing.

## W007-M08 — GESTURE NECESSITY
`specific action → what does it reveal that speech/interior summary does not?` If answer is nothing, cut or simplify.

## W007-M09 — MOTIVE HUMILITY
`OBSERVED ACTION → plausible motive set → evidence → contradiction → current best model`.
Do not prematurely reduce complex action to one explanatory cause.

## W007-M10 — LIKABILITY FIREWALL
Before adding injury, kindness, pet-saving, victimization or charm merely to win affection, ask whether the character is already compelling through desire, contradiction, competence, relation or uncertainty.

## W007-M11 — ENDING CONTRACT
`LOCAL STORY QUESTION → decisive result/changed state → emotional/relational aftermath → optional unresolved life/saga continuation`.
No requirement to tie every future thread.

## W007-M12 — READ-ALOUD LINE PASS
After structure/scene/POV are stable, read prose aloud for cadence, accidental repetition, awkward stress and paragraph breath. Fix only after diagnosing intended effect.

# 5. FAILURE MODES
- RULE OBEDIENCE OVER EFFECT — forcing fiction to prove a craft maxim;
- SHOW-DON'T-TELL MONOCULTURE — dramatizing information that should be narrated efficiently;
- SHORT-SENTENCE VIRTUE — confusing brevity with tension/quality;
- PARAGRAPH FRAGMENTATION — isolating every thought for fake emphasis;
- OMNISCIENT = NEUTRAL — pretending broad knowledge removes narrator attitude;
- CHARACTER DOSSIER OVERKILL — believing more biography automatically creates depth;
- MOTIVE REDUCTION — explaining a person with one wound/parent/diagnosis;
- LIKABILITY RETROFIT — sanding off difficult traits or injecting sympathy bait;
- DIALOGUE PERFECT LISTENING — every character processes every line with equal attention;
- PERIOD/FUTURE STIFFNESS — artificial speech used to signal historical/futuristic distance;
- DETAIL CATALOGUE — specificity without selection/function;
- SYMBOL OVERLOAD — every object forced to mean something;
- STOCK-GESTURE FILLER — coffee/cigarette/shrug choreography used as pacing;
- AUTHORIAL MORAL EXPLANATION — telling the reader what the consequences already show;
- CHANGE-ARC COMPULSION — forcing transformation where stasis/partial change is truer;
- TIDY-ENDING COMPULSION — resolving life beyond the local story contract;
- LINE-EDIT MURDER — polishing away voice, surprise or energy.

# 6. CROSS-SOURCE COMPARISON
## Truby / McKee / Brody
These schools are stronger on explicit architecture and causal design. Prose is a necessary counterweight: artistic success cannot be reduced to compliance. IVDIVO synthesis: use architecture to guarantee a real story; use close reading to prevent architecture from becoming schematic prose.

## LaPlante
Strong convergence: theory diagnoses rather than dictates; show/tell are modes; motivation and change may remain complex. Prose adds unusually deep micro-analysis of words/sentences/paragraphs.

## Chiarella / McKee Dialogue
Prose reinforces that dialogue is character/relationship language, including unequal listening and stylization. Exact wording matters more than generic realism.

## Lodge
Both oppose narrow rules and demonstrate formal diversity. Lodge is broader narratology; Prose is stronger as a line-by-line apprenticeship method.

# 7. IVDIVO OPERATIONALIZATION
## Writers' Room sequencing
Do not run every specialist during first-draft sentence generation.
`LIVE DRAFT → causal/scene validation → POV/dialogue pass → close-reading prose pass → read-aloud line edit`.

## Anti-chop production law
`CAUSAL ENGINE → SCENE NECESSITY → SHOW/TELL → POV/DISTANCE → DIALOGUE MODE → PARAGRAPH INTENT → SENTENCE RHYTHM → WORD CHOICE`.
Short isolated sentences survive only when contrast/function earns isolation.

## Orbital Youth voice
Do not manufacture youth through present-day slang. Character voice emerges from what each person notices, assumes, envies, fears, wants and refuses in a post-money orbital society.

## Conscious robots
No universal robot register. Use person-specific word choice, sentence rhythm, relationship shifts, gesture/embodiment equivalents, silence/refusal and independent interests.

## Consciousness schools
Narrative voice and dialogue must preserve epistemic status. A character's certainty is characterization, not automatic ontology.

# 8. MANUSCRIPT VALIDATION
## LESSON ZERO Book One
No evidence from Prose alone warrants reopening the development lock. Existing deliberate short beats must be judged by function rather than a global anti-fragment rule.

## Orbital Youth Book Two
The character bible's voice distinctions are useful, but prose must not repeat sample phrases until they become tics. Close-reading pass should test whether each character's word choice, syntax, attention and paragraph rhythm continue to vary by relationship and scene pressure.

Worldbuilding should be particular-first: one lived detail can establish post-money normality better than explanatory dialogue. Do not force every orbital detail to foreshadow later plot; some details legitimately establish lived reality.

# 9. FINAL SYNTHESIS
Francine Prose adds a crucial layer to the IVDIVO production system:

`STRUCTURE MAKES THE STORY WORK; CLOSE READING MAKES THE PROSE LIVE.`

The transferable discipline is to reverse-engineer reader effect at the smallest meaningful scale without turning discovered techniques into universal rules.
