# 2. PROVISIONAL CLAIMS / MECHANISMS
## W009-C01 — Hero's Journey is best treated as a flexible map
Vogler repeatedly warns against cookbook/formula use: stages may be deleted, added, moved or shuffled; form follows function; the map is not the journey.

## W009-C02 — Archetypes work better as temporary story functions than rigid personality types
A character can wear different archetypal masks in different scenes. This is useful for ensemble function analysis without reducing a person to one permanent label.

## W009-M01 — BASELINE → DISTURBANCE
`ordinary/baseline state → durable disturbance/call → changed need for action`.
The Call does not need catastrophe; it may be message, person, internal dissatisfaction, loss, opportunity or no-more-options condition.

## W009-M02 — SPECIAL WORLD AS RULE CHANGE
`baseline rules/rhythm/priority/error cost → threshold/transition → changed rules/rhythm/priority/error cost`.
The “Special World” need not be a different planet or realm. A new institution, relationship, training environment or social role can qualify if behavior and available choices truly change.

## W009-M03 — CONFLICTING CALLS
Multiple legitimate calls can expose competing commitments rather than creating a single obvious mission.

## W009-M04 — ARCHETYPE FUNCTION ROUTER
For a scene/chapter ask: who currently performs mentoring, warning, threshold-testing, comic pressure-release, shadow/opposition, ally or change-signal functions? Do not assume these functions require dedicated permanent characters.

## W009-M05 — COMPETENT THRESHOLD GUARDIAN
A block/test may be neutral, procedural or legitimately protective rather than villainous. Crossing it should demonstrate fit, readiness, negotiation or changed strategy rather than merely defeating an obstacle.

# 3. PROVISIONAL FAILURE MODES / FIREWALLS
1. **Hero's Journey Retrofit** — forcing every IVDIVO book through fixed stages.
2. **Archetype = Person Confusion** — reducing a full character to mentor/ally/shadow function.
3. **Mandatory Wound/Flaw** — inventing trauma or incompleteness because the school expects it.
4. **Mandatory Death/Sacrifice Stakes** — claiming story depth requires literal/psychological death or huge sacrifice.
5. **Life/Death Escalation Inflation** — rejecting trust, consent, responsibility, relationship, role, time, reputation, unique opportunity or safety as sufficiently meaningful stakes.
6. **Gender-Essentialist Shapeshifter** — anima/animus or “opposite sex = mystery/changeability” is not accepted as universal psychology.
7. **Love Interest Completes Hero** — rejected for IVDIVO personhood/relationship design.
8. **Villain Must Be Destroyed** — rejected; competent countermodels may require negotiation, reform, accountability, separation or bounded defeat.
9. **Hero Knowledge Monopoly** — one protagonist need not own all evidence, interpretation and decisive functions in an ensemble.
10. **Metaphysics by Craft Book** — Jungian collective unconscious, chakras, “vibrational rates,” synchronicity, telepathy/clairvoyance or mystical transcendence in Vogler are not scientific evidence and do not establish IVDIVO ontology.

# 4. IVDIVO OPERATIONALIZATION — PROVISIONAL
## ONE MAIN STORY
Use journey stages, if useful at all, as questions about transition/function rather than mandatory chapter slots.

## Orbital Youth
A change from ordinary orbital life into a training center, new social group, mixed-body environment or responsibility can function as a “Special World” only when rules and available actions actually change. Do not manufacture Earth-style money/rent/death stakes to make the threshold feel important.

## Conscious robots
Do not assign named robots permanent “Ally” or “Mentor” status as if they were utilities. Archetypal function is temporary; robot personhood requires independent desire, relationships, consent and right to refuse.

## Consciousness schools / dimensional knowledge
Vogler's spiritual/psychological metaphors are authorial symbolic frameworks only. They do NOT override the IVDIVO epistemic chain:
`practitioner observation → reproducible effect → school interpretation → competing interpretation → possible ontological conclusion`.
