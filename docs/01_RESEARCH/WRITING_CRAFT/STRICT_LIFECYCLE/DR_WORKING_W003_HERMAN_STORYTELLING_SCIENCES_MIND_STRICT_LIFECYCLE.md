## SCIENCE / CANON FIREWALL
This is a 2013 interdisciplinary narrative-theory/cognitive-science book.
- Cognitive-developmental, neuroscience, AI and psychology claims are not automatically current scientific authority.
- Any scientific claim used as factual worldbuilding guidance requires verification from current primary research when accuracy matters.
- Philosophical claims about persons/mind are not IVDIVO metaphysical canon.
- Narrative usefulness of a model is separate from truth of that model.

## PROVISIONAL CLAIMS FROM STRICT READ SO FAR

### H-C01 — Narrative analysis can be split into worlding and storying
`WORLDING THE STORY` = narrative as target of interpretation; readers construct a world/model from cues.
`STORYING THE WORLD` = narrative as a resource used by persons to make sense of experience.
IVDIVO should distinguish what the text asks the reader to infer from what characters themselves narrativize inside the story.

### H-C02 — Reasons for action are defeasible models
Interpreters build provisional accounts of why persons act. Those accounts can be wrong and should remain revisable under new contextual evidence.

### H-C03 — Communicative choices are actions with reasons
Narrational design—order, withholding, focalization, adaptation choices—can be interpreted as purposeful action, while allowing uncertainty about exact intent.

### H-C04 — Fictional world claims and nonfictional claims have different verification contracts
Nonfictional claims can be triangulated/falsified against competing accounts. Fiction projects an autonomous world whose facts are determined by the work/canon, not by real-world falsification in the same sense.
Useful for separating in-world testimony from project-level canon authority.

### H-C05 — Storyworld construction uses partial answers to WHEN / WHAT / WHERE / WHO / HOW / WHY
Readers do not require exhaustive answers at once. They fill dimensions only as needed for engagement.

### H-C06 — Person-level explanation resists feature-vector reduction
Herman's framework emphasizes persons in environments, relationships, histories and projects rather than reducing conduct to isolated traits or lower-level mechanisms.

### H-C07 — Narrative is especially suited to reasons distributed across life history
Reordering, iteration and comparison across time can show how current acts derive meaning from earlier patterns and how later acts revise interpretation of earlier ones.

### H-C08 — One action may have multiple partial reasons
Avoid single-cause motive monoculture. Larger history, immediate context, relationship, beliefs, desires and projects can contribute together.

### H-C09 — Narrative sense-making itself can shape conduct
People use stories about family, self, institutions and prior events to contextualize present experience and select future action. A story characters tell themselves can therefore become causal without becoming objectively true.

### H-C10 — Narrative does not explain all human understanding
Herman explicitly limits narrative's reach: embodied/interpersonal engagement can precede or exceed explicit narrative reasoning. IVDIVO should not force every emotional/social process into narrated explanation.

### H-C11 — Adaptation changes alignment by changing information distribution
Selection, omission, modality and medium can shift reader sympathy and contextual emphasis even when the headline event sequence remains recognizable.

### H-C12 — Story versions are revisable social models
Retelling is not neutral copying; exchanged stories change through selection, context and purpose. This matters for archives, testimony, rumor and institutional narratives in IVDIVO.

## PROVISIONAL IVDIVO MECHANISMS

### H-M01 — REASONS MODEL
`OBSERVED ACTION → MULTIPLE POSSIBLE REASONS → CONTEXT/HISTORY → PROVISIONAL MODEL → NEW EVIDENCE → MODEL UPDATE`.

### H-M02 — BEHAVIOR / REASON FIREWALL
`PREDICTED BEHAVIOR ≠ REASON ≠ CONSENT ≠ RIGHT TO ALLOCATE BURDEN`.
A system may predict action accurately and still lack access to motive or normative authority.

### H-M03 — PERSON-LEVEL INTEGRITY
Before reducing a character to category/trait/diagnosis/model output, ask what environment, relationship, history and project make the action intelligible at person level.

### H-M04 — WORLDING / STORYING SEPARATION
Track separately:
1. what reader evidence supports;
2. what a character/institution says the evidence means;
3. what story that interpretation causes them to act on.

### H-M05 — STORY-AS-CAUSE
`NARRATIVE MODEL → EXPECTATION → CHOICE → CONSEQUENCE → MODEL REVISED OR DEFENDED`.
A false/partial story can be causally real because people act from it.

### H-M06 — TEMPORAL RECONTEXTUALIZATION
Use flashback/iteration only when earlier or later material changes interpretation of current motive/action.

### H-M07 — MULTICAUSAL MOTIVE GATE
For major surprising behavior, generate several contributory reasons before locking a single explanation.

### H-M08 — READER WORLD DIMENSION BUDGET
Do not explain all six dimensions at opening. Provide WHEN/WHAT/WHERE/WHO/HOW/WHY only as required for orientation, stakes and inference.

### H-M09 — ADAPTATION / RETELLING PROVENANCE
For testimony/rumor/archive:
`EVENT/ACCOUNT → RETELLER → PURPOSE/CONTEXT → OMISSION/EMPHASIS → NEW VERSION → DOWNSTREAM BELIEF/ACTION`.

## PROVISIONAL FAILURE MODES
- Behavior treated as motive.
- Prediction treated as consent.
- Single-cause psychology.
- Character reduced to demographic/diagnostic/algorithmic label.
- In-world story treated as objective world fact.
- Narrative omnipotence: forcing every social/emotional process into explicit story explanation.
- Worldbuilding encyclopedia: answering all storyworld dimensions before reader needs them.
- Archive/testimony treated as transparent rather than retold/versioned.
- Cognitive/philosophical claims imported as IVDIVO metaphysics.
- 2013 cognitive claims cited as current science without verification.
