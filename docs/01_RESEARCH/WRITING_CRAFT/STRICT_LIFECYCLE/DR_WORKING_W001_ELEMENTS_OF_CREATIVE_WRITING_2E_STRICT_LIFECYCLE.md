## 4. PROVISIONAL CLAIMS FROM READ MATERIAL
### W001-C01 — Art has no single absolute craft law
The introduction explicitly frames the textbook as a plurality of editorial/writer perspectives rather than a claim to one correct method. IVDIVO uses this as an anti-formula control, not as evidence that all choices are equally effective.

### W001-C02 — Episodic accumulation is not progression
Fiction Ch1 begins from the author's experience of stories that added scene after scene while hoping change would emerge. A story needs shaped change rather than sheer continuation.

### W001-C03 — Pattern break can launch story movement
A routine becomes story-relevant when a meaningful deviation creates a new state and asks the character to respond. This is one opening model, not a mandatory inciting-incident formula.

### W001-C04 — Plot shapes are a repertoire, not one architecture
Fiction Ch2 explicitly surveys multiple shapes—epiphany, slice of life, visit/gathering, quest/scaffolding and combinations. Forms may be mixed and transformed.

### W001-C05 — Ordinary life works when the selected interval changes meaning
Slice-of-life fiction can be dramatically valid when concrete detail makes an ordinary environment legible and some pressure/non-routine event changes what the interval means.

### W001-C06 — Scaffolding from prior art can generate new work
A writer may use a prior scene/plot structure as scaffolding, then invert roles/outcomes/context. For IVDIVO this is `REFERENCE → ABSTRACT FUNCTION → TRANSFORM`, never importing source canon or wording.

### W001-C07 — Epiphany can be false, partial or ironic
A revelation is not automatically truth. A character may reach a false interpretation that remains psychologically credible and causally consequential.

### W001-C08 — Counterpoint can organize plot through relationship rather than victory
Fiction Ch3 contrasts battleground plotting with counterpointed characterization: two people with differing frames of reference generate movement through connection/disconnection and adjustment, not merely winner/loser opposition.

### W001-C09 — Life is not always a contest
Conflict can be a power struggle, but not every scene or relationship needs a zero-sum endgame. Understanding, altered relation, or clarified difference can be the meaningful state change.

### W001-C10 — Endings should grow from the character journey, not a mandatory effect
The chapter offers multiple ending operators: image/resonance, dialogue/callback, epiphany, false insight, narrative telling and other forms. IVDIVO treats these as options, not requirements.

### W001-C11 — Intelligent mistake joins plot and character
A reasonable error in judgment can advance causality while revealing bias/model limits. The error is stronger than arbitrary incompetence because the reader can reconstruct why the person made it.

### W001-C12 — Doubling/counterpoint can expose model revision
Repeated/similar situations become meaningful when the character's interpretation/tactic changes; repetition without new relation or meaning is inert.

## 5. PROVISIONAL IVDIVO MECHANISMS
### W001-M01 — EPISODICITY TEST
`SCENE N → what state changed? → why does Scene N+1 exist because of it?`
If scenes merely add more examples of the same pattern, insert a counter-movement, merge, compress or cut.

### W001-M02 — ROUTINE → DEVIATION → RESPONSE
Useful for post-money ordinary life: establish a normal orbital routine economically, then disturb something that matters to this person specifically.

### W001-M03 — PLOT-SHAPE ROUTER
Before forcing a three-act/beat structure, identify whether the local story is driven primarily by:
`problem / relationship counterpoint / visit-collision / investigation / quest / slice-of-life pressure / revelation`.
Then choose form to serve the engine.

### W001-M04 — INTELLIGENT MISTAKE
`LIMITED MODEL + REASONABLE EVIDENCE → PLAUSIBLE CHOICE → ERROR → COST → MODEL REVISION`.
This is especially useful for competent IVDIVO protagonists: they can be wrong without becoming stupid.

### W001-M05 — COUNTERPOINTED RELATIONSHIP ENGINE
`FRAME A ↔ FRAME B → interaction → mutual pressure → tactic/understanding shift → changed terms of relation`.
No requirement that one side be defeated.

### W001-M06 — ENDING OPERATOR CHOICE
Choose the ending effect after the causal/character arc is clear:
`decision / consequence / image resonance / dialogue callback / true insight / false insight / changed relation / bounded unresolved question`.
Do not append an epiphany because the genre expects a lesson.

## 7. EARLY CROSS-SOURCE POSITION
- **With LaPlante/Lodge:** strong anti-formula convergence; scene/story form chosen by effect and material.
- **With Truby/Rosenfeld:** retains causal state change but rejects the idea that only overt conflict creates causality.
- **With Corbett:** counterpointed characterization strengthens relation-specific personhood and character-specific responses.
- **With Zunshine:** false/partial epiphany and intelligent mistake align with evidence/model revision rather than omniscient motive certainty.
- **With Wolf/Cambridge SF:** ordinary future life should be specific and causal, not catalog worldbuilding.

## 8. EARLY IVDIVO APPLICATION
### Orbital Youth
A post-money youth story may begin with a normal, materially abundant day whose pattern breaks around a nonfungible stake: trust, place in a team, consent, unique time/experience, responsibility or relationship.

### Conscious robots
Counterpoint should not be `human position vs AI function`; it should be two persons with independent frames, evidence, tactics and relationships.

### Mystery
The intelligent mistake is valuable for competent investigators: the wrong model should be supported by actual partial evidence and later revised, not inserted through stupidity.

### LESSON ZERO
No automatic retrofit. Use the episodicity/intelligent-mistake tests only if a current sequence shows repeated function or unearned incompetence.
