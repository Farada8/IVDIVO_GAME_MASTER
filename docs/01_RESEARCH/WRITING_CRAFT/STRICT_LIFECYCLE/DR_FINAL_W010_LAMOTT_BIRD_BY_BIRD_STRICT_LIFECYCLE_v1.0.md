# 2. SOURCE PASSPORT
## Source type
Writer-process / craft / memoir hybrid. Strongest authority: generation practice, revision psychology, observation, discovery drafting, voice, peer feedback. Moderate authority: character/dialogue heuristics. Weak authority: universal story architecture, psychology, spirituality, publishing-market guidance.

## Date / context firewall
The book originates in the 1990s. Agent/publisher/query/publication mechanics are historical and must not be treated as current market guidance without external verification.

## Science / metaphysics firewall
Lamott’s language about intuition, unconscious, God, soul, spiritual truth, oneness, moral order and similar matters is autobiographical/philosophical testimony and craft metaphor. It is not scientific evidence or IVDIVO ontological canon.

# 3. STRUCTURE MAP
Lamott’s book is organized less as a formal theory than as a sequence of process problems:
1. getting words onto the page despite fear/perfectionism;
2. discovering material through small assignments and attention;
3. allowing character/plot to emerge through repeated contact;
4. revising structurally after discovery;
5. using dialogue, setting and specific detail as access routes;
6. managing the writer’s mental environment;
7. using research, peers and external readers;
8. separating publication validation from writing value;
9. developing an individual voice and ethical seriousness.

# 4. CORE CLAIMS — EXTRACTED AND TRANSFORMED
## C01 — Generation and evaluation are different cognitive jobs
Discovery requires tolerance for waste, false starts and uncertainty. Final-draft standards should not police every sentence while material is being generated.

## C02 — Small scope is a process tool, not a final-style rule
“Bird by bird” / short assignments reduce task size. They do not imply short paragraphs, short scenes or short sentences.

## C03 — Attention creates usable raw material
Specific lived details, overheard speech, remembered objects and social micro-rules often reveal character/world more effectively than abstract explanation.

## C04 — Character knowledge is iterative
A character model should update as new behavior appears. Early labels are hypotheses, not truth.

## C05 — Plot can emerge from character pressure and relationship change
Lamott is strongest as a counterweight to formula-first plotting. However, discovery does not remove the need for later causal architecture.

## C06 — Structural revision precedes polish
Her plot-treatment story is a strong example of reconstructing order, missing links, stakes and chapter-to-chapter causality before line editing.

## C07 — Dialogue is selected speech, not transcript
The writer compresses real-life speech into character-specific verbal action. Read-aloud testing is useful, but “terse” is not automatically superior.

## C08 — Setting should carry memory, values and material constraint
Rooms, gardens and environments reveal their occupants and can constrain or enable action.

## C09 — External feedback is evidence, not sovereign authority
Trusted readers can identify weak effects; their proposed repair remains a hypothesis.

## C10 — Voice cannot be permanently borrowed
Imitation can be a temporary learning scaffold, but final prose must come from the project’s own viewpoint, rhythm and attention system.

## C11 — Publication does not validate craft or self-worth
Transferable production lesson: separate manuscript diagnosis from external recognition/rejection.

# 5. MECHANISM EXTRACTION
## M01 — SHORT ASSIGNMENT ROUTER
`large intimidating problem → smallest coherent work unit → complete unit → reassess next causal unit`.

## M02 — DISCOVERY DRAFT
`generate freely → locate live material → identify actual subject/relationship → structural selection → rewrite → line polish`.

## M03 — HOT-SPOT EXTRACTION
`rough draft → find passage/image/voice/relationship carrying real energy → preserve mechanism → cut scaffolding that merely led to it`.

## M04 — CHARACTER MODEL UPDATE
`initial hypothesis → pressured behavior → contradiction/surprise → revise character model → test again in later scene`.

## M05 — DETAIL-TO-PATTERN DISCOVERY
`specific object/social rule/memory → repeated or connected details → pattern → possible meaning`.
Do not reverse this into `theme → select only confirming details`.

## M06 — FALSE-START INFORMATION
`attempt → mismatch → learn what story/character is not → sharpen model → new attempt`.

## M07 — PLOT-TREATMENT CAUSAL REBUILD
`chapter A begins at state X → meaningful change → state Y → Y must create or constrain chapter B`.
If the manuscript needs the author standing beside the reader explaining missing links, the links are not yet on the page.

## M08 — DIRECT / INDIRECT DIALOGUE ROUTER
Use direct dialogue when exact wording changes trust, consent, evidence, relationship, power, emotion or decision. Compress routine/process/redundancy.

## M09 — RESEARCH AS STORY DISCOVERY
`knowledge gap → consult specialist / observe real environment → obtain needed fact + unexpected adjacent material → retain only what serves current story`.

## M10 — FEEDBACK EFFECT / REPAIR SPLIT
`reader reports effect → record pattern → diagnose cause independently → generate repairs → test`.

## M11 — VOICE DE-BORROWING
`temporary imitation → identify what attracted writer (rhythm, permission, boldness, imagery, compression) → re-express through own character/world attention system`.

## M12 — COMPLETION THRESHOLD
A manuscript is not “perfect”; it becomes ready when major structural, continuity, POV, character, dialogue and line-level defects are resolved to the project’s evidence threshold and further changes mostly reshuffle taste.

# 6. FAILURE MODES
1. **Gate-at-Generation Overload** — every specialist audits each newborn sentence; prose becomes technically obedient and lifeless.
2. **Mess = Quality Confusion** — loose first drafts are a discovery privilege, not an excuse to skip revision.
3. **Character Mysticism** — “characters know better than author” used to avoid causal design; rejected.
4. **Plot Emergence Absolutism** — discovery-only plotting without later architecture creates beautiful fragments without a book spine.
5. **Likable Narrator Rule** — fascination/clarity/competence/mystery can build reader bond without likability.
6. **Bad Things Must Happen Rule** — meaningful pressure/cost is required; suffering inflation is not.
7. **Climax = Killing/Healing/Domination Rule** — too narrow. IVDIVO uses bounded decisive choice + consequence + local resolution.
8. **Mandatory Character Change** — rejected. Refusal, confirmation or partial change can be valid if consequences are real.
9. **Moral Absolutism** — Lamott’s personal ethics do not become universal story law.
10. **Intuition as Evidence** — intuition may generate hypotheses; validation still requires textual/world evidence.
11. **Spiritual/psychological autobiography as science** — rejected.
12. **Publication Validation Trap** — external attention substituted for manuscript diagnosis.
13. **Historical Market Advice as current guidance** — rejected unless freshly verified.
14. **Youth Voice by Borrowed Earth Slang** — direct conflict with IVDIVO world specificity.
15. **Robot as Assigned Plot Function** — discovery should be allowed to reveal independent personhood rather than force robot back into Ally/Mentor/tool role.

# 7. CROSS-SOURCE COMPARISON
## Lamott + LaPlante
Strong agreement on generation/revision separation, discovery through mess and later structural control. LaPlante offers stronger craft diagnostics; Lamott offers stronger process protection.

## Lamott + Bell, *The Art of War for Writers*
Shared principle: `GENERATE HOT → REVISE COOL`. Bell is tactical and outcome-oriented; Lamott better explains why perfectionism during generation suppresses useful surprise.

## Lamott + Francine Prose
Complementary. Lamott protects emergence; Prose supplies close-reading discipline at words/sentences/paragraphs once material exists.

## Lamott + Pushman
Both resist single-form dogma. Pushman is stronger on explicit form/genre analysis; Lamott on process and self-editing psychology.

## Lamott vs Truby / McKee / Brody / Vogler
Lamott is a necessary anti-formula counterweight. Her weakness is the reverse risk: trusting discovery so much that causal architecture arrives too late or remains incomplete. IVDIVO synthesis uses both:
`DISCOVERY → CAUSAL ARCHITECTURE → SCENE / POV / DIALOGUE → LINE EDIT`.

# 8. IVDIVO OPERATIONALIZATION
## Master production order
`DISCOVERY / GENERATION → HOT-SPOT EXTRACTION → ONE MAIN STORY / CAUSAL REBUILD → SCENE NECESSITY → POV / INFORMATION → DIALOGUE / SUBTEXT → PARAGRAPH INTENT → SENTENCE RHYTHM → WORDING`.

## 100-agent Writers’ Room
Do not run all specialist agents synchronously against every line. Use staged passes. During discovery, only a small set of guards remain active: current canon firewall, ONE MAIN STORY relevance, obvious continuity impossibility and safety/legal constraints. Deeper specialist gates activate after a scene/chapter exists.

## ONE MAIN STORY
Short assignments are work windows inside one causal engine. A sequence of excellent vignettes is not a novel unless their state changes accumulate and constrain later choices.

## Orbital Youth / post-money life
Use Lamott’s strongest method — small lived detail — to demonstrate post-money normality rather than explain it. Show what is easy/ordinary to obtain and contrast it with what cannot be manufactured: another person’s time, consent, trust, a unique mission slot, relational repair, reputation, mastery, lived practice, responsibility and irreversible experience.
Never import Lamott’s own money/publication anxiety into orbital youth as disguised credits/status currency.

## Conscious robots
Let discovery drafting reveal behavior outside the initially assigned function. Then validate it against robot personhood, embodiment, relationships, consent and canon. A robot must remain a person even when functioning as mentor, opponent or helper in one scene.

## Consciousness schools
Lamott’s intuition/spirituality material is not evidence for IVDIVO ontology. Preserve the project epistemic chain:
`practitioner observation → reproducible practice effect → school interpretation → competing interpretation → possible ontological conclusion`.

## “Обрубки” / prose rhythm
The Lamott synthesis reinforces that short work assignments have zero relationship to desired prose fragmentation. Repair order:
`scene necessity → show/tell mode → POV distance → dialogue mode → paragraph intent → sentence rhythm`.
If several short paragraphs continue the same thought/tactic/attention unit, merge before line-level ornamentation.

# 9. MANUSCRIPT VALIDATION
## LESSON ZERO Book One
No evidence from Lamott justifies reopening a development-locked book merely because a new craft source was completed. Only a current, demonstrable defect or external reader evidence should reopen structural work.

## Future IVDIVO books
Lamott is most valuable before and immediately after first prose generation:
- permit ordinary-life scenes to generate unexpected character material;
- retain only material that later survives ONE MAIN STORY and causal tests;
- use specialist research to make world detail concrete;
- use external readers to report effect, not dictate repair;
- do not retrofit trauma, death, “likability,” moral lessons or formula climax requirements.

# 10. SYNTHESIS
Lamott’s durable contribution to IVDIVO is a production principle rather than a plot system:

`PROTECT DISCOVERY, THEN BECOME RUTHLESS ABOUT STRUCTURE.`

The strongest prose often begins with material the writer could not have specified in advance. But discovery only earns its place in the finished book after causal, character, POV, scene, rhythm and reader-effect validation.
