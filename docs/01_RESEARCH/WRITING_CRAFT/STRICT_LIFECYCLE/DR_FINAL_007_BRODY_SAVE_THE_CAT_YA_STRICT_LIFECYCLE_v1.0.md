**Primary value for IVDIVO**

- prescriptive beat percentages;
- theme-as-lesson logic;
- B-story person instrumentalization;

- chosen-one / nemesis / curse defaults;

- strong diagnostic source for YA pressure, reader promise, premise payoff and common commercial story patterns;

# 5. CLAIM EXTRACTION — TRANSFERABLE CORE

## C-B42-01 — YA is an age lens
YA can host SF, mystery, thriller, romance, horror, comedy and social drama. The developmental meaning of the conflict matters more than decorative school/youth markers.

## C-B42-02 — The protagonist needs a visible present problem and want
Useful when translated away from formulaic `flaw/lesson` language.

## C-B42-03 — Disturbance must eventually generate commitment
A story becomes trackable when the protagonist accepts a goal, responsibility or course of action.

## C-B42-04 — The middle needs a pursuit
Premise delivery works best when experiences accumulate around a concrete objective instead of unrelated episodes.

## C-B42-05 — Relationships create developmental pressure
Correct, provided the other person remains an independent agent rather than a theme tutor or missing half.

## C-B42-06 — Institutions are powerful youth story engines
Belonging, rules, status, conformity, loyalty and dissent can externalize development.

## C-B42-07 — Story-type ingredients reveal reader promise
Brody's ten genre categories are useful as retrospective diagnostic lenses, not exhaustive natural laws.

## C-B42-08 — Capability should create a price
The useful core of `Superhero` is not chosen-one mythology but the relation between unusual capability/status and responsibility/cost.

## C-B42-09 — Journey milestones should alter the traveler
The useful core of `Golden Fleece` is cumulative progress plus changed people/relationships, not travel spectacle.

## C-B42-10 — Underdog status is not enough
`Fool Triumphant` usefully highlights institutional blind spots, but being underestimated is not proof the hero is correct.

## C-B42-11 — Match external problem to established capacity
`Dude with a Problem` is strongest when latent/ordinary competencies have been planted before crisis.

## C-B42-12 — Speculative capability needs coherent boundaries
`Out of the Bottle` usefully asks what the capability can/cannot do, what it costs and how it changes human choices.

## C-B42-13 — Mystery needs causal personal connection and layered evidence
`Whydunit` is useful for clue escalation and personal stakes; an unethical dark turn is optional, not required.

## C-B42-14 — Containment can be causal
`Monster in the House` usefully links threat, setting and constrained options. A moral `sin` is not mandatory.

## C-B42-15 — Pitch is a macro-diagnostic
If the story cannot be stated compactly, ONE MAIN STORY may be diffuse. Pitch form must not replace the story.

## C-B42-16 — Each series book must still be a book
Local goal/conflict/choice/resolution should close before the saga hook.

## C-B42-17 — Plans must remain falsifiable
Brody's final chapter usefully corrects her own rigidity: discovery and revision may change the original beat sheet.

# 7. FAILURE MODES

1. **BEAT-PERCENTAGE FETISH** — moving events to percentages instead of repairing causality.
2. **THEME-STATED LITERALISM** — characters announce the author's lesson.
3. **B-STORY-AS-TUTOR** — another person exists to complete/educate the hero.
4. **CONSCIOUS-PERSON-AS-DEVICE** — especially invalid for robotic persons.
5. **FUN-AND-GAMES TOURISM** — premise scenes without consequence.
6. **FALSE-VICTORY/DEFEAT FORCING** — midpoint taxonomy precedes story logic.
7. **STAKES-INFLATION TOOLBOX** — death, clock, secret or spectacle added because a template wants escalation.
8. **ALL-IS-LOST MANUFACTURING** — artificial catastrophe near a prescribed percentage.
9. **DARK-NIGHT MONOCULTURE** — every hero receives the same reflective collapse.
10. **FINALE MORAL PROOF** — climax exists to prove theme.
11. **EQUAL-POV FOG** — ensemble equality mistaken for equal book-level airtime.
12. **TEN-GENRE TOTALISM** — taxonomy treated as ontology.
13. **COMPLETION ROMANCE** — relationship partner framed as missing half.
14. **INSTITUTIONAL BINARY** — join/escape/destroy replaces negotiation/reform/partial membership.
15. **COMPANY-MAN CARICATURE** — loyalty equals moral corruption.
16. **CHOSEN-ONE INFLATION** — unusual ability/status becomes destiny and moral superiority.
17. **MANDATORY NEMESIS** — one villain invented because a genre ingredient expects one.
18. **CURSE AS REQUIRED TRAUMA** — capability price forced into melodramatic wound/punishment.
19. **FUNCTIONAL-TEAM INVENTORY** — people reduced to useful skill slots.
20. **UNDERDOG = RIGHT** — marginal status substitutes for evidence.
21. **LIFE-OR-DEATH DEFAULT** — emergency form applied to conflicts better served by trust, role, autonomy or relationship stakes.
22. **MAGIC/TECH LESSON MACHINE** — speculative mechanism exists to teach a moral.
23. **MANDATORY DARK TURN** — investigator violates values because mystery template expects it.
24. **MORAL SIN CAUSES THREAT** — contained-system problem forced into punishment allegory.
25. **SERIES MEGABEAT SHEET** — saga becomes one giant 15-beat story and individual books lose closure.
26. **CLIFFHANGER OBLIGATION** — book ending sacrificed to franchise propulsion.
27. **PITCH-DRIVEN RETROFIT** — story altered to fit a logline template.
28. **FRAMEWORK IMMUNITY** — failed prediction blamed on manuscript instead of testing the framework itself.

# 8. CROSS-SOURCE COMPARISON

- **Truby** is stronger on organic causality, desire/action/learning/decision and character/opposition webs than Brody's fixed beat positions.
- **Rosenfeld** is stronger on scene recursion: prior consequence → intention → complication/support → result → next intention.
- **Bell** is stronger on intelligent opposition, suspense modulation and stakes without compulsory genre ingredients.
- **Card** is stronger on character hierarchy, POV attention and independent personhood; it corrects equal-airtime ensemble drift.
- **Kress** is stronger on character-specific motivation/frustration and prevents generic `flaw → wrong strategy → lesson` psychology.
- **Chatman** protects meaningful satellites and separates story from discourse; not every useful passage must be a plot kernel.
- **Herman / Zunshine** are stronger on reasons, person-models and source monitoring, especially when systems infer human behaviour.
- **Le Guin / Clark** are stronger on prose rhythm and paragraph/sentence function; fast story movement does not justify chopped syntax.
- **Edgerton** converges with Brody on early pressure, but neither catastrophe nor giant catalyst is mandatory.

**Brody's unique contribution:** a very usable YA developmental lens, premise-payoff pressure, common commercial story-pattern vocabulary, accessible genre comparison, and series/pitch diagnostics.

**Brody's unique danger:** treating successful retrospective patterns as universal forward-design laws.

# 9. IVDIVO OPERATIONALIZATION — PRODUCTION GATES B42

**B42-G01 — YA DEVELOPMENT MUST BE CAUSAL**  
External conflict must affect autonomy, authority, peer belonging, identity, responsibility, intimacy, consent, status or future self.

**B42-G02 — TRACKABLE A-STORY**  
After commitment, name the concrete pursuit that accumulates scenes.

**B42-G03 — PREMISE MUST PAY OFF**  
Orbital Youth must show adolescence changed by orbital civilization, not Earth teen life with futuristic wallpaper.

**B42-G04 — TAXONOMY IS A LENS**  
Never insert an ingredient only because a Brody/STC story category names it.

**B42-G05 — NO CHOSEN-ONE DEFAULT**  
Unusual access/capability requires cause, limits and price; it does not imply destiny or moral superiority.

**B42-G06 — NO MANDATORY NEMESIS**  
Opposition may be environment, timing, competent institutions, peers, conflicting values or system constraints.

**B42-G07 — CAPABILITY MUST CHANGE CHOICE**  
If a technology/ability changes no options, risk, social relation or decision, it is decorative.

**B42-G08 — TEAM MEMBER HAS AN INDEPENDENT LIFE**  
Unique story function never substitutes for desire, relationships, boundaries and off-protagonist life.

**B42-G09 — POST-MONEY PRIZE TEST**  
A sought-after prize may be trust, role, unique mission, time, access, relationship or experience; never disguised money/credits as default.

**B42-G10 — UNDERDOG STATUS IS NOT EVIDENCE**  
Youth/outcast/local knowledge may matter without making institutions stupid.

**B42-G11 — PROPORTIONAL STAKES**  
Do not escalate to death/catastrophe if trust, responsibility, safety, autonomy or relationship loss carries the story.

**B42-G12 — SPECULATIVE RULE CONSISTENCY**  
New technology/phenomenon needs observable limits and consequences.

**B42-G13 — CONSCIOUSNESS-TRANSFER FIREWALL**  
Brody's body/magic examples do not authorize human consciousness transfer in current Orbital Youth canon.

**B42-G14 — FAIR-PLAY MYSTERY**  
Mystery evidence must be source-tracked and backward-supported; no mandatory dark ethical compromise.

**B42-G15 — CONTAINMENT IS PHYSICAL CAUSALITY, NOT MORAL PUNISHMENT**  
Orbital sectors/rings can constrain action without requiring a `sin` allegory.

**B42-G16 — PITCH ONE-MAIN-STORY TEST**  
Use a compact pitch only to test macro clarity, never to force beats.

**B42-G17 — LOCAL BOOK CLOSURE BEFORE SAGA HOOK**  
Resolve the book's concrete problem before opening the next saga question.

**B42-G18 — SERIES NO RESET**  
Relationships, competence, injuries, laws, knowledge and status persist into later books.

**B42-G19 — FRAMEWORK MUST LOSE WHEN STORY EVIDENCE DISAGREES**  
No craft model outranks current causal evidence or Founder canon.

**B42-G20 — DISCOVERY WITH CHANGE CONTROL**  
New discoveries may revise architecture, but every macro change requires an explicit causal reason; discovery is not scope drift.

Current production verdict remains **REPAIR, DO NOT REBUILD**.

Brody Ch8–17 validates several existing decisions while rejecting several tempting retrofits:

- **Ollie's bounded SAFE-STATE authority is stronger than a chosen-one arc.** It is earned, narrow, revocable and consequential.
- **Iri must remain an independent person, not a Golden-Fleece team function.** Existing independent household/performance life supports this.
- **Tomas's midpoint injury should remain a concrete consequence, not be inflated into mandatory life-or-death/whiff-of-death drama.**
- **Aoife/Source S1 consent conflict should not be forced into a Whydunit `dark turn`; respecting a boundary can itself be the costly choice.**
- **Ring/sector containment is useful physical causality, not proof of a moral `sin`.**
- **Technology boundaries remain governed by current canon.** No ordinary human consciousness transfer, no hidden credit economy, no mainstream dimensional science.
- **The book must close its local habitation/safety/migration problem before any saga hook.** The reconciler's `honest partial home` direction is compatible with this.
- **Brody Ch17 supports the project's existing evidence-based revision rule.** The current architecture may be repaired when story evidence demands it; it need not be rebuilt to satisfy a template.

### Working macro pitch diagnostic — NOT CANON TEXT
A young maker entering real orbital responsibility helps diagnose a newly opening habitat whose local environmental failures do not fit the city's standard model. Early competent fixes earn him bounded authority, but a coupled failure injures a worker and proves that no single elegant fix can solve the interaction among migration, timing, sensors, bodies and infrastructure. He must give up the fantasy of one clean answer and help build a segmented, accountable solution before the city's relocation process overtakes the available safety window.

Diagnostic verdict: **ONE MAIN STORY remains legible.** Do not retrofit a nemesis, chosen-one destiny, dark turn or Save-the-Cat percentage schedule.
