## 3. FINAL CLAIM SYNTHESIS
### Generation / discovery
1. Generative and evaluative work are different cognitive jobs; premature policing can kill living material.
2. Strong writing aims for **surprising yet convincing** rather than merely novel or merely plausible.
3. Discovery often begins by locating what the writer genuinely does not yet understand inside familiar material.
4. Theory is more reliable as diagnosis of existing material than as an external mold imposed before discovery.

### Detail / prose / meaning
5. Voice begins in attention: what a consciousness notices, ignores, compares and remembers.
6. Concrete specificity needs hierarchy; detail quantity is not detail quality.
7. Particulars can generate general meaning more powerfully than thesis-first illustration.
8. Metaphor should clarify or complicate perception, not decorate an unclear action.
9. Symbols gain force through accumulated context rather than retrofit labeling.
10. Meaning is not a detachable slogan; the experienced arrangement of event, detail, language and relation is itself part of meaning.
11. Emotional/intellectual force can be transferred onto concrete objects, actions and sensory events through accumulation.
12. In fiction, factual occurrence is not enough for believability; page-level rendering must earn conviction.

### Form / causality / character
13. Conflict-crisis-resolution, epiphany and transformation are useful models, not universal laws.
14. A character may fail or decline to change; refusal still needs meaningful consequence.
15. Causality may be mechanical, emotional, relational, social or epistemic.
16. Internal events such as memory, projection and interpretation become plot when they alter later action.
17. Overt villainy is unnecessary when desire, situation, opportunity, misperception and consequence create pressure.
18. Roundness is surprising-yet-convincing behavior, not biography volume.
19. Character evidence is relation-specific: the same person changes tactics and voice across different relationships while remaining recognizably continuous.

### Show / tell / POV / reliability
20. Scene and narration are complementary tools; `show, don't tell` is not a universal law.
21. Direct scene is warranted when exact words/actions materially change state; narration/summary is often superior for process, history, repetition, thought and time control.
22. Grammatical person, information access and narrative distance are independent POV variables.
23. Changing `I` to `he/she` cannot fix illegal knowledge, wrong distance or unstable attitude.
24. Reliability is calibrated rather than binary: a narrator may accurately report action while misreading motive, memory or causation.
25. Distorted POV needs observable traces, consequences or independent sources from which the reader can recover a more stable model.
26. Speculative strangeness must be distinguished from POV error, information gap and author/canon contradiction.

### Dialogue / opening
27. Dialogue is edited action, not realistic transcription.
28. Silence, gesture, objects and environment participate in dialogue.
29. Placeholder dialogue can be useful during generation, but revision must produce character-specific tactics, rhythm, indirection and consequence.
30. An opening promises an experience; it need not begin with physical action.
31. Genre-obligatory scenes should be challenged because convention easily turns into cliché.

### Revision / feedback
32. Revision has distinct stages: generation, deep creative re-imagining, constructive revision, then copyediting/line polish.
33. Early feedback is most useful when it identifies **hot spots** and reader-effect symptoms before prescribing repairs.
34. Exercise-based revision can deliberately create unusable material in order to discover a stronger story model.
35. Constraints can increase discovery by forcing new decisions.
36. Structural problems should sometimes be re-imagined rather than patched line by line.
37. Critical/editorial feedback is evidence about reader effect, not authority over authorial vision.
38. Consensus prescriptions can flatten a distinctive work; shared symptoms should be separated from proposed cures.

### Truth / ethics / epistemology
39. Creative nonfiction may shape experience but may not knowingly replace inconvenient facts with invented ones while claiming factuality.
40. Memory/reconstruction require epistemic humility.
41. Writer subjectivity is legitimate but must not be confused with objective fact.
42. Some truths remain unresolved; manufactured healing, symbolic closure or certainty can be less truthful than acknowledged incompleteness.
43. The writer's `I` must have a functional reason to be present; grammatical first person alone does not justify narrative centrality.

## 4. FINAL MECHANISM INDEX
### M-L19-01 — GENERATE / DIAGNOSE SPLIT
`GENERATE → IDENTIFY LIVE MATERIAL → STRUCTURAL DIAGNOSIS → DEEP REVISION → CONSTRUCTIVE REVISION → LINE EDIT`.

### M-L19-02 — SURPRISING-YET-CONVINCING
`FRESH? → CHARACTER/WORLD-TRUE? → CAUSALLY PREPARED? → RETROSPECTIVELY LEGIBLE?`

### M-L19-03 — UNKNOWN-INSIDE-KNOWN
`FAMILIAR MATERIAL → GENUINE UNKNOWN → PARTICULARS → ALTERNATIVE MODELS → TEST / CHOICE`.

### M-L19-04 — PARTICULAR → PATTERN → MEANING
Start from a person/object/action/place; permit pattern and possible meaning to emerge. Do not reverse the sequence into thesis-first evidence selection.

### M-L19-05 — DETAIL DOUBLE-PAY
A selected unusual detail should ideally perform at least two functions among POV, character, world, relation, causality, tension, foreshadowing or rhythm.

### M-L19-06 — SHOW/TELL ROUTER
`EXACT WORD/ACTION CHANGES STATE? → DIRECT SCENE`;
otherwise consider `NARRATION / SUMMARY / INDIRECT`.

### M-L19-07 — POV ACCESS MATRIX
`OBSERVE → KNOW → INFER → TEXT INDEPENDENTLY CONFIRMS → DISTANCE`.

### M-L19-08 — RELIABILITY CALIBRATION
`POV CLAIM → TRACE → INDEPENDENT SOURCE/CONSEQUENCE → READER MODEL UPDATE`.

### M-L19-09 — ONTOLOGY VS POV TEST
`STRANGE EVENT → WORLD RULE / POV DISTORTION / INFORMATION GAP / CANON ERROR? → DISCRIMINATING EVIDENCE`.

### M-L19-10 — DIALOGUE ACTION ROUTER
`WANT → VERBAL/NONVERBAL TACTIC → RESPONSE → TACTIC CHANGE → CHANGED STATE`.

### M-L19-11 — PLACEHOLDER → LIVING DIALOGUE
`AGENDA/INFORMATION → PLAIN GENERATIVE PASS → CHARACTER FILTER → INDIRECTION/RHYTHM/GESTURE/SUBTEXT → TRIM`.

### M-L19-12 — SOFT CAUSALITY
`EVENT → EMOTIONAL/RELATIONAL/EPISTEMIC PRESSURE → COPING/TACTIC → RESULT → NEXT CHOICE`.

### M-L19-13 — RENDER-HOW
`BEHAVIOR PATTERN → SPECIFIC CONTEXT → CONSEQUENCE → LAYERED WHY` rather than one-label backstory causality.

### M-L19-14 — SCENES-A-FAIRE TEST
For a supposedly mandatory genre scene, generate several alternate embodiments and choose the one most specific to the story engine, world and people.

### M-L19-15 — RELATIONAL CHARACTER MATRIX
For each major character: `WHAT DO THEY WANT FROM THIS PERSON? → WHAT DO THEY RISK? → WHAT TACTIC/VOICE CHANGES? → WHAT REMAINS CONTINUOUS?`

### M-L19-16 — OPENING CONTRACT
`ATTENTION / READER QUESTION → TONE → PHYSICAL/SOCIAL WORLD → CHARACTER/SITUATION → PROMISE OF STORY MODE`.

### M-L19-17 — MEANING TRANSFER
`CONCRETE OBJECT/EVENT → POV ATTENTION → ACCUMULATED RELATIONAL/EMOTIONAL PRESSURE → POSSIBLE MEANING`.
Meaning should be experienced before it is paraphrased.

### M-L19-18 — REVISION STAGE ROUTER
`GENERATIVE? → DEEP CREATIVE? → CONSTRUCTIVE? → COPY/LINE?`
Use the repair method appropriate to the stage; do not solve a structural problem with polish or destabilize a locked manuscript with endless rediscovery.

### M-L19-19 — HOT-SPOT FIRST FEEDBACK
`READER EFFECT → WHAT FEELS ALIVE / DEAD / UNCLEAR → SHARED SYMPTOM → AUTHOR DIAGNOSIS → REPAIR OPTIONS`.
Do not equate a critic's prescription with the problem itself.

### M-L19-20 — EXERCISE-BASED DEEP REVISION
When stuck, prototype alternate POV, tense, scene↔summary, disagreement, chronology, research or constrained variants; generated material is diagnostic and need not enter the manuscript.

### M-L19-21 — CRITIC AUTHORITY FIREWALL
`FEEDBACK = EVIDENCE OF EFFECT`, not canon. Preserve authorial intention while taking repeated reader symptoms seriously.

### M-L19-22 — FACT / INTERPRETATION / TRUTH LABELING
For nonfiction and knowledge-heavy fiction: `OBSERVED FACT → SOURCE → MEMORY/INTERPRETATION → UNCERTAINTY → CLAIM LEVEL`.

### M-L19-23 — NO HEALING RETROFIT
`PRESSURE → EXPERIENCE → RESULT`; permit unresolved loss, partial learning or refusal when that is the truthful causal outcome. Do not insert a lesson solely to complete an arc template.

## 6. CROSS-SOURCE COMPARISON
### Truby / McKee Story
Truby and McKee are stronger on external causal architecture, opposition and scene/book progression. LaPlante is the stronger counterweight against turning those diagnostics into compulsory creation formulas. IVDIVO uses causal rigor without requiring every scene to be overt conflict or every protagonist to transform conventionally.

### Rosenfeld — *Make a Scene*
Rosenfeld is stronger on scene engineering, intention and transitions. LaPlante is stronger on discovery, show/tell flexibility, ambiguity, POV/reliability and revision-stage discipline. Combined rule: engineer scenes after determining whether material should be a scene at all.

### Bell — *Conflict & Suspense*
Bell is stronger on pressure, uncertainty and propulsion. LaPlante prevents suspense from becoming conflict inflation, stakes inflation or compulsory bad outcomes. Quiet relational/epistemic pressure remains valid causality.

### Brody — YA
Brody supplies age-specific developmental pressure. LaPlante protects Orbital Youth from beat-template retrofits, lesson downloads and compulsory healing/change arcs.

### Card / McKee Character
Card and McKee supply strong cast hierarchy and choice-based character evidence. LaPlante adds attention fingerprints, relation-specific behavior, anti-pop-psych causality and the principle that roundness is surprising yet retrospectively convincing.

### McKee Dialogue
Strong convergence on dialogue as action. LaPlante contributes explicit support for narration/summary, nonverbal dialogue, placeholder drafts and the later revision of plain information into living voice.

## 7. IVDIVO PRODUCTION GATES
**L19-G01 Generate Before Policing** — first living material is not required to satisfy every production gate simultaneously.

**L19-G02 No Sentiment Buttons** — injury, death, child danger, robot innocence, rejection, first love and sacrifice do not automatically create depth.

**L19-G03 Grimness Is Not Depth** — cynicism and tragedy require the same causal specificity as hope.

**L19-G04 Character Attention Fingerprint** — major POVs notice different subsets of the same environment for character-specific reasons.

**L19-G05 Unknown-Inside-Known** — mystery/metaphysical material must retain testable uncertainties rather than author-certified answers.

**L19-G06 Particular Before World Thesis** — reveal post-money/orbital civilization through lived choices, not explanatory proof paragraphs.

**L19-G07 Detail Function** — unusual technology/object receives attention proportional to actual POV/story function.

**L19-G08 No Metaphor Repair** — first make physical/action meaning clear; metaphor cannot repair staging confusion.

**L19-G09 No Automatic Epiphany** — recognition requires accumulated evidence/experience and must change something if dramatized.

**L19-G10 Consequential Non-Change** — refusal to change is allowed, but it must alter relationships, options, cost or outcome.

**L19-G11 Scene-vs-Narration Gate** — do not dramatize routine logistics merely because scenes seem more cinematic.

**L19-G12 POV Knowledge Legality** — every important knowledge claim must have a legitimate path into the focal consciousness.

**L19-G13 Recoverable Reality** — unreliable perception cannot erase all stable evidence channels.

**L19-G14 Ontology Evidence Gate** — distinguish phenomenon, observation, interpretation and established world fact.

**L19-G15 Relationship-Specific Voice** — character voice changes with relationship/power/risk without turning into unrelated personalities.

**L19-G16 Dialogue State Change** — exact dialogue earns space when its wording materially changes state.

**L19-G17 No Universal Wit** — do not give the whole ensemble one authorial cleverness register.

**L19-G18 Soft Causality Accepted** — emotional/relationship/knowledge changes may carry causal weight without immediate external event triggers.

**L19-G19 Scenes-à-Faire Challenge** — genre expectations trigger alternative generation, not automatic inclusion.

**L19-G20 Opening Contract** — opening establishes attention/story mode; physical action is optional.

**L19-G21 Meaning Transfer** — recurring objects/images/events should gather meaning through experience before any explanatory sentence.

**L19-G22 Revision Stage Gate** — match repair to manuscript stage.

**L19-G23 Hot-Spot First** — in early drafts identify living material before conducting maximum fault extraction.

**L19-G24 Feedback Symptom vs Cure** — repeated reader reaction is high-value evidence; repeated reader prescription is not automatically the correct solution.

**L19-G25 Deep Revision Prototype** — test radical alternatives in sandbox material before rewriting accepted canon.

**L19-G26 No Healing Retrofit** — no mandatory trauma lesson or reassuring closure unsupported by the book.

**L19-G27 Epistemic Levels for Consciousness Schools** — maintain:
`OBSERVATION → REPRODUCIBLE PRACTICE EFFECT → SCHOOL INTERPRETATION → COMPETING INTERPRETATION → ONTOLOGICAL FACT`.
Few practitioners and limited long-term training capacity remain a world-development bottleneck; specialist claims do not become mainstream orbital science automatically.

**L19-G28 Choppy-Prose Repair Order** —
`SCENE NECESSITY → SHOW/TELL ROUTING → POV DISTANCE → DIALOGUE MODE → PARAGRAPH INTENT → SENTENCE RHYTHM → WORDING`.
Do not solve micro-dramatization by merely lengthening sentences.

**L19-G29 Locked-Manuscript Protection** — new craft insight cannot reopen a development-locked book without a concrete current defect or strong external reader evidence.

## 8. MANUSCRIPT / PROJECT VALIDATION
### LESSON ZERO Book One
No new defect from LaPlante is sufficient to reopen the development lock. Apply only to demonstrable local problems: illegal POV knowledge, scene inflation, explanation-after-action, revision-stage mismatch, dialogue fact conveyors or line-level rhythm after structural integrity is confirmed.

### Orbital Youth / future orbital books
High value: particular-before-thesis world reveal, relationship-specific voice, post-money stakes shown through lived nonfungible wants, concrete environment affecting action, and scene/narration compression of routine transport/logistics.

### Conscious robots
No default “robot voice.” A conscious robot needs relationship-specific behavior, independent wants, right to refuse, epistemic limits and a life not reducible to human story function.

### Specialist consciousness / dimensional schools
LaPlante's reliability and nonfiction epistemic discipline is directly applicable: practitioner report, reproducible training effect, school explanation and world ontology remain separate evidence levels. The small population of serious practitioners limits development regardless of broader technological capability.

### Mystery / evidence-heavy books
Preserve source labels and reality channels. `POV THINKS X` may never silently become `X IS TRUE`. Use observable traces, independent consequences and competing interpretations.

### Prose / line editing
LaPlante confirms that “choppy prose” is often a higher-level routing failure: too many micro-scenes, unnecessary direct dialogue, unstable POV distance or paragraph fragmentation. Structural compression precedes sentence smoothing.

## 9. FINAL SYNTHESIS
LaPlante is now a primary IVDIVO counter-formula craft authority for **discovery, attention, particularity, show/tell routing, POV/reliability, soft causality, dialogue generation, revision-stage discipline and epistemic humility**.

Use her most strongly when a draft is technically correct but lifeless, when worldbuilding has become thesis-first, when every moment is over-dramatized, when feedback is flattening the manuscript, when POV claims are outrunning evidence, or when line editing is being asked to repair a structural defect.

Do **not** use her to weaken causal rigor or excuse shapelessness. The combined IVDIVO position is:

`DISCOVER LIVE MATERIAL → BUILD ONE MAIN CAUSAL STORY → PRESERVE CHARACTER/WORLD TRUTH → ROUTE SCENE/NARRATION/POV CORRECTLY → REVISE AT THE RIGHT LEVEL → LINE EDIT FOR READER EFFECT`.
