# 3. PROVISIONAL MECHANISM EXTRACTION FROM CLOSED CHAPTERS

These are working extractions only. FULL READ is still open.

### M-B41-01 — ACTIVE COMMITMENT
`DISTURBANCE → PROCESSING → MEANINGFUL CHOICE/ACCEPTED RESPONSIBILITY → TRACKABLE OBJECTIVE`.
Useful. Percentage placement is not authoritative.

### M-B41-02 — TRACKABLE MIDDLE
The middle needs a concrete pursuit so scenes accumulate rather than drift.
IVDIVO form: `ONE MAIN STORY → objective → attempts → changed information/relations → strategy change`.

### M-B41-03 — PREMISE PAYOFF
A book should deliver the distinctive human experiences promised by its premise. For Orbital Youth, orbital life must alter adolescence causally; do not substitute technology tourism.

### M-B41-04 — MIDPOINT MODEL CHANGE
Transferable midpoint function: materially alter goal, method, model, relationship, evidence status or constraints. False victory/defeat is optional.

### M-B41-05 — AFTERMATH CAUSALITY
Reflection earns space when it changes interpretation, commitment, relationship or next action. A mandatory `Dark Night` does not.

### M-B41-06 — RELATIONSHIP PRESSURE, NOT THEME TUTOR
Brody often makes B-story/supporting characters thematic teachers. IVDIVO transformation: a relationship pressures growth because another independent person has their own want, boundary, cost and right to refuse.

### M-B41-07 — MULTI-POV CONNECTION
Single/multi/blended track language is useful for ensemble diagnosis. Every major POV still needs a causal connection to the one main story.
`SAGA EQUALITY ≠ BOOK-LEVEL EQUAL POV`.

### M-B41-08 — EARLY DISTURBANCE CAN CARRY SETUP
When pressure arrives early, reactions can reveal world, relationship and status quo. Do not require a separate exposition block.

### M-B41-09 — BACKSTORY PAYS PRESENT FUNCTION
Backstory should alter present understanding, action, relationship, stakes or reader expectation. Do not use an absolute `if removable, delete` rule; meaningful satellites can pay emotional/voice/foreshadowing functions.

### M-B41-10 — STORY-TYPE TAXONOMY AS LENS
Brody's ten genre categories can clarify dominant human pressure and reader promise. They are not an exhaustive ontology and must not overwrite organic causality.

### M-B41-11 — YA DEVELOPMENTAL PRESSURE
Rites-of-Passage material correctly emphasizes that adolescence intensifies identity, separation, changing adult authority, first responsibility, belonging and first intimacy. IVDIVO does not require avoidance → lesson acceptance as the only growth shape.

### M-B41-12 — BUDDY LOVE CORRECTION
Source model often describes `incomplete hero + counterpart who completes them`.
IVDIVO correction: relationships can transform both people, but neither should exist as the other's missing piece. Strong relationship engine = `two independent selves → attraction/need/common task → real incompatibility/constraint → mutual influence → changed relationship/self-strategy`.

### M-B41-13 — INSTITUTION AS SOCIAL ENGINE
Institutionalized is highly relevant to youth SF because institution can reveal rules, membership, belonging, conformity, legitimate benefits and real costs.
Transferable questions:
- What does membership enable?
- What does it demand?
- What is voluntary, assumed, inherited or coerced?
- Who benefits from the rules?
- What happens to dissent?
- What can be changed without simply destroying the institution?

### M-B41-14 — NAIF / INSIDER AS EXPOSITION POSITIONS
The useful core of Brody's naif/brando distinction is epistemic position: newcomer can discover rules with reader; insider can reveal invisible assumptions by friction. Do not reduce characters to exposition devices.

### M-B41-15 — NON-VILLAIN INSTITUTION
Brody tends toward `join / escape / burn it down` and company-man corruption. IVDIVO requires a wider model: an institution can be competent, beneficial and still contain contested norms, blind spots or misaligned procedures.

# 4. PROVISIONAL FAILURE MODES

- **BEAT-PERCENTAGE FETISH** — moving events to percentages instead of fixing causality.
- **THEME-STATED LITERALISM** — line exists mainly to announce the author's lesson.
- **B-STORY-AS-TUTOR** — supporting character exists to teach the protagonist.
- **CONSCIOUS-PERSON-AS-THEME-DEVICE** — especially invalid for conscious robotic persons.
- **FUN-AND-GAMES MONTAGE** — premise delivery detached from consequence.
- **FALSE-VICTORY/DEFEAT FORCING** — midpoint categorized before its actual causal function is understood.
- **STAKES-INFLATION TOOLBOX** — clock/secret/death/spectacle added because template wants escalation.
- **ALL-IS-LOST MANUFACTURING** — artificial catastrophe at an expected percentage.
- **DARK-NIGHT MONOCULTURE** — all heroes forced into the same reflective breakdown.
- **FINALE MORAL PROOF** — climax exists to demonstrate a lesson.
- **EQUAL-POV FOG** — ensemble members receive equal weight merely because all matter to the saga.
- **TRACK WITHOUT CONNECTION** — multiple POVs become unrelated mini-books.
- **CATALYST SIZE FETISH** — only huge external events are treated as story-starting.
- **DOUBLE-BUMP PATCHING** — second catalyst patches a first catalyst that generated no genuine choice.
- **CRYPTIC-ALLUSION DEBT** — future-catastrophe tease substitutes for intrinsically causal current pages.
- **BACKSTORY NECESSITY OVERREACH** — useful satellites deleted because plot can technically survive.
- **TEN-GENRE TOTALISM** — retrospective taxonomy treated as universal truth.
- **GENRE-INGREDIENT CHECKBOXING** — conventions inserted rather than story promise diagnosed.
- **WRONG-WAY MONOCULTURE** — competent young people forced into avoidable stupidity for an arc.
- **ACCEPTANCE-AS-ONLY-GROWTH** — all coming-of-age forced into one therapeutic shape.
- **COMPLETION ROMANCE** — another person framed as the missing piece required to make the hero whole.
- **RELATIONSHIP-WEDGE MANUFACTURING** — arbitrary misunderstanding/secret inserted merely to keep a couple apart.
- **INSTITUTIONAL BINARY** — institution must be joined, escaped or destroyed; reform, negotiated membership, plural governance and bounded dissent disappear.
- **COMPANY-MAN CARICATURE** — institutional loyalty automatically equals moral corruption.
- **NAIF INFO-DUMP TOUR** — newcomer exists only so the world can be explained.

# 5. PROVISIONAL CROSS-SOURCE COMPARISON

- **Truby** is stronger on organic causality (`desire → action → learning → decision`) than fixed beat placement.
- **Rosenfeld** is stronger at scene recursion (`prior consequence → intention → complication/support → result → next intention`).
- **Bell** plus Brody reinforces a necessary `NO STAKES INFLATION` firewall: pressure must come from existing causal conditions, not template demands.
- **Card** is stronger on character hierarchy/POV attention budget and corrects Brody's equal-page-time tendency.
- **Chatman** protects meaningful satellites from an overly strict necessity test.
- **Kress** supplies character-specific motivation/frustration, preventing generic `flaw → wrong way → lesson` YA psychology.
- **Herman/Zunshine** are stronger for person-level reasons, distributed intelligence and source-monitoring; these matter when institutions or algorithms model people.

# 6. PROVISIONAL PRODUCTION GATES B41

**B41-G01 — ACTIVE COMMITMENT, NOT ACT-2 PERCENTAGE**  
A disturbance must eventually generate meaningful choice/accepted responsibility; location is organic.

**B41-G02 — TRACKABLE A-STORY GOAL**  
After commitment, name a concrete objective that accumulates scenes.

**B41-G03 — RELATIONSHIP PERSONHOOD**  
No friend, lover, mentor, rival or conscious robot exists primarily to teach theme.

**B41-G04 — PREMISE MUST PAY OFF**  
Deliver the promised experience through causal scenes, not worldbuilding tourism.

**B41-G05 — MIDPOINT MODEL CHANGE**  
Mid-book must materially change model/goal/method/relationship/constraints; false victory/defeat optional.

**B41-G06 — NO STAKES INFLATION**  
Clock, secret, death, spectacle or catastrophe only if caused by existing story conditions.

**B41-G07 — NO MANDATORY ROCK BOTTOM**  
Use the consequence the story earns.

**B41-G08 — AFTERMATH CHANGES ACTION**  
Reflection stays when it changes next behavior or relation.

**B41-G09 — FINALE RESISTANCE WITHOUT TEMPLATE**  
Ending needs earned resistance, bounded choice and cost; first-plan failure/sacrifice not mandatory.

**B41-G10 — VISIBLE CHANGE, NO MORAL EXPLANATION**  
Prefer behavior/relationship/status evidence over a lesson sentence.

**B41-G11 — MULTI-POV CONNECTION**  
For every major POV, name its causal connection to ONE MAIN STORY and why that mind is needed.

**B41-G12 — SAGA EQUALITY IS NOT EQUAL SCREEN TIME**  
Book-level attention remains hierarchical and functional.

**B41-G13 — BACKSTORY PAYS PRESENT FUNCTION**  
Past material changes present understanding/action/relationship/reader expectation.

**B41-G14 — TAXONOMY IS A LENS, NOT AN ENGINE**  
Story-type labels clarify pressure/promise but never override causality.

**B41-G15 — YA DEVELOPMENT IS CAUSAL**  
External conflict must mean something specifically adolescent: autonomy, adult authority, peer belonging, identity, responsibility, intimacy, future self, status or consent.

**B41-G16 — COMPETENT YOUTH MAY STILL GROW**  
A correct but incomplete strategy may meet a more complex world; stupidity is not required.

**B41-G17 — RELATIONSHIP WITHOUT COMPLETION MYTH**  
Two people may transform each other while remaining complete agents with independent lives.

**B41-G18 — CONFLICT MUST BELONG TO THE RELATIONSHIP**  
Do not manufacture a wedge. Complication should arise from values, circumstance, history, goals, boundaries or real external conditions.

**B41-G19 — INSTITUTION HAS REAL BENEFITS**  
Before criticizing an institution, show why reasonable people join/stay and what it actually does well.

**B41-G20 — MEMBERSHIP HAS SPECIFIC TERMS**  
Make rules, access, obligations, authority and exit/voice mechanisms concrete rather than symbolic.

**B41-G21 — NO JOIN/ESCAPE/DESTROY TRINARY**  
Allow reform, negotiation, partial membership, plural institutions, bounded refusal and institutional learning when causally appropriate.

**B41-G22 — LOYALIST MUST REMAIN A PERSON**  
Institutional loyalty is not proof of villainy. Give legitimate reasons, relationships and tradeoffs.
