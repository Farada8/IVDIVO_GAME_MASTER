# 4. CLAIM EXTRACTION

## C1 — Begin where the story becomes causally active
The opening should not spend its strongest attention on pre-story routine if a later event is the first point that changes what the protagonist must do.

## C2 — Distinguish situation from story-triggering change
A bad situation can exist for years; story begins when something changes the character's operational state and creates a need for action.

## C3 — Surface problem and deeper problem should be coupled
The concrete thing the hero is trying to solve should pressure the more fundamental human problem instead of running beside it as a separate theme.

## C4 — Concrete surface goals are easier to dramatize
The immediate goal should be observable/actionable even when the deeper need is abstract.

## C5 — One dominant deep book problem can generate many surface problems
Multiple external complications work when each grows from the prior state and keeps pressing the same book-level human conflict.

## C6 — Setup is orientation, not biography
Give enough to understand the scene, not enough to pre-answer every later question.

## C7 — Backstory earns space when current action creates a need for it
History becomes stronger when it is compressed, delayed or woven into present action.

## C8 — Character is introduced most efficiently through meaningful reaction/action
A first impression built from behavior under pressure is more causally useful than dossier description.

## C9 — An opening establishes a reader contract
Tone, narrative attitude, kind of conflict, reality-level and story promise should not be bait-and-switched later.

## C10 — Specificity can perform several jobs at once
A precise object/action/detail can orient place, reveal character and create expectation simultaneously.

## C11 — The beginning can seed the ending
Early images, choices or constraints can become retrospectively meaningful when paid off later.

## C12 — Transitions should not dramatize logistics merely to prove continuity
Readers can infer many movements in time/place without explanatory bridges.

## C13 — Reader curiosity is stronger when generated by the actual story
A hook unrelated to the book's real engine is manipulation, not propulsion.

## C14 — Drama does not require spectacle
Small decisions/realizations can be more consequential than large explosions when they alter the character's model, choice or relationship.

## C15 — Trust the reader
Do not explain an implication immediately after behavior already made it legible.

# 5. MECHANISM EXTRACTION

## M-H01 — OPENING CAUSAL IGNITION
`PRE-EXISTING WANT / CONDITION -> DISTURBANCE -> CONCRETE NEW PROBLEM -> FIRST CHOICE -> CHANGED STATE`

Gate: if the first chapter can be removed without changing what the hero must do next, the story may begin too early or the chapter lacks causal ignition.

## M-H02 — SURFACE / DEEP COUPLING
`PHOTOGRAPHABLE GOAL -> RESISTANCE -> HUMAN MEANING -> CHOICE -> NEW EXTERNAL PROBLEM`

Example transformation for IVDIVO: not `prove post-money society works`, but `earn trust to perform a real habitat task`; the resulting failure/choice reveals what usefulness and responsibility mean to that person.

## M-H03 — PROBLEM DESCENDANCY
`SURFACE PROBLEM A -> ACTION -> CONSEQUENCE -> SURFACE PROBLEM B -> ACTION -> CONSEQUENCE -> ... -> BOOK-LEVEL HUMAN RESOLUTION`

Production test: later complications should be children of earlier choices/conditions, not a pile of unrelated crises.

## M-H04 — MINIMUM SETUP
Before prose, label every opening fact:
- REQUIRED TO UNDERSTAND NOW;
- CAN BE INFERRED;
- CAN ARRIVE LATER;
- NEVER NEEDED.

Only the first category is guaranteed opening space.

## M-H05 — ACTIVE BACKSTORY
`CURRENT ACTION -> HISTORY BECOMES RELEVANT -> MINIMAL HISTORICAL FACT -> CURRENT ACTION CONTINUES`

Backstory must alter present interpretation/action, not suspend the story for biography.

## M-H06 — CHARACTER-IN-ACTION FIRST IMPRESSION
`PRESSURE -> CHARACTER-SPECIFIC RESPONSE -> RELATIONSHIP/RESULT`

Do not explain a trait after the action already demonstrated it.

## M-H07 — READER CONTRACT
Opening should establish enough of:
- narrative distance/voice;
- seriousness/comic range;
- reality rules;
- central kind of pressure;
- protagonist lens;
- promise of the book's actual story.

Later novelty may expand the contract but should not invalidate it.

## M-H08 — DOUBLE-DUTY DETAIL
Every conspicuous opening detail should ideally perform at least two functions among:
`orientation / character / conflict / world rule / foreshadow / mood / later payoff`.

## M-H09 — ENTER LATE / LEAVE EARLY
Enter after routine setup when the meaningful state is already changing; exit once the scene's state change is achieved, not after all logistics are exhausted.

## M-H10 — OPENING/ENDING RESONANCE
Seed an early image/choice/relationship constraint whose meaning can change by the resolution. Do not mechanically foreshadow the answer.

## M-H11 — SMALL DRAMA, LARGE CONSEQUENCE
Prefer the smallest observable event that truly changes agency, trust, status, knowledge or responsibility over inflated spectacle with little human causality.

## M-H12 — QUESTION WITHOUT CHEATING
`OBSERVABLE ODDITY/CONFLICT -> LEGITIMATE QUESTION -> ACTION TO REDUCE UNCERTAINTY`

Do not create curiosity by withholding what the current POV character naturally knows without an in-world reason.

# 6. FAILURE MODES IN EDGERTON IF APPLIED LITERALLY

## F1 — Mandatory scene disaster
Edgerton repeatedly prefers scenes ending in disaster/partial failure. This is too rigid for IVDIVO. A scene may end in success, costly success, correct restraint, support, discovery or negotiated change provided the next state is materially different.

Replacement:
`OBJECTIVE -> RESISTANCE / COMPLICATION / SUPPORT -> RESULT -> CHANGED NEXT INTENTION`.

## F2 — Universal inciting-incident-at-the-opening rule
Useful default, not universal law. Some strong stories use a controlled pre-disturbance sequence, delayed recognition, frame, mystery setup or relationship pressure. The functional requirement is early causal ignition, not one prescribed page location.

## F3 — One-to-four-page opening norm
Historical craft heuristic, not a production metric. Opening length is determined by work completed, not page count.

## F4 — All important material must be scene
Too categorical. Summary/indirect narration can carry routine, repetition, time compression and process. Dramatize where exact action/wording changes the state.

## F5 — Antagonist-person requirement
Opposition can be institutional, physical, social, temporal, environmental, relational or generated by competing legitimate goals without a villain-shaped antagonist.

## F6 — Story-worthy problem always reducible to one buried psychological wound
Often useful, but reductive if forced. IVDIVO allows ethical, developmental, relational and identity conflicts that do not need a trauma diagnosis.

## F7 — Market claims treated as timeless
Claims about Borders, BookScan, literary-fiction decline, attention span and agent preferences are 2006–2007 observations. Do not operationalize them as 2026 market truth.

## F8 — Dialogue-density red flag as universal rule
Dialogue can create white space and action, but prose-rich openings are not automatically bad. Judge narrative pressure and reader processing, not dialogue percentage.

## F9 — Ban on dialogue openings
Not a law. The useful mechanism is immediate context legibility: if dialogue begins the book, reader should understand enough of speaker/situation quickly without costly backtracking.

## F10 — Adverb/adjective prohibitions
Treat as revision prompts, not grammar law. Specificity, cadence, voice and precision outrank blanket part-of-speech bans.

# 7. CROSS-SOURCE COMPARISON

## With Truby
Convergence:
- external desire must pressure inner need;
- causality should arise from character action;
- story is not a sequence of arbitrary events.

Difference:
- Edgerton packages this as surface problem versus story-worthy problem;
- Truby offers a broader character/moral web and does not require the deeper problem to be hidden in exactly Edgerton's form.

IVDIVO synthesis:
Use Edgerton for **surface/deep coupling**, Truby for the larger causal organism.

## With Rosenfeld
Convergence:
- scene needs intention and state change;
- openings/scene transitions should not float as vignettes.

Correction to Edgerton:
Rosenfeld supports multiple result types; therefore reject mandatory disaster.

## With Bell / Bickham / Swain
Convergence:
- concrete scene objective;
- resistance;
- consequence drives next movement;
- early story must make reader continue.

Correction:
Use causal changed-state rather than success/failure as the sole scene classifier.

## With Card
Convergence:
- character becomes legible through action and motive;
- reader contract and viewpoint clarity matter;
- do not overload the opening with equal-weight cast information.

## With Brody YA
Convergence:
- external genre engine should expose a developmental problem specific to the young protagonist.

IVDIVO synthesis:
A youth protagonist's deep problem must remain age- and relationship-specific; do not paste an adult existential thesis onto a teenager.

## With McKee / Lodge counterweight
Correction:
A book may create propulsion through tension, voice, contradiction, social pressure or cognitive question without a spectacular external incident. `Trouble` is best interpreted as **unstable meaningful pressure**, not mandatory action spectacle.

# 8. IVDIVO PRODUCTION GATES — HG SERIES

## HG01 — ACTUAL STORY START
What changes now that forces a different next action? If nothing, opening may precede the story.

## HG02 — ONE MAIN STORY / DEEP PROBLEM
Name one dominant book-level human problem. Subplots may refract it but may not compete as separate novels.

## HG03 — SURFACE-PROBLEM DESCENDANCY
Each major external complication must arise from prior action, prior condition or an already-established opposing agenda.

## HG04 — POST-MONEY STAKE FIREWALL
In mature orbital settings, do not use salary, rent, disguised credits or purchasing power as the easy opening problem. Use trust, role, mission access, responsibility, relationship, reputation, mastery, consent, safety, time or unique experience.

## HG05 — MINIMUM OPENING SETUP
World facts appear only as needed for current perception/action.

## HG06 — ACTIVE WORLD REVEAL
Technology/social systems must alter choice, movement, relationship or risk; no opening-tour inventory.

## HG07 — CHARACTER THROUGH RESPONSE
First significant character statement should be behavior under meaningful pressure before explanatory labeling.

## HG08 — NO FALSE HOOK
Opening question must belong to the actual A-story or directly generate it.

## HG09 — READER-CONTRACT CONTINUITY
The first chapter's tone, POV, seriousness and story type must honestly represent the book.

## HG10 — DETAIL PAYS TWICE
Conspicuous early detail must do at least two jobs or be exceptionally necessary for sensory clarity.

## HG11 — BACKSTORY NEED TEST
History enters when present action changes because of it.

## HG12 — NO EXPLANATORY ECHO
If action/dialogue already makes meaning legible, narrator does not immediately restate the lesson.

## HG13 — DRAMA / MELODRAMA TEST
Ask whether a smaller choice, hesitation, refusal, lie, act of trust or error would create more human consequence than escalating spectacle.

## HG14 — OPENING / ENDING RESONANCE
At least one early choice/image/relationship can gain transformed meaning at the resolution; no mandatory symbol planting.

## HG15 — TRANSITION ECONOMY
Do not dramatize transport/logistics unless they change story state. This is especially important in large orbital environments.

## HG16 — CONSCIOUS ROBOT SUBJECT TEST
If a named conscious robot can be replaced by an appliance without changing consent, agenda, relationship or conflict, robot personhood has not been written.

## HG17 — SPECIALIST KNOWLEDGE FIREWALL
High orbital technology does not imply mature public dimensional/soul science. Any such knowledge requires practitioner lineage, training time, reproducibility status and competing interpretation.

## HG18 — ERA-SENSITIVE ADVICE
Any publishing/market prescription from a historical craft book is tagged with its date and independently re-verified before market use.

# 11. FINAL SYNTHESIS

The durable value of *Hooked* for IVDIVO is not `start with an explosion` and not a list of first-page prohibitions.

It is this:

`START WHERE MEANINGFUL PRESSURE BECOMES CAUSAL`

then

`CONCRETE PROBLEM -> CHARACTER-SPECIFIC ACTION -> CONSEQUENCE -> NEXT PROBLEM`

while the external chain gradually exposes a deeper human question.

For IVDIVO, transform Edgerton's `story-worthy problem` into a **book-level human conflict** rather than a mandatory hidden trauma.

For Orbital Youth, the highest-value application is:

`EXTRAORDINARY CIVILIZATION` must arrive through `ORDINARY HUMAN WANT UNDER NEW CONDITIONS`, not through exposition and not through recycled Earth-money stakes.
