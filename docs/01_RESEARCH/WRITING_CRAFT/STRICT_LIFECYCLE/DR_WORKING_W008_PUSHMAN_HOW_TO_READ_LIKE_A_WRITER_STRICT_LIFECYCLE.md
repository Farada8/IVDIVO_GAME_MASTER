# 3. PROVISIONAL CLAIMS
## W008-C01 — Genre changes the reader's contract
The same sentence is processed differently as fiction, nonfiction or poetry because truth status, expectation and language stakes differ.

## W008-C02 — Writers should read for choices, not just content
A reading writer asks why the author chose genre, structure, language, scene/detail and what effect those choices produce.

## W008-C03 — Genre is a repertoire, not a prison
Publishing categories matter, but literary/commercial and genre boundaries are porous. The useful question is what a chosen form enables or constrains.

## W008-C04 — Selective focus is part of narrative truth
Even nonfiction does not need every event from lived experience. Relevance/focus determines what belongs while factual claims remain truthful.

## W008-C05 — Ordinary plots can carry coming-of-age
A meaningful narrative does not require life-altering catastrophe; a sensory/identity experience can become plot when it changes the young person's relation to self/world.

## W008-C06 — Reality inside fiction must become character-specific experience
Large real issues become narratively effective when embodied in specific character, setting and detail rather than inserted as abstract topic.

# 4. PROVISIONAL MECHANISMS
## W008-M01 — GENRE CONTRACT TEST
`WHAT FORM? → WHAT DOES THE READER ASSUME IS TRUE/INVENTED? → WHAT KIND OF PAYOFF/ATTENTION DOES THE FORM PROMISE? → WHICH CRAFT CHOICES SERVE THAT CONTRACT?`

## W008-M02 — WRITERLY READING NOTE
For a passage record:
`WOW/AH-HA EFFECT → exact technique → why it works here → what principle is transferable → new experiment, not imitation`.

## W008-M03 — SELECTIVE EXPERIENCE ROUTER
`TOTAL AVAILABLE REALITY → story focus → necessary events/details → omission of irrelevant material → coherent reader experience`.

## W008-M04 — ISSUE → PERSON FILTER
For a large social/technical/theme issue:
`ABSTRACT ISSUE → particular person → immediate want/problem → lived setting/detail → choice/consequence → reader inference`.

# 5. INITIAL IVDIVO OPERATIONALIZATION
## Orbital Youth
A post-money civilization should not be introduced as an essay topic. Filter its social differences through a young person's immediate goal, embarrassment, trust, relationship, routine or responsibility.

## Coming-of-age
Do not inflate every youth plot into catastrophe. A first real responsibility, changed relationship, loss of trust, boundary negotiation or unique experience can carry developmental weight.

## Science fiction
SF is a reader contract/mode but not automatically the causal engine. Identify the actual story promise inside the SF world.
