# IVDIVO CRAFT STUDY — BATCH 71
## Vogler: Journey Function · Polarity · Reader Contract · Aftermath

Status: WORKING SYNTHESIS
Date: 2026-08-17
Source: Christopher Vogler, *The Writer's Journey*, 25th Anniversary / Fourth Edition.
Strict source status: FULL LIFECYCLE COMPLETE as W009.

## PURPOSE
Keep the transferable mechanics of Vogler while preventing Hero's Journey retrofit, archetype reduction and mythic/metaphysical leakage into IVDIVO canon.

## PRODUCTION GATES
### B71-G01 — FLEXIBLE MAP
Journey stages are questions, never required chapter slots. Delete/reorder freely.

### B71-G02 — THRESHOLD MUST CHANGE RULES
A new place/role/institution counts as threshold only if options, obligations, relations, risk or error cost change.

### B71-G03 — COMMITMENT BEFORE LABEL
Ask what commitment changes, not whether the story has a correctly named “Crossing the Threshold.”

### B71-G04 — FUNCTION MIGRATION
Mentor/guardian/herald/shadow/ally/trickster are temporary scene functions. Never create or flatten a person merely to fill one.

### B71-G05 — COMPETENT GUARDIAN
A blocker may be correct, protective and evidence-based. Crossing can require negotiation/readiness rather than conquest.

### B71-G06 — REFUSAL OPTIONAL
No mandatory fear/refusal beat. Willing protagonists and externally distributed doubt are valid.

### B71-G07 — ORDEAL ≠ DEATH
Test for costly model/strategy/relationship change. Do not manufacture literal/psychological death or trauma.

### B71-G08 — PRE-DECISION REORGANIZATION
Before a hard choice, a preparation scene must change roles, evidence, resources, strategy or commitment; otherwise compress it.

### B71-G09 — AFTERMATH SPACE
High pressure may require decompression, relationship processing, celebration, grief, new evidence or changed responsibility before the next engine turn.

### B71-G10 — QUIET CLIMAX ALLOWED
Climax = decisive local choice/resolution, not volume or body count.

### B71-G11 — RETURN CHANGES ORDINARY LIFE
After local resolution, show changed responsibility/relationship/knowledge/terms before optional saga hook.

### B71-G12 — AUDIENCE ATTENTION CONTRACT
Define the promised experience created by opening/genre/voice/world. Pay or fairly revise it.

### B71-G13 — STATE RENEGOTIATION
A scene earns full dramatic space when it materially changes at least one important state: action, knowledge, relation, access, commitment, emotion, evidence or power.

### B71-G14 — POLARITY AS COMPETING MODELS
Build credible Model A and Model B. Permit synthesis, bounded coexistence, reform, separation or partial defeat; do not force villain destruction.

### B71-G15 — SECOND-ORDER CONSEQUENCE
After a successful action ask: what else did it change? Use `not only X, but also Y` for system-level causality rather than random escalation.

### B71-G16 — TENSION / RELEASE CONTRAST
Do not keep every scene at maximum pressure. Ordinary life, humor, intimacy and recovery can preserve reader sensitivity and deepen later stakes.

### B71-G17 — BODY RESPONSE IS EVIDENCE, NOT VERDICT
Reader tension, boredom, warmth, confusion or breath-holding are useful beta signals. Diagnose cause before editing.

### B71-G18 — MYTHIC CLAIM FIREWALL
Jungian, chakra, vibration, ESP, telepathy, synchronicity and “story is alive” claims are symbolic/authorial material only. They do not establish psychology, science or IVDIVO ontology.

### B71-G19 — POST-MONEY JOURNEY
Calls/rewards/elixirs in Orbital Youth derive from trust, responsibility, consent, relationship, role, qualification, time, reputation, unique access/experience—not wages, rent, credits or disguised currency.

### B71-G20 — ROBOT PERSONHOOD BEFORE ARCHETYPE
A conscious robot may temporarily mentor/guard/oppose, but must retain independent desire, relationships, consent and refusal.

### B71-G21 — CONSCIOUSNESS-SCHOOL EPISTEMICS
Maintain:
`observation → reproducible effect → school interpretation → competing interpretation → possible ontological conclusion`.
Vogler's mystical vocabulary cannot skip this chain.

### B71-G22 — NO RETROFIT OF LOCKED BOOKS
A new craft source does not reopen a development-locked manuscript without a concrete independent defect.

## CROSS-SCHOOL SYNTHESIS
- Truby/McKee/Brody: causal architecture and state change are retained; fixed geometry is not.
- LaPlante/Prose/Pushman: organic discovery and prose effect protect the novel from beat-sheet prose.
- Card/Corbett/McKee Character: temporary function is subordinate to full personhood.
- Zunshine/Herman: symbolic interpretation never substitutes for evidence provenance.
- Wolf: a threshold matters when world rules affect action, not when the lore catalogue changes.

## READER-EFFECT NOTE
Vogler's catharsis/body material supports modulation rather than choppy-suspense mythology. Preserve the IVDIVO hierarchy:
`causality → scene necessity → mode → POV → paragraph intent → sentence rhythm → wording`.

## ACCEPTANCE TEST
Use Vogler only when it clarifies transition, commitment, functional roles, high-pressure testing, aftermath, return or reader contract. If it merely adds a missing named stage, reject the intervention.
