### PASS68 mechanisms from Ch13
- DIAGNOSE BEFORE SURGERY: identify the level of the defect before rewriting.
- HOT-SPOT / IRREDUCIBLE CHANGE: preserve the scene's indispensable changed-state rather than trimming blindly.
- FLASHBACK PRESENT-CONSEQUENCE TEST: past material earns full scenic treatment only when it changes current understanding, relationship, evidence-status, decision or strategy; otherwise prefer summary/back-flash.
- TANGENT CAUSAL TEST: unplanned material must either produce a fresher character-true path into the dominant engine or be banked/discarded.
- CHARACTER OVER OUTLINE: an outline is a hypothesis, not permission to turn a character into a plot puppet.
- WRITER FATIGUE IS NOT AUTOMATICALLY A STORY DEFECT.
