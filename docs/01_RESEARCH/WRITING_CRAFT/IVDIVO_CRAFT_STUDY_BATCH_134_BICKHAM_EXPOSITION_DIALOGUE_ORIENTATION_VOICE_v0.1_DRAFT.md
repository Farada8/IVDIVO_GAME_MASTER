## 1. Exposition must have a present causal reason

Bickham's useful core is not simply "never lecture." The stronger mechanism is:

`INFORMATION → WHO NEEDS / NOTICES IT NOW? → WHY NOW? → SOURCE / ACCESS → USE IN ACTION, INTERPRETATION OR DECISION`

Information that exists only because the author wants the reader to know it is suspect.

### IVDIVO production gate
Before exposition enters a scene, answer:
- Who presently needs, recalls, notices, asks for, disputes or acts on this information?
- Why does it become salient now rather than earlier/later?
- What is its source and evidence-status?
- What changes after it is received?

This converges with Zinsser source distillation, Zunshine provenance, Fludernik story/discourse separation, LaPlante show/tell routing and McKee exposition-as-action.

## 2. Theme should survive removal of the lecture

Bickham is correct that a story collapses when characters become mouthpieces for a thesis. But his implication that fiction should never substantially argue an idea is too categorical.

IVDIVO standard:

`THEMATIC QUESTION → COMPETING MODELS → CHARACTER-TRUE ACTIONS → CONSEQUENCES → READER INFERENCE / PROVISIONAL ANSWER`

A political, ethical or philosophical novel may have an argument. The argument must remain causally embodied in people and consequences rather than depend on a sermon.

### Failure modes
- AUTHOR LECTURE
- THEME SERMON
- WORLD-BIBLE VOICE
- STRAWMAN COUNTERMODEL
- INFORMATION WITH NO PRESENT OWNER

## 3. Dialogue is shaped action, not transcript and not a research container

Sections 15–16 correctly identify two common failures: dialogue used as disguised exposition and multipoint speeches that exist because the writer is trying to unload notes.

Working model:

`CHARACTER WANT → VERBAL / NONVERBAL TACTIC → OTHER PERSON'S ACTUAL ATTENTION / RESPONSE → TACTIC CHANGE → CHANGED STATE`

Dialogue earns real-time space when exact wording changes one or more of:
- trust;
- consent;
- evidence-status;
- authority/status;
- relationship terms;
- decision;
- emotional state;
- future option space.

Otherwise consider indirect dialogue or summary.

### Bickham correction: one-on-one is not a law
Bickham recommends simplifying many conversations into one-on-one exchanges. This is a useful diagnostic for muddled scenes, not a universal production rule.

Baxter, Chiarella, Fludernik and the IVDIVO ensemble system show that group scenes can gain meaning from:
- asymmetric attention;
- interruptions;
- standing/status;
- different private goals;
- who hears or misses a line;
- conflicting evidence access;
- silence by one participant while another takes control.

Do not reduce a group scene merely to make dialogue engineering easier.

### Failure modes
- PUPPET LECTURE / AS-YOU-KNOW DIALOGUE
- RESEARCH-PRIDE DUMP
- MULTIPOINT SPEECH
- WINDBAG CONVERGENCE
- DIALOGUE-AS-TRANSCRIPT
- ONE-ON-ONE MONOCULTURE

## 4. Voice is not phonetic spelling

Section 17's useful core is that typographic dialect and fashionable slang can distract, date or stereotype. Its 1992-era editor/market claims are historical advice, not current market law.

Cross-source synthesis with Rasley, Roy Peter Clark, Kress, Bell Voice and Corbett:

`IDENTITY + WORLDVIEW + RELATIONSHIP + STATUS + PRESENT GOAL → WORD CHOICE + SYNTAX + RHYTHM + REGISTER + WHAT IS LEFT UNSAID`

### Orbital Youth
Do not manufacture youth voice by importing contemporary Earth slang. Let future youth voice emerge from what a teenager considers normal, embarrassing, prestigious, intimate, unfair or worth arguing about in that society.

### Conscious robots
There is no universal "robot voice." A named conscious robot must change register/tactic by interlocutor and relationship and retain the right not to answer.

## 5. Reader orientation around dialogue

Section 18 demonstrates a real problem: bare dialogue can leave the reader without a model of place, body, intent or emotional interpretation. But Bickham's expanded example also risks overcorrection; he himself notes it may be overdone.

IVDIVO minimum orientation model:

`WHERE / EMBODIMENT + PRESENT WANT + RELATIONSHIP / STATUS + CRITICAL PHYSICAL CHANGE + POV INTERPRETATION WHEN NEEDED`

Do **not** insert a sensory or gesture beat after every line.

### Anti-choppy application
Bad repair pattern:

`line → glance → sensory fragment → thought → line → hand movement → line`

Preferred:

`dialogue tactic → coherent perceptual / relational paragraph unit → changed attention or tactic → next exchange`

Sensory material should orient or change meaning, not create gesture confetti.

### Failure modes
- SENSORIUM CHECKLIST
- ACTION-BEAT FIDGETING
- MICROGESTURE CONFETTI
- DIALOGUE IN ABSTRACT SPACE
- ORIENTATION OVERCORRECTION

## 6. Attribution transparency

Section 19's useful core: dialogue attribution should usually be easy to process; conspicuous thesaurus-swapping can distract from the exchange.

Do **not** operationalize Bickham's numeric preference for "said" as a percentage KPI.

Router:

`CLEAR SPEAKER? → NO TAG NEEDED / TRANSPARENT TAG / MEANINGFUL ACTION BEAT / INDIRECT REPORT`

Use an action beat only when the action itself matters. Do not replace every "said" with meaningless movement.

### Failure modes
- THESAURUS TAGGING
- SAID-PERCENTAGE KPI
- TAG DOES EMOTION'S JOB
- ACTION BEAT AS TAG CAMOUFLAGE

## 7. Mystery and evidence application

Exposition must preserve provenance:

`WORLD FACT → SOURCE / TRACE → CHARACTER ACCESS → INTERPRETATION → CONFIDENCE → ACTION`

A character may lecture convincingly and still be wrong. Expertise, institutional authority, attraction or confidence cannot promote a claim to world fact.

For consciousness-practice schools:

`PRACTITIONER OBSERVATION → REPRODUCIBLE EFFECT → SCHOOL INTERPRETATION → COMPETING INTERPRETATION → INDEPENDENT TEST → ONTOLOGY CEILING`

## 8. Post-money worldbuilding

Do not explain post-money society in an authorial paragraph unless the narrative situation genuinely requires it.

Prefer ordinary causal evidence:

`NORMAL WORLD RULE → CHARACTER DOES NOT TREAT ORDINARY GOODS AS SCARCE → ATTENTION SHIFTS TO NON-FUNGIBLE STAKE → CHOICE / CONSEQUENCE`

Non-fungible stakes remain time, trust, consent, relationship, specific responsibility, unique opportunity, reputation, mastery, privacy and belonging.

## 9. Revision order reinforced

`ONE MAIN STORY / CAUSALITY → CHAPTER FUNCTION → SCENE NECESSITY → SCENE WEIGHT / MODE → POV / EVIDENCE → EXPOSITION OWNER → DIALOGUE TACTICS → PARAGRAPH INTENT / READER ORIENTATION → SENTENCE RHYTHM → WORDING`

Do not line-polish a lecture that should be removed, or add action beats to dialogue that should be summarized.
