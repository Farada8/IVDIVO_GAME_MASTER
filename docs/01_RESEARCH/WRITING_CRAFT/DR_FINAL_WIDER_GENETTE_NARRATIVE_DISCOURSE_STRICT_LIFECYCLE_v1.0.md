## High-value claims retained

### Story / discourse / narrating are distinct

`WHAT HAPPENED ≠ HOW IT IS PRESENTED ≠ WHO/WHEN THE TELLING INSTANCE IS`.

A defect in disclosure order, duration, frequency, focalization or voice does not automatically require changing the underlying story fact.

### Order

- Anachrony is not automatically a defect.
- `reach` = temporal distance of the anachrony from the current story point.
- `extent` = duration covered by that anachrony.
- Completing analepsis fills a gap; repeating analepsis returns to an already narrated event to modify/reframe its meaning.
- Advance notice explicitly points forward; advance mention plants material whose importance becomes legible later.
- Later revelation can change interpretation without changing the earlier trace.

### Duration

Written narrative has no literal scene=real-time equivalence. Narrative rhythm is always selective.

`ELLIPSIS / SUMMARY / SCENE / PAUSE` are functional discourse choices rather than quality rankings.

Description can remain story movement when it follows active perception rather than suspending action.

### Frequency

- `SINGULATIVE`: narrate once what happened once.
- `REPEATING`: narrate multiple times what happened once.
- `ITERATIVE`: narrate once what happened repeatedly.

Repeated narration earns reader time only if new POV, source, interpretation, evidence status, relationship meaning or action emerges.

Iterative narrative is especially useful for establishing a lived baseline and for showing how one singular event changes a routine.

### Mood — distance and perspective

Narrative information is regulated by both directness/distance and perspective/focalization. These are not the same variable.

`WHO SPEAKS ≠ WHO PERCEIVES / KNOWS`.

Direct speech, indirect speech, free indirect style, narratized speech and immediate speech create different information effects; none is automatically superior.

`SHOW ≠ GOOD / TELL ≠ BAD`.

### Focalization

Genette distinguishes:
- zero/nonfocalized;
- internal fixed / variable / multiple;
- external.

Focalization can change locally. It should not be treated as a single label permanently governing the whole book.

`PARALIPSIS` — narrating gives less than the active focalization code appears to authorize/require.

`PARALEPSIS` — narrating gives more than the active focalization code appears to authorize.

This distinction is critical for mystery fairness and POV continuity.

### Reader inference can exceed character understanding

A focal character may accurately perceive a trace without understanding its meaning; the reader can infer more than the focalizer without the narrator explicitly explaining it.

Therefore:

`OBSERVED TRACE ≠ POV INTERPRETATION ≠ READER INFERENCE ≠ WORLD FACT`.

### Voice

`NARRATOR ≠ AUTHOR` in fiction.

Time of narrating may be subsequent, prior, simultaneous or interpolated. The temporal location of the telling instance can affect knowledge and interpretation.

Narrative levels are relational:
- extradiegetic;
- intradiegetic/diegetic;
- metadiegetic.

Narrating itself can act causally inside a story; a story told to a character can change that character's decisions.

### Person

The useful distinction is not merely first vs third grammatical person but whether the narrator is absent from the story (`heterodiegetic`) or present (`homodiegetic`), with `autodiegetic` for narrator-as-hero.

Grammatical person does not by itself determine closeness, perspective or information access.

### Narrator functions

The narrator may perform:
- narrative;
- directing / organizational;
- communication;
- testimonial / attestation;
- ideological / commentary functions.

These are weighted functions, not a mandatory checklist.

## Failure modes extracted

1. `STORY/DISCOURSE CONFLATION` — rebuilding canon when only presentation is wrong.
2. `CHRONOLOGY TOURISM` — nonlinear order without interpretive or causal payment.
3. `RETCON MASQUERADING AS REINTERPRETATION` — later reveal changes the earlier trace rather than its meaning.
4. `PARALIPSIS CHEAT` — focal character knows critical information but narration hides it solely to surprise the reader.
5. `PARALEPSIS LEAK` — narration enters information/mental access unavailable under its established code without functional justification.
6. `RECAP-AS-PROGRESSION` — repeating an event without new evidence, POV, meaning or consequence.
7. `ITERATIVE FLATTENING` — habitual narration erases important variation or regime change.
8. `PSEUDO-ITERATIVE IMPROBABILITY` — allegedly repeated routine rendered with implausibly identical, hyper-specific detail.
9. `SHOW/TELL MORALISM` — treating direct dramatization as intrinsically superior.
10. `FOCALIZATION=PRONOUN` — confusing grammatical person with perception/knowledge.
11. `INNER-SPEECH TRUTH FALLACY` — treating thought/self-report as verified motive or world fact.
12. `NARRATOR=AUTHOR` — treating fictive commentary as automatic authorial belief.
13. `LEVEL CONFUSION` — treating an embedded report/document/dream as if it had the same source status as first-level world evidence.
14. `METALEPTIC LEAK` — accidental collapse of narrative levels in a non-metafictional passage.
15. `FORCED UNITY` — interpreting every inconsistency into a single elegant design instead of admitting contingency / revision history / partial incoherence.
16. `TAXONOMY OVERFIT` — adding scenes or forms because the analytic grid contains a named category.

## Cross-source comparison

### Chatman

Strong convergence on `story ≠ discourse`, temporal management and knowledge routing. Chatman gives a cleaner production router; Genette provides finer temporal/focalization diagnostics.

### Fludernik

Converges on separation of story facts, discourse presentation and experiential access. Fludernik broadens narrativity beyond classical event-sequences; Genette provides sharper order/duration/frequency terminology.

### Zunshine / Herman

Genette explains how information reaches the reader; Zunshine/Herman explain why source attribution and competing mental/causal models matter. Combined:

`WORLD FACT → TRACE/SOURCE → DISCOURSE PLACEMENT → FOCAL ACCESS → INTERPRETATION → ALTERNATIVE MODEL → CONFIDENCE → ACTION`.

### LaPlante / Rosenfeld / Scofield / Hodgins

These craft sources provide scene-level writing decisions; Genette explains why summary, ellipsis, iterative narration or delayed disclosure can be superior to full dramatization.

### Le Guin / Prose / Lodge / Clark / Zinsser / Swain

Converges on rejection of sentence-length monoculture. Temporal/discourse duration must be solved before sentence rhythm.

## IVDIVO operationalization

### Temporal architecture passport

For every major nonchronological block:

`TYPE → REACH → EXTENT → SOURCE/POV → FUNCTION → EVIDENCE EFFECT → RETURN/JUNCTION → PAYOFF`.

### Reveal fairness

`EARLY TRACE REMAINS TRUE → LATER EVIDENCE CHANGES MODEL → PRIOR DETAIL GAINS NEW MEANING`.

Do not repair mystery by mutating what the reader was already shown.

### Frequency passport

For repeated material classify:

`SINGULATIVE / REPEATING / ITERATIVE`.

For repeating: what materially changes on repetition?  
For iterative: what are the temporal bounds, recurrence pattern and exceptions?  
For singular exception: does it change the future routine or merely illustrate it?

### Mood / POV passport

For each scene or information unit:

`WHO PERCEIVES → WHAT THEY CAN ACCESS → WHAT THEY INFER → WHAT NARRATION STATES → WHAT READER MAY INFER → WHAT REMAINS UNKNOWN`.

### Speech mode router

Use exact direct wording when exact wording changes consent, evidence, authority, relationship, commitment or decision. Otherwise narratized/indirect speech and summary remain valid.

### Narrative-level provenance

For embedded records, testimony, memories, dreams, logs or institutional documents:

`LEVEL → ORIGINAL SOURCE → INTERMEDIARIES → EDIT/SELECTION → CURRENT CLAIM → CORROBORATION STATUS`.

Earlier traces may be retrospectively reclassified as evidence of personhood, but the traces themselves must remain literally true. Once independent personhood threshold is reached:

`ENTITY MODEL/PREFERENCE → CONSENT/REFUSAL → ENTITY CHOICE → HUMAN OPTION SPACE CHANGES`.

### Conscious robots

`MORE SENSORS ≠ NARRATIVE OMNISCIENCE`.

Self-report / immediate thought / human interpretation remain sourced information, not automatic motive fact.

### Consciousness-practice schools

Discourse order may delay or foreground testimony, but does not change the evidence ladder:

`PRACTITIONER OBSERVATION → REPRODUCIBLE EFFECT → SCHOOL INTERPRETATION → COMPETING INTERPRETATION → INDEPENDENT TEST → ONTOLOGY CEILING`.

## Final synthesis

Genette is most valuable to IVDIVO as a **presentation-architecture diagnostic**. The governing production principle is:

`STORY FACT → ORDER → DURATION → FREQUENCY → DISTANCE / FOCALIZATION → VOICE / LEVEL → READER MODEL → ACTION / INFERENCE`.

The grid is used to find the right defect level, never to force the manuscript into a taxonomy.
