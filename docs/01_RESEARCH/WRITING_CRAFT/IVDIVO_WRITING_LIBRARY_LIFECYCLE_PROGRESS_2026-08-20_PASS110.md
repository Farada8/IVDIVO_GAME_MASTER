## 5. Newly grounded Scofield material

Targeted same-file retrieval exposes the later scene-evaluation/economy layer in detail.

It explicitly asks the writer to determine:

- the scene event;
- the emotions connected to it;
- whether that event deserves a scene or could be summarized;
- the scene's function;
- whether the scene enters too early;
- whether it could be integrated into another scene;
- whether it repeats already explored behavior/action;
- whether it arises from prior action and leads to later action.

It also proposes:

- captioning scene functions;
- comparing action beats and emotion/response beats;
- finding a focal turn;
- testing scene utility and economy before revision.

## 7. New production gates

### A. Revision Admission Gate

Before line edit:

1. What is the scene event?
2. What is the primary function?
3. What is different at exit?
4. What later material depends on that difference?
5. Could summary or an adjacent scene carry the same value?
6. Can the scene enter later?
7. Does exact unfolding matter?

If structural admission fails, line editing is prohibited until repair.

### B. Function Duplication Audit

Represent scenes as:

`FUNCTION CAPTION + EXIT STATE`.

If nearby scenes have effectively the same function and exit state, test:

`MERGE / COMPRESS / DELETE`.

Two individually strong scenes can still be redundant.

### C. Reader-Time Budget

Real-time scene detail creates functional debt.

Protect exact unfolding when it changes evidence, consent, relationship terms, authority/access, decision ownership, personhood status, model/strategy or future options.

Compress repeated process, logistics, known setup, travel, routine interfaces, repeated briefing and exact wording that does not matter.

### D. Action/Emotion Coupling

Scofield's action/emotion comparison is operationalized as:

`ACTION / INFORMATION BEAT ↔ POV RESPONSE / RELATION / MODEL BEAT → FOCAL TURN → DOWNSTREAM CONSEQUENCE`.

Do not write it as a metronome.

Coupling may happen at sentence, paragraph, scene or delayed-scene level.

### E. Focal Turn ≠ Twist

A turn can be evidence-status change, consent/refusal, trust change, accepted responsibility, model revision, personhood threshold, authority change or success that creates new obligations.

No mandatory surprise/cliffhanger.

## 9. Anti-choppy prose integration

Pipeline is now:

`BOOK ENGINE → CHAPTER FUNCTION → SCENE ADMISSION → SCENE FUNCTION + EXIT STATE → SCENE WEIGHT / MODE → ORDER / DURATION / FREQUENCY → POV / EVIDENCE → ACTION-RESPONSE COUPLING → PARAGRAPH INTENT → READER-STATE CONTINUITY → SENTENCE RHYTHM → WORDING`.

This prevents:

- line-editing scenes that should be summarized or merged;
- adding one separate reaction sentence after every action;
- using short-sentence density as a suspense/intensity KPI.
