## 6. EARLY LODGE RESULTS

- suspense question can be intensified by slowing prose, not only accelerating it;
- teenage voice is a crafted illusion of attitude, lexical habits, syntax, implication and rhythm — not topical slang;
- addressee conditions voice in letters/messages/testimony;
- POV is allocation of information, subjecthood, sympathy and moral weighting;
- close POV does not require all narrator language to collapse into the character’s spoken vocabulary;
- mystery must first define what category of answer it owes: solvable puzzle / bounded operational answer / irrecoverable human mystery / retrospective reinterpretation;
- interior narration should modulate among external narration, free indirect style and direct thought rather than default to one maximum-depth texture.
