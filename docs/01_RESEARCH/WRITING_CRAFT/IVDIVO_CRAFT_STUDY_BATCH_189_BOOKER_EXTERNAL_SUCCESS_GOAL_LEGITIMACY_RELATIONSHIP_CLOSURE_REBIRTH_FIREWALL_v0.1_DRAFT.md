# IVDIVO CRAFT STUDY — BATCH 189
## Christopher Booker — External Success, Goal Legitimacy, Relationship Closure, Surface Rebirth

**Source status:** WIDER / FULL READ IN PROGRESS.  
**Strictly covered here:** *The Seven Basic Plots*, Part Three Ch22–25 only.  
**Do not use this batch to claim the whole source is synthesized.**

## 1. External success is a separate axis from local human resolution
Booker repeatedly contrasts outward achievement with deeper completion. His Jungian explanation is rejected, but the structural distinction survives.

### Gate B189-G01 — Multi-axis outcome
For major payoff/ending scenes track separately:
- objective result;
- relationship / consent state;
- evidence / knowledge state;
- authority / responsibility state;
- agency / decision ownership;
- future option space.

`OBJECTIVE SUCCESS ≠ COMPLETE STORY RESOLUTION`.

A character can win the role, save the station, marry, get promoted, obtain the artifact or solve a technical problem while the book's governing relationship/ethical/evidence problem remains unresolved.

## 2. Goal legitimacy audit
Quest momentum does not legitimate the goal.

### Gate B189-G02 — Goal legitimacy
Before treating a pursued objective as inherently good, ask:
`WHO DEFINED THE GOAL? → WHO BENEFITS? → WHO BEARS COST? → WHO MAY REFUSE? → WHAT SUCCESS CHANGES?`

`GOAL ACHIEVEMENT ≠ GOAL LEGITIMACY`.

Useful for institutions, audits, training systems, contact policy and predictive infrastructure.

## 3. Formal relationship outcome is not relational closure
Booker tends to equate marriage/union with archetypal completion. IVDIVO rejects that metaphysics but retains a useful contrary diagnostic.

### Gate B189-G03 — Relationship terms
`FORMAL LABEL / KISS / REUNION / MARRIAGE / TEAM MEMBERSHIP ≠ TRUST / CONSENT / NEW TERMS`.

A relationship is structurally changed when behavior under pressure demonstrates new boundaries, trust, access, responsibility, disclosure or future expectations.

## 4. Voyage/novum causal payment
Entering a new world is not enough.

### Gate B189-G04 — New-world payment
`NEW SYSTEM / PLACE / TECHNOLOGY → AFFORDANCE / CONSTRAINT → MODEL UPDATE → ACTION / RELATION CHANGE → CONSEQUENCE`.

If the only payoff is wonder or lore, compress or relocate the material unless wonder itself is the explicit reader promise of that passage.

## 5. Recognition as evidence-driven model revision
Booker's Comedy recognition becomes operationally useful when stripped of anima/animus metaphysics.

### Gate B189-G05 — Recognition
`OLD MODEL → DISCRIMINATING EVIDENCE / EXPERIENCE → RECOGNITION → CHANGED TACTIC / RELATIONSHIP TERMS / DECISION`.

Recognition is not a speech explaining theme. It should alter what can happen next.

## 6. Narrative glamour / alignment audit
Reader sympathy is affected by discourse allocation.

### Gate B189-G06 — Alignment cost visibility
Audit who receives:
- interiority;
- humour;
- charisma/beauty;
- explanatory context;
- screen/page time;
- sympathetic reaction shots / POV attention.

Then audit who bears the consequences of that person's actions.

`CHARISMA / FOCALIZATION ≠ MORAL LEGITIMACY`.

This is not a ban on antiheroes. It is a check against accidentally hiding costs because the protagonist is entertaining.

## 7. Surface reset versus durable change
Booker's rebirth discussion is useful only after rejecting mystical completion.

### Gate B189-G07 — Durable change
`AMNESIA / NEW NAME / NEW BODY / RELOCATION / NEW TITLE / NEW PARTNER ≠ CHARACTER CHANGE`.

Change is demonstrated by later pressure:
`MODEL / VALUE / RELATION → PRESSURE → NEW TACTIC / DECISION → CONSEQUENCE`.

Stable values remain allowed. A character may change strategy, relationships, responsibility or evidence model without personality replacement.

## 8. Corpus patterns without author psychoanalysis
Booker's Hardy chapter repeatedly diagnoses the author through recurring story patterns. That move is not evidentially safe.

### Gate B189-G08 — Corpus pattern restraint
`RECURRING TEXTUAL PATTERN → CRAFT TENDENCY HYPOTHESIS → TEST IN CURRENT MANUSCRIPT`.

Never:
`FICTION PATTERN → AUTHOR MOTHER COMPLEX / IMMaturity / PSYCHOLOGICAL FACT`.

## 9. Retrofit ending audit
Historical publication pressure can produce endings that do not follow the established causal state.

### Gate B189-G09 — Ending retrofit
`ENDING → DOES IT FOLLOW FROM ESTABLISHED OPTIONS / RELATIONSHIPS / EVIDENCE?`

If not, diagnose whether the ending is:
- market/template retrofit;
- thematic wish;
- sequel setup;
- convenience;
- genuine but insufficiently planted late discovery.

## 10. Coincidence stacking
### Gate B189-G10
`COINCIDENCE MAY COMPLICATE; IT MUST NOT REPEATEDLY SOLVE OR FORCE THE REQUIRED END STATE`.

One coincidence may open a problem. Repeated coincidences steering the protagonist toward the only desired ending signal authorial coercion.

## 11. Major rejected mechanisms / failure modes
- ARCHETYPAL ESSENCE AS PSYCHOLOGY
- HETEROSEXUAL UNION = WHOLENESS
- MASCULINE/FEMININE ESSENCE
- NONHUMAN BODY = MONSTER
- BIOGRAPHY-AS-TEXTUAL-PROOF
- CULTURE/NATION PSYCHOANALYSIS FROM STORIES
- OUTWARD SUCCESS = AUTOMATIC SHALLOWNESS
- NO TRANSFORMATION = FAILED CHARACTER
- SENTIMENTALITY DIAGNOSED ONLY BY ENDING TYPE
- QUEST GOAL ASSUMED MORALLY VALID
- FORMAL PAIRING = RELATIONSHIP CLOSURE
- SURFACE RESET = REBIRTH

## 12. Cross-source synthesis
- **Aristotle:** unity/causality outrank pattern labels.
- **Card/Corbett/McKee Character:** pressured choices and relationship-specific behavior outrank archetypal essence.
- **Oatley/Herman/Zunshine:** behavior supports motive models, not motive truth.
- **Genette/Chatman:** discourse alignment can influence reader judgment without changing story facts.
- **Hayes/Baxter/Kempton:** relationship state is demonstrated through boundaries, tactics and changed terms, not merely labels.
- **le Carré/Ephron:** objective legitimacy and suspicion require evidence/source lineage, not protagonist framing.
- **Weir/Skyward/DCC/Murderbot:** competence, personhood and moral status remain separate axes.

## 13. IVDIVO production summary
Retain:
- multi-axis endings;
- goal legitimacy;
- formal status versus relationship state;
- new-world causal payment;
- recognition as model revision;
- discourse glamour audit;
- durable change test;
- corpus-pattern restraint;
- retrofit ending audit;
- coincidence-stack audit.

Reject Booker's metaphysical/Jungian causal explanations unless independently supported by appropriate evidence.
