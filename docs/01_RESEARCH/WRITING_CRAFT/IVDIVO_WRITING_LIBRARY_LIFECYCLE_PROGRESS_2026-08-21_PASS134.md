## 5. Major new mechanisms retained

### A. Generation / craft alternation

`GENERATE LIVE MATERIAL → OBSERVE ACTUAL EFFECT → APPLY RELEVANT TOOL → REPAIR → RETEST`.

Hodgins explicitly warns that recently learned technique can paralyze generation if consciously held during every move.

### B. Narrative mode router

`EXPOSITION / NARRATIVE SUMMARY / PARTIAL SCENE / FULL SCENE`.

Reader-time is assigned by function, not by a blanket “show everything” rule.

### C. Direct anti-choppiness evidence

Hodgins directly identifies repeated short simple subject–verb sentences as jerky prose and recommends:
- joining related sentences;
- subordination;
- transitions;
- logical paragraph order;
- varied sentence lengths/patterns;
- rhythm matched to effect.

This is strong direct-source support for IVDIVO's anti-`обрубки` router.

### D. Social setting

Setting includes physical place, historical time, peer pressure, community standards and family values.

### E. Character precious particle

Selective revealing detail/action is stronger than catalogue or quirk accumulation.

### F. State-change middle

Progression depends on a changed position/model/relationship/obligation, not merely more obstacles.

### G. Structure as reader journey

Information order and POV alter reader interpretation; unusual structure must arise from material and pay for its complexity.

### H. Flashback present-dependency

Backstory must change present understanding, relation, evidence or decision; otherwise compress or remove it.

### I. Revision as re-seeing

Foundational POV/voice/staging defects may require a fresh imaginative reconstruction rather than pronoun swaps or local patching.

### J. Feedback effect vs repair command

All reader comments deserve consideration; proposed solutions remain hypotheses.
