## 1. HIGH-VALUE EARLY MECHANISM — ROMANCE IS A CAUSAL ARC
Romance cannot be treated as decorative chemistry layered over an unrelated plot.

IVDIVO provisional form:
`EXTERNAL EVENT / CHOICE → CHANGES TRUST / DESIRE / BOUNDARY → RELATIONSHIP STATE CHANGES → THAT CHANGE ALTERS NEXT EXTERNAL CHOICE`.

If the romance can be removed without changing external decisions, or the mystery/action plot can be removed without changing the relationship arc, the braid may be too weak for a romance-forward product.

## 2. TWO COMPLETE AGENTS
Hayes frames romance around two protagonists. IVDIVO adapts this without requiring exact equal page time:

`AGENT A WANT + BOUNDARY + MODEL → PRESSURE FROM AGENT B / WORLD → CHOICE`
`AGENT B WANT + BOUNDARY + MODEL → PRESSURE FROM AGENT A / WORLD → CHOICE`.

Neither person is the missing half, therapy device or prize of the other.

### Gate B106-G01 — MUTUAL CAUSALITY
At major relationship turns ask:
- What did A choose?
- What did B choose?
- What did each choice cost?
- How did each person's agency change the other's option set?

## 3. LEGITIMATE CONFLICTING EXTERNAL GOALS
A useful Hayes mechanism is conflict between two reasonable external goals.

IVDIVO:
`MODEL/GOAL A has real benefit + cost`
`MODEL/GOAL B has real benefit + cost`
`relationship attraction does not erase disagreement`.

This is preferable to manufacturing a villain inside the couple whenever structural pressure is needed.

## 4. RELATIONSHIP STATE LEDGER
For romance-bearing projects track independently:
- attraction/interest;
- trust;
- safety;
- respect;
- permitted vulnerability;
- consent/boundary clarity;
- shared knowledge;
- public/private alignment;
- future expectation;
- commitment.

A chemistry beat that changes none of these may be pleasant texture rather than progression.

## 5. PRODUCT WEIGHT BEFORE BEAT DENSITY
IVDIVO's existing Romance Weight Dial outranks any one romance formula.

### MYSTERY-PRIMARY
Romance = causal subplot.
Required principle: relationship beats alter trust/choice but mystery/case owns final proof and primary resolution.

### BALANCED
Romance + external plot co-drive.
Major turns should often alter both external state and relationship state.

### ROMANCE-PRIMARY
Relationship resolution is the main promise. Hayes's denser relationship progression becomes more directly diagnostic.

### Gate B106-G02 — PROMISE MATCH
Marketing promise and romance density must match the declared weight.

## 6. WOUND / FLAW FIREWALL
Hayes often frames romantic inability through wound/fear/misconception.

IVDIVO does **not** require trauma or brokenness.

Relationship resistance may arise from:
- incompatible current goals;
- timing;
- social obligations;
- privacy;
- consent boundaries;
- prior evidence about reliability;
- different models of responsibility;
- family/community expectations;
- fear without trauma;
- simple preference or uncertainty.

### Gate B106-G03 — NO THERAPY RETROFIT
Do not invent childhood damage merely to justify a romance beat.

## 7. EQUAL AGENCY != EQUAL SCREEN TIME
For romance-primary, both agents require real causal ownership. That does not automatically require equal POV count, chapter count, secrets or exposition.

`RELATIONAL EQUALITY / PERSONHOOD != NARRATIVE SYMMETRY`.

## 8. ROMANCE + MYSTERY
For romantic mystery:
`CLUE / REVELATION → changes what one person believes about the other → alters trust/boundary/action → new investigative possibility or cost`.

Avoid two independent tracks:
`mystery scene` followed by unrelated `romance scene` forever.

## 9. ROOM 917 APPLICATION
Use existing weight declaration rather than forcing full Hayes architecture.

For ROOM 917 as MYSTERY-PRIMARY:
- Elena/Julian chemistry must alter trust and evidence access;
- clues about Cate/Room 917 should change Elena's model of Julian;
- Julian's choices about truth/boundary should change investigative possibilities;
- case resolution remains structurally primary;
- romantic payoff must not substitute for proof.

## 10. ORBITAL YOUTH APPLICATION
Romantic lines in youth SF should grow out of future-normal life rather than Earth-2020s consumer scripts.

Possible stakes:
- trust;
- privacy;
- consent;
- belonging;
- time;
- future role/location;
- collaboration;
- family/community expectation;
- unique experience;
- reputation after a public choice.

No automatic salary/rent/credit conflict.

## 11. CONSCIOUS ROBOT / NONHUMAN RELATIONSHIP FIREWALL
If a conscious robot/nonhuman enters a romantic or intimate relational arc, do not use them as exotic emotional service.

Required:
`OWN WANT + OWN BOUNDARY + OWN RELATIONSHIPS + CONSENT/REFUSAL + OWN FUTURE OPTIONS`.
