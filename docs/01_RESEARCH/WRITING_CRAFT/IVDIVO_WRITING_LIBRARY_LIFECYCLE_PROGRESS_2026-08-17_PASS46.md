## IMPORTANT NEW EXTRACTIONS
1. Theme is stronger as a question tested by choices/consequences than as a stated moral.
2. Counterargument must be represented competently; thematic straw men weaken the book.
3. Character connection has multiple routes: recognition/empathy, fascination, mystery, competence, relationship and moral/tactical uncertainty.
4. Iglesias's instant-empathy devices are high-risk manipulation tools; victimization/injury/children/animals/sacrifice must have independent causal function.
5. Anticipation is best understood as future-state modeling generated by goals, plans, warnings, promises, rules and dramatic irony.
6. Act/sequence/scene/beat questions are useful diagnostics but become harmful as question-monoculture.
7. Red herrings and surprises require retrospective fairness.
8. Setup/payoff can involve skill, relationship, rule, habit, information or environment — not only objects.
9. Emotional design needs modulation; permanent maximum intensity is self-defeating.
10. Iglesias's conventional three-act stations are decomposed into functions and are not mandatory IVDIVO architecture.
