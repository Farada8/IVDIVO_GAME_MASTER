## Science firewall — Pass 170
Kaufman/Gregoire (2015) is retained as a creative-process synthesis, not a frozen neuroscience authority.

Later research supports some broad directions while adding important moderation:
- openness remains positively related to divergent/creative performance;
- creativity involves coordination between spontaneous/default and executive-control processes;
- mindfulness can benefit some creativity measures, but task and intervention type matter;
- mind wandering/incubation effects are context-dependent and not universally positive.

No popular-neuroscience claim becomes IVDIVO psychology or ontology without current evidence.
