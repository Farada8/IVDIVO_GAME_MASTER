## Structure map and principal claims
### Ch1 — Fiction as model / simulation
Fiction constructs selective models rather than copying reality. Readers actively complete causal, social and emotional relations from limited textual cues.

**Retain:** `WORLD DATABASE → SELECTED STORY MODEL → POV ACCESS → READER INFERENCE`.

### Ch2 — The space-in-between / play
Fictional engagement uses a shared frame in which invented conditions can be treated as provisionally true for action and inference.

**Retain:** story/world contract and experimental possibility.  
**Reject as science/ontology:** psychoanalytic/transitional-space speculation as established mechanism of mind.

### Ch3 — Creativity
Creative work grows through variation, association, recombination, analogy and social/distributed processes.

**Retain:** `SEED → MULTIPLE MATERIAL VARIANTS → CHARACTER/WORLD/CAUSAL TEST → KEEP / BANK / DISCARD`.

### Ch4 — Character, action, incident
Readers infer persons, motives and traits from behavior, context, relationships and reports; these models may need revision.

**Retain:** `OBSERVED BEHAVIOR → MULTIPLE MOTIVE MODELS → NEW EVIDENCE → UPDATE`.

### Ch5 — Emotions
Emotions are strongly related to concerns/goals, appraisals and changing prospects of plans. Character emotion and reader emotion are related but not identical.

**Retain:** `CONCERN / GOAL → PLAN → ACTION → WORLD RESPONSE → RESULT → APPRAISAL / EMOTION → NEXT PLAN`.

### Ch6 — Writing fiction
Ideas, plot, character, POV and imagery interact iteratively; drafting can discover a stronger form than planning predicted.

**Retain:** `PLAN = HYPOTHESIS`; `LIVE DRAFT → OBSERVE CHARACTER/STORY EVIDENCE → REPLAN`.

### Ch7 — Effects of fiction
Oatley reviews evidence and hypotheses about fiction, empathy and social understanding.

**Science firewall:** use as historical 2011 synthesis only. Current meta-analytic literature supports at most small, heterogeneous effects; do not make moral or clinical guarantees from fiction exposure.

### Ch8 — Talking about fiction
Meaning is also socially reconstructed through discussion; readers can form different text-constrained interpretations.

**Retain:** reader reports are evidence about reader models/effects, not automatic repair instructions or canon.

## Mechanism bank
1. **Simulation Model Gate** — `WORLD DATABASE → SELECTED STORY MODEL → POV ACCESS → READER INFERENCE`.
2. **Goal-Adoption Gate** — reader attachment can arise from a legible goal/plan/concern without compulsory likability or trauma.
3. **Emotion Causality Gate** — `CONCERN → EVENT → APPRAISAL → EMOTION → STRATEGY/CHOICE`.
4. **Behavior–Motive Separation** — behavior generates motive hypotheses, not motive facts.
5. **Character Model Update** — meaningful new behavior should reinforce, complicate or revise the reader's model.
6. **Reader-Effect Nonidentity** — `CHARACTER FEELS X ≠ READER FEELS X`.
7. **Interpretive Plurality / Load-Bearing Facts** — theme and motive may remain contestable while physical/evidence facts stay stable.
8. **Variant-as-Laboratory** — creative variants/exercises are experiments, not canon.
9. **Identification Without Humanization** — a reader can adopt a nonhuman person's goal/plan without that person becoming human-like.
10. **Prediction–Reason–Consent Firewall** — `PREDICTED BEHAVIOR ≠ REASON ≠ CONSENT`.
11. **Feedback-as-Model-Data** — beta response reports what reader model/effect formed; proposed repair remains a hypothesis.
12. **Reader-Model Continuity** — excessive discourse fragmentation can repeatedly reset the mental model; paragraph/topic continuity is a craft variable before sentence length.

## Cross-source comparison
- **Herman:** strengthens reasons-for-action and person-level/ecological context; prevents Oatley-style behavior modeling from reducing a person to predicted outputs.
- **Zunshine:** adds source monitoring/metarepresentation; motive inference must retain provenance and epistemic status.
- **Storr:** overlaps on prediction/model-building; Storr's stronger universals about control/status/sacred flaw remain rejected.
- **Card / Corbett / McKee Character:** align on action under pressure and relationship-specific self; Oatley contributes goal adoption and reader emotion around concerns.
- **LaPlante / Lamott / Bell:** converge on discovery before over-control and on replanning after live material appears.
- **Genette / Chatman:** provide stronger order/duration/frequency/focalization diagnostics for how the model reaches the reader.
- **Ephron / le Carré / Chudnoff:** impose proof/source-lineage discipline on Oatley-style mind inference. Reader simulation never substitutes for evidence.
- **Collins / Gillett:** useful on goal/action/emotion, but personal emotion memory is not required for authentic fiction.

## Science firewall — 2026 status
Contemporary meta-analytic/review work supports a **small and conditional**, not universal, relationship between fiction reading and social-cognitive outcomes. Therefore Oatley's broad claims about empathy/social understanding are not production guarantees and are not IVDIVO psychology canon.

Standing rule:
`NARRATIVE-THEORY MECHANISM ≠ CURRENT SCIENCE FACT ≠ IVDIVO ONTOLOGY`.
