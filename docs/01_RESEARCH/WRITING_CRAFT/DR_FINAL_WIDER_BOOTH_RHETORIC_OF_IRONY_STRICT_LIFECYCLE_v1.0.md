## Core claims retained
1. Stable irony is not mere ambiguity: the reader rejects an inadequate surface meaning and reconstructs a bounded alternative.
2. Reconstruction depends on a **clue ecology**, not one "tell": contradiction with known fact, internal inconsistency, style clash, belief clash, genre/context, explicit warning, and broader coherence.
3. Interpretation needs a **stopping rule**. Once an ironic reading begins, readers can otherwise invert indefinitely and over-ironize the work.
4. Irony requires enough shared ground for reconstruction. Obscure context can make a technically intended irony practically unreadable.
5. Stable and unstable irony require different reader contracts. Some passages promise a recoverable floor; others deliberately resist final stabilization.
6. Rhetorical effect and philosophical truth are different. Powerful nihilistic, religious, mystical, or political literature does not thereby prove its worldview.
7. Reader misreading is not automatically an author defect; clue availability, reader preparation, attention, prejudice, genre knowledge, and context all matter.
8. Irony can create intimacy/community between author, narrator, character, and reader; it is not merely mockery.

## Mechanism bank

### M1 — Irony Reconstruction
`SURFACE STATEMENT → IRONY CANDIDATE → CLUE SET → ALTERNATIVES → RECONSTRUCTED MEANING → STOP`

### M2 — Four-step stable-irony audit
1. Surface meaning becomes inadequate/incongruous.
2. Reader considers plausible alternatives.
3. Reader infers the speaker/narrator's likely knowledge or stance from available textual/contextual evidence.
4. Reader reconstructs a meaning that better fits the work's local and global coherence.

IVDIVO correction: do not rely on secret author biography; privilege textually recoverable discourse evidence.

### M3 — Clue Ecology
`DIRECT WARNING / KNOWN ERROR / INTERNAL CONFLICT / STYLE CLASH / BELIEF CLASH / GENRE-CONTEXT → CONVERGENCE`

No single eyebrow, pause, slang marker, or sarcastic word should carry a load-bearing ironic inference alone.

### M4 — Stable/Unstable Router
`BOUNDED SHARED FLOOR? → STABLE`
`DELIBERATE HIGHER-LEVEL UNRESOLUTION? → UNSTABLE`

Before "clarifying ambiguity," determine whether the reader is confused about the question/evidence or intentionally uncertain about the answer.

### M5 — Over-Ironizing Stop Test
`IRONIC READING → DOES IT EXPLAIN MORE OF THE ACTUAL WORK? → PRESERVE CHARACTER/CAUSALITY/GENRE? → IMPROVE WHOLE-WORK COHERENCE?`

If hidden irony only creates clever isolated fragments and makes the whole work less coherent, reject it.

### M6 — Meaning vs Significance
Local recoverable rhetorical meaning is not identical to every later historical, political, psychological, or personal significance a reader may attach.

### M7 — Ironic Portrait
`OBSERVED SPEECH/BEHAVIOR → SELF-MODEL / OTHER-MODEL HYPOTHESIS → FURTHER EVIDENCE`

A character can unintentionally reveal a gap between self-description and behavior, but the reader's inference remains a model, not automatic motive fact.

## Cross-source comparison
- **Baxter / Collins / Kempton:** subtext and communicative tactics; Booth adds reconstruction criteria and stopping rules.
- **Zunshine / Herman:** mind-model inference; Booth strengthens `interpretation ≠ motive`.
- **Genette / Chatman:** discourse placement/context controls reader model; Booth specifies one rhetorical mode of that inference.
- **Ephron / le Carré:** fair inference must remain grounded in true traces/source logic; sarcasm cannot falsify evidence.
- **Williams / Zinsser / Le Guin / Hodgins:** paragraph and sentence continuity preserve the contextual field required to reconstruct irony; fragmentation can destroy that field.
- **Aristotle:** function/coherence outrank abstract rule.
- **Edward James / genre history:** genre expectations are reader priors, not universal story laws.

## IVDIVO operationalization

### General Irony Gate
`SURFACE → REQUIRED CONTEXT → CLUE ECOLOGY → INTENDED RELATIONAL ACTION → LISTENER/READER MODEL → STABLE OR UNSTABLE? → EVIDENCE IMPACT`

Evidence impact is normally **NONE** unless independent trace/source logic changes.

### Productive Uncertainty
`CLEAR QUESTION + MULTIPLE PLAUSIBLE INTERPRETATIONS = PRODUCTIVE`
`UNCLEAR QUESTION / SOURCE / EVIDENCE STATUS = CONFUSION`

### Future-world YA
Load-bearing irony must be reconstructable from lived future norms and relationships. Do not depend on current Earth slang as the only clue. Character humor fingerprints must remain distinct.

### Conscious robots / nonhuman persons
Human sarcasm, system labels, pauses, compliance, or ironic descriptions reveal human models. They do not prove nonhuman motive or ontology.
`OWN WANT → OWN MODEL/EVIDENCE → OWN RELATIONSHIPS → CONSENT/REFUSAL → CHOICE → CONSEQUENCE`

### Consciousness schools
Literary irony, mystical experience, paradox, or philosophical elegance cannot raise a school interpretation to ontology.
`OBSERVATION → REPRODUCIBLE EFFECT → SCHOOL INTERPRETATION → SOURCE LINEAGE → COMPETING INTERPRETATION → INDEPENDENT TEST → ONTOLOGY CEILING`

## Final synthesis
Booth's strongest contribution to IVDIVO is not "write more irony." It is a disciplined **reader-inference architecture**: irony must be reconstructable from a sufficient ecology of cues, must know when to stop, must distinguish bounded ambiguity from unbounded instability, and must never be promoted into evidence about motive, consent, personhood, or ontology merely because the rhetorical effect is powerful.
