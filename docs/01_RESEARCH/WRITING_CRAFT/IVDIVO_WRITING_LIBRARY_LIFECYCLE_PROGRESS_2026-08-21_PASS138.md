## Main mechanisms added

### Reader-state continuity

`STATE LEFT BY SENTENCE A → WHAT SENTENCE B ASSUMES → MATCH / MISMATCH → REORDER / SIGNAL / MERGE / REWRITE`.

### Topic-chain coherence

Paragraph choppiness frequently comes from sentence-topic resets rather than raw sentence length.

### Old→new information movement

Orient with active/familiar information and deliberately place important new information where it can receive stress. Flexible principle, not rigid template.

### Agency visibility

`WHO ACTS? → WHO DECIDES? → WHO MAY REFUSE? → WHO BEARS COST?`

Passives/nominalizations are allowed but cannot accidentally hide responsibility or consent.

### Concision

`COMPRESSION ≠ CHOPPING`.

Remove repeated meaning and empty framing; do not turn coherent thought into fragments.

### Technical language

Noun stacks and prestige jargon must be unpacked when readers cannot recover actor/action/relationship. Particularly relevant to post-money institutional terms.
