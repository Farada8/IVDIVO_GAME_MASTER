## IVDIVO application

### ONE MAIN STORY
Emotional variation is story-relevant when it changes tactic, trust/relationship terms, consent, evidence handling, authority/access, commitment, responsibility, strategy/model, or future option space.

### YA
Allow partial, uneven, tactical, relational, negative, stable-under-test, and series-deferred growth. Do not force wound→lesson→healing.

### Mystery
`EMOTIONAL CUE → POV INTERPRETATION → ALTERNATIVE MODEL → CORROBORATION`.

### Conscious robots / nonhuman persons
`OWN PRIORITIES + EMBODIMENT + RELATIONSHIP + KNOWLEDGE LIMIT → TACTIC → CONSENT/REFUSAL → CONSEQUENCE`.

### Prose / choppiness
Do not render each emotional component as a separate microgesture or one-line paragraph. Preserve paragraph-level perceptual/decision unity before sentence-level punch.
