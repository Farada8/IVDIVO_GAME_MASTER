# IVDIVO CRAFT STUDY BATCH 183
## Wayne C. Booth — Irony Reconstruction · Reader Contract · Inference · Stopping Rules

**Source:** Wayne C. Booth, *A Rhetoric of Irony* (1974)  
**Source status:** `STRICT_LIFECYCLE_SYNTHESIZED_v1.0`  
**Authority class:** literary rhetoric / reader inference; historical, not modern psychology or science.

## Production mechanisms

### Gate B183-01 — Surface/Reconstruction Split
Before calling a line ironic, record:
`SURFACE MEANING → WHY SURFACE FAILS/UNDERPERFORMS → ALTERNATIVE → SUPPORTING CUES`.

### Gate B183-02 — Clue Ecology
Load-bearing irony requires more than one fragile cue. Check factual/contextual conflict, style, relationship history, genre prior, known belief, and local consequence.

### Gate B183-03 — Stopping Rule
Once the best-supported reconstruction explains the local passage and preserves global coherence, stop. Do not invert every later statement because "maybe it is ironic."

### Gate B183-04 — Stable/Unstable Router
`RECOVERABLE FLOOR → STABLE`
`DELIBERATE HIGHER-LEVEL OPENNESS → UNSTABLE`
Do not repair intentional instability as if it were mere confusion.

### Gate B183-05 — Productive Uncertainty
Reader may be uncertain about the answer while remaining clear about question, source and evidence status.

### Gate B183-06 — Shared-Ground Budget
If irony requires niche knowledge, supply enough lived context before making it load-bearing.

### Gate B183-07 — Character Humor Ownership
A joke or ironic stance must arise from character-specific attention, relationship, values and tactic. Do not give the ensemble one authorial snark voice.

### Gate B183-08 — Irony ≠ Evidence
`TONE / PAUSE / SARCASM / IRONY → STANCE OR MOTIVE HYPOTHESIS`.
No upgrade to guilt, motive, consent, personhood or world fact without independent evidence.

### Gate B183-09 — Reader Misread Diagnosis
One misread is not automatically a text defect.
`READER EFFECT → DID NECESSARY CUES EXIST? → WAS REQUIRED CONTEXT FAIR? → REPEATABILITY → REPAIR HYPOTHESES`.

### Gate B183-10 — Context Span / Anti-Choppiness
Do not fragment contextual cues into isolated one-line paragraphs if the reader must perceive their relationship to reconstruct irony.
`BEAT MAP ≠ SENTENCE BREAK`.

### Gate B183-11 — Philosophy Firewall
Powerful literary portrayal does not prove metaphysics, psychology, politics or ontology.

### Gate B183-12 — Intentionalism Firewall
Use `TEXTUALLY RECOVERABLE EFFECT`, not inaccessible author biography, as production authority.

### Gate B183-13 — Unstable Irony Cannot Repair Canon
Interpretive openness cannot excuse hard contradictions in established world facts, timestamps, source lineage or continuity.

### Gate B183-14 — Meaning/Significance Split
Do not confuse a line's immediate dramatic/rhetorical job with every broader theme later associated with it.

## Failure modes
1. **SINGLE-TELL IRONY** — one smirk/word must carry the whole inference.
2. **IRONY EVERYWHERE** — once a reader suspects irony, every statement becomes reversible.
3. **AUTHORIAL SNARK MONOCULTURE** — every character shares one ironic register.
4. **SARCASM-AS-EVIDENCE** — tone is treated as proof.
5. **UNSTABLE-IRONY PATCH** — ambiguity is used to excuse continuity mistakes.
6. **CONTEXT STARVATION** — load-bearing irony depends on assumptions the book never supplied.
7. **EARTH-SLANG LEAK** — future YA irony depends entirely on present-day slang.
8. **FRAGMENTED CLUE FIELD** — excessive one-line paragraphing destroys relations among cues.
9. **PHILOSOPHY-BY-AESTHETIC-POWER** — moving fiction is treated as evidence its worldview is true.
10. **MISREAD=DEFECT SHORTCUT** — any reader misunderstanding triggers rewrite without diagnostic evidence.

## Cross-source synthesis
Booth supplies the **stopping rule** missing from many subtext systems. Baxter/Collins/Kempton explain how indirect communication operates; Booth explains when a reader is justified in reconstructing an indirect meaning and when that reconstruction has become overreading. Genette/Chatman explain information architecture; Booth adds rhetorical cue relations. Ephron/le Carré add evidentiary fairness: ironic interpretation may change a suspect model, but not source lineage or trace truth.

## Standing prose implication
`ACTION / SUSPENSE / IRONY / SUBTEXT ≠ SHORT-SENTENCE DENSITY`.

Irony often needs a contextual field. Chopping every cue into separate one-line paragraphs can make a passage less interpretable, not more intense.
