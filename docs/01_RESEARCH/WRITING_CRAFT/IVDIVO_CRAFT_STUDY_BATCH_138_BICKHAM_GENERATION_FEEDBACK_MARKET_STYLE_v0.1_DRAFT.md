## Mechanism 1 — Protect generation from premature judgment
Bickham's useful craft distinction is not his dated left/right-brain explanation but the workflow distinction between making material and judging material.

Production translation:

`GENERATE / DISCOVER → IDENTIFY LIVE MATERIAL → DIAGNOSE → REVISE → POLISH`

A global verdict such as “this whole scene is dumb” is not a diagnosis. A useful revision note identifies a defect layer: causality, character motive, POV/evidence, scene function, paragraph continuity, rhythm, or wording.

### Gate B138-G01 — Critic Timing
Do not run full editorial control on every sentence during discovery. Move hard validation after a scene or working block exists.

## Mechanism 2 — Generation privacy vs later ethics
Section 28 argues against frightened self-censorship caused by imagined family or social judgment. The useful version is not “anything goes forever.” It is phase separation:

`PRIVATE DISCOVERY SPACE → FIND TRUE MATERIAL → LATER ETHICS / CANON / PERSONHOOD / PRIVACY PASS`

### Gate B138-G02 — Premature Audience Censorship
Do not delete a potentially truthful fictional possibility merely because an imagined critic may dislike it. Later revision still checks consent, privacy, stereotyping, harm, canon, and story necessity.

## Mechanism 3 — Emotional access is material, not proof
Bickham pushes writers to confront strong feeling rather than cool it into abstraction. The useful core is character-specific emotional access.

But his developmental psychology is dated craft rhetoric, not accepted science, and his “write on the edge of melodrama” advice is not a universal production law.

`CHARACTER FEELS X ≠ X IS TRUE`

Emotion may alter attention, tactics, relationship state and reader effect. It does not prove motive, causal explanation, guilt, personhood, or ontology.

### Gate B138-G03 — Emotion/Evidence Firewall
Never upgrade emotional certainty to evidence status.

## Mechanism 4 — Feedback must be decomposed
Bickham is too dismissive of amateur critique and too deferential to professional authority. The stronger cross-source synthesis is:

`READER REPORT → OBSERVED EFFECT → REPEATABILITY → DEFECT LEVEL → REPAIR HYPOTHESES → TEST → KEEP / REJECT`

A reader is authoritative about their own effect (“I was confused here”). Their proposed repair is a hypothesis. Professional craft advice deserves higher prior weight when domain fit is strong, but it is still tested against the manuscript.

### Gate B138-G04 — Feedback Provenance
Record who gave feedback, what they actually experienced, whether independent readers reproduce it, and whether the proposed repair fixes the effect without damaging other systems.

### Gate B138-G05 — Advice Domain Fit
Do not let prestige substitute for relevance. A romance editor, mystery specialist, audio director, line editor and beta reader answer different questions.

## Mechanism 5 — Market awareness is not story authority
Bickham's concrete 1992 submission and market examples are historically specific. The stable craft principle is narrower:

`MARKET SIGNAL → POSITIONING CONSTRAINT / OPPORTUNITY`, not `MARKET SIGNAL → STORY CANON`.

A trend may inform packaging or premise selection. It does not justify breaking ONE MAIN STORY, personhood, established canon, or a character's causal truth.

### Gate B138-G06 — Trend Independence
Before adopting a market-driven idea, ask whether the story remains worth writing if the trend disappears.

## Mechanism 6 — Style posture can occur at both extremes
Bickham usefully identifies two forms of authorial costume: ornamental “fine writing” and performative toughness/cynicism. His gendered examples are dated and rejected.

Production translation:

`INTENDED EFFECT → POV/CHARACTER FUNCTION → LANGUAGE/RHYTHM`

not

`DESIRED AUTHOR IMAGE → borrowed literary costume`.

### Gate B138-G07 — Purple-to-Choppy Overcorrection
When removing ornamental prose, do not replace it with faux-tough fragments, one-line paragraph confetti, or constant hard-boiled cadence.

### Gate B138-G08 — Voice Ownership
Ask whether a sentence belongs to this perceiver, relationship and moment. If the same “cool” line could be assigned to every major character, it is probably author posture.

## Cross-source comparison
- Lamott / LaPlante / Bell / Swain converge with Bickham on separating discovery from hard revision.
- Francine Prose / Le Guin / Lodge / Zinsser reject any equation between force and sentence shortness; this limits Bickham's anti-purple rhetoric.
- Rosenfeld / Oatley / Herman support emotion as a causal and reader-simulation variable, not proof of truth.
- Zunshine / le Carré require provenance and source independence; feedback and expert statements should be handled with analogous source discipline.

## Anti-choppy consequence
The repair order remains macro-to-micro:

`BOOK ENGINE → CHAPTER FUNCTION → SCENE NECESSITY → SCENE WEIGHT/MODE → POV/EVIDENCE → PARAGRAPH INTENT → READER-STATE CONTINUITY → SENTENCE RHYTHM → WORDING`

Do not “fix” ornate prose by mechanically chopping it into short sentences. Preserve functional syntactic range.
