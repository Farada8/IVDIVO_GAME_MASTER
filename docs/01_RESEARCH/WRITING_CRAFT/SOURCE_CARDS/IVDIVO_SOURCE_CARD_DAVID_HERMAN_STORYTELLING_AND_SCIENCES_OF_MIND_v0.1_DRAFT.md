## High-value Book Two application
Lattice can be highly accurate at predicting accommodation while still being unjustified in treating predicted cooperation as available consent/resource. This gives the novel a sharper non-villain technological conflict:

`prediction accuracy ≠ person-level understanding ≠ moral authorization`.

## Limits / cautions
- Cognitive narratology is an analytic framework, not a direct recipe for commercial plot construction.
- Herman explicitly warns against overextending narrative explanation to all aspects of persons/selfhood.
- Person-level analysis should not become an excuse for vague anti-technical romanticism.
- Claims about mind/science require stronger evidentiary vetting before being presented as empirical fact outside craft use.
