## Highest-value mechanisms

1. Storyteller/artifice balance: generation and diagnosis must alternate rather than compete.
2. Seed connection: story often ignites when separate materials form a new relation.
3. Narrative-mode routing: exposition, narrative summary, full scene and partial scene are legitimate tools.
4. Paragraph coherence: short/simple syntax becomes jerky when monotonous; join/subordinate/transition when the thought requires it.
5. Setting as physical + social system.
6. Selective “precious particle” characterization rather than catalogue/quirk accumulation.
7. Dialogue as tactic, relationship and concealment/revelation work.
8. Plot as causal changed position, not mandatory war.
9. Structure as reader-information architecture; form must arise from material.
10. Flashback present-dependency.
11. POV as authority/access/distance, not pronoun choice.
12. Emergent “driftwood” symbols rather than imported thematic props.
13. Revision as re-seeing at the correct defect level.
14. Reader feedback reports effects; repair remains a project decision.
