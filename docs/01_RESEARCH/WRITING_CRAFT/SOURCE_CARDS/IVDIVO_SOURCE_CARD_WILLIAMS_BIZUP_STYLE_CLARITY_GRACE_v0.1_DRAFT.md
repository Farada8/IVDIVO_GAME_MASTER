## Source passport

**Primary problem addressed:** how sentence and passage design affects reader comprehension and emphasis, and how revision can become informed choice rather than rule obedience.  
**School/tradition:** rhetoric / reader-oriented prose style / pedagogical revision.  
**Strongest IVDIVO contribution:** actionable sentence/passage diagnostics for `character as subject`, `important action as verb`, old→new information flow, stable topic strings and meaningful sentence stress.  
**Best IVDIVO use:** late-stage clarity/cohesion/emphasis pass after story and scene function are stable; especially useful for diagnosing accidental chopped prose and abstract institutional writing.  
**Weakness if over-applied:** source examples and some global organization guidance are primarily academic/professional prose; direct universalization can flatten fiction voice, free indirect style, suspenseful withholding, intentional fragments, lyric syntax or character-specific speech.  
**Not sufficient for:** fiction rhythm, scene dramaturgy, POV intimacy, dialogue, suspense, mystery or character voice by itself.

## High-value mechanisms currently extracted

1. **Style as informed choice in service of readers**, not obedience to rigid rules.
2. **Draft first, revise after.** Thinking about all style principles while generating can stop discovery.
3. **Main characters as grammatical subjects** often improves clarity because readers can track who/what is acting.
4. **Important actions as verbs** reduces abstract nominalized prose when abstraction is not serving a deliberate purpose.
5. **Old before new** supports local cohesion: familiar material helps readers enter the sentence before new information arrives.
6. **Topic-string coherence:** repeated/related sentence topics create a stable attention track; random subject shifts can make a paragraph feel chopped even when sentence lengths are individually fine.
7. **Stress position:** sentence endings carry disproportionate emphasis; ending repeatedly on weak procedural tails dissipates force.
8. **Clarity is not simplemindedness.** Complex ideas may require complex syntax; the goal is navigability, not permanent shortness.
9. **Elegance can combine simple character/action cores with complex balanced syntax and strong stressed endings.**

## Fiction transfer correction

For fiction, Williams/Bizup is never applied mechanically.

### Keep
- attention continuity;
- action clarity;
- topic progression;
- old→new transitions;
- stress control;
- concision diagnostics.

### Protect against over-editing
- deliberate fragments;
- character voice;
- free indirect discourse;
- suspenseful postponement;
- lyrical/associative syntax;
- paragraph breaks used for impact, time, focus or revelation;
- passive constructions that deliberately keep the important character/object in attentional focus.

Cross-check every fiction application with Le Guin, Francine Prose, Rosenfeld and POV/voice gates.
