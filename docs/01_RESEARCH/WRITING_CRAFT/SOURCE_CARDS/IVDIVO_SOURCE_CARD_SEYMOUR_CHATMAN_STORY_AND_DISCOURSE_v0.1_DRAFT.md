## Strongest current IVDIVO contribution
1. Story vs discourse separation.
2. Kernel vs satellite event hierarchy.
3. Order / duration / frequency as separate pacing controls.
4. Suspense and surprise as complementary.
5. POV and narrative voice as distinct analytic layers.
6. Character traits as reader-inferred patterns rather than plot labels.

## Limits / caution
- Structuralist vocabulary is analytic, not a universal writing formula.
- Kernel/satellite distinction is partly retrospective and interpretation-dependent.
- Satellites can be aesthetically essential despite logical expendability.
- Later narratology refines some of Chatman's categories; use as a foundational framework, not final doctrine.
- Do not equate discourse analysis with reader-response evidence; manuscript validation remains necessary.
