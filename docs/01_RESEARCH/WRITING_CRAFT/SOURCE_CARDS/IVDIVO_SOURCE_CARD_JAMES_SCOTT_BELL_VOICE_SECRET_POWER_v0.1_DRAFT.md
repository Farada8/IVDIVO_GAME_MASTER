## Source passport

**Primary problem addressed:** why technically competent prose can remain generic/forgettable, and how character + author sensibility + page-level craft create a recognizable voice.

**School/tradition:** pragmatic commercial-fiction craft with strong character-filter and style-exercise orientation.

**Strongest contributions for IVDIVO:**
- voice as character-filtered perception rather than decorative wording;
- attitude as a major differentiator of narration;
- voice journal as a diagnostic laboratory;
- variation of cadence/voice according to story need;
- emotion and sensory memory as inputs to voice;
- YA voice as intimacy + attitude rather than slang injection;
- description should be filtered through who notices it and why.

**Weakness if over-applied:**
- CAP can tempt writers into excessive biography/exercise before story causality is secure;
- forced metaphors can make every sentence perform;
- deliberate “freshness” can become authorial showing-off;
- “joy” is useful as generative practice but not a manuscript acceptance criterion;
- age-sixteen life-altering-event prompt is optional, not a universal character law;
- first-person YA examples do not imply first person/present is mandatory for YA.

**Not sufficient for:** macro causality, mystery fair play, social systems, scene architecture, ethics, orbital worldbuilding, or full YA psychology by itself.

## Cross-source positioning

### Bell ↔ Hodgins
Both make voice inseparable from stance/attitude and reader relationship. Hodgins is stronger on tone as a whole-story relation among narrator, character and reader; Bell is stronger on practical drills to generate differentiation.

### Bell ↔ Alicia Rasley
Rasley more precisely separates POV access, narrator attitude and interpretive bias. Bell supplies faster production exercises. Use Rasley to prevent voice from violating POV logic.

### Bell ↔ Rosenfeld
Rosenfeld is stronger on intimacy/distance and interior access. Bell is stronger on lexical/cadential character signature. Voice must not replace scene intention.

### Bell ↔ Williams/Bizup / Provost
Williams/Provost protect clarity, coherence and rhythm. Bell encourages surprise. IVDIVO rule: surprise is accepted only when it preserves clarity, POV, tone and character truth.
