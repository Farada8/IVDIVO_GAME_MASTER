## Source passport — current
Primary problem: make comedy craft diagnosable and repeatable without pretending any tool is universal.

Strongest transferable contributions for IVDIVO:
1. diagnostic repair rather than defensive attachment;
2. audience information contract for humor/reinterpretation;

## Current limits / failure modes
- Standup audience response is not identical to prose reader response.
- Sitcom circularity is not appropriate as a reset model for cumulative IVDIVO novels.
- A laugh is not the only valid function of humor in fiction.
- Joke mechanics cannot override character truth, dramatic seriousness, POV, scene causality or consent stakes.
- Character filter can become caricature if repeated without variation.
- Durable conflict must evolve after consequences; exact repetition becomes regression.
- Single-reader dislike remains weak evidence.
- Tools are heuristics, not mandatory templates.
