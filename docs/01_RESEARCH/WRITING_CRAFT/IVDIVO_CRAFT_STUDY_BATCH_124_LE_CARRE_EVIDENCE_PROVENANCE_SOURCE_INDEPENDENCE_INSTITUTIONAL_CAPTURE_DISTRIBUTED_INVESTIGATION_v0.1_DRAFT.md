## Core synthesis

A sophisticated mystery is not made smarter by adding more clues or more suspicious people. It becomes stronger when the reader can track **where knowledge came from, who had access, which reports are truly independent, what an institution is filtering out, and what evidence would change the current model**.

Working chain:

`WORLD FACT → TRACE / SOURCE → ACCESS POSITION → CLAIM → PROVENANCE → INDEPENDENCE → INTERPRETATION → CORROBORATION / CONTRADICTION → STATUS UPDATE → ACTION`.

## Production gates

### B124-G01 — Evidence Provenance Gate
Every major claim must have an identifiable source or trace. `Someone said X` is not sufficient.

### B124-G02 — Source Independence Gate
Two reports count as independent only if their evidentiary lineage is independent. Repetition through a social/institutional chain is not corroboration.

### B124-G03 — Official Version Firewall
An official report, archive entry, administrative explanation or school doctrine is a sourced claim, not automatic world fact.

### B124-G04 — Source Laundering Gate
Track when one original source is paraphrased, reprinted, quoted or socially repeated until it appears to be multiple sources.

### B124-G05 — Compartmentalized Competence Gate
Before making a capable character irrational, ask whether missing access, compartmentalization or role-specific information explains the error.

### B124-G06 — Negative Evidence Expectation Gate
Absence/silence becomes evidence only after the narrative has established what evidence or behaviour should normally be present.

### B124-G07 — Relationship / Evidence Separation
Attraction, trust, betrayal, jealousy, affection and history can explain access or interpretation. They cannot raise a factual claim's evidence status by themselves.

### B124-G08 — Memory Evidence Status
Memory is testimony with time, emotional context and distortion risk. Store it separately from independently recorded traces.

### B124-G09 — Distributed Investigation Gate
A climax should not require the protagonist to own all observation, interpretation, technical work and decision authority. Preserve unique contributions from multiple agents.

### B124-G10 — Proof Ceiling Gate
Define before drafting what the book can actually prove. Do not let the finale upgrade local operational evidence into global ontology.

### B124-G11 — Fair Retrospective Support
After the reveal, the reader should be able to re-evaluate earlier details and see why the final model fits better without requiring hidden author-only facts.

### B124-G12 — Information-State Scene Gate
Interview/meeting/archive scenes receive full dramatic time only if they change evidence, relationship, interpretation, authority or next tactic.

### B124-G13 — Repetition-With-New-Status Gate
A recurring name, file, object or event should return with changed meaning/evidence status. Pure reminder repetition is compressed.

### B124-G14 — Policy Predictability / Adversary Gate
A rational, stable policy can become exploitable if an adversary can predict the institution's response. Test policy robustness against modelled behaviour.

### B124-G15 — Institutional Countermodel Gate
Institutions should have real benefits, competent defenders and a plausible internal model. Avoid turning information-architecture failure into cartoon villainy.

### B124-G16 — Translation Style Firewall
The retained Russian translation may support narrative mechanics but not claims about le Carré's exact English cadence, syntax or punctuation.

## Failure modes

- Source Laundering
- Claim Count = Independence Fallacy
- Official Model Lock-In
- Compartmentalization Blindness
- Relationship-as-Proof
- Memory-as-Fact
- Absence-as-Proof
- Investigator Omniscience
- Institution-as-Cartoon-Villain
- Last-Minute Reveal Without Retrospective Support
- Meeting-as-Exposition
- Suspicion-by-Weirdness
- Translation-Cadence Leakage

## Cross-source convergence

**Zunshine** supplies source-monitoring and metarepresentation. **Fludernik** separates world fact, discourse and reader model. **Baxter** contributes non-listening/subtext but does not make social inference proof. **Crouch** adds multi-trace identity evidence. **Weir** contributes model testing. **Swain/Bell** contribute state-change scene diagnostics. **Prose/Lodge/Zinsser** reinforce the fact that quiet suspense does not require choppy syntax.

## IVDIVO production rule

Before a major mystery reveal, Writers' Room must be able to answer:

`WHAT DO WE KNOW? → HOW DO WE KNOW IT? → HOW MANY INDEPENDENT SOURCES? → WHO COULD HAVE SHAPED THOSE SOURCES? → WHAT WOULD DISPROVE THE MODEL? → WHAT ACTION IS JUSTIFIED AT THIS CONFIDENCE LEVEL?`

This gate is mandatory for ROOM 917, B07, archival/civilizational mysteries, AI/robot personhood investigations and consciousness-school evidence.
