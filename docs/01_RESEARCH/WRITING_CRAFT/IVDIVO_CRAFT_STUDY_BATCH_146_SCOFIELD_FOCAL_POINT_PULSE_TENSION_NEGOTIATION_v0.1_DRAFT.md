## 1. Core synthesis

A useful scene can be modeled as:

`BEFORE STATE → PULSE / WANT → TENSION OR NEGOTIATION → FOCAL POINT / TERMS CHANGE → RELEASE → AFTER STATE → DOWNSTREAM CONSEQUENCE`

This is a diagnostic profile, not a compulsory recipe.

## 2. Focal point

### Mechanism
`BEFORE STATE → X → AFTER STATE`

The focal point is the scene moment at which its important elements converge or its direction becomes different. It does not need to be the loudest moment.

### Production gate
Ask:
1. What was true/possible before X?
2. What becomes true/possible/impossible after X?
3. Does X alter a later choice, relationship, evidence status, access, responsibility, or model?

If not, X may be merely emphasis rather than the scene's structural focal point.

### Failure mode
**FOCAL-POINT-AS-TWIST:** forcing every scene to contain a revelation, disaster, epiphany, or sharp reversal.

## 3. Pulse

### Mechanism
`BOOK PULSE → SCENE PULSE → EVENT → CHANGED STATE`

Pulse is the persistent pressure beneath the visible event: need, desire, unresolved question, fear, obligation, relationship problem, identity pressure, or model under strain.

### Production gate
A scene may be quiet or procedural if the reader can still answer:
- What remains alive beneath the activity?
- Why does this event matter to this person now?
- What becomes different because the pulse met this event?

### Failure modes
- **PULSELESS ACTIVITY:** lots of movement, no live underlying question.
- **NEGATIVE-STATE-AS-PULSE:** treating generalized misery/anxiety as a substitute for a specific active pressure.
- **DISCONNECTED PULSE:** scene pressure unrelated to the book's main causal engine.

## 4. Tension

### Mechanism
`LIVE QUESTION → SOURCE OF UNCERTAINTY / PRESSURE → MAINTENANCE → RELEASE MODE → CONSEQUENCE`

Tension can arise from anticipation, self-consciousness, relational uncertainty, withholding, physical danger, evidence ambiguity, status, consent, or a feared conversation. It need not be confrontation.

### Release modes
- answer/revelation;
- reconciliation;
- refusal;
- postponement/deferral;
- suppression;
- confrontation;
- retreat;
- unexpected beneficial turn;
- deliberate carry-over into the next scene.

### Production gates
- Identify the exact question/pressure the reader is carrying.
- Identify whether the scene releases, redirects, or transfers it.
- Require a changed state after release.

### Failure modes
- **TENSION MONOCULTURE:** every scene tries to increase worry.
- **WITHHOLDING-AS-DEFAULT:** hiding information even when revelation would create the stronger next state.
- **CONFLICT=VIOLENCE REDUCTION:** assuming shouting or physical danger is required.
- **RELEASE WITHOUT CONSEQUENCE:** tension evaporates and nothing downstream changes.
- **CLIFFHANGER MONOCULTURE.**

## 5. Negotiation as an alternative conflict model

### Mechanism
`A WANT / BOUNDARY ↔ B WANT / BOUNDARY → TACTICS → TERMS SHIFT / REFUSAL / CONCESSION → NEW RELATION OF POWER → CONSEQUENCE`

Negotiation allows fiction to model pressure without converting every relationship into combat.

### Useful tactics
- direct request;
- evasion;
- reframing;
- evidence demand;
- silence;
- appeal to relationship/history;
- appeal to authority/rule;
- refusal;
- conditional consent;
- concession;
- withdrawal;
- changing the question itself.

### Decision-ownership gate
Before writing a negotiation, identify:
`WHO HAS FORMAL AUTHORITY? → WHO BEARS COST? → WHO OWNS THE DECISION? → WHO MAY REFUSE?`

These may be different people.

### Failure modes
- **NEGOTIATION-AS-YELLING.**
- **FALSE COMPROMISE:** the text treats coerced compliance as negotiated agreement.
- **PROCEDURAL POWER = LEGITIMACY.**
- **NEGOTIATING ABOUT A PERSON WITHOUT THEM.**
- **SUBTEXT-AS-PERMANENT-EVASION:** nobody ever makes a decision or changes terms.

## 6. Cross-source comparison

### Against mandatory disaster schools
Swain/Bell/Bickham give useful objective/resistance mechanics, but Scofield confirms that scene progression can come from terms changing, tension releasing, recognition, or agency shifts without mandatory defeat.

### With Rosenfeld / LaPlante
All converge on changed-state causality and the legitimacy of contemplative or quiet scenes when they alter the next intention, interpretation, relationship, or decision.

### With Baxter
Baxter's subtext and listener-attention model pairs well with Scofield negotiation: the real issue may be partly or wholly unspoken, but the scene still needs a legible pressure and consequential terms.

### With Zunshine / Herman / le Carré
Relational tension does not alter evidence provenance. Trust and attraction can change who shares evidence or what action is taken; they do not make a claim more true.

### With Le Guin / Prose / Lodge / Zinsser
Scene beats and tactics are not typographic commands. Narrative rhythm must remain functional and varied.

## 7. ONE MAIN STORY gate

For each scene:

`SCENE PULSE → FOCAL POINT / TERMS CHANGE → AFTER STATE → HOW DOES THIS ALTER THE MAIN ENGINE?`

If the changed state never affects the dominant causal engine or an earned relational/evidence payoff, the scene is a candidate for compression, merge, relocation, or deletion.

## 8. Choppiness / paragraph gate

Do not render analytic units as prose units:

Bad production logic:
`beat → sentence → beat → one-line paragraph → reaction → one-line paragraph`.

Correct logic:
`PERCEPTUAL / DECISION UNIT → PARAGRAPH INTENT → RHYTHMIC SENTENCE DESIGN`.

Short lines remain valid for real contrast, revelation, timing, pause, or end-weight; they are not the default representation of beats.

## 9. IVDIVO specialist gates

### Mystery
`TRUST / ATTRACTION / FEAR / AUTHORITY ≠ CORROBORATION`.

### Conscious nonhuman agents
Once personhood is established:
`OWN WANT → OWN MODEL → OWN RELATIONSHIPS → CONSENT / REFUSAL → OWN CHOICE → CONSEQUENCE`.
Humans cannot legitimately negotiate the entity's continuity solely among themselves.

### Orbital Youth
Institutional scenes should often be framed as negotiation over terms, standing, responsibility, evidence, access, consent, or appeal—not as adolescent-versus-evil-adult combat.

### Consciousness-practice schools
A negotiated or emotionally convincing interpretation remains an interpretation:
`OBSERVATION → REPRODUCIBLE EFFECT → SCHOOL INTERPRETATION → COMPETING INTERPRETATION → INDEPENDENT TEST → ONTOLOGY CEILING`.
