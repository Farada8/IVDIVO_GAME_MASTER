# IVDIVO CRAFT STUDY — BATCH 151
## Collins: Coloring Passions · Emotional Causality · Inner/Outer Rhythm

Source: Brandilyn Collins, *Getting Into Character: Seven Secrets a Novelist Can Learn From Actors*, Edition 2 (2015).
Status: Secret #4 strict-read complete; Secret #5 strict-read in progress. This batch is operational but source lifecycle is not yet synthesized.

## 1. Mechanism: emotional components, not emotion labels

A broad emotion label is not sufficient characterization.

Production translation:

`VALUE / CONCERN + DESIRE + RELATIONSHIP + CIRCUMSTANCE + CURRENT COST -> COMPONENT EMOTIONS -> TACTIC -> ACTION -> CONSEQUENCE`

A character can love and resent, trust and doubt, care and become angry, without becoming inconsistent. The sequence must be causally legible.

### Gate B151-01 — EMOTION COMPONENTS
For every major emotional beat, ask:
- What concern/value is active?
- What does the character want now?
- What circumstance changes the mix?
- What component emotion appears?
- What tactic/action follows?

## 2. Mechanism: credible opposite behaviour

Collins' useful insight is not “give every trait an opposite.” The useful mechanism is that changing circumstances or competing priorities can produce behaviour that appears opposite while remaining causally rooted.

`STABLE VALUE NETWORK + NEW PRESSURE -> DIFFERENT PRIORITY -> DIFFERENT SURFACE BEHAVIOUR`

### Gate B151-02 — OPPOSITE MUST HAVE A CAUSE
Do not insert a generous moment into a selfish character, or a cruel moment into a caring character, solely to manufacture complexity. Require a specific contextual/value conflict.

## 3. Mechanism: growth is a sequence, not a switch

Large late change requires earlier evidence that the capacity existed.

`CAPACITY SEED -> PRESSURE -> PARTIAL/CONTRADICTORY RESPONSES -> STRATEGY/RELATION CHANGE -> LATER CREDIBLE CHANGE`

### Gate B151-03 — NO EMOTIONAL TELEPORTATION
If a character's late decision could not be reconstructed from prior pressures and small capacities, repair the causal chain rather than adding an explanatory speech.

### Firewall
This is **not** a mandatory transformation/healing model. A character may remain fundamentally stable, change tactics, alter one relationship, revise one belief, or fail to change.

## 4. Mechanism: inner rhythm and outer rhythm are separable

Secret #5 adds a useful scene-performance variable:

`INNER PROCESS / PRESSURE RHYTHM <-> OUTER ACTION / ENVIRONMENT RHYTHM`

They may:
- align: urgent thoughts + urgent action;
- diverge: fast inner state + controlled exterior;
- alternate as tactics change;
- differ among characters sharing the same environment.

### Gate B151-04 — INNER/OUTER RHYTHM MAP
For a consequential scene, optionally record:
- inner pace/pressure;
- outer pace/environment;
- whether the character is leaking, suppressing, redirecting or exploiting the mismatch;
- what character-specific action/omission results.

## 5. Mechanism: translate rhythm through character, not author habit

Collins explicitly warns that bodily rhythm exercises are prompts. If the author copies their own physical stress behaviours into every character, differentiation collapses.

`DIAGNOSTIC FELT RHYTHM -> PERSONALIZING + OBJECTIVE + EMBODIMENT -> CHARACTER-SPECIFIC EXPRESSION`

### Gate B151-05 — NO AUTHOR-BODY CLONING
Five characters under stress must not all clench hands, pace, swallow, avoid eye contact and breathe shallowly in the same sequence.

## 6. Relationship to prose rhythm

Inner Rhythm is upstream of sentence rhythm, not identical to it.

Forbidden shortcut:

`FAST INNER RHYTHM -> MANY SHORT SENTENCES`

Correct router:

`READER EFFECT -> SCENE/POV STATE -> INNER/OUTER RELATION -> PARAGRAPH INTENT -> SENTENCE RHYTHM`

Long syntactic accumulation can express acceleration. A short sentence can express stopping, clarity, shock, refusal or comic timing. There is no one-to-one mapping.

### Gate B151-06 — RHYTHM IS NOT TYPOGRAPHY
Do not use fragment count, one-line paragraphs or average sentence length as a proxy for emotional intensity.

## 7. Implications for ONE MAIN STORY

Emotional colour only matters structurally when it changes the causal engine.

A vivid reaction earns space if it changes:
- tactic;
- trust/relationship;
- consent/boundary;
- evidence interpretation;
- authority/access;
- responsibility;
- commitment;
- future option space.

Otherwise it may be compressed even if psychologically plausible.

### Gate B151-07 — EMOTION MUST PAY DOWNSTREAM
Do not preserve a scene solely because its emotional description is rich. Ask what later choice is different because this reaction occurred.

## 8. YA application

Avoid the false equation:

`ADOLESCENCE = TRAUMA + HEALING ARC`.

Better:

`NEW RESPONSIBILITY / AUTONOMY / BELONGING PRESSURE -> EMOTIONAL MIX -> CHARACTER-SPECIFIC TACTIC -> RELATION/ROLE CONSEQUENCE`.

A teenager can remain recognizably the same person while gaining better judgment, changing one boundary, or accepting responsibility.

## 9. Mystery / evidence firewall

`EMOTIONAL DISPLAY -> POV INFERENCE`, not `WORLD FACT`.

Fear, confidence, hesitation, anger, attraction, silence or contradictory tempo can suggest models. They do not prove guilt, motive, consent, personhood or ontology.

### Gate B151-08 — AFFECT IS NOT CORROBORATION
Keep the evidence ledger independent of the emotional/subtext ledger.

## 10. Conscious nonhuman persons

Do not equate inner rhythm with human physiology.

Use:

`EMBODIMENT + OWN PRIORITIES + CURRENT MODEL + RELATIONSHIP + CONSTRAINT -> COMMUNICATION / ACTION RHYTHM`

A conscious robot may express internal pressure through response scheduling, selective disclosure, locomotion, task-switching, resource allocation, silence or refusal rather than facial/body tells.

### Gate B151-09 — NONHUMAN RHYTHM MUST BE EMBODIED
No default “robot voice,” no mandatory human nervous gestures.

## 11. Cross-source synthesis

- **Collins + Card/Corbett:** character differentiation comes from values, relationships, wants and tactics, not quirks.
- **Collins + McKee Character:** contradictory surface behaviour can reveal multidimensionality, but one pressure choice does not expose an immutable “true self.”
- **Collins + Stanislavski:** inner/outer tempo can diverge; external rhythm exercises must be justified by circumstances rather than performed mechanically.
- **Collins + Le Guin/Prose/Lodge/Zinsser:** cadence is functional and variable; short syntax is not a suspense KPI.
- **Collins + LaPlante/Lamott/Swain:** diagnostic frameworks belong mainly in revision, not as simultaneous constraints on discovery drafting.
