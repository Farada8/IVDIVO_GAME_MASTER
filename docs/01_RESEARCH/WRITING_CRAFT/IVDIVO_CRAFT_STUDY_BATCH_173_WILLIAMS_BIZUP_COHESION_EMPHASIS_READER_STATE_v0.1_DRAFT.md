## Williams & Bizup — Cohesion · Emphasis · Reader-State · Agency Visibility

**Source:** Joseph M. Williams & Joseph Bizup, *Style: Lessons in Clarity and Grace*, 13th ed.  
**Source lifecycle:** `STRICT_LIFECYCLE_SYNTHESIZED_v1.0`  
**Batch status:** DRAFT / OPERATIONALIZED  
**Pass:** 138

## 1. Core synthesis

The source adds a micro-discourse layer missing from many plot/scene systems:

`WHAT READER CURRENTLY HOLDS → HOW NEXT SENTENCE ENTERS → WHAT NEW INFORMATION IT ADDS → WHERE STRESS LANDS`.

Choppy prose often arises because every sentence restarts the reader from a new topic, not because each sentence is objectively too short.

## 2. Production gates

### B173-G01 — Topic Chain

For each paragraph identify the topic of every sentence. If the paragraph has no controlled chain around a small set of related topics, repair coherence before polishing individual sentences.

### B173-G02 — Reader-State Handoff

`STATE LEFT BY A → WHAT B ASSUMES → MATCH / MISMATCH`.

If B assumes a new actor, time, place, referent, epistemic status or conceptual frame without signaling the shift, reorder or bridge.

### B173-G03 — Old→New Information

Prefer to orient the reader with active/familiar material before placing major new information where it can receive stress. Treat as a flexible processing principle, not a template.

### B173-G04 — Stress Position

Place the sentence's intended new emphasis deliberately. Do not let bureaucratic tail material bury the point.

### B173-G05 — Agency Visibility

`WHO ACTS? → WHO DECIDES? → WHO MAY REFUSE? → WHO BEARS COST?`

Passives and nominalizations are allowed, but not when they accidentally erase governance, consent or responsibility.

### B173-G06 — Nominalization Audit

When abstract nouns accumulate, recover the hidden actions and actors. Keep the abstraction only if it improves compression and the relation remains legible.

### B173-G07 — Technical Noun-Stack Audit

Unpack a technical noun stack when a non-specialist reader cannot recover its internal relationships. Specialist vocabulary is allowed when it buys precision and is inferable/defined.

### B173-G08 — Concision ≠ Chopping

Delete repeated meaning, empty framing and needless abstraction. Do not convert one coherent thought into several sentence fragments merely to reduce word count.

### B173-G09 — Sentence-Length Function

No raw-length KPI. Long sentences may clarify accumulation/logic/continuity; short sentences may stop, contrast or emphasize.

### B173-G10 — End-Weight Variation

End-stress is powerful, not mandatory. Constant climax-at-the-end syntax becomes predictable and mannered.

### B173-G11 — Paragraph Before Sentence

Before repairing sentence rhythm, determine whether multiple microbeats should be one paragraph/one sentence, or whether the whole passage should be summary.

### B173-G12 — Style-on-Revision

Do not run this gate stack during discovery drafting unless a clear blockage demands it.

`LIVE MATERIAL → DEFECT → RELEVANT GATE → REPAIR → RETEST`.

### B173-G13 — Ethical Clarity

When prose carries institutional/scientific authority, clarity must expose enough agency, evidence status and logical relation for a reader to evaluate the claim.

### B173-G14 — Source Representation

`SOURCE CLAIM → QUOTE/PARAPHRASE/SUMMARY → MEANING-PRESERVATION TEST → ATTRIBUTION`.

Compression may not manufacture certainty the source did not contain.

## 4. Cross-source position

Williams/Bizup does **not** replace structural sources. Apply after story/scene/discourse defects have been routed.

Convergence:
- Le Guin / Prose / Hodgins / Clark: sentence length is functional and contrastive.
- Zinsser: clarity and clutter.
- Chatman / Genette: order and discourse before micro-style.
- Fludernik: reader model / focalization.
- Lamott / LaPlante / Swain: separate discovery from critique.
