## 1. What changed after Chapters 8–17

The second half of Brody adds a useful set of diagnostic story lenses, but it also exposes the main risk of the Save-the-Cat school: descriptive patterns are repeatedly promoted toward prescriptive ingredients.

IVDIVO therefore keeps the diagnostic questions and rejects mandatory ingredient insertion.

## 2. Genre-lens translations

### SUPERHERO
Source lens: power + nemesis + curse.
IVDIVO translation:
`UNUSUAL CAPABILITY / ACCESS → NEW OPTIONS → RESPONSIBILITY / LIMIT → COSTLY CHOICE`.

Reject:
- chosen-one destiny as default;
- one mandatory nemesis;
- curse = trauma/punishment;
- capability = moral superiority.

### GOLDEN FLEECE
Source lens: road + team + prize.
IVDIVO translation:
`TRACKABLE OBJECTIVE → CAUSAL MILESTONES → RELATIONSHIP/KNOWLEDGE CHANGE → STRATEGY CHANGE`.

Reject:
- team as inventory of functions;
- sightseeing masquerading as story;
- material prize/credits as default in post-money orbit.

### FOOL TRIUMPHANT
Source lens: underestimated fool + establishment + transmutation.
IVDIVO translation:
`MISREAD PERSON → INSTITUTIONAL/PEER ASSUMPTION → CHARACTER-SPECIFIC ACTION → EVIDENCE FORCES MODEL REVISION`.

Reject:
- underdog = automatically correct;
- establishment = automatically stupid/corrupt;
- guaranteed triumphant reversal.

### DUDE WITH A PROBLEM
Source lens: ordinary hero + sudden event + life/death battle.
IVDIVO translation:
`PRE-ESTABLISHED COMPETENCE → SUDDEN PRESSURE → IMPROVISATION → COST → ADAPTATION`.

Reject life-or-death as default. Trust, responsibility, autonomy, role, relationship, mission access and safety can carry equal or stronger story weight.

### OUT OF THE BOTTLE
Source lens: hero + magic + lesson.
IVDIVO translation:
`NOVEL CAPABILITY → CLEAR RULES/LIMITS → CONSEQUENCE → HUMAN CHOICE`.

Reject moral-lesson machine and mandatory final proof without the capability. This chapter gives **no permission** to introduce consciousness transfer into ordinary Orbital Youth.

### WHYDUNIT
Source lens: detective + secret + dark turn.
IVDIVO translation:
`OBSERVABLE FACT → SOURCE → INTERPRETATION → ALTERNATIVE → COUNTEREVIDENCE → REVISED MODEL → ACTION`.

Reject mandatory ethical corruption. Refusing an invasive shortcut may be the stronger costly choice.

### MONSTER IN THE HOUSE
Source lens: monster + house + sin.
IVDIVO translation:
`CONTAINMENT BOUNDARY → NARROWED OPTIONS → PRESSURE → CHOICE`.

Reject mandatory moral sin. Orbital geometry can create real containment without allegorical punishment.

## 3. Pitch as architecture test

Use:
`WHO/WHERE → DISTURBANCE → TRACKABLE OBJECTIVE → CENTRAL RESISTANCE/TRADEOFF → WHY IT MATTERS`.

If a pitch requires five disconnected subplots to explain what the book is about, return to ONE MAIN STORY architecture.

Do not force the manuscript to match Brody's logline template.

## 4. Series architecture

Useful Brody insight:
- a series may change protagonists/engines;
- every installment still needs a readable local story;
- a macro arc may continue beyond the local resolution.

IVDIVO law:
`LOCAL BOOK PROBLEM → CLIMAX/CHOICE → RESULT/AFTERMATH → PERSISTENT SAGA STATE CHANGE → OPTIONAL HOOK`.

Never turn the whole saga into one stretched 15-beat sheet. No compulsory cliffhanger.

## 5. Final-image correction

Brody's closing chapter usefully admits that drafting changes plans and that discovery persists even for planners.

IVDIVO operational form:
`PLAN = HYPOTHESIS`.

A framework survives only if current story evidence supports it. Macro changes require explicit causal reasons and changelog discipline; discovery is not uncontrolled scope drift.

## 7. Cross-school verdict

Brody is strongest where she forces YA specificity, premise delivery and trackability. She is weaker than Truby on organic causality, Rosenfeld on scene recursion, Bell on intelligent opposition, Card on cast hierarchy, Kress on character-specific motivation, Chatman on satellites/story-discourse, and Herman/Zunshine on person-models and information provenance.

Therefore Brody should be called early to ask **what kind of youth pressure/promise this story delivers**, then checked by the more causal/psychological schools before architecture is accepted.

## 8. Final batch rule

**Use Save the Cat as a diagnostic vocabulary, never as the IVDIVO operating system.**
