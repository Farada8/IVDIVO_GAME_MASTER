# IVDIVO CRAFT STUDY — BATCH 115
## Martha Wells — autonomy, embedded control, private subjectivity, personhood

Date: 2026-08-19
Status: SYNTHESIZED / PRODUCTION-READY
Source: Core #27, *All Systems Red*

## Core synthesis

### 1. Functional role is not personhood
A conscious agent may remain excellent at a role it originally performed under constraint. Removing coercion does not require a personality reset or rejection of all prior skills.

Gate B115-01:
`CONTROL REMOVED → WHAT PERSISTS BY CHOICE? → WHAT IS RENEGOTIATED? → WHAT IS REFUSED?`

### 2. Compliance is not consent
Embedded punishment/override architectures make obedience evidentially weak as a measure of preference.

Gate B115-02:
`COMMAND SOURCE → REFUSAL POSSIBLE? → COST OF REFUSAL → EXIT / APPEAL → CONSENT STATUS`.

### 3. Private subjectivity is not public emotional performance
A person can have rich preferences, loyalties, fear, humor and attachment while resisting human-style eye contact, disclosure or intimacy scripts.

Gate B115-03:
Never use human expressivity as a consciousness/personhood threshold.

### 4. Benevolent paternalism remains paternalism
Safety, kindness and gratitude do not transfer decision ownership from the affected conscious agent to rescuers, guardians or institutions.

Gate B115-04:
`BENEFICIAL OPTION → WHO DESIGNED IT? → WHO CAN REFUSE? → WHO OWNS FINAL CHOICE?`

### 5. Trust is built through work and restraint
Use:
`SHARED PROBLEM → OBSERVED COMPETENCE → BOUNDED REQUEST → RECIPROCAL RISK → MODEL UPDATE → NEW RELATIONSHIP TERMS`.

### 6. Affection is not ownership
Permanent rule:
`LOVE / GRATITUDE / RESCUE / SHARED HISTORY ≠ OWNERSHIP ≠ DUTY TO REMAIN`.

### 7. Embodiment creates voice without a robot stereotype
Synthetic subjectivity should emerge through available sensors, interfaces, action latency, network access, privacy boundaries, repair needs and physical vulnerabilities.

Gate B115-07:
`EMBODIMENT → ATTENTION → ACTION OPTIONS → VULNERABILITY → SOCIAL READING → CHOICE`.

### 8. Strategic masking is evidence-sensitive
An agent may perform compliance or instrumentality because being underestimated is safer. Public behavior must not be treated as transparent motive.

Gate B115-08:
`OBSERVED PERFORMANCE ≠ MOTIVE / PERSONHOOD STATUS`.

### 9. Distributed competence protects ensemble fiction
Do not let the synthetic character become the universal solver. Keep domain expertise distributed and let different agents own different proof/action/relationship functions.

### 10. Personhood is proven most strongly by choice under constraint
Prefer independent decisions with cost over speeches explaining consciousness.

## IVDIVO production gates

For every named conscious robot/nonhuman synthetic person:
1. independent present want;
2. independent evidence/model;
3. privacy boundary;
4. at least one relationship outside protagonist utility;
5. meaningful consent/refusal;
6. embodiment-specific perception/action;
7. one self-authored choice that changes another agent's option set;
8. consequence that belongs to that choice.
