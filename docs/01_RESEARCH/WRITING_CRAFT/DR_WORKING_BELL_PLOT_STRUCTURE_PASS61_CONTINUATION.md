## CORE MECHANISMS EXTRACTED THIS PASS

### 1. Bilateral Issue Test

When a story touches a contested issue, do not write one intelligent side and one strawman side.

IVDIVO translation:

`ISSUE → STRONGEST CASE A → STRONGEST CASE B → INDEPENDENT PERSON MOTIVES → ACTIONS → CONSEQUENCES → READER INFERENCE`

This is especially valuable for:
- autonomy vs coordinated responsibility;
- predictive efficiency vs consent;
- institutional safety vs individual discretion;
- specialist-practice interpretation vs empirical caution;
- human vs conscious-robot interests where neither side is a prop.

### 2. Generator / Validator Separation

Bell's 20 methods are best treated as divergent generators, not canon machines.

`SEED → MANY MATERIAL VARIANTS → TEMPORARY NO-CENSOR ZONE → CANON / PERSONHOOD / CHARACTER / CAUSALITY / ONE MAIN STORY / READER-EFFECT FILTER → KEEP / BANK / DISCARD`

A title, opening line, trend, occupation, prologue, ending image, obsession, genre flip or news item is allowed to generate a candidate. It does not acquire canonical authority by being vivid.

### 3. Pattern-Borrowing Firewall

Abstract story movement, genre grammar or mechanism may be studied. Exact character systems, distinctive plot construction, signature sequence or protected expression must not be copied.

For IVDIVO reference intelligence:

`REFERENCE → ABSTRACT MECHANISM → TRANSFORM THROUGH IVDIVO WORLD / CHARACTER / CAUSALITY → ORIGINAL ENGINE`

### 4. Research-to-Story Gate

Research is not story merely because it is fascinating.

`SOURCE / FACT → WHO CARES? → WHY NOW? → WHAT OPTION CHANGES? → ACTION / TEST → CONSEQUENCE`

If research changes no human/robot choice, relationship, evidence state or causal possibility, keep it in the world database rather than forcing it into prose.

### 5. Ending-First as Generator, Not Fate

A strong ending image or decisive choice can be generated first and reverse-engineered to discover possible story architecture.

But:

`ENDING CANDIDATE → CAUSAL BACKWARD TEST → CHARACTER-TRUTH TEST → PROOF-CEILING TEST → KEEP / MODIFY / DISCARD`

Do not distort a living manuscript to protect an early ending brainstorm.

### 6. Role ≠ Money Gate

Bell's occupation-based generation is useful, but in post-money orbital settings it must be translated as:

`ROLE / PRACTICE / TRAINING / RESPONSIBILITY / CONTRIBUTION / SOCIAL FUNCTION / RELATIONSHIP NETWORK / ACCESS TO UNIQUE TIME OR PLACE`

not as salary, rent, credits, purchasing power or disguised wage competition.

### 7. Sinker Without Death Inflation

Bell often uses extreme stakes as a commercial diagnostic. IVDIVO does not require death to make an idea large enough.

Preferred test:

`WHAT NON-FUNGIBLE VALUE CAN ACTUALLY BE LOST OR CHANGED?`

Possible answers:
- trust;
- consent;
- relationship;
- responsibility;
- unique role or mission;
- reputation;
- time;
- access to a specific person/place/window;
- evidence integrity;
- safety;
- belonging;
- mastery;
- a once-only experience.

### 8. Passion / Potential / Precision — IVDIVO Adaptation

- **Passion:** is there enough emotional/intellectual energy in the premise, characters and questions to sustain a full book?
- **Potential:** does the story make a clear reader promise and have enough differentiated causal material to sustain that promise? This is not merely trend-chasing.
- **Precision:** which elements actually serve this book's selected promise and ONE MAIN STORY, and which belong in the saga database / idea bank instead?

### 9. Prologue Firewall

A prologue can be a discovery exercise or a legitimate story component. It is not an opening requirement and does not need action, murder, mystery or shock merely because commercial thrillers often use those devices.

### 10. Obsession Firewall

An obsession can produce strong directional energy, but it is not a mandatory pathology, wound or trauma. Its value lies in repeated choices, relationship consequences and changing tactics.
