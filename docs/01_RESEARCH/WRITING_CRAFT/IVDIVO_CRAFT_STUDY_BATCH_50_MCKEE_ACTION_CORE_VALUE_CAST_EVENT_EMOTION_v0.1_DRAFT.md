## 1. WHAT TRANSFERS

### 1.1 Action must carry a human value change
Physical movement alone is not narrative action. A sequence matters when success/failure changes something the reader can care about: safety, trust, autonomy, consent, responsibility, relationship, access, reputation, civic consequence, unique opportunity.

Operational form:
`PHYSICAL PROBLEM → HUMAN VALUE AT RISK → CHOICE → CONSEQUENCE`.

### 1.2 Ensemble differentiation should survive identical pressure
McKee’s strongest cast principle is not the hero/villain/victim triad; it is that strong characters do not interpret and answer the same pressure identically.

IVDIVO test:
`SAME EVENT → DIFFERENT ATTENTION → DIFFERENT FIRST TACTIC → DIFFERENT COST → DIFFERENT NEXT STRATEGY`.

### 1.3 Concentrated decisive scenes should remove easy answers
The genre-specific “mercy scene” is transformed into a general bounded-choice convergence mechanism:
`REMOVE EASY TOOL / ACCESS / CERTAINTY → CONCENTRATE PRESSURE → FORCE EARNED CHOICE → CONSEQUENCE`.

This is stronger than simply increasing physical danger.

### 1.4 No visible off-switch
If a formidable opponent/system has a planted weakness whose final use is obvious, suspense becomes waiting. Prefer a property that reads as strength until accumulated evidence allows the protagonist to reinterpret and exploit it.

### 1.5 Resolution ownership
Coincidence may place a character inside trouble. It may not perform the decisive rescue/solution. The final causal link should belong to established knowledge, preparation, relationships, choice and action.

### 1.6 Excitement is modulated reader effect
Constant intensity produces adaptation. Quiet success, competence, recovery, intimacy and ordinary life can increase later impact by restoring contrast.

### 1.7 Excitement sources that survive genre translation
McKee’s categories can be abstracted into:
- resisting abused authority;
- entering an unknown system/place;
- frustration near a meaningful objective;
- conquering a real limitation;
- crossing a consequential boundary/taboo.

None is mandatory. None requires death stakes.

### 1.8 Convention must be separated from cliché
Working transformation protocol:
`FUNCTION OF CONVENTION → SPECIFIC WORLD/PEOPLE RESEARCH → MULTIPLE ORIGINAL EXECUTIONS → CHARACTER/WORLD FIT → RETROACTIVE SENSE`.

This converges with IVDIVO Five-Fork and Reference Intelligence rules.

## 2. WHAT DOES NOT TRANSFER AS A LAW

- `Action = Life/Death` is McKee/El-Wakil’s genre definition, not a universal rule of suspense or YA.
- `Hero = altruism / Villain = narcissism / Victim = helplessness` is not a universal cast ontology.
- one extreme choice does not prove a permanent “true self”;
- victimhood must not erase agency;
- lethal captivity is not required for a decisive convergence scene;
- the writer does not need a moralized villain when a competent countermodel, system conflict or mutual-risk problem is stronger;
- action prose does not require short-sentence monoculture.

## 3. FAILURE MODES

1. **Mortality Inflation** — death is added because the framework expects Life/Death.
2. **Moral Polarization** — competent opposition is simplified into evil.
3. **Rescue-Object Person** — a character exists only to be endangered.
4. **Helplessness Mandate** — threatened youth/nonhuman is denied meaningful agency.
5. **Mercy-Scene Retrofit** — every climax becomes stripped hero versus triumphant villain.
6. **Achilles-Heel Telemetry** — obvious early off-switch makes the ending predictable.
7. **Deus Ex Ally/System** — decisive agency is stolen by rescuer/system/luck.
8. **Intensity Monoculture** — every sequence tries to be louder/faster/more dangerous.
9. **Action-Syntax Fallacy** — short sentences are treated as inherent suspense.
10. **Convention Recycling** — cliché is defended as “genre requirement.”

## 4. PRODUCTION GATES — B50

**B50-G01 Human Value Before Spectacle** — What human/relational/civic state changes if this action succeeds or fails?

**B50-G02 Agency Preservation** — Does the threatened person still make meaningful choices where realistically possible?

**B50-G03 Favorite-Tactic Removal** — Before increasing scale, test removal of the protagonist’s easiest habitual tactic.

**B50-G04 No Obvious Off-Switch** — Could an attentive reader already name the exact final solution? If yes, redesign.

**B50-G05 Resolution Ownership** — Is the decisive outcome earned by established agency and causality?

**B50-G06 Intensity Modulation** — Is pressure varying in kind and rhythm rather than climbing one numeric ladder?

**B50-G07 Character-Specific Reaction** — Do major characters differ under the same pressure in attention, tactic and cost?

**B50-G08 Convention Function Test** — What function does the borrowed convention serve, and is there a more original IVDIVO execution?

**B50-G09 No Mortality Shortcut** — Can the sequence be made equally or more compelling through trust, responsibility, consent, autonomy, relationship, unique opportunity or civic consequence?

**B50-G10 Reader-Effect Syntax** — Choose sentence/paragraph rhythm from the desired reader effect, not from the label “action scene.”

## 5. IVDIVO / ORBITAL YOUTH APPLICATION

For mature orbital civilization, the most useful action pressure is often **responsibility under real constraints**, not material survival. Examples of non-fungible pressure that robots cannot simply manufacture away:
- one specific mission slot;
- a narrow decision window;
- another person’s trust;
- consent that can be withdrawn;
- reputation after a public decision;
- access to a unique event/place/person;
- responsibility for a shared system;
- a relationship that changes after a choice;
- evidence that must be gathered before authority can be exercised.

For conscious robots/nonhumans: never write “victim” as synonymous with passive object. A robotic person may be endangered while simultaneously choosing, refusing, negotiating, hiding information, protecting someone else, or pursuing a conflicting objective.

For consciousness/dimensional lines: high technology does not grant ordinary orbital civilization operational human soul/consciousness transfer or mature mainstream dimensional science. Action-tech invention must stay inside current canon boundaries.

## 6. LINE-EDIT CONSEQUENCE

Do not repair weak action by chopping prose into fragments. Diagnose in this order:
`VALUE / CAUSALITY → AGENCY → TACTIC → INFORMATION → CONSEQUENCE → POV → RHYTHM → SENTENCE`.

A long sentence can intensify suspense through slowed perception; a short paragraph can work as impact. Neither form is inherently “action prose.”
