## 3. Primary claims retained

### A. Material quality precedes prose polish
A well-written paragraph cannot compensate for absent reporting, missing evidence or a poorly bounded story. Durable IVDIVO version:

`DISCOVERY / SOURCE MATERIAL → STORY MODEL → ORGANIZATION → PROSE`.

### B. Story range is a causal fence
Strong long-form work decides what belongs inside the story and what must remain outside. A saga-scale world does not require saga-scale coverage in one book.

`SAGA SCALE ≠ BOOK SCALE`.

### C. One or two causal dimensions deserve primary emphasis
History, scope, reasons, impacts, countermoves and future possibilities are useful interrogation dimensions, not equal-weight obligations. Overdeveloping every dimension bloats the story.

### D. Sources have different epistemic roles
Direct actors, mid-level implementers, documents and high-level experts can each know different things. Source prestige does not equal knowledge completeness.

`SOURCE ROLE FIT → WHAT CAN THIS SOURCE ACTUALLY KNOW?`

### E. Extrapolation is a hypothesis, not a fact
A plausible causal projection must be challenged by external forces, countermodels and disconfirming evidence.

### F. Working theme statements are provisional
A theme/main statement is an organizing hypothesis that may change as evidence and material accumulate. It is not canon.

### G. Lived examples make systems legible but do not prove prevalence
Localization/person-level consequence is valuable; one vivid case cannot automatically stand for a whole institution, civilization or category.

### H. Plans are flexible tools
Blundell uses planning questions but resists over-rigid outlines. Strong convergence with Bell, Seger and Mayer:

`PLAN = HYPOTHESIS, NOT CANON`.

### I. Research needs a stopping condition
Reporting/research continues until the questions materially required for the story/decision are sufficiently covered—not until the author has exhausted the subject.

### J. Organize by story function while preserving provenance
Material can be indexed under history/reasons/impact/etc. for writing, but source identity must remain recoverable.

### K. Narrative line is presentation architecture, not the story engine
Block progression, chronology and thematic organization are discourse strategies. ONE MAIN STORY may use more than one presentation line.

### L. Transitions should grow from the reader-state left by prior material
Blundell's strongest transition principle is that what has just been written should suggest what follows. Empty bridge sentences usually signal weak organization.

### M. Direct quotation earns space when exact wording matters
Quotation is valuable for credibility, emotion, trenchant expression or voice. Routine content can often be paraphrased/summary. In IVDIVO mystery, provenance remains mandatory even if visible prose attribution is compressed.

### N. Endings must first complete local work
Circling back, looking ahead and spreading outward are useful ending shapes only after the local story question/result has genuinely been resolved.

### O. Wordcraft is downstream of story architecture
Idea, evidence, illustration and structure outrank ornamental sentence-level polish.

### P. Description must perform work
Description can progress story, support evidence, involve the reader or create contrast; decorative inventory is weak.

### Q. Speed is not short-sentence density
A long sentence with a simple grammatical core can move faster than several short sentences separated by stop-sign periods. Force can also require slowing down.

### R. Long-form complexity needs a reader-memory budget
Large casts, multiple narrative strands and long-range payoffs must be grouped, foreshadowed and reintroduced deliberately. Separate stories should remain separate when combining them creates overload without causal necessity.

### S. Character is strongly inferred from repeated choices
Repeated action/refusal under pressure can establish character more effectively than explanatory description.

### T. Reading craft means reverse-engineering effect
`READER EFFECT → BACKTRACK → WHAT TECHNIQUE / STRUCTURE CAUSED IT?`
This strongly aligns with the IVDIVO mechanism-extraction lifecycle.

## 4. Mechanism bank

### M-BLUN-01 — Causal Fence
`CENTRAL DEVELOPMENT → IN-SCOPE CONSEQUENCES / RESPONSES → OUT-OF-SCOPE MATERIAL`.

### M-BLUN-02 — Source Ecology
`CLAIM / QUESTION → DIRECT ACTOR + IMPLEMENTER + DOCUMENT + EXPERT / COUNTERSOURCE as relevant`.
Do not confuse quantity of sources with independent source lineages.

### M-BLUN-03 — Extrapolation Test
`OBSERVED DEVELOPMENT → CAUSAL MAP → EXTERNAL FORCES / ALTERNATIVE MODEL → TEST → CONFIDENCE STATUS`.

### M-BLUN-04 — Primary Dimension Selection
`HISTORY | SCOPE | REASONS | IMPACTS | COUNTERMOVES | FUTURES → select 1–2 primary dimensions`.

### M-BLUN-05 — Lived Consequence
`ABSTRACT SYSTEM CLAIM → PERSON-LEVEL EFFECT → ACTION / CHOICE / COST`.

### M-BLUN-06 — Research Stop Condition
`DECISION-RELEVANT QUESTIONS → SOURCE / TEST COVERAGE → SUFFICIENT FOR CURRENT STORY DECISION → WRITE`.

### M-BLUN-07 — Story-Function Index + Provenance
`SOURCE / TRACE ID → STORY FUNCTION → EVIDENCE STATUS → SCENE / SECTION USE`.

### M-BLUN-08 — Natural Transition
`STATE LEFT BY SECTION A → ACTIVE CONCEPT / CONSEQUENCE → SECTION B`.

### M-BLUN-09 — Reintroduction Cost
`NAMED SOURCE / CHARACTER RETURNS AFTER GAP → ORIENT / CONSOLIDATE / REMOVE NAME IF FUNCTIONALLY UNNEEDED`.
Preserve independent personhood and provenance where required.

### M-BLUN-10 — Lead Promise
`ATTENTION → ORIENTATION → RELEVANT STORY PROMISE → PAYOFF`.

### M-BLUN-11 — Direct-Wording Payoff
`DO EXACT WORDS CHANGE EVIDENCE / TRUST / CONSENT / AUTHORITY / RELATIONSHIP? → DIRECT; ELSE CONSIDER PARAPHRASE / SUMMARY`.

### M-BLUN-12 — Local Closure Before Spread-Out
`LOCAL RESULT → CONSEQUENCE / RESONANCE → OPTIONAL FUTURE VECTOR`.

### M-BLUN-13 — Minimum Necessary Explanation
`PRESENT QUESTION → MINIMUM EXPLANATION NEEDED FOR ACTION / INFERENCE → NEXT ACTION`.

### M-BLUN-14 — Flow Merge
`EMPTY TRANSITION LABEL + EXPLANATORY SENTENCE → ONE CONTENT-BEARING SENTENCE` where possible.

### M-BLUN-15 — Rhythm by Effect
`READER EFFECT → SUBJECTIVE DURATION / CONTINUITY → SENTENCE LENGTH / FORM / PARALLELISM`.

### M-BLUN-16 — Long-Form Memory Budget
`CAST / STRAND → GROUPING → FORESHADOW → REINTRODUCTION → PAYOFF`.

### M-BLUN-17 — Character Through Pressured Choice
`VALUE / WANT → PRESSURE → CHOICE / REFUSAL → CONSEQUENCE → READER MODEL`.

## 5. Failure modes

- **MATERIAL-EMPTY PROSE POLISH** — polishing language before sufficient story/evidence material exists.
- **STORY-RANGE BLOAT / SAGA-IN-ONE-BOOK** — trying to explain the whole world or causal system in one narrative.
- **EVERY-DIMENSION OVERLOAD** — giving history, scope, reasons, impacts, countermoves and futures equal weight.
- **CAUSAL-MAP CONFIRMATION BIAS** — treating extrapolation as prediction/fact.
- **THEME-STATEMENT-AS-CANON** — forcing new material to obey an early working thesis.
- **EXPERT CHORUS** — multiple prestigious voices mistaken for independent proof.
- **MICROCOSM OVERGENERALIZATION** — one vivid person treated as statistically/culturally representative without support.
- **INTERVIEWER-PLANTED INTERPRETATION** — question design manufactures the answer.
- **RESEARCH HOARDING** — continuing discovery after the current story decision has enough material.
- **TRANSITION AIRLOCK** — dead bridge prose inserted because organization is weak.
- **PROOF VARIETY WITHOUT SOURCE INDEPENDENCE** — multiple forms of one lineage mistaken for corroboration.
- **PICTORIALIZATION MUTATES EVIDENCE** — simplifying numbers/measurements until evidentiary meaning changes.
- **ATTRIBUTION-PROVENANCE LOSS** — prose compression destroys the evidence ledger.
- **EXPLANATION SATURATION** — explaining mechanics the reader does not need to follow the causal action.
- **FRAGMENT PERFORMANCE** — questions/exclamations/fragments used as visible energy rather than meaningful contrast.
- **SPEED=SHORT-SENTENCE FALLACY** — multiple stop-sign sentences used to manufacture pace.
- **CAST PHONEBOOK** — too many named people without differentiated causal work.
- **PARALLEL-STORY OVERLOAD** — two major causal stories forced into one because they share topic/world.
- **CRUELTY-AS-CRAFT** — treating author suffering or harshness as evidence of rigor.
- **MICRO-CUTS BEFORE STRUCTURAL DIAGNOSIS** — journalism deadline habits misapplied to novel development.
- **TONE / ANGLE DISTORTS FACTS** — desired framing selects/mutates material until evidence no longer controls interpretation.
- **COMMITTEE VOICE** — fear of criticism flattens individual prose.

## 6. Cross-source comparison

### With Genette / Chatman
Blundell supplies practitioner-level causal fencing, source gathering and organization; Genette/Chatman formalize order, duration, frequency, focalization and discourse. Combined router:

`CAUSAL FENCE + SOURCE MAP → DISCOURSE DESIGN → READER MODEL`.

### With Mayer / Seger / Bell
Strong convergence: plans/treatments are working hypotheses. Live material and actual story state can force re-planning.

### With Zunshine / Herman / le Carré
Blundell's multi-source reporting is strengthened by source-lineage discipline: several internal checks are not independent if they descend from one source/system.

### With Williams/Bizup
Blundell's natural-transition principle strongly converges with OLD→NEW/topic-chain continuity. Empty bridge sentences are a paragraph-level choppiness problem.

### With Le Guin / Clark / Hodgins / Collins
Strong convergence: speed and intensity are not proportional to short-sentence density. Long sentences can carry rapid continuous action; force may require slowing.

### With Doty / Scofield
Description and image selection should perform causal, emotional, evidentiary or reader-model work—not become catalogues.

### With IVDIVO revision law
Blundell sometimes privileges aggressive word-level cutting before major surgery because of newsroom constraints. IVDIVO explicitly overrides that sequence:

`BOOK ENGINE / STRUCTURE / SCENE → PARAGRAPH → SENTENCE → WORD`.

## 7. IVDIVO operationalization

### ONE MAIN STORY
Establish a causal fence and one or two primary dimensions. If a parallel strand does not materially alter the same dominant causal/epistemic/relational engine, consider banking it for another book.

### Mystery / evidence
`CLAIM → SOURCE → SOURCE LINEAGE → ACCESS → INTERPRETATION → ALTERNATIVE MODEL → CORROBORATION STATUS → ACTION`.
Multiple technical outputs from one instrument chain do not automatically become independent proof.

### YA / Orbital Youth
Use actor-level lived consequences to reveal post-money normality: ordinary manufactured things may be background while time, trust, consent, privacy, responsibility, mastery, belonging and unique opportunities carry real scarcity/value. A single teenager must not be treated as representative of an entire orbital society without evidence.

### Conscious nonhuman persons
A single robot/nonhuman individual is not a microcosm of a civilization. Preserve own priorities, relationships, model/evidence, consent/refusal and choice. Human interviews/labels remain external interpretations.

### Consciousness-practice schools
`PRACTITIONER OBSERVATION → REPRODUCIBLE EFFECT → SCHOOL INTERPRETATION → SOURCE/LINEAGE → COMPETING INTERPRETATION → INDEPENDENT TEST → ONTOLOGY CEILING`.

### Prose / anti-choppiness
Before sentence edits:

`BOOK ENGINE → CHAPTER FUNCTION → SCENE ADMISSION → ORDER/DURATION/FREQUENCY → SCENE WEIGHT/MODE → POV/EVIDENCE → PARAGRAPH TOPIC/READER-STATE → TRANSITION NEED → SENTENCE RHYTHM → WORDING`.

Specific new repair:

`EMPTY TRANSITION + EXPLANATION → MERGE / DELETE / REORDER`.

## 9. Final synthesis

Blundell's durable value for IVDIVO is a bridge between **source ecology, causal scope, organization and prose flow**. His strongest lesson is not journalistic style imitation but disciplined selection: decide what the story is actually about, gather source types appropriate to each question, preserve provenance, develop only the causal dimensions that deserve weight, and let paragraph/sentence form follow reader effect. His 1988 newsroom assumptions remain bounded historical context.

**Standing laws:**

- `NO NEW EVIDENCE → NO NEW GLOBAL REWRITE`.
- `PLAN = HYPOTHESIS, NOT CANON`.
- `CLAIM STRENGTH ≤ EVIDENCE STRENGTH`.
- `SPEED / SUSPENSE ≠ SHORT-SENTENCE DENSITY`.
