## 1. CENTRAL SYNTHESIS
Swain's high-value mechanism is not `continually make everything worse`. The useful deeper model is controlled alternation between consequential pressure and lower-pressure processing, with every major segment changing the reader's and character's state.

IVDIVO form:

`PRESSURE PEAK → CONSEQUENCE → VALLEY / PROCESSING / ORDINARY LIFE → MODEL / RELATION / OPTION CHANGE → NEXT STRATEGY → NEXT PEAK`

## 2. DELAY IS NOT COMPLICATION
### Delay
Consumes time or creates logistical inconvenience without materially changing future possibility.

### Complication
Changes at least one downstream state:
- available action;
- evidence status;
- relationship;
- authority/access;
- responsibility;
- safety;
- option space;
- cost of a later choice.

### Gate B103-G01 — DELAY/COMPLICATION
Before expanding a delay into a scene, ask whether it changes downstream possibility. If not, compress unless the lived-time effect is itself the point.

## 3. CAUSAL INVOLVEMENT / WHY DOES THE PERSON STAY?
Swain's `why doesn't he quit?` is retained as an adhesive diagnostic, but its coercive and economic defaults are rejected.

Valid IVDIVO anchors include:
- accepted responsibility;
- promise or consent-bound obligation;
- ownership of evidence created by one's own action;
- relationship commitment;
- bounded authority already accepted;
- unique role or irreplaceable opportunity;
- a physical/world rule that changes exit options;
- responsibility for foreseeable consequences.

Invalid shortcut:
`need stronger adhesive → add debt / rent / kidnapping / arbitrary death / secret villain`.

## 4. PEAKS AND VALLEYS
### Gate B103-G02 — TENSION MONOCULTURE
Three adjacent major units should not all attempt to increase engagement by the same emotional mechanism.

Reader-engagement palette may include:
- worry;
- hope;
- curiosity;
- intimacy;
- competence pleasure;
- wonder;
- humor;
- discovery;
- ethical uncertainty;
- earned relief;
- relationship pressure;
- model revision.

### Gate B103-G03 — VALLEY WITH FUNCTION
A lower-pressure unit survives if it changes at least one of:
- relationship;
- interpretation;
- commitment;
- evidence status;
- next strategy;
- reader bond;
- lived understanding of the world;
- contrast required for the next peak.

Otherwise compress/merge/delete.

## 5. ORDINARY LIFE AS STORY MATERIAL
Ordinary acts are legitimate when they establish lived reality and personhood rather than serve as filler.

For post-money orbital life, ordinary scenes should naturally reveal such facts as:
- ordinary manufactured goods are not the central scarce resource;
- time, trust, consent, reputation, mastery, unique placements, intimacy and lived experience remain non-fungible;
- conscious robots have lives and relationships outside human utility;
- infrastructure is normal to residents and should not be explained touristically.

## 6. PACING AND SYNTAX
Swain explicitly treats longer/more flowing syntax as one available way to lower immediate tension and warns against extremes.

### Gate B103-G04 — INTENSITY IS NOT SENTENCE LENGTH
`INTENSITY != SHORT-SENTENCE DENSITY`.

Choose syntax from desired reader effect:
- accumulation;
- speed;
- suspension;
- subjective time expansion;
- abrupt decision;
- revelation;
- breath/recovery;
- humor timing.

Short and long sentences must gain force through contrast and function.

## 7. POV SWITCHING COST
A viewpoint switch requires the reader to re-establish orientation.

### Gate B103-G05 — POV SWITCH PURCHASE
A switch is justified only if it purchases unique:
- evidence;
- lived consequence;
- relationship pressure;
- decision;
- countermodel;
- inaccessible action;
- contrast in interpretation.

`ensemble fairness` is not sufficient reason.

## 8. THREAD TAPERING
Subordinate threads should converge, pay off, transform, or close before the climax if they no longer alter the decisive local problem.

### Gate B103-G06 — NO ANTICLIMAX TAIL
Do not carry resolved subplots past the point where they cease changing the decisive choice merely to give every strand equal page time.

## 9. NO REHASH
Repeated information earns new space only when its status changes.

`SAME FACT + SAME SOURCE + SAME INTERPRETATION + SAME CONSEQUENCE = REHASH`

Possible legitimate return:
- new source;
- new context;
- changed reliability;
- changed interpretation;
- changed relationship meaning;
- new causal consequence;
- action now possible that was not possible before.

## 10. ENDING: KEEP FUNCTION, REJECT MORAL MECHANICS
Swain's moralized `secret self / principle / poetic justice / reward-punishment` system is not an IVDIVO law.

Retained functional ending engine:

`DOMINANT BOOK QUESTION → BOUNDED DECISIVE ACTION / CHOICE → CONSEQUENCE → COST → LOCAL RESOLUTION → AFTERMATH → CHANGED STATE → OPTIONAL SAGA VECTOR`

### Gate B103-G07 — SATISFYING != HAPPY
A satisfying ending may be:
- costly success;
- limited success;
- right refusal;
- tactical failure with decisive knowledge;
- partial reconciliation;
- operational mystery solved while global ontology remains unknown.

## 11. PROOF CEILING
The climax/end may conclude only what the book's evidence architecture can support.

`LOCAL CAUSALITY PROVEN` does not license `GLOBAL ORIGIN / METAPHYSICS PROVEN`.

This is mandatory for:
- consciousness-school phenomena;
- historical-civilization mysteries;
- temporal anomalies;
- robot personhood disputes;
- dimensional interpretations.

## 12. INTEGRATED STORY — SWAIN'S OWN ANTI-FORMULA LIMIT
Swain's ending discussion explicitly returns to the idea that analytical components are aids and that each story is a specific integrated case rather than a universal blueprint.

IVDIVO operationalization:

`TOOLS DIAGNOSE STORY; STORY DOES NOT EXIST TO VALIDATE TOOLS.`

## 13. CROSS-SOURCE COMPARISON
### Converges with
- LaPlante: theory after live material; scene/narration routing; no mandatory epiphany.
- Rosenfeld: contemplative/processing scenes; variable endings; transition economy.
- Bell: action/reaction and local closure, though Bell often overweights setback.
- Lodge / Prose / Le Guin / Zinsser: sentence length is functional, not a quality metric.
- Herman / Zunshine: evidence and causal explanation must not overclaim ontology.

### Tension with
- McKee/Truby/Brody when their stronger template language is treated as universal.
- Swain's own dated moral/gender/commercial assumptions.

## 15. PRODUCTION SUMMARY
For IVDIVO, the strongest middle/end architecture is not a line of ever-louder disasters. It is a sequence of qualitatively different pressures, real successes and failures, meaningful valleys, changed models and responsibilities, and a local ending whose proof does not exceed its evidence.
