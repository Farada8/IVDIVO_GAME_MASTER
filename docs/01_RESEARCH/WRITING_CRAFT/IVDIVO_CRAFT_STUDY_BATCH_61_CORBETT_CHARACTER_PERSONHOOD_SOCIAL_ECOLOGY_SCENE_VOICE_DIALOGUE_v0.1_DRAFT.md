# IVDIVO CRAFT STUDY — BATCH 61
## Corbett: Character Personhood · Desire · Social Ecology · Scene · POV · Voice · Dialogue

Date: 2026-08-17
Status: OPERATIONAL SYNTHESIS
Primary source: David Corbett — *The Art of Character* (strict lifecycle complete)

## Core synthesis
Corbett is most useful to IVDIVO when stripped of mandatory psychoanalytic interpretation and used as a personhood/behavior system.

`INDEPENDENT PERSON → SPECIFIC WANT → SOCIAL/RELATIONAL CONTEXT → CHARACTER-SPECIFIC TACTIC → PRESSURE → CHOICE → CONSEQUENCE → UPDATED MODEL`.

## High-value mechanisms
1. **Plot Puppet rejection** — every named major/supporting person needs a want and relationships beyond the protagonist-facing function.
2. **Nonfungible desire** — especially in post-money worlds, use trust, responsibility, consent, unique role, mastery, time, intimacy, belonging, reputation and experience rather than credits/status points.
3. **Frustration fingerprint** — obstacle sameness is acceptable; response sameness is not. Character differentiation lives in tactic choice and strategy update.
4. **Contextual contradiction** — apparently contradictory behavior becomes credible when relationship, power, risk or social setting changes.
5. **Multi-cause motive** — behavior supports motive hypotheses but does not prove one clean cause.
6. **Selective biography** — past belongs in the production model when it changes present behavior, not because a questionnaire asks for it.
7. **Embodiment causality** — body/sensorium/physical affordance must change choices and social response.
8. **Social ecology** — family, cohort, vocation, institution, habitat and lineage alter normality, leverage and obligation.
9. **Countermodel opposition** — strong opponents possess benefit, motive, evidence, leverage and cost/blind spot.
10. **Scene character engine** — externalize choice and responsibility; reflection stays only if it changes the next decision.
11. **POV ground** — who perceives, what they can know, what they infer, how close narration is.
12. **Voice system** — style + worldview + attitude + relation-specific register.
13. **Dialogue as tactic** — wording, silence and gesture are actions between people, not transcript.

## Corbett claims not promoted to IVDIVO law
- mandatory wound/trauma;
- mandatory secret/shame;
- psychoanalytic defense mechanisms as scientific truth;
- fixed human universals about gender/pathology/spirituality;
- every protagonist must transform;
- every opponent must be an equal-and-opposite antagonist;
- every scene must use overt conflict;
- one tidy motive explains behavior.

## Production gates
### Character gate
`OFF-AXIS WANT? / OFF-AXIS RELATIONSHIPS? / RIGHT TO REFUSE? / DISTINCT FIRST TACTIC? / DISTINCT SECOND TACTIC?`

### Ensemble gate
For two characters facing the same pressure, require at least one difference in:
`interpretation / immediate want / tactic / acceptable cost / person consulted / information sought`.

### Supporting-person gate
`BOOK FUNCTION ≠ STORYWORLD PERSONHOOD`.
A small role can remain small; the person cannot become a tool.

### POV gate
`OBSERVABLE / KNOWABLE / INFERRED / UNAVAILABLE` must be explicit for close POV.

### Dialogue gate
Direct speech earns space when exact words change:
`trust / consent / evidence / relationship / status / emotion / decision`.
Routine process is eligible for summary/indirect dialogue.

## IVDIVO implications
### Orbital Youth
Post-money life must produce different motivations, not merely delete money. The key diagnostic is: `What can robots/material abundance provide easily, and what still requires another person's choice, time, trust, skill or unique circumstance?`

### Conscious robots
Named conscious robots require personhood independent of service function. No universal AI speech style. Embodiment and culture should create affordances and habits without reducing the character to hardware.

### Consciousness schools
A practitioner is not equivalent to a doctrine. Social lineage, years of practice, relationships and reproducible effects shape the person; doctrine remains an interpretation until independently established.

### LESSON ZERO
Do not reopen development lock based on theory alone. Apply Corbett during regression only where a current character is demonstrably a plot puppet, tactically indistinct, POV-incoherent, or socially ungrounded.
