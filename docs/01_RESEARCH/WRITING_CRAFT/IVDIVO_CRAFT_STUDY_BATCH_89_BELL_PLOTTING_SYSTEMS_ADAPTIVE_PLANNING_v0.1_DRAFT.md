# IVDIVO CRAFT STUDY — BATCH 89
## Bell: Plotting Systems · Adaptive Planning · Outline-as-Hypothesis

Status: DRAFT / OPERATIONAL
Date: 2026-08-18
Primary source: James Scott Bell, *Plot & Structure*, Chapter 10

## Core synthesis
Planning is an uncertainty-reduction tool, not a personality identity and not canon.

`CURRENT UNCERTAINTY → CHOOSE MINIMAL PLANNING GRANULARITY → PLAN AS HYPOTHESIS → DRAFT → OBSERVE LIVE MATERIAL → REPLAN`

A stronger production loop:
`GENERATE → ORGANIZE → DRAFT → RE-OBSERVE → REPLAN → VALIDATE AGAINST CANON/CAUSALITY/PERSONHOOD`.

## Production gates
1. **Outline-as-Hypothesis:** outline may change when drafted material reveals a stronger causal/character path.
2. **Planning-Tool Fit:** cards, grid, treatment, headlights, letter-to-self, chapter summaries, Borg-style prompts are alternative tools, not simultaneous requirements.
3. **Granularity Fit:** plan only to the precision needed for the current uncertainty. Overplanning can destroy discovery.
4. **Generation Before Validation:** generate alternatives before canon/red-team filtering.
5. **Premise Compression:** back-cover/pitch summary may test causal clarity but does not become canonical truth.
6. **Living Manuscript Evidence:** character-specific behavior discovered in prose can justify replanning if it remains compatible with canon and ONE MAIN STORY.
7. **No Three-Act Numerology:** Bell percentages/act placement remain heuristics, not IVDIVO laws.
8. **No Plotter/Pantser Identity Lock:** writers may change method by project, stage, chapter, or uncertainty type.
9. **No Control-Document Leakage:** production specs guide the story; they do not appear in prose as exposition or force scenes to exist solely to satisfy a checklist.
10. **Robot/Collective Firewall:** Bell's Borg metaphor is only a planning metaphor. It says nothing about conscious robots, collective cognition, or IVDIVO ontology.

## Failure modes
PLOTTER/PANTSER IDENTITY LOCK; OUTLINE-AS-CANON; DISCOVERY SUPPRESSION; PLAN OVERGRANULARITY; CONTROL-DOCUMENT LEAK; BACK-COVER-COPY TYRANNY; PRODUCTIVITY SUPERSTITION; BORG FUNCTIONALIZATION; THREE-ACT NUMEROLOGY.

## Writers' Room rule
Do not send every discovery draft through all specialist gates simultaneously. First discover live material; then perform structural/canon/personhood/evidence passes in controlled sequence.
