## Main mechanisms added/strengthened

1. `PRIOR CONSEQUENCE → SCENE INTENTION → COMPLICATION → RESULT → NEXT INTENTION`.
2. Plot-based intention vs scene-specific intention.
3. Support/partial success is as important as opposition; permanent thwarting can stall plot.
4. Scene-ending operators: zoom-in / zoom-out translated into decision, consequence, reveal, reframing, pressure transfer and question upgrade.
5. Transition economy cross-supported by Gary Provost and James Scott Bell.
6. Contemplative scenes used as recovery/decision chambers, not parking lots.
7. First-scene burden: protagonist + current problem + ordinary world + curiosity, without encyclopedic exposition.
8. Adjacent-scene integrity: each scene evaluated as consequence of previous and cause of next.
