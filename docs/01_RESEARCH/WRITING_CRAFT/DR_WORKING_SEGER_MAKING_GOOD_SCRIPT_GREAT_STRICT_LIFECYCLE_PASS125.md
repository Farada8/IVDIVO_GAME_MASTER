## HIGH-VALUE MECHANISMS NOW CONFIRMED

### 1. FUNCTIONAL STRUCTURE, NOT PAGE ARITHMETIC
Seger's screenplay page ranges are historical/form-specific guidance, not IVDIVO laws.

Operational translation:
`PRIOR STATE → MATERIAL EVENT / DECISION / REVELATION → CHANGED OPTION SPACE → NEW GOVERNING PROBLEM / STRATEGY`

A transition is structural only if it changes objective, evidence, relationship/consent, authority/access, responsibility, model/strategy, cost, or future options.

### 2. MIDPOINT = MATERIAL RE-WEIGHTING
A midpoint need not be false victory/false defeat or a catastrophe.
It may strengthen, reframe, focus, or re-weight the central problem without reversing the whole story.

### 3. SUBPLOT VS THREAD
A true subplot has local causality and changes the A-story or a major character's later choices.
A recurring person/activity/motif may be a thread without needing a full independent plot architecture.

Operational test:
`LOCAL WANT → LOCAL CHANGE → EFFECT ON A-STORY / ANOTHER STRAND → PAYOFF`

### 4. MOMENTUM ≠ ACTION DENSITY
Adding chases, fights, attacks, anomalies, or louder incidents does not necessarily create momentum.

Operational model:
`MOMENTUM = CAUSAL STATE CHANGE + PRESSURE ON THE NEXT OPTION`

Slow scenes may have strong momentum; fast scenes may be structurally static.

### 5. OFFSCREEN / IMPLICIT EVENTS ARE LEGITIMATE
Not every consequential event needs full dramatization.

Router:
`OFFSCREEN EVENT / SUMMARY → VISIBLE CONSEQUENCE → NEXT ACTION`

This directly supports compression of logistics and repeated technical process.

### 6. FAIR SURPRISE
A reveal may overturn the reader's inference; it must not contradict established fact merely for surprise.

`TRUE TRACE → READER MODEL A → NEW CONTEXT/EVIDENCE → MODEL B → RETROSPECTIVE SUPPORT`

### 7. SCENE SEQUENCES
A cluster of linked scenes may form a local mini-story with its own question, turns, and payoff.
Not every individual scene needs a complete miniature three-act structure.

`SEQUENCE QUESTION → LINKED STATE CHANGES → SEQUENCE PAYOFF`

### 8. POV / INFORMATION ACCESS
POV controls access to information, emotional alignment, and suspense.
Multi-POV and crosscutting are legitimate when each switch buys unique evidence, action, relationship pressure, irony, or context.

`POV SWITCH → UNIQUE INFORMATION / EXPERIENCE → EFFECT ON MODEL OR ACTION`

### 9. VOICEOVER / RESTATEMENT
Narrative commentary earns its place when it adds layer, contrast, interiority, or information unavailable through immediate action.
It fails when it merely repeats what the reader already understood.

### 10. FLASHBACK PRESENT-CONSEQUENCE
Flashback is not prohibited; it must change the present reader/character model.

`PAST EVENT → PRESENT INTERPRETATION / EVIDENCE / RELATIONSHIP / DECISION CHANGES`

### 11. SCENE MULTIFUNCTION
A scene may advance plot, reveal character, develop theme, create image/world experience, or combine several functions.
These are options, not a mandatory checklist.

### 12. EXPOSITION OWNERSHIP
Information should enter through a character/story need, not because the author wants to brief the audience.

`WHO NEEDS/WANTS X? → WHY NOW? → SOURCE/ACCESS → MINIMUM NECESSARY INFO → ACTION/INTERPRETATION/DECISION → CHANGED STATE`

### 13. SCENE ENTRY / EXIT
Determine what a scene must accomplish, then enter at the latest point that preserves orientation and causal pressure.
Exit when the scene has produced its relevant changed state.

### 14. UNITY THROUGH SETUP / PAYOFF / MOTIF / CONTRAST
Repeated elements should accumulate or alter meaning.

`RETURN OF ELEMENT → NEW CONTEXT / NEW FUNCTION / NEW MEANING`

Repetition without new function is drag.

### 15. CHANGE IMPACT MAP
Changing a major scene/reveal may invalidate earlier setup, later payoff, character logic, and evidence architecture.
No isolated structural patch without downstream regression testing.

## IVDIVO FIREWALLS
- Screenplay page percentages are not novel/audio laws.
- Physical action is not the only form of story action.
- Success may create more responsibility rather than mandatory setback.
- Relationship state does not alter evidence status by itself.
- Conscious nonhuman persons retain independent objective, model, consent/refusal, and decision ownership.
- School/practitioner interpretation remains interpretation until independently tested.
- `MOMENTUM ≠ SPEED ≠ SHORT-SENTENCE DENSITY`.
