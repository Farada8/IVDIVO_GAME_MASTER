## 4. MAJOR MECHANISMS ADDED
1. Object of desire and motivation are separate design variables.
2. Qualitative progression outranks numeric danger escalation.
3. Capability limits generate strategy and prevent “why not just...” holes.
4. Specific opposing tactics are stronger than generic character flaws.
5. Set pieces must alter causal/action state, not merely provide spectacle.
6. Rhythm, tempo and intensity are distinct; sentence length is not a proxy for all three.
7. Repetition produces diminishing reader effect unless meaning/function changes.
8. Climax transfers as bounded decisive choice, not mandatory lethal sacrifice.
9. Local resolution should show relevant ripple and stop before franchise inflation.
10. Subgenre can identify source of opposition: environment, institution, individual strategy, time/irreversibility.
11. Labyrinth/geography is strongest when physical rules change available actions.
12. Form is not formula; page-count/Whammo/Hero's-Journey retrofit is rejected.
