## Synthesis target

Use Murderbot as a mechanism lab for writing conscious robots/nonhuman persons without collapsing personhood into humanity, competence into moral authority, or observed behavior into motive.

## Gate 115.01 — PERSONHOOD ≠ HUMANITY

A nonhuman person is not “developing” merely because it becomes more socially human, acquires a human body, adopts human sexuality, or enjoys more human customs.

Required personhood evidence:
`OWN WANT → OWN MODEL/EVIDENCE → OWN RELATIONSHIPS → CONSENT/REFUSAL → CHOICE → CONSEQUENCE`.

## Gate 115.02 — CONTROL PROVENANCE BEFORE BLAME

Before inferring motive/responsibility from behavior:
`ACTION → EXTERNAL CONTROL? → AVAILABLE CHOICE? → MODEL OF MOTIVE → RESPONSIBILITY STATUS`.

Apply to robots, biological persons under coercive systems, distributed minds, institutional agents and any future IVDIVO control technology.

## Gate 115.03 — EMBODIMENT CONSENT

Any body modification that changes appearance, sensorium, mobility, social legibility or risk must have a clear decision owner.

`OPTIMIZATION VALUE TO OTHERS ≠ CONSENT OF SUBJECT`.

## Gate 115.04 — COMPLEMENTARY INTELLIGENCE

Do not give every form of intelligence the same strengths.

Strong ensemble architecture:
`AGENT A DATA/COMPUTE → AGENT B EMBODIED ACCESS → AGENT C SOCIAL/LEGAL CONTEXT → AGENT D LIVED EXPERIENCE → BOUNDED DECISION OWNER`.

The climax improves when no single protagonist owns all sensing, interpretation and action.

## Gate 115.05 — PEER CHALLENGE, NOT ORACLE

A more capable AI/robot may challenge another agent's model, but must not become an infallible authorial oracle.

`CLAIM → COUNTERMODEL → DISCRIMINATING EVIDENCE → UPDATE`.

## Gate 115.06 — AUTONOMY ≠ ISOLATION

Autonomy requires the ability to choose connection as well as separation.

Valid arc:
`COERCED ROLE → FREED OPTION SET → VOLUNTARY DUTY/TEAM/RELATIONSHIP → REVOCABLE CONTINUATION`.

## Gate 115.07 — NONHUMAN RELATIONSHIPS NEED NOT MAP TO HUMAN TEMPLATES

Do not force every close robot/nonhuman bond into romance, parent/child, sibling or pet categories.

Relationship can be built through:
- shared epistemic work,
- negotiated boundaries,
- competence asymmetry,
- mutual protection,
- privacy negotiation,
- argument and repaired trust.

## Gate 115.08 — RELATIONSHIP STATE ≠ EVIDENCE STATUS

Trusting a robot or person can change what evidence they share and which test becomes possible. It cannot change what a trace objectively proves.

## Gate 115.09 — CONTROL REMOVAL ≠ COMPLETE LIBERATION

After technical control is removed, audit downstream dependencies:
`LEGAL STATUS → ACCESS → RELATIONSHIPS → RESOURCES/INFRASTRUCTURE → SOCIAL CATEGORY → FUTURE OPTION SPACE`.

## Gate 115.10 — SELF-KNOWLEDGE THROUGH EVIDENCE

Avoid narrator absolution or a single therapeutic speech.

Prefer:
`SELF-ACCUSATION / SELF-STORY → TRACE → COMPETING EXPLANATION → TEST → MODEL UPDATE → NEW CHOICE`.

## Failure modes

- Humanization-as-growth.
- Robot voice monoculture.
- AI-therapist/oracle exposition.
- Control-layer removal treated as moral clean slate.
- Intelligence hierarchy treated as ethical hierarchy.
- Nonhuman relationship forced into human romance/family template.
- Body redesign decided by caretakers/engineers rather than subject.
- Corporate-scarcity worldbuilding imported into post-money orbital cities.
- Technical control mechanics used as evidence for soul/consciousness transfer.

## IVDIVO production use

### Orbital Youth
Conscious robots must have off-protagonist relationships, own refusals, own ordinary life and own reasons to join/leave a team.

### B07 — THE UNLISTED CITY
Once distributed life clears the personhood threshold, humans lose the right to frame the climax solely as “how should we preserve it?” The entity must alter the decision space itself.

### Consciousness schools
No transfer from fictional governor modules/body modifications to IVDIVO metaphysics. Keep observed practice and ontology separate.

## Final gate sentence

**If a nonhuman character can be replaced by a smart tool without changing consent, motive, relationships or the final decision, personhood has not been written yet.**
