## 3. Primary claims retained

### A. Story, discourse, manifestation are different analytic layers
A story-event can be sound while its placement, duration, focalization or medium-specific rendering is wrong. Repair must target the defective layer instead of rewriting the underlying canon event automatically.

### B. Narrative is incomplete by design
Readers infer omitted links. Routine logistics need not be dramatized if causal continuity remains recoverable. This directly supports compression against micro-dramatization.

### C. Causality is not the only organizer
Classical narratives may be strongly causal; modern narratives may organize through contingency, state-of-affairs revelation, juxtaposition, consciousness or thematic relation. Quiet causality must still create meaningful downstream effects.

### D. Kernels and satellites are contextual, partly retrospective functions
A kernel changes the branching possibility of the story; satellites elaborate, prepare, characterize, foreshadow or resonate. Satellite does not mean worthless. A seemingly minor element can become kernel-like through later payoff.

### E. Suspense and surprise are distinct
Suspense can survive known outcomes; surprise depends on information management. Neither requires short sentences or cliffhangers.

### F. Order / Duration / Frequency are first-class narrative controls
Scene, summary, ellipsis, stretch and pause are discourse choices. Summary can occur inside scenes and brief scenic moments inside summaries.

### G. Character and setting are existents, not merely plot servants
Characters are reconstructed as open models from evidence. Trait inference is provisional and can be revised by contradictory behavior. Setting can perform mood, social, causal, symbolic and verisimilitude functions.

### H. POV is not narrative voice
`WHO PERCEIVES ≠ WHO CONCEPTUALIZES ≠ WHOSE INTERESTS ARE CENTERED ≠ WHO SPEAKS`. Close access does not require the narrator to use the character's exact vocabulary.

### I. Knowledge/access and narrator authority must be distinguished
A narrator can know more, less or differently from a character; discourse may shift mental access. Knowledge limitation is a design variable, not a synonym for first/third person.

### J. Unreliable narration is a discourse problem, not an immoral-person test
A narrator is unreliable when the narrated account conflicts with the story-model the implied reader can reconstruct. An unpleasant narrator may still be reliable; an honest narrator may be mistaken.

### K. Presupposition can smuggle claims
Narrative wording can present disputed information as already-given. Mystery/evidence prose must audit presupposed claims so interpretation does not silently become fact.

### L. Narrative theory should describe possibilities, not prescribe formulas
Chatman's own conclusion keeps definitions provisional and explicitly welcomes counterexamples. This strongly supports IVDIVO's anti-overfit law.

## 4. Mechanism bank

### M-CHAT-01 — Story / Discourse Router
`WORLD/STORY FACT → DISCOURSE SELECTION/ORDER/DURATION → READER MODEL → ACTION/INFERENCE`

### M-CHAT-02 — Inference Gap
`A → omitted recoverable transition → B`
Use when the missing process is routine and does not itself change decision, relationship, evidence or authority.

### M-CHAT-03 — Kernel/Satellite Test
`REMOVE EVENT → does possibility-space / decisive later state change?`
If yes, kernel-like. If no, test whether it still earns character, mood, foreshadowing, rhythm, verisimilitude or payoff.

### M-CHAT-04 — Time Control
`ORDER + DURATION + FREQUENCY → reader emphasis / suspense / interpretation`

### M-CHAT-05 — POV/Voice Matrix
`PERCEIVER | CONCEPTUALIZER | INTEREST OWNER | NARRATING VOICE | KNOWLEDGE ACCESS`

### M-CHAT-06 — Character Evidence Loop
`BEHAVIOR / SPEECH / RELATIONSHIP / REPORT → TRAIT HYPOTHESIS → CONTRADICTORY OR SUPPORTING EVIDENCE → MODEL UPDATE`

### M-CHAT-07 — Setting Causality
`SETTING RULE / MILIEU → AFFORDANCE / PRESSURE → CHOICE → CONSEQUENCE`

### M-CHAT-08 — Presupposition Audit
`SENTENCE → what is asserted? → what is presupposed? → is the presupposition established, sourced, inferred or contested?`

### M-CHAT-09 — Unreliability Test
`NARRATOR ACCOUNT → INDEPENDENT TRACE / STORY RECONSTRUCTION → DISCREPANCY → RELIABILITY STATUS`

### M-CHAT-10 — Medium Translation
`ABSTRACT NARRATIVE FUNCTION → MEDIUM AFFORDANCE / CONSTRAINT → IMPLEMENTATION`
Do not import film staging, screenplay whitespace or visual determinacy directly into prose.

## 5. Failure modes

- **STORY/DISCOURSE CONFLATION** — rewriting canon when the defect is placement/duration/POV.
- **CAUSALITY MONOCULTURE** — forcing every quiet or modern scene into overt conflict mechanics.
- **LOGISTICS OVERDRAMATIZATION** — refusing recoverable inference gaps.
- **KERNEL-BY-TEMPLATE** — calling a beat important because a framework names it.
- **SATELLITE PURGE** — deleting lived reality, humor, atmosphere or relational preparation because it is not a branching event.
- **SUSPENSE=SURPRISE COLLAPSE** — confusing known-danger pressure with withheld information.
- **TIME-BLIND REVISION** — ignoring order/duration/frequency before line editing.
- **POV=VOICE COLLAPSE** — forcing narrator diction to equal character diction.
- **ACCESS=OMNISCIENCE CONFUSION** — giving a POV knowledge it cannot possess.
- **UNRELIABLE-NARRATOR PATCH** — using unreliability to excuse continuity errors or unfair withholding.
- **PRESUPPOSITION SMUGGLING** — embedding a hypothesis grammatically as a fact.
- **TRAIT ESSENTIALISM** — treating one behavior as proof of permanent character essence.
- **SETTING CATALOGUE** — worldbuilding without affordance, pressure, relation or reader-model function.
- **SCREENPLAY SPATIAL LEAKAGE** — importing film's compulsory visual specification into prose.
- **INTERIOR-MONOLOGUE FRAGMENT FETISH** — assuming thought requires fragment density.
- **TAXONOMY OVERFIT** — forcing complex narratives into inherited universal plot categories.

## 6. Cross-source comparison

### With Truby / Bell / Swain
Chatman supplies a needed corrective: event causality is powerful but not exhaustive. IVDIVO keeps ONE MAIN STORY and downstream state change while allowing epistemic, relational and contemplative progression.

### With Scofield / Rosenfeld / LaPlante
Strong convergence: full scene and summary are modes, not status rankings. Chatman's duration system gives a more formal basis for scene-weight routing.

### With Zunshine / Herman / le Carré
Strong convergence on evidence discipline: POV interpretation, narrator account and world fact must remain separate. Chatman adds presupposition and unreliable-discourse analysis.

### With Card / Corbett / McKee Character
Chatman strengthens open character models: traits are hypotheses derived from patterns, not exhaustive essences; contradictory behavior need not invalidate the whole character.

### With Doty / Hodgins / Wolf
Setting and description are not neutral catalogues. Selection creates discourse-space; setting can carry social, emotional and causal function.

### With Le Guin / Prose / Zinsser / Hodgins
Chatman's scene/summary/ellipsis framework reinforces that pacing is not sentence-length percentage. Choppiness is a later-level prose issue after duration/mode selection.

## 7. IVDIVO operationalization

### ONE MAIN STORY
Multiple POVs/strands are allowed. Each strand must still alter the dominant causal/epistemic/relational engine. Parallel strands may have distinct local NOWs.

### Mystery / evidence
Store separately:
`WORLD FACT → TRACE/SOURCE → POV ACCESS → INTERPRETATION → DISCOURSE PLACEMENT → READER MODEL → ACTION`.
Never let presupposition silently upgrade a theory to fact.

### YA / character
A teenager can remain partly opaque and still be legible. Character depth comes from patterns, contradictions, relationships, choices and evolving reader models, not mandatory wounds or traits.

### Conscious nonhuman persons
More sensors do not equal omniscience. Their discourse-space follows embodiment + priorities + task + relationship + knowledge limits. Human trait labels remain hypotheses.

### Consciousness-practice schools
`PRACTITIONER OBSERVATION → REPRODUCIBLE EFFECT → SCHOOL INTERPRETATION → COMPETING INTERPRETATION → INDEPENDENT TEST → ONTOLOGY CEILING`.
Narrative vividness or a narrator's generalization cannot raise ontology status.

### Prose / anti-choppiness
`BOOK ENGINE → CHAPTER FUNCTION → SCENE ADMISSION → ORDER/DURATION/FREQUENCY → SCENE WEIGHT/MODE → POV/EVIDENCE → PARAGRAPH INTENT → READER-STATE CONTINUITY → SENTENCE RHYTHM → WORDING`.
Routine recoverable transitions should often be omitted or summarized rather than rendered as microbeats.

## 9. Final synthesis

Chatman's durable value for IVDIVO is not a new formula but a routing discipline: **first identify whether the problem belongs to story, discourse, medium, knowledge access, temporal arrangement, character inference or narrator reliability; only then edit the prose.** This directly supports ONE MAIN STORY while protecting quiet scenes, multiple POVs, summary, ambiguity, nonhuman personhood and fair-play mystery from structural overfitting.
