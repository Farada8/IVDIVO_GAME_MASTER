## 0. Purpose

Advance James Scott Bell's *Voice: The Secret Power of Great Writing* without pretending a full read is complete, cross-check its mechanisms against existing IVDIVO craft sources, and turn them into production gates for LESSON ZERO and the active Orbital/Confederation-cadet youth line.

Primary problem addressed:

> How do we stop technically competent prose from sounding like one intelligent author speaking through every character, every narrator and every institution?

---

# 1. Bell's strongest usable mechanism — character filter before style decoration

Bell's CAP formula can be abstracted as:

`CHARACTER MATERIAL + AUTHORIAL SENSIBILITY + PAGE CRAFT → VOICE`.

For IVDIVO the useful part is not the branding of the formula but the causal order:

`WHO IS PERCEIVING? → WHAT DO THEY NOTICE? → WHAT DOES IT MEAN TO THEM? → WHAT LANGUAGE/RHYTHM WOULD THEY USE? → ONLY THEN STYLE`.

This prevents a common generated-prose defect:

`AUTHOR HAS AN INTERESTING IMAGE → IMAGE INSERTED → CHARACTER COULD HAVE BEEN ANYONE`.

IVDIVO replacement:

`OBJECT / EVENT → CHARACTER-SPECIFIC ATTENTION → CHARACTER-SPECIFIC VALUE JUDGMENT → WORD CHOICE / RHYTHM`.

## Gate B32-G01 — CHARACTER FILTER BEFORE FIGURE

Before accepting a metaphor, simile, joke, descriptive detail or internal observation, ask:

1. Why does this character notice it?
2. Why now?
3. Why this comparison?
4. Would another POV character describe it differently?

If not, the line is probably author-voice leakage.

---

# 2. Dominant impression is a drafting accelerator, not a complete character

Bell uses a compact dominant-impression prompt. IVDIVO adopts only the fast diagnostic value.

Useful shorthand:

`HOW THIS PERSON GENERALLY MOVES THROUGH THE WORLD + WHAT ROLE THEY OCCUPY`.

But this cannot replace contradiction.

Example:

- Ethan: procedural/status-sensitive future professional.
- Maya: autonomy-sensitive social/physical challenger.
- Ollie: tool-first practical maker.
- Aoife: context/provenance-sensitive recorder.
- Samir: cautious pattern recognizer with strong boundary logic.

These are **starting filters**, not complete voices.

## Failure mode

If every line is optimized to prove the dominant impression, the character becomes a mascot.

## Gate B32-G02 — DOMINANT IMPRESSION + CONTRADICTION

Every major character voice sheet needs at least:

`BASE FILTER | WHAT BREAKS IT | WHAT THEY HIDE | WHAT THEY MISREAD | WHAT THEY FIND FUNNY | WHAT THEY REFUSE TO SAY`.

---

# 3. Voice journal as laboratory, not manuscript

Bell's voice-journal mechanism is useful because it allows uncontrolled lexical/rhythmic discovery outside canon prose.

IVDIVO use:

Write 500–1000 noncanonical words in a character's voice on a subject that matters personally but is not the book's exposition task.

Better prompts:
- first terrible job;
- the thing they hate about their room;
- who in the cohort irritates them and why;
- what they would buy if given one month's pay;
- what they miss from Earth;
- what Confederation adults misunderstand about teenagers;
- what they are embarrassed to enjoy;
- the last time they lied to a parent.

Do **not** ask them to explain the world bible.

## Gate B32-G03 — VOICE TEST OUTSIDE EXPOSITION

A voice is not proven by a clever paragraph explaining technology. It is proven when the character can talk/think distinctly about ordinary life.

---

# 4. Attitude is more valuable than slang

Bell's YA examples reinforce an important mechanism: intimacy comes from a narrator/POV character having an attitude toward what is happening, not merely from contractions, slang or pop-culture references.

IVDIVO YA voice hierarchy:

`ATTITUDE → SELECTION OF DETAIL → RHYTHM → VOCABULARY → SLANG (optional, low priority)`.

Bad youth calibration:

> Add current slang to adult policy reasoning.

Better:

> Give the teen a concrete social stake, incomplete authority, embarrassment, attraction, resentment, status anxiety, boredom or private joke; let those pressures change what they notice and how quickly they speak.

## Gate B32-G04 — NO SLANG SUBSTITUTE

If removing slang makes every teenager sound like the same adult analyst, the voice pass has failed.

---

# 5. YA intimacy does not require first person

Bell shows first-person YA effectively but does not establish it as mandatory. Existing IVDIVO close-third work already benefits from limited distance.

For IVDIVO:

`CLOSE THIRD + CHARACTER-SPECIFIC FREE INDIRECT FILTER` can preserve intimacy while allowing ensemble control.

The key test is not pronoun choice. It is whether description and interpretation belong to the POV.

Example pattern:

Neutral:
> The training hall contained six embodiment cradles and a field-control station.

Cadet who wants access:
> Six cradles. Two occupied, three sealed, one with the kind of yellow authorization band that made Ethan want to know exactly which sentence in the rules said no.

Same world fact; different voice.

## Gate B32-G05 — PRONOUNS DO NOT CREATE INTIMACY

No POV choice passes on grammatical person alone. Test character-filter density instead.

---

# 6. Description must reveal both world and observer

Bell's contrast between generic description and voice-filtered description is especially valuable for SF.

IVDIVO world-reveal rule becomes:

`WORLD OBJECT → FUNCTION IN CURRENT ACTION → CHARACTER ATTITUDE → SOCIAL IMPLICATION`.

Do not stop the story for catalog description.

### In a Confederation-built training city

Do not write:

> The city used advanced adaptive matter, embodiment laboratories and multidimensional simulation systems.

Write situations such as:

- a cadet's dorm wall changes thermal permeability because a nonhuman roommate returns early;
- a training body is checked out like restricted equipment and the deposit exceeds a month's youth stipend;
- a class is delayed because one species' environmental corridor has priority during a field transition;
- a student fails to recognize their friend after a supervised embodiment exercise because the gait is wrong;
- a cafeteria argument begins because a food synthesizer has classified one human fermented food as a controlled microbial culture.

Technology enters through inconvenience, money, desire, status and error.

## Gate B32-G06 — DESCRIPTION MUST PAY TWICE

Every significant SF description should pay at least two functions from:

`ACTION | CHARACTER | SOCIAL STATUS | RELATIONSHIP | CONFLICT | HUMOR | CLUE | FUTURE PAYOFF`.

---

# 7. Fresh figurative language must be character-owned

Bell encourages unusual metaphors and linguistic surprise. This is useful but dangerous for IVDIVO because generated prose easily becomes a chain of decorative comparisons.

Adopt:

`SURPRISE + FIT`.

Reject:

`SURPRISE FOR ITS OWN SAKE`.

A technical cadet may compare a social failure to an overloaded bus, feedback loop or bad seal.
A gravity athlete may think in momentum, balance, launch angle and recovery.
A recorder may think in clipping, missing context, room tone and edits.

## Gate B32-G07 — METAPHOR DOMAIN OWNERSHIP

Track each major character's recurring comparison domains. If everyone reaches for the same poetic imagery, authorial homogenization is occurring.

---

# 8. Cadence is part of voice, but cannot become a fingerprint gimmick

Bell's comparison of clipped and more expansive voices reinforces an existing IVDIVO conclusion: sentence length/rhythm can distinguish mode and character, but fixed ratios create artificiality.

Voice cadence dimensions:

- sentence expansion tolerance;
- interruption frequency;
- parenthetical thinking;
- directness versus qualification;
- noun-heavy vs verb-heavy observation;
- willingness to name emotion;
- joke placement;
- silence after conflict.

## Gate B32-G08 — CADENCE RANGE, NOT CADENCE COSTUME

A character can have tendencies, not a single repeated syntax.

Reject:
- Samir always = one-line deadpan;
- Maya always = interruption;
- Ethan always = numbered procedure;
- Aoife always = media metaphor;
- Ollie always = tool joke.

Predictability kills voice as surely as sameness.

---

# 9. Emotion should alter perception before it is explained

Bell's emotion section is compatible with Oatley/Rosenfeld: the reader should enter the emotional simulation through changed attention and action, not receive a moral label afterward.

Preferred:

`EVENT → BODY / ATTENTION SHIFT → CHOICE / SPEECH → IMPLICATION`.

Weak:

`EVENT → “he felt responsible” → explanation of why responsibility matters`.

## Gate B32-G09 — EMOTION CHANGES SELECTION

When emotion matters, ask what object/sound/person the POV notices differently because of it.

---

# 10. Voice variation across the saga

Bell explicitly argues that strong writers can vary voice to match the story. This matters for IVDIVO because the saga contains different lines.

### LESSON ZERO
Close, procedural, status-sensitive, dryly funny, contemporary YA social texture.

### ORBITAL / CONFEDERATION CADET YOUTH
More kinetic and sensory because bodies, gravity, environmental zones, transport and mixed-species life continually alter physical experience. Still youth-close; never a tourist brochure.

### SMITH / OLD EARTH SECURITY
Older institutional register, compressed threat assessment, historical weight, dry professional humor, greater negative space. Do not make Smith sound like Ethan aged up.

### Far-future embodiment books
Potentially more estranged sensory language because body/environment assumptions change. Terminology follows observable phenomenon, not precedes it.

## Gate B32-G10 — SERIES VOICE DIFFERENTIATION

A new line must earn a different narrative texture while preserving IVDIVO's global prose laws.

# 11. Cross-source correction

## Bell + Hodgins
Voice establishes reader relationship. Therefore line-level voice choices cannot be assessed separately from narrator distance and tone.

## Bell + Rasley
Character voice cannot grant knowledge the POV does not possess. Distinctiveness never overrides information access.

## Bell + Rosenfeld
Voice cannot rescue a scene without intention, resistance and change.

## Bell + Williams/Bizup
Freshness cannot break coherence. If a metaphor costs clarity, remove it.

## Bell + Oatley
Emotion is strongest when the reader can adopt the character's goal/plan. Voice supports identification; it is not a substitute for desire.

## Bell + Zunshine
In mystery, attitude may bias interpretation but must not silently convert belief into fact.

# 12. Active Book 2 application — Founder-directed Confederation training city

Newest Founder direction currently changes the Orbital Youth development target:

> the most radical red/orange technologies belong around Confederation educational/training centers; Confederation built a city at the edge of the Solar System where cadets are trained.

Production implication:

This environment should **not** sound more adult merely because the technology is more advanced.

The harder rule is:

**THE MORE ALIEN/ADVANCED THE TECHNOLOGY, THE MORE HUMANLY SPECIFIC THE POV MUST BECOME.**

Otherwise prose becomes encyclopedia.

### Cadet voice tests

A cadet encountering transferable embodiment should first care about something like:
- qualification score;
- being assigned the embarrassing body class;
- whether a friend recognizes them;
- deposit/liability;
- losing a weekend pass;
- being beaten by a rival;
- nausea/sensory mismatch;
- whether parents on Earth will see the recording;
- whether a synthetic cadet gets different rules.

Ontology comes later.

### Training-city voice rule

Confederation instructors may use precise professional vocabulary.
Cadets should not automatically absorb their syntax.

In mixed dialogue, status difference should be audible:

`INSTRUCTOR: exact scope / safety language`
`EARTH CADET: translates into familiar analogy`
`ORBIT-BORN CADET: treats station constraints as ordinary`
`NONHUMAN CADET: may notice assumptions humans do not know they are making`
`SYNTHETIC CADET: must have a personal voice, not “robot speech” by default`.

# 13. Voice matrix required before active Book 2 prose

For every major cadet record:

`PRIVATE WANT`
`STATUS FEAR`
`HOME / CLASS / FAMILY BACKGROUND`
`WHAT THEY NOTICE FIRST`
`DEFAULT ATTITUDE UNDER PRESSURE`
`METAPHOR DOMAINS`
`HUMOR METHOD`
`SENTENCE/RHYTHM TENDENCY`
`WHAT THEY OVEREXPLAIN`
`WHAT THEY REFUSE TO EXPLAIN`
`WHAT MAKES THEM SILENT`
`HOW THEY SOUND TO ADULTS`
`HOW THEY SOUND TO FRIENDS`
`HOW THEY SOUND WHEN ATTRACTED / JEALOUS / ASHAMED`
`ONE SURPRISING MODE THAT BREAKS THE PATTERN`.

This is a pre-prose specification, not character trivia.

# 14. Book One validation

Development-locked Book One already contains useful voice differentiation:

- Ethan converts uncertainty into schedules, rules and access questions;
- Maya attacks weak status/control logic;
- Ollie uses practical/tool reasoning;
- Aoife thinks in recording/context;
- Samir tends toward sparse, qualified observation.

Current risk is not total sameness. It is **over-regularization**: if these signatures become predictable jokes, the characters flatten.

Therefore Book One remains development-locked. No rewrite from this study alone.

Allowed future Book One action only if external/beta evidence finds:
- generic interior narration;
- same-wit ensemble effect;
- authorial metaphor inconsistent with POV;
- tone break;
- accidental adult-policy voice in teen dialogue.

# 16. Current verdict

Bell *Voice* is high-value for **character-specific filtering and prose differentiation**, particularly as IVDIVO moves from familiar Earth youth into Confederation-built training environments with radical technology.

Adopt:
- character filter;
- attitude;
- voice journal laboratory;
- voice variation;
- emotion-driven detail selection;
- controlled figurative surprise.

Modify/reject:
- no mandatory wound/event at a fixed age;
- no assumption YA requires first-person present;
- no forced metaphors;
- no “fresh voice” that sacrifices clarity or POV truth;
- no line-level voice work before story/scene function is stable.

**Lifecycle remains open. FULL READ not claimed.**
