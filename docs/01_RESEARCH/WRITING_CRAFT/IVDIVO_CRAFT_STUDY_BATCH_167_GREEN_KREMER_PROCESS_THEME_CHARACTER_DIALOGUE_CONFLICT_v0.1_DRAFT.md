# IVDIVO CRAFT STUDY — BATCH 167
## Green & Kremer — Process · Theme · Character · Dialogue · Conflict

**Source status:** FULL READ IN PROGRESS. This batch is provisional and may be revised after the complete book is read.

## Why this source matters

This is a broad 2007 novel-writing handbook rather than a specialist theory text. Its value is mainly as a cross-check against already stronger schools. Its broad prescriptions are therefore treated as candidate mechanisms, not authority by default.

## Mechanism synthesis

### B167-G01 — Process Pluralism

Useful claim: writers can plan, draft directly, use cards, use summaries or combine methods.

**Gate:**
`METHOD MUST REDUCE THE CURRENT UNCERTAINTY OR DEFECT; IT DOES NOT DEFINE THE STORY.`

Cross-source convergence: Lamott, LaPlante, Swain, Bell, Seger, Mayer.

### B167-G02 — Plan / Text Separation

**Gate:**
`PLAN → DRAFT EVIDENCE → CHARACTER/CANON/CAUSALITY TEST → KEEP / REPLAN / BANK / DISCARD`.

A neat outline cannot overrule a stronger causal relationship discovered in the live manuscript.

### B167-G03 — Reader-Time Hierarchy

Narrative length is itself a signal of importance.

**Gate:**
`FUNCTIONAL IMPORTANCE ↔ DISCOURSE WEIGHT`.

If routine setup, a decisive consent change and a major evidence turn receive the same page weight, the text communicates a false hierarchy.

### B167-G04 — Theme Through Consequence

**Gate:**
`THEMATIC QUESTION → COMPETING MODELS → CHOICE → CONSEQUENCE → READER INFERENCE`.

Delete the philosophical speech: if the theme disappears, causality is too weak.

### B167-G05 — Stakes Are Character-Relative

The objective size of the prize matters less than what it means to the character.

For post-money IVDIVO this is especially important. Strong stakes include trust, time, consent, privacy, responsibility, belonging, mastery, unique access/opportunity, relationships and safety.

### B167-G06 — Opening Contract

Replace `begin with a bang` with:

`ATTENTION → ORIENTATION → PERSON/SUBJECT BOND → STORY PROMISE → FORWARD PRESSURE`.

Action is one possible mode, not the compulsory mode.

### B167-G07 — Capability Limits

Any powerful competence/tool requires a causal boundary.

`CAPABILITY → LIMIT / COST / ACCESS CONDITION / COUNTERFORCE → CHOICE`.

This applies especially to AI, robots, specialist technology and institutional authority.

### B167-G08 — Relationship-Specific Self

**Gate:**
`PERSON + RELATIONSHIP + STATUS + HISTORY + PRESENT WANT → REGISTER / TACTIC / BOUNDARY`.

Voice differentiation should come from relationship and worldview, not dialect tricks.

### B167-G09 — Dialogue Is Edited Reality

Raw conversation is not a model for finished dialogue.

`LISTEN / OBSERVE → SELECT → SHAPE → CAUSAL COMMUNICATION`.

The exact wording deserves real-time space when wording changes trust, consent, evidence, authority, relationship terms or a decision.

### B167-G10 — Dialogue Knowledge Matrix

Before a conversation:

- What does the speaker know?
- What does the listener know?
- What does the reader know?
- What does the speaker believe the listener knows?
- What is deliberately omitted?

This is stronger than generic `subtext` because it preserves evidence provenance.

### B167-G11 — Behaviour Does Not Equal Motive

A line or non-response can support several explanations.

**Gate:**
`OBSERVABLE COMMUNICATION → MOTIVE HYPOTHESES → DISCRIMINATING EVIDENCE`.

Mandatory for mystery, romance and conscious nonhuman characters.

### B167-G12 — Conflict as Goal Gap, Not Villain Requirement

The source often over-identifies story with direct conflict. Salvaged mechanism:

`WANT / RESPONSIBILITY → RESISTANCE / SUPPORT → TACTIC → OUTCOME → CHANGED STATE`.

The resistance may be epistemic, physical, relational, institutional or ethical.

## Cross-source corrections

### Against Hero's-Journey / Seven-Story / Three-Act lock

Vogler, Truby, Brody, Bell, Seger and Warner collectively show that templates are best treated as lenses. This source does not justify a retrofit of IVDIVO books.

### Against action-first openings

Edgerton/Bell can be useful for forward pressure, while Lodge, Prose, Rosenfeld and others establish many valid opening modes. The production rule remains the opening contract, not mandatory danger.

### Against support-character functionalism

Green/Kremer sometimes describe minor characters as plot palette or helpers/hinderers. Corrected by Card, Corbett, Herman, Wolf and Murderbot:

`BOOK FUNCTION ≠ STORYWORLD PERSONHOOD`.

### Against dialogue-as-speed

Kempton, Chiarella, McKee Dialogue, Baxter and Le Guin show that dialogue may accelerate, slow, suspend or complicate time. No automatic pace rule.

## Failure modes

1. **PLAN BECOMES CANON** — outline wins over actual manuscript evidence.
2. **FRAMEWORK STACKING** — Hero Journey + seven stories + acts all become obligations.
3. **OPENING BANG RETROFIT** — a quiet but causal opening is replaced by artificial danger.
4. **INTENSITY KPI** — action scores replace state-change diagnosis.
5. **CHARACTER BY LABEL** — one trait or stereotype substitutes for causality.
6. **SUPPORTING PERSON AS PAWN** — story function erases independent life/consent.
7. **DIALOGUE TRANSCRIPTION** — realism produces repetition and dead process.
8. **DIALOGUE AS AUTOMATIC SPEED** — white space mistaken for pace.
9. **GESTURE→MOTIVE LEAP** — behaviour interpreted as proof.
10. **PHONETIC ACCENT CARICATURE** — voice reduced to spelling.
11. **VILLAIN REQUIREMENT** — legitimate countermodel flattened into enemy.
12. **2007 MARKET LEAK** — historical publishing practices treated as current truth.
13. **MONEY DEFAULT LEAK** — scarcity examples imported into post-money IVDIVO.

## Provisional IVDIVO production routing

`LIVE DRAFT → ACTUAL STORY STATE → DEFECT LEVEL → RELEVANT MECHANISM → MINIMAL CORRECT FIX → REGRESSION TEST`.

For line editing specifically:

`BOOK ENGINE → CHAPTER FUNCTION → SCENE ADMISSION → SCENE WEIGHT/MODE → POV/EVIDENCE → COMMUNICATION/EXPOSITION OWNERSHIP → PARAGRAPH INTENT → READER-STATE CONTINUITY → SENTENCE RHYTHM → WORDING`.

No structural diagnosis is replaced by cosmetic sentence surgery.
