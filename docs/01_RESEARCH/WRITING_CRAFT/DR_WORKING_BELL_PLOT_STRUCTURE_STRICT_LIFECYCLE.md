### COMPLETE — Chapter 1: What's a Plot, Anyway?

Bell's compressed LOCK model was read in full:

- Lead
- Objective
- Confrontation
- Knockout

Strict IVDIVO interpretation:

1. **Lead** = focal/primary reader-access agent for a given causal line. It does not require a single hero to monopolize discovery or decision-making.
2. **Objective** = trackable want, responsibility, prevention goal, answer, relationship objective, or state the agent is trying to produce/avoid.
3. **Confrontation** = meaningful resistance/friction/countermodel. It does not require a villain and does not forbid success.
4. **Knockout** = earned local closure/resonance. It does not require triumph, domination, death, or a spectacular climax.

Additional mechanisms:

- dialogue may operate tactically rather than as neutral information transfer;
- produce multiple scene possibilities rather than protecting the first adequate idea;
- originality can arise from character-specific choices, setting use and verbal strategy rather than exotic premise inflation;
- quiet/ordinary material survives when it changes relationship, model, commitment, evidence, choice or reader contract;
- plot principles are diagnostics, not a license to retrofit every IVDIVO book into one Bell template.

#### Mechanism 1 — DISTURBANCE IS NOT COMMITMENT

Bell distinguishes an early disturbance from the later event that actually binds the protagonist to the main causal engine.

IVDIVO form:

**DISTURBANCE → OPTIONS STILL OPEN → COMMITMENT TRANSITION → OPTION/OBLIGATION SET CHANGES → MAIN ENGINE SUSTAINS ITSELF.**

A new location, new technology or startling fact is not automatically a structural transition.

#### Mechanism 2 — WALK-AWAY DIAGNOSTIC

Bell's practical test asks whether the focal agent can simply leave and resume the prior life.

IVDIVO correction:

Ask not only `CAN THEY PHYSICALLY LEAVE?` but:

- what responsibility has been accepted?
- what relationship would be abandoned?
- what evidence now demands action?
- what consent or promise has been given?
- what institutional standing or bounded authority now exists?
- what world condition has changed the available options?

The binding force should be earned. Do **not** manufacture kidnapping, death, debt or catastrophe merely to prevent departure.

#### Mechanism 3 — ENDGAME-ENABLING TRANSITION

Bell's second doorway is a clue, discovery, setback or crisis that makes the final resolution path available/necessary.

IVDIVO form:

**NEW EVIDENCE / SUCCESS / FAILURE / AUTHORIZATION / RELATIONSHIP CHANGE → OLD STRATEGY NO LONGER SUFFICIENT → FINAL PROBLEM BECOMES BOUNDED → DECISIVE ACTION BECOMES POSSIBLE.**

A successful test may serve this function. The transition does not require a disaster.

#### Mechanism 4 — BEGINNING / MIDDLE / END AS FUNCTIONS, NOT PERCENTAGES

Useful functional reading:

- Beginning = establish focal life/world/reader contract and create a durable story problem.
- Middle = develop consequences, relationships, countermodels and changing strategies.
- Ending = resolve the local causal problem and create aftermath/resonance.

Bell's `~1/5` and `~3/4` signposts are historical craft heuristics, **not IVDIVO production laws**.

#### Failure modes extracted

1. **Scenery Doorway** — treating arrival in a new habitat/location as structural commitment when causal options are unchanged.
2. **Artificial Adhesive** — adding kidnapping/death/debt solely to force participation.
3. **Percentage Retrofit** — moving strong story events to satisfy 1/5 or 3/4 markers.
4. **Setback Monopoly** — assuming the second major transition must be failure rather than earned proof/success/authorization.
5. **Villain Adhesive** — requiring a personal villain when responsibility, relationship, evidence, environment or institution already supplies meaningful resistance.
6. **Death-Stakes Inflation** — translating Bell's physical/professional/psychological death language into mandatory extreme stakes.
7. **Act Labels Without State Change** — naming Act II/III while the available choices, obligations, evidence and strategy remain materially the same.

## Provisional cross-source synthesis

### Bell ↔ Truby / Vogler / LaPlante

Convergence: structural models are useful when they reveal missing causality or weak choices.  
IVDIVO firewall: theory diagnoses the story; story does not exist to validate theory.

### Bell ↔ McKee / Rosenfeld

Convergence: major structural turns change the state of the problem, not merely the scenery.  
IVDIVO refinement: track `ACTION STATE + INFORMATION STATE + RELATIONSHIP/OBLIGATION STATE` before and after a major transition.

### Bell ↔ Bernays/Painter / Bell Art of War

Convergence: generate alternatives before locking a major scene/turn.  
IVDIVO gate: choose by character truth + world truth + causal force + freshness + downstream payoff.

### Bell ↔ McKee Dialogue / Chiarella

Bell's dialogue-as-weapon is one valid verbal-action mode.  
IVDIVO correction: dialogue can also negotiate, cooperate, consent, withhold, bond, misdirect, repair or create parallel meanings. Use `VERBAL/NONVERBAL TACTIC`, not mandatory combat.

## ONE MAIN STORY adaptation

Do **not** translate Bell into `one protagonist = one objective = everyone else supports it`.

Use:

**ONE DOMINANT CAUSAL ENGINE + DISTRIBUTED CHARACTER OBJECTIVES + CAUSAL RECONVERGENCE.**

Each major objective must either alter, complicate, test, expose, repair or resolve the same dominant story engine.
