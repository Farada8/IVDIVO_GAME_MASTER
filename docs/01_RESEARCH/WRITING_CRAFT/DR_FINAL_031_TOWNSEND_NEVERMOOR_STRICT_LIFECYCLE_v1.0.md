## STRUCTURE MAP

### 1. Cursed identity as social infrastructure
Morrigan begins not merely disliked but institutionally classified as a causal explanation for unrelated misfortune. Family, bureaucracy and public expectation all treat her future death as normal.

### 2. Bid Day / alternative futures
Competing invitations expose three different relational frames: prank/public humiliation, Squall's offer of power/need, and Jupiter's offer of uncertain membership and escape.

### 3. Eventide / exit from inherited story
The expected death becomes an early proof that the public causal model can be wrong. Jupiter's rescue changes the physical world and the protagonist's option space.

### 4. Nevermoor / Hotel Deucalion as lived belonging
The new city is not introduced as neutral lore. It enters through transport, social rules, embodied surprise, household relationships, room changes and ordinary hotel life.

### 5. Wundrous Society / desired institution
The Society is genuinely attractive and offers lifelong belonging, status, education and family, while its patron system and trials also reveal competition, hidden information and imperfect fairness.

### 6. Friendship and trials
Hawthorne becomes a friend before becoming useful. Book/Chase/Fright trials expose honesty, strategy, courage, resourcefulness and institutional contradictions.

### 7. Evidence of hidden category
Morrigan's unusual memory, shadow event, Mr Jones/Squall appearances, Gossamer phenomena and Jupiter/Jack's knowledge gradually create evidence that the curse story and her identity are different from public belief.

### 8. Show Trial / classification crisis
The official talent-display framework becomes inadequate for Morrigan. Jupiter delays the Wundersmith category until Elders have behavioral evidence of her as a person.

### 9. Squall / causal truth of the curse
The 'curse' is revealed as a manufactured killing mechanism that accumulated social superstition and scapegoating around it. Public belief became self-reinforcing despite false causal premises.

### 10. Ending / category after personhood
Morrigan is a Wundersmith but the category does not determine her morality. The book closes local belonging and entrance questions while leaving training, Squall and Wunder ontology open.

## PRIMARY MECHANISMS

### 1. Scapegoat causal loop
`PUBLIC LABEL → ATTRIBUTION BIAS → REPEATED EXPLANATION → MATERIAL CONSEQUENCE → BEHAVIOR / SELF-MODEL → LABEL APPEARS CONFIRMED`.

A false causal category can become socially real through repeated attribution even when the underlying causal claim is false.

### 2. Correlation / category / cause separation
`EVENT CO-OCCURS WITH PERSON → PUBLIC ATTRIBUTION → INSTITUTIONAL LABEL` does not establish cause.

For IVDIVO mystery/governance:
`CORRELATION ≠ CAUSATION ≠ MOTIVE ≠ PERSONHOOD`.

### 3. Surface want / relational want
Morrigan initially appears to want survival and Wundrous Society membership. The Book Trial forces a more precise desire: to be liked, to belong, to have people who stand by her.

`PRESTIGE / ACCESS WANT → HONEST RELATIONAL WANT → CHOICES CAN BE RE-EVALUATED`.

### 4. Desired institution can still be imperfect
The Society is not a dystopian fake. It provides real community and opportunity. But patron privilege, leaked trial information, competition and safety choices reveal defects.

`REAL BENEFIT + REAL LOYALTY + REAL PROCEDURAL FAILURE` is stronger than cartoon institutional evil.

### 5. Formal values / operational selection pressure
The trials claim to assess worthy traits, yet individual trials and patron behavior reward ambition, secrecy, access and strategy in uneven ways.

`OFFICIAL VALUE CLAIM → ACTUAL TEST INCENTIVE → WHO GETS ADVANTAGE → WHAT BEHAVIOR IS REWARDED`.

### 6. Friendship vs alliance
Welcome frames relationships as useful alliances. Hawthorne instead offers friendship without strategic price.

`INSTITUTIONAL RELATIONSHIP FRAME ↔ FREELY CHOSEN MUTUAL ATTACHMENT`.

Useful for post-money IVDIVO: not every social bond should become an exchange mechanism.

### 7. Reciprocity without transaction
Morrigan aids Cadence at real cost; Cadence later aids Morrigan. The causal reciprocity matters, but neither act needs a negotiated exchange price.

### 8. Person-first / category-second
Jupiter wants Morrigan evaluated for honesty, determination, courage and resourcefulness before Elders learn the Wundersmith category.

`BEHAVIORAL / RELATIONAL EVIDENCE → PERSON MODEL → HIGH-IMPACT CATEGORY`.

This prevents a powerful label from overwriting the person.

### 9. Category does not dictate morality
`CAPABILITY / CATEGORY ≠ MORAL DESTINY`.

Squall argues Wundersmith identity fixes Morrigan's path. The book rejects that determinism.

### 10. Benevolent protection / information withholding
Jupiter hides her category and related danger because he believes it protects her. This has genuine benefits but also removes informed participation.

Production audit:
`PROTECTIVE INTENT → INFORMATION WITHHELD → SUBJECT'S DECISION RIGHTS → REVOCABILITY / APPEAL → COST`.

### 11. Decision-owner legitimacy
The safeguard discussion concerns Morrigan's future and danger while powerful adults negotiate around her.

Apply Crouch/Wells synthesis:
`BENEFICIAL OPTION ≠ RIGHT DECISION OWNER`.

### 12. Controlled cognition / memory provenance
Cadence's mesmerist ability demonstrates that recognition and memory access can be manipulated.

`MEMORY / RECOGNITION → CONTROL PROVENANCE → CORROBORATION → EVIDENCE STATUS`.

Do not treat recollection as automatically authoritative when cognitive influence is possible.

### 13. Place as relationship
The Deucalion room changes as it 'gets to know' Morrigan; hotel routines, staff and architecture create home through repeated lived experience.

`PLACE RULE / DETAIL → INTERACTION → EMOTIONAL / RELATIONAL ASSOCIATION → HOME MODEL`.

Worldbuilding is therefore causal and relational, not tourism.

### 14. Home can precede formal membership
By the time Morrigan receives the Society pin, she recognizes that family, friendship and belonging already exist at the Deucalion.

`FORMAL BELONGING SIGNAL ≠ FIRST MOMENT OF BELONGING`.

### 15. Public erasure as social action
Corvus's household treats Morrigan as dead and suppresses her name. Public identity can be actively removed even while the person remains alive.

`INSTITUTION / FAMILY NARRATIVE → WHO MAY BE NAMED → SOCIAL EXISTENCE / ACCESS CONSEQUENCE`.

### 16. Family models remain mixed
Grandmother is harsh but secretly helps rescue Morrigan; 'old bad family / new good family' is too simple.

`RELATIONSHIP HISTORY → CONTRADICTORY ACTIONS → UPDATED, NONBINARY MODEL`.

### 17. Source identity / nice intermediary
Mr Jones appears as a reasonable, useful intermediary before being identified as Squall. Source provenance matters more than tone.

`TRUSTWORTHY PRESENTATION ≠ TRUSTWORTHY SOURCE`.

### 18. Trial success / character evidence
Tests are most useful when they force behavior that creates evidence about a person. They become weak when a score simply declares character quality.

### 19. Speculative phenomenon / ontology ceiling
Wunder, Gossamer, Wundersmiths and consciousness travel are story-world mechanisms only.

`FICTIONAL PHENOMENON ≠ SCIENTIFIC EVIDENCE ≠ IVDIVO ONTOLOGY`.

### 20. Youth voice through interpretive attitude
Morrigan's voice emerges through literal misreadings, dark humor, imagined headlines, social vigilance and emotional self-protection—not merely slang.

### 21. Sentence/rhythm variability
The book uses long flowing narrative sentences, dialogue, comic timing and occasional short emphasis lines.

`YOUTH / SUSPENSE ≠ SHORT-SENTENCE DENSITY`.

## CROSS-SOURCE COMPARISON

- **Skyward:** both are excellent belonging/status labs. Skyward is stronger for role-specific competence, metric gaming and official-rule/informal-culture gaps; Nevermoor is stronger for scapegoating, desired-home architecture and belonging preceding formal membership.
- **Hunger Games:** Collins examines coerced public identity under an openly exploitative regime; Townsend is useful when the desired institution contains both authentic good and imperfect selection pressure.
- **Brody:** both support YA identity/autonomy, but Nevermoor demonstrates that developmental change can emerge organically without fixed YA beat arithmetic.
- **Wells / Murderbot:** use Wells for stronger consent/personhood under embedded control. Nevermoor contributes adult protective withholding and category-before-person risks.
- **Dark Matter:** reinforces decision-owner legitimacy when adults/institutions decide around the affected subject.
- **Zunshine/Herman:** strengthen source/motive/evidence provenance when memory, mesmerizing, public stories or category inference are involved.
- **Wolf:** use for canon/source hierarchy; Nevermoor's in-world myths are not equivalent to project canon facts.
- **Baxter / McKee Dialogue:** use for relationship-specific subtext and to prevent all children from sharing one comic register.

## IVDIVO OPERATIONALIZATION

### Orbital Youth
Use for:
- desired institution that actually offers something worth wanting;
- stigma/public-category pressure without making the institution uniformly malicious;
- belonging that grows through ordinary life before formal induction;
- friendship that is not reducible to networking/status exchange;
- trials/qualification where proxy metrics may not equal actual suitability;
- mentor protection that creates an informed-consent problem;
- personhood before talent/category.

### Post-money society
Do not translate Wundrous patronage, coins, wages or privilege economics literally.

Preserve nonfungible stakes:
`BELONGING / TRUST / CONSENT / ACCESS / TIME / ROLE / UNIQUE OPPORTUNITY / RELATIONSHIP / PRIVACY / REPUTATION`.

### Conscious robots / nonhuman persons
Fenestra is a useful warning against species-function assumptions. A named conscious nonhuman worker requires independent desire, relationships, consent/refusal and personhood beyond job role.

### Consciousness practices
Gossamer/Wunder mechanics are fiction-only. Preserve IVDIVO epistemic chain:
`OBSERVATION → REPRODUCIBLE EFFECT → SCHOOL INTERPRETATION → COMPETING INTERPRETATION → ONTOLOGY CEILING`.

### Mystery
Use scapegoat/public-story mechanics and source-provenance gates. Do not allow an attractive explanation to become fact by repetition.

### Prose
Do not imitate surface whimsy as house style. Preserve functional paragraph/sentence variation and character-specific attention.
