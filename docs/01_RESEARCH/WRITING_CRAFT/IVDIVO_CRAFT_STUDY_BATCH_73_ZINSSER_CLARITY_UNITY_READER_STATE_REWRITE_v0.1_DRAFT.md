# 1. WHY THIS SOURCE MATTERS TO IVDIVO
Zinsser is not used as a plot template. His strongest contribution is a post-architecture prose diagnostic: once ONE MAIN STORY, causality, scene necessity and POV are already sound, he offers precise ways to remove prestige fog, duplicated thought, false emphasis and reader-state discontinuity without flattening voice.

# 2. CENTRAL SYNTHESIS
`CLEAR THOUGHT → FUNCTIONAL SELECTION → READER-STATE CONTINUITY → RHYTHMIC / LEXICAL REWRITE`.

This sits below structural revision:
`ONE MAIN STORY → CAUSALITY → SCENE ACCEPTANCE → SHOW/TELL → POV → DIALOGUE MODE → PARAGRAPH INTENT → READER-STATE CONTINUITY → SENTENCE RHYTHM → WORDING`.

# 3. PRODUCTION MECHANISMS
## B73-G01 — FUNCTIONAL WORD / SENTENCE GATE
Every retained component must add at least one real function: new fact, relation, image, attitude, causal link, emphasis, rhythm, characterization, ambiguity or reader orientation. Mere repetition or status inflation is a cut/rebuild candidate.

## B73-G02 — CLARITY IS NOT SHORTNESS
Do not optimize sentence length. Optimize recoverability of actor, action, relation, time, space and emphasis.

## B73-G03 — READER-STATE CONTINUITY
For every adjacent sentence pair:
`WHAT A LEAVES ACTIVE → WHAT B ASSUMES → MATCH / MISMATCH → SIGNAL / REORDER / REWRITE`.
Track active subject, noun, image, time, location, knowledge and tone.

## B73-G04 — PARAGRAPH CONTINUITY BEFORE MICRO-CUTTING
Before deleting small words, ask whether several short paragraphs actually form one uninterrupted thought/attention unit. If yes, recombine first, then edit sentences.

## B73-G05 — BRACKET TEST
Bracket suspected clutter rather than immediately deleting it. Read with/without. If meaning, relation, voice and rhythm survive, deletion is likely safe.

## B73-G06 — PRESTIGE-FOG GATE
Technical/institutional language must identify an actual mechanism. Replace impressive abstractions when they obscure who can do what, under which constraint, with what consequence.

## B73-G07 — BOUNDED-CORNER GATE
A large world/book must select a bounded causal corner. `SAGA SCALE ≠ BOOK SCALE`.

## B73-G08 — LEAD CONTRACT
Opening function:
`ATTENTION + ORIENTATION + PROMISE`.
No mandatory action scene, mystery, fragment barrage or thematic speech.

## B73-G09 — FEATURE-LEAD THEATER REJECTION
Do not withhold basic subject matter while producing fake color, fragments or vague suspense. Specific fact/character/action can supply its own color.

## B73-G10 — STOP-WHEN-DONE ENDING
After the local work is complete, do not summarize the already-proved lesson. Prefer an earned image, fact, consequence, echo, quotation or clean cutoff.

## B73-G11 — MATERIAL SURPLUS / SELECTION
Research/world bible may be larger than prose:
`COLLECT > NEEDED → SELECT HIGH-UTILITY MATERIAL → DISCARD SURPLUS → WRITE`.

## B73-G12 — TRUST-READER GATE
Do not pre-label an observed fact as amazing, tragic, symbolic, ironic or important when staging lets the reader produce that response.

## B73-G13 — READ-ALOUD RHYTHM
At paragraph/chapter scale listen for same-length monotony, accidental choppiness, false punch lines and transitions that are logically clear on screen but aurally confusing.

## B73-G14 — REWRITE AS RESEQUENCING
Line revision is not only deletion. It may require moving information earlier, signalling a shift, changing sentence order or rebuilding the paragraph around the reader's active model.

# 4. FAILURE MODES
- **Shortness KPI:** treating concise prose as a percentage of short sentences.
- **Cutting Before Structural Diagnosis:** polishing or pruning a scene that should be merged/deleted/rebuilt.
- **Clarity = Neutral Voice:** removing character-specific diction and rhythm in the name of plain language.
- **Prestige Worldbuilding:** invented jargon with no causal mechanism underneath it.
- **False Economy:** deleting details that carry character, world-rule or emotional inference simply because they are not plot facts.
- **Reader Hand-Holding:** explaining the emotional or thematic value of a fact the reader already understands.
- **School-Essay Ending:** final paragraph restates the conclusion instead of ending the experience.
- **Fragment-Theater Opening:** artificial sentence shards used to impersonate suspense.

# 5. CROSS-SOURCE COMPARISON
## With Lamott
Lamott protects discovery; Zinsser disciplines the post-discovery text.
`DISCOVERY → STRUCTURAL SELECTION → CLARITY PASS → RHYTHM/VOICE PASS`.

## With Francine Prose
Prose protects sentence music, specificity and reader intelligence; Zinsser adds an explicit deletion/function test. Together they reject both clutter and mechanical minimalism.

## With Pushman
Both support intentional paragraphing and formal variation. Neither justifies paragraph confetti.

## With McKee / Truby / Rosenfeld
Those sources operate mainly at story/scene architecture. Zinsser comes later. A beautifully clarified unnecessary scene remains unnecessary.

## With Wolf / Ribó
Zinsser's bounded selection reinforces `WORLD DATABASE ≠ BOOK NARRATIVE` and `SAGA SCALE ≠ BOOK SCALE`.

# 6. IVDIVO-SPECIFIC APPLICATION
## Orbital Youth / post-money life
Terms like `allocation`, `priority`, `qualification`, `access`, `contribution`, `assignment` and `scheduling` must denote concrete mechanisms. They cannot smuggle salary/rent/credits back into the setting.

## Conscious robots
Clarity removes fog, not personhood. Named robots retain relation-specific voice, agency, consent and refusal.

## Consciousness schools
Production language must preserve evidence status:
`OBSERVED → REPRODUCIBLE EFFECT → SCHOOL INTERPRETATION → COMPETING INTERPRETATION → POSSIBLE ONTOLOGICAL CLAIM`.

## “Обрубки”
New combined repair gate:
`same thought/actor/time/focus across multiple short units? → test recombination → then rhythm edit`.
Protect short lines only when they create actual contrast, revelation, breath, beat, end-weight or changed focus.
