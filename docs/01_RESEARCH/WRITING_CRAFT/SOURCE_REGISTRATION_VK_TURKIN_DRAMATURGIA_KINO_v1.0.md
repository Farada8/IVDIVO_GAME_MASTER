## Historical-use firewall
The theoretical core originates in early Soviet / silent-to-sound cinema practice. Therefore the following are historical evidence, not current IVDIVO production laws unless independently justified:
- Soviet ideological/aesthetic prescriptions;
- fixed screenplay lengths and part/page arithmetic;
- silent-film and early-sound technical assumptions;
- camera/shot conventions tied to 1930s production;
- conflict/catastrophe canons presented as universal;
- gender/class/cultural assumptions of the period;
- claims about audience psychology not supported by modern evidence.

The source is most valuable for causal/visual dramaturgy, event vs spectacle, dialogue as action, object/setting function, preparation vs motivation, temporal/parallel composition, transition/montage logic and the separation of literary screenplay from shooting/technical documents.
