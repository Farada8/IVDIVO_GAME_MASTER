## 3. Structural map
### Phase A — Oxford baseline / first traces
Lyra's lived child-world, adult institutional politics, the attempted poisoning of Lord Asriel, Dust imagery, disappearances of children, Roger's abduction, and the alethiometer enter through immediate action and partial knowledge rather than a world encyclopedia.

### Phase B — Mrs Coulter / information asymmetry
Lyra is taken into Mrs Coulter's glamorous adult world, gains education and access, learns that care/status can coexist with control and surveillance, discovers the Gobbler connection, and escapes.

### Phase C — gyptian investigation
The missing-child problem becomes a collective investigation with pooled testimony, source lineage, rumor correction, institutional mapping, and travel north. Lyra's personal goal to rescue Roger and the larger Dust/Oblation problem increasingly become one causal engine.

### Phase D — Trollesund / nonhuman alliances
The story expands through witches, Iorek Byrnison, Lee Scoresby, and the North. The alethiometer creates new information but never removes the need for interpretation, logistics, alliances, consent, or action.

### Phase E — severed child / Bolvangar
The abstract term `intercision` becomes concrete through Tony Makarios and the caged daemons. Lyra infiltrates Bolvangar, discovers the experimental system from inside, connects euphemism to bodily consequences, triggers a collective escape, and reunites with the gyptian/witch/bear rescue force.

### Phase F — Svalbard / Iorek
Lyra reaches Svalbard, uses Iofur Raknison's desire to become human against him, enables a legitimate single-combat route for Iorek, and helps restore Iorek's kingship. The Iorek/Iofur contrast becomes a story about identity, imitation, institutional status, and self-models.

### Phase G — Lord Asriel / betrayal / reclassification
Lyra reaches Asriel believing she has brought him the alethiometer. The actual load-bearing resource is Roger. Asriel severs Roger from his daemon to release energy and open a bridge between worlds. Lyra's initial mission collapses morally and epistemically. Her final hypothesis—that Dust may be good because those acting cruelly call it bad—is a motivation for further inquiry, not demonstrated proof.

## 4. Major claim extraction
1. A child POV can carry complex institutional, scientific, religious, and political material when information is routed through live questions and consequences.
2. A protagonist can be active and intelligent while operating from incomplete or incorrect models.
3. Adults' knowledge and institutional status do not make their interpretations true.
4. Care, glamour, education, and protection can coexist with coercion and surveillance.
5. `Behavioral compliance / following ≠ informed consent`.
6. Institutional euphemism can hide bodily/moral reality.
7. A tool can output useful information while still requiring interpretation skill, question formulation, and epistemic humility.
8. Rumor and institutional claims need source lineage and independent corroboration.
9. Early true traces can be reinterpreted later without retcon.
10. Child/daemon embodiment changes what danger, intimacy, maturation, taboo, and separation mean.
11. Nonhuman personhood does not depend on human embodiment.
12. Identity can be undermined when an agent imitates another category rather than inhabiting its own capabilities and norms.
13. Multiple communities can hold different causal slices of the same problem; investigation becomes stronger when those slices are pooled.
14. Rescue/protection does not grant ownership or decision authority over another person.
15. A technically/scientifically ambitious project can be morally catastrophic despite genuine intellectual power.
16. Narrative prophecy/destiny can generate pressure without replacing current character choice.
17. A character's final explanatory hypothesis can be dramatically compelling and still remain uncorroborated.

## 5. Mechanism extraction
### GC-M01 — Lived Baseline Before Lore
`ORDINARY POV LIFE → MEANINGFUL DEVIATION → LOCAL QUESTION → WORLD RULE REVEALED THROUGH ACTION`.

The world feels large because Lyra treats much of it as normal rather than explaining it.

### GC-M02 — Child Salience / Adult Salience Split
`SAME WORLD → DIFFERENT STATUS / KNOWLEDGE / PRIORITY → DIFFERENT NOTICED DETAILS → DIFFERENT MODELS`.

Useful YA engine: children and adults can inhabit the same institution while perceiving different causal structures.

### GC-M03 — Incomplete-but-Actionable Model
`PARTIAL EVIDENCE → REASONABLE CHILD MODEL → ACTION → CONSEQUENCE → NEW EVIDENCE → MODEL UPDATE`.

Do not make youth characters stupid in order to let them be wrong.

### GC-M04 — Question-First Worldbuilding
`PRESENT QUESTION → MINIMUM NEEDED WORLD INFORMATION → ACTION / TEST → NEXT QUESTION`.

### GC-M05 — Truth Instrument / Interpretation Gap
The alethiometer has a fictional truth-output function, but Lyra still needs symbolic interpretation and good questions.

`TOOL OUTPUT ≠ AUTOMATIC UNDERSTANDING`.

For IVDIVO the perfect-truth property is not transferable; the useful mechanism is interpretation skill and evidence use.

### GC-M06 — Institutional Euphemism
`HARMFUL PRACTICE → CLEAN LABEL / PROCEDURE → DISTANCE FROM CONSEQUENCE → LEGITIMACY APPEARANCE`.

Examples: Gobblers / General Oblation Board / `intercision` / `Experimental Station` / `Maystadt process`.

### GC-M07 — Formal Care / Coercive Control
`BENEFIT / EDUCATION / PROTECTION → INFORMATION ASYMMETRY / SURVEILLANCE / THREAT → CARE CLAIM AUDIT`.

### GC-M08 — Compliance ≠ Consent
Children can follow charming adults, obey staff, or comply under captivity without informed, uncoerced consent.

### GC-M09 — Rumor Ecology
`EVENT / DISAPPEARANCE → RUMOR VERSIONS → SOURCE LINEAGE → FEAR / SOCIAL MODEL → LATER CORROBORATION / CORRECTION`.

### GC-M10 — Distributed Investigation
`FAMILY REPORTS + LOCAL OBSERVATION + CAPTURED AGENT TESTIMONY + INSTITUTIONAL SOURCE + ALETHIOMETER → STRONGER MODEL`.

No single investigator owns all evidence.

### GC-M11 — Under-Observed Population
`LOW STATUS / MARGINAL GROUP → LOWER INSTITUTIONAL ATTENTION → HARM PERSISTS LONGER`.

Missing children are not equally visible to authorities.

### GC-M12 — Embodiment Defines Social/Moral Stakes
Daemon embodiment makes touch, distance, settling, separation, death, and intimacy materially different.

Generalizable mechanism:
`EMBODIMENT → TABOO / AFFORDANCE / INTIMACY / RISK → SOCIAL INSTITUTION → STORY CHOICE`.

### GC-M13 — Institution Violates Personhood Through Procedure
`PERSON → MEASUREMENT / CATEGORY → PROCEDURE → SUBJECT REDUCED TO EXPERIMENTAL OBJECT`.

### GC-M14 — Breakout via Institutional Slack
`FORMAL CONTROL SYSTEM → PROCEDURAL GAPS / SHARED CIRCUIT / ROUTINE ASSUMPTION → AGENT OBSERVES WEAKNESS → COORDINATED ESCAPE`.

Competence need not equal omnipotence; institutions can be strong in one function and careless in another.

### GC-M15 — Collective Youth Agency
`ONE CHILD DISCOVERS → COMMUNICATES → PEER NETWORK → SHARED PLAN → COORDINATED ACTION`.

### GC-M16 — Hidden Knowledge vs POV Fairness
Adults know critical facts about Lyra, Dust, parentage, prophecy, and institutions. Reader access is controlled by Lyra's actual access, not by arbitrary narrator cheating.

### GC-M17 — Early True Trace / Later Motive Reclassification
The Master's poison attempt remains true; later information changes the motive model.

`TRUE TRACE → MOTIVE MODEL A → NEW SOURCE → MOTIVE MODEL B`.

### GC-M18 — Identity Through Own Mode, Not Mimicry
Iorek's armor functions as a self-made, species-specific extension of being; Iofur's attempt to become human destabilizes his bear identity and social order.

General salvage:
`SELF-SPECIFIC EMBODIMENT / CULTURE → OWN CAPABILITY` beats `IMITATE DOMINANT CATEGORY`.

Do not literalize `armor = soul` as IVDIVO ontology.

### GC-M19 — Created/Decorated Status vs Legitimate Identity
Court badges, faux daemons, baptism ambition, and human-style palace signal status imitation without demonstrating legitimacy or personhood.

### GC-M20 — Enemy Model Exploit
Lyra wins access by understanding what Iofur wants and constructing a model he will accept.

This is tactic, not truth.

### GC-M21 — Prophecy / Destiny Firewall
Prophecy creates stakes and constraints, but characters still act through local wants and incomplete knowledge.

For IVDIVO: prophecy/foreknowledge, if used, must not erase agency or become proof of ontology without evidence architecture.

### GC-M22 — Personal Mission → Systemic Engine
`RESCUE ROGER → GOBBLERS → INTERCISION → DUST → ASRIEL / COULTER → OTHER WORLDS`.

The book expands without abandoning the initial personal want because each larger layer causally explains or changes the rescue problem.

### GC-M23 — Technical/Scientific Capability ≠ Moral Legitimacy
Asriel correctly discovers/uses real properties in his world while murdering Roger to power the bridge.

`TECHNICAL SUCCESS ≠ ETHICAL LEGITIMACY`.

### GC-M24 — Mission Misidentification / Objective Collapse
Lyra assumes the alethiometer is what Asriel needs. The Master never actually told her so.

`ASSUMED MISSION → NO SOURCE CHECK → LONG CAUSAL EFFORT → REVEAL OF WRONG LOAD-BEARING OBJECTIVE`.

Strong warning for IVDIVO investigators/planners: verify who actually stated the mission.

### GC-M25 — Rescue → Unintended Delivery

`GOOD LOCAL ACTION → UNMODELED DOWNSTREAM COUPLING → MORAL CATASTROPHE → MODEL UPDATE`.

`CRUEL AUTHORITIES CLAIM X → CHARACTER INFERS NOT-X`.

This is psychologically understandable but not proof.

`DISREDITED SOURCE → ALTERNATIVE HYPOTHESIS`, not `ALTERNATIVE FACT`.

## 6. Failure modes / Red Team
### GC-F01 — Oracle Epistemology
The alethiometer is a fictional near-truth oracle. Do not import perfect epistemic tools into IVDIVO without explicit limits/provenance.

### GC-F02 — Prophecy Legitimacy
Witch prophecy is an in-world claim. It must not become a general production license for chosen-one authority.

### GC-F03 — Daemon/Soul Ontology Leak
Daemons, Dust, separation, settling, witch-distance, and parallel worlds are fictional metaphysics. They are not scientific evidence for IVDIVO consciousness/soul theory.

### GC-F04 — Intercision / Castration Analogy
The novel uses religious/medical analogies for control and puberty. Do not universalize historical/gender/sexual claims from the fantasy mechanism.

### GC-F05 — Child Harm as Sympathy Shortcut
Abduction/severing/death create strong stakes but are not default YA depth mechanisms.

### GC-F06 — Charisma = Motive Truth
Mrs Coulter's glamour changes behavior and access; it does not prove care, truth, or legitimacy.

### GC-F07 — Care = Ownership
Parents/institutions rescuing, educating, or creating children/persons do not thereby own their decisions.

### GC-F08 — Iofur / Nonhuman Mimicry Moralization
Do not infer that a nonhuman being is morally corrupt because it adopts human culture. The useful mechanism is misaligned identity/status aspiration, not species purity.

### GC-F09 — Witches / Gender Essentialism
Witch lifespan/gender/family system is fictional culture, not human gender psychology.

### GC-F10 — Religious Institution Simplification
Institutional critique is narratively coherent but does not license reducing all belief, religion, or institutional actors to one motive.

### GC-F11 — Expert Source = Truth
Asriel, witches, doctors, scholars, and alethiometer readers can all provide partial or theory-laden explanations. Maintain evidence status.

### GC-F12 — Final Reversal = Proof
`All cruel authorities say Dust is bad → Dust must be good` is a character hypothesis, not corroboration.

### GC-F13 — Violent Success as General Solution
Iorek/Lyra's tactics can be character-true without becoming universal governance or conflict-resolution laws.

### GC-F14 — Chosen Child Monopoly
Lyra is unusually capable/central, but IVDIVO ensembles should preserve distributed intelligence and independent personhood of allies.

### GC-F15 — Style Copying
Pullman's English source is valid for style study, but no syntax/cadence pattern becomes a house law. The novel itself uses long paragraphs and reflective/expository syntax alongside action.

## 7. Cross-source comparison
### With *Ender's Game*
Convergence: child instrumentalization, adult information asymmetry, institutionally hidden real stakes, youth competence, moral reclassification.  
Difference: Pullman centers a child's self-directed rescue/investigation against institutions; Card centers a child trained inside the institution.

### With *Nevermoor*
Convergence: belonging, stigmatizing categories, desired/ambivalent institutions, child agency. Pullman makes institutional danger more bodily and epistemic.

### With *Skyward*
Convergence: youth competence, institutional secrecy, official narrative vs lived evidence, peer coordination.

### With *Children of Time*
Convergence: nonhuman cognition/personhood must not be collapsed into human categories; source/doctrine distinction; other-world contact. Pullman's daemon/bear metaphysics remain fictional ontology.

### With *Project Hail Mary*
Convergence: shared questions, tool interpretation, unfamiliar beings, model revision. Weir uses testable technical protocols more strongly; Pullman uses mythic/fantasy epistemology.

### With Zunshine / Herman / le Carré / Ephron
Strong convergence on motive models, source lineage, testimony, alternative explanations, and the difference between observed behavior and world fact.

### With Genette / Chatman
Strong convergence on POV knowledge access, information order, reclassification, and the fairness of later reinterpretation.

### With Nevermoor / youth-institution layer
Belonging and care are not identical to legitimacy; protective adults/institutions need consent/knowledge audits.

## 8. IVDIVO operationalization

Allow a young protagonist to make reasonable wrong inferences. The correction must come from new evidence/consequence, not author humiliation.

### Gate 198-02 — Institutional Euphemism Audit

`LABEL → ACTUAL ACTION → WHO BEARS COST → WHO CAN REFUSE → WHO CAN APPEAL`.

### Gate 198-03 — Formal Care / Control Split
`BENEFIT / EDUCATION / SAFETY CLAIM ≠ CONSENT / LEGITIMACY`.

### Gate 198-04 — Truth Tool Limit
If an advanced system provides information, define:
- data provenance;
- query dependence;
- uncertainty;
- interpretation burden;
- failure modes;
- who can audit it.

### Gate 198-05 — Rumor / Source Lineage
`RUMOR → SOURCE → REPEATERS → INDEPENDENT LINES → CORROBORATION`.

### Gate 198-06 — Child Collective Agency
Do not make the protagonist the only young person capable of coordination or insight.

### Gate 198-07 — Embodiment / Taboo
If consciousness/personhood has different embodiment, make intimacy, distance, consent, vulnerability, and social norms causally specific.

### Gate 198-08 — Mission Source Check
Before a long quest/investigation:
`WHO ACTUALLY STATED THE MISSION? → SOURCE? → ASSUMPTION? → WHAT WOULD DISCONFIRM IT?`.

### Gate 198-09 — Local Good / Downstream Coupling
`GOOD LOCAL ACTION → WHAT SYSTEM DOES IT FEED? → WHAT NEW RISK DOES IT CREATE?`

### Gate 198-10 — Counter-Authority Hypothesis

### Gate 198-11 — Series Boundary
