# IVDIVO Craft Study Batch 150
## Brandilyn Collins — Emotional Dimensionality · Context · Growth

Status: DRAFT / source FULL READ still IN PROGRESS. Secret #3 is strict-complete; Secret #4 is strongly advanced but not yet strict-complete.

## Core extraction

Collins's strongest usable mechanism is not the slogan that characters need 'opposite emotions.' It is the more precise observation that broad labels such as love, grief, fear, courage, loyalty, anger, or care are composites whose visible form changes with circumstance.

Production model:

`VALUE / CONCERN + RELATIONSHIP + CIRCUMSTANCE + CURRENT COST → EMOTIONAL MIX → TACTIC → CHOICE/ACTION → CONSEQUENCE`.

The same stable concern may surface differently under different pressure. Care can produce patience in one scene and anger or refusal in another. Courage can coexist with fear. Trust can contain doubt. Grief may appear as attention, numbness, responsibility, irritation, memory, or practical action.

## Production gates

### B150-G01 — Broad Emotion Is Not a Scene State
Do not write `she is sad / he is brave / they are in love` as the whole emotional architecture. Identify component feelings and the current concern that makes them matter.

### B150-G02 — Contextual Surface
Ask why this emotion takes this surface form with this person, in this place, under this cost, now.

### B150-G03 — Same Value, Different Behavior
A stable value may yield different tactics under changed conditions. This is dimensionality, not inconsistency, when the causal bridge is legible.

### B150-G04 — No Random Opposite
Do not bolt on an opposite trait merely to manufacture complexity. Contradiction requires a root in competing priorities, relationships, knowledge, cost, embodiment, or circumstance.

### B150-G05 — Emotional Growth Needs Prior Capacity
A high-cost later choice must have earlier seeds: small choices, restrained impulses, partial experiments, or consequences that make the later behavior retrospectively believable.

### B150-G06 — No Mandatory Healing Arc
YA character development may be partial, tactical, relational, negative, stable-under-test, or deferred across the series. Do not retrofit every adolescent into wound → lesson → cure.

### B150-G07 — Emotion Does Not Upgrade Evidence
`DISPLAYED EMOTION ≠ MOTIVE FACT ≠ GUILT ≠ CONSENT ≠ PERSONHOOD ≠ ONTOLOGY`.

### B150-G08 — Relationship State Is Multicolored
Love/trust/loyalty are not one-note states. Allow care + anger, attraction + embarrassment, trust + doubt, loyalty + resentment, intimacy + boundary protection when causally earned.

### B150-G09 — Nonhuman Affect Must Be Embodiment-Specific
Conscious robots and other nonhuman persons need not display human facial or physiological emotion markers. Infer their internal state only from source-appropriate evidence.

### B150-G10 — No Microgesture Checklist
Do not translate emotional complexity into `dialogue → glance → hand → breath → thought → dialogue` by default. Use only the cues that change reader inference, relationship, tactic, physical possibility, or orientation.

### B150-G11 — Paragraph Before Sentence
When several emotional beats belong to one perceptual/decision unit, preserve paragraph continuity before optimizing sentence-level punch.

### B150-G12 — Character Model Remains Revisable
Unexpected behavior can update the author's model of the character, but it must still pass character truth, canon, causality, ONE MAIN STORY, and downstream consequence.

## Cross-source synthesis

### Collins × McKee Character
Both value contradiction/dimensionality. IVDIVO rejects McKee's tendency to treat contradiction as a route to a hidden 'true self.' Prefer contextual, relationship-specific behavior and observable choice.

### Collins × Corbett
Corbett's surprising-but-character-true constraint prevents Collins's 'opposite color' from becoming arbitrary. A counter-expression is valid only when retrospectively rooted.

### Collins × Card / Kress
Relationship and pressure change the visible self. Character differentiation should occur through priorities and tactics, not quirks/slang alone.

### Collins × Oatley
Emotion is tied to concerns, goals, plans, and changed circumstances. Reader emotion still remains separate from character emotion.

### Collins × Zunshine / le Carré / Fludernik
Emotional behavior is sourced evidence. It can alter a POV model without upgrading the claim to fact.

### Collins × Lamott / LaPlante / Swain
The decomposition of emotion into components is primarily a diagnostic/revision tool. Do not run it as a checklist while generating every sentence.

## IVDIVO production formulas

### Character scene
`CURRENT CONCERN → EMOTIONAL MIX → CHARACTER-SPECIFIC TACTIC → RESPONSE → UPDATED RELATION / MODEL / COMMITMENT`.

### YA growth
`EARLY CAPACITY → SMALL COSTLY CHOICE → CONSEQUENCE → CHANGED STRATEGY → LATER HIGHER-COST CHOICE`.

### Mystery
`EMOTIONAL CUE → POV INTERPRETATION → ALTERNATIVE MODEL → INDEPENDENT CORROBORATION`.

### Conscious robot / nonhuman person
`OWN PRIORITIES + EMBODIMENT + RELATIONSHIP + KNOWLEDGE LIMIT → COMMUNICATION/ACTION TACTIC → CONSENT/REFUSAL → CONSEQUENCE`.

### Anti-choppy line edit
`SCENE FUNCTION → POV ATTENTION → EMOTIONAL/RELATIONAL TURN → PARAGRAPH INTENT → READER-STATE CONTINUITY → SENTENCE RHYTHM`.

## Limits

Collins adapts Method Acting and uses psychological language as craft theory. None of these claims is imported as clinical psychology, cognitive science, or IVDIVO metaphysics. Secret #4 remains lifecycle-incomplete until its missing internal continuity is demonstrated.
