## 4. MAJOR EARLY MECHANISMS
### Story vs discourse vs reader model
`WORLD/STORY FACT → DISCOURSE ORDER/POV → READER MODEL`.
A weak presentation does not automatically require changing canon facts.

### Selection as perspective
No narrative can contain the entire world. Prose selection is a viewpoint; absence from the book does not imply absence from Saga Bible/canon.

### Source-constrained inference
History/mystery reconstruction should move:
`SOURCE → FIXED POINT → GAP → HYPOTHESIS → TEST`, not theory-first evidence fitting.

### Media transformation
The same story function can require different discourse tools in prose, audio, film or drama.

### Quiet narrativity
Physical event density is not the only kind of progression. Lived experience can alter relationship, model, commitment, identity, evidence or later choice. Deletion test still rejects filler.

### Theory-label firewall
Narratological labels describe. They do not prove quality or prescribe repair.
