## CORE SYNTHESIS

The useful principle is not `start with action`.

It is:

`ATTENTION → ORIENTATION → PERSON/SUBJECT BOND → STORY PROMISE → FORWARD PRESSURE`

The opening must make the reader willing to continue and accurately teach the reader what kind of experience this book is offering.

## B83 PRODUCTION GATES

### B83-G01 — Opening Promise Integrity
Opening excitement must belong to the same causal/emotional engine as the rest of the book.

### B83-G02 — Mode Before Spectacle
Choose opening mode before escalating stakes:
`ACTION / VOICE / RELATIONSHIP / EVIDENCE-MYSTERY / FRAME / PROLOGUE / TEASER / ORDINARY DISTURBANCE`.

### B83-G03 — Hook Without Catastrophe
No disaster is required if responsibility, voice, anomaly, relationship pressure or competence creates forward attention.

### B83-G04 — Prologue Necessity
A prologue must perform work that the main narrative cannot perform as well at the same cost.

### B83-G05 — Prologue Payoff
Every prologue creates an information/affect promise that must later reconnect causally or interpretively.

### B83-G06 — Teaser Debt
Borrowing a future charged moment requires later recontextualization; pure marketing preview is WATCH.

### B83-G07 — Bond Multiplicity
Reader connection may arise from empathy, fascination, competence, humour, relationship, values, mystery, specificity or moral uncertainty. Sympathy is not mandatory.

### B83-G08 — Sympathy Manipulation Audit
Jeopardy/hardship/vulnerability must have story function beyond making the reader feel sorry for the Lead.

### B83-G09 — Ordinary-World Deviation
`NORMAL PATTERN → SPECIFIC DEVIATION → PERSON-SPECIFIC RESPONSE → NEW STATE/QUESTION`.

### B83-G10 — Opening World Causality
World detail earns space when it changes an action, option, relationship, inference or consequence.

### B83-G11 — Early Opposition Personhood
Opposition receives its own rational model and cannot exist merely to flatter the protagonist.

### B83-G12 — No Likability Sanding
Do not remove the protagonist's defining edge merely to make them agreeable.

### B83-G13 — Early Load Budget
Opening pages must not simultaneously demand mastery of too many new names, technologies, institutions, historical facts and metaphysical concepts.

### B83-G14 — Tone/Genre Contract
If opening mode creates a promise of thriller, romance, mystery, comedy or philosophical SF, the book must pay that promise or intentionally renegotiate it.

## SCHOOL COMPARISON

### Bell vs Edgerton
Both favour early movement. IVDIVO retains `disturbance`, rejects the inference that movement must mean high physical action.

### Bell vs Lodge / Prose
Lodge and Prose demonstrate broader viable openings. Synthesis: Bell supplies useful commercial diagnostics, Lodge/Prose prevent action monoculture.

### Bell vs Rosenfeld
Rosenfeld's first-scene intention gives a stronger causal test than mere grab. Synthesis: hook should create or reveal a present intention/problem, not just attention.

### Bell vs Zinsser
Zinsser gives the cleanest umbrella:
`ATTENTION + ORIENTATION + PROMISE`.
Bell adds bond and disturbance diagnostics beneath it.

## READER-EFFECT MODEL

Opening reader effect is tracked on separate axes:

1. **Attention:** am I awake and curious?
2. **Orientation:** do I know enough to follow?
3. **Attachment/fascination:** whose experience matters?
4. **Promise:** what kind of book do I think I entered?
5. **Forward model:** what do I expect may change next?

A loud opening can score high on 1 and fail 2–5.

## IVDIVO CANON FIREWALLS

- Post-money society: do not use wages/rent/credits as automatic YA hook.
- Conscious robots: may be focal agents; must retain independent goals and refusal rights.
- Consciousness transfer: not established operational human technology.
- Specialist consciousness schools: opening mystery must not promote a school's interpretation to ontology.

## LINE-EDIT CONSEQUENCE

Do not optimize an opening by shortening every sentence. Opening force may come from voice, continuous perception, a long accumulating sentence, quiet interpersonal pressure or a specific ordinary-life deviation.

`OPENING EFFECT → MODE → POV/DURATION → PARAGRAPH → SENTENCE`, not `SHORTER = STRONGER`.
