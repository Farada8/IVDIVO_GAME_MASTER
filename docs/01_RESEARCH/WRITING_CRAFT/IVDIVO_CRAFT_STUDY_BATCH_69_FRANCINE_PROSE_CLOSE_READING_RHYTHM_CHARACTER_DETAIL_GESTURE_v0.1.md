Date: 2026-08-17

# KEY SYNTHESIS
## B69-M01 — EFFECT-FIRST CLOSE READING
`READER EFFECT → exact textual cause → technical function → transferable mechanism → IVDIVO transformation`.

## B69-M02 — MICRO/MACRO SEPARATION
A structural problem is not repaired by sentence polish; a sentence problem should not trigger a book rebuild.

## B69-M03 — SENTENCE RHYTHM BY FUNCTION
No short/long quota. Choose architecture for breath, duration, pressure, thought and emphasis; read aloud after structural lock.

## B69-M04 — PARAGRAPH INTENT
Break only for a meaningful focus/time/speaker/action/revelation/contrast/breath/emphasis change. One-line paragraphs are permitted when earned.

## B69-M05 — CHARACTER METHOD MIX
Character may emerge from action, narrated assessment, thought, dialogue, relation, detail, gesture or omission. Do not demand dossier completeness.

## B69-M06 — DIALOGUE ATTENTION ASYMMETRY
Track what each speaker says AND what the listener actually notices. Inattention/parallel concerns can reveal relationship more truthfully than perfect turn-taking.

## B69-M07 — DETAIL DOUBLE-PAY WITHOUT SYMBOL COMPULSION
Prefer specifics that build credibility + another function, but allow some detail simply to create lived reality.

## B69-M08 — GESTURE AS COMPRESSED CHARACTER
A precise, non-stock gesture can carry status, shame, desire, history or relation. Cut gestures that merely pass time.

## B69-M09 — MOTIVE HUMILITY
Do not reduce action to one wound/diagnosis/parental cause. Preserve uncertainty when evidence supports several models.

## B69-M10 — LIKABILITY FIREWALL
Reader engagement can come from curiosity, fascination, complexity, competence, danger, contradiction or fate. Do not inject sympathy bait by default.

## B69-M11 — OPEN END / LOCAL CLOSURE DISTINCTION
A book may close its local story while leaving life complicated. Do not confuse tidy future accounting with satisfying ending.

# PRODUCTION GATES
- **B69-G01 — RULE VS EFFECT:** when a craft rule conflicts with a successful reader effect, diagnose before enforcing the rule.
- **B69-G02 — SHOW/TELL ROUTER:** dramatize exact moments whose wording/action matters; narrate efficiently where presence adds little.
- **B69-G03 — WORD CHOICE PRESSURE:** important words should encode POV/social/relational meaning rather than generic description.
- **B69-G04 — READ-ALOUD LATE PASS:** rhythm QA only after story/scene/POV stability.
- **B69-G05 — NO PARAGRAPH FRAGMENT KPI:** isolation must be earned by function.
- **B69-G06 — NARRATOR ATTITUDE:** omniscient narration still has personality/values; close-third still filters language.
- **B69-G07 — NO DOSSIER REQUIREMENT:** disclose only character history that changes current inference/action.
- **B69-G08 — NO MOTIVE OVERCERTAINTY:** observed action and inferred motive remain distinct.
- **B69-G09 — DIALOGUE LISTENER MODEL:** listener attention/avoidance must be designed, not assumed.
- **B69-G10 — GESTURE SPECIFICITY:** stock gestures require replacement/cutting unless contextual function makes them specific.
- **B69-G11 — LIKABILITY NOT REQUIRED:** require engagement, not niceness.
- **B69-G12 — LOCAL ENDING CONTRACT:** resolve the book's core conflict before optional saga continuation.

# FAILURE MODES
show-don't-tell monoculture; short-sentence virtue; paragraph fragmentation; narrator-neutrality illusion; character dossier inflation; trauma/motive reduction; likability retrofit; perfect-listener dialogue; faux-futuristic stiffness; detail catalogue; symbol overload; gesture filler; moral explanation; mandatory transformation; tidy-ending compulsion; line-edit murder.

# IVDIVO APPLICATION
## Orbital Youth
Post-money normality should emerge through exact lived particulars, not explanatory dialogue. Youth voice = attitude + attention + word choice + syntax + relationship; not Earth-2020s slang.

## Conscious robots
Treat language, silence and gesture/embodiment as person-specific. Do not make all robots concise, literal or emotionless.

## Rare consciousness schools
Narrator/character confidence is not proof. Preserve `experienced / reproducible / interpreted / contested / established` levels.

## Anti-chop
`CAUSALITY → SCENE NECESSITY → SHOW/TELL → POV → DIALOGUE MODE → PARAGRAPH INTENT → SENTENCE RHYTHM → WORD`.

Structural or modal compression precedes sentence-length manipulation.
