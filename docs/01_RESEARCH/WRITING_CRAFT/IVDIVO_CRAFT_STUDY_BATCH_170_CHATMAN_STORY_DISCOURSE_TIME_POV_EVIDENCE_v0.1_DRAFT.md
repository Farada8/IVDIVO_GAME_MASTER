## Executive result

Chatman supplies a **diagnostic layer router**, not another beat sheet:

`STORY FACT → DISCOURSE SELECTION / ORDER / DURATION → POV / VOICE / ACCESS → READER MODEL → ACTION / INFERENCE`.

A prose defect can therefore be caused by the wrong event, wrong placement, wrong duration, wrong knowledge access, wrong narrator framing or only then wrong wording. The production system must diagnose the level before rewriting.

## New / strengthened production gates

### B170-G01 — STORY ≠ DISCOURSE
Do not rewrite canon because a reveal is too early, too long or through the wrong POV. First test presentation.

### B170-G02 — MANIFESTATION FIREWALL
A function that works in film/audio may require a different implementation in prose. Visual determinacy and screenplay whitespace do not automatically transfer to novels.

### B170-G03 — INFERENCE GAP
Routine recoverable transitions may be omitted. If A→B remains causally legible, do not dramatize suitcase/car/door/corridor/interface logistics by default.

### B170-G04 — KERNEL / SATELLITE CONTEXT
Kernelhood is contextual and can be recognized retrospectively. Do not delete satellites that earn character, mood, social reality, foreshadowing, rhythm or later payoff.

### B170-G05 — SUSPENSE / SURPRISE SPLIT
Known outcome pressure can still be suspense. Surprise requires information discontinuity. Neither implies cliffhanger or fragment density.

### B170-G06 — ORDER / DURATION / FREQUENCY BEFORE LINE EDIT
Before changing sentences, test whether the material should be scene, summary, ellipsis, stretch or pause, and whether repetition is purposeful.

### B170-G07 — EXPOSITION IS FUNCTION, NOT FORM
Exposition may appear through scene, summary, dialogue, document or narration. Do not create conflict merely to disguise information.

### B170-G08 — POV / VOICE SEPARATION
Track separately:
`PERCEIVER | CONCEPTUALIZER | INTEREST OWNER | NARRATING VOICE | KNOWLEDGE ACCESS`.

### B170-G09 — ACCESS LEGITIMACY
A POV may only know what it can perceive, remember, infer or receive through a sourced channel. Rich sensorium is not omniscience.

### B170-G10 — CHARACTER MODEL OPENNESS
`BEHAVIOR → TRAIT HYPOTHESIS`, not `BEHAVIOR → ESSENCE`.
Contradictory acts may update or complicate the model rather than invalidate the character.

### B170-G11 — SETTING AS EXISTENT + CAUSAL SYSTEM
`SETTING / MILIEU → AFFORDANCE / PRESSURE → CHOICE → CONSEQUENCE`.
Setting may also carry mood, verisimilitude, symbolism and social expectation.

### B170-G12 — PRESUPPOSITION AUDIT
For consequential sentences ask:
`ASSERTED? | PRESUPPOSED? | SOURCE? | STATUS?`
A disputed hypothesis must not be grammatically smuggled in as already established.

### B170-G13 — UNRELIABLE DISCOURSE TEST
`NARRATOR ACCOUNT → INDEPENDENT STORY/TRACE MODEL → DISCREPANCY → RELIABILITY STATUS`.
Do not use “unreliable narrator” to excuse continuity errors.

### B170-G14 — INTERIOR MONOLOGUE ≠ FRAGMENT KPI
Thought representation can mix direct/free/indirect modes. Fragmentation is optional and contextual.

### B170-G15 — MULTI-STRAND LOCAL NOW
Parallel strands may possess separate local NOWs. Convergence occurs when strands alter one another's option space, evidence, relationship or decision — not merely when they share a room.

### B170-G16 — THEORY MUST ACCEPT COUNTEREXAMPLES
Any structural lens that cannot accommodate a well-working current manuscript is suspect. `TOOLS DIAGNOSE; STORY DOES NOT EXIST TO VALIDATE TOOLS`.

## Cross-school synthesis

- **Truby/Bell/Swain:** keep causality and changed state, reject causality monoculture.
- **Scofield/Rosenfeld/LaPlante:** scene and summary are modes; Chatman adds formal duration/frequency control.
- **Zunshine/Herman/le Carré:** evidence provenance remains primary; Chatman adds narrator reliability and presupposition.
- **Card/Corbett/McKee Character:** character is reconstructed from evidence and remains open-ended.
- **Doty/Hodgins:** attention and setting selection build reader model; description is not catalogue.
- **Le Guin/Prose/Zinsser:** pacing is not short-sentence percentage; discourse duration precedes sentence tuning.
