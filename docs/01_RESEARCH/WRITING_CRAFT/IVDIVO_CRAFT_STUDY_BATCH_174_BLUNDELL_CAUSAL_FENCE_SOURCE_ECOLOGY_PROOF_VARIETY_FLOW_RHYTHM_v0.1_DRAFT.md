# IVDIVO CRAFT STUDY — BATCH 174
## William E. Blundell — Causal Fence · Source Ecology · Proof Variety · Flow · Rhythm

**Status:** DRAFT / SOURCE FULLY READ AND STRICT-LIFECYCLE SYNTHESIZED  
**Pass:** 139  
**Primary source:** William E. Blundell, *The Art and Craft of Feature Writing* (1988)

## Why this source matters

Blundell is a long-form journalism source, not a fiction formula. His durable value for IVDIVO is the connection between **story range, causal questions, source roles, organization, reader memory and sentence/paragraph flow**. His period-specific newsroom and market assumptions are bounded and do not become fiction law.

## Core synthesis

`CAUSAL FENCE → SOURCE / TRACE ECOLOGY → 1–2 PRIMARY DIMENSIONS → ORGANIZATION → READER MODEL → PROSE`

The story should not expand merely because the world is rich. Every extra layer, expert, detail or causal dimension consumes reader memory and attention.

## Production gates

### B174-G01 — Causal Fence / Story Range
State what causal problem this book/episode owns and what adjacent material remains outside.

### B174-G02 — Primary Causal Dimensions
From `history / scope / reasons / impacts / countermoves / futures`, fully develop only the dimensions required by this story.

### B174-G03 — Source Role Fit
For every important claim ask what kind of source can actually know it: direct actor, implementer, document/log, expert, counter-source, independent observer.

### B174-G04 — Source Independence / Lineage
Different outputs are not independent proof if they descend from one instrument, archive, person or doctrine.

### B174-G05 — Extrapolation Is Hypothesis
`OBSERVED DEVELOPMENT → PROJECTION` stays provisional until external forces/countermodels are tested.

### B174-G06 — Theme Statement Is Provisional
A working theme/main statement organizes discovery but cannot override new text/evidence.

### B174-G07 — Actor-Level Lived Consequence
Translate abstract systems into person-level effects without mistaking one case for prevalence.

### B174-G08 — Microcosm Representativeness
A vivid teenager, robot, practitioner or institution cannot stand for a whole class/civilization without independent support.

### B174-G09 — Countermove Must Change State
A response earns narrative weight when it changes policy, action, evidence, relation, access, responsibility or future options.

### B174-G10 — Projection Boundedness
Future claims require conditions, uncertainty and an explicit ceiling.

### B174-G11 — Interview / Question No-Leading
Question design must not manufacture the desired interpretation.

### B174-G12 — Research Stop Condition
Stop when the story's material questions are sufficiently covered for the next writing/decision step; do not research indefinitely for prestige.

### B174-G13 — Story-Function Index + Provenance
Organize material by story function while preserving exact source/trace identity and evidence status.

### B174-G14 — Narrative Line ≠ Main Engine
Chronological, block, thematic and braided presentation lines are discourse strategies; they do not create ONE MAIN STORY by themselves.

### B174-G15 — Natural Transition / Old→New
The next paragraph/section should pick up an active concept, consequence or question left by the previous one.

### B174-G16 — Reintroduction Cost
If a named source/character returns after a long absence, pay the orientation cost or consolidate the material.

### B174-G17 — Lead Promise Debt
Opening attention must promise the story actually delivered. Mystery is allowed; empty touting is not.

### B174-G18 — Number / Abstraction Integrity
Simplify numbers only when evidentiary precision is not story-relevant. Never beautify away forensic meaning.

### B174-G19 — Direct Quote / Exact Dialogue Worthiness
Use exact wording when wording itself changes evidence, trust, consent, authority, relationship or voice; otherwise consider paraphrase/summary.

### B174-G20 — Local Closure Before Spread-Out Ending
Resolve the local story contract before widening to future/saga implications.

### B174-G21 — Claim Strength ≤ Evidence Strength
Narrative confidence, expert prestige and writer tone cannot exceed the support of the evidence architecture.

### B174-G22 — Description Function
Description must affect story progression, evidence, POV model, contrast, relationship or reader involvement.

### B174-G23 — Metaphor ≠ Evidence
Metaphor can shape experience, not measurements/provenance/status.

### B174-G24 — One Intelligent Reader / Committee Firewall
Write clearly to a reader; do not flatten voice into a committee-safe average.

### B174-G25 — Empty Transition Merge
If a bridge sentence only labels the shift, merge it with the next content sentence, reorder, or delete it.

### B174-G26 — Minimum Necessary Explanation
`PRESENT QUESTION → MINIMUM EXPLANATION FOR NEXT ACTION → MOVE`.
Do not build the entire watch when the reader only needs the time.

### B174-G27 — Speed Can Use Long Sentences
Fast continuous action/thought can be carried by a long sentence with a clear grammatical core.

### B174-G28 — Force May Slow
Important moments may need extra reader-time, detail and syntactic room.

### B174-G29 — Variety / Parallelism
Sentence and paragraph form should vary by effect; parallelism can organize complex material.

### B174-G30 — Long-Form Cast / Memory Budget
Group/reintroduce many sources/characters; do not make the reader carry a phonebook.

### B174-G31 — Foreshadowing Payoff
Early delayed questions/promises must later be paid or consciously reinterpreted.

### B174-G32 — Structure Before Wordcraft
IVDIVO override of Blundell's newsroom micro-cut habits:
`BOOK ENGINE → STRUCTURE → SCENE → PARAGRAPH → SENTENCE → WORD`.

### B174-G33 — Voice Preservation / Committee Firewall
Revision should remove clutter and distortion without erasing individual voice.

## Cross-source convergence

### Blundell × Genette / Chatman
`CAUSAL FENCE + SOURCE MAP → ORDER/DURATION/FREQUENCY/FOCALIZATION → READER MODEL`.

### Blundell × Zunshine / Herman / le Carré
Source variety requires lineage independence. A chorus repeating one origin is not corroboration.

### Blundell × Williams/Bizup
Natural transitions operationalize OLD→NEW and reader-state continuity at paragraph/section scale.

### Blundell × Le Guin / Clark / Hodgins / Collins
Strong convergence:
`SPEED / SUSPENSE ≠ SHORT-SENTENCE DENSITY`.
A long sentence may move faster; force can require slowing.

### Blundell × Mayer / Seger / Bell
Plans and working themes are hypotheses. Actual material can force re-planning.

Blundell's 1988 newsroom practice is not 2026 publishing policy and not automatically fiction craft. Historical newsroom hierarchy, attribution shortcuts, market assumptions and adversarial self-edit rhetoric are retained only when validated by broader cross-source evidence.
