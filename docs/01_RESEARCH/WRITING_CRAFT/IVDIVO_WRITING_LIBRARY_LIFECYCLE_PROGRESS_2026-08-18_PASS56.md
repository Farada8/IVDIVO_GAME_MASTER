# 5. HIGH-VALUE NEW MECHANISMS
## Story / discourse / reader model
`WORLD FACT → SOURCE/TRACE → DISCOURSE ORDER/DURATION → NARRATOR/VOICE → FOCALIZER/ACCESS → READER MODEL`.

## Paratext contract
Title/blurb/genre framing can create reader expectations before page one.

## Primacy inertia
An early interpretation resists later correction; reframe must be earned by contradiction/evidence.

## Who speaks / perceives / evaluates
These are separate variables and must not be collapsed.

## Fallible POV ≠ unreliable narrator
A sincere character can be mistaken without the narration cheating.

## Narrative-level provenance
Institution/archive/specialist-school statements remain sourced claims until independently upgraded.

## Causal strand convergence
Parallel strands converge only when interaction changes shared action/evidence/relationship/decision; recap is not convergence.

## Order / duration / frequency
Use higher-level routing before sentence editing to avoid microdramatization.

## Reality-effect detail
Not every detail requires later plot payoff; some details legitimately establish lived reality, POV or social texture.

## Speech/thought continuum
Use reported/indirect/free-indirect/direct modes and psycho-narration according to function rather than `show don't tell` dogma.

## POV experience ≠ ontology
A genuine experience does not prove its metaphysical interpretation.
