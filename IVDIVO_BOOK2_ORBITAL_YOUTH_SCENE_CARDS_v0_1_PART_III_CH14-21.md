# MIDPOINT / PART-III STORY GATE

## CAUSAL RESPONSIBILITY
**GREEN STRONG.**
Ollie’s work does not single-handedly open the ring. It changes the decision environment by apparently falsifying one conservative trigger model. Competent adults update reasonably.

## FALSE VICTORY
**GREEN.**
The model succeeds twice before it fails. Ollie is rewarded for the exact reasoning that later proves incomplete.

## AOIFE ARC
**GREEN.**
She does not regress into careless promise-breaking. Her failure is inferential privacy: source approval was real, but context made identity/status derivable.

## ETHAN CONTAINMENT
**GREEN.**
Birthday/adult-track line occupies setup → temptation → relationship consequence only. It does not become Book Two’s A-story.

## MAYA CONTINUITY
**GREEN.**
Her Vela Night concealment develops as relational debt; no repeated work-zone authority violation.

## MIDPOINT EVENT
**GREEN STRONG.**
Real residents produce an ordinary movement transition the model did not encode. Embodied warnings lead fixed sensors. The failure directly attacks Ollie’s theory of evidence.

## ADULT COMPETENCE
**GREEN.**
Adults authorize under restrictions, monitor, slow inflow, evacuate competently, and suspend Ollie’s access appropriately.

## HERO PRICE
**GREEN.**
Ollie loses credibility, commissioning access, local trust, clean professional success and moral comfort in his own competence.
