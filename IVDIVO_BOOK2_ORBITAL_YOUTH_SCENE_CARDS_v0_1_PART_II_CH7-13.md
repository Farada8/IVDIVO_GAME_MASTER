# CHAPTER 7 — STATIC SAFE

## SCENE 19 — NOTHING IS BROKEN
**POV:** Ollie
**Immediate want:** identify a faulty component and close the issue quickly.
**Obstacle:** every certified subsystem passes its individual test.
**Action:** Ollie and local apprentices compare ventilation response, partition timing, pressure balance, thermal regulation, spin/load compensation and interface logs.
**Hidden pressure:** a clean component fault would let Ollie prove himself without challenging the commissioning model.
**Mara:** refuses to let the team diagnose by preference; every candidate failure must survive controlled reproduction.
**World reveal:** advanced habitat safety is built from redundancy, logging and verification rather than miraculous self-correction.
**Turn:** the anomalous settling delay does not repeat under static loading.
**Character delta:** Ollie is disappointed by good news because it deprives him of a solvable problem.
**Price:** he stays late again.
**Exit pull:** Iri says the test is artificial because nobody lives by standing still in a room.

## SCENE 20 — PEOPLE MOVE
**POV:** Ollie
**Immediate want:** prove Iri’s objection irrelevant by adding controlled movement.
**Obstacle:** movement introduces too many variables at once.
**Action:** participants cross one partition, change zones, use service points and return.
**Observation:** small lag appears only after a particular sequence, then disappears when participants are rearranged.
**Iri/body report:** one participant describes an early physical/environmental sensation before fixed sensors register deviation.
**Ollie response:** logs it but marks it non-certified.
**Hidden pressure:** he does not want the model to depend on data nobody can standardize.
**Turn:** a second run with nearly identical occupancy but different movement order does not produce the same lag.
**Mystery delta:** occupancy count is not enough; sequence may matter.
**Relationship delta:** Iri thinks Ollie hears words without granting them evidentiary weight.
**Exit pull:** Ollie needs a cleaner timing source.

## SCENE 21 — AFTER SHIFT
**POV:** Ollie
**Immediate want:** mentally keep working after the official shift ends.
**Obstacle:** Ethan is trying to arrange a group call and Ollie promised to join.
**Action:** Ollie replays test timestamps while eating with Iri/local apprentices.
**Ordinary life:** bad shift food, imported snacks, workshop booking, dating around incompatible schedules.
**World reveal:** local status attaches to desirable shifts, fabrication access and commute routes.
**Turn:** apprentices remember movement sequences socially, not as engineering logs.
**Character change:** Ollie writes a human sequence description beside sensor timestamps.
**Price:** he misses most of the group call anyway.
**Exit pull:** Samir’s sport orientation uses exactly the movement timing Ollie lacks.

# CHAPTER 8 — NOT A VISITOR SPORT

## SCENE 22 — THE GAME CHANGES GRAVITY
**POV:** Samir
**Immediate want:** participate as an ordinary new player, not “the kid from the Contact program.”
**Obstacle:** rules assume instinct for momentum changes, handoff timing and zone transitions.
**Action:** Samir learns by playing badly rather than watching an explanation.
**World reveal:** the game cannot exist unchanged on Earth; scoring depends on controlled movement through changing gravity and spatial lanes.
**Turn:** Samir makes one excellent timing read and gains attention he did not want.
**Pressure:** a player recognizes him from Book One coverage.
**Exit pull:** he must decide whether to leave.

## SCENE 23 — SAY IT ONCE
**POV:** Samir
**Immediate want:** stay on the team without becoming a story.
**Obstacle:** one teammate asks about his “profile thing”; another starts to intervene for him.
**Wrong-strategy temptation:** disappear.
**Action:** Samir says clearly what he will and will not discuss, once, without defending the boundary.
**Resistance:** disappointment, awkwardness, acceptance; no villain.
**Character delta:** Samir remains in the room.
**Ordinary payoff:** conversation shifts to whether he is terrible at one specific game move.
**Turn:** he is included through teasing, not protected distance.
**Exit pull:** late transit home routes the team beside commissioning activity.

## SCENE 24 — SEQUENCE
**POV:** Samir
**Immediate want:** get home before host return condition.
**Obstacle:** maintenance closure reroutes passengers through successive partition transitions.
**Action:** Samir notices bodies and airflow respond differently depending on crossing order.
**Observation:** a tiny version of the Ring Six settling pattern seems to follow sequence, not location.
**Uncertainty:** he does not know if it is the same phenomenon.
**Choice:** sends Ollie a narrow observation instead of a theory.
**Relationship delta:** Ollie asks for exact timing rather than praising him; Samir prefers that.
**A-line feed:** movement-order hypothesis gains independent support.
**Exit pull:** Ollie wants sport/transit timing data, but Samir refuses to turn the team into an experiment.
