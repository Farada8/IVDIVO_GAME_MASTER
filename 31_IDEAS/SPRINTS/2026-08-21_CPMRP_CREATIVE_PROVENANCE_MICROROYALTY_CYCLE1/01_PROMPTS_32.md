# CPMRP CYCLE 1 — 32 SEQUENTIAL PROMPTS

## P01 — Define the CPMRP problem and non-goals
**Prompt:** Define a protocol that lets creators publish machine-readable permissions/licences and receive micro-royalties when protected creative assets are actually used. Explicitly exclude ownership of abstract ideas and automatic legal infringement findings.

## P02 — Map idea vs expression vs rights-bearing asset
**Prompt:** Build a classification for IDEA, STYLE, TROPE, EXPRESSION, CHARACTER, WORLD_ELEMENT, TEXT_FRAGMENT, IMAGE, AUDIO, CODE, DATABASE and CONTRACTUAL_REFERENCE.

## P03 — Build a rights-basis matrix
**Prompt:** Separate COPYRIGHT_ASSERTED, CONTRACT_LICENSE, TDM_RESERVATION, OPEN_LICENSE, PUBLIC_DOMAIN, VOLUNTARY_TIP and UNKNOWN.

## P04 — Test EU TDM opt-out fit
**Prompt:** Assess Article 4 DSM/TDM reservation as one legal pathway for AI-training use in the EU.

## P05 — Test C2PA training/mining assertions
**Prompt:** Map C2PA training-mining values allowed/notAllowed/constrained into the protocol.

## P06 — Test TDMRep + ODRL fit
**Prompt:** Determine whether TDMRep/ODRL can carry a licence offer and compensation duty.

## P07 — Map EU AI Act/GPAI obligations
**Prompt:** Identify what the protocol can help AI providers prove without claiming compliance by itself.

## P08 — Assess EU-level TDM registry direction
**Prompt:** Use the 13 July 2026 Commission feasibility study to identify future-proof registry requirements.

## P09 — Benchmark AI Provenance Protocol prior art
**Prompt:** Compare APP-style AI-output provenance with creator-rights provenance.

## P10 — Define Asset ID
**Prompt:** Design stable asset identity independent of filename and hosting URL.

## P11 — Define version lineage
**Prompt:** Model edits, translations, derivatives and re-exports without overwriting origin.

## P12 — Design Rights Passport
**Prompt:** Specify minimum fields required before the system can publish a licence offer.

## P13 — Design policy semantics
**Prompt:** Define permission/prohibition/offer/negotiation states.

## P14 — Normalize usage intent
**Prompt:** Create a machine taxonomy for read, index, mine, train, inference-reference, reproduce, adapt, distribute and commercialize.

## P15 — Model €0.10 pricing
**Prompt:** Determine how to represent ten-cent royalties safely.

## P16 — Avoid payment-fee collapse
**Prompt:** Design settlement so ten-cent events do not become ten-cent card charges.

## P17 — Design licence receipt
**Prompt:** Define receipt binding asset, policy version, usage action, payer, price and consent.

## P18 — Guarantee idempotency
**Prompt:** Prevent retries from charging twice.

## P19 — Design similarity evidence engine
**Prompt:** Use similarity only to discover possible provenance, not to impose money.

## P20 — Design provenance graph
**Prompt:** Represent source/reference/derivative lineage across many assets.

## P21 — Defend against false positives
**Prompt:** Specify minimum requirements before a similarity alert is shown.

## P22 — Handle independent creation
**Prompt:** Prevent a creator from charging another creator for coincidental similarity.

## P23 — Handle public domain and open licences
**Prompt:** Resolve assets whose reuse is already permitted.

## P24 — Protect private prompts and source files
**Prompt:** Define privacy boundary for AI-assisted creative work.

## P25 — Design dispute/appeal flow
**Prompt:** Model claimant and user disagreement.

## P26 — Defend against claim spam/Sybil attacks
**Prompt:** Prevent mass registration of public-domain/common concepts and fraudulent ownership claims.

## P27 — Design platform API
**Prompt:** Define minimal endpoints for registry, policy resolution, licence acceptance, usage events, receipts and payout statements.

## P28 — Design creator UX
**Prompt:** Make the system usable by a person who only wants 'if you use it, send me 10 cents'.

## P29 — Design AI-agent integration
**Prompt:** Allow an AI system to resolve rights before using a source.

## P30 — Bridge to IVDIVO self-improvement
**Prompt:** Integrate without creating a second authority or auto-promoting the protocol.

## P31 — Define MVP
**Prompt:** Choose the smallest implementable product that proves technical value.

## P32 — Red-team and gate
**Prompt:** State GO/NO-GO conditions for moving from R&D to pilot.
