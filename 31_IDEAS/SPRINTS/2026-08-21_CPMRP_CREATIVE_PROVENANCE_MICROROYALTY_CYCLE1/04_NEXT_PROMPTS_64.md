# CPMRP CYCLE 2 — 64 NEXT EVIDENCE-DRIVEN PROMPTS

These are derived from Cycle 1 gaps. They are **designed, not blindly executed**. External legal, market, payment, KYC/tax and human evidence remain separate gates.
