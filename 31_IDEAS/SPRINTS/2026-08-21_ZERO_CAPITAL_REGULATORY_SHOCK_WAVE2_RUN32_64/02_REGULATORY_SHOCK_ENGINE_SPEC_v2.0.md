# REGULATORY SHOCK CAPTURE ENGINE v2.0

## Mission
Detect time-bound mandatory business work and convert it into a zero-founder-cash manual offer, then require real buyer/payment evidence before productisation.

## Engineering modules
1. `AuthorityRebase` — reads fresh GitHub/Drive frontier and rejects stale writes.
2. `RegulatoryShockIngestor` — captures new rules, effective dates, official guidance and enforcement starts.
3. `OfficialSourceGate` — regulatory claims require current first-party authority.
4. `DeadlineFreshnessGate` — recomputes urgency from current date before outreach.
5. `MandatoryPainDetector` — separates must-do compliance from optional optimisation.
6. `ZeroCashGate` — rejects founder cash-out before external proof.
7. `ManualDeliverabilityGate` — requires v0 service to be deliverable with existing/free tools.
8. `CompetitionPressureEstimator` — penalises free/government/commodity substitutes.
9. `LiabilityBoundaryGate` — separates operations/documentation from legal/certification claims.
10. `ChannelPartnerRouter` — tests one-to-many partner channels before direct scaling.
11. `OfferProductizer` — fixed inputs, outputs, turnaround, exclusions and price.
12. `ProspectFitEngine` — scores public evidence of likely use/need before contact.
13. `OutreachExperimentRunner` — controlled variants and small batches.
14. `ProofEventLedger` — immutable E0-E7 events; no model-generated proof promotion.
15. `KillPivotGovernor` — deterministic stop/pivot thresholds.
16. `SelfImprovementDeltaAdapter` — observed failure -> minimal repair -> regression -> provenance.

## State machine
`SIGNAL -> SOURCE_VERIFIED -> WEDGE -> ZERO_CASH_PASS -> LIABILITY_PASS -> MANUAL_SAMPLE -> TARGETED_OUTREACH -> E3 -> PAID_PILOT(E4) -> REPEAT(E5) -> PRODUCTISE -> FINANCE/SCALE`
