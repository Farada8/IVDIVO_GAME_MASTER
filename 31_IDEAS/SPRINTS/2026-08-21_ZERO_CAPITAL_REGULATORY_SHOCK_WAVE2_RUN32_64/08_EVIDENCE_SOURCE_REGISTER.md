## Competition/market observations
- Ireland provides free AI literacy through AIReady/CeADAR and low-cost LEO workshops; generic training is commodity risk.
- Private Irish AI governance/readiness consultancies and self-serve compliance tools already exist; differentiation must be operational implementation evidence, channel leverage, speed and bounded scope.

## Evidence rule
Official source = E1. Public competitor/market observation = E2. Neither may be promoted to E3/E4 without an observed buyer event/payment.
