# SAMPLE — WHITE-LABEL AI GOVERNANCE HANDOFF PACK v0.1

Status: DEMO / FICTIONAL LOW-RISK CLIENT / NOT LEGAL ADVICE
Purpose: show an AI implementation agency the client-facing evidence pack it can hand over after deployment.

## 1. Client AI-use intake
- Business purpose and workflow owner
- AI tools/models/vendors used
- User groups and affected persons
- Personal/confidential data categories
- Human review point
- External/customer-facing AI interaction
- AI-generated content publication channels
- Decision impact and escalation owner
- Staff groups operating the system
- Change/update responsibility

## 2. AI System Inventory — sample
System: Customer Enquiry Assistant
Purpose: classify inbound website enquiries and draft suggested replies.
Deployment role: client uses third-party model/API; final replies require staff approval.
Affected persons: website visitors/customers.
Autonomous decision: none intended.
Human oversight: named customer-service owner reviews before external send.

System: Marketing Content Assistant
Purpose: draft social/blog copy for human editorial review.
Deployment role: client uses third-party generative model.
Affected persons: public audience.
Human oversight: marketing owner approves final publication.

## 3. Role/risk triage
- Record whether client is provider, deployer, importer/distributor or mixed actor for each system.
- Record current risk hypothesis and the official source used.
- Any uncertain/high-risk classification is escalated to qualified counsel/specialist; this pack does not issue legal opinions.

## 4. Article 4 AI-literacy evidence log
Fields: person/team; role; AI systems used; training topic; context-specific risks; human-review requirement; training date; trainer/source; evidence link; refresher due.

## 5. Article 50 transparency checklist
- Does a person interact directly with an AI system? If yes, confirm required disclosure implementation using current official guidance.
- Is content AI-generated/manipulated and within a labelling/marking case? Record technical/visible disclosure control and responsible owner.
- Record exceptions/uncertainties for specialist review rather than guessing.

## 6. Human oversight / escalation matrix
Normal output -> named human reviewer.
Sensitive/confidential data -> do not enter unless approved workflow permits it.
Low-confidence/hallucination risk -> verify against authoritative source.
Complaint or harm signal -> stop automated path; escalate to named owner.
Material system/vendor change -> re-run inventory and controls.

## 7. Vendor/model/source register
Vendor; product/model; business owner; data location/terms link; DPA/privacy review status; model-change watch; contractual source link; last reviewed.

## 8. Change/incident log
Timestamp; system; change/incident; who detected; potential impact; action; escalation; closure evidence.

## 9. 30-day action register
Owner + due date + missing evidence + required external specialist decision + completion proof.

## 10. Handoff statement
This pack organises governance evidence and implementation controls. It does not certify compliance, provide legal advice, or replace regulator/legal/security guidance. Use current EU/Irish official sources and escalate uncertain legal classification.
