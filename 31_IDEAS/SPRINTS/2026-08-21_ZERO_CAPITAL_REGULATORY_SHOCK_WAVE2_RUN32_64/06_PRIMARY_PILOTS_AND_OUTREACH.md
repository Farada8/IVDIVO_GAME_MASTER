# PRIMARY + PILOTS + OUTREACH ARCHITECTURE

## PRIMARY — White-label AI Governance Handoff Pack
Buyer: Irish AI implementation/automation agencies and consultants.
End-user benefit: documented governance handoff after AI implementation.
Agency benefit: reduces handoff risk, adds professional evidence, can be rebranded/bundled.

### Manual deliverables
1. 10-question client AI-use intake
2. AI system inventory
3. provider/deployer/role map
4. Article 4 literacy evidence log
5. Article 50 transparency checklist
6. human oversight/escalation matrix
7. AI vendor/model/source register
8. change/incident log
9. 30-day action register
10. source/provenance page

Boundary: operational governance/documentation support, not legal advice or regulatory certification.
Pilot test price: €149 for one real client handoff after a free sample/demo.
Kill: 20 qualified partner contacts, <2 E3 and zero E4.

## PILOT 1 — CRA Reporting Readiness Drill
Why now: mandatory vulnerability/severe-incident reporting starts 11 September 2026.
Deliverable: 24h/72h/14d flow, RACI, evidence checklist, reporting contact tree, tabletop drill.
Pilot test price: €249.
Boundary: no security certification, legal opinion, or claim that a product is in/out of scope without specialist confirmation.

## PILOT 2 — PPWR Packaging Evidence Register
Why now: PPWR generally applies from 12 August 2026.
Deliverable: packaging SKU map, supplier-data request tracker, missing evidence backlog, specialist/counsel handoff.
Pilot test price: €149.
Boundary: no legal conformity determination.

## Outreach protocol
- 5 contacts per micro-batch.
- Every contact needs public fit evidence.
- Offer names the operational outcome, not generic consulting.
- No bulk scraping/spam.
- E3/E4 only from observed external events.
