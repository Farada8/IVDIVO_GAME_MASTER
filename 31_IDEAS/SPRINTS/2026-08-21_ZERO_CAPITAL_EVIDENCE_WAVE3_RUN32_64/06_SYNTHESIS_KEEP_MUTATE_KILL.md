## Governor decision

- **DEMOTE:** generic AI compliance/training, generic tender radar, broad 'consulting'.

## Why the immediate PRIMARY is Product Compliance Change Pack
1. PPWR is already generally applicable and green-transition consumer rules apply in late September 2026, creating an immediate change window.
2. The deliverables are concrete and low-capital: packaging/SKU register, claims inventory, guarantee/repair information map, missing-evidence backlog and CMS/change tickets.
3. These tasks can be done manually with existing/free tools and do not require us to certify compliance.
4. The same product/SKU/evidence primitives later extend naturally into DPP, making DPP the strategic platform path rather than forcing it as the first wedge.

## Why DPP remains the strategic platform
The new DPP Registry/API and staged product-group rollout create a longer-lived compliance-data infrastructure market. If later evidence supports demand, the engine can migrate from a manual Product Compliance Change Pack into a reusable DPP/compliance-data system.

## Critical limitation
No buyer contact is permitted, therefore willingness-to-pay, conversion, price and unit economics are **not measured**. Scores cannot promote any track above E2+.

## Path to goal
`public authority -> operational data schema -> synthetic/manual fixture -> measured delivery time -> public substitute/vendor map -> cross-regulation reuse -> user-approved external proof later -> paid proof -> automation -> finance -> assets`.
