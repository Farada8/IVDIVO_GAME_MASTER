# CHARACTER CONTRADICTION + SOCIAL REALITY PRESSURE CARDS

## N21 — Behavioral Contradiction Card

Required fields:
- CHARACTER / UNIT
- COMPETING VALUE OR DESIRE A
- COMPETING VALUE OR DESIRE B
- TRIGGER
- AVAILABLE ACTIONS
- CHOSEN ACTION
- IMMEDIATE BENEFIT
- COST / DAMAGE
- SELF-JUSTIFICATION OR LIE
- WHO NOTICES / WHO PAYS
- LATER REPEATED OR CHANGED PATTERN
- WHAT WOULD PROVE TRANSFORMATION

**Gate:** adjective pairs such as `strong but vulnerable` do not count unless the contradiction changes a choice under pressure.

Private biography/rehearsal remains private unless it changes observable action or must be known for reader inference.

### N22 validation
- D04 is a valid stress domain because the current project state protects the rule that Rowan must not carry erased affection into a new cycle; therefore remembered intimacy versus current consent creates behaviorally testable contradiction/choice rather than a label-only trait.
- A generic `cold hero who is secretly caring` fails unless care costs status/control/time/safety or changes the relationship state.
- A heroine described as independent but who never makes a costly autonomous choice fails.

**Verdict:** PASS at mechanism level; current-project sampling is bounded by available persisted summaries/state and is not a substitute for manuscript-wide character audit.

## N23 — Social Reality Pressure Card

Use only fields that can change strategy, access, risk, timing, status or relationship:
- MONEY / DEBT / INCOME
- WORK / SHIFT / CAREER RISK
- HOUSING / SPACE / PRIVACY
- CLASS / STATUS / REPUTATION
- FAMILY OBLIGATION / CARE DUTY
- LAW / CONTRACT / CONSENT / LIABILITY
- BUREAUCRACY / PERMISSION / JURISDICTION
- COMMUNITY / PEER NORMS
- TRANSPORT / GEOGRAPHIC ACCESS
- EDUCATION / CREDENTIAL / INSTITUTIONAL POWER
- TECHNOLOGY / INFRASTRUCTURE AS SOCIAL CONSTRAINT
- CULTURE / SPECIES / HABITAT NORM WHEN STORY-CAUSAL

For each active pressure record:
`PRESSURE -> WHO ENFORCES IT -> ACCESS/CHOICE CHANGED -> NEW RISK/PRICE -> CHARACTER RESPONSE -> RELATIONSHIP/STORY DELTA`.

Decorative references to poverty, wealth, school, bureaucracy or status fail if they do not change behavior.

### N24 controlled applications
- COMMERCIAL ROMANCE: money/status must change access, boundary, public/private cost or future choice; luxury description alone fails.
- ORBITAL YOUTH: housing, transport, maintenance, first jobs, habitat safety and mixed-species norms must materially alter adolescence/independence; Earth-school routines with space wallpaper fail.
- SMITH/OES: jurisdiction, old agreements, bureaucratic authority and historical liability must alter who can act, what methods are legal/known and what cooperation costs; policy exposition without action fails.

**Evidence class:** INTERNAL MODEL/MECHANISM VALIDATION. Promotion still requires project pilots.
