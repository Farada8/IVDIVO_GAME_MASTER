# IVDIVO LIBRARY — BASELINE SOURCE ROLES FROM STORY ENGINE v4.1

**Status:** inherited production intelligence; reconcile against the actual Drive originals during migration.  
**Purpose:** preserve the already-established functional routing so moving files does not reset the library to a pile of books.

## Core principle

Sources do not have equal jobs or equal weight. Use each for the problem it is demonstrably strong at solving. Duplicates do not increase weight. Fragments cannot support whole-book conclusions. Only IVDIVO corpus defines IVDIVO canon.

## Preserved source-role map (core entries)

- **John Truby — The Anatomy of Story** — story architecture: weakness/need, desire, opponent, revelations, moral choice, climax, transformation, scene/plot causality. Do not copy ready-made plots, dialogue voice or style.
- **John le Carré — Tinker Tailor Soldier Spy** — institutional realism / intelligence procedure: professional habits, information asymmetry, interviews, files, rival interpretations, compromised self-audit, career incentives. Do not transplant Cold War surface, Circus terminology, identities or exact mole structure.
- **Kazuo Ishiguro — The Remains of the Day (available opening sample in inherited audit)** — self-deception / service identity: role fused with identity, emotion displaced into procedure, restrained relationship conflict. Fragment limitation applies; do not imitate Stevens' diction.
- **Blake Crouch — Dark Matter** — identity / alternate-realization thriller: simple human want anchoring large ontology, alternate-self conflict, discrepancy tests, agency under multiplicity. Do not copy box/multiverse surface, characters or exact twists.
- **Blake Crouch — Recursion** — memory / causality / intervention ethics: symptom-before-mechanism, recursive consequence, origin vs symptom, knowledge hazard, ethical correction under uncertainty. Do not copy the chair or exact timeline architecture.
- **Andy Weir — Project Hail Mary** — discovery through action: observation → hypothesis → test → result → new question; competence shown in work; rules as measurable constraints; discovery changing mission. Do not imitate voice or transplant ship/alien plot.
- **Martha Wells — Murderbot** — autonomy / controlled-operator logic: choice proves personhood, control layer vs capability, professional competence, attachment through action, controlled-agent ethics. Do not copy SecUnit/Corporation Rim surface or distinctive sardonic voice.
- **Matt Dinniman — Dungeon Crawler Carl** — retention / modular progression: finite objectives, visible state changes, micro-payoffs, modular arenas, value choice with measurable cost, transition hooks. Do not import LitRPG UI/XP/floor surface or comic voice.
- **Jim Butcher — Storm Front** — recurring hidden-world case structure: case intake, rule as investigative tool, costed ability, clue → action, small-rule payoff, local closure + mythology drip. Do not copy wizard-detective surface, Chicago mythology or quip style.
- **Dan Brown — The Da Vinci Code** — clue chain / information pacing: question → clue → location/object/person → new question, physical information carriers, complementary expertise, micro-cliffhangers. Do not copy exact puzzles, secret-society mythology or treat historical claims as fact.
- **James Rollins — Sigma / The Sand Devil** — artifact/archive/expedition engine: stable professional team + new case, differentiated expertise, legend tested as mechanism, location chain changing hazards, institutional compromise, opponent seeking functional capability, aftermath redistributing assignments. Do not copy Sigma team, destinations, exact artifacts or action set pieces.
- **Sergey Lukyanenko / Watch material in inherited library** — hidden-service jurisdiction and layered oversight only where the actually available text supports it. Do not import Watch cosmology, terminology, faction identities or plot structures.
- **Leigh Bardugo / Ninth House material** — specialized offices, imperfect oversight, institutional privilege, hidden-world social friction. Do not copy Yale/Houses surface or character identities.
- **J. G. Ballard / Viktor Pelevin and other idea-lab sources** — conceptual laboratories, not default narrative architecture. Use for speculative pressure-testing and thematic possibility; do not let idea density replace human causality.
- **Comparative esoteric/metaphysical corpus** — world-source/comparative research only. It may inform hypotheses, historical interpretations and language, but does not override current IVDIVO canon.
