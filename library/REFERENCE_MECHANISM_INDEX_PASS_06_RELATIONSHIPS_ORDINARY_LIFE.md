# IVDIVO REFERENCE MECHANISM INDEX — PASS 06

Status: WORKING / REFERENCE ONLY / NOT CANON
Date: 2026-08-16
Scope: relationships, romance, friendship, family, youth status and ordinary-life story engines.

Primary law:
**Relationship is story when people want something from each other, resist, act, misread, risk, choose, pay and change.**

Do not use romance/friendship/family as decoration between “real plot” scenes.

# SOURCE SET L — GWEN HAYES / ROMANCING THE BEAT

Source: `_OceanofPDF.com_Romancing_the_Beat_-_Gwen_Hayes.epub`
Current location: ChatGPT File Library reference; not yet confirmed as a Drive master original.
Use: romance-arc architecture only.

## M094 — RELATIONSHIP HAS ITS OWN CAUSAL ARC

SOURCE MECHANISM:
The romance arc is treated as a structured movement of its own and must be braided with external plot.

STORY FUNCTION:
Prevents the relationship from becoming a reward handed to characters after the external climax.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
If an ORBITAL YOUTH book contains romance, its relationship state must change because of events in the main story, and relationship choices must alter the main story in return.

FAILURE MODE:
Action chapter → unrelated flirting scene → action chapter → kiss at ending.

---

## M095 — TWO PEOPLE, TWO INTERNAL OBSTACLES

SOURCE MECHANISM:
Both protagonists need a meaningful internal reason why full intimacy/commitment is difficult.

STORY FUNCTION:
Creates two-directional resistance.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
Both people in a major relationship need private desire, fear, wrong strategy and price. One person cannot exist only to heal the protagonist.

---

## M096 — ADHESION: A REASON THEY CANNOT SIMPLY WALK AWAY

SOURCE MECHANISM:
After initial attraction/resistance, story circumstances force continued interaction.

STORY FUNCTION:
Generates repeated tests instead of one encounter plus author convenience.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
Orbital adhesion can come from shared housing allocation, shift pairing, training cohort, transport delay, repair contract, expedition team, family obligation, competition, emergency duty or conflicting claims on the same resource.

FAILURE MODE:
Characters repeatedly meet by coincidence because the author wants more scenes.

---

## M097 — INTIMACY GROWS THROUGH MODEL REVISION

SOURCE MECHANISM:
A character sees the other do something that contradicts the simplistic model previously held: competence, kindness, restraint, courage, vulnerability, loyalty, humor, truth-telling.

STORY FUNCTION:
Attraction becomes knowledge.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
Every significant relationship step should answer: **What did A learn about B that A did not know before, and how does that alter behavior?**

---

## M098 — TWO STEPS FORWARD / ONE STEP BACK

SOURCE MECHANISM:
Relationship development advances through approach and retreat rather than smooth linear closeness.

STORY FUNCTION:
Preserves uncertainty while making progress cumulative.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
After a genuine moment of closeness, activate the fear/status/family/institutional problem that makes one person pull back — but do not reset them to zero.

---

## M099 — EXTERNAL GOALS SHOULD CREATE REAL RELATIONAL CONFLICT

SOURCE MECHANISM:
Strong romance pairs can want incompatible external outcomes without either person being immoral.

STORY FUNCTION:
Conflict arises from life, not misunderstanding alone.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
Example structure only: one youth needs to accept a prestigious transfer to another habitat; the other needs them to stay because a family/work obligation depends on the local team. Both can be right.

---

## M100 — MIDPOINT CHANGES THE MEANING OF THE RELATIONSHIP

SOURCE MECHANISM:
By the middle, the connection can no longer honestly be treated as the same thing it was at the start.

STORY FUNCTION:
Raises emotional stakes.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
The midpoint need not be a kiss/sex/confession. It can be trust under danger, choosing the other over status, revealing a secret, accepting bodily vulnerability, or publicly taking their side.

---

## M101 — RETREAT MUST REACTIVATE THE CORE WOUND/FEAR

SOURCE MECHANISM:
Doubt and retreat work when increased intimacy threatens the character's existing protective strategy.

STORY FUNCTION:
Breakup/withdrawal becomes causal rather than arbitrary.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
Do not create a late-book separation from one overheard sentence that could be solved in thirty seconds. Make the rupture emerge from a fear already demonstrated through behavior.

---

## M102 — REPAIR MUST COST SOMETHING SPECIFIC

SOURCE MECHANISM:
The late relationship choice/gesture proves change by confronting what previously prevented commitment.

STORY FUNCTION:
Turns declaration into action.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
A repair gesture must risk status, safety, career, autonomy, reputation, money, family approval, access or another value the character previously protected.

FAILURE MODE:
Perfect speech fixes months of damage.

---

## M103 — RELATIONSHIP RESOLUTION IS VISIBLE BEHAVIOR

SOURCE MECHANISM:
The end state should show that the people can now do something relationally that they could not do at the beginning.

STORY FUNCTION:
Payoff without explanatory moral.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
Show the new relationship through ordinary behavior: shared planning, boundary respect, public acknowledgment, division of work, honest disagreement, asking for help, or choosing future logistics together.

# SOURCE SET M — FRIDAY NIGHT LIGHTS / YOUTH SOCIAL ECOLOGY

## M104 — ATTRACTION IS EMBEDDED IN STATUS

SOURCE MECHANISM:
Romance, sex, friendship and reputation operate inside peer hierarchy, sport, family expectation and public judgment.

STORY FUNCTION:
A private relationship has social consequences.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
In orbital youth culture, relationships affect cohort standing, housing, team selection, feeds, family alliances, work assignments and mobility.

---

## M105 — PUBLIC ROLE / PRIVATE RELATIONSHIP ASYMMETRY

SOURCE MECHANISM:
A character can be admired publicly while privately ashamed, betrayed, uncertain or grieving.

STORY FUNCTION:
Creates pressure without exposition.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
Status systems should make a relationship harder, not just provide decorative popularity metrics.

---

## M106 — FAMILY LOVE CAN BE OPPOSITION WITHOUT VILLAINY

SOURCE MECHANISM:
Parents often want a child safe/successful while that child needs autonomy, privacy or a different future.

STORY FUNCTION:
Generates conflict between legitimate goods.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
Orbital families can disagree over dangerous training, relocation, synthetic-body use, mixed-species relationships, debt, education or work without one side being caricatured as backward.

---

## M107 — BODY CHANGE REWRITES RELATIONSHIPS

SOURCE MECHANISM:
Physical injury/disability changes family behavior, romance, self-image, dependence and ordinary routines.

STORY FUNCTION:
Body consequence propagates socially.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
Synthetic embodiment, injury or altered environmental body must affect touch, privacy, attraction, care, independence, family fear and social treatment — not only combat capability.

# SOURCE SET N — VERONICA MARS

## M108 — FRIENDSHIP CAN BEGIN WITH A COSTLY ACTION

SOURCE MECHANISM:
A relationship starts because one young person intervenes publicly on behalf of another under social risk.

STORY FUNCTION:
Trust is earned before it is named.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
Build friendship through choices: cover a shift, lose status, share scarce transport credit, defend someone in a public feed, reveal evidence, take blame, stay during an emergency.

---

## M109 — SOCIAL WORLD REVEALED THROUGH WHO MAY SIT WHERE / GET WHAT

SOURCE MECHANISM:
School hierarchy is shown through food access, seating, prestige, family power and exclusion.

STORY FUNCTION:
Worldbuilding becomes relationship pressure.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
Show orbital class/status through invitations, modules, queues, work privileges, recreation access, body upgrades, transport priority and who is socially allowed to associate with whom.

---

## M110 — PROTECTIVE VOICE CAN HIDE ATTACHMENT

SOURCE MECHANISM:
A sharp, ironic or dismissive voice may be a defense against betrayal and vulnerability.

STORY FUNCTION:
Voice and psychology reinforce each other.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
Do not make witty youth dialogue emotionally transparent. A joke can attack, test, deflect, flirt, beg for reassurance or hide fear.

# SOURCE SET O — MY SO-CALLED LIFE

## M111 — THOUGHT / SPEECH GAP IS A YOUTH ENGINE

SOURCE MECHANISM:
The young character's interior model can be articulate while actual speech becomes awkward, evasive, fragmented or badly timed.

STORY FUNCTION:
Creates age-specific truth and humor.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
Teenagers must not consistently deliver their best internal analysis aloud. Preserve the gap between what they understand, what they think they understand, and what they can say under social pressure.

---

## M112 — MISREADING IS CHARACTER, NOT PLOT CHEATING

SOURCE MECHANISM:
Young people infer attraction, rejection, status and motives from incomplete signals and often act on the inference.

STORY FUNCTION:
Miscommunication generates behavior.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
A believable misunderstanding must arise from prior desire/fear/status bias and cause a consequential action.

FAILURE MODE:
Characters hide obvious information for no reason to extend the plot.

---

## M113 — ORDINARY TASKS CAN CARRY INTIMACY

SOURCE MECHANISM:
Shared mundane activity creates space for indirect conversation, analogy, embarrassment and unexpected recognition.

STORY FUNCTION:
Allows emotional development without “relationship scene” labeling.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
Use repairs, cooking, suit maintenance, laundry, transit, shopping, habitat gardening, shift cleanup, body calibration or paperwork as relational arenas.

---

## M114 — CRUSHES COMPETE WITH OTHER POSSIBLE RELATIONSHIPS

SOURCE MECHANISM:
A person can remain fixated on one desired relationship while failing to notice another genuine possibility.

STORY FUNCTION:
Desire distorts attention.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
Youth relationships should contain bad timing, asymmetry and missed opportunities. Not every attraction must be reciprocated or narratively rewarded.

# SOURCE SET P — LINDA SEGER / RELATIONSHIP SUBPLOT ARCHITECTURE

## M115 — B-STORY NEEDS SETUP, TURNS AND PAYOFF

SOURCE MECHANISM:
A relationship subplot has its own structure and should intersect the main plot rather than merely run beside it.

STORY FUNCTION:
Creates causal braid.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
At least one major relationship turn should change a main-story decision; at least one main-story turn should permanently change the relationship.

---

## M116 — CLIMAXES CAN CONVERGE WITHOUT BECOMING IDENTICAL

SOURCE MECHANISM:
Main plot and relationship plot can reach decisive turns near each other and reinforce the protagonist's transformation.

STORY FUNCTION:
Emotional and external payoff arrive as one causal experience.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
The hero should not first save the habitat and then separately “deal with the relationship.” The climax choice can simultaneously decide safety, loyalty, independence and who the hero is willing to become.

# CROSS-SOURCE SYNTHESIS

## S17 — GENERAL RELATIONSHIP STORY LOOP

`PERSON A WANTS SOMETHING FROM B → B HAS A DIFFERENT WANT → REPEATED INTERACTION → MODEL REVISION → VULNERABILITY → TEST → BREACH/RETREAT → COSTLY CHOICE → NEW RELATIONSHIP STATUS`

Works for romance, friendship, siblings, parent-child, mentor-student and professional partnerships with different genre expectations.

---

## S18 — ORBITAL YOUTH RELATIONSHIP LOOP

`ATTRACTION/FRIENDSHIP NEED → PEER VISIBILITY → STATUS/FAMILY/WORK PRESSURE → IMMATURE OR DEFENSIVE TACTIC → PUBLIC/PRIVATE MISMATCH → SOCIAL CONSEQUENCE → EXTERNAL CRISIS TESTS THE BOND → CHOICE CHANGES INDEPENDENCE`

This is a core antidote to “Earth school transplanted into space.”

---

## S19 — ORDINARY INTIMACY LOOP

`SHARED TASK → SMALL FRICTION → COMPETENCE/WEAKNESS EXPOSED → INDIRECT PERSONAL REVEAL → CHANGED ASSUMPTION → CALLBACK LATER UNDER HIGHER STAKES`

Purpose:
Make readers care about relationships before the emergency.

---

## S20 — FAMILY / AUTONOMY LOOP

`FAMILY LOVE → DEPENDENCE/RESOURCE CONTROL → CHILD/YOUNG ADULT WANTS AUTONOMY → LEGITIMATE PARENTAL FEAR → SECRET OR BAD TACTIC → CONSEQUENCE → BOUNDARY NEGOTIATION → NEW DEGREE OF INDEPENDENCE`

No automatic villain parent.

---

## S21 — RELATIONSHIP / A-STORY BRAID TEST

For each major relationship ask:

1. What main-story event forces them together/apart?
2. What relationship decision changes the main-story plan?
3. What private fact changes tactical trust?
4. What public consequence changes status?
5. What does the climax force them to choose about each other?

If all five answers are “nothing,” the relationship is decorative.

---

# RELATIONSHIP STORY GATE v0.1

Before greenlighting a major relationship line:

1. What does A want from B now?
2. What does B want from A now?
3. Why can they not both get exactly what they want unchanged?
4. What is each person's private fear?
5. What ordinary-life context makes them real people outside the plot?
6. What first action earns or damages trust?
7. What does each initially misunderstand about the other?
8. What event revises that model?
9. What public/status/family pressure acts on the bond?
10. What choice costs one of them something real?
11. What is the breach/low point caused by?
12. Why can it not be solved by one obvious sentence?
13. What climax action proves change?
14. What new relationship state is visible in behavior?
15. Would removing this relationship alter the main story?

FAIL if major answers are only dialogue topics.

# RED TEAM — PASS 06

FATAL:
- relationship exists only because franchise planning says two characters will date;
- one character has no independent want or life;
- climax relationship choice costs nothing;
- relationship resolution contradicts established behavior without causal change.

MAJOR:
- teenage dialogue sounds like adult therapy/policy language;
- attraction replaces actual knowledge of the other person;
- one misunderstanding could be solved instantly but is stretched for chapters;
- family opposition is cartoonishly irrational;
- romance is detached from external plot;
- friendship is declared rather than earned;
- synthetic-body change has no effect on intimacy or social life.

MEDIUM:
- every relationship follows the same banter rhythm;
- every attractive pairing becomes romance;
- youth characters have no jealousy, embarrassment, status anxiety, boredom or bad timing;
- ordinary life disappears whenever plot begins.

---

# PRODUCTION CONSEQUENCE FOR ORBITAL YOUTH

Every major youth character should now have, in addition to plot function:

- family relationship;
- one friendship with independent pressure;
- one status/reputation vulnerability;
- money/work/housing reality;
- ordinary recurring task or hobby;
- attraction/romance state where relevant, including NONE;
- one relationship they routinely misread;
- one person who sees through their public persona;
- one relationship price paid during the book;
- a visibly changed social position by the resolution.

Story first. Character second. World through relationships and ordinary life.
