## C21 — JOHN LE CARRÉ — TINKER TAILOR SOLDIER SPY

**BEST FOR:** institutional realism, compromised trust, information asymmetry, old networks, quiet suspense.

### Mechanisms
- Threat may originate inside the trusted system; therefore access/history/status become weapons.
- Competence can be quiet: listening, comparison, memory, procedural patience.
- Information value depends on source, motive, chain of custody and who is allowed to know.
- Old relationships distort current judgment without requiring melodramatic betrayal speeches.
- Institutional language conceals fear, rivalry, guilt and self-protection.
- Investigation advances through revising a model of the organization, not merely collecting clues.
- A compromised institution can still contain honorable people; moral map ≠ organizational map.

### IVDIVO use
SMITH: Old Earth Security should contain historical successes, crimes, debts, factions and people whose loyalty cannot be reduced to ‘old system = good/bad.’ Confederation investigation must confront access asymmetry.

### Risk
Too much procedural opacity can become homework. Pair with a concrete human objective and local scene pressure.

## C22 — ANDY WEIR — PROJECT HAIL MARY

**BEST FOR:** discovery through action, first contact, error correction, competence with limits, cross-species trust.

### Mechanisms
- Observation → hypothesis → test → result → revised model is a story engine.
- Wrong hypotheses are productive when they change the next experiment.
- Scientific detail becomes dramatic when it alters survival/options.
- Language can be built from shared measurable referents rather than instant translation.
- Trust grows through fulfilled small promises and joint work before high-risk cooperation.
- Different bodies/senses imply different technologies and assumptions.
- Humor under pressure humanizes competence.
- Discovery can reveal character history and ethical weakness, not merely world facts.

### IVDIVO use
Nonhuman contact, AI partnership and synthetic-body adaptation should use observable shared phenomena before terminology/ontology.

### Risk
Competence porn: technical solution must not erase moral, relational or institutional price.

## C23 — BLAKE CROUCH — DARK MATTER

**BEST FOR:** identity, counterfactual life, family anchor, consequences of alternative choices.

### Mechanisms
- High concept gains emotional force by attaching itself to an ordinary intimate life before rupture.
- Identity is tested by continuity of memory plus behavior under changed circumstances.
- Alternative selves externalize roads not taken and competing values.
- Choice becomes meaningful precisely because infinite possibility does not remove attachment.
- Scientific mechanism escalates into personal competition over a single lived life.
- Familiar places/people become uncanny when history differs.
- Return is not enough: protagonist must choose what version of self to become after knowledge changes him.

### IVDIVO use
Embodiment/alternate bodies: continuity cannot be established by data alone; relationships, commitments and costly choices become identity tests.

### Risk
Do not copy multiverse machinery or chase structure; take only identity-pressure mechanism.

## C24 — BLAKE CROUCH — RECURSION

**BEST FOR:** memory, causality, intervention ethics, personal grief at conceptual scale.

### Mechanisms
- Abstract anomaly first appears as intimate suffering before global explanation.
- False/alternate memory is convincing because tiny ordinary details carry emotional reality.
- A technology built to heal personal loss can acquire civilization-level consequences.
- Repeated intervention does not reset moral cost; actors accumulate knowledge/trauma.
- Timeline change produces asymmetrical knowledge and therefore new power relations.
- Scientific obsession and family grief can be the same causal line.
- Escalation works by widening radius: individual → relationship → institution → society → reality model.

### IVDIVO use
Memory transfer, consciousness backup and synthetic embodiment must preserve psychological/relational consequences of competing autobiographical continuity.

### Risk
Concept escalation can flatten secondary characters. Always retain human anchors.

## C25 — KAZUO ISHIGURO — KLARA AND THE SUN

**BEST FOR:** artificial subjectivity, service identity, observation, obsolescence, human projection onto artificial persons.

### Mechanisms
- Subjectivity can emerge through selective attention and interpretation rather than philosophical speeches.
- Service role creates an asymmetry: the artificial person studies humans because survival/function depends on reading them.
- Market/model hierarchy creates fear of obsolescence among artificial beings.
- Human attachment may coexist with instrumental plans to replace/use the artificial person.
- Limited or mistaken ontology can still support sincere agency and care.
- Ordinary domestic scenes reveal legal/social status more effectively than exposition.
- Personhood becomes dramatic when care, imitation, replaceability and unique relationship conflict.

### IVDIVO use
Synthetic people should have work, status, market history, relationships, misunderstandings and private models of reality.

### Risk
Do not turn artificial characters into pure innocence or moral oracle.

## C26 — KAZUO ISHIGURO — NEVER LET ME GO

**BEST FOR:** normalized exploitation, institutional language, fate, professional identity, suppressed rebellion.

### Mechanisms
- Exploitative system becomes more disturbing when normalized through routine vocabulary and care institutions.
- Victims can build identity, pride, romance and professional competence inside an unjust structure.
- Partial knowledge changes life even before full explanation.
- Ordinary jealousy/friendship make systemic horror human rather than abstract.
- Euphemistic institutional language regulates what people imagine is possible.
- A completed revelation can arrive too late to create escape but still force ethical recognition.
- Lack of rebellion can itself be the story if socialization has shaped the horizon of imaginable action.

### IVDIVO use
Synthetic-body/personhood law: dangerous systems should sometimes be benevolent in tone and bureaucratically normal, not cartoonishly cruel.

### Risk
Do not imitate the specific donor/cloning premise; abstract only normalized-function/exploitation mechanics.

## C27 — MARTHA WELLS — MURDERBOT / ALL SYSTEMS RED CORPUS

**BEST FOR:** autonomy, controlled operator, private subjectivity, competence, privacy, human-machine relationships.

### Mechanisms
- Control technology creates story when the subject can technically circumvent it but still faces social/legal consequences.
- Private leisure/preferences prove personhood more efficiently than argument.
- Competence and social discomfort can coexist.
- The artificial/security worker may protect humans without wanting emotional intimacy.
- Corporate contractual structures create antagonism independent of a singular villain.
- Autonomy grows through self-chosen obligations, not merely escape from control.
- Humor emerges from mismatch between internal commentary and professional behavior.

### IVDIVO use
AI/synthetic operators need privacy, boredom, entertainment, boundaries, professional pride and self-selected loyalties.

### Risk
Do not copy governor-module/security-unit specifics or voice pattern.

## C28 — JIM BUTCHER — STORM FRONT

**BEST FOR:** case engine, hidden-world procedural, serial protagonist, rule reveal through investigation.

### Mechanisms
- A paid/professional case gives the protagonist an immediate ordinary reason to enter hidden-world danger.
- Parallel cases can converge causally, increasing apparent scale without separate lore plots.
- Magical/specialist knowledge is revealed while solving concrete problems.
- Institutional authorities may need the specialist while distrusting the specialist.
- Personal history complicates professional neutrality.
- Limits/costs on ability preserve investigation.
- Local case can close while protagonist/world relationships remain altered for series continuation.

### IVDIVO use
SMITH can use case logic, but must differentiate itself through Old Earth ↔ Confederation institutional conflict and non-monster outcomes (containment, negotiation, agreement, jurisdiction, removal, neutralization).

### Risk
Avoid generic lone wizard/private-investigator voice/structure.

## C29 — SUZANNE COLLINS — THE HUNGER GAMES

**BEST FOR:** youth under institution, public/private identity, family motive, status spectacle, social survival.

### Mechanisms
- Immediate sacrificial family choice creates a clean WHY NOW rooted in ordinary love.
- Survival is inseparable from performance for an audience/institution.
- Public image becomes a material resource through sponsorship/status.
- Hero's authentic anger may need translation into socially legible persona.
- Romance/public narrative can be both genuine feeling and survival strategy, creating productive ambiguity.
- Class/geography are embodied through food, work, skills and expectations.
- Institutional spectacle converts violence into social hierarchy and entertainment.
- Youth competence does not eliminate vulnerability, confusion or dependence on adults/mentors.

### IVDIVO use
Orbital Youth: ratings, public feeds, selection systems, internships, sport or expedition status may materially affect resources and relationships without copying dystopian spectacle.

### Risk
Avoid importing arena/reaping/rebellion structure. Use only social-performance/resource mechanisms.

## C30 — BRANDON SANDERSON — SKYWARD

**BEST FOR:** YA military/cosmic development, shame/status, team formation, competence, hidden institutional truth.

### Mechanisms
- Public family stigma gives protagonist's ambition both external obstacle and internal distortion.
- Heroic self-image can be a wrong strategy: courage is initially confused with refusal to retreat.
- Training works when team deaths/failures alter relationships and doctrine, not just skill level.
- Peer group gradually changes from competitors/labels into interdependent team.
- Secret technology/AI can be introduced through repair/use/argument rather than encyclopedia explanation.
- Institutional cover-up intersects personal family identity.
- Tactical competence must be revised by statistical/ethical reality; retreat may become courage.
- Humor and bravado can mask shame and loneliness.

### IVDIVO use
ORBITAL YOUTH jobs/training should have real professional stakes and status systems, while keeping family reputation, friendship and emotional immaturity active.

### Risk
Avoid military-academy transplant as default orbital youth model. Use only where story earns it.
