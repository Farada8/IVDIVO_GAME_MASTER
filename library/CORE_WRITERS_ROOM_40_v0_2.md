## SOURCE AUTHORITY

No book is canon. Authority is task-specific.

When sources disagree:
1. Current story causality.
2. Character truth and emotional consequence.
3. IVDIVO production canon.
4. Specialist source matching the problem.
5. Cross-source synthesis.

## CALL LIMIT

Default Writers’ Room call = **2–4 sources**, not all 40. See `CORE_ROUTING_MATRIX_v0_1.md`.

## STORY-FIRST SAFETY

The Core-40 exists to prevent weak story, not to generate more theory. Once a book architecture passes Story Gate, the Studio must proceed to scenes/prose instead of indefinitely mining references without new evidence.
