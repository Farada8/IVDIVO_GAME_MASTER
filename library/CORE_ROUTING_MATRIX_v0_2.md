# IVDIVO CORE ROUTING MATRIX — v0.2

Status: WORKING / OPERATING STANDARD
Date: 2026-08-16
Coverage: CORE_WRITERS_ROOM_40 v0.3 — 40/40 deep-read complete v0.1

Purpose: route a live manuscript/story problem to the smallest useful set of reference sources. The Studio does not consult all 40 sources for every task.

## ROUTING LAW

`EXACT STORY FAILURE → 2–4 SOURCES → ABSTRACT MECHANISM → IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION → STORY TEST → WRITE`

If the solution requires proprietary nouns, distinctive scene order or signature inventions from a reference, reject it and abstract further.

---

## 1. BROKEN CAUSALITY / EPISODIC STORY
Call:
- Truby — *Anatomy of Story*
- Swain — *Techniques of the Selling Writer*
- Bickham — *Scene & Structure*
Optional: Bell — *Plot & Structure*

Ask:
- What does the hero want now?
- What action follows from that want?
- What new information/result forces a decision?
- Does the next scene exist because of the previous scene?

STOP when a causal chain exists.

## 2. WEAK OPPOSITION / EASY HERO
Call:
- McKee — *Story* (antagonism)
- Bell — *Conflict & Suspense*
- Corbett — *Art of Character* if opposition is relational

Ask:
- What forces oppose the will—not just which villain?
- Does resistance force a more revealing choice?
- Is the protagonist an underdog against the total pressure system?

## 3. DEAD SCENE
Call:
- Rosenfeld — *Make a Scene*
- Bickham — *Scene & Structure*
- McKee — *Dialogue* if conversation-heavy
- Lodge — *Art of Fiction* if scene is technically correct but lifeless

Ask:
- Who wants what in the scene?
- Why now?
- What resists?
- What value/information/status changes?
- Is implication stronger than explanation?

## 4. WEAK OPENING / NO PAGE-TURN
Call:
- Edgerton — *Hooked*
- Bell — *Conflict & Suspense*
- Lodge — Beginning
- Truby — desire/problem check

Avoid fake action. The opening must create a story-worthy disturbance, question or desire.

## 5. FLAT CHARACTER
Call:
- McKee — *Character*
- Corbett — *Art of Character*
- Card — *Characters & Viewpoint*
- Zunshine if the character is opaque only because other characters/readers have nothing to infer

Ask:
- public goal vs private desire?
- fear/wound/contradiction?
- behaviour under pressure?
- ordinary life, money, family, friendship?
- what does this person wrongly believe about someone else?

## 6. ENSEMBLE CHARACTERS SOUND/ACT THE SAME
Call:
- Card — *Characters & Viewpoint*
- McKee — *Dialogue*
- Lodge — voice/skaz
- Zunshine — mind-state differentiation

Build differences in vocabulary, rhythm, attention, fear, lies, affection, status tactics and knowledge—not cosmetic quirks.

## 7. DIALOGUE SOUNDS LIKE AN ADULT POLICY COMMITTEE
Call:
- McKee — *Dialogue*
- Baxter — *Art of Subtext*
- Lodge — teenage skaz / voice
- Script Companion: *My So-Called Life*, *Veronica Mars*, *Friday Night Lights*

Dialogue must be action. Give each speaker objective, tactic, withheld information, status and interruption.

## 8. YOUTH CHARACTER DOES NOT FEEL YOUNG
Call:
- Brody — YA age lens
- Lodge — crafted teenage voice
- Nevermoor / Hunger Games / Skyward for different social-pressure functions
- Script Companion youth ecology

RED FLAG:
- Ender's Game is useful as a pressure/institution model but also as a warning against making every young person a military/political prodigy.

Scientific claims about adolescent development require a separate Youth Psychology corpus.

## 9. ORBITAL YOUTH FEELS LIKE AN EARTH SCHOOL MOVED TO SPACE
Call:
- Nevermoor — desired belonging/social institution *function only*
- Hunger Games — public/private status pressure
- Skyward — competence/team/status
- Wolf — infrastructures
- Cambridge Companion — SF social consequences

Force orbital consequences into:
- housing
- transport
- money/work
- maintenance
- family independence
- species mixture
- AI/synthetic neighbours
- sport/entertainment
- emergencies
- law/status

No magical-academy or military-school surface borrowing.

## 10. RELATIONSHIP / ROMANCE IS AN INSERT
Call:
- Hayes — *Romancing the Beat*
- McKee — *Dialogue*
- Baxter — subtext
- Zunshine — misreading/mind tracking

Relationship engine:
`two desires + two fears → forced continued interaction → revised perception → intimacy → retreat/status cost → choice → changed relationship`

The B-story must alter decisions in the A-story.

## 11. FAMILY FEELS LIKE GENERIC OPPOSITION
Call:
- Corbett — character/relationship
- Zunshine — competing beliefs and motives
- youth script companion

Give parents/family legitimate wants, fears, money/time/status pressures and partial knowledge. Neither generation automatically owns truth.

## 12. MYSTERY IS JUST WITHHELD LORE
Call:
- Zunshine — mind/source tracking
- Bell — suspense
- Ribó — information/focalisation diagnosis
- le Carré or Butcher depending institution/case scale

Mystery should progress through:
`observable anomaly → competing explanations → clue/test → revised suspicion → action → consequence → narrower/harder question`

## 13. SMITH BECOMES A GENERIC MONSTER HUNTER
Call:
- le Carré — institutions/trust/information asymmetry
- McKee — antagonism
- Butcher — case engine *function only*
- Constantine script companion — anomaly/procedure/price
- Wolf/Ribó only if continuity/information architecture is broken

Required question:
What does OLD EARTH SECURITY know that Confederation security does not, what does the Confederation understand better, and what past success/crime makes cooperation morally difficult?

## 14. JURISDICTION CONFLICT IS JUST BUREAUCRATIC ARGUMENT
Call:
- le Carré
- Children of Time — category/model error
- Weir — shared test / evidence / trust
- McKee — forces of antagonism

Make institutions disagree because they have different evidence, ontology, history, duties and acceptable-risk models.

## 15. FIRST CONTACT IS TOO EASY
Call:
- Weir — observation/test/error/trust
- Children of Time — independent nonhuman civilisation + category error
- Zunshine — attribution of minds
- Cambridge Companion for SF context

Contact engine:
`phenomenon → measurement → hypothesis → shared referent → failed assumption → correction → small cooperative test → earned risk increase`

## 16. NONHUMAN ENTITY EXISTS ONLY TO TEACH HUMANS
Call:
- Children of Time
- Wolf if civilisation infrastructure is thin
- Zunshine for mental-state asymmetry

Require independent want, social system, history, internal conflict and consequences that would exist without the human protagonist.

## 17. AI / SYNTHETIC PERSONHOOD IS ONLY PHILOSOPHY
Call:
- Murderbot
- Klara and the Sun
- Never Let Me Go
- Crouch / Weir depending identity or testing problem

Dramatic chain:
`assigned function/legal status → private subjectivity → control of body/software/memory → conflict of priorities → voluntary costly choice → others must revise category → social/legal consequence`

Technical feasibility requires separate current AI/robotics/neuro/bio sources.

## 18. CONSCIOUSNESS TRANSFER IS JUST A LORE CLAIM
Call:
- Crouch — identity/continuity consequences
- Ishiguro — personhood/social recognition
- Pullman — embodied rule before doctrine *function only*
- Weir — testable observation loop

IVDIVO engine:
`new body → sensorimotor learning → procedural memory → intimate memory → recognition dispute → responsibility → changed legal/social identity`

Do not use reference metaphysics as scientific proof.

## 19. METAPHYSICS BECOMES EXPOSITION
Call:
- Pullman — phenomenon before ontology
- Weir — observation/test logic
- Lodge — implication
- prior comparative metaphysics pass as REFERENCE ONLY

Show:
`phenomenon → practical consequence → failed model → action → price`; terminology arrives only when needed.

## 20. WORLD LORE DUMP
Call:
- Wolf
- Doty — telling detail
- Lodge — implication
- Ribó — setting vs plot diagnosis

Reveal through jobs, homes, transport, food, law, money, architecture, humour, mistakes and emergencies.

World depth engine:
`visible consequence/detail → implied infrastructure/history → minimum needed explanation → continuity check`

## 21. WORLD IS INVENTIVE BUT DOES NOT FEEL REAL
Call:
- Wolf
- Cambridge Companion if speculative consequences are thin
- Doty for perceptual detail

Check invention / completeness / consistency together. Every changed default should affect adjacent systems.

## 22. SERIES CONTINUITY IS BECOMING UNMANAGEABLE
Call:
- Wolf — canonicity/infrastructure/transnarrative growth
- IVDIVO Continuity Engine

Priority:
new direct instruction → approved canon → locked book canon → current architecture → bibles → room decisions → drafts → references.

Do not repair contradictions with lore unless the current story requires the repair.

## 23. SF FEELS LIKE SPACESHIPS + JARGON
Call:
- Cambridge Companion
- Wolf
- task-specific current science source

Ask what the speculative change does to:
work, money, family, law, status, politics, bodies, religion/ethics and ordinary life.

## 24. ACTION IS SPECTACLE WITHOUT STORY
Call:
- McKee — *Action*
- Bell — conflict/stakes
- Truby — desire/choice

Every action unit needs objective, tactic, resistance, reversal, price and changed options.

## 25. LONG STORY HAS NO RETENTION
Call:
- Bell — suspense
- DCC — micro-retention loop *without game surface*
- Edgerton — story questions
- Truby — revelation sequence

Use:
`danger/problem → discover constraint → attempt → result → payoff → harder constraint`

But never replace the book's main dramatic engine with artificial cliffhangers.

## 26. SERIAL EPISODE DOES NOT FEEL COMPLETE
Call:
- Butcher — case closure
- DCC — local closure before next escalation
- McKee — climax/resolution

Rule: resolve the episode's main conflict. Only then open the next hook.

## 27. PROSE EXPLAINS THEME AFTER THE SCENE
Call:
- Lodge — implication
- Baxter — subtext
- Doty — concrete selection

Prefer:
`OBJECT → ACTION → IMPLICATION`
not
`ABSTRACT REALISATION → EXPLANATION → MORAL`.

## 28. POV / INFORMATION FLOW IS CONFUSING
Call:
- Ribó — narrator vs focalisation
- Card — viewpoint/distance
- Zunshine — belief/source graph

Before rewriting mark:
- who speaks?
- who perceives?
- what do they know?
- what does reader know?
- what assumption should reader make now?

## 29. READER CAN PREDICT EVERYTHING
Call:
- Bell — suspense
- Zunshine — controlled mistrust
- Truby — revelation sequence
- Lodge — surprise/implication

Do not hide arbitrary facts. Change the reader's model with fair evidence.

## 30. STORY IS BECOMING A FRANCHISE BIBLE INSTEAD OF A NOVEL
Call only:
- Truby — desire/action/change
- McKee — antagonism/climax/resolution
- current IVDIVO Story Gate

Do **not** call Wolf or franchise/world sources until the book's own conflict closes.

# STOP-RESEARCH GATE

Research stops and writing begins when all are true:

- HERO is identified.
- WANT is active and concrete.
- WHY NOW is unavoidable.
- OPPOSITION can resist intelligently.
- WRONG STRATEGY is visible.
- PRICE exists.
- MIDPOINT changes the model/strategy.
- CLIMAX CHOICE cannot be delegated.
- RESOLUTION closes the main conflict.
- SERIES HOOK, if any, begins only after closure.

If a scene problem can be described and 2 independent Core sources converge on a causal repair, do not open five more books. Apply the repair and test the story.

**STORY > RESEARCH. CHARACTER > MECHANISM. WORLD THROUGH STORY.**
