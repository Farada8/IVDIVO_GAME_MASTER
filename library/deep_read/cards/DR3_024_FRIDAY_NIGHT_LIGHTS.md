2. **Public role vs private pain.** Athlete, cheer captain, coach, parent or star performs a social identity while carrying contradictory private reality.
3. **Reputation changes behavior before facts are settled.** Gossip and public labeling alter team/friendship/body confidence immediately.
4. **Gender norms are material pressures.** Different social rules change opportunity, shame, visibility and how achievement is valued.
5. **Body change rewrites identity and relationships.** Injury/disability affects home architecture, dependence, humor, romance and future, not only medical plot.
6. **Family advice can be loving and wrong.** Parents push resilience/status/safety through their own worldview; conflict need not require villainous family.
7. **Jobs/contracts pay bills and shape relationships.** Adult career choices alter household power; youth sport/education affects future mobility.
8. **Ordinary transport/home/locker-room scenes carry story.** Commute, bedroom, car, meal and practice can host status or relationship turns.

10. **Long-form growth does not reset.** Finale demonstrates accumulated choices: careers, marriages/proposals, moves, family positions and team futures inherit prior seasons.
11. **Humor survives pain.** Awkward jokes and mundane friction keep characters human during injury, shame and major decisions.
12. **Community institutions can love and exploit the same person.** Celebration, economic dependence and pressure coexist.
