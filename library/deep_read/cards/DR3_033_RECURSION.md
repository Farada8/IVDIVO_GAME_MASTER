## Unique strength
Recursion is strongest at treating memory as lived social reality. A changed timeline or remembered life matters because subjects retain intimate relational detail, grief and obligations—not because the premise is intellectually clever. It also shows how an ethically sympathetic personal motive can scale into catastrophic second-order consequences.

## Transferable mechanisms
1. **Memory identity is made of small repeated detail.** Smell, habits, school mornings and domestic routines make a remembered relationship feel real.
2. **Conflicting memory creates double belonging.** Subject can remember two lives strongly enough that neither is emotionally disposable.
3. **Technology begins in personal need.** Research motive rooted in mother's cognitive decline gives later intervention moral weight.
4. **Good motive does not immunize system from harm.** Tool built to preserve/recover memory can produce wider consequences its creator did not intend.
5. **Intervention changes social networks, not only individual mind.** Alter one past and families, careers, deaths and collective events reconfigure.
6. **Second-order effects should escalate after each 'fix'.** Solving one loss can create new casualties/inconsistencies.
7. **Knowledge of prior timelines becomes burden and leverage.** People who remember carry grief others cannot validate.
8. **Public phenomenon should first be experienced as personal pathology.** Society may name symptoms before understanding mechanism.
9. **Scientific breakthrough and institutional power attract each other.** Once capability exists, private research becomes geopolitical/organizational problem.
10. **Causal reset does not reset character.** Memory of prior failures accumulates, so later attempts must change strategy and ethics.
11. **Love can motivate destructive refusal to accept loss.** Emotional truth and ethical correctness may diverge.
12. **Resolution requires choosing what not to control.** Mature choice may be restraint/acceptance rather than perfect restoration.
13. **High-concept stakes remain legible through one relationship.** Global catastrophe gains meaning because characters remember specific people/lives lost.

## Failure modes
- Memory treated as downloadable data with no relationships/body effects.
- Reset mechanic erases consequence for reader.
- Scientist's good intent used as moral exemption.
- Every timeline change conveniently supports theme.
