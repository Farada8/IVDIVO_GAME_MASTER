## Unique strength
Children of Time is strongest at making intelligence, society and technology emerge from a genuinely nonhuman body, sensory system, ecology and reproductive structure. It also gives a long-timescale civilization story where myths, institutions and technologies reinterpret inherited signals across generations.

## Transferable mechanisms
1. **Body precedes culture.** Limbs, senses, scale, reproduction, predation and environment shape what tools, social roles and communication are plausible.
2. **Nonhuman intelligence should solve problems through its own affordances.** Do not give an alien human institutions with cosmetic anatomy.
3. **Learning can begin behaviorally before abstract self-modeling.** Trial, observation and inherited/distributed knowledge create intelligence gradually.
4. **Knowledge transmission is civilization technology.** How a species stores/transfers skills can matter as much as tools themselves.
5. **Signal can become religion before becoming science.** Repeated structured communication from incomprehensible source is interpreted through available worldview.
6. **Myth can preserve distorted technical truth.** Later generations may recover new meaning without the original tradition being 'stupid'.
7. **Deep time should change institutions, not merely dates.** Gender power, religion, science, warfare and governance evolve across generations.
8. **Recurring role-patterns can give continuity without immortal characters.** Long sagas may echo functions while society transforms.
9. **Human civilization can regress technologically while retaining fragments of archive.** Stored knowledge, usable competence and institutional memory are distinct.
10. **First-contact conflict often begins as classification failure.** Each side interprets the other through inadequate inherited categories.
11. **Communication requires shared medium plus model revision.** Translation is not simply vocabulary mapping when senses/embodiment differ.
12. **Empathy can be engineered/learned without erasing difference.** Cooperation does not require both species to become culturally identical.
13. **Complementary civilizations can combine technologies.** Each side's biological/technical strengths compensate for the other's limits.
14. **Hybrid infrastructure can become a third civilization state.** Ending should show new institutions/ships/work, not just treaty announcement.
15. **A civilization story still needs local human/nonhuman motives.** Species-scale history remains readable through individual problems, ambitions and fears.
16. **Artificial/biomechanical intelligence can become descendant rather than copy.** Kern-derived systems evolve beyond the original human while retaining lineage.

## Failure modes
- Aliens = humans with unusual bodies.
- Evolution used as automatic progress ladder.
- Religion portrayed only as ignorance and science only as correction.
- Deep-time skips erase causal continuity.
- Cooperation ends difference instead of managing it.
