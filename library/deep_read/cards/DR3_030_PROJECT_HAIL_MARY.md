## Unique strength
Weir is strongest at turning knowledge acquisition into visible action. Observation, measurement, hypothesis, experiment, failure and model revision form the story's propulsion. First-contact trust grows from shared testable work rather than instant cultural understanding.

## Transferable mechanisms
1. **Observable phenomenon before theory.** Start with a measurement/body/environmental fact that does not fit the current model.
2. **Hypothesis must create a test.** Explanation matters because it predicts what should happen next.
3. **Failed prediction is productive.** A wrong model narrows possibilities and forces revision; scientist is not humiliated for being initially wrong.
4. **Knowledge changes operational behavior.** Discovery changes tool, route, sample, safety rule or mission decision.
5. **Recover identity through capabilities and memories in layers.** Body control, procedural knowledge, factual memory and personal memory need not return together.
6. **Competence can coexist with fear/cowardice/avoidance.** Technical skill does not settle moral character.
7. **First contact begins with common referents.** Shared quantities, physical objects, repeated signals and demonstrable effects provide a bridge before fluent language.
8. **Translation is iterative model-building.** Each side tests words/units/assumptions and corrects errors.
9. **Different bodies imply different engineering common sense.** Atmosphere, pressure, material, senses and sleep change what each species assumes is normal.
10. **Trust grows through verified cooperation.** Small reliable exchanges precede higher-risk collaboration.
11. **Complementary ignorance creates partnership.** Each civilization/person knows things the other does not; neither is universal teacher.
12. **Friendship develops through work, humor and mutual care.** Emotional bond is caused by repeated joint problems.
13. **Global stakes stay legible through local experiments.** Large mission is carried by concrete next questions.
14. **Climax asks what knowledge/relationship is worth personally.** Final choice converts scientific mission into character decision and price.
15. **Aftermath shows a new ordinary life.** Resolution demonstrates how the protagonist now lives, teaches/works and belongs after the mission.

## Failure modes
- Science as magical vocabulary that always confirms first guess.
- Alien becomes human personality with cosmetic biology.
- Instant universal translator.
- Friendship declared before mutual reliability is demonstrated.
- Global stakes replace local human choice.
