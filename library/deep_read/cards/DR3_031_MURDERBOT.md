## Unique strength
Murderbot is strongest at showing personhood through private preference, refusal, embarrassment, voluntary risk and boundary-setting rather than philosophical proclamation. Legal/property status, bodily presentation and software control remain separate questions from actual agency.

## Transferable mechanisms
1. **Control removal does not answer 'what do I want?'** Hacking the governor module creates freedom from coercion but not identity, purpose or social confidence.
2. **Personhood is visible in private preference.** Media taste, privacy, avoidance, humor and selective attachment establish an inner life before others recognize it.
3. **Voluntary costly action is stronger evidence of agency than verbal claim.** The construct can ignore company priorities yet chooses to protect people.
4. **Legal category can lag behind actual subjectivity.** A being may be treated as equipment even while making independent moral choices.
5. **Corporate economics shape safety and embodiment.** Bad equipment, contracts and replaceability make personhood conflict material rather than abstract.
6. **Care can become paternalism.** Humans who mean well may still decide what the synthetic person should do; benevolence does not erase autonomy conflict.
7. **Bodily visibility governs social access.** Disguise, armor, face exposure and passing as augmented human affect movement and interaction.
8. **Software can weaponize the body against the self.** External combat/control modules create a direct conflict between subject intention and bodily action.
9. **The subject may ask others to destroy the body to prevent coerced harm.** This makes ownership/control stakes immediate and ethical.
10. **Intimacy can be non-romantic and non-touch-oriented.** Trust is expressed through network access, shared information, rescue and respecting physical/social boundaries.
11. **AI-to-AI relationship need not mirror human dialogue.** Ship intelligence and construct can negotiate through system access, competence and mutual curiosity.
12. **Autonomy includes the right to leave people who care about you.** Choosing uncertainty over assigned safety is a real freedom test.
13. **Identity investigation can be procedural.** The second novella turns unknown past violence into a self-investigation without reducing the protagonist to a mystery box.
14. **Competence and social discomfort can coexist.** Tactical mastery does not produce effortless social fluency.
15. **A synthetic protagonist needs ordinary downtime.** Entertainment, travel logistics, clothing/appearance and awkward social moments make subjectivity lived.

## Failure modes
- 'Robot becomes human' as the only arc.
- Freedom solved instantly by deleting one control routine.
- Human affection treated as permission to override synthetic person's decisions.
- Synthetic body presented only as power set.
- Philosophical speeches replacing observed preferences and choices.
