## Unique strength
MSCL is strongest at the gap between what adolescents can feel/think and what they can actually say or do under peer pressure, attraction, embarrassment and family dependence. Ordinary domestic activity becomes a carrier for identity and relationship conflict.

## Transferable mechanisms
1. **Inner sophistication / outer awkwardness.** Internal analysis can be articulate while spoken behavior is fragmented, evasive or badly timed.
2. **Attraction distorts attention.** A teen can correctly perceive many things while becoming irrational around one person/status need.
3. **Embarrassment is action pressure.** Fear of looking desperate, uncool, inexperienced or rejected changes tactics.
4. **Peer interpretation changes reality.** Friends tell characters what glances, invitations and silences 'mean'; those interpretations generate decisions.
5. **Identity is tried on behaviorally.** Hair, clothes, friendships, dance attendance, distance from parents and new speech styles are experiments in selfhood.
6. **Family dependence coexists with resistance.** Teen rejects parental framing while still relying on home, transport, money and emotional safety.
7. **Adult ordinary-life problems matter to youth stories.** Unemployment, marriage strain and household tasks reshape the teen's environment without becoming exposition.
8. **Third-thing intimacy.** Wallpapering, photography, school projects, errands and dances let characters talk indirectly about deeper wants.
9. **Friendship/romance asymmetry creates pain.** One character may see a bond as central while another barely recognizes the same meaning.
10. **Bad youth decisions can be emotionally logical.** The immediate need to preserve status/hope/avoid shame can outweigh distant rational cost.
11. **Humor comes from failed self-presentation.** Difference between intended coolness and actual behavior humanizes characters.
12. **Silence and misreading are legitimate dialogue actions.** Not saying, stalling or changing topic can advance relationship conflict.

## Failure modes
- Teens speaking polished adult psychology.
- Internal voice used as author essay.
- Every misunderstanding prolonged by implausible refusal to speak.
- Parents treated only as oppressive authority.
