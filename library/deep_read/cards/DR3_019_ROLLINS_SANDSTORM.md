## Unique strength
Rollins is strongest at turning one anomalous physical event into a multi-domain operational problem involving museum experts, police/security, military/science, field operators, historical material, expedition logistics and hostile competitors. Expertise is distributed and location itself becomes an active hazard.

## Transferable mechanisms
1. **Anomaly recruits multiple disciplines because no single model explains it.** Physical evidence should force archaeology/history/security/science to interact.
2. **Expertise has jurisdictional consequences.** Who is allowed to touch evidence, command site, interpret artifact or move people matters.
3. **Observable evidence precedes high concept.** Strange material/energy/biological effect gives characters something testable before theory expands.
4. **Field team needs complementary functions.** Operator, scientist, local/cultural expert, logistics and specialist knowledge solve different problems.
5. **Scientific hypothesis should alter operational plan.** Briefing is dramatic when it changes where/when/how the team must act.
6. **Location is opposition.** Terrain, weather, access, ruins, communications and distance can narrow options independently of villains.
7. **Artifact/history clue should have practical consequence.** Knowledge becomes route, warning, mechanism or tactical decision.
8. **Competing organization interprets same anomaly through different objective.** Rivalry is stronger when both understand part of truth.
9. **Expedition escalates by irreversible movement.** Deeper entry reduces retreat, resupply, communication and institutional support.
10. **Team expertise creates vulnerability.** The person who can interpret the next clue may become a protection target or bottleneck.
11. **Large threat stays legible through local tasks.** Survive site, open route, protect expert, test sample, stop device—concrete objectives carry global stakes.
12. **Action and discovery alternate.** Physical crisis exposes new evidence; evidence changes next action.

## Failure modes
- Techno-lecture disconnected from next decision.
- Team members as skills without private motives.
- Artifact as arbitrary magic key.
- Bigger explosions replacing deeper causal discovery.
