## Unique strength
Constantine is strongest at observable anomaly before ontology, reluctant expert re-entry, competence under imperfect control, and cases that reopen old failures/relationships. It also demonstrates training as vulnerability and knowledge as something ethically costly to use.

## Transferable mechanisms
1. **Observable anomaly before terminology.** Cockroach migration, possession, impossible physical behavior or object effect appears before full classification.
2. **Expert recognizes pattern but not full answer.** Competence begins with noticing what others dismiss, not instant omniscience.
3. **Reluctant re-entry needs personal fault line.** Hero who wants out returns because anomaly directly touches an unresolved debt/failure.
4. **Ordinary victim life anchors the case.** Work, boredom, debt, friendship or travel gives anomaly a human cost before cosmology.
5. **Case can arrive through old colleague/friend.** Present threat becomes non-interchangeable because professional history carries guilt, loyalty or betrayal.
6. **Past wrongdoing is operationally relevant.** Old failed ritual/decision changes who trusts hero and what risks he will take now.
7. **Training is dramatic when control requires vulnerability.** Mentor asks trainee to lower defenses, tolerate uncertainty or risk perception; relationship changes through attempt.
8. **Tools/wards/archives imply infrastructure.** Expert world should have prepared spaces, traps, maps and accumulated practice rather than improvising lore from nowhere.
9. **Unknown phenomenon crosses ordinary jurisdiction badly.** Customs/police/medicine can misread supernatural evidence through plausible existing categories.
10. **Expert choice can be morally ugly even when tactically necessary.** Case resolution should expose cost, not preserve hero as clean moral lecturer.
11. **Humor protects against dread without invalidating it.** Professional sarcasm/banter can coexist with genuine guilt and horror.
12. **Case resolution should leave personal consequence.** Old relationship, trainee trust, guilt or institutional standing changes even if entity is contained.
13. **Phenomenon can use communication rather than attack.** Anomaly may be message/signaling behavior; interpretation changes response.
14. **Ability needs false positives/limits/counterpressure.** Vision or expertise should expose character to manipulation and uncertainty.
