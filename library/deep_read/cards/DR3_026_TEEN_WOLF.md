## Unique strength
Teen Wolf is useful because the supernatural change immediately rewrites the hero's ordinary adolescent body, sport, attraction, status, secrecy, friendship and family life. The genre engine and youth engine are the same causal system rather than alternating plots.

## Transferable mechanisms
1. **Ability enters an existing ordinary-life desire.** Scott already wants first-line lacrosse/status before the bite, so power complicates rather than invents desire.
2. **Bad youthful choice opens the extraordinary plot.** Curiosity, friendship pressure and desire for something to happen lead into danger; the error has recognizable adolescent logic.
3. **Body change has social consequences.** Strength/senses/healing alter sport, desirability, confidence and suspicion.
4. **Power improvement creates exposure risk.** Every benefit can make concealment harder.
5. **Best friend is operational, not decorative.** Friendship carries investigation, cover stories, humor, disagreement and risk.
6. **Parent remains part of the ecology.** Work schedule, home rules and concern constrain hero even after extraordinary change.
7. **Status hierarchy becomes a pressure amplifier.** Existing outsider/jock structures make changes visible and emotionally meaningful.
8. **Romance and danger cross-contaminate.** Attraction can expose secret, distort judgment and connect hero to enemy/institutional lines.
9. **Training is identity drama.** Learning control is also learning what kind of person the hero may become.
10. **Supernatural taxonomy should emerge after symptoms.** Physical anomalies precede full explanation.
11. **Secret life creates scheduling and loyalty costs.** School/sport/family obligations collide with hidden-world demands.
12. **Group/pack metaphor works when membership has real obligations.** Belonging should change protection, status, rules and enemies.
