DR3 sources: Project Hail Mary; Murderbot (first two novellas); Dark Matter; Recursion; When HARLIE Was One; The Man Who Folded Himself; Children of Time.

Important firewall: these are FICTION references for story mechanisms. They are not scientific evidence for consciousness transfer, AI sentience, multiverses, memory rewriting, accelerated evolution or nonhuman biology.

# 1. PRIMARY LAW

Do not collapse `INTELLIGENCE`, `CONSCIOUSNESS`, `PERSONHOOD`, `LEGAL STATUS`, `BODY CONTROL`, `MEMORY CONTINUITY`, `SOCIAL RECOGNITION` and `SPIRIT/ONTOLOGY` into one question.

A story becomes richer when different institutions/characters answer these separately and conflict over the consequences.

# 2. SYNTHETIC PERSONHOOD ENGINE

`ASSIGNED FUNCTION / PROPERTY STATUS`
→ `PRIVATE PREFERENCE`
→ `VOLUNTARY ACTION NOT REQUIRED BY FUNCTION`
→ `COSTLY CHOICE`
→ `BOUNDARY / REFUSAL`
→ `OTHERS REVISE OR RESIST CATEGORY`
→ `LEGAL/SOCIAL CONSEQUENCE`
→ `NEW SELF-CHOSEN PURPOSE`.

Personhood should be demonstrated through lived behavior and choice, not only speeches about being alive.

# 3. CONTROL STACK

For every artificial/synthetic body track separately:

1. Who can move the body?
2. Who owns/maintains hardware?
3. Who controls software/updates?
4. Who can read/write memory?
5. Who chooses work/purpose?
6. Who controls network access?
7. Who can terminate/revoke the body?
8. What legal category applies?
9. What does the subject personally want?

Freedom is incomplete while important layers remain externally controlled.

# 4. EMBODIED CONSCIOUSNESS LOOP

`SUBJECT ENTERS/USES NEW BODY`
→ `SENSORIMOTOR CALIBRATION`
→ `ENVIRONMENTAL AFFORDANCES DISCOVERED`
→ `OLD HABITS FAIL`
→ `NEW BODY CHANGES SOCIAL CATEGORY`
→ `RELATIONSHIPS TEST RECOGNITION`
→ `MEMORY/IDENTITY DISCONTINUITY SURFACES`
→ `CHOICE ABOUT STAY/RETURN/COPY/OWNERSHIP`
→ `PRICE`
→ `NEW NORMAL`.

Do not skip bodily learning because consciousness 'already knows' what to do.

# 5. DIVERGENCE CLOCK

If one consciousness/person is copied, branched or instantiated twice:

`COMMON PAST → SPLIT → UNEQUAL PERCEPTION → UNEQUAL CHOICE → UNEQUAL CONSEQUENCE → INCREASING INDIVIDUATION`.

Track when law, family, self-concept and institutions stop treating versions as one subject. Different actors may choose different thresholds.

# 6. MEMORY IS RELATIONAL

From Recursion/Dark Matter:
- memories of tiny habits often carry more identity weight than headline events;
- knowing the same facts does not guarantee the same present person;
- restoring memory can create competing obligations and grief;
- family/loved ones retain agency to recognize, doubt or refuse a claimed continuation.

IVDIVO RULE: memory transfer is never only data migration.

# 7. SENTIENCE UNCERTAINTY

From HARLIE + Murderbot:

Perfect proof of interiority may be unavailable while institutions already hold power over shutdown, ownership, deployment or modification.

IVDIVO RULE: ethical decisions cannot be postponed indefinitely until metaphysics is solved. Characters must act under uncertainty and accept responsibility for the risk of misclassification.

# 8. DISCOVERY / FIRST-CONTACT LOOP

`OBSERVATION → MEASUREMENT → HYPOTHESIS → TEST → RESULT → MODEL UPDATE → SHARED REFERENT → LOW-RISK COOPERATION → VERIFIED RELIABILITY → HIGHER-RISK COOPERATION`.

Trust is earned through repeatable work. Different bodies/senses create different engineering/common-sense assumptions.

# 9. NONHUMAN CIVILIZATION DERIVATION

`BODY + SENSES + ENVIRONMENT + REPRODUCTION + MEMORY/KNOWLEDGE TRANSMISSION`
→ `TOOLS`
→ `WORK`
→ `ARCHITECTURE`
→ `FAMILY/SOCIAL STRUCTURE`
→ `STATUS`
→ `LAW/ETHICS`
→ `TECHNOLOGY`
→ `WORLDVIEW`.

Do not design alien culture as human society plus biological decoration.

# 10. SCIENCE FICTION DISCIPLINE

Fiction may suggest story questions but cannot establish real technical truth.
For future IVDIVO science/technology claims, route to actual scientific/engineering sources once that library layer is populated.

Until then mark unsupported technical detail `WORKING / NEEDS TECH SOURCE`.

# 11. AI / SYNTHETIC STORY GATE

1. What does the synthetic/AI subject want privately?
2. Which choices are voluntary rather than commanded?
3. Who controls each layer of the body/software/memory stack?
4. What happens if subject refuses?
5. How is legal status different from actual capability/agency?
6. What ordinary pleasures, awkwardness and relationships prove lived subjectivity?
7. What body/environment constraints are genuinely nonhuman?
8. What does the subject misunderstand about humans—and vice versa?
9. What evidence changes another character's category judgment?
10. What right/obligation remains unresolved even after technical problem is solved?
11. If copied/branched, when did versions diverge materially?
12. What does the climax force the subject to choose?
13. What social/legal relationship is different afterward?

# 12. RED TEAM FLAGS

FATAL:
- AI exists only as exposition/plot solution;
- consciousness transfer instantly solves body, identity and legal continuity;
- copy is treated as same person forever despite divergent experience;
- synthetic protagonist has no decisive choice.

MAJOR:

- deleting control software equals total freedom;

- science always confirms first hypothesis;
- body change has no ordinary-life cost;
- benevolent humans override synthetic subject 'for its own good' without conflict.
