## Mechanisms already extracted

### Les Edgerton — Hooked
- opening as dramatized inciting incident;
- surface problem vs deeper story-worthy problem;
- later surface problems arise causally from preceding problems;
- minimum viable setup/backstory;
- opening generates propulsion, establishes rules and forecasts direction;
- protagonist may act against a deeper problem before consciously understanding it.

### Robert McKee — Dialogue
- dialogue = verbal action;
- desire -> intention/tactic -> line -> reaction;
- said / unsaid / unsayable layers;
- subtext as pressure source;
- voice differentiation through vocabulary, syntax, rhythm and identity;
- scene design precedes wording.

### John S. Warner — Building Character Arcs
- plot / structure / character integration;
- lie/truth, want/need, ghost, characteristic moment;
- positive, flat and negative arc families;
- synchronize belief changes with plot milestones;
- use primarily as diagnostic/workbook support rather than master theory.
