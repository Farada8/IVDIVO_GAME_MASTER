## High-value mechanisms already extracted

### Les Edgerton — Hooked
- opening as dramatized inciting incident;
- surface problem vs deeper story-worthy problem;
- later surface problems should arise causally from preceding problems;
- minimum viable setup/backstory;
- opening should generate propulsion, establish rules and forecast the story direction;
- protagonist can act against the deeper problem before consciously understanding it.

### John S. Warner — Building Character Arcs 2 Books in 1
- integrate plot/structure/character rather than bolting arc onto plot;
- lie/truth, want/need, ghost, characteristic moment;
- distinguish positive, flat and negative arc families;
- synchronize belief changes with plot milestones;
- use as diagnostic/workbook support, not as the master structural authority.
