## Source profile

The volume is a broad synthesis/workbook rather than a single original dramatic theory. Its strongest practical value for IVDIVO is as a **coverage checklist**: it explicitly separates positive, flat and several negative arc families and repeatedly tracks belief, want, need, ghost/past wound, characteristic moment, plot points, midpoint, climax and resolution.

## Core mechanisms extracted

1. **Plot / structure / character integration.** Treat character arc as something that must be synchronized with plot movement rather than added after the event sequence is built.
2. **Lie / Truth axis.** Track the character’s governing false belief or truth-position and test how events strengthen, challenge, abandon or corrupt it.
3. **Want / Need separation.** External pursuit and deeper developmental requirement should not automatically be identical.
4. **Ghost as pressure source.** A formative past event can explain present defenses and choices, but it should function through current behavior rather than biography dump.
5. **Characteristic moment.** Early action should reveal the protagonist’s current operating pattern before the arc transforms or tests it.
6. **Arc family selection.** Do not force every protagonist into improvement. Positive change, flat/truth-bearing, disillusionment, fall and corruption arcs produce different story logic.
7. **Milestone synchronization.** First plot point, midpoint, third plot point, climax and resolution should change not only circumstances but the character’s relation to lie/truth/want/need.
8. **Supporting-character pressure.** Secondary characters can embody competing truths, lies, costs, temptations or alternative futures.
9. **Resolution state.** Character arc is incomplete if the plot ends but the protagonist’s belief/action pattern is unchanged without deliberate flat-arc design.
10. **Workbook stress testing.** Use explicit prompts/checklists during development and revision, but do not let the checklist replace causal scene writing.

## IVDIVO application

Use this source as a diagnostic companion, not as the master architecture. For Smith, separately track: operational want, deeper need, system-defined lie/truth, past conditioning/ghost, and the choices through which he acquires or rejects autonomy. For youth protagonists, age-specific lies and needs must be tested against peer status, family autonomy, attachment and future horizon rather than generic adult self-actualization.

## Limitations

- Derivative/synthetic coverage of multiple familiar structural systems; do not grant it authority over stronger specialized sources such as Truby/McKee/scene-specific research.
- Checklist language can encourage mechanical beat filling if used as a prose generator.
- Arc labels describe patterns; they do not create psychological specificity by themselves.
