# SOURCE SET F — BAILEY / EMBODIMENT INTERFACE

## M068 — SUBJECT → INTERFACE → VEHICLE

SOURCE MECHANISM:
The sampled Bailey model treats the soul/inner principle as expressing through an intermediate etheric mechanism and differentiated centers into the physical organism.

STORY FUNCTION:
Separates *subject* from *vehicle* without making embodiment irrelevant.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
Useful for synthetic embodiment: a consciousness may persist while requiring a nontrivial interface layer to operate a biological or artificial body.

FAILURE MODE:
Treating transfer as copying a file into hardware with zero adaptation, calibration, embodiment error or identity consequence.

---

## M069 — DIFFERENTIATED CHANNELS, NOT GENERIC ENERGY

SOURCE MECHANISM:
The model describes multiple centers with differentiated functions rather than one undifferentiated spiritual force.

STORY FUNCTION:
Creates specific capability channels, bottlenecks and failure modes.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
Synthetic/metaphysical bodies should have differentiated access systems: perception, motor control, memory integration, communication, environmental coupling, etc.

---

## M070 — VEHICLE QUALITY CONSTRAINS EXPRESSION

SOURCE MECHANISM:
An inner principle is not portrayed as expressing identically through every mechanism; the condition and organization of the vehicle matter.

STORY FUNCTION:
Prevents omnipotent consciousness independent of embodiment.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
A vacuum body, ocean body, biological body and stellar-environment body can host the same continuing subject yet produce different cognition, perception, timing, emotional feedback and agency.

---

## M071 — INNER/OUTER MODEL BRIDGE

SOURCE MECHANISM:
The sampled text explicitly tries to bridge introspective/eastern models with western physiological mechanism.

STORY FUNCTION:
Allows rival explanatory systems to describe different layers of one observable event.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
Excellent for OLD EARTH SECURITY ↔ CONFEDERATION SECURITY: the old system may have a metaphysical operational map while Confederation science models measurable interfaces; neither automatically owns the whole explanation.

# SOURCE SET G — AGNI-YOGA ORBIT / HIERARCHY, RESONANCE, CONTINUITY

## M072 — HIERARCHY AS CHAIN OF RELATION AND ACCESS

SOURCE MECHANISM:
Hierarchy functions not merely as rank but as a chain of connection, instruction, service and ascent.

STORY FUNCTION:
Makes hierarchy operational rather than decorative.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
Status should imply access channels, obligations, limits, supervision and consequences — not simply greater personal worth.

---

## M073 — RESONANCE SELECTS THE INFORMATIONAL ENVIRONMENT

SOURCE MECHANISM:
The sampled text repeatedly links the quality/state of thought with what sphere or influence consciousness becomes responsive to.

STORY FUNCTION:
Perception itself can be selective and risky.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
A psychic/metaphysical operator can receive genuine signal, self-generated pattern, hostile signal or category error. State of mind becomes an operational variable.

FAILURE MODE:
Every vision is automatically correct because it is supernatural.

---

## M074 — PSYCHIC CAPACITY AS CULTIVATED RESOURCE

SOURCE MECHANISM:
Psychic energy is treated as something accumulated, strengthened, disciplined and potentially depleted or degraded.

STORY FUNCTION:
Creates cost and training.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
Abilities require maintenance, recovery, discipline and thresholds; power cannot be consequence-free.

---

## M075 — CROSS-WORLD CONTINUITY OF KNOWLEDGE AND PURPOSE

SOURCE MECHANISM:
The sampled text describes earthly knowledge/experience as remaining relevant in a subtler world and depicts learning, service and contact continuing across levels.

STORY FUNCTION:
Death/transition does not erase all causal biography.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
Supports the saga line in which experience accumulated in one embodiment or environment becomes usable after transition — but must still be translated into the new body's affordances.

---

## M076 — RESPONSIBILITY GROWS WITH AWARENESS

SOURCE MECHANISM:
Higher access is associated with service, discipline and responsibility rather than entertainment or private spectacle.

STORY FUNCTION:
Power creates obligation.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
A character who gains broader perception also inherits harder duties, legal exposure and moral risk.

# SOURCE SET H — STEINER / DEVELOPMENTAL INFLUENCE AND FREEDOM

## M077 — NONPHYSICAL INFLUENCE THROUGH IMAGE/PATTERN FORMATION

SOURCE MECHANISM:
In the sampled lecture, angelic beings are described as forming images in the human astral body that prepare future human development.

STORY FUNCTION:
Influence can work by shaping patterns, intuitions or possibilities rather than issuing explicit commands.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
Useful for subtle intervention stories where an intelligence affects the option-space a person perceives rather than puppeteering decisions.

---

## M078 — PREPARATION RATHER THAN DIRECT CONTROL

SOURCE MECHANISM:
The angelic work described is developmental and preparatory; the human being still encounters and interprets what is prepared.

STORY FUNCTION:
Preserves agency under metaphysical influence.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
Whenever a higher intelligence influences a hero, require a remaining human choice that can succeed, misunderstand, refuse or corrupt the offered possibility.

---

## M079 — FREEDOM ALLOWS THE SIGNAL TO BE MISSED

SOURCE MECHANISM:
The lecture explicitly frames humanity as capable of consciously meeting or effectively “sleeping through” a developmental event.

STORY FUNCTION:
Prophecy/influence does not guarantee outcome.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
Future-directed metaphysical guidance should create opportunity, not certainty.

---

## M080 — ATTENTION/CONSCIOUSNESS AS ACCESS GATE

SOURCE MECHANISM:
The text distinguishes between processes that may occur and the human ability to become consciously aware of them.

STORY FUNCTION:
Observation changes practical access without making observation omnipotent.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
Training can focus on discrimination, attention and interpretation, not merely on “getting stronger.”

# SOURCE SET I — DIONYSIAN / ORTHODOX ANGELOLOGY

## M081 — MEDIATED TRANSMISSION THROUGH UNEQUAL CAPACITIES

SOURCE MECHANISM:
Higher and lower orders do not possess identical capacities; illumination/knowledge is transmitted through differentiated levels.

STORY FUNCTION:
Information changes as it crosses hierarchy.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
Orders from a high-level intelligence may arrive through intermediaries with partial understanding, translation loss or jurisdiction constraints.

---

## M082 — HIERARCHY AS FUNCTION, NOT MERELY STATUS

SOURCE MECHANISM:
The hierarchy is associated with distinct functions of purification, illumination and perfection.

STORY FUNCTION:
Ranks have jobs.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
Any IVDIVO hierarchy should be defined by what its members can do, must do, may not do and whom they answer to.

---

## M083 — DERIVED AUTHORITY

SOURCE MECHANISM:
Angelic authority is subordinate to a source beyond the angels themselves; intermediaries are not ultimate sovereigns.

STORY FUNCTION:
Prevents local powerful beings from automatically being the top of reality.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
A powerful entity can possess enormous jurisdiction while still operating under law, covenant, source, mandate or limits it did not create.

---

## M084 — THREE TRIADS / NINE ORDERS AS A CLASSIFICATION DEVICE

SOURCE MECHANISM:
The Dionysian tradition organizes nine named orders into three triads.

STORY FUNCTION:
Shows how a cosmology can become administratively legible through classification.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
Do **not** import the nine orders automatically. Abstract the mechanism: a large invisible ecology becomes manageable when grouped into functional tiers — but the IVDIVO taxonomy must be original and story-earned.

---

## M085 — EPISTEMIC HUMILITY INSIDE A HIERARCHICAL MODEL

SOURCE MECHANISM:
The text explicitly states limits on what humans can know about celestial orders and grounds the classification in what is believed to have been disclosed.

STORY FUNCTION:
Allows a sophisticated institution to admit uncertainty.

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:
OLD EARTH SECURITY archives should contain UNKNOWN / DISPUTED / OBSERVED-BUT-UNCLASSIFIED categories instead of pretending every anomaly was solved centuries ago.

STORY FUNCTION:

STORY FUNCTION:

IVDIVO TRANSFORMATION:

The new layer has different conditions, inhabitants and modes of perception.

STORY FUNCTION:

# CROSS-SOURCE SYNTHESIS — NOT CANON

## S11 — MULTI-LAYER EMBODIMENT LOOP

Derived from M068 + M070 + M089 + M091:

`SUBJECT → INTERFACE/COUPLING LAYER → VEHICLE → ENVIRONMENT → SENSORY/ACTION FEEDBACK → UPDATED SELF-MODEL`

Story use:
A continuing subject entering a new body must learn the body's sensory bandwidth, action limits and environmental coupling. Identity continuity does not erase embodiment friction.

---

## S12 — ACCESS / RESONANCE / DISCRIMINATION LOOP

Derived from M073 + M080 + M092:

`INTERNAL STATE + TRAINING + INSTRUMENT → ACCESS BAND → SIGNAL → INTERPRETATION → TEST → CONFIRM / REJECT / REVISE`

Story use:
Metaphysical perception must be fallible and testable inside the story. Signal is not automatically truth.

---

## S13 — HIERARCHY AS RIGHTS + DUTIES + LIMITS

Derived from M072 + M076 + M081 + M082 + M083:

`LEVEL OF ACCESS → SPECIFIC FUNCTION → OBLIGATION → SUPERVISION → LIMIT / PROHIBITION → CONSEQUENCE FOR MISUSE`

IVDIVO transformation option:
Higher status can mean broader access and heavier responsibility rather than superior personal value. This is compatible with IVDIVO's existing working line about rights/access being contingent on responsible use, but this synthesis itself does not canonize a specific metaphysical law.

---

## S14 — TRANSITION CONTINUITY LOOP

Derived from M075 + M090 + M091:

`OLD VEHICLE LOST/LEFT → SUBJECT CONTINUES? → MEMORY/KNOWLEDGE CHECK → NEW OPERATING CONDITIONS → ADAPTATION → NEW DUTIES/RELATIONSHIPS → RETURN OR FURTHER TRANSITION`

Story use:
This is a stronger engine for embodiment stories than “upload complete.”

---

## S15 — HIGHER INFLUENCE WITHOUT PUPPETRY

Derived from M077 + M078 + M079:

`POTENTIAL / IMAGE / SIGNAL → HUMAN INTERPRETATION → FREE RESPONSE → CONSEQUENCE`

Story use:
Keeps metaphysical influence dramatic because the character still owns the choice.

## S16 — OLD EARTH / CONFEDERATION DOUBLE-MODEL TEST

Derived from M071 + M085 + M092:

1. Old Earth Security observes a recurring phenomenon and owns historical protocol.
2. Confederation Security measures a different layer and rejects the old terminology.
3. Each predicts something the other cannot.
4. A live case tests both models.
5. Both models fail partially.
6. The surviving synthesis changes procedure and jurisdiction.

This prevents either side from becoming the automatic authorial truth.

# METAPHYSICAL STORY GATE v0.1

Before a metaphysical mechanism enters a chapter/book, answer:

1. What observable phenomenon occurs before terminology?
2. Who observes it, with what sensory/instrumental limits?
3. What is the first model used to explain it?
4. What alternative model exists?
5. What prediction distinguishes the models?
6. What action tests that prediction?
7. What can generate a false positive?
8. What does access cost physically, socially, psychologically or institutionally?
9. What choice remains genuinely free?
10. What right/duty changes if the phenomenon is real?
11. What does the current story resolve?
12. What remains UNKNOWN rather than converted into lore?

FAIL if the answer is primarily exposition.
