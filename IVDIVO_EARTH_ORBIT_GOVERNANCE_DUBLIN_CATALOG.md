# 1. POLITICAL PREMISE

## WORKING CONSTRUCTION

As Earth and orbital civilization become increasingly interdependent, leaders from planetary states/blocs and major orbital polities begin meeting in Dublin to discuss a new shared governance framework for humanity across Earth and orbit.

The immediate dramatic question is **not** “who becomes world president?” but:

> How can Earth and orbital societies create common institutions without one side absorbing or subordinating the other?

This remains a political architecture to develop through story, not a finished universal government to impose retroactively on earlier books.

---

# 2. WHY DUBLIN

## CANON-COMPATIBLE SETTING LOGIC

Dublin is uniquely credible as the meeting place because Ireland is simultaneously:

- a planetary society;
- part of the wider Eurasian commonwealth/council;
- the sole planetary society with privileged closed-orbital technology access;
- deeply integrated with orbital infrastructure;
- not itself equivalent to or sovereign over all orbital cities.

Therefore Dublin can function as a political threshold between Earth and orbit without automatically implying that either Washington, Beijing, the Eurasian system or one orbital capital controls the negotiation.

---

# 3. DUBLIN AS HUMANITY'S NEGOTIATION CAPITAL

## WORKING CONSTRUCTION

Dublin may become the place where humanity negotiates issues that exceed the authority of any single planetary country or orbital polity.

Potential agenda areas include:

- Earth-orbit legal relations;
- planetary defense;
- orbital traffic and collision governance;
- asteroid movement and resource claims;
- terraforming authorization;
- settlement rights on the Moon, Mars and later worlds;
- AI and synthetic-subject rights;
- artificial / transferred-body legal identity;
- interplanetary biosecurity;
- large-scale environmental engineering;
- relations with the Galactic Confederation and other external civilizations;
- shared emergency protocols.

Dublin does not need to replace existing national capitals. Its role may be closer to the place where **humanity negotiates with itself across jurisdictions**.

# 4. POSSIBLE GOVERNANCE MODEL

## OPTION / TO DESIGN

A useful model is a multi-layer structure rather than one all-powerful unitary world state.

Possible components:

### EARTH COUNCIL
Represents planetary states and/or major planetary commonwealth structures.

### ORBITAL COUNCIL
Represents orbital cities, habitats and later off-world settlements.

### COMMON HUMAN COUNCIL
Handles only civilization-scale issues that cannot be solved separately.

This is an OPTION, not yet locked constitutional canon.

Key unresolved questions:

- representation by population versus representation by polity;
- veto rights;
- orbital autonomy;
- voting status of lunar/Martian settlements;
- status of synthetic persons;
- whether Irish technological privilege creates conflicts of interest;
- relationship between the new structure and existing states/blocs;
- emergency powers;
- Confederation observer/liaison status.

# 5. DUBLIN BUILDING / CIVIC SYMBOL

## WORKING CONSTRUCTION

Dublin may contain a dedicated Earth-orbit governance complex.

Working names:

- `HOUSE OF EARTH AND ORBIT`
- `THE COMMON HOUSE`

The building should embody the multi-layer nature of the setting rather than look like a conventional parliament transplanted into a skyscraper.

Possible spatial logic:

- historic Dublin / protected civic fabric at ground level;
- subterranean diplomatic, security and transport layers;
- vertical civic and assembly spaces;
- atmospheric access;
- direct integration with orbital-transfer infrastructure;
- arrival routes from planetary, oceanic, atmospheric and orbital networks.

The architecture should physically express that Earth civilization is no longer one flat surface society.

# 6. CENTRAL ASSEMBLY SPACE

## OPTION

Instead of a conventional frontal parliament chamber, the principal assembly space may center on a dynamic model of the inhabited Solar System.

As the human settlement map expands, represented political subjects may include:

- Earth states and blocs;
- orbital cities;
- lunar settlements;
- Martian settlements;
- asteroid communities;
- synthetic-person constituencies;
- later new planetary settlements.

The political definition of “who counts as a polity” becomes a continuing story question.

---

# 7. FIRST SUMMIT SHOULD NOT BE EASY

## STORY LAW

Do not write the creation of Earth-orbit governance as a ceremonial consensus scene.

The first major Dublin summit should contain real structural conflict.

Examples:

- planetary powers demand population-weighted representation;
- orbital cities reject becoming demographic provinces of Earth;
- one-polity-one-vote models anger billion-person planetary regions;
- states resist surrendering control over asteroid resources;
- orbital leaders resist Earth jurisdiction over space infrastructure;
- Ireland is accused of possessing privileged access while mediating the negotiation;
- emerging lunar/Martian communities demand future independence guarantees.

A durable structure, if created, must be earned through conflict, bargaining, concessions and consequences.

# 8. SMITH / ENIA / DUBLIN CONNECTION

## CANON-COMPATIBLE STORY LINK

Smith's continuing Dublin line with Enia now exists in a city that may also host the most important Earth-orbit political negotiations in human history.

This creates a productive contrast:

- above / in public: leaders design new institutions for humanity's future;
- below / in hidden historical systems: Smith investigates older agreements, anomalies and Earth-security knowledge that may predate those institutions.

Do not make Smith a routine member of world government. His value comes from the possibility that the new system encounters realities, obligations or old agreements it does not understand.

# 9. STATUS DISCIPLINE

## CANON

- Ireland's unique orbital-technology exception is already canon.
- Dublin as a multi-layer planetary spaceport metropolis is already canon.
- Smith knows Ireland's status before arriving; this is already canon.

## WORKING / NOT YET LOCKED

- Dublin as permanent seat of a world / Earth-orbit government;
- exact constitutional structure;
- House of Earth and Orbit / Common House name;
- exact summit chronology;
- exact membership and voting system.

These should be developed through future story architecture before being promoted to final canon.
