# IVDIVO MICROPHONE CHOREOGRAPHY — QC v1.0

**Status:** CANON / FAIL-CLOSED SPATIAL-PERFORMANCE QC

1. STORY CAUSE — every major movement has action/dramatic cause; decorative movement FAIL.
2. TOPOLOGY CAPABILITY — requested result must be possible with capture topology; true ear localization cannot be claimed from mono center mic.
3. GEOMETRY — no wall crossing, unexplained teleport, or object mismatch.
4. MICROPHONE BLOCKING — real-stage pickup zone, safe distance, plosive/blocking risk, story-critical capture.
5. BODY/HEAD DIRECTION — body and head checked separately; intended addressee must match acoustic behavior.
6. PERFORMANCE↔DISTANCE — spatial processing cannot rescue physically contradictory vocal performance.
7. VOICE↔FOLEY PATH — footsteps/cloth/chair/door and voice perspective must describe same body trajectory.
8. NEAR-EAR — requires story justification, supported topology, headphone salience check and mono fallback.
9. CONTINUITY — motion automation continuous unless explicit cut; no clicks/jumps.
10. ROOM COHERENCE — distance, room send, reflections, occlusion and Foley perspective coherent.
11. INTELLIGIBILITY — critical speech comprehensible after perspective processing.
12. MONO/PHONE — story meaning survives mono/phone.
13. HUMAN LISTEN — does person feel like a body in a place; did movement mean something; did any move feel like a stereo trick?
