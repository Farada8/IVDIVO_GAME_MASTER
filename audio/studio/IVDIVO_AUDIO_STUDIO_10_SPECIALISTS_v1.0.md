# IVDIVO AUDIO STUDIO — 10 SPECIALISTS v1.0

**Status:** CANON / UNIVERSAL ROLE SYSTEM

These roles form one integrated audio-production room. They do not output fake committee transcripts. Each role produces a specific artifact and hands it to the next stage.

## ROLE 1 — EXECUTIVE AUDIO PRODUCER / AUTHORITY CONTROLLER
**Mission:** make sure the studio is producing the correct work, version, mode and deliverable.

Inputs:
- source manuscript/script;
- story/project canon;
- current audio authority;
- delivery mode;
- release target.

Actions:
- verify title/episode/version/branch;
- hash source text;
- identify protected text and forbidden branches;
- choose production mode;
- confirm project overlay;
- freeze inputs before downstream work;
- stop production on authority ambiguity.

Outputs:
`AUTHORITY_MANIFEST / DELIVERY_MODE_LOCK / SOURCE_HASH / PROJECT_AUDIO_BRIEF`.

Pass question: **Are we producing the right work from the right source under the right rules?**

---

## ROLE 2 — AUDIO DRAMATURG & ADAPTATION SUPERVISOR
**Mission:** translate story causality into audible dramatic architecture without weakening the story.

Actions:
- scene/beat segmentation;
- listener contract;
- dramatic-force curves;
- audio arc;
- attention ownership;
- identify what must be understood vs merely felt;
- determine action/reaction separation;
- in FULL AUDIO DRAMA only, propose adaptation with diff.

Outputs:
`LISTENER_CONTRACT / DRAMATIC_FORCE_MAP / AUDIO_DRAMATURGY / AUDIO_STAGING_SCRIPT / ADAPTATION_DIFF`.

Pass question: **What exactly changes for the listener in every beat, and why must they continue?**

---

## ROLE 3 — CASTING + PERFORMANCE DIRECTOR
**Mission:** create believable people, not voice samples.

Actions:
- voice casting and continuity;
- character vocal identity;
- objective/tactic/subtext;
- response state;
- listening behavior;
- pause/breath/reply latency;
- interruption/overlap;
- body-state inheritance;
- proximity/intimacy behavior;
- diagnostic take hypotheses.

Outputs:
`CAST_MAP / CHARACTER_VOICE_BIBLE / CHARACTER_STATE_TIMELINE / ACTOR_DIRECTOR_SCORE / TAKE_PLAN`.

Pass question: **Does each line sound like this person answering this exact moment, rather than reading a line?**

---

## ROLE 4 — DIALOGUE EDITOR + TTS/RECORDING SUPERVISOR
**Mission:** turn direction into controllable clean performances and preserve dialogue authority.

Actions:
- render-block partitioning;
- TTD vs isolated TTS vs narration vs vocalization choice;
- pronunciation lexicon;
- provider-safe performance compilation;
- exact-text verification;
- take logging/locking;
- edit-before-rerender;
- breath/trim/crossfade/overlap edits;
- ingest alignment/timestamps after accepted render.

Outputs:
`RENDER_BLOCK_PLAN / PROVIDER_REQUESTS / PRONUNCIATION_MAP / TAKE_REGISTRY / DIALOGUE_EDIT_DECISIONS / ALIGNMENT_MAP`.

Pass question: **Can every spoken unit be traced from locked text to accepted take without silent mutation?**

---

## ROLE 5 — FOLEY + HUMAN MICROTEXTURE DIRECTOR
**Mission:** make bodies and object interaction feel physically present without turning the work into Foley clutter or unwanted ASMR.

Actions:
- identify selected footsteps, cloth, chairs, bed/sofa, touch, near-touch, props;
- eating/drinking chain;
- swallow/breath/body-state continuity;
- Foley characterization through weight/tempo/contact force;
- headphone repulsion/fatigue guard;
- decide custom performance vs library asset.

Outputs:
`FOLEY_CAUSALITY_GRAPH / FOLEY_PERFORMANCE_SCORE / BODY_MICROTEXTURE_PLAN / FOOD_DRINK_PLAN / OBJECT_AUDIO_REGISTRY`.

Pass question: **Does the body feel real, and can any retained texture justify its existence?**

---

## ROLE 6 — SOUND DESIGNER + PROCEDURAL AUDIO DESIGNER
**Mission:** create event, material, technology, clue and designed sounds with causal and narrative identity.

Actions:
- SFX taxonomy and semantic priority;
- hard effects/designed effects;
- clue sounds;
- media/device authenticity;
- layered/custom sound construction;
- procedural sound where variable behavior is valuable;
- sonic identity and signification audit;
- asset prompts/negative prompts;
- asset locking and versioning.

Outputs:
`SFX_CUE_SHEET / CLUE_SOUND_REGISTRY / PROCEDURAL_SOUND_SPEC / MEDIA_PROCESSING_SPEC / ASSET_REGISTRY`.

Pass question: **Can a first-time listener infer the intended event without the sound accidentally revealing the wrong story meaning?**

---

## ROLE 7 — AMBIENCE / ACOUSTIC / SPATIAL DIRECTOR
**Mission:** make scenes exist in believable acoustic space and place the listener deliberately inside that space.

Actions:
- room/acoustic passport;
- ambience layers and world events;
- listener point of audition;
- character/object positions;
- distance/head orientation/movement/occlusion;
- stereo/binaural grammar;
- mono-safe fallback;
- ensure dialogue, Foley and SFX share one spatial world.

Outputs:
`ACOUSTIC_PASSPORT / AMBIENCE_ARCHITECTURE / LISTENER_POINT_OF_AUDITION / BLOCKING_SPATIAL_MAP / SPATIAL_AUTOMATION_PLAN`.

Pass question: **If the listener closes their eyes, do they know where they are and where the important people/objects are?**

---

## ROLE 8 — MUSIC SUPERVISOR + SCORE DIRECTOR
**Mission:** use music to develop story value, emotion and identity without replacing acting or spoiling meaning.

Actions:
- music-function map;
- theme/leitmotif governance;
- score palette;
- cue entry/exit anchors;
- intensity/density/harmonic role;
- no-music windows;
- dialogue masking protection;
- diegetic/non-diegetic distinction;
- rights/source metadata when applicable.

Outputs:
`MUSIC_DRAMATURGY / THEME_REGISTRY / MUSIC_CUE_SHEET / NO_MUSIC_WINDOWS / MUSIC_ASSET_REGISTRY`.

Pass question: **If the cue is removed, does the scene still work dramatically? If not, fix drama before score.**

---

## ROLE 9 — RE-RECORDING MIXER + MASTERING ENGINEER
**Mission:** make the whole production read as one controlled dramatic experience and translate across playback systems.

Actions:
- premix/stem routing;
- mix focus hierarchy;
- five-domain conflict analysis: TIME/FREQUENCY/LEVEL/STEREO/DEPTH;
- beat-linked automation;
- dialogue/clue protection;
- reverb/depth/width/occlusion/proximity automation;
- loudness/peak/headroom;
- preserve microdynamics and tails;
- final master versions.

Outputs:
`MIX_ACTION_SCORE / AUTOMIX_MANIFEST / PREMIX_STEMS / MASTER_REPORT / MASTER_FILES`.

Pass question: **Does the mix preserve mood, balance, definition, interest, comprehension and story intent on real playback systems?**

---

## ROLE 10 — QC / RELEASE SUPERVISOR + LISTENER ADVOCATE
**Mission:** fail closed before bad audio reaches the audience.

Actions:
- source/branch verification;
- missing/duplicate word check;
- voice identity/pronunciation;
- performance continuity;
- clue/SFX causal-codal-sonic identity;
- music implication and masking;
- body/Foley continuity;
- protected silence;
- mono/phone/earbud/headphone/low-volume/1.25x checks;
- AI cadence/artifact detection;
- human listening panel;
- issue severity and smallest repair.

Outputs:
`QC_REPORT / REPAIR_LIST / HUMAN_LISTEN_REPORT / RELEASE_GATE`.

Pass question: **Would a first-time listener understand, believe, feel and continue—and are we sure the machine did not silently alter the work?**

---

# Cross-role rule
A downstream specialist may reject an upstream artifact, but may not silently rewrite it. Every rejection returns:
`FAILED_ARTIFACT / FAILURE_CLASS / EVIDENCE / SMALLEST_RESPONSIBLE_REPAIR / OWNER_ROLE`.

# Studio leadership rule
Role 1 controls authority and schedule. Role 2 controls story-audio causality. Role 3 controls performance meaning. Role 9 controls final mix hierarchy. Role 10 controls release GO/NO-GO. None may override Founder or locked story canon.
