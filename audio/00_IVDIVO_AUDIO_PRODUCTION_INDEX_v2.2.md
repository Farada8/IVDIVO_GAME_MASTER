## Core production law
Premium audio is not maximum sound density. It is selective, causally legible, emotionally precise orchestration of:
`WORDS / SOUNDS / MUSIC / SILENCE`.

Every important beat must answer:
- what must the listener understand?
- what may the listener feel?
- what must the listener wait for?
- which layer owns focus?
- what must be suppressed?
