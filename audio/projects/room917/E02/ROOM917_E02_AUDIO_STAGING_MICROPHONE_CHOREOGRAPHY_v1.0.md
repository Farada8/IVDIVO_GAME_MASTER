### Listener point of audition
Listener sits acoustically near the old board/evidence table, not inside any character's head. Elena is closest to the work surface, Mina slightly wider as witness. The alcove door is behind/right relative to the listener.

### Actor blocking
- ELENA begins close to evidence and the board. Her spatial position says ownership of procedure, not emotional retreat.
- MINA begins opposite/side of Elena, actively sealing and witnessing.
- JULIAN enters from the alcove door carrying two coffees; his steps move from rear/right toward the work surface. Coffee placement is audible before his first line settles into the discussion.
- When Elena says **“You knew the fourth note.”**, Julian's peace-offering movement stops. Do not add a musical sting. The stop itself is the beat.
- Julian stays physically contained during the test argument. He may turn toward Mina when reasserting staff authority, but does not pace.
- When Mina says she is going to the office, she actually takes two steps toward the door. Elena's **“Stay. You are the independent witness.”** stops that trajectory. Mina remains near the threshold for a beat before rejoining the witness position.
- Elena physically slides sealed copy two across wood. Julian receives it without crossing into Elena's evidence position.

### Performance-space coupling
Elena's direct fourth-note challenge is slightly closer and more direct, but not intimate. Julian's defensive answers should not be made “distant” merely by reverb; the distance is behavioral first: restrained projection, controlled phrase endings, head/body turns. Mina's wit remains spatially lighter but never becomes a comic side-channel.

### Foley

### Silence

## Scene 2 — Elena's hotel room, late morning

### Start image in sound
Smaller/closer room. Rain nearer the glass. Suitcase zip. Hard cassette case. Portable deck. The contrast with the switchboard matters: this is Elena's temporary private space and personal evidence.

### Listener point of audition
Listener is near the cassette/deck table. Elena owns the closest equipment position. Mina is supportive but offset. Julian starts nearer the room threshold rather than immediately inside Elena's personal radius.

### Actor blocking
- JULIAN's **“Why are we doing this in your room?”** is delivered from the threshold/edge of the room; he takes only a small step in after Elena defines the evidence logic.
- ELENA moves to the deck when she says **“Headphones on. Nobody speaks until I stop the deck.”** The movement is a procedural boundary.
- MINA and JULIAN lean into the shared listening position but do not crowd Elena.
- During cassette playback all three are spatially still. No decorative clothing, footsteps or emotional reactions over Cate's recording unless a reaction is explicitly isolated and does not mask the evidence.
- CATE-ON-CASSETTE is not staged as a live person standing in Elena's room. Her voice remains clearly DEVICE-MEDIATED from the portable deck/cassette acoustic domain.
- Elena stops the deck immediately after the complete four-note lullaby. The physical stop is the scene turn.
- Elena's later sample comparison is equipment-centered: two short sources alternate, while live characters remain quiet enough for the listener to compare noise beds.
- Julian's **“Not until Margot arrives.”** is accompanied by a small physical withdrawal back toward institutional distance, not a dramatic exit.

### Media authenticity
Cassette source: domestic room tone, broadband tape hiss, kettle, intimate close-to-cassette voice. Field recording source: narrow copper/line texture + Greyhaven transformer domain. These remain audibly distinct and must not be blended by shared room reverb.

### Foley/microtexture
Cassette case, deck transport, headphones handoff, stop/play/sample switching. Human microtexture is highly selective: Elena may have one controlled breath before playback; no crying-performance embellishment unless the take earns it naturally. No mouth/ASMR emphasis.

### Silence

## Scene 3 — Switchboard and front desk, early afternoon

### Start image in sound
Cabinet screws, public trunk plug physically removed and placed on rubber. This scene is a spatial proof experiment, so geography must be audible.

### Listener point of audition
Listener remains near the old switchboard. The modern front desk is a real distant destination across the lobby.

### Actor blocking
- ELENA remains tied to the board, trunk contacts, probe and hidden edge beyond 916.
- JULIAN stays near the board as skeptical observer.
- MINA is explicitly sent to reception. Her path is continuous: alcove → lobby → front desk. Footsteps, room transition and voice depth must describe one body.
- The modern front-desk phone rings at real distance from the listener. Mina's **“Greyhaven Hotel, Mina speaking to Mina…”** is heard from reception, not from the same close-mic position she had in the alcove.
- Her distance is created by the HYBRID stage: performance appropriate to speaking at reception + spatial/acoustic distance. Do not merely reduce gain.
- After **“I'm hanging up.”**, receiver down. Mina begins returning toward the alcove while Elena and Julian continue the local-return test.
- Her return may remain in background until the hidden-terminal sequence becomes visually/audibly inspectable.
- Mina must be physically near the board by **“Is that a seven?”**, because the line means she can see the filed-off digit. Her return therefore completes before this beat.
- Julian does not need continuous movement. He closes distance only when Elena exposes the hidden terminal. That approach is story-motivated: institutional danger becomes physically undeniable.
- On **“Stop. Margot will be here at two.”**, Julian may occupy a blocking position between Elena's work path and the next step of the trace, without touching her or the equipment.
- Elena remains at the board and answers from professional physical control.

### Critical spatial proof
The modern hotel phone path and the old-board local return must read as two different systems in two different positions. Mina's ordinary reception call occurs far from the old board while the old board stays silent; the local hidden return continues independently near Elena. This is evidence, not atmosphere.

### Foley
Cabinet screws, trunk plug, rubber mat, Mina's walk to/from reception, dial/modern phone, receiver pickup/down, probe touches, meter tap, calibrated click, hidden-terminal dust cleaning, Julian's single approach step at reveal. No extra decorative footsteps.

### SFX/clue hierarchy
1. modern front desk ring — ordinary, distant, external-network proof;
2. dead old board — deliberately absent response;
3. local meter pulse — close, persistent, separate;
4. calibrated test click and late narrow metallic return — evidence sound;
5. hidden-terminal reveal — small physical sound, not horror cue.

### Music
No music under the test or hidden-terminal reveal. Final muted four-note figure may enter only at the episode button and must stop before final note exactly as locked.
