## New accepted mechanisms
### M39 — FOLEY TEAM IS A DISTINCT CRAFT CHAIN
Foley performance, Foley recording/mixing, cue management/editing and final integration are separable responsibilities even when one person covers several. IVDIVO consequence: preserve distinct artifacts for performance intent, recording asset, edit decision and final mix use.

### M40 — FOLEY SHOULD DISAPPEAR INTO REALITY
Well-made Foley normally supports or enhances reality without calling attention to itself. IVDIVO consequence: add salience/attention budget and reject self-advertising ordinary Foley.

### M41 — FOLEY STYLE FOLLOWS PROJECT STYLE
Sparse work remains sparse; heightened/animation-like worlds may legitimately use more stylized Foley. IVDIVO consequence: project overlay sets `FOLEY_REALISM_PROFILE` and `FOLEY_DENSITY_RANGE`; universal canon does not enforce one density.

### M42 — SOUND OBJECTS CAN BE BEHAVIORAL, NOT STATIC FILES
Procedural audio can represent a process that changes over time rather than a single fixed recording. IVDIVO consequence: add `PROCEDURAL_ASSET_SPEC` for recurring mechanical, environmental or synthetic phenomena where controlled variation matters.

### M43 — PROCEDURAL SOUND STARTS FROM CAUSE/PHYSICS
Before synthesis, analyze what produces the sound and which physical variables control behavior. IVDIVO fields:
`ENERGY_SOURCE / EXCITER / RESONATOR / MATERIAL / MOTION / RANDOMNESS / ENVIRONMENT / CONTROL_PARAMETERS`.

### M44 — PROVIDER/TOOL INDEPENDENCE IS A DESIGN ADVANTAGE
Professional sound knowledge should be expressed in abstract behavior and signal concepts rather than UI-specific recipes. IVDIVO consequence: keep universal score/provider adapter separation.

### M45 — DIRECTOR COORDINATES THE WHOLE ORCHESTRA
Radio-drama production evidence reinforces director control across performers, SFX technicians, musicians and technical flow. IVDIVO consequence: Audio Director owns beat focus and cross-department timing; departments cannot maximize independently.

### M46 — PERFORMANCE SCALE MUST FIT THE MICROPHONE
Audio performance is intimate and microphone-centered. IVDIVO consequence: casting/performance direction includes `MIC_RELATIONSHIP_PROFILE`, and broad stage projection is rejected unless story/device context requires it.

### M47 — SOUND EFFECTS, ACOUSTICS AND PERSPECTIVE ARE SEPARATE QUESTIONS
A production must distinguish event sound, nature of the space, and spatial relation of source/listener. IVDIVO consequence: `SFX_CUE_SHEET`, `ACOUSTIC_PASSPORT` and `LISTENER_POINT_OF_AUDITION/BLOCKING_MAP` remain separate artifacts.

### M48 — MIXING IS MULTI-DOMAIN STORY CONTROL
Izhaki’s time/frequency/level/stereo/depth model supports the IVDIVO mix action score. IVDIVO consequence: dense-beat mix diagnosis must identify the domain of conflict before repair.

### M49 — STEMS PRESERVE REPAIRABILITY
Independent stems reduce recall/rebuild cost and support localized fixes. IVDIVO consequence: minimum stem law remains mandatory for dramatized production.

### M50 — PRODUCTION MUST RETAIN SOURCE-QUALITY ASSETS
Avoid unnecessary format conversions before mix/master. IVDIVO consequence: archive provider/source originals and derive downstream working files through documented transforms.

## Studio-architecture consequence
The professional evidence supports a ten-role model with explicit interfaces:
`Producer → Dramaturg → Performance → Dialogue → Foley → Sound Design → Spatial/Ambience → Music → Mix/Master → QC/Release`.

This is a responsibility map, not a requirement for ten salaried humans. Small teams may combine roles, but the checks/artifacts may not disappear.

## Red Team
### Risk 1 — too many documents
FIX: stage artifacts are generated only when applicable; a simple narrated audiobook can skip irrelevant Foley/spatial/music subpackages.

### Risk 2 — process overwhelms story
FIX: Listener Contract and focus-owner rules remain upstream; no craft department gets to add work for its own sake.

### Risk 3 — synthetic/procedural sound becomes a technology hobby
FIX: procedural audio requires a real need for controllable behavior, repeatability or impossible-to-record sound.

### Risk 4 — Foley microdetail becomes fetishized
FIX: salience, repulsion, fatigue and evocative-detail gates remain mandatory.

### Risk 5 — studio roles become fake committee theater
FIX: no transcripts; each role returns an artifact or PASS/FAIL decision.

## Verdict
`PASS — STUDIO OS v3.0 JUSTIFIED`.
No replacement of v2.1/v2.2/v2.3 is needed. Studio OS v3.0 is an operational layer that coordinates those canon rules into a professional production organization.
