## Core law
Premium audio is not maximum sound density.
It is selective, causally legible, embodied and emotionally precise orchestration of:
`WORDS / PERFORMANCE / BODY / SOUNDS / SPACE / MUSIC / SILENCE`.

## Performance law
Every reactive line must know what caused it.
`HEARD_EVENT → RESPONSE_IMPULSE → PLAYABLE_BEHAVIOR → LINE → STATE_OUT`.

## Foley law
Foley is performance. Timing, weight, material and character handling matter as much as asset identity.

## Food/body law
Eating/drinking/body textures are allowed only when physically coherent and story-useful. Audible body state must affect performance when relevant. Protect against headphone repulsion/fatigue.

## Spatial law
Stereo/binaural staging requires explicit `LISTENER_POINT_OF_AUDITION`. Critical meaning remains mono-safe.

## Mix law
Resolve competition across `TIME / FREQUENCY / LEVEL / STEREO / DEPTH` and write a beat-linked `MIX_ACTION_SCORE` after real timing exists.

## Provider law
ElevenLabs and other TTS/SFX/music vendors are replaceable execution backends. IVDIVO owns authority, performance logic, sound semantics, timing, stems, QC and master lock.
