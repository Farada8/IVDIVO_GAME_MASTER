# IVDIVO — McKEE STORY MECHANICS REFERENCE

**Status:** REFERENCE ONLY  
**Source:** Robert McKee principles as summarized in the SlideShare deck “The Elements of Story by Robert McKee” (65 slides).  
**Source URL:** https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/the-elements-of-story-by-robert-mckee/46145777  
**Rule:** Reference mechanisms may improve craft choices but do not override Founder instruction or IVDIVO canon.

---

## PURPOSE

Use this module to prevent IVDIVO scenes, chapters and books from becoming exposition, fragments, lore catalogues or disconnected episodes.

The mechanisms below are abstractions of craft principles, not material to copy.

---

## 1. STORY EVENT / VALUE TURN

A meaningful story event changes the life situation of a character through conflict. For IVDIVO scene analysis, record:

- OPENING VALUE
- CLOSING VALUE
- TURN

Useful value shifts include trust -> suspicion, safety -> danger, belonging -> exclusion, hope -> despair, dependence -> autonomy, love -> betrayal, ignorance -> dangerous knowledge.

If the opening and closing state are substantially the same, challenge the scene’s necessity.

---

## 2. GAP

For each major protagonist action:

`ACTION -> EXPECTED RESULT -> ACTUAL RESULT`

The world should answer differently, more strongly, or both. The gap between expectation and actual result generates the next dramatic necessity.

Do not allow the world to obediently deliver the protagonist’s expected result scene after scene.

---

## 3. BEAT

A beat is treated operationally as:

`ACTION -> REACTION -> ADJUSTMENT -> COUNTERACTION`

Dialogue therefore must be behavior, not equal-airtime information exchange.

---

## 4. CAUSALITY

Preferred IVDIVO chain:

`MOTIVATED ACTION -> EFFECT -> NEW CAUSE -> STRONGER EFFECT -> ESCALATION -> CLIMAX`

Avoid coincidence as a resolution mechanism. Coincidence may initiate trouble, but the protagonist’s choices must drive the decisive outcome.

---

## 5. PROGRESSIVE PRESSURE

A story must not retreat to lesser actions after major escalation.

Each major complication should do at least one of the following:

- close an easier option;
- increase the price;
- expose a deeper opposition;
- make the previous strategy insufficient;
- force a harder choice;
- widen consequences from private to social or systemic.

---

## 6. TRUE CHARACTER = CHOICE UNDER PRESSURE

Character biography and traits are not proof of character.

True character is demonstrated when a person must choose between competing values under meaningful risk.

IVDIVO Character Director must therefore design dilemmas that test stated traits rather than merely describe them.

---

## 7. INCITING IMBALANCE

The central inciting event must disturb the hero’s life enough to generate a sustained quest / pursuit.

Test:

- What has become impossible to ignore?
- Why must the hero act now?
- What new object of desire appears?
- What happens if the hero refuses?

---

## 8. CRISIS / CLIMAX DISTINCTION

**CRISIS:** the unavoidable difficult choice.

**CLIMAX:** the protagonist acts on that choice and creates the decisive irreversible change.

Do not substitute realization, speech, explanation or thematic understanding for climactic action.

---

## 9. FINAL ACTION TEST

The climax should be the strongest necessary action of this particular story: after it, the audience should not be waiting for a more decisive action to resolve the same main conflict.

This does not forbid later-series hooks. It requires the current book’s central conflict to close first.

---

## 10. SCENE ANALYSIS ADAPTATION FOR IVDIVO

For every important scene record:

1. WHO WANTS WHAT?
2. WHY NOW?
3. OPPOSITION / CONFLICT.
4. OPENING VALUE.
5. MAJOR BEATS.
6. EXPECTED RESULT.
7. GAP / ACTUAL RESULT.
8. TURNING POINT.
9. CLOSING VALUE.
10. WHAT NEW CAUSE DOES THIS CREATE?

---

## 11. IVDIVO PRODUCTION SYNTHESIS

McKee-derived mechanics strengthen but do not replace the IVDIVO production chain:

`HERO -> WANT -> WHY NOW -> OPPOSITION -> ACTION -> GAP -> CONSEQUENCE -> PRICE -> ESCALATION -> CRISIS CHOICE -> CLIMACTIC ACTION -> RESOLUTION -> SERIES HOOK`

The governing IVDIVO law remains:

**STORY FIRST. CHARACTER SECOND. WORLD THROUGH STORY. SAGA THROUGH COMPLETED BOOKS.**
