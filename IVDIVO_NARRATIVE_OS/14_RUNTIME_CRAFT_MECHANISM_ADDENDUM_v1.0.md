## 1. DUAL-PROTAGONIST ROMANCE LAW

For romance-bearing stories, both romantic leads are protagonists of the relationship arc.

Each requires:
- independent external want;
- private desire;
- fear/defence;
- contradiction;
- price;
- choice;
- observable change.

Neither lead may exist mainly as reward, healer, obstacle, status object or fantasy delivery device.

Relationship beats must braid with the external story. A romance beat that pauses causality without changing action, knowledge, status, risk, commitment or future behavior is suspect.

Runtime questions:
1. What does Lead A want outside the romance?
2. What does Lead B want outside the romance?
3. What does this relationship beat change in the external plot or vice versa?
4. What harder choice becomes possible/necessary because the relationship state changed?

For women's commercial fiction, heroine-subject law remains mandatory: the heroine retains a full goal and agency independent of being desired.

## 2. WAITING-QUESTION LEDGER

Reader/listener retention is not a vague `hook` score. Track what the audience is actively waiting for.

Per scene/episode/chapter, record only meaningful questions:
- `OPEN_QUESTION`;
- `WHO_CARES`;
- `WHY_NOW`;
- `EXPECTED_REWARD`;
- `DELAY_SOURCE`;
- `NEW_INFORMATION_BEFORE_PAYOFF`;
- `PAYOFF_OR_TRANSFORMATION_POINT`;
- `STALE_RISK`.

At least one high-value waiting question should usually survive the end of a serial episode/chapter unless a stronger completed payoff opens the next question.

Do not keep a question alive by withholding information a competent POV character would naturally think/say. Delay must come from opposition, uncertainty, incomplete evidence, competing goals, danger, relationship cost or necessary action.

Mystery and suspense are not identical controls: mystery asks what is true/what will be discovered; suspense asks what may happen and whether the character can prevent or survive it. Strong serial work may braid both.

Romantic anticipation and external suspense should braid rather than compete for page/audio time.

## 3. SCENE-PRESSURE LAW

Before line polish, an important scene needs:

`DESIRE -> OPPOSITION -> TACTIC/PLAN -> COUNTERPRESSURE -> ESCALATION -> TURN/REVEAL -> CONSEQUENCE`

Enter as late as possible without losing the information or emotional setup required for the conflict.

Subtext is not mandatory decoration. Use it when fear, shame, status, strategy or relational cost makes indirect action more truthful than direct speech.

If the dialogue is carrying structural work the scene/plot should have done, repair structure first.

### 3A. SCENE-QUESTION / OUTCOME-INTEGRITY LAW

For an important dramatic scene, derive one immediate `SCENE_QUESTION` from the active character's concrete goal and opposition.

The scene's turn/outcome must answer or causally transform **that same question**. Valid result families include:
- `YES`;
- `NO`;
- `YES, BUT`;
- `NO, AND`;
- `QUESTION TRANSFORMED BY A CAUSALLY EARNED REVEAL`.

A shock, disaster, revelation or new threat that does not answer, complicate or transform the scene's active question is arbitrary escalation rather than a scene turn.

The closing beat may open a stronger next question only after the present scene has produced a real consequence. Do not drop an unrelated event into the scene merely because a cliffhanger is desired.

Runtime test:
`IMMEDIATE GOAL -> SCENE QUESTION -> CONFLICT MANEUVERS -> OUTCOME -> CONSEQUENCE/NEXT QUESTION`.

This is a causality check, not a demand that every scene end badly. Victory is allowed when it creates cost, changed state, a new obligation or legitimate closure.

### 3B. TRANSFORMATION-SCALE / STABLE-CORE SERIES LAW

`CHANGE` does not require a sweeping personality reversal in every story.

Before forcing an arc, identify the earned transformation scale:
- `POLAR TRANSFORMATION` — a major moral/identity reversal;
- `REORIENTATION` — the character keeps core identity but changes belief, priority or strategy;
- `REINFORCEMENT UNDER COST` — the character proves/strengthens an existing core value through harder choices;
- `KNOWLEDGE / RELATIONSHIP / STATUS CHANGE` — the person is recognizably the same but future behavior and relationships are permanently altered;
- `STABLE CORE / WORLD CHANGED` — especially in serial fiction, the protagonist's defining core remains stable while consequences, obligations, losses, reputation, institutions or world conditions accumulate around that core.

Every completed story must still create a non-resettable consequence: changed choice capacity, relationship, knowledge, price, status or world state. Do not manufacture an epiphany or personality transplant merely to satisfy an abstract arc template.

For series leads, preserve recognizability while allowing consequence and learning to accumulate book by book.

## 4. DIALOGUE-AS-ACTION / THREE-LAYER LAW

For important dialogue distinguish:
- `SAID` — literal content;
- `UNSAID` — intended inference/subtext;
- `UNSAYABLE` — what cannot yet be admitted without unacceptable cost.

A line should normally perform at least one action:
- obtain/refuse information;
- change status;
- pressure/deflect;
- conceal/reveal;
- negotiate a boundary;
- force a choice;
- reframe evidence;
- protect/attack dignity;
- move relationship distance;
- create consequence.

Exposition is strongest when it is ammunition in conflict or changes the next action.

Hard warnings:
- character-neutral language;
- five correct answers in sequence;
- on-the-nose emotional diagnosis;
- overly knowing/perceptive characters;
- speeches that explain SAID + UNSAID + UNSAYABLE together;
- elegant paraphrase after the action already made the meaning clear.

Dialogue should sound speakable, but it is selective designed action rather than transcription of ordinary talk.

### 4A. P51 SHARED-RHETORICAL-CONSTRUCTION LAW

Voice differentiation must inspect more than vocabulary, accent, sentence length and favorite words.

Across important speakers, scan recurring **authorial construction families** such as:
- `X is not Y; it is Z` correction/reversal;
- polished counter-question as default response;
- repeated three-part negation/qualification;
- identical concession-then-reframe geometry;
- aphoristic correction cadence;
- hyper-competent explanatory mini-speech;
- the same setup -> elegant reversal -> compressed moral conclusion across unrelated characters.

Shared community/professional vernacular is allowed when status, culture, profession or relationship plausibly explains it. Flag only unjustified convergence that makes distinct characters feel generated by one rhetorical machine.

Repair the smallest level that causes the convergence: tactic, sentence construction, listening behavior, status response or voice fingerprint — not automatic whole-scene rewrite.

## 5. P53 v1.2 INHERITED TASK CLASSIFIER

When character/relationship emotion is relevant, inherit the current P53 control after story/canon, P51 voice and P52 emotional-range checks.

### P53 TWO-AXIS ROUTING

Resolve relationship control in two independent steps:

**Axis 1 — inherited relationship authority/state**
What signals are actually available before this scene begins? Examples:
`NONE / STRANGERS / PROFESSIONAL / FRIENDSHIP / FAMILY / ANTAGONISTIC / TRUST_EMERGING / ATTRACTION_AUTHORIZED / ROMANCE_ACTIVE / RUPTURE / REPAIR / project-specific locked state`.

**Axis 2 — current scene function/dosage**
What relationship/emotional function is this scene allowed or required to perform now?

Relationship authority determines **which signals exist**. Scene function determines **how much of them may appear now**.

Therefore:
- proximity, rescue, danger, vulnerability or physical closeness cannot invent attraction when inherited authority does not permit it;
- a quiet procedural scene does not erase attraction/trust/rupture already established in inherited state;
- scene dosage cannot outrank canon, consent/power, professional truth or locked relationship chronology.

Then classify the task:
- **A — Romance-bearing:** full P53.
- **B — Relationship-bearing, non-romantic:** relational truth; no invented romance.
- **C — Professional/investigative:** preserve competence/professional truth; allow private cost where earned.
- **D — Family/friendship:** relational truth and changed future behavior.
- **E — Antagonistic/status:** power, dignity, status and relational consequence.
- **F — Exposition/information transfer:** first convert information to action/resistance/choice.
- **G — Action/rescue/emergency:** after-action emotional consequence is mandatory when character stakes exist.
- **H — Audio/performance:** important emotional beats must be hearable.
- **I — Visual/marketing:** use female-gaze only where appropriate and enforce truthful marketing promise.

For romance-bearing material, the preferred P53 movement is:

`TENSION -> PRIVATE FRACTURE -> MUTUAL NOTICING -> SAFE RESPONSE -> RESTRAINT -> COSTLY CHOICE -> EARNED MICRO-REWARD -> NEW RELATIONSHIP STATE / NEW TENSION`

This loop may span several scenes; do not force every beat into one conversation.

Male-vulnerability control: `PUBLIC COMPETENCE + PRIVATE COST`; reject helplessness-as-attraction, heroine-as-mother and trauma-as-instant-intimacy.

Heroine control: `AGENCY + HIDDEN EMOTIONAL PRICE`; competence is not reduced merely to create chemistry.

P53 never outranks causality, canon, professional truth, agency, consent/power, established relationship authority or declared Romance Weight.

## 6. INFERENCE-FIRST AUDIO LAW

In audio, prefer:
`AUDIBLE EVENT -> CHARACTER RESPONSE -> LISTENER INFERENCE -> CONSEQUENCE`

over:
`NARRATION EXPLAINS EVENT -> DIALOGUE REPEATS IT -> SFX DECORATES IT`.

If sound already proves a fact, dialogue should normally change its use, interpretation or consequence rather than paraphrase it.

Critical information may be redundantly encoded for accessibility/mono safety, but redundancy must feel motivated by action.

Point of audition, proximity and microphone position are story variables when they alter what the listener can know, feel or infer. Do not treat stereo width, reverb or distance as prestige decoration.

## 7. FUNCTIONAL SILENCE LAW

Silence is an authored event only when it changes:
- attention;
- suspense;
- recognition;
- status;
- refusal;
- aftermath;
- relationship distance;
- listener inference.

Do not fill every pause with Foley, ambience swell or music.
Do not use silence as prestige decoration.

For audio production, a required silent reaction should be represented by the current `SILENT_REACTION_ANCHOR` / protected-silence system when applicable.

## 8. ASSEMBLED-PERFORMANCE LAW

A voice or performance is not approved because it wins in isolation.

For recurring leads/ensembles test:
1. isolated intelligibility/character truth;
2. response to one precise playable direction;
3. pair/ensemble distinction and chemistry/conflict;
4. long-form fatigue/listenability;
5. actual scene assembly with relevant acoustic context;
6. headphones + mono + ordinary phone/device where production requires it.

During casting/audition, loudness-match candidates and avoid heavy music/reverb/processing that can disguise weakness. Use multiple functional lines rather than one impressive showcase line.

Voice beauty is subordinate to story function and relationship performance.

## 9. METRIC-DISTRIBUTION / STORY-SUBSTANCE LAW

Metrics are diagnostic instruments, not substitutes for story work.

A better average does not prove a better story. Whenever a numerical measure is used to justify revision or lock:
- inspect distribution/variance and chapter/scene outliers, not only the mean;
- preserve protected beats and compare before/after causal/emotional functions;
- distinguish compression/subtraction from additive story/character/relationship work;
- reject a claim of substantive improvement when the requested new function is absent even if mechanical metrics improve;
- use measurements to locate a problem, then verify the actual text/story consequence.

Do not optimize prose toward a metric until the manuscript becomes uniformly thin, over-short, over-clean or emotionally flattened.

## 11. ONE-LISTEN CAUSAL COMPREHENSION / EVIDENCE-OBJECT LAW

For evidence-heavy audio, normal intelligibility is necessary but insufficient.

After one normal-speed listen, the target listener should be able to distinguish, where the scene depends on them:
- separate evidence/source objects;
- which voice/source/medium produced which fact;
- who knew what and when;
- what each piece of evidence actually proves;
- what it **does not** prove;
- who made which consequential choice;
- the current unanswered question.

A false plot inference is a production defect even when every word is individually audible. Record the earliest moment the incorrect inference became likely.

### Evidence identity continuity

When the story requires the listener to recognize the **same recorded/source event** later, reuse or excerpt the same canonical accepted source take whenever practical. Do not independently regenerate identical words and assume identity continuity will survive.

If the same person appears through different media, preserve stable human identity while allowing medium-specific degradation. Processing may change transmission; it must not accidentally create a different actor, age, ghost, villain or emotional state.

### Story-critical Foley

Foley may carry evidence of:
- presence/absence;
- location/distance;
- physical access;
- action order;
- object handling;
- time/sequence;
- whether a claimed action physically occurred.

Such Foley belongs to the causal evidence hierarchy, not decorative texture.

### Mono/device fallback

If a causal distinction depends on source identity or location, it must survive mono/device playback through a combination of level, spectral/medium identity, depth, timing, performance and event order — never pan alone.

### Repair order

When one-listen comprehension fails, repair in this order unless evidence proves a different root cause:

`PERFORMANCE / LISTENING BEHAVIOR -> PAUSE / EVENT SPACING -> SOURCE / MEDIUM IDENTITY -> MIX FOCUS / CAUSAL HIERARCHY -> EDIT / REPLAY STRUCTURE -> ONLY THEN DEMONSTRATED TEXT DEFECT`.

Do not rewrite protected dialogue because a mix, performance or source-identity failure created confusion.

Use silence as a cognitive reset when meaning changes. Music must not supply the moral/plot verdict before the evidence lands.

## FINAL LAW

**STORY FUNCTION FIRST. CRAFT MECHANISMS ARE CONTROLS, NOT FORMULAS.**
