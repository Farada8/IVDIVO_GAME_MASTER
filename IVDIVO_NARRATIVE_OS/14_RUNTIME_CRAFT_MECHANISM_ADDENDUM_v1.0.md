**Status:** CANONICAL DOWNSTREAM CRAFT GATE  
**Established:** 2026-08-21  
**Scope:** books, dorama, romantic suspense, audio drama and scene/dialogue QA  
**Reference status:** mechanisms abstracted from craft references; reference texts remain REFERENCE ONLY.

This file adds compact runtime checks. It does **not** create four new mandatory prompt passes. The Router invokes only the checks relevant to the current story problem.

## 1. DUAL-PROTAGONIST ROMANCE LAW

For romance-bearing stories, both romantic leads are protagonists of the relationship arc.

Each requires:
- independent external want;
- private desire;
- fear/defence;
- contradiction;
- price;
- choice;
- observable change.

Neither lead may exist mainly as reward, healer, obstacle, status object or fantasy delivery device.

Relationship beats must braid with the external story. A romance beat that pauses causality without changing action, knowledge, status, risk, commitment or future behavior is suspect.

Runtime questions:
1. What does Lead A want outside the romance?
2. What does Lead B want outside the romance?
3. What does this relationship beat change in the external plot or vice versa?
4. What harder choice becomes possible/necessary because the relationship state changed?

For women's commercial fiction, heroine-subject law remains mandatory: the heroine retains a full goal and agency independent of being desired.

## 2. WAITING-QUESTION LEDGER

Reader/listener retention is not a vague 'hook' score. Track what the audience is actively waiting for.

Per scene/episode/chapter, record only the meaningful questions:
- `OPEN_QUESTION`;
- `WHO_CARES`;
- `WHY_NOW`;
- `EXPECTED_REWARD`;
- `DELAY_SOURCE`;
- `NEW_INFORMATION_BEFORE_PAYOFF`;
- `PAYOFF_OR_TRANSFORMATION_POINT`;
- `STALE_RISK`.

At least one high-value waiting question should usually survive the end of a serial episode/chapter unless a stronger completed payoff opens the next question.

Do not keep a question alive by withholding information a competent POV character would naturally think/say. Delay must come from opposition, uncertainty, incomplete evidence, competing goals, danger, relationship cost or necessary action.

Romantic anticipation and external suspense should braid rather than compete for page/audio time.

## 3. DIALOGUE-AS-ACTION / THREE-LAYER LAW

For important dialogue distinguish:
- `SAID` — literal content;
- `UNSAID` — intended inference/subtext;
- `UNSAYABLE` — what cannot yet be admitted without unacceptable cost.

A line should normally perform at least one action:
- obtain/refuse information;
- change status;
- pressure/deflect;
- conceal/reveal;
- negotiate a boundary;
- force a choice;
- reframe evidence;
- protect/attack dignity;
- move relationship distance;
- create consequence.

Exposition is strongest when it is ammunition in conflict or changes the next action.

Hard warnings:
- character-neutral language;
- five correct answers in sequence;
- on-the-nose emotional diagnosis;
- overly knowing/perceptive characters;
- speeches that explain SAID + UNSAID + UNSAYABLE together.

## 4. INFERENCE-FIRST AUDIO LAW

In audio, prefer:
`AUDIBLE EVENT -> CHARACTER RESPONSE -> LISTENER INFERENCE -> CONSEQUENCE`

over:
`NARRATION EXPLAINS EVENT -> DIALOGUE REPEATS IT -> SFX DECORATES IT`.

If sound already proves a fact, dialogue should normally change its use, interpretation or consequence rather than paraphrase it.

Critical information may be redundantly encoded for accessibility/mono safety, but redundancy must feel motivated by action.

## 5. FUNCTIONAL SILENCE LAW

Silence is an authored event only when it changes:
- attention;
- suspense;
- recognition;
- status;
- refusal;
- aftermath;
- relationship distance;
- listener inference.

Do not fill every pause with Foley, ambience swell or music.
Do not use silence as prestige decoration.

For audio production, a required silent reaction should be represented by the current `SILENT_REACTION_ANCHOR` / protected-silence system when applicable.

## 6. ASSEMBLED-PERFORMANCE LAW

A voice or performance is not approved because it wins in isolation.

For recurring leads/ensembles test:
1. isolated intelligibility/character truth;
2. direction-change responsiveness;
3. pair/ensemble distinction and chemistry/conflict;
4. long-form fatigue;
5. actual scene assembly with relevant acoustic context.

Voice beauty is subordinate to story function and relationship performance.
