## 1. LESSON ZERO — DIAGNOSTIC, NOT BULK REWRITE

### Objective
Determine whether the credibility concern is primarily source prose, localization, performance, or context-sensitive taste.

### Required sample
Build a representative 45–60 minute diagnostic corpus containing:
- early five-person interaction;
- Ethan/Aoife private scene including the investment-bank joke cluster;
- institutional Lesson Zero scene;
- one friendship conflict/boundary scene;
- one later high-stakes scene.

### Pass order
1. text-only 32-cycle mechanisms: evidence/perception, relationship shorthand, speech pattern matrix, house-style leakage;
2. properly directed exact-text audio;
3. Founder listener markers;
4. source/localization/performance triage;
5. only then minimal A/B patches for confirmed source-text defects.

### Forbidden
- whole-book rewrite from frequency counts;
- teen-slang injection;
- flattening intelligent characters;
- rewriting English source to solve weak Russian localization or TTS;
- treating one unusual metaphor as proof of global failure.
