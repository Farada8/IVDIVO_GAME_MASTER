# 02 — 32 PROMPTS — SEQUENTIALLY EXECUTED

**Status:** EXECUTED 32/32  
**Date:** 2026-08-21  
**Goal:** identify second-order causes of believable/unbelievable dialogue and convert them into testable IVDIVO production mechanisms.

---

## PROMPT 01 — WHAT DOES “NATURAL” ACTUALLY MEAN?
**Prompt:** Define the target quality without confusing believable fiction with transcription of ordinary conversation.

**Executed finding:** `SOURCE-DERIVED`. McKee and Truby converge: good dialogue is selective/compressed and may be more articulate than ordinary speech, but it must feel true to the fictional person, setting and moment. Therefore “more realistic” cannot mean adding filler, repetition and mundane chatter.

**Decision:** `KEEP + STRENGTHEN`. Use **FICTIONAL AUTHENTICITY** as the governing target.

---

## PROMPT 02 — DOES EVERY LINE DO SOMETHING?
**Prompt:** Test whether a line is verbal action or merely attractive language/information.

**Executed finding:** `SOURCE-DERIVED`. McKee treats speech as action generated by need/desire and expressed through tactic. A technically elegant line without an intended effect on listener/self is structurally suspicious.

**Decision:** `STRENGTHEN`. Require a playable action verb for important lines: test, deflect, corner, reassure, shame, recruit, hide, repair, delay, provoke, etc.

---

## PROMPT 03 — INNER DESIRE VS OUTER TACTIC
**Prompt:** Why can a line be logical yet still feel false?

**Executed finding:** `SOURCE-DERIVED + INFERENCE`. A line can make informational sense while failing motivational sense. Credibility rises when the outer verbal tactic can be traced to an inner need and present risk.

**Decision:** `ADD`. Introduce a **CAUSE-TO-LINE TRACE** for suspect beats: inner desire -> threat -> tactic -> wording.

---

## PROMPT 04 — EMPTY TALK
**Prompt:** Identify lines that contain story information but have no believable speaker motive.

**Executed finding:** `SOURCE-DERIVED`. McKee identifies empty talk as speech whose subtext offers no motive/cause. IVDIVO already checks exposition, but motive enforcement must operate line-by-line in high-risk scenes.

**Decision:** `STRENGTHEN`. Any exposition-bearing line without a speaker-caused action is `EMPTY_ACTION_RISK`.

---

## PROMPT 05 — OVERLY KNOWING TALK
**Prompt:** Detect characters who say more than they could reasonably know.

**Executed finding:** `SOURCE-DERIVED + CORPUS-RELEVANT`. Institutional/mystery stories are especially vulnerable because clean explanation can silently give speakers analyst-level knowledge. This can break both naturalism and clue causality.

**Decision:** `ADD`. Expand knowledge boundary into **EVIDENCE LADDER**: observed -> told -> inferred -> suspected -> unknown -> false belief.

---

## PROMPT 06 — OVERLY PERCEPTIVE TALK
**Prompt:** Detect characters who understand another person too quickly or too accurately.

**Executed finding:** `SOURCE-DERIVED`. Perfect psychological perception creates writer omniscience inside characters. Even emotionally intelligent people misread timing, motive and significance.

**Decision:** `ADD`. Require **PERCEPTION CONFIDENCE** and distinguish notice from interpretation.

---

## PROMPT 07 — EXPOSITION BURDEN
**Prompt:** When does necessary explanation become character-destroying speech?

**Executed finding:** `SOURCE-DERIVED`. Dialogue may carry exposition, but it should do so while acting. The danger is not factual density alone; it is information with no tactical reason.

**Decision:** `STRENGTHEN`. Tag factual lines with `WHY SAY NOW / WHAT EFFECT / WHY THIS LISTENER`.

---

## PROMPT 08 — IS DIALOGUE DOING STRUCTURE’S JOB?
**Prompt:** Test whether characters are verbally manufacturing a scene turn that should have been built through situation/action.

**Executed finding:** `SOURCE-DERIVED`. Truby explicitly warns against making dialogue do story structure’s heavy lifting. This explains some “writer talk”: a character is forced to verbalize a missing structural beat.

**Decision:** `ADD`. Create **DIALOGUE BURDEN CHECK**: if removing a speech destroys basic causality rather than only expression, inspect upstream scene architecture.

---

## PROMPT 09 — WHEN SHOULD SUBTEXT EXIST?
**Prompt:** Avoid both over-explanation and artificial constant indirectness.

**Executed finding:** `SOURCE-DERIVED`. Truby: subtext is not universally superior; it is caused by hidden desire, fear, pain, embarrassment or strategy. Therefore “add subtext” is too vague.

**Decision:** `STRENGTHEN`. Every major unsaid element requires a reason it cannot or should not be said directly.

---

## PROMPT 10 — HIDDEN DESIRE + INDIRECT PLAN
**Prompt:** How can subtext become executable rather than decorative?

**Executed finding:** `SOURCE-DERIVED`. Hidden desire becomes dramatic when paired with an indirect plan. This turns “what is unsaid?” into “what is the character trying to achieve without admitting the real objective?”

**Decision:** `ADD`. Use **HIDDEN-DESIRE / INDIRECT-PLAN PAIR** for romance, shame, authority and betrayal scenes.

---

## PROMPT 11 — VOCABULARY OWNERSHIP
**Prompt:** What is the most basic separability mechanism for character voice?

**Executed finding:** `SOURCE-DERIVED`. McKee explicitly roots dialogue originality in vocabulary. But vocabulary must arise from lived domains, not gimmick catchphrases.

**Decision:** `STRENGTHEN`. Speech Fingerprints must include source domains: work, family, hobbies, class, education, region, private obsessions, forbidden vocabulary.

---

## PROMPT 12 — SYNTAX / CADENCE OWNERSHIP
**Prompt:** Why can characters with different vocabulary still sound like one writer?

**Executed finding:** `CORPUS-DERIVED + INFERENCE`. LESSON ZERO risk is not only repeated words; multiple characters can share correction syntax, compact reversals and semantic jokes. Voice separability must include sentence shape and argument choreography.

**Decision:** `ADD`. Create **SPEECH PATTERN MATRIX** across speakers: sentence frames, correction forms, hedge habits, joke geometry, question type, response length.

---

## PROMPT 13 — KNOWLEDGE IS NOT PERCEPTION
**Prompt:** Separate what a character knows from what they notice in the current beat.

**Executed finding:** `INFERENCE`. A character may possess background knowledge but fail to retrieve/apply it under stress; conversely they may observe something without understanding it. Current knowledge cards need a perception layer.

**Decision:** `ADD`. Use `AVAILABLE KNOWLEDGE` vs `ACTIVE ATTENTION` vs `INTERPRETATION`.

---

## PROMPT 14 — LISTENING TRACE
**Prompt:** Does each reply actually respond to something the other person just did?

**Executed finding:** `SYSTEM-DERIVED + INFERENCE`. Current 24-pass stack already asks this, but it should be treated as a graph rather than a prose note. Writerly dialogue often responds to theme or reader knowledge instead of partner behavior.

**Decision:** `STRENGTHEN`. Mark each important reply with its trigger: word / gesture / silence / implication / misread.

---

## PROMPT 15 — MISUNDERSTANDING WITHOUT CONTRIVANCE
**Prompt:** How much misunderstanding should credible dialogue contain?

**Executed finding:** `INFERENCE`. Zero misunderstanding produces unnatural mind-reading; random misunderstanding creates soap-opera contrivance. The useful variable is **relationship-conditioned misreading**.

**Decision:** `ADD`. Build recurring `LIKELY MISREADS` per relationship, not random friction.

---

## PROMPT 16 — EMOTIONAL LATENCY
**Prompt:** Why do characters sometimes speak too perfectly too soon?

**Executed finding:** `CORPUS-DERIVED + INFERENCE`. Generative prose tends to convert emotional stimulus directly into articulate insight. Realistic fictional behavior often needs processing delay, substitution or action before verbal formulation.

**Decision:** `ADD`. Create **EMOTIONAL LATENCY REGISTER**: immediate / delayed / displaced / suppressed / privately processed.

---

## PROMPT 17 — POWER / STATUS / PERMISSION
**Prompt:** How does power alter what can be said, asked, interrupted or joked about?

**Executed finding:** `SYSTEM-DERIVED`. Existing Naturalism Engine includes status, but dialogue checks should model permission explicitly. The same sentence has different credibility under custody, employment, family dependence or romantic equality.

**Decision:** `STRENGTHEN`. Add `CAN ASK / CAN REFUSE / CAN LEAVE / CAN INTERRUPT / MUST MASK` to relationship state.

---

## PROMPT 18 — RELATIONSHIP SHORTHAND
**Prompt:** What should people with history no longer need to explain?

**Executed finding:** `CORPUS-DERIVED`. Strong LESSON ZERO moments work because shared knowledge allows compressed exchanges. Long relationships accumulate private references, known defenses and unfinished arguments.

**Decision:** `ADD`. Create **RELATIONSHIP SHORTHAND LEDGER**: mutually known facts, recurring arguments, private jokes, avoided subjects, gestures with established meaning.

---

## PROMPT 19 — BODY / NERVOUS SYSTEM CONSTRAINT
**Prompt:** Can a line be psychologically right but physiologically implausible?

**Executed finding:** `SYSTEM-DERIVED + INFERENCE`. Fatigue, pain, adrenaline and divided attention should affect timing and complexity. A character under acute load may still be articulate, but polished multi-clause reasoning requires justification.

**Decision:** `STRENGTHEN`. Compare line complexity to body/cognitive state in major pressure scenes.

---

## PROMPT 20 — SILENCE AS ACTION
**Prompt:** When is no line stronger and more believable than a line?

**Executed finding:** `SOURCE-DERIVED`. McKee treats silence as extreme language economy; wordless reaction can execute a turn. IVDIVO should not reward dialogue quantity.

**Decision:** `STRENGTHEN`. Include `SILENCE OPTION` at important beats; accept silence only if it changes another character’s behavior or scene state.

---

## PROMPT 21 — ACTION/REACTION CAUSALITY
**Prompt:** Can the dialogue beats be reordered without consequence?

**Executed finding:** `SOURCE-DERIVED`. Effective exchanges create action/reaction progression toward a turn. Reorderable lines indicate activity, exposition or banter detached from causality.

**Decision:** `ADD`. Use **BEAT DEPENDENCY TEST**: beat N+1 must be causally altered by N.

---

## PROMPT 22 — ENTER LATE / EXIT ON CHANGE
**Prompt:** Does false naturalism come from showing too much conversational runway?

**Executed finding:** `SOURCE-DERIVED`. Truby warns that starting early to seem realistic usually makes scenes dull. Naturalism is not chronology completeness.

**Decision:** `STRENGTHEN`. Scene dialogue should begin near pressure and exit after the meaningful value/tactic shift, unless aftermath itself is story action.

---

## PROMPT 23 — GROUP ATTENTION GRAPH
**Prompt:** Why do multi-character scenes often sound staged even when individual lines are good?

**Executed finding:** `INFERENCE + LIVE APPLICATION`. In groups, not everyone is attending to the same person or topic. People monitor alliances, doors, tasks, devices, status figures and private micro-signals. BLOODBOUND E07 already benefits when attention is distributed rather than every character answering the same speaker.

**Decision:** `ADD`. Build **GROUP ATTENTION GRAPH** for important 3+ character scenes.

---

## PROMPT 24 — HUMOR OWNERSHIP
**Prompt:** When does wit strengthen voice and when does it expose the author?

**Executed finding:** `CORPUS-DERIVED + INFERENCE`. Wit is credible when it belongs to a specific defense/style and costs/reveals something. Multiple characters using the same dry semantic reversal creates house-style convergence.

**Decision:** `ADD`. Each recurring character gets a humor mechanism and a `NOT THEIR HUMOR` boundary.

---

## PROMPT 25 — CLEVERNESS BUDGET
**Prompt:** Can individually good lines collectively damage belief?

**Executed finding:** `SYSTEM-DERIVED + CORPUS-DERIVED`. Yes. Current Naturalism Engine already flags clusters; BLOODBOUND E06 demonstrated a practical repair. The unit of failure is often a cluster, not one sentence.

**Decision:** `STRENGTHEN`. Review consecutive polished reversals and distribute intelligence through behavior, silence and asymmetry.

---

## PROMPT 26 — HOUSE-STYLE CONVERGENCE
**Prompt:** How do we distinguish author style from unacceptable speaker convergence?

**Executed finding:** `CORPUS-DERIVED + INFERENCE`. Narrator style may legitimately recur. Dialogue becomes risky when different characters repeatedly share the same rhetorical device, correction frame, metaphor family or argumentative rhythm without relationship/culture reason.

**Decision:** `ADD`. Separate **NARRATOR HOUSE STYLE** from **CROSS-SPEAKER LEAKAGE** in audits.

---

## PROMPT 27 — AGE / PROFESSION / CULTURE WITHOUT CARICATURE
**Prompt:** How should demographic/social context constrain speech?

**Executed finding:** `SYSTEM-DERIVED`. Age/profession/culture influence available vocabulary, reference domains, directness and status behavior, but “teen slang,” “scientist exposition,” or “trauma therapy speech” are stereotypes.

**Decision:** `STRENGTHEN`. Use capability/history-based plausibility: “would THIS person reach for these words now?”

---

## PROMPT 28 — INSTITUTIONAL LANGUAGE CONTAMINATION
**Prompt:** How do procedural worlds leak documentation syntax into everyone’s dialogue?

**Executed finding:** `CORPUS-DERIVED`. LESSON ZERO’s consent/access/provenance systems and BLOODBOUND’s registry/legal systems create a genuine risk. Technical vocabulary is legitimate only when role/tactic requires it; otherwise prose begins to sound like policy documents.

**Decision:** `ADD`. Introduce **INSTITUTIONAL REGISTER QUARANTINE**: who owns formal terms, who paraphrases, who resists, who misuses them, who avoids them.

---

## PROMPT 29 — COMPETENCE VS PERSONHOOD
**Prompt:** Why can highly competent characters still feel emotionally flat or interchangeable?

**Executed finding:** `INFERENCE + CORPUS-RELEVANT`. Competence dialogue often converges because everyone speaks in efficient problem-solving language. Personhood requires non-task stakes, private discomfort, status sensitivity, humor ownership, habits and misreads.

**Decision:** `ADD`. For competence scenes require at least one **PERSONHOOD SIGNAL** that affects behavior without derailing the task.

---

## PROMPT 30 — PAGE VS PERFORMANCE
**Prompt:** How do we prevent bad acting/TTS from causing unnecessary rewrites?

**Executed finding:** `SYSTEM-DERIVED + FOUNDER SIGNAL`. Current LESSON ZERO evidence is mixed; performance can make acceptable text sound artificial. Page and audio credibility must be diagnosed separately.

**Decision:** `KEEP + STRENGTHEN`. Two-channel validation remains mandatory before reopening development-complete prose.

---

## PROMPT 31 — TRANSLATION / LOCALIZATION DISTORTION
**Prompt:** Can a line feel false to a Russian-speaking listener because the translation/localization carries English rhetorical structure unnaturally?

**Executed finding:** `INFERENCE`. Yes, especially jokes, semantic corrections, register differences and sentence-final emphasis. Source-text, translation and acting are independent failure layers.

**Decision:** `ADD`. Use **TRI-LAYER DIAGNOSIS**: source wording / localization wording / performance.

---

## PROMPT 32 — WHAT IS THE MINIMUM SAFE REPAIR LOOP?
**Prompt:** Synthesize a fail-closed process that improves belief without flattening voice or reopening whole books.

**Executed finding:** `SYNTHESIS`. The correct loop is:
`HUMAN SIGNAL OR STATIC SIGNAL -> CLASSIFY EARLIEST FAILED LAYER -> CAUSE-TO-LINE TRACE -> SMALLEST A/B PATCH -> TEXT BLIND TEST -> PERFORMANCE/LOCALIZATION TEST IF RELEVANT -> SCENE REGRESSION -> CHAPTER/BOOK PATTERN REGRESSION -> ACCEPT / HOLD / ROLLBACK`.

**Decision:** `PROMOTE AS RESEARCH CONCLUSION`. No bulk rewrite from frequency counts, one disliked joke or flat TTS.

---

# 32/32 EXECUTION SUMMARY

### Findings that confirmed existing engine law
- fictional authenticity, not mundane transcription;
- dialogue as action;
- desire/tactic/subtext;
- knowledge boundaries;
- speaker-specific voice;
- silence and behavioral response;
- text-vs-performance separation;
- minimal repair and regression.

2. `EVIDENCE_LADDER + PERCEPTION_CONFIDENCE`.

5. `RELATIONSHIP_SHORTHAND_LEDGER + LIKELY_MISREADS`.
6. `SPEECH_PATTERN_MATRIX` beyond vocabulary.
7. `DIALOGUE_BURDEN_CHECK` against missing scene structure.
8. `BEAT_DEPENDENCY_TEST`.
9. `GROUP_ATTENTION_GRAPH`.
10. `HUMOR_OWNERSHIP + NOT_THEIR_HUMOR`.
11. `NARRATOR_STYLE vs CROSS_SPEAKER_LEAKAGE`.

### Overall conclusion
The next quality frontier is not “make dialogue more casual.” It is **increase causal resolution between human state and the exact next line while reducing cross-character rhetorical convergence**.
