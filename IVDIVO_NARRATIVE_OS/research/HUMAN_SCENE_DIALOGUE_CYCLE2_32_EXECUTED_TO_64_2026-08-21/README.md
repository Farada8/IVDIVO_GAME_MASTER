Cycle 1 created 32 executed research prompts and then 64 deeper prompts. Cycle 2 does not repeat that work. It takes **prompts 01–32 from the previous 64-prompt stack** and executes them on real evidence:

- `LESSON ZERO` forensic baseline + representative current manuscript scenes;

This is a **sample-based diagnostic/application cycle**, not a false claim that every line of LESSON ZERO or every future scene has been audited.

## Key discovery

The first Naturalism upgrade works, but does not eliminate house-style drift by itself.

`BLOODBOUND E06` caught and repaired one polished correction-cluster. Yet `BLOODBOUND E07`, while much stronger behaviorally, still contains a few compact correction ladders and moments of unusually fast mutual understanding. This proves that local scene cards are necessary but insufficient: the system also needs **corpus-level rhetoric control, epistemic calibration, attention budgets, latency tracking and power/exit geometry**.
