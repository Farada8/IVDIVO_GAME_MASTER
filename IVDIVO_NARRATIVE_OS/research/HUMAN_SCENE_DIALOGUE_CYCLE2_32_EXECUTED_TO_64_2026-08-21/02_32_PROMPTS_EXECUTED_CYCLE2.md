# 02 — 32 PROMPTS EXECUTED — CYCLE 2

**Status:** 32/32 EXECUTED  
**Execution basis:** LESSON ZERO representative corpus + preserved forensic baseline; BLOODBOUND E06/E07; McKee/Truby mechanisms; current IVDIVO Human Scene authority.  
**Important:** findings are evidence-bounded. No whole-book LESSON ZERO rewrite is implied.

---

## 01 — EVIDENCE LADDER PER LINE
**Execution.** Important assertions were tagged as OBSERVED / TOLD / REMEMBERED / INFERRED / SUSPECTED / BELIEVED / KNOWN / WRONG.

**Evidence.** BLOODBOUND E07 is strong here: Mira repeatedly separates `we have a wording change` from `we have intent`, and separates civil descent from active affinity. LESSON ZERO's Rowe/Ethan exchange also distinguishes Ethan's inference (`So it's a test`) from Rowe's actual statement.

**Finding.** The engine already protects mystery facts fairly well, but epistemic precision is often carried by polished correction dialogue. That can preserve logic while harming naturalness.

**Disposition:** STRENGTHEN.

**Engine delta:** certainty should be encoded upstream in an `EPISTEMIC STATE TAG`, not repeatedly explained through clever correction lines.

---

## 02 — PERCEPTION CONFIDENCE
**Execution.** Judgments about another person's internal state were checked for basis and disconfirming evidence.

**Evidence.** LESSON ZERO uses observational inference productively, but lines such as a POV character deciding another is `pretending not to watch` can read as fact unless the narrative voice clearly owns the inference. BLOODBOUND E07 contains `Nia noticed that she noticed it`, which is plausible but risks close-POV omniscience if treated as certainty.

**Finding.** The main failure mode is not wrong psychology; it is **unmarked confidence** in interpersonal inference.

**Disposition:** ADD.

**Engine delta:** `POV PERCEPTION FIREWALL` — every mental-state claim in close POV must be either observable, explicitly inferred, or narratively authorized.

---

## 03 — ACTIVE ATTENTION VS AVAILABLE KNOWLEDGE
**Execution.** Each scene was examined for facts characters technically know but would not actively retrieve under current load.

**Evidence.** LESSON ZERO's café sequence is strong: Maya attends to access, Ollie to technical requirements, Samir to Ethan's motive, Aoife to Ethan's decision. BLOODBOUND investigation scenes risk making all competent characters continuously available to all relevant evidence.

**Finding.** Competence can create an artificial `perfect RAM` effect.

**Disposition:** ADD.

**Engine delta:** `ATTENTION BUDGET` per character: task focus, threat focus, relationship focus, body/fatigue load, and ignored-but-known information.

---

## 04 — NOTICE / INTERPRET / ACT SPLIT
**Execution.** Key cues were decomposed into objective cue -> subjective interpretation -> action.

**Evidence.** BLOODBOUND E07: Tomas looks toward Rian; Mira waits instead of immediately naming motive. Strong. LESSON ZERO often uses physical observation before conversational interpretation.

**Finding.** This is one of the strongest realism mechanisms in both projects when actually used.

**Disposition:** KEEP + PROMOTE.

**Engine delta:** require one explicit `NOTICE / INTERPRET / ACT` chain for every high-stakes interpersonal turn.

---

## 05 — MEMORY ACCESS UNDER PRESSURE
**Execution.** Scenes were checked for instant, high-precision recall under stress.

**Evidence.** The reviewed BLOODBOUND E07 is archive/task work rather than acute crisis, so exact recall is not inherently suspicious. LESSON ZERO representative scenes do not provide enough fresh evidence to certify book-wide memory realism.

**Finding.** No proven current defect from the reviewed sample, but the risk is material for thriller/SMITH scenes.

**Disposition:** HOLD AS MANDATORY HIGH-STRESS TEST.

**Engine delta:** `RECALL FRICTION` field: retrieval delay, partial recall, confidence, contamination risk.

---

## 06 — FALSE BELIEF PRODUCTIVITY
**Execution.** Current false beliefs were tested for conversational consequences.

**Evidence.** LESSON ZERO: Ethan's status/badge hunger changes what he hears in the youth-module offer. BLOODBOUND: Rian's inherited `Crimson ended` family story shapes what he can accept and how quickly.

**Finding.** False beliefs are strongest when they alter **question choice, omission and interpretation**, not when merely listed in a character bible.

**Disposition:** PROMOTE.

**Engine delta:** `FALSE BELIEF -> LANGUAGE CONSEQUENCE` mapping for every major relationship arc.

---

## 07 — INFORMATION ASYMMETRY STRESS TEST
**Execution.** Shared assumptions were removed mentally to identify lines that rely on audience knowledge.

**Evidence.** LESSON ZERO Rowe/Ethan works because Rowe genuinely knows more and refuses premature explanation. BLOODBOUND E07 has legitimate task-based explanation, but institutional scenes remain at risk of participants explaining facts primarily for the audience.

**Finding.** Exposition quality depends on `who needs this now?`, not merely on whether the information is accurate.

**Disposition:** STRENGTHEN.

**Engine delta:** every exposition fact gets `OWNER / NEEDER / WHY NOW / COST OF NOT KNOWING`.

---

## 08 — MYSTERY-SAFE SPEECH AUDIT
**Execution.** Dialogue in BLOODBOUND was checked for conclusions ahead of evidence.

**Evidence.** Mira explicitly refuses to collapse descendants, affinity, intent and guilt into one conclusion. Block I gate already preserved that distinction.

**Finding.** Mystery-safety is currently strong. The risk is that repeated epistemic disclaimers become stylistically repetitive.

**Disposition:** KEEP, MOVE LOGIC UPSTREAM.

**Engine delta:** clue-order constraints belong in scene state so characters do not need to restate the same caution formula every episode.

## 09 — CAUSE-TO-LINE TRACE
**Execution.** Suspect lines were traced through stimulus -> meaning -> want -> risk -> tactic -> wording -> intended effect.

**Evidence.** LESSON ZERO positive control `Thanks / For what? / Nothing / Good` has a complete causal chain with face-saving as tactic. The `failing investment bank` joke has a plausible teasing tactic but weaker vocabulary provenance. BLOODBOUND E07 lines are usually motivated, but some semantic corrections appear more shaped by author rhythm than by immediate tactical necessity.

**Finding.** A good scene objective does not guarantee every individual line is caused.

**Disposition:** PROMOTE.

**Engine delta:** `CAUSE-TO-LINE TRACE` becomes mandatory for flagged/clever lines.

---

## 10 — LISTENING GRAPH
**Execution.** Major replies were attached to the exact preceding word/gesture/silence that caused them.

**Evidence.** LESSON ZERO café dialogue often works because Samir responds to Ethan's stated motive, not to theme. BLOODBOUND E07 has good action-response links, but occasional lines comment on the rhetorical quality of the previous line rather than the human problem.

**Finding.** `Writer listens to line` can replace `character listens to person`.

**Disposition:** ADD.

**Engine delta:** each major reply must have a `LIVE TRIGGER`; theme/outline is not a valid trigger.

---

## 11 — RESPONSE LATENCY PROFILE
**Execution.** Emotional responses were classified immediate / delayed / displaced / suppressed / action-before-words / private-processing.

**Evidence.** LESSON ZERO often allows Ethan to process rejection before choosing the youth module. BLOODBOUND E07 gives Rian silence after family-history destabilization and physical movement before speech.

**Finding.** Latency is a major credibility strength when present; generative drafting tends to collapse it under pressure to make every beat explicit.

**Disposition:** PROMOTE.

**Engine delta:** `EMOTIONAL LATENCY CURVE` across scene, not just isolated pauses.

---

## 12 — MISREAD SIGNATURE
**Execution.** Relationship-specific recurrent misreads were derived.

**Evidence.** LESSON ZERO's Ethan can read observation/protection as judgment or control. BLOODBOUND Mira is learning to distinguish Rian's patience from surveillance while legal custody makes both readings plausible.

**Finding.** Misreads should come from relationship history/power, not random misunderstanding.

**Disposition:** PROMOTE.

**Engine delta:** `MISREAD SIGNATURE` per important pair with evidence and disconfirming moments.

## 13 — REPAIR ATTEMPT DYNAMICS
**Execution.** Repair styles were compared.

**Evidence.** BLOODBOUND E07 has Mira explicitly say that she sounded as if she was finishing Rian's thought. This is psychologically plausible for a precise self-monitoring character, but unusually clean. LESSON ZERO often repairs through deflection, task or compressed acknowledgment.

**Finding.** Emotional literacy itself can become a house style.

**Disposition:** STRENGTHEN.

**Engine delta:** `REPAIR STYLE FINGERPRINT` — apology, joke, task-help, retreat, correction, silence, touch, gift, etc., plus forbidden repair modes.

## 14 — FAILED REPAIR
**Execution.** Scenes were tested for places where correct wording should not immediately restore trust.

**Evidence.** BLOODBOUND's custody inequality is too large to be repaired through one good sentence; later causal grid correctly requires Rian to relinquish authority. LESSON ZERO relationships also contain tensions that should survive verbal clarification.

**Finding.** Perfect sentences must not reset structural power/history.

**Disposition:** PROMOTE.

**Engine delta:** `REPAIR CEILING` — maximum relationship improvement possible before material action/consequence.

## 15 — ACTION BEFORE LANGUAGE
**Execution.** High-pressure beats were compared against physical-action alternatives.

**Evidence.** BLOODBOUND E07 uses Rian stepping closer, Tomas gripping paper, Mira putting down the pencil. LESSON ZERO forensic baseline identifies physical tasks as a strength.

**Finding.** Action-before-language is one of the most reliable antidotes to polished explanatory dialogue.

**Disposition:** PROMOTE.

**Engine delta:** high-risk emotional beats must test `DO FIRST / SAY SECOND / SAY NOTHING` variants.

---

## 16 — DELAYED MEANING PAYOFF
**Execution.** Candidate lines/gestures were checked for later reinterpretation potential.

**Evidence.** BLOODBOUND's restricted minutes, family phrases and classification language are positioned for later reinterpretation. The causal grid provides downstream proof, so the reinterpretation can remain fair-play.

**Finding.** Delayed meaning is powerful only when the original cue is sufficiently specific to feel earned in retrospect.

**Disposition:** KEEP + FORMALIZE.

**Engine delta:** `DELAYED MEANING LEDGER` linking first-heard meaning, hidden alternative, later evidence and payoff scene.

## 17 — SPEECH PATTERN MATRIX
**Execution.** Principal speech patterns were compared across correction forms, certainty, humor, questions and recurring frames.

**Evidence.** LESSON ZERO forensic baseline already found cross-character recurrence of `I know`, `That is...`, `apparently`, and correction/reframe syntax. BLOODBOUND E06 repaired one three-line cluster, yet E07 still contains a compact `Convenient / Normal / Those are not opposites` ladder and other polished semantic contrasts.

**Finding.** This is the strongest new cross-project result: **local Naturalism PASS does not guarantee book/season-level rhetorical separation**.

**Disposition:** PROMOTE TO MANDATORY CORPUS GATE.

**Engine delta:** `CORPUS RHETORICAL FINGERPRINT + HOUSE-STYLE COLLISION REPORT` per block/chapter set.

## 18 — REGISTER SWITCH TRIGGERS
**Execution.** Professional/intimate/defensive/public/family register switches were inspected.

**Evidence.** LESSON ZERO differentiates youth café from institutional scenes, but precision habits can follow Ethan across contexts. BLOODBOUND Mira remains highly forensic even during personal micro-beats; Rian remains operational/legal.

**Finding.** Voice identity requires not one fingerprint, but **conditional fingerprints by register**.

**Disposition:** ADD.

**Engine delta:** `REGISTER STATE MACHINE`: trigger -> allowed syntax/vocabulary -> forbidden carryover.

## 19 — VOCABULARY PROVENANCE
**Execution.** Unusual vocabulary was checked against life source.

**Evidence.** `failing investment bank` remains unresolved because Aoife's social tactic is plausible but the finance metaphor's personal provenance is weak in the current evidence. BLOODBOUND's legal/archive terms have clear occupational/institutional owners.

**Finding.** Vocabulary provenance is the cleanest way to distinguish unusual-but-owned speech from authorial cleverness.

**Disposition:** PROMOTE.

**Engine delta:** unusual line audit requires `WHERE DID THIS PERSON GET THIS LANGUAGE?`

## 20 — METAPHOR DOMAIN OWNERSHIP
**Execution.** Metaphor sources were mapped by speaker/narrator.

**Evidence.** LESSON ZERO has witty metaphor risk at narrator/character boundary. BLOODBOUND E07 uses paperwork/architecture metaphors around institutional truth; these fit the world but could become shared author language if many characters use them.

**Finding.** Thematic coherence can disguise voice convergence.

**Disposition:** ADD.

**Engine delta:** `METAPHOR DOMAIN LEDGER` per narrator and recurring speaker, with shared-cultural exceptions documented.

## 21 — SENTENCE-LENGTH UNDER PRESSURE
**Execution.** Calm vs pressured syntax was compared qualitatively.

**Evidence.** BLOODBOUND Mira does become terse in task pressure, a stated Block-I success. LESSON ZERO forensic evidence suggests characters can remain well-shaped even in tense exchanges.

**Finding.** Sentence length alone is insufficient; what matters is **stress deformation signature**: shorter, more formal, fragmented, faster, repetitive, silent, etc.

**Disposition:** STRENGTHEN.

**Engine delta:** `STRESS DEFORMATION PROFILE` rather than generic short-sentence rule.

## 22 — QUESTION STYLE IDENTITY

**Finding.** Question architecture is a strong speaker identifier and less superficial than catchphrases.

**Disposition:** PROMOTE.

**Finding.** Distinct disagreement mechanisms are mandatory to prevent all smart characters sounding like one editorial intelligence.

**Disposition:** PROMOTE.

## 24 — APOLOGY / FORGIVENESS LANGUAGE
**Execution.** Direct apology capacity and believable acceptance were tested.

**Evidence.** BLOODBOUND Mira's self-correction is clean and plausible but risks generic emotionally literate syntax if repeated. The later romance cannot be repaired by apology while Rian has custody authority.

**Finding.** Forgiveness language must be constrained by power and accumulated debt.

**Engine delta:** `APOLOGY BELIEVABILITY MATRIX`: what speaker can say, what listener believes, what material proof is still required.

## 25 — RELATIONSHIP SHORTHAND LEDGER
**Execution.** Mutually known facts/private references were checked for opportunities to say less.

**Evidence.** LESSON ZERO `Thanks / For what? / Nothing / Good` is the best positive control. BLOODBOUND Mira/Rian are early in relationship and therefore should have little true shorthand; premature fluency would be false intimacy.

**Finding.** Relationship shorthand must grow over time and can itself be tracked as arc evidence.

**Disposition:** PROMOTE.

**Engine delta:** `SHORTHAND AGE` — none / emerging / established / damaged / restored.

**Evidence.** BLOODBOUND's custody structure dominates all Mira/Rian dialogue: even helpful acts carry coercive context. LESSON ZERO friendships contain accumulated favors and social knowledge that affect how little needs to be said.

**Finding.** Relationship history changes semantic value of identical words.

**Disposition:** PROMOTE.

**Engine delta:** `RELATIONSHIP DEBT LEDGER` should feed scene state before dialogue generation.

## 27 — PERMISSION GEOMETRY
**Execution.** Who can ask/refuse/interrupt/leave/touch/accuse/command was mapped.

**Evidence.** BLOODBOUND is a strong test case: Rian has legal custody authority; the scripts correctly prohibit romantic consummation under that inequality. LESSON ZERO institutional scenes similarly have adult/youth access asymmetry.

**Finding.** Power must change not just plot decisions but sentence rights.

**Disposition:** PROMOTE TO HARD GATE.

**Engine delta:** `PERMISSION GEOMETRY` becomes required for custody, employment, medical, teacher/student, command and other asymmetrical scenes.

## 28 — STATUS REVERSAL MOMENT
**Execution.** Formal status and epistemic/emotional status were compared.

**Evidence.** In BLOODBOUND E07 Rian retains formal power while Mira often holds epistemic authority. The larger causal grid schedules a true material reversal only when he relinquishes custody/command.

**Finding.** Micro-status reversal can occur before formal reversal, but dialogue must not pretend the structural power is already gone.

**Disposition:** PROMOTE.

**Engine delta:** track `FORMAL / EPISTEMIC / EMOTIONAL / SOCIAL STATUS` separately.

## 29 — GROUP ATTENTION GRAPH

**Evidence.** LESSON ZERO café scene is strong: participants attend to different tasks and Ethan's decision differently. BLOODBOUND E07 has useful side attention (Nia sees Mira/Rian) but investigation scenes can drift toward orderly round-robin participation.

**Finding.** Ensemble realism depends on simultaneous unequal attention.

**Disposition:** PROMOTE.

**Engine delta:** `ENSEMBLE ATTENTION TOPOLOGY` with object/task/person targets and missed information.

## 30 — ALLIANCE MICRO-SIGNALS
**Execution.** Behavioral alliance cues were identified before explicit labels.

**Evidence.** BLOODBOUND: Rian refuses to answer for Tomas; Nia notices Mira/Rian and chooses not to tease/comment. LESSON ZERO friendships often show alliance through who lets whom answer.

**Evidence.** BLOODBOUND Mira/Rian cannot have an isolated pure romance because Council, family history and custody law are always in the room conceptually. LESSON ZERO Ethan/Aoife interactions are shaped by the wider peer group and cohort aspiration.

**Disposition:** PROMOTE.

## 32 — EXIT POWER
**Execution.** Who can end/leave/force continuation was examined.

**Evidence.** BLOODBOUND custody means Mira's freedom to leave is structurally constrained even when a scene does not mention it. That fact must affect concessions, safety and intimacy. LESSON ZERO café peers have much more symmetric exit power; Rowe controls institutional call/access boundaries.

**Finding.** Exit power is one of the simplest predictors of whether dialogue can feel coercive even without explicit threats.

**Disposition:** PROMOTE TO HARD SCENE FIELD.

**Engine delta:** `EXIT POWER / CONTINUATION POWER` required in Human Scene State Cards.
