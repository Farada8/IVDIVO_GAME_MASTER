## D. Craft/reference basis

### Robert McKee — `Dialogue: The Art of Verbal Action for Page, Stage, and Screen`
Library source.

Mechanisms used:
- target is **fictional authenticity**, not transcript-like chatter;
- every line is an action generated by need/desire/intention;
- action/reaction progression drives the scene;
- subtext is the invisible source of visible verbal action;
- character-specific vocabulary/style is mandatory;
- credibility failures include empty, overly knowing and overly perceptive talk;
- silence can outperform a line.

### John Truby — `The Anatomy of Story`
Library source.

Mechanisms used:
- scene structure must do structural work before dialogue;
- dialogue is selective language that feels possible, not ordinary raw talk;
- start scenes as late as possible without losing required structure;
- subtext is useful only when caused by desire/plan/fear/status, not as decoration;
- dialogue should not be forced to repair missing story structure.
