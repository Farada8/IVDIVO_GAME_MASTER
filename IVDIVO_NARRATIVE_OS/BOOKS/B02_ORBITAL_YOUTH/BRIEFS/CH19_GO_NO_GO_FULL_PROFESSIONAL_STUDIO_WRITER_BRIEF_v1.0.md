## SCENE 58 — TWO MODELS

Two defensible interpretations remain:

**Conservative:** small settling delays and early embodied/interface reports may still indicate a coupled instability that realistic mixed occupancy can amplify.

**Permissive:** under tested partial-opening conditions, the disputed fixed-system lag appears only when a specific transition overlap occurs; when that overlap is absent, observed fixed-system response stays within settling tolerance and does not propagate.

Ollie may say his analysis weakens/falsifies one proposed trigger model for the planned partial-opening profile. He may NOT say:
- Ring Six is safe;
- embodied reports are irrelevant;
- cause is proven;
- unrestricted habitation is justified.

Mara forces four explicit limits:
1. what was actually tested;
2. what was not tested;
3. what would falsify Ollie's current interpretation;
4. whether he can distinguish predictor from cause.

His analysis removes **one legitimate conservative objection**. It does not erase uncertainty.
