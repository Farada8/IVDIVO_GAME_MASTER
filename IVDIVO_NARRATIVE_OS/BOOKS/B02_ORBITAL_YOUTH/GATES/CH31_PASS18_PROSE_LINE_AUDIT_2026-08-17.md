## MEDIUM WATCH — REPEATED NEUTRAL LOOK GESTURE
A mechanical scan of the accepted Drive text found 29 uses of `looked` in the chapter, including 11 lines beginning `Ethan looked...` plus repeated `Aoife looked...` / `He looked...` constructions.

This is not a story problem and does not justify a full rewrite, but it is a real line-level pattern.

Why it matters:
- `looked` often functions as neutral conversational glue;
- repeated neutral glue can make otherwise distinct scenes share the same surface rhythm;
- Pass 18 specifically requires character-selected perception rather than repeated camera-direction beats.

Repair law:
- do NOT globally replace every `looked` with decorative synonyms;
- future drafts should often omit the beat entirely when dialogue already supplies the turn;
- when perception matters, replace neutral gaze with what Ethan actually selects/notices/compares;
- retain `looked` when physical orientation itself is causal.
