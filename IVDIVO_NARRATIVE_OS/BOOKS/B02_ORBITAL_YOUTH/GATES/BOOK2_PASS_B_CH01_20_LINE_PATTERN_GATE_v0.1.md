## 2. MANUSCRIPT-WIDE EARLY-BLOCK FINDING

A recurring authorial surface form appears across different POVs:

- `Not X. X.`
- `Not X. Not Y. Z.`
- `Not because X. Because Y.`
- `No X. No Y. No Z.`
- compact correction ladders such as `Not proof. Better than nothing.`

Because the same syntactic device occurs in Ollie, Aoife, Maya and Ethan narration, it cannot be treated as character voice alone.

A second concentration is neutral gaze glue:

- `X looked at Y.`
- `X looked at the screen/map/list.`
- `X looked away.`

Again, count alone is not proof. The strongest problem is repeated use where the object/action/dialogue already carries the turn.

By contrast, repeated `I know` frequently **is character-causal** and is often explicitly noticed by other characters. Preserve those cases unless local context proves redundancy.
