## PASS 19 MODEL EXTRACTED FROM CH32

### `SCHOOL ROUTE CHANGES BEFORE THE ROOM DOES`
A strong IVDIVO world-reveal principle:
a civilization becomes believable when subsystems change on different schedules.

This is particularly valuable for orbital civilization because high integration should NOT mean perfect synchrony.
