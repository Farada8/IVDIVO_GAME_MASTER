## FUNCTION

Aoife's first useful Dublin material is:
- a commuter complaining about a twelve-minute orbital delay;
- freight priority;
- dirty public transport;
- school-age kids fighting over a seat;
- a synthetic-bodied maintenance commuter bored enough to scroll messages;
- patched cable covers and maintenance work inside the futuristic staging system.

This establishes the core Orbital Youth world law early:
`FUTURE CIVILIZATION -> JOBS / DELAYS / FREIGHT / SCHOOL / MAINTENANCE / ORDINARY LIFE`.

The chapter gives Aoife a concrete truth/source problem before later Ring Six reporting:
- she debates whether an opening idea is hers or Ethan's influence;
- recording becomes restricted during transfer;
- her strongest embodied transition experience is not captured;
- she explicitly refuses to fake/rebuild the missing sound or borrow somebody else's account as if she captured it.

This causally prepares later source/consent/evidence discipline rather than introducing it only when plot needs it.

## RELATIONSHIP SETUP
Aoife/Ethan already contain:
- attraction/attention;
- irritation at unsolicited optimization;
- real help that is still intrusive;
- unfinished argument producing emotional pull.

This supports later trust/relationship arc without making it start at Ring Six crisis.

## WORLD REVEAL
Earth/orbital integration is shown through Dublin infrastructure and transit, not a lore lecture.
