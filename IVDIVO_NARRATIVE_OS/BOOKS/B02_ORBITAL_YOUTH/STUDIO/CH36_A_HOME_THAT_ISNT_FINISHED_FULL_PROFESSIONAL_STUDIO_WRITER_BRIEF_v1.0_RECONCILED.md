`PROFESSIONAL CONSEQUENCE -> HOME/CIVIC CONSEQUENCE -> FIVE LIVES / RELATIONSHIPS -> SMALL SERIES HOOK`.

Four braided scenes.

## SCENE A — GOOD ENOUGH TO WORK ON

**POV anchor:** Ollie.  
**Time:** several days after Ch35; enough time for adult reconstruction to produce a bounded operating/redesign disposition.

### Want
Ollie wants to know what his actual future role is after the incident without being either expelled from the work or promoted beyond sense.

### Resistance
Formal process keeps his unrestricted commissioning access on REVIEW HOLD. The event justified one action; it did not erase prior access restrictions or finish validation.

### Durable institutional outcome
Show through a credential/task update, not a speech:
- `REVIEW HOLD — UNRESTRICTED COMMISSIONING ACCESS NOT RESTORED` remains;
- new bounded authorization: `R3 RECOVERY REDESIGN / REVIEW CONTRIBUTOR / READ-ONLY TEST ACCESS` or equivalent;
- R3-S retained as a manual contingency in narrowed/reviewed form pending validation;
- state-aware automatic control remains under test;
- safe occupied segments have stable operating rules;
- unresolved transition paths have formal redesign/revalidation work orders.

### Iri / work payoff
Iri has a real maintenance/redesign issue: route-side behavior or a revised service sequence must be checked against how people/work actually move.

Mechanism:
`Iri asks for specific help -> Ollie proposes too much / Iri narrows task -> Ollie respects boundary -> they keep working.`

No forgiveness speech. A later practical invitation is enough.

### Character payoff
Ollie accepts legitimate narrow responsibility. He does not try to turn the incident into ownership of the whole redesign.

Exit: he leaves the work at a reasonable time because another part of life is waiting.

## SCENE B — ROOM 3-177

**POV:** may stay Ollie-observational or shift briefly to civic/household action; no gratitude scene.

### Want
Household 3-177 wants actual access to the room already assigned to them.

### Adult-cleared operational outcome
After reconstruction, Segment Three does NOT return to original normal transition profile. A conservative corrected inbound procedure permits a bounded reopening / handoff under deeper recovery holds and lower throughput.

Household 3-177 receives access because they retain their existing queue position from Ch35, not because Ollie intervenes.

### Physical home payoff
Use:
`credential -> door -> bag/food/school item/laundry/bed -> immediate mundane complaint`.

Possible mundane problem:
- storage shelf blocks school bag;
- room lighting is irritating;
- food container was delivered to wrong locker;
- laundry booking belongs to temporary address and has to be fixed.

They do not thank Ollie.

If Ollie sees them, they may acknowledge him neutrally or not at all. The civic result is the reward.

### Wider household signal
Show another household still waiting or moving under ordinary allocation rules so the book does not imply universal happy ending.

Lea's household may remain partly waiting / on revised plan. Do not magically fix all housing.

## SCENE C — FIVE PEOPLE, DIFFERENT LIVES

**POV:** controlled ensemble / Ollie anchor.

The five try to have the meal / gathering they have repeatedly failed to schedule.

### Resistance
They now genuinely have different orbital lives:
- Samir has Crossband practice or match preparation;
- Maya has local friends / mobility plan / a small Lea-related practical errand;
- Aoife has a bounded edit/source obligation;
- Ethan has later adult-track intake / placement preparation;
- Ollie has redesign shift.

Original time fails.

Ethan's old reflex is to optimize the group. He catches himself and asks for actual availability instead.

They negotiate an imperfect later overlap.

### Individual payoffs through behavior
**Ethan:** later intake is real; the lost preferred Ch31 placement is NOT refunded. He has a different concrete assignment/process, less ideal but legitimate.

**Aoife:** finishes/releases a smaller consent-safe work or chooses a bounded final cut. The S1/source-approved banal line may be used only within agreed context, or Aoife may decide the piece works without exploiting more. No fame reward.

**Maya:** Ch24 autonomy protocol functions boringly in background. Lea sends/asks one practical thing; Maya does exactly that and nothing extra. Trust functional, not repaired.

**Samir:** arrives from sport, talks/acts like someone with a real sport/social life; his technical-data boundaries remain intact. Do not make him a backup engineer in the resolution.

**Ollie:** chooses the gathering when the competing technical question is non-urgent. He can answer Iri/work later.

### Relationship payoff
Friendship survives because they choose an overlap; they are not synchronized by plot.

Ethan/Aoife may be warmer through earlier honesty, but no forced couple lock.

No arc-summary speeches, no toast, no perfect five-way dialogue.

### Emotional closing image before hook
Something ordinary and orbital: bad food, someone arriving late from a different gravity zone, school/work bag under a table, Crossband gear, route notice, missed transport, etc.

End the emotional book here before widening saga horizon.

## SCENE D — SERIES HOOK / OLD EARTH RESEMBLANCE

**Only after Scenes A–C have clearly resolved Book Two.**

### Mechanism
Routine post-incident historical/pattern crosscheck returns one old Earth archive object.

Preferred evidence object:
- a pre-Confederation Old Earth Security technical/containment diagram or scanned archive page;
- it shows a **structural relation** resembling the resolved Ring Six residual-return / false-clear problem;
- old terminology differs;
- access/context is incomplete;
- no proof of causation or shared source.

Possible minimal old note:
`DISPLAY CLEAR IS NOT RETURN CLEAR.`
Or equivalent older terminology.

Do not over-explain the archive, organization or cosmology.

### Ceiling
Allowed:
`modern resolved event -> old Earth archive structural resemblance -> one new question`.

Forbidden:
- Root/Matrix proof;
- mastermind;
- Confederation conspiracy;
- ancient civilization lore dump;
- new Ring Six emergency;
- Smith arriving to explain everything;
- overturning Book Two resolution.

The hook may route the archive toward Old Earth Security / a Smith-adjacent office, but must stay evidence-grade.
