## CHAPTER JOB

Create a technically convincing false victory.

Ollie must build a compact transition-state model that genuinely fits the known Ring Six lag events well enough for competent adults to authorize a bounded test. The model is not fake, foolish or obviously wrong.

Its weakness is produced by Ollie’s evidence hierarchy: resident/body/personal-support observations remain visible but secondary because they are heterogeneous, privacy-limited and not yet a common measurement class.

The reader should understand why Ollie is right to be excited and why Iri/Mara are right not to let the unresolved evidence disappear.
