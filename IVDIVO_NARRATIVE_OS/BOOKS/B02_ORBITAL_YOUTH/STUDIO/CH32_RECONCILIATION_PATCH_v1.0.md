## SCENE ENGINE

### S98 — JUST CARRY IT
Maya wants to improve things with Lea during move preparation.
Lea does not accept a second apology speech and does not offer restored trust.
Lea gives Maya one concrete useful task involving bags / school gear / a late transfer.
Boundary: help with the task; do not solve Lea's household or use familiarity to push allocation.
Turn: Maya accepts a smaller role than the one she wants.

### S99 — THE WRONG HELP
A route/transfer complication appears during ordinary relocation preparation.
Maya sees a clever workaround and begins treating it as the answer.
The workaround would require Lea's household to accept a trade they have not chosen — split timing, extra move, changed school/work route or similar.
Lea stops her: Maya was asked to help, not redesign what the household should tolerate.
Maya does not defend herself into correctness. She asks what Lea actually wants done.
Turn: competence becomes service rather than control.

### S100 — THE LATE ROUTE
Maya's own social plan changes while she is still helping.
The existing Ch24 autonomy protocol is used once, briefly, in background: truthful update before a material change; no live tracking; no new negotiation scene.
Nothing dramatic happens.
Because Maya now has legitimate late mobility and local route knowledge, relocation staff can use her in a bounded youth-support role: route accompaniment / signage reality check / handoff assistance, not safety command.
Lea may give Maya one further practical request or message first. This is the relationship payoff: functional trust, not forgiveness.
Exit: Maya enters Ch33 migration with real route competence and an imperfect but active friendship boundary.

Lea does not become moral judge. Her grievance remains personal: Maya previously made Lea responsible for keeping Maya's version true.
