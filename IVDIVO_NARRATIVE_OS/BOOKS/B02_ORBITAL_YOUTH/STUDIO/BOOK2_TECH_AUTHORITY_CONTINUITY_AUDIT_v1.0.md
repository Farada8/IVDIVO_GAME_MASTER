# 2. AUTHORITY LADDER — GREEN STRONG

The authority progression is causally clean:

`participant / apprentice`
→ `bounded model contribution`
→ `bounded test influence`
→ `failure / shutdown`
→ `REVIEW HOLD / restricted access`
→ `de-identified model support`
→ `adult-owned reproduction participation`
→ `civic consequence input without decision power`
→ `segment model support`
→ `read-only migration watch + STOP REQUEST`
→ `temporary adult-authorized R3-S secondary fail-safe`
→ `one local irreversible safe-state action`
→ `post-climax bounded redesign/review contributor`.

Crucially, the climax grants Ollie **less general power than a generic YA hero fantasy would** and more specific responsibility.

**Global line-pass law:** later chapters should not repeatedly explain this ladder in dialogue. Once earned, show it through permissions/actions/interfaces.

# 3. PRIVACY / DATA CHAIN — GREEN STRONG

The book consistently distinguishes:
- observation;
- operational safety data;
- identity/source data;
- publication rights;
- biometric/personal-interface data;
- sport/social data;
- de-identified event order.

Protected examples:
- Aoife's S1/source relationship cannot be converted into unrestricted evidence access;
- Ch22 safety correction does not restore source intimacy/publication rights;
- Ch23 evidence remains de-identified and bounded;
- Samir refuses capture/extraction and still contributes one smaller self-owned evidence object;
- Ollie's later model never secretly receives forbidden raw data.

**Verdict:** privacy is story mechanism, not decorative ethics speech.

# 4. REPORT / SENSOR ONTOLOGY — GREEN STRONG

The book avoids the dangerous shortcut `people feel it first, therefore people are magical perfect sensors`.

Accepted progression:
- early embodied/interface reports appear before ontology is known;
- some later events show reports, others do not;
- report timing stays separate from control-state inference;
- negative/no-report cases remain visible;
- Iri reports personal sensation only and cannot interpret system truth;
- R3-S arms from operational variables, not reports;
- Ch35 confirmation validates the action/event, not every report.

**Protect this globally.** Any earlier prose that says or strongly implies `the body knew first` as fact should be checked against this later evidence ceiling.

# 5. MAJOR WATCH — HOW MUCH MYSTERY DOES THE BOOK PROMISE TO SOLVE?

The accepted ending deliberately resolves the **commissioning problem** without claiming a complete universal mechanistic/root-cause theory.

This is intellectually sound.

The Reader Advocate risk is contractual:
- if early/mid book frames Ring Six primarily as `What is the hidden cause?`, readers may expect complete answer;
- if it frames Ring Six as `Can young people and adults learn enough to let real people live here safely without pretending certainty?`, the partial mechanistic endpoint is satisfying.

**Severity:** MAJOR WATCH, not confirmed MAJOR.

**Continuous-read test:** after Ch20, what exact question does prose teach the reader to wait for?

Desired question by Ch26–28:
`What operating relation is real enough to act on, and what are we allowed to do with incomplete knowledge?`

Dangerous question:
`What is the single true hidden cause of Ring Six?`

If the latter dominates, the repair is not to invent a deeper root cause. The minimum repair is to recalibrate mystery language earlier so the book promises an operational/epistemic answer rather than total ontology.

# 7. TECHNICAL SATISFACTION TEST

By the end, the reader should be able to understand at a non-engineering level:

1. the old system could show/assume recovery before the physical system was fully safe for the next transition;
2. the clean early model treated visible/equivalent sequences too simply;
3. live multi-path/load conditions exposed the mistake;
4. a residual recovery/shared-loop state relation could be reproduced under bounded tests;
5. this relation did not equal a complete cause of every dangerous event;
6. conservative holds/segmentation could let some people live safely now;
7. R3-S used a bounded predictive envelope to stop one local sequence before the older automatic threshold;
8. later evidence justified that early stop for the event;
9. the correct outcome was partial safe occupation + redesign, not pretending full success.

If a general SF reader cannot state something close to this after continuous read, the problem is clarity/compression—not missing lore.
