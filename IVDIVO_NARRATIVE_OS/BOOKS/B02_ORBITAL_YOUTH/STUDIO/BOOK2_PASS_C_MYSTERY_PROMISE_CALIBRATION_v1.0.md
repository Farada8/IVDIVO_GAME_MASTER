# 3. Ch20 — THE WRONG CAUSE

The title is the strongest potential overpromise and therefore the main calibration watch.

What the chapter actually does:
- all individually approved restrictions are followed;
- multiple allowed states combine into a distributed equivalent transition;
- embodied/interface reports lead the fixed threshold;
- Mara/adults pre-stop under reduced condition;
- fixed system later confirms the problem;
- residents are displaced again and Tomas is injured;
- Ollie's model authority breaks.

The reveal is **a missing causal class**, not a hidden defective part.

`Wrong cause` therefore means the first model classified the dangerous situation too narrowly. It does not obligate the book to reveal one final molecular/hardware cause.

### Watch
Because the title contains `cause`, a reader could briefly expect `the right cause` later. Subsequent chapters must continue making the epistemic ceiling explicit. They do.

No title change is justified without external-reader evidence.

# 10. RED TEAM

**REJECTED.**

The text repeatedly qualifies cause-language with `not proof`, `not naming causes yet`, negative cases, failed C2, candidate necessary condition, not sufficient, not complete root cause.

## Objection B — `Ch20 title THE WRONG CAUSE demands THE RIGHT CAUSE later.`
**WATCH / NONBLOCKING.**

The later second-model/control-reproduction sequence supplies a better causal class/state model, though not universal root cause. In context the title describes misclassification of the trigger class. Do not rename absent reader evidence.

## Objection C — `Technical mystery is unresolved because unsafe sections remain closed.`
**REJECTED.**

Unsafe sections remaining closed is a consequence of honest incomplete commissioning, not missing story closure. Main conflict closes through safe partial habitation + redesign path.

## Objection D — `Climax validates only one event, too little payoff.`
**REJECTED.**

Event-specific validation is precisely what permits Ollie's bounded action to be morally/technically credible. Universal validation would contradict the book's evidence discipline and adult-competence law.

## Objection E — `Readers may still want more ontology.`
**TRUE as possible curiosity, not a demonstrated defect.**

Curiosity can remain after story closure. Do not turn it into explanatory franchise lore.
