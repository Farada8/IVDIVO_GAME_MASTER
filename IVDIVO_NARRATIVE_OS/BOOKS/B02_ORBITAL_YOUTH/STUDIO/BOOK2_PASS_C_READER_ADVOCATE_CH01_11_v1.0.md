# Ch01 — THE LAST GROUND

## Why turn the page?
The five are about to leave Earth together but are already being separated by real Vela commitments.

The chapter gives each young person a private future before the transport leaves:
- Ollie chooses maintenance/commissioning and is privately stung that the Earth rover team can move on without him;
- Ethan is drawn toward adult responsibility/control;
- Aoife has documentary ambition and framing instincts;
- Maya is already fighting family/visibility/social control;
- Samir carries sport as an independent life.

## Emotional wait
**Will they still function as the same group once nobody organizes their lives around one another?**

## Ordinary-life / wonder
Food, luggage, group chat, equipment, schedules, family messages and departure sit beside the scale of leaving ground.

## Homework risk
LOW. Worldbuilding is attached to choices.

## Reader verdict
GREEN. The hook is not `mysterious system failure`; it is `five young people leave the life that kept them synchronized`.

# Ch02 — DUBLIN VERTICAL

## Why turn the page?
Aoife wants to capture the future and immediately discovers the future is a commuter swearing about freight priority.

The chapter gives a strong world-reveal principle on page:
- extraordinary infrastructure;
- ordinary delay;
- old stone/new transfer systems;
- synthetic-bodied commuter treated as normal;
- recorder restrictions;
- body-level transition to a new down.

## Emotional wait
Two simultaneous waits:
1. what does the Vertical actually feel like?
2. can Aoife experience anything without turning it into material?

The strongest transition is deliberately unrecorded. She chooses not to fake it later.

## Relationship pressure
Aoife/Ethan attraction-conflict is active through help/control/authorship rather than romantic exposition.

## Homework risk
LOW/MEDIUM only because the chapter contains substantial infrastructure texture. It survives because Aoife's recorder desire provides selection pressure.

## Reader verdict
GREEN STRONG. Wonder is earned through bodily experience rather than tourism narration.

# Ch05 — FIRST SHIFT

## Why turn the page?
Ollie finally gets the real work he wanted—and it is inventory.

The first suspense object is deliberately tiny: Rev B physical tool inside Rev C custody record. The chapter teaches the book's epistemic rule through action: boring layers matter; records can agree with themselves and still be wrong.

## Character wait
**Will Ollie earn deeper access by respecting boring work, or treat first-day limits as beneath him?**

## Payoff
He catches the mismatch, selects UNKNOWN rather than inventing cause, receives one additional narrow permission layer.

## Youth/work life
Strong apprentice teasing, break messages, Mara lunch neglect, Iri workshop frustration, schedule interference.

## Homework risk
MEDIUM. This is first chapter where procedure occupies substantial page space.

Why it survives:
- every technical object is tied to status/access;
- Ollie wants the crawler but gets inventory;
- group chat and Iri make work socially legible;
- the discovered mismatch has clear physical consequence.

## Reader verdict
GREEN, but it opens the Ch05–07 density watch.

Then the first weak mismatch appears under realistic use:

- eleven-second relation remains weak/ambiguous.

## Emotional wait
Two good waits coexist:

## Homework risk

## Reader verdict

# Ch07 — STATIC SAFE

## Why turn the page?
Every subsystem passes. Clean tests refuse to reproduce the occupied-state mismatch.

The question changes from `which component is broken?` to `what did real people do that the test removed?`

## Strong reader mechanism
- static tests boringly normal;
- Iri argues that people do not live like load simulators;
- controlled movement nearly reproduces the timing relation;
- changing person/movement order breaks it again;
- local regulator timing can lead fixed return but remains `Not proof`;
- Ollie realizes logs record doors/counts/loads, not socially meaningful movement sequence.

## Emotional wait
**Can Ollie investigate without turning residents/bodies into tracking instruments?**

## Homework risk
This is the highest technical-onboarding risk in Ch01–11.

Why it remains below repair threshold:
- the test repeatedly fails to give Ollie the answer he wants;
- Tomas bag joke, Nadia calendar pressure, Iri privacy boundary and lunch keep people present;
- the chapter ends with a new human problem (`Need better timing source`), not a solved equation.

## Reader verdict
GREEN / WATCH TECHNICAL DENSITY, no patch.
