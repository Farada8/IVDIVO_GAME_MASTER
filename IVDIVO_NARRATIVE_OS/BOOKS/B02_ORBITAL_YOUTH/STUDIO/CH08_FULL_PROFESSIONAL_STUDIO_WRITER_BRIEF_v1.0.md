# 3. SPORT — WORKING DESIGN

## Working name
**CROSSBAND — OPTION, NOT CANON.**

Do not lock the name if prose finds a better local term.

## Physical reason it is orbital
Played in a long radial recreation volume spanning three effective-gravity bands.

- inner band: low effective gravity, hand/rail movement;
- middle transition: momentum management and controlled handoffs;
- outer band: near-residential gravity, running/catching/braking.

The same pass changes behavior depending on where receiver and object are in the radial field.

## Basic play
Two small teams move one soft inertial ball/loop through a sequence of gates.

A score requires:
1. controlled possession in one band;
2. legal handoff across a band boundary;
3. receiver completes the route through a different-gravity scoring gate.

No combat.
No magical field.
No “zero-g Quidditch.”

## Why Samir is bad at first
Earth reflexes make him:
- push too hard in low-g;
- brake too late entering outer band;
- watch the object instead of the receiver’s momentum state.

## Why Samir gets one good read
His existing pattern attention lets him time a handoff to where a player **will become catch-capable**, not where they currently are.

It is one play, not instant mastery.

One ordinary player uses a complete artificial body.

Show only:

- ordinary teasing/competition.

Do NOT explain:
- consciousness transfer;

No anomaly may be blamed on the synthetic body.

# 5. RESOURCE FRICTION

Practice volume is scarce.

The team has a fixed booking window.

Consequences:
- late arrival costs real play time;
- equipment setup is fast and social;
- coach/captain does not pause for Earth visitor orientation;
- Samir learns in motion;
- losing future practice slots matters later.

This makes recreation part of city economy, not decorative abundance.

# 7. SCENE 23 — SAY IT ONCE

## Want
Stay without becoming “the profile kid.”

## Social resistance
- one teammate is curious;
- another tries to help/protect him;
- one person is simply annoyed because practice time is being wasted.

No bully required.

## Dialogue strategy
Keep the boundary short.

Possible functional shape, not locked dialogue:
- “Are you the—”
- “Yeah. I’m not talking about the profile stuff here.”
- somebody starts to explain for him;
- “I can answer myself.”
- awkward beat;
- someone asks if he is always this bad at the outer turn.

Then play resumes.

## Turn
Teasing becomes about the sport rather than his history.

## Change
He stays.

# 8. SCENE 24 — SEQUENCE

## Situation

Samir’s body is now primed to notice:
- order;
- spacing;
- reversal;
- who crosses before whom;
because that is what the sport forced him to track.

## Observation
A small environment-transition correction appears after a particular crossing/reversal sequence.

He does **not** know it is Ring Six.

## Message to Ollie
Send:
- location/route;
- approximate time;
- order/spacing;
- what visibly changed.

Do not send:
- names;
- team chat;
- body identity;
- theory.

## Ollie response
Asks for timing/order, not identity.

## Samir boundary
When Ollie asks whether the team can repeat/provide logs:
Samir refuses to turn teammates into test subjects.

He may offer only what is already his to report.

## Exit
Not a technical cliffhanger.
End on Samir returning to host conditions / team chat / sore body / next practice booking, with the observation unresolved.
