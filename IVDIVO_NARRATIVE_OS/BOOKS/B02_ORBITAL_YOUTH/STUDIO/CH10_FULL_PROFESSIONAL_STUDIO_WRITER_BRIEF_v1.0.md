# EXECUTIVE FUNCTION

Chapter 10 is the deliberate mode-switch after three increasingly technical/work-centered chapters.

It must prove that Book Two is about **young people living in orbital civilization**, not only diagnosing Ring Six.

Maya must:
1. win genuine, reasonable late-night autonomy;
2. experience a local youth night that is worth wanting for its own sake;
3. encounter an ordinary change of plan;
4. use technically true language to hide the practical truth;
5. turn privacy into concealment by choice;
6. suffer **no immediate punishment**.

The later route/environment closure creates the cost. Do not pre-pay that consequence here.

# STORY / CAUSAL SCENES

## SCENE 28 — THE DEAL

**Maya wants:** late youth event without live tracking or escort.  
**Host wants:** enough bounded information to respond if transport/environment conditions change.  
**Resistance:** the host asks for one scheduled check-in.  
**Maya action:** negotiates permitted zones, Lea as local contact, latest return condition, approved backup sleep location, no location stream, no escort, condition-based messaging only.  
**Turn:** Maya gets most of what she wants.  
**Critical causal lock:** later concealment cannot be blamed on unreasonable surveillance.

Host is practical, not villainous and not a policy lecturer.

## SCENE 29 — NORMAL ENOUGH

**Maya wants:** to be treated as part of a local peer group rather than a Contact/Yout Cohort visitor.  
**Pressure:** planet-born body habits, clothes, route knowledge, teasing, food, status, attraction, family messages.  
**Lea:** principal local friendship/gatekeeper; NOT automatic romance line.  
**Attraction:** may attach to Davi/another peer elsewhere in Lea's group. Keep light, specific, interruptive.  
**Local imperfection:** locals may be wrong, awkward or show off; no orbit-born perfection caste.  
**Turn:** group decides to stay later and sleep at Arel's, a safe place inside the permitted zone but outside Maya's agreed sleep plan.

No plot briefing. No Ring Six technical exposition.

## SCENE 30 — SAFE IS NOT THE SAME AS KNOWN

**Maya wants:** preserve the feeling of unmonitored freedom.  
**Host asks:** only whether the agreed return condition changed.  
**Maya action:** answers with facts that are literally true — she is with Lea, still in Zone Four, no hazardous route/environment change — while omitting that she intends to sleep at Arel's rather than the approved backup household.  
**Lea pressure:** objects personally because Maya is turning her into the person whose presence makes the misleading answer plausible.  
**Maya self-justification:** she did not technically lie. Do NOT state the lesson.  
**Turn:** Maya mutes the host thread. Privacy has become concealment.  
**Exit:** the night remains fun.
