**RESOLUTION:** she completes a dull relocation-support shift, signs off, and the work beacon actually stops. Later an ordinary social plan changes and she uses the already-existing Ch24 personal host update—not the work system. Nothing dramatic happens.  
**NEXT HOOK:** Ch33 migration begins with Maya now credible as a route-support participant because she followed a role protocol under real conditions, not because she gave up privacy.

**Want:** make the mobile role compatible with actual privacy.  
**Action:** Maya asks concrete questions while being issued straps, moving carts and correcting a wrong route label:

- what is retained?

- ordinary operational audit/incident retention only, no reusable personal movement profile;
- host household receives no live feed;
- automatic end at sign-off, with visible status confirmation;
- Maya can decline future mobile shifts and take static work instead.

**Turn:** Maya signs on.
