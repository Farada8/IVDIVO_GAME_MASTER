The chapter must simultaneously move:
- Aoife’s documentary/consent practice;
- Ethan/Aoife attraction and small jealousy;
- Samir’s independent sport/community belonging;

- Iri’s off-the-clock performance identity;

- the Ring Six A-story indirectly through real crowd movement that later matters.

No catastrophe during Vela Night.
