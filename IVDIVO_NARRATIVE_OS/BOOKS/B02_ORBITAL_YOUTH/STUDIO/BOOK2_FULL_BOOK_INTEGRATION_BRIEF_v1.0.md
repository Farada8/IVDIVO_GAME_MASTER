## SEVERITY STANDARD

**FATAL:** broken main causality, impossible hero motivation, missing climax/resolution, major Book1/canon contradiction.

**MAJOR:** technical plot overwhelms emotional story, Ollie loses protagonist role, midbook repetition, fake price, unearned climax authority, ensemble subplot detaches from main novel, housing desire disappears, ending fails to pay opening.

**MEDIUM:** slow chapter cluster, repeated dialogue function, redundant evidence explanation, weak transition/payoff, tonal monotony.

**POLISH:** syntax, repeated gestures, exact word choice, minor world clarity.

## REPAIR LAW

No endless rewrite.

For each identified problem produce:
`problem -> evidence across chapters -> severity -> minimum effective repair -> chapters touched -> continuity risk -> expected reader benefit`.

Prefer:
- cut/merge repeated function;
- move one setup/payoff;
- strengthen one personal consequence;
- replace exposition with ordinary action;
- clarify authority once and trust reader later;
- add one relationship beat only when a real missing causal bridge exists.

Avoid rewriting whole chapters that are already doing their job.
