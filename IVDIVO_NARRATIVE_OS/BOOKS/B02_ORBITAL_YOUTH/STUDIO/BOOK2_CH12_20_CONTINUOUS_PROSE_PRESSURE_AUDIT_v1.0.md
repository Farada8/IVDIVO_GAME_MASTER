# READER PRESSURE LEDGER

## CH12 — THE CLEAN MODEL
**Opening question:** can Ollie reduce the anomaly into a model that earns a real test?  
**Immediate want:** produce a usable state-sequence model.  
**Turn:** four apparently different events collapse into one repeatable transition order.  
**Resistance:** embodied/interface reports consistently lead fixed readings but cannot yet be treated as one measurement class.  
**Emotional stake:** Ollie wants professional legitimacy; Iri sees the blind spot inside the achievement.  
**Ordinary-life counterweight:** apprentice teasing, Iri qualification result, Vela Night leaking into the service bay, missed social life.  
**Technical load:** HIGH but dramatized through argument, versioning, worker knowledge and model choices.  
**Exit pressure:** bounded test approved while unresolved evidence remains locked into review.  
**Page-turn:** 4/5.

### Reader note
The chapter works because the model is not presented as truth. The same moment that rewards Ollie also preserves the evidence he is emotionally motivated to keep secondary.

### Line-pattern watch
The chapter contains several fragment stacks and repeated negative constructions (`No closure. / No route event. / Just...`, state lists, `Not a component model. / A state-sequence model.`). Some are functional technical rhythm; some belong in Pass B.

## CH19 — GO / NO-GO
**Opening question:** will the board authorize habitation when no option is clean and the technical evidence is bounded?  
**Immediate want:** Ollie must state what his model does and does not justify, then own a recommendation.  
**Turn 1:** conservative and permissive technical positions are both competent.  
**Turn 2:** residents disagree for legitimate household reasons.  
**Turn 3:** Iri converts embodied reports into an operational stop rule without demanding medical proof.  
**Climax choice inside chapter:** Ollie says YES to the restricted profile while naming unresolved evidence.  
**Technical load:** HIGH in first section, then converts into civic conflict.  
**Page-turn:** 3.5/5 first technical section -> 4.5/5 once residents enter.

### MEDIUM WATCH 2 — technical recap density
This is the only confirmed false-success-related pacing issue in Ch12–20.

The reader already experienced in Ch15:
- the 3.2-second allowed follow;
- no disputed lag under sequence restriction;
- early embodied/interface events without escalation;
- unresolved relation between smaller follow and prior lag;
- limits of the test.

Ch19 repeats much of that before the real new story begins: **who pays which risk and whether Ollie will publicly support opening**.

Repair threshold is met for a later bounded line pass: test a 10–20% compression of the technical recap while preserving the two-sided competence and the exact evidence ceiling.

Do NOT remove:
- the safety engineer’s credible objection;
- the distinction between expected settling and disputed lag;
- Ollie being forced to answer precisely;
- resident disagreement;
- Iri’s report-threshold contribution;
- Ollie’s explicit restricted-profile YES.

## CH20 — THE WRONG CAUSE
**Opening question:** will real habitation remain stable under the authorized restrictions?  
**Immediate want:** Ollie expects the occupied system to behave inside the model.  
**Escalation:** ordinary laundry/home life -> early reports -> review threshold -> movement reduction -> allowed service states interact -> fixed system finally follows -> oscillation -> evacuation -> worker injury / housing reset.  
**Midpoint reversal:** the prohibited partition overlap never occurs; the model predicts recovery anyway; therefore the model has identified a predictor, not the complete causal structure.  
**Price:** injured maintenance worker, equipment damage, displaced residents, Ollie access hold, Lea household disruption, professional trust damage.  
**Technical load:** HIGH but almost entirely action-bearing.  
**Page-turn:** 5/5.

### Reader note
This is the payoff that justifies the technical specificity of Ch12/15/19. The failure is not a random bigger explosion. It attacks the exact confidence the earlier chapters built.

# FALSE-SUCCESS SATURATION — FINAL FINDING

**REJECTED AS MAJOR.**

The felt sequence is not:
`model works -> model works -> model works -> model fails`.

It is:
`Ollie finds a seductive partial model`
-> `young life widens around him`
-> `Ethan/friend group begin separating`
-> `model survives real people`
-> `Aoife pays a privacy failure`
-> `Maya sees material housing cost and pays for her own lie`
-> `Ethan chooses how adulthood enters his relationships`
-> `public institutions must decide under uncertainty`
-> `real habitation reveals the model was incomplete`.

That is escalation, not repetition.

## What to repair later
Only Ch19’s **recap density**, not Ch12/15/19 architecture.
