## SCENE 58 — TWO MODELS

**Immediate want:** Ollie wants his timing analysis represented accurately enough to matter without pretending it proves more than it does.

**Situation:** formal GO/NO-GO review. Present two defensible interpretations of the same evidence.

**Conservative interpretation:**
- resident/interface reports repeatedly precede fixed response;
- 3.2-second follow in Ch15 proves the system can still echo occupancy change even without propagation;
- realistic mixed habitation may create combinations not yet tested;
- delay or tighter segmentation preserves more uncertainty margin.

**Permissive interpretation:**
- disputed fixed lag has not reproduced when the identified transition-overlap condition is absent;
- second messy Ch15 cycle remained stable;
- early reports did not escalate under those tested conditions;
- restricted opening can be configured specifically to avoid the current predictor condition while monitoring unresolved evidence.

Neither side may be caricatured.

**Ollie action:** explain sequence evidence, including Ch15 3.2-second follow, and state uncertainty himself.

**Mara action:** force Ollie to state what his model does **not** prove.

Required answer substance:
- does not prove root cause;
- does not classify early embodied/interface reports;
- does not establish unrestricted habitation safety;
- does not prove untested route/service combinations are equivalent to tested ones.

**Turn:** the review accepts that Ollie’s analysis removes one legitimate conservative trigger assumption for the proposed restricted profile. It changes the decision environment; it does not decide the decision.
