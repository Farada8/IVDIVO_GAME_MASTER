## 0. EXECUTIVE VERDICT

Structural regression remains GREEN. Pass B finds no fatal causality defect in Ch21–29.

However, two cumulative prose problems are real enough to patch:

### MAJOR B1 — DRY-SMART VOICE CONVERGENCE
Across Ollie, Iri, Mara, Ren, Nadia, Lea and sometimes Aoife/S1, dialogue repeatedly falls into the same clipped correction engine:
- `No.` / `Yes.`
- `I know.`
- `That is not...`
- `Better.` / `Good.`
- one character states a category; another corrects category wording;
- somebody notices “the face.”

Individually many beats work. In sequence they flatten character distinction and create generated-text rhythm.

### MAJOR B2 — CH21–29 REVIEW-PROCESS DENSITY
The causal spine is sound, but several consecutive chapters repeat a similar intellectual experience:
1. bounded/limited evidence arrives;
2. hero tries to infer too much;
3. another character narrows the claim;
4. model/review becomes more honest;
5. next bounded test/data layer opens.

This is correct story logic but risks reader fatigue because escalation is felt as process iteration rather than changing dramatic form.

**Required repair:** preserve causality while making each chapter feel like a different kind of story event.

# 1. GLOBAL LINE-PATTERN FINDINGS

## MUST REDUCE
- repeated `I know` response cadence, especially Ollie/Iri/Mara clusters;
- repeated `No` ladder used as a whole conversation architecture;
- repeated `That is not X` category-correction jokes;
- repeated “you made the face / that face” joke family;

- repeated short paragraph stacks where each paragraph only re-labels the previous thought;
- repeated procedural screens/forms when the reader already understands the rule.

These patterns are partly character-specific and sometimes dramatically useful. Keep selected high-value instances:
- Mara’s hard `No` when authority boundary must be unmistakable;
- S1’s repeated `I know` when refusing Aoife’s self-exonerating list;
- Samir’s bounded `No` as practical privacy behavior;
- Ollie’s literal category correction when it reveals his cognitive style.

The target is **distribution**, not sterilization.
