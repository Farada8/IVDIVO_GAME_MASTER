## STORY FUNCTION

Complete Maya's autonomy line before the migration climax. The chapter must separate three things she previously fused together: apology to the peer she used as cover; negotiation with her host household; actual use of the negotiated freedom. Repair must not purchase mobility. Mobility must not require surveillance. The payoff is deliberately ordinary: Maya tells the truth in a small update, changes plans, and nothing dramatic happens.

## HERO ENGINE

**HERO:** Maya.  
**WANT:** regain meaningful late mobility/privacy without live tracking and without making someone else responsible for a false version of her whereabouts.  
**WHY NOW:** Ch31 handoff moves the host meeting to tonight; migration support is increasing and her knowledge of night routes/closures is becoming useful.  
**OPPOSITION:** her prior concealment debt, host household's legitimate duty of care, the peer's anger, plans that change more than once, and Maya's instinct to treat any update as permission-seeking/control.  
**WRONG STRATEGY TO RESIST:** apologize in order to get freedom back; demand trust as a reward; accept surveillance just to end the argument; conceal a second route change because the first update was truthful.  
**PRICE:** the peer may accept the apology without agreeing to cover again; the host agreement still requires boring updates and escalation conditions.  
**MIDPOINT:** host asks the hard case—what if plans change twice?—forcing Maya to define an update protocol rather than a one-time exception.  
**CLIMAX CHOICE:** Maya accepts the obligation to update truthful condition when it materially changes, without asking permission again inside agreed bounds.  
**RESOLUTION:** trial privacy agreement accepted; Maya uses it during a late plan change; nothing dramatic happens.  
**HANDOFF:** her trustworthy night-route knowledge can now support migration; Ch33 begins move day.

## SCENE 98 — THE APOLOGY THAT ISN'T A DEAL

Maya meets the peer whose name/household she used as cover.

Want: repair the relationship.
Resistance: Maya also wants mobility back, making the apology suspect.

Required apology content:
- not `sorry I wanted privacy`;
- not `sorry you were upset`;
- specifically: `I made you responsible for information you did not know was false / I used your name or household so my story would work.`

Peer response:
- may believe apology;
- remains annoyed;
- does not promise to cover again;
- does not become Maya's moral tutor.

Turn: repair and mobility are separate questions.

Maya must leave the conversation without having bought anything.

## SCENE 99 — ZONE, HOST, CONDITION

Host meeting is practical and specific.

Maya proposes:
- truthful zone;
- truthful named host/contact;
- return condition/time band;
- update/escalation if route/environment changes materially;
- no continuous location stream;
- no fixed sleep-backup fiction unless actually activated.

Host asks:
- what if plans change once? Update condition;
- what if they change twice? Update again; not a permission request if still inside agreed bounds;
- what if named contact changes? Update before relying on the new contact;
- what if route/environment becomes materially different or unsafe? Escalation/contact, not silence.

Host must remain competent and not punitive. They have legitimate reasons to know enough to act if something goes wrong.

Turn: trial agreement accepted. It is bounded, reviewable, and does not equal unlimited freedom.

## SCENE 100 — USE IT

Same night or next relevant late plan, Maya actually uses the agreement.

Plan changes in an ordinary youth way: friend changes venue, route closes, practice/social event runs late, or relocation support changes meeting point.

Maya sends the small truthful update:
`zone changed / still with X / return condition now Y`.

She does not ask `can I?` if conditions remain inside the agreement.

Host acknowledges minimally.

Nothing dramatic happens.

This is the payoff: privacy/autonomy works because she tells enough truth for others not to carry false responsibility.

End with Maya continuing the night rather than admiring her maturity.
