## CONFIRMED PROBLEM

Ch06 story/world/technical engine is strong, but Ollie, Iri, Nadia and Mara repeatedly share the same dry category-correction rhythm. The patch does not rewrite the chapter. It changes only adjacent exchanges where different characters perform the same social function through `No / I know / That is not / Correct / Good` ping-pong.

## PATCH SET

### 1. View-gallery exchange — Iri
Old sequence repeatedly corrects Ollie's wording (`doesn't make sense -> rooms/windows -> that's worse -> not the same`).

New function:
- Ollie asks whether Vela books windows;
- Iri answers through the actual barrier/room scarcity;
- Ollie chooses food;
- one dry Iri response remains.

Result: local-world humor remains, but Iri no longer wins a chain of semantic corrections.

### 2. Preserve one Iri signature
`You read with your whole face` is deliberately retained as the one strong Iri face/correction beat in Ch06.

### 3. Nadia ↔ Mara freight blockage
Old version: `That is not my system -> it becomes your system -> I am not -> Correct`.

New version:
- Mara sees the blocking cart and clears the junction operationally;
- Nadia checks the route and returns to her relocation problem;
- Mara is already moving toward the test corridor.

Result: Mara's voice = command / decision / moving on. Nadia's = relocation burden / logistics ownership.

### 4. Nadia introduction
Removed Nadia's generic `I know` after Ollie's name. She shakes once and is already watching freight flow.

### 5. `This is allowed?`
Old version gives Nadia and Mara matched declarative answers followed by narrator explanation `Those were not the same answer. Both mattered.`

New version:
- Nadia physically redirects a cart while saying `This is living here.`
- Mara checks bands and gives a limit instruction;
- Ollie follows people rather than waiting for the answers to agree.

World/social distinction is now action-first.

### 6. Iri regulator-data boundary
Old ladder: `Not to you -> generally? -> yes -> could we compare -> not yours -> I know -> Good`.

New version:
- Iri covers the regulator with their other hand;
- says only that their regulator can export timing;
- cuts off the next step with `Use the fixed trace`;
- turns back to Lea;
- Ollie leaves the regulator alone.

This **strengthens personal-data boundary** while removing shared correction cadence. No private regulator trace is granted to Ollie.

### 7. Mara eleven-second response
Old ladder: `within expected settling -> that's not what I asked -> I know -> sends timing`.

New version: Mara checks both traces, pushes the timestamp pair, and gives both facts at once: `Eleven seconds. Still within expected settling.`

Evidence law unchanged.

### 8. Mara after first group
Old ladder: `don't fall in love -> I'm not -> you are -> want repeat -> allowed -> next group -> yes`.

New version: Mara closes the panel and announces group two; Ollie asks whether route differs; Mara orders him into position and moves on.

Mara's voice becomes operational rather than another correction partner.

### 9. Iri before group two
Old: `same route? -> mostly -> mostly isn't same -> that's the point`.

New: Iri asks whether group two takes west cut; Ollie identifies the changed final junction; Iri silently repositions for a better view.

### 10. Small narrator rhythm repair
`Nothing interesting. That was good. He knew it was good. He still wanted something to happen.`
becomes
`Nothing interesting. Ollie knew he should be pleased. He still wanted something to happen.`
