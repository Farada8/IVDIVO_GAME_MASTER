The book successfully avoids the largest ensemble failure: the five do not remain a synchronized task squad. They acquire separate housing, work, sport, media, mobility and adult-track pressures, then choose friendship around those lives.

The strongest non-Ollie relationship lines are **Aoife ↔ S1/source** and **Maya ↔ Lea** because each has a concrete boundary violation, price, changed behavior and incomplete repair.

The main integration question is whether **Ollie, as primary hero, receives enough private/emotional relationship pressure outside engineering** to remain a person first and a problem-solver second.

Second question: whether **Samir's inner arc** is as developed as his excellent sport/data-boundary function.

No new romance or subplot should be invented merely to fill a checklist. Verify through continuous read first.

# 1. OLLIE — PRIMARY HERO RELATIONSHIP FIELD

## Ollie ↔ Iri

**Starting state:** Ollie wants local competence/legitimacy; Iri represents actual lived/work reality rather than institutional abstract knowledge.

**Pressure:** Ollie's model-building instinct can turn local experience into something to classify, explain or own.

**Resistance:** Iri refuses oracle/mentor idealization and remains a worker/person with their own obligations, body/environment realities and limits.

**Key structural movement:**
- apprenticeship/local work creates earned respect;
- Iri does not simply validate the clean model;
- later embodied/local reports remain personal evidence rather than interpretation authority;
- Ch34 Iri explicitly refuses to make the system decision for Ollie;
- Ch36 practical sensor-position work becomes relationship payoff;
- Ollie accepts one bounded work invitation and also leaves nonurgent work for friendship/dinner.

**Behavioral resolution:** collaboration survives without dependency or coronation.

**Verdict:** GREEN STRONG as work/mentor-peer line.

**Watch:** do not let every emotionally meaningful Ollie scene remain technical. Continuous read must identify at least several places where Ollie's embarrassment, envy, friendship need, fear of exclusion or ordinary pleasure is visible without a model on screen.

## Ollie ↔ five-person friendship

**Starting pressure inherited from Book One:** friendship exists, but adulthood/orbit can pull the five into different institutions and priorities.

**Book Two movement:** separate addresses and obligations make divergence real; group is not permanently assembled for plot.

**Resolution:** Ch36 meal only happens after work/sport/source/host obligations disrupt the original schedule. Ollie leaves nonurgent work for the meeting.

**Verdict:** GREEN.

**Reader test:** Does Ollie actively miss/choose friends earlier in the book, or does the friendship payoff arrive mostly because the others continue appearing? If the latter, one existing choice/absence beat may need strengthening.

# 2. ETHAN ↔ AOIFE

**Locked inherited pressure:** attraction/tension under ambition, truth, timing, distance and management; no generic romance reset.

**Book Two pressure:** Ethan's coordination reflex extends into how he reveals choices and anticipates Aoife's reaction. Aoife resists being managed/framed.

**Key movement:**
- Ch18 adult-track opportunity creates trust pressure because timing/secrecy matters;
- the opportunity remains genuinely attractive;
- Ch31 Ethan tells Aoife before the deadline and does not ask her to choose for him;
- Aoife refuses romantic/moral certification;
- Ethan chooses later intake for his own mixed reasons and pays by losing the preferred immediate slot;
- Ch36 warmth remains inside ordinary group life, not a couple-lock payoff.

**Behavioral resolution:** more honest timing, less reaction-management, attraction unresolved but warmer.

**Verdict:** GREEN STRONG.

**Do not repair toward:** kiss-as-reward, `we are together now`, therapeutic clarity, or Aoife becoming Ethan's conscience.

# 3. AOIFE ↔ S1 / SOURCE

**Starting state:** Aoife's competence is seeing/frame-making; source relationship requires privacy/publication boundaries.

**Pressure:** Aoife can honor literal promises while still inferring/constructing a story the source did not consent to own publicly.

**Key movement:**
- Ch11 establishes source/publication boundary;
- Ch16 failure is inferential privacy, not careless promise-breaking;
- Ch22 safety-relevant correction enters de-identified review without restoring intimacy/publication rights;
- source does not forgive cheaply;
- Ch30 Aoife helps residents with recorder away;
- source offers one banal line under explicit context boundary;
- Aoife does not grab recorder immediately;
- Ch36 `Waiting Routes` uses smaller public/lived material and the bounded line once.

**Behavioral resolution:** Aoife still makes stories, but observation no longer automatically becomes ownership.

**Verdict:** GREEN STRONG / one of the best complete secondary arcs.

**Continuity law:** S1 species/body ontology remains UNKNOWN beyond accepted accommodation boundaries; do not accidentally disclose via subtitle/description wording.

# 4. MAYA ↔ LEA / HOST AUTONOMY

**Starting pressure:** Maya wants privacy/mobility and hates being managed; earlier concealment makes Lea responsible for keeping Maya's version true.

**Key movement:**
- Ch10 seeds sleep/location concealment and Lea cover burden;
- Ch17 housing/social consequence keeps debt alive;
- Ch24 `WHERE MAYA IS` makes the price relational, not another rule lecture;
- Maya's autonomy protocol distinguishes truthful zone/contact/return condition from live tracking;
- Ch32 failure evolves from concealment into over-help/control: Maya wants to solve more than Lea asked for;
- Lea retains ownership of household burden/choice;
- Ch36 message `desk not room. just the bag` / Maya obeys exactly.

**Behavioral resolution:** autonomy works because truth/boundary protocol becomes boring behavior; trust is functional but not fully healed.

**Verdict:** GREEN STRONG.

**Do not repair toward:** second honesty test, perfect trust reset, Maya as mobility superhero, Lea as policy voice.

# 5. SAMIR — SPORT / DATA / INNER ARC

**Strong external character functions:**
- Crossband is real social/status life, not clue factory;
- Samir refuses interview, band window, team capture, peer extraction, biometric/support data and Contact-profile context;
- he remains engaged rather than simply refusing plot participation;
- smaller self-owned evidence contribution is consent-bounded;
- Ch36 sport continues, with real match/injury/friends-as-spectators and no-sensor boundary.

This is excellent **boundary characterization**.

## MAJOR WATCH A — IS THERE ENOUGH INNER MOVEMENT?

A boundary is not automatically an arc.

Continuous read must identify Samir's:
- personal want beyond `do not extract me`;
- private fear/status pressure;
- mistake or cost generated by his own strategy;
- a changed behavior by the end that is not simply `my boundary was respected`.

Possible existing sources may already be in Crossband/social scenes. Do not invent a new subplot until the continuous read proves absence.

**Severity:** MAJOR WATCH because Samir is one of the five core young characters.

# 7. FIVE-PERSON ENSEMBLE

## What works

The book structurally rejects:
`five arrive -> five receive assignment -> five solve mystery together`.

Instead:
- Ch4 separates addresses;
- work/sport/media/host/adult-track lines diverge;
- side characters gain local relationships;
- technical evidence enters through different life domains without making everyone investigators;
- final gathering requires negotiation.

**Verdict:** GREEN STRONG.

## MEDIUM WATCH B2 — POV / AIRTIME VS EMOTIONAL CENTRALITY

Equal airtime is not the goal. But the continuous read should test whether each POV change arrives because that character owns the current pressure, not because the ensemble needs rotation.

Flag any chapter whose primary effect could be delivered more powerfully from another character's existing pressure.

Do not equalize chapter counts.

# 8. OLLIE PRIVATE LIFE — MAJOR WATCH B

Ollie has a strong work arc and a credible friendship resolution. The risk is that other core characters have more visibly personal relationship problems:
- Ethan/Aoife attraction + management;
- Aoife/S1 trust;
- Maya/Lea autonomy;
- Samir sport/status/boundaries.

Ollie's dominant intimate line is Iri/work plus the five-person friendship.

That can be enough **if** the prose gives him ordinary youth life:
- embarrassment;
- desire for approval;
- jealousy/status comparison;
- boredom;
- money/work concern;
- attraction or lack of attraction as lived reality rather than omission;
- private rituals/spaces;
- nontechnical friendship choices;
- pleasure in orbital life.

**MAJOR WATCH B:** continuous read must verify CHARACTER BEFORE ABILITY for the primary hero across the whole novel.

If weak, do not bolt on romance. Minimum repair could be strengthening existing ordinary-life beats around Ch3–5, Ch13–14, Ch24–25 adjacency, and Ch36.
