## Hubbard → measurement as uncertainty reduction

IVDIVO modules: `UNCERTAINTY_LEDGER`, `VALUE_OF_INFORMATION_ROUTER`, `CALIBRATED_CONFIDENCE`, `MEASURE_JUST_ENOUGH_GATE`.
Law: no metric merely because it is easy to count. Every measurement names the decision it can change and the uncertainty it reduces.

## The Mom Test → evidence interview hygiene
Mechanisms: compliments/fluff/hypotheticals are weak evidence; concrete past behavior, constraints and commitment are stronger.
