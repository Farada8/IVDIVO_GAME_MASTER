## RUNTIME CRAFT LAW

Reference-derived craft mechanisms are controls, not formulas.

Where relevant, enforce:
- **Dual-protagonist romance:** both romantic leads have independent want/fear/price/change; neither is merely reward/support; relationship and external plot braid.
- **Waiting-question ledger:** know what the reader/listener is waiting for, why payoff is delayed and when the question changes/pays off.
- **Dialogue as action:** distinguish SAID / UNSAID / UNSAYABLE; exposition changes action rather than pausing it.
- **Inference-first audio:** audible event -> response -> listener inference -> consequence; do not redundantly explain sound without a new action/function.
- **Functional silence:** silence must change attention, suspense, recognition, status, refusal or aftermath.
