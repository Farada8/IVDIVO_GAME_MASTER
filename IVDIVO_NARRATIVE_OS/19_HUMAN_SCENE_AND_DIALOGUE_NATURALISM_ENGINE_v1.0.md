# IVDIVO — HUMAN SCENE & DIALOGUE NATURALISM ENGINE v1.0

**Status:** CURRENT MANDATORY WRITING QUALITY LAYER  
**Established:** 2026-08-21  
**Scope:** novels, doramas, audio-first fiction, dialogue-heavy scenes, character interaction, scene revision, read-aloud/audio diagnostics.  
**Authority boundary:** this engine improves execution of already-authorized story/character material. It does not silently change locked canon, plot, chronology, culprit, relationship outcome, or world rules.

---

## 0. WHY THIS ENGINE EXISTS

The existing IVDIVO stack is strong at macro-story architecture, causality, mystery, continuity, escalation and production routing. A fresh forensic pass on `LESSON ZERO` exposed a different risk: technically functional scenes can still feel written rather than lived when multiple characters converge on the same polished rhetorical habits.

Observed corpus signals in the current `LESSON ZERO` reading edition:
- exact `I know.` occurs 94 times;
- `That is...` constructions occur 225 times;
- `apparently` occurs 51 times;
- several recurring correction patterns use `That is not... / That was not the question / I know`;
- some exchanges are strong because meaning remains unspoken (`Thanks / For what? / Nothing / Good`), proving the book can already support credible compressed subtext.

These counts are **diagnostic signals, not automatic defects**. Repetition can be intentional characterization. The failure condition is cross-character convergence: the same rhetorical move, cadence, cleverness and correction syntax being used by characters who should not naturally share them.

Core diagnosis:

> **The system must distinguish concise dramatic dialogue from authorial cleverness wearing different character names.**

---

## 1. GOVERNING PRINCIPLE

Do not imitate raw everyday chatter. Produce **fictional authenticity**: speech that is compressed enough to carry story, but feels spontaneously generated by this specific person, in this relationship, under this pressure, at this exact moment.

Dialogue credibility is downstream of behavior:

`PERSON -> STATE -> RELATIONSHIP -> PERCEPTION -> DESIRE -> RISK -> TACTIC -> LISTENING -> REACTION -> WORDS`.

Never reverse the chain into:

`THEME/EXPOSITION -> CLEVER LINE -> ASSIGN TO CHARACTER`.

---

## 2. HUMAN SCENE STATE — REQUIRED BEFORE IMPORTANT DIALOGUE

For every major participant define:

- **BODY STATE:** fatigue, pain, hunger, intoxication, adrenaline, illness, sensory load.
- **EMOTIONAL STATE:** dominant feeling + secondary/conflicting feeling.
- **COGNITIVE LOAD:** calm / divided attention / overloaded / tunnel vision.
- **IMMEDIATE WANT:** what they want in the next 30–180 seconds.
- **BACKGROUND WANT:** what larger need controls this interaction.
- **FEAR / LOSS:** what they are trying not to expose, lose or trigger.
- **BELIEF:** what they currently think is true.
- **MISBELIEF:** what they are wrong about or oversimplifying.
- **KNOWN / UNKNOWN / SUSPECTED:** information boundaries.
- **STATUS:** formal and emotional status relative to the other person.
- **RELATIONSHIP DEBT:** affection, resentment, shame, dependence, rivalry, attraction, obligation.
- **SPEECH RISK:** what cannot safely be said yet.
- **DEFAULT DEFENSE:** joke, precision, silence, anger, caretaking, withdrawal, intellectualization, attack, compliance.

If these do not constrain the language, the state card is decorative and FAILS.

---

## 3. LISTENING / RESPONSE LAW

A line is not valid merely because it is a good sentence.

For each reply ask:
1. What exact fragment of the previous behavior did the character register?
2. What did they miss, misunderstand or refuse?
3. Did they answer the literal statement, the implication, the emotional threat, or something else?
4. What changed in their body or attention before they spoke?
5. Why this response now rather than five seconds earlier/later?

Every important exchange must include believable **response mechanics**: acceptance, evasion, repair, misread, interruption, reframe, silence, delayed answer, topic shift, escalation, appeasement, attack, withdrawal or unexpected cooperation.

Perfect mutual comprehension is a special state, not the default.

---

## 4. SPEAKER IDENTITY MODEL

Each recurring character requires a **Speech Fingerprint**, but not gimmicky quirks.

Minimum dimensions:
- vocabulary domain;
- abstract vs concrete language;
- sentence length distribution;
- directness;
- certainty markers;
- hedging style;
- humor mechanism;
- profanity boundary;
- metaphor source domain;
- emotional vocabulary;
- question style;
- correction style;
- disagreement style;
- apology style;
- silence tolerance;
- interruption tolerance;
- speed under pressure;
- compression under stress;
- profession/age/class/culture effects;
- phrases the character would **never** naturally use.

A character voice PASS requires both:
- intra-character continuity;
- inter-character separability.

If names are removed from a multi-speaker scene and the lines remain largely interchangeable, MAJOR.

---

## 5. AGE / CULTURE / PROFESSION LAW

Age, profession and culture influence speech but never become caricature.

Do not write:
- teenagers as generic slang machines;
- professionals as walking textbooks;
- scientists as exposition robots;
- wealthy people as permanent aphorism engines;
- traumatized people as perpetual therapy language;
- romantic leads as people who always know the correct emotional sentence.

A seventeen-year-old may be unusually articulate. The test is not `would an average teenager say this?` but:

> **Would this teenager, with this history and knowledge, plausibly reach for these exact words in this exact state?**

---

## 6. CLEVERNESS BUDGET

A clever line is allowed. A room full of characters continuously trading clever lines is usually authorial convergence.

Required checks:
- no automatic ban on wit;
- track **clever-line density** per scene;
- track which character owns which humor mechanism;
- consecutive polished reversals by multiple speakers trigger review;
- an aphoristic line must cost something: reveal status, defense, intimacy, hostility, embarrassment, competence or avoidance;
- if a line mainly proves the writer is clever, cut or simplify.

**Hard review trigger:** 3+ consecutive polished/corrective lines with no behavioral friction, silence, misunderstanding, cost or tactical shift.

---

## 7. EXPOSITION MOTIVE GATE

Before any factual line:

`FACT -> WHO NEEDS IT -> WHY THEY NEED IT NOW -> WHY THIS SPEAKER WOULD SAY IT -> WHAT THE SPEAKER WANTS TO CAUSE -> WHY THE LISTENER DOES NOT ALREADY KNOW IT`.

FAIL patterns:
- characters telling each other shared history;
- technical explanation with no tactical reason;
- worldbuilding inserted because the reader needs it;
- institutional speech that sounds like documentation;
- a character naming the theme instead of pursuing an objective.

Prefer information to emerge as **ammunition, correction, refusal, bargaining chip, warning, test, accusation, joke, concealment, practical instruction or consequence**.

---

## 8. SUBTEXT / UNSAID RATIO

Not every line needs hidden meaning, but every major scene needs meaningful pressure between said and unsaid.

Track:
- what cannot yet be said;
- what is said instead;
- what both know but avoid;
- what one knows and the other does not;
- what the body reveals before language;
- what silence forces the other person to do.

If every emotional insight is spoken explicitly, MAJOR in intimate/dramatic scenes.

---

## 9. HUMAN FRICTION TOOLKIT

Use selectively, never as decorative noise:
- delayed answer;
- partial answer;
- wrong answer to the right question;
- right answer to the wrong question;
- interruption;
- self-correction;
- abandoned sentence;
- failed joke;
- joke taken literally;
- overlap;
- mishearing;
- physical distraction;
- task competing with conversation;
- emotional lag;
- status-induced politeness;
- false agreement;
- repair attempt;
- refusal to repair;
- topic change;
- silence;
- exit.

Naturalism is not adding `uh`, stammers and filler indiscriminately. Friction must arise from state and relationship.

---

## 10. BEHAVIOR BEFORE EXPLANATION

For important emotional information prefer:

`OBSERVABLE BEHAVIOR -> INTERPRETATION PRESSURE -> OPTIONAL WORDS`

over
`EXPLANATORY SPEECH -> STAGE DIRECTION CONFIRMING IT`.

Physical business should not be random gesture spam. It must alter timing, attention, status, distance, avoidance, intimacy or available action.

---

## 11. SCENE CONVERSATION ARC

Every substantive conversation should have:

`ENTRY STATE -> FIRST TACTIC -> RESISTANCE -> ADAPTATION -> MISALIGNMENT/REVELATION -> TACTICAL SHIFT -> TURN -> EXIT STATE`.

The emotional scene turn must be detectable in behavior, not merely declared in narration.

If the conversation can be reordered line-by-line without changing causality, it is likely activity rather than action.

---

## 12. CHARACTER-SPECIFIC CONFLICT

Different people fight differently.

For each recurring relationship define:
- what starts conflict;
- what escalates it;
- what one person does that uniquely wounds the other;
- how each tries to regain control;
- who pursues / who withdraws;
- what apology is believable;
- what apology would be rejected;
- what each person notices first;
- what each routinely misreads.

Do not give every relationship the same `precise accusation -> precise counterargument -> perfect thematic line` pattern.

---

## 13. HOUSE-STYLE CONVERGENCE AUDIT

Run at chapter/book level, not only scene level.

Track repeated:
- sentence frames;
- correction frames (`That is not...`, `That was not...`);
- acknowledgement frames (`I know`);
- hedges (`apparently`, `technically`, `exactly`);
- joke structures;
- narrator irony structures;
- gesture phrases;
- argument choreography;
- emotional-summary syntax.

For each high-frequency pattern classify:
- CHARACTER SIGNATURE;
- RELATIONSHIP SIGNATURE;
- WORLD/REGISTER NECESSITY;
- AUTHOR HOUSE STYLE;
- GENERATED-TEXT REPETITION.

Only the last two demand systematic reduction.

---

## 14. READ-ALOUD / AUDIO TRUTH GATE

Page credibility and performed credibility are related but not identical.

Before large-scale audio or final dialogue lock:
1. read/perform the scene aloud;
2. preserve exact text on first diagnostic render;
3. direct actors for listening, tactics, pauses, overlap and physical orientation;
4. classify discomfort as:
   - PERFORMANCE_DEFECT;
   - ADAPTATION_DEFECT;
   - WRITING_DEFECT;
   - TRANSLATION/LOCALIZATION_DEFECT;
   - NO_DEFECT / CONTEXT_REQUIRED;
5. rewrite only when text is proven the earliest failed layer.

A flat TTS performance is not sufficient evidence to rewrite locked prose.

---

## 15. TWO-CHANNEL VALIDATION

For major dialogue revisions use both:

### CHANNEL A — TEXT BLIND TEST
Reader sees text only.
Questions:
- Who is speaking without tags?
- What does each person want?
- What are they avoiding?
- Which line felt written rather than spoken?
- Where did belief break?

### CHANNEL B — PERFORMANCE BLIND TEST
Listener hears a properly directed performance.
Questions:
- Same as above;
- plus: was the problem voice/performance or wording?

Do not merge the two signals prematurely.

---

## 16. SEVERITY MODEL

### FATAL
- major character repeatedly speaks in a way incompatible with locked identity/knowledge;
- dialogue creates impossible information transfer or breaks mystery causality;
- relationship consent/authority meaning is materially falsified by wording.

### MAJOR
- scene functions only through authorial exposition;
- multiple principal characters are not verbally separable;
- emotional turn requires implausibly perfect understanding;
- repeated polished dialogue materially damages belief;
- adult/teen/professional register is persistently wrong;
- line-level cleverness repeatedly overwhelms character truth.

### MEDIUM
- local repeated syntax;
- one overly neat exchange;
- weak response mechanics;
- exposition could be better motivated;
- scene needs more behavioral friction.

### POLISH
- rhythm, diction, gesture repetition, filler, punctuation, micro-cadence.

---

## 17. REPAIR LAW

`OBSERVED BREAK -> CLASSIFY TEXT/PERFORMANCE/ADAPTATION -> LOCATE EARLIEST FAILED BEAT -> PRESERVE STORY FACTS -> REWRITE SMALLEST NECESSARY UNIT -> READ ALOUD -> CHARACTER-SEPARABILITY TEST -> SCENE REGRESSION -> CHAPTER REGRESSION`.

Never use a naturalism pass as permission to:
- change plot outcome;
- add arbitrary slang;
- erase intelligence;
- flatten stylized voice;
- make everyone hesitant;
- replace subtext with banal chatter;
- rewrite whole chapters when three lines are the actual defect.

## 19. SMITH / FUTURE BOOK LAW

Before drafting substantial new SMITH prose, the Human Scene state and Dialogue Naturalism gates are mandatory for:
- first appearance of every major character;
- first major relationship scene;
- first institutional confrontation;
- first intimate conflict;
- midpoint relationship turn;
- climax dialogue;
- final emotional resolution.

The goal is not merely `good dialogue`.

The goal is:

> **The reader should feel that the book discovered what these people said, rather than that the author supplied lines to them.**
