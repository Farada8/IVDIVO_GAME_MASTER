## Law

Book One remains **DEVELOPMENT LOCKED**. Beta reading tests reader experience; it does not authorize freeform rewriting.

Use real readers when possible. Internal AI beta simulation is diagnostic only and cannot substitute for independent human evidence.

## Reader panel

Minimum useful panel:
- 3–5 teen / near-teen readers;
- 2 adult YA/SF readers;
- 1 non-SF reader;
- at least 1 reader without prior IVDIVO investment.

## Sequence

### A — Blind opening
Give only Chapters 1–5. No lore bible, character bible, R-14 explanation, saga plan or author interpretation.

Pass when most readers understand Ethan’s goal and four-month loss, distinguish at least three of the five teens, want to know what Lesson Zero is, and voluntarily want Chapter 6.

### B — Full-novel chapter log
After each chapter record only: `ONE MORE? YES/MAYBE/NO`, emotion, most interesting person, next question, confusion, repetition/explanation, skim point.

A genuine NO/stop point is evidence. Do not persuade the reader immediately.

### C — Boredom map
After finishing/stopping, mark every chapter:
`A pulled forward / B normal-good / C slower-worthwhile / D wanted to skim / E nearly stopped-stopped`.

Do not reveal internal watch zones before this map is complete.

### D — Targeted post-read questions
Only after spontaneous reaction is captured, test Ethan, Samir/R-14, Aoife/Maya/Ollie, Confederation wonder, WCF climax, Book-One closure and epilogue curiosity.

### E — Audio/read-aloud
At minimum test Ch1–3, a group-heavy Annex section from Ch11–15, and WCF Ch30–33.

## Unlock conditions

Do not revise from isolated taste.

**LEVEL 1 — REPEATED SIGNAL:** at least three independent readers identify the same chapter, confusion or emotional failure.

**LEVEL 2 — SEVERE SIGNAL:** one reader stops for a concrete manuscript reason and another independently reproduces the same failure.

**LEVEL 3 — FACTUAL/CONTINUITY ERROR:** fix immediately at the smallest sufficient scope.

**LEVEL 4 — AUDIO FAILURE:** multiple listeners cannot follow the same speaker/action/information sequence.

No unlock because one reader wants more action, a different romance outcome, another genre, dislikes a character, or because author/AI rereading generates new stylistic anxiety.

## Scope control

- Opening failure -> inspect Ch1–5 only.
- Repeated D/E around Ch20–22 -> targeted midbook compression only.
- Chosen-one / confirmed-alien-conspiracy misread -> evidence-framing repair only.
- WCF clear intellectually but not tense emotionally -> physical-pressure pass; do not automatically add battle.
- Book feels unfinished before epilogue -> Ch34–35 only.
- Epilogue cancels closure -> epilogue only.
- No convergent signal -> **KEEP v1.1 LOCKED**.

## Protected elements

Do not remove without evidence: four-month wound; Annex; Samir ownership/data-rights resolution; ordinary-life obligations; Shared Sky; WCF consent/translation climax; Aaron receiving legitimate adult entry; concise `Source Unresolved` epilogue; no chosen-five; no evil-Calder; no adult exception; no early Matrix proof.

## Reconciler output

A19 receives feedback classified as:
`CONSENSUS FAILURE / REPEATED SIGNAL / SEGMENT ISSUE / PERSONAL TASTE / INTENTIONAL FRICTION / FACTUAL ERROR`.

It must return exactly one scope decision:
`KEEP LOCKED / MICRO PATCH / SCENE PATCH / SEQUENCE PATCH / STRUCTURAL REOPEN`.

Default: **KEEP LOCKED**.
