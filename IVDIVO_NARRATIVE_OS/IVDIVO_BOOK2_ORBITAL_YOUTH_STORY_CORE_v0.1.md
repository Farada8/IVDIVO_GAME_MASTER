# 1. CENTRAL PROMISE

A seventeen-year-old Orbitborn from a service-ring family wants one of a handful of places on the first youth apprenticeship attached to a new lunar-city atmosphere project. An Irish Earthborn exchange student — from the only planetary society trusted with closed orbital technologies — arrives with access the hero has spent a lifetime trying to earn. When evidence appears that a closed atmospheric-production kernel is being leaked toward Earth, the hero secretly investigates to prove the Irish newcomer is abusing privileged access. The investigation triggers a security lockdown, fractures the friend group and eventually reveals that someone in the hero's own family is involved in the leak.

The family member is not a cartoon villain: they believe the orbital cities are hoarding technologies that could transform billions of lives on Earth.

The hero must decide whether belonging means protecting family, protecting the city, protecting a friend, obeying the orbital secrecy regime, or exposing a truth that may cost the future they wanted.

# 2. STORY GATE

## HERO

**WORKING:** 17-year-old Orbitborn, raised in a maintenance/service-ring household. Gender/name open until voice tests.

Ordinary competence: real habitat maintenance experience; understands systems physically rather than academically.

Social wound: ashamed of being treated as “service-ring” in a city that claims old Earth class structures are obsolete.

## WANT

Win a place on the lunar atmosphere / habitat apprenticeship and leave the maintenance ring on their own terms.

## PRIVATE WANT

Be seen as someone who belongs to the future, not as inherited infrastructure labor.

## WHY NOW

Final selection takes place during a major city-wide youth build cycle. At the same time the hero's family housing/access status is under review because a parent contract is ending.

## INCITING IMBALANCE

An Irish Earthborn exchange student is placed on the hero's team and receives legitimate access to systems normally closed to planetary visitors. Soon afterward, a diagnostic trace suggests that a restricted atmospheric-production package has been copied toward an unauthorized planetary channel.

The hero believes catching the breach will prove merit and protect the apprenticeship.

## OPPOSITION

Multiple legitimate pressures rather than one simple villain:

- orbital technology-security law;
- competitive youth selection;
- class resentment between core districts and service rings;
- distrust of the Irish exception;
- family loyalty;
- a planetary network seeking access to closed technology;
- the Irish student's need to protect confidential obligations they are not allowed to explain;
- city systems whose safety logic the teenagers only partly understand.

## WRONG STRATEGY

The hero uses maintenance credentials, a personal AI and illicit borrowed access to investigate privately, believing that more information and a public exposure will solve the problem.

This repeats the hero's deeper mistake: equating access to hidden systems with worth and control.

## PRICE

The investigation causes consequences in ordinary youth life:

- friendship rupture;
- romantic jealousy / attraction contaminated by suspicion;
- family conflict;
- apprenticeship score damage;
- possible loss of housing priority;
- disciplinary risk;
- the Irish student may lose planetary-orbital exchange status because of the hero's accusation.

# 3. CAUSAL ENGINE / McKEE GAP CHAIN

## MOVEMENT I — I DESERVE A PLACE HERE

Hero expects: outperform others -> earn lunar seat.

Actual: Irish newcomer receives legitimate privileged access unavailable to most planetary people and immediately becomes a top candidate.

Value turn: confidence -> resentment.

Hero acts: decides to beat them through technical competence.

## MOVEMENT II — CATCH THE CHEATER

Hero discovers restricted data movement and expects: expose Irish student -> remove rival -> prove loyalty.

Actual: security lockdown treats the whole youth team as a possible breach source; friend's privacy is invaded; hero's own unauthorized investigation becomes evidence.

Value turn: righteous certainty -> culpability.

Hero hides their role and continues investigating.

## MOVEMENT III — THE LEAK IS HOME

Hero expects: find an external planetary buyer or corrupt official.

Actual: evidence leads to someone in hero's own family / household network.

The family member argues that closed orbital technology could save lives and transform planetary housing, atmosphere systems, medicine and infrastructure, while orbitals protect privilege behind safety language.

Value turn: family = safety -> family = moral conflict.

### MIDPOINT / POINT OF NO RETURN

Hero discovers that the copied package is not merely information. It is an operational atmospheric-production kernel that can command autonomous fabrication systems. Used outside the intended infrastructure, it can create cascading material / pressure / chemical failures.

At the same time the hero learns that the orbital authorities have concealed previous near-failures rather than publicly explaining why the technology is restricted.

Both sides are partly right and partly dangerous.

Hero is now causally responsible because their secret investigation has given the leak network access to a path it did not previously possess.

## MOVEMENT IV — EVERY SIDE WANTS THE FILE

Hero tries to repair the damage secretly.

Actual: each repair creates stronger pressure:

- family demands loyalty;
- security identifies the Irish student as the cleanest suspect;
- youth friends split over whether technology should be shared;
- the hero's AI refuses or limits cooperation because of legal safety constraints;
- the apprenticeship board suspends the cohort;
- the atmospheric kernel begins executing part of its process in an unintended construction zone.

Value turn: investigation -> emergency.

## MOVEMENT V — BELONGING COSTS SOMETHING

### CRISIS CHOICE

Hero can protect the family member and let the Irish student take the institutional blame; or expose the family connection, lose housing/status/apprenticeship and still risk helping the orbital authorities bury the larger truth.

A third easy option is explicitly removed.

### CLIMACTIC ACTION

Hero publicly authenticates the real causal chain, including their own illegal actions, while physically using maintenance knowledge to isolate the atmospheric kernel before the failure propagates through the habitat construction system.

They do not simply “upload evidence.” Their disclosure makes the technical containment possible and destroys their ability to control the narrative afterward.

### RESULT

- immediate habitat danger is stopped;
- Irish student is cleared of the leak;
- family member is exposed but not reduced to a one-note traitor;
- orbital authority cannot simply claim perfect safety because concealed failures are now public;
- the forbidden technology remains closed for now;
- the fundamental reason Ireland alone receives privileged access remains UNKNOWN;
- hero loses the lunar apprenticeship they spent the book chasing.

# 4. RESOLUTION

The hero remains in the orbital city but is no longer socially where they began.

They have lost the status prize but gained a real professional identity: people now know they can understand and repair systems under pressure, though they are also known as the teenager who broke protocol and exposed their own family.

The Irish student's relationship with the hero is not magically repaired. Trust must be rebuilt.

The family survives but is permanently changed.

The lunar atmosphere project proceeds without the hero — proving the world does not stop to reward protagonists.

A different maintenance / civic engineering path opens, not as consolation but as the consequence of what the hero actually proved capable of doing.

Series hook only after closure: invitation / disciplinary assignment / contact connected to an Irish orbital-Earth interface or later lunar problem, without explaining the Irish exception.

# 6. RED TEAM — CURRENT STATUS

## STRONG

- protagonist is active and causes worsening consequences;
- worldbuilding is attached to housing, work, status, friendship and family;
- Irish technological exception creates story pressure without solving its mystery;
- orbital secrecy has a morally credible opponent;
- climax requires protagonist choice and action, not rescue by superior adults;
- current book can close while larger politics remain open.

## RISKS / TO SOLVE

1. The leak technology must receive a technical passport so its danger is concrete rather than author-declared.
2. Family member's motive must be personal and specific, not a political speech.
3. The Irish student needs a full independent desire/arc and cannot function only as suspect/rival.
4. The protagonist's romantic line needs an actual person and pressure function.
5. Synthetic-person presence must be lived, not checklist representation.
6. Exact city age/date must be reconciled with Book 1 and Book 3 timeline before architecture lock.
7. The ending must not imply that orbital technology withholding is automatically justified; the fundamental policy remains contested.
