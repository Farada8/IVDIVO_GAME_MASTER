# 26. VELA AS AN ACCRETED CITY

Vela must visibly contain multiple construction eras.

## Central spine — oldest operational identity
- docking / freight;
- low-g transfer;
- service yards;
- fabrication;
- old logistics modules;
- ugly retrofits;
- routes designed for cargo before comfort.

## Mature inhabited rings
- denser social history;
- personalized public spaces;
- shops/services;
- schools;
- sport clubs;
- inherited maintenance compromises;
- neighborhoods whose residents defend their local identity.

## Ring Six
- newest civic/technical layer;
- cleaner interfaces;
- more flexible mixed-environment design;
- less accumulated social improvisation;
- therefore more vulnerable to a commissioning model that understands equipment better than lived behavior.

**Story implication:** the old city contains knowledge not present in clean Ring Six design data.

# 27. ORBITAL PHYSICS AS DAILY BODY KNOWLEDGE

## WORKING LOCK
Vela uses rotation for inhabited gravity environments.

Exact dimensions/RPM remain engineering-polish items, but prose must consistently obey:

1. effective gravity changes with radial distance;
2. local movement across radial connectors feels different from moving along the ring;
3. Coriolis effects matter most during rapid radial movement / thrown objects / sport, not as constant spectacle;
4. transition zones require embodied timing;
5. central spine is low-g / microgravity relative to inhabited ring floors;
6. locals develop habit, not superpowers.

## Social consequence
A planet-born person may know the physics and still move badly.

A Vela teenager may:
- throw correctly without calculating;
- choose a handhold before a direction change;
- know where to stand in transfer flow;
- judge when a drink/container will behave annoyingly;
- prefer a route because the gravity transition is less tiring.

This replaces generic “space kid” competence with embodied local culture.

# 28. GRAVITY AND EQUIPMENT

A major reason mature inhabited rings maintain stable gravity bands is reliability.

Human and compatible nonhuman civilization has enormous legacy investment in:
- food preparation;
- medical equipment;
- workshop tooling;
- fluid handling;
- sanitation;
- domestic objects;
- sport;
- human biomechanics.

Not every object is redesigned for microgravity.

Therefore Vela contains:
- true low-g systems where low-g is useful;
- rotation where gravity makes ordinary life/equipment more reliable;
- transfer interfaces where the two meet.

This makes the city technologically advanced **without making every spoon futuristic**.

# 29. OUTSIDE WORK RULE

Routine external inspection/repair should default to:
- hull robots;
- crawler systems;

Human EVA is:
- rarer;

# 30. ENVIRONMENTAL FAILURE — ORBITAL-SPECIFIC v0.3

Keep the existing `COUPLED OCCUPANCY OSCILLATION`, but make the physical mechanism more specific.

Ring Six uses dynamic environmental boundaries across a rotating habitat.

Relevant effects:
- radial pressure gradient;
- rotating-frame airflow;
- pressure/air curtains at compatible-environment transitions;
- thermal/humidity demand;
- fast personal environmental regulators;
- door/partition timing;
- crowd route substitution;
- service-return sensors averaging slower than local personal systems.

## Failure loop v0.3
1. a dense mixed group changes routes;
2. several transition doors/air curtains cycle in an unplanned order;
3. local personal/environment support systems react immediately;
4. those reactions shift airflow/humidity/thermal demand;
5. return-manifold/fixed sensors see the averaged state later;
6. AHE corrects against a state already changing;
7. a second route change moves the load again;
8. control corrections arrive out of phase;
9. oscillation grows locally.

This is an **environment/control oscillation**, not the ring physically shaking.

# 31. SAFE-STATE — PHYSICAL FUNCTION v0.3

When Ollie triggers the authorized local seal, the system does more than close a door.

`SAFE-STATE`:
- stops inbound transit feed;
- freezes nonessential adaptive optimization;
- reduces partition cycling;
- switches the segment to conservative certified environmental bands;
- isolates local control loop from neighboring adaptive demand;
- preserves individual life-support devices;
- increases monitoring frequency;
- reroutes service traffic.

This creates visible cost:
housing throughput collapses because the segment becomes safe by becoming less flexible.

# 32. CIVIC POLITICS — FORUM, NOT OMNISCIENT ADMINISTRATION

Vela needs a place where legitimate values can conflict.

Book Two does **not** need a full constitution.

For scenes, use a bounded civic process containing representatives of:
- habitat safety;
- commissioning/operations;
- housing/relocation;
- maintenance/work;
- affected residents.

Youth/apprentices may speak where relevant but do not rule.

## Rule
Engineering answers:
“What states are safe?”

Politics answers:
“Who bears the remaining cost?”

Neither answer replaces the other.

This directly strengthens Ch19, Ch28–30 and the final commissioning review.

# 33. STATION ECONOMY — MINIMUM LOOP v0.3

Vela's economy grew from:
- transfer/logistics;
- construction;
- fabrication;
- repair/maintenance;
- orbital services.

Ring Six matters economically because opening or delay affects:
- service businesses;
- apprenticeships;
- workshop/fabrication orders;
- freight scheduling;
- staffing;
- local retail/food;
- temporary-housing costs.

Do not convert this into macroeconomic exposition.

Show it through:
- a shift lost;
- a shop opening late;
- apprentice hours;
- a fabrication booking;
- a worker sleeping too little because of commute;
- a family choosing worse housing to preserve work/school.

# 34. SOCIAL STRATIFICATION WITHOUT CASTE

Vela is not classless.

Status/scarcity can attach to:
- private space;
- good commute;
- high-demand view space;
- preferred gravity/environment band;
- fabrication access;
- desirable shifts;
- recognized technical competence;
- local social networks;

But:
**no inherited Color/caste analogue.**

The city may contain pressure toward utility-based status, and characters can criticize it.

# 35. PRODUCTIVITY PRESSURE — OLLIE'S SOCIAL MIRROR

Closed habitats make visible that people consume shared systems.

This can create a cultural temptation:
> if everyone depends on everyone, everyone should be useful.

Book Two must **not endorse** this as human worth.

Vela can respect operational competence while also maintaining baseline rights that are not conditional on productivity.

This makes Ollie's private wound more powerful:
he wants to justify his place by becoming indispensable.

His ending rejects indispensability without rejecting useful work.

# 36. RECREATION IS INFRASTRUCTURE TOO

Vela must include genuinely desirable non-work environments:
- gravity gardens / green public volumes;
- food/market zones;
- view rooms;
- sport volumes;
- music/performance routes;
- maker clubs;
- private/social corners;
- quiet low-g spaces.

These consume scarce volume, maintenance and scheduling.

That is a feature, not irrational luxury.

A civilization that preserves only survival systems is not a city.

# 37. LOCAL YOUTH SPATIAL CULTURE

Local teens should differ from Earth cohort through practice:

- route intuition;
- gravity transition timing;
- knowing which queue is fake-short;

# 39. SOURCE S1 — WORKING ORBITAL-SPECIFIC SENSITIVE STATUS

**WORKING OPTION / recommended for Aoife line**

Source S1 is a local orbital-born human teenager on a private **gravity-load adaptation schedule**.

They are healthy enough for ordinary Vela life but periodically use a higher-load conditioning facility / wearable support because their developmental gravity history makes Earth-normal load adaptation nontrivial.

## Why exposure matters
Earth media can turn one private health/adaptation fact into:
- “orbital children are damaged”;
- “orbit-born humans cannot live on Earth”;
- political ammunition about orbital expansion.

## Inference chain
Aoife removes name and obvious identifiers, but the approved clip retains a combination of:
- background facility tone/marker;
- visible load-support cuff or interface icon;
- route timestamp;
- a location recognizable to specialists.

No single clue identifies status.
Together they do.

This makes the privacy failure **native to orbital civilization**, not generic social-media doxxing.

Status remains WORKING until Founder approves.

# 40. IRI — WORKING BODY/ENVIRONMENT PASSPORT v0.3

**Recommended working direction, not saga canon**

Iri is a nonhuman-origin Vela resident whose respiration is human-compatible but whose preferred microclimate differs from human default.

Minimal functional differences:
- cooler ambient preference;
- higher humidity preference;
- faster discomfort under rapid warm/dry transitions;
- compact legal personal environmental interface that manages local microclimate and logs adaptation timing;
- interface data are private by default.

## Why useful to story
Iri's personal system does not make Iri magically sensitive.

It reacts locally before distant fixed return-manifold sensors see an averaged environmental change.

This gives the story a technically meaningful Layer-C signal while preserving:
- privacy;
- uncertainty;
- nonhuman ordinary life.

## Vulnerability
Rapid repeated transitions are tiring/uncomfortable, not automatically fatal.

## Ordinary inconvenience
Shared cafés / social spaces sometimes choose a compromise climate nobody loves.

This belongs in jokes and plans, not lectures.
