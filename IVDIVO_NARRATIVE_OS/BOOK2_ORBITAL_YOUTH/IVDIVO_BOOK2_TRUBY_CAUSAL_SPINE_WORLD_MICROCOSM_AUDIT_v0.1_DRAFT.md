## 1. Verdict

**DO NOT REBUILD Book Two around Truby's seven steps.**

The transferable value is narrower and useful:
1. test ONE MAIN STORY as a single evolving causal spine;
2. distinguish the concrete book objective from developmental themes;
3. use Vela's world as causal social geography;
4. use bounded microcosms to make orbital civilization legible;
5. strengthen opposition as competing models/obligations without inventing a villain;
6. let theme emerge from accumulated consequences.

## 2. Causal-spine gate

The book should be expressible as one continuous chain, even if POV and ordinary life branch:

`ARRIVAL / LIMITED RESPONSIBILITY → LOCAL SUCCESS / TRUST → INTERDEPENDENT SYSTEM PROBLEM → WRONG OR INCOMPLETE MODEL EXPOSED → HUMAN COST → BROADER DIAGNOSIS → SEGMENTED / ACCOUNTABLE RESPONSE → RESULT + AFTERMATH`.

Exact chapter implementation remains controlled by the current reconciled source stack.

Failure condition:
- one character's chapters could be removed and the main problem would remain identical;
- a major branch develops its own climax unrelated to the shared problem;
- new spectacle generates a second book rather than complicating the first.

## 3. Objective versus development

Do not write the A-story objective as:
- “Ollie becomes independent”;
- “the Cohort learns orbital life”;
- “they grow up”;
- “they understand Vela.”

Those are developmental effects.

The operational objective must remain observable and tied to the concrete failure/problem selected by the active architecture. Reader should know when the local problem has been adequately solved/contained/handed off.

## 4. Vela as arena, not tour

Vela can contain many rings, households, transit paths, workshops and civic spaces without fragmenting the novel if they belong to the same story process.

World-detail acceptance test:
`PLACE / INFRASTRUCTURE RULE → WHAT IT CHANGES FOR THIS PERSON → DECISION / FRICTION → CONSEQUENCE`.

Reject/condense:
- impressive technology described only because it is impressive;
- repeated commute tutorials after the reader already understands the rule;
- architecture that does not alter action, relationship, timing, access or interpretation.

## 5. Microcosm rule

Use specific bounded systems to reveal civilization:
- one household;
- one transit sequence;
- one workshop/fabrication context;
- one environmental control zone;
- one public event;
- one training or civic process;
- one human/robot relationship network.

But explicitly prevent `MICROCOSM TOTALIZATION`: one ring or institution is not “how all orbital civilization works.” Different districts, robot communities, specialist schools and households can operate differently.

## 6. Opposition without villain retrofit

Truby's strongest useful principle is the **necessary countermodel**, not the same-goal antagonist rule.

For each major resistance source ask:
- What real benefit is it protecting?
- What evidence does it have?
- What cost would the protagonist's preferred solution create?
- What part of the protagonist's model does it correctly expose?

A competent adult, local resident, engineer, civic process or conscious robot can oppose a proposed action without becoming “the antagonist.”

## 7. Value-shadow gate

Do not invent generic flaws. Use demonstrated values that can become costly under pressure.

Possible examples only if already supported by current characterization:
- Ollie: usefulness/competence → reliable contribution / overcommitment or premature solution confidence;
- Ethan: responsibility → accountability / overcontrol;
- Aoife: documentation/public context → transparency / turning lived people into source material;
- Maya: autonomy → respect for agency / underestimating coordination needs;
- Samir: epistemic caution → disciplined evidence / delayed commitment when action is necessary.

These are diagnostics, not mandatory arcs.

## 8. Theme-through-consequence

Do not solve debates through speeches.

If the book explores optimization, responsibility, privacy, accommodation, civic cost or post-money motivation, show:
`reasonable local choice → repeated/combined consequence → who pays → who notices → what changes`.

The strongest opposing statement should remain partly true after the scene.

## 9. Post-money canon firewall

The active reconciler uses phrases such as `service economy` and several forms of scarcity. Read these under the newer Founder correction:

- **economy** may mean organization/allocation of resources, time, capacity and contribution;
- it must **not** silently restore wages, rent, credits or purchasing-power hierarchy;
- scarcity may remain where the thing is inherently non-fungible or capacity-bound: a particular view, private space, rehearsal slot, commute window, compatible environment, unique role, person's time/attention, mission place;
- ordinary manufactured objects are not scarce merely because “expensive.”

No Truby example is allowed to import money as default motivation.

## 10. Robot-personhood firewall

Truby's story-function/archetype language is especially dangerous here.

For every named conscious robot:
- independent want;
- right to refuse;
- relationships outside the Cohort;
- own value cluster and possible disagreement;
- cannot be replaced by smart equipment without changing the story.

## 11. Specialist consciousness-school firewall

Truby's mentor/magician archetypes must not flatten specialist practitioners into mystical exposition devices.

Require:
`LINEAGE / METHOD → YEARS OF PRACTICE → REPRODUCIBLE OBSERVATION → INTERPRETATION LIMIT → SCHOOL DISAGREEMENT → PERSONAL COST / MOTIVE`.

Mainstream orbital technology remains highly advanced while dimensional/consciousness knowledge remains narrow and practitioner-limited.
