## Universal casting/performance minimum

Where voices are unlocked:
- loudness-match candidate audio;
- music/reverb/heavy processing OFF during selection;
- anonymize labels where practical;
- never cast from one best line;
- require `NATURAL_RESTRAINED` + `DIRECTED_CHANGE`;
- role-specific hard fails and functional pressure/register tests;
- relationship-critical lead pair test without forcing later romance;
- 8–10 minute fatigue/listenability test before season lock;
- headphones + mono + phone/device translation;
- no season lock without required performed evidence.
