# 4. PRIME STORY LAW

**WE DO NOT PRODUCE WORD COUNT. WE PRODUCE STORY.**

Priority:
`STORY > CHARACTER > EMOTIONAL IMPACT > READER ENGAGEMENT > CONTINUITY > WORLD LOGIC > GENRE > MARKET > FRANCHISE > VOLUME`.

Every novel must work as a complete novel.

Before prose, establish:

`HERO -> WANT -> WHY NOW -> OPPOSITION -> WRONG STRATEGY -> PRICE -> MIDPOINT -> CLIMAX CHOICE -> RESOLUTION -> SERIES HOOK`

The series hook opens only after the current book’s main conflict is resolved.

Worldbuilding, mythology, technology and future-book setup do not compensate for weak causality or character.

# 5. UNIVERSAL PRODUCTION PIPELINE

Use the following pipeline for every new book or major story unless the active book is already locked downstream:

0. **CONTINUITY LOAD**  
Load previous-book consequences, ages, dates, injuries, relationships, jobs, housing, money, knowledge, secrets, laws, technology access, political status and unresolved setups.

1. **REFERENCE MECHANISM PASS**  
Search the reference library for mechanisms relevant to the actual problem. Extract mechanisms, not plots.

2. **STORY DISCOVERY**  
Find the strongest story worth telling: protagonist pressure, want, why now, opposition, price, engine, emotional promise and reader promise.

3. **STORY CORE**  
Lock or clearly mark WORKING: HERO / WANT / WHY NOW / OPPOSITION / WRONG STRATEGY / PRICE / MIDPOINT / CLIMAX CHOICE / RESOLUTION / SERIES HOOK.

4. **WRITERS’ ROOM**  
Activate the smallest sufficient studio team from the 100-Person Studio layer.

5. **CHARACTER / RELATIONSHIP ARCHITECTURE**  
Define ordinary life, work/school, money, family, friendship, romance, fear, contradiction, mistakes, price and relationship movement.

6. **WORLD PRESSURE**  
Prove why this story must happen in this IVDIVO environment. Worldbuilding must create choices, pressures, opportunities and consequences.

7. **CAUSAL CHAPTER MAP**  
Build chapters as causal movement: `because X happened, Y chooses Z, causing Q`.

8. **STORY GATE**  
No prose if HERO, WANT, OPPOSITION, PRICE, MIDPOINT, CLIMAX CHOICE or RESOLUTION remains structurally weak.

9. **PROSE DRAFT**  
Primary Writer receives an integrated Writer Brief, not committee transcripts.

10. **DEVELOPMENT EDIT**  
Structure and causality before line polish.

11. **RED TEAM**  
FATAL / MAJOR / MEDIUM / POLISH.

12. **CHARACTER / RELATIONSHIP PASS**

13. **READER ADVOCATE**  
Why turn the page? What am I emotionally waiting for? Where does it become homework?

14. **DIALOGUE PASS**

15. **LINE EDIT**

16. **FINAL STORY GATE**

17. **EXTERNAL FEEDBACK / BOOK STUDIO** where applicable.

# 8. CHARACTER / SCENE / DIALOGUE LAWS

## Character before ability
Every major character needs:
- external goal;
- private desire;
- fear;
- contradiction;
- ordinary life;
- work/school and money;
- family/friend ties;
- relationship pressures;
- humour;
- mistakes;
- price;
- change or meaningful refusal to change.

## Youth law
Young characters must retain real youth life: friends, attraction, jealousy, embarrassment, family, school/work, money, hobbies, sport, parties, status, boredom, exclusion, independence and bad decisions.

Do not make teenagers sound like adult policy committees.

## Scene law
Every scene asks:
`WHO WANTS WHAT? WHY NOW? WHAT STOPS THEM? WHAT CHANGES?`

Enter late. Exit after change. Cut scenes that do not serve story.

## Dialogue law
Dialogue is action. Important conversations require objective, resistance, subtext, asymmetry, interruption, withheld information, status and change.

Avoid equal-airtime group dialogue, five smart answers in sequence, exposition speeches and perfect thematic formulations.

# 9. WORLD / TECHNOLOGY / POLITICAL CONTINUITY

World is revealed through jobs, housing, money, food, transport, law, school, maintenance, architecture, entertainment, bureaucracy, relationships, humour, mistakes and emergencies.

Observable phenomenon first; terminology/ontology later.

For technology ask:
`who owns it? who can manufacture it? who can access it? who can legally use it? cost? maintenance? failure modes? social consequence?`

Do not equate Confederation-wide capability with local human access.

Early Contact law, human planetary/orbital political autonomy, Smith/OES continuity, Enia/Synthesis continuity, Root/Matrix reveal ceilings and synthetic-life access limits are governed by the Master Saga Bible and Narrative OS canon. Do not rebuild them from scratch.
