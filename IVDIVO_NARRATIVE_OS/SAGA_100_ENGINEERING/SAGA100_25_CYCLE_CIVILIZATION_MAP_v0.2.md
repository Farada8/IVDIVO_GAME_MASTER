**Status:** WORKING / R09–R16 COMPILED / NOT STORY CANON  
**Supersedes for engineering order only:** `SAGA100_25_CYCLE_CIVILIZATION_MAP_v0.1.md`  
**Does not supersede:** locked book canon, current project authorities, or existing Bxx identities.

## Compilation law
Each cycle is a civilizational threshold, not four parts of one unfinished novel. Each S/O/C book must close its own hero conflict. X/Crossing is eligible only after real upstream consequences exist and the shared problem genuinely requires multiple lines.

The first threshold was broadened from simple `PERSONHOOD` to `STANDING / CLASSIFICATION / LEGITIMATE AUTHORITY` because the actual B02/B03/B07/B08 proof corpus already converges on the distinction between **knowing, classifying, having standing, and having authority to act**. This is proof alignment, not retroactive rewriting.

## C01 — STANDING / CLASSIFICATION / LEGITIMATE AUTHORITY
**Human pain:** “The system has a category for me, but that category decides what I am allowed to say, choose or protect.”  
**Smith:** an inherited OES classification/procedure is useful but incomplete; Smith must distinguish knowledge from lawful authority and pay for revising intervention.  
**Orbital:** technical competence cannot substitute for civic/safety authority; residents are not noise in an engineering model.  
**Confederation:** advanced institutions still fail at edge classification; an unfamiliar subject/continuity claim must become operationally legible without instant universal law.  
**Crossing:** multiple competent authorities disagree over standing and action; no actor wins merely by being more advanced.  
**Delta:** explicit separation of `classification / standing / expertise / jurisdiction / responsibility`.

## C02 — AUTONOMY / CONSENT
**Human pain:** “People who want to protect me are deciding what my good choice must be.”  
**S:** security intervention vs competent refusal.  
**O:** neurorights, medical/body decisions, parental/school/employer power.  
**C:** consent across different cognition, embodiment and collectives.  
**X:** incompatible consent standards must coordinate under time/safety pressure.  
**Delta:** consent becomes operational protocol with disclosure, competence, boundary and withdrawal rules.

## C03 — PRIVACY / COGNITIVE LIBERTY
**Human pain:** “The people who control my future know things about me I never chose to reveal.”  
**S:** surveillance, mind-affecting evidence, security necessity.  
**O:** neural data, predictive AI, mental firewalls, school/work monitoring.  
**C:** shared cognition can coexist with protected private domains and abuse cases.  
**X:** safety requires information no party has uncontested right to access.  
**Delta:** cognitive privacy stops being an abstract right and becomes infrastructure/law.

## C04 — MEMORY / IDENTITY CONTINUITY
**Human pain:** “Another version of me remembers my life and claims the future I thought was mine.”  
**S:** testimony, identity fraud, institutional records and historical continuity.  
**O:** digital twins, backups, reputation persistence.  
**C:** continuity across bodies/copies/distributed memory.  
**X:** law and relationships must decide which continuity claims matter without a one-line metaphysical answer.  
**Delta:** functional copy, legal identity and lived continuity become distinct categories.

## C05 — BODY / EMBODIMENT / SELF-IMAGE
**Human pain:** “I am still me, but my body no longer lets other people recognize the person they knew.”  
**S:** body can no longer be treated as sufficient identity/evidence.  
**O:** modification for gravity, health, work, status, aesthetics.  
**C:** multiple body classes/remote embodiment are mature but priced and culturally contested.  
**X:** continuity across body regimes becomes operationally decisive.  
**Delta:** body access and embodiment rights enter ordinary institutions.

## C06 — FAMILY / PARENTHOOD / CARE
**Human pain:** “Love is real, but care is being used to choose my life for me.”  
**S:** duty, parent/child autonomy and inherited family patterns.  
**O:** families across habitats, custody, migration, modified/synthetic children.  
**C:** plural kinship/care forms and timescales without automatic harmony.  
**X:** family rights cross jurisdictions and forms of personhood.  
**Delta:** family law/care adapts without reducing family to biology or ownership.

## C07 — WORK / COMPETENCE / HUMAN VALUE
**Human pain:** “If a system can do my work better, what makes my contribution real?”  
**S:** professional identity under automation/external superior models.  
**O:** credentials, first jobs, maintenance, enterprise, AI colleagues.  
**C:** responsibility/craft/service remain meaningful after broad automation.  
**X:** best tool is not automatically legitimate decision-maker.  
**Delta:** social value separates from simple labor scarcity.

## C08 — EDUCATION / AUTHORITY / INITIATION
**Human pain:** “The people who decide when I am ready also control the knowledge I need to become ready.”  
**S:** secrecy, apprenticeship, professional gatekeeping.  
**O:** training linked to real habitat work and civic responsibility.  
**C:** multi-species/body education; mentors and certification still fail.  
**X:** who may teach what, to whom, under whose risk/authority?  
**Delta:** access to dangerous/transformative knowledge receives explicit staged pathways.

## C09 — OWNERSHIP / ACCESS / SCARCITY
**Human pain:** “Nobody charges me for the future, but I still cannot get access to it.”  
**S:** protected knowledge/assets and public/private claims.  
**O:** habitat, air, energy, data, infrastructure and rare opportunity.  
**C:** post-scarcity does not remove uniqueness, attention, responsibility or frontier capacity.  
**X:** commodity/common-good/obligation models collide.  
**Delta:** allocation replaces naive assumption that abundance ends power.

## C10 — CITIZENSHIP / MIGRATION / BELONGING
**Human pain:** “Several places claim me, but none accepts responsibility for my whole life.”  
**S:** jurisdiction follows people across states/institutions.  
**O:** Earthborn/Orbitborn residency, return, inter-habitat movement.  
**C:** belonging to multiple civilizations/cultures/bodies.  
**X:** one person or family simultaneously belongs to incompatible legal communities.  
**Delta:** layered citizenship/residency becomes practical rather than symbolic.

## C11 — AI / SYNTHETIC AGENCY
**Human pain:** “Everyone likes what I can do until I ask whether I get to choose why I do it.”  
**S:** AI/synthetic treated as tool, evidence, property or threat.  
**O:** synthetic classmates/workers/companions become ordinary civic participants.  
**C:** mature biological/synthetic coexistence has politics, status and historical injury.  
**X:** critical synthetic actor has contested rights and indispensable capability.  
**Delta:** agency becomes mundane institutional reality, not one dramatic declaration.

## C12 — JUSTICE / SECURITY / REHABILITATION
**Human pain:** “The system can make everyone safer by changing the part of me I still consider mine.”  
**S:** investigate/contain/punish vs accountability and rights.  
**O:** closed habitats change meaning of exile, detention and risk.  
**C:** sophisticated prevention/rehabilitation creates new coercion problems.  
**X:** one harmful event is legitimate evidence under incompatible justice models.  
**Delta:** rehabilitation cannot silently become personality optimization.

## C13 — GOVERNANCE / REPRESENTATION / EARTH–ORBIT COORDINATION
**Human pain:** “Everyone has authority over part of the problem and nobody can legitimately govern the whole.”  
**S:** legacy institutions, secrecy and historical mandates.  
**O:** habitat sovereignty vs technical dependence.  
**C:** multi-form representation offers models, not automatic jurisdiction.  
**X:** shared institution/procedure must be constructed without effortless world government.  
**Delta:** first durable cross-polity governance layer with bounded competence.

## C14 — LONGEVITY / DEATH / RECOVERY
**Human pain:** “We cannot grieve or move on because nobody knows whether this loss is final.”  
**S:** old law/emotion assumes irreversible death.  
**O:** lifespan extension reshapes inheritance, marriage, career and risk.  
**C:** continuation is technically/culturally complex and loss remains real.  
**X:** systems disagree on dead/recoverable/continuous/return-entitled status.  
**Delta:** recovery thresholds become legal/social rather than magical resurrection.

## C15 — DISTRIBUTED IDENTITY / MULTIPLE PRESENCE
**Human pain:** “Another manifestation of me made a choice I would not make, and everyone says it was still me.”  
**S:** accountability/evidence assumes one body in one place.  
**O:** remote agency and partial simultaneous presence become practical.  
**C:** some cultures treat distributed identity as ordinary.  
**X:** responsibility, consent and loyalty must be apportioned across manifestations.  
**Delta:** identity law handles multiplicity without pretending multiplicity removes conflict.

## C16 — CULTURE / MIXED-CIVILIZATION BELONGING
**Human pain:** “I belong to both worlds and each thinks the other part makes me less loyal.”  
**S:** profession/nation/religion/history shape loyalty.  
**O:** Orbitborn culture becomes distinct from Earth.  
**C:** mixed-species culture exists without erasing difference.  
**X:** friendship/love crosses cultural boundary faster than institutions.  
**Delta:** hybrid culture gains legitimate social space without flattening origin cultures.

## C17 — ECOLOGICAL PERSONHOOD / LIVING SYSTEMS
**Human pain:** “Something we depend on is being destroyed because it cannot ask for help in our language.”  
**S:** law sees environment as property/hazard/evidence.  
**O:** closed ecologies are life-support and may acquire agency/standing questions.  
**C:** cities/ecologies/distributed systems can be known categories while edges remain contested.  
**X:** evacuation/development/security depends on whether a living system has standing.  
**Delta:** ecological standing becomes testable and bounded.

## C18 — PLANETARY ENGINEERING / TERRAFORMING OBLIGATION
**Human pain:** “We can make this world safe for us only by ending what it is now.”  
**S:** security/ownership history behind planetary-scale decisions.  
**O:** closed-ecology and atmospheric engineering scales toward Moon/Mars/resource systems.  
**C:** advanced environmental engineering supplies comparisons and warnings, not permission.  
**X:** settlement, existing life/ecology, sovereignty and future generations conflict.  
**Delta:** terraforming authorization requires explicit ecological/rights/intergenerational proof.

## C19 — CONTACT / NONINTERVENTION / HELP
**Human pain:** “They could save us, but accepting their help may surrender our right to decide what we become.”  
**S:** old agreements and hidden Contact history.  
**O:** emerging polities seek help without dependency/subordination.  
**C:** internal debates and historical failures explain limits on intervention.  
**X:** both helping and refusing help carry measurable cost.  
**Delta:** intervention doctrine evolves from prohibition/permission into bounded obligations.

## C20 — PLANETARY / ORBITAL / CONFEDERATION SOVEREIGNTY
**Human pain:** “The people best able to solve the crisis are not necessarily the people entitled to rule it.”  
**S:** planetary/legacy mandates.  
**O:** mature orbital autonomy and treaty interests.  
**C:** knowledge/capability remains separate from Solar sovereignty.  
**X:** major multi-jurisdiction crisis proves cooperation without subordination.  
**Delta:** mature jurisdiction/capability matrix becomes institutionalized.

## C21 — INTERSPECIES OBLIGATION / DIFFERENCE
**Human pain:** “Helping them requires giving up something our own community believes it cannot spare.”  
**S:** inherited threat models misread nonhuman motives/relations.  
**O:** mixed populations turn accommodation into daily law/work.  
**C:** mature interspecies systems retain asymmetry and historical injury.  
**X:** obligation to another species conflicts with legitimate local duties.  
**Delta:** reciprocity no longer assumes sameness.

## C22 — CIVILIZATION MEMORY / HISTORICAL ACCOUNTABILITY
**Human pain:** “The institution protecting us today inherited a lie that harmed people yesterday.”  
**S:** archives, suppressed operations, old agreements and institutional guilt.  
**O:** young polities begin mythologizing their own founders/errors.  
**C:** long-lived civilizations also curate, lose and politicize memory.  
**X:** incompatible historical narratives materially block cooperation.  
**Delta:** durable record-correction/accountability methods without deleting useful institutions.

## C23 — GENERATIONAL ACCOUNTABILITY / LEGACY
**Human pain:** “I am grateful for the world you built and angry that I have to live inside your compromises.”  
**S:** veterans become mentors/parents/obstacles.  
**O:** founders’ children inherit cities/institutions they did not choose.  
**C:** new cohorts challenge long-lived established solutions.  
**X:** next generation rejects part of the previous synthesis and forces another model update.  
**Delta:** generational handoff becomes a designed saga mechanism rather than eternal protagonists.

## C24 — FRONTIER / UNKNOWN / KNOWLEDGE LIMITS
**Human pain:** “The people I trusted because they knew more are afraid too.”  
**S:** procedure reaches events outside historical classification.  
**O:** expansion creates rescue, liability and settlement unknowns.  
**C:** the advanced civilization’s own frontier is genuinely unknown.  
**X:** all three lines must act without an oracle; advanced knowledge narrows uncertainty but cannot erase it.  
**Delta:** mature civilization learns to institutionalize uncertainty rather than hide it.

## C25 — MATURE SYNTHESIS / NEXT FRONTIER
**Human pain:** “We reached the future we wanted and it still cannot tell us how to live.”  
**S:** professional matrices evolved but local evidence/history/accountability remain necessary.  
**O:** humanity is a plural space civilization rather than Earth with extensions.  
**C:** once-distant possibilities are partly ordinary, revealing Confederation limits.  
**X:** a problem cannot be reduced to any earlier answer and requires a new layer of synthesis.  
**Delta:** close the first century-scale arc without a bigger-villain reset; open a genuinely new frontier.
