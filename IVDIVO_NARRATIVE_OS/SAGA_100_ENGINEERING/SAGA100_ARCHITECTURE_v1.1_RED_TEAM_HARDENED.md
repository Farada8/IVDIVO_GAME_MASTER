## 0. Unchanged core

IVDIVO remains one evolving saga/universe with four long-horizon lenses:

`SMITH/OES | ORBITAL | CONFEDERATION/FRONTIER | CROSSING`

Each produced book must be a complete human story. `PROJECT_ID` remains durable. `SAGA_SLOT_ID` is a separate planning coordinate. NARR-009 remains the long-run Human Problem/Seed/Mechanism/Wave Bank. Locked/current project authority outranks this layer.

## 1. HARDENING A — 3+1 is an adaptive default, not a mandatory ordinal formula

Preferred long-run rhythm remains:

`S -> O -> C -> X`

but this is **cadence intent**, not an obligation to publish an X-book every fourth ordinal position.

A Crossing may enter production only if `CROSSING_ELIGIBILITY = PASS`.

If eligibility is not earned:
- set X slot to `DEFERRED`;
- do not invent a crossover problem to fill the grid;
- another independent line novel may occur when continuity, story value and production evidence justify it;
- the unresolved threshold remains open until a shared problem is genuinely generated by consequences.

`100 = capacity ceiling, not quota.` Empty/deferred/retired-unused slots are valid outcomes.

## 2. HARDENING B — Human Problem First outranks the philosophical curriculum

Order of story authority inside discovery:

`HUMAN WANT/PAIN -> CURRENT ARENA/PRESSURE -> STORY ENGINE -> IVDIVO AMPLIFIER -> CIVILIZATION THRESHOLD INTERPRETATION`

Never:

`THRESHOLD -> CHARACTERS ASSIGNED TO EXPLAIN IT`.

A threshold is useful when it helps the studio select/compare durable consequences. It must not become protagonist dialogue or a required thesis conclusion.

Acceptance canary:
> Can the book's central human pain be described without Matrix, Orbit, Confederation, embodiment, AI, dimension, synthesis or other IVDIVO ontology terms?

If no, the studio may have worldbuilding rather than a novel.

## 3. HARDENING C — legacy alignment cannot retrofit accepted books

B02/B03/B07/B08 align strongly enough to serve as a C01 proof corpus for `Standing / Classification / Legitimate Authority`.

That alignment means only:
- their existing mechanisms can test Saga100 contracts;
- their existing consequences can inform candidate long-horizon mapping.

It does **not** mean:
- rewrite the novel to emphasize C01;
- insert a missing philosophical speech;
- add a crossover hook;
- reorder publication numbers;
- force B04/B05/B06 to complete a neat table.

`PROJECT STORY > SAGA SLOT NEATNESS`.

## 4. HARDENING D — capability ladder is a proof model with an evidence-backed exception path

Default progression:
`EXISTS_ELSEWHERE -> KNOWN -> CONTACT_ACCESS -> RESTRICTED_USE -> LOCAL_PROTOTYPE -> LEGALIZED_USE -> SCALABLE_MANUFACTURE -> ORDINARY_LIFE`.

Real adoption may skip a state because of:
- emergency import;
- elite/black-market access;
- external manufacturing;
- abrupt law/court decision;
- captured/borrowed system;
- crisis deployment;
- other story-proven mechanism.

A skip is allowed only when the ledger records:
`FROM -> TO -> MECHANISM -> ACCESS_SCOPE -> EVIDENCE -> PRICE/EXTERNALITY -> WHY_INTERMEDIATE_STATE_DID_NOT_APPLY`.

A skipped state is not automatically erased for the rest of society.

## 5. HARDENING E — S-line is a professional/civilizational lens, not a promise of unexplained Smith immortality

Current canon remains: Smith is field-active throughout the currently established saga horizon and must not be made obsolete merely because Confederation knows more.

Long-horizon rule:
- use Smith personally while chronology, character value and canon support it;
- do not kill/retire him simply to satisfy generational architecture;
- do not keep him physically unchanged for a century merely to fill S-slots;
- later S-line functions may be carried by Smith, colleagues, students, descendants, successor professionals or evolved institutions **only when story/canon earns the transition**.

The invariant is not “Smith appears forever.” The invariant is:
> local evidence, historical memory, professional responsibility and accountable human institutions remain necessary even in a more advanced civilization.

## 6. HARDENING F — Confederation cannot become the saga's moral referee

Every major C/X use of Confederation must test:
`CAPABILITY + LIMIT + COST + ACCESS_BOUNDARY + GOVERNANCE + INTERNAL_DISAGREEMENT + FAILURE/HISTORICAL_ERROR + UNKNOWN`.

Additionally:
- a Confederation actor may be wrong, incomplete or locally inapplicable;
- an advanced concept cannot settle human/orbital jurisdiction by assertion;
- X-book resolution must include evidence/knowledge/authority unavailable to Confederation alone when the story uses a Confederation contribution;
- no recurring pattern of `advanced visitor states correct ethics -> humans adopt it`.

## 7. HARDENING G — shipping/WIP guard against self-perpetuating meta-work

Saga100 engineering is subordinate to production.

New macro-engine work is authorized only when at least one is true:
1. a live book/cycle decision is blocked by a missing contract/ledger;
2. a real production defect was observed;
3. an existing contract failed or produced ambiguous routing;
4. a promotion gate requires proof/regression;
5. the work materially reduces future repetition/continuity risk.

Otherwise route capacity to:
`complete book -> external reader/editor evidence -> audio/release -> market evidence -> next book`.

Forbidden:
- creating another 32/64 meta sprint merely because the previous one ended;
- rebuilding stable schemas without a defect;
- expanding 25 thresholds into 100 detailed plots before consequences exist.

## 8. HARDENING H — UNKNOWN/HOLD is a first-class roadmap state

Long maps must display unresolved authority rather than smoothing it away.

Current examples:
- B04 = `AUTHORITY_CONFLICT_HOLD`;
- B05 = `WORKING_DEFAULT_ONLY / HOLD`;
- B06 = `SOURCE_RECOVERY_REQUIRED / HOLD`;
- B08 = design-green but `CROSSING_EXECUTION_ELIGIBILITY_HOLD` until upstream substitutions.

A polished roadmap containing no UNKNOWN/HOLD across a 100-book horizon is itself a drift warning.

## 9. HARDENING I — a Crossing does not require equal cameo participation by all three line brands

Crossing eligibility requires **2+ irreducible line dependencies**, not equal screen time or mandatory cameo from all three.

The third lens may be absent if it has no causal function. If it appears, it must change evidence, authority, relationship, resource, choice or consequence.

This protects against franchise roll-call scenes.

## 10. HARDENING J — civilization delta may be local, not constitution-sized

Every produced book should leave a durable consequence, but “civilization delta” can be:
- a changed procedure;
- a local precedent;
- a failed pilot whose failure becomes knowledge;
- a bounded access rule;
- a professional price;
- a relationship bridge;
- a housing/work/institution consequence;
- a documented limit;
- an unresolved obligation carried forward.

Do not inflate every ending into planetary law.
