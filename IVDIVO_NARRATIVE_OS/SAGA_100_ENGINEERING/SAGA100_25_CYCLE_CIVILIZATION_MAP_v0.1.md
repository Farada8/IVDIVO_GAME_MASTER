# IVDIVO SAGA100 — 25-CYCLE CIVILIZATION PROGRESSION MAP v0.1

**Status:** WORKING QUESTION MAP / NOT LOCKED PLOT ORDER  
**Purpose:** long-horizon philosophical/civilizational progression for up to 25 four-book cycles.  
**Law:** each threshold must become four complete human stories; no cycle is produced merely because a row exists.

## C01 — PERSONHOOD / WHO COUNTS AS A SUBJECT?
- **Smith:** inherited classification systems distinguish citizen, threat, witness, asset, anomaly, property; a case exposes a category that fails morally/operationally.
- **Orbital:** synthetic/nonstandard persons must live, work, study, rent, travel and be protected by real law.
- **Confederation:** personhood already spans unfamiliar bodies/species/distributed agents, but edge cases and internal disputes remain.
- **Crossing:** different systems must decide who has standing, rights and responsibility in one shared emergency/conflict.
- **Durable delta:** first explicit expansion/clarification of subjecthood.

## C02 — AUTONOMY / CONSENT
- **Smith:** security intervention vs individual consent; protection can become coercion.
- **Orbital:** neurorights, parental authority, medical/body modification, AI mediation.
- **Confederation:** consent across embodiment, radically different cognition and collective systems.
- **Crossing:** incompatible consent standards collide; synthesis must protect agency without making safety impossible.

## C03 — MEMORY / IDENTITY CONTINUITY
- **Smith:** evidence, altered testimony, institutional memory, identity fraud or recovered histories.
- **Orbital:** digital twins, memory aids, backups, reputation persistence.
- **Confederation:** continuity across bodies, copies, distributed memory and long lifespans.
- **Crossing:** law/relationships must determine which continuity claims matter without a simplistic metaphysical answer.

## C04 — PRIVACY / COGNITIVE LIBERTY
- **Smith:** surveillance, secrecy, mind-affecting evidence, security necessity.
- **Orbital:** neural data, predictive AI, school/work monitoring, mental firewalls.
- **Confederation:** sophisticated cognition-sharing norms with protected private domains and failure cases.
- **Crossing:** public safety requires information no one system has an uncontested right to access.

## C05 — FAMILY / PARENTHOOD / CARE
- **Smith:** professional duty collides with parent/child autonomy and inherited family patterns.
- **Orbital:** families split across habitats, longevity, custody, migration, synthetic/modified children.
- **Confederation:** broader kinship/care forms, multi-body presence, species-cultural differences.
- **Crossing:** family rights and obligations cross jurisdictions and forms of personhood.

## C06 — WORK / COMPETENCE / HUMAN VALUE
- **Smith:** professional identity under systems that know more, automate more or classify differently.
- **Orbital:** first jobs, maintenance, enterprise, AI colleagues, credentialing and unemployment transitions.
- **Confederation:** work after automation/scarcity reduction still matters through responsibility, craft, service, discovery and status.
- **Crossing:** competence from different civilizations must cooperate without assuming the most advanced tool equals the best decision-maker.

## C07 — EDUCATION / AUTHORITY / INITIATION
- **Smith:** training, secrecy, professional mentorship and the danger of gatekeeping knowledge.
- **Orbital:** new education models tied to real habitats/jobs/AI and civic responsibility.
- **Confederation:** education across species, bodies and long developmental horizons; mentors still fail.
- **Crossing:** who may teach what, to whom, under whose authority, with what risk?

## C08 — OWNERSHIP / ACCESS / PROPERTY
- **Smith:** seized assets, protected knowledge, institutional ownership, private vs public claims.
- **Orbital:** habitat equity, air/energy access, data, AI ownership, infrastructure and land equivalents.
- **Confederation:** domains beyond ordinary scarcity still contain scarce access, responsibility and unique resources.
- **Crossing:** incompatible property/access regimes must resolve a concrete claim without importing one system wholesale.

## C09 — CITIZENSHIP / MIGRATION / BELONGING
- **Smith:** jurisdiction follows people across countries/institutions; records and status can trap them.
- **Orbital:** Earthborn/Orbitborn, residency, citizenship, movement between habitats and return to Earth.
- **Confederation:** belonging across civilizations, bodies and cultural identities.
- **Crossing:** a person belongs to more than one polity and no single system can define them completely.

## C10 — JUSTICE / SECURITY / REHABILITATION
- **Smith:** investigation, containment, punishment, risk assessment, institutional accountability.
- **Orbital:** small closed habitats make punishment/exile/security materially different.
- **Confederation:** advanced risk prevention and rehabilitation create new civil-liberty problems rather than eliminating justice.
- **Crossing:** one harmful actor/incident is judged under incompatible security philosophies.

## C11 — AI / SYNTHETIC AGENCY
- **Smith:** old systems treat AI/synthetics as tool, evidence source, asset or threat.
- **Orbital:** synthetic classmates, workers, companions and civic participants become ordinary enough to create mundane conflict.
- **Confederation:** mature synthetic/biological coexistence includes politics, status, culture and disagreement.
- **Crossing:** an AI/synthetic actor has knowledge/capability critical to a shared problem but contested authority/rights.

## C12 — BODY / EMBODIMENT / SELF-IMAGE
- **Smith:** identification and legal/professional assumptions fail when body no longer guarantees identity.
- **Orbital:** modification for gravity, health, aesthetics, work and social status.
- **Confederation:** multiple body classes and remote embodiment are mature but not psychologically costless.
- **Crossing:** a person’s legal/social continuity across body regimes becomes operationally decisive.

## C13 — LONGEVITY / DEATH / CONTINUITY
- **Smith:** old legal and emotional systems assume finite lives and irreversible death.
- **Orbital:** lifespan extension changes family, career, inheritance, marriage and risk.
- **Confederation:** continuation can be technologically/culturally complex while loss remains real.
- **Crossing:** systems disagree about when a person is dead, recoverable, continuous or entitled to return.

## C14 — DISTRIBUTED IDENTITY / MULTIPLE PRESENCE
- **Smith:** evidence/accountability assumes one person in one place.
- **Orbital:** telepresence/digital agency makes partial multiple presence practical.
- **Confederation:** distributed or multi-body identity may be normal in some cultures.
- **Crossing:** responsibility, loyalty and consent must be allocated across simultaneous manifestations.

## C15 — GOVERNANCE / REPRESENTATION
- **Smith:** institutions protect order but preserve legacy interests and secrecy.
- **Orbital:** habitats experiment with governance under technical dependence and small-population pressure.
- **Confederation:** multi-species/multi-form representation solves some old problems and creates others.
- **Crossing:** no side has uncontested sovereignty; legitimate coordination must be constructed for one crisis.

## C16 — SCARCITY / POST-SCARCITY BOUNDARIES
- **Smith:** scarcity drives corruption, secrecy, coercion and unequal access.
- **Orbital:** abundance in information coexists with scarce space, energy, launch capacity and trusted infrastructure.
- **Confederation:** some material scarcity may recede while attention, uniqueness, access, responsibility and frontier capacity remain scarce.
- **Crossing:** economic assumptions clash around a resource/service one side sees as commodity and another as obligation/common good.

## C17 — CULTURE / MIXED CIVILIZATION BELONGING
- **Smith:** tradition, religion, nation, profession and hidden institutional cultures shape loyalty.
- **Orbital:** distinct Orbitborn cultures emerge rather than copying Earth.
- **Confederation:** mixed-species culture exists without erasing deep difference.
- **Crossing:** personal relationship crosses a boundary faster than law/culture can absorb.

## C18 — CONTACT / NONINTERVENTION / HELP
- **Smith:** when does outside knowledge become interference in human institutions?
- **Orbital:** emerging polities want help without becoming dependent or subordinate.
- **Confederation:** advanced civilization has historical reasons for limits on intervention and debates them internally.
- **Crossing:** refusing help and giving help both carry real costs; no clean moral escape.

## C19 — ECOLOGICAL PERSONHOOD / LIVING SYSTEMS
- **Smith:** old law treats environments/systems as property, hazard or evidence.
- **Orbital:** closed ecologies are life-support infrastructure and potential actors in civic decisions.
- **Confederation:** some living systems may be distributed persons/ecologies/cities.
- **Crossing:** evacuation/development/security decisions depend on whether a living system has standing.

## C20 — PLANETARY / ORBITAL SOVEREIGNTY
- **Smith:** planetary agencies carry historical jurisdiction and security claims.
- **Orbital:** mature habitats/polities demand autonomy and develop distinct law/economy.
- **Confederation:** external knowledge does not settle Solar political legitimacy.
- **Crossing:** a crisis crosses sovereignty boundaries and tests whether cooperation can occur without subordination.

## C21 — INTERSPECIES OBLIGATION / DIFFERENCE
- **Smith:** inherited threat models misread beings whose motives/social structures differ from human assumptions.
- **Orbital:** mixed populations make accommodation, work and law practical daily issues.
- **Confederation:** mature interspecies institutions still face asymmetry and historical injury.
- **Crossing:** duty to another species conflicts with local human/orbital obligations.

## C22 — FRONTIER / RISK / UNKNOWN
- **Smith:** procedure is built from prior cases; frontier events exceed classification.
- **Orbital:** exploration/expansion creates new infrastructure and rescue responsibilities.
- **Confederation:** even advanced civilization has a frontier it does not understand.
- **Crossing:** all three systems face genuine unknown; advanced knowledge helps but cannot remove uncertainty.

## C23 — CIVILIZATION MEMORY / HISTORY / FORGOTTEN FAILURE
- **Smith:** archives, suppressed operations, institutional guilt and old agreements return.
- **Orbital:** young institutions create their own myths and begin forgetting founding mistakes.
- **Confederation:** long-lived civilizations also curate, lose, dispute and politicize memory.
- **Crossing:** incompatible historical narratives materially affect present cooperation.

## C24 — GENERATIONAL ACCOUNTABILITY / LEGACY
- **Smith:** older professionals become mentors/parents/obstacles and face consequences of systems they maintained.
- **Orbital:** founders’ children inherit institutions they did not choose.
- **Confederation:** long-lived mentors/cultures confront new cohorts rejecting established solutions.
- **Crossing:** next generation evaluates what all three civilizations built and refuses part of the inherited synthesis.

## C25 — MATURE SYNTHESIS / THE NEXT UNKNOWN FRONTIER
- **Smith:** old professional matrices have evolved but remain necessary as local memory/accountability.
- **Orbital:** humanity has become a genuinely plural space civilization rather than an extension of Earth.
- **Confederation:** the once-distant future is now partly ordinary, revealing the Confederation’s own limits/frontier.
- **Crossing:** mature cooperation faces a problem none of the three lenses can reduce to earlier answers.
- **Durable delta:** close the first century-scale developmental arc while opening a genuinely new horizon, not a bigger villain.

# Progression safeguards

1. These 25 rows are **questions/thresholds**, not locked plots or mandatory chronological decades.
2. Adjacent cycles may be reordered when actual book consequences demand it.
3. A later cycle may not assume a capability reached `ORDINARY_LIFE` unless the CivilizationProgressionLedger proves the transition.
4. Crossing resolves the current cycle’s shared conflict before opening the next threshold.
5. Every cycle must produce a durable world delta and a durable character/relationship consequence.
6. Smith/OES remains relevant through local responsibility, evidence, history, law and professional evolution; Confederation knowledge does not obsolete him by fiat.
7. Orbital remains the bridge of achievable human development, not visual scenery between Earth and Confederation.
8. Confederation remains aspirational/advanced but internally plural, fallible and bounded.
