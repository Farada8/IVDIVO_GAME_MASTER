## F. Audio/production engineering lessons that generalize

The current Audio Production stack and ROOM917 work demonstrate useful generic engineering habits:
- exact authority before execution;
- machine-readable manifests;
- PASS/FAIL acceptance gates;
- fail-closed unresolved anchors;
- selective repair rather than global rewrite;
- real human evidence kept separate from simulated model judgment;
- persistence/readback proofs.

Only these abstract mechanisms are generalized. Audio-specific clue chains, provider IDs, render details and project-specific solutions remain project-scoped.
