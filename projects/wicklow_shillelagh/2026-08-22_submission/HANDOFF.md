## Framing rules
- Generated Shillelagh images are `preliminary concept studies / concept visualisations`, not completed murals.
- Do not present concept visualisations as physically executed work.
- Final mural must be developed through verified local research, community input, actual site/wall assessment, budget and technical feasibility.
- Preferred role wording: `mural artist / design and execution lead`.
- Because the artist is currently Dublin-based, do not assert Wicklow lead-applicant eligibility. If required, work with an eligible Wicklow artist/organisation as formal lead.
