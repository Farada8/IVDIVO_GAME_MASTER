## Submission framing rules
- Shillelagh generated images are preliminary concept studies / concept visualisations, not completed murals.
- Do not present concept visualisations as physically executed work.
- Final mural must be developed through verified local research, community input, actual site/wall assessment, budget and technical feasibility.
- Preferred role wording: `mural artist / design and execution lead`.
- Do not assert Wicklow lead-applicant eligibility while the artist is Dublin-based; if required, work with an eligible Wicklow artist/organisation as formal lead.
