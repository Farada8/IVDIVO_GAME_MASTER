## Artist positioning
Yaromyr Farada — Visual & Monumental Artist, Dublin.

Professional facts used in the submission:
- National Academy of Fine Arts and Architecture, Kyiv, 2005; studio of Mykola Storozhenko.
- Member, National Union of Artists of Ukraine, since 2008.
- Bureau of Monumental Art, National Union of Artists of Ukraine, 2009–2017.
- Steel Art Studio, Kyiv, 2003–2010.
- Earlier private interior wall paintings / decorative mural commissions in Kyiv.
- A substantial part of the photographic archive of completed Kyiv mural commissions was lost with hard drives during the war in Ukraine; the surviving portfolio does not fully represent the earlier mural practice.
- Fine Art Ukraine 2011 / Mystetskyi Arsenal catalogue documentation is included as surviving professional evidence.

## Submission framing rules
- Shillelagh generated images are preliminary concept studies / concept visualisations, not completed murals.
- Do not present concept visualisations as physically executed work.
- Final mural must be developed through verified local research, community input, actual site/wall assessment, budget and technical feasibility.
- Preferred role wording: `mural artist / design and execution lead`.
- Do not assert Wicklow lead-applicant eligibility while the artist is Dublin-based; if required, work with an eligible Wicklow artist/organisation as formal lead.
