# Final email to Ciaran Kilbride

**Subject: Shillelagh Community Mural Project – Expression of Interest**

Dear Ciaran,

I am writing in response to your call for an artist for the Shillelagh Community Mural Project.

I am a visual and monumental artist currently based in Dublin, with professional experience in monumental and decorative art, painting, mural work, mosaic, stained glass, sculpture and architectural art.

I graduated from the National Academy of Fine Arts and Architecture in Kyiv in 2005, where I studied monumental art in the studio of Mykola Storozhenko. I have been a member of the National Union of Artists of Ukraine since 2008, and from 2009 to 2017 I worked with its Bureau of Monumental Art, developing and coordinating monumental and decorative art projects.

Before relocating to Ireland, I also worked on interior wall paintings and decorative mural commissions for private clients in Kyiv. Unfortunately, during the war in Ukraine, the hard drives containing a substantial part of the photographic archive of these completed projects were lost, so my surviving portfolio does not fully represent the extent of my earlier mural practice.

I was particularly interested in your intention that the Shillelagh project should reflect the identity, heritage, landscape and community of the place rather than simply function as decorative painting.

I have already begun developing a number of preliminary visual concepts specifically for Shillelagh, exploring themes such as Tomnafinnoge Wood, blackthorn craft, Rathgall, Aghowle, the old railway, local memory, maps, emigration, present-day community life and the relationship between heritage and future generations.

These are preliminary project studies rather than fixed final designs. I would expect the final mural to be developed through verified local research, community input, the actual wall and streetscape, technical conditions and the available budget.

I would be very interested in contributing to the artistic development, design and physical execution of the mural.

I understand that the Strategic Project Award has specific eligibility requirements concerning the lead applicant’s connection with County Wicklow. As I am currently based in Dublin, if an eligible Wicklow-based artist or organisation needs to formally lead the application, I would be very happy to participate as the mural artist and design/execution lead within that structure.

I have attached a short portfolio including my CV, documented previous work and several preliminary Shillelagh mural concepts.

Given the application deadline, I am available to discuss the project immediately.
