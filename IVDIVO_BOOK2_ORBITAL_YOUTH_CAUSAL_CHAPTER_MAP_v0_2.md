# PART I — UP

## CHAPTER 1 — THE LAST GROUND
**POV:** Ollie.  
**Want:** leave Earth as a worker, not a youth-program tourist.  
**Action:** departure day; rescue-rover maker team has a milestone he will miss.  
**Ordinary price:** he cannot be in both futures.  
**World reveal:** health/body/work compatibility packing, luggage mass, shift gear, personal-space limits.  
**Choice:** takes orbital maintenance placement rather than easier prestige placement.  
**Turn:** his team sends him the rover problem they will now solve without him.  
**Change:** ambition already costs belonging.

## CHAPTER 2 — DUBLIN VERTICAL
**POV:** Aoife.  
**Want:** record departure without making orbital travel a tourism commercial.  
**World reveal:** Ireland's exceptional deep access to orbital technology is visible through functioning infrastructure, not explained as lore.  
**Action:** historic/ordinary Dublin coexists with transfer architecture, work commuters, families and freight.  
**Relationship:** Ethan tries to suggest narrative structure; Aoife refuses.  
**Turn:** transfer processing physically separates the five into different routes/prep groups.  
**Change:** first real fracture of group synchrony.  
**Forbidden:** explaining why Ireland has the exception.

## CHAPTER 3 — ARRIVAL WITHOUT A FLOOR
**POV:** Maya.  
**Want:** look local immediately.  
**Obstacle:** spin transitions, route etiquette, body adaptation and host rules make her visibly planet-born.  
**World reveal:** braided city, gravity changes, mixed-species commuting, AI mediation.  
**Turn:** a local teenager corrects her after she blocks a transfer lane.  
**Change:** wonder + embarrassment, no punishment.

## CHAPTER 4 — FIVE ADDRESSES
**POV:** Ethan.  
**Want:** preserve one shared schedule.  
**Obstacle:** five hosts, jobs, training blocks, sport, documentary work and commute times.  
**World reveal:** housing compatibility, transit scarcity, private-space differences.  
**Turn:** even dinner requires negotiation.  
**Relationship:** Aoife enjoys refusing calendar absorption.  
**Change:** the five become a network rather than a single social unit.

## CHAPTER 5 — FIRST SHIFT
**POV:** Ollie.  
**Want:** be trusted with meaningful work.  
**Obstacle:** boring tasks first.  
**Action:** inspection/tagging/cleaning/calibration support.  
**Adult relationship:** Mara Saye (working name) is competent, unsentimental, not hostile.  
**Turn:** Ollie catches a mundane practical defect because he did the boring task correctly.  
**Payoff:** earns diagnostic work.

## CHAPTER 6 — THE RING PEOPLE WANT
**POV:** Ollie.  
**Want:** understand the opening deadline.  
**Action:** tour with future residents, not executives.  
**World reveal:** homes, school routes, mixed-environment modules, laundries, food access, youth spaces, view allocation.  
**Human anchors:** waiting household + worker with destructive temporary commute.

**Change:** anomaly enters through desired home, not abstract machinery.

**POV:** Ollie.  
**Want:** keep the segment open and preserve migration throughput.
