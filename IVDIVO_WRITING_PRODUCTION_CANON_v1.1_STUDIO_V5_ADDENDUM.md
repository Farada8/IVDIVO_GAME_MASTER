## 6. P51 — DIALOGUE FINGERPRINT

Mandatory before first script batch, at every batch, and Development Edit.

Every major speaking character receives:
- signature and forbidden lexicon;
- sentence shape;
- rhythm;
- verbal competence and break point;
- emotional access;
- subtext style;
- signature rhetorical move;
- plausible class/region/profession markers;
- lie style;
- intimacy/conflict style;
- stress degradation;
- repair language.

No two central characters may share the same combination of sentence shape + emotional access + subtext style.

Blind-swap craft gate: strip names/tags from 8–12 lines; target ≥80% speaker attribution from voice alone. This is an internal craft heuristic, not scientific validation. Repeat across multiple exchanges before final PASS.

## 7. P52 — EMOTIONAL DYNAMIC-RANGE GATE

Map major emotional beats on a 1–5 control/exposure scale:
1 fully composed;
2 controlled but leaking;
3 exposed but functional;
4 control significantly breaks;
5 raw / inarticulate / cathartic.

Major emotional arcs may not remain entirely at 1–2. Require at least one 4–5 exposure per major emotional arc unless an explicitly approved behavioral/silent equivalent performs the same dramatic function.

Do **not** demand a 4–5 break every episode. Contrast matters more than constant intensity. Catharsis should not default to another perfect thematic epigram.

For audio, P52 also controls microphone proximity, breath, silence, off-axis speech, room tone, object handling and music entry/absence. Music must not masquerade as factual evidence.

## 8. HUMAN SIGNAL LOOP

Before season/book lock, run a real blind beta-listen/read with **5–7 target-audience humans when feasible**.

Capture:
- attention/drop-off point;
- boredom/confusion point;
- “did you believe the feeling?”;
- “would you continue/play the next episode?”;
- optional one-sentence reason.

Evidence hierarchy:
internal panel < synthetic hypothesis < human blind signal < real retention/sales/listens.

Synthetic readers may not be reported as completed human testing. Internal scores may not be called “market validated.”
