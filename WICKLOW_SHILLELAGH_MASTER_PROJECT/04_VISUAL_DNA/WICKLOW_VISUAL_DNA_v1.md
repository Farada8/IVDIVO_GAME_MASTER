# WICKLOW VISUAL DNA v1

## Primary visual objective
Create civic murals that read at street distance, reward close viewing, and fuse local evidence with monumental spectacle rather than producing generic historical illustration.

## Current strongest stack for Shillelagh
**GIULIO ROMANO 40 / POZZO 25 / STO-ACADEMIC MONUMENTAL 20 / TIEPOLO-LIGHT 15**
- wall rupture / broken entablature / false projecting masonry
- deep perspectival windows and loggias
- controlled monumental figure groups
- luminous upper register and sky release

## Modern systems to retain
1. Hyperreal Cinematic Mural
2. Manga Historical Composition
3. Cyber-Celtic / High-Tech Heritage
4. Graffiti × Renaissance
5. Photoreal Archive Collage
6. Surreal Historical Realism / Tree That Remembers
7. Magical Realism
8. Anamorphic 3D Street Art
9. European Graphic Novel
10. Multi-era visual-language evolution

## Quality gates
- One dominant compositional device, not ten competing gimmicks.
- Local history must remain identifiable without labels.
- Faces/figures must have hierarchy; avoid crowd wallpaper.
- Street-level viewpoint controls perspective.
- Strong silhouette at 30–60 m; readable secondary scenes at 5–15 m.
- Real windows, corners, gutters, pipes and roof geometry are design inputs, not obstacles to paint over conceptually.
