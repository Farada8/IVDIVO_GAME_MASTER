# PROOF MODEL — MURAL BUSINESS VERTICAL

Reuses Business Engineering OS proof planes.

## K0–K5 Knowledge
K0 source present → K1 integrity → K2 canonical identity → K3 mechanism/provenance → K4 cross-source/conflict → K5 executable fixture/regression.

## S0–S4 Public Signal
S0 mention → S1 authoritative listing/source → S2 freshness/deadline verified → S3 corroborated context → S4 public buyer/budget/action mapping.

## E0–E7 Market
E0 hypothesis → E1 external signal → E2 observable problem/opportunity → E2+ corroborated public artifact → E3 real buyer/jury interaction → E4 money/PO/deposit → E5 repeat → E6 economics/retention → E7 scale.

## A0–A6 Application readiness (new vertical plane)
A0 opportunity known  
A1 official brief acquired  
A2 eligibility verified  
A3 site/technical unknowns bounded  
A4 portfolio/concept evidence mapped  
A5 budget/insurance/H&S/submission pack complete  
A6 submission receipt/readback proven

**Non-substitution:** A6 does not imply E4; S4 does not imply selection; K5 does not imply artistic quality or market demand.
