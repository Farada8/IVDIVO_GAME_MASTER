# WICKLOW 12 MURAL ENGINES v1

1. **GIULIO ROMANO SYSTEM** — architectural rupture; cracked masonry, displaced cornices, projecting fragments; history revealed through believable breaks.
2. **POZZO DEPTH SYSTEM** — false loggias, deep windows, staircases and vanishing architecture extending the building.
3. **TIEPOLO LIGHT SYSTEM** — upper register opens into light, air, allegory and future without losing civic readability.
4. **PANNINI / CAPRICCIO SYSTEM** — multiple local landmarks unified inside one plausible architectural fantasy.
5. **HYPERREAL CINEMATIC SYSTEM** — near-photographic people/objects, cinematic depth, controlled trompe-l'oeil.
6. **MANGA HISTORY SYSTEM** — dynamic panel logic, crops, diagonals and time-jumps while keeping rendering civic/realist rather than cartoon.
7. **CYBER-CELTIC SYSTEM** — stone/roots/rail become future networks; past→present→future transformation using Celtic geometry and restrained high-tech language.
8. **GRAFFITI × RENAISSANCE SYSTEM** — contemporary street energy and typography as structural field with academic figures emerging from it.
9. **ARCHIVE COLLAGE SYSTEM** — old photographs, maps, letters, plans and painted history layered as archaeology of memory.
10. **TREE THAT REMEMBERS SYSTEM** — hyperreal oak as time-axis: roots=deep history, trunk=community, branches=rail/migration/future.
11. **ANAMORPHIC LANDMARK SYSTEM** — one primary street viewpoint creates an extreme 3D illusion such as train/bridge/portal breaking the wall.
12. **MULTI-ERA LANGUAGE SYSTEM** — each historical period changes visual language; transitions themselves explain time.
