## Executive conclusion
The current E01 problem is no longer primarily a writing/TTS-generation problem. It is a **post-render persistence + physical-world + selective-repair + human-validation problem**.

## What the 32-prompt pass established
1. **Do not regenerate dialogue first.** Highest-leverage path is byte recovery -> exact interval map -> physical-world repair -> human listen.
2. **Premium is not “more layers”.** It is comprehension, acting truth, embodiment, room continuity, movement/perspective, clue immunity, selective music, translation and emotional pull working together.
3. **D003 remains the first proven mix-world defect.** Repair only classified intervals; never blanket-fill silence.
4. **D004 needs action-level evidence.** Foley should embody footsteps/props/body action and usually integrate without calling attention to itself.
5. **D005 is not proven.** Music enters after physical-world repair and only with explicit dramatic function.
6. **Room continuity is a production invariant.** Reference craft supports continuous ambience as a way to maintain scene continuum and disguise accidental sound holes, while dramatic tone is designed deliberately rather than by generic room tone alone.
7. **Words / Sounds / Music / Silence form one semantic system.** Dialogue remains the primary communication code; nonverbal codes enrich environment, emotion and imagination without obscuring speech.
8. **Perspective is causal information.** Movement must survive more than stereo pan and remain intelligible in mono/mobile conditions.
9. **Human Signal remains irreplaceable.** Acting truth, premium feel, tension, fatigue and sellability cannot be inferred from machine metrics.

## New quality architecture
`ASSET PERSISTENCE -> SIGNAL FORENSICS -> PHYSICAL WORLD -> BODY/ACTION -> PERFORMANCE/TRUTH -> MUSIC FUNCTION -> TRANSLATION -> HUMAN PREMIUM TEST -> CONTROLLED A/B/C -> SCALE`

A render is not considered durable production evidence merely because a filename, library pointer or previous chat can identify it.

## Reference-derived mechanisms
- Ambience/room tone supports continuity across edits and can hide accidental sound holes; dramatic tone still requires deliberate design rather than generic background.
- Foley primarily embodies what the actor physically does—footsteps, props, clothing—and should generally integrate so naturally that it does not call attention to itself.
- Radio drama communicates through the relationship among Words, Sounds, Music and Silence; speech remains the primary communication code while nonverbal sound builds environment, atmosphere and imagination.
- Movement and perspective are active storytelling information; stereo enhancement is useful, but causal geography must not depend on pan alone.
