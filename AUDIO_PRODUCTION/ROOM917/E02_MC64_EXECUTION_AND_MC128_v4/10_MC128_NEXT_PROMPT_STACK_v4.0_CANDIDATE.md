## Global v4 laws
1. Story authority and fresh shared state outrank prompt output.
2. Every claim carries provenance, evidence class and lifecycle validity.
3. Planning, live-machine evidence and human evidence are different states.
4. Unknown remains unknown; fake precision fails.
5. Listener causal inference outranks sonic spectacle.
6. Relationship authority controls spatial/intimacy coding.
7. Canonical source identity survives reuse/media transformation through lineage.
8. Absolute time comes only from accepted live/master alignment.
9. Missing room bed, missing causal Foley and insufficient music are different defects.
10. Human/commercial judgment is never simulated.
11. Repair earliest failed layer and smallest scope; regress true descendants only.
12. Full manifest is capacity, not spend permission.
13. Accepted assets must be retrievable across dialogs, not merely remembered.
14. Cross-AI agreement without source parity/independent evidence is not validation.
15. v4 itself must be deduped and proven before universal promotion.
