## What v3 materially improved

### 1. Evidence-aware staging
A movement, distance or object action now carries an evidence grade. Recovered-chat detail cannot silently become canon.

### 2. Causal geography
The strongest E02 example is Scene 3: `OLD BOARD -> LOBBY -> RECEPTION/MODERN PHONE -> RETURN -> HIDDEN TERMINAL`. Mina’s return is required to make her visual confirmation physically possible.

### 3. Relationship authority constrains space
Near-ear/breath-forward Elena–Julian treatment is rejected in E02. Acoustic intimacy cannot create romance the story has not authorized.

### 4. Human identity separated from media identity
Cate may remain the same woman across cassette/line/logger while each transport keeps its own physical/noise identity.

Envelope, recorder copy, cassette, trunk plug, mobile and hidden terminal alter what can be proved and who controls evidence.

Absolute automation is correctly refused before `SOURCE -> ACCEPTED TAKE -> VERIFIED ALIGNMENT -> RESOLVED EVENT`.

### 7. Human evidence fails closed
One-listen comprehension, same-person blind identity, spatial naturalness, commercial richness and release remain unpassed until real audio/human evidence exists.

## Weaknesses found in the 64-prompt architecture

### A. Running all 64 on every scene would become ritual
Many controls are lifecycle-specific. A pre-render scene should not execute post-render artifact detection as if evidence exists. **v4 requirement:** lifecycle routing and family activation.

### B. Evidence grade needs confidence + claim graph
Complex investigations require `CLAIM -> EVIDENCE OBJECT -> SOURCE/MEDIUM -> WHO KNOWS -> PROVES -> DOES_NOT_PROVE -> CONFIDENCE -> OPEN ALTERNATIVES`.

### C. Body continuity needs typed state
Narrative prose can hide teleportation/impossible sight or hearing. **v4:** body-state/access matrices and transitions.

### D. Listener inference needs negative constraints
Production must state not only what the listener should infer but what must not be inferred too early: guilt, romance, supernatural certainty, source identity or causality.

### E. Post-render forensics is underrepresented
Current real E01 P003A evidence proves aggregate pre-Scene3 room-bed undercoverage and has a causal-Foley underdensity candidate, but exact intervals are unresolved. **v4:** exact low-level interval localization/classification and defect evidence packs.

### F. Asset materialization/persistence must be first-class
Current E01 state proves a master may exist yet remain inaccessible to another dialog/tool surface. **v4:** master-byte identity/materialization/escrow gate.

### G. Music needs separate story and commercial authorities
Music cannot repair missing room bed or causal Foley. Commercial richness can still be tested through controlled A/B/C variants after proven repair.

### H. Prompt outputs need explicit downstream consumers
Each prompt should route output to Director Score, take registry, asset manifest, semantic anchor registry, edit list, QC, human-listen form, patch contract or learning registry.

### I. Every blocker needs cheapest decisive evidence
“Needs audio” is insufficient. The engine should name the smallest render, file, measurement, listen or decision capable of resolving the blocker.

### J. Planning, execution and evidence status need one lifecycle model
`PASS_AS_PLAN / READY / BLOCKED / HUMAN_REQUIRED / LIVE_EVIDENCE_REQUIRED` must be phase-aware rather than free text.
