# SCENE 1 — GREYHAVEN LOBBY / LATE AFTERNOON

## Dramatic function
Elena arrives as a professional, not a believer. Mina establishes ordinary hotel life and social warmth. Julian introduces real economic pressure. Attraction is not played yet; competence friction is.

## Listener geography
- Large stone lobby, tall west windows.
- Elena enters from revolving door side and initially occupies listener-front/near.
- Mina is already at or behind reception, slightly deeper in the room.
- Julian approaches across stone from a longer distance; his arrival is heard before he speaks.
- Transition to switchboard alcove narrows the acoustic field and removes lobby tail.

## Actor / microphone choreography
### Elena
- Entry: 0.9–1.2 m equivalent mic distance; frontal, dry enough to read intelligence.
- V.O. opening is not disembodied supernatural narration: keep it intimate and documentary, then hand naturally into in-room dialogue.
- When she opens the equipment case, move her perspective slightly lower/closer to the object, not closer to the listener for emotional emphasis.

### Mina
- First line from reception depth, then closes distance naturally as she introduces herself.
- Humor stays conversational; no sitcom timing, no exaggerated smile in the voice.

### Julian
- Approach is established by measured steps before first line.
- Start slightly farther than Elena/Mina; arrive to normal conversational distance by “Мисс Рид. Джулиан Эшкрофт.”
- Never center him with trailer-style dominance. Status comes from brevity, stillness and the others’ responses.

## Foley / body
- Atlantic rain against high windows: broad exterior layer, not hiss over dialogue.
- Revolving door: one heavy close event, then leave it.
- Stone footsteps must localize Julian’s approach and stop before/under his first line.
- Keys are **placed**, not thrown. Small status signal.
- Elena case: latches → metal edge → foam → cable handling. Close, tactile, causal.
- Walk to switchboard: lobby reflections shorten as floor changes from stone to carpet.
- Wooden cabinet/alcove door closes or narrows the room around them.

## Ambience hierarchy
1. Room air / stone lobby tail.
2. Rain and wind through architecture.
3. Distant hotel life: sparse cutlery/utility, not generic crowd bed.
4. Old radiator tick.
5. Old brass service bell only if physically motivated; no decorative repetitions.

## Protected beats
- After Julian’s “Семьдесят два человека…” leave enough room for the economic fact to land before Mina reframes it as families.
- “Привидений я не сертифицирую” should not receive a sting, bass drop or silence trick. It is professional boundary, not prophecy.
- “Лампы с первой по девятьсот шестнадцатую…” needs uncluttered intelligibility; this sets the physical impossibility later.

## Mono/mobile test
- Julian’s approach remains readable through level, transient and room-tail change even when panning collapses.
- Rain never masks consonants in Elena’s technical lines.

# SCENE 2 — SWITCHBOARD ALCOVE / EVENING

## Dramatic function
Elena proves method and control. Mina becomes a witness/assistant. Julian tests boundaries. The room is ordinary engineering territory before it becomes impossible.

## Listener geography
- Small, drier switchboard alcove.
- Elena is closest to panel and tools.
- Mina stands beside/just behind Elena with phone/camera and later second headset.
- Julian stays one step outside Elena’s working envelope; his distance should communicate supervision without looming.

## Actor / microphone choreography
### Elena
- Most lines 0.7–1.0 m equivalent, stable frontal axis.
- Technical explanations slightly slower but not “explainer voice.”
- On hands-on work, head turns/off-axis are allowed only when matched to a visible/audible action: clamp, bridge, switch, headset.

### Mina
- Begins socially loose, then becomes task-focused when evidence chain starts.
- When wearing the second headset, reduce room contribution subtly; do not telephone-filter her live voice.

### Julian
- Fewer movements than the others. Stillness is part of his status.
- “Останавливаетесь” must be compact, pressured, human — no growl, no cinematic alpha cadence.

## Evidence sound order — LOCKED
When Elena energizes the local system:
1. Single switch action.
2. Transformer wakes softly — recognizable **50 Hz identity**.
3. Short recognition space.
4. Neat wave of old relays.
5. Faint west-cable rustle/weather path.

Do not collapse this into one “old machine powers up” effect. The order teaches the listener how the system behaves normally.

## Normal 916 reference
Before the impossible event in Scene 3, 916 must have a clean reference identity:
- selector/switch action,
- expected relay/mechanical response,
- normal lamp/glass ping or equivalent accepted identity,
- no mysterious tail.
This reference becomes the listener’s control sample.

## Foley / body
- Isolation bridge clips: distinct small metal transients.
- Phone/camera photo handling: sparse, only when Mina is documenting.
- Headset lift/place: cable drag + earcup contact, very small.
- Elena’s hand to panel: sleeve/cloth only when physically necessary.

## Protected beats
- “Внешняя линия визуально разомкнута” must be clean.
- Explanation of current limiter/bridge must remain intelligible; do not bury it under electromechanical detail.
- Any first hint from west cable is quiet enough that characters must attend to it, but audible enough that the listener is not asked to believe an inaudible clue.

## Music
NONE.

## Mono/mobile test
- 50 Hz identity remains perceivable without consuming headroom.
- Normal 916 control sound remains distinguishable from later impossible double ring even with stereo collapsed.

# SCENE 3 — SWITCHBOARD ALCOVE / 01:17

## Dramatic function
The ordinary system violates its established rules. Elena does not become a believer; she becomes a disciplined witness. Mina corroborates. Cate’s fragment converts technical impossibility into personal stakes. Julian’s knowledge of the fourth note creates the final human/mystery turn.

## Room state before event
- Storm has moved farther out to sea.
- Very low room tone.
- Small clock mechanism.
- Recorder buttons.
- Same ordinary transformer identity as Scene 2.
- No suspense score.

The quieter room is not “horror silence”; it is an engineering environment late at night.

## Impossible event — clue chain
The following chain is treated as evidence, not decoration:
1. **IMPOSSIBLE_INTERNAL_DOUBLE_RING**.
2. Elena/Mina orient toward panel.
3. Field recorder is started.
4. Elena states exact time: **01:17:12**.
5. She states external trunk is physically open.
6. Unmarked lamp beyond 916 is active.
7. Mina independently confirms the lamp.
8. Copper-line presence/noise establishes a physical medium.
9. Cate fragment appears as an ordinary human voice carried by the impossible route.
10. “Лени-бёрд” remains acoustically exact; semantic translation is forbidden.
11. Lullaby presents the four-note identity with damage after note 3 / missing-fourth-note information preserved.
12. Line cut remains a **two-part** event.
13. Julian’s recognition of the last note occurs after the evidence chain, not before.
14. Elena’s final question: **«Тогда откуда вы знаете последнюю ноту?»**

## Microphone choreography
### Elena
- At first double ring, no vocal jump-scare reaction. Breath/action first if present.
- During formal recorder statement, center and stabilize her image; she is forcing herself into procedure.
- “Мина, подтвердите” is directed to Mina, not to the listener.
- On “Лени-бёрд” recognition, reduce motion. Emotional shock is expressed by stillness and narrowed voice, not volume.
- Final line to Julian: quieter, closer only if the blocking physically closes distance; otherwise keep distance and let attention carry the turn.

### Mina
- Confirmation must sound like a witness trying to be exact.
- Humor is fully off once the impossible lamp is confirmed.
- She does not become hysterical or supernatural exposition voice.

### Cate / line
- Generate clean human performance first.
- Post chain may include limited-band telephone/copper response, line noise, instability and damage, but must preserve lexical intelligibility of “Лени-бёрд”.
- Do not pitch her into “ghost voice.”
- Same human identity must remain plausible against E02 cassette material.

### Julian
- Do not foreshadow guilt or secret knowledge in earlier performance.
- When he supplies/recognizes the final note, the line should feel involuntary or too informed, not villainous.
- No ominous processing.

## Protected silence
- Short clean recognition gap after first impossible double ring.
- Clean gap before/after Mina’s independent confirmation.
- After “Лени-бёрд”, protect a small human processing space.
- After the damaged lullaby/line cut, do not fill the absence with a sting.
- Final line gets the full dramatic exit space.

## Final music button
Only after the final spoken line and protected exit space: one restrained low piano chord/button if the accepted English identity uses it. No melodic explanation, no horror hit.

## Mono/mobile test
The listener must still be able to distinguish:
- normal 916 reference vs impossible double ring,
- ordinary transformer vs copper/line anomaly,
- Cate speech vs room speech,
- three completed lullaby notes + damaged/missing fourth-note information,
- two-part line cut.
