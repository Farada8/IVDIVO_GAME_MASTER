## 3. LOCALIZATION LAW

Translation is **performable localization**, not literal sentence substitution.

Preserve exactly:
- causal facts;
- character objective / resistance / status;
- clue order;
- relationship state;
- jokes by function;
- technical meaning;
- protected numbers and timing;
- cliffhanger mechanism.

Russian must sound contemporary and spoken. Avoid:
- Soviet-film dubbing syntax;
- literary narrator inflation;
- bureaucratic calques where a working specialist would use a shorter phrase;
- invented slang that changes social class or age;
- extra explanation after a clue;
- extra supernatural language.

## 8. FOLEY + BODY PRESENCE — RU PILOT

The current English evaluation already identified a real risk of under-dense body/Foley presence before Scene 3. RU pilot therefore requires causal Foley from the start, but not blanket noise filling.

Per audible action ask:
`WHO MOVES → WHAT CONTACTS WHAT → DISTANCE → MATERIAL → STORY/RELATIONSHIP FUNCTION → MUST LISTENER NOTICE?`

Required families where caused by action:
- coat / wet fabric / flight-case handling;
- measured shoes on stone;
- key ring placed, not tossed;
- cabinet / switches / probe / leads;
- camera / evidence bags / tape / envelope;
- coffee cups;
- chair/body shift where status changes justify it;
- cassette case/deck/headphones in E02;
- screws / rubber mat / probe / meter handling;
- footsteps with actual entrances/exits.

No random “human texture” over protected evidence silence.

## 11. MIX / MASTER TARGETS

Dialogue is reference.

Starting audition relationships, not immutable mastering numbers:
- ambience roughly 18–26 dB below dialogue;
- ordinary Foley roughly 12–20 dB below dialogue;
- clue SFX roughly 6–12 dB below dialogue, briefly closer only when recognition is the action;
- music normally 20 dB or more below dialogue unless a dedicated non-dialogue button requires otherwise.

If a clue fails on phone / mono / low volume, repair mix before rewriting text.

Production target:
- 48 kHz source/timeline;
- final 24-bit WAV master after DAW/post where applicable;
- target around -16 LUFS integrated for the pilot unless platform delivery requires another spec;
- no clipping;
- explicit mono fold-down and cheap-earbud/phone tests.

Do not widen the mix merely to look stereo on a meter. Space must follow staging.
