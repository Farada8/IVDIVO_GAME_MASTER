# ROOM917_E01_SOUND_DIRECTION_SCORE_v1

**Project:** ROOM 917  
**Episode:** E01 — THE LAMP AFTER 916 / THE INSURABLE FIRE  
**Department:** Sound Design / Foley / Music / Mix  
**Status:** Production direction v1  
**Rule:** story, dialogue and causal scene logic remain locked.

## 1. Sound dramaturgy

E01 must sound like a real old building in which one detail is physically impossible, not like a haunted-house effects reel.

`credible acoustics -> small violation -> verification -> new violation -> physical proof -> human proximity -> blackout -> impossibility remains`

The more credible the first 70% of the acoustic world is, the stronger ROOM 917 becomes.

## 2. Acoustic passports

### AP-01 GREYHAVEN
Massive architecture; long corridor decay; material-dependent reflections; distant HVAC; structural settling; low mechanical vibration; rare remote doors; weak electrical infrastructure. Expensive, old and physically heavy. Never a permanent horror drone.

### AP-02 IMPOSSIBLE AMBER INDICATOR
`real relay click -> electrical transient -> short tonal component -> weak cabinet/wall resonance -> micro-tail`.

Forbidden: sci-fi zap, horror sting, supernatural whisper, reversed vocal. First impression: technical system. Second: that line should not exist.

### AP-03 IMPOSSIBLE CALL
Vintage line noise, relay switching, limited telephone bandwidth, mild line instability and physically plausible headset leakage. The impossibility comes from routing/context, not ghost processing.

### AP-04 FOUR-NOTE LULLABY / MISSING FOURTH NOTE
First three notes must be distinct, repeatable and dry enough for analysis. The fourth-note slot is protected silence: the world remains audible but the expected event is absent.

### AP-05 ROOM 916 WALL
Normal: `impact -> short dense decay`. Anomalous: `impact -> body resonance -> subtle delayed secondary component`. Never a cartoon hollow-wall effect.

### AP-06 TWO-TONE BELL
Tone A and Tone B have distinct fundamentals but one mechanical family and stable level relationship. Meaning must survive mono, phone and low-level listening.

### AP-07 CHILD-SIZE HATCH
Before opening: muffled cavity response. During movement: latch, friction, material stress, pressure shift. After opening: a genuinely new small room tone and reflection family. The important event is the acoustic appearance of new physical volume.

## 3. Spatial zones

### ZONE A SWITCHBOARD ALCOVE
Close, relatively dry, many small reflecting surfaces, localized electrical bed. Stereo width approximately 30–45%.

### ZONE B 9TH FLOOR CORRIDOR
Longer decay, wider field, strong foreground localization and intelligible distance. Stereo width approximately 65–80%.

### ZONE C ROOM 916
Closed-room perspective: corridor drops after door closes, HVAC spectrum changes, voices get closer, walls become perceptible. Stereo width approximately 45–60%.

### ZONE D SERVICE LANDING / LIFT
Colder and mechanical: lift machinery, cable/rail resonance, metal enclosure and door system. Vertical information matters. Stereo width approximately 50–70%.

### ZONE E KITCHEN / POST-BLACKOUT SAFE SPACE
Dry reset with human-scale object sounds, cloth, crockery and breathing. Minimal mystery bed.

## 4. Block direction

### BLOCK 01 GREYHAVEN ESTABLISHMENT
No music. Greyhaven room bed + distant ventilation + rare structural settling + remote mechanics + weak electrical hum. Elena footsteps, clothing, equipment and headset handling establish a real working body in a real building.

### BLOCK 02 SWITCHBOARD / IMPOSSIBLE CALL
Teach normal system operation before violation. `relay event -> indicator response -> line event`. Technical handling must be audible and causal. Phone processing is restrained and plausible. No sting.

### BLOCK 03 LULLABY / MISSING FOURTH NOTE
Three clearly articulated notes followed by a 300–700 ms starting protected-silence window, finalized from performance rhythm. Do not mute room/line noise. Music strictly off. Must pass earbuds, phone, mono, car-like noise and low-volume playback.

### BLOCK 04 JULIAN ENTERS / INVESTIGATION
Julian travels `far corridor -> doorway -> room`. Initial working geometry may place Elena slightly left and Julian slightly right; their image may converge only as physical cooperation closes distance. Foley priority: recorder, contact mic, cable, tape measure, wall contact, mallet and repositioning.

### BLOCK 05 ACOUSTIC MALLET TEST
Three distinct responses: NORMAL-A, NORMAL-B, ANOMALOUS. During tests ambience may duck 1–2 dB; music off. Preserve anomalous decay and a short discovery gap afterward.

### BLOCK 06 CONTACT MIC
Monitoring is a change of acoustic perspective, not a louder wall. Foreground structural vibration, solid-borne transmission and narrow body resonances. No invented monster rumble.

### BLOCK 07 TAPE GEOMETRY
Full tactile tape-measure Foley: case, extension, stop, metal contact, tension, controlled return and foot reposition. Dry, precise, investigative. Music off.

### BLOCK 08 TWO-TONE BELL
Tone A then Tone B, cleanly audible. No music hit on the bell. If score enters, only after the reaction beat.

### BLOCK 09 HATCH REVEAL
Latch -> resistance -> material stress -> movement -> pressure shift -> new cavity ambience. Width may open gradually by approximately 10–15%, but the cavity must remain physically plausible rather than portal-like.

### BLOCK 10 MARGOT / STRUCTURAL REPORT
Paper and voice dominate. No competing transient on the critical date/factual revelation. Music off during the fact itself.

### BLOCK 11 FIRST KISS / INTIMACY
Minimal cloth, weight shift, breath and contact. No amplified kiss Foley or large romantic swell. Spatial separation closes only through believable body movement. Optional minimal human-proximity score only after the emotional consent/boundary beat.

### BLOCK 12 CORRIDOR BLACKOUT
Blackout is system loss, not just a switch sound: electrical transient -> powered hum layers disappear -> architecture remains -> breathing, cloth, distance and footsteps gain salience. First seconds music off. Less infrastructure should produce more perceived space.

### BLOCK 13 MOVEMENT IN DARKNESS
Use real sources only. Spatial motion may cross front/side/rear axes, but mono must retain meaning through level, distance, early reflections and spectral perspective rather than pan alone.

### BLOCK 14 KITCHEN BOUNDARY
Acoustic change provides relief: drier room, shorter tails, local human-scale objects. Music preferably off.

### BLOCK 15 OLD PLAN / QUESTION
Large paper, unfold, drag, tracing and fold resistance. Protect the final factual/spatial question from competing sound and score.

### BLOCK 16 END SUSPICION FRAME
Reuse an established signature—relay, amber-indicator family, structural response or three-note architecture—in a new context. No new supernatural signature. End music is minimal and unresolved.

## 5. Foley manifest

Elena: multiple surface-specific steps, start/stop/turn/cautious move, close/medium cloth, wall contact, paper, recorder, headset and breath movement.  
Julian: regular walk, approach, investigative reposition, crouch/stand, clothing, equipment, wall contact, tape and mallet.  
Margot: controlled footsteps, suit cloth, report handling, page stop and deliberate object placement.  
Architecture: doors, latches, hinges, hatch, wall impacts, floor creaks, lift mechanics, lift door and switchboard hardware.

## 6. Music system

- `M0 NO SCORE` — default.
- `M1 GREYHAVEN HARMONIC FIELD` — sparse, almost static, never a horror drone.
- `M2 HUMAN PROXIMITY` — minimal Elena/Julian emotional layer.
- `M3 OPEN QUESTION` — unresolved final harmonic structure.

### Evidence windows where music is forbidden
Missing fourth note; mallet comparison; contact-mic evidence; critical tape measurement; two-tone bell identification; structural-report date; first seconds of blackout.

## 7. Mix architecture

Primary buses:
`DIALOGUE / CLUE_SFX / SFX / FOLEY / AMBIENCE / MUSIC`

Support:
`PHONE_PROCESS / CONTACT_MIC / VERB_SHORT / VERB_CORRIDOR / VERB_SERVICE / PARALLEL_DIALOGUE / PRINTMASTER`

Normal priority: `Dialogue > important Foley > ambience > music`.  
Clue priority: `Clue > dialogue reaction > relevant Foley > ambience > music`.

## 8. Loudness / dynamics

Working stereo target approximately `-16 LUFS integrated`, operational range `-17 to -15 LUFS`; true peak `<= -1.0 dBTP`, preferred safety around `-1.5 dBTP`. Preserve scene dynamics; do not flatten the programme to reach a number.

## 9. Spatial and translation policy

Binaural/spatial emphasis is allowed only when direction carries story information: actor approach, source behind wall, corridor travel, hatch volume or blackout movement. It is not decorative.

Every critical clue must pass `stereo -> mono fold -> phone speaker`, plus headphone and low-level listening. A clue that exists only through width is a FAIL. No critical clue may depend only on sub-bass.

## 10. Protected silence

- `PS-A EXPECTATION`: missing fourth note.
- `PS-B DISCOVERY`: after physical anomaly.
- `PS-C HUMAN`: emotional/consent proximity beat.
- `PS-D SYSTEM FAILURE`: blackout.

Protected silence is never digital zero; physical room reality remains.

## 11. Automation

Mandatory lanes: dialogue rides; ambience perspective; actor distance; music ducking; clue emphasis; reverb sends; phone transitions; blackout infrastructure; stereo width; hatch spatial transition.

## 13. E01 acceptance gates

S01 SPACE — primary locations distinguishable without visual context.  
S02 CLUES — acoustic evidence is clearly audible.  
S03 REALISM — no supernatural processing breaks investigative realism.  
S04 DIALOGUE — all story-critical speech intelligible.  
S05 MONO — clues survive.  
S06 MOBILE — clues survive.  
S07 HEADPHONES — spatial movement credible and comfortable.  
S08 MUSIC — never masks evidence.  
S09 DYNAMICS — no loudness-war compression.  
S10 CONTINUITY — room tones and actor geometry remain continuous across edits.

## 14. Directing principle

ROOM 917 sound does not announce the supernatural. It proves an engineering system, a measurement, a repeated test and a physical result—then leaves one event impossible.
