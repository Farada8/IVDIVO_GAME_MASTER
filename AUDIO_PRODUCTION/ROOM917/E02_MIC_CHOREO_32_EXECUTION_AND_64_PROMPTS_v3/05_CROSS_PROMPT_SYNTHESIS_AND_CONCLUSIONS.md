# E02 `LENI-BIRD` — CROSS-PROMPT SYNTHESIS + CONCLUSIONS

**Basis:** completed sequential MC-00…MC-31 execution.

## 1. PRIMARY CONCLUSION

The v2 Microphone Choreography stack is strong at converting story beats into spatial production intent, but the 32-prompt run exposed a deeper requirement: a professional audio choreography engine must control not only **where bodies/sources move**, but also **how certain we are that a movement is actually authorized by source**, **what causal inference the listener must make**, and **which evidence layer owns a later repair**.

The next version therefore needs to become an **evidence-aware scene embodiment engine**, not merely a movement/spatialization engine.

---

## 2. NEW EVIDENCE-GRADE MODEL

The strongest correction from the run is the distinction:

`SCRIPT_EXPLICIT / CONTINUITY_REQUIRED / DIRECTORIAL_INFERENCE / UNSUPPORTED`.

Earlier recovered prose occasionally described strong directorial choices as if they were literal script facts. That is dangerous because a later AI may treat the staging as canon and build further assumptions on it.

### Consequence
Every choreography assertion should carry `EVIDENCE_GRADE + SOURCE_ANCHOR + REVERSIBILITY`.

- `SCRIPT_EXPLICIT`: protected unless story authority changes.
- `CONTINUITY_REQUIRED`: derived from physical/causal necessity; may be implemented in several ways.
- `DIRECTORIAL_INFERENCE`: optional choice requiring director acceptance.
- `UNSUPPORTED`: prohibited until new evidence arrives.

This should become a universal v3 control.

---

## 3. MOTION ECONOMY > CONSTANT MOTION

E02 demonstrates that the most effective spatial event is often **stopping**:
- confrontation can stop an approach;
- witness authority can stop a departure;
- evidence listening can stop the room;
- discovery can stop movement long enough for inference.

Movement should therefore be budgeted like dialogue or music. Every move needs a function and every important stillness needs protection.

### New law
`NO MOTION WITHOUT CAUSE; NO STILLNESS WITHOUT FUNCTION; NO SPATIAL SPECTACLE THAT OUTRANKS INFERENCE.`

---

## 4. LISTENER POINT IS NOT CHARACTER POV

Elena is story POV, but the listener does not need to occupy her literal head. In E02 Scene 3 the strongest comprehension choice is to keep the listener broadly near Elena/old-board focus while Mina travels to reception. This allows **distance itself to prove system separation**.

### New law
Choose listener point according to `INFERENCE TASK`, not automatically according to POV character.

The engine should distinguish:
- narrative POV;
- point of audition;
- acoustic focus owner;
- temporary source focus.

---

## 5. SPACE MUST PROVE CAUSAL GEOGRAPHY

E02's best spatial sequence is not “left/right stereo”; it is:

`OLD BOARD -> PHYSICAL DEPARTURE -> MODERN RECEPTION -> MODERN PHONE -> RETURN -> VISUAL TERMINAL CONFIRMATION`.

This is **causal geography**: space changes what a person can know, hear, see or prove.

### New law
Spatial design should explicitly state `WHAT FACT BECOMES BELIEVABLE BECAUSE THIS GEOGRAPHY IS AUDIBLE?`

---

## 6. BODY-PATH CONTINUITY IS A LOGIC GATE

A character cannot deliver a visual-confirmation line from a place where the object is not visible. This sounds obvious, but AI-generated spatial plans can easily teleport actors between dialogue lines.

### New law
For every important line/action validate:
`CURRENT_POSITION -> CAN_HEAR? -> CAN_SEE? -> CAN_TOUCH? -> CAN_REACH? -> CAN_BE_HEARD_AS_STAGED?`.

This is a story-continuity check, not merely audio polish.

---

## 7. DEVICE DOMAIN IS PART OF CHARACTER IDENTITY

Cate on cassette is not simply “Cate with EQ.” The listener must perceive simultaneously:
- same human person;
- different time/source medium;
- physical playback object in present room;
- no ghost ontology implied by processing.

The same principle applies to phone, logger, radio, intercom, surveillance playback and synthetic communication.

### New law
`PERSON IDENTITY` and `MEDIA IDENTITY` are separate invariants. One must stay stable while the other changes audibly.

---

## 8. RELATIONSHIP AUTHORITY MUST CONTROL SPACE

E02 confirms that spatial proximity can silently rewrite a relationship even when text does not. Near-ear, breath salience, physical enclosure and slow close orbit are narrative signals.

### New law
Every intimate spatial cue needs `RELATIONSHIP_AUTHORITY + SCENE_FUNCTION + CONSENT/POWER CHECK`.

Technical capability never authorizes intimacy.

---

## 9. OBJECTS NEED OWNERSHIP AND TRANSFER LOGIC

Coffee, envelope, cassette and terminal are not generic Foley opportunities. They carry:
- social tactic;
- evidence custody;
- source identity;
- technical access;
- status/control.

### New law
Important object choreography should track:
`OBJECT -> OWNER -> LOCATION -> ACTION -> RECEIVER -> SOUND -> STORY FUNCTION -> NEW STATE`.

This is especially important for mystery/evidence audio.

---

## 10. UNKNOWN GEOMETRY MUST REMAIN UNKNOWN

The script does not give exact metres, yaw angles, carpet/tile composition or reverberation values. Fabricating them makes a plan look precise while reducing truth.

### New law
Use semantic zones and relational geometry until physical measurements or locked production design exist. Numeric precision is permitted only with provenance.

---

## 11. PERFORMANCE AND SPACE CANNOT BE SEPARATED LATE

A whispery close performance cannot convincingly become a shouted corridor line by reverb/volume alone. Mina's travel is the clearest example: if the performance does not support distance/projection/listening behavior, spatial processing should not hide the problem.

### New law
Before automation, run a `PERFORMANCE↔BODY↔SPACE PHYSICAL PLAUSIBILITY` gate. Rerender performance before mix when the body state is wrong.

---

## 12. MUSICAL FACTS NEED THEIR OWN AUTHORITY

The fourth-note/lullaby identity is not merely a sound effect. It is a recurring **story fact encoded musically**. E02's complete lullaby and E01's damaged-after-note-three version must share one identity.

Current ROOM917 state still keeps fourth-note pitch relationship verification open.

### New law
Recurring musical evidence requires a `MUSICAL_FACT PASSPORT`: motif identity, allowed transformations, medium versions, canonical source, prohibited substitutions, verification status.

Do not let an audio generator invent a new motif instance independently.

---

## 13. CLUE MASKING REQUIRES A FORMAL BUDGET

Movement, Foley, ambience and music can all be correct individually and still destroy the intended inference when combined.

### New law
For each clue/evidence beat assign:
`PRIMARY INFORMATION / ALLOWED COMPETITORS / SUPPRESSED ELEMENTS / MONO RULE / DEVICE RULE / SPEED RULE`.

This should be resolved before final mix automation.

---

## 14. ABSOLUTE TIME IS A DERIVED ARTIFACT

No script or synthetic fixture can safely supply final movement automation time. Absolute spatial time exists only after accepted rendered performance/alignment.

### New law
`SOURCE UNIT -> ACCEPTED TAKE -> ALIGNMENT -> SEMANTIC ANCHOR -> ABSOLUTE TIME -> AUTOMATION`.

Any missing lineage blocks final timing.

---

## 15. SELECTIVE REPAIR NEEDS DEFECT PROVENANCE

A later bad mix does not imply bad dialogue; bad distance does not imply bad performance; source-identity confusion may be device-domain rather than acting.

### New law
Every demonstrated defect gets:
`OBSERVED FAILURE -> EARLIEST FAILED LAYER -> SMALLEST PATCH -> PROTECTED UPSTREAM -> TRUE DESCENDANTS -> REGRESSION TEST`.

Do not rerender whole scenes by default.

---

## 16. HUMAN LISTEN REMAINS NON-SIMULATABLE

The 32-prompt plan can prove logical consistency, but cannot prove:
- real acting truth;
- fatigue;
- commercial pleasure;
- emotional salience;
- whether the mix actually feels spatially natural;
- whether a listener forms the correct causal model on first listen.

### New law
Machine/story gates prepare a candidate; actual perceptual claims require real audio + human listen where specified.

# E02 INTEGRATED CHOREOGRAPHY MODEL

## Scene 1 — Evidence work / knowledge pressure

Spatial grammar: `ARRIVAL -> SOCIAL NORMALIZATION -> KNOWLEDGE PRESSURE -> WITNESS STATUS -> CUSTODY ACTION`.

Use door and coffee to establish physical arrival; keep fourth-note confrontation uncluttered; optional approach/stop remains directorial; Mina's witness-hold is a status beat; envelope transfer is evidence geometry. No romantic close coding.

## Scene 2 — Listening / source identity

Spatial grammar: `LIVE ROOM QUIETS -> DEVICE TAKES FOCUS -> CATE IDENTITY/PATTERN -> REACTION SPACE`.

Movement drops. Cate belongs to cassette. Same woman/different medium. Complete four-note identity remains dependent on musical-fact authority.

## Scene 3 — Physical system test

Spatial grammar: `OLD SYSTEM -> HUMAN TRAVEL -> MODERN SYSTEM -> REMOTE SOURCE -> HUMAN RETURN -> OLD SYSTEM REVEAL`.

Mina's body path is part of the proof. Listener should not simply follow her like a camera; old-board/Elena context must remain intelligible. Return is continuity-required before terminal visual confirmation.
