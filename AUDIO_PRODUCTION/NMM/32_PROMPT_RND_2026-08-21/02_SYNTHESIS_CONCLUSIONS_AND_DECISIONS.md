# SYNTHESIS — ANALYSIS, STRUCTURE, CONCLUSIONS, DECISIONS

## Executive conclusion

The 32-prompt cycle does **not** identify a story-development deficit. NMM is already in a valid locked-story / audio-preproduction state. The remaining risk is whether the production chain can prove, with real evidence, that the selected voices, clue assets, provider behavior, assembled relationship performance, device translation and human comprehension satisfy the listener contract.

The strategic shift is:

`MORE SPECIFICATION -> LESS VALUE`

once authority/architecture is stable.

The next value frontier is:

`REAL EVIDENCE -> SELECTIVE REPAIR -> LOCK -> SCALE`.

## 12 consolidated conclusions

1. **Story lock stays closed.** Current problems are production-layer problems unless evidence demonstrates otherwise.
2. **Audio compiler count for E01 is 1494.** The source line `1496` is stale metadata; 269/269 spoken occurrences are covered exactly once.
3. **Voice casting is the primary live bottleneck.** No NMM lead voice IDs are currently locked.
4. **Casting must be staged.** Zero-cost preflight and canary precede paid comparison, pair tests and pilot.
5. **Isla is the endurance risk.** Precision must remain human and micro-reactive over long listening.
6. **Leo is the identity-continuity risk.** Recorded confession and live Leo must be recognizably one person without turning confession into guilt coding.
7. **Vivian has a hard anti-spoiler gate.** Voice, score, silence and processing may not pre-solve the mystery.
8. **The whistle is an evidence asset.** It requires identity lock and device translation, not decorative sound design.
9. **Replay consistency is mandatory.** Confession and clue replay use aliases/excerpts from one locked master.
10. **Provider-neutral architecture is correct.** ElevenLabs-specific schemas stay behind the adapter/alignment normalizer.
11. **Human listening remains mandatory.** Internal QA cannot certify one-listen comprehension, chemistry or spoiler neutrality.
12. **Parallel work is now possible.** Voice/provider cascade and clue/space asset preparation can run independently until hard-pilot convergence.

## New policies promoted by this cycle

### P1 — Evidence-per-render law
Every paid render must test a named hypothesis or produce an accepted production unit. No speculative rerolls.

### P2 — 1800-character project TTD ceiling
Provider guidance is ~2000 characters for reliable TTD. NMM should target <=1800 characters to retain margin and smaller failure blast radius.

### P3 — Exact-text normalization caution
For hard-locked text, `apply_text_normalization=off` is the preferred production candidate to verify during S0. `auto/on` may only remain if tests prove no harmful drift and exact source/alignment checks pass.

### P4 — Anti-spoiler casting veto
For roles whose hidden truth must remain concealed, spoiler-neutrality is a hard fail gate that overrides aggregate casting scores.

### P5 — Two-sample hard pilot
NMM needs both the evidence/mystery engine and relationship/status engine tested. A single cold-open sample is insufficient.
