## P25 — LESSON ZERO ACOUSTIC PASSPORTS

**Disposition:** PASS_DRY.

### Acoustic domain
`OUTDOOR_REFLECTING_POOL_INTIMATE`

### Listener geometry
- lived outdoor public space, not cinematic spectacle;
- listener remains near the Ethan/Aoife conversation while festival/contact life persists at a believable distance;
- narrator may remain perceptually direct without turning into omniscient trailer narration;
- diegetic recorder playback/capture is a separate media identity from base ambience;
- stereo may support geography, but no story-critical fact may depend on extreme left/right placement;
- mono/mobile fallback must preserve speaker distinction, recorder-medium distinction and the serious turn around the protected pause.

### Required functional buses
1. `DIALOGUE_NARRATION`
2. `FOLEY`
3. `AMBIENCE_PERFORMANCE_SOUND`
4. `MUSIC` — optional, disabled first pass

No ROOM917-specific CLUE_SFX topology is imported. Empty buses are not manufactured for schema symmetry.

### Occlusion/movement law
- movement/perspective may change only when caused by character or recorder position;
- no decorative widening;
- no reverb convention that turns an ordinary public orbital/social setting into mystical spectacle;
- media-state transition must be audible by source texture/perspective, not exposition.

## P27 — RECORDER FOLEY CAUSAL GRAPH

**Disposition:** PASS_DRY CAUSAL CONTRACT.

`CHARACTER INTENT -> HAND REACHES/OPERATES RECORDER -> MATERIAL HANDLING SOUND -> RECORD/PLAY STATE CHANGES -> LISTENER UNDERSTANDS OBJECT ACTION -> DIALOGUE/PERFORMANCE CONSEQUENCE`

Required `CUE_009` is micro-Foley only. It must identify handling/action without becoming prop-showcase sound.

Reject:
- random button clicks not tied to blocking;
- repeated generic tech beeps;
- Foley louder than the dialogue function it supports;
- object sound that implies an operation the scene did not perform.
