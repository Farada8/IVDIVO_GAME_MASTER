This addendum makes the P51/P52/P53 automatic emotional-dialogue control stack persistent across conversations and production sessions.

## Mandatory automatic route

For applicable important dialogue/relationship work:

**P51 VOICE FINGERPRINT → P52 EMOTIONAL DYNAMIC RANGE → P53 DIALOGUE ELECTRICITY / FEMALE GAZE / VULNERABILITY → RED TEAM → HUMAN SIGNAL when available.**

The studio must silently select and run the relevant P53 control when writing/revising:
- important dialogue;
- romance-bearing scenes;
- professional/investigative scenes between romantic leads when relationship state changes;
- action/rescue aftermath;
- episode/chapter completion;
- 3–6 episode/chapter block audits;
- Development Edit;
- audio direction;
- visual promo / Runway / key art / trailers;
- final lock regression.

## P53 does NOT mean “add romance everywhere”

Every scene is first classified. Non-romance scenes remain non-romance scenes. P53 may strengthen vulnerability, asymmetry, dignity protection, subtext and changed trust/status without adding flirtation.

## Canonized high-leverage mechanisms

1. Push–pull must come from real story pressure.
2. Male attraction pattern: public competence + private cost; vulnerability without helplessness.
3. Heroine remains agentic; competence may carry hidden emotional cost.
4. Female gaze: each lead sees a private truth others miss; boundaries are respected.
5. Safety enables chemistry; coercion is not romantic proof.
6. Use only 1–2 earned micro-rewards per scene.
7. **ACTION PEAK ≠ EMOTIONAL PEAK:** complete safe causal action first; emotional aftermath may peak afterward.
8. Necessary touch ends when function ends; the release may carry more charge than contact.
9. Transformation is proved by costly choice, not perfect speech.
10. Professional truth outranks romantic convenience.
11. Attraction may change attention, not flatten character voices.
12. Locked material receives regression audit before patching.

## Failure gates

FATAL:
- broken causality;
- coercion romanticized;
- heroine agency collapse;
- professional stupidity introduced for chemistry;
- abuse excused by vulnerability;
- canon/clue damage;
- false marketing relationship promise.

MAJOR:
- chemistry only reads as professional respect;
- male lead has no private cost across the arc;
- heroine is emotionally invulnerable;
- repetitive push–pull or micro-gesture;
- relationship state unchanged after major romance-bearing scene;
- generic romance/body-language language;
- P53 overwhelms story engine.

## Automatic internal control question

Before completing an applicable important scene, silently ask:

**WHAT DOES P51 PROTECT?  
WHAT TEMPERATURE DOES P52 REQUIRE?  
WHAT HUMAN VOLTAGE DOES P53 EARN?  
WHAT MUST NOT BE ROMANTICIZED?  
WHAT CHOICE CHANGES THE RELATIONSHIP?**

Do not print the checklist unless Founder requests the audit.

Do not reopen locked material because this addendum exists. Regression-audit first. Patch only FATAL, MAJOR, high-leverage MEDIUM, direct Founder instruction, verified external/human feedback, or factual/continuity error.
