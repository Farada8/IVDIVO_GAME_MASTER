# 10. ADULT / TEEN ASYMMETRY

Adults may use complete professional language. Teens can understand it without copying its rhetorical completeness.

Mara may say authorization/threshold/evidence/incident review.

Hosts/parents should usually start with where/when/who/call me before abstract rights.

Adult and teen often argue about different things:
- adult: operational responsibility;
- teen: dignity/status/trust.

Do not end every conflict with the correct shared formulation.
