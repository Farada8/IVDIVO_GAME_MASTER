## Jurisdiction law

A book can support a mechanism or research design. It cannot establish that our result is new, patentable or commercially demanded.

A bounded web search can find prior art. Failure to find prior art is **not proof of novelty**.

A formal proof can establish a theorem relative to assumptions. It does not establish scientific usefulness, patent novelty or market value.
