# BODYGUARD / IVDIVO — ZERO-COST EXTERNAL EVIDENCE POLICY v1.0

**STATUS:** CURRENT PROJECT LAW
**DATE:** 2026-08-21

## Founder decision
Paid external reviewers / consultants / practitioners are excluded from the BODYGUARD multilingual voice QA path.

## Replacement evidence ladder
1. official standards / provider documentation / technical manuals;
2. published practitioner interviews, public professional articles, public technical riders, glossaries and training materials;
3. public practitioner discussions / specialist forums / communities;
4. voluntary native-speaker / language-exchange / hobby-community review;
5. independent AI review only as secondary diagnostic evidence;
6. model inference = DISCOVERY_ONLY unless corroborated.

## Lock law
A paid reviewer is never a prerequisite.

For a technical term/line to advance from WORKING to LOCK_CANDIDATE, require either:
- one strong documentary/professional source + one independent public/practitioner source; or
- two independent practitioner/public sources; or
- one objective official standard/manual when the issue is definitional rather than conversational.

For spoken naturalness, documentary evidence alone is insufficient. Prefer 2+ independent voluntary native speakers or public native-language discussion with concrete line-level correction evidence.

## Hard firewall
Do not fabricate human responses.
Do not treat AI agreement as human consensus.
Do not pay for review merely to unblock production.
If zero-cost evidence is insufficient, mark HOLD and continue with reversible preparation.
