# IVDIVO — P53 AUDIO / VISUAL / MARKETING CONTROL PROMPTS v1.0

## AUDIO CHEMISTRY

P53 AUDIO CHEMISTRY PASS.

Convert invisible emotion into playable sound: timing, breath, silence, changed register, interruption, unfinished line, object handling, distance and functional SFX. Do not add narrator explanation if actors can perform it. Check male vulnerability and heroine hidden cost are hearable.

---

## VISUAL FEMALE-GAZE

P53 VISUAL FEMALE-GAZE PASS.

Preserve story causality and character identity. Check heroine agency, male competence, male private fracture, heroine hidden cost, attention asymmetry, eye-line logic, necessary contact remaining functional, release carrying chemistry, action peak separated from safe emotional aftermath, and no fake relationship escalation.

Cold audience must understand premise before prestige theme.

---

## KEY ART / THUMBNAIL

P53 KEY-ART GATE.

Score premise clarity, heroine agency, male competence, male vulnerability, chemistry, female-gaze fantasy, danger/mystery, mobile readability and truthful promise.

Reject if click promise implies a more explicit, coercive or different romance than the story delivers.

---

## TRAILER ROMANCE TRUTH

P53 TRAILER TRUTH PASS.

Trailer must sell the strongest real emotional promise. Do not fabricate kiss, possession, rescue dependency, villain reveal or intimacy stage.

Prefer choice, private fracture, attention, rescue aftermath, public cost and honored boundary.

---

## HUMAN SIGNAL INTERPRETATION

P53 HUMAN SIGNAL REVIEW.

Given blind-reader/listener/viewer feedback, separate repeated actionable defect, isolated taste, mix/performance misunderstanding, real script problem and marketing-promise mismatch.

Do not rewrite because one articulate person dislikes a choice. Prioritize repeated evidence and exact drop/confusion points.
