# BODYGUARD FOR THE FALLEN IDOL
## BLIND FEMALE-AUDIENCE LISTENER PROTOCOL v1.1

**STATUS:** MANDATORY PILOT TEST  
**TARGET:** 12 listeners if practical; minimum 10.  
**CORE AUDIENCE:** women 18–44, useful concentration 25–35.  
Preference clusters are hypotheses, not fixed psychotypes.

# BLINDNESS LAW
Before first listen do NOT tell listeners: culprit, “female gaze,” intended chemistry, endgame couple, key clues, P53/Grok findings, or intended dopamine/oxytocin effects.

# TEST ORDER
A — 64-second cold open.  
B — full E01.  
C — E01–E03 binge after E01 responses are locked.

# FREE RESPONSE FIRST
Ask:
1. What happened?
2. Who is Naomi?
3. Who is Eli?
4. What is the main danger/problem?
5. What are you most curious about next?
6. Describe Naomi/Eli relationship in your own words.
7. Any moment you wanted to replay? Why?
8. Where did you become bored, confused or emotionally detached? Approximate time.

# SCORED RESPONSE SECOND — 0–10
Hook clarity; action clarity; Naomi competence/appeal; Eli competence/appeal; Eli vulnerability; Naomi emotional dimension; chemistry; safety/trust; mystery curiosity; voice distinction; clue clarity; pacing; final-hook strength; desire to continue.

# FEMALE-GAZE DIAGNOSTICS
Only after free response:
- What makes Eli attractive/unattractive besides appearance/status?
- Did he feel helpless anywhere?
- Did Naomi feel emotionally flat anywhere?
- Did Naomi have her own objective?
- Did Eli respect competence/boundaries?
- Any maternal rather than romantic moment?
- Did chemistry exist without touch?
- Did either character seem owed romance because of rescue/protection?

# SLOW-BURN CALIBRATION
Choose closest:
A. Just colleagues.  
B. Becoming personally important but still professional.  
C. Already basically a couple.  
D. No relationship tension.  
E. Other.

Early-pilot target: B. Mostly A/D = underpowered. Mostly C = overheated/boundary blurred.

# OPTIONAL PREFERENCE CLUSTERS
Competent heroine; slow-burn/anticipation; vulnerability/healing; protective/chosen; power/status contrast; mystery/detail/re-watch; other. Use only to segment reactions.

# TIMESTAMP EVIDENCE
Record first confusion; first boredom; first rewind impulse; strongest Naomi moment; strongest Eli moment; strongest chemistry moment; strongest mystery moment; hypothetical app-stop moment. Clusters matter more than isolated eloquent opinions.

# DEVICE SPLIT
At least 4 headphone, 3 phone/small-speaker, 3 mono-reference first exposures.

# PICKUP TRIGGERS
Consider change when 3+ independent listeners identify the same actionable problem; the same drop/confusion timestamp clusters; 2+ performers/voices fail the same line; mono/phone destroys a required clue; continuity/professional/factual error appears; or later retention data confirms it.

One articulate dislike = WATCH, not rewrite.

# HARD ROMANCE FAILS
Escalate if repeated: Eli helpless/childlike; Naomi maternal; Naomi emotionally flat; chemistry only work respect; they already seem secretly together in E01; public defense possessive; safety authority coercive; attraction slows emergency action.

# SUCCESS
Blind listeners can independently retell, in substance: a threatened performer trusted a new bodyguard's command; she saved him; public images can make her look guilty; the audio route is wrong; and they are beginning to matter to each other without already being a couple.
