## HARD CLUE LOCKS

Russian localization must preserve as distinct audible concepts:

1. FOUR vs TWO.
2. RIGHT = approved people/talk path.
3. LEFT = music/rehearsal path.
4. STANDBY ≠ ACKNOWLEDGEMENT ≠ GO.
5. Speech on LEFT is abnormal.
6. Final inference is insider access to a non-speech path, not merely “an unusual message”.
