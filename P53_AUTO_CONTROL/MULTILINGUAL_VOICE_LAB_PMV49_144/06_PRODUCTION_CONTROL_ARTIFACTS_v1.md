## 3. RU Naomi Voice Design Candidates v1

### N1 — GROUNDED PRECISION
Baseline. Early/mid-30s low-mezzo/alto impression; natural contemporary Russian; exact economical speech; professional authority without military pressure. Danger = clearer/faster, not louder. Private cost = quieter, never breathy.

### N2 — WARM UNDER CONTROL
Slightly more accessible timbre while preserving command clarity. Minimal human warmth in neutral speech; private pressure expressed mainly through timing/silence.

### N3 — DRIER OPERATIONAL
Boundary challenger. Strong operational precision and dryness; must prove it does not collapse into ice-queen monotone or robot affect.

**Naomi forbidden modes:** maternal protector; seductive bodyguard; ice-queen monotone; action-hero growl; breathy romance; shouting-as-authority.

## 4. RU Eli Voice Design Candidates v1

### E1 — PRIVATE MUSICIAN
Natural medium/medium-low early-30s male voice; musician timing; no announcer polish. Public warmer/more controlled, private shorter/lower/less performed. One rhythm/breath fracture may reveal cost without helplessness.

### E2 — PUBLIC TO PRIVATE
Stronger public/private contrast. Stage-trained, attractive but natural. Music observations must sound like native professional thought rather than exposition.

### E3 — UNDERPLAYED CONTROL
Boundary challenger. Very restrained private delivery; must preserve cognition, agency and musician specificity rather than becoming flat/apathic.

**Eli forbidden modes:** helpless celebrity; possessive alpha; permanent charming smile; trauma exhibition; announcer; romantic pressure.

## 7. RU Native Blind Listener Protocol v1

Blind candidate labels, loudness matched, no music, no final reverb, identical text inside comparison, randomized order where practical.

Ask free-response first:
- what does the character want now;
- natural modern Russian 0–10;
- dubbing/announcer/translation feel;
- inferred profession/status;
- unnatural timestamp;
- subtext felt but not stated;
- confusing professional term;
- wrong read: aggression / helplessness / flirtation / maternal care / villain coding;
- willingness to listen for 10 minutes;
- preferred anonymous candidate and concrete reason.

Pair test asks control/trust/boundary/relationship-state before asking directly about attraction.

Evidence rule: one opinion = WATCH. Three repeated independent issues at same function/timestamp = PATCH CANDIDATE, except a qualified reviewer can identify an objective native/pro terminology error directly.

## 8. Multilingual Locale Risk Matrix v1

- **RU:** translationese, dubbing/announcer tone, professional loanwords, brevity misread as hostility. Pilot priority 1.
- **EN:** source authority; risks are RU-master accent leakage, back-translation contamination, public/private drift. Priority 2.
- **ES-ES / ES-LATAM-MX:** regional fork mandatory; risks include pronouns, status language, warmth inflation, false “neutral Spanish.” Priority 3 after business decision.
- **DE:** bureaucratic calque, compound jargon, sentence-final load, false emotional distance. Prep only.
- **IT:** warmth/intimacy inflation, melodramatic cadence, professional jargon naturalness. Prep only.
