**STATUS:** MANDATORY WORKING PRODUCTION STANDARD

This v1.1 retains the full v1.0 P51/P52/P53 auto-control system and adds the following mandatory advanced micro-beat controls:

## ADVANCED MICRO-BEAT CONTROL

### A. PUBLIC SILENCE / PRIVATE RECOGNITION
Support does not require a public romantic gesture. A character may remain publicly disciplined while a private look, timing shift or altered attention communicates recognition.

### B. BREATH SYNCHRONY — ONE-TIME DEVICE
Brief breath synchronization may be used as a rare embodied chemistry beat after safety is established. Use once per meaningful block unless a new function justifies repetition.

### C. ROLE RECOVERY
After a private fracture or micro-intimate beat, characters may return to professional/public roles sooner than the audience wants. The recovery itself creates restraint and anticipation.

### D. MICRO-BEAT REPETITION GATE
Each micro-beat must perform a different function: respect / fear / rescue / cost / dignity / recognition / public choice. Repeated long looks, fist clenches, delayed blinks, breath catches or almost-touches become mannerism.

### E. PROFESSIONAL CONTACT LAW
Before voluntary intimacy is earned, physical contact must remain functionally justified where professional roles require it: protection, medical assessment, rescue, positioning or safety. Contact ends when function ends. Chemistry may live in the release, aftermath or attention.

### F. RECOGNITION WITHOUT CARETAKING
A lead may notice fear, fatigue, shame or physiological reaction and choose not to expose it. Protecting dignity can be more intimate than comforting. Male vulnerability must not create a maternal heroine dynamic.

### G. PUBLIC CHOICE / PRIVATE SEARCH
A costly public statement may create a private relational beat if the speaker then looks for the other person without converting the public act into possession or claim. The other person need not perform gratitude.

### H. MASK RECONSTRUCTION
A strong beat often ends with both characters rebuilding public/professional masks after the private truth has already been seen. The new relationship state survives beneath restored control.

### I. FANTASY CALIBRATION
UNDER = “They are just colleagues.”
OVER = “They are already basically a couple.”
TARGET = “They are trying very hard to remain only colleagues.”

These rules are additive refinements to the full v1.0 system: Story causality > character truth > emotional voltage > continuity > packaging.
