## INITIAL CANONIZED LEARNINGS

### SYS-20260821-P53-001 — After-action emotional peak
**Signal:** external review + Bodyguard application.
**Finding:** rescue/action chemistry becomes stronger and professionally safer when action completes first and emotional consequence lands in the short safe aftermath.
**Decision:** ACCEPT WITH MODIFICATION.
**Boundary:** never delay safe action or prolong functional contact.
**Write-through:** P53 / visual / dialogue runtime.

### SYS-20260821-P53-002 — Male public competence + private fracture
**Signal:** repeated Bodyguard review.
**Finding:** emotionally controlled male leads can read flat; one precise private cost can increase attraction without helplessness.
**Decision:** ACCEPT.
**Boundary:** no infantilization or heroine-as-mother.

### SYS-20260821-P53-003 — Female gaze through recognition and choice
**Signal:** Bodyguard visual/dialogue passes.
**Finding:** chemistry strengthens when he notices heroine competence/cost and she sees the private price under his public control; attraction should change attention before it changes behavior.
**Decision:** ACCEPT.

### SYS-20260821-PRODUCTION-004 — Text is not always earliest failure
**Signal:** E01–E24 regression + pilot preparation.
**Finding:** after story lock, chemistry/clarity defects may belong to casting/performance/mix. Rewrite must be last-cost intervention.
**Decision:** ACCEPT.
**Write-through:** pickup hierarchy, human-evidence gate, self-improvement engine.

### SYS-20260821-PERSISTENCE-005 — Chat-only work is not reliable authority
**Signal:** cross-conversation production.
**Finding:** important adjacent-chat results must be persisted and reconciled before becoming authority.
**Decision:** ACCEPT.
**Write-through:** global autorun + delta-sync.

### SYS-20260821-PROMPTS-006 — Prompt growth can become its own defect
**Signal:** accumulation of P51/P52/P53/routing standards.
**Finding:** duplicate full-text rules increase contradictions and context cost.
**Decision:** ACCEPT.
**Action:** use inheritance headers + specialist routing; do not paste the full global standard into every prompt.
