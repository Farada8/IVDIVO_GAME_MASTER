## Core mechanisms

- push–pull from real story pressure;
- male competence + private cost;
- heroine agency + hidden emotional cost;
- female gaze through private recognition, not possession;
- safety + chemistry;
- earned micro-rewards;
- action peak may differ from emotional peak;
- necessary professional contact ends when function ends; release may carry more charge;
- public silence can still carry private recognition;
- breath synchrony is rare, not a recurring code;
- role recovery after a private fracture can increase slow-burn tension;
- micro-beats must perform different functions across a block;
- transformation through costly choice;
- professional truth before romantic convenience;
- anti-cliché / anti-symmetrical generated dialogue;
- story remains first.
