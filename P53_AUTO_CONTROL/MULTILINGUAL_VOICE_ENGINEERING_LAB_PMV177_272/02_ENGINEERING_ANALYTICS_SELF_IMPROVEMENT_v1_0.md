## Findings

1. Ask experts less, not more: 5 precise questions outperform a request to rewrite an episode.
2. Reviewer output enters a decision ledger and cannot edit authority directly.
3. One baseline preview per lead precedes challenger variants.
4. Secrets remain runtime-only; provider capability facts may persist.
5. The first provider call must buy information, not volume.
6. A universal rule requires independent evidence and rollback.
7. A 3–4 minute slice is the correct first render-scale unit after voice/render lock.
8. Prompt count is now a scheduling interface; quality improvement comes from evidence, tests, telemetry and selective invalidation.
