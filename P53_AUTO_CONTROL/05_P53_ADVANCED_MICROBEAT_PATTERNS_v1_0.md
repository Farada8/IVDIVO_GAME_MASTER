# IVDIVO — P53 ADVANCED MICRO-BEAT PATTERNS v1.0

**Status:** MANDATORY supplementary control under P53 v1.1

These patterns generalize the strongest external-review findings. They are not a separate P54 program.

## 1. PUBLIC SILENCE / PRIVATE RECOGNITION
Support can remain publicly disciplined while private recognition registers through timing, gaze, changed formality or attention. Do not require a public romantic gesture.

## 2. BREATH SYNCHRONY — RARE DEVICE
A short shared breathing rhythm may carry chemistry only after safety is established. Use once per meaningful block unless a new function justifies repetition.

## 3. ROLE RECOVERY
A private fracture becomes stronger when both characters rebuild their professional/public masks sooner than the audience wants. Restraint creates anticipation.

## 4. MICRO-BEAT FUNCTION DIVERSITY
Across a block, micro-beats must not repeat the same function. Track distinct functions such as respect, fear, rescue, cost, dignity, recognition and public choice.

## 5. PROFESSIONAL CONTACT LAW
Before voluntary intimacy is earned, physical contact may occur only when functionally justified by rescue, medical assessment, protection, positioning or safety. Contact ends when function ends. Emotional charge may live in the release and aftermath.

## 6. RECOGNITION WITHOUT CARETAKING
One lead may see fatigue, fear, shame or physiological reaction and choose not to expose it. Protecting dignity can be intimate without creating a maternal/caretaker dynamic.

## 7. PUBLIC CHOICE / PRIVATE SEARCH
After a costly public statement, the speaker may privately look for the other person. This works only if it remains recognition rather than possession, claim or demand for gratitude.

## 8. MASK RECONSTRUCTION
The relationship state can change even when visible control returns. The audience should remember what was seen before the mask came back.

## 9. FANTASY CALIBRATION
UNDER: “They are just colleagues.”
OVER: “They are already basically a couple.”
TARGET: “They are trying very hard to remain only colleagues.”

## 10. AUDIT QUESTIONS
- Is this micro-beat performing a new function?
- Is the male lead vulnerable without losing competence?
- Is the heroine emotionally affected without losing agency?
- Does the beat protect dignity rather than demand care?
- Is touch functional and promptly released where power/professional boundaries require it?
- Does role recovery increase rather than kill tension?
- Does the scene end with a changed relational state?

Story causality and professional truth remain higher authority than emotional ornament.
