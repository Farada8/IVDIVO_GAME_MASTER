# IVDIVO — MULTI-AI REVIEW & HANDOFF PROTOCOL v1.0

**STATUS:** CURRENT CROSS-MODEL STANDARD

## PURPOSE

Use GPT/Claude/Grok/other systems as specialized evidence generators without turning model agreement into canon or forcing the Founder to shuttle context manually.

## ROLE ASSIGNMENT

Models are assigned by task, not brand prestige:

- **Primary Integrator** — restores authority, reconciles findings, produces final write-through.
- **Structural Adversary** — hunts causality, motivation, climax, escalation and contradiction failures.
- **Cold Reader / Retention Adversary** — asks what creates anticipation, boredom, confusion and next-action desire.
- **Character / Relationship Specialist** — P51/P52/P53, agency, vulnerability, power/consent, chemistry.
- **Mystery / Evidence Specialist** — clue fairness, alternatives, proof domains, no-confession logic.
- **Production Specialist** — audio/visual/tool feasibility, costs, QC, medium translation.

One model may fill multiple roles, but findings must remain labeled by role.

## SAME-SOURCE LAW

For comparative review, give reviewers the same locked source/version and the same protected invariants.

Do not compare reviews generated from different drafts and call disagreement “model quality.”

## REVIEW OUTPUT CONTRACT

Every external reviewer must separate:

### DIAGNOSIS
What fails? Where? Why? Severity? Evidence in source?

### PROPOSED FIX
Smallest change that solves the diagnosed failure.

### RISKS
What could the fix break?

### TEST
How would we know the fix is better?

No reviewer may silently rewrite canon as part of diagnosis.

## INTEGRATION DECISIONS

Primary Integrator classifies every finding:
- ACCEPT;
- ACCEPT WITH MODIFICATION;
- HOLD FOR TEST;
- DUPLICATE / ALREADY COVERED;
- CONFLICT WITH AUTHORITY;
- REJECT.

Model voting is prohibited.

Three models repeating the same assumption are still one weak evidence family if they share the same source and framing.

## P53 CROSS-MODEL REVIEW

For important romance-bearing dialogue, external reviewers may be asked specifically:
- does male vulnerability preserve competence?
- does heroine hidden cost preserve agency?
- is chemistry stronger than professional respect but weaker than couple-status?
- does safety increase attraction rather than flatten conflict?
- are micro-beats pair-specific rather than stock?
- does action peak precede emotional aftermath correctly?
- does choice change relationship state?

External findings feed the P53 Dialogue Runtime; they do not replace it.

## PERSISTENCE / HANDOFF

A useful external-AI result must be persisted as:
1. source review or concise review digest;
2. integration decision record;
3. accepted patch in the relevant CURRENT artifact;
4. learning-ledger entry if mechanism generalizes;
5. updated next-state/handoff if it changes frontier.

If it remains only in a neighboring chat, mark DISCOVERY_ONLY.

## HANDOFF PACKET

When assigning work to another AI, include only:
- active project/book;
- exact source/version;
- canon/locked invariants;
- specific role;
- exact question/task;
- required output contract;
- known accepted mechanisms relevant to task;
- forbidden changes;
- desired evidence/test.

Do not dump the entire franchise unless the task requires it.

## RETURN PACKET

External AI should return:
- SOURCE VERSION REVIEWED;
- ROLE;
- FINDINGS ranked FATAL/MAJOR/MEDIUM/POLISH;
- EVIDENCE;
- PROPOSED FIXES;
- RISKS;
- TESTS;
- UNCERTAINTIES.

The Primary Integrator then performs reconciliation and write-through.

## FINAL LAW

**External AI expands the writers’ room; it does not become a hidden authority layer.**
