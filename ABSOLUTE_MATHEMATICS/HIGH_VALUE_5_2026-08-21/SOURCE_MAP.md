# SOURCE MAP — HIGH-VALUE FIVE

1. Creutzig, Globerson, Tishby (2009), *Past-future information bottleneck in dynamical systems* — structural phase transitions corresponding to additional predictive-state dimensions.
https://journals.aps.org/pre/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevE.79.041925

2. Wu & Fischer (2020), *Phase Transitions for the Information Bottleneck in Representation Learning* — established IB phase-transition analysis.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.01878

3. Palmer et al. (2021), *Optimal prediction with resource constraints using the information bottleneck* — predictive compression and resource-constrained phase transitions.
https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1008743

4. Causal-state review — minimal sufficient predictive states, essentially unique up to isomorphism.
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2849313/

5. nLab, *idempotent monad* — idempotent reflection/localization analogy for Promotion fixed points.
https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/idempotent+monad

6. Williams & Beer (2010), *Nonnegative Decomposition of Multivariate Information* — PID, synergy and redundancy.
https://arxiv.org/abs/1004.2515

7. van Enk (2023), *Pooling probability distributions and partial information decomposition* — ambiguity/freedom in PID definitions.
https://journals.aps.org/pre/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevE.107.054133

8. Lyu, Clark, Raviv (2026), *Multivariate partial information decomposition: constructions, inconsistencies, and alternative measures* — recent multivariate PID consistency subtleties.
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41998903/

9. Capocchi & Santucci (2025), *Deterministic and heuristic criteria for optimized Markov chain aggregation* — optimal/heuristic Markov aggregation.
https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s12597-025-00969-z

10. *Markov Chain Aggregation with Error Bounds* (2024) — epsilon-almost exact lumpability and coarse partition search.
https://www.unibw.de/technische-informatik/mitarbeitende/professoren/siegle/publikationen/asmta_2024.pdf

Conclusion: novelty, if any, must lie in a precise integrated Promotion Feasibility/Complexity framework rather than in IB transitions, minimization fixed points, PID, or Markov aggregation individually.
