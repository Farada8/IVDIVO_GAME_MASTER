# SOURCE MAP — BATCH 32

1. Singh, James, Rudary — Predictive State Representations: A New Theory for Modeling Dynamical Systems. https://arxiv.org/abs/1207.4167
   Relevance: state represented by predictions of observable futures; strong analogue for future-equivalence/minimal predictive state.

2. Crutchfield & Shalizi — Thermodynamic Depth of Causal States / causal-state minimal predictive representations. https://journals.aps.org/pre/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevE.59.275
   Relevance: minimal necessary predictive causal states; warning against arbitrary macrostate choices.

3. Universal algebra quotient/congruence lecture notes. https://math.hawaii.edu/~ralph/Classes/619/UA-Valeriote.pdf
   Relevance: quotient operations are well-defined under congruence; basis for homomorphic-image theorem.

4. Capocchi & Santucci (2025) — Deterministic and heuristic criteria for optimized Markov chain aggregation. https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s12597-025-00969-z
   Relevance: exact lumpability gives a Markov aggregate; quasi-lumpability gives approximate reduction.

5. Minimal state-space realization overview. https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0377042700003411
   Relevance: minimal iff controllable/observable; minimal realizations unique up to similarity.

6. Franke et al. — Markov Switching Time Series Models. https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/B9780444538581000053
   Relevance: established regime-switching / hidden Markov framework.

7. Linderman et al. — Recurrent Switching Linear Dynamical Systems. https://arxiv.org/abs/1610.08466
   Relevance: decomposition into simpler dynamical units with state/observation-dependent switching.

8. Balsells-Rodas et al. — On the Identifiability of Switching Dynamical Systems. https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.15925
   Relevance: identifiability and coordinate ambiguity in switching latent systems.

9. Israeli & Goldenfeld — Coarse-graining of cellular automata, emergence, and predictability. https://arxiv.org/abs/nlin/0508033
   Relevance: established cellular-automaton coarse-graining and induced rule hierarchies.

10. Memory in coarse-grained simulations / Mori-Zwanzig review. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8154603/
    Relevance: removed degrees of freedom can reappear as memory and fluctuating force.

These sources ground analogues and controls. None by itself establishes the project's combined Promotion/Order framework as novel mathematics.
