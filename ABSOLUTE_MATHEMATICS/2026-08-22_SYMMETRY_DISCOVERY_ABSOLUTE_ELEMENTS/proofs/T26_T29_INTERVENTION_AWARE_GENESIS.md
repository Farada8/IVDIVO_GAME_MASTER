# T26–T29 — INTERVENTION-AWARE GENESIS

## T26 — Operational symmetry group

A symmetry relevant to next-tier promotion must preserve not only current observables/tasks but also the declared intervention structure.

Let G_T be the symmetry group of the task family and G_I the symmetry group compatible with the intervention labels/actions. Define:

G_operational = G_T intersect G_I.

Only permutations in G_operational may be quotiented away by a promotion that claims to preserve both observation and intervention capability.

Thus observational equivalence alone is insufficient for operational promotion.

## T27 — More distinguished tasks/interventions can only refine relation types

Let H <= G act on the same relation set R.

Every G-orbit is a union of H-orbits, because H has fewer transformations available to identify points.

Therefore:

|R/H| >= |R/G|.

So when new tasks/interventions shrink the accepted symmetry group, the number of distinguishable relation types cannot decrease.

## T28 — Four-peer addressability ladder

Start with four observationally interchangeable peers.

### No addressable role
G = S4, |G| = 24. Relation types = 4.

### One separately addressable peer
Its label must be fixed. G becomes the stabilizer of one point, isomorphic to S3, |G| = 6. Relation types = 7.

### Two separately addressable peers
Fix two labels; remaining two may swap. G ~= S2, |G| = 2. Relation types = 11.

### All four separately addressable
G = identity. Relation types = 15.

Hence the same four-peer system admits the ladder:

4 -> 7 -> 11 -> 15

relation types as addressable operational distinctions are introduced.

## T29 — Promotion invalidation/refinement law

A compressed next-tier representation P certified under (G_old, Tasks_old, Interventions_old) must be reconsidered if new requirements produce:

G_new < G_old

or if hidden residual exceeds the certified threshold.

The old projection may remain sufficient, but this is no longer guaranteed.

A safe engine must either:
1. prove P remains sufficient under the refined requirement set; or
2. refine P by adding invariants/features until sufficiency is restored.

Therefore next-tier promotion is not permanent ontology. It is a versioned contract relative to capabilities.

# Consequence for Absolute Elements

Absolute elements should be defined relative to the strongest intended capability set, not merely the current passive observations.

Otherwise a seemingly valid symmetric compression can erase distinctions required by future control.
