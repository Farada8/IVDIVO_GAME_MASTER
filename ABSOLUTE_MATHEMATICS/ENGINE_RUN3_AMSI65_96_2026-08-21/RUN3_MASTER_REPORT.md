## Core mathematical corrections

### Generator novelty is language-relative

On the finite Boolean control, `NOT` is not term-definable from `{AND}`, so it is `NEW_RELATIVE_TO_L` for that declared weak language. Adding a definitional extension containing NOT makes the same operation `DEFINABLE`.

Therefore a context-free claim of generator novelty is invalid. Active notation is `G-Rise_L`, with an explicit closure level and extension audit.

### Genesis complexity is vocabulary-relative

Target value 8 has depth 3 from primitive `1` plus doubling, but depth 0 if `8` is admitted as a primitive. Thus genesis depth is not intrinsic unless invariance under an admissible vocabulary family is proved.

### Unique genesis normal form requires confluence evidence

The rewrite control `ab→a`, `ab→b` produces a nonjoinable critical branch. Feedback process graphs are now summarized through SCC modules; uniqueness claims require termination/confluence evidence rather than naming a normal form.
