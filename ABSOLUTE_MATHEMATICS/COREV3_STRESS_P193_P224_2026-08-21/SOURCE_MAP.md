# SOURCE MAP — CORE v3 STRESS BATCH

1. Probabilistic bisimulation / approximation metrics survey
https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1571066113000820
Supports approximate behavioral metrics for Markov processes.

2. Buchholz — Exact and ordinary lumpability in finite Markov chains
https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-applied-probability/article/abs/exact-and-ordinary-lumpability-in-finite-markov-chains/2DC748F09D80BEEB03CCF18036E149D7
Supports exact/ordinary lumpability and near-lumpability distinction.

3. Proportional lumpability and proportional bisimilarity
https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00236-021-00404-y
States ordinary lumpability conditions and relationship to exact aggregation.

4. Approximate probabilistic bisimulation for CTMCs (2025)
https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.15587
Recent approximate-bisimulation tolerances and reachability bounds.

5. Predictive State Representation spectral/Hankel learning
https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0925231218305642
Supports Hankel-matrix/SVD approach to PSR learning.

6. Causal-state minimal sufficient prediction
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2849313/
Supports causal states as essentially unique minimal sufficient predictive statistics.

7. MDP abstraction / homomorphism similarity taxonomy
https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10994-022-06242-4
Supports state-action abstraction and MDP homomorphism perspective.

8. Minimal state-space realization overview
https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0377042700003411
Supports minimal iff controllable/observable and uniqueness up to similarity.

9. Sussmann — nonlinear minimal realization uniqueness
https://www.researchwithrutgers.org/en/publications/existence-and-uniqueness-of-minimal-realizations-of-nonlinear-sys/
Supports uniqueness up to isomorphism for an analytic nonlinear system class.

10. Israeli & Goldenfeld — coarse-graining of cellular automata
https://journals.aps.org/pre/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevE.73.026203
Established coarse-graining/factor hierarchies in cellular automata.

11. Cousot & Cousot — Abstract Interpretation Frameworks
https://pcousot.github.io/publications/CousotCousot-JLC-n2--3-p103--179-1992.pdf
Supports abstraction/concretization via Galois connections.

These sources reduce novelty risk by showing that many state-reduction/behavior-preservation pieces are established. Novelty, if any, must be demonstrated at the level of a precise new theorem or the integrated falsification protocol.
