## Interpretation

These sources make it unsafe to claim novelty for quotient/factor dynamics, minimal predictive state, exact lumpability, minimal realization, predictive compression, CA coarse graining or semiconjugacy itself. Any novelty claim must concern a precise new theorem, benchmark methodology or integration not reducible to those established frameworks.
