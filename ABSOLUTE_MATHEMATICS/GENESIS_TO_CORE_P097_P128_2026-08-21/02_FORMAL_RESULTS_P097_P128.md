# ABSOLUTE MATHEMATICS — FORMAL RESULTS AFTER P097–P128

## T3 — Quotient operation well-definedness requires congruence

Let A be an algebra with operation f and ~ an equivalence relation. Define candidate quotient operation by `fbar([x1],...,[xn])=[f(x1,...,xn)]`. This is well-defined exactly when `xi~yi` for all i implies `f(x1,...,xn)~f(y1,...,yn)`. The fresh Z12→Z3 control passed for + and ×; a deliberately bad partition on Z3 supplied a counter-witness.

## T4 — Composition of exact Promotions is exact

If `B0∘F0=F1∘B0` and `B1∘F1=F2∘B1`, then `(B1∘B0)∘F0=F2∘(B1∘B0)` by direct substitution. Therefore exact Promotion depth is flattenable unless direct composition is excluded by a separate admissibility/resource/type condition.

## T5 — Approximate rollout defect bound

Suppose macro map G is L-Lipschitz and one-step defect satisfies `d(BF(x),G(Bx))<=epsilon`. Let `e_t=d(BF^t(x),G^t(Bx))`. Then `e_(t+1)<=epsilon+L e_t`; for e0=0, `e_t<=epsilon sum_(j=0)^(t-1) L^j`. Thus L<1 gives a bounded geometric sum, L=1 at most linear growth, and L>1 can amplify geometrically. Small one-step defect is insufficient without stability information.

## T6 — Context enlargement refines behavioral equivalence

Define `x~_K y` iff every context in K yields equal observations. If K1 is a subset of K2, then equivalence under K2 implies equivalence under K1, so `~_(K2)` refines `~_(K1)`. A context-free nontrivial compressed absolute number therefore cannot generally be inferred from one coarse equivalence alone.

## C2 — No universal compression→memory monotonicity

Finite Markov chains were constructed with more compression increasing lumpability defect, leaving it unchanged, and decreasing it. Therefore no universal monotonic law holds for this closure/memory criterion.

## C3 — Finite predictive state need not exist

For regular modulo-3 behavior, finite-horizon future-equivalence classes saturate at 3. For `a^n b^n`, prefixes `a^0,...,a^N` are pairwise distinguishable by continuations `b^0,...,b^N`, so the number of distinguishable predictive classes grows at least N+1. A Reactor that always returns a finite predictive primitive is necessarily nonselective.
