# DETAILED RESULTS P121–P128

## P121 — DIRECT PREDICTIVE-STATE ROUTE

**STATUS:** KNOWN

### Strongest supported claim
Direct predictive-state reconstruction is the established route for asking what information from history is sufficient for future prediction.

### Evidence
Causal states and PSRs avoid guessing a particular memory/regime ontology and instead define state by future predictions/tests.

### Red Team
Pure prediction still does not settle intervention contexts, G-Rise or non-flattenability.

### Decision
Make predictive-state reconstruction the primary state-discovery comparator.

## P122 — CANONICALITY UP TO ISOMORPHISM

**STATUS:** KNOWN

### Strongest supported claim
Minimal state coordinates are generally not unique, while behavior may be unique up to isomorphism/similarity in established classes.

### Evidence
Causal-state theory gives essential uniqueness of the minimal sufficient statistic; LTI minimal realizations are unique up to similarity.

### Red Team
Nonlinear/stochastic latent models need separate identifiability conditions.

### Decision
Replace 'canonical coordinates' with 'canonical equivalence class / representative by convention'.
