# Engineering Contracts — Absolute Mathematics Engine v0.1

## Authority contract
Generic project authority resolution is delegated to merged SI-0012.
Absolute Mathematics receives only a validated SI plan and emits candidate feedback.

## PromotionProblem
Required:
- problem_id;
- micro state universe / representation;
- context/intervention set;
- tolerance;
- admissibility/resource constraints;
- provenance.

## Promotion gate order
1. Authority / source identity.
2. Context completeness.
3. No-Go.
4. Candidate representation.
5. Exact/approx dynamic closure.
6. History sufficiency.
7. Micro sufficiency.
8. Recursive updateability.
9. Construction spectrum.
10. Evidence classification.
11. Candidate decision.

## Self-improvement contract
A change requires:
- immutable parent revision;
- falsifiable hypothesis;
- bounded mutation scope;
- forbidden paths;
- preregistered benchmarks/gates;
- rollback reference;
- regression floor;
- evidence class.

The mutation executes in a sandbox/candidate branch only.

## Self-improvement archive
Do not use pure hill climbing.
Retain:
- best candidates;
- failed informative candidates;
- diverse stepping stones;
- lineage relations;
- evidence explaining failures.

Parent priority may include a bounded descendant-potential proxy, but promotion still requires direct
benchmark/regression evidence.

## Recursive-state contract
A macro-state claim is incomplete until either:
- an online update rule `Z_{t+1}=U(Z_t,o,a)` is provided and validated; or
- an explicit recursive estimator/filter is provided.

Offline compression of full history is not automatically an autonomous state.
