### 6. Production principles

Priority:

`STORY AUTHORITY -> LISTENER COMPREHENSION -> CHARACTER/PERFORMANCE -> BODY/ACTION -> SPACE -> SOUND -> MUSIC -> MIX -> MASTER -> QC -> RELEASE`.

Dialogue/performance is action, not text recitation.
Use microphone-centered performance: proximity, orientation and movement change acting, not merely volume.
Sound must have causal/story function.
Foley embodies action; it does not fill silence.
Music never substitutes for drama or tells the answer early.
Critical clues must remain understandable in mono/phone conditions.
Provider raw alignment must be normalized before timeline logic consumes it.
