FORENSIC TECH: Employment signatures and shift roster corroborate both.

SHAW: Incident time?

[Mara goes still.]

ARCHIVIST: Facilities report says the panel broke inward after the latch jammed during closure. No deliberate damage finding. First-aid note: protected student, left palm-edge laceration, approximately three centimeters, cleaned and closed with strips. No hospital transfer.

### SCENE 2 — OLD HAWTHORNE GREENHOUSE WALKWAY — MIDDAY

[SFX: Outdoor gate. Shoes on gravel. Wind through greenhouse vents.]

NARRATOR: The greenhouse still existed, though half the glass had been replaced by safety panels and the old west door no longer stuck. Mara asked the facilities manager to leave it unlocked and then made everyone walk the route instead of talking over a diagram.

[Mara walks.]

### SCENE 3 — HAWTHORNE VISITOR COURTYARD — AFTERNOON

[SFX: Bench creaks. School sports whistle far away.]

NARRATOR: Adrian arrived without counsel because Mara had not summoned a company officer. She had asked an old witness whether he was willing to sit on a school bench for twenty minutes.

ADRIAN: You found the greenhouse file.

MARA: We found three independent school records and a route assignment.

ADRIAN: And?

MARA: Before I tell you the body mapping, I want your memory unprompted. What do you remember about the girl who was with you?

ADRIAN: I called her Evelyn.

MARA: That is what you believed then.

ADRIAN: Yes.

MARA: What happened?

ADRIAN: The west door jammed. I pulled it too hard. One pane cracked. She pushed me back because glass was coming inward and cut her left hand.

MARA: Where?

ADRIAN: Along the side of the palm. She wrapped it in my handkerchief and told me not to look at it because I was making her nervous.

[Mara looks at her left hand.]

ADRIAN: Then she got angry when I offered to carry her bag.

EVELYN: That part sounds portable.

ADRIAN: She said, “It’s my hand. I can carry my own bag.”

MARA: I have said that.

ADRIAN: More than once.

MARA: Do you remember anything that identifies her beyond the name you believed?

ADRIAN: No. I remember a girl. The cut. Her being furious that security made a bigger scene than she did. I remember thinking she was brave. None of that gives me a legal identity.

[Mara studies him.]

ADRIAN: I have been listening.

MARA: Apparently.

ADRIAN: There is another thing. She came back ten minutes later because she had left a blue notebook under the bench.

SHAW: Hawthorne blue ledger?

MARA: Maybe. We authenticate before linking.

ADRIAN: Of course.

MARA: The independent route records place the protected subject using Mara Quinn on Evelyn’s public route during that incident. Yesterday’s biometric comparison maps that Mara Quinn subject to me.

[Adrian does not answer immediately.]

ADRIAN: So that was you.

MARA: Physically, the evidence supports that.

ADRIAN: I spent years believing that memory belonged inside my marriage.

EVELYN: It did not belong to a marriage yet. We were seventeen.

ADRIAN: Fair.

[Mara waits.]

ADRIAN: I do not know what I am supposed to do with it.

MARA: Nothing today.

ADRIAN: I am not going to call it fate.

MARA: Good.

ADRIAN: I am also not going to pretend it means nothing.

MARA: You do not have to.

ADRIAN: Does it change what you remember?

MARA: I remember wet soil and antiseptic. I do not know whether I remembered them before reading the file. That is the answer I have.

ADRIAN: All right.

NARRATOR: Adrian did not reach for her hand. Mara noticed because five years earlier she would have noticed only if he had.
