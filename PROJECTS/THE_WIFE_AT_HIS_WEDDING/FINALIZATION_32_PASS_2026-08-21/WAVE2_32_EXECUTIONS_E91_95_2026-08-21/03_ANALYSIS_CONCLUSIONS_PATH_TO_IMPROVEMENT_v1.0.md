## 1. What materially improved

### A. The final arc now has a living present-tense conflict
The identity mystery is no longer only archival. Grace Kellan/Heritage records-risk counsel embodies a rational institutional objective: avoid contradictory corrections across civil, financial, medical and family systems. She can oppose Mara without becoming a secret mastermind. This is stronger than a faceless “system” because scenes can produce choices, concessions and leverage.

### B. The proof chain is now cumulative and epistemically explicit
E91–E95 establish a clean ladder:
1. E91 — two separately tracked live newborns + one certificate number in the maternity closeout + an attempted Baby B correction packet.
2. E92 — independent pediatric and transfer records establish Baby B remained alive after hospital transfer and entered protected placement without permanent civil name.
3. E93 — Bellweather/Orison linked the protected Baby B index to a dormant Quinn source before Noah’s involvement; source is authenticated as deceased Mara Elise Quinn.
4. E94 — Noah is partially exonerated on original source mining but remains responsible for later withholding and post-fire reuse of the vaccination-card chain.
5. E95 — Victoria objected to active use of the dead child’s shell; later an authenticated protected teen knowingly chose to keep Mara Quinn while separate lawful identity remained unresolved; dual biometric cards now open body mapping.

This ladder reduces “one magic clue” risk. Each reveal states what it proves and what it does not.

### C. Present-life price arrived before the midpoint
E93 introduces a real forensic-client pause and licensing identity review. This is important: the final arc is no longer “documents about the past.” Historical truth now changes income, professional reliability and daily administration before E101–E105. Future blocks must propagate rather than reset this cost.

### D. Mara/Evelyn finally disagree about remedy, not just memory
E95 creates the first explicit remedy disagreement: Evelyn wants the public record to acknowledge two live births now; Mara wants narrow wording until identity mapping is stronger. Both positions are rational. This is the correct sister-conflict engine for E96–E115.

### E. Noah’s moral structure is cleaner
The wave separates four different acts:
- Bellweather/Orison mine Mara Elise’s damaged record before Noah’s involvement;
- Noah later discovers use of his sister’s identity;
- he blocks one Bellweather request but withholds the discovery from Mara;
- after the yacht he reuses the vaccination card to operationalize the already-existing shell.
This preserves mixed responsibility rather than villain/absolution collapse.

## 2. What remains weak or risky

### MEDIUM 1 — document-density risk remains
All five episodes now include non-desk behavior, but the block is still evidence-heavy by design. E96–E100 must increase ordinary/field action and emotional consequence instead of producing five more archive rooms.

### MEDIUM 2 — corpus rhetorical concentration in Mara
The current Human Scene authority requires corpus-level rhetoric, not only scene naturalism. Mara appropriately owns clipped verification language, but `good / do not / exact distinction / proof-does-not-prove` functions recur frequently. Several lines were minimally varied in Wave 2. At E100 one E91–E100 corpus pass must test C3M12 CONTRASTIVE_SYNTAX_FINGERPRINT and C3M13 ACKNOWLEDGMENT_FUNCTION_MATRIX before declaring the block fully naturalistic.

### MEDIUM 3 — institutional experts can still converge
Shaw, forensic techs, archivists and Kellan share accuracy/procedure vocabulary. Their functions are distinct, but performance could flatten them. Future drafting should separate:
- Shaw = evidentiary strategy / next action;
- Forensic Tech = source mechanics / confidence;
- Archivist = custody / access limits;
- Kellan = administrability / exposure / remedy;
- Mara = sequence / distinction / causal proof.

### EXTERNAL GATE — legal jurisdiction
The story uses high-level plausible court/registrar processes only. Exact forms, statutory burdens, citizenship/tax/licensing consequences remain unverified and must not be presented as real-law authority.

### EXTERNAL GATE — Human Signal / real audio
Text gates cannot prove listener belief, performance naturalness or commercial retention. Those remain downstream.

## 4. Strongest path to the goal

The goal is not “more twists.” The goal is a finished 120-episode season whose final identity resolution feels:
1. earned by cumulative evidence;
2. emotionally human rather than procedural;
3. non-zero-sum for the two sisters;
4. romantically satisfying without turning Adrian into a prize;
5. audible and serially compelling;
6. robust against continuity and legal-magic objections.

Therefore the path is:

E96–E100: BODY MAPPING, not “identity solved.”
Use biometrics to map current bodies to historical teenage subjects, then pay off greenhouse / no-direct-substitution / bride / maternal chain. Keep civil identity unresolved.

E101–E105: MAKE THE CLEAN ANSWER COSTLY.
Turn historical clarity into present civil damage, client/licensing/bank/housing friction and settlement pressure.

E106–E110: SEPARATE NAME / SOURCE / MARRIAGE.
Authenticate informed name choice, Noah source consent, Evelyn’s independent legal motion and Adrian/Evelyn’s separate marriage decision.

E111–E115: PROVE A SECOND PERSON WITHOUT ERASING THE FIRST.
Standing must be cumulative; the legal remedy must be “two records,” not winner-take-all correction.

E116–E120: CHOICE + CONSEQUENCE + ORDINARY LIFE.
Mara’s climax is choosing separate registration/current name without claiming Evelyn’s marriage/maternity. E119 closes the external conflict. E120 proves success through ordinary life, not sequel bait.
