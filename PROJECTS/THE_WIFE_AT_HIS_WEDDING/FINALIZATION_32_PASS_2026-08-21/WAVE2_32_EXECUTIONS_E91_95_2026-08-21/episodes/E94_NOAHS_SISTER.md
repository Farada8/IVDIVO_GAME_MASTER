THE WIFE AT HIS WEDDING
EPISODE 94 — NOAH’S SISTER

SCENE 1 — INDEPENDENT COUNSEL CONFERENCE ROOM — MORNING
[SFX: Recorder starts. Two folders set down.]

NARRATOR: Mara did not invite Noah to explain himself. Explanation was the old currency between them. She asked him to bring documents, counsel and whatever part of the truth could survive without his intentions attached.

NOAH: This is my mother’s original identity-theft complaint. Certified copy from the reconstructed municipal file.

MARA: Chain.

NOAH’S COUNSEL: Obtained directly under yesterday’s source order. Repository certification attached.

SHAW: Bellweather access history is in the second folder.

MARA: Noah, before we open either one: when did you first learn somebody was using Mara Elise’s record?

NOAH: Four months before Rosegate.

MARA: Not eleven years after the birth.

NOAH: No.

MARA: Not when Bellweather first linked Quinn to B-Q-seven.

NOAH: No.

MARA: How do we prove that besides your memory?

NOAH: Employment records. I was in residency abroad when the Bellweather access happened. Different hospital, different country. My first Bellweather contact is years later.

FORENSIC TECH: We have already verified the residency period and no Bellweather credential tied to him during the original source access.

MARA: Good. What did you know four months before Rosegate?

NOAH: A Bellweather contact asked whether I still possessed Mara Elise’s childhood vaccination card.

[Silence.]

MARA: Why would they ask you?

NOAH: They said an old continuity record had a document gap.

MARA: Did they tell you whose continuity?

NOAH: No.

MARA: Did you give them the card?

NOAH: No.

MARA: Did you tell me?

NOAH: No.

MARA: Why?

NOAH: Because I thought if somebody had reached into my dead sister’s file, the safest thing was to close the door before they knew I understood what they were doing.

MARA: That explains your tactic. It does not explain making the decision for me.

NOAH: I know.

MARA: Do not spend “I know” as payment.

NOAH: All right.

SCENE 2 — DOCUMENT RECONCILIATION — LATER
[SFX: Pages align. Keyboard.]

FORENSIC TECH: Orison access to the Quinn source: eleven years after birth. Noah absent. Bellweather candidate-shell record created same session. Then, years later, a Bellweather access-control note flags “family source contact — N.Q.”

SHAW: That is the contact Noah describes.

MARA: Date matches?

FORENSIC TECH: Within one day.

NOAH: They called twice.

MARA: Do phone records support that?

NOAH’S COUNSEL: Two incoming calls from a Bellweather switchboard number. Preserved billing data.

MARA: Good.

FORENSIC TECH: After the calls, Bellweather adds: “Family source uncooperative. Alternative documentary recovery required.”

EVELYN: So Noah blocked them.

MARA: At that point.

EVELYN: You really cannot let one good act stay good for more than three seconds.

MARA: It is a good act. It is not the whole timeline.

NOAH: She is right.

EVELYN: I was not asking you.

NOAH: Sorry.

MARA: Keep going.

FORENSIC TECH: Rosegate period. Bellweather creates a later operational identity packet for the protected Mara route using the same dormant Quinn source family. Different document bundle, same source lineage.

MARA: Noah’s involvement then?

FORENSIC TECH: We have evidence he supplied one vaccination card after the yacht.

NOAH: After I found her alive.

MARA: Say exactly what you did.

NOAH: I used the card to bridge a vaccination-history gap in the post-fire record so the identity package would pass ordinary medical intake.

MARA: Knowing the name belonged to your dead sister.

NOAH: Yes.

MARA: Knowing I did not know that.

NOAH: Yes.

[Beat.]

MARA: That is yours.

NOAH: Yes.

NARRATOR: Noah did not say he had saved her. He had. He did not say the lie was necessary. He had believed it was. Mara had learned that both sentences could be true and still fail to cancel the third: he had chosen for her.

SCENE 2B — BUILDING LOBBY — CONTINUOUS
[SFX: Elevator doors. Rain against glass.]

EVELYN: I am going to say something unfair.

MARA: Useful warning.

EVELYN: Part of me is angry that Noah gets a document proving he was not there at the beginning.

MARA: Why?

EVELYN: Because I do not have a document proving where I was for half my life.

MARA: His exoneration on one act is not a verdict on your history.

EVELYN: I know.

MARA: You are allowed to dislike the asymmetry.

EVELYN: I dislike that word too.

MARA: You have a growing list.

EVELYN: Administrable. Asymmetry. Acceptable.

MARA: Acceptable is retired.

EVELYN: Fine. I still hate it.

[Beat.]

EVELYN: Are you angry at him?

MARA: Yes.

EVELYN: You look calm.

MARA: I am working.

EVELYN: And after?

MARA: After, I may continue being angry with better documentation.

EVELYN: That sounded like you.

MARA: Unfortunately, yes, apparently.

NARRATOR: Evelyn did not ask Mara to forgive Noah, and Mara did not ask Evelyn to be pleased by his partial clearance. The sisters had started learning a difficult form of loyalty: allowing the other woman’s evidence to change without demanding the same emotional result.

SCENE 3 — HOSPITAL CAFETERIA — MIDDAY
[SFX: Coffee machine. Tray slides.]

NOAH: I almost brought the vaccination card.

MARA: Why did you not?

NOAH: Because last time you told me to keep it until you asked.

MARA: Good.

NOAH: I do not know whether “good” means I followed an instruction or I am becoming less terrible.

MARA: It means you followed an instruction.

NOAH: Clear.

[Beat.]

NOAH: Mara Elise hated bananas.

MARA: You told me she liked them.

NOAH: No. I told you our mother made banana bread when she was sick. Different thing.

MARA: I remembered it wrong.

NOAH: Yes.

MARA: That happens.

NOAH: I used to think every small thing you did that matched her meant something.

MARA: It meant you were looking.

NOAH: I know that now.

MARA: There is that phrase again.

NOAH: Sorry.

MARA: You can stop apologizing for every sentence.

NOAH: You have difficult operating instructions.

MARA: They are evolving.

NOAH: She had a red bicycle. She fell off it twice in one afternoon because she refused to admit the seat was too high.

MARA: Why are you telling me this?

NOAH: Because I want her to exist in this conversation as a person, not just as the source file somebody stole.

[Mara is quiet.]

MARA: That is allowed.

NOAH: Thank you.

MARA: It is not permission to decide what my name means.

NOAH: I understand.

MARA: Say it without understanding language.

NOAH: Her name was hers. The paperwork was stolen. You are not required to give your life back to a dead child because other people used her record on you.

[Beat.]

MARA: Better.

NOAH: Painfully direct.

MARA: Keep that version.

SCENE 4 — SOURCE ACCESS REVIEW — AFTERNOON
[SFX: Archived memo opens.]

SHAW: We found the first internal objection to the Quinn source.

MARA: Author authenticated?

FORENSIC TECH: Victoria Arden. Signature certificate, source repository and later reference chain all match.

EVELYN: When?

FORENSIC TECH: Two days after Bellweather created the candidate-shell note.

MARA: Read the minimum.

FORENSIC TECH: “Do not convert deceased-child source into active subject identity. Dormant record may support tracing only. It may not become the legal container for a living protected person.”

NOAH: She knew.

MARA: Victoria knew Bellweather intended active use. That is what this proves.

SHAW: There is a response from Helena’s office.

MARA: Today?

SHAW: We can read the header. Substance is in the next sealed series.

MARA: Header.

SHAW: “Objection received. Threat-priority review required.”

EVELYN: So they overruled her.

MARA: We do not know that yet.

EVELYN: The shell was used.

MARA: Later. We still need the decision path.

EVELYN: You are exhausting.

MARA: Yes.

FORENSIC TECH: There is one more Victoria entry months later. Different classification.

MARA: What classification?

FORENSIC TECH: “Subject choice / name continuity.”

NOAH: What does that mean?

MARA: Tomorrow we find out.

[SFX: File closes.]

NARRATOR: Noah had not created Mara Quinn. He had later helped keep the stolen record alive. Victoria had objected before he ever knew the system existed. And somewhere after that objection, the file had changed from a dead child’s shell into a living girl’s choice.
END OF EPISODE
