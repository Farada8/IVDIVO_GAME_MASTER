[SFX: Two archival sleeves placed on a padded table. Camera shutter. Barcode scanner.]

INDEPENDENT EXAMINER: I will compare only features that can survive seventeen years of aging: ridge detail where readable, stable scar geometry, palm-edge pattern and proportional measurements. Height is supporting information, not identity proof.

[SFX: Gloves snap on. Camera shutter.]

EVELYN: Same limitation. I consent.

### SCENE 2 — COMPARISON LAB — LATE MORNING

[SFX: Fingerprint glass. Scanner tone. Measuring tape retracts.]

NARRATOR: The first surprise was that nothing dramatic happened. No alarm, no gasp, no machine deciding which woman was real. Mara pressed her thumb to glass. Evelyn did the same. The examiner asked them to turn their hands sideways under a light.

INDEPENDENT EXAMINER: Mara, there is an old linear scar on the left palm edge.

MARA: Kitchen glass. I was seventeen or eighteen.

EVELYN: You told me it was a broken window.

MARA: I told several people several versions because I did not know which memory belonged in which file.

INDEPENDENT EXAMINER: I am not using the story. I am measuring the scar.

MARA: Please continue.

INDEPENDENT EXAMINER: Evelyn, right index first.

[SFX: Scanner.]

EVELYN: I feel ridiculous.

MARA: Why?

EVELYN: Because I want one of these cards to be mine.

MARA: One of them probably is.

EVELYN: That is not what I mean.

[Mara waits.]

EVELYN: I want a piece of paper older than the lies to point at me and say, there. You existed separately.

MARA: I want that too.

EVELYN: You say it like you are ordering toner.

MARA: I am trying not to make the examiner responsible for my emotional life.

INDEPENDENT EXAMINER: Thank you.

EVELYN: See? He approves of you too.

INDEPENDENT EXAMINER: I approve of quiet during ridge capture.

[Mara almost laughs.]

[SFX: Second scan. Keyboard.]

MARA: That is the method I want.

EVELYN: Saved argument?

MARA: Two of them.
