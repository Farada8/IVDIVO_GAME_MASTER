THE WIFE AT HIS WEDDING
EPISODE 92 — BABY B
Status: WORKING

SCENE 1 — SPECIAL MASTER’S CHAMBERS — MORNING
[SFX: Papers arranged. Recorder tone.]
SPECIAL MASTER: We are not deciding identity today. We are deciding access to the transfer record and the Baby B correction packet.
PRICE: Heritage requests in-camera review first. The juvenile file includes third-party identities and threat assessments.
MARA: I support redaction of unrelated people. I oppose using unrelated privacy as a blanket over the one person whose record is missing.
PRICE: You do not yet know you are that person.
MARA: Correct. Which is why I am asking for the evidence instead of asserting the answer.
SPECIAL MASTER: Ms. Price, can the transfer ledger be separated from threat attachments?
PRICE: Yes.
SPECIAL MASTER: Then produce it under protective order.
PRICE: We also request that no party use the ledger to alter active civil credentials before full adjudication.
MARA: Fine.
SHAW: Mara—
MARA: I said fine because a transfer ledger should not rewrite a passport by itself.
PRICE: Thank you.
MARA: Do not thank me. Narrow rules help me too.
SPECIAL MASTER: The court will issue an interim identity-integrity order. Existing credentials are not invalidated by this hearing, but agencies may flag discrepancies for review. No automatic cancellation without their own process.
MARA: That matters.
PRICE: It also prevents new propagation of a possibly compromised record.
MARA: There is the leverage.
PRICE: There is the risk-control function.
MARA: Same action. Different nouns.
SPECIAL MASTER: Save the argument for a scene with worse coffee. Produce the ledger by noon.

SCENE 2 — FORENSIC RECORDS LAB — AFTERNOON
[SFX: Document camera clicks. Paper sleeve opens.]
FORENSIC TECH: Original transfer ledger is carbon-copy paper from the hospital transport office. Separate microfilm confirms page sequence.
MARA: Baby B.
FORENSIC TECH: Female neonate. Band 4413. Live discharge 16:42. Destination: Bellweather Protective Placement Intake.
EVELYN: Bellweather existed that early?
PRICE: A predecessor entity did.
MARA: Name field.
FORENSIC TECH: “No permanent civil name assigned. Use case infant designation pending registrar correction.”
EVELYN: They moved a baby without a name.
FORENSIC TECH: Hospitals can transfer infants under temporary identifiers. The unusual part is that the correction never resolves.
MARA: Who received her?
FORENSIC TECH: Placement officer code BW-17. Signature authenticated to a deceased Bellweather social-protection contractor.
PRICE: The contractor’s estate is not a witness.
MARA: Nobody said it was.
SHAW: Follow the case number.
FORENSIC TECH: HRT-JV-204 becomes BW-204 after Bellweather transition. Three months later the index adds “identity source pending.”
MARA: Source for what?
FORENSIC TECH: It does not say.
EVELYN: Was Baby A in the same file?
FORENSIC TECH: Separate subject index. Baby A has a certificate-linked protected identity. Baby B stays under case designation.
MARA: Two subjects.
FORENSIC TECH: Yes.
PRICE: At this point, yes, two subject files. That does not prove current adult mapping.
MARA: Agreed.
EVELYN: You two are becoming unbearable.
PRICE: Professional chemistry is not required.
MARA: Definitely not.
FORENSIC TECH: There is a later index event at age eleven.
MARA: Read it.
FORENSIC TECH: “Civil usability problem escalated. Dormant identity source approved for contingency review.”
SHAW: By whom?
FORENSIC TECH: Approval field: continuity legal and medical joint review. Names redacted in this copy.
MARA: Can they be unredacted under the order?
PRICE: Not without another showing.
MARA: Then keep moving.
FORENSIC TECH: Age thirteen: “Subject self-identification preference differs from source record.”
EVELYN: She knew the name was not hers?
MARA: We do not know what she knew.
FORENSIC TECH: Age seventeen: “Current preferred field name retained.”
MARA: What field name?
FORENSIC TECH: The index does not display it here.
SHAW: Next line.
FORENSIC TECH: “Dormant identity source — QUINN.”
[Silence.]
EVELYN: Quinn.
MARA: A source. Not proof of my body.
PRICE: Correct.
MARA: Stop sounding pleased when I say the rule correctly.
PRICE: I am not pleased. I am relieved.
MARA: Why?
PRICE: Because if you start treating every matching word as identity, this court will hurt both of you.
[Beat.]
MARA: Fair.

SCENE 3 — MARA’S CLIENT SITE — LATE AFTERNOON
[SFX: Security turnstile rejects badge.]
SECURITY GUARD: It says credential review.
MARA: Try the professional license number, not the civil ID.
GUARD: Same flag.
CLIENT MANAGER: Mara, we can bring the device out to you.
MARA: The chain-of-custody room is inside.
CLIENT MANAGER: I know.
MARA: Then I cannot sign the examination outside.
CLIENT MANAGER: We have a filing deadline tomorrow.
MARA: Use another examiner if you need the work completed today.
CLIENT MANAGER: I would rather use you.
MARA: I would rather enter the room.
[SFX: Phone keypad.]
MARA: Licensing office, please.
LICENSING CLERK: Your license is active. There is a document-integrity review attached to your identity profile. Some private systems may reject until cleared.
MARA: Can you issue a temporary verification letter?
CLERK: Yes, by tomorrow morning.
CLIENT MANAGER: We can wait until then.
MARA: You should not if the deadline is real.
CLIENT MANAGER: It is real.
MARA: Then assign someone else. I will transfer my preliminary notes with no fee.
CLIENT MANAGER: Mara—
MARA: Do not turn this into kindness. It is chain integrity.
CLIENT MANAGER: I was going to say I am sorry.
MARA: That is allowed.
NARRATOR: She signed away a day of billing because pretending the badge failure was cosmetic would turn a personal records dispute into a professional evidence problem. The price was smaller than losing her license and large enough to make the word temporary irritating.

SCENE 4 — MARA’S APARTMENT — EVENING
[SFX: Grocery bag. Door closes.]
ADRIAN: I brought groceries because you texted “food yes, company maybe.”
MARA: That was an accurate specification.
ADRIAN: I have learned not to improvise romance from procurement.
MARA: Put the bread there.
[SFX: Bag set down.]
ADRIAN: You lost the job?
MARA: I gave it away before a deadline forced the client to fire me.
ADRIAN: Because of Price’s notice?
MARA: Because the notice gave their security vendor a reason to reject a mismatch. Price did not design their software.
ADRIAN: You are defending her.
MARA: I am assigning acts.
ADRIAN: Right.
MARA: I dislike her very much today.
ADRIAN: Also allowed.
[SFX: Phone call connects.]
SHAW: I have the next index layer.
MARA: Speaker.
SHAW: Bellweather migrated BW-204 into a dormant-identity pool. There is a source case cross-reference.
MARA: Name?
SHAW: Not yet. The source case is sealed under an old flood and identity-theft matter.
ADRIAN: Flood?
MARA: Where?
SHAW: Queens. Family named Quinn.
[Silence.]
MARA: Date.
SHAW: Years before Noah says he first met either of you.
MARA: So the name existed in the system before Noah had any documented contact with Bellweather or either of us.
SHAW: Yes.
MARA: Do not call him yet.
SHAW: Why?
MARA: Because if the source is his family, I want authentication before I make him hear it from my suspicion.
SHAW: Agreed.
MARA: What do we need?
SHAW: Court permission to compare the dormant-source index with the sealed flood case.
PRICE: You will have my opposition by nine.
MARA: Are you on this call?
PRICE: Shaw conferenced me because the order requires notice.
MARA: Of course he did.
SHAW: Growth everywhere.
MARA: Shaw.
SHAW: Stopping.
PRICE: Ms. Quinn, I am not trying to prevent you from learning the truth. I am trying to prevent a protected dead child’s file from becoming public collateral damage.
MARA: Then tomorrow we argue scope. Not existence.
PRICE: Agreed.
MARA: Good night.
[SFX: Call ends.]
ADRIAN: Do you want me to stay?
MARA: For twenty minutes. Then leave before I mistake not being alone for not being afraid.
ADRIAN: Twenty.
NARRATOR: Baby B had left the hospital alive. She had entered protection without a permanent civil name. Years later, somebody had gone looking for a dormant identity called Quinn. The next door did not belong to Mara. It belonged to a dead family she had not yet earned the right to disturb.
SCENE 5 — INDEPENDENT COUNSEL CALL — NIGHT
COUNSEL: The interim order protects your existing credentials from automatic cancellation. It does not force private vendors to accept them without review.
MARA: So tomorrow may contain more turnstiles.
COUNSEL: Probably.
MARA: Can Heritage use the delay to pressure settlement?
COUNSEL: They can offer terms. They cannot lawfully condition access to evidence on your surrender of claims without scrutiny.
MARA: Keep the line between lawful delay and leverage visible.
COUNSEL: Then document every practical consequence.
MARA: Already started.

END OF EPISODE
