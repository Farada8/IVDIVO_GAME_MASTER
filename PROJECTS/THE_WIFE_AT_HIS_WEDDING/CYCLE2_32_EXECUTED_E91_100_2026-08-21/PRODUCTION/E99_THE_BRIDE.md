THE WIFE AT HIS WEDDING
EPISODE 99 — THE BRIDE
Status: WORKING

SCENE 1 — BIOMETRIC LAB — MORNING
[SFX: Evidence scanner.]
EXAMINER: Pre-wedding file contains Asset One thumbprint captured forty-eight hours before the ceremony, independent registrar identity check, security arrival image hashes and a sealed handwriting sample from the marriage-license application.
MARA: Compare in independent order.
EXAMINER: Thumbprint first. Asset One matches current Evelyn.
[Silence.]
EVELYN: Confidence?
EXAMINER: Very high.
MARA: Registrar check?
EXAMINER: Same woman. Security images are lower quality but consistent. Handwriting supports, not proves, the same mapping.
ADRIAN: Ceremony day?
EXAMINER: Continuous secured route from residence to registrar anteroom to ceremony. No unexplained substitution window.
PRICE: Heritage accepts the body mapping.
MARA: So do I.
EVELYN: Say the sentence.
MARA: Current Evelyn is the body who completed the pre-wedding identity check and occupied the continuous ceremony route.
EVELYN: Thank you.
MARA: That means the legal bride evidence maps to you. It does not tell you what to do with the marriage now.
EVELYN: I remember the rule.
ADRIAN: Evelyn—
EVELYN: Not here.
ADRIAN: Right.
NARRATOR: The answer landed without music. Evelyn had been the bride. Not because Mara conceded a man to her, not because a scar chose sides, and not because the law needed one woman to win. The evidence finally let the marriage belong to the two adults who had actually lived it.

SCENE 2 — COURTHOUSE HALLWAY — LATE MORNING
EVELYN: I thought I would feel relieved.
MARA: Do you?
EVELYN: A little. Mostly I feel stupid for needing a thumbprint to tell me my marriage happened.
MARA: You spent years being told your memories might be contamination.
EVELYN: That is not the same as being unsure.
MARA: No. It is worse.
EVELYN: And you?
MARA: I am not losing Adrian today.
EVELYN: I can live with that.
MARA: I am losing one lazy theory that could have made everything simple.
EVELYN: Which theory?
MARA: That I was the real Evelyn all along and the world could be repaired by moving the correct woman back into place.
EVELYN: That theory would erase me.
MARA: Yes.
EVELYN: I was afraid you wanted it.
MARA: I wanted the simplicity for about five minutes. Then I met you.
EVELYN: Rude.
MARA: Accurate.
[Beat.]
EVELYN: I am glad it is dead.
MARA: Me too.

SCENE 3 — REGISTRAR REVIEW — AFTERNOON
[SFX: Computer terminal.]
REGISTRAR: The marriage file is now sufficiently mapped to current Evelyn for this proceeding. We can sever most marriage materials from the Baby B case.
MARA: Continue.
PRICE: Heritage agrees.
EVELYN’S COUNSEL: We will file separately for marital-status clarification given the death declaration and later reappearance.
ADRIAN: Separately.
EVELYN: Yes.
REGISTRAR: There is another linked family record the court previously kept sealed: Lily’s maternal admission security file.
MARA: Why is it linked?
REGISTRAR: Because the same historical identity-security system verified the maternal patient.
EVELYN: I want that resolved.
MARA: You do not have to use Lily.
EVELYN: I am not using Lily. I am using the hospital’s adult admission record.
MARA: Then use the adult record only.
PRICE: Heritage will support an adult-record-only review. No child testing, no child interview.
ADRIAN: Agreed.
MARA: Agreed.
REGISTRAR: Then we can compare maternal admission biometrics, pregnancy-care chain and secured delivery transfer.
EVELYN: Tomorrow.
MARA: One more question. Does proving Evelyn was bride help identify Baby B?
REGISTRAR: No. Marriage identity and birth identity are separate legal questions.
MARA: Put that sentence in the order.
REGISTRAR: I will.

SCENE 4 — MARA’S OFFICE — LATE AFTERNOON
[SFX: Email arrives.]
DIRECTOR: Your public professional notice went live.
MARA: Complaints?
DIRECTOR: Two supportive emails, one conspiracy theory, one client asking whether you are actually dead.
MARA: Excellent.
DIRECTOR: I replied with the verification letter.
MARA: Better than what I would have written.
DIRECTOR: The warehouse client approved your report.
MARA: Payment?
DIRECTOR: Processing.
MARA: I like them again.
DIRECTOR: You also have a new inquiry.
MARA: External chain-of-custody?
DIRECTOR: Yes. They are willing to wait for manual clearance.
MARA: Take it.
DIRECTOR: You sound happier about a compliance queue than any person should.
MARA: It is evidence that I am still employed.
DIRECTOR: Low bar.
MARA: Current bar.

SCENE 5 — EVENING SEQUENCE — COURTHOUSE BENCH / EVELYN’S KITCHEN / MARA’S APARTMENT / TEXTS
[SFX: Distant elevator.]
ADRIAN: I am glad you asked for the bride question to be answered without me in the lab.
EVELYN: I did not want your face deciding how I felt before I knew.
ADRIAN: Fair.
EVELYN: I was the bride.
ADRIAN: Yes.
EVELYN: Do you want me to say what that means now?
ADRIAN: Only if you want to.
EVELYN: It means we were married. It mattered. I loved you. I was angry at you. I stayed too long in some fights and left too late in others. Then I disappeared from your life and the law called me dead. None of that obligates us to resume anything.
ADRIAN: I agree.
EVELYN: That was too fast.
ADRIAN: I have had five years to know I cannot restore a marriage by proving it existed.
EVELYN: I had less time.
ADRIAN: Take more.
EVELYN: Do not become perfect now. It is irritating.
ADRIAN: I am not perfect. I am terrified of saying the wrong thing to you and hurting Mara by accident.
EVELYN: That answer I believe.
ADRIAN: That is apparently the family medal.
[Evelyn laughs despite herself.]
EVELYN: I will file for clarification, not restoration.
ADRIAN: I will cooperate.
EVELYN: Separately from Mara.
ADRIAN: Separately.

[TRANSITION: Later, Evelyn’s kitchen.]
[SFX: Pan on stove. School papers.]
LILY: I need someone to sign the science-trip form.
EVELYN: Me.
LILY: Dad already signed the other page.
ADRIAN: I signed the emergency-contact page.
LILY: Mara, you are not on this form.
MARA: A tragedy.
LILY: You can help me make the model volcano.
MARA: That sounds more dangerous than court.
LILY: It uses baking soda.
MARA: Confirmed.
[Evelyn signs.]
EVELYN: Done.
LILY: Thanks.
[Lily leaves.]
ADRIAN: That was almost aggressively normal.
MARA: Then we leave it there.
EVELYN: Tomorrow the hospital record tells us whether my body was the maternal patient.
ADRIAN: How are you?
EVELYN: I do not know.
MARA: You do not have to know before the record.
EVELYN: I remember the rule.
[Beat.]
EVELYN: Sorry.
MARA: For what?
EVELYN: I was about to say I know because you taught me. That would have been unbearable.
MARA: Correct.
[SFX: Phone vibrates.]
MARA: Hospital security released the adult maternal admission file.
EVELYN: Any problem?
MARA: One note. The maternal identity was verified under the same secured-body mapping system as Asset One.
ADRIAN: So tomorrow may close it.
MARA: The maternity role. Not the birth certificate problem.
EVELYN: One thing at a time.
NARRATOR: The bride was Evelyn. That answer did not make Mara smaller. It stopped forcing one woman to contain every role. Tomorrow they would do the same with motherhood, using the adults’ records and leaving Lily free to worry about volcanoes.

[TRANSITION: Later, Mara’s apartment.]
[SFX: Invoice notification.]
MARA: The warehouse paid.
ADRIAN: Congratulations.
MARA: That sounded appropriately boring.
ADRIAN: I can be boring professionally.
MARA: Keep practicing.
ADRIAN: Evelyn and I talked.
MARA: About the marriage?
ADRIAN: Yes. I will not give you details unless she wants that shared.
MARA: That is enough.
ADRIAN: We agree it existed. We do not agree yet on every legal consequence.
MARA: Keep that separate too.
ADRIAN: You are not asking whether I still love her.
MARA: Because whatever the answer is, it does not appoint me to a role.
ADRIAN: I do love parts of what we were.
MARA: Thank you for telling me because you chose to, not because I interrogated you.
ADRIAN: And I want dinner with you Friday.
MARA: That is a different sentence.
ADRIAN: Yes.
MARA: Friday still stands.

[TRANSITION: Night, text exchange.]
[SFX: Phone vibrates twice.]
EVELYN: Text: I told Lily the court confirmed I was the bride. She asked whether I still have the dress.
MARA: Text: Do you?
EVELYN: Text: Unfortunately.
MARA: Text: That sounds like an ordinary family problem.
EVELYN: Text: It has storage damage.
MARA: Text: Even better.
NARRATOR: The proof did not decide what the marriage meant now. It moved the question from identity to adult consequence. That was enough for one night.

NARRATOR: Mara marked the bride question closed in her notebook and left a blank line beneath it for the consequences.

END OF EPISODE
