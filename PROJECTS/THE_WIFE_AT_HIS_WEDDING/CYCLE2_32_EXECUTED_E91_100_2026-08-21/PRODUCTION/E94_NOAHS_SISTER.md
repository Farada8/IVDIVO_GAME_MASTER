THE WIFE AT HIS WEDDING
EPISODE 94 — NOAH’S SISTER
Status: WORKING

SCENE 1 — PUBLIC LIBRARY PRIVATE STUDY ROOM — MORNING
[SFX: Door closes. Low HVAC.]
NARRATOR: Noah chose a library. No family home. No Vale office. No place where the walls could pretend they had a claim on the conversation.
NOAH: I brought a lawyer.
NOAH’S COUNSEL: I am here to stop either of you from turning grief into consent.
MARA: Useful job.
NOAH: You said Mara Elise.
MARA: The sealed source file identifies Mara Elise Quinn, age eight, deceased. Bellweather later copied parts of her dormant identity bundle into BW-204.
[Long silence.]
NOAH: Show me only what I am allowed to see.
MARA: Start there.
[SFX: Paper slides.]
NOAH: Her vaccination number.
MARA: We have not authenticated family custody of the original card yet.
NOAH: I have it.
MARA: The original?
NOAH: In a safe-deposit envelope. My mother kept it after the fraud case. I kept it after she died.
NOAH’S COUNSEL: Noah, do not produce it today.
NOAH: Counsel already warned me.
MARA: Your counsel is right.
NOAH: You used that number after the yacht.
MARA: A version of it appears in the post-fire reconstruction Noah made for my shell.
NOAH: Say I did it.
MARA: You used your sister’s vaccination record after the fire to make Mara Quinn operational again.
NOAH: Yes.
MARA: But this file shows Bellweather had already stolen from the same source years earlier.
NOAH: I did not give it to them.
MARA: Current access logs support you.
NOAH: Does that make anything better?
MARA: It makes one accusation wrong.
NOAH: I spent five years thinking I had made you from her.
MARA: You rebuilt paperwork after the fire. You did not invent the earlier protected girl.
NOAH: And you are still wearing my sister’s name.
MARA: I am using a name that came through her stolen record.
NOAH: You sound like the report.
MARA: It is also the sentence I can survive this morning.
[Silence.]
NOAH: Fair.

SCENE 2 — SAME ROOM — LATER
NOAH’S COUNSEL: The family theft file may contain your mother’s statements, the original fraud investigation and the child’s medical identifiers. We can authorize a limited evidentiary extract without opening everything.
MARA: I want source provenance, access permissions, Bellweather queries and the original card hash if possible. I do not need Mara Elise’s pediatric history.
NOAH: Thank you.
MARA: Do not thank me for not taking a dead child apart.
NOAH: I was thanking you for asking.
[Beat.]
MARA: Did you know anyone from Bellweather before Rosegate?
NOAH: No.
MARA: Did your mother?
NOAH: She hated insurers, data brokers and anyone who said “legacy system.” None of that proves anything.
MARA: It is a character reference for software.
NOAH: She would appreciate that.
MARA: We have a Bellweather contractor query years after the fraud case closed. I need to know whether your family ever authorized reuse for protective placement.
NOAH: Never.
NOAH’S COUNSEL: He can sign that statement. We should also obtain the old court order.
MARA: Yes. Independent document before memory.
NOAH: You do not trust me.
MARA: Not alone.
NOAH: That is the answer I wanted.
MARA: That sounded strange.
NOAH: It was supposed to.
NOAH’S COUNSEL: There is another issue. If Mara’s present documents are attacked because of the source bundle, Noah could be treated as a knowing participant in the later reconstruction.
NOAH: I was.
MARA: In the later reconstruction, yes. Not the earlier Bellweather theft.
NOAH: I am not asking you to protect me.
MARA: I am not. I am preventing one act from swallowing another one.
NOAH: You keep doing that.
MARA: It is the only useful habit I have this month.

SCENE 3 — HERITAGE TRUST CONFERENCE ROOM — AFTERNOON
[SFX: Conference phone.]
PRICE: The old court order confirms the Quinn file could be queried for fraud prevention only. No consent for protective identity reuse.
MARA: So unauthorized export is established.
PRICE: Yes.
SHAW: Who approved Bellweather’s use?
PRICE: The disputed layer is authorization. The contractor account copied the bundle. The governance approval is in a later continuity file.
MARA: Produce it.
PRICE: I cannot under the current order. It contains medical and threat material concerning both protected girls.
MARA: Redact.
PRICE: I am seeking a ruling.
MARA: When?
PRICE: Tomorrow morning.
MARA: Meanwhile my institute is manually approving every case.
PRICE: I see the cost.
MARA: Your notice is creating real cost.
PRICE: Your record has a real defect.
MARA: Both facts.
PRICE: Yes.
MARA: Would Heritage suspend the notice if I agreed to keep the identity matter confidential pending review?
PRICE: I did not offer that.
MARA: Would you?
PRICE: I would consider a confidential administrative stabilization agreement.
MARA: There it is.
PRICE: There what is?
MARA: Existence becomes easier if I stop making it public.
PRICE: I said administration, not existence.
MARA: The difference matters to you because your credentials work.
PRICE: Fair.
SHAW: We are not negotiating today.
MARA: No. I wanted the shape of the leverage on the record.
PRICE: Then put my answer on the record too. I am trying to keep banks, licensing bodies and courts from relying on a chain we now know contains unauthorized source data.
MARA: And you are also trying to limit Heritage exposure.
PRICE: Yes.
MARA: Put it that way.
PRICE: If you want me as a villain, I will disappoint you. I want a record a registrar can defend ten years from now.
MARA: I do too. We disagree about who should carry the waiting cost.
PRICE: Accurate.

SCENE 4 — NOAH’S STORAGE UNIT — LATE AFTERNOON
[SFX: Metal door rolls up.]
NOAH: My mother kept everything in bad boxes.
MARA: I inherited a radiator. We all have burdens.
NOAH: Here.
[SFX: Small archive envelope.]
NOAH: I am not opening it.
MARA: Leave it sealed.
FORENSIC TECH: I can photograph seal condition, transport it to the lab and compare the vaccination-card print pattern to the Bellweather copy without exposing unrelated content.
NOAH: Do that.
MARA: You can stop the process before we open the court file.
NOAH: I heard you.
MARA: I am saying it because five years of “protection” taught everyone consent was implied by urgency.
NOAH: Understood.
[Beat.]
NOAH: Sorry. That one sounded like the old pattern.
MARA: It did.
NOAH: I mean I understand.
MARA: Put it that way.
[SFX: Evidence bag seals.]
NOAH: Mara.
MARA: Yes?
NOAH: If you end up proving the name came from her, what happens?
MARA: Legally? I do not know. Personally? I am not giving you an answer today.
NOAH: I was not asking you to give it back.
MARA: Then we are not solving ownership today.
NOAH: I was asking whether hearing her name every day hurts you.
MARA: Today, yes.
NOAH: Me too.
[Silence.]

SCENE 5 — LATE-AFTERNOON-TO-DUSK SEQUENCE — SHAW CALL / LIBRARY STEPS
SHAW: We got the governance index.
MARA: Source authorization?
SHAW: Bellweather committee review. Helena Vale medical-risk office. Lenora Voss operational note. And one formal objection.
MARA: By whom?
SHAW: Victoria.
MARA: Text?
SHAW: “A dead child’s identity cannot become a reusable safety asset. If the second subject needs a lawful separate record, create one. Do not borrow a life.”
[Silence.]
NOAH: She wrote that?
MARA: Shaw, authenticate before we use it.
SHAW: Already in progress. There is a signed paper copy and a meeting minute referencing the objection.
MARA: Keep me posted.
NOAH: What happened to the objection?
SHAW: It was overruled.
MARA: By who?
SHAW: The minute identifies it.
MARA: Tomorrow.
NARRATOR: Noah had not created the original Mara Quinn shell. His family had never authorized it. That cleared one piece of his guilt and left another intact. More important, it gave the dead child back a boundary: her stolen record had been recognized as wrong at the time. Victoria had said no. Someone had answered yes.
[TRANSITION: Library steps at dusk.]
NOAH: My lawyer thinks I should stop talking to you directly for a while.
MARA: Sensible.
NOAH: I do not want to.
MARA: Also allowed.
NOAH: And I am not treating that as permission to call whenever I panic.
MARA: Then we understand each other.
NOAH: I hate that phrase from you.
MARA: Then learn faster.
[Noah laughs once, then stops.]
NOAH: Sorry.
MARA: Do not apologize for laughing near grief.

END OF EPISODE
