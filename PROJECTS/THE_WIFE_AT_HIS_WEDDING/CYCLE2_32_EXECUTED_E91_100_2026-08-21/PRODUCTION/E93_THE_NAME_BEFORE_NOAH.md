THE WIFE AT HIS WEDDING
EPISODE 93 — THE NAME BEFORE NOAH
Status: WORKING

SCENE 1 — SEALED RECORDS REVIEW — MORNING
[SFX: Secure terminal tone.]
SPECIAL MASTER: Limited comparison only. No public release of third-party medical details.
PRICE: Heritage agrees to source-name, dates, access history and disposition. We reserve objection to family contents.
MARA: That is enough to answer provenance.
FORENSIC TECH: Source case: Quinn family flood-loss identity theft investigation. Opened twenty-two years ago. Minor child: Mara Elise Quinn.
[Silence.]
MARA: Status.
FORENSIC TECH: Deceased.
EVELYN: Age?
FORENSIC TECH: Eight.
MARA: Cause is not relevant.
PRICE: Thank you.
FORENSIC TECH: After the death, the family reported attempted use of the child’s health and vaccination identifiers in an insurance fraud. The case was sealed after prosecution of an unrelated actor.
SHAW: How does Bellweather get to it?
FORENSIC TECH: Thirteen years later, a Bellweather contractor queries dormant juvenile identities through a legacy contractor data exchange.
MARA: Authorized?
FORENSIC TECH: The query was technically permitted for fraud screening. Exporting the identity bundle for protective use was not.
PRICE: That matters.
MARA: It matters a lot.
FORENSIC TECH: Access log shows a contractor account copied name, date-of-birth scaffold, old vaccination reference and county provenance into BW-204.
EVELYN: So they stole from a dead child to give Baby B paperwork.
MARA: They copied a dormant identity bundle into a protection file. We do not yet know who ordered the use.
EVELYN: You can say stole.
MARA: I will when we establish unauthorized use, not just access.
PRICE: The export itself exceeded the fraud-screening purpose.
MARA: Then yes. Unauthorized taking.
EVELYN: Thank you.
MARA: Do not thank me for verbs either.

SCENE 2 — INDEPENDENT COUNSEL OFFICE — NOON
[SFX: Printer. Stapler.]
INDEPENDENT COUNSEL: The source name being Mara Elise Quinn creates a serious distinction problem.
MARA: Explain it as if I am a client, not a forensic examiner.
COUNSEL: Biological person, historical protected subject, civil record and chosen adult name are four different things. Right now, your current identity chain uses a name that may have originated in the record of a dead child.
MARA: Does that make me the dead child legally?
COUNSEL: No.
MARA: Does it automatically make my documents void?
COUNSEL: Also no. But agencies may investigate whether foundational documents were lawfully issued.
MARA: My professional license?
COUNSEL: Active, but vulnerable to administrative hold if identity continuity cannot be verified.
MARA: Lease?
COUNSEL: Your landlord may run another check at move-in renewal or payment service onboarding.
MARA: Bank?
COUNSEL: Compliance review is possible.
MARA: Travel?
COUNSEL: Do not assume anything until passport authority speaks.
MARA: Keep it boring.
COUNSEL: I admire the phrase.
MARA: Do not.
COUNSEL: One more thing. Price’s interim notice is not frivolous. If she had done nothing and a bank relied on a record later found fabricated, Heritage would be accused of concealing the defect.
MARA: She can be correct and dangerous at the same time.
COUNSEL: Frequently true of lawyers.
MARA: Present company?
COUNSEL: Especially.

SCENE 3 — FORENSIC INSTITUTE — AFTERNOON
[SFX: Office printer. Colleague closes door.]
DIRECTOR: Your temporary verification letter arrived.
MARA: Can I access secure evidence again?
DIRECTOR: Our system accepts the professional license, but compliance wants manual approval for each new case.
MARA: How long?
DIRECTOR: They say forty-eight hours.
MARA: Which means I cannot take emergency-response work.
DIRECTOR: For now.
MARA: Put it in writing.
DIRECTOR: I was going to ask whether you wanted us to keep this quiet.
MARA: Quiet from whom?
DIRECTOR: Staff. Clients.
MARA: Do not announce private details. Do not lie if a client asks why another examiner is assigned.
DIRECTOR: That will cost you work.
MARA: I know.
DIRECTOR: You can take internal review cases that do not require external chain-of-custody portals.
MARA: Assign me one.
DIRECTOR: There is a warehouse sprinkler-loss review.
MARA: Cruel.
DIRECTOR: I can find something less thematic.
MARA: No. Give me the sprinklers.
DIRECTOR: You are not required to be funny about this.
MARA: I am not. I am required to bill hours.
DIRECTOR: Then I will send the file before you leave.
NARRATOR: Work did not stop because her identity became interesting. It narrowed into the jobs that could survive a manual compliance queue. Mara hated that more usefully than she hated the headlines.

SCENE 4 — MARA’S APARTMENT — EVENING
[SFX: Cardboard box opens. Radiator.]
EVELYN: One box. I am respecting the treaty.
MARA: You brought labels.
EVELYN: Labels are not surveillance.
MARA: Depends what they say.
EVELYN: Kitchen. Books. Things you refuse to classify.
MARA: That last category is too large.
[Evelyn tapes a box.]
EVELYN: Did they confirm Quinn?
MARA: Mara Elise Quinn. Eight years old. Dead before Bellweather copied her identity bundle.
EVELYN: Noah’s sister.
MARA: Likely. We authenticate family relationship before telling him.
EVELYN: He has the same surname.
MARA: So do thousands of people.
EVELYN: You know it is him.
MARA: I suspect it is him.
EVELYN: Sometimes your precision feels like you are making me wait outside a locked room.
MARA: Sometimes your certainty feels like you are trying to kick the door before we know who is behind it.
[Beat.]
EVELYN: That was almost a fight.
MARA: We can schedule one.
EVELYN: Tomorrow breakfast?
MARA: Nine.
EVELYN: Fine.
[SFX: Phone vibrates.]
MARA: Price.
PRICE: Ms. Quinn, the source court granted a family-notice requirement before deeper review. The surviving next of kin must be notified.
MARA: Who is listed?
PRICE: Noah Quinn.
[Silence.]
MARA: Is he already notified?
PRICE: No. The order allows your counsel to do it because of the existing relationship.
MARA: Thank you for not calling him first.
PRICE: That was not courtesy. It was the least harmful procedure.
MARA: I can still appreciate procedure.
PRICE: There is another complication. The copied bundle was accessed years before Noah had any documented contact with Bellweather.
MARA: So he could not have supplied it then.
PRICE: Correct.
MARA: That weakens the theory that Noah created the Mara Quinn identity.
PRICE: It weakens that early theory. It says nothing yet about later reuse.
MARA: Good distinction.
PRICE: I am recording this conversation because I refuse to have anyone quote us as friends.
MARA: Safe.
[SFX: Call ends.]
EVELYN: You are enjoying her a little.
MARA: I am enjoying accurate opposition. It is a disease.

SCENE 5 — NIGHT SEQUENCE — NOAH’S VOICEMAIL / WAREHOUSE REVIEW
[SFX: Phone held. Mara records, stops, records again.]
MARA: Noah. I need to talk to you about a sealed family file. I am not going to leave the subject on voicemail. It concerns your sister Mara Elise. Call me when you are somewhere private. You can bring counsel. You can say no to me and speak only through counsel if that is what you need.
[Mara stops recording.]
EVELYN: Send it.
MARA: I hate that this is my name now.
EVELYN: Is it?
MARA: What?
EVELYN: Her name was Mara Elise Quinn. Yours is Mara Quinn. The source can be stolen without every later use meaning the same thing.
MARA: That sounded suspiciously like my kind of distinction.
EVELYN: I am infected.
[SFX: Mara sends message.]
NARRATOR: The name had existed before Noah met either woman. That fact cleared him of one old suspicion and opened a worse one: someone inside the protection machinery had gone looking for a dead child’s civil shadow long before Noah could have helped them.
[TRANSITION: Later, warehouse review.]
[SFX: Sprinkler riser room. Clipboard.]
COLLEAGUE: You really took the sprinkler case.
MARA: It pays.
COLLEAGUE: People are asking whether the identity review affects your old testimony.
MARA: Does it?
COLLEAGUE: Not technically.
MARA: Then say that.
COLLEAGUE: You are very calm.
MARA: I am measuring a failed valve while waiting to tell a man that his dead sister’s identity was stolen twice. Calm is currently task-shaped.
COLLEAGUE: Fair.
MARA: Read me the pressure log.
COLLEAGUE: Two hundred ten kilopascals at 03:12.
MARA: Again. Slower.
NARRATOR: For twenty minutes, the worst thing in Mara’s world was a sprinkler valve that should have opened and did not. She was grateful for the scale of the problem.

END OF EPISODE
