THE WIFE AT HIS WEDDING
EPISODE 97 — THE GIRL ADRIAN MET
Status: WORKING

SCENE 1 — MARA’S APARTMENT — MORNING
[SFX: Breakfast dishes. Tablet opens.]
MARA: Route fields only. No private guest list. No unrelated security notes.
EVELYN: You gave me the order yesterday.
MARA: I am saying it for myself.
EVELYN: That I can use.
FORENSIC TECH: Event date: Vale winter conservatory fundraiser. Historical Evelyn was scheduled to enter through east receiving corridor. Historical Mara Quinn was scheduled for off-site academic transport.
MARA: And the change?
FORENSIC TECH: A threat alert moved Evelyn’s vehicle. A crowd formed at the west entrance. Security substituted Mara into the east visible route for twelve minutes while Evelyn was held in a service office.
EVELYN: Without telling either girl why?
FORENSIC TECH: The note says “subjects informed only as necessary.”
MARA: Meaning probably not.
FORENSIC TECH: During the twelve-minute window, east-route subject enters greenhouse corridor. Incident log records broken pane, hand cut and contact with Adrian Vale.
[Silence.]
EVELYN: That was you.
MARA: Current body mapping says yes.
EVELYN: The scar?
MARA: Same hand.
FORENSIC TECH: Historical Mara card notes a healing cut at the matching location three days later. Independent school nurse record agrees.
MARA: Keep going.
EVELYN: You met him as me.
MARA: I met him while being routed through your visible path.
EVELYN: That is what I said.
MARA: It is not.
EVELYN: Fine. You met him while security wanted the world to think you were the Evelyn route.
MARA: Now I understand what you mean.
EVELYN: I hate that we grade each other like case notes.
MARA: We can replace it with stickers.

SCENE 2 — ADRIAN’S OFFICE — LATE MORNING
[SFX: Door closes. No conference equipment.]
ADRIAN: I remember blood on the greenhouse latch. You said not to call security because they would cancel something.
MARA: I do not remember the whole conversation.
ADRIAN: I do.
MARA: Memory is not a recording.
ADRIAN: You are right.
MARA: Sorry. Continue.
ADRIAN: You were angry that a driver had been changed without telling you. You asked me for a towel. I gave you my handkerchief because apparently I was born eighty years old.
MARA: That sounds like you.
ADRIAN: You laughed at it.
MARA: That sounds less like me.
ADRIAN: You were seventeen.
MARA: Fair.
ADRIAN: I kept the handkerchief.
MARA: Of course you did.
ADRIAN: I am not going to produce it as evidence.
MARA: Thank you.
ADRIAN: I am also not going to say this proves we were meant to find each other.
MARA: Thank you again.
ADRIAN: It does hurt.
MARA: What part?
ADRIAN: I thought the first time I met my future wife was honest, even if everything after it became complicated. Now I know I met you during a substitution neither of us understood.
MARA: I was not pretending to be your future wife. I was following a route.
ADRIAN: Exactly. I am angry at the system, not at you.
MARA: And at yourself?
ADRIAN: A little. I keep finding ways I benefited from not asking questions because the answers arrived conveniently.
MARA: You were seventeen.
ADRIAN: I have used that excuse for exactly one thing today.
MARA: Keep it for the handkerchief.
[Beat.]
ADRIAN: Does this change Friday?
MARA: No.
ADRIAN: Then Friday stays.
MARA: It changes one memory. Do not make one memory into a marriage proposal from history.
ADRIAN: Understood.

SCENE 3 — EVENT ARCHIVE REVIEW — AFTERNOON
[SFX: Security map unfolds.]
PRICE: Heritage requested this additional review because the substitution could be misunderstood as evidence of long-term romantic impersonation.
MARA: For once, I appreciate the alarm.
PRICE: The event log shows direct cross-cover ended after the greenhouse incident.
FORENSIC TECH: Victoria note, same week: “No more direct Adrian substitutions. Relationship contamination now foreseeable.”
EVELYN: She wrote that phrase?
FORENSIC TECH: Yes.
MARA: It is ugly and useful.
PRICE: Later route tables keep the girls’ private family and romantic-contact paths separate.
MARA: Define later.
FORENSIC TECH: From this incident through wedding year. Mara Quinn appears in public event cover, medical-access decoy and security visibility tasks. Evelyn retains direct private meetings with Adrian, engagement travel, marriage-preparation appointments and household continuity.
EVELYN: So the greenhouse was the exception.
FORENSIC TECH: The only direct Adrian substitution we have found after reviewing the route set.
MARA: That protects more than romance. It protects consent history.
PRICE: Yes.
MARA: Any missing periods?
FORENSIC TECH: Three short archive gaps. None overlap documented private Adrian encounters. We cannot prove an absolute negative, but the preserved pattern is strong.
MARA: Say exactly that in the report.
PRICE: I will support it.
EVELYN: You are both ruining every simple emotional sentence.
MARA: We are saving you from a worse one.

SCENE 4 — SCHOOL PICKUP OUTSIDE — LATE AFTERNOON
[SFX: Children leaving. Traffic.]
LILY: Mara, Mom says you have a new apartment with a bad radiator.
MARA: Your mother is leaking classified information.
EVELYN: It is not classified.
LILY: Does it really bang?
MARA: Twice before breakfast.
LILY: You should name it.
MARA: No.
LILY: Then it will name itself.
ADRIAN: I support the child on this.
MARA: Nobody asked you.
LILY: Are you coming to pizza Friday?
[Mara looks at Evelyn, then Adrian.]
MARA: Not this Friday. I have dinner plans.
LILY: With Dad?
ADRIAN: Lily.
LILY: What? I can see both of you.
MARA: Yes, with Adrian.
LILY: Okay. Mom, can I get the green notebook now?
EVELYN: That is the whole reaction?
LILY: I need the notebook.
MARA: Excellent priorities.
NARRATOR: Lily wanted a notebook. No one asked her who her mother was. No one asked her to identify a woman from a childhood memory. The adults carried their own evidence for once.

SCENE 5 — NIGHT SEQUENCE — MARA’S APARTMENT / KITCHEN / BUILDING EXIT
[SFX: Old window latch. Radiator quiet.]
EVELYN: I have been thinking about the greenhouse.
MARA: Dangerous.
EVELYN: I used to resent any memory Adrian had that might belong to you.
MARA: That sounds exhausting.
EVELYN: It was. Now I am mostly angry that they made us compete for memories we did not design.
MARA: The bride records are still yours if the evidence says they are yours.
EVELYN: I can live with that.
MARA: And the greenhouse is mine if the evidence says it is mine.
EVELYN: And that one too.
MARA: You hate this part.
EVELYN: I hate that you sound relieved when categories hold.
MARA: I am relieved.
EVELYN: I am too.
[Beat.]
EVELYN: That is worse.
MARA: Probably healthier.
[SFX: Phone notification.]
MARA: Victoria’s note has an attachment.
EVELYN: What?
MARA: A route prohibition list through the wedding year. It says direct private and marital paths stay with Evelyn subject only.
EVELYN: Then tomorrow we stop wondering whether my marriage was built out of your body.
MARA: We stop if the independent records agree.
EVELYN: Of course.
NARRATOR: The greenhouse memory did not crown Mara the true wife. It did something smaller and more honest. It gave one old moment back to the body that had lived it and drew a boundary around what came after.
[TRANSITION: Later, in Mara’s kitchen.]
[SFX: Tap running. Plate set in rack.]
ADRIAN: I keep replaying the greenhouse and trying to remove the substitution from it.
MARA: You cannot.
ADRIAN: I cannot.
MARA: You can remove the idea that either of us chose the substitution.
ADRIAN: That helps.
MARA: Does it make the memory yours again?
ADRIAN: It was always mine. I just did not know who else it belonged to.
MARA: Now you do.
ADRIAN: Maybe.
MARA: Very high confidence.
ADRIAN: You are flirting in evidentiary language.
MARA: I am tired.
ADRIAN: Better answer.
MARA: I am also afraid tomorrow will prove every later private memory belongs to Evelyn and I will feel relieved and hurt at the same time.
ADRIAN: I can carry my part of that without asking you to make it romantic.
MARA: I can hear that.
ADRIAN: I should go.
MARA: Yes.
[Beat.]
MARA: Come Friday anyway.
ADRIAN: I will.

[TRANSITION: Building entrance, as Adrian leaves.]
ADRIAN: One thing before I go. I do not regret the greenhouse memory because it was you.
MARA: Careful.
ADRIAN: I mean I do not regret being kind to a bleeding seventeen-year-old whose identity I misunderstood.
MARA: Now I understand you.
ADRIAN: The part I regret is how easily I let the system tell me who she was.
MARA: That regret belongs to you.
ADRIAN: I will remember that.
MARA: Good night.
ADRIAN: Good night.

END OF EPISODE
