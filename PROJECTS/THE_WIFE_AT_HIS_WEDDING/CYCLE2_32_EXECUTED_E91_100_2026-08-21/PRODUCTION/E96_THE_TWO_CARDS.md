THE WIFE AT HIS WEDDING
EPISODE 96 — THE TWO CARDS
Status: WORKING

SCENE 1 — INDEPENDENT BIOMETRIC LAB — MORNING
[SFX: Evidence seals photographed. Scanner calibration tone.]
NARRATOR: The two cards were small enough to fit under one hand. Mara disliked that immediately. Every old system in her life had begun by making two people fit inside one convenient category.
NEUTRAL EXAMINER: Card One is labeled field name Mara Quinn. Card Two is labeled Evelyn, protected subject. Both date to the same intake week at age seventeen.
MARA: Chain first.
EXAMINER: Victoria archive seal, Source Guardian transfer ledger, court custody, current lab receipt. No unexplained gap.
PRICE: Heritage accepts chain for examination, not identity conclusion.
MARA: Same.
EVELYN: I am developing an allergy to that word.
EXAMINER: Current exemplars were collected separately yesterday. I did not know which card counsel expected to match which adult.
MARA: Proceed.
EXAMINER: Fingerprint minutiae first. Card One matches current Mara Quinn. Card Two matches current Evelyn Vale.
[Silence.]
EVELYN: Say it again.
EXAMINER: Historical card labeled Mara Quinn maps to the current woman using Mara Quinn. Historical card labeled Evelyn maps to current Evelyn.
MARA: At seventeen.
EXAMINER: Correct. This proves body continuity from those cards to the current adults. It does not identify which newborn band belonged to which current woman.
PRICE: Thank you.
EVELYN: Nobody thanked you.
PRICE: I thanked the examiner.
MARA: Evelyn.
EVELYN: Fine.
EXAMINER: Facial ratios and old scar notes independently support the same mapping. Handwriting is consistent but not dispositive.
MARA: Any contradiction?
EXAMINER: None strong enough to change the conclusion.
MARA: Confidence?
EXAMINER: Very high for card-to-current-body mapping.
[Beat.]
MARA: So I was the historical girl the system called Mara Quinn at seventeen.
EXAMINER: Yes.
MARA: That does not make the stolen civil shell lawful.
EXAMINER: Correct.
MARA: It also does not make me Baby B.
EXAMINER: Correct.
EVELYN: But it does mean you existed separately before the yacht.
MARA: Yes.

SCENE 2 — HAWTHORNE RECORDS ROOM — LATE MORNING
[SFX: School archive drawer. Paper rustle.]
FORENSIC TECH: With the biometric mapping, we can now assign the Hawthorne school withdrawal file to current Mara with high confidence.
MARA: Show source chain.
FORENSIC TECH: Fingerprint card reference, school medical scar notation, handwriting sample and the same blue-ledger subject number.
ADRIAN: The blue ledger we traced before the Ravenmere fire review.
MARA: Yes. Existing evidence becomes interpretable. It does not become new evidence because we like the answer.
PRICE: That is an important distinction.
MARA: You can stop grading me.
PRICE: I am trying to survive this assignment.
FORENSIC TECH: Hawthorne record shows the Mara-labeled student withdrew after a protected-route exposure involving the Vale family event calendar.
ADRIAN: Greenhouse year.
MARA: Date?
FORENSIC TECH: Same month. Not same day.
MARA: Do not connect it yet.
ADRIAN: I did not.
MARA: Your face did.
ADRIAN: My face has poor counsel.
EVELYN: Finally, something we agree on.
FORENSIC TECH: There is also a security routing note referencing one temporary cross-cover between the two protected girls during that event season.
MARA: One?
FORENSIC TECH: One specifically preserved incident. We need the event file to know which route.
PRICE: That file contains private family-security material.
MARA: Then we ask for the route fields only.
PRICE: I can support that scope.
ADRIAN: You two are terrifying when aligned.
MARA: It is temporary.

SCENE 3 — BANK COMPLIANCE CALL — AFTERNOON
[SFX: Phone speaker. Laptop keyboard.]
BANK OFFICER: Ms. Quinn, your account remains open. We need updated identity documentation within thirty days because a court notice indicates your foundational civil record is under review.
MARA: Can salary and rent payments continue?
BANK OFFICER: Yes.
MARA: International transfer?
BANK OFFICER: Manual review above the normal threshold.
MARA: Card?
BANK OFFICER: Active.
MARA: Please send the exact document request in writing.
BANK OFFICER: We can also accept a stabilization order confirming you are a living claimant distinct from the deceased source record.
MARA: Price’s order.
BANK OFFICER: I do not know counsel names. I know the order type.
MARA: Fair.
PRICE: I am on the line because your counsel added me.
MARA: You are still on the line. I can hear you not breathing.
PRICE: Heritage can stipulate to that distinction today.
MARA: Without confidentiality?
PRICE: Without confidentiality. It says only that the current living Mara Quinn is not the deceased Mara Elise Quinn and that identity adjudication is pending.
MARA: I will take that.
PRICE: Then we agree on scope.
MARA: Do not mistake taking a narrow order for accepting your preferred final remedy.
PRICE: I was not going to.
MARA: You were thinking efficiently.
PRICE: That is my job.
NARRATOR: Mara accepted the order because it kept rent and work from collapsing while preserving the real dispute. Refusing every temporary measure would not make her more separate. It would only make her poorer.

SCENE 4 — MARA’S APARTMENT — EVENING
[SFX: Takeout containers. Radiator knocks twice.]
EVELYN: You accepted Price’s stabilization order.
MARA: Yes.
EVELYN: But not my consolidation idea.
MARA: Correct.
EVELYN: Explain the difference without making me feel stupid.
MARA: Your proposal put me under your existing certificate. Price’s narrow order says I am a separate living claimant whose foundation is disputed.
EVELYN: One borrows my identity. One protects yours while it is being built.
MARA: Yes.
EVELYN: I still think you could have said that yesterday.
MARA: I did not understand the difference cleanly yesterday.
EVELYN: That is new.
MARA: What?
EVELYN: You admitting the argument changed you.
MARA: Do not document it.
EVELYN: Too late.
[Beat.]
ADRIAN: Can I enter the food discussion or is this a constitutional moment?
MARA: You may distribute noodles.
ADRIAN: Finally, authority I understand.
[SFX: Containers slide.]
EVELYN: The cards prove you were Mara at seventeen.
MARA: They prove I was the body using that field name.
EVELYN: You really cannot let me have one sentence.
MARA: Not if it carries the wrong century.
EVELYN: Fine. Does it feel like anything?
[Mara pauses.]
MARA: Less than I expected. More than I want.
EVELYN: Same.
ADRIAN: I am going to eat until someone asks me a direct question.
MARA: Sensible.
[SFX: Phone notification.]
MARA: Event-route fields unsealed.
EVELYN: Greenhouse?
MARA: Maybe.
ADRIAN: Do you want me here when you read it?
MARA: No.
ADRIAN: All right.
MARA: I want to read it with Evelyn first.
[Evelyn looks at her.]
EVELYN: All right.
NARRATOR: The cards had done one clean thing. They had proved that the woman now called Mara Quinn was the same body recorded under that name at seventeen. For the first time, a piece of her history belonged to her without needing Noah, Adrian or a fire to make it true. The next file would test whether one of Adrian’s oldest memories belonged there too.
SCENE 5 — NIGHT SEQUENCE — HERITAGE REVIEW / MARA’S HALLWAY
PRICE: One additional point before tomorrow. The historical card labeled Mara Quinn does not cure the source theft, but it does change Heritage’s risk analysis.
MARA: How?
PRICE: We can no longer describe your present name as merely a post-fire fabrication. There is authenticated continuity of use at seventeen.
MARA: That helps.
PRICE: It helps one layer. It complicates another. If the teen knowingly preferred Quinn, an adult-name remedy becomes more plausible, but Heritage may face liability for maintaining a knowingly defective civil shell instead of pursuing lawful registration.
MARA: So evidence that helps me hurts your institution.
PRICE: Yes.
MARA: And you are telling me anyway.
PRICE: I am an officer of the court before I am a risk manager.
MARA: Keep saying things like that and I will have to reduce my hostility budget.
PRICE: Please do not. It keeps meetings efficient.
[Beat.]
PRICE: Also, Evelyn’s consolidation proposal is no longer Heritage’s preferred route.
MARA: Why?
PRICE: Because merging you under her certificate after proving separate historical bodies would create a fresh defect to solve an old one.
MARA: Then we do not create a new defect.
PRICE: You sound disappointed that I agree.
MARA: I had prepared a speech.
PRICE: Save it. We will disagree tomorrow.

[TRANSITION: Later, Mara’s hallway.]
EVELYN: Did the cards make you feel more real?
MARA: No.
EVELYN: Good answer.
MARA: They made one part of the timeline less arguable. Useful, yes. Not the same as making me more real.
EVELYN: I am trying to stop asking evidence to do emotional labor.
MARA: Keep trying.

END OF EPISODE
