## Audit law
Expansion is justified only where omission causes a story, character, relationship, causal, consequence or reader-engagement deficit. Word count alone is not a defect.

Classifications:
- `DEFICIT` — missing story function; unlock could be justified.
- `OPTIONAL_ENRICHMENT` — additional texture could work, but current story is complete without it.
- `DO_NOT_EXPAND` — expansion is more likely to weaken pace, mystery discipline, climax or resolution than improve the book.

## Whole-book result
**DEFICIT: 0.**

The manuscript is not behaving like an unfinished 62k draft. It is behaving like a deliberately compact procedural SF mystery/thriller. The current structure already performs the functions that an expansion would normally need to repair:
- ordinary rescue reality before anomaly;
- escalating warning/response feedback loop;
- Smith/OES entry after local competence is established;
- real human cost when Davor dies despite bounded intervention;
- experimental/counterfactual learning instead of lore revelation;

- Confederation advisory science without Earth jurisdiction capture;

- controlled series residue limited to precursor origin/mechanism UNKNOWN.

## Block-by-block expansion classification

### CH01–03 — warning hook / proof escalation
**DO NOT EXPAND.**
CH01 already opens inside ordinary 112 work, weather pressure, road/hydro constraints, Nika/Jana competence and Andrej's ordinary work before the impossible warning becomes real. The early book gains force because the strange material is surrounded by routine procedure rather than exposition. CH02–03 escalate from anomaly to useful warning and altered response. Extra setup would delay the engine.

### CH04–07 — Smith / Dublin / OES entry
**OPTIONAL_ENRICHMENT, NOT DEFICIT.**
This is the safest possible expansion zone if later external editorial evidence demands more length, but current text already gives Smith a life outside case-function: future Dublin as lived environment, Enia, books/workshop activity, and repeated daughter-message beats. The daughter is not merely a final tag; her repair/tool thread recurs before and during case escalation. Any expansion here must obey F1 and may not invent daughter name, mother, custody, household or backstory.

Potential reserve if explicitly unlocked later: +1,000–1,800 words of action-based ordinary life/OES routine, with zero new family ontology and zero lore lecture.

### CH08–10 — Davor / cost of silence and intervention
**DO NOT EXPAND.**
The blocked warning is allowed to remain unresolved long enough for present evidence and the precursor report to diverge. Davor has ordinary working interaction before the collapse; his death is not a disposable off-screen statistic. The sequence already creates the central moral pressure: bounded intervention changed conditions, but did not buy certainty or refund human cost. More aftermath here would weaken the forward pressure and duplicate later institutional reckoning.

### CH11–15 — forwarded warning / experiment / response-coupled evidence
**DO NOT EXPAND.**
This block is causal machinery. Its strength is disciplined progression from observation to discriminating test to evidence that the response itself can enter later precursor content. Adding explanation would turn discovery into lecture and reduce mystery fairness.

### CH16–18 — Morgen Pass / OES crime-memory / two futures
**OPTIONAL_ENRICHMENT WITH HIGH RISK.**
The book already contains the missing-people/report problem, historical doctrinal compression, Jana's challenge to abstraction, and the distinction between useful comparison and operational authority. This satisfies the OLD EARTH SECURITY ↔ NEW CONFEDERATION SECURITY engine. Additional OES history could deepen institutional weight, but it is the area most likely to become franchise lore or exposition.

Potential reserve only after a specific editorial note: +1,000–1,500 words tied to a present decision and a named human cost; no encyclopedia history, no precursor explanation, no Confederation command expansion.

### CH19–24 — real storm / distributed local ownership / failover / relay
**DO NOT EXPAND structurally; OPTIONAL MICRO-ENRICHMENT only.**
The storm shifts the novel from analysis to operational consequence. Tina/Andrej and local teams already carry work, joking, family/contact texture and field competence; central systems increasingly lose complete route ownership. The sequence must accelerate. A new standalone subplot would be harmful.

If later external evidence demands more character intimacy, the only defensible reserve is +800–1,500 words distributed inside existing work scenes, never a new chapter and never at the cost of operational tempo.

### CH25–28 — cascade / relay / best warning / third route
**DO NOT EXPAND.**
This is the climax chain. The present form already forces the central choice: the best forecast is not allowed to own the rescue; local observation and human route-finding produce the third route. Andrej survives but pays a lasting physical price. Additional action, technology or explanation would dilute climax ownership.

### CH29 — aftermath / accounting / Dublin reset
**DO NOT EXPAND by default.**
The chapter closes the current rescue, preserves human and institutional cost, returns Smith to ordinary life and completes the daughter/`BIT?` callback. The precursor mechanism remains UNKNOWN only after the Book One conflict is closed. Extra coda material risks over-explaining consequences or turning a clean ending into series advertising.

A publisher-requested aftercare expansion could be considered only if the requested function is explicit; it must not invent Andrej diagnosis/prognosis or family facts and must not reopen the case.
