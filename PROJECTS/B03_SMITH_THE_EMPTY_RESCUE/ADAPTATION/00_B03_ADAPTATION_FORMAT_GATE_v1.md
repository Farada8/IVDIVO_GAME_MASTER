## Decision

**Recommended screen format: 4 × 50–55 minute limited series.**

This is an adaptation-production choice, not saga canon and not a change to the novel.

## Why 4 × 50–55

The source is a compact 62,300-word procedural SF thriller with a complete conflict, multiple causal casualty branches, a late-arriving Smith axis, strong local-command characters, a historical OES reveal, a real-weather escalation and a contained climax.

Four episodes preserve the complete causal engine without requiring filler:
1. impossible warning → reversible intervention → Unit Four benefit/cost → Smith/OES arrival;
2. suppression doctrine → Davor cost → Tina/Andrej exception → proof that response architecture enters the warning;
3. Morgen Pass truth → two futures → real storm → distributed-command/relay decision;
4. relay/cascade → Andrej rescue choice → institutional accounting → Dublin daughter closure.

## Rejected / secondary formats

### 6 × 45–50 — NOT RECOMMENDED AT CURRENT SOURCE LENGTH
Likely failure mode: invented subplots, repeated precursor tests, franchise lore, or character padding to manufacture episode mass. That would violate Story > volume.

### 110–120 minute feature — POSSIBLE SECONDARY OPTION
Commercially plausible, but high compression risk. Likely casualties: Luka/Davor distinction, Nika’s unauthorized disclosure, Morgen Pass moral ambiguity, Jana’s lawful-authority arc, and OES↔Confederation asymmetry. A feature version should be developed only as a separate compression exercise after the limited-series architecture is stable.

### Full audio drama adaptation
A separate downstream branch. The current audiobook/dramatized-audio pipeline already has its own authority and live-provider gate; do not merge its production metadata with this screen adaptation.

## Adaptation laws

- Preserve Smith as HERO even though the screen hook begins with Nika.
- Nika is the pilot incident-carrier, not secretly promoted into a replacement protagonist.
- Jana remains lawful local operational authority; Smith never becomes generic outside commander.
- OES history is both useful and compromised.
- Confederation science is broader but has no Earth command jurisdiction.
- Precursor calls are observable information first; origin/ontology remains UNKNOWN.
- Do not encode precursor voices as supernatural through acting, score or VFX.
- Luka and Davor must not be collapsed into one casualty: they prove different causal failures.
- Tina/Andrej disclosure must remain unauthorized but causally meaningful.
- Andrej’s final injury remains severe/lasting without invented precise diagnosis.
- Final rescue must not prove that ignoring the warning was “correct”; the counterfactual remains unknowable.
- CH29 institutional resolution must remain on-screen before any series hook.
- F1 family scope: Smith has a teenage daughter; no new mother/divorce/custody/household facts.
