## 3.1 Role origin

**INHERITED causal seed:** B02 relocation staff needed young-route reality checks; Maya did them with staff confirmation and no decision authority.

**WORKING NEW B04 FACT:** after several weeks of repeated migration/service-route checking, the local mobility/service operator has formalized a small paid **route-validation assistant** placement for youth/late-teen participants and other supervised newcomers.

Purpose:
- physically walk traveler-facing routes;

- flag route changes and accessibility/attention problems;
- record handoff reality against the map;
- never authorize habitat emergency policy or high-consequence life-safety changes.

Adult/service staff retain final sign-off.

## 3.2 Maya's concrete want

At B04 opening Maya is already receiving base pay for limited shifts.

She wants the next-cycle **independent low-consequence route-validation endorsement**: the ability to sign routine traveler-facing corrections inside a narrow service class without waiting for a staff member to repeat the same physical walk.

This endorsement matters because it gives her:
- more paid shifts;
- more scheduling independence;
- stronger claim to remain in orbital work on her own merits after the original cohort/placement structure becomes less central.

No salary number or immigration/housing entitlement is invented.

## 3.3 Primary ordinary-life price — LOCKED FOR WORKING B04 ENGINE

**WORKING ENGINE LOCK:** Maya's **current endorsement window is finite**. If she does not complete the required observed evaluation shifts inside the current service cycle, she remains a base assistant and must wait for the next cycle before reapplying.

This is the ONE primary ordinary-life price.

It is not expulsion, poverty melodrama or parental punishment.
It matters because:
- she has done enough work to want the upgrade for reasons beyond status;
- the incident begins during the final evaluation window;
- her early externality-blind decision causes the service supervisor to pause one assessment, not because Maya broke a rule, but because the effect on other users reveals a real competence gap;
- at the climax, the fast local option would still let her complete the final required evaluation action on time, while the slower shared-safe sequence will cause the current endorsement window to expire.

Maya knowingly lets the window expire.

### Resolution payoff
She keeps the base paid role if ordinary post-incident review allows it. She does **not** receive the endorsement as a heroic reward. The next cycle exists, but she must re-enter it normally.

This closes the ordinary-life price without manufacturing a permanent career catastrophe.

# 6. B04 STORY CORE — NORMALIZED

## HERO
**Maya — WORKING preferred lead.**

## CO-LEAD
**Smith — historical evidence owner / institutional liability bearer; no orbital command.**

## HERO WANT
Earn the current independent low-consequence route-validation endorsement and establish that her orbital working life can continue beyond the special status of being an Earth youth-cohort arrival.

## PRIVATE DESIRE
Be treated as somebody who belongs in Vela's ordinary working systems, not as a visiting teenager whose competence is always provisional.

## WHY NOW
During Maya's final endorsement evaluation window, two apparently empty orbital spaces trigger the same rare `unattributed occupancy / local actuation` incident code. One unified emergency response begins to make one site safer while worsening or obscuring the other.

## OPPOSITION
- one-code incident model under uncertainty;
- competent orbital incident commander responsible for system-wide externalities;
- civic/housing/service pressure for a simple answer and restored routes;
- OES legacy category and evidence restrictions;
- Confederation analysis that can identify Incident A's living ecology class but cannot explain Incident B;
- Maya's own belief that transparent in-scope action plus willingness to pay direct cost is sufficient responsibility.

## WRONG STRATEGY
`If I tell the truth, stay inside my authority and accept the cost to me, my legitimate local choice should stand.`

## PRICE
Primary ordinary-life price: current endorsement cycle.
Secondary human inconvenience only: lost/missed ordinary social time and friction with local users. Do not stack housing catastrophe or romance loss.

## MIDPOINT
A controlled comparison proves:
1. the two incidents are not one causal family;
2. Incident A is substantially explainable as a lawful living support ecology misclassified by human/orbital semantics;
3. Incident B is operationally material-bound but ontologically unresolved;
4. OES's historical umbrella class pooled unlike response clusters;
5. Confederation discrimination is superior for A but cannot solve B or own local response.

Both old and new frameworks lose monopoly on the answer.

## CLIMAX CHOICE
The split responses compete for shared service redundancy/time.
The fast local sequence would improve Maya's own zone and preserve her final endorsement evaluation window but export risk/service loss to the other group.

Maya chooses the slower split sequence that protects the external population/environment, knowingly lets her endorsement window expire, acts only inside her bounded service authority, and leaves habitat-wide command to the incident commander.

In parallel Smith releases the damaging OES classification/outcome ledger required to make the split operationally defensible, accepting independent review of the historical category.

## RESOLUTION
- Incident A's living ecology is preserved/relocated and locally reclassified.
- Incident B's material component is immobilized under controlled evidence custody; ontology remains UNKNOWN.
- the current emergency ends under two response classes.
- OES umbrella classification becomes explicitly non-unitary / under review.
- local authority remains local.
- Confederation advisory channel closes or returns to ordinary bounded status; no permanent team.
- Maya remains in the base paid service role, misses the current endorsement cycle, and may reapply next cycle through normal procedure.
- friendship/ordinary life resumes after the case rather than being replaced by franchise duty.

## SERIES HOOK — AFTER RESOLUTION ONLY
One historical non-OES/non-Earth record contains an evidence-grade correspondence to **one** separated subtype. It is not origin proof, not universal theory and not a call to assemble a permanent team.
