# 2. REFERENCE-MECHANISM PASS — ABSTRACT ONLY

Reference mechanisms used at this stage are generic craft mechanisms, not borrowed plots:

1. **Procedural jurisdiction conflict** — institutional/procedural stories gain pressure when competent actors have overlapping but non-identical authority, evidence and duties. Jurisdiction itself can generate countermoves rather than merely delay the plot.
2. **Central protagonist inside multi-POV institutional conflict** — multiple perspectives can expose the larger system, but one protagonist must still own the book's decisive desire, price and climax choice.
3. **Science fiction as social consequence** — speculative mechanisms matter because they alter law, work, social relations, ownership, responsibility and ordinary life; technology/anomaly cannot substitute for story.
4. **Experienced-reader freshness** — familiar crossover/procedural shapes must become fresh through character-specific perception, conflicting legitimate duties and a non-obvious causal split, not merely a larger threat.

Transformation rule applied:
`REFERENCE MECHANISM -> B01–B03 CONSEQUENCE FILTER -> B04 ORIGINAL ENGINE`.

# 3. ENGINE COMPETITION

## ENGINE A — TWO CASES / ONE CODE
Status: WORKING CANDIDATE A

### Premise
Two separate orbital incidents are automatically and institutionally merged because they trigger the same rare official anomaly/emergency classification. The station therefore applies one response model to both. A youth field participant notices that the physical sequences are not equivalent. Smith is brought in when an OES archive cross-reference matches the same umbrella category. His archive initially appears to support the single-event theory — until an old after-action discrepancy suggests OES historically bundled different phenomena under one operational label. Confederation science can discriminate modern signatures but cannot interpret the lost historical field context.

The danger is not that one side is stupid. The danger is that every institution has compressed different reality for a legitimate reason.

### Youth-lead candidate
**MAYA — WORKING LEAD CANDIDATE, NOT LOCKED.**
Reason: portfolio freshness. Ethan already led B01; Ollie already led B02. Samir/Aoife have strong evidence/privacy functions that can support the investigation without repeating their prior arcs. Maya supplies embodied orbital movement, ordinary-life stakes and a less-used central POV.

Exact B04 job/placement remains to be continuity-normalized. Working requirement only: Maya has an earned orbital service/work responsibility that puts her in direct contact with the two incidents without giving her station-wide command.

### HERO
Maya.

### WANT
Keep the independent orbital life/work responsibility she has earned and get the affected people/services back to ordinary life without outsiders converting the incident into an excuse to remove local agency.

### WHY NOW
Two incidents hit the same rare code close enough together that the station merges them into one spreading event. A one-model response begins producing incompatible results.

### OPPOSITION
- a competent orbital incident authority whose duty is to treat same-signature unknowns as one until evidence supports a split;
- incomplete OES archives and secrecy boundaries;
- limited Confederation analysts who can measure more than they are entitled to command;
- public/service/housing pressure demanding one simple answer;
- Maya's own confidence in witnessed physical sequence as sufficient basis for wider action.

### WRONG STRATEGY
`I was there; I can show the two sequences differ; therefore the system should split them now.`

This is not Book-One rule rebellion. Maya is telling the truth and may be substantively right. Her mistake is treating local eyewitness/operational knowledge as enough to externalize risk onto people outside her scope.

### PRICE
- risk to her earned independent work status;
- residents/workers remain displaced while the dispute continues;
- a friend/source relationship is strained when evidence Maya wants to use belongs partly to somebody else;
- Smith risks OES credibility and institutional protection if he exposes the archive's historical conflation.

### MIDPOINT
A controlled comparison proves the two incidents are **not one causal family**. Simultaneously it proves something more damaging: OES's old umbrella category preserved useful response observations but conceptually conflated unlike events, while the Confederation model correctly separates signatures but lacks the historical variable needed to decide which current response belongs where.

Both sides lose the right to say `our framework already contains the answer`.

### SECOND-HALF ESCALATION
- one intervention improves Incident A while worsening/obscuring Incident B;
- archival records reveal that historical OES outcome statistics were contaminated by subtype conflation;
- public narrative still treats both as one spreading anomaly;
- local authority cannot simply cancel all precautions because the split is proven before the full mechanism is known;
- Maya, Smith and the local commander must construct two different bounded operational models from evidence that was never designed to be combined.

### CLIMAX CHOICE
Maya must choose whether to use her bounded local authority/responsibility to execute the split response in the zone she actually knows while **refusing to claim the whole-system certainty she has spent the book demanding**. Smith's paired climax choice is to release the damaging OES historical failure record needed to make the split legally/operationally defensible, rather than preserve institutional authority by offering only the useful rule.

The local orbital commander retains broader command and changes tactics when the pre-agreed evidence threshold is crossed.

### RESOLUTION
The two present incidents are resolved under two different response classes. Ordinary orbital life resumes unevenly but concretely. The old OES umbrella classification is retired or formally marked non-unitary. Confederation analysis also gains a new limit: similar modern signatures do not imply common ontology. Maya keeps or loses some professional privilege according to the cost actually earned, but she exits with a more mature distinction between firsthand truth, proof sufficient for her action, and proof sufficient for imposing risk on others.

### SERIES HOOK
Only after resolution: one historical record from outside the Earth/OES chain contains a correspondence to one of the separated subtypes. It is evidence-grade correspondence, not origin proof.

### WHY THE CROSSOVER IS NECESSARY
- Maya/orbital workers know the lived physical sequence the normalized incident model erased.
- Smith/OES knows historical operational outcomes and the hidden classification history.
- Confederation science can separate present signatures at a resolution Earth/OES never had.
- local orbital authority alone lacks the archive; OES alone lacks modern discrimination and jurisdiction; Confederation alone lacks historical context and jurisdiction.

### ANTI-REPETITION
Not B02: no coupled commissioning/control-loop failure, no Ollie protagonist, no `one early warning saves Ring Six` engine.
Not B03: no precursor call/rescue/storm; no OES recognized-warning case; Smith does not own local tactics.
Not B08: no moving-corridor/sealed-transfer-spine engine and no Boundary Order Divergence.

## ENGINE B — THE EVIDENCE QUARANTINE
Status: WORKING CANDIDATE B

### Premise
A routine orbital incident produces a citizen-owned recording/sensor package that contains the only preserved context distinguishing an apparently impossible event from ordinary system noise. OES recognizes a historical evidentiary pattern and asks that raw material be restricted; local orbital privacy law protects the subjects; Confederation analysis can test the pattern but cannot compel disclosure. The investigation becomes a fight over who may use evidence, under what terms, while a second event approaches.

### HERO
Aoife — WORKING candidate.

### WANT
Do serious public-interest work without converting other people's lives into material she owns.

### WRONG STRATEGY
After learning restraint, she overcorrects into believing that refusing release is the safest ethical position whenever proof and consent are incomplete.

### MIDPOINT
A source-consented bounded analysis proves the raw context contains an operational discriminator unavailable anywhere else. Withholding everything is no longer neutral; unrestricted release is still unacceptable.

### CLIMAX CHOICE
Aoife must surrender exclusivity/control over the evidence into a tightly bounded operational chain, sacrificing the story she could own in order to make the evidence usable without turning private people into public anomaly subjects.

### RESOLUTION
Immediate incident resolved; a durable evidence-rights protocol is created locally; public account remains incomplete by design.

### STRENGTH
Strong character evolution, rights, privacy, institutional tension, non-military crossover.

### RISK
MAJOR CANDIDATE: too much screen/archive/data work; climax can become a correct-information-sharing formulation rather than embodied action. Also close to Aoife's earlier privacy/authorship development unless the external physical plot is exceptionally strong.

## ENGINE C — THE CLAIMED HABITAT
Status: WORKING CANDIDATE C

### Premise
A current orbital housing/service dispute becomes a sovereignty problem when a nonhuman historical record appears to recognize an old Earth custodial obligation associated with a physical habitat system. Current residents have lawful present rights. OES has fragmentary history of the old obligation and evidence that Earth authorities previously concealed its cost. Confederation Contact can authenticate the record class but cannot adjudicate local property/civic law.

### HERO
Youth lead TBD; Maya is plausible but not assumed.

### WANT
Protect an ordinary home/work future already earned in orbital society.

### WRONG STRATEGY
Treat historical legitimacy and current lived legitimacy as mutually exclusive: either the present residents own the problem completely or the old claim overrides them.

### MIDPOINT
The historical instrument is authentic but does **not** mean what either side first claims: it creates a bounded duty/access relationship, not ownership.

### CLIMAX CHOICE
The youth lead must support a solution that preserves resident rights while accepting a concrete costly obligation to an external/historical claimant, instead of winning by invalidating one side.

### RESOLUTION
Current civic dispute fully resolved under a new bounded local arrangement; no permanent interstellar authority is created.

### STRENGTH
Very different from B02/B03; uses housing, law, sovereignty and ordinary life; naturally dramatizes `knowledge != jurisdiction`.

### RISK
MAJOR: the story can become treaty/lore exposition. `Old agreement` is an existing OPTION specifically forbidden from auto-promotion. Requires a much stronger physical/action spine before selection.

# 5. CURRENT INTEGRATED DECISION

## WORKING WINNER
**ENGINE A — TWO CASES / ONE CODE**

Status: **PREFERRED WORKING ENGINE / NOT CANON / NOT STORY-LOCKED.**

Why it wins:
- directly fulfills the B04 reveal mandate rather than merely carrying saga lore;
- creates a necessary crossover from asymmetric competence, not fan service;
- permits a youth lead to remain causal while Smith remains an adult co-lead with his own price;
- gives the local orbital institution a legitimate opposing duty;
- turns `old system vs new system` into a test where both are right and wrong in different ways;
- can reveal civilization through work, housing/service disruption, public narrative, privacy, authority and ordinary orbital routines;
- does not require daughter kidnapping, Agreement 14, Sanctuary Clause, permanent agency, cosmic villain or Root/Matrix answer.

## WORKING YOUTH LEAD
**MAYA — preferred for next Character/Relationship competition, NOT LOCKED.**

Reasons:
- avoids third consecutive Ethan/Ollie centrality;
- gives the crossover embodied orbital causality rather than an archive-first feel;
- her prior growth can be inherited rather than reset;
- Aoife, Samir, Ethan and Ollie can remain differentiated support pressures rather than equal-airtime committee voices.

## SMITH FUNCTION
Co-lead / historical evidence owner / institutional liability bearer.
He does not command orbital response.
His decisive contribution is not `I know the monster`; it is identifying that OES's classification history itself is contaminated and paying the price to expose that fact.
