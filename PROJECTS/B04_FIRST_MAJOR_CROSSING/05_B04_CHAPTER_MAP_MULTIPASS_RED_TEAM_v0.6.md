# 2. BINDING REPAIR A — MAYA'S AUTHORITY AT CLIMAX

## Problem in v0.5
CH01 says Maya proposes routine corrections and waits for staff sign-off because she does **not** yet hold the independent endorsement. CH26–27 then made her effectively `sign` a system-relevant FAST/SLOW sequence. That risks giving her more authority in the climax than the book's ordinary-life engine permits.

## Repair
Keep the moral/physical choice, but separate **choice of route implementation** from **authorization of system resources**.

### Base-role authority — WORKING LOCK
Maya's current paid base role may:
- walk and validate traveler-facing routes;
- activate or select among **already pre-cleared** detours/route implementations during a supervised shift;
- report downstream dependency observations;
- execute assigned traveler/service handoffs.

Maya's current base role may NOT:
- independently certify a new permanent route correction;
- allocate habitat-wide redundancy;
- authorize living-ecology handling;
- authorize anomalous evidence movement/containment;
- change emergency classification.

The desired endorsement would allow independent sign-off only for a narrow class of routine low-consequence traveler-facing corrections after required observation/evaluation.

### Climax authority geometry — replacement for CH26–27 wording
Kira sets the habitat-wide safety envelope and allocates the same bounded resource limits for both viable sequences.
Environmental specialists own Incident A handling. Evidence/containment staff own B-17 custody. The route/service supervisor pre-clears two route implementations because both remain inside those hard limits.

Maya, as the on-shift route-validation operator who physically knows the affected approaches, is assigned the **implementation choice between the two pre-cleared route sequences**. She does not approve resources or emergency policy.

- FAST remains legal and genuinely useful locally.
- SLOW remains legal and protects downstream people/environment at the cost of Maya's current endorsement window.

Maya selects/executes SLOW. Her route/service supervisor confirms the implementation under ordinary supervisory authority.

### Dialogue/wording prohibition
Do not write `Maya signs the emergency plan` or equivalent.
Preferred action vocabulary:
`selects / submits / activates / executes the pre-cleared route sequence`.
