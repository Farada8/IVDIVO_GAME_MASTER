# 2. PHENOMENON-PAIR COMPETITION

The engine requires two current incidents that can reasonably receive one surface classification but demand different responses.

Hard rules:
- observable phenomenon first;
- no universal ontology;
- no B03 call/precursor repetition;
- no B02 coupled environmental-control failure;

## PAIR P1 — FALSE OCCUPANCY / TWO KINDS OF PRESENCE
**PREFERRED WORKING PAIR.**

### Shared surface signature
A local habitat/service space behaves as **occupied and locally active** even though no registered person is physically present:
- environmental demand changes as if a body/occupant is present;
- one or more local interfaces/actuators enter a legitimate occupied-state branch;
- no credentialed human/nonhuman resident can be identified as the actor;
- physical sweep does not find an ordinary occupant.

Reader-facing language should stay concrete: `the room keeps behaving as if someone is in it`.

### Incident A — LIVING SUPPORT ECOLOGY
Working causal family: a lawful nonhuman living environmental system / distributed support ecology in a mixed-species residential or service module.

It is not a hidden monster and not a person-shaped alien. It is part of how a resident/environment normally lives.

Why the station misreads it:
- the human/orbital incident taxonomy counts `occupant` and `environmental support` as different categories;
- this living system crosses that boundary by producing real metabolic/electrical/environmental demand and limited local interface activity;
- the raw observations are true; the label is wrong.

Confederation contribution:
- recognizes the general biological/environmental class;
- can identify metabolic/distributed-life evidence;
- does NOT own local command and does NOT know the second incident.

Wrong response if incidents are merged:
- blanket purge/shutdown/isolation treats a lawful living support system as contamination and can make the resident environment less safe.

Correct bounded response:
- preserve/relocate the ecology under proper environmental conditions;
- change local classification/sensor semantics;
- manage the household/service impact as a normal rights/safety problem.

### Incident B — MATERIAL-BOUND UNATTRIBUTED ACTUATION
Working causal family: an unresolved event attached to a **specific removable material component**, not to a person, room population or distributed living system.

Observable facts:
- the anomalous occupied-state/actuation signature follows one component when the component is physically moved;
- it persists after people leave and after normal network command paths are excluded;
- no metabolic signature equivalent to Incident A;
- when the component is absent, the original space stops producing the same event.

OES contribution:
- old Earth files contain carrier/material-bound cases with similar operational behavior;
- OES never established ontology;
- historical response notes show that removing/isolating the material carrier changed outcome;
- the old umbrella classification merged these cases with other `presence without attributable person` reports.

Wrong response if incidents are merged:
- treating B as a habitat-wide biological exposure causes the component to keep moving through service logistics while people/rooms are isolated unnecessarily.

Correct bounded response:
- stop the component's movement;
- preserve chain of custody;
- move it to a controlled unoccupied cradle/container under local authority;
- keep ontology UNKNOWN.

### Why P1 is strong
- immediately visual and physical;
- one case is living social infrastructure, one case is material-bound unknown;
- modern science is clearly better at A; OES history is clearly better at B;
- Maya's route/service knowledge can notice `A stays with habitat ecology; B follows hardware movement`;
- local residents/housing/work are directly affected;
- no universal theory is needed.

### Science caution
The exact biology/interface mechanisms must remain fictional-but-constrained. Do not claim one raw sensor magically identifies a person. The misclassification should emerge from a real bundle of environmental demand + local automation semantics, not a single impossible detector.

# 3. JURISDICTION GEOMETRY

## MAYA / YOUTH SERVICE ROLE — WORKING
KNOWS: physical sequence, route/handoff reality, what moved when, local user behavior.
AUTHORITY: only the work/service actions assigned to her role.
CANNOT: order station evacuation, classify anomaly for whole habitat, access classified OES archive by default.
LIABILITY: actions inside her assigned operational scope; inaccurate handoff/route decisions affect real users.

## ORBITAL INCIDENT COMMANDER
KNOWS: station-wide risk picture, emergency resources, current sensor/incident model, local legal duty.
AUTHORITY: habitat emergency response, closures, evacuation/service restrictions inside current law.
CANNOT: compel Confederation sovereign action; rewrite OES archive custody unilaterally; ignore resident rights without emergency basis.
LIABILITY: everyone in habitat, including people Maya never meets.
DRAMATIC POSITION: legitimate opposition when Maya wants to split the incidents before system-wide burden of proof is met.

## CIVIC / HOUSING / SERVICE AUTHORITY
KNOWS: displaced households, access, service continuity, work/school/housing costs.
AUTHORITY: allocation/compensation/service routing inside civic scope.
CANNOT: decide anomaly ontology or take emergency command.
DRAMATIC POSITION: makes delay costly without becoming reckless villain.

## SMITH / OES
KNOWS: legacy case classes, outcome histories, hidden classification changes, historical failures.
AUTHORITY: OES evidence custody and old-Earth-security action only where legally applicable.
CANNOT: command orbital response; seize local evidence merely by claiming legacy relevance; define Confederation science.
LIABILITY: truthfulness of OES evidence and consequences of OES doctrine/history.

## PLANETARY / CONTACT COORDINATION
KNOWS: inter-institutional agreements, access channels, political consequences.
AUTHORITY: coordination/request channel.
CANNOT: substitute for orbital local command or Confederation authority.

## CONFEDERATION TECHNICAL ADVISORY
KNOWS: living support ecology class, advanced analysis, comparative nonhuman-system knowledge.
AUTHORITY: analysis/advice only under permitted Contact channel.
CANNOT: order Solar-System local actions, certify OES history, infer B's ontology from similarity.
LIABILITY: epistemic precision — must distinguish `known class A` from `unknown B`.

### Jurisdiction conflict rule
Every major disagreement must be expressible as:
`WHAT I KNOW` ≠ `WHAT I MAY ORDER` ≠ `WHAT I WILL BE BLAMED FOR`.

3. stops the Incident-B component's movement;
4. executes only the local handoff she is authorized to perform;
5. leaves habitat-wide command with the incident commander.

Her change is not obedience.
It is the realization that **responsibility includes people who were never in the room when your boundary was negotiated.**
