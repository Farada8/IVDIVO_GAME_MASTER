## 2. HUMAN SCENE SPINE

### SCENE A — A ROUTE THAT IS RIGHT ON THE SCREEN
**Maya wants:** finish the first of her final observed shifts cleanly and get away on time for food with Lea.

**Rhea wants:** see whether Maya tests the route from the actual user's approach instead of defending the display.

**Resistance:** Maya's proposed correction is technically valid but its first instruction arrives after a traveler has already committed to the wrong branch.

**Action:** Rhea refuses to debate the map and makes Maya walk it from the arrival side.

**Change:** Maya finds the flaw herself. This proves B02 growth: she accepts physical verification and does not treat being right once as authority.

**Dialogue dynamic:**
- Maya argues from route behavior.
- Rhea answers with action requests, not philosophy.
- Rhea should interrupt any abstract defense with a physical test.
- status = supervisor / trainee, but Maya is not timid.

### SCENE B — THE PEOPLE WHO ACTUALLY USE IT
**Maya wants:** place the correction early enough to pass the observed task.

**Resistance:** commuters, school handoff traffic, service carts and one mixed-species household-support movement make the obvious location worse at the wrong time.

**Action:** Maya walks two approaches and notices different users see different signs. She proposes the earlier sign + one low-band reinforcement.

**Rhea response:** checks operations restrictions before confirming. Maya waits for confirmation.

**Change:** Maya earns a clean observed action, but not independent authority.

**World reveal:**
- ordinary mixed-species orbital traffic;
- route boards that update around freight/support windows;
- paid youth/newcomer service work;
- no lore lecture.

### SCENE C — REDUNDANCY IS NOT EMPTY SPACE
**Maya wants:** use a faster alternate path to finish early.

**Resistance:** Rhea shows the path is intentionally unavailable during a scheduled household/environmental support window because the same service capacity is reserved elsewhere.

**Key rule to seed:** capacity that appears unused locally may already belong to someone downstream.

Do NOT state the theme. Maya may call the constraint annoying or overprotective; Rhea only gives the practical reason.

**Change:** shared redundancy enters reader memory as boring infrastructure.

### SCENE D — ORDINARY SOCIAL CLOCK
Lea is not present physically unless pacing wants a brief crossing. Prefer device messages so CH01 stays work-centered.

Working social beat:
- Lea confirms a small food/meet plan after Maya's shift.
- Lea has her own cutoff/schedule; she will not wait indefinitely.
- Maya wants to make it.

No apology, no privacy recap, no relationship speech.

### SCENE E — THE FIRST INTERRUPTION
At the end of the observed route check, before Maya can leave:
- traveler board changes;
- a residential/service path becomes restricted;
- rare code is visible in operational language only;
- registered-person count does not match the occupied/local-actuation state.

Maya's first reaction should be practical: the closure destroys the route she just validated / threatens the evaluation slot / blocks people who were already committed to it.

Do NOT show the whole empty room yet. CH02 owns the first physical inspection.

**EXIT QUESTION:** Why is the station treating an empty zone as occupied?

## 4. RHEA MALIK VOICE CONTRACT — WORKING / NOT CANON

Rhea is not Nadia Park.

**Function:** route/service supervisor for Maya's observed endorsement shifts.

**Voice:**
- economical;
- dry;
- uses operational verbs;
- lets Maya discover errors by walking them;
- mild humor through understatement;
- never becomes a teacher delivering the book's thesis.

**Rhea objectives in CH01:**
1. test whether Maya verifies from user approach;
2. prevent Maya from confusing valid route logic with independent authority;
3. keep the observed shift on schedule.

**Rhea resistance tools:**
- `Walk it.`
- ask which user is approaching from where;
- ask what other service owns the same window;
- wait silently for Maya to see the problem.

Do not overuse any one phrase as a motif.
